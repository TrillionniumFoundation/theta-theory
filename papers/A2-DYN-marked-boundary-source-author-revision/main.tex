\begin{abstract}
We study exact return, displacement, collision and physical-time records
in the original triangular Lorentz gas for $R\in[9/20,47/100]$.
A true marked-clearance angular source has a uniform momentum kernel
at every collision, including its positive-side zero-clearance trace.
This gives marked incidence moments of every order $1\le\gamma<2$
without changing the conditional event.
An exact boundary-flux identity retains the physical normal velocity,
and an optical split at the actual marked foot determines both endpoint
incidence factors. On the specified zero-offset critical components,
a uniform inverse-gradient Taylor cancellation bounds that velocity
geometrically in the mark's endpoint depth. The complete covered
sum over marks of proportional endpoint depth consequently decays
after the original collision normalization.
Finally, common-minimum localization compares any two actual records
with the same lifted word and matching interior homogeneity strips.
It gives uniform cross-curve distortion with the exact terminal
incidence, stable and optical factors; a genuine terminal grazing
family proves why that factor must be retained.
All preceding proof bodies, including complete weak-roof and singular
multiplier estimates, remain in the manuscript.
These results advance the original raw pointwise local limit and
four-coordinate inverse problem. Nonzero-offset and uncovered boundary
charges, shallow-source capacity, unrestricted pressure and the final
uniform spectral and conditional interfaces remain explicitly open.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Introduction}
A physical collision record carries more information than the
displacement of a particle at a large time.  In the triangular
Lorentz gas considered here, the observations are the two integer
lattice coordinates, the total unit-speed physical time, and the
integer occupation of a specified collision section.  Conditioning
on the original initial and terminal section tests identifies the
actual return event.  The problem is to obtain its raw pointwise
local limit, with all collision labels and microscopic roof windows
retained, and then the four-coordinate local inverse theorem.

The fixed family consists of equal circular scatterers on the
triangular lattice, with $R\in[9/20,47/100]$.  The collision
probability is $d\alpha\,dp/(4\pi)$ and its original section
normalization is $c_*=91/(10000\pi)$.  The event
\eqref{eq:v76-event}, arithmetic reference \eqref{eq:v76-reference}
and target \eqref{eq:v76-target} are unchanged.  In particular
occupation counts the initial state and excludes the terminal
state, whose membership is tested separately.  All reflections are
actual first collisions.  The physical flight bounds
$\tau_-=3/50$ and $\tau_+\le\sqrt{3847/124}<28/5$ and the finite
lattice budgets are proved in Section~\ref{sec:eh-horizon}.

\subsection*{The complete comparison problem and its inherited gap}
For an actual regular reference $Q_0,\ldots,Q_m$ with ordered
disk centers $O_i$, minimize
\[
 A(Y)=\sum_{i=0}^{m-1}|Y_{i+1}-Y_i|
 \quad\hbox{over}\quad
 \mathcal K=\prod_{i=0}^{m}\overline B(O_i,R).
\]
A minimum of this compact convex problem need not be a physical
billiard record.  Its contacts can be inactive and its flights can
meet unlisted obstacles.  Those possibilities are retained throughout
the comparison argument.

The inherited dual certificate $D_Q$ and positive gap
$G=A(Y)-D_Q$ satisfy, on the original length sublevel,
\[
 0\le G\le E_R(Q),\qquad E_R(Q)=R(2-c_0-c_m).
\]
With $\delta_i=Y_i-Q_i$, $e_i$ the difference of the unit edge
directions, $F_i=\ell_i|e_i|^2$ and
$H_i=c_i|\delta_i|^2$, the flight and contact parts consume one
shared positive gap.  The preceding complete sublevel theorem,
Theorem~\ref{thm:ss-quartic}, already proves
$\sum_i|\delta_i|^4\le10^8E_R(Q)$, including fixed-word closed
physical references.  The exact all-center clearance and observation
transport of module 303 is also inherited.  Neither result is
reclassified as new here.

\subsection*{Localization of every minimum without a physical path}
The retained Lemma~\ref{lem:bl-caccioppoli} uses the Cartesian minimum
condition in every disk.  It bounds the relative contact and
direction energy inside a cutoff by work in its two transition
layers.  All comparison edges remain separated from zero length,
so this argument applies at inactive contacts as well as at
physical minima.  The physical turning theorem supplies an
interior contact with $c_i\ge a_0=1/416640$ in every complete
$N=94$-flight reference block.  It follows from the explicitly
proved lattice horizon and bending bound, not an abstract
hyperbolic comparison model.

The first consequence, Theorem~\ref{thm:bl-tail}, is the uniform
algebraic estimate
\[
 \sum_{i=d}^{m-d}|\delta_i|^4
 \le\min\{10^8E_R(Q),K_{\rm tail}(d+1)^{-3}\}.
\]
Its proof pays the cutoff work, obtains a nonlinear recurrence and
checks every depth including an isolated central contact.  It also
provides localized contact and flight energies.  The full proof
is retained because its constants and its exact mechanism give
independent useful comparison bounds.

The anchor interval identity permits a stronger conclusion.
Define the full energy at depth $d$ by
\[
 \mathcal E_d=\frac1R
  \sum_{\substack{0<i<m\\ d\le i\le m-d}}H_i
            +\frac12\sum_{i=d}^{m-d-1}F_i.
\]
Theorem~\ref{thm:ae-layer} proves
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ae-intro}
 \mathcal E_{d+94}\le q_{\rm a}\mathcal E_d,\qquad
 \mathcal E_d\le E_R(Q)q_{\rm a}^{\lfloor d/94\rfloor},
 \qquad q_{\rm a}=\frac{3263680}{3263681}<1.
\end{equation}
The two chosen anchors pay the exact work at both cuts from the
removed layer.  Their contact energy is disjoint from the interior
energy being estimated.  Young's inequality retains both cut
flights, including the case when the cuts share a single flight.
The initial energy is at most $G$, hence at most $E_R(Q)$.
This proves a contraction of the complete energy with no
collision-count multiplier.

The constants in \eqref{eq:ae-intro} are conservative.  Their
mathematical significance is that they are fixed uniformly before
all word and collision limits.  The deterministic $q_{\rm a}$
does not replace the original guard constant $q_0=47/53$.
Lemma~\ref{lem:ae-common-minima} further proves that all global
minima have the same unit directions, active index set and active
vertices.  Their possible differences lie along inactive straight
chains.  No uniqueness of the full minimum, physical continuation
to it, or billiard flow through a singular reference is asserted.

\subsection*{Clearance of all actual obstacles and an exact source}
A change parallel to an original flight should be accounted for
using its two actual incident disks.  Lemma~\ref{lem:tb-strip}
does this with
$\beta_i=\max(|v_i^\perp\cdot\delta_i|,
             |v_i^\perp\cdot\delta_{i+1}|)$:
\[
 \Gamma_i(Y)\ge\min\{\Gamma_i(Q),\tau_-\}-\beta_i
                              \quad(2\beta_i<\tau_-).
\]
Here $\Gamma_i$ is the clearance of the complete closed segment
from every third lattice disk, including a disk visited elsewhere
in the same word.  The proof treats the strip projections of the
incident disks, the longitudinal extensions, endpoints and changes
of the minimizing center.  Its quadratic transverse budget is the retained
refinement of the fourth-power Hausdorff budget.

Together with \eqref{eq:ae-intro}, the strip theorem provides
exponentially small true clearance and incidence witnesses for any
deep physical failure of any minimum.  A separate pointwise phase
estimate supplies the witness for disagreement with the exact
original section.  The original section's collar satisfies the
explicit bound $\nu\{\operatorname{dist}_\infty
 (x,\partial Y_R^*)\le\rho\}\le\rho/(2\pi)$ for
$0<\rho\le1/200$.

Theorem~\ref{thm:ec-uniform-source} defines a Borel source
$\mathcal E^{\rm obs}_{D,R,m}$ using only these original physical
observables and proves
\[
 \sup_{m,R,n,k,\,0\le h\le1}
 \left\|(L_{m,R})_*
  (\one_{\mathcal E^{\rm obs}_{D,R,m}}\one_{\Pi_{n,k,m,R}}
                                  h\,d\nu_R^*)\right\|_{\rm TV}
 \le\epsilon_D\longrightarrow0\quad(D\to\infty).
\]
The displayed $\epsilon_D$ is an explicit sum of three geometric
tails.  This is a complete positive-source estimate: the countable
physical word sum precedes the uniform bound, and no independence
between flight times is assumed.  It uses the exact one-flight
clearance flux, invariant collision probability and the original
section conditioning once.

On its complement, every minimum has an actual first-hit central
record $Y_D,\ldots,Y_{m-D}$ with exactly the original section
indicator at each of its contacts.  Its displacement, collision
count, occupation and two endpoint tests are therefore exact.
No assertion of physicality for the omitted end portions is needed
or made.  This comparison class is an explicitly measured part of
the original source, and is not substituted for the original
conditional event.

\subsection*{The physical clock, its endpoint work, and roof height}
For integers $d\ge1$ and $2d<m$, put $b=m-d$; on a proved
physical central comparison,
Theorem~\ref{thm:cp-clock} gives the exact identity
\[
 L_d(Y)-L_d(Q)
   =v_{b-1}\cdot\delta_b-v_d\cdot\delta_d
     +\frac1R\sum_{i=d+1}^{b-1}H_i
                    +\frac12\sum_{i=d}^{b-1}F_i .
\]
The first two terms form the boundary work $W_d$.  They are
retained in the central Fourier phase; the nonnegative remainder
is the small physical clock cost.  The section proof pays all
contact-state changes, duplicates only the terminal incoming edge
as required, and keeps the terminal indicator separate.

The unconditional comparison statements are positive inequalities
of measures pushed forward by $L_{m,R}(Q)$, the original
\emph{full} physical roof.  The inherited estimates in
Theorem~\ref{thm:nc-normalized} and
Corollary~\ref{cor:ec-normalized} identify exactly the additional
input for a roof density: Input~\ref{input:v112-geometric-pressure},
with the specified physical stable arclength, optical coordinate
factors and uniform word-pressure constant.  Under that input the
small costs have bounded density on the whole roof line.  For
$d_m=\lceil\theta m\rceil$, $0<\theta<1/2$, the exponential
costs satisfy an $O(m^2e^{-\lambda_0m})$ bound after collision
normalization, with
$\lambda_0=\theta|\log q_{\rm a}|/94$.
Their central clock Fourier error is uniformly small on
$|s|\le e^{\lambda m}$ for every $\lambda<\lambda_0$, after its
exact boundary phase, and uniformly in both lattice twists and
the return twist.

These inherited conditional density estimates concern small comparison
costs on the exact central class.  Total variation of its
exceptional source does not estimate that source's roof essential
height.  Neither statement by itself supplies all physical
multipliers, a full unbounded Fourier integral or roofwise capacity.
The inherited Theorem~\ref{thm:nh-records} removes the pressure dependence
for these costs on the full specified $\mathcal G_\varepsilon$,
while the inherited Theorem~\ref{thm:nh-bad-height} pays the exceptional
source's normalized height on that same class.  The remaining
source and capacity obligations retain their original scope.

 \lim_{B\to\infty}\limsup_{m\to\infty}\sup_{R,n,k}
