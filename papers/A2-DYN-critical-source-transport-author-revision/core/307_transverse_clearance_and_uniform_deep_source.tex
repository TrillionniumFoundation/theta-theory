\section{Transverse clearance and a uniform physical boundary layer}
\label{sec:tb-clearance}
Longitudinal displacement of a contact need not be charged as a loss
of clearance: a sufficiently narrow comparison strip confines that
part of the comparison flight to its actual incident disks.  This
gives a quadratic obstruction budget and a summable depth schedule.
We keep the original physical reference, disks, constants and notation
of Section~\ref{sec:bl-global}.  Statements about arbitrary sublevel
points will be distinguished from those requiring a global minimum.

For any feasible comparison polygon define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:tb-beta}
 \beta_i=\max\{|v_i^\perp\cdot\delta_i|,
                   |v_i^\perp\cdot\delta_{i+1}|\},\qquad
 \Gamma_i(Y)=\inf_{z\in\Lambda\setminus\{O_i,O_{i+1}\}}
                      \operatorname{dist}(z,[Y_i,Y_{i+1}])-R.
\end{equation}
Every third center is retained, including a center visited elsewhere
in the same word.  Put $\Gamma_i(Q)$ for the original closed flight.

\begin{lemma}[A strip comparison with both incident disks retained]
\label{lem:tb-strip}
If $2\beta_i<\tau_-$, then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:tb-strip}
 \Gamma_i(Y)\ge\min\{\Gamma_i(Q),\tau_-\}-\beta_i.
\end{equation}
The statement holds for arbitrary feasible comparison endpoints.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use coordinates in which the original endpoints are $(0,0)$ and
$(\tau_i,0)$.  Intersect each of its two incident closed disks
with the strip $|y|\le\beta_i$.  The longitudinal projections of
these compact convex sets are disjoint intervals: otherwise two
points, one in each disk, would have the same longitudinal coordinate
and distance at most $2\beta_i<\tau_-$, contradicting disk separation.
The first interval precedes the second, since they contain respectively
$0$ and $\tau_i>0$.  Thus the comparison segment has strictly
increasing longitudinal coordinate and stays in the strip.

The portion projected into $[0,\tau_i]$ is within $\beta_i$ of
the original closed segment.  If a left extension exists, let $Z$
be its intersection with the line of longitudinal coordinate zero.
The segment from $Y_i$ to $Z$ is within $\beta_i$ of the segment
from $Y_i$ to $Q_i$, using the same convex parameter.  The latter
segment lies in the first incident disk.  The right extension is
treated identically with the second disk and $Q_{i+1}$.
Every third disk is at distance at least $\tau_-$ from each incident
disk.  Its distance from a point of the comparison segment is
therefore at least $\Gamma_i(Q)-\beta_i$ on the central portion,
and at least $\tau_- -\beta_i$ on either extension.  Taking the
infimum over the full segment and every third disk proves the result.
The argument includes endpoints, changes of the closest center, and
changes of the minimizing foot.  It does not replace a closed flight
by its infinite supporting line.
\end{proof}

Define the uniform coefficients
\begin{align}
 B_H&=3\left(\frac{1+3N}{a}+12N^2\right)+8,
 &B_F&=9N^2T,\label{eq:tb-local-constants}\\
 B_G&=\frac{3r}{a}+\frac{9Nr}{a}+18N^2T+36N^2r+12r,
 &B_E&=36Nr+4r.\label{eq:tb-global-constants}
\end{align}
In particular $B_G=167591049$ and $B_E=1592.36$.

\begin{theorem}[Quadratic transverse budgets]
\label{thm:tb-budget}
For every feasible comparison polygon, with its original positive
gap $G$, one has
\begin{equation}\label{eq:tb-global-budget}
 \sum_{i=0}^{m-1}\beta_i^2\le B_GG+B_EE_R(Q).
\end{equation}
For every $0\le u<v\le m$ one also has
\begin{equation}\label{eq:tb-local-budget}
 \sum_{i=u+N}^{v-2}\beta_i^2
 \le B_H\sum_{u<i<v}H_i+B_F\sum_{u\le i<v}F_i.
\end{equation}
For a global minimum, put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:tb-depth-constants}
 K_\perp=\frac{(256rB_H+1024B_F)\sqrt{K_{\rm tail}}}{\tau_-},
 \qquad C_\perp=\sqrt{K_\perp}.
\end{equation}
Then for every integer $d\ge2N$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:tb-depth-budget}
 \sum_{i=d}^{m-d-1}\beta_i^2\le K_\perp d^{-3}.
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Write $b_i=v_{i-1}^\perp\cdot\delta_i$ for $1\le i\le m$
and $b_0=v_0^\perp\cdot\delta_0$.  At an interior contact,
\[
 |v_i^\perp\cdot\delta_i|^2
 \le2b_i^2+8H_i.
\]
The transverse telescope from Lemma~\ref{lem:bl-interior}, with
anchors paid directly, gives on an interior interval
\[
 \sum_{i=u+N}^{v-1}b_i^2
 \le\left(\frac{1+3N}{a}+12N^2\right)\sum_{u<i<v}H_i
                         +3N^2T\sum_{u\le i<v}F_i.
\]
Since $\beta_i^2\le|v_i^\perp\cdot\delta_i|^2+b_{i+1}^2$,
summing gives the stated local coefficients, with all target
indices contained in the preceding interval.

For completeness the two true endpoints can be paid without
assuming their incidences bounded away from zero.  Put
$E_0=R(1-c_0)$ and $E_m=R(1-c_m)$, so $E_0+E_m=E$.
At either endpoint the disk support gap and the decomposition into
longitudinal and transverse components give
\[
 b_0^2\le4r(h_0+E_0),\qquad
 b_m^2\le4r(h_m+E_m).
\]
Indeed the square of the comparison transverse coordinate relative
to its disk center is at most $2Rh_0$ or $2Rh_m$; the reference
square is at most $2RE_0$ or $2RE_m$.  The difference-square
inequality supplies the factor two.

Use a preceding physical anchor for every nonanchor after the first
anchor, and use endpoint $0$ for the at most $N$ initial indices.
The exact telescope for these initial indices has no reflection
term at $0$.  Its initial-value payment is at most $3Nb_0^2$.
The flight and contact reuse bounds are still at most $N$, including
these initial intervals.  Because $(1+3N)/a\ge12N$, the anchor
payment and the $12Nrh_0$ part of the initial payment can be paid
together by the same complete positive gap.  Using
$\sum H_i\le rG$ and $\sum F_i\le2G$ for the remaining terms
therefore gives
\[
 \sum_{i=1}^{m-1}b_i^2
 \le\left(\frac{(1+3N)r}{a}+6N^2T+12N^2r\right)G
                                      +12NrE.
\]
Finally
\[
 \sum_i\beta_i^2
 \le3\sum_{i=1}^{m-1}b_i^2+8\sum_{i=1}^{m-1}H_i+b_0^2+b_m^2.
\]
The endpoint pair is at most $4r(G+E)$.  Substitution proves
\eqref{eq:tb-global-budget}, including its exact coefficients.

For a global minimum and a nonempty deep target, apply the local
bound on $[u,v]=[d-N,m-d+N]$.  It contains all the target flights.
The contact and flight sums are contained in the energy at depth
$d-N\ge d/2$.  Corollary~\ref{cor:bl-energy} gives
\[
 \sum_{i=d}^{m-d-1}\beta_i^2
 \le\frac{(32rB_H+128B_F)\sqrt{K_{\rm tail}}}
                       {\tau_-(d-N)^3}
 \le K_\perp d^{-3}.
\]
An empty target needs no interval argument.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[A stronger geometric obstruction certificate]
\label{cor:tb-source-inclusion}
For any $0<\rho\le\tau_-/2$, a third-disk violation
$\Gamma_i(Y)\le0$ at a flight with $\Gamma_i(Q)>\rho$
forces $\beta_i\ge\rho$.  Consequently
\begin{equation}\label{eq:tb-obstruction-count}
 \#\{i:\Gamma_i(Y)\le0,\ \Gamma_i(Q)>\rho\}
       \le\rho^{-2}(B_GG+B_EE).
\end{equation}
On the original variational sublevel the right side is at most
$\rho^{-2}(B_G+B_E)E$.  This is a quadratic transverse payment,
not a fourth-power Hausdorff payment.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
If $2\beta_i\ge\tau_-$ the implication is immediate.  Otherwise
the strip lemma gives $\min(\Gamma_i(Q),\tau_-)\le\beta_i$,
which proves it as well.  Sum the nonnegative squares and use the
global budget.  The sublevel inequality is $G\le E$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[An inactive deep minimum forces a true small incidence]
\label{lem:tb-inactive}
Put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:tb-inactive-constant}
 C_{\rm inactive}^2=512\sqrt{K_{\rm tail}}/\tau_-^2.
\end{equation}
If a global minimum is inactive at an interior contact of depth
$d_i=\min(i,m-i)\ge2$, then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:tb-inactive-witness}
 c_i(Q)\le C_{\rm inactive}d_i^{-3/2}.
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Inactivity means $\widehat v_{i-1}=\widehat v_i$.  Thus
$2c_i=|v_i-v_{i-1}|\le|e_i|+|e_{i-1}|$, and
\[
 c_i^2\le\tfrac12(|e_i|^2+|e_{i-1}|^2)
 \le\frac1{2\tau_-}\sum_{l=d_i-1}^{m-d_i}F_l
 \le\frac{64\sqrt{K_{\rm tail}}}{\tau_-^2(d_i-1)^3}
 \le C_{\rm inactive}^2d_i^{-3}.
\]
The two adjacent flights are retained, including when the contact
is the central singleton.  No inverse comparison incidence occurs.
\end{proof}

Let $d'_i=\min(i,m-1-i)$ be flight depth, and put
$\delta_{\rm flux}=3/200$.  Fix the explicit integer
\begin{equation}\label{eq:tb-D0}
 D_0=\left\lceil\max\left\{2N+2,
 (2C_\perp/\delta_{\rm flux})^{2/3},
 (2C_{\rm inactive})^{2/3}\right\}\right\rceil.
\end{equation}
For fixed $R,m$ and $D\ge D_0$, let $\mathcal B_{D,R,m}$ be
the original regular records whose ordered disk word admits a global
minimum with an inactive contact at depth at least $D$, or a flight
at depth at least $D$ with nonpositive third-disk clearance.
The definition uses all global minima.  At fixed radius and word
the existence in question is a property of the word itself.
Consequently $\mathcal B_{D,R,m}$ is a countable union of original
Borel physical word cells.  No measurable choice of a minimum is
needed.

\begin{theorem}[A complete positive-source boundary-layer bound]
\label{thm:tb-uniform-source}
Keep the original exact event $\Pi$, conditional measure
$\nu_R^*=c_*^{-1}\mathbf1_{Y_R^*}\nu$, physical roof $L_R$,
radius range and exact labels $n,k$.  For any Borel $0\le h\le1$,
define the positive original-roof measure
\begin{equation}\label{eq:tb-bad-measure}
 \mu_{D,R,m,n,k,h}
   =(L_R)_*(\mathbf1_{\mathcal B_{D,R,m}}\mathbf1_\Pi h\,d\nu_R^*).
\end{equation}
On every original Borel roof set its mass, and hence its total
variation on the whole roof line, is at most
\begin{equation}\label{eq:tb-uniform-mass}
 \frac{4C_\perp}{R_-c_*\sqrt{D-1}}
          +\frac{C_{\rm inactive}^2}{c_*(D-1)^2}.
\end{equation}
In particular
\begin{equation}\label{eq:tb-uniform-limit}
 \lim_{D\to\infty}\sup_{R,m,n,k,\,0\le h\le1}
                      \|\mu_{D,R,m,n,k,h}\|_{\rm TV}=0.
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The deterministic estimates hold simultaneously over all minima.
A deep blocked comparison flight has
$\beta_i\le C_\perp(d'_i)^{-3/2}\le\delta_{\rm flux}/2<\tau_-/2$.
The strip lemma therefore forces
$\Gamma_i(Q)\le C_\perp(d'_i)^{-3/2}$.
The inactive-contact witness is
$c_i(Q)\le C_{\rm inactive}d_i^{-3/2}\le1/2$.
Thus the entire bad source is contained in the union of these
explicit small-clearance and small-incidence events of the original
physical record.

The retained exact one-copy flux gives
$\nu\{\Gamma_i(Q)\le u\}\le u/R$ for
$0<u<\delta_{\rm flux}$, by collision invariance.  For the
closed threshold, decrease slightly larger open widths to $u$;
continuity of finite measures gives the same bound.  The exact
incidence marginal and invariance give
\[
 \nu\{c_i\le u\}=1-\sqrt{1-u^2}\le u^2\qquad(0\le u\le1).
\]
For any Borel roof set multiply the full finite union bound by
$\mathbf1_\Pi h\mathbf1_{\{L_R\in A\}}$ and integrate with
the original conditional measure.  Drop factors between zero and
one only in the positive upper bound.  This pays exactly one
$c_*^{-1}$, with no replacement of the event or roof.

There are at most two flights or contacts at any prescribed depth.
The complete infinite tails satisfy
\[
 \sum_{d=D}^\infty d^{-3/2}\le2(D-1)^{-1/2},
 \qquad \sum_{d=D}^\infty d^{-3}\le[2(D-1)^2]^{-1}.
\]
The resulting constants are those in \eqref{eq:tb-uniform-mass}.
All actual words and all collision counts were included before
taking this upper bound.  Its constants are independent of every
displayed supremum variable, proving the stated limit without
interchanging the original microscopic limits.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[A true central comparison subrecord]
\label{cor:tb-central-physical}
If $D\ge D_0$, $m>2D$, and the original regular record is outside
$\mathcal B_{D,R,m}$, then every global minimum has the actual
regular first-hit subrecord $Y_D,\ldots,Y_{m-D}$ in the original
Lorentz gas.  The omitted end portions are not asserted physical.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Every included contact is active, so the one-coordinate minimum
condition places it on its original circle with the exterior
specular law and strictly positive incidence.  Each included flight
therefore stays outside its two incident disks; strict positive
clearance excludes every third disk.  These facts prove genuine
first-hit behavior on each successive central flight.  The
implications are simultaneous in the global minimum.
\end{proof}

The source theorem controls total mass for bounded positive weights.
It does not control normalized roof-density peaks, arbitrary optical
Jacobians, unbounded marked multiplicity, or singular pairing traces.
Its deterministic clearance mechanism and exact source limit do not
alter the original section, exponential guard or microscopic window.
