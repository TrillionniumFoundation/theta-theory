\subsection*{Full physical source and the actual singular multiplier}
The preceding author revision proves the fixed-margin height estimate
of Theorem~\ref{thm:at-height}, the normalized guarded-source bounds
of Theorem~\ref{thm:nh-bad-height}, and the explicit optical comparison
of Section~\ref{sec:eo-optical}. Their complete bodies are retained.
The new question is how much of the physical source can be controlled
when no positive incidence or clearance margin is imposed. No unchanged
proof is counted as a new contribution.

For any Borel selector $0\le H\le1$, with the entire original event
included if desired, write $f_H$, $f_H^I$ and $f_H^Z$ for the densities
of the original full roof $L_{m,R}$ with weights $H$, $HI_m$ and
$HZ_m$, respectively, where
\[
 Z_m=I_m/|\nabla_qF|,\qquad
 I_m=1+\sum_{i=1}^{m-1}q_0^{\min(i,m-i)}/c_i.
\]
The source is the original $\nu_R^*$ with the endpoint cap
$|p_0|,|p_m|\le5/6$, which contains its complete exact return event.
It is not renormalized on that cap. Sections~\ref{sec:fr-full-roof}
and \ref{sec:sm-singular-multiplier} prove, uniformly in $m,R$ and
all the original labels,
\[
 \|f_H\|_{3/2,\infty}\le C,
 \qquad \|f_H^I\|_{6/5,\infty}\le C_I,
 \qquad \|f_H^Z\|_{12/11,\infty}\le C_Z.
\]
These are weak Lebesgue norms on the entire original physical roof
line. Every positive physical word is summed before the norms are taken.
There is no lower bound on a positive interior incidence or an unvisited
obstacle's clearance in these conclusions.

The first proof combines an exact physical margin tail with the actual
finite-overlap atlas. For each $0<\varepsilon\le1/200$,
\[
 \nu(\mathcal C_{m,R}\setminus\mathcal G_{\varepsilon,m,R})
 \le B_\Delta\varepsilon+B_c\varepsilon^2,
 \qquad C_{\rm at}=\mathfrak A\varepsilon^{-2}.
\]
The constants sum the original depth schedules; clearance is first order
and incidence is second order in its physical threshold. Thus, for every
Borel roof set $S$ and every finite collision count,
\[
 \mu_H(S)\le\mathfrak A\varepsilon^{-2}|S|
                         +B_\sharp\varepsilon.
\]
This quantitative inequality proves absolute continuity of the complete
source and its weak $L^{3/2}$ bound. The choice of margin occurs inside
an inequality already valid at each finite count. It changes no physical
event or microscopic roof window and exchanges none of the original
ordered limits. The layer-cake estimate
$\int hI_m\,d\nu\le(97/3)(\int h\,d\nu)^{1/2}$ then pays the actual
incidence insertion on the very same roof preimage.

The inverse-gradient multiplier requires a stronger geometric joint
estimate. Put $r=|(p_0,p_m)|$. The already proved physical momentum
kernel of Theorem~\ref{thm:mc-kernel}, used first at collision count
$m$ and then at the actual interior count $i$, gives
\[
 \nu\{r<s\}\le A_0s^2,\qquad
 \nu\{r<s,c_i<u\}\le A_1s u^2,
 \quad A_0=C_{\rm mom}/4,
 \quad A_1=C_{\rm mom}/\pi.
\]
The second estimate bounds the true event by the joint coordinates
$(p_0,p_i)$; it does not multiply probabilities from two blocks.
Decomposition into actual momentum annuli proves
\[
 \nu\{(r c_i)^{-1}>t\}
  \le5A_0^{1/3}A_1^{2/3}t^{-4/3}.
\]
Summing the unchanged incidence weights, whose total is at most $47/3$,
gives the complete physical inequality
\[
 \int hZ_m\,d\nu
       \le J_\times\left(\int h\,d\nu\right)^{1/4},
 \qquad 0\le h\le1.
\]
The exact factor $|\nabla_qF|=Rr$ is paid using $R\ge R_-$.
Under the original conditional measure the prefactor becomes
$J_\times c_*^{-3/4}$, with one conditioning and no hidden radius
or collision multiplier. This proves the formerly missing full bulk
singular integrability. It also extends the original complete Borel
critical-band estimate to $I_m/|\nabla_qF|$, with order $s^{1/4}$
in a physical band of width $s$.

The new source bounds apply to the unchanged exceptional union
$E_D=\mathcal E^{\rm obs}_{D,R,m}$ of clearance, incidence and exact
section obstructions. For $D_m=\lceil\theta m\rceil$,
$0<\theta<1/2$, let $\lambda_b>0$ be the retained uniform geometric
rate. The full measures, including the former guard complement, satisfy
\begin{align*}
 \|f_{D_m,H}\|_{3/2,\infty}&=O(e^{-\lambda_bm/3}),&
 \|f^I_{D_m,H}\|_{6/5,\infty}&=O(e^{-\lambda_bm/6}),\\
 \|f^Z_{D_m,H}\|_{12/11,\infty}&=O(e^{-\lambda_bm/12}).
\end{align*}
Their strong norms below the indicated exponents and their total masses
also decay exponentially, with the exact rates proved in the new bodies.
Each displayed norm still tends to zero after multiplication by $m^2$.
The original full roof and all exact marks remain in the pushforward.
The full-source Fourier comparison retains the compensating phase
$e^{-isW_d}$ for the actual endpoint work, and is uniform over the
stated exponentially growing bounded frequency range. It is not an
unproved exchange with an unbounded Fourier inversion integral.

Finally, Corollary~\ref{cor:sm-variation} applies the native
regular-variation inequality of Section~\ref{sec:el-variation}
under its retained one-copy physical coarea convention. It proves a
finite complete regular-interior variation budget and a normalized
exponentially small budget on $E_{D_m}$, at each original guard
parameter fixed before the collision limit. No selector is differentiated.
This charge concerns the original interior derivative measure; a positive
endpoint minimum on a short segment still requires its actual trace or
capacity argument. In particular the new weak norms do not suppress
all roof spikes in an essential supremum. That pointwise requirement
and the original four-coordinate raw inversion remain the scientific
goals of the manuscript.
