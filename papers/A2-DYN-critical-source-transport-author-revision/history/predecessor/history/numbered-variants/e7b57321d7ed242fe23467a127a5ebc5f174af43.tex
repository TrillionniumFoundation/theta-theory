\section{Absolute optical control and physical traces}
\label{sec:v93-traces}
We begin with an estimate before taking the sum over physical sheets.
Unlike a logarithmic derivative, its constant does not contain an
inverse incidence. This estimate will justify the complete signed
balance, not an estimate obtained by counting words.

\begin{lemma}[Cancellation in the positive optical denominator]
\label{lem:v93-cancellation}
For every regular physical word and every internal contact $1\le i<m$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v93-cancellation}
 \omega\le C_0:=R_+/\tau_-,\qquad
 \frac{\omega}{c_i}\le C_1:=\frac{R_+^2}{2\tau_-^2},\qquad
 \omega I_m\le C_0+\frac{2q_0}{1-q_0}C_1.
\end{equation}
The constants are independent of the collision count, the word,
the radius and every positive incidence. The internal assertion is
empty when $m=1$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
All entries of the positive recursion in Input~\ref{input:v93-geometry}
are nonnegative and $y_j$ is increasing. Thus $y_m\ge y_1=\ell_0$.
For an internal contact,
\[
 y_m\ge y_{i+1}\ge \ell_i V_i y_i
          \ge \frac{2\ell_i\ell_0}{Rc_i}
          \ge \frac{2\tau_-^2}{Rc_i}.
\]
Insert these two lower bounds in $\omega=Rc_0c_m/y_m$ and use
$c_0c_m\le1$. Finally, each positive depth occurs at most twice, so
$\sum_{i=1}^{m-1}q_0^{d_i}\le2q_0/(1-q_0)$.
The estimate is for the absolute density; it does not assert that
$\nabla\log\omega$ is bounded near grazing.
\end{proof}

On a maximal physical segment $\sigma$, write
\[
 A_\sigma(u)=\frac{\omega(\Psi_\sigma(u))}
                   {4\pi c_*\chi(\Psi_\sigma(u))},\qquad
 f_\sigma^\varepsilon(u)=A_\sigma(u)W^\varepsilon(\Psi_\sigma(u)),
 \qquad a_\sigma<u<b_\sigma.
\]
The transverse measure $d\sigma$ denotes the countable disjoint sum
of the angular measures in the retained Borel parametrization.

\begin{theorem}[Uniform traces on each complete physical sheet]
\label{thm:v93-traces}
There is a physical constant $C$ such that
\begin{align}
 0\le A_\sigma&\le C,&
 |\partial_u f_\sigma^\varepsilon(u)|
       &\le C(1+\varepsilon^{-1})z(\Psi_\sigma(u))^{-1},
                                                   \label{eq:v93-absolute-derivative}\\
 |f_\sigma^\varepsilon(v)-f_\sigma^\varepsilon(u)|
       &\le C(1+\varepsilon^{-1})\sqrt{|v-u|}
       &&(u,v\in[a_\sigma,b_\sigma]).              \label{eq:v93-sheet-holder}
\end{align}
In the last assertion the endpoint values are the unique one-sided
traces. The trace is zero at any endpoint where an internal incidence
vanishes. A backward critical endpoint is not required to have zero
trace.

At each fixed collision count all the following quantities are finite:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v93-fixed-count-variation}
 \int\left(f_\sigma^\varepsilon(a_\sigma+)
       +f_\sigma^\varepsilon(b_\sigma-)
       +\int_{a_\sigma}^{b_\sigma}
                    |\partial_u f_\sigma^\varepsilon(u)|\,du\right)d\sigma.
\end{equation}
No collision-uniform bound for \eqref{eq:v93-fixed-count-variation}
is asserted.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The retained full-density estimate is $|A_\sigma'|\le CA_\sigma I_m/z$.
The original guard is locally Lipschitz on the physical graph and
$|(W^\varepsilon)'|\le C\varepsilon^{-1}/z$ almost everywhere.
The product rule, $0\le W^\varepsilon\le1$, the lower bound for $\chi$
and Lemma~\ref{lem:v93-cancellation} prove
\eqref{eq:v93-absolute-derivative}. A change of the minimizing third
disk in a clearance does not affect this almost-everywhere argument.

Radial growth gives, by taking the earlier roof down to $a_\sigma$,
\[
 z(\Psi_\sigma(s))^2\ge2\lambda_\circ(s-a_\sigma).
\]
For $a_\sigma<u<v<b_\sigma$, integration yields
\[
 \int_u^v\frac{ds}{z(\Psi_\sigma(s))}
 \le\sqrt{\frac2{\lambda_\circ}}
          (\sqrt{v-a_\sigma}-\sqrt{u-a_\sigma})
 \le\sqrt{\frac2{\lambda_\circ}}\sqrt{v-u}.
\]
This proves the extension and its modulus. If $c_i\to0$, then
$\omega\le C_1c_i\to0$; the other factors are bounded.

For completeness, the transverse parameter space has finite measure
at fixed count. There are finitely many candidate physical words,
because the flight horizon is finite. In finitely many rational
circle-coordinate charts their reflection, first-hit and section
decisions are definable. On the regular graph the direction $g/|g|$
is also definable. Its one-dimensional fibers have finitely many
connected components, with a bound for this fixed definable family.
Each component is exactly one maximal roof segment: its tangent is
$X$ and $XF=1$. Definable finiteness, equally expressed in finitely
many bounded angular charts, therefore gives
$\int d\sigma\le C N_m<\infty$ for some $N_m$.
The Borel rational-roof selection counts each component once and
preserves its angle. This argument concerns the geometric domain,
not definability of the smooth guard. See \cite{Coste} for the
finite-family principle.

The roof lies in an interval of length at most $m\tau_+$.
The derivative integral on each sheet is consequently at most
$C(1+\varepsilon^{-1})\sqrt{m\tau_+}$ and its two traces are bounded.
This proves \eqref{eq:v93-fixed-count-variation}. The number $N_m$
is used only for existence of these fixed-count measures. Every
collision-normalized estimate below instead uses the exact physical
coarea identity.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[A critical trace is not point mass in the original area]
The original area of a critical point is zero. Its directional trace
may nevertheless give an atom in the derivative of the roof density.
For example, the pushforward of planar area by $F(q)=|q|^2/2$ near
its minimum has density $2\pi\mathbf1_{\{t>0\}}$ and derivative
$2\pi\delta_0$ there. We retain this distinction, already present in
the historical angular-residue calculation. Critical entrances are
not replaced by artificial positive area and are not deleted.
\end{remark}
