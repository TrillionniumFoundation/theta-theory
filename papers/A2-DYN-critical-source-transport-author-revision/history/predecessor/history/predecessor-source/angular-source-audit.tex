\subsection*{External curve growth and physical coordinates}
The inspected author version of \cite{DZUniform} dates from
10 October 2013. Its Lemma 3.2(b) is the homogeneous stable
curve-Jacobian sum; its proof specifies (H2)--(H4).
Theorem 2.7 states uniform properties for its geometric family,
and Remark 2.9(b) discusses a small change of boundary arclength.
We use none of these statements as an exact-label roof local limit
theorem or as a sum over all physical cylinders. Input~\ref{input:ag-growth}
states exactly the forward counterpart needed here; source-curve
membership and a common curvature allowance are essential parts
of its invocation. Theorem~\ref{thm:ag-density} verifies a concrete
bound $K_0$ for that membership check. It does not independently
re-prove expansion and distortion for the admissible class.

Time reversal is $\mathcal I(s,\varphi)=(s,-\varphi)$, an isometry
of the original collision-coordinate arclength, and
$\mathcal I T_R\mathcal I=T_R^{-1}$ on the regular set. Thus
forward pieces of an unstable curve correspond to backward pieces
of its reflected stable curve, with equal arclength Jacobians.
This conversion does not reverse the original measured return
record in a theorem statement; it only verifies the curve estimate.

For a local common boundary coordinate at reference radius $R_0$,
write $A_R(s,\varphi)=(R_0s/R,\varphi)$. If $m_A\le|A_Rv|/|v|
$ and $|A_Rv|/|v|\le M_A$, then conjugating a curve map gives
\[
 J^{\rm common}T_R^l(x)=J^{\rm physical}T_R^l(x)
       \frac{|A_R v_l|/|v_l|}{|A_R v_0|/|v_0|}.
\]
There is only an endpoint ratio, at most $M_A/m_A$, not a factor
$(M_A/m_A)^l$. A compact finite local cover of $[R_-,R_+]$ gives
common constants whenever the cited geometric curve hypotheses
are verified in those local charts. This is a coordinate comparison,
not an affine deformation of the triangular physical lattice. The
input is kept explicit rather than asserting that compactness
alone verifies (H2)--(H4) or the larger pressure and pairing chain.
