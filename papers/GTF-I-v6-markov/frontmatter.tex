\begin{abstract}
We prove matching finite-state prediction laws for separated Markov
repellers with forbidden transitions, bounded conformal distortion,
and delay-observable H\"older readouts. Acquisitions may have
nongeometric renewal times. A strict renewal-energy inequality makes
the entire adaptive admissible tree suffix closed, with linear state
count even at unary vertices. Its geometric profile is comparable to
weighted-orbit quantization and to optimal causal risk over all
randomized time-dependent observers. Two pressure roots determine the
squared-error exponent. On Markov similarity systems, polynomial
renewal tails produce distinct power--log and iterated-log critical
corrections, with a uniform finite-size profile window. A second
realization theorem uses posterior contraction rather than a common
regeneration component. An inward finite quantizer gives matched
$M^{-2/d}$ excess risk on unbounded filtering models, including
Gaussian hidden chains with no global minorization at any fixed
number of steps. Exact simulations compare finite-memory interfaces.
\end{abstract}
