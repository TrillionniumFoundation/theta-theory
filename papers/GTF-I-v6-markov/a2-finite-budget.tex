\section{A finite-register refinement of the current contact experiment}
\label{sec:v6-a2}
This application uses the actual known-mark count-to-contact protocol
of A2 revision 118 \cite[Theorems on contact experiments and quantized
scores]{QiA2v118}. It is independent of the preceding dynamical
hypotheses. We give the probability estimates explicitly so that no
historical local-limit assertion is needed.

Fix $\lambda^0\in(0,\infty)^m$, a matrix $B\in\R^{m\times p}$,
a compact parameter set $H\subset\R^p$, and a rank-$r$ matrix
$C\in\R^{r\times m}$, with $r\ge1$. Observe independent counts
\[
 N_j\sim\operatorname{Pois}(N\lambda_j^0+\sqrt N(Bh)_j),
 \quad h\in H,
 \qquad S_N=C(N_\cdot-N\lambda^0)/\sqrt N.
\]
All means are positive for sufficiently large $N$.
Put $\Lambda=\diag(\lambda^0)$, $A_*=CB$, and
$\Gamma=C\Lambda C^{\mathsf T}>0$. In A2's notation,
$B=B_*=\Lambda D^{\mathsf T}(D\Lambda D^{\mathsf T})^{-1}$ and
$C=F^{\mathsf T}\mathsf L D$, so $A_*=F^{\mathsf T}\mathsf L$.
The exposure centre, mark law, compression and range frame are fixed
before observing the counts. They are not estimated by an uncharged
pilot.

For integers $J\ge1$ and $R\ge1$, partition $[-R,R)^r$ into $J^r$
cubes of side $\eta=2R/J$. Retain the cell containing $S_N$, or one
overflow symbol. This is a deterministic physical statistic with a designated alphabet
of $J^r+1$ labels (some may have zero probability). For experiments indexed by $h\in H$,
write $\Delta$ for the maximum of the two deficiencies in total
variation, with parameter-independent randomization kernels.

\begin{theorem}[Joint sample and label bound]\label{thm:v6-a2-budget}
There are positive constants $C_0,c_0,R_0$, depending on the fixed
matrices, centre and compact parameter set, such that, for $R\ge R_0$
and $0<\eta\le1$, the finite-label experiment $\mathcal Q_{N,J,R}$
satisfies
\begin{equation}
 \Delta\bigl(\mathcal Q_{N,J,R},\{N(A_*h,\Gamma):h\in H\}\bigr)
 \le C_0\left\{N^{-1/5}+\frac{J}{R\sqrt N}
                         +\frac RJ+e^{-c_0R^2}\right\}.
                                                        \label{eq:v6-a2-budget}
\end{equation}
Thus, for $M\ge2$, one may impose a prescribed register budget by
choosing $J=\lfloor(M-1)^{1/r}\rfloor$, whenever $2R/J\le1$. In particular, for sufficiently large
fixed $K$, the choices $R=K\sqrt{\log N}$ and
$J=\lceil2RN^{1/4}\rceil$ give deficiency $O(N^{-1/5})$ with
\begin{equation}
                  M=O(N^{r/4}(\log N)^{r/2}).           \label{eq:v6-a2-count}
\end{equation}
The rank $r$ is the rank of the actual contact score. This is a
sufficient finite-label budget, not a claim of its minimax necessity.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Adjoin independent $U_j$ uniform on $(-1/2,1/2)$ for comparison only.
The law of
$X_N^\circ=(N_\cdot-N\lambda^0+U)/\sqrt N$ is within $C N^{-1/5}$
in total variation of $N(Bh,\Lambda)$, uniformly on $H$.
Here is a direct bound. In one coordinate let
$\mu_N=N\lambda_j^0+\sqrt N(Bh)_j$. On cells with
$|k-\mu_N|\le\sqrt{\mu_N}N^{1/10}$, Stirling's formula with its
$O(k^{-1})$ remainder gives the ratio of the piecewise constant
jittered Poisson density to the normal density with matching mean
and variance as $1+O(N^{-1/5})$. Indeed the logarithmic error is
$O((1+|z|^3)/\sqrt N)$ for $|z|\le C N^{1/10}$; variation of the
normal within a cell contributes only a smaller term. The discarded
mass is $O(N^{-1/5})$ by Chebyshev's inequality, uniformly in $h$.
Replacing variance $\mu_N/N$ by $\lambda_j^0$ costs $O(N^{-1/2})$
by the normal density derivative bound. Product total variation is
at most the sum of the coordinate errors. This proves the assertion.
Applying $C$ contracts total variation, so
$S_N^\circ=CX_N^\circ$ is within $CN^{-1/5}$ of $N(A_*h,\Gamma)$.

Under this coupling, $\|S_N-S_N^\circ\|_\infty\le d_N=C_1/\sqrt N$.
The finite quantizers differ only when a coordinate crosses a grid
boundary, including a boundary of the retained cube. For a normal
marginal density $f$ and any translated grid,
\[
 \eta\sum_{j\in\mathbb Z}\sup_{[j\eta,(j+1)\eta]}f
      \le1+\eta\int|f'|.
\]
The total probability within $d_N$ of that grid is therefore at most
$Cd_N/\eta$ if $d_N\le\eta/2$. For larger $d_N$ the same bound
follows from the trivial bound one. A union bound and the preceding
total variation comparison show that the physical labels and the
labels of the limiting Gaussian have distance at most
$C(N^{-1/5}+d_N/\eta)$.

In the reverse direction, from a finite label generate a uniform
point in its cell, and send the overflow label to a fixed point.
On the retained cells the resulting histogram differs from the
normal density in $L^1$ by at most
$C\eta\int\|\nabla f\|\le C'\eta$; this follows by integrating the
mean-value estimate over pairs of points in each cube. Since $H$ is
compact and $\Gamma$ fixed positive definite, the Gaussian mass
outside $[-R,R)^r$ is at most $C e^{-c_0R^2}$ for $R\ge R_0$.
Its replacement by the overflow point costs at most this mass in
total variation. Quantization in the other direction is a fixed
kernel. Composing these two comparisons proves
\eqref{eq:v6-a2-budget}. Choosing the stated $R,J$ makes
$d_N/\eta$ and $\eta$ of order $N^{-1/4}$ and the tail at most
$N^{-1/5}$, proving \eqref{eq:v6-a2-count}.
\end{proof}

For every bounded decision problem, these parameter-independent kernels
also transfer the corresponding optimal risks with additive error
bounded by \eqref{eq:v6-a2-budget} times the loss range. The physical
statistic remains deterministic; the random kernels occur only in the
comparison of experiments. Across contact strata the rank and
conditioning of $C\Lambda C^{\mathsf T}$ must be reevaluated. The
constants are not uniform through a collapsing positive eigenvalue.
The conductor flags and nonreduced determinant powers of the current
A2 edition specify algebraic failure schemes. They do not, without
this specified observation protocol, supply the Gaussian covariance
or a noise exponent. This application consequently uses the current
statistical experiment, not an analogy with its scheme multiplicity.
