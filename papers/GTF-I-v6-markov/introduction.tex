\section{Introduction}\label{sec:v6-intro}
The information retained by a causal observer must serve predictions
whose time of use is not known when that information is acquired.
A description of the current state can therefore be inadequate even
when it is an optimal quantization at the acquisition time. There is
also a converse difficulty: a good finite description of an entire
future orbit need not admit a closed update on the same finite state
set. These two questions link the geometry of prediction to the
implementation of a finite memory.

This paper develops two realization principles for that link. The
first is an exact suffix-closure principle for renewal-weighted
cylinder codes on a mixing Markov repeller. The second is a contracting
posterior principle for continual partial observation on an unbounded
state space. Both are compared with the full class of randomized,
time-dependent observers having a bounded persistent register.

\subsection{The intrinsic law}
Let $\Sigma_A$ be a topologically mixing subshift of finite type, with
forbidden transitions allowed, and let $K$ be its strongly separated
geometric realization by contractions of bounded conformal distortion.
The class includes similarities in any Euclidean dimension and
nonconstant smooth inverse derivatives in dimension one. The measure
on $K$ is a stationary H\"older Gibbs law. A H\"older observable may
be noninjective provided a finite delay vector recovers the geometric
state with a uniform snowflake lower bound.

Exact acquisitions occur at independent renewal times. The survival
law need not be geometric. A finite cylinder energy combines the mass
of the acquired cylinder, the successive suffix contraction scales,
and the tail of the stationary renewal age. Splitting maximal-energy
cylinders defines a finite profile $G_M$. Our first conclusion is
\[
       \mathcal R_M\asymp e_M^2(Z_w^f)\asymp G_M,
\]
where $Z_w^f$ is the weighted observable orbit and $\mathcal R_M$ is
the optimal long-time average prediction risk. The constants hold for
every budget, not merely along a sequence of cylinder depths.

The realization argument must accommodate vertices with a unique
successor and suffix cylinders with a unique predecessor. Strict
inequality of cylinder masses is then unavailable. We instead prove
strict decrease of the renewal distortion under prefix attachment.
Together with stationarity, it forces the complete greedy tree to be
suffix closed. Unary vertices are counted, and the whole state set
has size at most a graph-dependent constant times the number of
leaves. The lower bound uses separated tubes in Hilbert space and is
valid for arbitrary off-image centres. Thus neither admissibility nor
causal execution is hidden in the definition of a static codebook.

When the renewal weights have exponential rate $q$, the squared-error
exponent is determined by the larger of two pressure roots,
\[
 P_A(s_g\varphi+2\beta s_g\psi)=0,
 \qquad P_A(s_t\varphi)+s_t\log q=0.
\]
The first combines mass with geometric contraction; the second
combines mass with survival through unobserved dynamics. We prove the
existence of the quantization exponent from these equations. It is
not an additional hypothesis on an infinite-dimensional orbit image.
For a Markov source, zeros of the transition matrix remain zeros in
the transfer matrices.

The first-order exponent does not determine the critical correction.
For the Parry law on any primitive graph and equal-ratio similarities,
renewal survival $q^k(k+1)^{-\kappa}$ gives, at the common critical
value, corrections $(\log M)^{1-\kappa}$ for $\kappa<1$,
$\log\log M$ for $\kappa=1$, and a bounded factor for $\kappa>1$.
The underlying geometry and pressure threshold can be kept fixed.
A uniform finite-size profile limit describes the window
$q_n=q_c e^{x/n}$. These statements describe actual renewal laws and
matching risk orders; they do not assert an optimal leading constant
from a pair of inequalities.

\subsection{A nonregenerative realization principle}
For continual observations, let $m_t$ denote the true complete-history
conditional mean. Suppose it obeys a finite-dimensional Borel recursion
which contracts conditionally in fourth mean in its previous mean
coordinate. Individual updates may expand. An inward rational
quantizer preserves fourth-moment bounds on an unbounded state space.
The resulting recursive observer attains excess squared risk
$O(M^{-2/d})$ using at most $M$ persistent values. If the stationary
posterior mean has positive density on a cube, a matching lower bound
holds against all observers. The Bayes floor here is the
\emph{full-history} Bayes error, not the error of the latest observation.

For a stationary Gaussian autoregression with Gaussian observation
noise, all hypotheses are verified and the risk is
$P+\Theta(M^{-2})$, where $P$ is the steady conditional variance.
Every fixed-step hidden transition kernel in this example lacks a
nonzero global common minorizing measure. The realization proof is
therefore genuinely different from inserting an independent
reference experiment at each acquisition. The general theorem states
its posterior contraction hypothesis explicitly; it is not a
contraction theorem for all nonlinear filters.

\subsection{The resource and its invariances}
Only the number of values of the persistent state is constrained.
Fixed programs, fixed real constants, the absolute clock, and
transient computation are separate resources. A renewal indicator is
an input to the update, not a free post-update observation.
Theorem~\ref{thm:v6-interface} proves exact operational simulations:
adding a $K$-valued event to the decoder costs at most a factor $K$
in state count; splitting registers costs their product; binary
checkpoint encodings are related by $M=2^B$. These transformations
preserve the exponent and, under fixed-budget dilation, the risk
order. A real-valued free observation at decoding is a different
experiment, as the explicit uniform example shows.

\subsection{Relation to previous work}
Thermodynamic formulas for \emph{spatial} quantization predate the
present work. Lindsay and Mauldin \cite{LindsayMauldin2002} relate
quantization dimension for finite conformal systems to the temperature
function. Atnip, Roychowdhury and Urba\'nski
\cite[Theorem 3.1]{AtnipRoychowdhuryUrbanski2018} extend that relation
to an infinite conformal family under their summability and strong
open set hypotheses. Roychowdhury \cite{Roychowdhury2014} treats
Gibbs-like measures on cookie-cutter sets. Those spatial results
include systems beyond the finite, strongly separated class used
here. We do not claim a generalization of their geometric hypotheses.
The additional object here is the survival-weighted observable
\emph{future orbit}, with an unrestricted causal converse and an
admissible, suffix-closed finite-state realization. Classical pressure
and greedy variable-to-fixed trees \cite{Bowen2008,Tunstall1967} are
inputs, not inventions of this paper.

Finite-memory and zero-delay approximation likewise have a substantial
literature. Linder and Y\"uksel \cite{LinderYuksel2014} and Wood, Linder
and Y\"uksel \cite{WoodLinderYuksel2017} establish structural and
stationary coding results. Ghomi, Linder and Y\"uksel
\cite[Theorem 4]{GhomiLinderYuksel2022} prove stationary optimality
for linear vector Markov sources and control finite-horizon
approximation to the infinite-horizon distortion. Their channel
alphabet and their history-dependent coding policy are not a single
finite persistent register. Our filtering result is not a new proof
of stationary zero-delay coding optimality.

Kara and Y\"uksel \cite[Theorem 12]{KaraYuksel2022} prove exponentially
accurate finite-memory feedback under their POMDP stability hypotheses.
Cregg, Alajaji and Y\"uksel \cite[Theorem 2 and Corollary 2]{CreggAlajajiYuksel2026}
bound the discounted suboptimality of sliding-window source-channel
policies by predictor stability, with an exponential window bound
under sufficient Dobrushin conditions. Thus stability-based
finite-memory approximation is already established. In contrast,
Theorem~\ref{thm:v6-filter} concerns the number of retained values,
a matched squared-error exponent against the full-history Bayes
floor, and an unbounded continuous observation model. It is a
quantitative realization result in that resource, not a Q-learning
or optimal-control theorem. Recursive filter quantization itself is
also classical; see Pag\`es and Pham \cite{PagesPham2005}.

Static functional quantization \cite{LuschgyPages2004}, dynamical
rate distortion \cite{LindenstraussTsukamoto2018}, and data-rate
control \cite{TatikondaMitter2004} constrain different objects.
The distinction is operational: a block code or information-rate bound
does not by itself specify a closed update on a bounded persistent
state set. Conversely, the state-count theorems here give no runtime,
program-description, or channel-capacity bound.

\subsection{Organization and dependencies}
Sections~\ref{sec:v6-model}--\ref{sec:v6-pressure} prove the Markov
profile and pressure theorems. Section~\ref{sec:v6-critical} gives the
critical classification and uniform window. Sections~\ref{sec:v6-filter}
and~\ref{sec:v6-operational} prove the nonregenerative and interface
results. The general posterior-orbit converse is included with its
complete proof in Section~\ref{sec:v4-orbits}.
The complete edition additionally retains the earlier Gibbs,
dependent-acquisition, noisy expansion, calibration, root-collision,
filter, control and foundational developments. They have their own
hypotheses; the current Markov and filtering proofs do not depend on
the singular-statistics or unbounded-operator conclusions.

The programme's T07--T08 question is the passage from acquired
predictive geometry to actual finite-state evolution. The two
realization mechanisms proved here resolve that question in their
stated classes, including a noncompact nonregenerative model. The
historical physical models require further, model-specific results.
The active A2 conductor and nonreduced-generator edition
\cite{QiA2v118} is an algebraic input to a different observation
interface: its scheme multiplicities do not specify a noisy
observation kernel. The accompanying snapshot audit records that
interface at its current source edition rather than identifying
algebraic multiplicity with a statistical exponent. The complete edition
also proves a joint sample--label deficiency bound for its actual
known-mark count-to-contact protocol. The overflow alphabet and the
comparison randomization are explicitly charged and distinguished from
the physical statistic.
