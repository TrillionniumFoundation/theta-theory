## What the comparison supports

The revision's claimed mathematical content is the joint proof of admissible renewal-energy suffix closure, the matching arbitrary-coder risk profile, its two-pressure exponent, the explicitly classified renewal critical corrections, and a separate nonregenerative hard-register realization. The A2 application states a sufficient sample/alphabet bound for its actual protocol. These precise statements, rather than an assertion that adjacent fields had no finite-memory results, are submitted for novelty assessment.
