## Current A2-v118 native sources

The current title is *Conductor strata and nonreduced multiplication failure schemes*. The following files were read in the downloaded source-bound edition and copied unchanged under `history-sources/A2-v118/` as **uncompiled consultation material**:

- `README.md`;
- `parts/02a-higher-defect.tex`;
- `parts/03a-nonreduced-generators.tex`;
- `parts/04-statistical-experiments.tex`.

The first new proof uses exact-rank strata for the generated algebra and its action on the quotient; conductors commute with base change on the specified strata, not on an arbitrary rank-jumping family. Its nilpotent example detects a squared rank-jump equation. The second computes nonreduced multigenerator determinant powers for a truncated local algebra and transports the conductor to fat points in arbitrary projective dimension. These are scheme-theoretic results, not prescriptions for observation noise.

The statistical source separates a physical cone likelihood, a known-mark direct count-to-contact experiment and deterministic finite precision. In the latter, the actual counts are coarsened rather than replaced by independent noisy distance measurements. `thm:v6-a2-budget` uses that same local exposure protocol, displays every fixed matrix, and derives the sample/label comparison with a Gaussian-tail overflow radius. The sufficient label growth improves the cost of using a polynomial truncation radius while preserving the displayed `N^(-1/5)` comparison order. No minimax necessity is inferred.

The source's own outstanding Ballico (1993) priority-access item remains its own issue. This revision neither resolves that source-access question nor treats it as evidence of originality. The current A2 proofs are consulted for model typing; the new probability estimate is proved in this manuscript rather than resting on an unreviewed algebraic assertion.

## Preservation versus validation

All old GTF source trees and mathematical bodies are retained. The new principal view contains the complete retained converse actually needed, and the complete view preserves every earlier quantitative/foundational chain. Fixed Git identities, line ranges and hashes identify what was consulted. They do not establish independent correctness of the historical proofs. The audit is deliberately component-specific, so an active modern statistical improvement is neither erased by an old report nor promoted into an unrelated physical theorem.
