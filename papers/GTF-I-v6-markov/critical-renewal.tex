\section{Critical corrections and a uniform renewal window}\label{sec:v6-critical}
The exponential rate of the acquisition tail does not determine the
critical second order. We give a classification in a family allowing
arbitrary primitive transition graphs, every ambient dimension in the
similarity construction, and delay-observable outputs.

Assume that all branch ratios equal $r$, and that $\mu$ is the Parry
measure of $\Sigma_A$. Write $\lambda=\lambda_A$ and $a=r^{2\beta}$.
For $q\in(0,1)$ and $\kappa\ge0$ take the actual survival law
\begin{equation}
 \Pp(L>k)=q^k(k+1)^{-\kappa},\quad
 c_{q,\kappa}=\left(\sum_{k\ge0}q^k(k+1)^{-\kappa}\right)^{-1}.
                                                        \label{eq:v6-polynomial-tail}
\end{equation}
Its weights satisfy \eqref{eq:v6-weights}, with lower ratio
$q2^{-\kappa}$ and upper ratio $q$. The common depth-$n$ energy scale is
\begin{equation}
 V_{q,\kappa}(n)=c_{q,\kappa}\left[
 a^n\sum_{k<n}(q/a)^k(k+1)^{-\kappa}
       +\sum_{k\ge n}q^k(k+1)^{-\kappa}\right].          \label{eq:v6-depth-profile}
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[Critical power--log classification]\label{thm:v6-critical}
Let $n_M=\lfloor\log M/\log\lambda\rfloor$. In this family,
\begin{equation}
                    \mathcal R_M\asymp V_{q,\kappa}(n_M).
                                                        \label{eq:v6-depth-risk}
\end{equation}
For fixed $\kappa$ the constants can be chosen uniformly for $q$ in
a compact subinterval of $(0,1)$. The critical value is $q_c=a$.
With $\alpha=\log(1/a)/\log\lambda$, at this value, as $M\to\infty$,
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal R_M\asymp M^{-\alpha}
 \begin{cases}
  (\log M)^{1-\kappa},&0\le\kappa<1,\\
  \log\log M,&\kappa=1,\\
  1,&\kappa>1.
 \end{cases}                                             \label{eq:v6-critical-laws}
\end{equation}
In particular the same graph, geometry, observable, pressure roots,
and critical exponential survival rate admit different critical
corrections. The specification of the renewal law is essential.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Perron eigenvectors give, uniformly in $v\in\mathcal L_n$,
$\mu[v]\asymp\lambda^{-n}$ and $|\mathcal L_n|\asymp\lambda^n$.
All these cylinders have scale \eqref{eq:v6-depth-profile}.
A fixed-depth complete tree has $O(\lambda^n)$ vertices (sum the
geometric level counts), is suffix closed, and gives average loss
at most $CV_{q,\kappa}(n)$ by the pointwise construction in
Lemma~\ref{lem:v6-realization}. Choose $n=n_M-C_0$ so that its state
count is at most $M$.

For a lower bound choose $n=n_M+C_1$ with at least $4M$ cylinders.
The disjoint tubes used in Lemma~\ref{lem:v6-realization} then leave
at least three quarters of the cylinders unoccupied by any $M$
centres. Their masses are comparable and their scales equal, giving
$e_M^2\ge cV_{q,\kappa}(n)$. A fixed change in depth changes $V$
by factors bounded above and below by \eqref{eq:v6-child}'s scale
proof, uniformly for $q$ in the specified compact set. The converse
gives \eqref{eq:v6-depth-risk}. This argument neither treats the
Parry measure as independent nor fills forbidden transitions.

At $q=a$, the first term in \eqref{eq:v6-depth-profile} is
$c_{a,\kappa}a^n\sum_{j=1}^n j^{-\kappa}$; the tail is
$O(a^n(n+1)^{-\kappa})$. Integral comparison gives the three
possibilities in \eqref{eq:v6-critical-laws}. Also
$P_A(s\varphi)= (1-s)\log\lambda$ and
$P_A(s\varphi+2\beta s\log r)=(1-s)\log\lambda+s\log a$.
Thus the two roots coincide at $q=a$, as asserted.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform finite-size profile window]\label{thm:v6-window}
Fix $\kappa\ge0$ and put $q_n=ae^{x/n}$. Uniformly for $x$ in a
bounded real interval, the following limits hold:
\begin{align}
 \frac{V_{q_n,\kappa}(n)}{a^n n^{1-\kappa}}
 &\longrightarrow c_{a,\kappa}\int_0^1 t^{-\kappa}e^{xt}\,dt,
                                      &&0\le\kappa<1,\label{eq:v6-window1}\\
 \frac{V_{q_n,1}(n)}{a^n\log n}&\longrightarrow c_{a,1},
                                      &&\kappa=1,\label{eq:v6-window2}\\
 \frac{V_{q_n,\kappa}(n)}{a^n}&\longrightarrow
                c_{a,\kappa}\sum_{j\ge1}j^{-\kappa},
                                      &&\kappa>1.\label{eq:v6-window3}
\end{align}
The optimal risk is uniformly comparable to these profiles at budgets
of order $\lambda^n$. The displayed leading coefficients are profile
coefficients, not asserted exact optimal-risk coefficients.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The normalizing constants tend uniformly to $c_{a,\kappa}$ by a
common geometric majorant. For $0\le\kappa<1$, split the normalized
sum at $k=\delta n$. Away from zero, its summands form a uniformly
convergent Riemann sum for $t^{-\kappa}e^{xt}$. The part below $\delta n$
is bounded uniformly by $C\delta^{1-\kappa}$; then let
$\delta\downarrow0$. The normalized tail is $O(n^{-1})$ uniformly,
using $q_n\le q_+<1$ and $q_n^n/a^n=e^x$.
For $\kappa=1$,
\[
 \left|\sum_{k<n}\frac{e^{xk/n}-1}{k+1}\right|\le C
\]
uniformly on bounded $x$, while the unperturbed harmonic sum is
$\log n+O(1)$. The tail divided by $a^n\log n$ vanishes.
For $\kappa>1$, dominated convergence applies to the summable sequence
$(k+1)^{-\kappa}$, and the tail divided by $a^n$ is $O(n^{-\kappa})$.
Finally use the uniform comparison in Theorem~\ref{thm:v6-critical}.
\end{proof}

The classification concerns the stated Markov--Parry similarity family
and the renewal tails \eqref{eq:v6-polynomial-tail}. For unequal
H\"older derivative cocycles the finite profile theorem and pressure
exponent still apply; \eqref{eq:v6-critical-laws} is not imposed on
that larger class. In the geometric case $\kappa=0$ the first window
profile is $(1-a)(e^x-1)/x$, continuously extended at $x=0$.
