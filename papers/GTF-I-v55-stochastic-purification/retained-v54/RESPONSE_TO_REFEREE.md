## Changes of substance

The main theorem now gives the exact constrained Chebyshev-radius threshold for complete finite categorical/joint outputs on intrinsic compact groups with finitely many components. Its converse covers every nonuniform clocked stochastic realization and bounds arbitrary separated narrow cuts. A cofinite return-language condition replaces an identity letter. A second principal theorem gives matching polynomial width for a Diophantine family throughout the entire subcritical noise interval. A third result proves effective classification for rational orthogonal/polynomial inputs. The original scalar theorem, observable quotient and effective algebraic-circle estimates remain consequences or sections of the current article; the whole preceding native theory remains unchanged in a separate retained directory.

The principal proofs are in the main article. The build receipt and exact finite tests are reproducibility evidence, not independent mathematical certification.

## Replies to the required revision items

### R35 §8.1 — Standalone title

The descriptive title now stands alone. The General Theta Foundations I identifier remains only in repository provenance, not as a mathematical premise or journal masthead.

### R35 §8.2 — Conventional priority comparison

The comparison section and LITERATURE_AUDIT.md distinguish Rabin/Paz, weighted series, positive realization, quantum automata, intrinsic compact recognition, distribution metrics, and current closure algorithms. The requested six-column matrix records hypotheses, quantifiers, norms and conclusions. No exhaustive negative priority assertion is made.

### R35 §8.3 — Intrinsic compact-group formulation

Theorem thm:radius54 works for compact Hausdorff groups with finitely many connected components. Real representative functions replace a faithful finite-dimensional embedding. Finite component number is retained explicitly; it is not automatic for every compact group.

### R35 §8.4 — Matching quantitative theory

Theorem thm:matched54 proves Theta(N^(s/(2s+1))) for s dual badly approximable planar angles, at every fixed 0<=epsilon<rho/2 and 0<rho<1, with or without the specified reflection. The lower bound covers the full interval via high harmonics; the exact polygon upper bound and a proved comparable-denominator lemma match it. The exponent and geometric upper mechanism are credited to the historical small-noise argument. The endpoint rho=1 and arbitrary additional upper-bound letters are not smuggled into the theorem.

### R35 §8.5 — Minimal boundary construction

Theorem thm:quotient54 gives an exact equivariant-partition/radius characterization of the least deterministic component-quotient machine, including a computable finite search and an exact future-output equivalence. It does not equate this with the unrestricted stochastic minimum. Theorem thm:effective54 separately computes the unrestricted minimum at each fixed N. Thus the finite quotient issue is resolved in a precise natural class; a universal stationary stochastic minimum is not represented as proved.

### R35 §8.6 — Finitely encoded effective class

Theorem thm:effective54 specifies rational orthogonal commands, an identity letter, and rational polynomial categorical maps. It computes real components, the exact algebraic radius and boundary centers, a subcritical occupation bound, and each finite-N optimum. Termination of positive localizer and word-law searches is proved. Existing closure and real-algebraic algorithms are credited. No practical implementation or inherited complexity bound for the whole construction is claimed.

### R35 §8.7 — Identity-free synchronization

The main theorem assumes executable identity returns of all sufficiently large lengths. Finite exact-return words with relatively prime lengths suffice, by the proved numerical-semigroup argument; a three-letter identity-free example has return lengths 2 and 3. The occupation proof accounts explicitly for bounded initial/final padding losses. This is a sufficient replacement, not a necessary-and-sufficient language characterization.

### R35 §8.8 — Categorical and joint output laws

The exact radius theorem treats simplex-constrained probabilities, whole finite joint laws, and compact-convex numerical outputs. The three-category example separates the joint threshold 2/3 from every separate event threshold 1/2. The measure-once POVM application treats an entire selected measurement law, not a fictitious joint distribution of incompatible measurements.

### R35 §8.9 — Local pipeline claims

The new argument is self-contained and the A/B/C/D analytic graph remains independent. No global Foundations completion, analytic gate closure, or venue acceptance is inferred from revision count or software success.

## Scope of the remaining broader suggestions

The report's §9 offered several distinct ways to produce a substantially stronger article. This revision develops general finite output laws, a matching full-subcritical width theorem over a specified broad Diophantine class, and effective rational classification. It does not claim that every possible continuation is now solved. An arbitrary irreversible compact-semigroup classification, compact groups with infinitely many components, unrestricted stationary stochastic boundary minimization, general output processes with online observations, and efficient implementations of the full algebraic algorithms are not conclusions of the manuscript. These are precise hypothesis boundaries rather than a lowering of the results actually proved.

The permissive lower model remains deliberate: the lower theorem survives free horizon/clock advice and arbitrary real row tables. The paper now pairs that obstruction with an explicit exact upper construction and a separately defined effective input class. Neither addition retroactively assigns finite-bit costs to all real decoders.
