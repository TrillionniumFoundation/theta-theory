## Closest mechanisms

Benvenuti–Farina's positive-realization work and the inherited bibliography provide the positive-cone, order, and nonnegative-intertwining background. Static nonnegative factorization/extended formulations are compared through Yannakakis; weighted-automaton realization through the Balle–Panangaden–Precup references already in the predecessor. Neither static factorization nor a linear realization is treated as a stochastic realization with the required common command rows.

C. E. T. Dórea and J. C. Hennet, “(A, B)-invariant polyhedral sets of linear discrete-time systems,” *Journal of Optimization Theory and Applications* 103 (1999), 521–542, DOI `10.1023/A:1021727806358`, was checked at the publisher's primary abstract. It derives polyhedral invariance conditions through linear-algebraic relations and nonnegative-matrix results, including a disturbed-system setting. These are closely related mechanisms; the present classification concerns nonuniform finite-horizon numerical stochastic realizations and its positive-error consequence, not a new invention of polyhedral invariance.

T. Mejstrik, “Improved invariant polytope algorithm and applications,” arXiv `1812.03080`, version 3 dated 21 June 2020, was checked at its author-deposited arXiv record. It is a computational invariant-polytope approach to joint spectral radii. The revision does not conflate its own finite-group/noisy classification with a new joint-spectral-radius algorithm.

M. O. Rabin, “Probabilistic automata,” *Information and Control* 6 (1963), 230–245, DOI `10.1016/S0019-9958(63)90290-0`, is discussed as the conventional probabilistic-automaton antecedent. The numerical signed-seed/all-query semantics and nonuniform clock are stated separately from language recognition. No exact theorem-number priority claim is made where the original full text was not obtained in this session.

## Standard results explicitly credited

Normalized Haar measure on compact groups is used only for existence of a full-support testing law; the finite executable law is then constructed in the manuscript. Folland, *A Course in Abstract Harmonic Analysis*, second edition, is the conventional reference. No new harmonic-analysis result is claimed.

M. Putinar, “Positive polynomials on compact semi-algebraic sets,” *Indiana University Mathematics Journal* 42 (1993), 969–984, and J. B. Lasserre, “Global optimization with polynomials and the problem of moments,” *SIAM Journal on Optimization* 11 (2001), 796–817, DOI `10.1137/S1052623400366802`, are the classical positivity and hierarchy mechanisms in `thm:sos52`. Their Archimedean strict-positivity/convergence statements and bibliographic entries were cross-checked in L. Huang's author-deposited paper “On the complexity of matrix Putinar’s Positivstellensatz,” arXiv `2406.13980v2`. That paper is corroboration of the classical mechanism, not a claim that this revision proves its matrix-valued degree bounds. The theorem here explicitly adds a ball generator and takes a positive residual gap, which is enough for a finite infeasibility certificate.

The active inherited citations for real-algebraic decision, difference-body volume, toric tensor relations, arithmetic rotation estimates, and rational sampling are retained. Source genealogy is recorded in `HISTORY_AUDIT.md`; it is not substituted for conventional published citation.
