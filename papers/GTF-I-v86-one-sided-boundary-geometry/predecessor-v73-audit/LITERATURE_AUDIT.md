# Primary-source comparison — Revision 73

This is an author-side theorem comparison. It is not an independent expert priority report. The new theorem does not claim that the Heisenberg-to-standard noise crossover, GHZ states, classical channel simulation, purification inequalities or metric packing were invented in this revision.

## Direct metrological antecedent

**Demkowicz-Dobrzanski, Kolodynski and Guta, The elusive Heisenberg limit in quantum enhanced metrology.** Nature Communications 3 (2012), 1063; DOI 10.1038/ncomms2067; arXiv:1201.3940v2. The classical-simulation section, equation (4), expresses channels as fixed operations selected by parameter-dependent classical probabilities. The dephasing example and Table 1 give the factor sqrt(1-lambda^2)/lambda (their visibility is denoted eta). This is a direct antecedent for the noise factor and the common-extremal-channel mechanism, not merely distant motivation.

The new section uses an exact pairwise secant construction for binary effects, rather than extracting a finite-distance claim from a local Fisher-information or Cramer-Rao bound. It retains a finite-N fidelity expression and proves a reverse operational modulus using finite GHZ blocks and a majority inequality. The stated consequence is a covering count uniform in N, lambda and small delta, including both endpoints, with arbitrary legal centres and a direct rational circle code. The familiar scaling itself is explicitly not a novelty claim. This comparison does not exclude equivalent finite-distance formulations elsewhere.

## Environment and channel-box antecedents requested by r46

**Das–Wilde, Quantum reading capacity: General definition and bounds.** IEEE Transactions on Information Theory 65 (2019), 7566–7583; DOI 10.1109/TIT.2019.2929925; arXiv:1703.03706v3. Definition 3 and Proposition 2 use a common interaction and reduce adaptive processing to environment-state processing. This is the direct adaptive-to-common-programme antecedent. A reading capacity is not by itself a rank-aware finite-use family covering or a rational codec.

**Wilde–Berta–Hirche–Kaur, Amortized channel divergence for asymptotic quantum channel discrimination.** Letters in Mathematical Physics 110 (2020), 2277–2336; DOI 10.1007/s11005-020-01297-7; arXiv:1808.01498v2. Definition 36 and Theorem 37 give environment seizing and exact finite-use discrimination equalities. The v72 covering transfer, retained here, additionally specifies an ambient state domain and transforms arbitrary covering centres through one common processor/seizer. Its channel/state reduction is credited. The new noisy upper proof is pair dependent and is not asserted to meet this global seizing hypothesis.

**Wang–Wilde, Resource theory of asymmetric distinguishability for quantum channels.** Physical Review Research 1 (2019), 033169; DOI 10.1103/PhysRevResearch.1.033169; arXiv:1907.06306v2. Section III.A treats common channel-box/state-box transformations for environment-seizable channels. The reference concerns channels (033169), not the state companion (033170). Its superchannel framework remains a direct antecedent; a classical codeword in this manuscript is not the size of a universal quantum processor.

## Boundary of the present priority assertion

The uniform finite-distance covering and rational realization in the explicit noisy equatorial family are the new written assertions. The conditional-state extension keeps observable outcomes and public visibility. It is not a noisy metrology learning theorem, an unknown-lambda coding theorem, an arbitrary coherent/support-changing tangent-cone classification, or a mutable workspace/physical hardware result. An independent quantum-statistical-experiment expert may still identify a prior equivalent formulation; no author-side search can honestly be described as that clearance.
