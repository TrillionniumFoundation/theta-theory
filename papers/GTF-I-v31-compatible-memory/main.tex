\begin{abstract}
We distinguish the separate positive realization of continuation arrays
from their simultaneous realization by a finite causal machine. In the
clocked atomic stochastic-row model, canonical affine sections yield a
simultaneously rank-minimal machine whenever those sections are simplices;
in particular, full-support arrays of cut rank at most two have no
compatibility obstruction. We construct a full-support family of cut rank
three whose two individual minimum state counts are both three, but whose
exact Pareto-minimal profiles are $(3,4)$ and $(4,3)$. Thus the compatibility
obstruction occurs already at rank three and persists away from support
boundaries. In the opposite direction, every additive stochastic encoder
admits an online realization with no increase in its label alphabet.
Consequently every simplex enclosing a contracted cube gives an online
query encoder with the same number of states. This transfers all classical
critical cube--simplex inclusions, including the non-Hadamard dimensions
five and nine, to optimal online realizations. We also identify exact
conditional query synthesis with classical worst-case random-access
geometry at a checkpoint, and separate that equivalence from the cost of
computing an encoder. Explicit rational tables, a finite fair-bit sampling
ledger, and internal stochastic-language conventions complete the
realization statements.
\end{abstract}
