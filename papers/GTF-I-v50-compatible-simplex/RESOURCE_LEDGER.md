# Resource ledger — v50

The primary resource counts all available persistent labels at every cut, including unused labels and any retained selector. Horizon, epoch, input command and prescribed row tables are free. Tables can be horizon-dependent and have real entries; lookup, construction and exact atomic sampling are not priced. The held binary answer has two labels. This is not ordinary program size, uniform workspace, an anytime machine, or a finite-fair-bit implementation model.

The rank-tight theorems use D-dimensional orthogonal targets, signed coordinate seeds, all coordinate queries and the identity letter. Their occupation budget applies to selected D+1-width cuts even with arbitrarily wider intermediate cuts; it does not eliminate hidden rows at those wider cuts. The four-state upper machine uses fixed permutation rows and is uniform across horizons within the atomic model. Its sufficiently-small positive-error interval is not explicitly quantified.

The cubic orthogonal example uses dimension nine, three epochs, two commands, one pair of rational unit seeds and only the e_000 query. Its all-two-state optimal error is certified. The general word embedding uses 2^n+1 dimensions; this cost is explicit and exponential, not a succinct-input efficiency claim.

The sign/magnitude optimizer receives the full expanded table. Sign assignments can be exponential; ray multiplicities and algebraic degrees have the explicit coarse bounds in `prop:complexity50`. A global rejection may require a record for every chamber. Exact algebraic order comparisons, large integer powers and field construction are charged when discussing output verification; a shared radical formula is not a polynomial-time bit-complexity claim.

The original finite-bit compiler, with its separate assumptions and rounding/scratch accounting, is retained. It does not price a generic exact algebraic optimizer or turn irrational optimal rows into exact finite-fair-bit samplers. Rational machines approximate the cubic optimum from above but do not attain it at the fixed profile when the signal is rational.
