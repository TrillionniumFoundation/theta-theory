# A2-DYN v3: focused primary-source audit

Checked on 5 October 2026. This is a theorem-use audit, not an exhaustive novelty or priority search.

**Baladi--Demers--Liverani.** *Exponential decay of correlations for finite horizon Sinai billiard flows*, Inventiones 211 (2018), 39--177. The reference is arXiv:1506.02836v3, last revised 23 February 2020, including the correction note. The arXiv record states that the note corrects Lemmas 3.2, 4.6 and 7.5. Corollary 1.3 and the correction were read in the text. In the new return-stability proof, only ergodicity of each fixed table is used, via flow mixing and the suspension of collision-invariant sets. No parameter-uniform rate, spectral space for the new inducing set or differentiability statement is imported.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02836v3

**Dolgopyat--Nandori.** *On mixing and the local central limit theorem for hyperbolic flows*, ETDS 40 (2020), 142--174; arXiv:1710.08568v1. Definition 2.2 and Proposition 6.3 were checked against the fixed-test mixing local-limit formulation. They do not by themselves supply the raw mixed-density norm and geometric uniformity in the present article. The exact local inversion criterion is stated with its own hypotheses.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.08568v1

**Demers--Zhang.** *A functional analytic approach to perturbations of the Lorentz gas*, CMP 324 (2013), 767--830; arXiv:1210.1261. The primary arXiv record states continuity of spectra and spectral projections for the stated perturbations. This is not imported as twice differentiable parameter control of an unbounded first-return record. The new fixed-n L1 theorem instead uses direct finite-itinerary geometry, coarea and exact first moments.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.1261

**Retained classical inputs.** The Szász--Varjú Lorentz-process LLT and Young's statistical framework remain cited as prior theory, not as a proof of all hypotheses of the proposed A2-DYN raw LLT. Kac's tower identity, Scheffé's nonnegative-integral argument, compactness in L1, total-variation contraction and a ratio estimate are used with proofs or explicit reductions; no novelty is claimed for those general tools.
