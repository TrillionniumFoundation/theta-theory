\section{Resonant polygons and exact synthesis}\label{sec:resonance}
A regular polygon need not be enlarged by the worst possible angular
misalignment. Choosing its order from a convergent changes the cost from
quadratic to cubic in its number of vertices.

\begin{lemma}[Resonant containment and stochastic rows]\label{lem:resonance}
Let $q\ge5$, $(p,q)=1$, and $|\alpha-p/q|<q^{-2}$. Put
\[
 a_q=\pi/q,\quad \delta=2\pi(\alpha-p/q),\quad
 \lambda=\frac{\cos(a_q-|\delta|)}{\cos a_q},\quad
 w=\frac{\sin|\delta|}{\lambda\sin(2a_q)}.
\]
Then $1\le\lambda$, $0\le w\le1$, and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:lambda}
                         \log\lambda\le40/q^3.
\end{equation}
For $v_j=(\cos(2\pi j/q),\sin(2\pi j/q))$ and
$P_t=r_t\conv\{v_j:0\le j<q\}$, $r_{t+1}=\lambda r_t$,
\[
                         P_t\cup R_\alpha P_t\subset P_{t+1}.
\]
The two stochastic rows realizing the inclusions are
\begin{align}
 T_0(j,\cdot)&=\lambda^{-1}\delta_j
                      +(1-\lambda^{-1})\operatorname{Unif}[q],
                                                        \label{eq:idle-row}\\
 T_1(j,\cdot)&=(1-w)\delta_{j+p}+w\delta_{j+p+\operatorname{sgn}\delta},
                                                        \label{eq:rotate-row}
\end{align}
with indices modulo $q$; when $\delta=0$, take the pure shift.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Here $|\delta|<2\pi/q^2\le a_q$. The point $r_tv_{j+p}$ rotated
by the residual angle $\delta$ lies on the edge of $P_{t+1}$ joining
the two displayed vertices. The imaginary coordinate gives the coefficient
$w$; the real coordinate identity is
\[
              \lambda=\cos|\delta|+\tan(a_q)\sin|\delta|.
\]
This proves both barycentric equality and $0\le w\le1$ by edge
containment. The idle row uses the zero barycenter of a regular polygon.
Finally,
\[
 \log\lambda=\int_{a_q-|\delta|}^{a_q}\tan u\,du
       \le |\delta|\tan a_q
       \le(2\pi/q^2)(2\pi/q)<40/q^3.
\]
We used $a_q\le\pi/5<\pi/4$, where $\tan u\le2u$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Cube-root synthesis]\label{thm:upper}
Let $q_N$ be the first convergent denominator of $\alpha$ at least
$\max\{5,(40N)^{1/3}\}$. There is an exact vector-state realization
of \eqref{eq:array} with $q_N$ states at every command cut.
When the partial quotients are bounded by $A$,
\[
 q_N<(A+1)\max\{5,(40N)^{1/3}\},\qquad
                     V_N(\alpha)=\Theta_\alpha(N^{1/3}).
\]
The command matrices can be the same at all epochs. The final decoder
and the chosen polygon order are horizon-specific.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Take $q=q_N$, $r_0=1/4$ and $r_t=r_0\lambda^t$.
The inradius of $P_0$ is at least
$\cos(\pi/5)/4>\sqrt2/10$, so all seed vectors lie in $P_0$.
By \eqref{eq:lambda}, $r_N\le e/4<3/4$, hence all polygons lie
inside $D$. Initialize by any convex representation of $x/10$ in
$P_0$, and use \eqref{eq:idle-row}--\eqref{eq:rotate-row}.
Given final label $s$ and query $j$, use binary mean $r_Ne_j^Tv_s$.
The row identities imply by induction that the distribution's barycenter
in $P_t$ is exactly the rotated seed. This proves every conditional row
of \eqref{eq:array}, not an approximation to the angle.
For an explicit initialization, the ray through $x$ meets one edge of
$P_0$; represent that boundary point by its two endpoints and mix with
the uniform zero barycenter to obtain $x/10$.
The continued-fraction recurrence bounds the first crossing denominator
by $(A+1)$ times the threshold. Theorem~\ref{thm:profiles} supplies the
matching vector-state lower order.
\end{proof}

This proves \eqref{eq:main-hidden}--\eqref{eq:main-vector}. In the
normalized label coordinates, both command matrices contract the
observable barycenter by $\lambda^{-1}$ at each command. Multiplying the
final decoder by $r_N$ compensates for exactly $N$ contractions. Querying
that same decoder earlier generally gives the wrong amplitude. Thus the
stationary command rows in this construction are not a clock-free
anytime realization, and do not contradict Section~\ref{sec:auto}.

The construction uses direct vertex states; it does not exploit the
logarithmic extensions of regular polygons. The matched order for
$V_N$ is a theorem about all vector-state chains, not evidence that
$W_N=V_N$. The gap
$\Omega_\alpha(\log N)\le W_N\le O_\alpha(N^{1/3})$ remains the
quantitative hidden-lift question. The new upper bound improves the
previous $O(N^{1/2})$ construction without asserting its own optimality
in the unrestricted class.

\begin{corollary}[A contractive comparison]\label{cor:contraction}
Replace the active mean map by $\rho R_\alpha$, $0<\rho<1$, and keep
the idle map. A single horizon-independent vector-state device realizes
all command lengths with any convergent order
$q\ge\max\{5,(40/(-\log\rho))^{1/3}\}$. For bounded-type angles this
uses $O_\alpha(1+(-\log\rho)^{-1/3})$ processing labels.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Use one fixed polygon of radius $1/4$. The resonant estimate implies
$\rho\lambda\le1$, so $\rho R_\alpha P\subset P$ and the idle map
preserves $P$. The active row is the boundary row of
\eqref{eq:rotate-row} mixed with uniform mass, with weight
$\rho\lambda$ on that row. A fixed decoder reads the same polygon at
all times. This is a quantitative instance of the classical invariant
polytope construction \cite{BF04}, not a new existence principle.
\end{proof}
