## Zelditch

*Inverse spectral problem for analytic domains, II: Z2-symmetric domains*,
Annals of Mathematics 170 (2009), 205–269.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.4007/annals.2009.170.205
Publisher record: https://annals.math.princeton.edu/2009/170-1/p06
Primary preprint text read: https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0111078
Locations: Section 1.1 defining the classes, Theorems 1.1 and 1.4,
proof overview and Section 6.1. The comparison refers to the stated class
hypotheses rather than claiming all analytic domains with an involution
are covered. The fixed bouncing-ball length in that class is distinguished
from A2's supplied labelled gap and curvatures.
