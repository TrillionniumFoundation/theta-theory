\begin{abstract}
We determine the finite-state prediction memory of positive sparse
monomial experiments, uniformly across additive exponent collisions.
The sharp profile is the maximum of fractional powers of exterior
Vandermonde volumes, truncated by the dimension attainable from the past.
Its lower bound is realized by positive-probability histories under one
common exploration law; a separate global cover and a causal recurrence
give the matching realization. The prior may be any full-support
probability. We also determine all linear widths of the local prior
ambiguity body: they are the full Newton scales, without the past-dimension
truncation. Exact lower-moment information exposes the individual
vanishing uncertainty directions. For an imperfect common moment name,
a dominated prior family and fixed interior slack, one program has
sharp minimax regret equal in order to the memory profile plus the
squared input error. Complete integer-table realizations require no
collision-stratum information. Their numerical interface, sufficient
precision bounds and acceptance checks are given separately from the
intrinsic classification.
\end{abstract}
