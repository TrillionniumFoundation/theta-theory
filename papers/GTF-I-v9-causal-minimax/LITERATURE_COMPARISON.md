## Attribution discipline

The occupancy theorem is deliberately identified with the designer/common-information tradition. Its finite minimax application is not advertised as inventing dynamic programming. The risk-body proof uses classical convex minimax and convex geometry. The saturation construction uses a backward refinement principle and elementary Bayes identities. The claims under reconsideration are the stated combination of information constraints, exact common-encoder converse, resource-sensitive randomization, constructed finite bridge and temporal comparison, with all their hypotheses visible.

The independent A2 primary algebraic results are not sources for those control claims and are not reassigned to GTF. Source hashes document which edition was used, not who has mathematical priority. Further independent literature assessment can change a novelty judgment without changing a correctness claim or a source identity.
