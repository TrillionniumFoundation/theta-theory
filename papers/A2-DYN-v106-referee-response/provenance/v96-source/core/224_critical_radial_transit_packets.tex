\section{Critical histories which cross a radial shell}
\label{sec:v94-radial}
The entrance and subsequent growth of one history should not always
be estimated separately. We now bound the actual target source of
all histories which traverse a prescribed radial shell in the backward
window. This includes births at critical directional entrances and
arbitrarily small internal incidences.

Let $q_*>0$ be a fixed small-gradient range for
\eqref{eq:v94-critical-finite}. For $0<b<D$ define the actual shell source
\[
 v^{b,D}(u)\,du=F_\#(I_m z^{-1}\one_{\{b\le z<D\}}\bar\nu).
\]
\begin{lemma}[The upper-gradient gain with its finite-count remainder]
\label{lem:v94-shell-source}
For every fixed auxiliary band and $0<b<D<q_*$ sufficiently small,
\begin{align}
 m^2\int_Jv^{b,D}
 &\le C D^{\alpha-1}(|J|+B_*^{-1})\notag\\
 &\quad+C_{B_*}(1+B_*|J|)m^2 b^{-1/2}\theta_{B_*}^m.
                                             \label{eq:v94-shell-finite}
\end{align}
In particular its ordered fixed-window mass is at most
$C D^{\alpha-1}|J|$, with leading constant independent of the
previously fixed positive $b$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The positive majorant
\[
 I_m z^{-1}\one_{\{b\le z<D\}}
 \le D^{-1}I_m\one_{\{z<D\}}
                  +\int_b^D q^{-2}I_m\one_{\{z<q\}}\,dq
\]
permits direct use of \eqref{eq:v94-critical-finite}. The leading
integral is bounded by
$D^{\alpha-1}(1+1/(\alpha-1))$. Its finite-count error is bounded
by $D^{-1/2}+\int_b^D q^{-3/2}\,dq\le2b^{-1/2}$.
Tonelli applies to this finite positive layer cake. Fix $b,D,J,B_*$,
take the collision limsup and then enlarge the auxiliary band.
No integration to $b=0$ is made.
\end{proof}

For a live target ancestor $a_\sigma<t<c_\sigma$, let
$u_\sigma^0=\max\{a_\sigma,t-r\}$ and use the right trace at that
roof. Write $\mathcal T_{c;r,b}(t)$ for all such ancestors satisfying
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v94-transit-selector}
             z_\sigma(u_\sigma^0+)<b,
                     \qquad z_\sigma(t)\ge2b.
\end{equation}
Define their actual target contribution by
\[
 N_{c;r,b}^\varepsilon(t)
       =\int_{\mathcal T_{c;r,b}(t)}f_\sigma^\varepsilon(t)\,d\sigma.
\]
The selector is Borel and contains no positive residence or incidence
cutoff. A crossing itself supplies the geometric length used below.

\begin{theorem}[Complete radial-transit target height]
\label{thm:v94-transit}
Put $D=(b^2+2\Lambda_\circ r)^{1/2}$ and suppose $D<q_*$.
At every finite count and almost every target roof,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v94-transit-finite}
 N_{c;r,b}^\varepsilon(t)
 \le\frac{2\Lambda_\circ}{3b^2}\int_{t-r}^t p_{2b}(u)\,du
       +C(1+\varepsilon^{-1})\int_{t-r}^t v^{b,D}(u)\,du.
\end{equation}
Thus, for fixed $r,b>0$ before the collision limit,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v94-transit-height}
 \limsup_m\sup_{R,n,k,c}m^2\|N_{c;r,b}^\varepsilon\|_\infty
 \le C r b^{\alpha-2}
       +C(1+\varepsilon^{-1})rD^{\alpha-1}.
\end{equation}
In particular $b=\sqrt r$ gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v94-transit-power}
 \limsup_m\sup_{R,n,k,c}m^2\|N_{c;r,\sqrt r}^\varepsilon\|_\infty
 \le C r^{129/256}
             +C(1+\varepsilon^{-1})r^{257/256}.
\end{equation}
This is an essential-height estimate for the complete selected target
source, not an integrated starting-roof estimate.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If $D\le2b$, the selected family is empty. Otherwise $2b<D<q_*$,
so the small-gradient source bounds apply throughout the shell.
For a selected ancestor, strict radial monotonicity gives unique
roofs $u_b<u_{2b}\le t$ with $z(u_b)=b$, $z(u_{2b})=2b$.
Both lie after $u_\sigma^0$ and hence in $(t-r,t]$. Equation
\eqref{eq:v94-radial} gives
\[
 u_{2b}-u_b\ge\frac{3b^2}{2\Lambda_\circ},\qquad
                  z(t)<(b^2+2\Lambda_\circ r)^{1/2}=D.
\]
For $v\in(u_b,u_{2b})$, the same selected prefix contains the whole
interval $(v,t)$. The fundamental theorem gives
\[
 f_\sigma^\varepsilon(t)\le f_\sigma^\varepsilon(v)
                   +\int_v^t[(f_\sigma^\varepsilon)'(u)]_+\,du.
\]
Average $v$ over the shell. Bound its reciprocal roof length by
$2\Lambda_\circ/(3b^2)$, and the derivative integral by the integral
from $u_b$ to $t$. In this latter interval $b\le z<D$ and
$[(f^\varepsilon)' ]_+\le
 C(1+\varepsilon^{-1})A I_m/z$.
Complete summation of the shell interiors is a submeasure of the
original source restricted to $z<2b$; complete summation of the
later intervals is a submeasure of its $v^{b,D}$ insertion. This
uses the Borel bijection with multiplicity one at each intermediate
roof, not a bound for the number of ancestors. Since $W^\varepsilon\le1$,
the first sum is bounded by $p_{2b}$. This proves
\eqref{eq:v94-transit-finite} without any inverse-Jacobian deletion.

The critical volume bound and Lemma~\ref{lem:v94-shell-source}, at
interval length $r$, imply \eqref{eq:v94-transit-height} after fixing
all widths and the auxiliary band before the collision limit. The
finite-count error contains $b^{-1/2}$ and is not declared uniform
at $b=0$. The choice $b=\sqrt r$ is made before that limit; it yields
$\alpha/2=129/256$ and $(\alpha+1)/2=257/256$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{What is estimated in a signed critical birth packet}
For an ancestor born within the window, integration by parts gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v94-birth-packet}
 k_{r,t}(a_\sigma)f_\sigma^\varepsilon(a_\sigma+)
 +\int_{a_\sigma}^t k_{r,t}(u)(f_\sigma^\varepsilon)'(u)\,du
 =f_\sigma^\varepsilon(t)-\frac1r\int_{a_\sigma}^t f_\sigma^\varepsilon(u)\,du.
\end{equation}
The positive part of the left side is at most the actual target
value. Hence Theorem~\ref{thm:v94-transit} estimates the complete
signed entrance-and-growth packet of every critical or near-critical
newborn ancestor satisfying \eqref{eq:v94-transit-selector}.
It does not estimate its two positive terms separately. A grazing
birth has zero trace but may have subsequent growth; it is included.
A critical birth may have a nonzero trace; it is included as well.
Histories which stay below $2b$ at the target are not covered by this
crossing theorem and remain in the exact pointwise problem.
