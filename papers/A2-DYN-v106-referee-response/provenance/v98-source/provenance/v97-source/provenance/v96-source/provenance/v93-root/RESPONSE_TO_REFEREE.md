## 1. Upper-spike persistence — requests 18.1 and 18.4

Module 220 uses the positive continuant directly: `y_m >= ell_i V_i y_i >= 2 tau_-^2/(R c_i)`. Consequently `omega/c_i <= R_+^2/(2 tau_-^2)` for every internal contact. Together with the depth sum, this bounds `omega I_m` uniformly on every regular sheet, without an incidence corridor. The full weighted angular density has derivative at most `C(1+epsilon^-1)/z`, hence a uniform one-half modulus along each complete roof segment. This gives one-sided traces even at critical and grazing ends. It is an absolute estimate; the logarithmic optical derivative is not claimed bounded.

The finite-count total trace and variation measures are justified by the physical tame graph and the radial integral. Qualitative fixed-count component finiteness is used only for existence of these measures. It is never inserted as a uniform word count in a height estimate.

Module 221 proves the exact signed derivative of every selected source. The original exceptional union is an initial prefix on each physical segment. Its interior cutoff has a **negative** coefficient. Positive increments come only from a true entrance trace or positive variation of the original weighted physical density. Grazing entrance traces vanish; backward critical directional traces remain. The historical critical residue is credited, not presented as newly discovered or removed because a critical point has zero area.

The ancestral comparison then proves a genuine prescribed-interval persistence dichotomy: a source value at least lambda persists above lambda/2 on the full backward interval of length r unless the same target's actual ancestral entrance and positive-growth budget is at least lambda/2. Only segments still selected at the target roof enter that budget. Ended prefixes and later re-entries cannot create a positive ancestor. This is not an integrated crossing theorem. Uniform smallness of the exceptional budget is not proved, so unconditional exclusion of all upper spikes is not asserted.

## 2. Prescribed-scale pointwise maximal route — request 18.2

Theorem `thm:v93-pointwise` proves, at finite count and almost every original roof,

`R_c(t) <= r^-1 integral_(t-r)^t R_c(s) ds + B_(c,r)(t) + G^+_(c,r)(t)`.

The radius r is prescribed before the collision limit. The left side is a source value, not its starting-roof integral. The entrance and gain expressions retain the triangular averaging weight and the target's ancestor selection. The proof starts with a sheetwise Stieltjes identity and uses the exact physical density `omega/(4 pi c_* chi)`; the averaging ramp is not a replacement Jacobian.

Module 222 estimates the complete noncritical gain by `C(1+epsilon^-1) integral_(t-r)^t v^rho`. The retained positive-cutoff volume theorem gives ordered height `C(1+epsilon^-1) r`, with one leading constant for all previously fixed rho>0. This estimate sums the original intermediate fibers with multiplicity one, not individual word constants.

The new comparison is stronger in topology than the preceding integrated oscillation estimate, but its positive entrance and critical-growth terms are not shown small. Thus it does not yet meet the full sufficient condition in request 18.2. The distinction is printed in the main theorem and abstract.

## 4. Limit order — request 18.5

The finite-count signed identity and full ancestor sums are formed before any collision supremum. In the noncritical estimate r, rho and all auxiliary bands are fixed first. The noncritical leading constant is rho-independent, but its finite-count remainder is not; the remaining critical term stays inside the normalized essential supremum and collision limsup before rho decreases. The outer reconstruction band B remains last. Choosing, for example, r(B)=c epsilon(B)^2 pays the explicit drift term but is not used to claim either positive forcing term vanishes.

The final bound is on the unchanged scalar error:

`E_M <= C_M B^(-1/192) + I_(epsilon(B),M)(r) + C_(epsilon(B),M)(r) + C(1+epsilon(B)^(-1)) r`.

The first and last terms are estimated. The two middle terms are the weighted ancestral entrance and critical positive-growth heights. Their vanishing is a sufficient physical target, not a theorem already proved here or an equivalence imported from small finite norms.
