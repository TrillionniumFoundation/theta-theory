\begin{abstract}
We study exact displacement, collision count and flight time for actual
returns in a triangular periodic Lorentz gas. We construct the global
distributional current of the complete physical boundary source,
including first incidence, competing-hit clearance and all states
outside the selected critical disks. Its chart representation retains
the entrance and exit atoms of every moving window. An exact compact
primitive identifies the discrepancy between the original source and
a fixed-width average. Keeping the previously controlled source, we
recover a portion of every remaining physical defect with ordered
height $O(\chi^6+K\varepsilon^{1/16})$. A directed local-flux bound
then gives an explicit sufficient estimate for the unrestricted
pointwise arithmetic law. The quantitative decay of the complete
current excess remains to be established; neither a window law nor
a finite-count variation bound is substituted for that estimate.
\end{abstract}

\paragraph{The exact record.}
For $R\in[9/20,47/100]$ consider the actual return record
$J_{n,R}=(K_{n,R},N_{n,R},T_{n,R})$ under
$\nu_R^*=c_*^{-1}\nu|_{Y_R^*}$, where $c_*=91/(10000\pi)$.
Occupation counts collisions $0,\ldots,m-1$; terminal membership
is imposed at collision $m$. The density $p_{n,R}(k,m,t)$ uses
counting measure in $(k,m)$ and Lebesgue measure in $t$. Our pointwise
problem is unchanged:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:intro-v72-target}
 \sup_{R,n,k}\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{t:\,|Z_{m,R}|\le M}
 \left|m^2p_{n,R}(k,m,t)-\mathcal L_{m,R}(k_1,k_2,t,n)\right|
                  \longrightarrow0,
\end{equation}
where $Z_{m,R}=(k_1,k_2,t-m\bar\tau_R,n-mc_*)/\sqrt m$.
The finite arithmetic transition kernel in $\mathcal L_{m,R}$,
including its zero classes, is retained. No exact label is replaced
by a block or by an average over return indices.

\paragraph{A current on the complete source.}
At fixed $m,R,n,k,\varepsilon$, let $b=b^\varepsilon_{n,k,m,R}$
be the density of the original first-physical-defect source. Its
complete positive decomposition is
\[
 b=s_{71,1}+d_{71,1}+b^{\varepsilon,\mathrm{inc}}+e_{70}.
\]
Here $s_{71,1}$ is the previously recovered selected-disk source,
and $e_{70}$ includes every original physical state outside those
disks. We define its distributional current directly on the original
collision space by
\[
 \langle\mathfrak D^\varepsilon,\phi\rangle
 =-\int_{\Pi_{n,k,m,R}}(1-H_{m,R}^\varepsilon)
                           \phi'(L_{m,R})\,d\nu_R^*.
\]
It is $Db$, independently of a chart partition. Theorem~
\ref{thm:v72-window-current} gives the missing entrance and exit
atoms when a moving selected-chart window is differentiated.
Theorem~\ref{thm:v72-complete-current} includes the incidence and
outside pieces, and Proposition~\ref{prop:v72-coarea-flux}
identifies the oriented coarea flux on regular physical patches.
No finite-measure or collision-uniform variation bound is built into
this definition.

\begin{leadtheorem}[Recovery on the whole physical source]
\label{thm:intro-v72-recovery}
Fix $0<\varepsilon\le\chi/4$, $0<\chi\le1/10$, $\delta>0$
and $K\ge1$ before the collision limit. Write
$A_\delta b(t)=(2\delta)^{-1}\int_{t-\delta}^{t+\delta}b(v)\,dv$.
There are complementary weights on the original physical source,
with densities $s_{72,K},r_{72,K}$, such that
\begin{align*}
 b&=s_{72,K}+r_{72,K},\\
 s_{72,K}&\ge s_{71,1},\qquad
 \mathcal H(s_{72,K})\le C\chi^6+CK\varepsilon^{1/16},\\
 0\le r_{72,K}&\le[b-KA_\delta b]_+.
\end{align*}
The residual is a displayed nonnegative weighting of all three
original remainders $d_{71,1}$, $b^{\varepsilon,\mathrm{inc}}$
and $e_{70}$. All original states, guards and exact labels remain
accounted for.
\end{leadtheorem}
\begin{proof}
Lemma~\ref{lem:v72-preserved-recovery} allocates only unused
capacity and preserves the old controlled source. The original
fixed-width local-variation bound estimates $A_\delta b$ in the
legally ordered limit. Theorem~\ref{thm:v72-global-recovery}
then proves the height bound without a word or witness count.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{The quantitative remaining estimate.}
The compact odd kernel $\kappa_\delta$ of
\eqref{eq:v72-average-kernel} satisfies
$D\kappa_\delta=\delta_0-(2\delta)^{-1}\one_{(-\delta,\delta)}dt$.
Consequently
\[
 b-A_\delta b=\kappa_\delta*\mathfrak D^\varepsilon
\]
as distributions and almost everywhere as densities. This identity
holds even when the current is not a measure. Define
\[
 \mathcal U_{\varepsilon,\delta}(K)
 =\limsup_m\sup_{R,n,k}m^2\|[b-KA_\delta b]_+\|_\infty.
\]
It uses the complete physically weighted current at a common roof,
not wordwise absolute variations. With the fixed-band choices
$\varepsilon(B)=A_0B^{-1/12}$ and
$\chi(B)=\sqrt{\varepsilon(B)}$, Theorem~
\ref{thm:v72-complete-budget} gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:intro-v72-budget}
 \begin{split}
 \mathcal E_M\le{}&C_MB^{-1/192}+CB^{-1/4}
             +CK(B)B^{-1/192}\\
             &+\mathcal U_{\varepsilon(B),\delta(B)}(K(B)).
 \end{split}
\end{equation}
Thus $K(B)B^{-1/192}\to0$ and vanishing of the last term suffice
for \eqref{eq:intro-v72-target}. For a complete current which is a
finite measure, Proposition~\ref{prop:v72-flux-height} bounds that
last term by half its directed local variation. In particular a
bound $\mathcal V_\varepsilon(\delta)\le A\varepsilon^{-q}\delta^\beta$
would give the explicit rate in \eqref{eq:v72-flux-power-consequence}.
These current bounds are not proved on the unrestricted Lorentz
family. The current construction and recovery theorem therefore do
not by themselves establish the unrestricted pointwise law or its
same-roof conditional consequences.

The new argument is given in
Sections~\ref{sec:v72-window-current}--\ref{sec:v72-all-source-recovery}.
Its principal quantitative inputs are the unchanged full-source
local-variation theorem, Theorem~\ref{thm:v49-physical-remainder-local},
and the selected-source estimate, Theorem~\ref{thm:v71-pooled-height}.
All preceding mathematical inputs are retained below. The exact
preceding opening is retained in Appendix~\ref{app:v72-retained-front}.

\paragraph{The retained return-window and bridge route.}
The new local constraint is proved in
Sections~\ref{sec:v37-occupation-local} and~\ref{sec:v37-return-window}.
It uses the exact section geometry, the inherited compensated
covariance and its nondegeneracy, and the compact collision spectrum
of the preceding part. The return index is resolved on its diffusive scale;
single-index inversion and pointwise roof inversion remain distinct
from that theorem. Sections~\ref{sec:v38-four-dimensional-concentration}
and~\ref{sec:v38-return-resolution} then replace the diffusive window
by any diverging integer block, prove a single-index upper bound, and
locate the exact inverse on a fixed occupation torus. The new
Sections~\ref{sec:v40-occupation-powers}--\ref{sec:v40-fixed-count-local}
then give a full fixed-band spectral reduction and evaluate the
fixed-radius interval law with its arithmetic coefficient. Sections~
\ref{sec:v41-transition}--\ref{sec:v41-residue-filters} then evaluate
the uniform transition kernel and prove the exact-event return bridge. The
complete inherited arithmetic, raw edge and return-frequency analysis
continues on the same physical record.
