None. Standard open-billiard coding and Ruelle theory are cited external inputs.

## Requested specialist checks

1. Check the explicit no-eclipse margins and the use of the fixed obstacle code.
2. Check the periodic-orbit proof of the mean-roof shape derivative.
3. Check that nonconjugacy is a flow statement and is not confused with symbolic conjugacy.
4. Check the Ruelle response theorem only in the declared trapped-set Markov-Gibbs scope.
5. Verify that benchmark results are not imported into the flagship theorem.
