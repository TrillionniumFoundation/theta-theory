\begin{abstract}
We establish a same-platform geometric response theorem for a concrete
non-similarity family of planar specular open dispersing billiards.  Three
strictly convex disks satisfy the no-eclipse condition uniformly, while the
radius of one obstacle varies.  The non-wandering set is uniformly separated
from grazing and is coded by one fixed subshift of finite type.  Pulling the
collision map, the free-flight roof, and physical observables to this fixed
symbolic space converts moving-boundary differentiation into ordinary
differentiation of Hölder potentials.

For a prescribed invariant Markov--Gibbs ensemble on the trapped set, the mean
free-flight time has the strict shape derivative
\[
 \bar\tau_a'
 =-2\int \mathbf 1_{\{\omega_0=1\}}\cos\varphi_a(\omega)\,d\nu(\omega)<0.
\]
Consequently the suspension transfer operator has a nonzero geometric source,
and its leading eigenvalue, pressure, spectral projector, reduced resolvent,
and every prescribed finite parameter jet are differentiable.  Two marked
periodic orbits prove that distinct parameters are not related by a
label-preserving conjugacy with one constant time rescaling.

We also isolate two benchmark mechanisms that remain useful but are no longer
the physical flagship of the series.  A similarity Lorentz family is retained
as the exact conjugate control and as a maximality theorem for coordinate
bypasses.  A four-branch moving-seam collision model is retained as an exact
all-order response and finite-difference benchmark.  Platform identifiers are
kept separate throughout: no theorem is assembled from different systems
without an explicit bridge.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

The central difficulty in response theory for billiards is not ordinary
operator perturbation.  A moving boundary changes collision points, flight
times, singularity sets, and the spaces on which transfer operators act.
Earlier formulations encoded this difficulty in broad structural assumptions.
The present paper uses a narrower but genuine physical class in which the
singular geometry can be removed before differentiation.

The primary platform, denoted \texttt{OB3-MG-v1}, is a three-obstacle
no-eclipse open billiard.  Its trapped set is compact, uniformly hyperbolic,
and uniformly non-grazing.  Obstacle itineraries give one symbolic phase space
for all parameters.  The moving obstacle remains physically nontrivial:
periodic flight lengths and the mean roof change.  Thus the construction is not
a similarity or zero-source bypass.

Two benchmark platforms are kept for different purposes.  The similarity
Lorentz platform \texttt{SL-SIM-v1} retains genuine periodic specular Lorentz
dynamics but moves only along an exact conjugacy orbit.  The four-branch
platform \texttt{FB4-EXACT-v1} has a genuinely moving partition and completely
explicit transfer-operator derivatives, but its full-cover structure is much
more rigid than a billiard.  Their roles are comparison, exact calculation,
and regression testing; the load-bearing physical theorem uses
\texttt{OB3-MG-v1} only.

We use standard open-billiard coding and smooth deformation results
\cite{Bowen1975,ChernovMarkarian2006,Wright2016}, classical Ruelle
perturbation theory \cite{Ruelle1978,ParryPollicott1990,Kato1995}, and elementary
shape differentiation of optical length.  The main contribution is a single
typed chain from explicit moving geometry to a nonzero suspension source.

\section{The explicit no-eclipse family}\label{sec:geometry}

For
\[
 I=\left[-\frac1{20},\frac1{20}\right]
\]
let
\[
 K_1(a)=\overline B((0,0),1+a),\qquad
 K_2=\overline B((6,0),1),\qquad
 K_3=\overline B((0,9),1).
\]
The billiard domain is the exterior
\[
 \Omega_a=\R^2\setminus\bigl(K_1(a)\cup K_2\cup K_3\bigr),
\]
and reflection is specular.

\begin{proposition}[Uniform separation and no eclipse]\label{prop:no-eclipse}
For every $a\in I$, the obstacles are pairwise disjoint and satisfy the
no-eclipse condition.  All separation and no-eclipse margins admit a positive
lower bound independent of $a$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The center distances are
\[
 d_{12}=6,\qquad d_{13}=9,\qquad d_{23}=\sqrt{117}=3\sqrt{13}.
\]
Since $r_1(a)\le21/20$, the pairwise separation margins are bounded below by
\[
 \frac{79}{20},\qquad \frac{139}{20},\qquad 3\sqrt{13}-2.
\]
For two closed balls,
\[
 \operatorname{conv}(B(c_i,r_i),B(c_j,r_j))
 \subset [c_i,c_j]+B(0,\max\{r_i,r_j\}).
\]
The distances from the third center to the three center segments are
\[
 9,\qquad 6,\qquad \frac{18}{\sqrt{13}}.
\]
The corresponding no-eclipse margins are at least
\[
 \frac{139}{20},\qquad \frac{79}{20},\qquad
 \frac{18}{\sqrt{13}}-\frac{41}{20}>0.
\]
\end{proof}

Let $M_a$ be the non-wandering collision set.  Define
\[
 \Sigma=\{\omega\in\{1,2,3\}^{\mathbb Z}:
 \omega_{k+1}\ne\omega_k\}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Uniform symbolic platform]\label{thm:coding}
For every finite $r\ge1$, after viewing the circle boundaries in fixed angular
coordinates, there are coding maps
\[
 \chi_a:\Sigma\longrightarrow M_a
\]
such that
\[
 B_a\circ\chi_a=\chi_a\circ\sigma.
\]
The family $a\mapsto\chi_a$ is $C^r$ as a map into a Hölder space whenever the
obstacle family is $C^{r+2}$.  The collision angles on all coded trajectories
satisfy
\[
 \cos\varphi_a(\omega)\ge c_*>0
\]
with $c_*$ independent of $a$ and $\omega$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The no-eclipse condition gives a unique billiard trajectory for every
admissible bi-infinite obstacle code and uniform hyperbolicity of the trapped
set.  The collision point sequence is the fixed point of the usual
contraction/graph-transform equation on exponentially weighted sequences.
The constants in that contraction are uniform by \cref{prop:no-eclipse}.
Differentiating the fixed-point equation gives $C^r$ parameter dependence.
Compactness of $I\times\Sigma$ and exclusion of tangencies give the uniform
non-grazing bound.  This is the standard open-billiard coding construction;
see \cite{ChernovMarkarian2006,Wright2016}.
\end{proof}

\section{A marked-period nonconjugacy test}\label{sec:nonconjugacy}

The normal period-two orbit between $K_1(a)$ and $K_2$ has full period
\[
 L_{12}(a)=2\bigl(6-(1+a)-1\bigr)=8-2a.
\]
The period between $K_2$ and $K_3$ is
\[
 L_{23}=2(\sqrt{117}-2).
\]

\begin{theorem}[No constant-time conjugacy]\label{thm:nonconjugacy}
If $a\ne0$, the billiard flow at parameter $a$ is not related to the flow at
$a=0$ by a label-preserving topological conjugacy with one constant time
rescaling.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
A constant-time conjugacy multiplies every marked periodic orbit length by the
same factor $\lambda$.  The unchanged $23$ orbit forces $\lambda=1$.  The
$12$ orbit then forces $8-2a=8$, hence $a=0$.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
The collision maps on their trapped sets are symbolically conjugate.  The
theorem asserts that the physical suspension data are not reduced to one
global time scale.
\end{remark}

\section{The actual roof and its shape derivative}\label{sec:roof}

Write $q_k(a,\omega)$ for the collision point coded by $\omega_k$ and define
the free-flight roof
\[
 \tau_a(\omega)=
 \abs{q_1(a,\omega)-q_0(a,\omega)}.
\]
By \cref{thm:coding}, $a\mapsto\tau_a$ is $C^r$ in a Hölder norm and
\[
 0<\tau_*\le\tau_a\le\tau^*<\infty
\]
uniformly.

Fix the stationary Markov measure $\nu$ on $\Sigma$ with transition matrix
\[
 P=\begin{pmatrix}
 0&2/3&1/3\\
 1/3&0&2/3\\
 2/3&1/3&0
 \end{pmatrix},
 \qquad \pi=(1/3,1/3,1/3).
\]
Put
\[
 \bar\tau_a=\int_\Sigma\tau_a\,d\nu.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Strict mean-roof response]\label{thm:roof-response}
The mean roof is $C^r$ and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:roof-response}
 \bar\tau_a'
 =
 -2\int_\Sigma
 \mathbf 1_{\{\omega_0=1\}}
 \cos\varphi_a(\omega)\,d\nu(\omega).
\end{equation}
In particular,
\[
 \bar\tau_a'\le-\frac{2c_*}{3}<0.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
First take a periodic code $\omega$ of period $n$.  Its marked orbit is a
critical point of the cyclic optical-length functional
\[
 \mathcal L_\omega(a)
 =\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}
 \abs{q_{k+1}(a,\omega)-q_k(a,\omega)},\qquad q_n=q_0.
\]
When $\mathcal L_\omega$ is differentiated, tangential derivatives of every
collision point cancel between the incoming and outgoing legs; this is the
Fermat stationarity condition, equivalently the specular law.  At a collision
with $K_1(a)$ the radial boundary velocity is one, and the two adjacent legs
contribute $-\cos\varphi$ each.  Hence
\[
 \frac{d}{da}\mathcal L_\omega(a)
 =
 -2\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}
 \mathbf 1_{\{\omega_k=1\}}
 \cos\varphi_a(\sigma^k\omega).
\]
The left side is also the derivative of
$\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}\tau_a(\sigma^k\omega)$.

Periodic orbit measures are weak-star dense in invariant measures of the
mixing finite-type shift.  Both functions in the identity are uniformly
Hölder, so the identity passes to $\nu$.  Since
$\nu(\omega_0=1)=1/3$ and $\cos\varphi_a\ge c_*$, the strict bound follows.
\end{proof}

\section{A fixed Ruelle family and nonzero source}\label{sec:ruelle}

Choose a one-sided Hölder representative $\widehat\tau_a$ cohomologous to
$\tau_a$.  Let $g:\Sigma^+\to\R^d$ be a Hölder collision observable and define
\[
 (\mathcal R_{a,q,s}F)(x)
 =
 \sum_{\sigma y=x}
 P_{y_0x_0}
 \exp\{q\cdot g(y)-s\widehat\tau_a(y)\}F(y).
\]
The operator acts on one fixed Hölder space $C^\alpha(\Sigma^+)$.

\begin{proposition}[Geometric source]\label{prop:source}
The parameter derivative is
\[
 \partial_a\mathcal R_{a,q,s}F
 =
 -s\,\mathcal R_{a,q,s}
 \bigl[(\partial_a\widehat\tau_a)F\bigr].
\]
The Hölder source $G_a=\partial_a\widehat\tau_a$ is nonzero and satisfies
\[
 \int G_a\,d\nu=\bar\tau_a'<0.
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Differentiate the exponential weight in the fixed symbolic coordinate.
Cohomologous roofs have the same invariant mean, so the final identity follows
from \cref{thm:roof-response}.
\end{proof}

Let $\lambda(a,q,s)$ be the simple leading eigenvalue near $(q,s)=(0,0)$,
$\Pi_{a,q,s}$ its spectral projection, and $R_{a,q,s}$ the reduced resolvent.

\begin{theorem}[Finite-order response package]\label{thm:response-package}
For every prescribed finite $N$, the maps
\[
 (a,q,s)\longmapsto
 \lambda,\quad \Pi,\quad R,\quad \log\lambda
\]
are $C^N$ near $I\times\{0\}\times\{0\}$, provided the obstacle family is
$C^{N+2}$.  Every derivative is a finite sum of well-typed resolvent words.
For analytic obstacle families the maps are real analytic.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The family $\mathcal R_{a,q,s}$ is $C^N$ in operator norm on
$C^\alpha(\Sigma^+)$.  The untwisted Markov--Ruelle operator has a simple
eigenvalue one and a uniform spectral gap because the shift is mixing and the
parameter set is compact.  A common Riesz contour therefore exists.
Differentiate
\[
 \Pi_{a,q,s}
 =\frac1{2\pi i}\int_\Gamma
 (z-\mathcal R_{a,q,s})^{-1}\,dz,
\]
using
\[
 D(z-\mathcal R)^{-1}
 =(z-\mathcal R)^{-1}(D\mathcal R)(z-\mathcal R)^{-1}.
\]
Induction yields all finite resolvent words.  Analyticity follows from the
analytic implicit-function theorem for the coding and analytic perturbation
theory.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Pressure and equilibrium response]\label{cor:pressure}
The collision pressure and the associated equilibrium functionals have every
prescribed finite parameter derivative.  The first physical geometric
derivative is not identically zero.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Pressure is $\log\lambda$.  Nontriviality follows from
\cref{prop:source}: the $s$-direction source has strictly negative invariant
mean.
\end{proof}

\section{The similarity Lorentz control}\label{sec:similarity}

Let $T_0$ be a fixed finite-horizon periodic Lorentz collision map and let
\[
 S_a(x)=b_a+s_aR_ax,\qquad s_a>0,\quad R_a\in O(2).
\]
Transport the lattice and all obstacles by $S_a$.

\begin{proposition}[Exact similarity formulas]\label{prop:similarity}
There is a collision conjugacy $H_a$ such that
\[
 H_a^{-1}T_aH_a=T_0.
\]
If $\kappa_0$ is the cell displacement and $\tau_0$ the roof, then
\[
 \kappa_a=s_aR_a\kappa_0,\qquad
 \tau_a=s_a\tau_0.
\]
Consequently every pressure and diffusion response is obtained by finite
dimensional chain rules.
\end{proposition}

\begin{theorem}[Similarity maximality]\label{thm:similarity-maximality}
Let $F:\R^2\to\R^2$ be a $C^2$ global diffeomorphism that sends every free
flight line to a line, preserves the specular equal-angle law for every
direction, and changes speed by one direction-independent positive factor.
Then
\[
 F(x)=b+sRx
\]
for some $b\in\R^2$, $s>0$, and $R\in O(2)$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
A global line-preserving diffeomorphism is affine, $F(x)=Ax+b$.  Preservation
of all angles gives $A^TA=s^2I$.  The polar decomposition yields $A=sR$.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
The result is retained as a control and a no-bypass theorem.  It is not used to
prove the nonzero source on \texttt{OB3-MG-v1}.
\end{remark}

\section{The four-branch exact benchmark}\label{sec:four-branch}

For
\[
 a\in[-1/40,1/40]
\]
define
\[
 (w_1,w_2,w_3,w_4)
 =(1/10+a,1/5+2a,3/10-a,2/5-2a),
\]
let $s_i=\sum_{j\le i}w_j$, and set
\[
 T_a(x)=\frac{x-s_{i-1}(a)}{w_i(a)}
 \quad\text{on }[s_{i-1}(a),s_i(a)).
\]
Its transfer operator is
\[
 (L_ah)(y)
 =\sum_{i=1}^4w_i(a)
 h(s_{i-1}(a)+w_i(a)y).
\]

Let $v=(1,2,-1,-2)$,
$A_i=\sum_{j\le i}v_j$, and
$r_i(y)=A_{i-1}+v_iy$.

\begin{proposition}[All-order derivative]\label{prop:all-order}
For every $k\ge1$,
\[
 \partial_a^kL_ah
 =
 \sum_{i=1}^4
 \left[
 k v_i r_i^{k-1}h^{(k-1)}(\psi_i^a)
 +w_i(a)r_i^kh^{(k)}(\psi_i^a)
 \right],
\]
where $\psi_i^a(y)=s_{i-1}(a)+w_i(a)y$.  Hence
\[
 \partial_a^kL_a:W^{r+k,1}\longrightarrow W^{r,1}.
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Differentiate
$w_i(a)h(\psi_i^a)$ repeatedly.  Both $w_i$ and $\psi_i^a$ are affine in
$a$, so all terms except the two displayed families vanish.
\end{proof}

Let $\Pi h=\int_0^1h$ and $Q_a=L_a-\Pi$.

\begin{theorem}[Exact product response]\label{thm:four-branch-product}
For every fixed $r\ge1$ there are $C_r<\infty$ and
$\rho=9/20<1$ such that, for centered $g$,
\[
 \norm{Q_a^mg}_{W^{r,1}}\le C_r\rho^m\norm g_{W^{r,1}}.
\]
For every $k\ge1$ and bounded test $f$,
\[
 \abs{\pair{Q_a^n(\partial_a^kL_a)Q_a^mg}{f}}
 \le C_{r,k}\rho^{m+n}
 \norm g_{W^{r+k,1}}\norm f_\infty.
\]
The corresponding finite-difference arrays converge in
$\ell^1(\N^2)$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Each inverse branch contracts variation by at most $\max_iw_i(a)=9/20$.
The derivative in \cref{prop:all-order} loses exactly $k$ Sobolev levels.
Apply the contraction before and after the derivative.  Taylor's formula gives
finite-head convergence, while the common product bound controls the tail.
\end{proof}

\section{Platform discipline}\label{sec:platform}

\begin{definition}[Platform-typed theorem]
A theorem is platform typed if its statement records the dynamical system,
invariant law, clock, information structure, and parameter scope.  A
same-platform conclusion may use only imports carrying the same platform
identifier, unless a separate bridge theorem is stated and proved.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Roles of the three platforms]\label{prop:roles}
The results of this paper have the following noninterchangeable roles:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{platform}&\text{role}\\ \hline
\texttt{OB3-MG-v1}&\text{physical moving-obstacle response}\\
\texttt{FB4-EXACT-v1}&\text{exact all-order and finite-DQ benchmark}\\
\texttt{SL-SIM-v1}&\text{conjugate control and maximality boundary}.
\end{array}
\]
No theorem in later papers may import a coefficient from one row and a
probabilistic limit from another row while calling the result same-platform.
\end{proposition}

\section{Main conclusion}

\begin{theorem}[Geometric-response main theorem]\label{thm:main}
The family \texttt{OB3-MG-v1} is a uniformly no-eclipse, specular,
non-similarity moving open billiard.  On one fixed Hölder symbolic space it has
a $C^N$ Ruelle response package for every prescribed finite $N$, a nonzero
suspension source, and a strictly varying mean physical roof.  These data are
the only response imports used by Papers II and III.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Combine \cref{prop:no-eclipse,thm:coding,thm:nonconjugacy,thm:roof-response,prop:source,thm:response-package}.
\end{proof}

\section*{Scope and review status}

The theorem concerns the trapped non-wandering set and the specified
Markov--Gibbs ensemble.  It does not claim SRB or Liouville response for a
periodic Sinai billiard with global moving grazing singularities.  The
manuscript is a research draft prepared for independent external review.
