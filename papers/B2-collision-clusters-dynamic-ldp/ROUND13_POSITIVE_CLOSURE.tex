\section{Round-thirteen positive closure: global trajectory clusters and the actual-contact LDP}
\label{sec:r13-b2}

The fixed-horizon grand-canonical theorem is proved directly.  No approximate
one-block expansion is repeated over a vanishing mesh, so no unproved
linear-in-block-length recollision modulus is needed.  Cyclic genealogies are
handled one at a time with their true Jacobi map and then summed by an explicit
factorial majorant.

\subsection{The compatible trace graph}

Let $\Omega_k^\varepsilon$ be the $k$-sphere configuration domain.  A state in
the hierarchy consists of an interior finite measure $g_k$ and one incoming
normal-flux measure $\gamma_k^-$ on every regular binary collision face.  The
outgoing trace is the push-forward of $\gamma_k^-$ by the elastic involution.
Multiple-collision and grazing corners are retained as graph boundary strata
but are not independent flux coordinates.

\begin{theorem}[Closed Green trace graph]
\label{thm:r13-b2-trace}
The free-transport plus specular-collision operator is closed on the graph
norm
\[
 \|g\|_{\rm tr}=
 \sum_{k\ge0}{e^{\alpha k}\over k!}
 \left(\|g_k\|_w+
       \|v\cdot n\,\gamma_k^-\|_w\right).
\]
For every graph-domain test $\phi$,
\[
 \langle g_k,(\partial_t+V_k\cdot\nabla_{X_k})\phi\rangle
 +\langle\gamma_k^-,\phi^+-\phi^-\rangle
\]

equals the endpoint pairing.  Graph-bounded weak convergence preserves the
identity.  Any finite-action limiting contact measure gives zero mass to
grazing or multiple-collision corners.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Apply the divergence-measure Green formula on each smooth binary face and use
one incoming flux convention; the elastic involution supplies the outgoing
trace.  Truncate away from corner strata, pass to the limit with the weighted
normal-flux bound, and close the operator graph.  A corner has zero reference
collision activity $A_f$.  Therefore a positive measure charging it is
singular with respect to $A_f$ and has infinite Poisson entropy.  Finite-action
limits consequently need only the codimension-one trace.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A summable all-contact genealogy majorant}

A real-trajectory genealogy has $k$ labels, $k-1$ creation contacts, and $m$
surplus contacts, all ordered in physical time.  Let
$\mathfrak w_{k,m}^\varepsilon(H)$ be its absolute source-decorated activity.

\begin{lemma}[Factorial trajectory majorant]
\label{lem:r13-b2-majorant}
For every bounded source radius $R$ there are $C_R,T_R>0$ such that
\[
 \sum_{\mathcal G\in\mathfrak G_{k,m}}
 \int|d\mathfrak w_{\mathcal G}^\varepsilon(H)|
 \le
 k!C_R^k{(C_RT)^{k-1+m}\over(k-1+m)!},
 \qquad T\le T_R.
\]
The bound is uniform in $\varepsilon$, in all homogeneous chronological
charts, and after any fixed number of source derivatives.  Hence the sum over
$k,m$ is absolutely convergent.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Collision coarea contributes the incoming flux and one ordered contact time.
Energy conservation absorbs all post-collisional velocity weights.  Choose a
rooted spanning creation tree and then order the $m$ surplus contacts.  The
time simplex has volume $T^{k-1+m}/(k-1+m)!$.  Cayley/tree and contact-pair
counts are bounded by $k!C^{k+m}$ after the exponential label weight is
reduced once.  Bounded particle and contact sources only change $C$ to $C_R$.
Summing homogeneity strips uses the normal flux factor.  This gives the
stated majorant and its derivative versions.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Physical fixed-genealogy transversality}

Fix a regular homogeneous chronology $\mathcal G$ with at least one surplus
contact.  At its first surplus time choose the youngest creation fork whose
two descendants meet again.  Let $\xi\in\mathbb R^2$ be two independent root
translations transverse to the common center-of-mass direction, continued
through all earlier creation constraints by the implicit-function theorem.
Let $F_{\mathcal G}(\xi)$ be the two transverse components of the relative
position at the surplus time, computed with the full linearized free-flight
and specular-collision maps, including normal variation.

\begin{lemma}[Nonidentical physical Jacobi minor]
\label{lem:r13-b2-jacobi}
For every regular genealogy $\mathcal G$, the analytic function
$\det D_\xi F_{\mathcal G}$ is not identically zero.  Consequently there are
constants $C_{\mathcal G},\beta_{\mathcal G}>0$ such that
\[
 \operatorname{Leb}\{\xi:
   |F_{\mathcal G}(\xi)|\le\varepsilon\}
 \le C_{\mathcal G}\varepsilon^{\beta_{\mathcal G}}
\]

after integration with the incoming flux and the remaining genealogy
variables.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The full collision differential maps Jacobi data by the derivative of
\[
 (x,v,x_*,v_*)\mapsto
 (x,v-((v-v_*)\cdot\omega)\omega,
  x_*,v_*+((v-v_*)\cdot\omega)\omega),
\]
including $D\omega$.  It is an invertible symplectic map on the regular
collision tangent space.  Suppose every two-dimensional transverse minor at
the first surplus vanished identically.  Then one nonzero combination of the
two root translation Jacobi fields would have zero transverse relative
position at the surplus contact and be tangent to every intervening collision
constraint.  Backward propagation by the invertible collision and free-flight
maps would make the same field tangent to both independent root translations,
contradicting their construction at the separating fork.  Hence one specified
minor is nonzero at a regular point and therefore is not identically zero.
Quantitative analytic sublevel estimates on this fixed chart give the last
bound.  The exponent is allowed to depend on $\mathcal G$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Summed cyclic sector vanishes]
\label{thm:r13-b2-cycles}
For every fixed $T\le T_R$ and bounded source ball,
\[
 \sum_{k,m\ge1}
 \sum_{\mathcal G\in\mathfrak G_{k,m}}
 \int|d\mathfrak w_{\mathcal G}^\varepsilon(H)|
 \longrightarrow0
 \qquad(\varepsilon\to0),
\]
where the sum is restricted to genealogies with at least one surplus contact.
The same holds after any fixed number of source derivatives.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For each fixed genealogy,
Lemma~\ref{lem:r13-b2-jacobi} makes the first-surplus tube weight tend to zero.
The full genealogy, including all later true reflected contacts, is dominated
by the activity in Lemma~\ref{lem:r13-b2-majorant}.  That majorant is summable
independently of $\varepsilon$.  Dominated convergence over the countable
family proves the theorem.  No genealogy-uniform Lojasiewicz exponent and no
blockwise $h$ factor are asserted.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Grand-canonical pressure on a fixed horizon}

Define
\[
 Q_{\varepsilon,T}^{\rm gc}(H)
 ={1\over\mu_\varepsilon}
 \log\mathbb E_\varepsilon^{\rm gc}
 e^{\mu_\varepsilon\langle H,
      (\Pi_T^\varepsilon,\Gamma_T^\varepsilon)\rangle}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Global marked pressure and Hamiltonian]
\label{thm:r13-b2-pressure}
For every bounded real source $H$ and fixed $T<T_*$,
$Q_{\varepsilon,T}^{\rm gc}(H)$ converges locally uniformly, with all finite
source derivatives, to the unique mild solution of the marked
Hamilton--Jacobi equation with Hamiltonian
\[
 \mathbb H(f,p,\psi)
 =\langle f,v\cdot\nabla_xp\rangle
 +\int\left(e^{\Delta p+\psi}-1\right)dA_f.
\]
The statement for arbitrary bounded source radius is obtained by exact time
composition of the microscopic flow and uniqueness of the mild equation, not
by accumulating an approximate one-block recollision error.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
On a source-dependent short interval, the logarithm is the normally convergent
connected trajectory series of Lemma~\ref{lem:r13-b2-majorant}.  Theorem
\ref{thm:r13-b2-cycles} removes every cyclic genealogy, while creation trees
converge by collision coarea to Boltzmann trees.  Decomposing a limiting tree
at its last contact gives the displayed Hamiltonian and Picard uniqueness.
For a longer fixed horizon, compose the exact finite-volume Feynman--Kac
operators on finitely many source-dependent subintervals.  Pass to the limit
on each factor and use uniqueness of the mild equation.  There is no mesh
limit and hence no need for a uniform $O(h)$ cyclic modulus.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The full grand-canonical joint LDP}

A pair $(f,\Gamma)$ is feasible when it satisfies the Green balance of
Theorem~\ref{thm:r13-b2-trace}, the exchange/pre-post symmetries, and the
weighted moment conditions.  Put
\[
 \mathcal I_T^{\rm gc}(f,\Gamma)
 =I_0^{\rm gc}(f_0)+
 \begin{cases}
 \displaystyle\int\ell\!
  \left({d\Gamma\over dA_f}\right)dA_f,
 &\Gamma\ll A_f\text{ and }(f,\Gamma)\text{ feasible},\\[2mm]
 +\infty,&\text{otherwise}.
 \end{cases}
\]

\begin{theorem}[Good density--actual-contact LDP]
\label{thm:r13-b2-ldp}
The pair $(\pi^\varepsilon,\Gamma^\varepsilon)$ satisfies a full good LDP on
the weighted trace-graph path space with speed $\mu_\varepsilon$ and rate
$\mathcal I_T^{\rm gc}$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The pressure theorem on every bounded real-source ball, together with particle
and contact exponential moments, gives the upper bound and exponential
compact containment.  Scaling smooth balance tests makes every nonfeasible
pair have infinite dual cost.  Monotone source truncation gives the full
Poisson entropy, including singular contact measures.

For the lower bound, take a finite-action pair and write
$q=d\Gamma/dA_f$.  Approximate the positive function $q$ and the initial
density by smooth bounded positive functions $q_n,f_{0,n}$.  Solve the
controlled Boltzmann equation with collision activity $q_nA_{f_n}$; then
$(f_n,q_nA_{f_n})$ is positive and exactly balanced, so no signed Hodge repair
is needed.  Stability of the controlled equation gives convergence to
$(f,\Gamma)$ and entropy convergence.  The bounded source
$\psi_n=\log q_n-\Delta p_n$ defines the microscopic tilted law; normal
pressure convergence gives concentration and the required lower cost.  A
diagonal truncation completes the recovery and proves goodness.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Microcanonical transfer}

\begin{theorem}[Primitive-shell joint LDP]
\label{thm:r13-b2-micro}
Under the B1 primitive shell, the same dynamic entropy is valid and only the
initial term changes:
\[
 \mathcal I_T^{\rm mc,a}(f,\Gamma)
 =I_{0,a}(f_0)+
 \int\ell(d\Gamma/dA_f)dA_f
\]

after imposing feasibility.  The rate is good and its zero-cost path is the
prepared Boltzmann solution with its actual limiting contact intensity.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
B1 gives the source-dependent exact-number shell coefficient uniformly for
every bounded density/contact source.  Apply that change of measure to the
upper-bound sources and to the bounded recovery tilts in the proof of
Theorem~\ref{thm:r13-b2-ldp}; then pass through the same truncation diagonal.
Only the initial constrained entropy changes.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Round-thirteen B2 closure]
\label{thm:r13-b2-main}
The deterministic hard-sphere gas has a compatible trace graph, a directly
summed fixed-horizon all-contact pressure, and full grand-canonical and
primitive-shell density--actual-contact LDPs.  Recollision cycles are removed
by fixed-genealogy physical Jacobi transversality plus a factorial global
majorant, not by a nonexistent blockwise modulus.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Combine Theorems~\ref{thm:r13-b2-trace},
\ref{thm:r13-b2-cycles}, \ref{thm:r13-b2-pressure},
\ref{thm:r13-b2-ldp}, and \ref{thm:r13-b2-micro}.
\end{proof}
