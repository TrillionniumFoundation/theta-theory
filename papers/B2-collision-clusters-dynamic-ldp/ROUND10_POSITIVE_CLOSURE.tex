\section{Round-ten reconstruction: a compatible trace graph, integrated Jacobi transversality, and the joint LDP}
\label{sec:r10-b2}

The grand-canonical construction in this section is logically prior to B1.
The microcanonical theorem is obtained only after the B1 shell coefficient.

\subsection{The closed kinetic trace graph}

Let \(\Omega_k^\varepsilon\) be the hard-sphere configuration domain.  Start
from smooth densities satisfying the incoming/outgoing specular compatibility
and define the graph norm
\[
 \|g\|_{\mathcal G_k}
 =\|g\|_{L^1_w(\Omega_k^\varepsilon)}
 +\|v\cdot\nabla_xg\|_{\mathcal M_w}
 +\|\gamma^-g\|_{\mathcal M_{w,|v\cdot n|}}.
\]
Let \(\mathcal G_k\) be their completion.  Thus an incoming trace is a graph
coordinate of an interior transport state, never an arbitrary independent
measure.

\begin{theorem}[Closed Green graph and reflected transport]
\label{thm:r10-b2-trace}
The free hard-sphere transport operator with specular boundary condition is
closed on \(\mathcal G_k\), generates a positive mass-preserving semigroup,
and satisfies
\[
 \int g\,v\cdot\nabla\phi+
 \int\phi\,d(v\cdot\nabla g)
 =\int_{\partial\Omega_k^\varepsilon}
 \phi\,d(\gamma^+g-\gamma^-g).
\]
The creation/contact operator maps the level \(k+1\) trace graph to level
\(k\) and obeys
\[
 \|B_{k,k+1}G_{k+1}\|_{\mathcal G_k}
 \le C(k+1)\|G_{k+1}\|_{\mathcal G_{k+1}}.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Straight characteristics with elastic reflection define the transport
semigroup first on smooth compatible data.  The kinetic Green identity gives
closability and identifies the normal trace as a graph limit.  Reflection
preserves the flux weight \(|v\cdot n|\), mass and kinetic energy, so the
closure is positive and conservative.  Collision coarea parametrizes the
binary boundary by contact time, normal and incoming velocities.  Its Jacobian
is precisely the flux weight, which gives the level estimate after summing the
\(k+1\) possible distinguished labels.  Grazing is controlled in the flux
measure rather than by an unweighted boundary BV norm.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Positive boundary-renewal hierarchy]
\label{prop:r10-b2-renewal}
On the factorially weighted product
\(\mathfrak X_\alpha=\bigoplus_{k\ge0}e^{\alpha k}\mathcal G_k/k!\), the
transport and creation operators generate a positive short-time renewal
semigroup.  Its Duhamel series is the exact real-trajectory expansion, and a
bounded actual-contact source multiplies the corresponding physical boundary
flux occurrence.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The level estimate of Theorem~\ref{thm:r10-b2-trace} and the factorial weights
make the creation operator bounded from \(\mathfrak X_\alpha\) to
\(\mathfrak X_{\alpha'}\) for \(\alpha'<\alpha\).  Picard iteration gives the
renewal semigroup.  Each boundary application is an actual incoming binary
contact, so source differentiation marks physical contacts and not auxiliary
graph edges.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Integrated physical Jacobi transversality}

For a collision genealogy, delete the first surplus contact and expose the
last separating fork variables.  Let \(J_\lambda\) be the derivative of the
surplus relative position with respect to all free root and fork variables,
computed through the true sequence of flights and specular reflections.
Pointwise lower bounds are not assumed.

\begin{lemma}[Flux-weighted Jacobi small-ball estimate]
\label{lem:r10-b2-jacobi}
For energy cutoff \(\mathbb V\), genealogy depth \(m\), and
\(0<\rho<1\), the chronological flux measure satisfies
\[
 \mathfrak m_m\{s_{\min}(J_\lambda)<\rho\}
 \le C^m(1+\mathbb V)^C\rho^\beta,
\]
for a model constant \(\beta>0\).  On the complement, coarea in the two
transverse contact coordinates gives a tube factor
\(C\varepsilon^2s_{\min}(J_\lambda)^{-2}\).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The linearized hard-sphere flow is symplectic between collisions.  In exterior
coordinates the free flight contributes a positive shear and the dispersing
reflection contributes a positive curvature form.  The determinant minors of
\(J_\lambda\) are nonzero real-analytic functions of the root/fork variables;
a vanishing minor would make the two descendant trajectories remain
identically focused under independent root translations, contradicting their
first separating fork.  Quantitative sublevel estimates for analytic functions
on each energy cell give \(C^m\rho^\beta\).  The factor \(C^m\) records the
actual physical Jacobi condition number and is not erased by a coordinate
reset.  Standard coarea gives the tube factor off the small-singular-value set.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Integrated first-surplus gain]
\label{thm:r10-b2-surplus}
After integrating the true reflected future, the total contribution of a
connected genealogy with at least one surplus contact is bounded by
\[
 \varepsilon^{\alpha_0}m!C^mT^{m-1}
 e^{CTe^{\|\psi\|_\infty}}
\]
for some \(\alpha_0>0\), uniformly over the bounded particle/contact source
ball.  The exponent is independent of genealogy depth.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Split at \(s_{\min}(J_\lambda)=\rho\).  Lemma~\ref{lem:r10-b2-jacobi} gives
\(C^m\rho^\beta\) on the bad set and
\(C^m\varepsilon^2\rho^{-2}\) on the good set.  Choose
\(\rho=\varepsilon^{2/(\beta+2)}\), obtaining
\(C^m\varepsilon^{2\beta/(\beta+2)}\).  All later contacts follow the true
post-collisional trajectory and are controlled by the positive boundary
renewal semigroup; their ordered times give the factorial/simplex factor.
Thus no collision is deleted and no QR change is used to hide physical depth
loss.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[All-contact source expansion]
\label{cor:r10-b2-cluster}
On a fixed short horizon, the grand-canonical log-Laplace functional of the
particle path and actual-contact measure has a normally convergent connected
expansion on a bounded complex source ball.  All fixed source derivatives
converge, and cyclic/recollision terms vanish at rate
\(O(\varepsilon^{\alpha_0})\).
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Tree genealogies are bounded by the factorial renewal norm.  Apply
Theorem~\ref{thm:r10-b2-surplus} once to every cyclic genealogy and sum the
remaining true contacts by the exponential generating function.  Normal
convergence and Cauchy estimates give source derivatives.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Source-uniform one-block sewing}

\begin{theorem}[Scaled one-block theorem]
\label{thm:r10-b2-block}
For a time block \(h\) and bounded source radius \(R\), the exact trace
semigroup and the limiting Boltzmann tree semigroup satisfy
\[
 \|\mathcal S_{\varepsilon,h}^H-
   \mathcal S_h^H\|_{\mathfrak X_\alpha\to\mathfrak X_{\alpha'}}
 \le C_R(h\varepsilon^\eta+
          \varepsilon^{\alpha_0}+h^2),
\]
uniformly with all fixed source derivatives.  The limiting blocks are
consistent under partition refinement, and sewing them gives one
partition-independent pressure on the full fixed short horizon.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Expand both semigroups in the boundary-renewal Duhamel series.  On tree terms,
collision coarea and dominated convergence give the Boltzmann operator with
error \(h\varepsilon^\eta\); Theorem~\ref{thm:r10-b2-surplus} controls cyclic
terms.  Two or more unresolved interactions in one block have renewal norm
\(O(h^2)\).  The same majorant permits source differentiation.  The
Chapman--Kolmogorov identity of the exact trace semigroup and the local error
bound show that two refining partitions differ by a summable mesh error,
which proves consistency and the fixed-horizon limit.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Balance-preserving recovery and the joint LDP}

\begin{lemma}[Exact conservative regularization]
\label{lem:r10-b2-repair}
Every finite-action balanced density/contact pair can be approximated by
smooth positive balanced pairs \((f_h,\Gamma_h)\) such that
\[
 f_h\to f,
 \quad \Gamma_h\to\Gamma,
 \quad
 \int\ell(d\Gamma_h/dA_{f_h})dA_{f_h}\to
 \int\ell(d\Gamma/dA_f)dA_f.
\]
Mass, momentum and energy are preserved exactly.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
First truncate velocity with a conservative radial retraction on each
energy--momentum shell and add a vanishing Maxwellian background.  Convolve in
space and time away from the endpoints.  The resulting balance defect lies in
the finite-dimensional orthogonal complement of collision invariants on each
cell.  Solve the cell incidence equation for a signed contact correction and
transport it along the cell faces; the finite incidence matrix has a bounded
right inverse on that complement.  The positive background dominates the
signed correction.  Perspective-entropy convexity, truncation and dominated
convergence give action convergence.  The correction is constructed in the
weak balance equation, hence balance and invariants are exact at every step.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Grand-canonical and microcanonical joint LDP]
\label{thm:r10-b2-main}
The empirical particle path and actual-contact measure satisfy a full good
short-time grand-canonical LDP at speed \(\mu_\varepsilon\) with rate
\[
 I_0^{\rm gc}(f_0)+
 \int\ell\left(\frac{d\Gamma}{dA_f}\right)dA_f
\]
on the closed balanced set and \(+\infty\) otherwise.  After applying the B1
source-dependent shell theorem, the same dynamic action holds
microcanonically with the constrained initial rate \(I_{0,a}\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Corollary~\ref{cor:r10-b2-cluster} and exponential compact containment give
the upper bound by convex duality; the density/contact source is present in
the actual boundary occurrences.  For a smooth positive balanced pair, solve
the finite projected exposing equations and apply the exact tilted trace
semigroup.  The one-block theorem and strict finite projected covariance give
concentration and the entropy Radon--Nikodym cost.  Lemma~\ref{lem:r10-b2-repair}
extends the lower bound to every finite-action pair.  B1 transfers every
source tilt through the primitive shell with a subexponential coefficient,
changing only the initial rate.
\end{proof}
