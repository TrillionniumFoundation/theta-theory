## Overall assessment

The paper proposes a potentially valuable extension of the short-time deterministic hard-sphere fluctuation theory: an empirical measure of actual binary collisions, a collision-source-decorated real-trajectory cluster expansion, and a local joint LDP for density paths and collision flow.

The revision does not supply the new mathematics needed for that extension. The marked-cluster theorem is justified by attaching \(e^\psi\) to created tree edges and asserting that recollision estimates remain unchanged. The local LDP is then obtained by saying that the density-only mechanism applies. Neither step proves a theorem for the actual microscopic collision point process. There is also a basic normal-orientation inconsistency, an incorrect prepared initial cost, and dependence on B1's false ensemble-transfer theorem.

## Major objections

### 1. The empirical collision measure is not consistently oriented

The manuscript defines

\[
\omega=(x_i-x_j)/\varepsilon
\]

and calls a contact incoming when

\[
(v_i-v_j)\cdot\omega>0.
\]

With this convention, the squared interparticle distance has derivative

\[
\frac d{dt}|x_i-x_j|^2
=2\varepsilon (v_i-v_j)\cdot\omega.
\]

An incoming contact must therefore have negative dot product. One may choose the opposite normal, but then the collision rule, positive-part kernel, empirical mark, weak balance, and reference intensity must be rewritten consistently.

For unordered collisions, the quotient under particle exchange and normal reversal must also be specified. As written, the exact microscopic balance and the intensity \(A_f\) are not consistently typed.

### 2. Marking created tree edges does not prove a theorem for all actual collisions

Real hard-sphere trajectories contain ordinary collision trees, recollisions, overlap constraints, and exceptional collision graphs. The empirical measure \(\Gamma_T^\varepsilon\) counts every actual contact event. A limiting Boltzmann creation edge is not automatically in one-to-one correspondence with those events.

A rigorous marked expansion must:

- define the marked polymer or trajectory-graph class;
- prove that each microscopic collision contributes exactly once;
- track the orientation and exchange symmetry of each mark;
- rederive the cluster norm with an extensive number of collision weights;
- prove source-uniform recollision and overlap estimates; and
- identify the limiting marked cumulants.

The proof does none of this. It says that every created edge receives \(e^\psi\) and that recollision graphs stay negligible after replacing the time constant by \(T e^{C\|\psi\|}\). Positive collision sources favor trajectories with many contacts at exponential scale; source-uniform negligibility is precisely the new theorem that must be proved.

### 3. The cited density-only hard-sphere LDP does not contain the claimed actual-collision extension

The deterministic hard-sphere fluctuation/LDP literature cited by the manuscript establishes short-time density/empirical-measure results under regularity assumptions. A joint LDP for the actual collision point process, with the exact mark topology used here, is an additional result. It cannot be obtained by referring to a formal kinetic collision source appearing in another stochastic or kinetic model.

The paper must distinguish a formally natural Hamiltonian from a theorem derived from microscopic marked trajectories.

### 4. Analytic convergence of a local cumulant is not the stated joint LDP

Even assuming a source-decorated cumulant limit, a joint LDP requires:

- a precise Polish topology for the density path and collision measure;
- exponential tightness at speed \(\mu_\varepsilon\);
- a separating source class;
- closure of the weak balance and collision symmetries;
- local upper and lower bounds;
- approximation of singular collision measures; and
- control in unbounded velocity.

The proof says that the same well-posedness and exponential-tightness mechanism as the density-only theorem applies. The new collision-measure coordinate has a different dual space and compactness problem. No theorem checks these requirements.

### 5. The local lower bound is absent

The manuscript restricts the result to a “regular biased-solution class generated by bounded Lipschitz cotangents,” but this class is not defined in a way that supports a lower bound. A deterministic lower bound requires construction of a microscopic tilted law approximating a prescribed \((f,\gamma)\), computation of its Radon–Nikodym cost, and recollision control under that biased law.

Formal Hamilton–Jacobi duality and scalar entropy conjugacy do not produce this microscopic construction. The lower bound is the central difficulty, and it is omitted.

### 6. The Poisson relative entropy is only a formal local conjugate

The identity

\[
\sup_\lambda\{q\lambda-a(e^\lambda-1)\}
=a\ell(q/a)
\]

is elementary. It does not prove that deterministic collision counts have an asymptotically Poisson local cost conditional on a density path. Feasible collision flows may require additional exchange, involution, conservation, and integrability constraints beyond the weak balance displayed in the paper.

The manuscript must characterize the closure of microscopically realizable collision flows before assigning \(+\infty\) merely outside the weak balance class.

### 7. The prepared initial rate is misstated

The theorem sets the initial rate to zero at \(f_a^0\) and \(+\infty\) off the prepared initial constraint. Conditioning finitely many moments does not make every other empirical measure within the constraint set infinitely costly. The conditional empirical measure should retain a nontrivial relative-entropy-type rate on the constraint set, with zero at the information projection.

More importantly, the preparation transfer imported from B1 is false for arbitrary extensive sources. Thus the prepared version of the marked theorem cannot be deduced even if a grand-canonical collision LDP were available.

### 8. The closure theorem simply concatenates unproved assertions

The final theorem says that B1 supplies preparation, the marked cluster theorem controls connected molecules, and the local LDP follows from the Laplace principle. Since B1 is false and the two B2 steps are not proved, the closure theorem adds no mathematical content.

## Status of prior objections

The manuscript now emphasizes a local short-time regular class and admits dependence on a separate preparation theorem. This is an appropriate narrowing. It does not address the collision orientation, the microscopic marked combinatorics, the lower bound, or the false B1 input.

## Required reconstruction

A viable paper should focus on one genuinely new result: a source-uniform marked real-trajectory cluster expansion for the actual collision point process, with an exact orientation convention and a proved correspondence between microscopic contacts and marks. A local LDP should be stated only after topology, tightness, and a biased-law lower bound are supplied. The microcanonical extension must use a corrected constrained preparation pressure.

## Editorial recommendation

**Reject.** The manuscript presents a plausible kinetic formula and a research plan, not a proved deterministic collision-marked LDP. Its principal upstream preparation theorem is also false.
