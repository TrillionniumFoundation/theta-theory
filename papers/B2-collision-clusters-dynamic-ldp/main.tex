\begin{abstract}
Actual hard-sphere contacts are represented by a compatible Green trace
and genuine open pre-contact evaluation maps.  A surplus collision is imposed
as a zero-set constraint; reflection and the later hard-sphere future are
introduced only on that zero set.  Genealogywise coarea bounds are summed by a
fixed-horizon factorial majorant.  The lower bound uses conservative smoothing
and positive Maxwellian mixing to approximate every finite-action balanced
pair inside the positive collision cone.  This gives the grand-canonical and,
after exact-number conditioning, microcanonical joint density--contact LDPs.
\end{abstract}
