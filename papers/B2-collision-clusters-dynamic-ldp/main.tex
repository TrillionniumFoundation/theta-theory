\begin{abstract}
We retain a factorial hierarchy of complete, time-ordered collision histories
as the microscopic interface state.  A spanning-tree reduction turns every
surplus contact into a loop-closure tube.  Quantitative coarea on regular
charts and a determinant-ideal Łojasiewicz estimate on their complement give
an additional positive power of the sphere diameter beyond the standard
Boltzmann--Grad cross-section.  The exact history composition and a cutting
argument propagate the connected expansion to every time below the regular
Boltzmann lifespan.  Exact deterministic tilts, concentration, and positive
control recovery then yield grand-canonical and microcanonical joint
density--contact LDPs.
\end{abstract}
