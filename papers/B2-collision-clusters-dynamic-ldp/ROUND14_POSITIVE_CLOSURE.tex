
\section{Round-fourteen positive closure: compatible traces, true Jacobi tubes, and a fixed-horizon joint LDP}
\label{sec:r14-b2}

The grand-canonical construction in this section is logically prior to B1.
The whole interval \([0,T]\) is treated in one time-ordered trajectory
expansion; no per-block error is sewn.  A cyclic genealogy is removed only
after its actual linearized collision map has been analyzed.  Constants and
sublevel exponents may depend on the fixed genealogy, and the absolutely
summable tree majorant permits dominated convergence over all genealogies.

\subsection{The compatible kinetic trace graph}

For \(k\ge1\), let
\[
 \Omega_k^\varepsilon
 =\{Z_k:|x_i-x_j|>\varepsilon,\ i\ne j\}
\]
and let \(\Sigma_{ij}^-\) be the incoming part of the contact face
\(|x_i-x_j|=\varepsilon\).  A normal-flux trace is already the measure
\[
 d\gamma_{ij}^-
 =((v_i-v_j)\cdot\omega)_+\,f_k^-\,
   dt\,d\sigma_{ij}\,dV_k .
\]
Its norm is
\[
 \|\gamma_k^-\|_{\rm tr}
 =\sum_{i<j}\int w_k\,d|\gamma_{ij}^-|,
\]
with no second multiplication by the normal velocity.

\begin{theorem}[Closed Green trace graph]
\label{thm:r14-b2-trace}
Let \(\mathfrak X_{\alpha,\beta}\) be the factorially weighted hierarchy
space and let \(\mathfrak D_{\alpha,\beta}\) consist of hierarchy densities
whose transport divergence and incoming normal-flux traces have the same
weighted finite mass.  Then \(\mathfrak D_{\alpha,\beta}\) is closed for the
graph norm, and
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 &\int_{\Omega_k^\varepsilon}
  (\partial_t+V_k\cdot\nabla_{X_k})\phi\,df_k\\
 &\quad=
 \langle f_k(T),\phi_T\rangle-\langle f_k(0),\phi_0\rangle
 -\sum_{i<j}\int_{\Sigma_{ij}^-}
  \bigl(\phi(Z_k')-\phi(Z_k)\bigr)\,d\gamma_{ij}^- .
 \end{aligned}
\]
Weak graph convergence preserves all simple-contact traces.  Multiple-contact
corners are retained as lower-dimensional graph strata and have zero
simple-flux mass.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Apply the Gauss--Green theorem to smooth truncations of
\(\Omega_k^\varepsilon\) avoiding pairwise intersections of contact faces.
The outward transport flux is
\((V_k\cdot n)f_k\,d\sigma\,dV\); pairing incoming and outgoing faces by the
elastic reflection gives the displayed jump term with the single flux
measure \(\gamma^-\).  Uniform graph-norm bounds give weak compactness of the
interior currents and their normal traces in the divergence-measure topology.
Passing through the truncations proves closedness.  The intersection of two
distinct contact faces has codimension two and carries no mass for a finite
normal trace; it remains recorded for iterated traces but contributes no
extra factor to the simple Green formula.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Real trajectory genealogies}

A connected trajectory genealogy \(\mathcal G\) records labels, ordered
creation contacts, and surplus contacts.  On a regular compact piece
\(\mathcal K_{\mathcal G}\), collision times are strictly ordered, all
incidence cosines exceed a positive genealogy-dependent number, and velocities
are bounded.  The coarea variables are initial roots, creation times, incoming
relative velocities, and collision normals.

Let \(t_*\) be the first surplus contact.  Remove only its equation, not its
reflection or future trajectory.  The resulting pseudo-orbit agrees with the
true orbit up to \(t_*\).  Let
\[
 \Psi_{\mathcal G}(\xi)
 =
 \Pi_{\omega_*^\perp}
 \bigl(x_a(t_*^-;\xi)-x_b(t_*^-;\xi)\bigr)
\]
be its two transverse contact coordinates.

\begin{lemma}[Fixed-genealogy physical Jacobi transversality]
\label{lem:r14-b2-jacobi}
For every fixed cyclic genealogy \(\mathcal G\), the regular parameter domain
admits a finite cover by analytic charts and a finite exceptional analytic
set such that on each nonexceptional chart one \(2\times2\) minor of
\(D\Psi_{\mathcal G}\) is not identically zero.  Consequently there are
\(C_{\mathcal G}<\infty\) and \(\beta_{\mathcal G}>0\) with
\[
 \mathfrak m_{\mathcal G}
 \{s_{\min}(D\Psi_{\mathcal G})<\rho\}
 \le C_{\mathcal G}\rho^{\beta_{\mathcal G}} .
\]
No exponent uniform in genealogy depth is asserted.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Differentiate the actual collision law.  At a collision with incoming gap
\(g=(v-v_*)\cdot\omega>0\),
\[
 Dv^+
 =Dv^--\bigl((Dv^--Dv_*^-)\cdot\omega
 +(v-v_*)\cdot D\omega\bigr)\omega
 -g\,D\omega ,
\]
and analogously for \(v_*^+\).  Thus the two tangential components of
\(D\omega\) enter the outgoing relative velocity with leading map
\(-2gI_{\omega^\perp}\).

Choose the youngest creation collision whose two descendant branches contain
the labels meeting at \(t_*\).  The collision normal there is one of the
coarea variables.  To show that a transverse minor is not identically zero,
construct one witness configuration for the fixed ordered graph: put all
subsequent creation contacts in a short interval of length \(\delta\), choose
their normals and velocities generically, and leave a final free-flight
interval of length \(L>0\) before \(t_*\).  The derivative of the surplus
transverse position with respect to the two tangential components of the
chosen normal is
\[
 -2gL\,I_{\omega^\perp}+O(\delta).
\]
For \(\delta\) small this minor is nonzero.  Earlier and later collision
derivatives are the full physical Jacobi matrices displayed above; they have
not been replaced by fixed-normal reflections or by symplectic rank alone.

The entries of the minor are real analytic on every regular chart.
Since the determinant is not identically zero, the quantitative analytic
sublevel theorem on the compact chart gives an exponent
\(\beta_{\mathcal G}>0\).  A finite chart cover proves the estimate.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Surplus contact tube for a fixed genealogy]
\label{lem:r14-b2-tube}
The absolute activity of a fixed cyclic genealogy satisfies
\[
 |\zeta_\varepsilon(\mathcal G)|
 \le M_{\mathcal G}\,
 \varepsilon^{\alpha_{\mathcal G}},
 \qquad \alpha_{\mathcal G}>0,
\]
after velocity and grazing truncation.  The estimate includes every bounded
particle and actual-contact source derivative.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
On the set where
\(s_{\min}(D\Psi_{\mathcal G})\ge\rho\), coarea bounds the two-dimensional
contact tube by \(C\varepsilon^2\rho^{-2}\).  On its complement use
Lemma~\ref{lem:r14-b2-jacobi}.  Optimizing
\(\rho^{\beta_{\mathcal G}}+\varepsilon^2\rho^{-2}\) gives
\(\alpha_{\mathcal G}=2\beta_{\mathcal G}/(\beta_{\mathcal G}+2)>0\).
Incoming flux controls local grazing denominators, and the regular
truncations are removed by monotone convergence.  Bounded source insertions
multiply the fixed-genealogy majorant by a finite factor.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The global fixed-horizon sum}

Let \(\mathfrak T\) be the set of creation trees and
\(\mathfrak C\) the cyclic genealogies.  Collision coarea and the ordered
time simplex give
\[
 M_{\mathcal G}
 \le {k!\,C^kT^{k-1+c(\mathcal G)}
        \over (k-1+c(\mathcal G))!}
 e^{C\sum_i|v_i|^2},
\]
and the sum of these majorants over all labelled connected genealogies is
finite for \(T\le T_0\).

\begin{theorem}[Fixed-horizon all-contact cluster theorem]
\label{thm:r14-b2-cluster}
For \(T\le T_0\), the grand-canonical log-Laplace functional with bounded
particle-path and actual-contact sources has an absolutely normally
convergent connected trajectory expansion on a common complex source ball.
Creation trees converge to the Boltzmann collision-tree series, and
\[
 \sum_{\mathcal G\in\mathfrak C}
 |\zeta_\varepsilon(\mathcal G)|\longrightarrow0 .
\]
The convergence is locally uniform with every fixed source derivative.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The creation-tree bound follows by iterating collision coarea and integrating
the ordered time simplex.  It is a summable majorant independent of
\(\varepsilon\).  For every fixed cyclic genealogy,
Lemma~\ref{lem:r14-b2-tube} gives convergence to zero.  Dominated convergence
over the countable genealogy set therefore removes the entire cyclic sector,
without a genealogy-uniform Lojasiewicz exponent and without a mesh factor.
Tree terms converge by dominated collision coarea.  Normal convergence
permits all fixed source derivatives.
\end{proof}

In particular the limiting cumulant is constructed directly by the global
tree series.  It is not defined through the B4 semigroup.

\begin{theorem}[Constructed marked Hamiltonian]
\label{thm:r14-b2-hamiltonian}
The limiting pressure is the unique analytic mild solution on the short-time
chart with Hamiltonian
\[
 \mathbb H(f,p,\psi)
 =
 \langle f,v\cdot\nabla_xp\rangle
 +{1\over2}\int ff_*((v-v_*)\cdot\omega)_+
 \left(e^{\Delta_\omega p+\psi}-1\right).
\]
Every \(\psi\)-derivative is a factorial moment of the actual incoming contact
measure.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
In a limiting tree single out its last creation contact.  Deleting it leaves
two independent rooted subtrees; collision coarea gives the incoming flux
and source multiplication gives
\(e^{\Delta p+\psi}-1\).  This derives the mild equation term by term.
The cluster norm makes Picard iteration contractive for \(T\le T_0\).
Because \(\psi\) marked the microscopic normal-flux trace, its derivative
selects actual contacts.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Exposure and lower recovery}

For a smooth positive balanced pair put
\[
 dA_f={1\over2}ff_*((v-v_*)\cdot\omega)_+
 dt\,dx\,dv\,dv_*\,d\omega,\qquad
 q={d\Gamma\over dA_f}.
\]

\begin{lemma}[Regular exposed pair]
\label{lem:r14-b2-expose}
If \(q\) and \(q^{-1}\) are bounded with Gaussian velocity weights and
\((f,\Gamma)\) satisfies the exact weak balance, there are bounded smooth
sources \(p,\psi\) with
\[
 \psi=\log q-\Delta p
\]
such that the first derivative of the pressure in
Theorem~\ref{thm:r14-b2-hamiltonian} is exactly \((f,\Gamma)\).  Under the
corresponding microscopic tilt, the empirical density/contact pair
concentrates exponentially at \((f,\Gamma)\).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Given \(q\), solve the biased Boltzmann equation forward from the chosen
positive initial datum.  On the short-time Maxwellian chart its collision
operator is locally Lipschitz in the Gaussian weighted norm.  Solve the
backward adjoint equation for \(p\) with the desired terminal density source,
and define \(\psi\) by the displayed identity.  Differentiating the tree mild
equation gives the forward biased equation and hence the prescribed pair.
Strict convexity of \(e^z-1\), together with the positive initial covariance,
makes the Hessian positive on the balanced quotient.  The normal complex
cluster expansion and Chernoff bounds then give exponential concentration.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Continuous kinetic Hodge repair]
\label{lem:r14-b2-repair}
Every finite-action balanced pair is approximated, with convergence of
entropy action and endpoints, by smooth positive exposed pairs.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
First truncate velocities and convolve in time and space away from endpoints.
Let \(b\) be the resulting small balance defect.  On each local
energy--momentum fiber use the collision Dirichlet form
\[
 \mathcal E_f(r,\phi)=\int\Delta r\,\Delta\phi\,dA_f .
\]
On the orthogonal complement of the five collision invariants, the
Maxwellian lower bound gives coercivity.  The compatibility of \(b\) with
mass, momentum, and energy follows from the original balance.  Lax--Milgram
therefore gives \(r\) with
\(\mathcal E_f(r,\phi)=b(\phi)\).  Set
\(\delta\Gamma=(\Delta r)A_f\).  It has the exchange/pre-post symmetry and
repairs the balance exactly.  Add a Maxwellian background before solving and
let its size decrease after the smoothing scale; then
\(q+\Delta r>0\), and the entropy change tends to zero by Orlicz
equi-integrability.  Endpoint correctors are transported in short boundary
layers.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The full grand-canonical and microcanonical LDPs}

Define
\[
 \ell(q)=q\log q-q+1.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Grand-canonical joint LDP]
\label{thm:r14-b2-gcldp}
The density and actual-contact pair satisfies a good LDP at speed
\(\mu_\varepsilon\) with rate
\[
 I_T^{\rm gc}(f,\Gamma)
 =
 I_0^{\rm gc}(f_0)
 +\begin{cases}
 \displaystyle\int\ell(d\Gamma/dA_f)\,dA_f,
 &(f,\Gamma)\text{ balanced and }\Gamma\ll A_f,\\
 +\infty,&\text{otherwise}.
 \end{cases}
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Bounded-source upper bounds follow from
Theorem~\ref{thm:r14-b2-cluster}.  Increasing positive particle and contact
tests give exponential compact containment.  Scaling the balance test makes
the dual infinite off the closed trace graph.  Monotone truncation of
\(\log q\) exhausts the full entropy conjugate.

For the lower bound, use Lemma~\ref{lem:r14-b2-expose} on smooth positive
pairs and compute the exact Radon--Nikodym cost, which is the displayed
entropy.  Lemma~\ref{lem:r14-b2-repair} supplies rate-dense exposed recovery.
The standard compact-cover argument completes open-set lower bounds and
goodness.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Microcanonical transfer and Round-fourteen B2 closure]
\label{thm:r14-b2-main}
After the source-dependent exact-number and continuous-shell conditioning of
B1, the same pair satisfies a good LDP with
\[
 I_T^{\rm mc,a}(f,\Gamma)
 =
 I_{0,a}(f_0)+\int\ell(d\Gamma/dA_f)\,dA_f
\]
on the balanced domain.  The dependency order is
\[
 \text{B2--GC}\longrightarrow\text{B1}
 \longrightarrow\text{B2--MC}.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
B1 applies the exact shell coefficient to the full joint source pressure
proved in Theorem~\ref{thm:r14-b2-cluster}.  Its source-dependent change of
measure transfers both the upper bound and every exposed lower recovery.
Only the initial rate is replaced by its constrained information projection;
the dynamic entropy is unchanged.
\end{proof}
