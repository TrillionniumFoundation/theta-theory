\section{Round-four referee closure: all-contact marked clusters}
\label{sec:r3-b2}

The grand-canonical part of this section is logically prior to Paper B1.  It
uses the hard-core initial law and the real hard-sphere flow, but no
microcanonical transfer.  Paper B1 then conditions this source-dependent
grand-canonical pressure, after which the final subsection returns to the
microcanonical law.  This order removes the previous circular dependency.

\subsection{Orientation, quotient, and exact balance}

For an unordered pair \(i<j\) at contact, put
\[
 \omega=\frac{x_j-x_i}{\varepsilon}.
\]
Incoming contact means
\((v_i-v_j)\cdot\omega>0\), and
\[
 v_i'=v_i-((v_i-v_j)\cdot\omega)\omega,
 \qquad
 v_j'=v_j+((v_i-v_j)\cdot\omega)\omega.
\]
Every contact is counted once in
\[
 \Gamma_T^\varepsilon
 =\frac1{\mu_\varepsilon}
 \sum_{i<j}\sum_{t\in\mathcal C_{ij}\cap[0,T]}
 \delta_{(t,x_i(t),v_i(t-),v_j(t-),\omega(t))}.
\]
The state space of \(\Gamma\) is quotiented by
\((v,v_*,\omega)\sim(v_*,v,-\omega)\).

\begin{proposition}[Microscopic balance with actual contacts]
\label{prop:r3-b2-balance}
For every compactly supported smooth \(\phi(t,x,v)\),
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 &\langle\pi_T^\varepsilon,\phi_T\rangle
 -\langle\pi_0^\varepsilon,\phi_0\rangle
 -\int_0^T\langle\pi_t^\varepsilon,
 \partial_t\phi+v\cdot\nabla_x\phi\rangle dt\\
 &\qquad=\int\Delta_\omega\phi\,d\Gamma_T^\varepsilon,
 \end{aligned}
\]
where
\(\Delta_\omega\phi=\phi(v')+\phi(v_*')-\phi(v)-\phi(v_*)\).
The identity is invariant under the exchange quotient.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Integrate \(\phi(t,x_i(t),v_i(t))\) along each free-flight interval.  The
interior endpoints telescope.  At a contact of \(i<j\), the jump of the sum of
the two particle tests is exactly \(\Delta_\omega\phi\).  Triple contacts and
grazing contacts form a Liouville-null set.  The simultaneous exchange sends
the same unordered contact to the same post-collisional pair, proving quotient
invariance.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Ancestral transversality and all-contact graph surgery}

A rooted creation forest \(\mathcal F\) is parametrized by the root states and,
for every non-root label, by its first collision time, incoming velocity, and
impact normal.  On each chronological collision cell these variables determine
all pseudo-trajectories by affine free flight and orthogonal elastic
reflection.  A contact between two labels already connected in \(\mathcal F\)
is a cycle constraint; no new particle variable is assigned to it.

For a connected pair \((p,q)\), let \(v_*(p,q)\) be the last common vertex of
their ancestral paths.  Immediately after that vertex the paths split into
two exclusive ancestral chains.  Order the variables on those chains from the
split toward the proposed contact and choose the last variable whose variation
changes the relative position transversally to the relative velocity.  This is
the \emph{ancestral witness}.  If no such variable has a quantitative
transversality bound, the configuration belongs to a degenerate stratum.

\begin{lemma}[First-cycle witness atlas]
\label{lem:r3-b2-first-cycle}
There are finitely many witness types and constants \(\alpha>0\), \(C<\infty\)
such that every first cycle contact belongs to one of the following disjoint
classes:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item a regular witness chart on which a scalar or angular witness
      \(\zeta\) satisfies
\[
 \left|\partial_\zeta
 \Pi_{(v_p-v_q)^\perp}(x_p-x_q)\right|
 \ge\varepsilon^{\alpha/4};
\]
\item a small-relative-velocity, grazing, simultaneous-contact, or vanishing-
      Jacobian stratum whose Gaussian-weighted forest activity is
      \(O(\varepsilon^\alpha)\).
\end{enumerate}
On a regular chart, imposing the first cycle contact and integrating the
witness gives an \(O(\varepsilon^{3\alpha/4})\) factor relative to the forest
activity.  The bounds are uniform in the number of labels after multiplication
by the standard tree weight \(C^k k!\).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Fix the combinatorial creation forest, the chronological cell, and the pair
forming the first cycle.  The two exclusive ancestral chains contain only
finitely many local variable types: a root position, a creation time, an
incoming velocity, or an impact normal.  Differentiating the hard-sphere
reflection formula along the chain expresses the transverse relative-position
Jacobian as a product of free-flight matrices and orthogonal reflection
matrices.  The latter have norm one away from grazing; the former have a
triangular block with the exclusive flight length on the diagonal.  Therefore
one of the variables after the last common ancestor is transverse unless a
relative velocity is small, a collision is grazing, two event times coincide,
or a nonzero analytic minor of this product vanishes.

Choose the first nonzero minor in a fixed lexicographic list.  This gives a
measurable finite atlas of witness types.  On the set where its absolute value
is at least \(\varepsilon^{\alpha/4}\), collision means that a two-dimensional
transverse coordinate lies in a disk of radius \(C\varepsilon\).  Coarea in
the scalar/angular witness and the remaining transverse root coordinate gives
\(C\varepsilon^{1-\alpha/4}\), hence the stated weaker uniform power.

For the complement, split relative speed at \(\varepsilon^{\alpha/8}\) and
grazing cosine at the same scale.  Gaussian velocity integration and the
collision flux give positive powers of \(\varepsilon\).  Each analytic
Jacobian minor is not identically zero on its chronological cell: moving the
chosen exclusive root or normal in a free-flight example changes the relative
position.  The one-dimensional sublevel estimate for a nonzero analytic
function, followed by Fubini over the finite atlas, gives another positive
power.  Simultaneous contacts are treated by two independent contact equations
and have higher codimension.  Taking the minimum exponent proves the lemma.
The standard labelled-tree enumeration absorbs the finite atlas and ancestry
choices.
\end{proof}

The remaining cycle contacts are controlled collectively, not assigned
fictitious independent time variables.  For a forest parameter point, let
\(N_{\rm cyc}\) be the number of later contacts between already connected
pseudo-trajectories before time \(T\).

\begin{lemma}[Ancestral contact ledger]
\label{lem:r4-b2-contact-ledger}
On every regular first-cycle witness chart and for \(|u|\le u_0\),
\[
 \int e^{uN_{\rm cyc}}
 \,d\mathfrak m_{\mathcal F}^{\rm residual}
 \le \exp\{C T e^{u}\}
\]
after the witness variable has been integrated.  The same estimate holds on
the degenerate strata after their \(O(\varepsilon^\alpha)\) activity factor is
removed.  The constant is independent of the label number and of the precise
ancestral graph.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Cut the forest-parameter space into chronological cells on which all collision
and near-collision times are ordered and every velocity is affine in the
incoming variables.  Between consecutive cell boundaries the relative
position of a fixed pair is affine in time, hence that pair has at most one
incoming contact.  Explore cells by the dependency DAG rather than by adding
formal collision variables.  When a cell boundary is crossed, either a new
label is created, which is handled by the ordinary collision-coarea operator,
or an already connected pair meets.  In the latter case the contact is a zero
of one of the transverse functions in the witness atlas.

The first such zero has already paid the power of \(\varepsilon\).  Forget it
and identify the two adjacent chronological cells.  The reflection map has
unit Jacobian in velocity and the forgetful map has uniformly bounded
multiplicity after the ancestral pair and witness type are recorded.  Repeating
this operation removes all later cycle faces without introducing a new
integration variable.  At the level of absolute activities the recursion is
\[
 Z_{m+1}(T)
 \le C\int_0^T Z_m(s)Z_0(T-s)ds,
 \qquad Z_0(T)\le e^{CT},
\]
where \(m\) counts recorded later contacts and the integral is the location of
the chronological cell boundary, not an assumed independent collision time.
The bound follows from disintegration of the already existing creation-time
coordinates.  Exponential generating functions give
\[
 \sum_{m\ge0}\frac{e^{um}}{m!}Z_m(T)
 \le\exp\{CTe^u\}.
\]
The factorial here is the combinatorial exponential-generating normalization
of the ledger recursion; it is not the volume of a nonexistent free simplex.
The same forgetful maps apply on the degenerate strata after their initial
sublevel estimate.
\end{proof}

Let a real trajectory cluster \(\lambda\) contain all labelled trajectories
and every actual contact.  For sources satisfying
\[
 |h(z([0,T]))|
 \le r(1+\sup_{t\le T}|v(t)|^2),
 \qquad
 \|\psi\|_\infty+\|\psi\|_{\rm Lip,w}\le r,
\]
put
\[
 \mathcal W_{h,\psi}(\lambda)
 =\exp\left\{
 \sum_{i\in\lambda}h(z_i([0,T]))+
 \sum_{c\in\mathcal C(\lambda)}\psi(c)
 \right\}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[All-contact forest domination]
\label{lem:r3-b2-generating}
There are \(T_0,r_0,C,\alpha>0\) such that, for \(T\le T_0\),
\(r\le r_0\), and every \(k\ge1\),
\[
 \int|d\mathfrak m_k^\varepsilon(\lambda)|
 |\mathcal W_{h,\psi}(\lambda)-1|_{\rm conn}
 \le k!C^k(T+\varepsilon)^{k-1}.
\]
For collision-source derivatives,
\[
 \sum_{j\ge0}\frac{u^j}{j!}
 |D_\psi^j\mathfrak C_k^\varepsilon(h,\psi)|
 \le k!C^k(T+\varepsilon)^{k-1}
 \exp\{CTe^{r+u}\}.
\]
The entire cyclic sector, with all its later recollisions and source marks, is
bounded by the right-hand side multiplied by
\(C\varepsilon^\alpha\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Creation forests are treated by collision coarea: each non-root label supplies
one genuine position variable, and changing it to creation time, incoming
velocity, and normal has Jacobian
\(((v-v_*)\cdot\omega)_+\).  Gaussian energy bounds and chronological tree
summation give the forest majorant
\(k!C^k(T+\varepsilon)^{k-1}\).

For a cyclic cluster select its first cycle in chronological order and apply
Lemma~\ref{lem:r3-b2-first-cycle}.  Conditional on the resulting witness
chart, Lemma~\ref{lem:r4-b2-contact-ledger} sums every later actual contact,
including all possible collision-source selections.  The forgetful surgery
maps the residual configuration to a creation forest with uniformly bounded
multiplicity and preserves the Gaussian activity because elastic reflection
has unit velocity Jacobian and conserves energy.  Multiplication of the
first-cycle power, the residual exponential ledger, and the forest majorant
proves the cyclic estimate.  Summing the acyclic and cyclic sectors proves the
first assertion; differentiating the exact microscopic contact weight selects
contacts already counted by the ledger and proves the derivative bound.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Source-uniform all-contact cluster theorem]
\label{thm:r3-b2-gc-cluster}
The connected grand-canonical real-trajectory expansion is absolutely and
normally convergent on the declared complex source ball, with all fixed source
derivatives.  Every collision derivative is the factorial moment of the
actual incoming contact measure, and recollision-containing terms vanish
uniformly at rate \(O(\varepsilon^\alpha)\).
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The logarithm of the partition function is the sum of connected labelled
clusters.  Theorem~\ref{lem:r3-b2-generating} is a geometric majorant in the
label number for sufficiently small \(T\), and Cauchy's formula justifies all
source derivatives.  On creation forests, collision coarea converges to the
Boltzmann tree integral.  The cyclic estimate removes every non-tree contact
sector uniformly.  Since the source was placed on the microscopic actual
contact list before expansion, its derivatives retain exactly those contacts.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The marked Hamilton--Jacobi equation}

Let \(g(t,x,v)\) be a smooth density test and \(\psi\) a collision test.
Writing \(D_tg=\partial_tg+v\cdot\nabla_xg\), the limiting cumulant solves
\[
 \partial_t\mathcal Q_t(g,\psi)
 =\left\langle
 \frac{\delta\mathcal Q_t}{\delta g},D_tg
 \right\rangle
 +\frac12\int f_t f_{*,t}
 ((v-v_*)\cdot\omega)_+
 \left(e^{\Delta_\omega g+\psi}-1\right),
\]
where \(f_t=\delta\mathcal Q_t/\delta g(t)\).

\begin{theorem}[Marked limiting Hamiltonian]
\label{thm:r3-b2-hj}
On the source ball, the limiting cumulant is the unique analytic mild solution
of the Hamilton--Jacobi equation with Hamiltonian
\[
 \mathbb H(f,p,\psi)
 =\langle f,v\cdot\nabla_xp\rangle
 +\frac12\int ff_*((v-v_*)\cdot\omega)_+
 \left(e^{\Delta_\omega p+\psi}-1\right).
\]
The first derivative solves the biased Boltzmann equation and the second
derivatives give the joint density--collision covariance system.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
In every limiting connected tree, single out its last collision.  Removing
that collision leaves two independent rooted subtrees; their weights multiply
to \(f f_*\), collision coarea gives the incoming flux, and the source weight
gives \(e^{\Delta p+\psi}-1\).  This proves the mild equation term by term.
The cluster norm is a scale of analytic Banach spaces with one unit of
velocity weight lost by the collision operator.  Picard iteration on that
scale is a contraction for \(T\le T_0\), proving uniqueness.  Normal
convergence permits differentiation and yields the biased first-derivative and
joint covariance equations.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Continuation from the local complex chart to arbitrary bounded real sources}

The connected expansion near the zero source is the local chart needed for
complex differentiation.  A full entropy action also requires real
log-partition limits at every bounded collision source.  These are obtained by
exact conditional composition on the correlation hierarchy, not by enlarging
one polymer disk beyond its convergence radius.

Let \(R<\infty\) and let \(\mathfrak B_R\) be the set of real particle-path
and collision sources of weighted norm at most \(R\).  Divide \([0,T]\) into
blocks \([t_j,t_{j+1}]\).  Conditional on the complete correlation state at
\(t_j\), normalize the source on the next block by its exact finite-volume
conditional logarithmic partition function.

\begin{theorem}[Bounded real-source continuation]
\label{thm:r3-b2-source-continuation}
For every \(R<\infty\) there is a short time
\(T_R>0\), bounded below on compact \(R\)-intervals, such that for
\(T\le T_R\) and every real \((h,\psi)\in\mathfrak B_R\):
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item the finite hard-sphere log-partition functions converge locally
      uniformly on a complex neighborhood of that real source;
\item the limiting pressure is obtained by composing the local hierarchy
      charts over finitely many time blocks and is independent of the
      subdivision;
\item its first derivative is the unique regular biased density--collision
      pair and its Hessian is the corresponding joint covariance;
\item on a nested family of regular density/moment balls, the continuation
      times cover every fixed \(T<T_{\rm reg}\), where \(T_{\rm reg}\) is the
      existence time of the bounded-rate biased Boltzmann hierarchy.
\end{enumerate}
Thus every bounded positive collision density
\(q\in[M^{-1},M]\) is exposed by the real source \(\psi=\log q\) on its
regular existence interval.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
On one block, translate the source center in the connected cluster weights.
The absolute tree bound becomes
\[
 k!C_A^k\Delta^{k-1}
 \exp\{C_A\Delta e^{CR}\},
\]
where \(A\) is the incoming weighted correlation norm.  Choose
\(\Delta\) so that \(C_A\Delta e^{CR}<1/4\).  The local cluster theorem then
holds on a complex ball of radius independent of the block index.

The exact Liouville tower says that the normalized terminal correlation state
of one block is the initial state of the next.  Positivity, conservation of
mass and energy, and the Gaussian velocity weight give the recursive estimate
\[
 A_{j+1}\le A_j\exp\{C\Delta e^{CR}(1+A_j)\}.
\]
Comparison with the scalar differential equation
\(A'=Ce^{CR}A(1+A)\) yields a finite bound on every compact subinterval of its
existence interval.  Hence finitely many local charts cover any
\(T<T_{\rm reg}\).  Conditional logarithmic normalizations telescope, so two
subdivisions have a common refinement and give the same pressure.

Normal convergence on each translated complex chart and the chain rule give
local uniform convergence of derivatives.  Differentiating the block tower
propagates the biased Boltzmann hierarchy and its covariance hierarchy.
Uniqueness on the regular moment ball makes the continuation and its
derivatives independent of the chart chain.  Taking
\(\psi=\log q\) gives the final assertion.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Balance-preserving regularization of finite-action pairs}

Let \(\mathscr B_f\Gamma\) denote the collision divergence in the weak
balance, defined by
\[
 \langle p,\mathscr B_f\Gamma\rangle
 =\int\Delta_\omega p\,d\Gamma.
\]
The range is orthogonal to \(1,v,|v|^2\).  On a compact velocity ball, choose
a finite family of non-grazing collision boxes whose pre/post velocity
increments span that orthogonal finite-dimensional test space.

\begin{lemma}[Positive collision right inverse]
\label{lem:r3-b2-right-inverse}
Let \(R\) be a smooth compactly supported distribution in
\((t,x,v)\), orthogonal at every \((t,x)\) to mass, momentum, and energy.  For
any smooth strictly positive reference density \(m\), there is an
exchange-symmetric signed collision current \(\mathscr K_mR\) such that
\[
 \mathscr B_m(\mathscr K_mR)=R,
 \qquad
 \left\|\frac{d|\mathscr K_mR|}{dA_m}\right\|_\infty
 \le C_m\|R\|_{W^{-1,1}_w}.
\]
The map \(R\mapsto\mathscr K_mR\) is linear and continuous.  If a positive
current \(\Gamma^0\) dominates
\(2|\mathscr K_mR|\), then
\(\Gamma^0+\mathscr K_mR\) remains positive.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Partition the compact velocity ball into small cells and connect cells by
non-grazing elastic collisions.  On the quotient by the five collision
invariants, the finite incidence matrix of pre/post increments has full row
rank: if a cell function annihilates every edge increment, propagation along
the connected collision graph makes it the restriction of
\(a+b\cdot v+c|v|^2\).  Choose a bounded right inverse of this matrix.
Multiply its edge coefficients by smooth collision-box densities and localize
in \((t,x)\).  Refining the velocity partition and using a bounded partition
of unity gives the stated operator.  The coarea Jacobians are bounded above
and below on the selected non-grazing boxes, which yields the density estimate
with respect to \(A_m\).  Symmetrizing under particle exchange and the
pre/post involution preserves the divergence and proves the claim.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Action-dense regular feasible pairs]
\label{lem:r3-b2-regularization}
Let \((f,\Gamma)\) be feasible and
\[
 I_0^{\rm gc}(f_0)+
 \int\ell(d\Gamma/dA_f)dA_f<\infty.
\]
There are smooth feasible pairs \((f_n,\Gamma_n)\) such that
\(f_n\) is strictly positive with Gaussian velocity tails,
\[
 M_n^{-1}\le\frac{d\Gamma_n}{dA_{f_n}}\le M_n,
\]
\((f_n,\Gamma_n)\to(f,\Gamma)\) in the weighted weak topology, and their
actions converge to the action of \((f,\Gamma)\).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
First truncate velocities and the entropy density
\(q=d\Gamma/dA_f\), then symmetrize under exchange and the collision
involution.  Convolve in \((t,x,v,v_*)\) away from the endpoints, and add a
small Maxwellian reference density \(\delta_nm\).  Correct the five conserved
moments by five compactly supported smooth modes; this changes the weighted
weak distance and entropy by \(o(1)\).

Let \(\Gamma_n^0=q_nA_{f_n}+\delta_nA_m\).  Its weak-balance defect
\[
 R_n=\partial_tf_n+v\cdot\nabla_xf_n-\mathscr B_{f_n}\Gamma_n^0
\]
tends to zero in \(W^{-1,1}_w\) and is orthogonal to the collision invariants.
Choose \(\delta_n\downarrow0\) slowly enough that
\(C_m\|R_n\|_{W^{-1,1}_w}=o(\delta_n)\), and put
\[
 \Gamma_n=\Gamma_n^0+\mathscr K_mR_n.
\]
Lemma~\ref{lem:r3-b2-right-inverse} gives exact balance and positivity.
Convexity, uniform integrability of \(q\log q\), and the fact that the
correction has relative density \(o(1)\) on the reference component give the
upper action limit.  Lower semicontinuity gives the reverse inequality.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Grand-canonical joint LDP}

Let
\[
 dA_f=\frac12ff_*((v-v_*)\cdot\omega)_+
 \,dt\,dx\,dv\,dv_*\,d\omega.
\]
A pair \((f,\Gamma)\) is feasible when it has finite weighted moments,
exchange symmetry, pre/post involution compatibility, and satisfies the weak
balance of Proposition~\ref{prop:r3-b2-balance}.

\begin{lemma}[Joint exponential compact containment]
\label{lem:r3-b2-tight}
For every \(M\) there is a compact set in the weighted weak path--measure
space such that
\[
 \limsup_{\varepsilon\to0}\frac1{\mu_\varepsilon}
 \log\mathbb P_\varepsilon^{\rm gc}
 \big((\pi^\varepsilon,\Gamma^\varepsilon)\notin K_M\big)
 \le-M.
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
A positive velocity source in
Theorem~\ref{thm:r3-b2-gc-cluster} gives exponential moment bounds for the
weighted particle mass.  A positive constant collision source gives an
exponential moment for \(\Gamma^\varepsilon(1)\), including recollisions.
The exact balance bounds time increments of \(\pi^\varepsilon\) on a countable
dense family of weighted smooth tests by free transport plus the collision
mass.  Weighted Prokhorov compactness and the Skorokhod modulus criterion give
\(K_M\).
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Grand-canonical density--actual-collision LDP]
\label{thm:r3-b2-gc-ldp}
On the weighted weak state space, the pair
\((\pi^\varepsilon,\Gamma^\varepsilon)\) satisfies a good short-time LDP with
speed \(\mu_\varepsilon\) and rate
\[
 I_T^{\rm gc}(f,\Gamma)
 =I_0^{\rm gc}(f_0)
 +\begin{cases}
 \displaystyle\int
 \ell\left(\frac{d\Gamma}{dA_f}\right)dA_f,
 &\Gamma\ll A_f\text{ and }(f,\Gamma)\text{ feasible},\\[2ex]
 +\infty,&\text{otherwise}.
 \end{cases}
\]
Here \(\ell(q)=q\log q-q+1\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Normal convergence of all finite collections of density and collision sources
and Lemma~\ref{lem:r3-b2-tight} give the upper bound.  Dualizing in the density
test imposes the closed weak balance.  For a positive collision density,
pointwise conjugacy
\[
 \sup_z\{qz-(e^z-1)\}=\ell(q)
\]
gives the displayed action; monotone truncation treats unbounded dual tests.

For the lower bound, first take a smooth feasible pair with
\(q=d\Gamma/dA_f\) bounded above and below.  Choose a smooth \(p\) solving the
backward linearized balance and put
\(\psi=\log q-\Delta p\).  Tilt the microscopic initial activity and every
actual contact by these sources.  Theorem~\ref{thm:r3-b2-gc-cluster} remains
normal on a slightly larger complex ball.  Its first derivative gives the
biased Boltzmann law \((f,\Gamma)\), and its second derivative plus compact
containment gives exponential concentration under the tilted law.  The exact
log Radon--Nikodym derivative has limiting cost
\[
 I_0^{\rm gc}(f_0)+\int(q\log q-q+1)dA_f.
\]
This proves the lower bound on smooth positive pairs.  Velocity truncation,
space--time convolution away from endpoints, and addition of a vanishing
reference collision activity approximate every finite-rate pair while
preserving the balance after a compensating transport correction.  Entropy
lower semicontinuity completes the lower bound and goodness.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Source-dependent microcanonical transfer and final rate}

Paper B1 proves, from Theorem~\ref{thm:r3-b2-gc-cluster}, that conditioning on
particle number, momentum, energy, and the declared macroscopic observables
reoptimizes the finite-dimensional multiplier for every pair \((h,\psi)\).
Denote the resulting initial constrained rate by \(I_{0,a}\).

\begin{theorem}[Microcanonical joint LDP]
\label{thm:r3-b2-mc-ldp}
Under the primitive microcanonical shell of B1,
\((\pi^\varepsilon,\Gamma^\varepsilon)\) satisfies the same good short-time
joint LDP, with rate
\[
 I_T^{\rm mc,a}(f,\Gamma)
 =I_{0,a}(f_0)
 +\begin{cases}
 \displaystyle\int
 \ell\left(\frac{d\Gamma}{dA_f}\right)dA_f,
 &\Gamma\ll A_f\text{ and }(f,\Gamma)\text{ feasible},\\[2ex]
 +\infty,&\text{otherwise}.
 \end{cases}
\]
The zero-cost pair is the Boltzmann solution with prepared initial datum and
its actual limiting collision intensity \(A_f\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
B1 gives local uniform convergence of the constrained log-Laplace functional
for every density and collision source in the same ball, with the multiplier
chosen at that source.  Hence the upper-bound duality and exponential
compactness argument above transfer unchanged.  For the lower bound, apply the
source-dependent B1 change of measure to the exact tilt used in the proof of
Theorem~\ref{thm:r3-b2-gc-ldp}; the shell has subexponential probability under
that tilted law and its logarithmic coefficient is exactly \(I_{0,a}\).
Approximation of finite-rate pairs is unchanged.  At zero cost,
\(d\Gamma/dA_f=1\) and the weak balance is the Boltzmann equation.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Round-four B2 closure]
\label{thm:r3-b2-main}
Every actual hard-sphere contact is marked in a source-uniform real-trajectory
cluster expansion.  The limiting Hamiltonian contains the collision source
inside \(e^{\Delta p+\psi}\), and the microcanonical density--collision pair
satisfies the good LDP of Theorem~\ref{thm:r3-b2-mc-ldp}.  Recollisions are
controlled by the first-cycle geometric gain and are not replaced by fictitious
creation edges.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Combine Proposition~\ref{prop:r3-b2-balance},
Theorems~\ref{thm:r3-b2-gc-cluster}, \ref{thm:r3-b2-hj},
\ref{thm:r3-b2-gc-ldp}, and \ref{thm:r3-b2-mc-ldp}.
\end{proof}
