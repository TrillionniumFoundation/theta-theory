\section{Positive physical remainders and a pointwise lower local law}
\label{sec:v51-positive-remainder}
The local variation theorem does not bound the height of the physical
remainder. It does, however, bound the height of its convolution at
any fixed band. Positivity then gives a one-sided pointwise theorem
for the original density. More precisely, the entire density error
is, in essential supremum, approximated by the positive physical
remainder. This identifies which sign of concentration remains to be
controlled; it does not discard that concentration.

All densities in this section are defined up to Lebesgue-null sets.
Every supremum in the roof variable is an essential supremum. Set
\[
 P_{m,R}^{n,k}(u)=m^2p_{n,R}(k,m,u),\qquad
 G_{m,R}^{n,k}(u)=\mathcal L_{m,R}(k_1,k_2,u,n).
\]
The labels always satisfy $R\in[0.45,0.47]$, $1\le n\le m$ and
$k\in\Z^2$. The source of $p$ is the original $\nu_R^*$.
In particular the factor $1/c$ has already been included; it is not
inserted again in the estimates below. The real, even reconstruction
kernel is $K_B(u)=BK_1(Bu)$ from
Section~\ref{sec:v43-full-source-inversion}. It has integral one and is a
Schwartz function; it is not presumed nonnegative.

\subsection{A fixed convolution of a locally small source}
\begin{lemma}[Local mass controls a fixed smooth convolution]
\label{lem:v51-local-convolution-height}
Let $K\in W^{1,1}(\R)$ and let $f$ have finite
$\|f\|_{1,h}^{\rm loc}$ for some $h>0$. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v51-convolution-height}
 \|K*f\|_\infty\le
 \bigl(h^{-1}\|K\|_1+\|K'\|_1\bigr)\|f\|_{1,h}^{\rm loc}.
\end{equation}
Consequently, if a family $f_{m,\lambda}$ satisfies
\[
 \limsup_{m\to\infty}\sup_\lambda
       h^{-1}\|f_{m,\lambda}\|_{1,h}^{\rm loc}\le s
                 \quad\hbox{for every fixed }h>0,
\]
with the same $s$, then for every fixed $K\in W^{1,1}(\R)$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v51-convolution-limsup}
 \limsup_{m\to\infty}\sup_\lambda\|K*f_{m,\lambda}\|_\infty
                                                   \le s\|K\|_1.
\end{equation}
No analogous conclusion for $\|f_{m,\lambda}\|_\infty$ is asserted.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Take the absolutely continuous representative of $K$. On each interval
$I_j=[jh,(j+1)h]$, the fundamental theorem of calculus gives
\[
 \sup_{I_j}|K|\le h^{-1}\int_{I_j}|K|+\int_{I_j}|K'|.
\]
For any $x$, the integral of $|f(x-y)|$ over $I_j$ is at most
$\|f\|_{1,h}^{\rm loc}$. Multiply by the displayed supremum and
sum over $j\in\Z$. The sum is finite, and Tonelli proves
\eqref{eq:v51-convolution-height}, including absolute convergence.
At fixed $h$, its collision limsup is at most
$s(\|K\|_1+h\|K'\|_1)$. Now let $h\downarrow0$, after that
limsup and with $K$ still fixed. This proves
\eqref{eq:v51-convolution-limsup}. The last operation regularizes
$f$ by the fixed kernel throughout; it does not differentiate a
local-window theorem at a count-dependent scale.
\end{proof}

The following elementary principle separates the new logical step
from its billiard inputs.
\begin{proposition}[A positive-remainder lower principle]
\label{prop:v51-positive-principle}
Let $P_{m,\lambda}\ge0$ be densities and $G_{m,\lambda}$ real
functions. For each fixed $B\ge B_0$, suppose
$P=A_{B,m,\lambda}+R_{B,m,\lambda}$ with $R_{B,m,\lambda}\ge0$,
and suppose
\begin{align*}
 \sup_\lambda\|K_B*P_{m,\lambda}-G_{m,\lambda}\|_\infty&\longrightarrow0,\\
 \limsup_m\sup_\lambda\|A_{B,m,\lambda}-K_B*A_{B,m,\lambda}\|_\infty&\le r_B,\\
 \limsup_m\sup_\lambda h^{-1}
                 \|R_{B,m,\lambda}\|_{1,h}^{\rm loc}&\le s_B
                         \quad(h>0\hbox{ fixed}).
\end{align*}
Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v51-positive-principle}
 \limsup_m\sup_\lambda
 \|P_{m,\lambda}-G_{m,\lambda}-R_{B,m,\lambda}\|_\infty
                                  \le r_B+\|K_1\|_1s_B.
\end{equation}
The same upper bound holds for the limsup of
$\|(G_{m,\lambda}-P_{m,\lambda})_+\|_\infty$.
If $r_B+s_B\to0$, this last lower-law error tends to zero as
$m\to\infty$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The exact identity is
\[
 P-G-R=(A-K_B*A)+(K_B*P-G)-K_B*R.
\]
Apply Lemma~\ref{lem:v51-local-convolution-height} to the last term,
keeping $B$ fixed, and use $\|K_B\|_1=\|K_1\|_1$.
This proves \eqref{eq:v51-positive-principle}. Since $R\ge0$,
$(G-P)_+\le|P-G-R|$ almost everywhere. Its limsup is therefore
bounded by the same expression for every fixed $B$. Only now send
$B$ to infinity. Neither the regular part $A$ nor $K_B$ needs to be
positive for this argument; the sign of the remainder is indispensable.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Application to the complete exact return coefficient}
Use the exact physical-only remainder $b^{\varepsilon,1}$ of
Section~\ref{sec:v48-physical-remainder}. The source decomposition is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v51-exact-positive-split}
 p=f^{\varepsilon,1}+d^{\varepsilon,\varepsilon,1}
                                    +b^{\varepsilon,1},\qquad
 b^{\varepsilon,1}=b_{\rm inc}^{\varepsilon,1}
                                    +b_{\rm clr}^{\varepsilon,1}\ge0.
\end{equation}
All terms have the same original labels. The first-physical-defect
partition defining the two terms on the right is disjoint before
pushforward, including the transition region of every smooth guard.
There is no new normalization of a component.

\begin{lemma}[Smoothed physical remainder at the local height scale]
\label{lem:v51-smoothed-physical-height}
For every fixed $B>0$, every sufficiently small fixed
$\varepsilon>0$, and all $|w|\le M$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v51-smoothed-physical-height}
 \limsup_{m\to\infty}\sup_{R,n,k,|w|\le M}
       m^2\|K_B*b^{\varepsilon,w}_{n,k,m,R}\|_\infty
                         \le CM\varepsilon^{1/16}\|K_1\|_1.
\end{equation}
The constant is independent of the fixed reconstruction band $B$.
This is not a supremum over count-dependent bands inside the limsup.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Equation~\eqref{eq:v49-local-limsup} gives the hypothesis of
Lemma~\ref{lem:v51-local-convolution-height}, with
$f_{m,\lambda}=m^2b^{\varepsilon,w}$ and
$s=CM\varepsilon^{1/16}$. Its uniformity in $w$ is by domination
of the original positive source. Apply
\eqref{eq:v51-convolution-limsup} with this fixed $K_B$.

There is also a useful finite-count reading. Put $h=B^{-1}$ in
\eqref{eq:v51-convolution-height} and use
\eqref{eq:v49-physical-window-bound}. With
$C_K=\|K_1\|_1+\|K_1'\|_1$, the upper bound is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v51-smoothed-finite}
 C_KM\left[C\varepsilon^{1/16}(1+B/B_*)
       +C_{B_*}(B+B_*)m^3\rho_{B_*}^{m/2}\right].
\end{equation}
Here $B$ and $B_*$ are independently fixed before $m$ tends to
infinity. The auxiliary band may then be enlarged. In the sharper
norm bound \eqref{eq:v51-smoothed-physical-height}, the final
$h\downarrow0$ in the lemma is taken only after these limits.
No orbit is continued across an incidence or competing-hit seam.
\end{proof}

Choose once and for all a sufficiently large constant $A_0$ and put
$\varepsilon(B)=A_0B^{-1/12}$, for large $B$, as in
Theorem~\ref{thm:v48-two-source-reduction}. Write
\[
 \mathfrak b_{B,m,R}^{n,k}=m^2b^{\varepsilon(B),1}_{n,k,m,R},\qquad
 \mathfrak h_{B,m,R}^{n,k}
       =m^2(f^{\varepsilon(B),1}_{n,k,m,R}
                  +d^{\varepsilon(B),\varepsilon(B),1}_{n,k,m,R}).
\]
The symbol $\mathfrak h_{B,m,R}^{n,k}$ here denotes a controlled
source density; it is not the arithmetic residue $\mathfrak a_R$.

\begin{theorem}[Uniform positive-remainder representation of the error]
\label{thm:v51-positive-error}
For every sufficiently large fixed $B$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v51-positive-error}
 \limsup_{m\to\infty}\sup_{R,n,k}
 \|P_{m,R}^{n,k}-G_{m,R}^{n,k}-\mathfrak b_{B,m,R}^{n,k}\|_\infty
                                                   \le CB^{-1/192}.
\end{equation}
In particular the complete original density obeys the arithmetic
pointwise lower local law
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v51-lower-local-law}
 \delta_m:=\sup_{R,n,k}
       \|(G_{m,R}^{n,k}-P_{m,R}^{n,k})_+\|_\infty
                                      \longrightarrow0.
\end{equation}
The main term is the full finite transition kernel, uniformly in
$R$. On central compact sets at a fixed radius it can be replaced
by $c\mathfrak a_R(k,n,m)g_{\Omega_R}(Z(u))$, with the unchanged
arithmetic coefficient and collision normalization.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each fixed $B$, Theorem~\ref{thm:v46-protected-correction}
gives
\[
 \limsup_m\sup_{R,n,k}m^2
 \|f^{\varepsilon(B),1}-K_B*f^{\varepsilon(B),1}\|_\infty
                                                    \le CB^{-1/2}.
\]
Indeed $B\ge C_2\varepsilon(B)^{-12}$ follows by choosing
$A_0^{12}\ge C_2$. Corollary~\ref{cor:v48-decision-all-band}
gives for the entire decision source the bound
$C\varepsilon(B)^{1/16}=CB^{-1/192}$ in the same normalized
essential-supremum norm. Thus the controlled source has error
$r_B\le C(B^{-1/2}+B^{-1/192})$.

The fixed-band arithmetic reconstruction on the original source,
used in Theorem~\ref{thm:v49-raw-local-TV}, gives
$\sup\|K_B*P-G\|_\infty\to0$.
The physical remainder has the local variation bound
$s_B\le C\varepsilon(B)^{1/16}$ of
\eqref{eq:v49-local-limsup}. Apply
Proposition~\ref{prop:v51-positive-principle} to the exact
split \eqref{eq:v51-exact-positive-split}. This proves
\eqref{eq:v51-positive-error}. Positivity of $\mathfrak b$ gives
$\limsup_m\delta_m\le CB^{-1/192}$ for every fixed large $B$;
let $B\to\infty$ to obtain \eqref{eq:v51-lower-local-law}.
The fixed-radius specialization follows from the uniform central
specialization in Theorem~\ref{thm:v41-uniform-transition}.

The three source estimates are used in their own norms. Only the
convolution of the physical remainder is converted from local
variation to height. Its unconvolved height has not been estimated.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A positive, rather than signed, remaining criterion}
For a central radius $M_0<\infty$, put
\[
 \|H\|_{\infty,M_0}
 =\sup_{R,n,k}\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{u:\ |Z(u)|\le M_0}|H_{R,n,k}(u)|,
 \qquad
 Z(u)=\frac{(k_1,k_2,u,n)-m(0,0,\bar\tau_R,c)}{\sqrt m}.
\]
The essential supremum on an empty set is zero.

\begin{corollary}[Equivalence with positive physical concentration]
\label{cor:v51-positive-criterion}
For every fixed $M_0$, the two-sided arithmetic pointwise local law
on this central set is equivalent to
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v51-positive-criterion}
 \lim_{B\to\infty}\limsup_{m\to\infty}
         \|\mathfrak b_{B,m}\|_{\infty,M_0}=0.
\end{equation}
It is also equivalent to the two corresponding positive-height
conditions for $m^2b_{\rm inc}^{\varepsilon(B),1}$ and
$m^2b_{\rm clr}^{\varepsilon(B),1}$ separately. Moreover
\[
 \left|\|(P_m-G_m)_+\|_{\infty,M_0}
             -\|\mathfrak b_{B,m}\|_{\infty,M_0}\right|
 \le\|P_m-G_m-\mathfrak b_{B,m}\|_\infty.
\]
Thus a cancellation between the two signed physical corrections
cannot eliminate a positive height obstruction left by
\eqref{eq:v51-positive-criterion}.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
For real $x$ and $b\ge0$, $|x_+-b|\le|x-b|$. Apply this pointwise
to $x=P_m-G_m$ and then take essential suprema to get the last
inequality. Equation~\eqref{eq:v51-positive-error}, restricted to
the central set, proves both directions of the equivalence by the
triangle inequality, with $m\to\infty$ before $B\to\infty$.
The negative part already tends to zero by
\eqref{eq:v51-lower-local-law}. Finally each nonnegative physical
component is at most their sum, and the norm of that sum is at most
the sum of their norms. This proves the separate equivalences.
The signed criterion in \eqref{eq:v48-physical-residual-criterion}
is not assumed: its equivalence with the new positive criterion
follows also from \eqref{eq:v51-smoothed-physical-height}.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{What remains to be bounded.}
The conclusion excludes downward density defects at the microscopic
scale and replaces a possible signed-cancellation argument by the
positive criterion \eqref{eq:v51-positive-criterion}. It does not
prove that criterion. Positive, narrow incidence or clearance spikes
are still compatible with the local variation theorem and with the
new lower law. The original two-sided raw endpoint is retained with
its correct arithmetic main term.
