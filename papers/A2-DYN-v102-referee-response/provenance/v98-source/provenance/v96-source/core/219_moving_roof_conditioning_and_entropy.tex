\section{Moving roof selections and relative entropy}
\label{sec:v92-entropy}
We now distinguish an exact same-roof kernel from a law conditioned
on a measurable set of roofs. The latter has a useful stability theorem
even though uniform pointwise inversion has not been established.
The scalar labels remain fixed throughout; no neighboring collision,
displacement or occupation classes are averaged together.

There are two window conventions. Locally, $K_m$ is any interval of
fixed length $L$ and $d\lambda_m=dt$. On the full central window,
$K_m=[m\bar\tau_R-M\sqrt m,m\bar\tau_R+M\sqrt m]$ and
$d\lambda_m=dt/\sqrt m$. In either case $\lambda_m(K_m)\le V$ for
a fixed $V$. All sources below are restricted to the central class.

Fix $d,\zeta>0$. Let $E_m\subset K_m\cap D_m(d)$ be any measurable
set, possibly depending on every finite-count parameter, with
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v92-selection}
 H_E:=\int_{E_m}G\,d\lambda_m\ge\zeta.
\end{equation}
Put $F_E=\int_{E_m}P\,d\lambda_m$. The two roof laws and two joint
roof--path laws are
\begin{align*}
 d\mathbb P_E^{\rm roof}&=\one_{E_m}P\,d\lambda_m/F_E,
 &d\mathbb G_E^{\rm roof}&=\one_{E_m}G\,d\lambda_m/H_E,\\
 d\mathbb P_E(t,x)&=d\mathbb P_E^{\rm roof}(t)\,d\mathsf Q_t(x),
 &d\mathbb G_E(t,x)&=d\mathbb G_E^{\rm roof}(t)\,d\mathsf W_R(x).
\end{align*}
The mass floor in \eqref{eq:v92-selection} allows irregular and moving
roof sets, but does not allow their reference mass to collapse to zero.
No reference law is constructed on an arithmetic zero class.

\begin{theorem}[Uniform stability under measurable roof conditioning]
\label{thm:v92-moving}
Under the three stated inputs, uniformly over all sets satisfying
\eqref{eq:v92-selection}, one has $F_E/H_E\to1$ and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v92-roof-tv}
 \|\mathbb P_E^{\rm roof}-\mathbb G_E^{\rm roof}\|_{\TV}
                                                        \longrightarrow0.
\end{equation}
For the joint laws, the discrepancy tested by every Borel $\Phi(t,x)$
whose path sections belong to the unit bounded-Lipschitz class also
tends to zero, uniformly over that whole test class and over $E_m$.
No regularity of the test in $t$ is imposed. This joint conclusion is
not total variation on path space.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Set
\[
 a_m=\int_{K_m\cap\mathcal C(M)}|P-G|\,d\lambda_m,
 \qquad v_m=\int_{K_m\cap\mathcal C(M)}\mathfrak d_m\,d\lambda_m.
\]
Inputs~\ref{input:v92-critical} and Theorem~\ref{thm:v92-numerator}
give $a_m,v_m\to0$ uniformly in either window convention. In
particular $|F_E-H_E|\le a_m$, so $F_E\ge\zeta/2$ eventually.
Subtract the joint finite measures before normalizing. Their tested
error is at most
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v92-joint-bound}
 \frac{v_m+|F_E-H_E|}{F_E}
                         \le\frac2\zeta(v_m+a_m).
\end{equation}
The same computation with scalar densities gives
$\|\mathbb P_E^{\rm roof}-\mathbb G_E^{\rm roof}\|_{\TV}
\le2a_m/F_E$. These bounds do not depend on the geometry of $E_m$.
Measurability follows from the original common kernels; alternatively
a countable path norming class bounds every Borel path-section test by
$\mathfrak d_m$ before the roof integral. The proof neither chooses a
separate exceptional roof for each test nor normalizes any zero reference.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Entropy bounds without an upper likelihood bound]
\label{lem:v92-entropy}
For every $1<p<2$ there is $C_p<\infty$ such that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v92-entropy-functions}
 x\log x-x+1\le C_p|x-1|^p\quad(x\ge0),\qquad
 x-1-\log x\le C_p|x-1|^p\quad(x\ge1/3).
\end{equation}
The first expression is interpreted continuously at zero. The lower
restriction in the second inequality is essential; no upper bound on
$x$ occurs in either inequality.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
On $|x-1|\le1/2$, Taylor's integral formula and a bound for the
second derivatives give $C|x-1|^2\le C|x-1|^p$. On the remaining
compact intervals the ratios in \eqref{eq:v92-entropy-functions}
are continuous and bounded. For $x\ge2$, the elementary bound
$\log x\le x^{p-1}/(p-1)$ gives the first estimate after enlarging
$C_p$. The second left side is at most $x$ there, which is bounded
by $2^p|x-1|^p$. This proves both assertions.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Two-sided roof relative-entropy convergence]
\label{thm:v92-entropy}
For the roof laws of Theorem~\ref{thm:v92-moving}, and any fixed
$1<p<Q=32/31$, for all sufficiently large $m$,
\begin{align}
 &\operatorname{KL}(\mathbb P_E^{\rm roof}\Vert\mathbb G_E^{\rm roof})
 +\operatorname{KL}(\mathbb G_E^{\rm roof}\Vert\mathbb P_E^{\rm roof})
                                                        \notag\\
 &\hspace{15mm}\le C_{M,V,d,\zeta,p}
       \int_{K_m\cap\mathcal C(M)}|P-G|^p\,d\lambda_m
                              \longrightarrow0.\label{eq:v92-entropy-limit}
\end{align}
The bound is uniform over all permitted moving sets and exact labels.
The statement concerns the one-dimensional roof marginals only.
It asserts neither relative entropy between path laws nor uniform
convergence of the forward likelihood.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Both roof densities are positive almost everywhere on $E_m$, by
Theorem~\ref{thm:v92-lower}. Their forward likelihood is
\[
 R_E=\frac{P}{G}\frac{H_E}{F_E}.
\]
Uniformly, $F_E/H_E\to1$ and $P/G\ge1-d_m(M)/d$. Thus $R_E\ge1/3$
for large $m$, while no upper likelihood bound is needed. Also
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v92-likelihood-lp}
 |R_E-1|\le\frac{2}{d}|P-G|+\frac{2}{\zeta}a_m.
\end{equation}
Since $G\le D_M$ on the central class and $H_E\ge\zeta$,
H\"older on a set of measure at most $V$ gives
\[
 \int|R_E-1|^p\,d\mathbb G_E^{\rm roof}
 \le C_{M,V,d,\zeta,p}
           \int_{K_m\cap\mathcal C(M)}|P-G|^p\,d\lambda_m.
\]
The right side tends to zero because unweakened weak $L^Q$
convergence implies strong $L^p$ convergence on the fixed local or
rescaled window when $p<Q$.

The identity $\int R_E\,d\mathbb G_E^{\rm roof}=1$ rewrites the two
relative entropies as the integrals of $R_E\log R_E-R_E+1$ and
$R_E-1-\log R_E$, respectively. Apply Lemma~\ref{lem:v92-entropy}.
The same inequalities prove finiteness of these entropies for large
$m$. No boundedness of $R_E$ from above and no strong raw $L^Q$
convergence is used. Since actual collision paths and the reference
paths need not be mutually absolutely continuous, none of this
argument is applied to path-space Radon--Nikodym derivatives.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[The unremoved upper spike]
\label{rem:v92-scope}
The pointwise lower bound, the bounded reciprocal, conditional-kernel
finite norms, and roof entropy convergence are compatible with an
upper spike of fixed height on a shrinking roof set. For example,
$G=1$ and $P_j=1+\one_{(0,\delta_j)}$, with $\delta_j\downarrow0$,
have all these properties while $\|P_j-G\|_\infty=1$. This example
is a topology check, not a physical counterexample. The remaining
source-specific task is still to prove that the original upper
exceptional-source essential-height charge is zero. None of the new
conditional conclusions supplies that missing persistence theorem.
\end{remark}
