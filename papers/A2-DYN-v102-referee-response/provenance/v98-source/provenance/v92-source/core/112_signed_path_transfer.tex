\section{A signed-measure principle for exact-label path inversion}
\label{sec:v53-signed-transfer}
We separate the measure-theoretic step from the collision geometry.
The distinction between tight total variations and small total variation
is essential. The first gives compactness of signed inverses; it does
not give total-variation convergence on path space.

\begin{lemma}[Tight signed measures on continuous path space]
\label{lem:v53-signed-compactness}
Let $\sigma_j,\sigma$ be finite real signed measures on
$\mathcal C=C([0,1],\R^d)$. Suppose that
$\sup_j\|\sigma_j\|_{\TV}<\infty$, the measures $|\sigma_j|$ are
uniformly tight, the total masses converge, and
\[
 \int \exp\!\left(i\sum_{a=1}^r v_a\cdot x(t_a)\right)d\sigma_j(x)
 \longrightarrow
 \int \exp\!\left(i\sum_{a=1}^r v_a\cdot x(t_a)\right)d\sigma(x)
\]
for all finite lists of rational times and real vectors. Then
$\|\sigma_j-\sigma\|_{\mathrm{BL}^*}\to0$, with the supremum path
metric and the bounded-Lipschitz convention of
Section~\ref{sec:v52-path-inversion}.
It suffices to dominate $|\sigma_j|$ by positive finite measures
$\tau_j$ of bounded masses which are uniformly tight.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The Jordan parts of $\sigma_j$ have bounded masses and are tight.
Positive finite-measure compactness, applied to both parts along a
subsequence, gives a weakly convergent signed subsubsequence.
Equivalently, under the last hypothesis, apply this compactness to
$\tau_j+\sigma_j$ and $\tau_j-\sigma_j$; these measures are positive
because $|\sigma_j|\le\tau_j$. Cylinder characteristic functions
identify every subsequential limit with $\sigma$. Indeed uniqueness
of the Fourier transform identifies each finite-dimensional signed
marginal. Rational evaluations generate the Borel sigma field on
continuous path space; uniqueness on their cylinder sets identifies
the finite signed measure. Mass convergence includes the constant
cylinder and rules out an unstated loss of mass.

Given $\epsilon>0$, choose a compact path set carrying all but
$\epsilon$ of every variation and of $|\sigma|$. The restrictions of
unit bounded-Lipschitz functions to this set have a finite uniform
$\epsilon$-net. Choose its representatives from the original test
class, so they are defined on the whole path space. Convergence on
these finitely many bounded continuous functions and the variation
bound control the supremum on the compact set. The complement costs
at most $2\epsilon$. Letting $\epsilon\downarrow0$ proves the claim.
The argument applies to every subsequence and hence to the full
sequence. It proves a bounded-Lipschitz conclusion, not convergence
of the Jordan parts in total variation.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Positive remainders shared by all tests}
The following statement applies to a compact family of reference path
laws and any collection of discrete labels. The labels may vary with
the integer $m$. Write $\lambda$ for their aggregate, and let
$\mathsf W_\lambda$ range over a compact subset of the probability
measures on $\mathcal C$ in the weak topology.
For each $(m,\lambda)$ let $\mathbf P_{m,\lambda}(u)$ be a Borel
kernel of positive finite path measures, of finite total mass after
integration in $u$. Put $P_{m,\lambda}=\mathbf P_{m,\lambda}(1)$.
Let $G_{m,\lambda}$ be a real scalar reference. Convolution is in
$u$ only. Suppose $K_B\in W^{1,1}(\R)$ and
$\sup_B\|K_B\|_1\le\kappa<\infty$.
All the essential norms below use common versions obtained from a
countable bounded-Lipschitz norming class.

\begin{theorem}[Exact-label path inversion with a positive remainder]
\label{thm:v53-abstract-transfer}
For each fixed $B$, suppose there are positive kernels
$0\le\mathbf R_{B,m,\lambda}\le\mathbf P_{m,\lambda}$ and put
$\mathbf A_B=\mathbf P-\mathbf R_B$. Assume the following four
properties, in each of which $B$ is fixed before $m\to\infty$.

\emph{Scalar and cylinder inversion.} The masses of
$\sigma_{B,m,\lambda,u}=(K_B*\mathbf P_{m,\lambda})(u)$ satisfy
$\sup_{\lambda,u}|\sigma_{B,m,\lambda,u}(1)-G_{m,\lambda}(u)|\to0$.
For each rational-time characteristic cylinder $\phi$,
\[
 \sup_{\lambda,u}|\sigma_{B,m,\lambda,u}(\phi)
             -\sigma_{B,m,\lambda,u}(1)\mathsf W_\lambda(\phi)|\to0.
\]

\emph{Variation compactness.} The variations of the signed measures
$\sigma_{B,m,\lambda,u}$ have bounded masses and are uniformly tight,
including over the labels and $u$.

\emph{Controlled-source correction.} For numbers $r_B\ge0$,
\[
 \limsup_m\sup_{\lambda,F\in\mathcal F}
 \|\mathbf A_{B,m,\lambda}(F)
          -K_B*\mathbf A_{B,m,\lambda}(F)\|_\infty\le r_B.
\]

\emph{Positive local mass.} For numbers $s_B\ge0$ independent of
$h$, and every fixed $h>0$,
\[
 \limsup_m\sup_{\lambda,t}\frac1h
       \int_t^{t+h}\mathbf R_{B,m,\lambda}(u)(1)\,du\le s_B.
\]
Then
\begin{align}
 &\limsup_m\sup_\lambda\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_u
 \|\mathbf P_{m,\lambda}(u)-G_{m,\lambda}(u)\mathsf W_\lambda
                  -\mathbf R_{B,m,\lambda}(u)\|_{\mathrm{BL}^*}
                      \le r_B+\kappa s_B,\label{eq:v53-abstract-positive}\\
 &\limsup_m\sup_{\lambda,t}\frac1h\int_t^{t+h}
 \|\mathbf P_{m,\lambda}(u)-G_{m,\lambda}(u)\mathsf W_\lambda
                              \|_{\mathrm{BL}^*}\,du
                      \le r_B+(\kappa+1)s_B.\label{eq:v53-abstract-local}
\end{align}
In particular $r_B+s_B\to0$ gives a path-valued local law and
same-roof conditional bounded-Lipschitz convergence in mean whenever
$\int_{I_t}(G_{m,\lambda})_+\ge dh$ for fixed $d,h>0$.
If, in addition, the ordered essential height of
$\mathbf R_{B,m,\lambda}(1)$ tends to zero on a set where
$G_{m,\lambda}\ge d$, the conditional path convergence is uniform
in essential supremum on that set.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The first two hypotheses and Lemma~\ref{lem:v53-signed-compactness}
give, at fixed $B$,
\[
 \sup_{\lambda,u}\|K_B*\mathbf P-G\mathsf W_\lambda
                                      \|_{\mathrm{BL}^*}\to0.
\]
For completeness, if uniformity failed, choose a violating sequence.
Compactness of the reference laws and boundedness of the signed
masses allow a subsequence with limits $\mathsf W$ and $a$.
The signed-measure lemma identifies its limit as $a\mathsf W$,
contradicting the violation. Thus no uniformity over a growing band
has been used.

For $F\in\mathcal F$ the exact source identity is
\[
 \mathbf P(F)-G\mathsf W_\lambda(F)-\mathbf R_B(F)
 =\mathbf A_B(F)-K_B*\mathbf A_B(F)
   +K_B*\mathbf P(F)-G\mathsf W_\lambda(F)-K_B*\mathbf R_B(F).
\]
Since $|\mathbf R_B(F)|\le\mathbf R_B(1)$, the local mass hypothesis
and Lemma~\ref{lem:v51-local-convolution-height} bound the last
term, after the collision limsup, by $\|K_B\|_1s_B$ uniformly in
$F$. A countable norming class supplies one exceptional roof-null
set. This proves \eqref{eq:v53-abstract-positive}. Integrating and
using $\|\mathbf R_B(u)\|_{\mathrm{BL}^*}=\mathbf R_B(u)(1)$
proves \eqref{eq:v53-abstract-local}.

Write $\mathbf P(u)=P(u)Q_u$. The constant function one belongs to
$\mathcal F$, so
\[
 P(u)d_{\rm BL}(Q_u,\mathsf W_\lambda)
       \le2\|\mathbf P(u)-G(u)\mathsf W_\lambda\|_{\mathrm{BL}^*}.
\]
Also $|P-G_+|\le|P-G|$, since $P\ge0$. The integrated positive
reference bound therefore gives $\int_{I_t}P\ge dh/2$ eventually.
Integration and division by this unchanged source mass prove the
conditional mean assertion.

For the last assertion put
$\mathbf E=\mathbf P-G\mathsf W_\lambda-\mathbf R_B$ and
$R_B=\mathbf R_B(1)$. Exactly,
\[
 P(Q_u-\mathsf W_\lambda)
   =\mathbf R_B-R_B\mathsf W_\lambda
                          +\mathbf E-\mathbf E(1)\mathsf W_\lambda.
\]
Positivity gives a norm bound $2R_B+2\|\mathbf E\|_{\mathrm{BL}^*}$.
Moreover \eqref{eq:v53-abstract-positive} gives the lower law
$P\ge G-o(1)$ in essential supremum when $r_B+s_B\to0$.
Thus $P\ge d/2$ eventually on the stated set. Divide there and
first take the collision limit, then the reconstruction-band limit.
The required positive height is an additional hypothesis, not a
consequence of positive local mass.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Verification for the present return record.}
Here $\lambda=(R,n,k)$, $\mathbf R_B=\mathbf b_B$ from
\eqref{eq:v52-bad-path-density}, and the reference is the bridge of
covariance $\Omega_R$. Lemma~\ref{lem:v52-path-endpoint-budget}
verifies the insertion step on each protected physical word.
Lemma~\ref{lem:v52-fixed-band-path} supplies cylinder inversion and
a positive variation dominator. The protected correction and the
all-depth decision bound give
$r_B\le C(B^{-1/2}+B^{-1/192})$; the physical local-variation theorem
gives $s_B\le CB^{-1/192}$. These are separate, source-pinned inputs
to the abstract theorem. No hypothesis here realizes a discontinuous
observable on a new Banach space, proves a physical seam estimate,
or adds an independent application outside this billiard family.
