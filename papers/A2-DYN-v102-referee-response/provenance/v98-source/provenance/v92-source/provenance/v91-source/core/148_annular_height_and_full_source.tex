\section{Ordered nonlinear caustic height and the complete source}
\label{sec:v68-annular-height}
We apply the annular comparison before the collision limit. This
controls angular births directly at the density level. It neither
differentiates an interval local limit nor passes from a finite-count
analytic radius to a uniform radius without a cutoff.

For each fixed $K,\chi,\varepsilon$, let
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v68-persistence-stratum}
 \mathcal P_{m,R,\lambda}(K)=
 \{z:g_z>0,\ \ell_{z,\lambda}\not\equiv0\text{ as a germ},\
                                  \mathfrak p_{z,\lambda}\le K\}.
\end{equation}
The certificate and this selection do not depend on $H$. There is
no cutoff on the birth order. Use the same disjoint physical itinerary
restrictions as in \eqref{eq:v66-complete-positive-partition}, and put
\begin{align}
 \mathcal Q^{\mathrm p,K}_{m,2H;\lambda,R}
       &=\sum_{z\in\mathcal P_{m,R,\lambda}(K)}q_{z,2H}\delta_{t_z},
                                                   \notag\\
 \mathcal C^K_{m,H;\lambda,R}
       &=\sum_{z\in\mathcal P_{m,R,\lambda}(K)}
            \gamma_{z,\lambda}(2H)^{d_{z,\lambda}/2}\delta_{t_z},
                                               \label{eq:v68-scale-trace}\\
 b^{\mathrm p,K,H}_{m;\lambda,R}(t)
       &=\sum_{z\in\mathcal P_{m,R,\lambda}(K)}
          \one_{\{0<t-t_z<H\}}
                       b_{z,\lambda}^{\varepsilon,\mathrm{clr}}(t).
                                               \label{eq:v68-physical-collar}
\end{align}
The second measure is scale weighted. Except on its order-zero part,
it is not the zero-width trace $\mathcal B$. The last density is a
restriction of the original source, not of its leading approximation.

\begin{theorem}[All-order original-source collar height]
\label{thm:v68-all-order-height}
Fix $K,\chi,\varepsilon,H$ before taking $m\to\infty$, with
$2H<h_\chi$ and $2K\sqrt H<1$. For every $h>0$,
\begin{align}
 \limsup_{m\to\infty}\sup_{R,\lambda,t}
 m^2\mathcal C^K_{m,H;\lambda,R}([t-h,t+h])
 &\le\frac{2Ce^{2K_\chi\sqrt H}}{1-2K\sqrt H}(2h+2H),
                                               \label{eq:v68-scale-concentration}\\
 \limsup_{m\to\infty}\sup_{R,\lambda}
 m^2\|b^{\mathrm p,K,H}_{m;\lambda,R}\|_\infty
 &\le6CH F_{\chi,K}(H).                       \label{eq:v68-all-order-height}
\end{align}
The constant $C$ is the unchanged physical collar constant. It is
independent of the number of words, labels and active margins, of
coincident critical values, and of every birth order. In particular,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v68-ordered-vanishing-height}
 \lim_{H\downarrow0}\limsup_{m\to\infty}\sup_{R,\lambda}
             m^2\|b^{\mathrm p,K,H}_{m;\lambda,R}\|_\infty=0.
\end{equation}
This includes zero-tangent-fraction nonlinear sectors of every finite
order and non-seam critical centers with positive center guard, when
they belong to the stated stratum. A bounded measurable insertion is
controlled by its supremum norm times the same positive height bound.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For an interval $I$, sum \eqref{eq:v68-mode-annulus} over its unchanged
critical centers. This is a positive comparison with
$\mathcal Q^{\mathrm p,K}_{m,2H}(I)$. Theorem~\ref{thm:v66-ordered-collar-trace}
applies to this subfamily, at the fixed collar width $2H$. Its bound
is $C(2h+2H)$, which proves
\eqref{eq:v68-scale-concentration}.

At roof $t$, each summand of \eqref{eq:v68-physical-collar} has its
critical center in $[t-H,t]$. By \eqref{eq:v68-inner-annulus},
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v68-finite-count-height-comparison}
 b^{\mathrm p,K,H}_{m;\lambda,R}(t)
       \le2F_{\chi,K}(H)
           \mathcal Q^{\mathrm p,K}_{m,2H;\lambda,R}([t-H,t])
                   \quad\hbox{for almost every }t.
\end{equation}
The common coarea representative excludes only a finite-count null
roof set; taking the finite union over words and exact labels does
not enlarge it to positive measure. Apply the same collar theorem to
an interval of length $H$, now centered at $t-H/2$. Its normalized
limsup is at most $C(H+2H)=3CH$. This proves
\eqref{eq:v68-all-order-height}. At fixed $K,\chi$, the factor
$F_{\chi,K}(H)$ tends to one as $H\downarrow0$, proving
\eqref{eq:v68-ordered-vanishing-height}. Variation domination at the
original positive source gives the insertion assertion.

The argument sums all birth orders at each fixed count before taking
any limit. It uses one common outer annulus, not a countable sum of
dyadic roof scales. Thus no interchange of an infinite scale sum and
the collision limsup is needed. Coincident centers add with their
positive coefficients throughout.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Relation to the angular-condition tail}
On a seam, the order-zero part has exactly the physical coefficient
$J_z\theta_{z,\lambda}$ of Section~\ref{sec:v67-stratified-trace}.
The positive-order part instead has zero such coefficient, although
its nonlinear source is covered by
Theorem~\ref{thm:v68-all-order-height}. This is precisely the part
which an estimate on $E(K)$ alone could not control.

The persistence cutoff $\mathfrak p$ is not the angular cutoff
$\mathfrak a$ in \eqref{eq:v67-condition-number}. The original
estimate $E(K)\to0$ has not been derived from the analytic
classification. At fixed $m,R$, every positive-center-guard nonzero
angular germ has a finite persistence number. Neither this statement
nor the order-independent constant in
\eqref{eq:v68-all-order-height} implies tightness after the supremum
in $R,\lambda$ and the collision limsup. In particular there is no
choice $K=K_m$ in the theorem.

\subsection{The full positive source and one canonical order of limits}
At fixed admissible parameters the original first-clearance source has
an exact disjoint restriction and its positive complement:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v68-complete-source-partition}
 b^{\varepsilon,\mathrm{clr}}
       =b^{\mathrm p,K,H}+r_{68}^{\varepsilon,\chi,K,H},
                    \qquad r_{68}^{\varepsilon,\chi,K,H}\ge0.
\end{equation}
The complement retains the entire outside source in
\eqref{eq:v66-complete-positive-partition}: failed incidence taper,
selected grazing, noncritical components and all uncovered states.
Within the selected charts it also retains zero-center-guard source,
source outside a certified angular-germ disk, large persistence
numbers and the portions outside the chosen inner collar. A zero
angular germ can delete source only inside its certified zero disk;
its possible outer source is not deleted. In particular, zero tangent
fraction alone is no longer an exclusion from the controlled term.

Write $\mathcal H(f)=\limsup_m\sup_{R,n,k}m^2\|f_{m;n,k,R}\|_\infty$.
Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v68-complete-clearance-budget}
 \mathcal H(b^{\varepsilon,\mathrm{clr}})
    \le6CH F_{\chi,K}(H)+
                      \mathcal H(r_{68}^{\varepsilon,\chi,K,H}).
\end{equation}
The complete first-incidence source remains separate. Its finite-count
inverse-incidence gain in Section~\ref{sec:v61-inverse-incidence}
has an exponential count constant and is not substituted for its
ordered height.

For clarity let $\mathcal E_M$ denote the normalized central-window
pointwise error limsup in the original raw-density problem. The exact
positive-error identity of Theorem~\ref{thm:v51-positive-error} and
\eqref{eq:v68-complete-clearance-budget} give, for each fixed
reconstruction band $B$ and every admissible fixed $\chi,K,H$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v68-canonical-endpoint-budget}
 \mathcal E_M\le C_M B^{-1/192}
   +\mathcal H(b^{\varepsilon(B),\mathrm{inc}})
   +6CH F_{\chi,K}(H)
   +\mathcal H(r_{68}^{\varepsilon(B),\chi,K,H}),
 \qquad \varepsilon(B)=A_0B^{-1/12}.
\end{equation}
First fix $B$ and its physical width, then choose admissible
$\chi,K,H$, and only then take the collision limsup. Subsequent
outer choices of these parameters may depend on $B$, but never on
$m$. To conclude $\mathcal E_M=0$, the complete incidence height
must tend to zero with $B\to\infty$, and there must be fixed
admissible choices for which the last two terms tend to zero as well.
Only the explicit inner-collar term is estimated here. No decrease
of $H$ is asserted to make its complementary source small.

The finite arithmetic transition kernel, all zero arithmetic classes,
and the original unrestricted pointwise target are unchanged. The
same-roof bridge, essential-likelihood and conditioned-path implications
retain their full positive-source hypotheses. The local-limit inputs
\cite{SV,DN,DPZ} are not used as density differentiation rules or as
an unproved estimate on the complementary height. The new proof route
is the analytic classification, relative radial persistence, and the
single positive annular comparison above.
