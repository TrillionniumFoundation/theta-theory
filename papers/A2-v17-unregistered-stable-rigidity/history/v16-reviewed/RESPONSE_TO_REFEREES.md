## The principal information and globality objections

The previous theorem supplied gap, individual curvatures and numerical contact frames. The new experiment supplies none of these. Its raw observation is a boundary-arclength endpoint subprobability law with failures retained, considered modulo one coherent reversal. The two endpoints are not observed in the ambient plane. Proposition `prop:calibration` proves

`g=T0/2`, `beta_b=2g/(3c_b)`, `kappa_b=2/(3 beta_b)-1/g`, and `A=1/(4 alpha sqrt(c0 c1(c0 c1-1)))`.

Only two leading quadratic moments, masses and signed first-moment germs, together with onset, are extracted from the full intrinsic laws. Thus the first moment is now a canonical compression of a specified intrinsic observation, not a supplied Cartesian function on a preknown graph. The arclength sensor is still richer than a count sensor; this is stated in the first section and is not hidden by terminology.

The arclength substitution is not assumed harmless. Lemma `lem:filter` and equation `eq:arcvariation` prove that its own highest-jet variation starts two weights later. Hence all odd and even diagonal blocks remain exactly the previously checked blocks, with new lower-order remainders computed recursively. This holds at arbitrary lower asymmetry, not just near an even jet.

Theorem `thm:network` then yields a genuine whole-periodic-table conclusion under a finite explicit information contract. Coherent signed arclength registration identifies different contact occurrences on each recovered boundary. A connected graph places all obstacles; two independent marked lattice cycles determine the period matrix by equation `eq:latticerecover`. The proof includes loops, changes of representative copies, backwards tree traversal, and nonunimodular integer cycle matrices. Proposition `prop:networkexamples` gives open physical examples and an exact shear control showing why an unseen lattice direction cannot be inferred from one channel. Registration is additional data, not asserted to follow from the local invariant.

## 1. Precise information category

The title and first abstract sentence now say **intrinsic arclength-marked**. Equation `eq:intrinsicmeasure` defines the observation, including its failure mass and its single common sign orbit. The orbit is not a reflection-averaged measure, and edgewise or windowwise sign changes are not allowed. The older framed Cartesian theorem is retained as a named component with its original information contract. Numerical frames occur only as coordinates chosen in the proof after calibration.

## 2. Count-only recovery modulo reflection

Section `sec:countfibers` goes beyond repeating the elementary reflection obstruction. Proposition `prop:countfibers` identifies analytic finite-jet fibers of dimension `2M-2` and realizes them in actual analytic periodic families at fixed leading geometry and area. Proposition `prop:formalcount` constructs all-order formal fibers and smooth physical realizations with identical count Taylor series. It explicitly proves the smooth extension and area adjustment rather than substituting formal jets for physical tables.

Remark `rem:analyticcount` separates these statements from the exact analytic count-germ quotient problem: formal convergence is not proved, and identical smooth Taylor series need not be identical exact germs. We do not claim either resolution of that analytic problem or a prediction of its outcome from the formal recursion. The positive intrinsic theorem recovers odd jets directly and has no such convergence gap. This distinction is a correction of the logical scope, not a replacement of the inverse-rigidity objective by a negative conclusion.
