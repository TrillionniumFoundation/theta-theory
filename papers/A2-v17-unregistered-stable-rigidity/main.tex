\begin{abstract}
We study near-onset collision laws with intrinsic endpoint-arclength
marks in periodic dispersing billiards. For an isolated labelled normal
channel, the onset, masses, signed first moments and quadratic limits
recover its gap, separate curvatures, free area and all asymmetric contact
jets. The even and odd inverse blocks are explicit and nonsingular,
including at equal curvatures. Analyticity determines the entire
participating obstacle pair. On a connected full-rank channel network,
asymmetric obstacle images synchronize independently reflected local
observations, determining the whole labelled table and marked lattice
without supplied cross-channel registration. Noncircular obstacles leave
only finitely many assemblies; intrinsic registration also gives rigidity
for symmetric tables. On uniformly analytic classes separated from
symmetry, we prove convergent whole-table reconstruction from finitely
many noisy moment windows, including estimation of the unknown onsets.
The error separates finite-jet conditioning, analytic continuation and
lattice inversion. We retain the count-only finite and formal fibers,
physical realization, finite-dimensional experiments, and smooth relative
boundary theory, distinguishing Taylor agreement from equality of exact
analytic count germs. No finite-horizon hypothesis is imposed.
\end{abstract}
