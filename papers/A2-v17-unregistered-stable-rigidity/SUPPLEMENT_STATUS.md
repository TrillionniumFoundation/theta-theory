# Submission volumes

The current primary article is `main.tex`, A2 v17, locally compiled to 29 pages. The new theorems and their complete proofs are in the primary article, not in a future-work plan.

`complete/main.tex` is Supplement S, *Nonlinear boundary laws: complete geometric proofs and auxiliary experiments*, included in this submission package. `complete/two_collision.tex` is its retained auxiliary document. The entire complete tree is preserved byte-for-byte from v16. Its historical front matter and references are retained with their original provenance; it is not cited as an independently published paper.

`history/v16-reviewed` is an archival copy of the complete reviewed v16 paper and nested history. It is not an additional new submission. The original directories elsewhere in the repository are also unchanged.

The local v17 validation built only the primary. Historical supplementary page counts and passing receipts remain historical. The new hosted validator stages a copy of the preserved supplement, builds both entry points twice to resolve cross-document references, and checks final logs and source immutability. Only an actual successful run establishes a fresh full-package build.
