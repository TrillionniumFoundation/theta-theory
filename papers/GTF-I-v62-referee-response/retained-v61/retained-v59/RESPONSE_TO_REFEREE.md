## Main mathematical response

A finite future-response quotient is necessary, and it gives an exact stationary physical action. Below error one half its cardinality is the eventual minimum; an all-profile occupation bound holds. Command returns are not assumed here because repeatable probes themselves provide physical holds. This is not a return-free version of the old terminal theorem.

`thm:adaptivecompiler59` constructs rational and dyadic instruments for arbitrary supplied finite rational SO(3) lists, rational unit seeds and positive affine probes. An auxiliary trajectory law, a unit-or-zero mean-square estimate, relative-entropy chain rule and joint-kernel coupling prove uniform error on the entire adaptive transcript. Neither rare-history conditioning nor correctness of marginal means is used as a shortcut. `thm:processlower59` gives a spectral-gap-free, explicit testing converse for the rational experiment. The process exponents are not claimed sharp, and the terminal logarithmic gap remains unchanged.
