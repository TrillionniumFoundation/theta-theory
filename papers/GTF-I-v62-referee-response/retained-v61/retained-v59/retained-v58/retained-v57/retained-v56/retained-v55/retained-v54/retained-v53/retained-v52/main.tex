\begin{abstract}
For controlled orthogonal experiments with signed coordinate seeds and
coordinate readout, we prove that bounded clocked stochastic width at
any fixed total-variation tolerance $0\le\varepsilon<\rho/(2D)$ is
equivalent to finiteness of the generated command group, and hence to
one stationary exact permutation realization. The proof separates the
finite-orbit subspace and applies executable word distortion on its
orthogonal complement; it imposes no rank or conditioning restriction
on hidden registers. Rational planar rotations admit a logarithmic
width lower bound from denominator height, including fixed noncommuting
alphabets. A stochastic trace inequality makes antipodal one-surplus
expansion explicit and gives a numerical sufficient six-state horizon
in dimension three. We also prove a computable unconditional noisy
six-state interval and an explicit conditioned interval. Reachable
sections, a complete global residual certificate hierarchy, and a
priced rational finite-group realization connect the classification
to positive realization and finite computation. Earlier simplex,
two-state, arithmetic and finite-bit theorems are preserved in the
complete article.
\end{abstract}
