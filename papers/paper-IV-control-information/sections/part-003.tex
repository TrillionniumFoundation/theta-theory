
\section{Main theorem and dependency boundary}\label{sec:main}
\begin{theorem}[Typed control and information extension]\label{thm:main}
The periodic-Sinai theta-semigroup of Paper III admits a controlled pure-saddle
extension with the same mechanically derived $q$ and $D_{R,q}$.  Fixed-law
second variations remain covariance curvatures; controlled second variations
include the explicit optimizer-response term.  Partial observation is a
separate product-platform theorem, and exact full-state observation is
separated by \cref{thm:full-no-go}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Combine \cref{thm:full-no-go,prop:controlled-consistency,thm:pure-saddle,thm:envelope,thm:controlled-limit,thm:filter}.
\end{proof}

\section*{Truth boundary}
Controls do not change the billiard geometry or canonical current preparation.
A controlled moving-scatterer problem would require a nonstationary spectral
packet and is not silently included.  The filter product is deterministic in
its full shift state but uses a specified product preparation.
