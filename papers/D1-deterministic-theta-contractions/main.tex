\begin{abstract}
Microscopic phase events are defined by exact order-parameter cells whose
boundaries are separated in rate and whose noncompact complement is controlled
by exponential tightness.  Source-inserted local coefficients and a
Morse--Bott expansion, not the LDP alone, produce all exponential and
polynomial phase weights.  Conditional component LDPs contract through a
labelled experiment.  Risk-sensitive control carries the complete phase
posterior and uses one policy shared by every phase.  Complex dominance and
phase-centred Gaussian mixtures retain all saddle, lattice, and conditioning
prefactors.
\end{abstract}
