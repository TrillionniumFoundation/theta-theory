\begin{abstract}
We construct projectively compatible positive phase labels from peripheral positive bands of the microscopic platform operator, including an explicit complementary label. Normalized component pressures, phase-local LDPs, exact mixture rates, shell coefficients, Gaussian tangents, and dynamic semigroups are assembled on one joint labelled law. Phase costs are counted once and label contraction yields the nonconvex minimum rate.
\end{abstract}
