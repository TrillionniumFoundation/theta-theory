\begin{abstract}
We construct genuine positive microscopic phase labels from separated components of an already proved platform rate, retain an explicit boundary band, and use the same label at every observable projection.  Restricted and conditional component laws, normalized component pressures, phase-local coefficients and exact positive mixture contraction yield the nonconvex physical rate without Riesz projections as probabilities or double counting phase costs.
\end{abstract}
