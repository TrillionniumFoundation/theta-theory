The phase variable is now constructed on the original finite-volume sample
space.  Separated minimizing sets of a declared order parameter define
disjoint Borel phase events, including a retained boundary component.  Their
conditional laws and weights therefore give an exact mixture identity.  The
A2 or B1 inserted local theorem supplies the component path LDPs, exponential
costs, polynomial exponents, saddle determinants, and positive leading
constants at one common speed.

The information state is enlarged to
`(rho_j, pi_j)_j`: every phase carries its own hidden/filter belief.  C1's
stratified reference measure gives common likelihood domination and measurable
multiplicative increments under adaptive policies.  Scalar phase weights or
one common physical state are not called sufficient when component dynamics
differ.

For a fixed observation-based policy, the component exponential values are
summed with the posterior weights.  The supremum over policies is taken only
after that sum.  The Bellman recursion therefore describes a controller who
does not observe the latent phase; it never combines separately optimized
component values.

The zero-free complex chart is derived from the already proved A2/A4 or
B1/B2 leading spectral/coefficient expansion.  Its analytic amplitude is
bounded away from zero near the origin and the remainder is uniformly smaller,
so the logarithm follows by Rouche's theorem rather than assumption.

`(J, sqrt(a)(TX-m_J))` and has phasewise Gaussian mean zero.  The label-aware
shifted version has mean `m_J`, while the unscaled statistic converges to a
mixture of point masses.  `gamma_*` is explicitly the smallest polynomial
exponent among exponentially minimizing phases, and the constants include
phase weights, local partition factors, saddle determinants, and any
conditioning-window factors.

All contractions use the full phase-conditioned belief and one shared policy.
The positive synthesis theorem is retained without a no-go or theorem
strength downgrade.
