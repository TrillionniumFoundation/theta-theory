## Required reference-sensitive physical boundary

S. G. Naik, M. Zartab, N. Gisin and M. Banik, *No-Go Theorem for Generic Simulation of Qubit Channels with Finite Classical Resources*, arXiv:2501.15807v2, 16 July 2025. Primary text: https://arxiv.org/html/2501.15807v2 . Theorems 2 and 5 distinguish perfect qubit-channel simulation by finite classical messaging from noisy depolarizing cases in their reference-sensitive joint-measurement task. Our decoded Choi blocks and programme vertices are genuine quantum instruments, not a physical classical messaging protocol for an unknown entangled input. The numerical algorithms take specified matrices. This comparison is now in both the article and bibliography; it does not supply independent novelty clearance.

## Classical programme method

R. Demkowicz-Dobrzanski, J. Kolodynski and M. Guta, *The elusive Heisenberg limit in quantum-enhanced metrology*, Nature Communications 3 (2012), 1063, DOI 10.1038/ncomms2067; arXiv:1201.3940v2. Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1201.3940v2 . Equations (4)–(6) and Methods use parameter-dependent classical mixtures of fixed channels. The present finite two-point inequality uses this established principle, then proves a complete adaptive-tester description-cover statement with an intrinsic rational code. We do not claim the mixture method or square-root scaling as new metrology. Its applicability fails uniformly at extremal unitary boundaries, which the manuscript demonstrates separately.

## Learning an unknown channel is a different charge

A. A. Mele and L. Bittel, *Optimal learning of quantum channels in diamond distance*, arXiv:2512.10214v3 (2026). Primary source: https://arxiv.org/html/2512.10214v3 . Theorem I.1 addresses unknown-channel query complexity and establishes dimension optimality at fixed accuracy; its dimension and accuracy lower contributions are separate. Sections III.1.1 and IV.1.1 discuss physical estimator regularization and channel packing. Our input is already specified; our lower bound counts possible reusable descriptions, not queries used to learn them. It neither replaces their learning protocol nor proves its query complexity.

A. Oufkir and F. Girardi, *Improved Lower Bounds for Learning Quantum Channels in Diamond Distance*, arXiv:2601.04180v4 (2026). Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.04180v4 . Their accuracy-dependent query lower bounds reinforce the distinction between identifying an unknown channel and encoding a supplied description. No unknown-channel access model is introduced into the codec theorem.

## Classical ingredients and claimed increment

The Choi affine codimension, finite-dimensional volume comparison, binary threshold testing, Schur congruence and bordered-minor identity are classical. Their complete proofs are included to expose constants and conventions. The new manuscript gives the exact intrinsic coefficient for the specified legal instrument body, an explicit s-coordinate Gaussian-rational code, a finite common-adaptive-tester modulus, and a matching fixed-margin repeated-use description exponent. The theorem statements identify which parameter limits are sharp and which dimension/error constants are only safe. This is not a claim of priority for each ingredient or a general mutable-space classification.
