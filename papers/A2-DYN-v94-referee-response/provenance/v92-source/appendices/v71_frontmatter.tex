\section{The preceding opening}
\label{app:v72-retained-front}
\subsection*{Previous abstract}

We study exact displacement, collision count and flight time for
actual returns in a triangular periodic Lorentz gas. A common physical
collar recovers angular loss by combining receiving surpluses before
taking a positive deficit. The construction increases the previously
controlled source, preserves its $O(\chi^6)$ ordered height, and
reduces the residual without changing any physical or integer label.
We express the new residual through a signed two-sided radial current,
including late births, atomic deaths and cancellation at a common
roof. This gives the complete selected-source height on a receiving-
reserve class without a wordwise variation or monotonicity bound.
The original arithmetic raw-error identity then retains only the net
current excess, the outside source and complete first incidence.



\paragraph{The exact local problem.}
For $R\in[9/20,47/100]$ consider the actual return record
$J_{n,R}=(K_{n,R},N_{n,R},T_{n,R})$ under
$\nu_R^*=c_*^{-1}\nu|_{Y_R^*}$, where $c_*=91/(10000\pi)$.
Write $p_{n,R}(k,m,t)$ for its density relative to counting measure
in $(k,m)$ and Lebesgue measure in $t$. Occupation counts collisions
$0,\ldots,m-1$; terminal membership is imposed at collision $m$.
The pointwise target remains
\begin{equation}\label{eq:intro-v71-target}
 \sup_{R,n,k}\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{t:\,|Z_{m,R}|\le M}
 \left|m^2p_{n,R}(k,m,t)-\mathcal L_{m,R}(k_1,k_2,t,n)\right|
 \longrightarrow0,
\end{equation}
where $Z_{m,R}=(k_1,k_2,t-m\bar\tau_R,n-mc_*)/\sqrt m$.
The finite arithmetic transition kernel in $\mathcal L_{m,R}$,
including its zero classes, is retained throughout. The estimates
below concern the complete positive sources in this same problem.

\paragraph{A common receiving capacity.}
For a selected critical chart use $F_z=t_z+|y|^2/2$,
$r_\chi=r_0\chi^3$, $h_\chi=r_\chi^2/2$ and
$K_\chi=C\chi^{-2}$. Its angular fraction
$L_{z,\lambda}(u)$ includes every physical and section test and the
exact label $\lambda=(n,k)$. Set $h=h_\chi$, $j=h/2$ and, at a
common roof $t$, sum only over $0<u_z=t-t_z<h$:
\[
 \mathsf X_\lambda(t)=\sum_zJ_zL_{z,\lambda}(u_z),\qquad
 \mathsf Y_\lambda(t)=\sum_z\frac{J_z}{j}
                                  \int_0^jL_{z,\lambda}(v)\,dv.
\]
Both sums use the same active charts, the same collision count and
the same exact label. The physical coefficient $J_z$ already contains
the section normalization. The old angular overlap defines the
controlled source $s_{70}$ and its positive loss $d_{70}$ on these
complete disks. We keep $s_{70}$ and recover a bounded measurable
fraction of $d_{70}$ using the receiving capacity not yet used by
the old overlap.

\begin{leadtheorem}[Recovery on the original physical source]
\label{thm:intro-v71-recovery}
Fix $0<\varepsilon\le\chi/4$, $0<\chi\le1/10$ and $K\ge1$
before the collision limit. There are complementary physical weights
$s_{71,K},d_{71,K}$ such that
\[
 b^{\varepsilon,\mathrm{clr}}=s_{71,K}+d_{71,K}+e_{70},\qquad
 s_{71,K}\ge s_{70},\quad 0\le d_{71,K}\le d_{70},
\]
where $e_{70}$ is exactly the original outside source. Moreover
\begin{align*}
 \mathcal H(s_{71,K})
   &\le \tfrac32 CK h_\chi
              e^{(1+1/\sqrt2)K_\chi r_\chi}\le C'K\chi^6,\\
 d_{71,K}(t)&\le e^{K_\chi r_\chi}
                         (\mathsf X_\lambda(t)-K\mathsf Y_\lambda(t))_+.
\end{align*}
All selected-disk annuli are included. No physical trajectories are
coupled and no capacity is borrowed from a different exact label.
\end{leadtheorem}
\begin{proof}
The capacity identities of Lemma~\ref{lem:v71-capacity-recovery}
recover the maximal common fraction allowed by the unused receiving
capacity. Theorem~\ref{thm:v71-pooled-height} compares the resulting
source with one positive physical collar trace after summing all active
charts. The center interval and collar width add to $3h_\chi/2$.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Signed radial balance.}
Let $\mu_{z,\lambda}=DL_{z,\lambda}$ be the physical radial
current. It is a signed measure at each fixed word; it retains jump
atoms and the actual Boolean boundary orientation. With
$F_j(s)=\min(s/j,1)$, set
\[
 k_j(u,s)=F_j(s)\one_{s<u}-(1-F_j(s))\one_{s>u}.
\]
Theorem~\ref{thm:v71-net-current} proves the exact identity
\begin{equation}\label{eq:intro-v71-current}
 \mathsf X_\lambda(t)-\mathsf Y_\lambda(t)
  =\sum_{z:\,0<t-t_z<h}J_z
         \int_{(0,h)}k_j(t-t_z,s)\,d\mu_{z,\lambda}(s).
\end{equation}
An inward comparison pays positive births below the source roof as
well as negative deaths above it. Their signed contributions are added
with the physical weights and common-roof translations before a
positive part is taken. Fixed-word bounded variation is not an ordered
bound for that sum.

\begin{leadtheorem}[The complete selected source with a reserve]
\label{thm:intro-v71-reserve}
Suppose $\mathsf X_\lambda(t)\le K\mathsf Y_\lambda(t)$ for
almost every roof, uniformly in $R,\lambda$ at all sufficiently
large counts. Then the entire selected-chart source, including its
old angular-loss component, has ordered height at most $C'K\chi^6$.
In particular a nonpositive signed current in
\eqref{eq:intro-v71-current} suffices with $K=1$. Individual
profiles may have arbitrarily many births and deaths; no wordwise
variation bound or individual no-loss condition is required.
\end{leadtheorem}
\begin{proof}
The residual in Theorem~\ref{thm:intro-v71-recovery} is zero.
Apply the same ordered estimate to the complete selected source;
see Corollary~\ref{cor:v71-reserve-class}.
\end{proof}

This condition is not asserted for every Lorentz record. It identifies
a larger complete-source class than a chartwise no-loss condition,
while retaining the original model and endpoint. For general records
write $\mathcal N_\chi(K)$ for the ordered essential height of
$(\mathsf X-K\mathsf Y)_+$. The legal fixed-band choices
\[
 \varepsilon(B)=A_0B^{-1/12},\qquad
                   \chi(B)=\sqrt{\varepsilon(B)}
\]
give the complete estimate
\begin{equation}\label{eq:intro-v71-budget}
 \begin{split}
 \mathcal E_M\le{}&C_MB^{-1/192}+C'K(B)B^{-1/4}
                    +C''\mathcal N_{\chi(B)}(K(B))\\
                 &+\mathcal H(b^{\varepsilon(B),\mathrm{inc}})
                    +\mathcal H(e_{70}).
 \end{split}
\end{equation}
The ordered vanishing of the last three heights remains to be proved
on the unrestricted physical family. Neither source mass, finite-word
perimeter nor a collision-count-dependent band is substituted for
these estimates. In particular the unrestricted same-roof and
essential-likelihood conclusions are not inferred from the recovery
construction alone.

The principal new argument is contained in
Sections~\ref{sec:v71-pooled-collar}--\ref{sec:v71-two-sided-current}.
Its quantitative input is the unchanged physical comparison collar
of Theorem~\ref{thm:v69-comparison-collar}; its geometric current
input is Theorem~\ref{thm:v70-physical-current}. The complete
preceding mathematical development follows, and the preceding opening
is retained in Appendix~\ref{app:v71-retained-front}.
