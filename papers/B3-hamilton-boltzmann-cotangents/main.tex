\begin{abstract}
On a perturbative Maxwellian phase chart we prove a nonautonomous kinetic energy estimate and construct the joint density--contact Gaussian field directly from microscopic marked cumulants.  The collision noise acts on the exact dual coefficient $\Delta p+\psi$, including direct contact variance.  The covariance kernel, Cameron--Martin domain, second epi-derivative and stopping-time process tightness are then identified without omitting the contact coordinate.
\end{abstract}
