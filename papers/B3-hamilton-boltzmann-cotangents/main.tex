\begin{abstract}
We prove a finite-time hypocoercive observability estimate and closed
range for the linearized balance operator.  Exact localized finite-volume
cumulants then yield a tight joint density--contact Gaussian process on a
nuclear dual space.  The collision random measure uses control $qA_f$ and
integrand $\Delta p+\psi$, so the biased intensity is counted once.  An
independent exactly balanced quadratic recovery establishes Mosco convergence
and twice epi-differentiability of the action on the Cameron--Martin domain
$\operatorname{Ran}\Sigma^{1/2}$.
\end{abstract}
