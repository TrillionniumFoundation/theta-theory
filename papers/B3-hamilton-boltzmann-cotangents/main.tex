\begin{abstract}
On a globally Maxwellian-comparable regular Boltzmann path we prove a
transport--collision observability estimate and closed balance range modulo
the explicit collision invariants.  The full collision-history hierarchy
supplies conditional stopping-time cumulant bounds and a tight joint
Gaussian density--contact process.  For the action Hessian we use the normal
defect $h=\delta\Gamma-D A_f[u]$.  The collision quadratic form is
$\frac12\int h^2/A_f$, hence vanishes along the zero-cost manifold, and its
closed-range inverse is the Gaussian covariance.
\end{abstract}
