\section{Round-twenty-nine reconstruction: full contact-cycle geometry and revealment process limits}
\label{sec:r29-b3}

The joint density/contact theory must not quotient away a nonzero contact
cycle merely because its gain-minus-loss marginal vanishes.  The present
paper keeps the full contact Hilbert space and decomposes it orthogonally into
normal and cycle directions.  The quotient appears only after an explicit
contraction to density alone.  Variable coefficients are treated as a global
small perturbation of the Maxwellian graph estimate, avoiding the false
$O(\ell)$ localization commutator.

\subsection{Normal and cycle contact defects}

Fix a smooth positive near-Maxwellian Boltzmann path $f$ and set
\[
 A_f=\tfrac12ff_*B\,dt\,dx\,dv\,dv_*\,d\omega.
\]
A contact tangent is represented by a density
$h=d(\dot\Gamma-DA_f[u])/dA_f$ in
\[
 \cH_c=L^2(A_f),\qquad \norm h_c^2=\int h^2\,dA_f.        \tag{B3.1}
\]
Let $\mathcal C:\cH_c\to\cY$ be the closed gain-minus-loss map.  Put
\[
 \mathcal K=\ker\mathcal C,\qquad
 h=h_{\rm n}+h_{\rm cyc},
 \quad h_{\rm n}\in\mathcal K^\perp,
 \quad h_{\rm cyc}\in\mathcal K.                         \tag{B3.2}
\]
The linearized balance operator is
\[
 \mathfrak B(u,h)=
 \bigl(\partial_tu+v\cdot\nabla_xu-DQ_f[u]-\mathcal Ch_{\rm n},\ u(0)\bigr).
                                                                    \tag{B3.3}
\]
Although $h_{\rm cyc}$ does not enter the first component, it remains part of
the state and of the action.

\begin{lemma}[Full contact cost and density contraction]
\label{lem:r29-b3-split}
The collision Hessian of the joint density/contact action is
\[
 {1\over2}\norm h_c^2
 ={1\over2}\norm{h_{\rm n}}_c^2+{1\over2}\norm{h_{\rm cyc}}_c^2.        \tag{B3.4}
\]
A nonzero $h_{\rm cyc}$ has strictly positive cost and is detected by contact
tests.  Only after contracting to the density tangent does one obtain
\[
 \inf_{\mathcal Ch=r}{1\over2}\norm h_c^2
 ={1\over2}\norm{\mathcal C^\dagger r}_c^2,              \tag{B3.5}
\]
where $\mathcal C^\dagger r\in\mathcal K^\perp$ is the minimum-norm solution.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Equation (B3.4) is Pythagoras in the closed orthogonal decomposition (B3.2).
The relative-entropy expansion of $(1+\eta h)A_f$ is
$\eta^2\int h^2dA_f/2+o(\eta^2)$ and therefore charges both summands.  The
constraint $\mathcal Ch=r$ fixes only the normal component; minimizing over
the free cycle component sets it to zero and gives (B3.5).
\end{proof}

\subsection{A global variable-coefficient graph estimate}

Let $M$ be a Maxwellian and suppose
\[
 \sup_{t\le T}\norm{f(t)/M-1}_{W^{3,\infty}_{x,v}(M^{-1/2})}
 +\int_0^T\norm{\partial_tf(t)}_{W^{1,\infty}}dt
 \le\epsilon_* .                                         \tag{B3.6}
\]
Let $\Pi$ project onto the five collision invariants.  For the frozen
Maxwellian adjoint operator $\mathcal A_M^*$, Fourier transform in $x$ and the
standard Kawashima multiplier give
\[
 \norm p_{\cX}^2\le C
 \left(\norm{-\partial_tp-v\cdot\nabla_xp-\mathcal L_M^*p}_{\cY^*}^2
       +\int A_M(\Delta p)^2\right),                       \tag{B3.7}
\]
with terminal condition $p(T)=0$ and the global invariant gauge.

\begin{theorem}[Global near-Maxwellian observability]
\label{thm:r29-b3-observe}
For $\epsilon_*$ sufficiently small, every adjoint test satisfies
\[
 \norm p_{\cX}^2\le C
 \left(\norm{-\partial_tp-v\cdot\nabla_xp-\mathcal L_f^*p}_{\cY^*}^2
       +\int A_f(\Delta p)^2\right).                       \tag{B3.8}
\]
Consequently $\mathfrak B$ has closed range on the normal component and a
bounded right inverse
\[
 \mathcal R_{\rm n}:\operatorname{Ran}\mathfrak B
 \longrightarrow\cX_u\oplus\mathcal K^\perp.             \tag{B3.9}
\]
The cycle component is carried independently in $\mathcal K$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The difference
$\mathcal L_f^*-\mathcal L_M^*$ is a bounded operator from $\cX$ to
$\cY^*$ with norm $C\epsilon_*$, and the forms $A_f$ and $A_M$ are uniformly
equivalent.  Insert
$\mathcal A_M^*p=(\mathcal A_f^*p)+
(\mathcal A_M^*-\mathcal A_f^*)p$ into (B3.7), use
$2ab\le\delta a^2+\delta^{-1}b^2$, and absorb the perturbation for small
$\epsilon_*$.  Time dependence is handled by the integrable coefficient term
in (B3.6) and Gronwall.  No spatial partition is introduced, so no
$1/\ell$ commutator occurs.  The Hilbert closed-range theorem gives (B3.9).
\end{proof}

The full adjoint, pairing the output $(r,r_0)$ with (B3.3), is
\[
 \mathfrak B^*(p,p_0)=
 \left(-\partial_tp-v\cdot\nabla_xp-\mathcal L_f^*p,
       -\Delta p,\ p_0-p(0)\right),\qquad p(T)=0,           \tag{B3.10}
\]
where the contact source $-\Delta p$ lies in $\mathcal K^\perp$; an arbitrary
cycle test is an additional independent contact cotangent.

\subsection{Stopped cluster revealment}

The deterministic microscopic future is never claimed to regenerate after
conditioning on a complete phase point.  Instead, label the initial roots and
reveal them in deterministic order.  For a bounded path observable $F$, let
\[
 M_j=E[F\mid\mathcal R_j],\qquad
 \mathcal R_j=\sigma(\hbox{first }j\hbox{ root variables}).              \tag{B3.11}
\]
Then $(M_j)$ is a Doob revealment martingale.  Changing root $j$ can affect
$F$ only through the collision cluster containing that root.  Let
$C_j(I)$ be the number of vertices of that cluster intersecting a time
interval $I$.

The B2 factorial kernel estimate implies, uniformly in $j$,
\[
 E\exp\{\eta C_j(I)\}\le1+C|I|+C\mu_\varepsilon^{-1}.      \tag{B3.12}
\]

\begin{lemma}[Stopped revealment bracket]
\label{lem:r29-b3-reveal}
Let $Z^\varepsilon$ be the centred
$\mu_\varepsilon^{-1/2}$ density/contact empirical field.  For every stopping
time $\tau_\varepsilon$ of its natural empirical/history filtration and
$0\le h\le\delta$,
\[
 E\left[\norm{Z^\varepsilon_{\tau_\varepsilon+h}
                  -Z^\varepsilon_{\tau_\varepsilon}}_{\mathcal S_{-m}}^2
\right]
 \le C(\delta+\mu_\varepsilon^{-1}).                       \tag{B3.13}
\]
For scalar tests, conditional cumulants of order $r\ge3$ are bounded by
$C_r\mu_\varepsilon^{1-r/2}(
\delta+\mu_\varepsilon^{-1})$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Apply the Efron--Stein identity to the revealment martingale (B3.11).  The
squared influence of one root on an increment supported in
$[\tau_\varepsilon,\tau_\varepsilon+h]$ is bounded by
$C\mu_\varepsilon^{-1}C_j(I)^2$ times the test norm.  Summing roots and using
(B3.12) gives the scalar second-moment estimate, even when the left endpoint
is a stopping time because the interval indicator is predictable in the
empirical filtration.  Connected revealment diagrams carrying $r$ marked
influences have one cluster meeting $I$ and give the stated cumulant scale.
This argument has no conditional-density denominator and makes no stochastic
restart assertion.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A concrete nuclear path space}

Let $e_{k,n,\ell}$ be Fourier--Hermite--spherical modes for density and contact
tests.  Define
\[
 \norm\phi_{\mathcal S_m}^2
 =\sum_{k,n,\ell}(1+|k|+|n|+|\ell|)^{2m}
       |\phi_{k,n,\ell}|^2.                               \tag{B3.14}
\]
If $m_{j+1}-m_j>D/2+2$, where $D$ is the total polynomial mode dimension, the
embedding $\mathcal S_{m_{j+1}}\hookrightarrow\mathcal S_{m_j}$ is
Hilbert--Schmidt because
\[
 \sum_{k,n,\ell}(1+|k|+|n|+|\ell|)^{-2(m_{j+1}-m_j)}<\infty.             \tag{B3.15}
\]
Thus
$\mathcal S=\cap_j\mathcal S_{m_j}\subset\cH\subset\mathcal S'$ is a
nuclear Gelfand triple.

\begin{theorem}[Joint density/contact process CLT]
\label{thm:r29-b3-clt}
The fields $Z^\varepsilon$ converge in
$D([0,T];\mathcal S')$ to the unique continuous Gaussian solution of the
linearized balance martingale problem
\[
 d\ip{Z_t}{(\varphi,\psi)}
 =\ip{Z_t}{\mathcal A_t^*(\varphi,\psi)}dt+dM_t(\varphi,\psi),           \tag{B3.16}
\]
with covariance
\[
 d\langle M(\varphi,\psi),M(\tilde\varphi,\tilde\psi)\rangle_t
 =\int A_{f_t}
 [\Delta\varphi+\psi][\Delta\tilde\varphi+\tilde\psi] .  \tag{B3.17}
\]
In particular, contact-cycle tests remain present in (B3.17).  Exact-number
preparation replaces only the initial covariance by the B1 Schur complement.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
B2 pressure derivatives give finite-dimensional second cumulants and make
higher cumulants vanish.  Lemma~\ref{lem:r29-b3-reveal}, summed through the
Hilbert--Schmidt embedding (B3.15), gives Aldous tightness and compact
containment.  The maximum microscopic jump has
$\mathcal S_{-m}$ norm $O(\mu_\varepsilon^{-1/2})$ times a factorially tight
cluster size, so every limit is continuous.  The exact Green identity
identifies (B3.16)--(B3.17).  If two solutions have the same martingale noise,
their difference pairs to zero with every adjoint solution; Theorem
\ref{thm:r29-b3-observe} implies uniqueness.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The full second epi-derivative}

At the zero-cost path $(f,A_f)$ use the positive chart
\[
 f_\eta=f\exp\{\eta u/f-\psi_\eta\},\qquad
 \Gamma_\eta=A_{f_\eta}
 \exp\{\eta(h_{\rm n}+h_{\rm cyc})+r_\eta\}.              \tag{B3.18}
\]
The order-$\eta$ balance uses only $h_{\rm n}$.  The order-$\eta^2$ residual
is corrected with the normal right inverse (B3.9); the prescribed cycle
component is not changed.

\begin{theorem}[Full-contact Mosco Hessian]
\label{thm:r29-b3-mosco}
On the Hilbert tangent space of exactly balanced density/contact pairs,
\[
 d_e^2I[u,h]
 ={1\over2}\norm{u_0}_{\Sigma_0^{-1}}^2
  +{1\over2}\norm{h_{\rm n}}_c^2
  +{1\over2}\norm{h_{\rm cyc}}_c^2,                      \tag{B3.19}
\]
and the rescaled actions Mosco-converge to this closed form.  Its inverse is
the joint covariance in Theorem~\ref{thm:r29-b3-clt}.  Contracting (B3.19)
over contact cycles and normal representatives gives the density-only
quotient form (B3.5), and only then.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The entropy Taylor expansion in (B3.18) gives the full square in (B3.19).
Bounded rescaled action yields weak compactness in $L^2(A_f)$, and the exact
balance equation identifies the normal constraint; weak lower
semicontinuity gives the liminf.  For the recovery sequence, truncate $u$ and
both contact components, use (B3.9) to correct only the normal second-order
residual, and add a vanishing positive background before exponentiation.
The cycle component remains the specified physical representative and stays
positive in the exponential chart.  The Gaussian Cameron--Martin form
associated with (B3.17) is exactly (B3.19).
\end{proof}

The revised graph theory therefore preserves every observable positive-cost
contact direction, avoids the localization-scale error, derives stopping-time
tightness from root revealment, and matches the joint Hessian to the joint
Gaussian field.
