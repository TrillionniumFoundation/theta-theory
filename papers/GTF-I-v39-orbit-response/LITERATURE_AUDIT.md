# Theorem-level literature and source audit — v39

The new claim is an intrinsic minimum-orbit/constituent-maximum numerical width law under a full group gap. Neither static orbit dimension, the classical covariance identity, stochastic positive embedding, nor external compact-group expansion is claimed new. The list below distinguishes inspected full material and unresolved original-source access.
