# Literature and provenance audit — v47

This is a targeted source comparison, not exhaustive independent priority clearance.

The current new proof is self-contained. The affine coordinate of a two-state stochastic matrix, removal of odd-target offsets, parity linear algebra and a Lipschitz comparison of minimax distances are elementary ingredients. They are not advertised as newly invented tools. The claim is the exact bounded stochastic optimization reduction and the resulting numerical profile statements in the explicitly defined task.

## Primary sources revisited

- Benvenuti–Farina, *A Tutorial on the Positive Realization Problem* (2004): author-hosted full preprint, Theorems 2–3 and adjacent invariant-cone discussion. Source: https://sites.math.rutgers.edu/~sussmann/papers/res-farina-tutorial-positive-realization.pdf . Positive cones and shift-invariance are classical; the article uses finite normalized prefix/suffix constraints, not a first positive-realization principle.
- Balle–Panangaden–Precup, arXiv:1501.06841v3: full parsed text and theorem pages inspected. Theorem 2 identifies rational weighted series with finite Hankel rank; Theorem 11 concerns approximation in its stated weighted-automata norm and hypotheses. Ordinary signed dimension and such approximation are not automatically the bounded stochastic, finite worst-word feasible set. https://arxiv.org/pdf/1501.06841v3
- Ohta, arXiv:2008.11487v1: parsed paper and Theorem 6 page inspected. Its reachable/null-space tensor reductions are for stationary HMM observation laws; this does not make arbitrary externally commanded two-label numerical feasibility a tensor-rank problem without stochastic bounds. https://arxiv.org/pdf/2008.11487v1
- Tzeng (1992), DOI 10.1137/0221017: publisher record and abstract inspected. The complete original proof was not acquired in this session. Polynomial equivalence of supplied probabilistic automata is explicitly classical; checking a candidate is not minimizing stochastic order. https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/0221017

The prior bibliography and realization comparison are retained, including the separate categorical Shitov reduction, real-algebraic effectiveness, DGLPS arithmetic overlap, Bugeaud–Laurent exponent conventions and quantum/cutpoint distinctions in the archived predecessor line.
