## 2. First variation and the monotone angular image

    F' = p_0 p_0'/(c_0 V_−) + p_m p_m'/(c_m V_+).

The backward term is positive in this expression. If the path is split at the foot, the endpoint variations at that foot are v·dq_f and −v·dq_f and cancel exactly. No artificial event changes the first variation.

    XX'/K_X + YY'/K_Y = 0,    K_X,K_Y>0,

    (−Y/X)' = (−Y'X+YX')/X²
