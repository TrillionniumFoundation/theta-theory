\section{Collision-normalized payment of the original section-critical fans}
\label{sec:scf-section-critical}

The regular section-side budget in Section~\ref{sec:cs-canonical}
does not include the incoming critical fan at its puncture
$p_0=p_m=0$. We pay that fan here on every original section side
whose finite physical trajectory is regular. All zero-clearance
critical germs, including simultaneous section contacts and collapsed
physical slices, are first assigned to the retained zero-clearance
theorem below. Together these estimates give a complete boundary
critical-fan payment at the original collision normalization.
The zero-clearance critical-fan payment of Section~\ref{pc:section}
is retained and is combined with the new result only after a one-copy
assignment. Its proof is not claimed as new.

Throughout $R\in[9/20,47/100]$ and $m\ge1$ is the true collision
count. A critical germ means the auxiliary strict visited word
with positive visited incidences, normal endpoints, and its actual
physical-side sectors. No reflection is inserted at an unvisited
tangency. The original section, both endpoint membership tests and
every exact label remain in those sectors. Distinct descriptions of
the same physical germ are united and disjointized. Write
$\beta_{\ell,y}(x)$ for its normalized angular fraction with exact
label $\ell$; their disjointness gives
$\sum_\ell\beta_{\ell,y}(x)\le1$.

Retain precisely the canonical-source constants of
Section~\ref{sec:cs-canonical}, with its qualified enlarged curve
class $\mathcal W_R^u(\widehat B_{\rm cv})$ from
\eqref{eq:mc-class} and that class's own
$\widehat k_0,\widehat\ell_*,\widehat C_g$, and put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:scf-constants}
 \sigma=47/59,\quad E=E_{\rm cs}=47/6,\quad C_0=53/12,
 \quad H_h=H_{\rm hor},\quad H_v=H_{\rm ver},
 \quad C_J=\frac{170}{18c_*}.
\end{equation}
The branch-supremum input below is exactly
Theorem~\ref{thm:cs-half-sup}; it uses the native physical
curve-growth theorem for that fixed class. A pointwise endpoint
kernel alone would not suffice. No physical-word pressure or
normalized comparison-pool abundance is used as a premise.
Lemma~\ref{lem:mc-hessian} gives the exact critical identity
\begin{equation}\label{eq:scf-critical-optical}
 \mathfrak J(x)=\frac{\sqrt{\det G_x}}{2R^2c_*}\,u_x
       \le C_Ju_x,\qquad
 u_x=|\partial_{\alpha_0}p_m(x)|^{-1}.
\end{equation}
At an unvisited boundary the last expression denotes the limiting
strict-word optical weight. It is not a derivative assigned to a
singular physical collision map. The bound uses
$\sqrt{\det G_x}/R^2\le170/9$, including $m=1$.

\subsection{The exact physical side level and normal endpoint separation}

At an interior index $1\le i\le m-1$ fix one original section
side, either $p_i=b$ or $\alpha_i=a$, with its original finite
side interval. In the first case use source parameter
$v=\alpha_i$; in the second use
$v=\varphi_i=\arcsin p_i$, in a local lift of $\alpha_i$.
Translating the actual disk at index $i$ to the origin only names
the same canonical source.

\begin{lemma}[Physical representatives at the original section side]
\label{lem:scf-representatives}
Consider a critical germ on this side whose visited incidences
are positive and whose unvisited clearances are all strictly positive.
It is a regular point of the true finite collision map, even if
several original section tests change there. It is the limit, on
the exact original canonical source, of actual regular points off
the cuts and exceptional sets of both one-sided partitions in
Theorem~\ref{thm:cs-half-sup}.

Distinct one-copy germs on this source have distinct source
parameters. For every finite family, its representatives can be
chosen simultaneously with both endpoint momenta tending to zero.
A single source seam can be fixed away from that family's
parameters before making the partitions.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
At fixed $R,m$ only finitely many disks can intersect the finitely
many flight neighborhoods, by the physical finite-horizon bound.
Positive visited incidences and strictly positive unvisited
clearances therefore give an open neighborhood with exactly the
same true collision word. The physical finite map is smooth and
invertible there. Its section membership tests may change, but
those tests do not change the collision map. No section test is
removed from the coefficient being estimated.

The point at index $i$ has an open neighborhood in the collision
coordinates $(\alpha_i,p_i)$. Its intersection with the prescribed
canonical source contains a nonempty open source interval through
the germ. On a vertical source $\varphi_i=\arcsin p_i$ is smooth
since the visited incidence is positive. The two exceptional sets
of Theorem~\ref{thm:cs-half-sup} have zero source length and cannot
exhaust any such interval. Choose nearby points outside their union
converging to the germ. They are actual physical points, with the
same finite word and with both endpoint momenta tending to zero.
They need not retain a chosen section-side label: the complete
canonical-source bound includes all labels, and is used only to
upper-bound the coefficient of the original labelled germ.

The source parameter at the exact prescribed level determines the
whole normalized collision state at index $i$. In either time
direction the next actual positive-incidence reflection is unique.
Iterating fixes the entire finite physical word; recentering its
starting disk in the original fundamental cell is unique as well.
Two germs with the same source state are therefore one physical
germ, whose section sectors have already been united. This proves
distinctness without a lattice or word multiplicity. A finite
subset of a circular source admits a seam outside that subset,
and the canonical-source constants allow any one such seam.
Choose representatives for each member of a finite family in its
own neighborhood, all converging at a common sequence parameter.
No lower bound for these neighborhood sizes uniform in $m$ is used.
\end{proof}

Let $A_-^h,A_+^h$ denote the absolute derivatives of $p_0,p_m$
on a horizontal source and $A_-^v,A_+^v$ the corresponding
derivatives on a vertical source, continued to a limiting strict word.

\begin{lemma}[Both half sums at normal endpoint germs]
\label{lem:scf-halfsums}
For one interior index and one original section side, for the
regular physical germs of Lemma~\ref{lem:scf-representatives}, one has
\begin{equation}\label{eq:scf-halfsums}
 \sum_x(A_-^h(x))^{-1},\quad\sum_x(A_+^h(x))^{-1}\le H_h,
 \qquad
 \sum_x(A_-^v(x))^{-1},\quad\sum_x(A_+^v(x))^{-1}\le H_v,
\end{equation}
using the appropriate pair for that side.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Take a finite family of distinct merged germs and representatives
on the exact original source level. Their source parameters have a positive
minimum separation, whereas both endpoint momenta tend to zero.
If two representatives belonged to the same backward partition
interval, monotonicity and Theorem~\ref{thm:cs-half-sup} would give
\[
 |p_0(x(r))-p_0(\widetilde x(r))|
 \ge E^{-1}\sigma^{-(i-1)}
                    |v(x(r))-v(\widetilde x(r))|.
\]
The left side tends to zero and the right side stays positive.
This is impossible for sufficiently small $r$. The forward
partition gives the same contradiction with $p_m$ and exponent
$m-i-1$. These statements apply with $v=\alpha_i$ on a horizontal
source and $v=\varphi_i$ on a vertical source.

The representatives therefore occupy distinct intervals in each
partition. Sum their inverse derivative values using the actual
interval-supremum bounds $H_h$ or $H_v$. The finite strict-word
optical derivatives depend continuously on the representatives,
because all visited incidences at the limiting germ are positive.
Pass $r\downarrow0$ in the finite sum, then exhaust finite
subfamilies. This proves the full nonnegative sums.
All local limits occur at fixed $R,m$ before exhaustion;
uniformity follows from the same final constants.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Every original side and every collision index}

Let $\mathcal K^{\rm sec}_{m,R}$ be the one-copy family of
critical germs with positive visited incidences, strictly positive
unvisited clearances and positive physical angular fraction that
meet an original section side at some index $0\le i\le m$.
Include corners and simultaneous section contacts. All unvisited
zero-clearance germs are assigned first to Theorem~\ref{pc:fan},
including those with simultaneous section contacts. Occupation retains its
original values on the incident sectors; at index $m$ there is
only a terminal membership test. Define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:scf-Bsec}
 B_{\rm sec}(m)=10C_JE\left[
 (C_0H_h+\tfrac12H_v)(m-1)\sigma^{(m-2)/2}
                   +2\sigma^{m-1}\right],
\end{equation}
where the first summand is zero when $m=1$.

\begin{theorem}[All regular physical section-critical fans]
\label{thm:scf-section-payment}
For every radius and true collision count,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:scf-section-payment}
 \sum_{x\in\mathcal K^{\rm sec}_{m,R}}\sum_\ell
       \mathfrak J(x)\beta_{\ell,y}(x)\le B_{\rm sec}(m).
\end{equation}
Original exact-label and critical-roof-value restrictions, and
further coefficient weights between zero and one, may be retained.
For every fixed $q\ge0$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:scf-normalized}
 \sup_R m^q\sum_{x\in\mathcal K^{\rm sec}_{m,R}}\sum_\ell
       \mathfrak J(x)\beta_{\ell,y}(x)\longrightarrow0.
\end{equation}
In particular this pays the full regular physical section-critical coefficient
at the original $m^2$ normalization, including all shallow indices.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For one horizontal side at an interior index $i$, the exact
two-sided determinant gives
\[
 u_x\le C_0\sqrt{1-b^2}(A_-^h(x))^{-1}(A_+^h(x))^{-1}.
\]
The individual inverse derivatives are at most $E\sigma^{i-1}$
and $E\sigma^{m-i-1}$. Bound either factor individually and sum
the other using Lemma~\ref{lem:scf-halfsums}. Taking the smaller
of the two results gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:scf-h-weight}
 \sum_xu_x\le C_0\sqrt{1-b^2}H_hE
                      \sigma^{\max(i-1,m-i-1)}.
\end{equation}
For a vertical side use
$u_x\le\tfrac12(A_-^v)^{-1}(A_+^v)^{-1}$ to get
\begin{equation}\label{eq:scf-v-weight}
 \sum_xu_x\le\tfrac12H_vE\sigma^{\max(i-1,m-i-1)}.
\end{equation}
These are products of the two optical derivatives of the same
actual record, not products of probabilities. Both physical
section-side sectors belong to the same regular germ and create
no extra copy. The horizontal incidence factor remains
$\sqrt{1-b^2}$ until the positive
upper bound $\sqrt{1-b^2}\le1$.

The five original rectangles have ten horizontal and ten vertical
side occurrences. Repeated levels or coincident pieces can only
reduce the one-copy source. The moving centers do not affect the
canonical-source constants, which are uniform in the source level.
Thus at each interior index the complete inverse-weight sum is at
most
\[
 10E(C_0H_h+H_v/2)\sigma^{\max(i-1,m-i-1)}.
\]
Since $\max(i-1,m-i-1)\ge(m-2)/2$, summing the $m-1$ indices
and multiplying by $C_J$ gives the first term in
\eqref{eq:scf-Bsec}.

For the endpoint indices, a critical roof has $p_0=p_m=0$.
Every horizontal side of the original section has a nonzero
momentum level. The levels are $\pm h_0$, $-1/2\pm h_0$,
$\sin\theta_R\pm h_0$, and $\pm3/20$, with $h_0=1/200$.
Their absolute values are at least $h_0$, since
\[
 \sin\theta_R-h_0\ge
 \frac{2(49/100)}{1+(49/100)^2}-\frac1{200}>\frac1{200}.
\]
Hence no normal critical germ lies on an endpoint horizontal side.
On an initial vertical side $\alpha_0=a$, normality fixes the
initial collision state completely. Strict-hit uniqueness leaves
at most one positive-visited strict germ. The horizontal-source
derivative at $p_0=0$, or its continuous strict-word limit,
satisfies $u_x\le E\sigma^{m-1}$.
At a terminal vertical side reverse the same actual finite record
and recenter its final disk. Collision-area preservation gives the
same inverse weight in the reversed direction. The original section
tests are transported, not assumed invariant under reversal.
There are at most twenty vertical endpoint-side occurrences in
total, giving the second term $20C_JE\sigma^{m-1}$.

At each germ the exact-label sector fractions have sum at most one.
Use \eqref{eq:scf-critical-optical} in all the preceding positive
sums. A germ meeting several sides or indices is assigned to the
first in a fixed ordering; summing all the side estimates is an
upper bound for this one-copy source. This proves
\eqref{eq:scf-section-payment} without a word or label count.
The right side is uniform in $R$, labels and roof values, and is
a polynomial times a strictly decaying exponential.
This proves \eqref{eq:scf-normalized}. No width depends on $m$,
no side-distance condition has been inserted into the guard, and
no original outer limit has been exchanged with the collision limit.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The complete physical-boundary critical stratum}

Let $\mathcal K^\partial_{m,R}$ contain the critical germs on a
genuine unvisited-disk boundary or an original section side.
Include visited-grazing limits with their separately proved zero
critical coarea trace. Artificial homogeneity cuts and coordinate
seams are not additional physical boundaries.

\begin{corollary}[All physical-boundary critical fans]
\label{cor:scf-boundary-payment}
Put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:scf-Bboundary}
 B_\partial(m)=B_{\rm sec}(m)+C_{\rm pc}m\sigma^{(m-1)/2},
\end{equation}
where $C_{\rm pc}$ is the inherited constant in Theorem~\ref{pc:fan}.
The complete positive coefficient of $\mathcal K^\partial_{m,R}$,
summed over every original exact record and critical roof, is at
most $B_\partial(m)$.

For $w=1$ or the original $W^\varepsilon$ and the smooth tests of
Theorem~\ref{thm:wo-bv}, let $b_\ell(t)$ be this once-only part
of the signed atomic coefficient, grouped at the same physical
roof and exact label. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:scf-tested-boundary}
 \sum_{\ell,t}|b_\ell(t)|\le a_0b_0 B_\partial(m).
\end{equation}
Every fixed power of $m$ times this bound tends to zero uniformly
in $R$ at every fixed original outer guard stage.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Assign visited-grazing limits first. Their actual one-sided
coarea trace is zero by Lemma~\ref{lem:rbv-endpoints};
collision-area nullness is not used in place of that theorem.
For the remaining positive-visited germs, assign every germ with
an unvisited zero-clearance witness to its first witness in a
fixed ordering of true flight indices, lattice centers and impact
signs. Theorem~\ref{pc:fan}, summed over indices, bounds this
once-only source by $C_{\rm pc}m\sigma^{(m-1)/2}$.
Assign every remaining boundary germ to its first original
section side and apply Theorem~\ref{thm:scf-section-payment}.
This covers simultaneous unvisited/section corners once.
The endpoint cap has no critical boundary because its momentum
levels are nonzero.

At a germ the exact-label sector fractions sum to at most one.
The original guard and its strict-word trace are between zero
and one. The smooth test multiplier has modulus at most $a_0b_0$.
Combine terms at the same exact label and physical roof with
their true signs before applying the triangle inequality.
This gives \eqref{eq:scf-tested-boundary}, with every roof
coincidence retained. Exponential decay proves the last
assertion. The conditioning factor has already been included
once in $C_J$ and $C_{\rm pc}$.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{What this payment leaves.}
All positive-visited incoming critical coefficients on a genuine
physical or original section boundary are now paid at the original
collision normalization. Strictly interior critical germs and the
complete absolutely continuous roof derivative remain.
Regular constant-roof boundary arcs are not the critical source
estimated here and retain their exact signed coefficients.
The original normalized low-load comparison shortage and the
alternative complete local derivative condition are unchanged.
The full raw theorem and its four-coordinate inverse are not
concluded from this boundary payment.
