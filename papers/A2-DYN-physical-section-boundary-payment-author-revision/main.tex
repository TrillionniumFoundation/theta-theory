\begin{abstract}
We study the original triangular Lorentz gas for
$R\in[9/20,47/100]$, retaining every physical collision, the
unit-speed clock, the original section law and the exact return,
displacement and collision records. We continue the complete
native proof chain through its physical roof derivative and
marked jump spectrum. A simultaneous normal-endpoint matching
argument gives an exponentially decaying, complete sum of
critical incoming coefficients on the original section sides,
including all shallow collision indices. Combined once with the
inherited zero-clearance theorem, it pays the entire physical
boundary critical stratum at the original collision normalization.
For regular endpoint section sides, the physical mixed optical
derivative gives an all-count exponential bound for their
constant-roof coefficients. More strongly, an explicit uniform
second-derivative calculation proves strict convexity of every
horizontal endpoint restriction from collision count $114$;
vertical restrictions are strictly convex at every count.
Consequently these regular endpoint sides then create no
constant-roof atoms. On every original interior section side, a
two-half stationary-roof balance and a truncated optical overlay
give a complete exponential payment of its regular constant-roof
charge. The same genuine endpoint balance on the marked-flight
source pays every regular unvisited-boundary constant-roof term,
using its directly constructed zero-clearance trace. Thus all
regular physical boundary atoms, as well as all boundary critical
coefficients, are paid at every fixed polynomial normalization.
An exact primitive estimate transfers the
paid coefficients to the unchanged raw density and every
positive microscopic roof-window width, with an explicit error
for conditioning on an actual event of stated probability.
All inherited physical, arithmetic, spectral, pressure and
inverse results retain their full proofs and actual hypotheses.
The remaining normalized shallow positive-source shortage,
or the alternative complete local derivative-charge estimate,
is still unproved. The full raw pointwise local limit theorem
and its four-coordinate inverse consequence remain the objective.
\end{abstract}
