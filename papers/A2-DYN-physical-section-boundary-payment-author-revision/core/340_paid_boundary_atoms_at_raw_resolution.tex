\section{The paid physical boundary atoms at the original raw resolution}
\label{sec:pa-raw}

We record the exact consequence of the preceding geometric
theorems for the original density and its microscopic intervals.
The argument applies to specified contributions of the physical
derivative; it does not remove a collision or change an event.
The elementary integration step is not claimed as an independent
new principle. Its input is the newly paid complete physical
boundary atomic source, with the original normalization and all labels.

Let $f_\ell=f^{w;a,b,\Psi}_{\ell,m,R}$ be the unchanged densities
of Theorem~\ref{thm:sbv-physical}. Let $b_\ell(u)$ be the following
part of their actual atomic coefficient at roof $u$:
first assign every zero-clearance critical germ once to
Theorem~\ref{pc:fan}; next assign the positive-clearance original
section-critical germs by Theorem~\ref{thm:scf-section-payment};
then include the regular endpoint-section interfaces of
Theorem~\ref{thm:es-payment} and the complete interior-section
regular interfaces of Corollary~\ref{cor:iso-all-interior};
finally include the complete regular unvisited interfaces of
Corollary~\ref{cor:umi-signed}. These are contributions with
different physical roles, including their actual signs and tests.
At every stage unite repeated descriptions of a germ or interface.
Combine all selected contributions at the same roof and exact
label before defining $b_\ell(u)$. This definition need not select
the entire atom $a_\ell(u)$ at that roof: selected and unselected
terms can cancel there.

Put
\begin{align}
 B_\partial(m)&=C_{\rm pc}m\sigma^{(m-1)/2}+B_{\rm sec}(m),
                                                    \label{eq:pa-boundary}\\
 B_{\rm ep}(m)&=4N_h A_V F_{\rm cs}C_{\rm mom}
                   \kappa_{\rm es}\sigma^{m-1}
                         \one_{\{m<114\}},\notag\\
 B_m^V&=A_V B_\partial(m)+B_{\rm ep}(m)
                     +2A_V\{B_{\rm iso}(m)+B_{\rm umi}(m)\}.
                                                    \label{eq:pa-budget}
\end{align}
The indicator in the second line uses the exact absence theorem;
the all-count exponential bound without that indicator is valid
as well. The preceding physical theorems give
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pa-all-atoms}
                   \sum_{\ell,u}|b_\ell(u)|\le B_m^V.
\end{equation}
The bound is uniform over the full radius interval. It holds
for $w=1$ or $W^\varepsilon$, with any original positive guard
width, and for the fixed admitted observation norms. It does
not pay a positive-width neighborhood of a critical germ.

\subsection{An exact bounded primitive, with its compensating term}

Choose once a smooth nondecreasing function $\eta$ on $\mathbb R$
equal to zero on $(-\infty,0]$ and one on $[1,\infty)$.
Set $T_m=m\tau_++1$ and $\eta_m(t)=\eta(t-T_m)$.
With the Heaviside function $H$, define the analytical functions
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pa-primitive}
 c_\ell(t)=\sum_u b_\ell(u)\{H(t-u)-\eta_m(t)\},
 \qquad \widetilde f_\ell=f_\ell-c_\ell,
 \qquad \beta_\ell=\sum_u b_\ell(u).
\end{equation}
The transition in $\eta_m$ lies strictly above every possible
physical roof. The functions $c_\ell$ and $\widetilde f_\ell$
are analytical devices; $\widetilde f_\ell$ is not asserted to
be a positive physical measure or the density of another event.
All probability statements below concern $f_\ell$ itself.

\begin{proposition}[Exact payment without a lower roof-window width]
\label{prop:pa-primitive}
For the complete original source one has
\begin{align}
 \sum_\ell\|c_\ell\|_\infty&\le B_m^V,&
 \sum_\ell |Dc_\ell|(\mathbb R)&\le2B_m^V,
                                                    \label{eq:pa-norms}\\
 D\widetilde f_\ell
  &=(d_\ell+\beta_\ell\eta_m')\,dt
             +\sum_u(a_\ell(u)-b_\ell(u))\delta_u.
                                                    \label{eq:pa-derivative}
\end{align}
For every original exact record and every physical roof interval
$I$ of arbitrary length $h>0$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pa-microscopic}
 \frac{m^2}{h}\left|\int_I(f_\ell-\widetilde f_\ell)(t)\,dt\right|
       \le m^2\sum_u|b_\ell(u)|\le m^2B_m^V.
\end{equation}
In particular these errors vanish uniformly in radius, labels,
interval location and every positive microscopic width, after
multiplication by any fixed power of the collision count.
The bound applies in particular to every prescribed positive
$m$-dependent microscopic width, without enlarging that window
or adjusting an outer guard parameter.
For any collection of label-dependent intervals $I_\ell$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pa-summed-intervals}
 \sum_\ell\left|\int_{I_\ell}c_\ell(t)\,dt\right|
       \le \bigl(\sup_\ell |I_\ell|\bigr)B_m^V.
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Every atom lies in $[m\tau_-,m\tau_+]$. Thus
$0\le H(t-u)-\eta_m(t)\le1$ for every $u,t$, apart from
the irrelevant convention at a step point. The difference
vanishes both below all physical roofs and above $T_m+1$.
Hence $c_\ell$ has compact support and its supremum is at
most $\sum_u|b_\ell(u)|$. Its distributional derivative is
\[
 Dc_\ell=\sum_u b_\ell(u)\delta_u-\beta_\ell\eta_m'(t)\,dt.
\]
Since $\int\eta_m'=1$ and $|\beta_\ell|\le\sum_u|b_\ell(u)|$,
this proves both norm bounds and \eqref{eq:pa-derivative}.
The compensating continuous term is required: deleting it
would generally leave a nonzero constant above the physical
roof range and an incorrect total derivative mass.

Integration over any interval costs its actual length times
the supremum. This proves \eqref{eq:pa-microscopic} and,
after the complete nonnegative label sum,
\eqref{eq:pa-summed-intervals}. Equations \eqref{eq:scf-Bsec}, \eqref{pc:fan-bound},
\eqref{eq:es-payment}, \eqref{eq:iso-Bint} and
\eqref{eq:umi-all-flights} show that $B_m^V$ is bounded by a
fixed polynomial times a strictly decreasing exponential.
Thus $m^q B_m^V\to0$ for every fixed $q\ge0$.
All physical summations precede this limit and its uniform
suprema. Arbitrarily small $h$ causes no loss, because the
actual factor $h$ cancels before taking any limit.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[The unchanged raw and local-variation interfaces]
\label{cor:pa-interfaces}
Let $\mathcal L$ be precisely the retained finite-peak arithmetic
reference. Replacing $f_\ell$ by $\widetilde f_\ell$ in its raw
error changes that error by at most $m^2B_m^V$. For any measurable
roof sets $J_\ell$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pa-local-variation}
 \left|\sum_\ell |Df_\ell|(J_\ell)
              -\sum_\ell|D\widetilde f_\ell|(J_\ell)\right|
                         \le2B_m^V.
\end{equation}
If every $J_\ell$ lies below $T_m$, the factor two can be replaced
by one. Therefore these paid contributions create no additional
obligation in the ordered local-charge criterion of
Proposition~\ref{prop:ui-inherited-bridge}. That criterion for
the remaining derivative is still unproved.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The raw comparison follows from the reverse triangle inequality
in the same essential-supremum norm, with exactly the same
reference and central window. For finite complex measures,
$||\mu|(J)-|\nu|(J)|\le|\mu-\nu|(J)$; use
\eqref{eq:pa-norms} and sum. Below $T_m$ the compensating
continuous derivative is absent. The estimate holds at each
fixed outer guard width before the collision limsup.
Its right side tends to zero even after the original $m^2$
normalization. The subsequent outer limit is unchanged.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Conditioning retains its actual denominator}

For conditioning on an original event, set $w=1$ in this subsection;
the denominator also has observation $V=1$. For an original
exact-record roof interval $I$ of length $h$,
let $P=\int_I f^1_\ell(t)\,dt$ be its actual unweighted
probability and $N=\int_I f^V_\ell(t)\,dt$ its weighted
numerator. Both include the original initial law and terminal
test. Suppose $|V|\le A$, and set
$\widetilde P=\int_I\widetilde f^1_\ell$,
$\widetilde N=\int_I\widetilde f^V_\ell$ using the same
physical term assignment. Let $B_m^1$ be \eqref{eq:pa-budget}
with $A_V=1$.

\begin{proposition}[A conditional error with an explicit physical floor]
\label{prop:pa-ratio}
If the actual event satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pa-denominator}
             P\ge c\,h\,m^{-2},\qquad c>m^2B_m^1,
\end{equation}
then $\widetilde P>0$ and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pa-ratio}
 \left|\frac NP-\frac{\widetilde N}{\widetilde P}\right|
              \le\frac{2A m^2B_m^1}{c-m^2B_m^1}.
\end{equation}
The floor is a hypothesis about this actual conditioned event,
with its exact arithmetic label; it is not supplied by the
boundary payment theorem.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Put $\delta=hB_m^1$. Proposition~\ref{prop:pa-primitive} gives
$|P-\widetilde P|\le\delta$. Applying the same positive
geometric budgets directly with the pointwise bound $|V|\le A$
gives $|N-\widetilde N|\le A\delta$; this does not require
identifying $A$ with the possibly larger product $a_0b_0$.
The original positive probability law also gives $|N|\le AP$.
Thus $\widetilde P\ge P-\delta>0$, and
\[
 \left|\frac NP-\frac{\widetilde N}{\widetilde P}\right|
 \le\frac{|N-\widetilde N|}{\widetilde P}
       +\frac{|N||P-\widetilde P|}{P\widetilde P}
 \le\frac{2A\delta}{P-\delta}.
\]
Insert \eqref{eq:pa-denominator}. No second factor $c_*^{-1}$,
new independence assertion or interchange of limits occurs.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{What remains to close.}
The newly paid terms are explicit physical boundary contributions,
not the whole positive source. Critical germs with positive
clearance strictly inside every original section chamber,
nonconstant side densities and the interior integrable
derivative still require a normalized estimate. Positive-width
critical remainders and the original shallow low-load shortage
remain. A continuous thin concentration can have no jumps at all;
paying atoms alone cannot establish the raw theorem. The full
same-roof capacity comparison \eqref{eq:lc-final-shortage}, or
the complete local-charge route with its inherited fixed-window
input, retains its original quantifiers. The four-coordinate
inverse retains its complete Jacobian, arithmetic, denominator
and error hypotheses. These statements neither reduce the
scientific target nor declare its unresolved final step complete.
