## Exact additional statements and limits

`thm:ui-inversion` proves the unchanged interval event equals an absolutely convergent integral over the full three-dimensional torus and real roof frequency, with factor `(2pi)^-4`. Its proof uses the product of the inherited marked variation bound and the exact interval transform. The approximate identity is only an intermediate justification and disappears from the conclusion. No arithmetic-zero label is removed.

For an interval union of length `d` with at most `J` components, the tail is `D/pi` times `d[1+log(2J/(dS))]` when `dS<=2J`, and `2J/S` when `dS>=2J`. The common upper bound is `2JD/(pi S)`. `cor:ui-all-labels` sums individual derivative budgets after torus coefficient extraction; it does not multiply a uniform bound by the number of records. `cor:ui-normalization` displays the actual `m^2/d` cost. `prop:ui-conditional` is an actual selected-event ratio estimate for the specified observations, with positivity certified by `D_S>E_0`; it is not an arbitrary-selector total-variation theorem.

`lem:ui-reference-modulus` explicitly derives the vanishing global roof derivative of the retained reference. Real stationary peak centers give real Gaussian arguments. Compactness and the inherited implication `kappa_j=0 => b_j=0` yield `max_j sup_R |b_j| exp(-m kappa_j)->0`, while the Gaussian derivative contributes `m^-1/2`. The exact majorants are `M_0=(2pi)^-2 a^-2` and `M_1=M_0 sqrt(2/(pi a))`.

`prop:ui-inherited-bridge` credits and uses native 87: for every fixed `h_B>0`, its interval law and the sharp tail pay the scalar comparison `Xi`, and the reference derivative pays `Omega_g`. The sharper direct inequality is `raw_error_m <= Omega_p(m,B)+E_m(h_B)`. Thus `lim_B limsup_m Omega_p(m,B)=0` is a sufficient remaining closing condition on this route, under native 87 at its inherited analytic scope. It is not proved here and is not asserted necessary. A new strong-operator estimate on growing frequency bands is not required for this fixed-analysis-window implication. Nothing permits an arbitrary collision-dependent analysis width or changes the original microscopic conditioning event.

The spike example establishes a limit of inference: unscaled BV and vanishing normalized fixed-window errors do not imply normalized raw essential-supremum control. It is an abstract counterexample to that implication, not an asserted Lorentz-gas trajectory law or a no-go result for the original research problem.
