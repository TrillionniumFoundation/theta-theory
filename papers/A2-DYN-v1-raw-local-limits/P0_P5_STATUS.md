## The concrete P3 repair

For the explicitly constructed inducing set, the raw joint characteristic
function is not globally L1: the component with zero displacement and 2n
physical collisions has a positive density jump at `2n(1-2R)`. Thus the old
candidate's global raw-L1 route cannot simply be imported for this inducing
set. This says nothing adverse about a central local limit, whose window may
be far from that endpoint.

The replacement is exact edge extraction plus residual inversion. The data
and the target density remain raw. The article proves the required subtraction
for the isolated normal edge and gives an exponential bound for each regular
critical word. Controlling the complete collection of edges and singular
boundaries is still required. A single-word bound is not a branch-sum bound.
