# A2-DYN v1 — scoped primary-source comparison

Date: October 4, 2026. This is a scoped comparison, not an exhaustive priority
certificate. General CLTs, local limits, Morse/coarea calculus and abstract
Fourier inversion are not claimed as new.

## Local limits

Szász and Varjú, *Local limit theorem for the Lorentz process and its recurrence
in the plane*, ETDS 24 (2004), 257–278, DOI 10.1017/S0143385703000439: the
publisher record is the bibliographic authority used here. This is a baseline
for displacement local limits, not a citation for our induced four-coordinate,
raw-roof density or its geometric parameter uniformity.

Dolgopyat and Nándori, *On mixing and the local central limit theorem for
hyperbolic flows*, ETDS 40 (2020), 142–174. The full author version was read at
https://arxiv.org/html/1710.08568v1 . Definition 2.2 is tested against continuous
compactly supported functions and includes cohomology corrections and a minimal
group. Section 6.4, Proposition 6.3 applies the framework to finite-horizon
Sinai flows with its own assumptions. These results are essential baselines;
they are not imported as absolute L1 integrability of the particular raw
finite-time characteristic functions in this manuscript. Our exact edge
calculation is consistent with a tested local limit and rules out a stronger
raw L1 assertion for the explicitly chosen inducing set.

## Perturbations and flow mixing

Demers and Zhang, *A functional analytic approach to perturbations of the
Lorentz gas*, CMP 324 (2013), 767–830; https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.1261 . Its
spectral stability framework is relevant to geometric perturbations. A new
common C2 parameter bundle for the unbounded induced records cannot be
inferred just from a spectral-continuity statement. The abstract/publication
record was checked; a complete theorem-by-theorem reconstruction of that
paper was not performed in this execution.

Baladi, Demers and Liverani, *Exponential decay of correlations for finite
horizon Sinai billiard flows*, Inventiones 211 (2018), 39–177;
https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.02836v3 . The author record explicitly includes a
correction note. It supplies the established flow-mixing baseline, not a
free proof of all induced-record density, parameter-derivative, or tail
hypotheses printed here.

## Present increment and remaining comparison

The concrete increment is the actual winding orbit and augmented inducing
records, the exact alternating-edge coefficient, the regular critical-word
classification and its uniform individual coefficient bound, and an exact
residual formulation that corrects the old pipeline's raw-L1 route. The
abstract inversion and downstream estimates are supplied with full proofs,
but no full-billiard realization or universal priority for the combined target
is claimed. A future full LLT must be compared against the above theorems in
its exact observables, group, norm, time parameter and uniformity class.
