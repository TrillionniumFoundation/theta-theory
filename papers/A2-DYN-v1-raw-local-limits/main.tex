\begin{abstract}
For triangular periodic billiards with disk radius in $[0.45,0.47]$, we construct explicit mechanical return records with a unimodular augmented lattice matrix. The winding trajectories are defined by a scalar reflection equation and verified by rational interval bounds, rather than by assigned roof values. We derive a marked suspension identity retaining the first residual and last incomplete flight. We also compute the exact one-sided density at the minimal length of an alternating collision word. Every regular critical word is normal at its endpoints, is unique for its center sequence, and has an exponentially bounded jump coefficient. The nonzero jumps exhibit $|b|^{-1}$ boundary contributions to raw Fourier inversion. The raw finite induced records have coarea densities. An explicit subtraction preserves the original law while giving an integrable remainder for the isolated edge. We prove a residual-inversion criterion for a uniform mixed lattice--continuous local limit, and quantify its consequences for central conditioning and geometric prediction. The mechanical and edge results are unconditional. The uniform long-time local limit is a theorem under printed spectral and residual-tail hypotheses; their realization for the full induced billiard remains a separate analytical task.
\end{abstract}
