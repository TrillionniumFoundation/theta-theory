\begin{abstract}
For a positive finite detector spanning the monomials with exponents in
$A$, we identify the generic dimension of the attainable information for
$n$ past and $m$ future observations as
$\min\{n(|A|-1),|mA|-1\}$. The formula follows from an attainable
binomial product tangent and strict mixed-moment positivity for every
full-support prior. We then determine what remains of this law when
additive exponents coalesce. Divided differences yield a nonsingular
pairing with logarithmic jets, while the actual query metric assigns
successive powers of the collision parameter to these directions. In
the full-future regime this gives a uniform, scale-sensitive checkpoint
memory law determined by the cluster multiplicities. For the five-trial
family $A_\theta=\{0,1,2+\theta\}$ we prove the stronger streaming law
\[
 \mathcal R_{M,5}^{\theta}\asymp
       \max\{M^{-1/2},\theta^{2/5}M^{-2/5}\},
\]
uniformly in both the number $M$ of persistent states and
$0\le\theta\le1/2$. Its upper realization requantizes after every
report, without division by $\theta$; its lower bound retains the
probability of the acquired history. In one common binary decision task,
erasing the weak direction loses order $\theta^2$, and a streaming
controller recovers a fixed fraction of that value once
$M$ exceeds a constant times $\theta^{-4}$. The general exact causal
state, fixed-calibration control bounds, and a counted mechanical
collision-bit comparison are also established.
\end{abstract}
