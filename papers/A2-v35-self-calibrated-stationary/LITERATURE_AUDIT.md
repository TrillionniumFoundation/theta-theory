# Focused literature audit — A2 v35

Checked on 4 October 2026. This is a focused information-category comparison, not an exhaustive priority search.

* **Locatelli–Carpentier–Kpotufe**, *An Adaptive Strategy for Active Learning with Smooth Decision Boundary*, PMLR 83 (2018). Its active line-search setting is relevant, but it does not directly furnish the collision mean, two-scale support correspondence or present C2 table loss.
* **Schneider**, *Convex Bodies: The Brunn–Minkowski Theory*, Chapters 3–4. Minkowski addition, support measures, erosion and parallel-area formulas are credited rather than claimed as new abstract principles.
* **Brunel–Klusowski–Yang**, *Estimation of convex supports from noisy measurements*, arXiv:1804.09879. Its continuous noisy-support setting does not supply the present scalar collision observations.
* **Shannon**, the source-coding reference already present in v34. The new binary-range and stopped-information lemmas are proved directly and are not claimed as a new general coding theorem.

The proposed contribution is the observation-level combination of unknown functional footprint recovery from two fixed scales and a geometry-specific expected-bit penalty under one fixed common law. No passive dynamical invariant or optimal stationary rate is inferred.
