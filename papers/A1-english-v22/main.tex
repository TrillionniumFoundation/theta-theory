\begin{abstract}
We determine the sharp finite-memory prediction law for positive sparse
monomial experiments at a fixed horizon, uniformly through all additive
exponent collisions. Maximal Vandermonde products of future exponents,
truncated by the dimension acquired by the past, determine both
checkpoint error and the error of one causal finite-state filter.
A general transfer theorem separates the required whole-image geometry,
unconditional acquisition mass, and stable reachable-state updates; it
also gives the accumulated error for unequal stage budgets. The monomial
verification uses complete acquired Newton--Hermite flags and raw-moment
updates without reciprocal collision gaps. A second multistep theorem
for bounded finite observation algebras identifies the covariance
exterior-power profile uniformly over all priors and all rank losses;
its proof verifies the same transfer hypotheses by exact product
coordinates and an unconditional density argument. Complete companion results
treat command-polynomial rank strata on compact latent spaces, the
uniform one-step covariance profile, exact kernels of continuous moment
pairings, circular contrast, operational reconstruction of the resolution
profile, and prior ambiguity. The kernel criterion combines dimension-free
full-support feasibility with the rank of a quotient multiplication
pencil and supplies arbitrarily small bounded-density witnesses.
\end{abstract}
