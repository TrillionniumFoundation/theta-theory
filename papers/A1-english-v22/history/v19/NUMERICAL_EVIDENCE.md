# Numerical and source evidence — v18

The current execution receipt is `validation/EXECUTION_REPORT.json`. It is written only after every requested suite and the complete manuscript build succeeds. `validation/EXECUTION_PROGRESS.json` is an incomplete progress record and must not be represented as a completed run.

The new standard-library program `tests/verify_v18.py` uses exact rational arithmetic. Its finite checks cover the augmented normalized rank, projective inverse/Jacobian/evidence density, physical weighted covariance prediction, determinant integration and sign examples, an interior full-support rank stratum, a positive rank-zero experiment with two distinct report states, and an exact scalar uniform quantization integral.

The unchanged v10–v15 and v17 author programs are rerun, not described as a new independent referee audit. Their source digests are checked against the archived v17 manifest. Earlier execution reports are kept under `history/`; they are historical records, not current evidence.

`PRESERVATION_REPORT.json` records the actual compiled v17-to-v18 statement/proof inclusion check. `BUILD_REPORT.json` records the three-pass typesetting result. None of the finite checks certifies the full continuum of encoders, uniform inverse-function constants, or a theorem's mathematical validity. The written proofs carry those obligations. Clean source and PDF checks are separate from an editorial recommendation.
