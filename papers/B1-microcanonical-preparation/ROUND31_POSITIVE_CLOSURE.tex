\section{Round-thirty-one reconstruction: stratified quadratic anchors and event-independent smoothing}
\label{sec:r31-b1}

A momentum--energy Jacobian cannot be uniformly full rank at equal anchor
velocities.  The preparation is therefore split into a regular coarea region
and a quadratic relative-energy region.  The latter is integrated by an
explicit radial stationary-phase calculation.  The reserved anchor variables
are excluded from the good-block event, so conditioning on that event never
cuts the anchor density.

\subsection{Nested preparation and a reserved anchor set}

Let $P^0_{\varepsilon,N}$ be the normalized exact-number hard-sphere
preparation.  Partition labels into a fixed reserved anchor set $A_N$ of
$m_0\ge7$ particles and non-anchor blocks $B_1,\ldots,B_J$.  The increasing
filtration is
\[
 \cF_j=\sigma(B_1,\ldots,B_j;\text{exterior non-anchor data}),           \tag{B1.1}
\]
and the anchor variables are not included in any good-block test.  Thus the
event
\[
 \mathsf G_N=\{\#\text{ good non-anchor blocks}\ge c_*N\}                \tag{B1.2}
\]
is measurable with respect to the non-anchor sigma-field.

The grand-canonical pressure from B2 is first tilted to its source-dependent
saddle $s_H$; numerator and denominator use $s_H$ and $s_0$ separately.
The continuous exact constraint is written in centre-of-mass/relative
coordinates for the anchor velocities:
\[
 p={1\over m_0}\sum_{i\in A_N}v_i,\qquad
 w_i=v_i-p,\qquad\sum_iw_i=0,                              \tag{B1.3}
\]
\[
 P_A=m_0p,\qquad
 E_A={m_0\over2}|p|^2+{1\over2}\sum_i|w_i|^2.             \tag{B1.4}
\]
The relative space has dimension $d_\perp=3(m_0-1)$.

\subsection{Regular coarea away from equal velocities}

Put $r=(\sum_i|w_i|^2)^{1/2}$.  On $r\ge\eta$, the momentum rows are the
$p$ coordinates and the energy derivative in a radial relative coordinate is
$r$.  A finite boundary-flat chart cover therefore has minors bounded below
by $c\eta$.

\begin{lemma}[Regular anchor coarea]
\label{lem:r31-b1-regular}
Conditionally on every non-anchor exterior, the push-forward of the reserved
anchor law from $\{r\ge\eta\}$ by the continuous constraint map has a
zero-extended $W^{s,1}$ density satisfying
\[
 \norm{r^{\rm reg}_{N,\mathrm{ext},\eta}}_{W^{s,1}}
 \le C_s\eta^{-q_s}.                                      \tag{B1.5}
\]
The estimate holds after four centred source derivatives.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Use $p$, one radial relative coordinate, and the remaining angular variables
as local coordinates.  The selected determinant is $m_0^3r$ times a bounded
position-constraint minor and is at least $c\eta$.  Hard-core faces are
handled by tangential coarea charts.  Boundary-flat partitions eliminate
extension-by-zero distributions.  The non-anchor exterior only changes a
finite set of smooth exclusion factors on the regular preparation shell, so
all derivative constants are uniform.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Quadratic anchor at the rank-degenerate set}

At $w=0$, the energy differential is a combination of momentum differentials,
but the relative energy is the positive quadratic form
\[
 e_\perp(w)={1\over2}|w|^2.                               \tag{B1.6}
\]
Let $a_{N,\mathrm{ext}}(p,w)$ be the conditional anchor density, including the
hard-core position charts.  On $|w|\le2\eta$ it is $C^{s+2}$ and compactly
supported after a Maxwellian truncation, with uniform derivatives.

\begin{lemma}[Quadratic relative-energy smoothing]
\label{lem:r31-b1-quadratic}
For every momentum frequency $u_P$ and energy frequency $u_E$,
\begin{equation}\tag{B1.7}
 \left|\int e^{i[u_P\cdot m_0p+u_E(m_0|p|^2+|w|^2)/2]}
       \chi_\eta(w)a_{N,\mathrm{ext}}(p,w)\dd p\dd w\right|
 \le C_s(1+|u_P|+|u_E|)^{-\sigma},
\end{equation}
where
\[
 \sigma<\min\left\{{3\over2},{d_\perp\over2}\right\}
\]
for one anchor group.  Using $L$ disjoint anchor groups multiplies the bound
and gives any prescribed exponent below
$L\min\{3/2,d_\perp/2\}$.  Constants are uniform in the exterior and four
centred source derivatives.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Complete the square in $p$ when $u_E\ne0$ and use the Fresnel estimate in
three dimensions.  In the relative variables use polar coordinates:
\[
 \int_0^{2\eta}e^{iu_Er^2/2}r^{d_\perp-1}A(r)\dd r.
\]
The substitution $q=r^2/2$ gives a compactly supported amplitude
$q^{d_\perp/2-1}\widetilde A(q)$ and hence decay
$(1+|u_E|)^{-d_\perp/2}$ up to endpoints; boundary-flat cutoffs remove the
endpoint loss.  If $|u_P|$ dominates, integrate in a $p$ direction.  A finite
conic partition combines the cases.  Disjoint anchor groups are conditionally
independent up to a uniformly smooth hard-core factor, so their oscillatory
bounds multiply after iterated integration.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Good non-anchor blocks}

A non-anchor block is good if its conditional covariance on the conservation
quotient is at least $cI$, its lattice support generates the required class,
and its chart has a regular boundary-flat density.  Packing and the B2
short-slice cluster estimate give
\[
 P\{B_j\text{ good}\mid\cF_{j-1}\}\ge p_*.              \tag{B1.8}
\]
The predictable Bernoulli supermartingale yields
\[
 P(\mathsf G_N^c)\le e^{-cN}.                             \tag{B1.9}
\]
Because $A_N$ is absent from every definition in (B1.1)--(B1.2), both
$1_{\mathsf G_N}$ and $1_{\mathsf G_N^c}$ are constants during anchor
integration.

\subsection{All-component Fourier damping}

Choose enough disjoint anchor groups that the exponent $S$ in
Lemma~\ref{lem:r31-b1-quadratic} satisfies
$S>\dim E_R+8$.  Split the relative anchor space at
$\eta=\langle v\rangle^{-\theta}$, apply
Lemma~\ref{lem:r31-b1-regular} outside and
Lemma~\ref{lem:r31-b1-quadratic} inside, and optimize $\theta$.

\begin{theorem}[Stratified anchor damping]
\label{thm:r31-b1-damping}
Let $\Phi_N(u,v)$ be the characteristic function of the centred exact
constraint.  Uniformly under four centred source derivatives,
\[
 |\Phi_N(u,v)|
 \le C(1+|v|)^{-S_0}
 \left[e^{-cN\min\{|(u,v)|^2,1\}}+e^{-cN}\right],         \tag{B1.10}
\]
for some $S_0>\dim E_R+5$.  The polynomial factor holds on both
$\mathsf G_N$ and $\mathsf G_N^c$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
On $\mathsf G_N$, successive conditioning on the increasing non-anchor
filtration gives the Gaussian and compact-annulus contraction.  On
$\mathsf G_N^c$ retain only (B1.9).  After either event is fixed, integrate the
reserved anchors.  Since the event is non-anchor measurable, it creates no
anchor boundary.  The regular region gives
$C\eta^{-q}(1+|v|)^{-s}$ and the quadratic region gives
$C(1+|v|)^{-S}$.  The optimized split yields $S_0$ and proves (B1.10).
Equal-velocity configurations are handled by the quadratic calculation, not
by a nonexistent full-rank minor.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Mixed local and Edgeworth coefficients}

Let $d_Z$ and $d_R$ be the lattice and continuous dimensions.  At the saddle
$s_H$, let $\Sigma_H$ be the covariance on the conservation quotient.

\begin{theorem}[Exact-number mixed Edgeworth theorem]
\label{thm:r31-b1-edgeworth}
For central deviations $z_N=O(\sqrt N)$,
\begin{equation}\tag{B1.11}
 P_H\{G_{N,Z}=k_N,G_{N,R}\in\dd y_N\}
 ={\operatorname{covol}(\Lambda_H)
 e^{-x_N^T\Sigma_H^{-1}x_N/2}
  \over(2\pi N)^{(d_Z+d_R)/2}\sqrt{\det\Sigma_H}}
 \left[1+N^{-1/2}P_{1,H}(x_N)+N^{-1}P_{2,H}(x_N)
 +o(N^{-1})\right]\dd y_N.
\end{equation}
The remainder and four source derivatives are uniform.  Exact continuous
conditioning is the coarea disintegration of this density.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The central saddle is expanded through sixth order and its amplitude through
fourth order.  Theorem~\ref{thm:r31-b1-damping} is an integrable majorant for
all four derivatives, so the noncentral integral is
$o(N^{-(d_Z+d_R)/2-1})$.  Gaussian integration gives the Edgeworth
polynomials and the full Schur covariance.  Coarea, not a fixed window,
defines the exact continuous fibre.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Dimension-consistent separate-saddle ratio]
\label{thm:r31-b1-ratio}
For numerator and denominator coefficients of the same exact constraint,
\[
 {A_N(H)\over A_N(0)}
 =e^{N[q(H)-q(0)]}
  \left({\det\cH_0\over\det\cH_H}\right)^{1/2}
  {c_H\over c_0}\left[1+N^{-1/2}d_H+O(N^{-1})\right].      \tag{B1.12}
\]
The common factor $N^{-(d_Z+d_R)/2}$ cancels; the constraint dimension never
changes with the source.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Deform the positive-coefficient activity integral through $s_H$ and $s_0$
separately.  Lattice aperiodicity gives a strict modulus gap off the real
class.  Apply (B1.11) to both coefficients and divide.  No undefined index or
source-dependent Gaussian dimension appears.
\end{proof}

The exceptional Fourier contribution is now smoothed on every component, the
equal-velocity set is handled by its true quadratic geometry, and event
conditioning cannot destroy the reserved anchor regularity.
