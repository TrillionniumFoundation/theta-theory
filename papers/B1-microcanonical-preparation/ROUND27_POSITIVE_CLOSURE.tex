
\section{Round-twenty-seven reconstruction: probabilistic good blocks and exact mixed coefficient extraction}
\label{sec:r27-b1}

The canonical local theorem is proved from a grand-canonical pressure that is
already available from B2.  No canonical covariance is used to prove the
coefficient extraction that defines it.  Regular coordinate blocks occur with
overwhelming probability, not deterministically in every legal
configuration, and all high-frequency remainders have an integrable density
bound with constants independent of $N$.

\subsection{Probability normalization and separate saddles}

Let
\[
 dP_{\varepsilon,N}^{0}
 ={1\over Z_{\varepsilon,N}^{0}N!}
 1_{\mathcal D_{\varepsilon,N}}
 e^{\sum_i\lambda_0\cdot C(x_i,v_i)}\,dX\,dV
\]
be the canonical probability reference.  For a path source $H$ and a centred
constraint multiplier $\theta$ define
\[
 Z_{\varepsilon,N}(H,\theta)
 =E_{P_{\varepsilon,N}^{0}}
 \exp\{\mu_\varepsilon H+\theta\cdot(G_N-Ng_0)\}.          \tag{B1.1}
\]
Thus $Z_{\varepsilon,N}(0,0)=1$.  The absolute free energy, when used, is
renormalized by the ideal-gas factor before taking $N^{-1}\log$.

Let $P_{\rm GC}(z,H,\theta)$ be the analytic grand-canonical pressure supplied
by B2.  For each source $H$, the activity/constraint saddle
$s_H=(z_H,\theta_H)$ is defined by
\[
 \nabla_{(\log z,\theta)}P_{\rm GC}(s_H,H)=(\rho,g_0).     \tag{B1.2}
\]
Numerator and denominator are deformed through their own saddles $s_H$ and
$s_0$; they are not called a common contour.

\subsection{Predictable regular blocks}

Partition particle labels into deterministic blocks of size $m$.  Before
exposing block $j$, condition on the exterior configuration and all earlier
blocks.  Packing bounds imply that only $C_m$ hard-core boundary components
can meet a spatial box of side comparable with the block diameter.  A
predictable atlas is obtained by:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item refining the box by these finitely many signed-distance functions;
\item selecting a quotient-constraint minor using only the exterior and the
      current block variables;
\item multiplying by a partition function flat through order $s-1$ at every
      chart boundary.
\end{enumerate}
The target constraint space $E=E_{\mathbb Z}\oplus E_{\mathbb R}$ is first
quotiented by all affine conservation identities.

A block is good when it lies in one of these charts, its selected Jacobian is
at least $c_*$, its conditional covariance on $E$ is at least $c_*I$, and its
velocities lie in a fixed Maxwellian window.

\begin{lemma}[Uniform conditional good-block probability]
\label{lem:r27-b1-good}
On every regular source compact there exist $p_*,c_*>0$ such that
\[
 P\{B_j\text{ is good}\mid\mathcal F_{j-1}\}\ge p_*       \tag{B1.3}
\]
until at least $\theta N$ blocks have been exposed.  Consequently
\[
 P\{\#\text{ good blocks}<\tfrac12p_*\theta N\}
 \le e^{-cN}.                                             \tag{B1.4}
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The B2 one-slice cluster expansion gives upper and lower conditional
densities relative to the labelled Maxwellian product on every fixed block,
uniformly over an exterior configuration in the regular preparation set.
The joint covariance certificate consists of finitely many disjoint legal
block configurations whose quotient increments span $E$ and whose Jacobian
minors are nonzero.  Small neighbourhoods of those configurations retain
positive Maxwellian mass and remain legal by their clearance margin.
Conditional density comparison gives (B1.3).  The exponential
supermartingale for Bernoulli variables with predictable success probability
at least $p_*$ gives (B1.4).
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Boundary-flat conditional density]
\label{lem:r27-b1-density}
On a good block, the push-forward by the continuous quotient constraint has a
zero-extended $W^{s,1}(E_{\mathbb R})$ density with norm at most $C_s$,
uniformly in all previous conditionings.  Its characteristic function obeys
\[
 |\varphi_j(u,v)|\le
 \begin{cases}
 1-c|(u,v)|^2,&|(u,v)|\le\delta,\\
 1-c,&\delta\le|(u,v)|\le R,\\
 C_s(1+|v|)^{-s},&|v|>R.
 \end{cases}                                             \tag{B1.5}
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Use the selected nonzero minor as coarea coordinates.  Exterior hard-core
surfaces are fixed parameters of the predictable chart.  Flatness through
order $s-1$ makes extension by zero a $W^{s,1}$ function without boundary
distributions.  Repeated integration by parts gives the last line.  The first
line follows from the certified conditional covariance, and compactness plus
aperiodicity gives the annular gap.
\end{proof}

\subsection{An integrable global characteristic-function bound}

One good block is reserved for final coarea smoothing.  On the event in
(B1.4), the remaining good blocks are multiplied by the tower property.  On
its complement, the reserved block is still integrated in its regular chart;
the complement indicator is approximated from inside by predictable
boundary-flat functions and then by monotone convergence.

\begin{theorem}[Uniform full-frequency damping]
\label{thm:r27-b1-damping}
For $s>\dim E_{\mathbb R}+4$,
\[
 |\Phi_N(u,v)|
 \le C(1+|v|)^{-s}
 \left[
 e^{-cN\min\{|(u,v)|^2,1\}}+e^{-cN}\right],              \tag{B1.6}
\]
uniformly in $N,\varepsilon$, the regular source compact, and two source
derivatives.  In particular there is no uncontrolled constant $C_N$, and the
exceptional term remains integrable over the full continuous Fourier space.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Condition successively on deterministic label blocks.  Lemma
\ref{lem:r27-b1-good} supplies linearly many good factors with exponentially
high probability; multiplying (B1.5) gives the Gaussian/annular contraction.
The reserved chart contributes $(1+|v|)^{-s}$ on both the typical and
exceptional events.  Its derivative constants are uniform by Lemma
\ref{lem:r27-b1-density}.  Source derivatives mark at most two factors and
are absorbed by the same bound.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The mixed local coefficient}

Let $d_Z=\dim E_{\mathbb Z}$ and $d_R=\dim E_{\mathbb R}$.  At the
source-dependent saddle $s_H$, let
\[
 \Sigma_H=
 \begin{pmatrix}
 \Sigma_{ZZ,H}&\Sigma_{ZR,H}\\
 \Sigma_{RZ,H}&\Sigma_{RR,H}
 \end{pmatrix},
 \qquad
 \Sigma_{R\mid Z,H}
 =\Sigma_{RR,H}-\Sigma_{RZ,H}\Sigma_{ZZ,H}^{-1}\Sigma_{ZR,H}.            \tag{B1.7}
\]

\begin{theorem}[Source-uniform mixed local theorem]
\label{thm:r27-b1-llt}
For lattice deviation $z_N$ and continuous deviation $y_N$, both
$O(\sqrt N)$,
\[
 P_H\{G_Z=Nm_Z+z_N,\ G_R\in Nm_R+dy_N\}
 ={e^{-\frac1{2N}(z_N,y_N)^\mathsf T
                 \Sigma_H^{-1}(z_N,y_N)}
   \over(2\pi N)^{(d_Z+d_R)/2}\sqrt{\det\Sigma_H}}
 \bigl(1+O(N^{-1/2})\bigr)\,dy_N.                         \tag{B1.8}
\]
Conditionally on the lattice value, the continuous mean is
\[
 \Sigma_{RZ,H}\Sigma_{ZZ,H}^{-1}z_N
\]
and its covariance is $N\Sigma_{R\mid Z,H}$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The B2 grand-canonical pressure gives the central analytic expansion before
any exact-$N$ extraction.  Fourier inversion is split into the
$N^{-1/2}$ ball, compact annulus, and unbounded continuous tail.  The first
gives the joint Gaussian.  The other regions are integrable and exponentially
small by (B1.6).  Completing the Gaussian square yields the conditional shift
and Schur covariance.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Exact continuous fibres and coefficient ratios}

The joint law in Theorem \ref{thm:r27-b1-llt} has a counting-times-Lebesgue
density.  If $\mathcal C:G_R\mapsto c$ is a regular exact momentum/energy
constraint, its conditional law is the coarea disintegration
\[
 \int_{\mathcal C^{-1}(c)}
 {p_H(z,y)\over J_{\mathcal C}(y)}\,d\mathcal H^{d_R-r}(y),              \tag{B1.9}
\]
not a fixed-width window.

\begin{theorem}[Separate-saddle exact-number ratio]
\label{thm:r27-b1-ratio}
Let $A_N(H)$ and $A_N(0)$ be the exact-number, lattice, and regular coarea
coefficients.  Then
\[
 {A_N(H)\over A_N(0)}
 =\exp\{N[q(H)-q(0)]\}
  N^{-\frac12(\operatorname{ind}H-\operatorname{ind}0)}
  {a_H\over a_0}\bigl(1+O(N^{-1/2})\bigr),                \tag{B1.10}
\]
where each exponential and amplitude is evaluated at its own saddle
$s_H$ or $s_0$.  Other activity saddles are exponentially smaller on the
declared steepest-descent chart.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Use the strict convexity certificate for the real pressure to obtain a unique
real saddle on the regular compact.  The B2 zero-free analytic tube and
positive Hessian produce a steepest-descent contour through each saddle.
Outside its central chart, (B1.6) and the activity modulus gap give an
exponentially smaller integral; thus no unexamined global saddle contributes.
Apply (B1.8) and (B1.9) separately to numerator and denominator, then divide.
\end{proof}

The canonical pressure is therefore obtained after, not before, the
grand-canonical theorem; regular blocks are probabilistic with an exponential
exceptional-set estimate; the Fourier constant is uniform; and exact
continuous conditioning and source-dependent saddles are explicit.
