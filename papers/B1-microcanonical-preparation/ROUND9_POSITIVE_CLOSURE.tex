\section{Round-nine reconstruction: the full source pressure and a weighted exact-saddle shell coefficient}
\label{sec:r9-b1}

The compound-Poisson gas is used only to expose the nondegenerate one-particle
mechanism.  The finite hard-sphere pressure contains in addition the connected
hard-core and trajectory remainder proved in the grand-canonical part of B2.
All saddle, Fourier, and shell estimates below are established for that full
pressure.  Moreover the source-dependent likelihood is kept inside the shell
integral; it is not replaced by its value at the shell centre when the shell
has an extensive width.

\subsection{The exact grand-canonical source pressure}

Let $H$ denote the bounded particle-path/contact source from B2 and let
$\lambda\in\mathbb R^d$ couple to the constraint mark
\[
 X(z)=\bigl(1,v,|v|^2/2,\chi_1(z),\ldots,\chi_m(z)\bigr).
\]
For the hard-sphere law at scale $\varepsilon$, with
$\mu_\varepsilon\to\infty$, put
\[
 Q_\varepsilon(H,\lambda)
 =\mu_\varepsilon^{-1}\log
 \mathbb E^{\rm gc}_\varepsilon
 \exp\left\{
   \mu_\varepsilon\langle H,\mathcal X_\varepsilon\rangle
   +\lambda\cdot C_\varepsilon
 \right\}.
\]
B2 proves on every compact real source chart and a fixed complex thickening the
normally convergent decomposition
\[
 Q_\varepsilon(H,\lambda)
 =Q^{(1)}_\varepsilon(H,\lambda)
  +R_\varepsilon(H,\lambda),
\]
where $Q^{(1)}_\varepsilon$ is the singleton/compound contribution and
$R_\varepsilon$ is the sum of connected hard-core trajectory clusters.

\begin{assumption}[Admissible constraint chart]
\label{ass:r9-b1-admissible}
On a compact chart $\mathcal K$ the following hold.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item the affine span of $X$ is all of $\mathbb R^d$;
\item finitely many product patches have a full-rank sum map and cover a
positive fraction of the tilted one-particle activity;
\item the B2 connected remainder is holomorphic on one complex neighbourhood
of $\mathcal K$ and its first four derivatives satisfy the cluster bounds
stated in B2.
\end{enumerate}
Redundant constraints are removed before the chart is declared.
\end{assumption}

\begin{lemma}[Typical-sector covariance for the full pressure]
\label{lem:r9-b1-full-covariance}
There are $c,C>0$ such that, uniformly on $\mathcal K$ and for all small
$\varepsilon$,
\[
 cI_d\le D^2_{\lambda\lambda}Q_\varepsilon(H,\lambda)
 \le CI_d.
\]
The lower bound comes from the linearly many particles in the typical
compound activity and is stable under the connected remainder.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The singleton Hessian equals the intensity-weighted second moment of $X$.
The affine-span condition and compactness give a lower bound $2cI_d$.
Differentiate the connected cluster series twice.  Its terms with at least two
labels are bounded by the absolutely summable B2 tree norm.  On the chosen
Boltzmann--Grad/source chart their operator norm is at most $c$ after reducing
the common chart once; no conditioning on a fixed low-particle sector is used.
The upper bound follows from the same cluster majorant.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Exact finite source-dependent saddle]
\label{cor:r9-b1-saddle}
For every target $a$ in a compact subset of the mean image and every
$H\in\mathcal K$, the equation
\[
 D_\lambda Q_\varepsilon(H,\lambda_{H,\varepsilon})=a
\]
has a unique solution.  The map $(H,a)\mapsto\lambda_{H,\varepsilon}$ is
analytic in $H$, $C^2$ in $a$, and converges with its first two derivatives to
the limiting saddle $\lambda_H$.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Apply the quantitative inverse-function theorem to
Lemma~\ref{lem:r9-b1-full-covariance}.  Normal convergence of the full B2
pressure and Cauchy estimates give convergence of the maps and their
derivatives.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Regenerative smoothing with the connected remainder}

Choose disjoint phase-space cells separated by more than the hard-core
interaction range at time zero.  In each cell expose a fixed number $n_0$ of
marks.  A cell is good when its marks lie in one of the full-rank patches from
Assumption~\ref{ass:r9-b1-admissible}.  Let $M_\varepsilon$ be the number of
good cells.

\begin{lemma}[Conditional good-block minorization]
\label{lem:r9-b1-regeneration}
There are $c,p>0$ and a sigma-field $\mathcal G_\varepsilon$ containing the
outside configuration such that
\[
 \mathbb P_{H,\lambda,\varepsilon}
 \left(M_\varepsilon<c\mu_\varepsilon\right)
 \le e^{-c\mu_\varepsilon},
\]
and, conditionally on $\mathcal G_\varepsilon$, every selected cell has a
minorized full-rank mark-sum density
\[
 \mathcal L(S_j\mid\mathcal G_\varepsilon)
 \ge p\,g_{j,\varepsilon}(y)dy,
 \qquad
 \sup_j\|g_{j,\varepsilon}\|_{W^{k,1}}\le C_k.
\]
The remainder of its conditional law is retained as an arbitrary probability
measure.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Use cells whose enlarged collision cylinders are disjoint.  The hard-core
Gibbs specification and the B2 connected-source Radon--Nikodym derivative
change the conditional activity in one such cell by factors bounded above and
below uniformly on the chart.  Coarea on the finite full-rank patches gives
the density minorization.  Reveal alternating cell families to recover
conditional independence; a martingale Chernoff bound gives linearly many
successful cells.  Iterating over the finite colouring of the cell graph
proves the exponential estimate.  No complement of a local patch is declared
smooth.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Full mixed lattice--continuous characteristic estimate]
\label{thm:r9-b1-fourier}
Let $t$ range in the fundamental domain of the lattice constraints and let
$u$ be the continuous Fourier variable.  Uniformly on compact source/target
charts,
\[
 \left|\Phi_{H,\lambda,\varepsilon}(t,u)\right|
 \le
 \begin{cases}
  \exp\{-c\mu_\varepsilon(|t|^2+|u|^2)\},
   & |t|+|u|\le\delta,\\
  e^{-c\mu_\varepsilon},
   & \delta\le |t|\le\pi,\ |u|\le R,\\
  e^{-c\mu_\varepsilon}+C_k(1+|u|)^{-k},
   & |u|\ge R,
 \end{cases}
\]
for every fixed $k$.  The estimate is for the exact hard-sphere tilted law,
not for an ideal compound proxy.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The central estimate is the Taylor expansion of the full complex pressure at
the exact saddle, using Lemma~\ref{lem:r9-b1-full-covariance}.  Lattice
aperiodicity follows from the span-one particle-number activity and the finite
mark lattice.  On compact minor arcs, conditional good blocks have a Fourier
modulus uniformly below one.  At large continuous frequency, expand the
minorized part in every good cell; choosing $k$ cells and integrating their
$W^{k,1}$ densities by parts gives the polynomial factor, while all remaining
good cells give exponential contraction.  The event with too few good cells
has probability $e^{-c\mu_\varepsilon}$ by
Lemma~\ref{lem:r9-b1-regeneration}.  The conditional specifications already
contain $R_\varepsilon$, so no later perturbative replacement is made.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The weighted shell integral}

Write the exact constraint as $(L_\varepsilon,Y_\varepsilon)$, with lattice
part $L_\varepsilon$ and continuous extensive part $Y_\varepsilon$.  Let
\[
 \mathcal S_\varepsilon
 =\{L_\varepsilon=\ell_\varepsilon,
 Y_\varepsilon-y_\varepsilon\in W_\varepsilon\},
\]
where $W_\varepsilon$ is a bounded Borel set whose diameter may be
$o(\sqrt{\mu_\varepsilon})$ but need not be $o(1)$.  Put
$\Sigma_{H,\varepsilon}=D^2_{\lambda\lambda}
Q_\varepsilon(H,\lambda_{H,\varepsilon})$ and let
$z=(Y-y)/\sqrt{\mu_\varepsilon}$.

\begin{theorem}[Exact-saddle weighted local coefficient]
\label{thm:r9-b1-coefficient}
Uniformly on the admissible chart,
\[
\begin{aligned}
 &\mathbb E^{\rm gc}_\varepsilon
 \left[e^{\mu_\varepsilon\langle H,\mathcal X_\varepsilon\rangle};
 \mathcal S_\varepsilon\right]\\
 &\quad=
 \exp\left\{
 \mu_\varepsilon Q_\varepsilon(H,\lambda_{H,\varepsilon})
 -\lambda_{H,\varepsilon}\cdot(\ell_\varepsilon,y_\varepsilon)
 \right\}
 \frac{1+o(1)}{(2\pi\mu_\varepsilon)^{r/2}}
 \int_{W_\varepsilon/\sqrt{\mu_\varepsilon}}
 e^{-\lambda_{H,\varepsilon}^{c}\cdot\sqrt{\mu_\varepsilon}z}
 \varphi_{\Sigma^c_{H,\varepsilon}}(z)\,dz .
\end{aligned}
\]
Here $r$ is the number of lattice coordinates and $\Sigma^c$ is the
conditional continuous covariance after the lattice block is fixed.  The
factor involving $\lambda^c$ may be replaced by one only when the original
continuous shell has diameter $o(1)$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Change measure to the exact $(H,\lambda_{H,\varepsilon})$ law.  On the shell,
the inverse likelihood is
$\exp\{-\lambda^L\ell_\varepsilon-
\lambda^c\cdot(y_\varepsilon+\sqrt\mu z)\}$; retain its $z$ dependence.
Use Fourier inversion for the lattice coordinates and the density inversion
for the continuous coordinates.  Theorem~\ref{thm:r9-b1-fourier} makes the
minor and large arcs negligible relative to the Gaussian coefficient, while
the central pressure expansion gives the displayed integral.  Uniform
covariance bounds supply domination for every declared shell scale.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Full microcanonical pressure and prepared LDP]
\label{thm:r9-b1-main}
The conditioned log-Laplace functional satisfies
\[
 Q^{\rm mc,a}(H)
 =\inf_\lambda\{Q(H,\lambda)-\lambda\cdot a\}
  -\inf_\lambda\{Q(0,\lambda)-\lambda\cdot a\},
\]
with the saddle reoptimized at every $H$.  The primitive shell laws satisfy
the good prepared LDP obtained by conditioning the full B2 grand-canonical
rate, and their Gaussian covariance is the corresponding Schur complement.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Apply Theorem~\ref{thm:r9-b1-coefficient} separately to the source numerator
and the zero-source denominator.  Their weighted Gaussian shell integrals
have only subexponential logarithms; no unjustified pointwise cancellation is
used.  This gives the pressure formula.  The upper bound follows from the full
conditioned Laplace principle and exponential tightness.  For the lower bound,
use the B2 source-tilted recovery law at a regular point and the same weighted
coefficient under that law, then approximate arbitrary finite-rate points by
the B2 balance-preserving recovery theorem.  Differentiating the exact saddle
formula twice gives the Schur complement.
\end{proof}
