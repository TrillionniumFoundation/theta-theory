\begin{abstract}
We derive the source-dependent microcanonical denominator directly from
the positive interacting exact-particle-number hard-sphere integral.  No
independence of connected components and no probabilistic interpretation of
signed polymer activities are used.  Sequential physical-cell exposure and
velocity integration by parts give one integrable characteristic-function
majorant over all frequencies, a uniformly positive covariance, and a
geometric mixed-shell local coefficient theorem.  The proof order is
strictly grand-canonical B2, then B1 conditioning, then the microcanonical B2
large-deviation theorem.
\end{abstract}
