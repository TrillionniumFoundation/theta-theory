\begin{abstract}
We normalize the exact-particle-number ensemble relative to a canonical
probability, and display the ideal-gas correction separately when absolute
free energy is needed.  Hence the pressure is finite at the origin and cannot
contain the spurious $-\log N$ term.  Deterministic labelled blocks, adapted
boundary-flat atlases, and fixed-order coarea estimates give a global Fourier
majorant.  The mixed lattice--continuous local theorem uses the full joint
covariance, including the conditional mean shift and Schur complement, and
feeds exact-number conditioning back to the hard-sphere path LDP.
\end{abstract}
