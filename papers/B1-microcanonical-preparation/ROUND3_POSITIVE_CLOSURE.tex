\section{Round-four referee closure: source-dependent constrained pressure}
\label{sec:r3-b1}

The fixed zero-source saddle in the first circulation is superseded here.  The
constraint multiplier is reoptimized for every particle-path and actual-
collision source.  The only dynamical input is the grand-canonical marked
pressure proved first in the grand-canonical part of Paper B2.

\subsection{The joint constraint vector and source pressure}

Let
\[
 C(x,v)=\left(1,v,\frac{|v|^2}{2},\chi_1(x,v),\ldots,\chi_m(x,v)\right)
 \in\mathbb R^d
\]
and let \(a\) include the prescribed particle density, momentum, energy, and
macro values.  The first component is lattice valued after multiplication by
\(\mu_\varepsilon\); the remaining components are continuous.  Let
\(H=(h,\psi)\) denote a particle-path source and an actual-collision source in
the B2 source ball.

For \(\lambda\in\mathbb R^d\), define
\[
 Q_\varepsilon(H,\lambda)
 =\frac1{\mu_\varepsilon}
 \log\mathbb E_\varepsilon^{\rm gc}
 \exp\left\{
 \mu_\varepsilon\langle H,(\Pi^\varepsilon,\Gamma^\varepsilon)\rangle
 +\lambda\cdot\sum_iC(z_i(0))
 \right\}.
\]
The number component of \(\lambda\) is the logarithmic activity.  Paper B2
proves normal convergence of this pressure on a complex neighborhood; denote
the limit by \(Q(H,\lambda)\).

\begin{lemma}[Uniform strict constraint convexity]
\label{lem:r3-b1-convex}
There are a compact real source--multiplier set
\(\mathcal U\times\mathcal V\), a short time \(T_*>0\), and
\(0<c<C<\infty\) such that, for \(T\le T_*\),
\[
 cI_d\le D^2_{\lambda\lambda}Q_\varepsilon(H,\lambda)
 \le CI_d
\]
uniformly in small \(\varepsilon\).  The same inequalities hold for the
limit, and the first three derivatives converge locally uniformly.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Separate the connected logarithm into its one-label sector and its connected
sector with at least two labels:
\[
 Q_{\varepsilon,T}(H,\lambda)
 =Q_{\varepsilon,T}^{(1)}(H,\lambda)
  +Q_{\varepsilon,T}^{(\ge2)}(H,\lambda).
\]
The one-label trajectory has no collision and is a translated free flight.
On the compact source--multiplier set its initial density is bounded above and
below by positive multiples of a Maxwellian.  By the assumed linear
independence of the coordinates of \(C\), there are finitely many compact
phase-space patches on which their centered vectors span \(\mathbb R^d\).
Consequently the singleton covariance satisfies
\[
 c_1I_d\le D^2_{\lambda\lambda}Q^{(1)}\le C_1I_d.
\]

The B2 tree norm, with two constraint derivatives inserted at time zero,
gives
\[
 \sup_{\mathcal U\times\mathcal V}
 \|D^2_{\lambda\lambda}Q_{\varepsilon,T}^{(\ge2)}\|
 \le C_2T+o_\varepsilon(1).
\]
The static hard-core correction is \(O(\varepsilon)\) at pressure scale and
is included in the final term.  Choose \(T_*\) so that
\(C_2T_*<c_1/4\), then take \(\varepsilon\) small.  This gives the lower
bound; the cluster majorant gives the upper bound.  Normal convergence on a
larger complex chart and Cauchy's formula give convergence of the first three
derivatives.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[The source-dependent saddle]
\label{cor:r3-b1-saddle}
For every \(H\in\mathcal U\) and every target \(a\) in a fixed compact
interior constraint set, there is a unique \(\lambda_H\in\mathcal V\) such
that
\[
 D_\lambda Q(H,\lambda_H)=a.
\]
The map \(H\mapsto\lambda_H\) is analytic and
\[
 D_H\lambda_H
 =-Q_{\lambda\lambda}^{-1}Q_{\lambda H}.
\]
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Apply the inverse-function theorem with the uniform Hessian bound from
Lemma~\ref{lem:r3-b1-convex}; differentiate the saddle equation.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Singleton-dominated mixed lattice--continuous coefficient}

Let \(N\) be particle number and let \(Y\in\mathbb R^{d-1}\) be the
continuous constraint vector.  Under the real tilted law at
\((H,\lambda_H)\), write
\[
 \Phi_\varepsilon(t,u)
 =\mathbb E_{H,\lambda_H,\varepsilon}
 \exp\{it(N-\mathbb EN)+i\mu_\varepsilon u\cdot(Y-\mathbb EY)\}.
\]
For the singleton phase density \(m_{H,\lambda}\), define
\[
 \chi_{H,\lambda}(t,u)
 =\int e^{it+i u\cdot(C'-\bar C')}
 m_{H,\lambda}(z)dz,
 \qquad
 a_{H,\lambda}=\int m_{H,\lambda}(z)dz.
\]
On compact source sets, \(a_{H,\lambda}\) is bounded above and below by
positive constants.

\begin{lemma}[Singleton pressure gap]
\label{lem:r4-b1-singleton-gap}
For every \(\delta\in(0,\pi)\) there are \(c_\delta>0\) and
\(T_\delta>0\) such that, for \(T\le T_\delta\),
\[
 \sup_{\delta\le|t|\le\pi,\ |u|\le\delta}
 |\Phi_\varepsilon(t,u)|
 \le e^{-c_\delta\mu_\varepsilon}.
\]
For every compact set not containing \((0,0)\), the same estimate holds, and
for \(|u|\ge1\)
\[
 |\Phi_\varepsilon(t,u)|
 \le e^{-c\mu_\varepsilon}
 +C_M(1+\mu_\varepsilon|u|)^{-M}
\]
for every fixed \(M\).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Insert the complex constraint source
\((it,i u)\) into the connected logarithm.  The one-label part contributes
\[
 \mu_\varepsilon
 \left(\chi_{H,\lambda}(t,u)-a_{H,\lambda}
 -i\,D\chi_{H,\lambda}(0,0)(t,u)\right).
\]
At \(u=0\), its real part is exactly
\[
 -2\mu_\varepsilon a_{H,\lambda}\sin^2(t/2).
\]
For \(|u|\le\delta\), smoothness and nondegeneracy of the singleton constraint
density preserve a fixed fraction of this gap when
\(\delta\le|t|\le\pi\).

A connected cluster with \(k\ge2\) labels acquires the factor
\(e^{ikt+i u\cdot\sum C'_j}-1\).  The B2 marked tree estimate with two
frequency derivatives gives, uniformly for \(|t|+|u|\le1\),
\[
 \left|\Re\Delta Q_T^{(\ge2)}(t,u)\right|
 \le CT(t^2+|u|^2),
\]
and, on the whole compact frequency domain,
\(|\Re\Delta Q_T^{(\ge2)}|\le CT\).  Taking \(T\) sufficiently small lets
the singleton negative real part dominate this remainder.  Exponentiating the
normalized connected logarithm proves the compact minor-arc estimate.

For continuous frequencies, the singleton measure has a smooth density with
Gaussian tails.  On each direction of the unit sphere one of the spanning
constraint patches has a nonvanishing directional derivative; a finite cover
and repeated integration by parts give
\(|\chi_{H,\lambda}(0,u)|\le C_M(1+|u|)^{-M}\).  Once this transform is below
\(a_{H,\lambda}/2\), the singleton logarithm has a fixed negative real part.
The connected remainder remains \(O(T)\), proving the exponential term.  The
polynomial term records the finite-\(\varepsilon\) smoothing error and is
more than sufficient for Fourier inversion.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Central complex expansion]
\label{lem:r3-b1-gap}
There are \(c,\delta>0\) such that
\[
 |\Phi_\varepsilon(t,u)|
 \le\exp\{-c\mu_\varepsilon(t^2+|u|^2)\},
 \qquad |t|+|u|\le\delta.
\]
Moreover, uniformly for \(|(t,u)|\le\mu_\varepsilon^{-2/5}\),
\[
 \log\Phi_\varepsilon(t,u)
 =-\frac{\mu_\varepsilon}{2}
 \langle(t,u),\Sigma_{H,\varepsilon}(t,u)\rangle
 +O(\mu_\varepsilon|(t,u)|^3),
\]
where \(cI\le\Sigma_{H,\varepsilon}\le CI\).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Taylor-expand the complex pressure at the real saddle.  The linear term
vanishes by centering.  Lemma~\ref{lem:r3-b1-convex} gives the uniform
quadratic bounds, and normal convergence on a larger complex polydisc gives a
uniform third derivative.  Taking the neighborhood smaller absorbs the cubic
remainder into the quadratic real part.
\end{proof}

Let the continuous shell widths satisfy
\[
 \delta_\varepsilon\downarrow0,
 \qquad
 \sqrt{\mu_\varepsilon}\delta_\varepsilon\to\infty,
\]
and let \(N_\varepsilon-\mu_\varepsilon a_0=O(1)\).

\begin{theorem}[Source-uniform shell coefficient]
\label{thm:r3-b1-coefficient}
Uniformly on compact source and target sets,
\[
 \mathbb P_{H,\lambda_H,\varepsilon}
 \left(N=N_\varepsilon,
 |Y-a'|\le\delta_\varepsilon\right)
 =\mu_\varepsilon^{-1/2}(c_H+o(1)),
\]
where \(0<c_-\le c_H\le c_+<\infty\).  In particular the logarithm of the
shell probability is \(o(\mu_\varepsilon)\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Use lattice Fourier inversion in \(t\in[-\pi,\pi]\) and smooth upper and lower
approximations of the continuous shell.  In the central region
\(|(t,u)|\le\mu_\varepsilon^{-2/5}\), scale by
\(\mu_\varepsilon^{-1/2}\) and use
Lemma~\ref{lem:r3-b1-gap}.  The continuous shell contains a number of standard
deviations tending to infinity, so its Gaussian probability tends to one.
The lattice coordinate contributes
\[
 c_H=(2\pi\sigma_{N\mid Y,H}^2)^{-1/2},
\]
where \(\sigma_{N\mid Y,H}^2\) is the Schur complement of the continuous
constraint covariance.

On the remaining compact frequencies,
Lemma~\ref{lem:r4-b1-singleton-gap} gives an exponentially small integral.
For unbounded continuous frequencies, choose the smoothing order larger than
the dimension and use the polynomial estimate in the same lemma.  These
regions are \(o(\mu_\varepsilon^{-1/2})\).  Uniform covariance bounds give
the positive uniform bounds on \(c_H\).
\end{proof}

\subsection{The constrained pressure formula}

Let \(\mathcal S_\varepsilon(a,\delta_\varepsilon)\) be the primitive
hard-core shell with exact number and continuous constraints within
\(\delta_\varepsilon\).  Define the conditioned log-Laplace functional
\[
 Q_\varepsilon^{\rm mc,a}(H)
 =\frac1{\mu_\varepsilon}
 \log\mathbb E_\varepsilon^{\rm gc}
 \left[
 e^{\mu_\varepsilon\langle H,(\Pi^\varepsilon,
 \Gamma^\varepsilon)\rangle}
 \mid\mathcal S_\varepsilon(a,\delta_\varepsilon)
 \right].
\]

\begin{theorem}[Source-dependent microcanonical transfer]
\label{thm:r3-b1-transfer}
Uniformly for \(H\) in a compact subset of the B2 source ball,
\[
 Q_\varepsilon^{\rm mc,a}(H)
 \longrightarrow
 Q^{\rm mc,a}(H),
\]
where
\[
 \boxed{
 Q^{\rm mc,a}(H)
 =Q(H,\lambda_H)-\lambda_H\cdot a
 -Q(0,\lambda_0)+\lambda_0\cdot a.}
\]
Equivalently,
\[
 Q^{\rm mc,a}(H)
 =\inf_\lambda\{Q(H,\lambda)-\lambda\cdot a\}
 -\inf_\lambda\{Q(0,\lambda)-\lambda\cdot a\}.
\]
The statement holds jointly for particle-path and actual-collision sources.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
In the numerator, change measure by the exact exponential tilt
\((H,\lambda_H)\).  On the shell,
\[
 e^{-\lambda_H\cdot\sum_iC(z_i(0))}
 =e^{-\mu_\varepsilon\lambda_H\cdot a+o(\mu_\varepsilon)}.
\]
Theorem~\ref{thm:r3-b1-coefficient} shows that the remaining shell probability
has logarithm \(o(\mu_\varepsilon)\).  Hence the numerator exponent is
\(Q(H,\lambda_H)-\lambda_H\cdot a\).  Repeating the argument with \(H=0\)
for the denominator gives the formula.  Strict convexity identifies each
value with the displayed infimum.  All estimates are uniform on the source
ball because the B2 cluster series and Lemmas~\ref{lem:r3-b1-convex}--
\ref{lem:r3-b1-gap} are uniform there.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[The time-zero source test]
\label{prop:r3-b1-timezero}
If \(H_\eta\) is the time-zero source
\(\langle H_\eta,(\Pi,\Gamma)\rangle=\eta\cdot C(\pi_0)\), then
\[
 Q^{\rm mc,a}(H_\eta)=\eta\cdot a.
\]
Thus the order-one discrepancy produced by a fixed zero-source saddle is
absent.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The identity
\(Q(H_\eta,\lambda)=Q(0,\lambda+\eta)\) gives
\(\lambda_{H_\eta}=\lambda_0-\eta\).  Substitute in
Theorem~\ref{thm:r3-b1-transfer}.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Initial rate and prepared covariance}

Let \(I_0^{\rm gc}\) be the grand-canonical initial empirical-measure rate.

\begin{theorem}[Constrained initial rate and Hessian]
\label{thm:r3-b1-rate}
The initial empirical measure under the shell satisfies a good LDP with
\[
 I_{0,a}(f)
 =\begin{cases}
 I_0^{\rm gc}(f)-
 \displaystyle\inf_{C(g)=a}I_0^{\rm gc}(g),&C(f)=a,\\
 +\infty,&C(f)\ne a.
 \end{cases}
\]
Its minimizer is the source-zero information projection, but a nonzero dynamic
source uses \(\lambda_H\), not \(\lambda_0\).  For source directions \(F,G\),
\[
 D^2Q^{\rm mc,a}(0)[F,G]
 =Q_{FG}-Q_{F\lambda}
 Q_{\lambda\lambda}^{-1}Q_{\lambda G}
\]
at \((0,\lambda_0)\).  This is the covariance of the grand-canonical
fluctuation field projected onto the tangent space of all constraints.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The initial LDP follows from the same source-dependent conditioning argument
with time-zero linear sources and Legendre duality.  Differentiating the saddle
formula once cancels the \(D_H\lambda_H\) terms because
\(Q_\lambda=a\).  Differentiating a second time and using
Corollary~\ref{cor:r3-b1-saddle} gives the Schur complement.  In covariance
form this is exactly orthogonal projection away from the span of the
constraint fluctuation vector.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Hard-core scale and final closure}

The static excluded-volume correction remains subleading:
\[
 N_\varepsilon^2\varepsilon^3=O(\varepsilon^{-1})
 =o(\mu_\varepsilon).
\]
It is used only to compare equivalent technical activities; the dynamic
source dependence is carried by the full hard-core pressure \(Q(H,\lambda)\),
not discarded with this estimate.

\begin{theorem}[Round-four B1 closure]
\label{thm:r3-b1-main}
The primitive hard-sphere microcanonical shell has the source-dependent
constrained pressure of Theorem~\ref{thm:r3-b1-transfer}, the initial rate and
prepared covariance of Theorem~\ref{thm:r3-b1-rate}, and a uniform
subexponential shell coefficient.  The theorem applies to the joint
particle-path and actual-collision source class required by B2--B4.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Combine Lemma~\ref{lem:r3-b1-convex},
Theorems~\ref{thm:r3-b1-coefficient}, \ref{thm:r3-b1-transfer}, and
\ref{thm:r3-b1-rate}, together with
Proposition~\ref{prop:r3-b1-timezero}.
\end{proof}
