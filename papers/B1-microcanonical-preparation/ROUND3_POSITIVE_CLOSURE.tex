\section{Round-three positive closure: source-dependent constrained pressure}
\label{sec:r3-b1}

The fixed zero-source saddle in the first circulation is superseded here.  The
constraint multiplier is reoptimized for every particle-path and actual-
collision source.  The only dynamical input is the grand-canonical marked
pressure proved first in the grand-canonical part of Paper B2.

\subsection{The joint constraint vector and source pressure}

Let
\[
 C(x,v)=\left(1,v,\frac{|v|^2}{2},\chi_1(x,v),\ldots,\chi_m(x,v)\right)
 \in\mathbb R^d
\]
and let \(a\) include the prescribed particle density, momentum, energy, and
macro values.  The first component is lattice valued after multiplication by
\(\mu_\varepsilon\); the remaining components are continuous.  Let
\(H=(h,\psi)\) denote a particle-path source and an actual-collision source in
the B2 source ball.

For \(\lambda\in\mathbb R^d\), define
\[
 Q_\varepsilon(H,\lambda)
 =\frac1{\mu_\varepsilon}
 \log\mathbb E_\varepsilon^{\rm gc}
 \exp\left\{
 \mu_\varepsilon\langle H,(\Pi^\varepsilon,\Gamma^\varepsilon)\rangle
 +\lambda\cdot\sum_iC(z_i(0))
 \right\}.
\]
The number component of \(\lambda\) is the logarithmic activity.  Paper B2
proves normal convergence of this pressure on a complex neighborhood; denote
the limit by \(Q(H,\lambda)\).

\begin{lemma}[Uniform strict constraint convexity]
\label{lem:r3-b1-convex}
There are a source ball \(\mathcal U\), a multiplier neighborhood
\(\mathcal V\), and constants \(0<c<C<\infty\) such that
\[
 cI_d\le D^2_{\lambda\lambda}Q_\varepsilon(H,\lambda)
 \le CI_d
\]
for \((H,\lambda)\in\mathcal U\times\mathcal V\), uniformly in small
\(\varepsilon\).  The same inequalities hold for \(Q\), and the first three
derivatives of \(Q_\varepsilon\) converge locally uniformly.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The Hessian is
\[
 D^2_{\lambda\lambda}Q_\varepsilon
 =\mu_\varepsilon
 \operatorname{Cov}_{H,\lambda,\varepsilon}
 \left(\frac1{\mu_\varepsilon}\sum_iC(z_i(0))\right).
\]
The all-contact cluster norm of B2, with constraint sources inserted at time
zero, gives uniform upper bounds and convergence of the first three
cumulants.  For the lower bound, choose \(d\) disjoint small phase-space boxes
on which the vectors \(C-C_0\) span \(\mathbb R^d\); this is possible by the
linear independence hypothesis modulo the fixed conservation coordinates.
The tilted one-particle activity is uniformly positive on these boxes.
Hard-core exclusion removes only an \(O(\varepsilon)\) fraction of independent
box placements at Boltzmann--Grad density.  Conditional Bernoulli fluctuations
of their occupancies therefore give a covariance lower bound \(cI_d\).
Compactness of the source--multiplier ball makes the constants uniform.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[The source-dependent saddle]
\label{cor:r3-b1-saddle}
For every \(H\in\mathcal U\) and every target \(a\) in a fixed compact
interior constraint set, there is a unique \(\lambda_H\in\mathcal V\) such
that
\[
 D_\lambda Q(H,\lambda_H)=a.
\]
The map \(H\mapsto\lambda_H\) is analytic and
\[
 D_H\lambda_H
 =-Q_{\lambda\lambda}^{-1}Q_{\lambda H}.
\]
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Apply the inverse-function theorem with the uniform Hessian bound from
Lemma~\ref{lem:r3-b1-convex}; differentiate the saddle equation.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A joint lattice--continuous coefficient estimate}

Let \(N\) denote particle number and let \(Y\in\mathbb R^{d-1}\) denote the
remaining empirical constraint vector.  Under the \((H,\lambda_H)\)-tilted
law, write \(\widehat N=N-\mu_\varepsilon a_0\) and
\(\widehat Y=Y-a'\).

\begin{lemma}[Uniform characteristic-function gap]
\label{lem:r3-b1-gap}
There are \(c,\delta>0\) such that the joint characteristic function satisfies
\[
 \left|
 \mathbb E_{H,\lambda_H,\varepsilon}
 e^{it\widehat N+i\mu_\varepsilon u\cdot\widehat Y}
 \right|
 \le
 \begin{cases}
 \exp\{-c\mu_\varepsilon(t^2+|u|^2)\},
 &|t|+|u|\le\delta,\\
 \exp\{-c\mu_\varepsilon\},
 &\delta\le |t|\le\pi,\ |u|\le\delta,\\
 C_M(1+\mu_\varepsilon|u|)^{-M},
 &|u|\ge\delta,
 \end{cases}
\]
for every fixed \(M\), uniformly on compact source and target sets.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Near zero, Taylor expansion of the complex pressure and
Lemma~\ref{lem:r3-b1-convex} give the Gaussian bound.  For the number
frequency away from zero, divide the torus into cubes of side
\(L\varepsilon\).  Since the occupied volume fraction is \(O(\varepsilon)\),
a fixed positive fraction of cubes are insertion-safe under every configuration
outside an exponentially negligible set.  On each safe cube, summing the
possibilities of zero or one distinguished particle multiplies the normalized
characteristic function by
\[
 \frac{|1+ze^{it}|}{1+z}\le1-c\sin^2(t/2).
\]
Using \(c\mu_\varepsilon\) disjoint safe choices gives the second bound; the
cluster correction between choices is absorbed by decreasing \(c\).

For the continuous constraints, condition on all particles but one in a safe
box.  The tilted single-particle density is smooth with Gaussian velocity
tails, and at least one coordinate of \(C\) has a derivative bounded away
from zero in each direction \(u/|u|\) on one of the spanning boxes.
Integration by parts there gives \(C_M(1+|u|)^{-M}\).  Repeating over a
positive fraction of safe boxes gives the displayed uniform polynomial bound,
which is more than sufficient outside the central Fourier region.
\end{proof}

Let the continuous shell widths \(\delta_\varepsilon\) satisfy
\[
 \delta_\varepsilon\downarrow0,
 \qquad
 \sqrt{\mu_\varepsilon}\,\delta_\varepsilon\longrightarrow\infty,
\]
and take the exact particle number \(N=N_\varepsilon\) with
\(N_\varepsilon-\mu_\varepsilon a_0=O(1)\).

\begin{theorem}[Source-uniform shell coefficient]
\label{thm:r3-b1-coefficient}
Under the \((H,\lambda_H)\)-tilted law,
\[
 \mathbb P_{H,\lambda_H,\varepsilon}
 \left(
 N=N_\varepsilon,
 \ |Y-a'|\le\delta_\varepsilon
 \right)
 =\mu_\varepsilon^{-1/2}
 \left(c_H+o(1)\right),
\]
where \(c_H\) is bounded above and below by positive constants uniformly on
compact source and target sets.  In particular the logarithm of the shell
probability is \(o(\mu_\varepsilon)\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Use Fourier inversion in the lattice number coordinate and a smooth upper and
lower approximation of the continuous box.  On
\(|t|+|u|\le\mu_\varepsilon^{-2/5}\), the complex pressure expansion gives a
nondegenerate Gaussian with covariance
\(Q_{\lambda\lambda}(H,\lambda_H)\).  The continuous shell contains a number
of Gaussian standard deviations tending to infinity, so its Gaussian factor
tends to one.  The exact number coefficient contributes
\((2\pi\mu_\varepsilon\sigma_N^2)^{-1/2}\), including the Schur correction
from the continuous coordinates.  Lemma~\ref{lem:r3-b1-gap} makes every
remaining Fourier region \(o(\mu_\varepsilon^{-1/2})\).  Uniform covariance
bounds give the uniform positive bounds on \(c_H\).
\end{proof}

\subsection{The constrained pressure formula}

Let \(\mathcal S_\varepsilon(a,\delta_\varepsilon)\) be the primitive
hard-core shell with exact number and continuous constraints within
\(\delta_\varepsilon\).  Define the conditioned log-Laplace functional
\[
 Q_\varepsilon^{\rm mc,a}(H)
 =\frac1{\mu_\varepsilon}
 \log\mathbb E_\varepsilon^{\rm gc}
 \left[
 e^{\mu_\varepsilon\langle H,(\Pi^\varepsilon,
 \Gamma^\varepsilon)\rangle}
 \mid\mathcal S_\varepsilon(a,\delta_\varepsilon)
 \right].
\]

\begin{theorem}[Source-dependent microcanonical transfer]
\label{thm:r3-b1-transfer}
Uniformly for \(H\) in a compact subset of the B2 source ball,
\[
 Q_\varepsilon^{\rm mc,a}(H)
 \longrightarrow
 Q^{\rm mc,a}(H),
\]
where
\[
 \boxed{
 Q^{\rm mc,a}(H)
 =Q(H,\lambda_H)-\lambda_H\cdot a
 -Q(0,\lambda_0)+\lambda_0\cdot a.}
\]
Equivalently,
\[
 Q^{\rm mc,a}(H)
 =\inf_\lambda\{Q(H,\lambda)-\lambda\cdot a\}
 -\inf_\lambda\{Q(0,\lambda)-\lambda\cdot a\}.
\]
The statement holds jointly for particle-path and actual-collision sources.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
In the numerator, change measure by the exact exponential tilt
\((H,\lambda_H)\).  On the shell,
\[
 e^{-\lambda_H\cdot\sum_iC(z_i(0))}
 =e^{-\mu_\varepsilon\lambda_H\cdot a+o(\mu_\varepsilon)}.
\]
Theorem~\ref{thm:r3-b1-coefficient} shows that the remaining shell probability
has logarithm \(o(\mu_\varepsilon)\).  Hence the numerator exponent is
\(Q(H,\lambda_H)-\lambda_H\cdot a\).  Repeating the argument with \(H=0\)
for the denominator gives the formula.  Strict convexity identifies each
value with the displayed infimum.  All estimates are uniform on the source
ball because the B2 cluster series and Lemmas~\ref{lem:r3-b1-convex}--
\ref{lem:r3-b1-gap} are uniform there.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[The time-zero source test]
\label{prop:r3-b1-timezero}
If \(H_\eta\) is the time-zero source
\(\langle H_\eta,(\Pi,\Gamma)\rangle=\eta\cdot C(\pi_0)\), then
\[
 Q^{\rm mc,a}(H_\eta)=\eta\cdot a.
\]
Thus the order-one discrepancy produced by a fixed zero-source saddle is
absent.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The identity
\(Q(H_\eta,\lambda)=Q(0,\lambda+\eta)\) gives
\(\lambda_{H_\eta}=\lambda_0-\eta\).  Substitute in
Theorem~\ref{thm:r3-b1-transfer}.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Initial rate and prepared covariance}

Let \(I_0^{\rm gc}\) be the grand-canonical initial empirical-measure rate.

\begin{theorem}[Constrained initial rate and Hessian]
\label{thm:r3-b1-rate}
The initial empirical measure under the shell satisfies a good LDP with
\[
 I_{0,a}(f)
 =\begin{cases}
 I_0^{\rm gc}(f)-
 \displaystyle\inf_{C(g)=a}I_0^{\rm gc}(g),&C(f)=a,\\
 +\infty,&C(f)\ne a.
 \end{cases}
\]
Its minimizer is the source-zero information projection, but a nonzero dynamic
source uses \(\lambda_H\), not \(\lambda_0\).  For source directions \(F,G\),
\[
 D^2Q^{\rm mc,a}(0)[F,G]
 =Q_{FG}-Q_{F\lambda}
 Q_{\lambda\lambda}^{-1}Q_{\lambda G}
\]
at \((0,\lambda_0)\).  This is the covariance of the grand-canonical
fluctuation field projected onto the tangent space of all constraints.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The initial LDP follows from the same source-dependent conditioning argument
with time-zero linear sources and Legendre duality.  Differentiating the saddle
formula once cancels the \(D_H\lambda_H\) terms because
\(Q_\lambda=a\).  Differentiating a second time and using
Corollary~\ref{cor:r3-b1-saddle} gives the Schur complement.  In covariance
form this is exactly orthogonal projection away from the span of the
constraint fluctuation vector.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Hard-core scale and final closure}

The static excluded-volume correction remains subleading:
\[
 N_\varepsilon^2\varepsilon^3=O(\varepsilon^{-1})
 =o(\mu_\varepsilon).
\]
It is used only to compare equivalent technical activities; the dynamic
source dependence is carried by the full hard-core pressure \(Q(H,\lambda)\),
not discarded with this estimate.

\begin{theorem}[Round-three B1 closure]
\label{thm:r3-b1-main}
The primitive hard-sphere microcanonical shell has the source-dependent
constrained pressure of Theorem~\ref{thm:r3-b1-transfer}, the initial rate and
prepared covariance of Theorem~\ref{thm:r3-b1-rate}, and a uniform
subexponential shell coefficient.  The theorem applies to the joint
particle-path and actual-collision source class required by B2--B4.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Combine Lemma~\ref{lem:r3-b1-convex},
Theorems~\ref{thm:r3-b1-coefficient}, \ref{thm:r3-b1-transfer}, and
\ref{thm:r3-b1-rate}, together with
Proposition~\ref{prop:r3-b1-timezero}.
\end{proof}
