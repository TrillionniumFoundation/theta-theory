## D. Structural consequences and their status

Source: `v25/graph_consequences.tex`.

| Result | Proof supplied | Status |

| `cor:v25-cutwidth` | The largest nonzero profile index at a cut is the sum of attained edge dimensions; optimize over finite orders | Gives a fixed-calibration graph-layout exponent. Classical cutwidth itself is not claimed as new. |
| `cor:v25-star` | Explicit positive detector, all nine formal future sums, six separated collision groups, determinant contact orders, and every three-edge star partition | Shows a change of the optimal order with resolution and a diverging penalty for fixing the same order at two accuracies. |

The star has exactly 24 raw trials. At its two selected accuracies, the leading edge weights are respectively `(7/2,3,1/2) log2(1/theta)` and `(14,15,2) log2(1/theta)`. The optimizing isolated edge changes. The theorem concerns sufficiently small positive theta; theta equals zero remains covered by the general classification without taking its logarithm.
