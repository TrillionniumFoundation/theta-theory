\section{Complete mixed optical moments at finite collision count}
\label{sec:v86-moments}
The finite-count cap, rather than the limiting Gaussian term alone,
allows an optical moment strictly greater than one. This extra moment
will be used before summing infinitely many microscopic roof scales.
Write $z=|g|$, $\alpha=129/128$, $\beta=17/16$, and
$\bar\nu=\nu_R^*|_\Omega$. All constants below are uniform in $m,R,n,k$.
Dependence on the fixed moment exponents is permitted.

\subsection{The capped input}
The joint marked estimate retained in Supplement
Theorem~\ref{supp-v81archive:thm:v81-joint-local} has, after the
fixed conversion from endpoint momentum to $z$, the form
\begin{align}
 m^2\!\int \one_{\{F\in J,z<\zeta,c_i<s\}}\,d\bar\nu
 \le{}& C\zeta^\alpha s^\beta(|J|+B_*^{-1})\notag\\
 &+C_{B_*}(1+B_*|J|)m^2
            \min\{\zeta\rho_{B_*}^{m/2},s^2\}.
                                      \label{eq:v86-joint-input}
\end{align}
It holds for $0<\zeta<z_*$, $0<s<s_*$ and every internal mark.
The source retains its exact displacement and occupation coefficient;
dropping endpoint section tests is used only in its positive upper
comparison. Its endpoint-only counterpart is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v86-endpoint-input}
 m^2\!\int\one_{\{F\in J,z<\zeta\}}\,d\bar\nu
 \le C\zeta^\alpha(|J|+B_*^{-1})
   +C_{B_*}(1+B_*|J|)m^2\zeta\rho_{B_*}^{m/2}.
\end{equation}
The stronger endpoint exponent in that proof implies the displayed
one. Without a gradient restriction the retained marked-incidence
estimate has exponent $5/4$ and cap
$\min\{\rho_{B_*}^{m/2},s^2\}$; see Supplement
Theorem~\ref{supp-v80archive:thm:v80-superlinear-incidence}.
These are explicitly inherited spectral inputs. Their four-record
extensions are not being attributed to an external billiard theorem.

\begin{lemma}[A capped power integral]\label{lem:v86-cap}
For $0<p<2$, $0<D\le1$ and $0<s\le1$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v86-cap}
 s^{-p}\min(D,s^2)+p\int_0^s v^{-p-1}\min(D,v^2)\,dv
 \le \frac{2}{2-p}\min\{s^{2-p},D^{1-p/2}\}.
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For $s\le\sqrt D$ the left side is $2s^{2-p}/(2-p)$.
For $s>\sqrt D$, split the integral at $\sqrt D$. The terms
$Ds^{-p}$ cancel, leaving $2D^{1-p/2}/(2-p)$. Thus the inequality
is in fact an equality. The same computation justifies monotone
removal of the lower integration endpoint.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual mixed moments]\label{thm:v86-moments}
Fix
\[
 1<p<\beta,\qquad \sigma_p=1-p/2,\qquad 0<\eta<\sigma_p.
\]
The actual densities
\[
 U_p^\zeta(t)\,dt=F_\#(I_m^p\one_{\{z<\zeta\}}\bar\nu),\qquad
 V_{p,\eta}^\zeta(t)\,dt
       =F_\#(I_m^p z^{-\eta}\one_{\{0<z<\zeta\}}\bar\nu)
\]
are finite at each count. On every fixed auxiliary band,
\begin{align}
 m^2\int_J U_p^\zeta
 &\le C_p\zeta^\alpha(|J|+B_*^{-1})
 +C_{B_*,p}(1+B_*|J|)m^2
                 \zeta^{\sigma_p}\rho_{B_*}^{m\sigma_p/2},
                                            \label{eq:v86-moment-finite}\\
 m^2\int_J V_{p,\eta}^\zeta
 &\le C_{p,\eta}\zeta^{\alpha-\eta}(|J|+B_*^{-1})
 +C_{B_*,p,\eta}(1+B_*|J|)m^2
            \zeta^{\sigma_p-\eta}\rho_{B_*}^{m\sigma_p/2}.
                                            \label{eq:v86-mixed-finite}
\end{align}
The unrestricted actual source
$V_{p,\eta}(t)\,dt=F_\#(I_m^p z^{-\eta}\bar\nu)$ satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v86-full-moment}
 m^2\int_J V_{p,\eta}
 \le C_{p,\eta}(|J|+B_*^{-1})
 +C_{B_*,p,\eta}(1+B_*|J|)m^2\rho_{B_*}^{m\sigma_p/2}.
\end{equation}
The estimates hold at finite count, for all intervals and thresholds
in their stated ranges. The leading constants do not depend on the
fixed band. No strong-space multiplier $M_{I_m^pz^{-\eta}}$ is assumed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Put $S_m=1+\sum_{i=1}^{m-1}q_0^{d_i}\le1+2q_0/(1-q_0)$.
Weighted Jensen gives the pointwise inequality
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v86-jensen}
 I_m^p\le S_m^{p-1}\left(1+\sum_{i=1}^{m-1}q_0^{d_i}c_i^{-p}\right).
\end{equation}
For each marked power below $s_*$ use the positive layer cake
\[
 c_i^{-p}\one_{\{c_i<s_*\}}
 =s_*^{-p}\one_{\{c_i<s_*\}}
   +p\int_0^{s_*}v^{-p-1}\one_{\{c_i<v\}}\,dv.
\]
Insert \eqref{eq:v86-joint-input} before any limit. The leading
integral converges since $p<\beta$. Lemma~\ref{lem:v86-cap}, with
$D=\zeta\rho_{B_*}^{m/2}$, bounds its entire remainder by
$C_p\zeta^{\sigma_p}\rho_{B_*}^{m\sigma_p/2}$.
The bounded complementary powers and the constant one use
\eqref{eq:v86-endpoint-input}. Their errors are smaller, after
adjusting fixed constants. Sum the geometric weights in
\eqref{eq:v86-jensen}; there is no physical-word count. This proves
\eqref{eq:v86-moment-finite}.

Now apply the positive gradient layer cake with weight $I_m^p$:
\[
 z^{-\eta}\one_{\{z<\zeta\}}
 =\zeta^{-\eta}\one_{\{z<\zeta\}}
   +\eta\int_0^\zeta q^{-\eta-1}\one_{\{z<q\}}\,dq.
\]
Both terms of \eqref{eq:v86-moment-finite} are integrable at zero:
$\alpha-\eta>0$ and $\sigma_p-\eta>0$. Their coefficients are,
respectively, multiplied by $\alpha/(\alpha-\eta)$ and
$\sigma_p/(\sigma_p-\eta)$. This proves
\eqref{eq:v86-mixed-finite} by monotone convergence of the actual
source, including its finite-count remainder.

Split the unrestricted source at $z_*/2$. Above that threshold,
$z^{-\eta}$ is bounded. Apply the unconditioned capped incidence
input and the same Jensen and power integrals; $p<\beta<5/4$.
Below it use \eqref{eq:v86-mixed-finite}. Enlarge $\rho_{B_*}<1$
if necessary to combine finitely many remainders. This proves
\eqref{eq:v86-full-moment}. At fixed count the roof has bounded
support, so taking a containing interval proves finite mass.
Absolute continuity follows on compact regular charts by coarea,
then on their increasing union by positivity; the removed critical
set has zero source area. All densities use the common kernels
specified at the first use of the segment map in this article.
\end{proof}

The strict inequalities on $p$ and $\eta$ are needed for two different
integrals. In particular no untruncated first inverse gradient is
obtained by substituting $\eta=1$. The new gain is a genuine power
of the complete optical insertion above one, with a summable
finite-count remainder.
