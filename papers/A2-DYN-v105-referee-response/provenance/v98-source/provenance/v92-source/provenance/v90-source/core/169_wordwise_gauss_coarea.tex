\section{A wordwise coarea estimate without a complexity factor}
\label{sec:v75-gauss-coarea}
All measures below are on the original physical trajectories. The
convex envelope is used only to bound a level integral.
For a regular endpoint level let $\mathsf t$ be its unit tangent and
put $H_T=\mathsf t^{\mathsf T}D^2F_w\mathsf t$.
The normal angle $\theta$ refers to the two-dimensional endpoint
coordinate plane of the fixed cap pair, not to a collision angle.

\begin{lemma}[Total turning of all physical level pieces]
\label{lem:v75-total-turning}
For a fixed word and cap pair, and almost every roof value $t$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v75-count-free-coarea}
 \int_{\mathcal U_{w,a}\cap\{F_w=t\}}
                  \frac{d\mathcal H^1}{|\nabla F_w|}
                           \le\frac{2\pi}{\lambda}.
\end{equation}
Every Borel subset of the physical endpoint image satisfies the same
bound. On its regular level pieces the outward Gauss map is
one-to-one, apart from null sets, and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v75-weighted-gauss}
 \int_{\{F_w=t\}}\frac{g}{|\nabla F_w|}\,d\mathcal H^1
       =\int_{\Theta_{w,a}(t)}\frac{g(s_{w,a}(t,\theta))}
                                  {H_T(s_{w,a}(t,\theta))}\,d\theta
\end{equation}
for every nonnegative Borel weight on any such subset.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Intersect the convex sublevel set $\{\Phi_{w,a}\le t\}$ with
$Q_R$. A physical point at which $\nabla F_w\ne0$ lies on its
boundary, with outward normal $\nabla F_w/|\nabla F_w|$.
On every smooth physical arc the curvature is
$\kappa=H_T/|\nabla F_w|>0$. Two distinct points of a convex
boundary with the same supporting normal would lie on one exposed
segment. A positive-curvature point in an open physical arc cannot
belong to that segment. Thus the Gauss map is injective on all
these arcs together. Its image has angular measure at most $2\pi$,
even when physical exclusions split the level into many pieces.
The one-dimensional change of variables $d\theta=\kappa\,d\mathcal H^1$
gives \eqref{eq:v75-weighted-gauss}, and $H_T\ge\lambda$ gives
\eqref{eq:v75-count-free-coarea}. Apply the formulas on compact
regular exhaustions and then use monotone convergence. There is at
most one stationary endpoint point in a convex cap image, so its
roof value causes no difficulty for an almost-everywhere statement.
The rectangle boundary and nonsmooth portions of the auxiliary convex
boundary are not added to the physical source.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The unchanged exact-label density}
Let $B_m=\partial_{p_0}\alpha_m$ on a regular word. It is nonzero.
For a source weight $W\ge0$ on the original exact event
$\Pi=\Pi_{n,k,m,R}$, use the first-admissible endpoint partition
from Lemma~\ref{lem:v75-cap-cover}. At a selected physical point
$s=s_{w,a}(t,\theta)$ define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v75-gauss-density}
 \gamma^W_{w,a}(t,\theta)=
 \frac{W(x)\one_\Pi(x)\one_a(x)}
 {4\pi c_* R^2(n_0\cdot e_{j_0})(n_m\cdot e_{j_m})
                         |B_m(x)|H_T(s)}.
\end{equation}
Set this expression to zero when no selected physical point exists,
and put $\Gamma^W=\sum_{w,a}\gamma^W_{w,a}$. Every word is a
physical $m$-flight word. Its terminal center fixes its displacement;
its actual occupation and terminal section test remain in $\one_\Pi$.

\begin{theorem}[Positive Gauss coarea at every original label]
\label{thm:v75-exact-gauss}
The pushforward of $W\one_\Pi\nu_R^*$ has density
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v75-exact-gauss}
                      p_W(t)=\int_0^{2\pi}\Gamma^W(t,\theta)\,d\theta.
\end{equation}
The formula holds for nonnegative weights by monotone limits; for
bounded signed or complex insertions it holds by linearity. Moreover,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v75-geometric-majorant}
 \gamma^W_{w,a}(t,\theta)
 \le C_G\frac{W(x)\one_\Pi(x)\one_a(x)}{|B_m(x)|},
 \qquad C_G=\frac{2R_+}{\pi c_*R_-^2}.
\end{equation}
The constant and the number of cap pairs are independent of $m$.
There is no semialgebraic level-complexity or monotonicity factor in
these two formulas.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Endpoint injectivity and the nonzero twist give
$d\alpha_0\,dp_0=|B_m|^{-1}d\alpha_0\,d\alpha_m$.
Since $d\alpha_i=ds_i/[R(n_i\cdot e_{j_i})]$, the original
section probability $d\alpha_0\,dp_0/(4\pi c_*)$ becomes the
numerator of \eqref{eq:v75-gauss-density} without its factor $H_T$.
Coarea followed by \eqref{eq:v75-weighted-gauss} proves the formula
on each selected physical piece. The pieces are disjoint at the
source. Sum them by Tonelli, keeping $\one_\Pi$ throughout.
The two cap scalar products exceed $1/2$ and $H_T\ge\lambda$;
these inequalities give \eqref{eq:v75-geometric-majorant}.

For completeness the inverse $(t,\theta)\mapsto s_{w,a}$ can be
chosen Borel. On each regular piece the roof and its Gauss map are
smooth, and the pair is injective: two points with the same roof
and normal were excluded in Lemma~\ref{lem:v75-total-turning}.
The inverse of an injective Borel map between standard Borel spaces
is Borel on its image. A finite disjoint refinement of physical and
cap pieces, or a countable compact exhaustion, gives the stated
version. There are finitely many words at fixed $m$; exceptional
roof sets may depend on $R$. The order remains
$\sup_R\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_t$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A sharper complete inverse-incidence estimate}
Let $\mathcal I_m=\prod_{i=0}^m c_i^{-1}$. Denote by $D$ the
cardinality of a fixed finite alphabet containing every next-center
increment. This $D$ is a cardinality, not a displacement coordinate.
The inherited contact-determinant identity states, in endpoint
arclengths $(u,v)$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v75-incidence-determinant}
 \mathcal I_m|F_{uv}|\le (R/2)^{m-1}\prod_{i=0}^{m-1}\ell_i^{-1}.
\end{equation}
Its proof factors the internal contact matrix into its positive
Dirichlet Laplacian and the diagonal $2/(Rc_i)$; it requires no
incidence margin.

\begin{corollary}[No level-complexity loss in the complete height]
\label{cor:v75-inverse-incidence}
Under the full collision probability, the inverse-incidence roof
density satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v75-explicit-inverse-height}
 \|\rho^{\mathcal I}_{m,R}\|_\infty
 \le \frac{1024R_+}{R_-\tau_-}\,
             D^m\left(\frac{R_+}{2\tau_-}\right)^{m-1}.
\end{equation}
A positive exact-return restriction has this upper bound divided by
$c_*$; a bounded insertion costs its supremum norm. The geometric
factor formerly needed to count or turn all level components of a
word is absent. The remaining word sum is not asserted to have
central-scale order $m^{-2}$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
In endpoint arclengths the collision density is
$|F_{uv}|/(4\pi R)$. On the cap coordinates,
$du\,dv=ds_0\,ds_m/[(n_0\cdot e_{j_0})(n_m\cdot e_{j_m})]$,
so \eqref{eq:v75-incidence-determinant} bounds the weighted source
density by
$(R_+/2)^{m-1}\tau_-^{-m}/(\pi R_-)$.
Use \eqref{eq:v75-count-free-coarea}, sum at most 256 cap pairs
and at most $D^m$ words, and insert $\lambda^{-1}=2R_+$.
Truncation and monotone convergence prove finiteness for the unbounded
insertion as well. Exact-label and bounded-weight assertions follow
by positive restriction at the source, not by replacing their labels.
\end{proof}
