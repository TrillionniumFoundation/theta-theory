# Reference comparison for v10

This is a source-specific comparison, not a certificate of exhaustive priority.

## Freshly examined primary texts

**C. de Boor, Divided Differences**, *Surveys in Approximation Theory* 1 (2005), 46–69; arXiv:math/0502036v1. Proposition 7 on printed page 48 (PDF page 3) identifies an initial Newton expansion with the Hermite interpolant on the initial multiset. The page was inspected as an image as well as parsed text. The v10 prefix lemma records this principle for arbitrary ordering and complete multiplicity blocks; no new interpolation priority is asserted.

**Y. Zhang and J. Kileel, Covering Number of Real Algebraic Varieties and Beyond**, arXiv:2311.05116v4, 6 June 2025. Lemma 2.18 gives coefficient-independent regularity for semialgebraic sets of bounded format; its finite-union convention is explicit. This is the component-count input retained in the scalar thin-rectangle proof. Neither this result nor a generic ambient tensor covering theorem is used to remove the individual past caps in the product experiment.

**T. Guo, N. Karagodin and E. Stepanov, Optimal functional product quantization**, author preprint `guokaragste22a8.pdf`, hosted by CVGMT. Sections 1.1–1.2 define separately quantized input components and compare that optimization with classical quantization. Section 5.5, Theorem 5.18, treats a separable sum with independent inputs under quadratic cost and proves the corresponding separation of optimal component quantizers. The title page and relevant text were inspected. The bibliography pins the author version rather than assigning a publication year from inconsistent catalogue metadata.

The relevant distinction is not that multiplication of product covering numbers is new. In v10 the input is an actually acquired history, the query menu spans a tensor observation algebra, the lower bound permits arbitrary joint M-message encoders, the constants remain uniform at calibration coincidences, and the upper bound is one index-only sequential transducer. The product-quantization comparison alone neither proves nor disproves these statements.

## Retained comparisons and their exact use

The v9 bibliography and full scalar proofs retain the classical total-positivity, squared-error quantization, sumset/Hilbert-function, Leja and real Vitushkin-entropy references. The clustered Fourier Vandermonde paper by Batenkov–Diederichs–Goldman–Yomdin supplies relevant spectral context; v10 instead proves its ordinary square real Vandermonde comparison directly. It does not assert identical hypotheses or claim that that paper proves a posterior-attainment theorem.

The thin-rectangle proof retains the classical real entropy inequality cited through Yomdin–Comte and the real introductory formulas in Comte–Halupczok. No nonarchimedean theorem is substituted for the real statement. The other inherited references are retained with their earlier scope; the revision does not claim that every cited book or every paper in the repository was freshly re-audited.

The shared-state theorem, its unrestricted joint converse and its scheduling consequences are the concrete new claims submitted for review. A wider search or a subsequent referee may identify closer prior formulations. The comparison here does not use absence of a found identical theorem as a proof of originality.
