\section{Convex collision energies and phase boundaries}\label{sec:tree-convexity}
For completeness we give the finite allocation consequence of
Theorem~\ref{thm:collision-tree}. This consequence, including nested
optimizers and the ordering of phase boundaries, is recorded in the
technical note \cite{A1v9note}; it is not an additional independent
contribution of the shared-memory theorem.

\begin{proposition}[Convexity of the collision energies]\label{prop:tree-convexity}
Under the hypotheses of Theorem~\ref{thm:collision-tree}, set
$\beta_0=\beta_1=0$ and write $\beta_\ell=\beta_{m,\ell}$ up to
the number $q$ of distinct labels along the path. Then
\[
 \beta_{\ell+1}-\beta_\ell\ge\beta_\ell-\beta_{\ell-1}
                 \quad(1\le\ell<q).
\]
Optimal subsets for these energies can be chosen nested in $\ell$.
For a past cap $p\le q$ the adjacent asymptotic power boundaries of the
checkpoint law, with $M$ of order $\theta^{-b}$, are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:convex-phase-boundaries}
 b_\ell=\ell\beta_{\ell+1}-(\ell+1)\beta_\ell,
       \quad1\le\ell<p,
 \qquad b_\ell\ge b_{\ell-1}\quad(2\le\ell<p).
\end{equation}
Equal consecutive boundaries give an intermediate regime of zero
length in the power parameter $b$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The leaf cost sequence is $(0,0)$ on its feasible range. At an internal
cluster the recurrence \eqref{eq:tree-dp} is
\[
 F_C(k)=\Delta_C\binom{k}{2}
       +\min_{\sum_Dk_D=k}\sum_DF_D(k_D),\qquad\Delta_C\ge0.
\]
Suppose inductively that each child sequence is discretely convex.
Its successive marginal costs are then nondecreasing. The minimum
convolution is obtained by taking the $k$ cheapest members of the union
of the child marginal lists. Ties can be resolved respecting predecessors
within each list; the selection then corresponds to a feasible child
allocation. Every other allocation selects $k$ members and cannot have
a smaller sum. Its successive marginal costs are therefore the sorted
union of the child lists, a nondecreasing sequence. Adding
$\Delta_C\binom{k}{2}$ adds $\Delta_C(k-1)$ to the $k$th marginal
and preserves monotonicity. This additional term does not change the
minimizing allocation at fixed $k$.

The prefix-respecting selection gives nested minimizing child counts.
By the inductive nested-subset assertion in each child, their union
can be chosen nested at $C$. Induction from the leaves to the root proves
both assertions for $F_{\mathrm{root}}(\ell)=\beta_\ell$.

For $M\asymp\theta^{-b}$ the $\ell$th regret term has power
$2(\beta_\ell+b)/\ell$ in $\theta$. Equality of adjacent powers
gives the first formula in \eqref{eq:convex-phase-boundaries}.
Subtraction gives
\[
 b_\ell-b_{\ell-1}
   =\ell(\beta_{\ell+1}-2\beta_\ell+\beta_{\ell-1})\ge0.
\]
The ordered adjacent intersections describe the lower envelope of these
powers: below the next intersection the smaller adjacent index has the
smaller power, and above it the larger index does. Iterating this
comparison across the ordered boundaries proves that no nonadjacent
term creates an intervening open regime. A repeated intersection thus
has no interval on which the intermediate index alone is active.
The past cap stops the list at $p$. These statements concern power
orders; fixed comparison constants do not specify exact integer
thresholds.
\end{proof}
