\title[Uniform collision-threshold laws]{Uniform collision-threshold laws and marked response
 in a periodic Lorentz gas}
\author{Qian Qi}
\date{September 9, 2026}
\subjclass[2020]{37D50, 37A50, 60G55, 70H09}
\keywords{Lorentz gas, collision thresholds, specular trajectories, marked counting laws,
 hyperbolic periodic orbit, parameter response}
\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
For a triangular periodic array of circular scatterers, we determine the
onset of every collision-count tail under the full equilibrium preparation.
If $g=1-2R$ is the shortest flight and $\chi=\arcosh((1-R)/R)$, then
$\Pr\{N_{jg+\varepsilon}=j+1\}$ has leading coefficient
$3\lambda_R/(2R\sinh(j\chi))$ times $\varepsilon^2$.
The relative remainder is $O(\varepsilon)$ uniformly in every integer
$j\ge1$ and in compact radius intervals in $(0,1/2)$.
We prove the corresponding source-dependent expansion, with summable
bounds for each fixed mixed derivative of the radius, source and threshold
coordinate. The argument localizes the whole threshold event to alternating
specular itineraries and solves their boundary-value problem with constants
independent of their length. A trace-class determinant comparison controls
the exponentially small flux Jacobian without losing relative accuracy.
We also identify the limiting joint impact record and its sampling-time cuts.
The quadratic onset persists at every order, in contrast with the
order-$j+1$ onset for independent roofs having the same one-flight marginal.
The threshold coefficients recover the instability of the normal period-two
orbit. The previously established fixed-window two-collision response is
retained as a complete companion.
\end{abstract}
