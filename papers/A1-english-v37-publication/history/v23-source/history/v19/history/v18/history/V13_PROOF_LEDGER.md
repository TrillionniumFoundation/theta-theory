bounds c_-,c_+, reference mu_0, and positive interior margin lambda.

1. `|f|<=1` and `mu(f)=0`, so `(1 +/- epsilon f)mu` are probabilities.
2. `epsilon*c_+ <= lambda` keeps both priors in the same dominated family.
3. Every formal test lies in [0,1], so its moment moves by at most epsilon.
   A name already within delta/2 remains delta-accurate for both alternatives.
4. For every acquired history of length n, the posterior dominates

5. The variational formula for variance therefore bounds posterior variance

   selected independently after encoding, and all its trials are counted.
7. Both acquired-history laws dominate `(1-epsilon)` times the reference law.
   The same-data predictor has the same conditional kernel. The exact
   squared-loss identity yields the unconditional lower bound.
8. Combine this with the center's full intrinsic memory lower bound. Transfer
