\begin{abstract}
We classify the information attainable in positive finite-horizon
monomial experiments and the memory required for its causal retention.
Maximal Vandermonde volumes of the formal future exponents determine
the optimal prediction regret at every integer label budget, uniformly
through additive collisions. The upper bound is realized by one filter
charged after every report. For the same shared-latent experiment, we
characterize common controllers exactly by a compact one-step
implementability body and finite occupancy polynomials. This yields an
attained finite-moment formula for the optimal mean checkpoint risk,
exact removal of private transition randomization for atomless command
laws, and polyhedral optimal updates for scalarized risks. The controller
domain remains fixed at collisions. We also give finite global
certificates with arbitrary specified precision and evaluate an exact
continuous-command two-label experiment with matching global bounds.
\end{abstract}
