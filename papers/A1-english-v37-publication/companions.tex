\begin{abstract}
We give complete developments accompanying the collision classification
for positive monomial experiments. They include a transfer principle
from attainable geometry to causal compression and its monomial
verification, a prior-uniform covariance classification for bounded
finite observation algebras, structural and exact-kernel theorems,
other attainable geometries, operational reconstruction, uncertainty,
and effective realization. Each result retains its own hypotheses.
All statements and proofs are included; the elementary saturated
algebra geometry is distinguished from delayed acquisition in the
monomial problem.
\end{abstract}
