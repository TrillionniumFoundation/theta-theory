\begin{abstract}
We study acquisition and whole-table certification for periodic dispersing
billiards from intrinsically marked endpoint laws. On a compact analytic
class with numerical shape, symmetry and geometric margins, we give an
explicit finite fingerprint separating all bounded oriented bridge classes
coexisting in one table. Its order and separation are computed from the
stated priors by quantitative continuation and rigid-motion estimates.
A finite vector of local boundary values has the same recognition property.
Under certified local chart-value and distance-to-solid access, this vector
and intrinsic endpoint offsets can be acquired without derivative or
arclength queries. Independent bounded-time launches then discover a
saturated witness with positive uniform hazard, without a supplied period
lattice, complete-body catalogue or periodic-gate installation oracle.
Two growing endpoint histograms per record retain absolute normalization
under laboratory-square preparation. Their geometric inverse and completion
defect yield simultaneous whole-table validity at all stopping epochs.
We separate launch, local-measurement and range costs, and state the tail
exponent required for finite expected probe work. The explicit constants
need not be practical; the result remains conditional on quantitative
analytic priors and active local geometric access. All preceding local,
registered, moment and relative-law conclusions are retained.
\end{abstract}
