`pi(l,l') = 1_{l'=l+r} w_l w_{l+r} / sum_h w_h w_{h+r}`.

## 2. Pointwise roof inversion: what the new extraction establishes

New module 90 addresses the raw coarea side, not another occupation-torus average. It uses the inherited collision differential, action identity, general regular-word critical classification, guard construction and cumulative-return tail.

The stopped guard is the product of one-collision guards only through the actual terminal collision `m=N_n`. It has no separate factor removing `p=0`, and does not inspect future collisions after `m`. The guard remains conservative: its auxiliary zeros may exclude regular points as well as genuine singular or decision boundaries. Such points remain in the explicitly retained nonnegative remainder source.

The proof supplies:

* a quantitative Morse chart at each guarded critical point, with the initial-coordinate Hessian `R [[AC,BC],[BC,BD]]` and determinant `R^2 BC`;
* intrinsic right roof jets `a_0=2 pi g(0)` and `a_1=pi Delta g(0)` for the local source;
* the explicit edge `1_{s>=0} exp(-s) [a_0+(a_0+a_1)s]`, whose Fourier transform has the two rational terms `a_0/(1-ib)` and `(a_0+a_1)/(1-ib)^2`;
* matching of both the density and its first derivative, so the second distributional derivative of the residual has no edge atom;
* summation over all guarded center words and section decisions below `L`, without requiring separation of their critical roof values;
* the full finite-cutoff bound `sum_z (|a_0,z|+|a_1,z|) + sum_label ||q_label||_{W^{2,1}} <= exp{C(L+1)log(C/epsilon)}`;
* the exact source decomposition `p=e+q+r`, with `r>=0` and mass at most `C[exp(an-c_0 L)+(L+1)epsilon^(1/16)]`;
* an explicit pointwise residual-inversion error at bandwidth `B` of at most `A_{L,epsilon}/(pi B)`.

The compatible coefficients stabilize on each fixed critical word with positive margins for every guard factor. In particular, the stabilized physical leading coefficient on the actual section is `1/(2 c R sqrt(B_m C_m))`. The stronger phrase “every regular critical point” without the guard qualification is not used: unused discriminants and auxiliary coordinate zeros can be present at a physically regular point.

This improves the earlier geometric extraction, which removed the normal strip instead of extracting its critical jets. It is not yet the full uniform common correction. The infinite critical sum, the singular/decision/auxiliary-boundary source, and the full raw high-roof-frequency contribution are not bounded at the required long-time pointwise scale. A small mass of `r` is used only for an exceptional-output-set estimate, never as a pointwise upper bound on `r`.
