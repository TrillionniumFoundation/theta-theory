\section{Pointwise removal of deep and numerous incidence witnesses}
\label{sec:v111-witness}
We apply the marked pressure theorem to the unchanged positive
persistent source of Section~\ref{sec:v110-persistence}. The new
payment concerns its value at the original target roof, rather than
an average in that roof. Clearance witnesses require a separate
estimate and will not be identified with incidence witnesses.

\begin{lemma}[Domination of the surviving coefficient at every roof]
\label{lem:v111-domination}
For $\gamma\ge1$, on every original physical segment and every
point of its closed interval,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v111-domination}
       0\le c_{\rm per}^{\varepsilon,\gamma}
                         \le f^\varepsilon(u,\theta)\le A(u,\theta).
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Total variation bounds the decrement from an endpoint attaining
$c^\varepsilon$, so $f^\varepsilon(u)\ge c^\varepsilon-\mathcal V^\varepsilon$.
If $c_{\rm per}>0$, its value is
$c^\varepsilon-\gamma\mathcal V^\varepsilon
\le c^\varepsilon-\mathcal V^\varepsilon$. If $c_{\rm per}=0$,
nonnegativity suffices. The last inequality is the unchanged bound
$0\le W^\varepsilon\le1$.
\end{proof}
Thus a target-dependent Borel restriction of the persistent paired
source is bounded by the corresponding restriction of the original
uncut roof measure. This assertion concerns the surviving coefficient,
not the full endpoint minimum on an arbitrary segment.

For $\varepsilon>0$, use precisely the incidence witness thresholds
already forced by the original guard:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v111-incidence-witnesses}
 E_i^\varepsilon=\{c_i<\eta_i^\varepsilon\},\qquad
 \eta_i^\varepsilon=2\varepsilon q_0^{d_i/4},\quad
 d_i=\min(i,m-i),\quad 1\le i<m.
\end{equation}
Write $N^\varepsilon=\sum_{i=1}^{m-1}\one_{E_i^\varepsilon}$.
There are at most two indices at each depth. Hence, for an integer
$L\ge1$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v111-depth-sum}
 \sum_{d_i\ge L}\eta_i^\varepsilon
 \le\frac{4\varepsilon q_0^{L/4}}{1-q_0^{1/4}},\qquad
 \sum_{i=1}^{m-1}\eta_i^\varepsilon
 \le A_\varepsilon:=
                  \frac{4\varepsilon q_0^{1/4}}{1-q_0^{1/4}}.
\end{equation}
For any Borel target event $E$, let
\[
 \mathsf U_B^{\rm per}[E](t)=\int
 c_{\rm per}^{\varepsilon(B),\gamma_B}(\theta)
 \one_{\{a<t<b\}}\widetilde H_B(\Psi(\theta,t))
                   \one_E(\Psi(\theta,t))\,d\theta.
\]
These are restrictions of the source in
\eqref{eq:v110-source-identity}; no new normalization is used.

\begin{theorem}[Uniform target-roof witness estimates]
\label{thm:v111-witness-payment}
For every finite $m$, every $B$ and integers $L,s\ge1$,
\begin{align}
 \|\mathsf U_B^{\rm per}[\bigcup_{d_i\ge L}E_i^{\varepsilon(B)}]\|_\infty
 &\le \frac{4C_\star K_\star\varepsilon(B)}{1-q_0^{1/4}}
                         q_0^{L/4},\label{eq:v111-deep-finite}\\
 \|\mathsf U_B^{\rm per}[N^{\varepsilon(B)}\ge s]\|_\infty
 &\le C_\star\frac{(K_\star A_{\varepsilon(B)})^s}{s!}.
                                      \label{eq:v111-many-finite}
\end{align}
The norms are essential suprema on the whole roof line; constants
are uniform in the radius and original exact labels.

Fix any $\eta>0$ and define, at finite count,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v111-collision-depth}
 \begin{split}
 L_m&=\left\lceil\frac{4(2+\eta)}{|\log q_0|}\log(m+1)\right\rceil,\\
 s_m&=\left\lceil\frac{4\log(m+e)}{\log\log(m+e^e)}\right\rceil,\\
 \mathcal D_{B,m}&=\bigcup_{d_i\ge L_m}E_i^{\varepsilon(B)},\qquad
 \mathcal A_{B,m}=\{N^{\varepsilon(B)}\ge s_m\},\qquad
 \mathcal Z_{B,m}=\mathcal D_{B,m}\cup\mathcal A_{B,m}.
 \end{split}
\end{equation}
An empty union contributes zero. For every fixed outer $B$ and $M$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v111-zero-height}
                 \mathcal H_M(\mathsf U_B^{\rm per}[\mathcal Z_{B,m}])=0.
\end{equation}
More precisely the deep part satisfies, at every count,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v111-deep-rate}
 \sup_{R,n,k}m^2
 \|\mathsf U_B^{\rm per}[\mathcal D_{B,m}]\|_\infty
       \le C\varepsilon(B)(m+1)^{-\eta}.
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By Lemma~\ref{lem:v111-domination}, each source in the first two
assertions is bounded by the original uncut density restricted to
the same target event. Apply Corollary~\ref{cor:v111-witness-moments}
with $s=1$ to the depth tail and with general $s$ to all indices,
then use \eqref{eq:v111-depth-sum}. This proves the two finite-count
bounds after the physical sum, with no selected uniform witness.

They hold for every $L$ and $s$ at every $m$. Substituting $L_m$
in the first gives
$q_0^{L_m/4}\le(m+1)^{-(2+\eta)}$, which proves
\eqref{eq:v111-deep-rate}. For fixed $B$, put
$a=K_\star A_{\varepsilon(B)}<\infty$. The elementary factorial
bound $s!\ge(s/e)^s$ gives
\[
 m^2\frac{a^{s_m}}{s_m!}
 \le\exp\{2\log m+s_m(1+\log a-\log s_m)\}
                       =m^{-2+o(1)}\longrightarrow0.
\]
Here $s_m\log s_m=(4+o(1))\log m$ and $s_m=o(\log m)$.
The case $a=0$ is immediate. If $s_m>m-1$, the event is empty.
Subadditivity and positivity complete the proof of
\eqref{eq:v111-zero-height}.

The depth and multiplicity thresholds in this proof are substitutions
into finite-count inequalities valid simultaneously for all their
values. No fixed-window spectral estimate is evaluated at a
collision-dependent window. The original outer quantities
$\varepsilon(B),h_B,r_B,\gamma_B,K_B$ remain fixed before the
collision limit. Thus the proof does not exchange that limit with
any outer width or band.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The retained source after this pointwise payment}
Define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v111-retained-source}
 \begin{split}
 \mathsf U_B^{\rm loc}&=\mathsf U_B^{\rm per}[\mathcal Z_{B,m}^{\,c}],\\
 \mathsf U_B^{\rm exc}&=\mathsf U_B^{\rm per}[\mathcal Z_{B,m}],\qquad
 \mathsf R_B^{\rm loc}=(\mathsf U_B^{\rm loc}-K_BP_{r_B}^{\rm ng})_+.
 \end{split}
\end{equation}
The superscript $\mathrm{loc}$ records localization of all incidence
witnesses to the two collision-end layers; it makes no claim that
clearance witnesses are localized to those layers. The comparison
pool is the same full nongrazing pool, without a source-dependent
restriction.

\begin{corollary}[Exact preservation and collision-zero residual difference]
\label{cor:v111-residual}
At finite count the original persistent source satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v111-exact-preservation}
 \mathsf U_B^{\rm per}=\mathsf U_B^{\rm exc}+\mathsf U_B^{\rm loc},\qquad
 0\le\mathsf R_B^{\rm per}-\mathsf R_B^{\rm loc}\le\mathsf U_B^{\rm exc}.
\end{equation}
For every fixed $B,M$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v111-residual-height}
 \mathcal H_M(\mathsf R_B^{\rm per}-\mathsf R_B^{\rm loc})=0,
 \qquad
 \mathcal E_M\le\mathcal H_M(\mathsf R_B^{\rm loc})+C_MB^{-1/384}.
\end{equation}
The unchanged raw pointwise assertion is equivalent to
$\mathcal H_M(\mathsf R_B^{\rm loc})\to0$ for every fixed $M$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The source partition is an identity of nonnegative original
submeasures. Monotonicity and the unit Lipschitz bound of the
positive-part map give its residual inequality. Apply
\eqref{eq:v111-zero-height} and then
Theorem~\ref{thm:v110-residual}. For necessity use
$0\le\mathsf R_B^{\rm loc}\le\mathsf U_B^{\rm micro}$ and the
retained scalar equivalence. Every removed term has been retained
in the identity and paid in the target essential-height topology.
\end{proof}

For all sufficiently large $B$, $2\varepsilon(B)<c_\circ$, so the
original endpoint section cap rules out incidence witnesses at
$i=0,m$. At a target point with positive surviving coefficient the
original guard still requires an incidence or a clearance witness,
by Theorem~\ref{thm:v110-persistence}. On the retained part, every
incidence witness has depth below $L_m$, and fewer than $s_m$ such
witnesses occur. A clearance witness may occur at any original
flight, with its original positive weight. These alternatives allow
a witness which changes with the roof; they assert no common witness
on an entire physical segment.

The precise remaining estimate is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v111-remaining}
 \lim_{B\to\infty}\limsup_{m\to\infty}\sup_{R,n,k}m^2
 \esssup_{t\in\mathcal C_{m,R,n,k}(M)}
       (\mathsf U_{B,m}^{\rm loc}(t)-K_BP_{r_B,m}^{\rm ng}(t))_+=0.
\end{equation}
The target-roof pressure theorem proves a new physical weighted
summation and the pointwise removal in
Theorem~\ref{thm:v111-witness-payment}; it does not prove
\eqref{eq:v111-remaining}. In particular the all-incidence bound
$C\varepsilon$ furnished by \eqref{eq:v111-deep-finite} with $L=1$
is unscaled. At a fixed outer $B$ it cannot be multiplied by $m^2$
and declared small. Nor does an inverse-incidence insertion estimate
an inverse third-disk clearance. The remaining source includes the
original shallow incidence witnesses, unvisited tangencies, critical
entrances, section cuts and capacity deficiencies which are not paid
by the proved estimates. No change of the original theorem or of its
arithmetic classes is made in stating this boundary.
