## Narrow additional primary check: BDL and the erratum

Primary sources read on October 18, 2026:

- [Published BDL article, author-hosted copy](https://faculty.fairfield.edu/mdemers/research/2018.02.16.bflow.invent.final.pdf), Section 1.1, printed pages 43--45, especially Corollary 1.3.
- [Authors' February 2020 erratum](https://www.faculty.fairfield.edu/mdemers/research/2020.02.28.erratumBDL.pdf), Sections 1--3.

The article's stated setting has disjoint positively curved C3 scatterers and finite physical horizon on a Euclidean torus. Corollary 1.3 gives mixing for Holder tests. The paper distinguishes the generator's spectral information from a spectral gap for the time-one operator. No radius-uniform constant or exact-label roof-density theorem is obtained merely by citing that corollary.

The erratum changes the admissible stable-curve condition and neutral-norm interpretation. It weakens the claimed smooth-function embedding and adds continuity of the flow-direction derivative to the hypotheses used in Theorem 1.2. These are flow-space corrections, not the collision-map H2--H4 conditions audited in the newer Demers--Zhang paper.

The precise inherited implication used in module 11 is elementary once fixed-radius flow ergodicity on the actual physical quotient is supplied: a nontrivial invariant collision set would suspend to a nontrivial invariant flow set. Thus collision ergodicity, and saturation of a positive-measure section, follow. This uses no uniform mixing rate and changes neither the section nor the physical clock. The Euclidean metric on the triangular quotient must be preserved; a non-isometric affine change followed by use of the standard Euclidean reflection rule is not a legitimate model identification. This follow-up does not independently reconstruct the entire flat-torus/function-space adaptation.

The frozen literature audit remains a truthful earlier snapshot with three primary theorem/object checks and Boyd background. This later, limited BDL check is not retroactively described as a complete BDL certification. The remaining historical applications and the named physical inputs retain their accurately declared status.
