For clarity, the prescribed-count raw theorem still needs compatible
bounds for the three quantities in
\eqref{eq:roof-box-raw-terms} and
\eqref{eq:roof-box-raw-budget}: the combined local edge correction,
the finite-band residual integral, and the second-derivative sum at
a linear count cutoff. The analogous quantities for the isotropic
kernel are defined separately in
\eqref{eq:rate-preserving-raw-terms}. Changing a kernel requires a
new edge estimate. Fixed-packet constructible finiteness alone gives
neither estimate uniformly in $n$ and $R$.

The physical conditioning theorem further needs weighted versions for
its actual indicator class, a raw-scale denominator asymptotic, and a
relative comparison of the two physical events. A same-event marked
estimate divides by its identical probability and does not replace an
event. An $L^q$ clock comparison likewise does not establish that
relative replacement. For the all-moment result the order $d$ is fixed,
with $\rho_d=1/2$ for odd $d$ and $\rho_d=1$ for even $d$.
The induced covariance statement is Cesaro convergence, whereas
absolute correlation summability is established on the collision clock.

The local-space norm identifications are recorded in
Appendix~\ref{app:collision-norm-map}. The source packet also contains
separate maps for the qualitative product geometry, finite-record
preparation, phase defects, fixed-order cumulants and multiscale
stopping. These identify the external inputs used by the respective
proofs; source/build checks are not independent continuum verification.
