### 7.2. Clearance-only witnesses

## 10. Specific mathematical routes still available

The previous report identified several routes. They remain open because revision 113 contains no new mathematics.

### Route A: target-roof clearance summation

Develop an analogue of the incidence marked-pressure theorem for clearance witnesses. This requires an exact weight for a clearance event, physical multiplicity control, handling of a third disk not visited by the trajectory, depth bookkeeping, and a roof-fiber summation theorem. The proof must not replace clearance by incidence or count arbitrary disks without a weighted geometric bound.

### Route B: direct source-side clearance payment

Construct a positive source identity in which the clearance-supported coefficient is dominated by an original uncut geometric measure with a collision-uniform target-roof estimate. The source selector must stay at the original roof and the comparison capacity must not be spent twice.

### Route C: low-load incidence scarcity

Prove that shallow low-load incidences are themselves collision-uniformly scarce in the exact-label pointwise topology. The existing unscaled marked-pressure bound is insufficient; a new gain tied to physical depth, geometric exclusion, or exact-event structure is needed.

### Route D: capacity abundance

Prove that the full nongrazing comparison pool has enough capacity on the retained low-load source at a schedule-compatible fixed window. Finite-count exhaustion as the comparison window shrinks is in the wrong quantifier order and cannot be substituted for this estimate.

### Route E: hybrid positive decomposition

Partition the retained source into clearance-only, low-load-incidence, critical/section, singular-endpoint, and capacity-deficient classes. Prove a normalized positive payment for each class before summing. Every class must retain its original coefficient until its own estimate is established.
