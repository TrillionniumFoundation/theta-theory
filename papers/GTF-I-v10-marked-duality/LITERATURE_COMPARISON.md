# Nearest-neighbour comparison — v10

**Audit date:** 23 September 2026. This file distinguishes primary theorem-text inspection from bibliographic/abstract inspection. It is not a proof that no other antecedent exists. The bibliography in the article provides the cited editions.

## Stochastic-team topology and randomization

**Naci Saldi, arXiv:1711.00639. Primary PDF inspected:** policy topology in Section III; Theorems 6, 7 and 9 and their compactness/static-reduction arguments. The weak-star topology for policies and existence technique are established antecedents. V10 specializes the product topology to a common finite register across tasks, proves uniform continuity of the entire compact row-risk function and constructs public-seed representatives. The novelty assertion is not that weak-star policy compactness was invented here.

**Serdar Yüksel and Naci Saldi, arXiv:1609.07685v2. Primary PDF inspected:** Theorem 2.1 on strategic-measure convexification and randomization, and Theorems 2.6–2.8 on existence and closure issues. The public-mixture convex hull and private nonconvexity are acknowledged. V10's strict mixture example is a worst-row problem, not a contradiction of the scalar expected-cost randomization result. Our product domination hypothesis is used explicitly rather than assuming conditional-independence constraints are weakly closed.

## Causal coding and coordinator information

**Serdar Yüksel, arXiv:1010.4824v4. Primary author text inspected:** Theorems II.4–II.6, the posterior/common-information definitions and the associated proof discussion. These are lossless structural reductions of causal source coding to posterior/common-output information. Such information is generally not a fixed finite retained register. V10 fixes and charges the executable state before optimization and couples one encoder across tasks and parameter rows; it does not claim the original structural separation theorem.

**Nayyar, Mahajan and Teneketzis, arXiv:1209.1695. Primary PDF inspected:** coordinator formulation, Theorem 3 and Corollary 5. Their common-information dynamic program is an antecedent of the v9 offline occupancy recursion. An offline designer occupancy is not an online memory state. The v10 compact proof does not convert that offline object into free encoder memory.

**Witsenhausen (1979), DOI 10.1002/j.1538-7305.1979.tb02263.x:** bibliographic record and the theorem as reproduced in Yüksel's Theorem II.1 were inspected. The complete original proof was not freshly inspected. This entry is not counted as a full primary-proof audit.

## Filtered experiments and robust finite controllers

**Norberg (2002), DOI 10.1524/strm.2002.20.14.1:** the filtered-experiment antecedent is explicitly acknowledged. Retrieval of the original full article was unsuccessful. Its complete randomization/deficiency theorem is not claimed to have been excluded by a proof-level priority comparison. The article therefore does not claim to originate causal comparison itself. This remains a specific bibliographic boundary for independent re-review, not a missing premise in the self-contained v10 proof.

Recent robust finite-state-controller methods are relevant to the surrounding computational literature, including Galesloot et al., *Pessimistic Iterative Planning for Robust POMDPs* (arXiv:2408.08770), and Hudák et al., *Finite-State Controllers for (Hidden-Model) POMDPs using Deep Reinforcement Learning* (arXiv:2602.08734). Primary abstracts were inspected, not all proofs or algorithms. They are not used to assert a priority exclusion or a polynomial-time lossy synthesis result. V10 supplies an exact compact variational theorem and a separate exact-state output-sensitive compiler, not a competitive controller-learning algorithm.

## Continuation spaces and positive states

**Stefan Kiefer, arXiv:2009.01217v1. Primary text inspected:** Proposition 2.1 and Theorem 2.3, including the forward-space span construction and equivalence algorithm. The new compiler uses this classical rational linear-algebra mechanism. Its output is a positive normalized continuation quotient, not a minimal signed weighted automaton. The rank-two Bernoulli example proves that linear rank cannot replace retained-state cardinality. Tzeng's 1992 equivalence algorithm is credited via its publisher record and Kiefer's account; the original full Tzeng proof was not freshly audited.

Predictive-state and automaton equivalence also underlie the conceptual history of the quotient. The new necessity proof is stated for operational restart prediction risks with the actual target mark, including randomized state overlap, rather than asserting that all predictive-state minimality is new.

## Application mechanisms

**Bäuerle and Rieder, arXiv:1504.03530v3; Math. Oper. Res. 42 (2017), 1180–1196. Primary PDF inspected:** Theorem 3.3, the joint hidden-state/accumulated-cost augmentation, and Section 4.4's exponential specialization. Tilted information is classical. V10's latent-phase calculation makes the shared action and finite-memory interface explicit and verifies its own finite model; it does not claim to discover risk-sensitive filtering.

**Nisio, Publ. RIMS 12 (1976), 513–537, DOI 10.2977/prims/1195190727:** official EMS/J-STAGE bibliographic record checked. The original general semigroup theorem is an antecedent, not a new contribution claimed here. V10 proves its finite-dimensional comparison, range condition and marked mesh directly. It does not invoke an unread theorem to establish an infinite-dimensional kinetic domain.

## Claim submitted for judgment

The article's proposed contribution is the conjunction of an **executable common bounded-register** risk-function theorem for dominated standard-Borel observations, its compact/countable row variants and width-uniform marked approximation, and an **operationally characterized marked continuation quotient** with output-sensitive positive-state synthesis. The exact three-task witness and model consequences test the quantifiers and interfaces. Classical minimax, separation, team compactness, automaton elimination and filtering/control mechanisms remain explicitly credited. This audit improves the nearest-neighbour comparison; it does not certify top-four originality.
