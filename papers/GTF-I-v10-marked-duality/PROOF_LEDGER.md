## Analytic checks still requiring independent scrutiny

The weak-star argument uses domination on a product of report-time spaces; it is not a general closure theorem for singular strategic measures. The marked minimality theorem uses a deliberately explicit, operational restart category; it is not a statement about every abstract simulator. Output-sensitive synthesis may still have exponentially many positive states. The application proofs discharge their stated model hypotheses, not all historical microscopic extensions. Priority beyond the named theorem-level comparisons is not established by this ledger.
