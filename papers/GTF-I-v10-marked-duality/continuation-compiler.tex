\section{Synthesis from continuation spaces}
\label{sec:v10-synthesis}

The continuation quotient can be computed without first expanding
all controlled histories. Its linear dimension and its number of
positive states are different quantities; the algorithm uses the
former to test equivalence and the latter to bound its output.

Assume the finite marked instrument of Section~\ref{sec:v10-marked}
has rational data. At time $t$ write $n_t=|\Theta|\,|Z_t|$ and
$n=\max_t n_t$. Its block-diagonal transition matrices are
\[
 M_{t,a,y}((\theta,z),(\theta',z'))
 =\1_{\{\theta=\theta'\}}K_{\theta,t}(z',y\mid z,a).
\]
The terminal columns are
$e_{\theta,w}(\theta',z)=\1_{\{\theta=\theta'\}}
L_\theta(w\mid z)$. Define rational continuation spaces backwards:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v10-spaces}
 V_T=\operatorname{span}\{e_{\theta,w}:\theta,w\},\qquad
 V_t=\operatorname{span}\{M_{t,a,y}v:a,y,\ v\in V_{t+1}\}.
\end{equation}
They have dimensions at most $n_t$, and contain the all-ones column.
For the latter assertion sum the terminal columns, and then use
$\sum_yM_{t,a,y}\1=\1$ for any fixed $a$.

\begin{lemma}[Finite linear test for a marked class]\label{lem:v10-span}
Two normalized posterior rows $\alpha_t(h),\alpha_t(h')$ determine
the same marked continuation class if and only if they agree on
$V_t$. It suffices to compare their evaluations on any rational basis
$B_t$ of that space.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The probability of a specified future report word and terminal pair
$(\theta,w)$ under an action word is the row $\alpha_t(h)$ applied
to the column
\[
 M_{t,a_t,y_{t+1}}\cdots M_{T-1,a_{T-1},y_T}e_{\theta,w}.
\]
These columns span exactly $V_t$, by induction in
\eqref{eq:v10-spaces}. Equality of all these probabilities is precisely
Definition~\ref{def:v10-continuation}. Equality on their span is
equivalent to equality on a basis.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Output-sensitive exact synthesis]\label{thm:v10-synthesis}
Let $B$ be the total binary length of the rational source description,
including any finite target augmentation and the positive reference
prior. The marked quotient, one representative posterior per class,
and its deterministic transition tables can be constructed in time
polynomial in
\[
 B+T+n+\sum_t|A_t|\,|Y_{t+1}|+\sum_{t=0}^TK_t.
\]
No factor equal to the number of expanded histories is required.
The stored transition table has
$\sum_{t<T}K_t|A_t||Y_{t+1}|$ entries, including null entries.
A bit-serial implementation retains
$\lceil\log_2K_t\rceil$ state bits, with finite transient workspace
and a finite table-lookup running-time bound determined by this table.
This is an exact-state synthesis theorem, not a polynomial-time
algorithm for optimal lossy common-encoder design.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Compute the spaces \eqref{eq:v10-spaces} backwards by rational
elimination. At each stage there are at most
$|A_t||Y_{t+1}|n$ candidate columns. Retain independent columns, rather
than repeatedly replacing the basis by arbitrary normalized linear
combinations. Each retained column is then a product of at most $T$
input matrices with a terminal column. A common denominator for all
input probabilities bounds the binary length of its entries by a
polynomial in $B,T,n$. Fraction-free elimination, or determinant
bounds for rational elimination, therefore takes polynomial bit time.

Start at the initial posterior $\alpha_0=(\pi(\theta)\nu_\theta(z))$.
At stage $t$ maintain one posterior representative $\alpha$ for each
class found. For every $a,y$, form
\[
 r=\alpha M_{t,a,y}\1.
\]
If $r=0$, record the null transition. Otherwise form
$\alpha'=\alpha M_{t,a,y}/r$, evaluate $\alpha'B_{t+1}$, and compare
this vector with the stored keys at $t+1$. Reuse its label if equal;
otherwise store a new label and the representative $\alpha'$.
By Lemma~\ref{lem:v10-span}, the key is the marked continuation class.
The successor result ensures that all histories represented by
$\alpha$ have this same transition, even though their full hidden
posteriors can differ. Thus retaining one representative loses no
reachable class. Induction over $t$ proves that precisely the $K_t$
classes are generated.

There are only the displayed number of transitions to examine.
Every representative is the normalized posterior of some history of
length at most $T$. The numerator and denominator are finite sums
of products of rational input probabilities. Their binary lengths
are polynomial in $B,T,n$, even if their magnitudes are very small.
Exact comparison of keys, either by pairwise search or rational
sorting, and all matrix operations consequently take polynomial
bit time in the stated input and output parameters.

The executor needs only the class label and a fixed time-indexed
table. A sequential scan of that finite table, with binary counters
and the current action/report held transiently, is one explicit
implementation. It has program length bounded by the table length
times the binary length of an entry, and polynomial transient
workspace and scan time in the table description. A more efficient
random-access convention may improve this bound, but is not needed
for finite effectivity. Report acquisition remains a separate
interface cost.
\end{proof}

The invariant-space computation is the classical weighted-automaton
equivalence method; see Kiefer~\cite{Kiefer2020}, Proposition 2.1 and
Theorem 2.3, and Tzeng~\cite{Tzeng1992}. What is being synthesized
here is a \emph{positive, normalized, marked causal quotient}, not a
minimal signed linear representation. Linear dimension alone would
not give its persistent-state cost.

\begin{example}[Small linear rank and growing positive width]
\label{ex:v10-bernoulli}
Let $\Theta\in\{0,1\}$ have positive prior and, conditional on it,
let the reports be independent Bernoulli variables with parameters
$p\ne q$ in $(0,1)$. Retain $\Theta$ as a terminal mark. The hidden
linear dimension is two at every time. After $t$ reports, the posterior
odds for a history with $k$ successes are the initial odds multiplied by
\[
 (q/p)^k\bigl((1-q)/(1-p)\bigr)^{t-k}.
\]
These $t+1$ values are distinct since $p\ne q$. The parameter posterior
is a marginal of the marked continuation law, and conditional future
laws depend only on this posterior. Hence $K_t=t+1$, whereas the
expanded report tree has $2^t$ leaves. The algorithm constructs the
quotient in polynomial time in $T$, without mistaking rank two for
width two.
\end{example}
