# Literature and attribution — A2 v16

The full v15 bibliography is retained, with two additions: the latest independent memorandum and Levin–Peres, with contributions by Wilmer, *Markov Chains and Mixing Times*, second edition (AMS, 2017). The authors' book PDF, Proposition 4.7, was checked for the ordinary total-variation/coupling characterization: https://pages.uoregon.edu/dlevin/MARKOV/mcmt2e.pdf . The proof in the manuscript supplies its own measurable density coupling and stopped-kernel argument; no novelty is claimed for the elementary coupling inequality.

The scalar linearization attribution remains Sternberg and the review of the smooth one-dimensional theorem in Eynard-Bontemps–Navas, arXiv:2212.13646v2. The latter's low-regularity failures are not used as a substitute for the smooth theorem. The analytic normal-form comparison remains De Simoi–Kaloshin–Leguil, arXiv:1905.00890v4, and the corresponding *Inventiones Mathematicae* article. These primary sources were consulted during this revision.

The exact rational mixed-parameter benchmark is attributed to the source-pinned A2 v15 memorandum, not to the present revision as an independent discovery. Existing attributions for deautoconvolution, two-flight comparison and earlier supplied benchmarks remain in the bibliography and mathematical text.

This is a targeted attribution check, not an exhaustive priority search or a claim that no related adaptive experiment theorem exists in the literature. The mathematics newly added here is presented as an application and extension of the physical relative theorem in the specified experiment.
