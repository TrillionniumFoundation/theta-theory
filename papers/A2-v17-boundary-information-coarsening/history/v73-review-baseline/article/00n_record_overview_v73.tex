\subsection{What is determined when failures are retained}

The same mechanism identifies the fibers of the local experiment, not
only an injective contact coordinate map.  Let $\pi_{N,b}$ be the
success probability and let $f_{N,b}$ be the conditional density on its
specified gate.  The exact phase-volume formula has the central anchor
\[
 \pi_{N,b}f_{N,b}(0,0)=\frac{D_{N,b}d_b}{2\pi\mathcal A},
\]
where $\mathcal A$ is the free area of the marked cell.  Both the offset
and the Jacobi normalization on the right are determined by the limiting
laws.  Thus the unconditioned record recovers the one global scalar
which conditioning had canceled.

\begin{theorem}[The complete charged local invariant]
\label{thm:v73-main}
Under the hypotheses of Theorem~\ref{thm:v72-main}, the germs of the
charged reset experiments determine exactly the smooth reflecting
contact germs, the fixed offsets and the free cell area.  Equality of
these three quantities is also sufficient for equality of all sufficiently
localized finite-flight records.  Conditional limiting laws alone
correspond to the first two quantities.  One phase's normalized success
prefactor, together with these laws, recovers the area.

On the bounded classes of Theorem~\ref{thm:v72-sampling}, a measurable
estimator taking values in actual table--offset pairs recovers all three
components from $rB$ charged preparations.  With the explicit conditions
and returning-length choice in Corollary~\ref{cor:v73-budget}, its
profile, offset and logarithmic area error is at most
\[
 C t\log(e/t),\qquad t=(L_B/B)^\beta+\delta^\gamma,
\]
outside an event of probability at most $2\zeta$.  Here $\beta,\gamma$
are the exponents in Theorem~\ref{thm:v72-sampling}.  The count used for
area recovery is part of the same record, not an extra preparation group.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Proposition~\ref{prop:v73-area-anchor} proves the exact anchor.
Theorem~\ref{thm:v73-fibers} combines it with smooth contact recovery
and exact locality to identify both directions of the observation fibers.
Lemma~\ref{lem:v73-log-twist} retains the flight-length amplification
of curvature error in the rare normalization.
Theorem~\ref{thm:v73-charged-invariant} and
Corollary~\ref{cor:v73-budget} give the jointly realizable finite
construction and its displayed rate.
\end{proof}

The conclusion separates a local geometric invariant from a global
normalizing invariant within one physical experiment.  Equal-area
remote deformations remain exact fibers of the record.  The theorem
therefore recovers the full object identifiable by this observation
without converting a contact theorem into a whole-table assertion.
Its content is the combination of relative physical normalization,
actual smooth separation and the unconditioned anchor; the scalar
identity by itself is not a substitute for either analytic interface.
