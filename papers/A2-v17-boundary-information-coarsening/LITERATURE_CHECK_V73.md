## Position of the new conclusion

The phase-volume anchor is an elementary consequence of the manuscript's exact Liouville subdensity at the marked central trajectory. It is not offered as a new general phase-volume identity. The complete-invariant statement additionally requires the actual smooth inverse and exact local completion calculation. Its finite estimate reuses classical density/count concentration and measurable near-minimum selection, with a new finite realizable-image interface and an explicit logarithmic twist sensitivity. These elementary tools are not relabeled as independent general theories.

The novelty claim to be assessed is the demonstrated physical synthesis and its precise geometric consequence: actual smooth contact germs, fixed unreported offsets and free cell area classify the charged local observation, and are jointly estimated by an actual table–offset pair under the stated preparation model. Whole-table smooth determination, minimax sharpness, efficient inversion and an exhaustive first-in-literature claim are not asserted.
