## New proof responsibilities

`article/23j_generic_finite_channel_rigidity_v45.tex` proves the finite-distance and separation lemma, obstruction descent with a gap budget, finite full-deck skeleton reduction to N+1 channels, fixed-gate persistence, selected-channel locality, a graph-theoretic minimal count, open dense obstacle asymmetry, generic finite-channel determination, explicit image-stage cochain reconstruction, and its qualified compact-family physical corollary. The introductory theorem repeats the main consequence without replacing these proofs.

The graph walk is not a physical multi-edge orbit. Tangencies are obstructions. Chosen independent gain vectors need not be an integer basis. Fourier coefficients belong to recovered complete images, not added observations. Genericity is relative support-topological genericity, not a measure statement; C² neighborhoods are not compact analytic families. No unmarked-channel discovery, effective analytic-continuation rate, or ordinary marked-length inverse is inferred.
