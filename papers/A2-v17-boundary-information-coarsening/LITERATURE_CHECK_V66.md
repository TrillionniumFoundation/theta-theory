S. Bolotin and D. Treschev, *Hill's formula*, arXiv:1006.1532, Russian Mathematical Surveys 65 (2010), 191–257, DOI 10.1070/RM2010v065n02ABEH004671. Primary record: https://arxiv.org/abs/1006.1532 ; primary PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1006.1532 . ## Marked length asymptotics

P. Bálint, J. De Simoi, V. Kaloshin and M. Leguil, *Marked Length Spectrum, homoclinic orbits and the geometry of open dispersing billiards*, arXiv:1809.08947, DOI 10.1007/s00220-019-03448-x. Record: https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.08947 . Author-hosted primary PDF: https://leguil.perso.math.cnrs.fr/Articles/Balint_DeSimoi_Kaloshin_Leguil.pdf . Theorem D, Corollary E and Remark 2.3 on printed pp. 9–10 were read and the two pages were inspected as screenshots. These address general-word length asymptotics and marked Lyapunov information; the remark discusses separating individual higher-period contact parameters from those asymptotics. Here a supplied polygon and phase-resolved function laws provide individual action responses. No reduction from their length data to our boundary laws is proved or claimed.

## Analytic and enriched-data rigidity

J. De Simoi, V. Kaloshin and M. Leguil, *Marked Length Spectral determination of analytic chaotic billiards with axial symmetries*, arXiv:1905.00890, DOI 10.1007/s00222-023-01191-8; https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.00890 . The primary record retains the analytic open-billiard symmetry/genericity assumptions. Our fixed-polygon positive-curvature uniqueness has different observations. It does not remove their assumptions for their problem.

D. Finamore and M. Leguil, *A CAT(0)-approach to the marked length spectral rigidity of Sinai billiards*, arXiv:2510.18983; https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.18983 . The fetched primary record describes an enriched marked-length datum in the finite-horizon Sinai setting. That enriched datum is not identified with our phase-resolved conditional densities.

A. Florio and M. Leguil, *Smooth conjugacy classes of 3D Axiom A flows*, arXiv:2010.04120v5; https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.04120v5 . The version-5 notice explicitly removes the affected earlier geometric spectral-rigidity assertion after an error in Proposition 3.1 while retaining dynamical conclusions. We do not cite the removed geometric assertion as an established comparison theorem.

## Consequence for the present framing

The new exact global theorem removes a closeness assumption inside the supplied-polygon boundary-law model. It does not yield marked-length spectral rigidity, unmarked reconstruction, or a new general-period acquisition rate. The manuscript explains this distinction beside its main theorem. Neither these targeted comparisons nor the referee's placement judgment establish priority, redundancy or exceptional significance.
