# Cover letter for A2, revision 66

Dear Editor,

We submit the revised manuscript *Boundary laws and rigidity of periodic dispersing billiards* for further mathematical assessment. The paper studies the nonlinear geometry retained by conditional endpoint laws of increasingly long billiard bridges. Its principal mechanism combines a relative physical boundary law, obtained before normalization of an exponentially rare event, with the actual stationary response of the reflecting boundaries.

The latest author-requested referee-style report found no new mandatory core repair within its stated coverage, but remained unconvinced by the exceptional-significance case. We have responded by reorganizing the opening around that mechanism and distinguishing the supplied-polygon, weaker alternating, and charged acquisition models. We also prove a substantive strengthening: a boundary Schur complement yields a globally single-valued positive-curvature inverse with an observable contraction algorithm. The signed higher-order response then gives exact marked analytic contact identification, and under fixed lattice and full visitation exact obstacle-image identity, without assuming the candidates are close.

This exact strengthening does not globalize the analytic norm estimates. The complete local function-space inverse and conditional real-law stability retain their analytic priors and radius loss. The original weaker-data alternating reconstruction, unregistered lattice results and preparation-budget theorems are retained with their full proofs and their separate hypotheses.

The principal article and full technical manuscript contain the complete new proof, and the companion is retained. A point-by-point response, dependency ledger, historical derivation audit and source-matched verification evidence accompany the revision. None is presented as a commissioned journal report, formal proof certificate or assurance of editorial acceptance.

Sincerely,

Qian Qi
