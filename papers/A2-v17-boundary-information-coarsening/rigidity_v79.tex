\begin{abstract}
We reconstruct generating actions from normalized endpoint laws with
unknown sampling weights.  Anchored log-density ratios give explicit
split coordinates on the observation quotient: every observation near
a nonconstant shared-weight law decomposes uniquely into an action and
a complementary residual.  This yields a derivative-preserving robust
inverse, a complete description of action-confounded clock-dependent
weights, and finite-record validation.  On bounded H\"older classes the
minimax action risk is the usual uniform nonparametric rate plus a sharp
linear misspecification term.  We also prove that six fixed deadline
laws of contact suspensions determine an arbitrary uniformly convex
negative-twist generating function on a prescribed compact square.
All acquisition settings are chosen from class bounds; no unknown action
is evaluated by the observation rule.  For marked dispersing billiards,
we retain the root-selection-free recovery of final-flight distances,
finite whole-boundary reconstruction and nonanalytic smooth examples.
Their finite-record geometric bound is extended to approximate weight
sharing on bounded fixed-atlas neighborhoods, with branch-resolution
costs charged to raw preparations.
\end{abstract}
