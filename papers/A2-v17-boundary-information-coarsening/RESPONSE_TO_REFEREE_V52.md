`dot Z_W = integral_W (dot w H + w dot H)`.

The derivative of H includes both origin transport terms. The new proof then follows the finite-order action and graph-jet derivatives, analyticity of the support variation on a smaller common strip, the actual continuously followed alignment branch, and the lattice cochain.
