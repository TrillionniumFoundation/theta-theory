## The missing functional step and its resolution

The earlier derivation had all-order smooth jet factorization and an analytic inverse. It correctly did not identify arbitrary smooth germs from equal Taylor series. The referee explicitly preserved that distinction. Revision 68 uses additional information already present in the observation: equality of the complete real action functions.

For a segment between two local graph representatives, the future tail starting at its first collision is the unique half-line at the next phase. This gives a composition of one-step contractions, rather than merely an estimate with an uncontrolled leading constant. The actual envelope can then be integrated along the segment. Its initial graph evaluation has a uniformly positive coefficient. A function vanishing to order `m` loses a factor `a^(jm)` at the `j`-th later visit. At a fixed sufficiently large finite order the weighted tail is dominated by the initial evaluation. Flatness supplies finiteness of the weighted norm; the full action identity makes that norm vanish.

The argument therefore completes a **functional** inverse that was not supplied by the graded recursion alone. For noisy real data, the same route first aligns the finitely many low jets by a small polynomial and then controls the remaining complete profile. A finite-smoothness prior provides the uniform differentiated estimates. The new flat equal-area family tests the exact distinction this step resolves.
