\begin{abstract}
We study normalized endpoint laws of the form $a(T-W)/\int a(T-W)$,
where the action $W$ and the positive joint weight $a$ are unknown.
For three distinct clocks, the quotient of these laws by common positive
reweighting is identified with the space of absolute actions.  Determinism
and a positive stationary Hessian then recover one-step generating
functions from a prefix and its extension, without a supplied root-selection
mark.  In a dispersing billiard, an initial-free-time-independent source
and clock-independent retention produce these laws.  For a finite
identifying experiment with exact branch, scalar and lift labels, they
determine complete smooth boundary patches and their Euclidean placement;
finite-horizon tables with locally nonconic boundaries admit whole-table
experiments.  As a second application, a fixed six-law action-threshold
protocol recovers an unknown mass and a whole smooth potential on a
uniformly convex discrete mechanical class.  Quantitative twist and
acceptance bounds retain the cost of long prefixes.  On bounded billiard
classes, smoothing, root solving and patch blending give a constructive
finite-record inverse with an actual geometric output.
\end{abstract}
