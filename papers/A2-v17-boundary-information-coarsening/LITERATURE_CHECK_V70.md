## Positive comparison internal to the paper

The actual equal-area flat family proves that the full boundary-law record separates tables whose contact and action Taylor records agree to every order. The finite charged experiment supplies a test at each fixed nonzero profile separation once its explicit error radius is small enough. This is a same-model comparison established in the manuscript. It is not a constructed pair with equal full marked spectra, a statement about normalized density jets, or an inexpensive uniform test at vanishing contact scale.

The main introduction identifies the relative physical normalization and the actual smooth-contact envelope as the geometric contribution. Kernel approximation, measurable minimum-distance selection, concentration and rate balancing are classical ingredients of the acquisition consequence. No first-priority, optimal-rate, universal-acquisition or automatic journal-placement claim is inferred from the targeted check.
