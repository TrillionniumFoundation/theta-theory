\begin{abstract}
We study the recovery of geometric generating systems from clocked
endpoint records with unknown sampling weights and imperfect branch
labels.  When successful records and failed preparations are both
retained, two deadlines determine an absolute action and its raw weight,
including constant actions.  A calibrated full-rank channel permits
incorrect positive word and lift labels: inversion must act on joint
subdensities before normalization.  The resulting action data recover
marked periodic dispersing billiards and, on covered phase domains,
exact return systems from relatively prime iterates.  For a uniformly
convex negative-twist class, four fixed raw deadline laws recover an
unknown two-variable generating function on a prescribed compact square.
We give a finite-order regularity budget and a finite-raw-record
geometric estimate including calibration, coordinate and retention
errors.  Smooth Bernoulli retention realizes the nuisance tangent;
compensation along noncongruent billiard deformations proves a sharp
linear physical efficiency floor.  The three-conditional-law quotient,
its action--residual split, and its sharp abstract action-risk theory
are included for archives in which success masses have been discarded.
\end{abstract}
