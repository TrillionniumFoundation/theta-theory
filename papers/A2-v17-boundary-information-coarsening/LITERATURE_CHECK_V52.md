# Primary-source comparison — A2 v52

Checked September 15, 2026. This is a targeted, version-sensitive comparison, not an exhaustive priority search. The revision adds no external theorem as an unproved premise of the billiard inverse.

## Dependence and association

**Holland and Wang.** P. W. Holland and Y. J. Wang, *Dependence function for continuous bivariate densities*, Communications in Statistics — Theory and Methods 16(3) (1987), 863–876, DOI `10.1080/03610928708829408`. The publisher's bibliographic and abstract record was checked. The mixed logarithmic dependence invariant used in Section 14 is attributed, not claimed as a new invariant. This check is not a full proof audit of the external article. Primary record: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/03610928708829408.

**Osius.** G. Osius, *Asymptotic inference for semiparametric association models*, Annals of Statistics 37(1) (2009), 459–489, DOI `10.1214/07-AOS572`, arXiv:0903.0702v1, March 4, 2009. The versioned record and the opening odds-ratio formulation in the primary PDF, including its rendered printed page 2, were checked. Separate marginal-factor invariance is established context; no asymptotic efficiency or sampling-equivalence theorem from that framework is transferred to the present window experiment. Primary record: https://arxiv.org/abs/0903.0702.

The actual centering and anchored-action formulas needed here are proved internally. In particular, separate unequal factor cancellation is already present in the inherited four-density calculation. The window extension changes the retained part of the record; it does not invent a second association invariant.

## Billiard rigidity records

**Finamore–Leguil.** *A CAT(0)-approach to the marked length spectral rigidity of Sinai billiards*, arXiv:2510.18983. The current primary record lists v1, October 21, 2025. Its finite-horizon Sinai-billiard result uses an enriched marked length spectrum. That is not the selected conditional endpoint-law datum here. Primary record: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.18983.

**De Simoi–Kaloshin–Leguil.** *Marked Length Spectral determination of analytic chaotic billiards with axial symmetries*, arXiv:1905.00890, current v4, August 17, 2022, DOI `10.1007/s00222-023-01191-8`. Its analytic open-billiard setting includes the stated non-eclipse, symmetry and genericity hypotheses. No reduction between its marked lengths and the present record is established or used. Primary record: https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.00890.

**Florio–Leguil.** *Smooth conjugacy classes of 3D Axiom A flows*, arXiv:2010.04120v5, June 3, 2021. The version notice explicitly removes an earlier geometric spectral-rigidity assertion affected by an error while retaining dynamical results. The existing bibliography remains version-pinned, and the removed assertion is not a premise of this manuscript. Primary record: https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.04120v5.

These records neither prove that the present law inverse is already known nor justify describing it as simply dropping hypotheses from a marked-length theorem. Observation maps, regularity conditions and supplied markings have to be compared separately.
