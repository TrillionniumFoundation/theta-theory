`recorded interaction -> unknown transverse origins -> separately reweighting-invariant signed action -> actual smooth contact jets -> analytic images -> the same finite periodic reconstruction fiber`.

Its differentiated version uses two simple roots on fixed positive slices before the finite action inverse. The degree-by-degree graph recursion then feeds the controlled analytic support variation and the actual local congruence/lattice cochain. The projection of the joint nuisance-and-table derivative kernel onto table tangents is exactly the common Euclidean tangent; no full nuisance identifiability is claimed.

The mixed log derivative and odds ratio are classical association invariants, explicitly cited to Holland–Wang and Osius. The manuscript proves the identities it needs and imports no semiparametric efficiency or sampling-equivalence result. The specific boundary-law shape makes the two contact origins visible; this is what permits the observation extension. Complete positivity, known physical transverse units/directions, known excess time and gaps, and marked design remain explicit.

The proof does not differentiate a moving cap boundary in a global strong density norm, extract arbitrary derivatives from total variation, infer differential injectivity from exact uniqueness, assume all base symmetries persist, assume a unimodular gain matrix, or convert a finite law-map extraction into a Markov kernel acting on individual samples.
