## Zelditch: published theorem and its orbit-local mechanism

Steve Zelditch, *Inverse spectral problem for analytic domains, II: Z2-symmetric domains*, Annals of Mathematics 170 (2009), 205–269; DOI [10.4007/annals.2009.170.205](https://doi.org/10.4007/annals.2009.170.205).

Primary sources: [publisher page](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/2009/170-1/p06) and [published article](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/wp-content/uploads/annals-v170-n1-p06-p.pdf). The published introduction, the definition of `D_{1,L}` and Theorem 1.1 on printed pp. 207–208 were inspected. The displayed theorem and definition on p. 208 were also visually checked in the PDF. The separate dihedral statement, Theorem 1.4 on p. 211, was checked to avoid conflating the two classes; it is not the theorem invoked in our new comparison.

Theorem 1.1 is a Dirichlet or Neumann spectral determination statement within the specified simply connected analytic symmetry class. Its assumptions include an involution reversing a nondegenerate bouncing-ball orbit, multiplicity-one iterated lengths and further endpoint/nonresonance conditions. The proof uses localized wave-trace invariants and recovers boundary coefficients under the specified symmetry. Our text cites the named class rather than suggesting that arbitrary analytic symmetric domains satisfy the theorem.

**Manuscript use:** credit the earlier orbit-local, all-order mechanism and distinguish the endpoint-law observation, relative normalization and signed smooth inverse. No reduction between spectral and law data, no removal of the spectral theorem's hypotheses and no blanket originality claim are inferred from this comparison.
