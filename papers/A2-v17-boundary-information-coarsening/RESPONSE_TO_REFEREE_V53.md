## R52-Q1 — From the functional benchmark to actual tables and an attained scale

The report's quadratic functional benchmark is credited at the start of the new section, in the acknowledgments, and in a frozen bibliography entry. Lemma 23.1 proves the same leading constant for even actual-smooth actions with nonzero higher coefficients:

`H^2(Q_{0,h},Q_{1,h}) = 7(a_1^2-a_0^2)^2 h^8/(16200 d^4) + O(h^10)`.

Here `a_i=S_i''(0)`. The calculation uses only `S_i(u)=a_i u^2/2+O(u^4)`, and explicitly expands the window normalizer. The leading coefficient is independent of the fourth and higher even jets; those jets have not been set to zero.

Theorem 23.2 realizes the premise with

`k_s(theta)=R+s(cos(4 theta)-1)`, `R=1/10`, `s in [R/128,R/64]`, `L=I_2`.

Curvature radius is at least `3R/4`, every body lies in the radius-`R` disk, and all nonzero center displacements have length at least one. The two axis channels have the same gap `4/5` and are uniformly clear of all third obstacles. Reflection and quarter-turn symmetries identify the four same-type actions and make them even. The existing half-line Hessian formula gives

`a_s^2 = kappa_s^2+2 kappa_s/g`, `kappa_s=(R-16s)^{-1}`.

The two endpoints of the parameter interval give `a_0^2=7800/49`, `a_1^2=1900/9`, hence a fixed nonzero difference `22900/441`. These are actual nonlinear billiard actions on a common analytic collar, not a claimed realization of exact quadratics. The perimeter changes, so the two obstacles are not congruent. All-lattice separation and clearance are proved geometrically, not inferred from a finite simulation.

For these two laws the theorem proves all three regimes. If `n h^8` tends to zero, testing becomes asymptotically random. If it tends to a positive finite `lambda`, the optimal equal-prior error is asymptotically `Phi(-sqrt(lambda Jcal)/2)`, with `Jcal=7(a_1^2-a_0^2)^2/(4050 d^4)`. If it diverges, a bounded statistic `F(z,w)=1/9-z^2 w^2` gives consistent testing with exponential error in `n h^8`. The same mean test, with the midpoint of the two exact means as threshold, attains the critical error. The likelihood expansion retains its cubic remainder and the two different signs of the limiting mean.

This makes `h^{-8}` an attained two-point successful-record scale, not merely a necessary one. It is not asserted to be the minimax rate over all geometries and all profiles, nor a guarantee for a test which has not been given two specified alternatives. No lower bound is asserted for the richer experiment retaining waiting counts or acceptance probabilities.
