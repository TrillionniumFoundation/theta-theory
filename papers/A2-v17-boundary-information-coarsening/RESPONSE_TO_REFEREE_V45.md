## 2. R44-E1, first reservation: the local mechanism and its consequences

The relative determinant and smooth functional-remainder arguments remain essential. The extension does not replace either with the rank-one density identity, a formal jet calculation, or cycle algebra. Its dependency chain is:

1. A selected clear channel gives the relative nonlinear bridge law, with normalization before taking the rare-event limit.
2. The single-offset four-density cancellation recovers both unsymmetrized actions without knowing the boundary amplitudes.
3. The actual smooth finite-remainder inverse recovers the two complete contact jets.
4. Analytic continuation determines connected complete obstacle images in separate channel frames.
5. Generic obstacle asymmetry, the finite clear skeleton, and two independent marked gains register those images and recover the lattice in one common frame.

The extension supplies a broad geometric domain on which the last step is available. It does not assert that the first three steps are routine or newly reprove every inherited local theorem. We revisited their relevant hypotheses, especially the difference between a globally minimum-gap count event and a specified alternating channel event. The new selected-locality lemma proves that a selected clear channel need not minimize the gap of the entire table. Small excess over its own `jg_e` onset localizes the already specified alternating event; the weighted local proof then applies with its original higher-norm conventions.

## 3. R44-E1, second reservation: availability beyond one realization

This is the principal mathematical change.

**Obstruction descent.** If the unique closest segment between two lifted convex bodies meets a third body, including tangentially, the two replacement gaps sum to at most the original gap. Each is smaller by at least the uniform positive separation. Induction therefore terminates in clear pairs; the total terminal gap is bounded by the original gap. This is a statement about an incidence graph, not a specular multi-channel orbit or its physical travel time.

**Finite full-deck skeleton.** Apply descent to `N−1` seed pairs reaching all obstacle types and two seed pairs reaching the primitive deck translates of a root obstacle. Their union is finite, connected, and has closed gains generating the marked deck group. An explicit distance-sublevel bound controls all candidate translate labels. No finite spatial simulation is used in place of the all-translate proof.

**N+1 channels.** A spanning tree leaves fundamental non-tree gains generating all closed gains. Retain two independent such gains. The selected graph has exactly `N−1+2=N+1` edges, including loops when `N=1`. The two chosen gains need not be unimodular; reconstruction uses their actual invertible integer matrix `M`, with `L=VM^{-1}`. The count is minimal for a connected graph carrying two independent gain cycles. It is not a universal information-theoretic lower bound.

**Persistent admissibility.** Closest contacts have a positive definite two-endpoint Hessian. Selected clear contacts have strict all-translate clearance. Distinct clear incidences on an obstacle cannot have the same closest point, because the nearer target would obstruct the farther segment. Finite fixed reference gates can consequently be chosen with positive margins and persist under small C² geometric perturbations. The physical gates are not moved using an unknown lattice.

**Open dense signature rigidity.** A centered support has trivial proper rotational symmetry precisely when the indices of its nonzero harmonics have greatest common divisor one. A finite subset already witnesses this property, proving openness. Small independent perturbations of the second and third harmonics prove density while preserving analyticity, positive curvature and separation. A further explicit nonzero phase polynomial gives an open dense sublocus with no individual reflection symmetry either. Higher analytic harmonics are unrestricted.

**Full intrinsic inverse.** Every complete oriented contact signature is unique on an obstacle with trivial proper symmetry. The selected tree and both cycles can therefore be placed in one starting channel frame using recovered images, without an additional registration oracle. The original single-offset global theorem then determines the complete table and unknown lattice. A competing realization is not separately assumed asymmetric; it inherits the same recovered complete images. The entire signed datum may be quotiented by one common orientation reversal, as before.

These arguments extend the v44 nonvacuity demonstration to every positively separated periodic table at the skeleton level and an open dense class at the law-to-table level. The v44 construction and its four-channel redundancy are retained as a concrete illustration; they are not deleted merely because three channels can suffice for the connected two-cycle mechanism when `N=2`.

## 4. R44-E1, third reservation: the physical experiment

The distinction between intrinsic law data and the richer position records is unchanged. The direct graph-sampling benchmark, charged pilot, capped acquisition, and fresh-stage budget convention remain in the manuscript. We do not argue that the difficult intrinsic inverse is necessary for reconstruction by every richer sensor.

A new corollary applies the existing physical theorem on compact analytic subfamilies lying inside one persistent skeleton neighborhood and satisfying the original uniform analytic and record assumptions. It does not infer analytic compactness or uniform arbitrarily high derivatives from a C² neighborhood. It does not insert an uncharged discovery step for the selected marked channels. A compact set crossing multiple design neighborhoods is not claimed to have one common design without further argument.

On the open dense harmonic locus, an explicit formula recovers relative rotations from the second and third coefficients of *already recovered complete images*. Their center-displacement cochain then gives the lattice and placements for arbitrary `N`. Its local Lipschitz bound is in the uniform support-image topology. It is not an effective endpoint-law-to-entire-analytic-boundary rate, a total-variation inverse bound, or a sharp analytic minimax theorem.
