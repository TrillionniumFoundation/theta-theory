# A2 v57 — targeted primary-record check

Checked September 15, 2026. This is a scope and attribution check, not an exhaustive priority investigation or a new proof audit of the cited papers. No new external theorem is introduced into the principal geometric proof by this revision.

**Finamore–Leguil.** *A CAT(0)-approach to the marked length spectral rigidity of Sinai billiards*, arXiv:2510.18983v1, October 21, 2025. The current primary record lists v1. The enriched datum and Theorem A were also read on printed PDF pages 4–5. The datum is indexed through the lifting-surface construction and is not simply the ordinary list of periodic-orbit marked lengths. The stated rigidity theorem concerns finite-horizon Sinai billiards with the same enriched spectrum. No reduction to or from the present conditional boundary-law datum is claimed. Primary record: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.18983 ; PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.18983 .

**De Simoi–Kaloshin–Leguil.** *Marked Length Spectral determination of analytic chaotic billiards with axial symmetries*, arXiv:1905.00890v4, August 17, 2022; DOI 10.1007/s00222-023-01191-8. The primary record identifies analytic open billiards with non-eclipse and the stated symmetry/genericity hypotheses. This is a different observation problem, not a theorem made redundant or generalized solely by comparing hypothesis lists. Primary record: https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.00890 .

**Osius.** *Asymptotic inference for semiparametric association models*, Annals of Statistics 37 (2009), 459–489, DOI 10.1214/07-AOS572; arXiv:0903.0702v1. The record states the odds-ratio association framework without restricting marginals. It supports the attribution of marginal cancellation to established background, not the derivation of the nonlinear billiard boundary law or its smooth geometric inverse. Primary record: https://arxiv.org/abs/0903.0702 .

**Florio–Leguil.** *Smooth conjugacy classes of 3D Axiom A flows*, arXiv:2010.04120v5, June 3, 2021. The version-specific primary comment records removal of the earlier geometric spectral-rigidity assertion affected by an error while retaining dynamical conclusions. No removed geometric assertion is used as a premise. Primary record: https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.04120v5 .
