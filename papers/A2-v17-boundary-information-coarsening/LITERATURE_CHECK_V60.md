# Primary-source scope check — A2 v60

Records accessed September 16, 2026. This is a targeted scope and attribution check, not a priority survey or a re-proof of the external papers.

**Lloyd N. Trefethen,** *Quantifying the ill-conditioning of analytic continuation*, BIT Numerical Mathematics 60 (2020), 901–915, DOI `10.1007/s10543-020-00802-7`; arXiv `1908.11097`. The publisher and Oxford records were checked, and the arXiv PDF's first page was viewed. The paper distinguishes unrestricted ill-posed continuation from conditional control with a complex-domain bound. The present elementary interpolation lemma is proved internally and is not advertised as a new general analytic-continuation principle. Primary records: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10543-020-00802-7 and https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.11097.

**Douglas Finamore and Martin Leguil,** *A CAT(0)-approach to the marked length spectral rigidity of Sinai billiards*, arXiv `2510.18983`, whose current primary record lists v1, October 21, 2025. It concerns finite-horizon Sinai billiards and enriched marked length data. No equivalence with the conditional boundary-law datum is inferred. Primary record: https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.18983.

**Jacopo De Simoi, Vadim Kaloshin and Martin Leguil,** *Marked Length Spectral determination of analytic chaotic billiards with axial symmetries*, arXiv `1905.00890v4`, August 17, 2022; related DOI `10.1007/s00222-023-01191-8`. Its analytic open-billiard class has non-eclipse and stated symmetry/genericity assumptions, and a different observation. Primary record: https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.00890v4.

**Gerhard Osius,** *Asymptotic inference for semiparametric association models*, Annals of Statistics 37 (2009), 459–489; DOI `10.1214/07-AOS572`, arXiv `0903.0702`. The primary record supplies established association-model context with unrestricted marginals. It does not supply the billiard relative law or the action-to-geometry inverse. Primary record: https://arxiv.org/abs/0903.0702.

**Anna Florio and Martin Leguil,** *Smooth conjugacy classes of 3D Axiom A flows*, arXiv `2010.04120v5`, June 3, 2021. The explicit correction notice removes an affected geometric spectral-rigidity assertion while retaining dynamical results. That removed assertion remains excluded as a premise or competing established geometric theorem. Primary record: https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.04120v5.
