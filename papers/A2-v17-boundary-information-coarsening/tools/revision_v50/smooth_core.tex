The passage from smooth graphs to finite jets is a separate analytic
step in this inverse.  For two actual graph pairs with the same gap
and the same $M$-jet, interpolate the graphs and differentiate a
finite stationary action sum.  Interior orbit variations cancel;
the terminal term is retained until the weighted half-line estimates
make it tend to zero.  Integrating this finite identity in the
interpolation parameter before taking its limit gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v50-smooth-filtration}
 |S_b^{[1]}(u)-S_b^{[0]}(u)|
       \le \frac{C_M}{1-\rho^{M+1}}|u|^{M+1}.
\end{equation}
Thus the finite action jet factors through the finite graph jet
independently of the smooth representative.  At degree $n\ge3$,
a boundary visit is counted once and every interior visit twice.
The geometric sums give the explicit last-jet block
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v50-jet-block}
 M_n=\begin{pmatrix}
 \coth(n\gamma)&\mathfrak r_0^n\operatorname{csch}(n\gamma)\\
 \mathfrak r_1^n\operatorname{csch}(n\gamma)&\coth(n\gamma)
 \end{pmatrix},\qquad \mathfrak r_0\mathfrak r_1=1,
 \qquad \det M_n=1.
\end{equation}
The positive factors $\mathfrak r_b$ are the alternating type ratios
of the linear half-line orbit in \eqref{eq:v22-linear-coefficients}.
Write $q_n=(\psi_0^{(n)}(0),\psi_1^{(n)}(0))^t$ for the two
anchored graph coefficients and $s_n=(S_0^{(n)}(0),S_1^{(n)}(0))^t$.
After the lower-order contribution $R_n$ has been computed, the
recursion is $q_n=M_n^{-1}(s_n-R_n)$.  All these operations are
performed at a fixed finite order, with the required functional
boundary bounds.  No infinite-order conditioning estimate is inferred
from the determinant.  Equality of every smooth contact jet is not
identified with equality of arbitrary smooth boundary germs; the
subsequent whole-image assertion uses analyticity.
