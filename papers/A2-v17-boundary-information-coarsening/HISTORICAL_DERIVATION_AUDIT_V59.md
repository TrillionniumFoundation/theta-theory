## New mathematical source and proof boundary

`article/23a2_analytic_contact_inverse_v59.tex` contains all new proofs. The first lemma constructs the half-lines on an open ball of bounded holomorphic graph functions with fixed leading jets, using only graph values at the initial endpoint and derivatives at protected internal arguments. The second lemma passes from finite envelopes to a norm-convergent derivative formula; its finite-rank approximation is on the same disc. The theorem explicitly inverts the entire derivative by a finite-low/high-tail splitting, proves the nonlinear inverse by contraction, and records the induced analytic quotient-norm and coefficient estimates.

The fixed-disc analytic norm is stronger than the old real smooth or probabilistic norms. Real nearby graph pairs are convex on a smaller physical collar; arbitrary complex-neighborhood elements are not claimed to give convex entire boundaries or globally admissible periodic tables. The existing exact-global, nuisance-window, differential and acquisition conclusions retain their own classes. They are preserved, not freshly certified by this audit. In particular, the entire adaptive/probability catalogue, all full-phase prerequisites, global continuation and the companion were not independently re-proved in this round.
