## Corrected planar source

L. Noakes and L. Stoyanov, *Lens Rigidity in Scattering by Unions of Strictly Convex Bodies in R2*, SIAM J. Math. Anal. **52** (2020), 471–480, DOI [10.1137/19M1270409](https://doi.org/10.1137/19M1270409). The primary publisher record confirms that the separate planar proof is required. The inspected theorem is [arXiv:1803.02542v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.02542v2), revised October 10, 2019, Theorem 1.1 on printed p. 3. Definitions of travelling times and scattering length spectra appear in the introduction. The PDF theorem page and definition pages were visually inspected in addition to parsed text.

The hypothesis uses finite disjoint compact strictly convex C3 domains. Travelling times are indexed by incoming position and direction on an enclosing circle; scattering length sets are indexed by the pair of incoming/outgoing directions. The equality notion is almost everywhere in the corresponding label space. The conclusion determines the entire compact union. Trapping is not excluded by a global nontrapping assumption. The earlier 2015 argument alone does not establish this planar theorem.
