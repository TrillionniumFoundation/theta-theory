## Primary records and page-level check

**Bálint–De Simoi–Kaloshin–Leguil**, *Marked Length Spectrum, homoclinic orbits and the geometry of open dispersing billiards*, Communications in Mathematical Physics 374 (2020), 1531–1575, DOI 10.1007/s00220-019-03448-x. Primary record: https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.08947. Author-hosted manuscript: https://leguil.perso.math.cnrs.fr/Articles/Balint_DeSimoi_Kaloshin_Leguil.pdf. Printed pages 9–10 were rendered and inspected in this round. Theorem D concerns length asymptotics for general periodic words, and Corollary E gives marked Lyapunov recovery. Remark 2.3 distinguishes the information in those relations from individual contact-parameter separation. That remark is not an impossibility theorem for all uses of the marked length spectrum. The present supplied polygon and real phase-resolved two-offset laws are different input, even when the output is now a smooth contact germ.

**De Simoi–Kaloshin–Leguil**, *Marked Length Spectral determination of analytic chaotic billiards with axial symmetries*. Primary record https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.00890, version 4 dated August 17, 2022; DOI 10.1007/s00222-023-01191-8. The analytic open-billiard statement uses suitable symmetry and genericity. The present removal of analyticity at visited contacts uses a different, explicitly functional endpoint-law observation; it is not advertised as removal of those hypotheses from the marked-length theorem.

**Finamore–Leguil**, *A CAT(0)-approach to the marked length spectral rigidity of Sinai billiards*. Primary record https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.18983, version 1 dated October 21, 2025. Its finite-horizon Sinai result uses an **enriched** marked length spectrum. We do not omit the enrichment or identify it with the two-offset endpoint law. No dominance or equivalence of these observations is proved here.

**Florio–Leguil**, *Smooth conjugacy classes of 3D Axiom A flows*. Primary version-5 record https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.04120v5, June 3, 2021. The notice identifies a mistake in Proposition 3.1 and removal of the affected geometric spectral-rigidity assertion. The retained dynamical conjugacy assertion is distinct. The removed geometric result is not cited as an available competing theorem.

**Bolotin–Treschev**, *Hill's formula*, Russian Mathematical Surveys 65 (2010), 191–257, DOI 10.1070/RM2010v065n02ABEH004671; https://arxiv.org/abs/1006.1532. The previously checked determinant/monodromy context remains in the bibliography. No fresh full-paper proof audit is claimed in this revision. It is not a substitute for the Dirichlet corner cofactor, relative physical law or actual graph-envelope inverse.

## What the new comparison does and does not show

The previous analytic example separates a nonlinear law from its specified leading hierarchy. The new smooth example separates a complete real law from **all formal graph and future-action jets**, with the polygon and free area fixed. These are internal information comparisons in actual billiard tables. Neither example constructs equal full marked length spectra.

The new smooth inverse addresses a genuine boundary between formal jet identification and functional geometry: a smooth flat difference cannot be removed by analytic continuation, but is detected and separated by the complete action identity and contracted visits. This explanation identifies the mathematical role of the extension. It is not an assertion that no related functional argument occurs in the literature, a proof of priority, or a guarantee of exceptional journal significance.
