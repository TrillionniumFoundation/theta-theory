\title[Stratified spectral laws]{Stratified spectral laws:\\ classification, uniform normal form, and exact constants}
\author{Qian Qi}

\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
For a binary boundary cubic family we classify the exact spectral fibre
across both channel ranks, prove a whole-model square-root law with a
uniform linear remainder, and compute its leading constant by finite
active-set formulas. The arguments include identifiable rank-one
regions isolated by a weight constraint and continuity across rank
changes. Exact rational examples evaluate the resulting constant.
\end{abstract}
