\begin{abstract}
We study long alternating collision bridges in smooth periodic dispersing
billiards, the labelled contact geometry encoded by their signed endpoint
records, and the nonregular information carried by the resulting moving
support.  On an offset collar independent of the number of flights, the
normalized mixed endpoint derivative has a uniform nonlinear relative limit,
including every fixed mixed geometric and offset derivative.  The limiting
physical flux factors into two half-line determinant amplitudes, which are
also the normalized scalar linearizing densities of the physical stable
returns.

Retaining signed endpoint positions recovers the two unsymmetrized half-line
actions from support thresholds.  Their quadratic terms together with
physical onset recover the gap and both contact curvatures.  At every order
$n\ge3$ the next pair of labelled graph jets enters the action jets through an
explicit $2\times2$ block of determinant one.  We prove the all-order formula
by a quantitative weighted inverse for the nonlinear half-line stationarity
operator, a finite-truncation envelope identity with vanishing tail, and a
homogeneous jet filtration which separates orbit derivatives from action
order.  The finite signed-jet map has an everywhere invertible differential
with a compact-uniform lower singular-value bound.  Hence arbitrary smooth
labelled contact jets, odd and even, are recursively determined without
reflection symmetry, equal contacts, or supplied curvatures.

For connected real-analytic obstacle boundaries this local inverse has a
table-level consequence.  A finite registered family of channels meeting
every obstacle label determines the entire labelled analytic billiard table:
each channel recovers the analytic germs at its two contacts, analytic
continuation recovers the complete participating boundary images, and a
spanning channel cover reaches every obstacle.

For a linearly vanishing density on a moving regular hypersurface, the local
family is generally non-dominated.  We separate its support-exclusive mass
and show that, at
$np_n\delta_n^2\log(1/\delta_n)\to1$, it is uniformly asymptotically
equivalent on compact local sets to a dominated common-collar LAN experiment
with intrinsic information matrix
\[
 \mathcal J_\Sigma=
 \int_\Sigma\frac{a_0VV^t}{|\nabla w_0|}\,d\sigma.
\]
Finite local subexperiments converge to the Gaussian shift on the identifiable
space $\operatorname{Ran}\mathcal J_\Sigma$; testing, contiguity and local
asymptotic minimax statements are formulated at their corresponding levels
without assigning a nonexistent finite-sample likelihood to the original
moving-support family.

We then realize this experiment on parameter-independent physical time
windows in one fixed laboratory chart.  Same-type designs use even flight
numbers.  In the anchored registered family
\[
 (-\psi_{0,\vartheta}(u),u),\qquad
 (g+\psi_{1,\vartheta}(v),v),
\]
the labels, transverse origin and tangent direction are fixed, and the
observed endpoints are literal laboratory coordinates.  Around a reference
gap $g_0$, the scaling
\[
 g=g_0+\delta_n a/j_n,\qquad \vartheta=\delta_nh
\]
makes gap and contact geometry enter the same first-order support velocity.
The exact fixed-window billiard endpoint family is compared with its ideal
moving-boundary family by an explicit Hellinger/Le Cam estimate at the
critical logarithmic scale.  The tangent inverse yields finitely many
positive same-type windows with positive-definite summed information on every
fixed finite labelled contact-jet model.

Preparation caps are chosen once from the reference success probabilities and
are independent of the compact local set; $j_n\delta_n\to0$ makes the same
caps eventually adequate on every fixed compact set.  If $k_n$ successful
endpoint records are collected and $k_n\tau^{j_n}\to0$, the complete stopped
transcript at the declared endpoint/residual-time record level---including
failures, designs and stopping times---is uniformly asymptotically equivalent
in total variation to its boundary transcript.  Every failed preparation is
charged.  A further endpoint-output coarsening inherits the Gaussian local
experiment and its minimax bound.  No total-variation comparison is asserted
for the complete growing collision array, and waiting counts are not declared
ancillary.

Thus one nonlinear boundary structure controls the long-bridge relative law,
an all-order determinant-one geometric inverse with a finite-channel analytic
global consequence, and a physically realized non-dominated endpoint
experiment on fixed observation coordinates.
\end{abstract}
