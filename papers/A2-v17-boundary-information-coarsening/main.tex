\begin{abstract}
We study long alternating collision bridges in smooth periodic dispersing
billiards, the local contact geometry encoded by their signed endpoint
records, and the statistical information carried by the resulting moving
support.  On an offset collar whose size is independent of the number of
flights, the normalized mixed endpoint derivative has a uniform nonlinear
relative limit, including every fixed mixed geometric and offset derivative.
The limiting physical flux factors into two half-line determinant amplitudes,
identified with the normalized scalar linearizing densities of the physical
stable returns.

Retaining the signed endpoint positions recovers the two unsymmetrized
half-line actions from support thresholds.  Their quadratic terms, together
with physical onset, recover the gap and both contact curvatures.  At every
order $n\ge3$ the next pair of labelled graph jets enters the action jets
through an explicit $2\times2$ block of determinant one.  Hence arbitrary
smooth labelled contact jets, odd and even, are recursively determined
without reflection symmetry, equality of the two contacts, or supplied
contact curvatures.  Real-analytic endpoint-law germs determine the two
participating analytic contact germs.

For a general linearly vanishing density on a moving regular hypersurface we
prove a self-contained vector boundary-LAN theorem with intrinsic information
matrix
\[
 \mathcal J_\Sigma=
 \int_\Sigma\frac{a_0VV^t}{|\nabla w_0|}\,d\sigma,
\]
at the rate $np_n\delta_n^2\log(1/\delta_n)\to1$, including the Gaussian
testing profile and the finite-dimensional local asymptotic minimax bound.
We then formulate the billiard experiment on parameter-independent physical
time windows and fixed registered endpoint coordinates.  Around a reference
gap $g_0$, the local scaling
\[
 g=g_0+\delta_n a/j_n,\qquad \vartheta=\delta_n h
\]
makes the unknown gap and the contact geometry enter the same moving-support
likelihood.  Signed rigidity implies that finitely many positive same-type
endpoint windows have a positive-definite summed information matrix on every
fixed finite labelled contact-jet model.  If $k_n$ successful endpoint records
are collected by a stopping rule and $k_n\tau^{j_n}\to0$, the complete
finite-bridge stopped transcript, with failures and stopping times retained,
is uniformly asymptotically equivalent in total variation to its boundary
transcript.  The endpoint-output coarsening therefore inherits the physical
LAN experiment while every failed preparation remains charged.  A separate
charged onset pilot is shown to justify the centered-window formulation when
desired.

For the fixed-table finite-versus-boundary comparison, determinant-one
whitening gives the exact logarithmic endpoint coefficient, while complete
records distinguish at the earlier exponential scale and success bits erase
the leading tangent shape information.  Thus the relative boundary law,
signed local rigidity and the raw physical local experiment are controlled by
the same boundary structure.
\end{abstract}
