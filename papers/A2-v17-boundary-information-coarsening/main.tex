\begin{abstract}
We study the geometry encoded by long alternating collision bridges in
periodic dispersing billiards.  On an offset collar independent of the flight
number, the normalized mixed endpoint derivative has a nonlinear relative
limit with every fixed mixed geometric and offset derivative.  The two
same-type signed endpoint supports recover the unsymmetrized half-line
actions.  The physical onset supplies the gap; their quadratic jets then
recover both contact curvatures.  At every order the next labelled graph
jets enter the next action jets through an explicit two-by-two block of
determinant one.  This gives an all-order contact inverse without reflection
symmetry, equal contacts or supplied curvatures.

For connected real-analytic boundaries, intrinsic channel data determine
complete obstacle images in their separate channel frames.  Under a
signature-rigid incidence structure, two cycles with independent marked deck
displacements recover the Euclidean realization of the period lattice,
including its Gram form and scale; a rooted spanning tree places the
remaining obstacle orbits.  No common channel registration or lattice metric
is supplied.  On compact analytic classes, a uniformly selected finite
curvature signature has both a local embedding bound and a global separation
margin.  We prove stable unique matching under controlled signature-curve
perturbations and, separately, a uniform finite-data inverse modulus.

The retained physical record determines the local information.  Successful
endpoint--residual-time observations have positive density at a moving
ceiling and are asymptotically equivalent to a generally non-dominated
Poisson boundary-shift experiment.  Explicit parameter-independent kernels
control the layer, reconstructed bulk and corner errors.  Removing residual
time produces a linearly vanishing endpoint density and its nonregular
Gaussian limit.  Waiting counts identify the normal quotient to the level
set of the hyperbolic exponent at a faster scale than the tangent endpoint
information; deleting counts gives a further coarsening.

Finally, we construct global estimators on a common physical endpoint-position
observation space.  A deterministically capped near-onset pilot estimates
each contact center, tangent and gap using only long even same-type bridges.
Mixed onset--law separators and a finite-template estimator then give uniform
fixed-order estimation with every preparation charged.  A diagonal choice
of order, design and flight number yields uniform consistency in every fixed
labelled \(C^q\) loss while the smallest used flight number tends to infinity.
The argument neither uses a short-flight inverse nor assumes that the
compact analytic class is finite dimensional.
\end{abstract}
