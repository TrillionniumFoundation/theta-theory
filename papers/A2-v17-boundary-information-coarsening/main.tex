\begin{abstract}
We study long alternating collision bridges in smooth periodic dispersing
billiards, the local contact geometry encoded by their signed endpoint
records, and the statistical information carried by the resulting moving
support.  On an offset collar whose size is independent of the number of
flights, the normalized mixed endpoint derivative has a uniform nonlinear
relative limit, including every fixed mixed geometric and offset derivative.
The limiting physical flux factors into two half-line determinant amplitudes,
identified with the normalized scalar linearizing densities of the physical
stable returns.

Retaining the signed endpoint positions recovers the two unsymmetrized
half-line actions from support thresholds.  Their quadratic terms, together
with physical onset, recover the gap and both contact curvatures.  At every
order $n\ge3$ the next pair of labelled graph jets enters the action jets
through an explicit $2\times2$ block of determinant one.  We prove this
all-order statement directly in a weighted half-line sequence space, using the
uniform inverse of the nonlinear Jacobi operator and the stationary envelope
identity, and differentiate the triangular recursion to obtain a quantitative
tangent inverse.  Hence arbitrary smooth labelled contact jets, odd and even,
are recursively determined without reflection symmetry, equality of the two
contacts, or supplied contact curvatures.  Real-analytic endpoint-law germs
determine the participating analytic contact germs; a finite registered
channel network determines every incident analytic boundary image.

For a general linearly vanishing density on a moving regular hypersurface, the
finite-sample local family need not be dominated.  We construct a
parameter-independent collapse of a reference collar of width
$q_n=\delta_n(\log(1/\delta_n))^{1/4}$.  At the critical scale
$np_n\delta_n^2\log(1/\delta_n)\to1$, the original non-dominated experiment is
at $o(1)$ Le Cam distance from the resulting dominated experiment.  On the
latter we prove uniform LAN with intrinsic information matrix
\[
 \mathcal J_\Sigma=
 \int_\Sigma\frac{a_0VV^t}{|\nabla w_0|}\,d\sigma,
\]
and then transfer the Gaussian shift limit, mutual contiguity, testing profile,
and finite-dimensional local asymptotic minimax bound back to the original
moving-support family.  No global Radon--Nikodym derivative is assigned to a
finite-$n$ alternative with support-exclusive mass.

We next construct the billiard experiment in a single anchored Euclidean chart:
the two contact origins and tangent axes are fixed throughout the local family,
endpoint coordinates are literal common laboratory observables, and physical
times are programmed as $t_{j,\ell}=jg_0+d_\ell$.  Around a reference gap,
\[
 g=g_0+\delta_n a/j_n,\qquad \vartheta=\delta_n h
\]
makes the gap and the labelled graph geometry enter the same moving support.
A standalone Hellinger comparison shows that the exact fixed-window billiard
endpoint family is $o(1)$-equivalent to its ideal linearized boundary family at
the critical scale.  The tangent signed-jet inverse implies that finitely many
positive same-type endpoint windows have a positive-definite summed
information matrix on every fixed finite labelled contact-jet chart.  Thus the
exact endpoint-output experiment converges to a nondegenerate Gaussian shift,
with gap rate $\delta_n/j_n$ and graph-geometry rate $\delta_n$.

If $k_n$ successful endpoint records are collected by deterministically capped
stopping rules and $k_n\tau^{j_n}\to0$, the complete finite-bridge stopped
transcript---with failures, designs and stopping times retained---is uniformly
asymptotically equivalent in total variation to its boundary transcript.  The
endpoint-output coarsening therefore inherits the Gaussian experiment while
every failed preparation remains charged.  Waiting counts are not declared
ancillary and may contain additional faster information.  A separate charged
pilot theorem states the exact compared sigma-field and justifies centered
windows when desired.

For the fixed-table finite-versus-boundary comparison, determinant-one
whitening gives the exact logarithmic endpoint coefficient, while complete
records distinguish at the earlier exponential scale and success bits erase
the leading tangent shape information.  Thus the relative boundary law,
signed local rigidity, and the registered physical endpoint experiment are
controlled by the same boundary structure.
\end{abstract}
