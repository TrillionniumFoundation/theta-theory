# A2 v61 — primary-source scope check

Checked September 16, 2026. These are source-scope and attribution checks for the revised exposition, not a claim to have re-proved the cited papers or exhausted the literature. The manuscript bibliography is retained unchanged.

| Primary record | Verified scope and use here |
|---|---|
| Douglas Finamore and Martin Leguil, *A CAT(0)-approach to the marked length spectral rigidity of Sinai billiards*, arXiv:2510.18983. https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.18983 | Finite-horizon Sinai billiards with an enriched marked-length datum. No equivalence to ordinary periodic-orbit marked lengths or to the present conditional endpoint laws is inferred. |
| Jacopo De Simoi, Vadim Kaloshin and Martin Leguil, *Marked Length Spectral determination of analytic chaotic billiards with axial symmetries*, arXiv:1905.00890v4. https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.00890v4 | Analytic open-billiard determination with the stated non-eclipse, symmetry and genericity framework. It is not silently extended to the observation or periodic class of this paper. |
| Gerhard Osius, *Asymptotic inference for semiparametric association models*, Annals of Statistics 37 (2009), 459–489. https://arxiv.org/abs/0903.0702 | Association/odds-ratio context for separating marginal factors. Attribution is retained; this does not supply the billiard relative determinant or actual-smooth action inverse. |
| Lloyd N. Trefethen, *Quantifying the ill-conditioning of analytic continuation*. https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.11097 | Context for conditional continuation under a complex-domain bound. The explicit interpolation lemma is a transfer estimate in this manuscript, not a newly claimed general continuation principle. |
| Anna Florio and Martin Leguil, *Smooth conjugacy classes of 3D Axiom A flows*, arXiv:2010.04120v5. https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.04120v5 | The version-5 correction removes the affected geometric open-billiard spectral-rigidity assertion. It is not used as an available competing theorem or premise; the remaining dynamical conjugacy results are not dismissed. |

The revised article makes an information comparison within its own experiment: a realized periodic family with identical leading endpoint metrics and count amplitudes has different nonlinear laws. It does not conclude that endpoint laws are weaker, stronger or equivalent to a marked length spectrum. The contribution is argued through its own relative physical limit and geometric inverse, not an unsupported claim that neighboring rigidity results are irrelevant or redundant.
