\begin{abstract}This supplement contains the complete primary-boundary,
relative-specialization and exact-coordinate theory accompanying the
reconstruction article.  It also gives the ambient component-pencil
classification and its scheme-theoretic residual involution.
These results are not hypotheses of the smooth-locus inverse theorem.
References with ordinary numerical section numbers refer to the
reconstruction article; sections of this supplement are prefixed S.
\end{abstract}
