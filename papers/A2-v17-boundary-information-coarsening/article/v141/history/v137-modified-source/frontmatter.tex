\author{Qian Qi}
\date{September 23, 2026 \quad (Revision 137)}

\keywords{Intrinsic reconstruction, webs of quadrics, Fitting schemes, Schur functors, exterior support, Jacobian quartics, nilpotent filtration}
\hypersetup{pdftitle={Intrinsic reconstruction from nonreduced failure schemes, revision 137},pdfauthor={Qian Qi}}
\begin{abstract}
We prove that the abstract nonreduced multiplication-failure scheme
of a cube-zero algebra with Hilbert function \((1,4,6)\) determines
its relation web up to projective equivalence whenever the web is
basepoint-free and its Jacobian quartic is smooth.  No further
genericity condition is required.  The deepest normal cone recovers
two Pluecker components through an oriented rank-one ruling and an
intrinsic residual ideal.  We prove an exact contraction-kernel theorem
on the determinant-twisted degree-\(n\) Jacobian component for every
bilinear rank and every dimension \(n\ge2\), including the
nondegenerate even-dimensional exception.  Its exterior-support gap
excludes secant and infinitesimal recombination for \(n\ge4\).
We also establish a reusable intrinsic coefficient criterion and apply
it to polar-restriction Fitting schemes of arbitrary homogeneous linear
systems, in every dimension and degree.  These schemes have a fixed
reduced determinant support, but their abstract nonreduced structures
recover the original systems.  The web theorem separates the ambiguity
left by the polarized Jacobian K3 invariant.
\end{abstract}
