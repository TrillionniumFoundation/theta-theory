\begin{abstract}
We study the nonreduced Fitting scheme on a Grassmannian on which
multiplication fails for cube-zero algebras with Hilbert function
\((1,4,6)\).  The first nilpotent layer reconstructs the ramification
quartic K3 surface together with its quartic polarization.  Revision
127 determines substantially more of the singular Schubert boundary.

On every projection-rank chart write
\[
 \mathcal I_{\widehat D_R}=dJ,\qquad
 d=\det T,\qquad
 J=I_6[A_H,B_H(1_H\otimes T),C_H\Sym^2T].
\]
A determinant-completion identity gives
\[
 d^5\in J
\]
without a rank hypothesis.  Hence the intrinsic nilradical satisfies
\(\widehat{\mathcal N}_R^6=0\) on the whole Grassmannian, so the
normal cone has at most five positive graded layers.

At projection corank two, whenever \(A_H\) is injective, the first
graded support is the unsplit quartic contact cone without any
mixed-regularity assumption.  The remaining boundary is organized by
the rank of
\[
 \overline B_H:H\otimes(V/H)\longrightarrow S_R/\im A_H
\]
and by the position of \(\Pj(\ker\overline B_H)\) relative to the
Segre quadric in \(\Pj(H\otimes(V/H))\).  This gives nine point/line/plane orbit types: nondecomposable and
decomposable kernel points, secant and tangent kernel lines, the two
ruling families distinguished by which tensor factor is fixed,
nondegenerate and decomposable kernel hyperplanes, and the zero mixed
map.  On the
coefficient-transverse open of each orbit type we compute the exact
nilpotency index and the Hilbert function of the second and third
graded supports.  Rank drops of \(A_H\) are governed by a finite
determinantal valuation atlas, with an explicit depth lower bound and
the universal depth-five cutoff.

At projection corank three the cubic determinant power is excluded by
the \(GL_3\)-types of the leading residual minors; the local
nilpotency index is therefore exactly five or six, separated by one
degree-twelve Macaulay rank condition.  At projection corank four the
decomposition
\[
 \bigwedge\nolimits^6\Sym^2\C^4
 \simeq
 \mathbb S_{(5,4,2,1)}\C^4
 \oplus
 \mathbb S_{(4,4,4,0)}\C^4
\]
excludes \(d^3\).  The second summand is dual, up to the determinant
twist, to the Jacobian-quartic covariant of the web; its nonvanishing
on \(G_4^\circ\) forces \(d^4\in J\).  Thus the deepest projection
stratum has exact nilpotency index five.

The extended Rees algebra of the intrinsic nilradical is retained as
the standard deformation-to-the-normal-cone mechanism.  The
manuscript-specific content is the graded boundary atlas above, the
polarized K3 reconstruction, and the explicit primary and collision
laws on the first singular Schubert boundary.
\end{abstract}
