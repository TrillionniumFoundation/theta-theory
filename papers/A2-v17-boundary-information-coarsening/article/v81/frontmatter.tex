\begin{abstract}
We recover absolute generating actions from endpoint observations whose
branch identities, readout channels and exposures are unknown.  Two
conditionally independent readouts, invariant across deadlines but allowed
to depend on the endpoint, produce an observable matrix pencil.  Two raw
deadlines determine its visible component count, absolute actions and
channels.  Three projectively normalized deadlines suffice when at least
two actions are separated, even under arbitrary positive endpoint- and
clock-dependent branch-blind retention.  The inverse is locally Lipschitz
without loss of spatial derivatives.  Unpaired regular generating sheets
then determine exact return systems from coprime iterates on covered phase
domains.  A delayed acquisition gives common deadlines without branch
auditing.  In contrast to common detectors, branch-selective retention
admits exact compensation along noncongruent billiard deformations with a
fixed raw source.  We give finite-observation bounds without a calibration
sample and retain the conditional-law, calibrated, convex-suspension and
marked-billiard reconstructions as companion regimes.
\end{abstract}
