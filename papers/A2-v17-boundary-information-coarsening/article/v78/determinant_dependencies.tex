\subsection{Dependency chain for the two-end estimate}
The four error terms in Corollary~\ref{cor:v77-periodic-blocks} use the
following parts of the periodic proof.  This chain is included to make
the application of the trace-ideal lemma explicit, rather than to
introduce a second inverse theorem.
\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.21\linewidth}p{0.69\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
Error or margin & Inherited input and operation\\
\midrule
$\rho^r$ & Endpoint localization in the proof of
Theorem~\ref{thm:v64-periodic-relative}; summation of the deleted
tridiagonal entries in trace norm.\\[3pt]
$q_G^{N-2r}$ & Green reflection estimate
\eqref{eq:v64-green-reflection}; separated end projections and
Hilbert--Schmidt summation.\\[3pt]
$\rho^{N-r}+q_G^{2(N-r)}$ & Gluing of half-actions
\eqref{eq:v64-half-actions}, reflected Green comparison, and the
half-line amplitude \eqref{eq:v64-half-amplitude}.\\[3pt]
$q_*<1$ & Small-collar product bound, compression, and the block
sign-conjugation interpolation displayed in the corollary.\\[3pt]
$q_G^{2(N-2r)}$ & Vanishing first off-diagonal trace variation and
Proposition~\ref{prop:v76-determinant}.  Its error is quadratic in
the cross-block Hilbert--Schmidt norm.\\[3pt]
Conditioned law & The relative logarithm
\eqref{eq:v64-relative-log} and
Corollary~\ref{cor:v76-relative-conditioning}, after the exact scalar
twist has been factored out.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
