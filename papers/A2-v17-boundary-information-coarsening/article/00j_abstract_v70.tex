We show that conditional endpoint laws of long bridges near a supplied
nongrazing periodic billiard polygon determine the complete smooth
reflecting contact germs.  The success mass is exponentially small, but
an exact Jacobi normalization and a two-ended determinant comparison give
a relative physical law on a fixed contact collar.  Two positive offsets
separate its nonlinear half-line actions without supplied curvature or
flux amplitudes.  An envelope identity for actual boundary variations,
a global quadratic inverse and contracted later visits then identify the
smooth contacts, including their flat parts, without analyticity or a
closeness assumption.  A finite-order real-law norm controls complete
profiles on bounded positive smoothness classes.  Independent finite
preparations yield a realizable profile estimator with failures charged
and bounded post-acceptance endpoint errors.  Actual equal-area families
with identical contact and action Taylor jets but different laws exhibit
the information beyond the formal all-order record.  Analytic
continuation and intrinsic multichannel reconstruction are developed
separately under their respective observation hypotheses.
