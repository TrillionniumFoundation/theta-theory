We study the nonlinear geometry retained by conditional endpoint laws
near nongrazing periodic orbits of dispersing billiards.  An exact Jacobi
normalization and a two-ended trace-class comparison give a relative
boundary law on a collar independent of flight number, despite the
exponentially small success mass.  For a supplied marked polygon,
phase-resolved laws at two positive offsets separate the unequal
half-line actions without supplied amplitudes.  A boundary Schur
complement yields a globally injective curvature map and a convergent
reconstruction with an observable contraction constant.  The stationary
boundary envelope has signed cyclic Cayley blocks at every higher order.
Together these identify all smooth contact jets and complete analytic
contact germs without a closeness assumption.  If every obstacle is
visited, exact analytic continuation determines the labelled table in
the supplied lattice and placement.  The complete analytic action map
is locally biholomorphic; under a bounded analytic prior, real-law error
gives conditional stability on a smaller disc.  For alternating channels, a weaker-data theorem uses only the gap and
one same-type law at each end.  Its unregistered multichannel
consequences recover an unknown lattice with finite ambiguity, and its
calibrated statistical consequences charge every preparation, including
rejected attempts.  These observation models share the relative and
contact mechanism but keep their different supplied data and estimates.
