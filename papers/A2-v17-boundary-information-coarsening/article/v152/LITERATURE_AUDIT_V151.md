## Primary sources inspected for the new proofs

1. The Stacks Project, Tag 02LS, Lemma 37.44.1, https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/02LS. The equivalence between finite and proper-with-finite-fibres morphisms is used only after projectivity and finite divisor choices are proved.

3. The Stacks Project, Tag 035E, Section 29.55, https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/035E. The affine integral-closure description and finite birational maps to a normal scheme are checked. The article supplies its own finite-local Nakayama argument and normalization proof.
4. The Stacks Project, Tag 04V2, Subsection 112.5.7, https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/04V2. The literature guide distinguishes ordinary rigidification from the normal, potentially noncentral subgroup case treated by Abramovich–Olsson–Vistoli. The manuscript cites this guide as a guide, not as a newly read complete AOV appendix, and proves the universal property needed here directly.
