\begin{abstract}
Every complex quadratic pencil, including singular pencils, is recovered
from its unmarked, ungraded finite failure algebra at a sharp order.
The relative inverse recovers moving pencils and their actual source
bundles over smooth connected projective reductions.
We study the intrinsic first-relation boundary on the
maximal-lower-Hilbert-function stratum through the finite normalization
of a degree-prescribed common-divisor image.  Beyond its exact normal
locus and nonreduced normalization fibres, we determine completed
local rings on the coprime two-divisor stratum and along collisions
of two common binary roots.  These models give conductors, intrinsic
singular ideals, branch intersections, and exact Cohen--Macaulay
criteria.  A scalar-matrix contraction connects this geometry to the
pencil inverse: the first-relation orbit closures of all pencils
contain a common ramified boundary point.  Its exact gcd and entire
normalization-fibre algebra follow from the osculating filtration of
a flag coefficient representation.  In dimension three the unique
normalization point has a fibre of embedding dimension forty-four.
The all-pencil inverse, nonreduced-base rigidifications, transverse
fixed-support families, covering-map reconstruction, and spectral
Fitting data form a single intrinsic reconstruction theory.
\end{abstract}
