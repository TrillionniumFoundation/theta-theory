# Proof-scope audit — revision 152

The v151 proofs and their stated hypotheses are unchanged. This note records the additional proof obligations discharged in the new sections.

**Split conductor.** Exact gcd ab, only two degree-g divisors, gcd(a,b)=gcd(L)=1, h>g, and residual rank feasibility are explicit hypotheses. Completed branches are formal closed immersions by the cotangent criterion. Their intersection is identified on local Artin bases using a lifted graded Koszul complex, before passing to a fibre product. The conductor, singular Fitting ideal, multiplicity, and depth are computed in that full image ring. The theorem is not asserted at overlapping divisors outside this stratum.

**Binary collision.** An exact double common root and gcd-free residual space are required. A universal Grassmannian frame has independently variable two-jets. Formal Weierstrass division yields a versal incidence, not a chosen slice. Elimination uses the R⊕Rt decomposition and Koszul syzygies without radicalization. The completed image, conductor, depth, and exact Jacobian-minor singular scheme follow. Coarse quotient singularities are not identified with atlas singularities.

**Degree intersections.** The finite common-part formula is a reduced global intersection equality. The manuscript explicitly distinguishes it from the full formal branch intersection and the nonreduced collision scheme.

**Pencil orbit boundary.** The action is on all n² cotangent variables. For the flag pencil, T=aI+tau B is invertible as a linear substitution whenever tau≠0. Adapted primitive relations retain rank at zero; multiplication by the determinant power has constant fibre rank. Thus the family is finite-flat. The flag coefficient osculating order is proved for all n≥3, with a complete n=3 basis and an attaining quartic for n≥4. Orbit-closure inclusion gives a common boundary for every initial pencil via a two-stage specialization. No classification of all orbit closures or one uniform direct one-parameter subgroup is claimed.

**Pure-power fibre.** Coprime residual cancellation is proved over local Artin bases. The monic complementary factor is unique, and reciprocal equations describe its full infinitesimal fibre. Finiteness makes completion equal the entire Artin fibre. Tangent dimension is distinguished from length and from the dimension of the image local ring.

**Computations.** The 42 new checks and 27 inherited scripts test finite identities only. They do not prove all-dimensional statements. Machine preservation is evidence of nondeletion, not evidence of universal correctness.

**Literature.** Normalization criteria, conductor squares, ordinary pinch points, and normal-subgroup rigidification are credited as established mechanisms. Ballico 1993 remains documentary-open; there is no fabricated nonanticipation or historical-priority certification.
