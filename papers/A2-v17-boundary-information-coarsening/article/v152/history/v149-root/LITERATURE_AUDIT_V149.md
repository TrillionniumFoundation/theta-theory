## 2. Primary references checked for the new proof interfaces

**Proper unramified universally injective maps.** Stacks Project, Lemma 41.7.2, Tag 04XV, `https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/04XV`. The exact equivalence with a closed immersion is used after proving those hypotheses for the multiplication and power maps. It is not used as a substitute for the differential or reduced-target arguments.

**Quotient-stack algebraicity.** Stacks Project, Theorem 97.17.2 and Section 97.18, `https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/06PI`. The groups in the new comparison are smooth affine complex groups and the actions are on locally closed schemes; the required flatness and finite-presentation hypotheses hold.

**Smoothness of the quotient.** Stacks Project, Lemma 101.33.7, Tag 0DLS, `https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0DLS`. Smoothness is applied to an open Grassmannian or the pencil Grassmannian. The tangent complexes are derived separately by graph coordinates and infinitesimal group action in the paper.
