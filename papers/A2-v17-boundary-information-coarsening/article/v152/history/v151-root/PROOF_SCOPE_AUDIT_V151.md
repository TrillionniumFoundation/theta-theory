# Proof-scope audit — A2 revision 151

## New proof dependencies

**Incidence.** The map L⊗S_h → S_D has constant fibre rank; nonzero maximal minors split it over arbitrary bases. The incidence is the associated relative Grassmannian. This proves its actual scheme structure rather than testing geometric points.

**Finiteness.** Every geometric divisor line divides the fixed gcd polynomial. Unique factorization gives a finite set of degree-g factors; projectivity makes the map finite. In several variables a gcd of larger degree need not have a degree-g divisor.

**Fibre equations.** A chosen invertible multiplication block solves for the residual coefficients. The complementary-row equations are imposed without taking radicals. The full scheme fibre, not only its reduced support, is thus determined.

**Tangent kernel.** Infinitesimal division is possible exactly when f divides dot(f)H, where H is the residual gcd. Valuations give dot(f)=(f/c)w, c=gcd(f,H), and quotienting the projective scalar direction gives S_deg(c)/Cc. This proof is independent of numerical rank experiments.

**Normalization and normal locus.** The exact-gcd open has one reduced fibre at each point; finite-local Nakayama gives a genuine isomorphism. Smooth source plus finite birationality gives normalization. At any geometric point, one reduced normalization fibre gives an isomorphism by the same argument. The criterion is uniqueness of a degree-g divisor plus zero overlap. Smoothness equals normality here because the normalization is smooth, not by a general assertion that all normal varieties are smooth.

**Base change.** Incidence and pulled-back fibres are functorial on arbitrary complex schemes. Formation of the image and normalization is not claimed to commute with arbitrary nonflat base change. Nor is every ambient family with fibrewise common divisors declared to lift to the incidence.

**Algebra boundary.** The rank and Hilbert function follow from the homogeneous direct-sum presentation. Descent to [Y_g/GL(E)] uses the intrinsic first-relation proposition, not assumptions on factor symmetry. It does not classify arbitrary smoothings outside this fixed Hilbert stratum.

**Rigidification.** Local lifts and isomorphism sheaves give fppf gerbes. Killing relative inertia forces a genuine descent cocycle, which proves the mapping property for any target stack and for natural transformations. Right-factor inertia over a torsor is twisted by conjugation. The nonlinear substitution law is not treated as additive; projective pencil stabilizers are retained.

**Common pencil specialization.** Nonconstant q1/q0 has a nonzero differential in characteristic zero. A frame with q0's x1² and q1's x1x2 coefficients nonzero gives polynomial rescaled generators under diag(1,t,t²,...). Independence at zero proves a subbundle, and the flag orbit is closed by a projective scheme immersion. The finite-flat failure family is written explicitly from the Pluecker line and determinant-power factorization. The closed-orbit assertion concerns the full pencil Grassmannian, or the pencil locus in a fixed determinant frame, not the full relation Grassmannian with degenerating determinant forms.

## Claims not supplied by finite checks

The exact script verifies concrete coefficient ideals, differential matrices, and polynomial degenerations. It is not a formal verifier for any universal theorem above. No complete congruence-orbit adjacency classification, full algebra smoothing classification, universal normalization base-change theorem, six-axis Ballico comparison, historical priority clearance, or top-four acceptance certificate is claimed.
