\title[Finite failure schemes and quadratic pencils]{Finite failure schemes and the reconstruction of quadratic pencils}
\author{Qian Qi}
\date{September 24, 2026}
\subjclass[2020]{14M12, 14B05, 14D20, 13C40}
\keywords{Quadratic pencil, nonreduced degeneracy locus, intrinsic reconstruction, normalization, rigidification}
\hypersetup{pdftitle={Finite failure schemes and the reconstruction of quadratic pencils, revision 151},pdfauthor={Qian Qi}}
\begin{abstract}
We recover every complex quadratic pencil, including singular pencils,
from its unmarked, ungraded finite failure algebra.  Over a smooth
connected projective reduction the same inverse recovers a moving
pencil, one constant left transformation, and the actual source bundle.
We also study a natural boundary of arbitrary homogeneous first
relations.  The incidence of a degree-prescribed common divisor has a
finite forgetful map whose source is the normalization of its
scheme-theoretic image.  We determine the exact normal locus by
uniqueness of the divisor and its coprimality with the residual common
factor, and give local equations retaining all nilpotents of the
normalization fibres.  This yields a canonical boundary for the
first-relation algebra stack, without pencil image equations.
For pencils the exact divisor is the $(n-1)$st power of the determinant.
Two explicitly characterized rigidifications recover the usual pencil
stack over arbitrary complex bases.  An explicit one-parameter
specialization takes every pencil to a common closed flag orbit and
lifts to a finite-flat failure family.  The sharp reconstruction order
$n^2+2n-4$, transverse fixed-support families, global covering-map
reconstruction, and spectral Fitting data remain part of the inverse
theory.
\end{abstract}
