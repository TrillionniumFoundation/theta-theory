## Primary texts inspected for the new local-algebra discussion

**Elias–Rossi, short Gorenstein local rings.** J. Elias and M. E. Rossi, *Isomorphism classes of short Gorenstein local rings via Macaulay's inverse system*, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 364 (2012), no. 9, 4589–4604, DOI 10.1090/S0002-9947-2012-05430-4. The full author text inspected was arXiv:0911.3565v1: https://arxiv.org/html/0911.3565v1. Theorem 3.3 proves canonical grading for Hilbert function (1,e,e,1); Corollaries 3.4–3.5 give the graded/projective classification consequence. The proof removes an inhomogeneous lower-degree inverse-system term. Our K is already homogeneous and occurs only at the truncation's top degree; its nonlinear corrections vanish for that reason. The pencil-specific problem is unmarked orbit rigidity of K, not the canonical-grading assertion. The new principal source gives this comparison and the explicit inverse-system formula. The AMS bibliographic record was checked; its full PDF endpoint returned 403, so the full-text theorem pins are explicitly to the author version.

**Elias–Rossi, compressed local algebras.** *Analytic isomorphisms of compressed local algebras*, Proc. Amer. Math. Soc. 143 (2015), no. 3, 973–987, DOI 10.1090/S0002-9939-2014-12313-6. Full author PDF inspected: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1207.6919, version 1. Theorem 3.1 is on PDF page 9 (zero-based page 8), with proof continuing on page 10; it concerns compressed/extremal Gorenstein algebras of socle degree at most four. Examples 3.3–3.5 mark limitations of canonical grading. The displayed theorem and formulas were also checked on the rendered PDF page. The present pencil algebras have much higher degree and a top socle subspace of dimension greater than one; difference of classes alone is not a claim of nonanticipation.

**Marcus–Moyls.** *Transformations on tensor product spaces*, Pacific J. Math. 9 (1959), no. 4, 1215–1221, DOI 10.2140/pjm.1959.9.1215. Original publisher PDF: https://msp.org/pjm/1959/9-4/pjm-v9-n4-p17-s.pdf. Theorem 1, printed pp. 1218–1219 (PDF pages 5–6), treats rank-one-preserving linear maps on tensor products. The field assumptions in the introduction are algebraic closure and characteristic zero. We use only the square invertible complex case. The new lemma gives a complete proof of precisely that case, including the determinant-to-rank-one reduction. This is cited classical input.

**Algebraic group schemes.** The Stacks Project, Section 39.8, Lemma 39.8.2 (Cartier smoothness in characteristic zero), https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BF6. The statement and its proof were inspected. We use it to pass from the computed complex-point stabilizer to equality of the smooth closed subgroup schemes after the local automorphism functor has independently been represented by polynomial equations.
