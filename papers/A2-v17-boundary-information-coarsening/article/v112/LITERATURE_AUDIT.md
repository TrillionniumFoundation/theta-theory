# Primary-source comparison for A2 v112

This is a documented comparison with the closest examined sources, not an exhaustive priority certificate. Mathematical source references are ordinary bibliography entries; this process record stays outside the article.

## Quadratic normality and failure geometry

**Green--Lazarsfeld (1986)**, *On the projective normality of complete linear series on an algebraic curve*, Inventiones Mathematicae 83, 73--90. Examined the authors' full reprint's introduction and statements of Theorems 1--3, in particular printed pages 73--76. Theorem 1 is a normal-generation criterion for a complete very ample line bundle on a smooth curve, expressed through degree, genus, special cohomology and Clifford index. Theorem 3 detects failure by special effective divisors and finite secant configurations under its degree/cohomology hypotheses. This already establishes the important general philosophy that secant configurations can explain failure of normal generation. It is not claimed as new here.

Author reprint: https://www.math.stonybrook.edu/~roblaz/Reprints/Green.Laz.Proj.Norm.Alg.Curves.pdf
DOI: 10.1007/BF01388754.

**Ballico (1996)**, *On the failure cycles for the quadratic normality of a projective variety*, Pacific Journal of Mathematics 172(2), 307--313. Examined the complete seven-page mathematical article, including Theorem 0.2, the Grassmannian incidence/cohomological setup, and Proposition 2.2. The main theorem concerns a fixed smooth embedded surface with a complete very ample line bundle. Under a cohomological inequality it detects quadratic failure on a finite codimension-two linear section; the later discussion treats higher multiplication under additional hypotheses. This is genuine prior failure-cycle geometry, not merely generic maximal rank.

Publisher PDF: https://msp.org/pjm/1996/172-2/pjm-v172-n2-p01-p.pdf

**Difference in parameter problem.** In A2 the complete degree-n series on P1 is fixed and normally generated. The parameter space is Gr(c,H0(O(n)))^L, and the map is the direct sum of within-contact quadratic multiplications. No cross-contact products are present. The stable-range theorem determines the components, generic scheme smoothness and reduced Cohen--Macaulay structure in expected codimension of this particular moving-subseries scheme. The examined complete-series criterion and fixed-surface failure-cycle theorem do not state the signed multi-contact classification or the residual (k-2,c-1) wall. We make this comparison at the level of their actual hypotheses and conclusions, not because our displayed formula is absent from their titles.

Ballico's cited 1993 paper on higher-order failure loci is a relevant further antecedent. Its full text was not available in the present audit, so this revision does not claim to have excluded all overlap with that or other unexamined failure-locus literature. No absolute first-in-literature assertion is made.

## Classical geometric and commutative-algebra inputs

**Conca--Mostafazadehfard--Singh--Varbaro (2018)**, *Hankel determinantal rings have rational singularities*, Adv. Math. 335, 111--129, arXiv:1709.05685. The exact dimension display on arXiv page 2 is dim R=2t-2. Taking t=r+1 supplies affine dimension 2r for the rank-at-most-r Hankel locus. Equation (2.0.1) gives its secant parametrization. The paper's stronger singularity results for the Hankel ring are not silently transferred to our multiplication failure scheme.

Primary preprint: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1709.05685

**Eagon--Northcott (1962)**, *Ideals defined by matrices and a certain complex associated with them*, Proc. Roy. Soc. London A269, 188--204, DOI 10.1098/rspa.1962.0170. The original publication metadata was checked. The exact applicable grade criterion was read in **Kustin**, *Canonical complexes associated to a matrix*, arXiv:1509.04367, immediately following (1.0.2) and in Section 2.9. It says that the maximal-minor complex resolves the quotient when the maximal-minor ideal has the expected grade, and yields the Cohen--Macaulay conclusion over the regular chart ring. We use this criterion, not a generic-entry matrix hypothesis. We do not claim to have inspected the unavailable full original EN article.

Primary accessible statement: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1509.04367

**Kempf--Laksov (1974)**, *The determinantal formula of Schubert calculus*, Acta Math. 132, 153--162, DOI 10.1007/BF02392111; and **Fulton**, *Intersection Theory*, second edition, Theorem 14.4. These are the classical source and standard formulation of the expected-codimension cycle formula. The v112 cycle is its application to the dual map from the trivial rank-p bundle to the dual quadratic tautological bundle. No new general determinantal formula is claimed. The present article proves the requisite codimension and reducedness before applying it.

## Other retained attributions

The rational maximal-rank input remains attributed to Ballico--Ellia and Larson, including its exact genus-zero specialization in the appendix. It is not the proof input for the full-range saturated codimension theorem. Gaussian linear compression, nuisance projection and the matrix information projector remain standard asymptotic/linear-algebraic steps. The quantitative Poisson and finite-precision comparisons are proved in full in the article rather than relying on a vague local-limit citation.

## Priority boundary

The supported contribution claim is the particular structural theorem and its residual deformation mechanism, with its stated stable hypotheses and statistical applications. This audit does not prove that every possible reformulation in the literature has been ruled out. The new referee should evaluate both the proofs and the comparison with the exact antecedents recorded here.
