## 1. Complete quadrics: precise scheme-level input

A. Massarenti, *On the birational geometry of spaces of complete forms I: collineations and quadrics*, arXiv `1803.09161`.

Primary full text: `https://arxiv.org/pdf/1803.09161`.

Remark 2.5 identifies the symmetric exterior-power graph. Construction 2.6 describes the successive blow-ups of the symmetric rank loci and explicitly attributes their properties to Vainsencher, Theorem 6.3. These passages were rechecked in the primary PDF, including the rendered page. They supply the specific fact used in Paper II, Theorem 10.2: over the locus of rank at least n-2, the complete-quadric map is just the blow-up of the smooth codimension-three rank-(n-2) locus. A new complete copy of Vainsencher's original proof was not obtained. The theorem citation is transparently mediated by the inspected construction.

The classical ambient construction does not, on its own, identify the normalized Hilbert graph of pencil curves. Revision 160 separately proves that on the simple corank-two pencil open this graph is the blow-up of a codimension-two incidence centre. Its proof constructs a flat embedded nodal family and identifies the normal Hilbert graph by a finite birational morphism. The new statement is about that specific incidence problem and its multiplication data, not about the discovery of complete quadrics.

## 5. Scope of the new incidence result

The new result has three connected parts:

1. The full normalized Hilbert incidence space over the simple corank-two open is the blow-up of its exact smooth incidence centre, in every n >= 3. This determines the complete exceptional projective line, every embedded limiting curve over that open, and the flat nodal universal family.
2. Every power system on the whole exceptional plane is the complete Veronese system of the stated degree. Its line restrictions include tangencies to the residual determinant conic, not only squarefree ordinary residual pencils.
3. The incidence ideal and Rees algebra are recovered from the failure multiplication diagram on the effective first-relation image. A first normal deformation selects the embedded limit, while the closed algebra alone determines the full possible-direction space.

The proof uses familiar determinantal normal coordinates, Rees algebras, Hilbert representability and smooth blow-ups. The asserted content is their exact identification in the specified failure/pencil incidence problem. An exhaustive literature exclusion for every possible equivalent formulation has not been established. The manuscript does not infer such an exclusion from this audit.
