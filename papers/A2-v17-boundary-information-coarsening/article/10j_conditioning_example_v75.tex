\begin{remark}[Explicit fixed-gate conditioning]\label{rem:v75-conditioning}
On $J=[-R,R]$, let $p\ne0$ and $d>2|p|R$.  The positive normalized
rank-two model
\[
 f(u,v)=\frac{d+pu-pv}{4R^2d}
\]
has moments
\[
 \mathbb E U=\frac{pR^2}{3d},\qquad
 \mathbb E V=-\frac{pR^2}{3d},\qquad \mathbb E(UV)=0.
\]
Indeed the odd integrals vanish and
$\int_{-R}^R u^2\,du=2R^3/3$.  Hence
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v75-affine-determinant}
 H_f=\begin{pmatrix}1&-pR^2/(3d)\\pR^2/(3d)&0\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad \det H_f=\frac{p^2R^4}{9d^2}.
\end{equation}
Thus even this simple model loses the determinant at fourth order in the
gate radius and second order in obliquity.  For $a=pR^2/(3d)$,
$H_f^{-1}=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1/a\\-1/a&1/a^2\end{smallmatrix}\right)$,
so the inverse-matrix norm also diverges.  This is an algebraic model,
not an assertion that affine half-line actions are realized by a
positive-curvature billiard.  It illustrates why the fixed-class
constants in Proposition~\ref{prop:v74-nondegeneracy} retain their
gate and slope dependence.  Rescaling coordinates changes moment units;
it does not prove uniform physical stability in a degenerating class.
The affine computation was supplied in the author-requested memorandum
\cite{A2ReviewV74}; it is included here with its derivation.
\end{remark}
