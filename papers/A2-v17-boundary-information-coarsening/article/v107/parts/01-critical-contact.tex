\section{Nonlinear contact of synchronized root pairs}\label{sec:synchronized}
Keep the calibrated binary family, score matrix $V=[L,D]$, and positive exposures of Section~\ref{sec:binary-hankel}. Partition $D=[D_x,D_z]$ into its $k$ interior and $e$ endpoint columns. Reorder columns when necessary; every congruence below uses this same physical ordering. Fix nonzero loadings $a_i\in\R^d$, chosen independently of the exposures, and require the $i$th root pair to have the form
\begin{equation}\label{eq:synchronized-roots}
 r_i+s_i/2+a_i^{\mathsf T}\eta,\qquad
 r_i+s_i/2-a_i^{\mathsf T}\eta.
\end{equation}
The sums $s_i$, mixing coordinate, and inward endpoints remain free. This is a subfamily of the polynomial observation model, specified by shared root perturbations, not by a prescribed metric. Put
\[
 q_A(\eta)=((a_i^{\mathsf T}\eta)^2)_{i=1}^k,
 \quad \mathcal A_A(S)=(a_i^{\mathsf T}Sa_i)_{i=1}^k,
 \quad \mathcal V_A=\mathcal A_A\{S\ge0:\rank S\le1\}.
\]
Assume the matrices $a_i a_i^{\mathsf T}$ span $\Sym_d$. This convenient sufficient condition is stronger than injectivity modulo sign in general phase retrieval; it will make every identification used here elementary. It implies $k\ge d(d+1)/2$ and that the $a_i$ span $\R^d$.

For $b\in\R_+^k$ let $p^{\rm target}(t)$ be the law in the unrestricted calibrated family with interior variances $tb$, zero sums, zero inward endpoint shifts, and the central mixing coordinate. A target outside the synchronized subfamily is allowed. We use the convention
\[
 h^2(p,q)=\sum_c(\sqrt{p_c}-\sqrt{q_c})^2,
\]
so that $4h^2(p,p+\delta)=\sum_c\delta_c^2/p_c+o(|\delta|^2)$. The infimum below is over a sufficiently small fixed neighbourhood of the central root configuration in the synchronized family.

\begin{theorem}[Hankel-weighted nonlinear root contact]\label{thm:synchronized}
Let the clock and root separation, probability, and exposure margins be positive. Then the following assertions hold.

The complete normalized probability residual at scale $t$ is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:synchronized-E}
 E_b=\{\mathsf La+\mathsf D_x(q_A(\eta)-b)+\mathsf D_z z:
 a\in\R^{k+1},\ \eta\in\R^d,\ z\in\R_+^e\},
\end{equation}
where the probability score blocks are those of Theorem~\ref{thm:unification}. This set is closed. Its quotient by the free score space is a translate of the injective linear image of $\mathcal V_A\times\R_+^e$. In particular, for $d\ge2$ it is not a finite union of affine spaces.

The exact metric contact formula is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:synchronized-cost}
 \lim_{t\downarrow0}\frac4{t^2}\inf
 \sum_j\lambda_j h^2(p_j,p_j^{\rm target}(t))
 =\min_{\eta\in\R^d,z\ge0}(q_A(\eta)-b,z)^{\mathsf T}Q(q_A(\eta)-b,z)
 =\min_{v\in\mathcal V_A}\widehat G_Q(v-b).
\end{equation}
Here $Q$ is exactly the nuisance Schur complement of the unrestricted binary score system. Consequently its inverse has the model-forced Hankel form in Theorem~\ref{thm:binary-hankel}, with the fixed-budget image and fibres in Theorem~\ref{thm:budget} when the exposures sum to one. The right side is zero precisely when $b\in\mathcal V_A$; otherwise it is strictly positive and the Hellinger distance has order $t$.

Suppose moreover that all variable factors have one colour, and that either $e=1,k\ge2$ or $e=2,k\ge3$. The labelled function $G_Q$ on $\R_+^k$ determines the right side of \eqref{eq:synchronized-cost} for every $A,b$. Indeed it reconstructs $Q$ up to endpoint scales and permutations, and these ambiguities preserve $\widehat G_Q$ on all of $\R^k$. Thus a fully visible endpoint profile determines the nonlinear contact of every synchronized subfamily with the same native experiment.

All residual and minimum limits are uniform for bounded $b$ on compact parameter sets with the stated margins and a positive lower bound for the spanning measurement map. Replacing $a_i^{\mathsf T}\eta$ in \eqref{eq:synchronized-roots} by analytic or semialgebraic $C^2$ functions $\ell_i(\eta)=a_i^{\mathsf T}\eta+O(|\eta|^2)$, with uniform remainder bounds, leaves the leading residual and contact formula unchanged.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The polynomial factor of pair $i$ is exactly
$(T-r_i-s_i/2)^2-(a_i^{\mathsf T}\eta)^2$.
The map $\mathcal A_A$ is injective on $\Sym_d$ by its spanning hypothesis. Its restriction to rank-one positive matrices therefore has precisely the sign ambiguity $\eta\sim-\eta$ away from zero. Since the loadings span $\R^d$, compactness of the unit sphere gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:quadratic-coercivity}
 \|q_A(\eta)\|\ge c_A|\eta|^2.
\end{equation}
It follows that $q_A$ is proper and that $\mathcal V_A$ is closed. This also proves the uniform version of this assertion under the stated bounds.

Write $\theta=(s,\text{mixing},v,z)$ for the symmetric coordinates of the unrestricted calibrated family. The square score matrix $V$ is invertible by rational interpolation. Each calibrated table determines its scalar clock mixing probability, so the observation map is locally bi-Lipschitz in $\theta$, on an open extension of these coordinates. This statement is about the ambient symmetric model; its restriction to \eqref{eq:synchronized-roots} is singular.

A bounded probability residual divided by $t$ forces $\theta-\theta^{\rm target}(t)=O(t)$. Thus sums and mixing are $O(t)$, endpoints are $O(t)$, and $q_A(\eta)=O(t)$. Equation~\eqref{eq:quadratic-coercivity} gives $\eta=O(\sqrt t)$. Put sums and mixing equal to $ta$, endpoints equal to $tz$, and $\eta=\sqrt t\,\xi$. Taylor expansion in the ambient symmetric coordinates then gives
\[
 t^{-1}(p-p^{\rm target}(t))
 =\mathsf La+\mathsf D_x(q_A(\xi)-b)+\mathsf D_z z+o(1)
\]
uniformly on bounded sets. Bounded residual sequences have bounded rescaled variables; taking subsequences proves one inclusion in \eqref{eq:synchronized-E}. Conversely each displayed point is realized by this substitution, and all such roots are feasible for sufficiently small $t$ by interior separation. The full score map is injective and has a bounded left inverse. Combined with properness of $q_A$, this proves that $E_b$ is closed.

For $d\ge2$, the cone of positive matrices of rank at most one is not a finite union of affine spaces. Any affine space contained in that cone has dimension at most one: two independent rank-one positive directions have a rank-two positive sum, while a complete nonconstant affine line cannot stay positive in both directions. More directly, its infinitely many distinct rank-one rays cannot be covered by finitely many such affine pieces. The injective quotient score map preserves this obstruction. Intersecting with $z=0$ proves the same assertion with endpoints included. Thus the residual in \eqref{eq:synchronized-E} is genuinely non-affine after the free coordinates have been removed.

Taking zero free increments and $\eta=z=0$ gives cost $O(t^2)$. The local inverse and \eqref{eq:quadratic-coercivity} therefore localize every minimizing sequence as above. On the resulting bounded sets the Hellinger expansion is uniform. Its quadratic term is the score Gram matrix. Minimizing over $a$ gives its Schur complement $Q$, and minimizing over $z\ge0$ gives \eqref{eq:synchronized-cost}. Positive definiteness yields coercivity in $(q_A(\xi)-b,z)$ and hence in $\xi,z$; the limiting minimum is attained. Testing a limiting minimizer gives the upper bound and localized minimizing sequences give the lower bound. The minimum is zero exactly when $q_A(\xi)=b$ and $z=0$.

The Hankel assertion is now Theorem~\ref{thm:binary-hankel}, applied before imposing synchronization; imposing it changes the feasible residual set, not the ambient Fisher metric. The native visibility theorem and Theorem~\ref{thm:reconstruct} recover the normalized endpoint representation. Endpoint congruences are changes of variables in $z\ge0$ for every real $y$, not just $y\ge0$. Hence every recovered representative has the same extension $\widehat G_Q$ and the same nonlinear contact formula.

Finally $\ell_i(\eta)^2=(a_i^{\mathsf T}\eta)^2+O(|\eta|^3)$, uniformly. For a small enough fixed neighbourhood, the analogue of \eqref{eq:quadratic-coercivity} still holds. At $\eta=\sqrt t\,\xi$ the extra normalized term is $O(\sqrt t)$ on bounded sets. The same localization and two-sided minimum argument proves the perturbation and compact-uniform conclusions.
\end{proof}

\begin{example}[Two distinct observed sheets and a critical quadratic cone]\label{ex:synchronized-critical}
Take $d=2,k=3$ and loadings $(1,0),(0,1),(1,1)$, with any three separated interior sites and zero, one, or two endpoints. The root-variance image is exactly
\[
 \mathcal V_A=\{v\in\R_+^3:(v_3-v_1-v_2)^2=4v_1v_2\}.
\]
To prove the converse, choose $\xi_1=\sqrt{v_1}$ and, when $v_1>0$, choose $\xi_2=(v_3-v_1-v_2)/(2\sqrt{v_1})$; the identity gives $\xi_2^2=v_2$. The case $v_1=0$ follows directly.

The marked fast map $F(\eta)=(\eta_1^2,\eta_2^2)$ has four real inverse points over $t(b_1,b_2)$ with $b_1,b_2>0$. The third variance has the two distinct observed values
\[
 t(\sqrt{b_1}+\sqrt{b_2})^2,
 \qquad t(\sqrt{b_1}-\sqrt{b_2})^2.
\]
They correspond to the relative sign, not the simultaneous sign which is observationally redundant. The slow graphs in fast coordinates are
$g_\pm(u)=u_1+u_2\pm2\sqrt{u_1u_2}$.
On a tube of radius $\epsilon t$ about $t(b_1,b_2)$, with fixed sufficiently small $\epsilon>0$, their slopes are bounded and their second derivatives have supremum comparable to $t^{-1}$. If the target third variance is $tb_3$ different from at least one of the displayed values, the largest centre residual is comparable to $t$, so $cM\asymp1$. Here $c/r$ also need not vanish: the critical theorem does not assume an artificially separated inverse tube. Formula~\eqref{eq:synchronized-E} gives the entire cone, including the loci where the fast sheets join.

With two endpoints and one colour, $k=3$ is fully visible for every positive exposure vector. Thus in this explicit singular family the labelled endpoint profile reconstructs the native metric for the non-affine critical contact, and the fixed-budget native information lies on the hypersurface of Theorem~\ref{thm:budget}. No metric was selected to manufacture these properties.
\end{example}

\begin{remark}[Exact rank constraint]
The rank-one constraint in $\mathcal V_A$ is part of the observation geometry. Replacing it by the whole positive semidefinite cone is a convex relaxation and can change \eqref{eq:synchronized-cost}. The rank-one lifting and sign quotient are classical in phase retrieval \cite{BCE,CSV}; the claim here is the displayed coupling to the binary experiment's forced inverse information and reconstructed endpoint profile. Statistical tangent-cone limits in \cite{Drton} likewise motivate, but do not supply, this model-specific coupling.
\end{remark}
