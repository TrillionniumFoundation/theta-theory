\subsection*{Evidence and assistance}
The proofs are the primary mathematical evidence. The accompanying exact rational and symbolic checks concern specified identities and finite examples, not universal theorem verification. Native build receipts record the checked source commit, input hashes, compiler versions, commands, diagnostics, and output hashes. Replaying the pinned v106 source is recorded separately from compiling the new principal manuscript, so a successful new build cannot be mistaken for evidence about an older source.

Language-model assistance was used in developing and drafting the synchronized-root, budget-fibre, weighted-jet, and endpoint-completion arguments and in the source checks. This disclosure is not a claim of mathematical validation. The manuscript and its proofs are submitted for independent author and referee examination; no acceptance decision or independent theorem certification is inferred from the build.
