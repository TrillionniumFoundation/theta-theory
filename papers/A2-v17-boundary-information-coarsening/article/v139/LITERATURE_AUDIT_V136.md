## The six exterior comparison questions

| Referee question | Inspected antecedent and precise additional assertion |
|---|---|
| Is the Schur embedding new? | No novelty is claimed for its representation-theoretic occurrence or pure-power polarization. The displayed normalization fixes j_n and C_n j_n=2 id. |
| Has the first flattening been restricted to this component? | The cited sections formulate the ambient first flattening. They do not state the kernel of i_q j_n. The new theorem proves this restriction explicitly; it does not claim an exhaustive search of every restriction in the literature. |
| Are all bilinear-rank kernels already given there? | Those inspected statements do not give the singular radical kernel or the nondegenerate parity alternative for this map. The present general formula includes every rank and every n>=2. |
| Are the reduced pullbacks given there? | The cited ambient definitions do not state the pullbacks along j_4. The exact reduced support 4,7/8,9 pullbacks remain the specialized consequences proved in the article. Their ideal-theoretic multiplicities are not asserted. |
| Is secant exclusion an immediate corollary? | Yes, once the restricted support exceeds 2n, exclusion follows from the classical closed-secant containment. That final implication is not presented as a new general secant theorem. |
| What remains after classical support language is removed? | The exact restricted contraction theorem, its uniform support formula and parity exception, and reconstruction from the complementary Jacobian pair on a nonempty open in each n>=4, followed in n=4 by intrinsic recovery of that pair from the nonreduced scheme. |

The general n theorem was developed in this revision, not read in these references. Its full proof and finite checks are supplied. No claim of priority for the algebraic Wick recurrence or the cofactor-divergence identity is made; they are proved directly as tools.
