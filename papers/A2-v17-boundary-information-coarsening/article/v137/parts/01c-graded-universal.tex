\begin{corollary}[Universal primary finiteness for symmetric-power quotients]
\label{cor:universal-primary-finiteness}
Fix \(e,r\ge1\) and \(1\le s\le\dim\Sym^rV\), and fix a target
vector space \(E\) of dimension \(s\).  Let \(\mathcal U\) be the
open parameter space of surjections \(\gamma:\Sym^rV\twoheadrightarrow E\).
On a coefficient affine chart write
\[
 A=\mathcal O_{\mathcal U}[t_{ij}],\quad \deg t_{ij}=1,
 \quad\deg\mathcal O_{\mathcal U}=0,\quad d=\det T,
 \quad J=I_s(\gamma\Sym^rT),\quad N=\binom{e+r-1}{r-1}.
\]
There is a finite locally closed stratification of \(\mathcal U\)
on which all the colons \(K_q=(J:d^q)\), \(0\le q<N\), commute
with arbitrary base change within a stratum.  The graded modules
\[
 M_q=A/(d,K_q),\qquad L_j=M_{j-1}(-ej),\quad1\le j\le N,
\]
are flat.  Their geometric associated-support packets, containment
posets, minimal and embedded status, and canonical local torsion
lengths are constant.  Their Hilbert functions, and therefore their
Hilbert polynomials, are constant on each connected final stratum.

The finite family of natural multiplication maps meant here is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:universal-finite-multiplications}
 \mu_{i,j}:L_i\otimes_A L_j\longrightarrow L_{i+j},
 \qquad i,j\ge1,\quad i+j\le N.
\end{equation}
These maps are surjective homogeneous maps; their sources, targets,
and images are flat and their ranks in every degree commute with
base change and are constant.  A prescribed finite list of additional
maps between fixed coefficient-degree pieces can also be included
in the refinement.  No simultaneous rank assertion for an unspecified
infinite family of such additional maps is intended.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The universal determinant-completion identity gives \(d^N\in J\).
Apply Theorem~\ref{thm:bounded-principal-colon-stratification} to the
finite list of homogeneous modules \(M_q\).  The primary models and
relative torsion construction in
Propositions~\ref{prop:relative-primary-models} and
\ref{prop:no-new-packet-torsion} give the associated-support and
multiplicity assertions.  Apply Lemma~\ref{lem:graded-flat-diagram}
to obtain the Hilbert functions and polynomials.  This is an additional
graded consequence, not a clause of an ungraded flattening theorem.

The colon ideals form an increasing chain.  Thus, with the indicated
shifts, \(L_i\otimes_A L_j\) is the quotient
\(M_{\max(i-1,j-1)}\), and the multiplication in
\eqref{eq:universal-finite-multiplications} is the canonical quotient
to \(M_{i+j-1}\).  Its source and target are among the already flat
modules and its cokernel is zero.  Lemma~\ref{lem:graded-flat-diagram}
applies in every degree.  Finally, any additionally prescribed finite
list of maps between finite coefficient-degree modules is handled by
flattening its finite list of images and cokernels.  Affine coefficient
charts admit a common finite refinement.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Primary signature and primary components]
In the universal statements a \emph{primary signature} is the package
of residual modules, associated-support packets, their containment
poset and local torsion lengths, graded Hilbert functions, and the
finite family \eqref{eq:universal-finite-multiplications}.  This
package is intrinsic.  A primary decomposition means an actual
intersection of primary ideals or primary submodules.  Such
noncanonical decompositions are constructed on finite splitting covers
by Proposition~\ref{prop:relative-primary-models}; they need not
descend as individually labelled components and are not asserted to
be canonical or explicitly classified throughout the universal family.
\end{definition}
