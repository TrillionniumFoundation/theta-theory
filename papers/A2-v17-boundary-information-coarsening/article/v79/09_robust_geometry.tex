\section{Raw preparation costs and robust geometric output}
\label{sec:v79-geometric-robustness}

The preceding finite-record theorem counts every attempt at a setting.
We now specify precisely what such an attempt contains when branch
resolution is performed after launch.  This also makes explicit how
approximate cross-clock sharing enters the geometric error bound.

\begin{definition}[Raw-launch branch-resolved acquisition]
\label{def:v79-raw}
A setting is a prescribed source, clock, and post-launch classification
rule.  One attempt is one draw from its normalized raw phase source,
followed by the trajectory and classification, whether or not it reaches
the desired gate or branch.  The apparatus either supplies the exact
prescribed word and lift labels or returns failure.  There are no false
positive labels.  On the desired branch the probability of successful
classification is $c_\gamma(s,t)>0$, independent of the initial free-time
coordinate and common across the clocks, as is the subsequent retention
efficiency $e_\gamma(s,t)$.  Both factors can be unknown.  The raw
source density in the physical flow box is $\rho_\gamma^{\rm raw}(s,p)$,
independent of that free-time coordinate.  It is the restriction of the
normalized source for all raw launches, not a density renormalized after
selecting the desired branch.
\end{definition}

This is an apparatus hypothesis about available marks.  Euclidean
contact positions, tangents, chart speeds and lattice vectors remain
unknown.  The supplied scalar charts and their overlap maps are fixed
labels, not geometric observations.  A finite identifying atlas remains
attached to a table and persistent on its neighborhood; no universal
unmarked billiard acquisition theorem is implied by this convention.

\begin{proposition}[Acceptance with classification fully charged]
\label{prop:v79-raw-cost}
Under Definition~\ref{def:v79-raw}, the retained raw density is
\[
 c_\gamma e_\gamma\rho_\gamma^{\rm raw}
        (s,-W_{\gamma,s})|W_{\gamma,st}|(T_j-W_\gamma).
\]
Consequently, on the bounded reference-atlas classes of
Theorem~\ref{thm:v78-uniform}, a raw-success floor is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v79-raw-success}
 p_{\rm raw}=c_-e_-\rho_-^{\rm raw}u_*r_-q_->0.
\end{equation}
If a physical preselection stage accepts a raw launch with probability
$q_{\rm entry}$ and the density subsequently used in the model is a
normalized conditional density $\rho^{\rm cond}$, then
$\rho^{\rm raw}=q_{\rm entry}\rho^{\rm cond}$ on that component;
its acceptance cost must be included.  When the density is already
raw, this factor must not be counted twice.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Apply the change of coordinates and free-time integration of
Lemma~\ref{lem:v77-physical} to the normalized raw source and multiply
by the two conditional success probabilities.  Integration on the
endpoint gate gives the actual probability of a successful raw launch.
Inserting all pointwise floors and the gate area gives
\eqref{eq:v79-raw-success}.  The last assertion is the elementary
factorization of the unconditional mass of a selected component into
its selection probability and conditional probability density.
\end{proof}

All branch hits that are missed, all rejected labels, and all late
arrivals are failures already paid for in $B$.  Unmodelled false positive
labels would create branch mixtures; they are not corrected simply by
adding clocks.  The mixture identity after
Proposition~\ref{prop:v78-two-clock} explains why this distinction is
mathematical, not only experimental terminology.

\begin{theorem}[Robust finite-record billiard reconstruction]
\label{thm:v79-robust-geometry}
Fix the bounded reference-atlas neighborhood, derivative bounds and
positive certificate margins in Theorem~\ref{thm:v78-records}, with
$k\ge2$ and $s\ge1$.  Suppose the true conditional laws on every
branch have the form
\[
 f_{\gamma,j}\ \propto\
     a_\gamma(T_j-W_\gamma)e^{\eta_{\gamma,j}},
 \qquad
 \max_{\gamma,j}\|\eta_{\gamma,j}\|_{C^{k+2}}\le\epsilon,
\]
on the needed larger gates.  Assume also the uniform
$C^{k+2+s}$ density bounds required for smoothing.  Make $B$ independent
raw launches at every one of $M$ settings, with a common actual success
floor $p>0$ for these possibly perturbed settings.  Such a floor follows
from~\eqref{eq:v79-raw-success} if the corresponding raw factors retain
the displayed floors.  Allow coordinate errors of magnitude at most
$\delta$.

The explicit reconstruction has, for sufficiently small total error,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v79-robust-geometric-rate}
 d_k(\widehat{\mathcal T},\mathcal T)
 \le C\left\{
  \left(\frac{\log(C_0MB/\zeta)}{Bp}\right)^{s/[2(k+2+s)+2]}
       +\delta^{s/(k+s+5)}+\epsilon\right\}
\end{equation}
with probability at least $1-2\zeta$.  On this event the output is a
smooth strictly convex disjoint periodic table.  Its raw preparation
cost is $MB$.  This is a sufficient geometric bound, not a claim that
the geometric statistical exponent or derivative loss is optimal.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The cap and kernel argument in Theorem~\ref{thm:v78-records} applies
to the actual conditional densities and the actual raw-success floor.
With $m=k+2$ it bounds the $C^m$ estimation error by the first two terms
in braces, on an event of probability at least $1-2\zeta$.
The misspecification theorem gives an additional $C^m$ action error
$C\epsilon$ relative to the true physical actions.  The finite corner
choice remains possible because the true anchor margins are fixed;
small errors preserve the tests on a finite candidate list.

The root, distance, positive square-root, trilateration and registration
maps used in the geometric theorem are defined on an open neighborhood
of the exact physical data.  They need not map intermediate noisy data
to exact stationary actions.  Their stated local bounds require only
closeness to the true actions and persistence of the denominators and
root collars.  Applying these bounds and the fixed partition-of-unity
blending gives~\eqref{eq:v79-robust-geometric-rate}.  The open
$C^2$ geometric validity conditions proved there apply to the same
blended output when the total radius, now including $\epsilon$, is
small enough.  The cap uses raw launches, so the budget is exactly $MB$.
\end{proof}

The residual test in Proposition~\ref{prop:v79-test} can be applied
before this reconstruction.  Its acceptance does not certify a billiard
model or exact source sharing: action-confounded efficiencies can have
zero residual.  Conversely a detected residual need not destroy useful
geometric accuracy when the independent misspecification bound is small.
Validation and robust inversion are separate conclusions, both needed
to interpret the experiment.
