\title[Polarized ramification and multiplication failure]{Polarized ramification and higher-corank structure\texorpdfstring{\\}{}in multiplication failure}
\author{Qian Qi}
\date{September 23, 2026 \quad (Revision 123)}
\subjclass[2020]{14M12, 14J28, 13C40, 14B05, 14D20}
\keywords{Multiplication failure, quadratic relations, K3 surfaces, primary decomposition, intrinsic polarization}
\hypersetup{pdftitle={Polarized ramification and higher-corank structure in multiplication failure},pdfauthor={Qian Qi}}
\begin{abstract}
We study the scheme of multiplication failure for cube-zero algebras
with Hilbert function $(1,4,6)$. Its nilpotent structure determines not
only an abstract ramification K3 surface but its quartic polarization.
The reconstruction uses the canonical bundle of the intrinsic embedded
support and its nilradical module to recover a Veronese section ring.
The resulting family has a nine-dimensional image in polarized K3
moduli, and the relation tensor is determined up to finitely many
projective equivalence classes over a general point of that image.
We also determine the fixed-tensor primary structure on a nonempty
open part of the projection-corank-two stratum. Four ramification
branches meet there, and a further embedded component makes the
nilradical have index three, with square of transverse length one.
On the corank-one locus we retain the ramification normal form,
all ordinary ideal powers, and their intrinsic torsion, and establish
precise relative flatness and base-change statements. The polarized
reconstruction applies to the failure scheme on the whole Grassmannian,
since the smooth locus of its reduction is intrinsic. The proof includes
an explicit rational relation system and exact finite rank certificates.
\end{abstract}
