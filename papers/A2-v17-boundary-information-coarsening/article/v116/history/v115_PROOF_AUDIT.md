# Adversarial checks on the new higher-product theorem

1. **Base points.** The pencil propagation argument is used only for a base-point-free hyperplane. Quadratic surjectivity implies this hypothesis. A hyperplane with a base point is exactly an evaluation hyperplane and is treated separately.
2. **Tangent collisions.** The secant support includes length-two nonreduced divisors. The tangent hyperplane is normalized using the first-jet action, not assumed to be a limit with constant corank. Its product and derivative ranks are computed directly.
3. **Residual plane derivative.** For both split and tangent contacts, the n independent graph variables act on a^m and wa^(m-1); products with two W factors form the polar kernel. Thus the residual image calculation does not assume injectivity of Sym^m U.
4. **Actual versus expected dimension.** Regularity off the evaluation curve follows from tangent dimension three and the already established three-dimensional support, not from an expected-grade hypothesis that fails in this family.
5. **Evaluation corank.** U=l V_(n-1) gives U^m=l^m V_(m(n-1)), hence corank m, not one. Schur minors there have order at least m.
6. **Nonzero nilpotents.** A secant determinant has a nonzero quadratic initial term at an evaluation point. Its j-th power has exact order 2j in the regular ambient local ring, so it cannot lie in the maximal-minor ideal when 2j<m. Radical membership alone is not used to prove nonvanishing.
7. **Associated points.** The argument concerns coherent ideal sheaves on projective space. It excludes an irrelevant-ideal issue in a chosen homogeneous affine presentation. All possible embedded supports are confined to the evaluation curve before equivariance is applied.
8. **Finiteness and equivariance.** Associated points form a finite set. Transitivity on closed points of the evaluation curve excludes additional closed associated points; existence of the generic embedded point follows from the nonzero coherent nilradical.
9. **Boundary m=2.** The full support/corank/smoothness/saturation results include m=2. The embedded-point conclusion and nilpotency lower bound are not asserted for m=2.
10. **Sharpness and breadth.** No exact nilpotency index, explicit embedded primary ideal, arbitrary-contact higher-product classification, or completed Ballico priority comparison is inferred.

The written arguments address these tests. This audit records an internal check, not independent referee certification.
