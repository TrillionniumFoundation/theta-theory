# Primary-source audit for revision 114

Checked on 2026-09-22. The mathematical comparison is now in Section 9 of `paper.tex`; this file records the evidentiary boundary.

## Directly rechecked primary texts

**Ballico 1996.** Complete publisher PDF: https://msp.org/pjm/1996/172-2/pjm-v172-n2-p01-s.pdf . Theorem 0.2 on journal page 308 and Proposition 2.2 on pages 312–313 were checked against rendered page images. They concern failure on finite sections of a fixed embedded surface. They are not a classification on the product of Grassmannians of proper binary subseries. The paper cites these results rather than claiming novelty for the general secant-failure principle.

**Conca–Mostafazadehfard–Singh–Varbaro.** https://arxiv.org/pdf/1709.05685 , Sections 1–2 and Theorem 2.1. These are classical-input comparisons for the Hankel annihilator parameter space, not a replacement for the proof concerning its isotropic-plane image.

**Stacks reducedness.** https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/031R , Lemma 10.157.3. The manuscript now uses this exact lemma rather than only the containing section tag 031O.

## Scope of novelty language

The cotangent presentation, Fitting-ideal calculus, Schur elimination, and determinantal resolution are standard tools. The manuscript supplies the polar residual identification, its application to the constrained varying-subseries multiplication problem, and the stated component/wall arguments. Neither a working bibliography nor successful diagnostics establish absolute priority.
