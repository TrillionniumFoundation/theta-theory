\begin{abstract}
For a symmetric multiplication system, we identify a polar residual space whose multiplication kernel measures the tangent excess of its annihilator incidence. In expected codimension this gives the full singular support of the maximal-minor scheme and, on every corank-one polar stratum, all cotangent Fitting ideals as residual minors. Applied to quadratic binary subseries, the criterion covers every annihilator rank, including colliding support. We determine the failure components in all dimensions, prove their orientation descent explicitly, and construct the relative residual determinant divisor. Its normal derivative yields an ordinary double crossing on a nonempty dense open subset; the complete residual rank stratification controls the complementary tangent-excess loci. We also determine same-annihilator signed intersections, give a primary decomposition of the rank-two isotropy fibre, and establish algebraic dihedral descent for higher-product two-point sectors. The general polar theorem applies to arbitrary symmetric linear systems. Exact information-recovery, native-realization and statistical consequences are developed in the applications and appendices.
\end{abstract}
