\begin{abstract}
For symmetric multiplication systems, polar residual spaces measure the tangent excess of annihilator incidences. We identify joint polar relations at every corank and construct exact residual matrix equations retaining all nonlinear terms. Transversality gives generic determinantal neighbourhoods; at corank one, the polar kernel counts the equations of a minimal presentation and determines their quadratic obstruction. Residual minors describe restricted cotangent Fitting ideals and the expected-codimension singular support. For quadratic binary subseries we determine the failure components in all parameter dimensions, with maximal-dimensional components classified in the excess regime, and construct a relative residual determinant divisor with a dense ordinary-double-crossing locus. For hyperplane subseries in every symmetric degree, we determine the entire failure support and every corank. In degree at least three the associated subvarieties are precisely the secant threefold and an embedded evaluation curve; the nilpotency index grows unboundedly with the degree. The arguments also give exceptional orientation descent, same-annihilator signed intersections, rank-two fibre primary decomposition, and algebraic dihedral descent for higher-product two-point sectors. Full information-recovery, native-realization and statistical applications are retained in the accompanying appendices.
\end{abstract}
