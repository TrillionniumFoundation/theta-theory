\begin{abstract}
We study the schemes on spaces of subseries where multiplication
of sections fails to be surjective. For hyperplanes in the complete
quartic binary series, we determine the full primary ideal in every
symmetric degree $m\ge2$: it is the intersection of the secant cubic
ideal and the $m(m+1)/2$-th power of the evaluation-curve ideal.
We give its exact transverse equation, nilpotency index, infinitesimal
layers and normal cone. For binary series of all degrees $n\ge4$,
a filtered residual matrix proves quadratic growth of the embedded
thickness. A conductor identity then identifies all Fitting ideals
for divisor-conditioned subseries with those of a finite contact
algebra. On residue-pencil Grassmannians of unbounded codimension,
this yields the complete primary decomposition, every corank, and
weighted collision singularities for arbitrary contact multiplicities,
with an explicit wall in the symmetric degree. The residual framework
also gives the quadratic binary component theorem, including all
maximal-dimensional excess components, and a relative determinant
divisor with a dense ordinary-double-crossing locus. General polar
relations identify the multiplication-specific normal derivative;
standard determinantal elimination supplies its local matrix form.
The complete information-recovery and statistical applications are
retained in accompanying appendices.
\end{abstract}
