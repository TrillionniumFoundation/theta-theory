## Ballico 1993: actual access boundary

E. Ballico, *On the Failure Locus of Higher Order Properties of Embeddings in Projective Spaces*, Math. Nachr. 163 (1993), 5–13; DOI `10.1002/mana.19931630102`.

The official Wiley abstract/bibliographic page and reference list were available. The full, PDF, and ePDF routes did not return usable theorem pages. Targeted author/institution searches did not supply a full text. The preceding revision's first-page inspection is inherited as such; it is not relabelled as a complete reading performed here.

Accordingly, no statement is made that this paper implies, excludes, or anticipates the contact-cokernel theorem, the primary formula, or the normalization. The exact parameter space, variation of subseries, degree range, scheme structure, coranks, and associated-prime content of its actual theorems remain to be compared from the full text. New mathematical results do not close a missing-source comparison by themselves.

## Novelty boundary

The manuscript does not claim invention of Schur complements, intrinsic rank derivatives, Fitting ideals, finite-algebra power bases, Vandermonde determinants, or discriminant identities. The claim submitted for assessment is the exact relative conductor transport and the complete contact-pencil failure-scheme package, including all multiplicity strata and the smooth finite normalization of the integral moving divisor. Historical originality of that package requires the unresolved nearest-source comparison as well as ordinary editorial review.
