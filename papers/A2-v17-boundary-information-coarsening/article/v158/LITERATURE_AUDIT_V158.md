# Primary-source comparison for A2 v158

The comparisons below delimit what was checked; bibliographic discovery is not a proof-level reading of an inaccessible original.

## Symmetric invariant ideals and products

Henriques–Varbaro, *Test, multiplier and invariant ideals*, Advances in Mathematics 287 (2016), 704–732, DOI `10.1016/j.aim.2015.09.028`, arXiv `1407.4324`, §2.4, Theorems 2.6–2.8. This section explicitly recalls Abeasis's classification in the multiplicity-free symmetric coordinate ring, products indexed by diagrams, integral closures and their symbolic-power descriptions. Theorem 4.8 gives the symmetric determinantal multiplier formula. These theorem statements, rather than a generic citation to determinantal ideals, delimit the classical scope used by the manuscript.

The original Abeasis paper is cited there as *Gli ideali GL(V)-invarianti in S(S²V)*, Rendiconti di Matematica 13 (1980), 235–262. This audit does not claim an independent complete reading of that original. Ruitenburg's 1989 Compositio paper is further bibliographic evidence for the general multiplicity-free invariant-ideal framework, not a source claimed here to have been fully proof-audited.

The paper's ordinary ideal identity identifies the image of a particular apolar dual multiplication map with a balanced product. It is not represented as a new classification of invariant ideals. No exhaustive claim that the exact formula was never stated under another terminology is made.

## Complete quadrics and their scheme-level graphs

Massarenti, *On the birational geometry of spaces of complete forms I: collineations and quadrics*, Proceedings of the London Mathematical Society 121 (2020), 1579–1618, DOI `10.1112/plms.12377`, arXiv `1803.09161v2`, Remark 2.5 and Construction 2.6: the exterior-power graph and successive symmetric rank-locus blow-ups; the construction points to Vainsencher's Theorem 6.3. The submitted bibliography retains Vainsencher 1982 as the original source. The precise construction is used, not a claim that blow-up descriptions agree only on closed points.

Casarotti–Corniani–Massarenti, *Complete Singular Collineations and Quadrics*, International Mathematics Research Notices 2023, no. 18, 15370–15407, DOI `10.1093/imrn/rnac271`, arXiv `2111.02940`, Definition 2.5, Remark 2.6 and Theorem 2.14: the classical singular-rank varieties and their smooth boundary geometry. The article's new assertion is their exact realization by all nonzero powers in a fixed algebra, with the last power recovering the image plane. It does not claim those varieties or their orbit stratification as newly discovered.

## Congruence and minimal indices

R. C. Thompson, *Pencils of complex and real symmetric and skew matrices*, Linear Algebra and its Applications 147 (1991), 323–371, DOI `10.1016/0024-3795(91)90238-R`. The classical complex symmetric congruence theorem is explicitly an external classification input. The local DVR diagonalization needed for contacts is proved in full in v158; the kernel vector, splitting, Toeplitz nullity inversion and degree formula are computed in the manuscript. A left-right equivalence theorem is not silently used in place of symmetric congruence.

## The unresolved Ballico documentary comparison

E. Ballico, *On the Failure Locus of Higher Order Properties of Embeddings in Projective Spaces*, Mathematische Nachrichten 163 (1993), 5–13, DOI `10.1002/mana.19931630102`. Bibliographic/publisher listings were available, but the theorem and proof text was not obtained. This limitation is retained in both submitted PDFs. No nonanticipation conclusion is drawn from it.

The classical ideal, apolar, complete-quadric, multiplier and congruence results remain credited. Finite searches do not establish exceptional originality, and the bibliography is not a substitute for the new proofs.
