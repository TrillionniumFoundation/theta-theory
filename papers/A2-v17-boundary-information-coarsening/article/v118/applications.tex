\begin{abstract}
These appendices accompany the geometric article \emph{Conductor strata
and nonreduced multiplication failure schemes}. They reproduce the
complete contact, realization, earlier component, likelihood, boundary,
finite-precision and estimation developments retained from the preceding
revision. Their theorem and equation numbering agrees with the appendices
of the full archival manuscript. References to the full geometric complements are
external cross-references, not hypotheses imported without a statement.
\end{abstract}
