## Classical inputs

**Conca–Mostafazadehfard–Singh–Varbaro.** *Hankel determinantal rings have rational singularities*, Advances in Mathematics 335 (2018), 111–129; arXiv:1709.05685. Primary arXiv text was available. Sections 1–2 identify Hankel minor ideals under a change of rectangular shape and the secant model. For a rank bound r in the k by k Hankel matrix, the relevant maximal-minor presentation is (r+1) by (2k-r-1); affine dimension is 2r and projective dimension 2r-1. Exact rank is open in the rank-at-most closure, including confluent limits. These classical facts supply `lem:hankel-strata`, not the new component enumeration or wall normal form.

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.05685 and https://arxiv.org/pdf/1709.05685 . PDF text retrieval succeeded; screenshot requests failed in the browser cache and are not represented as successful visual inspection.

**Eagon–Northcott and determinantal cycles.** The retained bibliography attributes the grade criterion to Eagon–Northcott and Kustin and the cycle formula to Kempf–Laksov and Fulton. The revision checks expected grade before applying a resolution. It uses the dual map for the Chern-class convention. No independent-entry or generic-linear-matrix hypothesis is assumed.

**Stacks Project, Tag 031O.** Primary text was checked for the equivalence of no embedded associated primes with S1 and for reducedness from R0 and S1. These are classical final commutative-algebra steps, isolated as propositions rather than presented as new algebra.

Primary source: https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/031O .
