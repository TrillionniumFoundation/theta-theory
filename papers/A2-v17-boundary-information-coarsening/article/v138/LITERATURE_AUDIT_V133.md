## Primary text inspected

Martin--Mezzedimi--Veniani, *Nodal Enriques surfaces are Reye congruences*,
author preprint arXiv:2306.11661, especially Proposition 3.6, Theorem 3.7,
Remark 3.8, and Remark 1.2. The relevant mathematical diagrams and formulas
were inspected in page images as well as extracted text. The new article
subsection `sec:inverse-data-comparison` compares the supplied inverse data,
not just dimensions. It distinguishes an Enriques Fano polarization/Reye
bundle and exchanged K3 line bundles from one unmarked quartic polarization
and from the abstract nonreduced failure scheme.

Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.11661 (preprint pages 8--10; Remark 1.2 on page 2).

Ballico, *On the failure cycles for the quadratic normality of a projective
variety*, Pacific J. Math. 172 (1996), 307--313: complete extracted theorem
text was inspected; pages 307--308 were also checked as page images. Theorem
0.2 concerns failure witnessed by finite linear sections of an embedded
surface, using the numerical failure of quadratic normality; Section 1
constructs an incidence family and a multiplication map over a Grassmannian.
These are substantive antecedents, not merely a shared title. This does not
supply the missing theorem text of the distinct 1993 paper.

Source: https://msp.org/pjm/1996/172-2/pjm-v172-n2-p01-s.pdf
