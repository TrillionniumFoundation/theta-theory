\title[Universal determinant completion and boundary laws]{Universal determinant completion, effective Pieri multiplication, and intrinsic primary boundary laws in multiplication failure}
\author{Qian Qi}

\keywords{Multiplication failure, Fitting scheme, determinant completion, Pieri multiplication, nilpotent filtration, primary specialization, quartic K3 surface}
\hypersetup{pdftitle={Universal determinant completion, effective Pieri multiplication, and intrinsic primary boundary laws in multiplication failure},pdfauthor={Qian Qi}}
\begin{abstract}
We study the nonreduced Fitting scheme on a Grassmannian on which
multiplication fails for cube-zero algebras with Hilbert function
\((1,4,6)\).  Its nilpotent filtration is intrinsic and recovers,
on the first layer, the ramification quartic K3 surface with its
polarization.

A determinant-completion theorem supplies the uniform algebraic bound
behind the entire boundary theory.  If \(V\) has dimension \(e\),
\(r\ge1\), and \(\gamma:\Sym^rV\twoheadrightarrow S\), then for the
generic endomorphism \(M\),
\[
 (\det M)^{\binom{e+r-1}{r-1}}
 \in I_{\dim S}(\gamma\Sym^rM).
\]
We prove that any finite-presentation family with such a bounded
principal determinant power admits a finite locally closed
stratification on which every residual colon commutes with arbitrary
base change, the positive graded layers are flat, and their geometric
associated-point packets, embedded multiplicities, Hilbert polynomials,
and multiplication ranks are constant.  Thus the universal determinant
bound and the intrinsic primary boundary atlas are two parts of one
finite mechanism.

For the \((1,4,6)\) problem the bound is \(d^5\in J\).
At projection corank two we determine the generic primary signatures
of all mixed-kernel and pure-block rank types.  The two nontrivial
rank-drop slices are certified over their rational function fields:
the four-line slice has factor
\[
 u_0u_1\bigl((s+9)u_0^2+(3s+6)u_0u_1-7u_1^2\bigr),
\]
with nonzero discriminant \(9s^2+64s+288\), while the two-line slice
has factor \(10u_0^2+u_0u_1-4u_1^2\) and an embedded vertex module of
length three, computed after generic saturation.  At projection
corank three the determinant depth is five or six.  At projection
corank four it is exactly five: right \(GL(V)\)-stability of the
maximal-minor ideal, semisimple projection, and explicit
Cauchy--Pieri bideterminant multiplication give \(d^4\in J\), while
\(d^3\notin J\).

The resulting graded algebra specializes in finite families and is
packaged canonically by deformation to the normal cone.  The abstract
nonreduced failure scheme continues to determine the polarized quartic
K3 surface.
\end{abstract}
