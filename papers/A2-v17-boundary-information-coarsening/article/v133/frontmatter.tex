\title[Web reconstruction and primary boundary laws]{Intrinsic web reconstruction and primary boundary laws for multiplication-failure schemes}
\author{Qian Qi}
\date{September 23, 2026 \quad (Revision 133)}
\subjclass[2020]{14M12, 14J28, 13C40, 14B05, 14D20, 05E05}
\keywords{Multiplication failure, intrinsic reconstruction, Fitting scheme, determinant completion, primary decomposition, nilpotent filtration, boundary specialization, quartic K3 surface}
\hypersetup{pdftitle={Intrinsic web reconstruction and primary boundary laws, revision 133},pdfauthor={Qian Qi}}
\begin{abstract}
We study the intrinsic nonreduced geometry of multiplication-failure
Fitting schemes.  For cube-zero algebras with Hilbert function
\((1,4,6)\), we prove that the abstract failure scheme determines
the original relation web up to projective equivalence on a nonempty
invariant open.  The deepest Schubert stratum is intrinsic.  Its
normal cone has two distinguishable tensor rulings, and its residual
ideal recovers both irreducible Pluecker components of the web.
The universal Schur coefficient map identifies the specific left
coefficient lines, and a functorial ruling argument transports both by
one projective transformation.  Two explicit Pluecker quadrics prove
generic uniqueness of their recombination.  Outside that open, the
component invariant has reduced, double, secant, or flag-line fibres;
we describe its residual involution and tangent degeneracy.  This separates the generic finite ambiguity left by
the polarized Jacobian quartic K3, which is itself recovered from
the first nilpotent layer.

In every dimension and symmetric degree, determinant completion bounds
the residual colon tower.  For finite-presentation families over
noetherian bases, finite primary models give exact geometric
associated-point exhaustion.  Galois descent of orbit ideals in
characteristic zero, and power-certified packet ideals in arbitrary
characteristic, place the packets on the original strata.  Coherent
relative torsion modules and their geometric local lengths commute
with base change there; the graded Hilbert functions and a specified
finite family of multiplication ranks are constant.

On the smooth, basepoint-free Jacobian open the nilradical has global
bound \(\widehat{\mathcal N}^{5}=0\), with exact index five at
projection coranks three and four.  A normalized Cauchy--Pieri
projection proves the upper bound.  The first two positive layers
have global minimal supports described by Jacobian incidence and
rank-two determinantal geometry.  An incidence resolution has four
contact points, a plane quartic of genus three, and the K3 as
successive fibres; an explicit two-parameter pullback has seven
reduced components.  Complete generic corank-two primary tables and
collision laws are accompanied by exact function-field certificates.
The global support and depth laws are distinguished from a full
embedded-primary atlas for the higher residual layers.
For quotient-induced symmetric powers the classical maximal-minor
identity also yields explicit primary decompositions, sharp exponents,
and embedded and layer-length formulas.
\end{abstract}
