\section{Physically realizable retention nuisance}
\label{sec:v80-physical-nuisance}

An abstract clock-dependent weight is not automatically a perturbation
of a physical experiment.  In this section the dynamics, source,
retention stage and classifier are specified separately.  The source is
normalized at the raw-launch level.  On a regular branch let $J_W(z)>0$
denote the endpoint Jacobian times the raw source density and, when
applicable, the known uniform-delay factor.  Before classification the
retained subdensity is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v80-physical-raw}
                    r_j(z)=e_j(z)J_W(z)(T_j-W(z)).
\end{equation}
A retention stage means an actual Bernoulli decision, conditional on
the endpoint record, with probability $e_j(z)$.  It requires neither a
measured momentum nor a measured trajectory length in the retained
record.  The statement that a particular function is an admissible
retention probability is a description of the statistical class, not
an instruction to an observer to compute an unknown action.

\begin{proposition}[The physical retention tangent]
\label{prop:v80-retention-tangent}
Fix a regular physical branch and its normalized raw source.  Suppose
the baseline retention probability satisfies
$e_-\le e(z)\le1-e_-$ on a compact gate, with $0<e_-<1/2$.
Every sufficiently small tuple $\xi_j\in C^m(Q)$ is physically
realizable, with the dynamics and source unchanged, by
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v80-realization}
                       e_j=e\exp(\xi_j).
\end{equation}
The resulting log-weight perturbation in the clock model is exactly
$\xi_j$.  Consequently the tangent space of independently variable
endpoint-retention probabilities is the full tuple $(C^m(Q))^J$.
For conditional laws the corresponding scores are
$\xi_j-\int_Q\xi_j f_j$.  For raw laws the successful-density
variation is $\dot r_j=r_j\xi_j$ and the failure-mass variation is
$-\int_Qr_j\xi_j$.  If retention is constrained to be shared by all
clocks, only the common-function subspace is available.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Choose $\|\xi_j\|_\infty$ small enough that
$e_-\exp(-\|\xi_j\|_\infty)>0$ and
$(1-e_-)\exp(\|\xi_j\|_\infty)<1$.
Then~\eqref{eq:v80-realization} is a legitimate Bernoulli probability.
Substitution in~\eqref{eq:v80-physical-raw} proves the exact log-weight
assertion.  Differentiating the raw density and its complementary mass
gives the raw variations.  Differentiating its normalization gives the
conditional score.  Conversely every differentiable path of strictly
interior positive retention probabilities has a log derivative of this
form.  The statement about a shared stage follows by imposing
$e_1=\cdots=e_J$ before differentiation.
\end{proof}

This proposition identifies a nuisance cone inside the physical
apparatus class, rather than inferring physical realizability from an
algebraic fit.  It does not say that every laboratory has an arbitrary
endpoint-dependent detector.  When the apparatus restricts retention,
its allowed tangent is correspondingly smaller.  General robust
contamination models address a different perturbation mechanism;
see, for example,~\cite{UppalSinghPoczos2020}.  Here the disturbance
is a smooth retention probability attached to actual trajectories.

\begin{theorem}[Exact compensation along physical deformations]
\label{thm:v80-physical-compensation}
Consider finitely many regular billiard branches, or regular return
charts, with fixed gates and deadlines.  Let a smooth one-parameter
family of actual systems have actions $W_b^{(t)}$ and raw Jacobian-source
factors $J_b^{(t)}>0$ on those gates.  Suppose the gates and time windows
retain strict margins, these factors and actions depend smoothly on
$t$ in $C^m$, and the baseline efficiencies $e_b$ lie strictly between
zero and one.  There are physically admissible clock-dependent
retention probabilities
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v80-compensating-retention}
 e_{j,b}^{(t)}(z)=e_b(z)\frac{J_b^{(0)}(z)}{J_b^{(t)}(z)}
                \frac{T_j-W_b^{(0)}(z)}{T_j-W_b^{(t)}(z)}
\end{equation}
for all sufficiently small $|t|$.  They satisfy
$\|\log(e_{j,b}^{(t)}/e_b)\|_{C^m}\le C|t|$ and make the
\emph{full raw laws}, not merely their conditional parts, exactly equal
to their values at $t=0$, at every specified clock.  A fixed label
channel, including false positive labels, preserves this equality.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
All denominators have fixed positive lower bounds on the finite gates.
The reciprocal and logarithm maps are smooth there.  At $t=0$ the
multiplier in~\eqref{eq:v80-compensating-retention} is one, so uniform
continuity and the strict efficiency bounds keep it an admissible
Bernoulli probability for small $|t|$.  Differentiating on this compact
family and applying the mean value theorem gives the $C^m$ log bound.
Direct substitution gives
\[
 e_{j,b}^{(t)}J_b^{(t)}(T_j-W_b^{(t)})
                    =e_bJ_b^{(0)}(T_j-W_b^{(0)}).
\]
Every successful joint measure is therefore unchanged.  The total mass
of their complement, which includes all missed launches, is unchanged
as well.  Applying a fixed stochastic channel to equal measures
preserves equality.  The argument also applies to audited calibration
records which reveal the true branch only after this retention stage;
it does not cover an extra sensor that supplies independent geometric
measurements before retention.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[A sharp physical efficiency floor for billiard geometry]
\label{cor:v80-physical-floor}
Fix a smooth periodic dispersing reference table with a finite
identifying atlas and strict certificate margins.  In a sufficiently
small bounded neighborhood of this atlas, allow independent
clock-dependent endpoint efficiencies with $C^{k+2}$ log deviations
at most $\epsilon$, where $k\ge2$.  For every sufficiently small
$\epsilon>0$, there are two noncongruent physical tables in this
neighborhood, at geometric distance at least $c\epsilon$, whose full
raw record laws agree at all the finitely many prescribed settings.
This remains true for any fixed calibrated label channels.
Consequently any estimator from arbitrarily many such records has
worst-case expected $d_k$ error at least $c'\epsilon$ on this class.
The linear dependence on $\epsilon$ in
Theorem~\ref{thm:v80-finite-geometry} is therefore sharp for this
physical retention class.  No optimality of its sampling exponent is
asserted.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Deform one obstacle on a small open boundary arc by outward normal
displacement $t\varphi$, where $\varphi\ge0$ is smooth, supported
inside the arc and not identically zero.  Keep the lattice and all
other obstacles fixed.  For sufficiently small $t\ge0$, smooth strict
convexity and disjointness persist.  The obstacle expands, so a
finite-horizon reference table acquires no new free corridor.
All the finitely many clear-flight, nongrazing, chart, root and distance
certificate margins persist by continuous dependence.  Hence the same
identifying atlas remains valid; no assertion about constructing a new
universal protocol is used.

The first variation of the area of this obstacle is
$\int\varphi\,ds>0$.  On the bounded $C^1$ family, area is locally
Lipschitz in the boundary embeddings and invariant under a common
Euclidean isometry.  The geometric distance, which controls these
embeddings modulo that isometry, therefore satisfies
$d_k(\mathcal T_t,\mathcal T_0)\ge c_0t$ for sufficiently small $t$.
In particular the two tables are not congruent.

Choose a smooth family of normalized raw sources in the common scalar
phase charts, positive on a larger compact neighborhood of all required
initial states and constant in the free-time coordinate on the required
windows.  Extend them outside these compact flow boxes and normalize
before selection.  The same source is used at every deadline of a
branch; its normalization is included in $J_b^{(t)}$.  The finite regular-action implicit equations give smooth
$C^{k+2}$ dependence of $W_b^{(t)}$ and $J_b^{(t)}$ on $t$.
Choose strictly interior baseline retention probabilities.  Theorem~
\ref{thm:v80-physical-compensation} supplies admissible probabilities
with identical full raw laws and log deviations bounded by $C_0t$.
Taking $t=\epsilon/C_0$ proves the asserted separation after decreasing
the permitted range of $\epsilon$ if necessary.

Under these two parameter values any estimator has the same output
law, for any number of trials.  The triangle inequality gives the sum
of its two expected errors at least $d_k(\mathcal T_t,\mathcal T_0)$.
The larger expected error is at least half that distance.  This is a
two-point physical lower bound, not an inference from an abstract
weighted-function pair.
\end{proof}

In the convex contact class an even simpler physical action example is
$L_t=L_0+t$: the return map is unchanged but its roof changes.  Formula~
\eqref{eq:v80-compensating-retention} compensates the resulting return
actions at the chosen counts.  Thus absolute action has a physical
linear efficiency floor there as well.  This example does not replace
the noncongruent billiard deformation in the preceding corollary.
