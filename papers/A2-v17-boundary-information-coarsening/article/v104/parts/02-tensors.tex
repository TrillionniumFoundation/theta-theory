\section{The tensor quotient extracted by reduction}\label{sec:tensors}
For a closed residual set $E\subset\R^m$ define
\[
 \cC(E)=\cl\bigl(\conv\{yy^{\mathsf T}:y\in E\}+\Sym_m^+\bigr).
\]
The order and the residual set in Theorem~\ref{thm:reduction} give, for every $H>0$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:metric-contact}
 \min_N\|R(x,t)\|_H=a_Ht^\rho+o(t^\rho),\qquad
 a_H^2=\min_{y\in\cE_\rho}y^{\mathsf T}Hy.
\end{equation}
The second equality follows either directly from \eqref{eq:relative} or by the two normalized-residual inclusions in its proof. We record the finite representation to make the reduction's output explicit.

\begin{theorem}[Finite moment lift and metric faces]\label{thm:lift}
Let $E=\bigcup_{b=1}^B(c_b+L_b\R^{r_b})$, where $L_b$ has full column rank and $c_b\notin\ran L_b$. Put $A_b=[L_b,c_b]$. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:lift}
 \cC(E)=\left\{\sum_b A_bM_bA_b^{\mathsf T}+P:
 M_b\ge0,\ P\ge0,\ \sum_b(M_b)_{r_b+1,r_b+1}=1\right\}.
\end{equation}
This set is closed without taking an additional closure. For $H>0$, let $y_b(H)$ be the unique $H$-nearest point to zero on the $b$th sheet, and put $q_b(H)=y_b(H)^{\mathsf T}Hy_b(H)$. The exposed face at the minimum of $S\mapsto\tr(HS)$ is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:faces}
 \conv\{y_b(H)y_b(H)^{\mathsf T}:q_b(H)=\min_cq_c(H)\}.
\end{equation}
The envelope is recovered from its positive-metric costs by
\[
 \cC(E)=\bigcap_{H>0}\{S\in\Sym_m:\tr(HS)\ge\min_bq_b(H)\}.
\]
The active-sheet sets form a finite semialgebraic partition of the positive metric cone. Applied to \eqref{eq:intrinsic-sheets}, all these objects are invariants of the marked feasible observation germ.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The second moments of finitely supported affine random vectors give the identity
\[
 \conv\{(u,1)(u,1)^{\mathsf T}:u\in\R^r\}
 =\{M\ge0:M_{r+1,r+1}=1\}.
\]
For clarity, if $M=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}K&m\\m^{\mathsf T}&1\end{smallmatrix}\right)$, its covariance is $K-mm^{\mathsf T}\ge0$. Diagonalize the covariance and realize each eigendirection by symmetric pairs about $m$; the resulting finite distribution has second moment $M$. Conversely every second moment is positive semidefinite.

For each matrix in \eqref{eq:lift}, let $\lambda_b$ be its last diagonal entry. When $\lambda_b>0$, divide by $\lambda_b$ and use the identity just proved. When $\lambda_b=0$, positivity makes its last row and column zero, so $A_bM_bA_b^{\mathsf T}$ may be absorbed into $P$. This proves equality before closure. Full column rank of $A_b$ gives
\[
 \tr(A_bM_bA_b^{\mathsf T})\ge\sigma_{\min}(A_b)^2\tr M_b.
\]
For a convergent sequence of represented matrices all positive summands therefore have bounded traces. Taking subsequences of their moment matrices and of $P$ proves closedness.

Complete the square on each affine sheet. Equality in the minimum over \eqref{eq:lift} forces $P=0$, zero covariance on every positive-weight sheet, and weight only on minimizing sheets, since $H$ is positive definite. This gives \eqref{eq:faces}. To recover the envelope, strictly separate an exterior matrix by a symmetric $G$. The positive-upper-set property forces $G\ge0$. Replacing $G$ by $G+\epsilon I$ preserves the strict separation for sufficiently small $\epsilon>0$, since the envelope consists of positive semidefinite matrices. The asserted intersection follows. Finally
\[
 y_b(H)=c_b-L_b(L_b^{\mathsf T}HL_b)^{-1}L_b^{\mathsf T}Hc_b
\]
shows that all comparisons of $q_b$ are semialgebraic. The final invariance is Corollary~\ref{cor:invariance}.
\end{proof}
The lift is a finite representation, not a claim of minimal semidefinite extension size. Nonzero ties of the leading costs do not determine which branch minimizes at finite time. The exact-zero assertion of Theorem~\ref{thm:reduction}, by contrast, is a finite-time equivalence.
