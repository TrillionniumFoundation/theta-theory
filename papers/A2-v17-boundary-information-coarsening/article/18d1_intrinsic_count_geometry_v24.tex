\section{Intrinsic normal--tangent geometry of the count--endpoint limit}
\label{sec:v24-intrinsic-count-geometry}

The splitting used in Section~\ref{sec:v23-count-endpoint} is a convenient
coordinate representative of a canonical quotient construction.  We now
state the product experiment invariantly and verify directly that the
hyperbolic differential is nonzero.

Fix a finite anchored contact-jet model at a reference channel.  Let
\(V=T_{\vartheta_0}\Theta_M\) be its shape tangent space at fixed gap and let
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v24-hyperbolic-covector}
                         \lambda=d\gamma_{\vartheta_0}\in V^*.
\end{equation}
The two contact curvatures are coordinates of this finite jet model.  With the
notation of \eqref{eq:intro-v23-leading},
\[
 c_b=1+g\kappa_b,
 \qquad c_0c_1=\cosh^2\gamma,
 \qquad
 \gamma=\operatorname{arcosh}\sqrt{c_0c_1}.
\]
Consequently, at fixed \(g>0\),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v24-gamma-curvature-derivatives}
 \frac{\partial\gamma}{\partial\kappa_0}
 =\frac{g c_1}{2\sqrt{c_0c_1}\sinh\gamma}>0,
 \qquad
 \frac{\partial\gamma}{\partial\kappa_1}
 =\frac{g c_0}{2\sqrt{c_0c_1}\sinh\gamma}>0.
\end{equation}
In particular \(\lambda\ne0\) at every dispersing reference channel in the
model; no generic non-cancellation hypothesis is needed.

Put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v24-normal-tangent-spaces}
 H=\ker\lambda,
 \qquad N=V/H.
\end{equation}
The covector \(\lambda\) induces the canonical isomorphism
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v24-normal-coordinate}
 \bar\lambda:N\longrightarrow\mathbb R,
 \qquad [v]\longmapsto\lambda(v).
\end{equation}
Thus the fast count coordinate is intrinsically an element of the
one-dimensional normal quotient \(N\), normalized by \(\bar\lambda\), not a
preferred vector in \(V\).

Let \(\mathcal K\) be the positive endpoint information quadratic form of the
fixed positive design on \(\mathbb R_{\rm gap}\oplus V\).  Its slow component
is the restricted quadratic form
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v24-tangent-information}
 \mathcal K_{\rm tan}
 =\mathcal K\big|_{\mathbb R_{\rm gap}\oplus H}.
\end{equation}
This restriction is positive definite by the full finite-design theorem.

\begin{theorem}[Coordinate-free two-speed information theorem]
\label{thm:v24-coordinate-free-count-endpoint}
Under the rate assumptions of
Theorem~\ref{thm:v23-count-endpoint-joint}, the count--endpoint experiment has
the intrinsic product limit
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v24-intrinsic-product}
 \mathcal G^{\rm count}_{\bar b}
 \otimes
 \mathcal G^{\rm end}_{(a,h)},
 \qquad
 \bar b\in N,\quad a\in\mathbb R_{\rm gap},\quad h\in H,
\end{equation}
where the count factor has quadratic information
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v24-normal-information}
 I_{\rm count}(\bar b)=|\bar\lambda(\bar b)|^2
\end{equation}
and the endpoint factor has information tensor
\(\mathcal K_{\rm tan}\).  The factors are independent.

This formulation is invariant under every \(C^2\) reparameterization of the
finite jet model.  In particular, choosing any splitting vector
\(v_\gamma\in V\) with \(\lambda(v_\gamma)=1\) identifies \(N\) with the
coordinate called \(b\) in Section~\ref{sec:v23-count-endpoint}, but the limit
experiment does not depend on that choice.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Let \(v_\gamma\) be any representative with
\(\lambda(v_\gamma)=1\).  Theorem
\ref{thm:v23-count-endpoint-joint} gives the product limit with count
information one in the scalar coefficient of \(v_\gamma\) and endpoint
information \(\mathcal K_{\rm tan}\) on the tangent coordinates.  If
\(v'_\gamma\) is another representative, then
\(v'_\gamma-v_\gamma\in H\).  At the fast physical scale
\(\eta_n=(j_n\sqrt{k_n})^{-1}\), replacing one representative by the other
adds an \(H\)-displacement of size \(O(\eta_n)\).  The endpoint experiment
cannot see this displacement because the proof of
Theorem~\ref{thm:v23-count-endpoint-joint} gives
\[
 k_n\eta_n^2\log(1/\eta_n)\longrightarrow0.
\]
The count likelihood sees only the first differential of \(\gamma\), hence
only \(\lambda(v)\), and therefore gives the same scalar shift for the two
representatives.  This proves that the limit descends to the quotient
\(N=V/H\) and that its quadratic form is
\eqref{eq:v24-normal-information}.

Under a \(C^2\) change of shape coordinates, \(d\gamma\) pulls back as a
covector, its kernel maps to the tangent space of the same level set, and
\(\mathcal K\) transforms as a quadratic tensor.  Therefore both
\eqref{eq:v24-normal-information} and
\eqref{eq:v24-tangent-information} are coordinate invariant.  The exact
waiting-count/mark factorization gives independence, as before.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Geometry of the two rates]
\label{rem:v24-two-rate-geometry}
The faster Gaussian channel is the normal quotient to the level set of the
hyperbolic exponent; the slower endpoint channel is tangent to that level set
and includes the independently scaled gap direction.  A choice of
\(v_\gamma\) is analogous to choosing a connection for writing a quotient as
a direct sum.  It is useful for formulas but is not part of the experiment.
Equation \eqref{eq:v24-gamma-curvature-derivatives} also closes the previously
implicit assertion that the normal quotient is genuinely one dimensional.
\end{remark}
