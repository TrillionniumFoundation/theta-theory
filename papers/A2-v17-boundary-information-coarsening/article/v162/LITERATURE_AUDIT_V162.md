# A2 v162 — primary-source comparison

This is a record of the sources used and the distinction between moduli problems. It is not an exhaustive priority certificate. General proofs are in the manuscripts; finite checks and unsuccessful searches do not establish novelty.

## Congruence-miniversal deformations

A. Dmytryshyn, *Miniversal deformations of pairs of symmetric matrices under congruence*, arXiv:1104.2530v2 (2018).

Primary full text: `https://arxiv.org/pdf/1104.2530`.

Theorem 2.1, Corollary 2.1 and Lemma 3.1 were inspected in the primary PDF, including the rendered theorem page. They provide the known congruence-miniversal principle and tangent-space criterion. Revision 162 does not claim the first miniversal classification of symmetric pencils. It gives the explicit residual coefficient module needed for the embedded Hilbert calculation, realizes it with actual pencil perturbations, and then proves a separate Hilbert-chart inverse.

## Parameterized maps and resultant compactifications

Y. Hu, J. Lin and Y. Shao, *A compactification of the space of algebraic maps from P1 to Pn*, Communications in Analysis and Geometry 19 (2011), 1–30; arXiv:math/0701255v4.

Primary full text: `https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0701255`.

Theorem 1.1 and the opening construction were inspected, including the rendered theorem page. The paper constructs a smooth compactification of parameterized maps using resultant strata and exterior-map graphs. Revision 162's object is instead the reduced embedded Hilbert graph in a fixed target and then the pencil incidence graph in complete quadrics. Euclidean division and resultants are not presented as new general tools. The cohomological inverse is needed to identify the particular Hilbert chart; a map-space compactification alone would not prove that assertion.

## Hilbert, Simpson and Kontsevich objects

K. Chung, J. Hong and Y.-H. Kiem, *Compactified moduli spaces of rational curves in projective homogeneous varieties*, arXiv:1010.0068.

Primary full text: `https://arxiv.org/pdf/1010.0068`.

The opening definitions and the degree-two comparison around Theorem 1.2 were inspected in the primary PDF and its rendered page. Their work compares compactifications of rational curves, including low-degree multiple-cover behaviour. The revision does not infer a new moduli principle simply from distinguishing a Hilbert thick tail from a stable map. Its specific new component statement concerns the actual fibre of the fixed symmetric-pencil Hilbert graph: a jet-tail component and a distinct component containing conic tails. No exclusion of every equivalent formulation in the wider literature has been established.

## Complete quadrics

A. Massarenti, *On the birational geometry of spaces of complete forms I: collineations and quadrics*, arXiv:1803.09161.

Primary full text: `https://arxiv.org/pdf/1803.09161`.

Remark 2.5 and Construction 2.6 were rechecked in the PDF, including the rendered page. They identify the symmetric exterior graph and rank-locus blow-up construction and attribute the latter to Vainsencher, Theorem 6.3. The original Vainsencher proof is not described as freshly obtained. This supplies the classical smooth codimension-three normal-plane blow-up at corank two, not the new identification of an ambient pencil Hilbert neighbourhood.

## What is and is not being asserted

The contribution submitted for review is the ambient contact-to-Hilbert comparison, an open Euclidean chart with a regular Hilbert inverse and exact gcd fibre law, and a non-equidimensional actual pencil fibre at contact type (2,2). Congruence miniversality, complete quadrics, Euclidean division, generic Rees constructions and standard singularity theory are credited. The full proper fibre is not claimed classified. No independent external proof audit of Paper I was obtained. No journal acceptance, formal proof certification, or exhaustive priority conclusion is asserted.
