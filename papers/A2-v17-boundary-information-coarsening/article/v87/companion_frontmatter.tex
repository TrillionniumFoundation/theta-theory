\begin{abstract}
This companion preserves the shared-apparatus, polynomial, rational,
meromorphic, geometric, and acquisition regimes accompanying
\emph{Action recovery from projective matrix curves}. It includes
all mathematical inputs of the preceding expanded edition, with their
own hypotheses and proofs. Additional arguments give a finite-level
continuity lemma, a quantitative four-anchor calibration certificate,
explicit determinant perturbation bounds, and precise period and
branched-fibre conventions. The principal article gives the arbitrary-rank
singular spectrum theorem; the present volume provides the other
observation regimes and their derivations without conflating their
nuisance assumptions.
\end{abstract}
