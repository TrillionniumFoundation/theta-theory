\author{Qian Qi}

\begin{abstract}
Every complex quadratic pencil is recovered from its unmarked,
ungraded finite multiplication-failure algebra at a sharp order.
The relative inverse recovers moving pencils and their actual source
bundles. We develop the divisor geometry of the first relation beyond
this reconstruction locus. For binary forms the normalization is finite
flat on every exact-common-divisor stratum, and all its fibres are
products of explicitly graded complete intersections. On the entire
squarefree-common-divisor locus we determine the completed image,
conductor, all branch intersections, depth, and multiplicity by a
uniform block monomial model. A simultaneous incidence atlas describes
arbitrary binary root collisions. In any number of variables we compute
the minimal relation module of every pure-power divisor fibre and
classify its complete-intersection cases. The resulting structural
invariants apply to an explicit ramified point in every full
first-relation coordinate-change orbit closure of a pencil failure
algebra.
\end{abstract}
