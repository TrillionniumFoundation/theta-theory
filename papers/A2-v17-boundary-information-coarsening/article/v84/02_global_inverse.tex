\section{Observable components and the exact global inverse}
\label{sec:v83-components}
At a fixed endpoint, positivity and full column rank give
$B=\operatorname{rank}P_1$. Choose sets $I,J$ of $B$ rows and columns
such that $(P_1)_{IJ}$ is invertible. The reduced operators are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v83-operators}
 K_j=(P_j)_{IJ}(P_1)_{IJ}^{-1}
 =U_I\diag(q_{j,b})U_I^{-1},\qquad q_{j,b}=\pi_{j,b}/\pi_{1,b}.
\end{equation}
\begin{lemma}[Joint separation and component recovery]
\label{lem:v83-components}
In the polynomial model the vectors $(q_{2,b},\ldots,q_{q+2,b})$ are
pairwise distinct. More generally, whenever the available joint vectors
are pairwise distinct, their common rank-one projectors determine
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v83-component}
 E_{j,b}=(P_1)_{:J}(P_1)_{IJ}^{-1}R_b(P_j)_{I:}
        =\pi_{j,b}U_{:b}V_{:b}^{\mathsf T}.
\end{equation}
Consequently
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v83-channel}
 \pi_{j,b}=\one^{\mathsf T}E_{j,b}\one,\quad
 U_{:b}=E_{1,b}\one/\pi_{1,b},\quad
 V_{:b}=E_{1,b}^{\mathsf T}\one/\pi_{1,b}.
\end{equation}
The outputs are independent of the minor and spectral combination up
to one simultaneous permutation.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If two polynomial-model joint vectors were equal, the log ratio of
unnormalized weights would be constant at $T_1,\ldots,T_{q+2}$.
For unequal actions, its $(q+1)$st derivative is a nonzero constant
multiple of $(T-W_b)^{-q-1}-(T-W_c)^{-q-1}$, with a strict constant
sign above the actions. Repeated Rolle's theorem forbids those equal
values. For equal actions, polynomial interpolation forces all
nonconstant coefficient differences to vanish, contrary to the tuple
hypothesis.

A real linear combination of the available $K_j$ avoiding finitely many
collision hyperplanes has simple spectrum and the desired common
projectors. In their common basis,
$R_b=U_Ie_be_b^{\mathsf T}U_I^{-1}$ and
$(P_1)_{:J}(P_1)_{IJ}^{-1}=UU_I^{-1}$. Substitution gives
\eqref{eq:v83-component}; summation and normalization give
\eqref{eq:v83-channel}. These are the actual component matrices, so
all valid choices agree as unordered objects.
\end{proof}
The spectral choice can be made finite and deterministic. Set
$L=B(B-1)/2$. For $q\ge1$ use
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v83-finite-combinations}
 A_t=\sum_{\ell=0}^{q}t^\ell K_{\ell+2},\qquad t=0,1,\ldots,qL.
\end{equation}
Each pair excludes at most $q$ choices, because its eigenvalue difference
is a nonzero polynomial of degree at most $q$. One of the $qL+1$
choices separates all pairs. For $q=0$, $K_2$ already has simple spectrum.
For general detector spaces use the analogous polynomial combination
of all available operators; the same finite counting argument applies.

The recovered scalar ratios are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v83-logratios}
 \ell_j^{bc}=\log(\pi_{j,b}/\pi_{j,c})
 =\log(a_b/a_c)+\sum_{\nu=1}^q
 (\theta_{b,\nu}-\theta_{c,\nu})T_j^\nu+L_{W_b}(T_j)-L_{W_c}(T_j).
\end{equation}
A nonzero first contrast in \eqref{eq:v83-contrasts} selects an unequal
pair. Recover it absolutely, use the fixed-anchor inverse for the
other components, and centre all coefficients as in
\eqref{eq:v83-center}.

\begin{proof}[Proof of exact identification and sharpness in
Theorem~\ref{thm:v83-main}]
Rank, component recovery and scalar inversion give every centred
parameter. They also give uniqueness against another full-rank
representation with unknown visible count. The common $C^m$ gauge
\eqref{eq:v83-gauge} multiplies all weights at a clock by a common
positive factor and is the entire remaining continuous ambiguity.

Define the fibre over $z$ to be the unordered recovered columns
$U_{:b}(z)$. They are distinct by full rank. Every spectral
neighbourhood gives $B$ disjoint local continuous sections, defining
a covering $\cC\to Q$, and the inverse formulas define $\Xi$ on it.
On connected overlaps the transition permutation is constant. This
alone does not give a global enumeration. If $Q$ is simply connected,
path lifting from a chosen base fibre is independent of path and
gives one; two such enumerations differ by a constant permutation.

For sharpness, fix positive full-rank two-component channels. The
$q+2$-clock scalar fibre keeps every log probability ratio fixed, hence
every normalized matrix, while changing an action. An interior segment
preserves strict probability and action constraints. The common
retention baseline may first be decreased to satisfy a strict upper
probability bound on the finite clock interval. Thus the lower bound
lies in the same physical observation class.
\end{proof}

\section{A reference-local noisy inverse over the whole endpoint domain}
\label{sec:v83-global}
Globality over $Q$ is distinct from uniform recovery across rank or
covering strata. In this section the reference rank and covering type
are fixed. The finite-record theorem is a different statement: it first learns the covering from data under additional
spatial sampling and signal assumptions.

\subsection{Quantitative local charts}
Fix $q,m,u,v,B$, the clocks, $Q$ and its finite coordinate atlas.
On the reference exact field bound the $C^m$ norms of the centred
parameters and observations by $L_0$, and suppose
\begin{gather}
 r\le T_j-W_b\le R,\qquad \pi_{j,b}\ge p_*,\qquad
 \sigma_B(P_1)\ge\sigma_*,\label{eq:v83-margins}\\
 \min_{b\ne c}\max_{2\le j\le q+2}|q_{j,b}-q_{j,c}|\ge\delta,
 \qquad \max_bW_b-\min_bW_b\ge\gamma>0.\nonumber
\end{gather}
Selected minors satisfy $\sigma_{\min}((P_1)_{IJ})\ge\mu>0$.
Fix a finite refinement of the charts and a subordinate partition of
unity before perturbing the data. Constants may depend on the finite
cover and its partition $C^m$ norms, the atlas, all the displayed
margins, the selected spectral gap, inverse minor bounds and the
centred coefficient bound. No unobservable common detector polynomial
is bounded as a recovered parameter.

These choices can be selected from exact observations: rank gives the
minor size, singular values select the minor, candidate spectra select
a separating combination, and component contrasts select an anchor.
Continuity preserves the choices locally and compactness gives a finite
subcover. This is not a certification of interstitial margins from an
arbitrary finite noisy mesh.

\begin{lemma}[Uniform local extension]\label{lem:v83-local}
On a fixed quantitative chart with selected minor, separating spectrum
and unequal anchor, a pointwise smooth extension $F_a$ to locally
enumerated centred parameters satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v83-local-estimate}
 \|F_a(\widehat P)-F_a(P)\|_{C^m}
       \le C_a\max_j\|\widehat P_j-P_j\|_{C^m}.
\end{equation}
Its target is the ambient affine space of normalized channel columns
and centred coefficients. At a simplex boundary the extension may have
signed entries; no smooth open-neighbourhood retraction into that
simplex is claimed. No spatial derivative above order $m$ is used.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For $q\ge1$, let $C_V$ be the largest inverse-Vandermonde infinity
norm on any $q+1$ distinct integers in $\{0,\ldots,qL\}$. If a
pair's eigenvalue polynomial has magnitude below $\delta/(2C_V)$ at
$q+1$ choices, every coefficient has magnitude below $\delta/2$,
contradicting the joint gap. Each pair excludes at most $q$ choices
at that threshold, so some combination has all gaps at least
$\delta/(2C_V)$. For $q=0$ use the gap $\delta$. Shrinking the chart
preserves half the chosen gap.

Matrix inversion is smooth near the selected minor. At a simple root,
the derivative of the characteristic polynomial is the product of the
root separations. The implicit function theorem gives smooth real
eigenvalue branches: a small real perturbation has one root in each
disjoint root disk, and conjugation forces that root to be real.
The projectors are
\[
 R_b=\prod_{c\ne b}\frac{A_t-\lambda_c\id}{\lambda_b-\lambda_c}.
\]
Thus \eqref{eq:v83-component}--\eqref{eq:v83-channel}, divisions by
positive component masses and logarithms extend smoothly. The
perturbed operators need not commute or be jointly diagonalized by
these projectors.

Set $a_0=\gamma/R^{q+2}$. Some exact first contrast has magnitude at
least $a_0$, being an integral over an action interval of length at
least $\gamma$ with integrand at least $R^{-q-2}$. If all contrast
errors are below $a_0/4$, the largest observed contrast exceeds
$3a_0/4$. Accept a pair above $a_0/2$ and freeze it on the chart.
Its exact contrast exceeds $a_0/4$, so its action separation is at
least $a_0r^{q+2}/4$. Finite difference coefficients, the probability
floor and the preceding spectral estimates control the contrast
error. A merely nonzero noisy contrast is not treated as evidence of
an anchor.

The anchor inverse is smooth with uniform derivative bounds on the
compact separated class. For each remaining component, the fixed-anchor
contrast has derivative $w_0(W_b)>0$, also when its action equals the
anchor. Interpolate its nuisance polynomial on a fixed set of $q+1$
nodes after subtraction; off the model the unused residual coordinates
need not vanish. Centre the resulting coefficients. Product and chain
rules through order $m$ give \eqref{eq:v83-local-estimate}. The formulas
have controlled derivatives through order $m+1$ as finite-dimensional
maps; these are not unknown spatial derivatives of order $m+1$.
\end{proof}
With a strict entrywise channel margin, small perturbations also keep
channel positivity. Without it, column sums remain one but signed
entries are allowed in the ambient extension. Exact observation
identities are asserted on the exact image, not on arbitrary noisy data.

\subsection{Quantitative overlap matching}
Fix a finite good cover $Q_a$ and a subordinate partition $\chi_a$.
The local outputs are centred before matching. Full rank and compactness
give a positive signature margin
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v83-signature-gap}
 s_*\le\inf_{z\in Q}\min_{b\ne c}\|U_{:b}(z)-U_{:c}(z)\|.
\end{equation}
If $\sigma_B(U)\ge s_U$, one may take $s_*=\sqrt2s_U$.
Comparisons use columns in the original readout space, not eigenvectors
from unrelated minor coordinates.

\begin{lemma}[Synchronization under off-model perturbations]
\label{lem:v83-match}
If every extended channel column is within $e<s_*/4$ of its exact
counterpart, nearest-column matching on overlaps is a unique bijection.
It is constant on connected overlaps, satisfies the exact permutation
cocycle on triple overlaps and agrees with the reference covering.
It is unchanged on a sufficiently small data neighbourhood.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Two approximations of the same column are within $2e$; approximations
of different columns are separated by at least $s_*-2e>2e$.
Nearest matching is therefore exactly matching to the same true
component. The strict separation makes it locally constant, hence
constant on connected overlaps. On a triple overlap all matchings
identify the same reference column, so their composition equals the
direct matching, not just approximately. The strict inequalities persist
under a further small perturbation. Reference labels verify this
observable nearest-column rule; they are not its input.
\end{proof}
Write $g_{ab}$ for these permutations. They define a covering
$\widehat\cC\simeq\cC$ and act on the centred local vectors. In chart
$a$ put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v83-gluing}
 \widehat\Xi_a=\sum_b\chi_b\,g_{ab}F_b(\widehat P).
\end{equation}
Products supported on overlaps are extended by zero. The cocycle
$g_{ab}g_{bc}=g_{ac}$ makes these sums transform by the required
transition permutation. Thus they form a field on the covering;
no unmatched labels or incompatible branch gauges have been averaged.

\subsection{The quotient and its quantitative scope}
For isomorphic coverings over $Q$, define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v83-quotient-metric}
 d_m([\Xi],[\Xi'])=
       \min_{\iota:\cC\simeq\cC'}\|\Xi-\iota^*\Xi'\|_{C^m}.
\end{equation}
The norm uses local lifts of the fixed base atlas. An isomorphism is
determined by its action on one fibre because $Q$ is connected, even
when the total covering is disconnected. Only finitely many fibre
permutations extend: in a fixed base fibre, an automorphism must commute
with the monodromy action, and precisely such permutations extend by
path lifting. Thus the minimum is finite and attained. On a simply
connected base it is the minimum over one constant permutation.
The continuous gauge has already been removed. This metric compares
the same rank and isomorphic covering types; it does not identify
fields in different monodromy or rank strata.

\begin{theorem}[Reference-local global quotient inverse]
\label{thm:v83-global}
Fix an exact $B$-component reference field satisfying
\eqref{eq:v83-margins}--\eqref{eq:v83-signature-gap}, its quantitative
finite chart cover, partition and $C^m$ bounds. There is an open
$C^m$ neighbourhood $\mathcal N$ of its normalized matrices and a
reconstruction $\mathcal R$ into ambient centred affine fields on its
\emph{fixed covering stratum}, modulo covering isomorphisms. It is
exact on nearby exact $B$-component data in the prescribed class and,
for arbitrary real matrix fields $P',P''\in\mathcal N$, satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v83-global-estimate}
 d_m(\mathcal R(P'),\mathcal R(P''))
              \le C\max_j\|P'_j-P''_j\|_{C^m}.
\end{equation}
The constant depends on the fixed atlas, cover, partition norms,
centred coefficient bounds, residual and probability bounds, inverse
minors, spectral and anchor separations and channel-signature margin.
After identifying the covering with the reference one, the map is
locally Lipschitz between $C^m$ spaces, with no loss of derivatives.
Monodromy is fixed on this neighbourhood. This is global over $Q$
but local in the data and the covering stratum, and may be signed at
a channel simplex boundary.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Shrink the neighbourhood so that every frozen minor, separating
combination, positive division and scalar inverse remains valid, and
all local column errors are below $s_*/8$. The matching lemma gives
transition permutations fixed throughout this neighbourhood. Use them
in \eqref{eq:v83-gluing}. On exact model data the matched local outputs
are the same centred field, and the partition sums to one, proving
exactness. For two arbitrary fields, compare on the common reference
covering, differentiate the finite sum through order $m$ and apply
the product rule, partition bounds and local Lipschitz estimates.
This proves the bound for that covering identification and hence for
the minimum in \eqref{eq:v83-quotient-metric}. The same transition
cocycle gives the stated monodromy.
\end{proof}
The ambient neighbourhood may contain models with additional arbitrarily
weak components. The fixed-rank extension is not their model-order
inverse. On a rectangle, a base-fibre labelling and transport through
a spanning tree give a global representative; the cocycle and simple
connectedness make it independent of the remaining paths. A local
permutation statement alone would not give this conclusion.

\begin{proposition}[A genuine monodromy example]\label{prop:v83-monodromy}
There are smooth log-affine observations on a circle with all the
strict margins and a component covering with three-cycle monodromy.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For $t\in[0,2\pi]$, $b=0,1,2$, put
$\phi_b=t/3+2\pi b/3$, $W_b=-C+\cos\phi_b$,
$\theta_{b,1}=\sin\phi_b$ and $a_b=1$. Choose $C$ large enough for
the residual bound. For orthonormal $e,f$ in the coordinate-sum-zero
plane in $\R^3$, set
\[
 U_{:b}=V_{:b}=\tfrac13\one+
               \epsilon(e\cos\phi_b+f\sin\phi_b).
\]
Small $\epsilon>0$ gives positive entries. The three columns are
affinely independent in their probability plane and hence linearly
independent. The action--rate pairs are distinct points on a circle,
and the action coordinates are never all equal. Compactness gives
uniform strict margins. At $t=2\pi$ all tuples are the cyclic
permutation of those at zero, so the unordered matrices are smooth
on the endpoint circle. Following one column once around moves to
the next; no global enumeration exists. Two independent draws from
the specified, here identical, channel laws realize conditional
independence.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Visible rank and error transfer]\label{cor:v83-error}
With the known signal lower bound $\sigma_*$, operator-norm error
below $\sigma_*/4$ in $P_1$ permits recovery of $B$ by threshold
$\sigma_*/2$. If joint densities $H_j$ satisfy
$f_j=\one^{\mathsf T}H_j\one\ge f_*>0$ and bounded $C^m$ norms,
then $C^m$ error at most $\varepsilon$ in those densities gives
quotient parameter error at most $C\varepsilon$ after normalization
and reconstruction. Additional $C^m$ model error $\eta$ changes this
to $C(\varepsilon+\eta)$ while the reference margins persist.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Singular values change by at most the operator error; the zero values
stay below the threshold and the nonzero ones above it. Summation and
the density floor keep perturbed marginals above $f_*/2$. Product and
reciprocal estimates control the normalization error; apply the global
quotient estimate and the triangle inequality.
\end{proof}
This corollary is a conditional error-transfer result, not a finite
sample rate or unrestricted order test. Theorem~\ref{thm:v84-mesh}
supplies a separate finite-record experiment, learns the covering type,
and states the spatial and signal information needed for that task.
