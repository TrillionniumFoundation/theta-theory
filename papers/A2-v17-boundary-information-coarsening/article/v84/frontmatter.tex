\begin{abstract}
We recover absolute actions from projective observations with unknown
selective detection and two unknown conditionally independent readouts.
For polynomial log-detectors of degree at most $q$, exactly $q+3$
deadlines suffice in the worst case. We characterize regular identification
for a finite-dimensional detector function space by the secants and
tangents of the logarithmic action curve, and prove that this criterion
admits a stable finite deadline design on every compact action class.
For a regularly separating $d$-dimensional analytic space, almost every
set of $d+5$ clocks works simultaneously for all admissible actions.
For log-detectors with prescribed singularities we give uniform clock
bounds. In particular, six deadlines identify a family for which every
nonzero annihilator kernel changes sign, beyond the positive-kernel
criterion. We also parametrize the complete underdetermined polynomial
fibres. A finite-record theorem reconstructs the component covering,
including its monodromy, from a sufficiently fine spatial mesh without
supplying its covering type. Absolute generating sheets give observable
compatibility tests for coprime return lifts. As a geometric application,
two-arm selective travel-time records determine a simple surface metric
up to a boundary-fixing isometry. All geometric conclusions retain their
explicit observation domains.
\end{abstract}
