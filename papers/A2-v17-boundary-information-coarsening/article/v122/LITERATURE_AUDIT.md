## New source inspected

D. Huybrechts, *Lectures on K3 surfaces*, author-hosted prepublication draft:
`https://www.math.uni-bonn.de/people/huybrech/K3Global.pdf`.

The draft's Chapter 1, Example 1.3(i), printed page 8, was read in text and in the rendered PDF page: smooth quartic hypersurfaces have trivial canonical bundle and vanishing H^1(O), hence are K3 surfaces. Chapter 9, proof of Corollary 4.4, printed pages 179–180, was read; the rendered page 179 was also inspected. The proof uses the nonzero two-form to exclude characteristic-zero uniruledness. The new manuscript supplies the actual cancellation and birational K3 argument it needs. No uninspected theorem number for these steps is attributed to the source.

The source is labelled a prepublication draft for personal use and not redistribution. No copy is included in this repository or review bundle. The new bibliography identifies the draft and exact internal locations.
