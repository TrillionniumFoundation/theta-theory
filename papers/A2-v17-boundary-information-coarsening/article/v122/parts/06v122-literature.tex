\section{The ramification comparison and its scope}
\label{sec:v122-literature}
The Jacobian description of ramification, the hypersurface
adjunction formula, and the cohomology of projective bundles are
classical. The K3 facts used here are the quartic example and the
characteristic-zero non-uniruledness argument in
\cite[Chapter 1, Example 1.3(i); Chapter 9, proof of Corollary 4.4]{HuyK3};
the needed birational cancellation is proved in
Lemma~\ref{lem:nonuniruled-cancellation}. The Hesse invariant remains
attributed to \cite{AD09}. None of these ingredients is presented
as a new theorem of this article.

The result proved here links them to multiplication failure by the
cokernel identification \eqref{eq:relation-cokernel}, the primary
identity \eqref{eq:ramification-primary-ideal}, and the intrinsic
annihilator \eqref{eq:canonical-embedded-torsion}. Its conclusion
is a reconstruction statement for the nonreduced failure scheme,
not a claim to have introduced ramification hypersurfaces or K3
surfaces. The general determinantal input and the retained
finite-contact comparisons are discussed in
Sections~\ref{sec:v121-literature} and the preceding literature sections.

The nearest-source comparison with Ballico's higher-order failure-locus
article \cite{Ballico93} is not completed. The bibliographic record
and the inherited record of its first-page inspection do not supply
its full theorem statements. The present revision again did not obtain
the complete article by the accessible publisher or Library routes.
No statement that it anticipates, or fails to anticipate, the results
above is inferred from that access limitation. The accompanying
literature audit specifies the comparison axes and the evidence
available for each. Proof of the displayed results, an assessment of
their priority, and a journal decision are distinct questions.
