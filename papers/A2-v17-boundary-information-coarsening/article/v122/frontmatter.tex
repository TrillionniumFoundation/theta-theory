\begin{abstract}
We show that a nonreduced multiplication-failure scheme can intrinsically
recover a smooth genus-one curve or a K3 surface although its reduced
divisor is rational and independent of the quadratic multiplication
relations. For a nonempty open class of cube-zero algebras of Hilbert
function $(1,3,3)$ or $(1,4,6)$, the embedded support on the entire
projection-corank-at-most-one open is a vector bundle over the
ramification variety of a finite quadratic projective morphism, times
a projective space. Its full failure ideal is the product of the
determinant-divisor ideal and the embedded-support ideal. We determine
an irredundant primary decomposition of every ordinary power, its exact
nilradical index, and the canonical embedded torsion and its annihilator.
These identify the embedded support without choosing a primary
representative; birational cancellation then recovers the ramification
curve or K3 surface from the abstract failure scheme. The associated
families and all specified ideal-power thickenings are flat over the
admissible relation space. We also give universal determinantal primary
flags in arbitrary embedding dimension, a complete classification for
Hilbert function $(1,2,2)$ including the extreme-corank locus, and sharp
codimension-controlled stabilization and relative conductor comparisons
with global section multiplication under explicit regularity hypotheses.
\end{abstract}
