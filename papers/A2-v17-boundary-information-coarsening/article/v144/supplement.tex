\begin{abstract}
This supplement contains the web reconstruction theorem, the all-ranks
exterior contraction calculation and its Jacobian--Casimir factorization,
the polarized and polar constructions, and the complete primary-boundary,
collision, specialization, and exact-coordinate theory.  All these
results retain their stated hypotheses and complete proofs.  The pencil
inverse theorem and its spectral and likelihood consequences are proved
in the main article without the web contraction theorem.  References
with ordinary numerical section numbers refer to that article; sections
of this supplement are prefixed S.
\end{abstract}
