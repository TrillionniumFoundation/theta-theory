# Mathematical audit — sharp finite-neighbourhood theorem

1. **Order convention.** Z^[k] is defined by b^(k+1). The actual generators have degree d=n+2(N-2), so order d-1 is still independent of R and order d retains the first relations. No off-by-one switch to b^k or an arc-space jet is made.
2. **Actual neighbourhood.** Projection to S identifies an open neighbourhood of the entire socle Grassmannian with Tot Hom(U,V). The maximal-minor identity is literal and homogeneous there; no formal local splitting is used as a global statement.
3. **Intrinsic first relation.** The finite scheme's reduction is B. Its nilradical recovers L=n/n^2 and K=ker(Sym^d L -> n^d/n^(d+1)). Since the original ideal is generated in degree d, Sym(L)/(K) is the full normal-cone algebra. Taking a radical here would destroy the required coefficients and is not done.
4. **Unmarked descent.** The proof uses the recovered cone over its recovered base, the global rank-one Fano scheme, and its uniquely trivial ruling. It does not extend an isomorphism of cones to the ambient Grassmannian. A single local slice lacks this global orientation.
5. **Coefficient rank.** F=wedge^(N-2) Sym^2 is irreducible. The nonzero equivariant map F(V)^* tensor F(U) -> Sym^(2p) Hom(U,V)^* is injective. Multiplication by a determinant polynomial is injective in the polynomial ring. Thus K_R has constant rank binomial(N,2) for all pencils, not just regular pencils.
6. **Family logic.** The parameter morphism factors through three closed immersions. The line-to-subspace immersion is proved by scalar-identity blocks on Grassmannian charts over arbitrary complex algebras. This establishes nonreduced-base faithfulness, rather than inferring it from injectivity on closed points.
7. **Flatness.** The finite coordinate algebra has constant pieces below d and a locally free quotient at d. Its total rank is binomial(n^2+d,d)-binomial(N,2). This does not assert flatness of an unrestricted infinite local algebra.
8. **Sharpness.** Pencils with and without a common linear factor are inequivalent in every n>=3, while all lower-order neighbourhoods agree. The hyperelliptic locus additionally supplies positive-dimensional moduli, but is not required for n=3 sharpness.

`checks/revision139_exact.py` verifies a finite n=3 coefficient-rank witness, complementary minors, homogeneous scaling, a full standard-chart tangent calculation, and exact degree/Hilbert arithmetic. The manuscript contains the general proofs. The checks are not formal machine verification of those proofs, and neither the checks nor this audit certify historical priority.
