## Map signatures, rather than shared vocabulary

`j_n: det(V) tensor Sym^n(V) -> wedge^n Sym^2(V)` is the specified Jacobian-polarization embedding. Classical exterior-symmetric plethysm supplies the representation occurrence (Cheng–Wang, Theorem 3.5); it does not alone evaluate a further restricted contraction.

For nondegenerate quadratic `q`, the apolar map `q(partial): Sym^n(V) -> Sym^(n-2)(V)` has the harmonic kernel of dimension `binomial(2n-1,n)-binomial(2n-3,n-2)`. In contrast `iota_q j_n` maps to `wedge^(n-1) Sym^2(V)`, with the zero kernel in odd dimension and the quadratic-power line in even dimension proved in the manuscript. These are not the same operators. For singular `q` the radical-shear argument is essential; a nondegenerate orthogonal branching statement does not by itself cover that case.

The previously inspected De Bie–Eelbode–Roels harmonic decomposition, Kupferman–Shachar Piola identity, and Landsberg–Ottaviani/Sheridan skew-support facts remain attributed. No computation is offered as historical nonanticipation evidence. The new automatic coefficient proof uses classical matrix coefficients and Cauchy decomposition; its geometric assertion is that the correctly realized left subspaces are recovered intrinsically and share one projective coordinate change.
