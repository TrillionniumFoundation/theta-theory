## Primary statements newly inspected

### Exterior powers of symmetric squares

S.-J. Cheng and W. Wang, *Howe Duality for Lie Superalgebras*, Compositio Mathematica 128 (2001), 55–94, DOI 10.1023/A:1017594504827. Complete author preprint: https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0008093 (v1). Theorem 3.5, PDF page 12, gives a multiplicity-free exterior-symmetric-square decomposition indexed by nested hooks (l_i+1,l_i); setting odd dimension zero gives the ordinary classical case. Its proof cites Howe and a classical appendix, but those underlying works were not themselves newly inspected here. The inclusion of the determinant-twisted symmetric power is not claimed as new. The theorem is a decomposition statement, not an evaluation of the manuscript's restricted contraction map. The manuscript separately computes which harmonic summands survive that map.

### Harmonic decomposition and the precise group convention

H. De Bie, D. Eelbode and M. Roels, *The harmonic transvector algebra in two vector variables*, Journal of Algebra 473 (2017), 247–282, DOI 10.1016/j.jalgebra.2016.10.039. Complete author preprint: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1510.06566 (v3). Section 1, equations (1)–(3), states the Fischer decomposition and its orthogonal/Howe interpretation; Theorem 2.1 supplies the two-variable setting. The dimension convention in that paper is m > 4. It is therefore not used as a missing low-dimensional theorem. The new self-contained full-O_n lemma proves all n >= 2, including the O_2 reflection and the common determinant twist. The decomposition and irreducibility themselves are explicitly classical, not claimed as a new contribution. A PDF page image containing Theorem 2.1 was also inspected.

### Cofactor divergence

R. Kupferman and A. Shachar, *A geometric perspective on the Piola identity in Riemannian settings*, Journal of Geometric Mechanics 11 (2019), 59–76, DOI 10.3934/jgm.2019004. Complete author preprint: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1805.12365 (v1). Equation (1.1) states the Euclidean Piola identity; the discussion and Section 3.1 identify mixed-partial cancellation and its geometric interpretation. The manuscript's cofactor step is attributed to this classical identity, not presented as a newly discovered cancellation. The tensor-specific polarization, Gaussian moment use, and contraction kernel conclusion are additional steps written in the manuscript. The general identity is not confused with the local-diffeomorphism hypothesis of Corollary 3.2.

### Bounded conclusion

Classical plethysm, harmonic decomposition, and Piola cancellation supply the representation and differential identities. They do not replace the displayed calculation of the particular contraction on j_n or the intrinsic reconstruction proof. The audit distinguishes: (a) classical structure, (b) the map-specific singular/radical and full-rank calculations as proved here, and (c) the geometric use of the resulting support gap and residual coefficient readout. It does not assert that no equivalent formula exists elsewhere. The predecessor's theorem-level Landsberg–Ottaviani and Sheridan comparisons remain in the article and prior audit; they are not relabeled as newly read sources.
