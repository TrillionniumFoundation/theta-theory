\section{Exact separating pencils}
\label{app:separating-pencils}

The smooth integral witness \(C_*\) of \eqref{eq:C-star} provides
a direct arithmetic check inside the geometric locus.  Its two residual
linear forms have determinant \(27787/432\).  We retain below both
this exact check and the five-coordinate square-web calculation.
Neither is needed for the structural support proof of
Theorem~\ref{thm:global-schur-recombination}.

Choose a basis \(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4\) of \(V\), and order the
monomial basis of \(W\) as
\[
 (b_1,\ldots,b_{10})=
 (x_1^2,x_1x_2,x_1x_3,x_1x_4,x_2^2,
 x_2x_3,x_2x_4,x_3^2,x_3x_4,x_4^2).
\]
For increasing indices put \(e_{ijkl}=b_i\wedge b_j\wedge
b_k\wedge b_l\).  Let
\[
 w_*=e_{1,5,8,10}.
\]
The Jacobian map
\[
 C:E\longrightarrow\det V\otimes\Sym^4V,\qquad
 q_1\wedge\cdots\wedge q_4\longmapsto
 \det(\partial q_i/\partial x_j)
\]
is equivariant, with the determinant factor understood in the chosen
basis.  It is nonzero, since \(Cw_*=16x_1x_2x_3x_4\).
Its kernel is \(E_{175}\).

\begin{lemma}[An exact separating pencil]
\label{lem:exact-plucker-pencil}
The projection of \(w_*\) to \(E_{35}\) is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:exact-projector-witness}
 Qw_*={1\over3}e_{1,5,8,10}
       -{2\over3}e_{1,6,7,9}
       -{2\over3}e_{2,3,6,10}
       +{2\over3}e_{2,4,7,8}
       +{2\over3}e_{3,4,5,9}.
\end{equation}
On the pencil \(aPw_*+bQw_*\), two Pluecker quadrics restrict to
\begin{equation}\label{eq:two-separating-quadrics}
 -{2\over9}(b-a)(b+2a),\qquad
 -{4\over9}(b-a)^2.
\end{equation}
Consequently \(\mathscr L_*\cap\Gr(4,W)\) is the single
reduced point \([w_*]\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use the Fischer Hermitian forms: a degree-\(r\) monomial
\(x^\alpha\) has squared norm \(\alpha!\), and the exterior
basis has the induced product norm.  The determinant factor has norm
one.  These forms are unitary invariant.  Hence the adjoint \(C^*\)
is unitary equivariant and, by complexification, is \(GL(V)\)-
equivariant.  Schur's lemma gives \(CC^*=c\,\mathrm{id}\).
The only exterior basis vectors contributing to the monomial
\(x_1x_2x_3x_4\) are the five in
\eqref{eq:exact-projector-witness}.  Their Jacobian coefficients
are respectively \(16,-4,-4,4,4\), and their squared norms are
\(16,2,2,2,2\).  The quartic monomial has squared norm one.
It follows that
\[
 c={16^2\over16}+4\,{4^2\over2}=48,
 \qquad Q={C^*C\over48}.
\]
Applying this identity to \(w_*\) gives
\eqref{eq:exact-projector-witness}.

Extend the Pluecker coordinates \(z_{i_1i_2i_3i_4}\)
alternatingly, with repeated-index coordinates zero.  For an ordered
triple \(I\) and an increasing five-tuple \(J=(j_1,\ldots,j_5)\),
use the quadratic relation
\[
 F_{I,J}(z)=\sum_{k=1}^5(-1)^{k-1}
              z_{I,j_k}\,z_{J\setminus j_k}.
\]
Substitution of \eqref{eq:exact-projector-witness}, together with
\(Pw_*=w_*-Qw_*\), gives \eqref{eq:two-separating-quadrics}
for
\[
 \begin{split}
 I&=(1,5,8),\quad J=(1,6,7,9,10),\\
 I&=(1,6,7),\quad J=(2,3,6,9,10),
 \end{split}
\]
respectively.  These two formulas can thus be checked with five
nonzero coordinates.  Their linear quotients after removing
\(b-a\) are independent.  As ideal sheaves on \(\Pj^1\),
they generate \((b-a)\): the two remaining linear forms have no
common projective zero.  Every other restricted Pluecker quadric
vanishes at \([1:1]\), so the full restricted ideal sheaf is also
\((b-a)\).  This proves both uniqueness and reducedness, including
at the endpoints and at infinity.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[The same condition at the smooth integral witness]
The smooth web \(C_*\) of \eqref{eq:C-star} also satisfies the
rank-two condition.  For its Pluecker vector, in the monomial basis
above, the same two quadrics restrict respectively to
\[
 { (a-b)(339a+89b)\over24},\qquad
 {37(a-b)(31a+17b)\over72}.
\]
The determinant of their linear quotients by \(b-a\) is
\(27787/432\ne0\).  These identities follow by taking the
four-by-four minors of the displayed integral coefficient matrix
\eqref{eq:C-star}, applying \(Q=C^*C/48\), and substituting in
\(F_{I,J}\).  They are reproduced by
\texttt{checks/revision132\_exact.py}, together with equivariance
for all sixteen matrix-unit generators of \(\mathfrak{gl}_4\).
The smoothness and basepoint-freeness of this same web are certified
independently in Lemma~\ref{lem:differential}.  The smooth witness checks the geometric family directly.  The
five-coordinate example gives a transparent normalization check,
while the support theorem proves the assertion for every smooth web.
\end{remark}
