## Scope of the new family statement

The closed immersion concerns fixed V,U and degree-d coefficient subspaces. It is universally a monomorphism. The unmarked intrinsic theorem concerns complex schemes supported on the entire projective socle Grassmannian. We do not identify these two formulations, assert an equivalence of moduli stacks, or claim that an unmarked Artinian normal slice alone reconstructs the pencil.
