## 3. Restricted contraction: equality-level dictionary

The map in question is kappa_(n,q)=iota_q o j_n, with j_n:det(V) tensor Sym^n V -> wedge^n Sym^2 V and target wedge^(n-1) Sym^2 V after contraction. The active supplement retains the all-rank kernel formula and v142 coefficient proof, mixed Jacobian identity and Casimir spectrum. `parts/27-operator-dictionary-v143.tex` makes the comparison explicit for this composite.

Inspected classical ingredients and their boundaries:

* The multiplicity-free exterior-square plethysm decomposition in the Cheng–Wang discussion (Section 2, Theorem 3.5 in the version recorded in the predecessor audit) identifies the representation containing j_n. Decomposition alone does not compute the restricted contraction kernel.
* The classical harmonic/Fischer decomposition and angular Casimir eigenvalue calculate the nondegenerate angular operator. De Bie–Eelbode–Roels, arXiv:1510.06566, Section 1, equations (1)–(3), and Section 6, Definition 6.1 and Lemma 6.2, provide the stated harmonic/Fischer conventions and adjunction. The new dictionary specifies the actual coefficient identity transferring those calculations to kappa; the classical decomposition is not presented as new. That source's dimensional hypotheses for its two-variable theory are not imported as an unsupported proof in the manuscript's low dimensions.
* The preceding audit's Howe, Piola/cofactor, apolar and skew-flattening comparisons are retained. Those mechanisms do not replace the displayed mixed coefficient identity. The singular-q proof uses radical shears/mixed partials, separately from a nondegenerate harmonic decomposition.

The precise operator equivalence is now visible to a reader instead of being inferred from analogy. It does not imply that all possible historical formulations have been searched. We do not certify that the kernel formula, its equivalent angular description or its normalization is globally first. The complete theorem remains proved and dimension-uniform. Its role is the supplementary web/coefficient mechanism, not an unverified premise of the principal pencil theorem's priority claim.

Primary public text rechecked: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1510.06566

## 5. Reciprocal likelihood and the new relative theorem

C. Fevola, Y. Mandelshtam, B. Sturmfels, *Pencils of Quadrics: Old and New*, Le Matematiche 76 (2021), 319–335, arXiv:2009.04334. Conjecture 4.5 asks for real data with 2r-3 distinct real critical points for a definite diagonalizable pencil with r distinct eigenvalues. Neither positivity of every data entry nor membership of every critical point in the positive definite cone is part of that assertion. The inherited v141 proof treats exactly that assertion, as the second v141 referee also observes.

Primary text: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2009.04334

Revision 143 retains that proof. The new result concerns the relative score zero scheme and congruence-covariant data section. This is a stated theorem with a full proof; the reconstruction of the universal correspondence is a functorial consequence of the pencil inverse theorem, while the explicit algebra/splitting is proved directly. No claim is made that the elementary root argument logically requires the failure invariant, or that complex reconstruction creates a real definite structure.
