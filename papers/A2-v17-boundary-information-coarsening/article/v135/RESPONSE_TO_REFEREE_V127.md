## E127.1 — corank-four membership `d^4 in J`

**Resolved.**  The previous text passed too quickly from occurrence of the Schur type `(4,4,4,0)` to actual ideal membership.  Revision 128 inserts Lemma `lem:effective-pieri-matrix-ring`.  In the matrix coordinate ring
`A = Sym(V^* tensor V)`, actual polynomial multiplication from the Cauchy components indexed by `(4,4,4,0)` and `(4)` has nonzero projection to `(4,4,4,4)`.  The proof uses standard bideterminants: multiplying the relevant standard bitableaux and straightening produces the `(4,4,4,4)` bideterminant with coefficient one.  Pieri multiplicity is one, so the resulting map is the nonzero evaluation pairing after the determinant twist.

The Jacobian projection lemma still identifies the nonzero `(4,4,4,0)` component of the residual minors with the Jacobian quartic.  Since the `(4,4,4,4)` Cauchy component is the one-dimensional line generated by `d^4`, actual multiplication now proves `d^4 in J`.  The argument no longer relies on abstract representation occurrence.

## E127.2 — genericity of the nine mixed-kernel rows

**Resolved by exact generic-point computation.**  The seven rows not already covered by the earlier mixed-regular/decomposable-kernel theorems are treated by the new `thm:generic-slice-certification`.  For each mixed normal form we compute the stabilizer Lie algebra and its tangent action on the nine-dimensional quadratic-block space.  Secant, tangent, and H-ruling have transverse slices of dimensions 3, 2, and 2; the W-ruling, the two rank-one rows, and the zero mixed map have open stabilizer orbits in the quadratic-block space.

On the transverse slices the residual colons are computed over the rational function fields of the slice parameters.  Their generic initial ideals give exactly the Hilbert functions in the nine-orbit table.  The only nonconstant denominator divisors needed for the displayed bases are `s2-2` in the secant slice and `s0+2*s1-5` in the H-ruling slice; both are nonzero at the integral base point.  Thus the old integer matrices are retained only as base points, not as genericity arguments.  The exact calculation is reproduced by `checks/generic_boundary_atlas.py`.

## E127.3 — fibrewise associated primes and base change

**Resolved with a relative-assassin proof.**  The old argument used flattening too late.  Revision 128 first considers the exact sequence whose kernel is `(J:d^{j-1})`, and stratifies the coefficient base so that the cokernel is flat.  Only after this flatness is imposed is the sequence pulled to geometric fibres; consequently colon formation commutes with base change on each stratum.

The quotient modules defining `W_j` and the cokernels of the graded multiplication maps are then flattened.  Relative assassins are spread out from the generic point, using the flat relative-assassin results of the Stacks Project (Tags 05AS, 0GSF/0GSJ and the specialization criterion 05L2).  Noetherian induction handles loci on which extra associated points appear.  A final generic-freeness refinement fixes generic lengths.  The resulting Proposition `prop:associated-prime-refinement` therefore controls fibrewise associated points, minimal-versus-embedded status, incidences, generic lengths, Hilbert polynomials, and multiplication ranks.

## E127.6 / M127.5 — significance beyond the `(1,4,6)` case

**Resolved by a general theorem.**  The new universal determinant-completion theorem applies to every `e`, every symmetric degree `r`, and every quotient `gamma: Sym^r V -> S`.  If `M` is the generic endomorphism and `J` is the maximal-minor ideal of `gamma Sym^r M`, then

`(det M)^{binom(e+r-1,r-1)} in J`.

The proof is a single Laplace-completion argument after adjoining a complementary quotient.  The exponent is sharp when `gamma` is an isomorphism.  The old `d^5 in J` statement is precisely the `(e,r)=(4,2)` case.  This is the requested broader determinant-power mechanism and is independent of the inverse-problem ambiguity.
