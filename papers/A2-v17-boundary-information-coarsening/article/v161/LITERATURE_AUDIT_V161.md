## Arrangement and incidence blow-ups

**Li Li, Wonderful compactification of an arrangement of subvarieties**, Michigan Mathematical Journal 58 (2009), 535–563; arXiv:math/0611412.

Primary full text: https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0611412

Directly inspected Definition 1.1 and Theorems 1.2–1.3, including rendered pages. Theorem 1.2 gives smoothness and the nested boundary-intersection description. Theorem 1.3 identifies a wonderful model with the blow-up of the product of the building-set ideals and gives admissible ordered blow-ups. These statements already supply the general arrangement mechanism underlying independent incidence centres. The v161 paper explicitly credits them. Its general transverse-incidence lemma includes a direct proof of the independent-coordinate case rather than claiming a new general wonderful-model theorem.

The pencil-specific work is to show joint normal independence for every regular pencil with reduced corank-two incidence, identify the unlabelled global centre as a Fitting ideal, construct the flat embedded universal curve, and identify its parameter modification with the specified normalized Hilbert graph. A product of exceptional projective spaces does not by itself prove that last identification. The nonreduced Jordan-contact calculation is outside this smooth reduced arrangement model.

## Stable maps, tangency, and Hilbert limits

**Andreas Gathmann, Gromov-Witten invariants of blow-ups**, arXiv:math/9804043.

Primary full text: https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/9804043

Directly inspected the introduction and Section 7, especially Theorem 7.1 on printed page 41 and its motivating strict-transform diagram, including the rendered page. The theorem concerns enumerative conditions on generically injective stable maps with a prescribed tangent direction, expressed through invariants of blow-ups. It is not an identification of the embedded Hilbert graph of the pencil family considered here. The v161 comparison uses this distinction of moduli objects: after ramified base change a tail is a degree-m stable map, while the flat Hilbert limit has an m-fold scheme-theoretic thickening. The equality of cycles does not equate these objects. No new Gromov-Witten enumerative formula is claimed.

**Veronica Arena and Terry Dekun Song, Logarithmic Hilbert schemes of curves as weighted blow-ups and their integral Chow rings**, arXiv:2605.17922v1, May 18, 2026, with an appendix by Dhruv Ranganathan.

Primary full text: https://arxiv.org/html/2605.17922v1

Directly inspected the introduction and Theorems A and B. Their moduli object consists of finite subschemes on expansions of a smooth pointed curve; the intermediate stability spaces are related by weighted blow-ups of specified centres. This is relevant broader modular blow-up literature, but different from a Hilbert scheme of one-dimensional pencil curves embedded in a fixed complete-quadric variety. It is recorded as a comparison, not used as a proof input or as a claimed special case of the new theorem. No claim is made to have independently reverified all proofs in that preprint.

These sources broaden the comparison beyond complete quadrics alone. They do not constitute an exhaustive survey of every Fano, Nash, Rees or Hilbert modification, and no exhaustive nonanticipation conclusion is asserted.

Directly inspected the introduction, Definition 0.1 and Theorem 0.2, including rendered printed pages 307–308. Under its numerical hypothesis for a projective surface that is not quadratically normal, the theorem produces a finite linear-section subscheme exhibiting the failure, with a further finite subscheme of the stated length. ## What this revision claims

The reduced-incidence theorem identifies the whole normalized Hilbert graph on the stated large open, with no extra independence hypothesis. The contact theorem computes the whole two-parameter graph for every displayed symmetric Jordan slice, including its nonreduced fibres, local deformation module and ramified stable-map comparison. The effective-stack theorem specifies the category, local multi-Rees equations and relative lifting classes. The proof gives these identifications directly. General smooth blow-up geometry, canonical forms, classical deformation calculations, an exhaustive priority result and classification of all higher-corank ambient fibres are not claimed as new outputs.
