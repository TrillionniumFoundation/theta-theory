\subsection{Dependency and novelty map}

\noindent\emph{Foundational geometry.}
The multiplication matrix and literal Fitting ideal give the deepest
normal cone. Its rank-one rulings orient the tensor factors;
determinant ideal quotient and coefficient extraction give the
intrinsic inverse theorem. These steps do not depend on spectral
classification or real likelihood.

\noindent\emph{Web branch.}
The contraction-kernel calculation supplies the exterior-support gap
for recombining web coefficients. Section~\ref{sec:jacobian-casimir}
gives a second all-ranks proof and exact singular values. Harmonic
decomposition, the Jacobian covariant and angular eigenvalues are
classical; the specified composite and normalization are proved here.

\noindent\emph{Pencil branch.}
The irreducible coefficient module and exterior duality recover the
pencil without web recombination. Single-degree generation gives the
sharp order. The parameter immersion and universal family retain
arbitrary complex base change with a specified relative socle and
tensor presentation; neither equates unmarked moduli stacks.

\noindent\emph{Spectral branch.}
Classical elementary-divisor and orthogonal-orbit theorems give
spectral classes and dominance. Reconstruction and the universal
family give the intrinsic finite invariants, all allowed flat
specializations and the first separating order. The Hilbert
interpretation uses the usual first graded Betti space.

\noindent\emph{Independent real branch.}
The totally real reciprocal likelihood theorem uses real spectral
normalization, interpolation and separated-root signs, not the web
or contraction theorem. A real structure and definite member are
supplied, not extracted from an unmarked complex scheme.

\noindent\emph{Supplement and evidence.}
All primary-boundary, collision, nilpotent-depth and relative-filtration
proofs remain in the supplement; they are not hidden hypotheses of
the inverse statements. Exact scripts test finite instances and
normalizations. They are not premises of structural proofs or
certificates of historical priority.
