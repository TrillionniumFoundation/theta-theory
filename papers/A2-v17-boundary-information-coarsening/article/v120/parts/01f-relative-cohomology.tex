\subsection{Cohomology and arbitrary base change in conductor transport}
\label{sec:relative-cohomology-details}
We make explicit the bundle assertions used in
Theorem~\ref{thm:relative-regularity-conductor}. Write
$p:\Pj^e_S\to S$ and $q:\mathcal Z\to S$. The sheaf
$\mathcal I_{\mathcal Z}$ is $S$-flat: it is the kernel of a
surjection between $S$-flat sheaves $\mathcal O$ and
$\mathcal O_{\mathcal Z}$. For
$t\ge\max\{0,r-1\}$ the two modules
\[
 E_t=p_*\mathcal O(t),\qquad F_t=q_*\mathcal O_{\mathcal Z}(t)
\]
are locally free and commute with arbitrary base change. The first
is the familiar homogeneous polynomial bundle; for the second use
that $q$ is finite locally free and $\mathcal O_{\mathcal Z}(t)$
is invertible. The fibre regularity bound gives
$H^1(\Pj^e_{\bar s},\mathcal I_{Z_{\bar s}}(t))=0$ in this range.
Hence the restriction map $E_t\to F_t$ is surjective on each
geometric fibre and, by Nakayama's lemma, on $S$.

Its kernel $K_t$ is locally free because the quotient $F_t$ is
locally free, so
\[
 0\longrightarrow K_t\longrightarrow E_t\longrightarrow F_t
             \longrightarrow0
\]
is locally split. The long exact sequence of direct images identifies
$K_t=p_*\mathcal I_{\mathcal Z}(t)$ and gives
$R^1p_*\mathcal I_{\mathcal Z}(t)=0$. In fact all higher direct
images vanish in this range: $\mathcal Z$ is finite over $S$,
and $\mathcal O(t)$ on projective space has no higher direct
images for $t\ge0$.

After any base change $S'\to S$ the displayed split sequence stays
exact. The corresponding sequence of sheaves on projective space
also stays exact because the original family is flat. Pushforward
identifies the new kernel with $p'_*\mathcal I_{\mathcal Z'}(t)$;
restriction is still surjective and its first higher direct image
is still zero. This proves the arbitrary base-change assertion
directly, without assuming $S'$ reduced, flat over $S$, or
Noetherian.

Now form the relative generating Grassmannian and let $\mathcal U$
be the inverse image of its tautological restricted series. For
$m\ge2$ the map
\[
 K_n\otimes\Sym^{m-1}\mathcal U\longrightarrow K_{mn}
\]
is a morphism of locally free sheaves. If the uniform bounds are
$\operatorname{reg}I_{\bar s}\le r$ and
$\operatorname{reg}(I_{\bar s}^2)\le s$, the single-fibre conductor
proof applies for $n\ge\max\{r,s-1\}$. If only $r$ is given,
Sidman's ordinary-square bound supplies $s=2r$. These bounds are
used on the geometric fibres only: no flatness or base change is
required for $\mathcal I_{\mathcal Z}^2$ or its conormal quotient.
Thus the displayed morphism is fibrewise surjective, and its
finitely presented cokernel is zero. It remains surjective after
every pullback.

Finally the locally split sequence
$0\to K_n\to\mathcal U\to\mathcal A\to0$ shows that the kernel
of $\Sym^m\mathcal U\to\Sym^m\mathcal A$ is generated by
$K_n\otimes\Sym^{m-1}\mathcal U$. Its multiplication image is
exactly the target restriction kernel $K_{mn}$. The induced
isomorphism of multiplication cokernels is therefore canonical
restriction and commutes with arbitrary base change. This explains
all relative assertions without replacing a coherent cokernel by
its fibre dimensions.
