## Generator schemes: comparison at the level actually checked

Arpin, Bozlee, Herr and Smith, *The scheme of monogenic generators I: representability*, **Research in Number Theory 9, article 14 (2023)**, DOI `10.1007/s40993-022-00419-5`. The publisher's version-of-record metadata was checked at `https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40993-022-00419-5`; the theorem text was checked in `https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.07185`, with the preprint numbering explicitly identified in the bibliography.

Definition 3.12 already gives the closed index-form non-monogenerator scheme; Proposition 3.14 gives the polygenerator open through maximal minors. The new paper expressly credits both constructions. The present comparison concerns a different task: exact image stabilization by initial codimension and the primary geometry of the complete multiplication scheme at its conductor boundary. Generic use of determinants or representability is not claimed as new.

## Algebra length

The field-level length bound follows by the dimension argument reproduced in the article. No novelty is assigned to it. The relevant literature now includes C. J. Pappacena, *An upper bound for the length of a finite-dimensional algebra*, J. Algebra 197 (1997), 535–545; and O. V. Markova, *Upper bound for the length of commutative algebras*, Sb. Math. 200:12 (2009), 1767–1787.

The publisher record for Pappacena was located by article identifier `S0021869397971404`. The historical context and bibliography were cross-checked against M. A. Khrystik, arXiv:2605.00141v1, Sections 1 and 6 and references: `https://arxiv.org/html/2605.00141v1`. Full independent theorem-level audits of Pappacena and Markova are not asserted. No specialized numerical estimate from them is used in a proof here. The new sharpness proposition is proved directly for every codimension and every generating rank at least two. The substantive relative claim remains equality over coefficient rings, without assuming intermediate images flat.

## Global transport: the classical regularity input

J. Sidman, *On the Castelnuovo–Mumford regularity of products of ideal sheaves*, arXiv:math/0110184v2, checked at `https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0110184`. Theorem 1.3 gives the regularity/cohomology-and-saturation facts used by the conductor proof. Theorem 1.8 gives the ordinary homogeneous product bound when the projective intersection is finite; taking the same zero-dimensional scheme twice yields the square bound. This inequality is credited as an input. The coherent multiplication-cokernel comparison and the new primary boundary realizations are separately proved in the manuscript.

## Scope of the new boundary result

The new complete primary classification covers all complex local algebras with Hilbert function `(1,2,2)`, including the extreme-corank locus. The factorization that precedes it applies more generally to split augmented algebras with cube-zero augmentation and locally free layers. These are different scopes. For arbitrary finite algebras, the inherited result remains a full codimension-two presentation plus conductor incidence, not an unproved universal primary classification.
