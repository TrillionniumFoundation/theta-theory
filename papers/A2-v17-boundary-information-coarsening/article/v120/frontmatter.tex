\begin{abstract}
We study multiplication failure through its full coherent cokernel,
including the primary structure on the conductor boundary. For every
complex local algebra with Hilbert function $(1,2,2)$ we classify the
complete failure scheme of three generators. The factors of the
quadratic relation determine its embedded surfaces and a possible
embedded point at the extreme-corank locus. The reduced support is
a normal determinant divisor; its generic multiplicity is two, while
the full scheme has nilradical index four. The proof uses a factorization
of the full Fitting ideal valid for every cube-zero augmentation ideal.
A conductor-kernel formula locates the associated strata and their
action-rank jumps, and an algebra deformation relates the two primary
types. More generally, products of a unital codimension-$r$ subbundle
of a finite locally free algebra over a $\mathbb Q$-scheme stabilize
in degree $r+1$. We prove that this bound is sharp in every codimension
and generating rank. The codimension-two presentation, its proper
quotient-algebra incidence, and a connected higher-contact family with
unbounded nilpotent complexity complement the boundary classification.
A regularity-controlled conductor theorem identifies the finite and
global multiplication cokernels for zero-dimensional projective schemes,
including finite flat families and arbitrary base change.
\end{abstract}
