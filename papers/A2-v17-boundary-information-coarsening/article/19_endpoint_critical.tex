\section{The endpoint critical experiment}
\label{sec:v17-endpoint-critical}
We apply the preceding theorem to a fixed physical channel. The two
hypotheses are its finite-flight law and its boundary model for the
\emph{same} table. The observation space, the failure symbol, and the
physical preparation cost are those of Section~\ref{sec:v6-transfer}.
Write $P^{\rm end}_{j,d}$ and $P^{\rm end}_{\partial,j,d}$ for the
pushforwards retaining the two endpoint positions on success and the
failure symbol otherwise. Thus residual time is not reported. Their
conditional versions are denoted by $\Pi^{\rm end}_{j,d}$ and
$\Pi^{\rm end}_{\partial,j,d}$. Superscript $0$ denotes the corresponding
quadratic right-limit laws of Section~\ref{sec:v7-critical}.

The common whitening in \eqref{eq:v7-whitening} is used only to prove
the comparison for this fixed table. It does not give an observation
rule knowledge of an unknown gap or curvature. Total variation and
Hellinger distance are unchanged by this common invertible map.

\subsection{Exact tangent density and its information}
Set
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v17-q-rates}
 q_j=e^{-j\gamma},\qquad
 \zeta_j=\log\coth(j\gamma/2)
        =\log\frac{1+q_j}{1-q_j},\qquad
                r_j=q_j^2\log(1/q_j).
\end{equation}
After whitening and a common orthogonal rotation, the endpoint density
of the finite tangent law is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v17-cap-density}
 f_{\zeta_j}(x)=\frac2\pi
       (1-e^{-\zeta_j}x_1^2-e^{\zeta_j}x_2^2)_+,
             \qquad x\in\R^2,
\end{equation}
and the boundary density is $f_0(x)=2(1-|x|^2)_+/\pi$.
The raw pair has the same mass
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v17-common-mass}
                       p_{j,d}^0=\frac{d^2}{2A\sinh(j\gamma)}
\end{equation}
on these successful densities, and the same failure mass $1-p_{j,d}^0$.

\begin{lemma}[The endpoint information coefficient]
\label{lem:v17-cap-information}
The densities \eqref{eq:v17-cap-density} are normalized. In the
parameter $\zeta$ their boundary information coefficient is one, and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v17-cap-hellinger}
 H^2(f_{\zeta_j},f_0)=r_j+O(q_j^2).
\end{equation}
For every $j,d$, the raw tangent pair has identical success-indicator
laws, even though its endpoint laws differ. These statements hold for
unequal facing curvatures and both parities of $j$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The eigenvalues of the whitened Hessian are
$\lambda_j=\tanh(j\gamma/2)=e^{-\zeta_j}$ and
$\lambda_j^{-1}=e^{\zeta_j}$. Integrating residual time in the
uniform cap gives \eqref{eq:v17-cap-density}. The change
$(y_1,y_2)=(e^{-\zeta/2}x_1,e^{\zeta/2}x_2)$ has determinant one,
and $\int_{|y|<1}(1-|y|^2)\,\dd y=\pi/2$; this proves
normalization. The same determinant identity in
\eqref{eq:v7-whitening} gives the common mass
\eqref{eq:v17-common-mass}. In particular the two success bits are
exactly Bernoulli with the same parameter, not merely equal to leading
order in $q_j$.

Take $w_\zeta=1-e^{-\zeta}x_1^2-e^\zeta x_2^2$ and $a_\zeta=2/\pi$
on a fixed large disk. Then $v=x_1^2-x_2^2$ and
$|\nabla w_0|=2$ on the unit circle. Consequently
\[
 \mathcal I_\Sigma=
       \frac1\pi\int_0^{2\pi}\cos^2(2\theta)\,\mathrm d\theta=1.
\]
One can also see the logarithm directly. For $w=1-|x|^2$ the score is
$S=(x_1^2-x_2^2)/w$. Polar integration gives the exact identities
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v17-cap-score}
 \Pr_0\{0<w<h\}=h^2,\qquad
 \mathbb E_0[S\one_{\{w\ge h\}}]=0,\qquad
 \mathbb E_0[S^2\one_{\{w\ge h\}}]
        =\log(1/h)-\frac32+2h-\frac{h^2}{2}.
\end{equation}
Indeed the last integral is $\int_h^1(1-w)^2\,\mathrm dw/w$;
the angular integral of $\cos^2(2\theta)$ is $\pi$.
Theorem~\ref{thm:v17-boundary-information} now gives
$H^2=\zeta_j^2\log(1/\zeta_j)/4+O(\zeta_j^2)$.
Since $\zeta_j=2q_j+O(q_j^3)$, this is
\eqref{eq:v17-cap-hellinger}. All dependence on the individual
curvatures was removed by a common invertible whitening, not by assuming
that the curvatures agree.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Sharp endpoint testing profile]
\label{thm:v17-endpoint-tangent}
Let $j\to\infty$. If $k_j r_j\to b\in[0,\infty]$, then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v17-endpoint-conditional}
 \| (\Pi^{0,\rm end}_j)^{\otimes k_j}
       -(\Pi^{0,\rm end}_{\partial,j})^{\otimes k_j}\|_{\rm TV}
                         \longrightarrow 2\Phi(\sqrt b)-1.
\end{equation}
If $n_jp_{j,d_j}^0r_j\to b\in[0,\infty]$, the same limit holds
for the raw tangent endpoint laws with $n_j$ preparations. At
$0<b<\infty$, the log likelihood under the boundary hypothesis
converges to $\mathcal N(-2b,4b)$. The minimum equal-prior testing
error tends to $\Phi(-\sqrt b)$. At $b=\infty$ the distance tends
to one and the testing error to zero.

In either experiment, vanishing total variation is equivalent to the
corresponding effective sample size times $r_j$ tending to zero.
In particular the sharp conditional endpoint scale is
$e^{2j\gamma}/(j\gamma)$, rather than $e^{j\gamma}$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply Theorem~\ref{thm:v17-boundary-information} with
$t=\zeta_j$, first with $p=1$ and then with $p=p_{j,d_j}^0$.
The row size in that theorem may of course run through the integers
$k_j$ or $n_j$. The information scales satisfy
\[
            \zeta_j^2\log(1/\zeta_j)=4r_j(1+o(1)).
\]
Lemma~\ref{lem:v17-cap-information} supplies coefficient one.
This proves the limits and the likelihood normalization. For necessity
in the last assertion, if the effective scale does not tend to zero,
pass to a subsequence on which it is bounded below by a fixed positive
number. A further subsequence either has a finite positive limit or
diverges. The corresponding distance limit is strictly positive.
Sufficiency is the case $b=0$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Positive-offset physical transfer}
As in Lemma~\ref{lem:v7-tangent}, put
\[
 \omega(d)=\sqrt d\quad\hbox{for general smooth contacts},\qquad
 \omega(d)=d\quad\hbox{if both contact graphs are even}.
\]
No assumption that the two even contacts coincide is made.

\begin{theorem}[The physical endpoint transition]
\label{thm:v17-endpoint-physical}
Suppose $d_j>0$ belongs to the common physical collar and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v17-physical-collar}
                       \frac{\omega(d_j)}{r_j}\longrightarrow0.
\end{equation}
If $k_jr_j\to b\in[0,\infty]$, then the actual conditional
endpoint laws satisfy
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v17-physical-conditional}
 \| (\Pi^{\rm end}_{j,d_j})^{\otimes k_j}
       -(\Pi^{\rm end}_{\partial,j,d_j})^{\otimes k_j}\|_{\rm TV}
                       \longrightarrow 2\Phi(\sqrt b)-1.
\end{equation}
If $n_jp_{j,d_j}^0r_j\to b\in[0,\infty]$, the same limit holds
for the actual raw endpoint laws with $n_j$ independent preparations.
The minimum equal-prior errors have the same limits as in
Theorem~\ref{thm:v17-endpoint-tangent}. Under
\eqref{eq:v17-physical-collar}, vanishing physical total variation is
equivalent to the corresponding effective scale tending to zero.
The estimates and conclusions are uniform on the compact geometric
families under consideration, including variable geometry and either
parity, provided the displayed scaling assumptions hold uniformly.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Projection onto the endpoint observation cannot increase total variation.
Lemma~\ref{lem:v7-tangent}, telescoping independent products, and the
reverse triangle inequality therefore give
\begin{align}
 |\mathrm{TV}^{\rm end}_{j,k,d}-\mathrm{TV}^{0,\rm end}_{j,k}|
                   &\le Ck\omega(d),\label{eq:v17-transfer-conditional}\\
 |\mathrm{TV}^{\rm end,raw}_{j,n,d}
                  -\mathrm{TV}^{0,\rm end,raw}_{j,n,d}|
                   &\le Cnp_{j,d}^0\omega(d).
                                           \label{eq:v17-transfer-raw}
\end{align}
The symbols in these two inequalities denote the distances of the
corresponding simple pairs of product laws. If $b<\infty$, their
right sides tend to zero by \eqref{eq:v17-physical-collar}.
Theorem~\ref{thm:v17-endpoint-tangent} proves the assertions in this
case.

If $b=\infty$, fix $B>0$. The conditional experiment eventually
contains its first $\lfloor B/r_j\rfloor$ observations. Their
accumulated tangent error is at most
$CB\omega(d_j)/r_j=o(1)$, and their limiting distance is
$2\Phi(\sqrt B)-1$. Projection onto this subsample gives the same
lower bound for the lower limit of the full distance. Let $B\to\infty$.
For raw preparations use instead
$\lfloor B/(p_{j,d_j}^0r_j)\rfloor$ observations.
Both rounding errors in the effective scales tend to zero.
This proves the supercritical assertion without requiring the tangent
error accumulated over the entire supercritical sample to vanish.

The same fixed positive subsample proves necessity of a vanishing
effective scale, as in Theorem~\ref{thm:v8-supercritical}.
The uniform version uses the uniform tangent estimates and the fact that
$\gamma$ is bounded below by a positive number; the whitened family
\eqref{eq:v17-cap-density} itself is independent of the remaining
geometry. Finally use the exact equal-prior testing identity.
\end{proof}

The positive-offset conditions are nonempty. For any $\epsilon>0$,
$d_j=r_j^{2+\epsilon}$ in the general smooth case and
$d_j=r_j^{1+\epsilon}$ in the even case satisfy
\eqref{eq:v17-physical-collar}. The preparation counts are finite for
every $j$. Since $p_{j,d}^0\asymp d^2e^{-j\gamma}$, the raw
endpoint transition occurs at order
\[
                         d_j^{-2}e^{3j\gamma}/(j\gamma).
\]
Failures have not been conditioned away in this statement.

\subsection{A strict hierarchy of observations}
\begin{corollary}[Three distinct comparison experiments]
\label{cor:v17-coarsening-hierarchy}
Under \eqref{eq:v17-physical-collar}, suppose
$n_jp_{j,d_j}^0r_j\to b\in(0,\infty)$. For the same two actual
physical models and the same $n_j$ preparations, the respective total
variation distances have the limits
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v17-three-limits}
 \begin{array}{c|c}
 \text{retained observation on success}&\text{limiting distance}\\ \hline
 \text{both endpoints and residual time}&1\\
 \text{both endpoints, without residual time}&2\Phi(\sqrt b)-1\\
 \text{success indicator only}&0.
 \end{array}
\end{equation}
Every experiment retains the same failed preparations. In the tangent
models the last distance is identically zero at every sample size.
Conversely, at the full-record critical scale
$n_jp_{j,d_j}^0q_j\to c\in(0,\infty)$, assuming only
$\omega(d_j)/q_j\to0$, the full-record distance tends to
$1-e^{-2c/\pi}$ while the endpoint-only distance tends to zero.
The analogous assertions hold for a prescribed number of independently
prepared successes, with the success-only conditional observation
constant.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
At the endpoint scale,
\[
 n_jp_{j,d_j}^0q_j
       =\frac{n_jp_{j,d_j}^0r_j}{q_j\log(1/q_j)}\longrightarrow\infty,
 \qquad \frac{\omega(d_j)}{q_j}
       =\frac{\omega(d_j)}{r_j}q_j\log(1/q_j)\longrightarrow0.
\]
Theorem~\ref{thm:v8-supercritical} gives the first limit in
\eqref{eq:v17-three-limits}, and
Theorem~\ref{thm:v17-endpoint-physical} gives the second.
For the last, both tangent success-indicator laws are exactly the same.
Their actual counterparts differ from these by at most
$Cn_jp_{j,d_j}^0\omega(d_j)=o(1)$ in total variation, by coarsening
Lemma~\ref{lem:v7-tangent}. This proves the last limit without
postulating any rate for a count experiment outside this tangent regime.

At the full-record critical scale the known support calculation gives
$1-e^{-2c/\pi}$, whereas
$n_jp_{j,d_j}^0r_j=(n_jp_{j,d_j}^0q_j)q_j\log(1/q_j)\to0$.
The tangent endpoint distance tends to zero by
Theorem~\ref{thm:v17-endpoint-tangent}; its physical approximation error
is $O(n_jp_{j,d_j}^0\omega(d_j))=o(1)$ under the stated weaker collar
condition. The conditional proof replaces $n_jp_{j,d_j}^0$ by $k_j$.
\end{proof}

The full-record limit is an erasure limit because its tangent laws have
equal densities on their common support
(Theorem~\ref{thm:v7-exact-tv}). The endpoint limit is instead Gaussian:
its supports are still different, but their exclusive mass is only
$O(q_j^2)$, whereas its Hellinger information is
$q_j^2\log(1/q_j)(1+o(1))$. The success bit loses this tangent shape
information entirely through the exact determinant cancellation. Thus
neither a one-record weak limit nor a coarsening of the full-record
coupling bound identifies the sharp endpoint experiment.

These are intrinsic scales involving the exact hyperbolic multiplier,
not a supplied upper convergence certificate $\tau$. They establish a
sharp observation-dependent validity law in an explicitly nonempty
physical tangent regime. They do not identify two unknown tables,
assert equivalence of arbitrary noisy smooth-profile experiments, or
turn the sufficient full-profile acquisition exponent into a minimax
one. The nonlinear relative law on a fixed collar and the complete
boundary-profile inverse retain their separate statements and proofs.
