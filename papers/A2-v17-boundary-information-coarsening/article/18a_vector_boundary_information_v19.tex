\section{Vector boundary information and unknown-geometry local experiments}
\label{sec:v19-vector-information}

The preceding boundary-information theorem compares a one-parameter pair.
For geometric inference one needs the corresponding local experiment when
the moving support is generated by several unknown geometric coordinates.
The logarithmic singularity is still carried by the normal motion of the
support, but its coefficient becomes an intrinsic information matrix.

Let $\theta\in\Theta\subset\mathbb R^r$ vary in a neighborhood of zero and
suppose
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v19-vector-density}
 f_\theta(x)=a_\theta(x)(w_\theta(x))_+,
 \qquad \int_U f_\theta(x)\,dx=1,
\end{equation}
with the same uniform $C^3$, positivity, compact-support and regular-boundary
hypotheses as in Section~\ref{sec:v17-boundary-information}, now jointly in
$(\theta,x)$.  Put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v19-boundary-information-matrix}
 V(x)=D_\theta w_\theta(x)|_{\theta=0}\in\mathbb R^r,
 \qquad
 \mathcal J_\Sigma=
 \int_\Sigma\frac{a_0(x)V(x)V(x)^t}{|\nabla w_0(x)|}\,d\sigma(x).
\end{equation}
This matrix is positive semidefinite and is unchanged by multiplying the
defining function $w_\theta$ by a positive smooth factor and compensating
$a_\theta$ accordingly.

\begin{theorem}[Boundary LAN and the intrinsic information matrix]
\label{thm:v19-vector-boundary-lan}
Let $\delta_n\downarrow0$, $0<p_n\le1$, and assume
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v19-vector-rate}
 n p_n\delta_n^2\log(1/\delta_n)\longrightarrow1.
\end{equation}
For $h$ in a fixed compact subset of $\mathbb R^r$, let
\[
 P_{n,h}=(1-p_n)\delta_\dagger+p_nf_{\delta_n h}\,dx.
\]
Then, under $P_{n,0}^{\otimes n}$, there are random vectors
$\Delta_n\Rightarrow N_r(0,\mathcal J_\Sigma)$ such that, uniformly for
$h$ in compact sets,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v19-lan-expansion}
 \log\frac{dP_{n,h}^{\otimes n}}{dP_{n,0}^{\otimes n}}
 =h^t\Delta_n-\frac12h^t\mathcal J_\Sigma h+o_P(1)
\end{equation}
on an event whose null probability tends to one.  The support-exclusive
probability is $o(1)$ uniformly on every compact set of local parameters.
Consequently the finite-dimensional local experiment converges to the
Gaussian shift with information matrix $\mathcal J_\Sigma$.

In particular, for every fixed $h$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v19-vector-testing-profile}
 \|P_{n,h}^{\otimes n}-P_{n,0}^{\otimes n}\|_{\rm TV}
 \longrightarrow
 2\Phi\!\left(\frac{\sqrt{h^t\mathcal J_\Sigma h}}2\right)-1.
\end{equation}
If $\mathcal J_\Sigma$ is positive definite, all local geometric
directions are identifiable at the boundary rate
$(np_n\log(1/\delta_n))^{-1/2}$, and the limiting Gaussian experiment has
covariance lower bound $\mathcal J_\Sigma^{-1}$ for regular estimation of
the local parameter.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Only the vector bookkeeping is new; the boundary truncation is the one used
in the proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:v17-boundary-information}.  Define the
vector score on $D=\{w_0>0\}$ by
\[
 \mathsf S(x)=D_\theta\log a_\theta(x)|_0+
                         \frac{V(x)}{w_0(x)}.
\]
For $u,v\in\mathbb R^r$, the coarea calculation applied to
$(u^tV)(v^tV)$ gives, as $q\downarrow0$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v19-vector-score-covariance}
 \mathbb E_0\!\left[(u^t\mathsf S)(v^t\mathsf S)
                 \mathbf1_{\{w_0\ge q\}}\right]
 =u^t\mathcal J_\Sigma v\log(1/q)+O_{u,v}(1).
\end{equation}
The corresponding third and fourth truncated moments satisfy the same
$q^{-1}$ and $q^{-2}$ bounds as in
Lemma~\ref{lem:v17-coarea}, uniformly for unit directions.  Differentiating
normalization gives $\mathbb E_0\mathsf S=0$ in the integrable sense.

Put $L_n=\log(1/\delta_n)^{1/4}$ and $q_n=\delta_n L_n$.  Represent one
null observation by its Bernoulli success indicator $Y_i$ and, on success,
by $X_i\sim f_0$.  The probability that any successful observation lies in
$0<w_0<q_n$ is
\[
 O(np_nq_n^2)
 =O\!\left(
   \frac{np_n\delta_n^2\log(1/\delta_n)}
        {\log(1/\delta_n)}L_n^2\right)=o(1),
\]
uniformly for bounded $h$; the alternative-exclusive part has the same
bound.  On the common-support event, joint Taylor expansion gives
\[
 \log\frac{f_{\delta_nh}}{f_0}
 =\delta_n h^t\mathsf S
 -\frac{\delta_n^2}{2}(h^t\mathsf S)^2
 +\delta_n^2R_{n,h},
\]
where the summed remainder is $o_P(1)$ uniformly for $h$ in compact sets.
Indeed the parameter-Taylor remainder has the same
$1+w_0^{-1}$ bound as \eqref{eq:v17-density-ratio}, and the cubic logarithm
is controlled by the truncated third moment.  Thus
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v19-vector-prelan}
 \log\frac{dP_{n,h}^{\otimes n}}{dP_{n,0}^{\otimes n}}
 =h^t\Delta_n-rac12 h^tQ_nh+o_P(1),
\end{equation}
where
\[
 \Delta_n=\delta_n\sum_{i=1}^nY_i
       \mathsf S(X_i)\mathbf1_{\{w_0(X_i)\ge q_n\}},
\]
\[
 Q_n=\delta_n^2\sum_{i=1}^nY_i
       \mathsf S(X_i)\mathsf S(X_i)^t
       \mathbf1_{\{w_0(X_i)\ge q_n\}}.
\]
Equation~\eqref{eq:v19-vector-score-covariance} and
\eqref{eq:v19-vector-rate} show that
$\operatorname{Cov}(\Delta_n)\to\mathcal J_\Sigma$ and
$\mathbb EQ_n\to\mathcal J_\Sigma$.  Every scalar projection of a centered
summand in $\Delta_n$ is $O(1/L_n)$ on the truncated set, so the
Lindeberg condition holds uniformly on the unit sphere.  Cram\'er--Wold
gives
$\Delta_n\Rightarrow N_r(0,\mathcal J_\Sigma)$.  The fourth-moment bound
gives $Q_n\to\mathcal J_\Sigma$ in probability, entry by entry.  Substitution
in \eqref{eq:v19-vector-prelan} proves \eqref{eq:v19-lan-expansion}.

Joint convergence of likelihood ratios for every finite set of local
parameters, together with the uniform compact expansion, identifies the
limit as the Gaussian shift experiment.  Formula
\eqref{eq:v19-vector-testing-profile} is the exact equal-covariance Gaussian
shift testing profile.  When $\mathcal J_\Sigma$ is positive definite, its
inverse is the covariance matrix of the efficient estimator in the limit
experiment and therefore the local asymptotic covariance lower bound for
regular estimators.  The support-exclusivity assertion follows from
$np_n\delta_n^2=o(1)$, which is a consequence of
\eqref{eq:v19-vector-rate}.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Application to an unknown billiard geometry}
Fix a parity and a positive offset $d$ inside the common collar.  Let
$\eta\in\mathbb R^r$ parameterize a smooth finite-dimensional local family
of billiard tables and keep the selected contact labels and signed contact
frames.  The limiting conditional endpoint density supplied by
Theorem~\ref{thm:v8-main-relative} is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v19-billiard-endpoint-family}
 f_{\eta,d}(u,v)=C_{\eta,d}
 B_{0,\eta}(u)B_{p,\eta}(v)
 \bigl(d-S_{0,\eta}(u)-S_{p,\eta}(v)\bigr)_+,
\end{equation}
where $C_{\eta,d}>0$ is the normalizing factor.  Its support function is
\[
 w_{\eta,d}(u,v)=d-S_{0,\eta}(u)-S_{p,\eta}(v).
\]
Strict convexity makes $|\nabla_{u,v}w_{0,d}|>0$ on its support boundary.
Therefore Theorem~\ref{thm:v19-vector-boundary-lan} applies directly, with
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v19-billiard-information-matrix}
 \mathcal J_d=
 \int_{\{S_0(u)+S_p(v)=d\}}
 \frac{C_{0,d}B_0(u)B_p(v)\,
       \dot w_d(u,v)\dot w_d(u,v)^t}
      {|\nabla(S_0\oplus S_p)(u,v)|}\,d\sigma,
\end{equation}
where
$\dot w_d=-D_\eta S_{0,\eta}|_0-D_\eta S_{p,\eta}|_0$.
This is a local asymptotic experiment for an \emph{unknown geometric
parameter}, rather than a comparison of two known laws for a fixed table.

For a finite-dimensional contact-jet family, use the two same-type
orientations $b=0,1$ and allow $d$ to range in any small open offset
interval.  If a tangent vector $h$ lies in the kernel of every resulting
boundary information matrix, then
$D_hS_b(u)+D_hS_b(v)=0$ on every level
$S_b(u)+S_b(v)=d$ in that interval.  Taking levels approaching
$S_b(u)$ with the other endpoint at zero gives $D_hS_b(u)=0$ on a contact
neighborhood.  The block recursion of
Theorem~\ref{thm:v19-signed-rigidity} then gives $h=0$ on the graph-jet
parameter space.  By compactness of the unit sphere in a fixed finite
dimension, finitely many positive offsets already have a summed
information matrix which is positive definite.  Hence a finite physical
endpoint design has a nonsingular logarithmic information matrix for every
fixed finite labelled contact-jet model, without an evenness assumption.

Finally the relative law transfers this limit experiment from the boundary
model to actual long finite bridges.  If $k_n$ is the number of retained
successful endpoint records and $j_n$ is the flight number, the sufficient
condition
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v19-finite-bridge-lan-transfer}
                    k_n\tau^{j_n}\longrightarrow0
\end{equation}
makes the product total-variation error from the uniform finite-to-boundary
endpoint approximation vanish.  In the raw-preparation experiment the
corresponding sufficient condition is
$n_np_{j_n,d}\tau^{j_n}\to0$, and the LAN normalization uses the effective
sample size $n_np_{j_n,d}$.  Thus the information matrix
\eqref{eq:v19-billiard-information-matrix} is observable in an actual
positive-offset billiard experiment, with all failed preparations retained
in the cost.
