\section{Compact local experiments}
\label{sec:v32-compact-experiments}

The finite-subexperiment conclusions above identify the Gaussian limit.  We
now establish the separate assertion of convergence in Le Cam distance on an
arbitrary fixed compact local parameter set.  The additional step is a
uniform continuity estimate for the experiments, not a reinterpretation of
finite-dimensional likelihood convergence.  Throughout, total variation is
\(\|P-Q\|_{\rm TV}=\sup_A|P(A)-Q(A)|\), and the Hellinger convention is
\eqref{eq:v22-hellinger-convention}.

\begin{lemma}[Passage from finite sets to compact sets]
\label{lem:v32-finite-net}
Let \(K\) be a compact metric space and let
\(\mathsf E_n=(P_{n,t}:t\in K)\) and
\(\mathsf E=(Q_t:t\in K)\) be experiments on standard Borel spaces.
Suppose that their restrictions to each finite \(F\subset K\) satisfy
\(\Delta(\mathsf E_n|_F,\mathsf E|_F)\to0\).  Put
\[
 \omega_n(r)=\sup_{d(s,t)\le r}\|P_{n,s}-P_{n,t}\|_{\rm TV},
 \qquad
 \omega(r)=\sup_{d(s,t)\le r}\|Q_s-Q_t\|_{\rm TV}.
\]
If
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v32-net-moduli}
 \lim_{r\downarrow0}\limsup_{n\to\infty}\omega_n(r)=0,
 \qquad \lim_{r\downarrow0}\omega(r)=0,
\end{equation}
then \(\Delta(\mathsf E_n,\mathsf E)\to0\).  Neither experiment is required
to be dominated by one of its own members.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Fix \(r>0\) and a finite \(r\)-net \(F\subset K\).  For every
\(\varepsilon>0\), choose a Markov kernel \(R_n\) whose error on \(F\)
is at most \(\delta(\mathsf E_n|_F,\mathsf E|_F)+\varepsilon\).
For \(t\in K\), choose \(s\in F\) with \(d(s,t)\le r\).  Contraction of
total variation gives
\begin{align*}
 \|R_nP_{n,t}-Q_t\|_{\rm TV}
 &\le \|P_{n,t}-P_{n,s}\|_{\rm TV}
       +\|R_nP_{n,s}-Q_s\|_{\rm TV}+\|Q_s-Q_t\|_{\rm TV}\\
 &\le \omega_n(r)+\delta(\mathsf E_n|_F,\mathsf E|_F)
       +\varepsilon+\omega(r).
\end{align*}
The same argument for a reverse kernel, followed by
\(\varepsilon\downarrow0\), proves
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v32-net-inequality}
 \Delta(\mathsf E_n,\mathsf E)
 \le \Delta(\mathsf E_n|_F,\mathsf E|_F)+\omega_n(r)+\omega(r).
\end{equation}
First let \(n\to\infty\) with this finite net fixed, and then let
\(r\downarrow0\).  The net point is used only to estimate the error of a
single kernel; it is not an unknown-parameter input to that kernel.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Compact moving-support Gaussian approximation]
\label{thm:v32-compact-vector}
Under the hypotheses of Theorem~\ref{thm:v22-vector-boundary-gaussian},
for every compact \(K\subset\mathbb R^r\),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v32-compact-vector-limit}
 \Delta\!\left(
   \{P_{n,h}^{\otimes n}:h\in K\},
   \{N_{\mathsf H}(\mathcal J_{\mathsf H}\Pi_{\mathsf H}h,
                    \mathcal J_{\mathsf H}):h\in K\}
          \right)\longrightarrow0.
\end{equation}
Here \(\mathsf H\), \(\Pi_{\mathsf H}\) and
\(\mathcal J_{\mathsf H}\) are as in
\eqref{eq:v22-identifiable-space}--\eqref{eq:v22-gaussian-central-experiment}.
The assertion includes singular information, with the zero-dimensional
Gaussian interpreted as the one-point experiment.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Fix \(K\) and a compact neighborhood containing every line segment between
its points.  Joint smoothness and the common regular collar imply, for
\(h,h'\in K\) and \(s=\|h-h'\|\le1\),
\[
 \|w_{\delta_nh}-w_{\delta_nh'}\|_{C^1}
   +\|a_{\delta_nh}-a_{\delta_nh'}\|_{C^0}
 \le C_K\delta_ns.
\]
Lemma~\ref{lem:v22-hypersurface-stability}, with constants uniform in this
neighborhood, therefore yields
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v32-local-H-modulus}
 H^2(f_{\delta_nh},f_{\delta_nh'})
 \le C_K\delta_n^2s^2
       \left(1+\log\frac1{\delta_n}+\log\frac1s\right),
 \qquad 0<s\le1.
\end{equation}
The failure atoms have identical masses.  Hence their one-record squared
Hellinger distance is \(p_n\) times the successful-density distance.
Product subadditivity and \eqref{eq:v22-vector-rate} give, for all large
\(n\),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v32-product-H-modulus}
 H^2(P_{n,h}^{\otimes n},P_{n,h'}^{\otimes n})
 \le C_Ks^2\bigl(1+\log(1/s)\bigr).
\end{equation}
Since \(\|P-Q\|_{\rm TV}\le H(P,Q)\) with our convention, this supplies
the first modulus in \eqref{eq:v32-net-moduli}.

For the displayed Gaussian laws, writing \(v=h-h'\), direct Gaussian
integration gives
\[
 H^2(Q_h,Q_{h'})=2\left(1-\exp\{-v^t\mathcal J_\Sigma v/8\}\right).
\]
This identity is evaluated on \(\mathsf H\); it does not require an inverse
of \(\mathcal J_\Sigma\) on its kernel.  It supplies the second modulus.
The finite-set convergence is item 2 of
Theorem~\ref{thm:v22-vector-boundary-gaussian}.  Apply
Lemma~\ref{lem:v32-finite-net}.

In particular, the comparison here is for the original moving-support
family.  The common-collar representative is needed to identify its finite
limits, but no likelihood ratio with respect to the original reference law
has been asserted on support-exclusive observations.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Compact fixed-window endpoint experiments]
\label{cor:v32-compact-fixed-window}
With the finite design, rate conditions and reference-based caps of
Theorem~\ref{thm:v22-fixed-window-gaussian}, the capped boundary
endpoint-output experiment converges in Le Cam distance, on every fixed
compact set of \(z=(a,h)\), to the Gaussian experiment with information
\(\mathcal K_{\rm design}\).  The same conclusion holds after restriction
to any linear subspace of the local coordinate.  Under
\eqref{eq:v22-transfer-condition}, it holds also for the actual finite-bridge
endpoint-output experiment, at the same declared observation level.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Use the normalized ideal densities
\eqref{eq:v22-ideal-endpoint-density} in the independent design batches.
Their defining functions and normalized amplitudes satisfy the uniform
bounds used in \eqref{eq:v32-local-H-modulus}; the normalization is positive
and smooth on a common small parameter neighborhood.  Sum the resulting
one-record bounds over the \(\sum_\ell k_{n,\ell}=k_n\) records.
The critical success scale gives the product modulus
\eqref{eq:v32-product-H-modulus} with \(n p_n\) replaced by \(k_n\).
The finite limits have information
\(\sum_\ell\alpha_\ell\mathcal K_{b_\ell,d_\ell}
=\mathcal K_{\rm design}\), by
Theorem~\ref{thm:v22-fixed-window-gaussian}.  Lemma~\ref{lem:v32-finite-net}
and Gaussian continuity give compact convergence for the ideal experiment.

Proposition~\ref{prop:v22-fixed-window-reduction} transfers it to the exact
boundary densities with a uniform \(o(1)\) error.  The cap contributes at
most \(\sum_\ell e^{-c k_{n,\ell}}\), by
Lemma~\ref{lem:v22-reference-cap}.  Restriction cannot increase deficiency.
Finally, Theorem~\ref{thm:v22-stopped-local-transfer} contributes
\(C_Kk_n\tau^{j_n}=o(1)\) on the common endpoint-output space.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
Compact local convergence is not an assertion of uniform equivalence over
an unbounded local parameter space or over the complete analytic class of
tables.  The kernels may depend on the fixed compact set, the reference
channel and the declared design, but never on the unknown local parameter.
\end{remark}
