## Ohta: primary theorem pins obtained in this revision

T. Ohta, *The singularities of the closures of nilpotent orbits in certain symmetric pairs*, Tohoku Math. J. (2) 38 (1986), 441–468; DOI 10.2748/tmj/1178228456.

Publisher full PDF: https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/tmj1949/38/3/38_3_441/_pdf/-char/en

The parsed text and page images were inspected at printed pp.444,447,456. Section 1.1, p.443 specifies the group G(V) of all isometries and the self-adjoint space. In epsilon=1 this is the full complex orthogonal group, not just SO and not the skew-adjoint nilpotent cone. Proposition 1 and Remark 1, p.444 supply the partition classification. Theorem 1, p.447, uses the dominance order of Section 1.4, p.446; its proof occupies pp.447–451. Section 2.4, Remark 8(i), p.456 gives the half-dimension identity. There is a different Remark 8 later, so the section/page qualifiers are retained.

`parts/30-orthogonal-input-v145.tex` isolates exactly this input and supplies independent dimension and determinant-component arguments. No new orbit-classification priority is claimed. Trevisiol's useful account remains in the preserved v144 archive but is no longer the sole citation carrying the main proof.
