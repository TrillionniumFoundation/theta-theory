\section{Signed endpoint rigidity without contact symmetry}
\label{sec:v19-signed-rigidity}

The scalar onset laws of Section~\ref{sec:v9-compatibility} deliberately
integrate out the signs of the two endpoint coordinates.  Their natural
inverse is therefore the symmetrized energy profile.  The physical record
contains more information: when the two signed endpoint positions are
retained, the support itself records the two half-line actions before this
symmetrization.  This observation removes the even-contact hypothesis from
the local rigidity statement.

Fix signed transverse coordinates in the two labelled contact frames.  For
$b\in\{0,1\}$, use an even number of flights and the orientation whose first
and last impacts have type $b$.  Let $\Pi^{\mathrm{end},\infty}_{bb,d}$ be
the limiting conditional law of the two endpoint coordinates after the
residual time has been integrated out.  By
Theorem~\ref{thm:v8-main-relative}, it has a positive density on its
interior proportional to
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v19-signed-endpoint-density}
 (d-S_b(u)-S_b(v))_+B_b(u)B_b(v).
\end{equation}
Thus its support, rather than a chosen normalization of its density, is the
relevant invariant.

\begin{theorem}[Signed-endpoint action and contact rigidity]
\label{thm:v19-signed-rigidity}
Let the two labelled facing contacts be $C^\infty$ and strictly convex.  No
evenness, reflection symmetry, equality of the two contacts, or supplied
contact curvature is assumed.  Suppose the selected physical channel and
its signed endpoint coordinates are retained.

For all sufficiently small $d>0$, the two same-type limiting endpoint laws
$\Pi^{\mathrm{end},\infty}_{00,d}$ and
$\Pi^{\mathrm{end},\infty}_{11,d}$ determine the two half-line actions
$S_0,S_1$ on a fixed contact neighborhood.  If the physical time origin is
retained, the onset times also determine the gap $g$.  Consequently the
gap, the two contact curvatures, and every finite jet of both labelled
contact graphs are determined by the signed endpoint laws.

More explicitly, write in the fixed signed contact frames
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v19-general-contact-jets}
 \psi_b(y)=\frac{\kappa_b}{2}y^2+
       \sum_{n\ge3}\frac{q_{b,n}}{n!}y^n,
 \qquad q_n=(q_{0,n},q_{1,n})^t,
\end{equation}
and put
\[
 a_b=S_b''(0),\qquad
 s_n=(S_0^{(n)}(0),S_1^{(n)}(0))^t.
\]
Then the leading geometry is recovered from
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v19-leading-recovery}
 \gamma=\operatorname{arsinh}\!\bigl(g\sqrt{a_0a_1}\bigr),\qquad
 c=\cosh\gamma,
 \qquad
 c_0=c\sqrt{a_0/a_1},\quad c_1=c\sqrt{a_1/a_0},
 \qquad
 \kappa_b=\frac{c_b-1}{g}.
\end{equation}
For every $n\ge3$, $s_n$ depends only on the leading geometry and
$q_3,\ldots,q_n$, is affine in $q_n$, and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v19-general-jet-block}
 D_{q_n}s_n=
 \begin{pmatrix}
 \coth(n\gamma)&\mathfrak r_0^n\csch(n\gamma)\\
 \mathfrak r_1^n\csch(n\gamma)&\coth(n\gamma)
 \end{pmatrix},
 \qquad
 \mathfrak r_b=\sqrt{c_b/c_{1-b}}.
\end{equation}
The determinant of this block is exactly one.  Hence the graph jets are
recovered recursively at every finite order, with a locally Lipschitz
inverse on compact positive geometric and finite-jet sets.

For real-analytic contacts, equality of the two signed endpoint-law germs
and of the physical onset determines both labelled analytic graph germs.
If the participating obstacle boundaries are connected and real analytic,
their boundary images are then determined in the fixed contact frames.
For a smooth compact family and every fixed $M$, the same reconstruction
from the actual finite even-flight endpoint supports recovers the two graph
jets through order $M$ with error $O_M(\tau^j)$, where $\tau<1$ is the
relative-law rate of Theorem~\ref{thm:v8-main-relative}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Because $B_b$ is strictly positive on the common endpoint box, the interior
of the support in \eqref{eq:v19-signed-endpoint-density} is exactly
\[
 \{(u,v):S_b(u)+S_b(v)<d\}.
\]
Define its support-threshold function by
\[
 T_b(u,v)=\inf\{d>0:(u,v)\in
       \operatorname{int}\operatorname{supp}
       \Pi^{\mathrm{end},\infty}_{bb,d}\}.
\]
Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v19-support-threshold}
 T_b(u,v)=S_b(u)+S_b(v),\qquad S_b(u)=T_b(u,0),
\end{equation}
because $S_b(0)=0$.  In particular, retaining the sign of the endpoint
coordinate recovers the full action $S_b$ and not only its even energy
pushforward.  This is precisely the information lost in
\eqref{eq:v9-energy-profile}.

The gap is the physical onset per flight: for any fixed selected itinerary,
the infimum of physical times at which its near-onset event has nonzero
probability is $jg$.  Thus $g$ is observed from the time axis and is not a
curvature input.  From the relative theorem,
\[
 a_b=\frac{c\sinh\gamma}{g c_{1-b}},
 \qquad c=\sqrt{c_0c_1}=\cosh\gamma.
\]
Therefore
\[
 a_0a_1=\frac{\sinh^2\gamma}{g^2},
 \qquad \frac{a_0}{a_1}=\frac{c_0}{c_1},
\]
which gives \eqref{eq:v19-leading-recovery}.  In particular neither
$\kappa_0$ nor $\kappa_1$ is supplied.

It remains to recover the nonsymmetric higher jets.  The finite-jet
dependence is proved exactly as in Theorem~\ref{thm:v12-two-contact}, but
without discarding odd endpoint degrees.  Differentiate the stationary
half-line equations at the origin in the weighted sequence space.  At each
order the highest orbit derivative is multiplied by the same positive
quadratic Jacobi operator; all other terms involve only lower orbit and
graph jets.  Hence $s_n$ uses no graph derivative above order $n$.

Keep the lower graph jets fixed and vary only $q_{r,n}$.  By the envelope
identity, the degree-$n$ first variation of the stationary action is
obtained by evaluating the degree-$n$ pure endpoint powers on the linear
half-line orbit.  For an orbit starting at type $b$, the linear coefficients
are
\[
 t_i^{(b)}=
 \begin{cases}
 \varrho^i,&i\ \text{even},\\
 \mathfrak r_b\varrho^i,&i\ \text{odd},
 \end{cases}
 \qquad \varrho=e^{-\gamma}.
\]
The boundary site is counted once and each interior site twice.  Absolute
convergence of the geometric sums gives
\begin{align}
 \partial_{q_{b,n}}S_b^{(n)}(0)
   &=1+2\sum_{k\ge1}\varrho^{2nk}=\coth(n\gamma),
       \label{eq:v19-own-action-variation}\\
 \partial_{q_{1-b,n}}S_b^{(n)}(0)
   &=2\mathfrak r_b^n\sum_{k\ge0}
          \varrho^{n(2k+1)}
     =\mathfrak r_b^n\csch(n\gamma).
       \label{eq:v19-other-action-variation}
\end{align}
Lower-jet corrections to the stationary orbit cannot alter this highest
new homogeneous degree.  Reversing the labels gives
\eqref{eq:v19-general-jet-block}.  Since
$\mathfrak r_0\mathfrak r_1=1$,
\[
 \det D_{q_n}s_n
   =\coth^2(n\gamma)-\csch^2(n\gamma)=1.
\]
Thus, after the lower jets have been reconstructed, the next pair is
obtained from
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v19-general-recursion}
 q_n=
 \begin{pmatrix}
 \coth(n\gamma)&-\mathfrak r_0^n\csch(n\gamma)\\
 -\mathfrak r_1^n\csch(n\gamma)&\coth(n\gamma)
 \end{pmatrix}
 (s_n-R_n),
\end{equation}
where $R_n$ is the contribution determined by the already recovered lower
jets.  The coefficients are smooth on positive compact geometric sets, so
finite-order local Lipschitz bounds follow by induction.  Notice that no
denominator involving $\kappa_0-\kappa_1$ and no parity restriction on $n$
appears.

For analytic contacts, equality of all recovered signed jets gives equality
of the convergent graph germs.  Analytic continuation along a connected
analytic boundary and uniqueness for the planar Frenet equations then give
the stated boundary-image conclusion, as in
Corollary~\ref{cor:v12-analytic-boundaries}.

Finally, for the actual finite even-flight law, positivity of the physical
flux shows that its endpoint support threshold is the exact stationary
excess action $E_{j,b}(u,v)$.  The relative estimate
\eqref{eq:v8-relative} gives
\[
 \|E_{j,b}-(S_b\oplus S_b)\|_{C^M}\le C_M\tau^j.
\]
Taking the slice $v=0$ and applying the finite-order locally Lipschitz
recursion above proves the $O_M(\tau^j)$ physical reconstruction claim.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Why the scalar profile remains useful]
Theorem~\ref{thm:v19-signed-rigidity} does not invalidate the smooth
profile inverse of Section~\ref{sec:v9-compatibility}.  The two theorems
refer to different coarsenings of the same physical record.  Integrating
the signed endpoints produces the symmetrized profile and necessarily
forgets the odd branch displacement; retaining the endpoint coordinates
recovers the unsymmetrized action support and hence the full labelled local
jets.  This distinction is part of the observation hierarchy rather than a
symmetry assumption on the billiard table.
\end{remark}
