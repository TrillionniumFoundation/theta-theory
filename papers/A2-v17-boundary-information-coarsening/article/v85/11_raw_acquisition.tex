\section{Raw acquisition costs and quantitative conventions}
\label{sec:v85-raw}
The budget $N$ in the preceding readout experiment counts retained
pairs. A physical acquisition must also count failures. We now give a
fixed-attempt experiment, without assuming that its success probability
is calibrated or that a positive lower bound is supplied to the
confidence procedure.

\begin{theorem}[Fixed raw attempts with observed acceptance]
\label{thm:v85-raw}
At each of $mJ$ cells make $B$ independent raw attempts. An attempt
returns either a failure symbol or a readout pair. Its success
probability $r_{ij}\in[0,1]$ is unknown, and the conditional law of a
successful pair is $\Phi_j(\omega_i)$ from
\eqref{eq:v85-forward}. Different cells need not be independent.
Let $K_{ij}$ be the observed number of successes. For $K_{ij}>0$ put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v85-random-radius}
 \eta_{ij}=\left\{\frac{uv}{2K_{ij}}
                      \log\frac{4mJuv}{\zeta}\right\}^{1/2},
\end{equation}
and let $\widehat P_{ij}$ be the empirical conditional matrix.
Construct action confidence sets by imposing the residual bound
$\eta_{ij}$ at each nonempty cell and imposing no constraint at empty
cells. These sets cover all true actions simultaneously with probability
at least $1-\zeta/2$, for every choice of the success probabilities.
In particular they give the metric certificate
\eqref{eq:v85-metric-certificate} in the geometric experiment.

On a true regular channel class, suppose in addition that
$r_{ij}\ge r_*>0$ at every cell, where $r_*$ need not be known to the
procedure. If
\[
                     B r_*\ge8\log\frac{2mJ}{\zeta},
\]
then, for sufficiently large $B r_*$, with probability at least
$1-\zeta$ the largest action interval width is at most
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v85-raw-width}
                 C_\kappa\left\{
                  \frac{\log(4mJuv/\zeta)}{B r_*}\right\}^{1/2}.
\end{equation}
The raw budget is exactly $mJB$, including every failure. In the
boundary experiment the resulting metric error is at most
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v85-raw-metric}
 C\left\{h+
        \left(\frac{\log(4m_hJuv/\zeta)}{B r_*}\right)^{1/2}
                                                 \right\}^{\mu}.
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Conditional on $K_{ij}=k>0$, the retained categorical observations in
that cell have the multinomial law of $k$ independent draws from
$\Phi_j(\omega_i)$. Apply the entrywise exponential bound conditional
on this count. The radius in \eqref{eq:v85-random-radius} gives
failure probability at most $\zeta/(2mJ)$ for that matrix, uniformly
in $k$. Integrate over $K_{ij}$ and take a union bound over cells.
Empty cells impose no constraint and hence cannot exclude the truth.
This proves the first assertion without a success floor. A finite-grid
implementation uses cellwise tolerances $\eta_{ij}+L_\Phi\delta$
and the same action padding as Proposition~\ref{prop:v85-grid}.

For the rate, a binomial lower-tail bound gives
\[
 \Pr\{K_{ij}<B r_*/2\}\le \exp(-B r_*/8).
\]
One elementary derivation applies Markov's inequality to
$e^{-tK_{ij}}$, uses
$(1-r_{ij}+r_{ij}e^{-t})^B\le
\exp\{-B r_{ij}(1-e^{-t})\}$, and takes $t=\log2$;
the resulting exponent is at most $-B r_{ij}/8$ at half its mean.
The event with threshold $B r_*/2$ is smaller when $r_{ij}\ge r_*$.
A union bound and the displayed budget condition make the total
failure probability at most $\zeta/2$. On the remaining event every
radius is bounded by a constant times the square root in
\eqref{eq:v85-raw-width}. Repeat the regular compact inverse argument
of Theorem~\ref{thm:v85-confidence}, using the largest cell radius.
Theorem~\ref{thm:v85-geometric} then gives
\eqref{eq:v85-raw-metric}. All attempts, including late arrivals,
missed detections and rejected readings represented by the failure
symbol, have already been counted in $mJB$.
\end{proof}
The conditional law of a successful readout remains a model assumption.
Unmodelled false readings change that law; they cannot be repaired by
only charging their cost. At very small success probabilities the
certificate remains valid but may stay wide. Thus no uniform raw
budget rate is asserted without actual acceptance information in the
class. The rate parameter $r_*$ is not a hidden calibration input to
the procedure.

\subsection{Closed classes for covering reconstruction}
For clarity, the compact classes used in
Theorem~\ref{thm:v84-mesh} can be specified without chart choices.
Fix all its margins and an additional bound $A_0$. For each
$2\le B_0\le\min(u,v)$ define $\mathcal K_{B_0}$ as the following
closed subset of centred pointwise parameters:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v85-closed-covering-class}
\begin{split}
 \mathcal K_{B_0}=\{\Xi={}&(W,U,V,\alpha,\vartheta):\
 U\in(\Delta_{u-1})^{B_0},\quad V\in(\Delta_{v-1})^{B_0},\\
 &\sum_b\alpha_b=0,\quad \sum_b\vartheta_{b,\nu}=0,\quad
             \max(\|W\|_\infty,\|\alpha\|_\infty,
                           \|\vartheta\|_\infty)\le A_0,\\
 &r\le T_j-W_b\le R,\quad \pi_{j,b}\ge p_*,\quad
                         \sigma_{B_0}(P_1)\ge\sigma_*,\\
 &\min_{b\ne c}\max_{2\le j\le q+2}
       |\pi_{j,b}/\pi_{1,b}-\pi_{j,c}/\pi_{1,c}|\ge\delta,\\
 &\max_bW_b-\min_bW_b\ge\gamma,\quad
                  \min_{b\ne c}\|U_{:b}-U_{:c}\|\ge s_*\}.
\end{split}
\end{equation}
All component and clock inequalities are imposed, and empty classes
are omitted. The weights $\pi$ and matrices $P$ are computed from
\eqref{eq:v83-model} in the centred gauge. The inequalities are
continuous on the stated residual and coefficient bounds, so these
sets are compact. Selected minors and separating combinations are
local tools for proving the inverse theorem, not extra unclosed
choices in the definition of the estimator.

With $d_{\rm perm}$ the Euclidean distance modulo simultaneous
permutation, a precise admissible inverse constant is any upper bound
for
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v85-C0}
 \max\left\{1,\ \max_{B_0}\sup_{d_{\rm perm}(\Xi,\Xi')>0}
   \frac{d_{\rm perm}(\Xi,\Xi')}
        {\max_j\|P_j(\Xi)-P_j(\Xi')\|_F}\right\}.
\end{equation}
The suprema are over the declared classes. Their finiteness follows
from the compact exact inverse proof preceding
Theorem~\ref{thm:v84-mesh}. Thus $C_0$ is a global prior-class
modulus, not a quantity inferred from the finite records or from one
local Jacobian. The new two-arm confidence construction does not
require this constant as input.

For the experiment in Theorem~\ref{thm:v84-mesh}, independence is
required among the $n$ readings in each vertex--clock cell. Its union
bound does not require independence between cells. For the topological
step, the edge permutations and triangular-face relations describe
the covering on the two-skeleton of the triangulation. Cellular
approximation identifies its fundamental group with that of the whole
complex, and covering classification extends the same monodromy over
all higher-dimensional simplices. No further higher-dimensional
cocycle or unobserved permutation relation is being assumed.
