\begin{abstract}
We study the nonreduced Fitting scheme on a Grassmannian on which
multiplication fails for cube-zero algebras with Hilbert function
\((1,4,6)\). The first nilpotent layer reconstructs the ramification
quartic K3 surface together with its quartic polarization. On the
whole Grassmannian the nilpotent algebra is controlled by a canonical
residual-colon filtration. If
\[
 \mathcal J^{\mathrm{res}}=(\mathcal I_{\widehat D}:\mathcal I_{\widehat\Delta}),
 \qquad
 \mathcal L=\mathcal I_{\widehat\Delta}/\mathcal I_{\widehat\Delta}^{\,2},
\]
then
\[
 \mathcal N^j/\mathcal N^{j+1}
 \simeq
 \mathcal L^{\otimes j}\otimes\mathcal O_{W_j}.
\]
Revision 126 upgrades this graded description to a canonical
specialization theorem. The extended Rees algebra
\[
 \mathscr R_{\mathcal N}
 =
 \mathcal O_{\widehat D}[t,\mathcal N t^{-1}]
 \subset
 \mathcal O_{\widehat D}[t,t^{-1}]
\]
is flat over \(\mathbb A^1\); its generic fibre is the original
multiplication-failure scheme and its special fibre is the intrinsic
normal cone
\[
 C_{\widehat\Delta/\widehat D}
 =
 \operatorname{Spec}_{\widehat\Delta}
 \operatorname{gr}_{\mathcal N}\mathcal O_{\widehat D}.
\]
Thus all projection coranks and rank-drop loci occur in one
functorial degeneration rather than as unrelated local normal forms.

Across the first singular Schubert boundary we compute that special
fibre explicitly. On the mixed-regular projection-rank-two locus,
\[
 \mathcal I_{\widehat D}=\delta I_{\mathrm{ct}}\cap\mathfrak m^5.
\]
The vertex factor remains an actual primary component through contact
collisions; a contact of multiplicity \(e\) has generic primary type
\[
 (\delta^2,\delta q^e,q^{e+1}).
\]
If the restricted quartic vanishes identically, the exact local ideal
is \(\delta^2\mathfrak m=(\delta^2)\cap\mathfrak m^5\). Crossing the
first decomposable mixed-kernel wall changes the second intrinsic
depth support to a canonical length-three nonreduced thickening.

The same abstract failure scheme reconstructs the polarized quartic
K3. The classical Reye/nodal-Enriques geometry accounts for the
nine-dimensional source quotient, while the nonclassical theorem is
the intrinsic polarized reconstruction together with the global
Rees/normal-cone specialization and its explicit singular-boundary
algebra. On dense opens the remaining Torelli ambiguity is a finite
etale cover with transitive geometric monodromy.
\end{abstract}
