\subsection{Analytic continuation of a registered boundary germ}

\begin{lemma}[A registered analytic germ determines the connected boundary]
\label{lem:v23-analytic-germ-globalization}
Let \(C,C'\subset\mathbb R^2\) be connected embedded real-analytic strictly
convex closed curves.  Suppose there are points \(p\in C\), \(p'\in C'\) and
an orientation-preserving Euclidean motion \(A\) carrying the oriented point--
tangent frame at \(p\) to that at \(p'\), such that \(A(C)\) and \(C'\)
agree on a nonempty boundary arc containing \(p'\).  Then
\[
                              A(C)=C'.
\]
Equivalently, equality of a nonempty registered analytic boundary germ implies
equality of the complete connected boundary image.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Parametrize \(C\) and \(C'\) by oriented arclength from \(p\) and \(p'\),
respectively.  Their signed curvature functions are real analytic and agree on
a nonempty interval.  By the identity theorem they agree on the maximal common
arclength continuation, hence on the full periodic arclength parameter after
identifying the periods.  The planar Frenet system
\[
 X'=T,\qquad T'=\kappa N,\qquad N'=-\kappa T
\]
has unique solutions from a prescribed oriented point--tangent frame.  Since
\(A\) matches those initial data, the two parametrized curves agree for all
arclength values.  Their images are therefore equal.
\end{proof}
