\subsection{Analytic continuation of a registered boundary germ}

\begin{lemma}[A registered analytic germ determines the connected boundary]
\label{lem:v23-analytic-germ-globalization}
Let \(C,C'\subset\mathbb R^2\) be connected embedded real-analytic strictly
convex closed curves.  Suppose there are points \(p\in C\), \(p'\in C'\) and
an orientation-preserving Euclidean motion \(A\) carrying the oriented
point--tangent frame at \(p\) to that at \(p'\), such that \(A(C)\) and
\(C'\) agree on a nonempty boundary arc containing \(p'\).  Then
\[
                              A(C)=C'.
\]
Equivalently, equality of a nonempty registered analytic boundary germ implies
equality of the complete connected boundary image.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Parametrize \(C\) and \(C'\) by positively oriented arclength from \(p\) and
\(p'\), with lengths \(L\) and \(L'\).  Their positive signed curvature
functions \(\kappa\) and \(\kappa'\) are real analytic.  Equality of the
registered arcs gives equality of the curvature germs at the origins.
Analytic continuation on the universal arclength covers therefore gives one
real-analytic positive function \(\widetilde\kappa:\mathbb R\to(0,\infty)\)
which is both \(L\)-periodic and \(L'\)-periodic and restricts to the two
curvature functions.

Strict convexity gives total turning
\[
 \int_0^L\widetilde\kappa(s)\,ds=2\pi
 =\int_0^{L'}\widetilde\kappa(s)\,ds.
\]
If, say, \(L'<L\), positivity would give
\(\int_{L'}^L\widetilde\kappa>0\), contradicting these two equalities; the
opposite inequality is identical.  Hence \(L=L'\).

Finally the planar Frenet system
\[
 X'=T,\qquad T'=\widetilde\kappa N,\qquad
 N'=-\widetilde\kappa T
\]
has unique solutions for a prescribed oriented point--tangent frame.  The
motion \(A\) matches those initial data, so the two arclength parametrizations
agree on \([0,L]\).  Their complete boundary images are therefore equal.
\end{proof}
