## Classical graph and blow-up input

The Stacks Project, Section 31.33, Tag 01OF, **Blowing up**:
https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01OF

The definition by the Rees algebra, affine ratio charts, compatibility with flat base change, the effective-Cartier-divisor criterion, and the universal property were checked. These are classical inputs. The manuscript does not claim that taking the closure of a rational Hilbert map or removing an invertible ideal factor is a new representability theorem. The invariant coefficient-free graph should also be distinguished from a chosen degree-m determinant ideal. The normalized Rees formula in this revision is proved on the marked surface family, not asserted over every B_a.

## Tropical Grassmannians and coefficient chambers

D. Speyer and B. Sturmfels, **The tropical Grassmannian**, Advances in Geometry 4 (2004), 389–411, DOI 10.1515/advg.2004.023; arXiv:math/0304218.
https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0304218

The full primary paper was retrieved; the definition/equivalent characterizations of tropicalization, Theorem 2.1, its finite Gröbner description, and the Plücker setting were consulted. Actual maximal minors over a valued field satisfy the tropical Plücker relations. This is not a claim that every solution of only the quadratic tropical relations is realizable. Primitive residues contain information that valuations alone omit. The old all-monomial-support arrangement is a possibly redundant coefficient-presentation subdivision, not the tropical Grassmannian itself. In the new marked family, the Newton fan has exactly the rays (i,1), 1 <= i <= a, and the distinct adjacent embedded-curve formulas prove that all these walls are active. General polynomial coordinate changes do not preserve the class of exact monomial arcs; only actual Hilbert graph points have the general covariance statement.

## Comprehensive Gröbner specialization

M. Manubens and A. Montes, **Improving DISPGB algorithm using the discriminant ideal**, Journal of Symbolic Computation 41 (2006), 1245–1263, DOI 10.1016/j.jsc.2005.09.013; arXiv:math/0601763.
https://mat.upc.edu/en/people/antonio.montes/dispgbdiscriminant.pdf

The author-hosted primary PDF was read, especially the introduction and the construction of specialization strata through discriminant information. Comprehensive Gröbner systems organize parameter-dependent ideal bases; the former decorated-minor theorem organizes leading coefficient cancellations for a rational projective map. Neither construction is advertised as a new general Gröbner mechanism. In contrast, the new chain charts have monic Gröbner bases over their whole polynomial parameter rings: no coefficient division or further parameter partition is required. Their standard monomials prove flatness and supply low-degree equations for every boundary curve.

V. Weispfenning, **Comprehensive Gröbner bases**, Journal of Symbolic Computation 14 (1992), 1–29, is credited as foundational background. Bibliographic information was cross-checked through primary citations and official documentation; a complete original-text nonanticipation audit is not claimed.

## Quot and stable-map comparisons

A. Marian, D. Oprea, and R. Pandharipande, **The moduli space of stable quotients**, Geometry & Topology 15 (2011), 1651–1706, DOI 10.2140/gt.2011.15.1651; arXiv:0904.2992.
https://arxiv.org/html/0904.2992v3

The primary text, including the quotient and stability conditions in Section 2 and the properness statement, was consulted. The new paper constructs an actual fixed-source Quot morphism, not merely a verbal distinction of targets. The family O(-a) -> O^3 remains injective on every source fibre and its quotient is flat over S_a. At the origin it is O_{a[1:0]} direct-sum O^2. Hence the map from the chain Hilbert model to the fixed-source Quot scheme contracts its whole exceptional fibre to one quotient, while the Hilbert map separates that fibre. With three fixed markings away from the base point, the stated stable-quotient conditions hold: torsion avoids the markings and the required rational ampleness inequalities are positive. No isomorphism of these compactifications is claimed.

C. Manolache, **Stable maps and stable quotients**, Compositio Mathematica 150 (2014), 1457–1481, DOI 10.1112/S0010437X14007258; arXiv:1301.4393.
https://arxiv.org/html/1301.4393v1

The primary introduction and comparison-space construction were consulted; the published volume/pages were checked against the arXiv journal record. The existence of comparison spaces and virtual-class comparisons prevents any inference that distinct targets admit no comparison. The new statement is narrower and concrete: a reduced nodal source has reduced scheme image, whereas the calculated higher-contact Hilbert limits have a nonzero punctual nilradical. Thus scheme image alone cannot reproduce those limits. The cycle likewise loses the punctual module. This does not rule out comparison spaces or modifications of stable-map/Quot constructions.
