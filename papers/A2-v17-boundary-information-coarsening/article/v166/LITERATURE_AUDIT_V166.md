# Primary-source and documentary audit — A2 v166

Date: 2026-09-26. This record separates sources actually inspected from full-text or independent-audit obligations that remain unfulfilled. It supplements, rather than replaces, the preserved v164 literature audit.

## General horizontal construction

A. Grothendieck, *Techniques de construction et theoremes d'existence en geometrie algebrique IV: les schemas de Hilbert*, Seminaire Bourbaki, exp. 221 (1960/61), pp. 249–276. Publisher archive: https://www.numdam.org/articles/SB_1960-1961__6__249_0/ . The bibliographic record and its erratum indication were inspected. The v166 relative-Hilbert construction uses the classical projective representability theorem; it is explicitly attributed, not claimed as a new general existence theorem. The online inspection in this session was bibliographic, not a new proof audit of the full exposition.

The Stacks Project, Tag 05PS, https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/05PS . The definition of the usual universal flattening functor was inspected. It concerns flatness of the full pulled-back sheaf. The v166 functor instead classifies flat horizontal closed quotients with a schematically dense specified open. The manuscript proves that property directly; it does not conflate the projective horizontal modification with the monomorphism in the usual flattening definition.

## Formal comparison and exactness

B. Bhatt, *Algebraization and Tannaka duality*, arXiv:1404.7483, https://arxiv.org/abs/1404.7483 . The relevant formal-gluing statements in Proposition 5.6(4), Example 5.8, and the surrounding hypotheses were read from the paper. A rendered PDF page was inspected; unsuccessful additional screenshot fetches were not treated as successful visual checks. The application is to flat Noetherian ring-theoretic completion and coherent quotient algebras. It does not invoke a torsion-free-only patching statement or infer existence of descent from faithful flatness alone.

The Stacks Project, Tag 00MI, Lemma 10.99.5, https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00MI . The fibrewise exactness/flatness criterion was inspected and is cited at the Hilbert--Burch application. The manuscript separately proves the necessary height-two assertion at all parameter values and at infinity.

The Stacks Project, Tag 0BJ0, https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BJ0 . The geometrically reduced formal-fibre statement was inspected. The manuscript combines it with flat completion and the injection into the minimal-prime fraction fields to explain reducedness of the completed reduced graph. It does not substitute the word excellent for that argument.

The prior exact references to etale monomorphisms, completed local criteria, proper birational connectedness, and valuation-ring flatness remain in the predecessor bibliography. The new appendix gives the actual algebraic morphisms and normality/Noetherian hypotheses before applying them.

## The square-zero algebra extension

The Stacks Project, Section 91.2, Tag 08S3, including Lemma 91.2.3 and Remark 91.2.4, https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/08S3 . The square-zero extension classification and its presentation by a conormal homomorphism modulo derivations were inspected. The manuscript applies that statement to the actual local ring, computes the quotient N/CN, and globalizes the image of the given algebra class. It does not replace the cotangent complex by the sheaf of differentials or claim the full cotangent complex has length two.

## Degeneration and compactification comparisons

J. Li, *Stable morphisms to singular schemes and relative stable morphisms*, J. Differential Geom. 57 (2001), pp. 509–578, arXiv:math/0009097, https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0009097 . The primary abstract and bibliographic data were inspected. The comparison is at the level of the moduli problem: expanded targets and relative stable maps versus a fixed-target coefficient-indexed Hilbert parameter space. It is not a theorem-by-theorem comparison of the whole paper.

J. Li and B. Wu, *Good degeneration of Quot-schemes and coherent systems*, arXiv:1110.0390, https://arxiv.org/abs/1110.0390 . The primary abstract was inspected. The comparison concerns expanded degeneration and admissibility for coherent systems; no equivalence of those moduli spaces with the present horizontal family is asserted.

Li Li, *Wonderful compactification of an arrangement of subvarieties*, Michigan Math. J. 58 (2009), pp. 535–563, arXiv:math/0611412, https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0611412 . The primary abstract was inspected and the existing repository citation retained. The v166 construction is not claimed to be a wonderful compactification without an arrangement and its requisite hypotheses.

Logarithmic, stable-quotient, quasimap, and expanded frameworks are distinguished explicitly in the introduction, but no new equivalence theorem with those frameworks is claimed. The comparison is deliberately limited to the inspected sources and to definitions that identify the different objects. It is not an exhaustive priority certificate for all compactifications.
