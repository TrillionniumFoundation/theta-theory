\author{Qian Qi}

\hypersetup{pdftitle={Nilpotent depth, stratified contact algebra, and polarized reconstruction in multiplication failure},pdfauthor={Qian Qi}}
\begin{abstract}
We study the nonreduced Fitting scheme on a Grassmannian on which
multiplication fails for cube-zero algebras with Hilbert function
$(1,4,6)$.  The first nilpotent layer reconstructs the ramification
quartic K3 surface together with its quartic polarization.  We determine
the next layers simultaneously across the first singular Schubert
boundary.  On the mixed-regular projection-rank-two locus the failure
ideal has the root-free form
\[
 \mathcal I_{\widehat D}
   =\delta I_{\mathrm{ct}}\cap\mathfrak m^5,
\]
where \(I_{\mathrm{ct}}\) is the saturated unsplit contact ideal on the
rank-one cone.  Its projectivization is the Cartier divisor cut out by
the restricted binary quartic.  Consequently \(\mathfrak m^5\) is the
actual vertex-primary component through all contact collisions, not
merely a globally defined ideal.  If a contact has multiplicity \(e\),
its generic primary type is
\[
 (\delta^2,\delta q^e,q^{e+1});
\]
if the restricted quartic vanishes identically, the exact local ideal
is \(\delta^2\mathfrak m=(\delta^2)\cap\mathfrak m^5\).

The same calculation yields an intrinsic associated-graded algebra:
the first graded nilpotent piece is supported on the unsplit contact
cone and the second on the rank-two Schubert stratum.  Thus the
ramification support on projection corank one and the rank-two support
are specializations inside one nilpotent algebra rather than unrelated
local models.  Crossing the first non-mixed-regular wall, where the
one-dimensional mixed kernel becomes decomposable, the second
annihilator support acquires a canonical length-three nonreduced
structure.  The zero-quartic containment locus is the finite reduced
Fano scheme of lines on the smooth quartic K3.

At every projection rank we retain an exact residual block
presentation of the Fitting ideal; we distinguish this presentation
from the finer structure theorems just stated.  For the K3 family an
exact integral differential witness gives a nine-dimensional polarized
image and generic finite ambiguity.  The classical Reye/nodal-Enriques
geometry accounts for the nine-dimensional source quotient; the
intrinsic contribution is the recovery of the polarized K3 and of the
stratified nilpotent algebra from the abstract multiplication-failure
scheme.
\end{abstract}
