\title[Intrinsic reconstruction from failure schemes]{Intrinsic reconstruction from nonreduced failure schemes}
\author{Qian Qi}
\date{September 24, 2026}
\subjclass[2020]{14M12, 14J28, 13C40, 14B05, 14D20, 05E05, 62R01}
\keywords{Intrinsic reconstruction, pencils and webs of quadrics, Fitting schemes, Schur functors, exterior support, hyperelliptic curves, nilpotent filtration}
\hypersetup{pdftitle={Intrinsic reconstruction from nonreduced failure schemes, revision 142},pdfauthor={Qian Qi}}
\begin{abstract}
We reconstruct every quadratic pencil in dimension \(n\ge3\)
from the abstract \((n^2+2n-4)\)-th neighbourhood of the socle
Grassmannian in its nonreduced multiplication-failure scheme.
Every lower-order neighbourhood is independent of the pencil,
including on the singular-pencil locus. The proof intrinsically
recovers a normal cone, its oriented rank-one ruling, and a residual
coefficient module carrying the full Pluecker line. The first
relation spaces embed the entire pencil parameter scheme and glue
to a flat projective family of actual finite neighbourhoods under
arbitrary complex base change. They recover the spectral Fitting
incidence schemes and realize all Segre-stratum closures. A
rank-preserving specialization distinguishes spectral partitions
missed by the full discriminant and reduced rank loci. Independently,
for every definite real pencil with \(r\) distinct eigenvalues,
we give explicit real data for which all \(2r-3\) reciprocal
likelihood critical points are real and nondegenerate, proving the
assertion of Fevola--Mandelshtam--Sturmfels, Conjecture~4.5. We also
retain the intrinsic reconstruction of smooth-Jacobian four-dimensional
webs, the all-ranks contraction-kernel and exterior-support theorems,
the polarized K3 and hyperelliptic consequences, and the full boundary
and polar-restriction constructions. The restricted exterior contraction
admits an exact Jacobian factorization through the angular Casimir,
yielding its complete singular-value decomposition and a sharp inverse
bound. At fixed discriminant and reduced rank data, finite neighbourhoods
realize and distinguish every dominance-ordered spectral specialization;
their first relation is the degree-\(d\) first graded Betti space
on a marked Hilbert locus.
\end{abstract}
