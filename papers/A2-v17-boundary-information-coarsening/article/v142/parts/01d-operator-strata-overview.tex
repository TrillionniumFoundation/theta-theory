\subsection{An exact operator identity and fixed spectral strata}

The all-ranks contraction theorem has a more precise operator form.
For \(h\in\Sym^2V\), \(q\in\Sym^2V^*\), and \(f\in\Sym^nV\),
Theorem~\ref{thm:mixed-jacobian-contraction} gives
\[
 C_n(h\wedge\kappa_{n,q})(\epsilon\otimes f)
 =\epsilon\otimes\left(\frac4n\mathcal D_{hq}f
 -\frac{2}{n(n-1)}h\Delta_q f\right).
\]
A radical square gives a direct differential proof of the singular
kernel. At full rank, \(h=q^{-1}\) gives the angular Casimir;
its scalar multiple is exactly \(\kappa_{n,q}^*\kappa_{n,q}\)
for the induced Hermitian tensor products.
Theorem~\ref{thm:contraction-singular-values} determines every
singular value, its multiplicity and the Moore--Penrose inverse.
The condition number on the kernel complement is \(\sqrt{2n}\)
for odd \(n\) and \(\sqrt{n-1}\) for even \(n\), in the
specified orthonormal normalization. This identifies the map's
classical differential packaging, not just its source representation.

Theorem~\ref{thm:fixed-spectral-classification} treats the entire
locus with fixed discriminant and reduced rank data. At a root of
multiplicity \(m_i\) and corank \(c_i\), labelled classes are
partitions of \(m_i\) into \(c_i\) parts. Every componentwise
dominance transition is realized over a smooth pointed curve by a
flat family of actual finite failure neighbourhoods. Every strict
transition is invisible below \(d=n^2+2n-4\) and visible at that
order, even after forgetting all markings. Orbit classification and
dominance are classical; the neighbourhood assertions use intrinsic
reconstruction and the universal family.
Proposition~\ref{prop:first-syzygy-hilbert} identifies the first
relation with \(\Tor_1^S(Q_R,\C)_d\) and exhibits the pencil
parameter scheme as a closed locus of a standard graded Hilbert
scheme, retaining its coordinate quotient presentation.
