\subsection{First-moment profiles and differentiated reconstruction}

The limiting law also permits a reduction in smoothing dimension without
a change in the physical record.  For a fixed positive gate $J^2$,
let $M_0,M_1$ be its marginal density in $u$ and its first $v$-moment
profile, and let $N_0,N_1$ be the analogous functions in $v$.
The same accepted pairs give the matrix
\[
 H_f=\mathbb E_f\!\left[\binom{1}{U}(1\ \ V)\right].
\]
The opposite marked slopes of the two half-line actions imply
$\det H_f>0$ on a sufficiently small common gate.  The full limiting
law is then recovered by
\[
       f(u,v)=(M_0(u),M_1(u))H_f^{-1}
                                (N_0(v),N_1(v))^{\mathsf T}.
\]
This is not reconstruction from ordinary marginals alone.  The weighted
profiles use paired coordinates, and all moments use the full success
gate with its original normalization.

\begin{theorem}[Recovery by first-moment profiles]
\label{thm:v74-main}
In the oblique bounded class of Theorem~\ref{thm:v73-main}, the
limiting moment profiles and their matrix determine the same contact
germs and fixed offsets as the complete limiting law.  The charged
counts additionally determine the free area.  On every fixed class and
small common gate as specified in Section~\ref{sec:v74-moments},
the reconstruction of the density from these profiles is Lipschitz
in $C^m$, without loss of derivatives.

Under the same finite forward smoothness assumptions, a measurable
estimator with values in actual table--offset pairs satisfies the
charged estimates of Theorem~\ref{thm:v73-charged-invariant}, with
its smoothing exponents improved to
\[
 \alpha_1=\frac{s}{2(m+s)+1},\qquad
 \gamma_1=\frac{s}{m+s+2},\qquad
 \beta_1=\frac{\alpha_1\omega}{\omega+\alpha_1\Gamma}.
\]
More precisely, under the count, bandwidth and small-error conditions
of Theorem~\ref{thm:v74-charged} and Corollary~\ref{cor:v74-budget},
$rB$ charged resets give local profile and offset error at most $Ct$
and joint local-and-logarithmic-area error at most $Ct\log(e/t)$,
where $t=(L_B/B)^{\beta_1}+\delta^{\gamma_1}$, with probability at
least $1-2\zeta$.  Preparations are normalized unit-speed Liouville
resets and success tags are exact.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Lemma~\ref{lem:v74-skeleton} proves the moment identity and its
$C^m$ stability.  Proposition~\ref{prop:v74-nondegeneracy} derives
its determinant floor from the signed geometric slopes, rather than
postulating a nondegenerate statistical model.  Corollary~\ref{cor:v74-invariant}
then applies the actual smooth inverse and the charged area anchor.
Proposition~\ref{prop:v74-finite-stability} retains the finite-flight
rank defect.  Lemma~\ref{lem:v74-estimation} proves the weighted
one-dimensional kernel bound, and Theorem~\ref{thm:v74-charged}
fits the finite image of actual pairs.  Corollary~\ref{cor:v74-budget}
gives the budget-dependent flight length.
\end{proof}

The low-rank matrix identity is classical in character; the geometric
content here is its forced nondegeneracy, its compatibility with the
relative physical limit, and its use before the smooth contact inverse.
The finite density is not assumed to have exact rank two.  The inherited
normal-incidence two-offset theorem and the two-dimensional estimator
remain separate valid results, and the improved upper bounds do not
assert statistical optimality.
