\section{Signed endpoint rigidity without contact symmetry}
\label{sec:v21-signed-rigidity}

The scalar onset laws of Section~\ref{sec:v9-compatibility} integrate out the
signs of the two endpoint coordinates and therefore retain only a
symmetrized energy profile.  The physical endpoint record contains more
information.  When the two signed endpoint positions are retained, the
support records the two half-line actions before symmetrization.  We prove
below that this determines arbitrary labelled contact jets, odd and even,
and we give the all-order stationary-sequence calculation explicitly.

Fix signed transverse coordinates in the two labelled contact frames.  For
$b\in\{0,1\}$, use an even number of flights and the orientation whose first
and last impacts have type $b$.  Let
$\Pi^{\mathrm{end},\infty}_{bb,d}$ be the limiting conditional law after
residual time has been integrated out.  By
Theorem~\ref{thm:v8-main-relative}, its interior density is proportional to
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v21-signed-endpoint-density}
 (d-S_b(u)-S_b(v))_+B_b(u)B_b(v),
\end{equation}
with $B_b>0$ on a common endpoint box.

\begin{theorem}[Signed-endpoint action and contact rigidity]
\label{thm:v21-signed-rigidity}
Let the two labelled facing contacts be $C^\infty$ and strictly convex.  No
evenness, reflection symmetry, equality of the two contacts, or supplied
contact curvature is assumed.  Suppose the selected physical channel and
its signed endpoint coordinates are retained.

There is $d_*>0$ such that the two families
\[
 \{\Pi^{\mathrm{end},\infty}_{00,d}:0<d<d_*\},\qquad
 \{\Pi^{\mathrm{end},\infty}_{11,d}:0<d<d_*\}
\]
determine the two half-line actions $S_0,S_1$ on a fixed contact
neighborhood.  If physical time is retained, the onset determines the gap
$g$.  Consequently the gap, the two contact curvatures, and every finite jet
of both labelled contact graphs are determined by the signed endpoint laws.

More explicitly, in the fixed signed contact frames write
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v21-general-contact-jets}
 \psi_b(y)=\frac{\kappa_b}{2}y^2+
       \sum_{n\ge3}\frac{q_{b,n}}{n!}y^n,
 \qquad q_n=(q_{0,n},q_{1,n})^t,
\end{equation}
and put
\[
 a_b=S_b''(0),\qquad
 s_n=(S_0^{(n)}(0),S_1^{(n)}(0))^t.
\]
Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v21-leading-recovery}
 \gamma=\operatorname{arsinh}\!\bigl(g\sqrt{a_0a_1}\bigr),\qquad
 c=\cosh\gamma,
 \qquad
 c_0=c\sqrt{a_0/a_1},\quad c_1=c\sqrt{a_1/a_0},
 \qquad
 \kappa_b=\frac{c_b-1}{g}.
\end{equation}
For every $n\ge3$, $s_n$ depends only on the leading geometry and
$q_3,\ldots,q_n$, is affine in $q_n$, and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v21-general-jet-block}
 D_{q_n}s_n=
 M_n:=
 \begin{pmatrix}
 \coth(n\gamma)&\mathfrak r_0^n\csch(n\gamma)\\
 \mathfrak r_1^n\csch(n\gamma)&\coth(n\gamma)
 \end{pmatrix},
 \qquad
 \mathfrak r_b=\sqrt{c_b/c_{1-b}}.
\end{equation}
The determinant of $M_n$ is exactly one.  Hence the graph jets are recovered
recursively at every finite order, with a locally Lipschitz inverse on compact
positive geometric and finite-jet sets.  The differential of the finite-jet
forward map is an isomorphism at every point of those sets.

For real-analytic contacts, equality of the two signed endpoint-law germs as
$d\downarrow0$ and of the physical onset determines both labelled analytic
graph germs.  If the participating obstacle boundaries are connected and
real analytic, their boundary images are then determined in the fixed
contact frames.  For a smooth compact family and every fixed $M$, the same
reconstruction from actual finite even-flight endpoint-support families over
$0<d<d_*$ recovers both graph jets through order $M$ with error
$O_M(\tau^j)$.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{Support thresholds and the leading geometry}

\begin{proof}[Recovery of the actions and the leading geometry]
Since $B_b$ is positive, the interior support of
\eqref{eq:v21-signed-endpoint-density} is exactly
\[
 \{(u,v):S_b(u)+S_b(v)<d\}.
\]
Define
\[
 T_b(u,v)=\inf\{d>0:(u,v)\in
       \operatorname{int}\operatorname{supp}
       \Pi^{\mathrm{end},\infty}_{bb,d}\}.
\]
Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v21-support-threshold}
 T_b(u,v)=S_b(u)+S_b(v),\qquad S_b(u)=T_b(u,0),
\end{equation}
because $S_b(0)=0$.  Thus the signed support recovers the full action, not
only its even pushforward.

For a selected itinerary the infimum of physical times at which the
near-onset event has nonzero probability is $jg$, so the time axis gives
$g$.  The half-line Hessian identities give
\[
 a_b=\frac{c\sinh\gamma}{gc_{1-b}},
 \qquad c=\sqrt{c_0c_1}=\cosh\gamma.
\]
Therefore
\[
 a_0a_1=\frac{\sinh^2\gamma}{g^2},
 \qquad \frac{a_0}{a_1}=\frac{c_0}{c_1},
\]
which is equivalent to \eqref{eq:v21-leading-recovery}.  In particular no
contact curvature is supplied as input.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Weighted stationary equations and their differentiated inverse}

We now prove the all-order statement in a form independent of the inverse
conclusion.  Let $\ell_b(y,z)$ denote the local flight length from a point of
type $b$ with transverse coordinate $y$ to the facing point of type $1-b$
with coordinate $z$.  For a half-line beginning at type $b$, write
$x_0=u$ and impose, for $i\ge1$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v21-stationary-equations}
 \mathscr F_{b,i}(x,u;q)
 :=\partial_2\ell_{b+i-1}(x_{i-1},x_i)
   +\partial_1\ell_{b+i}(x_i,x_{i+1})=0,
\end{equation}
where contact types are read modulo two.  Choose
$\rho$ with $e^{-\gamma}<\rho<1$ and set
\[
 X_\rho=\left\{x=(x_i)_{i\ge1}:
        \|x\|_\rho:=\sup_{i\ge1}\rho^{-i}|x_i|<\infty\right\}.
\]

\begin{proposition}[All-order stationary-sequence calculus]
\label{prop:v21-stationary-calculus}
On a sufficiently small common contact neighborhood the map
\eqref{eq:v21-stationary-equations} defines a unique smooth stationary
half-line $x^{(b)}(u;q)\in X_\rho$.  At $(u,x)=(0,0)$ its derivative in the
interior variables is the positive Jacobi operator $H_b$, whose inverse has
kernel \eqref{eq:v4-half-green}.  The inverse is bounded on $X_\rho$.
For every finite endpoint derivative order $N$, all derivatives
$\partial_u^k x^{(b)}$ with $k\le N$ obey uniform $X_\rho$ bounds on compact
positive geometric and finite-jet sets.

Moreover, the coefficient $S_b^{(n)}(0)$ depends only on the graph jets of
orders at most $n$.  Holding all lower graph jets fixed, the first variation
with respect to $q_{r,n}$ is determined entirely by the linear half-line
orbit; variation of the stationary orbit contributes no degree-$n$ term to
that first variation.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The first statement is the weighted implicit-function argument underlying
Lemma~\ref{lem:v4-halfline}, which we recall because it is the operator used
at every jet order.  The quadratic interior Hessian has diagonal entries
$2c_{(b+i)\bmod2}/g$ and adjacent entries $-1/g$.  Its inverse is
\[
 G^{(b)}_{ik}=\frac{g\sigma_i^{(b)}\sigma_k^{(b)}}
 {2c\sinh\gamma}
 \left(e^{-\gamma|i-k|}-e^{-\gamma(i+k)}\right),
 \qquad i,k\ge1,
\]
which is bounded on $X_\rho$ because $e^{-\gamma}<\rho<1$ leaves a strict
exponential margin.  The nonlinear remainder in
\eqref{eq:v21-stationary-equations} is local, vanishes quadratically, and
has derivative $O(|u|+\|x\|_\rho)$.  The contraction/implicit-function
argument therefore gives a unique small stationary sequence.

Differentiate \eqref{eq:v21-stationary-equations} $k$ times in $u$.  At each
stage the highest derivative $\partial_u^kx$ is multiplied by the same
invertible operator obtained by linearizing the stationarity equations along
the already constructed small orbit.  All remaining terms contain only
lower orbit derivatives and local graph derivatives of order at most $k$.
The inverse retains a uniform $X_\rho$ bound after shrinking the common box.
Induction gives the asserted weighted estimates and shows that the action jet
of order $n$ cannot involve a graph derivative of order greater than $n$.

For the last assertion, write the stationary action as
\[
 \mathscr A_b(u,q)=\sum_{i\ge0}
 [\ell_{b+i}(x_i^{(b)},x_{i+1}^{(b)})-g].
\]
The differentiated sums are absolutely convergent by the weighted bounds.
If $q_{r,n}$ varies while all lower graph jets are fixed, stationarity gives
the envelope identity
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v21-envelope}
 \partial_{q_{r,n}}S_b(u)
 =\sum_{i\ge0}
 \partial_{q_{r,n}}\ell_{b+i}
       (x_i^{(b)}(u),x_{i+1}^{(b)}(u)).
\end{equation}
No derivative of $x^{(b)}$ appears in the first variation because all
interior action derivatives vanish and the tail boundary term tends to zero.
For the graph representation
$(-\psi_0(y),y)$, $(g+\psi_1(z),z)$, differentiation with respect to an
$n$th graph jet has leading homogeneous term $y^n/n!$ or $z^n/n!$ in the
length, since the horizontal separation divided by the flight length equals
one at the contact and differs from one only by positive endpoint degree.
The linear part of $x_i^{(b)}(u)$ depends only on the quadratic geometry.
Thus the coefficient of $u^n$ in \eqref{eq:v21-envelope} is obtained by
substituting the linear orbit, and lower nonlinear orbit terms cannot change
that degree.  This proves the claim.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The determinant-one all-order block}

\begin{proposition}[Last-jet block at arbitrary degree]
\label{prop:v21-last-jet-block}
For every $n\ge3$, after the lower graph jets are fixed, $s_n$ is affine in
$q_n$ and its derivative is the matrix $M_n$ in
\eqref{eq:v21-general-jet-block}.  In particular
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v21-last-jet-det}
                         \det M_n=1.
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The linear half-line orbit beginning at type $b$ is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v21-linear-orbit}
 x_i^{(b)}(u)=t_i^{(b)}u+O(u^2\rho^i),
 \qquad
 t_i^{(b)}=
 \begin{cases}
 \varrho^i,&i\ \mathrm{even},\\
 \mathfrak r_b\varrho^i,&i\ \mathrm{odd},
 \end{cases}
 \qquad \varrho=e^{-\gamma}.
\end{equation}
By Proposition~\ref{prop:v21-stationary-calculus}, the degree-$n$ first
variation is the pure endpoint-power sum on this linear orbit.  A boundary
site is counted once and every interior site twice.  Varying the $n$th jet of
the starting contact therefore gives
\begin{align}
 \partial_{q_{b,n}}S_b^{(n)}(0)
 &=1+2\sum_{k\ge1}\varrho^{2nk}
   =\frac{1+e^{-2n\gamma}}{1-e^{-2n\gamma}}
   =\coth(n\gamma),                         \label{eq:v21-own-sum}
\end{align}
whereas varying the opposite contact gives
\begin{align}
 \partial_{q_{1-b,n}}S_b^{(n)}(0)
 &=2\mathfrak r_b^n\sum_{k\ge0}
     \varrho^{n(2k+1)}
   =\mathfrak r_b^n\csch(n\gamma).          \label{eq:v21-cross-sum}
\end{align}
Every sum is absolutely convergent, with all multiplicities displayed.
Interchanging the labels gives the second row of
\eqref{eq:v21-general-jet-block}.  Since
$\mathfrak r_0\mathfrak r_1=1$,
\[
 \det M_n
 =\coth^2(n\gamma)
  -(\mathfrak r_0\mathfrak r_1)^n\csch^2(n\gamma)
 =\coth^2(n\gamma)-\csch^2(n\gamma)=1.
\]
The dependence on $q_n$ is affine because no product containing two
occurrences of a degree-$n$ graph term can contribute to endpoint degree
$n$, while all other degree-$n$ terms depend only on lower graph jets.
\end{proof}

The inverse block is therefore
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v21-general-recursion}
 M_n^{-1}=
 \begin{pmatrix}
 \coth(n\gamma)&-\mathfrak r_0^n\csch(n\gamma)\\
 -\mathfrak r_1^n\csch(n\gamma)&\coth(n\gamma)
 \end{pmatrix}.
\end{equation}
If $R_n$ denotes the contribution of already reconstructed lower jets, then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v21-recursion}
                         q_n=M_n^{-1}(s_n-R_n).
\end{equation}
On every positive compact geometric set and for every fixed maximal order
$M$, the coefficients in this recursion and all of their derivatives are
uniformly bounded.  This gives the finite-order local Lipschitz inverse.

\begin{proposition}[Tangent injectivity of the finite signed-jet map]
\label{prop:v21-tangent-jet-isomorphism}
Fix $g>0$ and $M\ge2$.  Let
\[
 \mathscr A_M:
 (\kappa_0,\kappa_1,q_3,\ldots,q_M)
 \longmapsto (s_2,s_3,\ldots,s_M),
 \qquad s_2=(a_0,a_1)^t.
\]
Then $D\mathscr A_M$ is an isomorphism at every strictly convex labelled
contact jet.  On a compact positive geometric and finite-jet set there is
$c_M>0$ such that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v21-tangent-lower-bound}
 \|D\mathscr A_M[h]\|\ge c_M\|h\|.
\end{equation}
In particular, if $D S_0[h]=D S_1[h]=0$ as germs through order $M$, then
$h=0$ in the finite $M$-jet chart.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The explicit formula \eqref{eq:v21-leading-recovery} is a smooth inverse of
the forward map
$(\kappa_0,\kappa_1)\mapsto(a_0,a_1)$ at fixed $g$.  Differentiating the
identity between these maps shows that the first $2\times2$ diagonal block
of $D\mathscr A_M$ is invertible.  For each $n\ge3$, Proposition
\ref{prop:v21-last-jet-block} shows that the new pair $q_n$ enters $s_n$
through the diagonal block $M_n$ with determinant one, while dependence on
lower jets contributes only blocks strictly below the diagonal.  Thus
$D\mathscr A_M$ is block lower triangular with invertible diagonal blocks.
It is therefore an isomorphism.

Equivalently, if $D\mathscr A_M[h]=0$, the $s_2$ equations first give
$h_{\kappa_0}=h_{\kappa_1}=0$.  Assuming inductively that all components
through order $n-1$ vanish, the $s_n$ equation reduces to
$M_nh_{q_n}=0$, hence $h_{q_n}=0$.  The quantitative bound follows by
continuity of the least singular value on a compact set.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Completion of Theorem~\ref{thm:v21-signed-rigidity}]
The support and leading-geometry steps were proved above.  Propositions
\ref{prop:v21-stationary-calculus} and
\ref{prop:v21-last-jet-block} give the all-order recursion
\eqref{eq:v21-recursion}; Proposition
\ref{prop:v21-tangent-jet-isomorphism} gives the stated tangent-level
inverse as well as the quantitative finite-dimensional lower bound.

For analytic contacts, equality of all recovered signed jets gives equality
of the convergent graph germs.  Analytic continuation along a connected
analytic boundary and uniqueness for the planar Frenet equations then give
the stated boundary-image conclusion, as in
Corollary~\ref{cor:v12-analytic-boundaries}.

For the actual finite even-flight law, positivity of the physical flux makes
its endpoint support threshold the exact stationary excess action
$E_{j,b}(u,v)$.  The relative estimate \eqref{eq:v8-relative} gives
\[
 \|E_{j,b}-(S_b\oplus S_b)\|_{C^M}\le C_M\tau^j.
\]
Taking the slice $v=0$ and applying the finite-order locally Lipschitz inverse
proves the $O_M(\tau^j)$ reconstruction claim.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Coordinate dependence and geometry]
Odd graph jets depend on the chosen oriented contact frames, as they must:
reversing a transverse coordinate changes their signs.  The theorem is
therefore a labelled fixed-frame inverse statement.  Its geometric content
is invariant under applying the same known Euclidean registration to the
observed channel and to the recovered graphs.  Section
\ref{sec:v21-raw-physical} makes the stronger statistical requirement
precise by fixing one laboratory chart for the entire local experiment.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Why the scalar profile remains useful]
Theorem~\ref{thm:v21-signed-rigidity} does not invalidate the smooth profile
inverse of Section~\ref{sec:v9-compatibility}.  The two results concern
different coarsenings of the same record.  Integrating the signed endpoints
produces the symmetrized profile and forgets the odd branch displacement;
retaining the endpoint coordinates recovers the unsymmetrized action support
and hence the full labelled local jets.
\end{remark}
