\begin{abstract}
We determine embedded primary structures in multiplication failure
for finite algebras. For universal quadratic multiplication tensors
of Hilbert function $(1,e,q)$, with $q\le\binom e2$, we give an
irredundant primary decomposition on the projection-corank-one open
where products with the projected hyperplane span the quadratic
layer. Every embedded support is a quadratic restriction-rank
stratum; we determine its symbolic thickness, normality, a projective
resolution, and the exact local nilradical index. The proof identifies
the full Fitting ideal with a weighted determinantal ideal and
computes its primary flag coefficient by coefficient. A family of
Hilbert function $(1,3,3)$ has a smooth embedded support which is a
rank-four bundle over an elliptic curve times a projective plane.
Its nilradical is an invertible sheaf on that support with square
zero, while the reduced failure divisor stays fixed and the elliptic
$j$-invariant varies. We also retain the complete primary classification
for Hilbert function $(1,2,2)$, including its extreme-corank locus,
and relate the primary strata to conductor kernels. For general
finite locally free algebras, products of a unital codimension-$r$
subbundle over a $\mathbb Q$-scheme stabilize in degree $r+1$,
sharply in every codimension and generating rank. Relative conductor
comparisons identify finite-algebra and global section-multiplication
cokernels under explicit regularity hypotheses, with arbitrary base
change in their stated range.
\end{abstract}
