# Literature audit for v121

## New primary texts actually inspected

1. W. Bruns, T. Römer, A. Wiebe, *Initial algebras of determinantal rings, Cohen–Macaulay and Ulrich ideals*, Michigan Math. J. 53 (2005), 71–81. Author-hosted PDF: https://www.home.uni-osnabrueck.de/wbruns/brunsw/pdf-article/BrRoeWie.published.pdf . Theorem 3.5(c), printed p.75, states that a generic determinantal quotient is a normal domain and Cohen–Macaulay, with the indicated singularity refinements. We use only domain/normality. The weighted t-primary intersection and its irredundancy are proved separately in v121.

2. M. Artebani, I. Dolgachev, *The Hesse pencil of plane cubic curves*, Enseign. Math. (2) 55 (2009), 235–273. Publisher PDF: https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/44190 . Section 2, printed pp.239–241, especially equations (4)–(5) on p.240, was inspected in text and page images. It supplies Weierstrass coefficients and the classical elliptic moduli calculation. Substituting (u0,u1)=(1,-k/2) gives the standard j formula used in the paper. We do not claim the Hesse pencil or generic determinantal normality as new.

## Retained generator and length comparisons

The v120 audit remains at `../v120/LITERATURE_AUDIT.md` and its theorem-level discussion remains in the article. Generator-scheme representability and maximal-minor constructions are credited to Arpin–Bozlee–Herr–Smith in the identified preprint version. No claim is made that a field-level algebra-length estimate by itself proves equality of coefficient-ring image modules or Fitting ideals. The coefficient-ring stabilization proof, its sharpness family, and the relative comparison are all retained.

The v121 identity should also be evaluated against the wider literature on determinantal symbolic powers. Its proof does not require an unverified general symbolic-power formula, and the paper does not claim that primary algebra alone establishes exhaustive novelty. The mathematical contribution offered for review is the connection of the full multiplication ideal to the uniform primary flag and the resulting non-isotrivial embedded geometry.
