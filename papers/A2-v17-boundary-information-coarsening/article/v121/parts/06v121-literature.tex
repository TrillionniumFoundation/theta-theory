\subsection{Determinantal inputs and the elliptic family}
\label{sec:v121-literature}
The primality and normality of a generic minor ideal are classical;
we use \cite[Theorem 3.5(c)]{BRW05}. We also use the elementary
rank-stratum codimension count and prove the needed symbolic-order
inclusion by a Schur complement. The weighted ideal in
Theorem~\ref{thm:weighted-primary} is not offered as a new theory
of generic determinantal varieties. Its contribution here is the
explicit primary intersection for the full multiplication ideal,
including the extra linear determinant factor, and its use for
the universal restriction flag. The incidence resolution is the
usual image-subspace construction, applied to the specific embedded
supports and with its fibres proved in
Theorem~\ref{thm:flag-resolution}.

The geometry of the Hesse pencil is also classical. We use the
Weierstrass coefficients in \cite[Section 2]{AD09} to normalize
the $j$-invariant. We do not claim a new classification of plane
cubics. The additional statements are the explicit finite algebra
\eqref{eq:elliptic-algebra}, the full failure ideal on $U$, the
square-zero line module \eqref{eq:elliptic-nilradical}, and the
realization of the varying elliptic curves as the bases of its
embedded supports. Those statements are derived from multiplication
in the algebra and not from a resemblance between reduced equations.

The distinction from generator representability and the elementary
field-level algebra-length bound is explained in
Section~\ref{sec:v120-literature-position}. In particular a
field-level dimension bound does not assert equality of image
submodules over a nonreduced coefficient ring or identify their
Fitting ideals there; the coefficient-ring argument and sharpness
construction are provided in full in this article.

The full text of \cite{Ballico93} has not been obtained for a
theorem-by-theorem comparison. Its bibliographic record and publisher
reference listing are not sufficient evidence to affirm or deny
anticipation of any theorem here. No exhaustive priority conclusion
with respect to that article is asserted. This documentary question
is separate from the proofs above and remains explicitly recorded
in the accompanying response and literature audit.
