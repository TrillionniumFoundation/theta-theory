\section{Primary flags and elliptic boundaries}
\label{sec:v121-introduction}
An embedded component of a multiplication-failure scheme can carry
moduli which disappear after taking the reduced support. We prove this
by a uniform primary decomposition over the parameter space of quadratic
multiplication tensors, and by a family in which the embedded support
is a vector bundle over an elliptic curve times a projective plane.
The reduced failure divisor is independent of the elliptic parameter.

Here and in Sections~\ref{sec:weighted-flags}--\ref{sec:elliptic-boundary},
the ground field is $\C$. Let $V,S$ have dimensions $e,q$, and let
$\gamma:\Sym^2 V\twoheadrightarrow S$. The augmented algebra
$B_\gamma=\C\oplus V\oplus S$ has multiplication $\gamma$ in degree two
and zero products involving $S$ and $V\oplus S$.
For $K\in\Gr(e,V\oplus S)$ set $W=\C\oplus K$.
The scheme $D_\gamma$ is defined by the zeroth Fitting ideal of the
cokernel of $\Sym^m W\to B_\gamma$; it is independent of $m\ge2$.
These are unital planes. They contain a unit and hence generate the
corresponding sheaf, but need not generate $B_\gamma$ as an algebra.
This distinction is essential at a failure point.

Put $b=e-1$, $p=\binom e2$, and suppose $1\le q\le p$.
Allow $\gamma$ to vary over all surjections. On the projection-corank-one
locus write $H=\operatorname{im}(K\to V)$ and impose the open condition
$\gamma(HV)=S$. If $\Delta$ denotes the projection determinant divisor
and $Z_j\subset\Delta$ is defined by
$\rank(\gamma|_{\Sym^2 H})<j$, our primary flag theorem gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v121-intro-flag}
 \mathcal I_D=\mathcal I_\Delta\cap
 \bigcap_{j=\max\{1,q-e+2\}}^q
             \mathcal I_{Z_j}^{(q-j+2)}.
\end{equation}
The intersection is irredundant; these are exactly its associated
supports. The superscript denotes a symbolic power in the smooth
ambient parameter space. The reduced divisor has multiplicity one.
At a point where the restricted quadratic map has rank $a$, the
nilradical has exact index $q-a+1$. The supports $Z_j$ are normal,
and their natural Grassmannian incidences are projective resolutions
with explicitly determined fibres
(Theorems~\ref{thm:universal-primary-flag} and
\ref{thm:flag-resolution}). Thus the result is a primary classification
on a specified universal open set, in arbitrary embedding dimension;
it is not an assertion about unspecified specializations or about the
omitted higher projection-corank strata.

The underlying algebra is particularly simple. For a generic $q$ by
$p$ matrix $A$, introduce one independent parameter $t$. We prove
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v121-intro-weighted}
 t\sum_{i=0}^q t^{q-i}I_i(A)
   =(t)\cap\bigcap_{j=1}^q(t,I_j(A))^{(q-j+2)}.
\end{equation}
A coefficientwise symbolic-order argument gives both equality and
explicit witnesses to irredundancy. The geometric input identifies
the full multiplication ideal with the left side, not merely with
its radical. The normality of generic determinantal supports is a
classical input, not a new assertion of this paper.

For $e=q=3$, the family
\[
 B_\lambda=\C[x,y,z]/\bigl((x,y,z)^3,
          xy-\lambda z^2,yz-\lambda x^2,zx-\lambda y^2\bigr)
\]
has Hilbert function $(1,3,3)$. For an explicit nonempty open set of
parameters, its full failure ideal on the entire projection-corank-at-most-one
open is
\[
 \mathcal I_{D_\lambda}=\mathcal I_\Delta\cap\mathcal I_{E_\lambda}^2,
 \qquad
 0\longrightarrow\mathcal O_{E_\lambda}(-\Delta)
 \longrightarrow\mathcal O_{D_\lambda}
 \longrightarrow\mathcal O_\Delta\longrightarrow0.
\]
Here $E_\lambda$ is smooth and normal, and is the total space of a
rank-four bundle over $C_\lambda\times\Pj^2$, where
\[
 C_\lambda:\quad
 \lambda(a^3+b^3+c^3)+(1-4\lambda^3)abc=0.
\]
The curves $C_\lambda$ have nonconstant $j$-invariant. In particular,
this is a positive-moduli family of algebras and embedded primary
supports, not two orbit types presented with a redundant parameter.
The nilradical is an invertible sheaf on $E_\lambda$ and has square
zero. A smooth projective compactification of $E_\lambda$ has
Albanese variety $C_\lambda$. The conductor has constant rank one
on this boundary, while the quadratic restriction retains the elliptic
moduli (Theorem~\ref{thm:elliptic-boundary}).

There are three distinct scopes in the article. The coefficient-ring
stabilization and conductor comparison apply to general finite locally
free algebras. The quadratic factorization applies to split augmented
algebras with cube-zero augmentation ideal. The primary flag theorem
classifies their universal first-order-spanning corank-one open;
the $(1,2,2)$ theorem, by contrast, treats that entire fixed
Hilbert-function class, including its extreme-corank locus. We retain
the complete latter theorem and the higher-contact, relative-incidence,
and global section-multiplication results. Their hypotheses are not
replaced by the new open conditions.

The article is self-contained as an algebraic-geometric contribution.
No theorem from a paper numbered A1, or an unverified application in a
later paper, is part of its significance argument. Literature inputs
and the precise remaining full-text comparison are separated in
Section~\ref{sec:v121-literature}. The accompanying complete edition
retains all complementary geometry and statistical proofs; these are
not premises of the primary flag theorem.
