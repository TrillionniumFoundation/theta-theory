\begin{abstract}
We determine absolute generating actions from projective endpoint records
with unknown branch-selective detection. Two independent, uncalibrated
readouts give finite matrices whose channels may depend on the endpoint.
For detectors with unknown branchwise log-affine dependence on the
deadline, five observations determine the visible component count,
absolute actions, channels, relative weights and relative detector rates.
The only continuous ambiguity is a common weight and exponential factor.
The inverse is locally Lipschitz without loss of spatial derivatives;
actions may coincide when their detector rates distinguish the components.
The proof combines observable joint eigenspaces with a five-value rigidity
lemma for logarithmic ratios. We give finite-degree polynomial extensions,
a three-clock ambiguity, and the exact equal-action obstruction.
Unpaired regular sheets recover exact return dynamics on covered domains.
A nonconvex three-sheet return system realizes the hypotheses through an
action crossing, with a fixed source and a physical survival detector.
The full revision retains the conditional, calibrated, convex and marked
billiard developments as companion observation regimes.
\end{abstract}
