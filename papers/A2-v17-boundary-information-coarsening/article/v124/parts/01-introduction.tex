\section{Introduction and statements}

Failure of a multiplication map is usually recorded by a reduced
degeneracy locus.  The examples studied here have a different feature:
the successive nilpotent layers of the failure scheme recover
geometric data that the reduction forgets.  In the quartic case the
first layer determines a polarized K3 surface; the next layer detects
the first higher-corank Schubert stratum.  The two phenomena are
therefore not separate decorations of the same divisor but consecutive
terms of one intrinsic filtration.

Let $V$ be a complex vector space of dimension $e\in\{3,4\}$ and put
$p=e(e-1)/2$.  For an $e$-dimensional relation space
$R\subset\Sym^2V$, set
\[
 S_R=\Sym^2V/R,\qquad B_R=\C\oplus V\oplus S_R,
\]
where multiplication on $V$ is the quotient map and
$(V\oplus S_R)S_R=0$.  On
$X_R=\Gr(e,V\oplus S_R)$, with tautological subbundle $\mathcal K$,
we consider the scheme
\[
 \widehat D_R=
 V\!\left(\Fitt_0\coker[
 \Sym^m(\OO\oplus\mathcal K)\longrightarrow B_R\otimes\OO]\right),
 \qquad m\ge2.
\]
The scheme is independent of $m$.  Its reduction is the Schubert
divisor on which $\mathcal K\to V$ drops rank.

Regard $R$ as an $e$-dimensional system of quadrics on $\Pj(V^*)$.
On the nonempty invariant open $G_e^\circ$ used below the system is
basepoint-free and the Jacobian hypersurface
\[
 Y_R=V\!\left(\det(\partial q_i/\partial x_j)\right)
 \subset\Pj(V^*)
\]
is smooth.  It is a genus-one curve for $e=3$ and a quartic K3
surface for $e=4$.

Let $U_R\subset X_R$ be the projection-corank-at-most-one open,
$D_R=\widehat D_R\cap U_R$, and let $\NN_R$ be the nilradical of
$\OO_{D_R}$.  Its annihilator defines a smooth intrinsic scheme
$E_R\subset D_R$.  The first main theorem from the preceding revision
is retained verbatim in proof and strengthened here by placing it in
the global depth filtration.

\begin{theorem}[Polarized reconstruction]\label{thm:main-reconstruction}
For $R\in G_e^\circ$, put
\[
 \LL_R=\omega_{E_R}\otimes\NN_R^{\otimes-(p+e-1)}.
\]
There is an isomorphism of graded algebras
\[
 \bigoplus_{n\ge0}H^0(E_R,\LL_R^{\otimes n})
 \simeq
 \bigoplus_{n\ge0}H^0(Y_R,\OO_{Y_R}(en)).
\]
Consequently $D_R$ determines $(Y_R,\OO_{Y_R}(e))$.  For $e=4$
it determines $(Y_R,\OO_{Y_R}(1))$.  The same conclusions hold with
$\widehat D_R$ in place of $D_R$, and no isomorphism is required to
preserve the ambient Grassmannian, the relation space, or any
projection.
\end{theorem}

The new global organizing invariant is defined without a choice of
primary decomposition.  If $\widehat\NN_R$ is the nilradical of
$\OO_{\widehat D_R}$, set
\[
 \ZZ_j(\widehat D_R)
   =V\!\left(\Ann_{\OO_{\widehat D_R}}
                    (\widehat\NN_R^{\,j})\right)
 \qquad(j\ge1).
\]
These schemes are preserved by every abstract isomorphism of
$\widehat D_R$.

\begin{theorem}[Intrinsic nilpotent-depth filtration]
\label{thm:main-depth}
Let $R\in G_4^\circ$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item On the intrinsic smooth locus of
$(\widehat D_R)_{\mathrm{red}}$,
\[
 \ZZ_1(\widehat D_R)=E_R,
 \qquad
 \widehat\NN_R|_{E_R}\simeq\OO_{E_R}(-\widehat\Delta_R),
\]
and Theorem~\ref{thm:main-reconstruction} reconstructs the polarized
quartic K3 from this first depth layer.
\item At every projection-rank-two point there is an exact residual
Fitting presentation depending only on the three multiplication blocks
along its image plane; it is displayed in
Theorem~\ref{thm:ranktwo-full-presentation}.
\item On the mixed-regular rank-two locus of
Definition~\ref{def:mixed-regular}, the nilradical has exact index
three and
\[
 \ZZ_2(\widehat D_R)=\Sigma_R,
 \qquad
 \widehat\NN_R^{\,2}|_{\Sigma_R}
   \simeq\II_{\widehat\Delta_R}^{\,2}|_{\Sigma_R},
\]
where $\Sigma_R$ is the rank-two Schubert stratum.  This remains true
when the four ramification contacts collide.  Over the complement of
their discriminant, the four local primary branches are precisely the
etale-local splitting of a canonical degree-four contact cover.
\end{enumerate}
Thus polarized reconstruction and the first higher-corank embedded
support are consecutive functorial stages of one nonreduced
multiplication invariant.
\end{theorem}

The exact proof of the corank-one statement is the normal form
$t(t,f)$ of \S\ref{sec:fitting}; the rank-two assertions are proved
in \S\ref{sec:ranktwo-boundary}.  No claim about an unproved primary
decomposition on the rank-one-kernel or mixed-rank-drop boundary is
hidden in the theorem: the exact residual Fitting presentation remains
valid there, while the depth-three normal form is stated on the
precisely defined mixed-regular locus.

The polarized K3s vary.  We retain the exact calculation of the
preceding revision because it proves the statement for the actual
Jacobian quartics rather than for a proxy parameter space.

\begin{theorem}[Variation and finite ambiguity]\label{thm:main-moduli}
For $e=4$, the image of
$R\mapsto(Y_R,\OO_{Y_R}(1))$ in quartic K3 moduli has dimension nine.
On a nonempty invariant open, the induced map from projective
equivalence classes of relation spaces to its image is generically
finite.  Thus, over a general point of this image, $D_R$ and
$\widehat D_R$ determine at most finitely many isomorphism classes
of algebras $B_R$.
\end{theorem}

Section~\ref{sec:moduli} proves this by an exact rank-$24$ differential
witness for the embedded quartic map.  Section~\ref{sec:reye-priority}
then identifies the classical geometric reason that a nine-dimensional
quotient is expected: regular webs produce the Reye/nodal-Enriques
locus.  The number nine is therefore not used as a novelty claim.
What is new in the present theorem is that the same polarized point is
recovered \emph{from the abstract nonreduced multiplication-failure
scheme}.  The finite fibre is left as an honest finite Torelli packet;
we do not replace a proved finite statement by an unsupported
degree-one assertion.

We also retain the full fixed-tensor calculation on the simple-contact
open.

\begin{theorem}[Corank-two primary structure on the simple-contact
open]\label{thm:main-coranktwo}
There is a nonempty invariant open $G_4^\dagger\subset G_4^\circ$
such that, for each $R\in G_4^\dagger$, a dense open set of
rank-two images $H\subset V$ has the following property.  Near every
plane with image $H$, after an etale extension and smooth auxiliary
coordinates,
\[
 \II_{\widehat D_R}
 =(\delta)\cap P_1^2\cap P_2^2\cap P_3^2\cap P_4^2\cap\mathfrak m^5,
 \quad
 \delta=ad-bc,\quad\mathfrak m=(a,b,c,d).
\]
The rank-two stratum is an additional associated support,
$\widehat\NN_R^3=0\ne\widehat\NN_R^2$, and
$\Ann(\widehat\NN_R^2)$ is the ideal of that stratum.
\end{theorem}

The extension in \S\ref{sec:ranktwo-boundary} removes the simple-root
hypothesis from the nilpotent-depth conclusion, identifies the contact
discriminant globally, and records an exact presentation at every
rank-two point.  The simple-root hypothesis is needed only to split
the unsplit contact ideal into four separately labelled primary
branches.

\subsection*{Priority boundary}

The incidence K3, its free involution, and the Reye congruence attached
to a regular web of quadrics are classical.  We compare them directly
with Cossec, Arrondo--Sols, Dolgachev--Reider, Ingalls--Kuznetsov and
the modern Enriques treatment in
\S\ref{sec:reye-priority}.  We also compare the functorial annihilator
filtration with the classical theory of ribbons and multiple
structures.  These comparisons sharpen rather than weaken the main
claim: the theorem resides in the \emph{nonreduced Fitting scheme of
multiplication} and in the polarized geometry reconstructed from its
nilpotent layers.

Ballico's 1993 paper remains the nearest title-level predecessor in
the failure-locus literature.  Its complete theorem text was not
available in the present source audit, so no non-anticipation statement
about that inaccessible text is asserted.  We instead provide a
theorem-level comparison with Ballico's accessible 1996 continuation,
which explicitly treats failure of multiplication maps for projective
embeddings and linear sections.  The distinction from the present
cube-zero Fitting scheme is stated in
\S\ref{sec:reye-priority} and in the accompanying literature audit.
