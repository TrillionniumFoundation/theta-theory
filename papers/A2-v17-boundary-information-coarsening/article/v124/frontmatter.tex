\author{Qian Qi}

\keywords{Multiplication failure, nilpotent filtration, Reye congruence, K3 surface, primary decomposition, intrinsic polarization}
\hypersetup{pdftitle={Intrinsic nilpotent depth, Reye geometry, and higher-corank structure in multiplication failure},pdfauthor={Qian Qi}}
\begin{abstract}
We study the nonreduced scheme on a Grassmannian on which multiplication
fails for cube-zero algebras with Hilbert function $(1,4,6)$.  The
nilpotent structure has a functorial depth filtration: the annihilator
of the nilradical recovers the ramification support on projection
corank one, while the annihilator of its square recovers the rank-two
Schubert support on the mixed-regular projection-corank-two locus.
The first stage determines the ramification quartic K3 surface together
with its quartic polarization by an intrinsic section-ring construction.
The second stage is governed by an exact fixed-tensor residual Fitting
presentation valid at every projection-rank-two point.  On the
mixed-regular locus this gives a root-free normal form, so collisions
of the four ramification contacts remain inside the same intrinsic
nilpotent-depth theorem; off the collision divisor the normal form
splits \'etale locally into the four primary branches previously visible
only after choosing roots.  The collision divisor is the discriminant
of a universal binary quartic and has Pl\"ucker class $12H$.

For the K3 family we retain an exact integral differential certificate
showing a nine-dimensional polarized image and generic finite
ambiguity.  We separate this arithmetic witness from the classical
moduli geometry: regular webs of quadrics produce the classical
Reye/nodal-Enriques locus, whose nine-dimensionality is not claimed
as new.  The new invariant is the polarized K3 and its successive
nilpotent-depth supports extracted from the abstract multiplication-
failure scheme.  We give theorem-level comparisons with the accessible
failure-cycle literature, the classical Reye literature, and the
theory of ribbons and multiple structures, while recording explicitly
the remaining access limitation for Ballico's 1993 article.
\end{abstract}
