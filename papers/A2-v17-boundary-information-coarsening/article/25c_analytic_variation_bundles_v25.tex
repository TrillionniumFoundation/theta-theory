\section{Compatible analytic variations and uniform finite positive designs}
\label{sec:v25-analytic-variation-bundle}

The compact analytic class used for global consistency need not be a
finite-dimensional manifold.  Nor does retaining finitely many target jets
make a positive-offset statistical law depend on finitely many parameters.
We give a precise finite-rank variation construction for the separate local
information assertion.  The global estimator of
Theorem~\ref{thm:v25-global-physical-reconstruction} does not depend on this
construction.

Fix a compact family \(\mathfrak K_0\) with a fixed pattern of coincident
and distinct marked contacts on each obstacle.  Coincident contacts are
identified before specifying variations.  Assume the normal angles of
distinct contacts on the same obstacle have a positive uniform separation.
This is a compact contact stratum; no such stratification is needed in the
global separator theorem.  Choose an oriented ambient angular coordinate for
each obstacle in a fixed root-frame gauge.  Its analytic support function is
\(h_i(\theta)\), with radius of curvature
\(R_i=h_i+h_i''>0\).  At a contact angle \(\theta_a\), its point is
\[
       X_i(\theta_a)=h_i(\theta_a)n(\theta_a)
                               +h_i'(\theta_a)t(\theta_a).
\]
Consequently a support perturbation with zero value and first derivative at
\(\theta_a\) preserves that point and its oriented tangent exactly.
It also preserves the gaps of the anchored channel network when this is
imposed at every marked incidence.  We vary the obstacle shapes in an ambient
analytic family of dispersing tables; an arbitrary closed set
\(\mathfrak K_0\) is not asserted to contain these variations.

\begin{lemma}[Uniform analytic Hermite right inverse]
\label{lem:v25-hermite-right-inverse}
Fix an integer \(M\ge2\).  For \(l\) distinct contact angles there is a
finite-dimensional space \(\mathscr T_D\) of real trigonometric polynomials
such that the jet evaluation map
\[
 E_T:\mathscr T_D\longrightarrow\mathbb R^{l(M+1)},\qquad
 E_T f=(f^{(r)}(\theta_a))_{a,\,0\le r\le M}
\]
is onto.  One may take \(D=(l-1)(M+1)+M\).  In a fixed real Fourier basis,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v25-hermite-inverse}
                         R_T=E_T^t(E_TE_T^t)^{-1}
\end{equation}
is a continuous right inverse, uniformly bounded on the compact contact
stratum.  It depends smoothly on the distinct contact angles.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
For each contact \(a\), put
\[
 F_a(\theta)=\prod_{b\ne a}(1-\cos(\theta-\theta_b))^{M+1}.
\]
This polynomial is nonzero at \(\theta_a\) and vanishes to order
\(2(M+1)\) at all other contact angles.  The \(M+1\) functions
\(F_a(\theta)\sin^r(\theta-\theta_a)\), \(0\le r\le M\), have
zero jets through order \(M\) at other contacts.  At \(\theta_a\) their
jet matrix is triangular, with diagonal \(r!F_a(\theta_a)\ne0\).
Their degrees are at most \(D\).  They therefore interpolate arbitrary
jets at this contact, independently of the other contacts.  Summing over
\(a\) proves surjectivity.  The Gram matrix in
\eqref{eq:v25-hermite-inverse} is positive definite and varies smoothly with
the contact angles.  Its least eigenvalue has a positive minimum on the
compact separated-angle set.  This proves the remaining assertions.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Realization of compatible finite contact variations]
\label{prop:v25-analytic-jet-bundle}
Over \(\mathfrak K_0\), assign one labelled oriented graph jet
\((q_{a,2},\ldots,q_{a,M})\) to each \emph{distinct physical contact}.
Repeated incidences use the same jet, transformed to their prescribed
oriented frames.  These coordinates define a finite-rank vector bundle
\(\mathcal V_M\) with a continuous norm.  Its vectors have analytic
realizations through their base tables which preserve every marked contact
point, tangent and gap.  The realizations vary continuously with the base
and smoothly with the fiber variable; on a uniform fiber neighborhood they
remain strictly convex separated dispersing tables with the same persistent
channels.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Take the direct sum of the right inverses of
Lemma~\ref{lem:v25-hermite-right-inverse} over the obstacles.  Require the
zeroth and first support-jet increments to vanish.  The remaining support
jets through order \(M\) are local coordinates for graph jets through order
\(M\) at a fixed point and tangent.  Here is the triangular reason.
Since \(dX/d\theta=R t\), at the contact the transverse graph coordinate
satisfies \(dy/d\theta=R>0\).  The graph curvature is \(q_2=R^{-1}\).
Repeated differentiation with \(d/dy=R^{-1}d/d\theta\) at the contact
shows that, after lower support jets have been fixed, the coefficient of a
variation of \(h^{(m)}\) in \(q_m\) is nonzero (in the convex graph
convention it is \(-R^{-m}\)).  Thus the differential from support jets
\(h^{(2)},\ldots,h^{(M)}\) to graph jets \(q_2,\ldots,q_M\) is triangular
and invertible.  Changing the oriented transverse sign multiplies the
corresponding odd-order coordinates by minus one and does not affect
invertibility.

The finite-dimensional inverse function theorem now assigns support-jet
increments to each sufficiently small desired graph-jet increment.  Extend
these increments by \(R_T\), with the first two jets zero.  The result is
an actual real-analytic support function, not independent formal contact
series.  The interpolation imposes all incidences on each obstacle
simultaneously, so compatibility is automatic.  Uniform curvature and
separation margins, compactness and the uniform bounds on \(R_T\) give a
common small fiber neighborhood on which the realized tables are admissible.
The periodic lattice and obstacle reference placements are kept fixed.

In each choice of labelled frames the fibers are the direct sum of the
finite jet-coordinate spaces just described.  On overlaps, frame changes
and relabellings induce the differentials of the corresponding jet-coordinate
changes.  They are continuous invertible matrices and satisfy the cocycle
identity because the underlying coordinate changes do.  This specifies the
bundle and its transition maps.  A finite frame atlas and a partition of
unity give a continuous fiber norm.  Its unit sphere bundle over the compact
base is compact.  This construction does not give a tangent bundle to an
arbitrary closed analytic class; it gives a specified bundle of ambient,
compatible analytic variations through it.
\end{proof}

For \(v\in\mathcal V_M(T)\), let \(\dot S_{e,b}[v]\) be the action
variation along this realization.  Define the positive-offset information
form by \eqref{eq:v22-information-matrix} with boundary velocity
\[
                 U_{e,b}(u,w)[v]=\dot S_{e,b}(u)[v]
                                      +\dot S_{e,b}(w)[v].
\]
The gap is fixed in this bundle.  A gap direction in a separately specified
anchored one-channel local family is handled by the additional constant
velocity in Lemma~\ref{lem:v22-finite-positive-design}; no independent gap
directions are assigned to an arbitrary globally compatible network here.

\begin{lemma}[Uniform finite positive design on the specified bundle]
\label{lem:v24-uniform-positive-design}
For each fixed \(M\), there are finitely many labelled same-type positive
offsets \((e_r,b_r,d_r)\), positive weights \(\alpha_r\) summing to one,
and \(c_M>0\), independent of the base table in \(\mathfrak K_0\), such
that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v24-uniform-information}
 \sum_r\alpha_r\mathcal K_{T,e_r,b_r,d_r}[v,v]
                                  \ge c_M\|v\|^2
 \qquad(v\in\mathcal V_M(T)).
\end{equation}
This statement concerns the explicitly realized finite-rank bundle, not all
variations of the infinite-dimensional analytic class.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
If a vector lies in the kernels for both types of every channel and every
small positive offset, positivity of the boundary weight implies
\[
                  \dot S_{e,b}(u)[v]+\dot S_{e,b}(w)[v]=0
 \quad\text{when }S_{e,b}(u)+S_{e,b}(w)=d.
\]
Set \(w=0\) and \(d=S_{e,b}(u)>0\).  The anchored variation satisfies
\(\dot S_{e,b}(0)[v]=0\), hence every \(\dot S_{e,b}\) vanishes near the
contact.  The fixed-gap finite action-jet inverse in
Theorem~\ref{thm:v22-signed-rigidity} forces the variations of all marked
contact jets through order \(M\) to vanish.  These jets are the fiber
coordinates, so \(v=0\).

The forms are continuous in the base, direction and each fixed positive
offset.  Indeed the realized support families and their finite derivatives
are continuous; the half-line inverse transports this continuity to the
actions and amplitudes; and each positive-offset level has a regular
boundary with a common local coordinate description.  The coarea formula
then gives continuity of the information integral.  Every unit vector in
the compact sphere bundle is detected by a design, and this remains true on
a neighborhood.  A finite subcover supplies finitely many designs.  Their
positive weighted sum has a positive continuous minimum on that sphere
bundle, proving \eqref{eq:v24-uniform-information}.
\end{proof}

The hypotheses about the contact stratum and the compatible analytic
realizations belong to this local-design lemma alone.  In particular, a
finite-rank information argument is not needed to pass from the compact
analytic class to the global physical consistency theorem.  That passage
uses the augmented observable separator lemma and its finite capped policy.
