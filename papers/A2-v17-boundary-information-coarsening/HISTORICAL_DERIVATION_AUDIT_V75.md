## Primary-source comparison checked

The following sources were checked at their primary manuscript pages on September 17, 2026; the comparison is targeted rather than an exhaustive novelty search.

- L. Noakes and L. Stoyanov, *Lens Rigidity in Scattering by Unions of Strictly Convex Bodies in R2*, arXiv:1803.02542v2, corrected planar argument; SIAM J. Math. Anal. 52 (2020), 471–480. Global exterior data and finite obstacle unions are different from the present marked internal local experiment. Smooth determination is not claimed to begin with this paper.
- J. De Simoi, V. Kaloshin and M. Leguil, *Marked Length Spectral determination of analytic chaotic billiards with axial symmetries*, arXiv:1905.00890v4. The hypotheses and marked-length information are not the data of the present theorem.
- D. Finamore and M. Leguil, *A CAT(0)-approach to the marked length spectral rigidity of Sinai billiards*, arXiv:2510.18983v1. Its enriched spectrum and finite-horizon setting are distinguished from conditional endpoint laws.
- K. Hamm and L. Huang, *CUR Decompositions, Approximations, and Perturbations*, arXiv:1903.09698v2. Exact low-rank factorization is classical context for the moment identity; it neither proves the physical law nor the smooth contact inverse.

The proof-contribution claim is the relatively normalized physical comparison combined with an actual flat-sensitive contact inverse in this observation model. The algebraic quotient, matrix identity and concentration machinery are supporting steps. A new version number and a more focused principal entry are not themselves new mathematical results.
