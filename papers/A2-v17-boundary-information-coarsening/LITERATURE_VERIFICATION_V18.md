# Literature verification — A2 v18

## Scope

This note records the literature checks added in response to C17-E2. It is a positioning audit, not a claim to have exhaustively searched all nonregular statistics literature.

## Moving support and nonregular likelihood

1. **M. Akahira (1975a,b).** The bibliographic records and titles were checked through the reference list exposed by the Oxford Academic record for Smith's related nonregular work. These papers treat rates and distributional bounds for estimation of a location parameter in nonregular cases. They are cited as early background, not as a source for the manuscript's hypersurface coefficient.

2. **I. A. Ibragimov and R. Z. Has'minskii, _Statistical Estimation: Asymptotic Theory_.** Springer metadata was checked. The book contains chapters on local asymptotic normality and on classification of singularities for i.i.d. observations. It is cited for the general nonregular asymptotic framework, not for the billiard calculation.

3. **R. L. Smith, _Maximum likelihood estimation in a class of nonregular cases_, Biometrika 72 (1985), 67–90.** The official Oxford Academic metadata and abstract were checked. The abstract considers a density zero below a parameter-dependent endpoint and asymptotic to a power at the boundary. It explicitly states that the linearly vanishing case (`alpha=2`) has asymptotically efficient normal MLEs at a different rate. The manuscript therefore does not claim the existence of this one-dimensional logarithmically modified normal regime as new.

4. **K. Hirano and J. R. Porter, _Asymptotic Efficiency in Parametric Structural Models with Parameter-Dependent Support_, Econometrica 71 (2003), 1307–1338.** Publisher metadata and abstract were checked. The paper studies nonregular parameter-dependent support using Le Cam limits of experiments and local asymptotic minimax efficiency. It is cited to make clear that parameter-dependent support and experiment limits are established subjects.

## Claim retained for this manuscript

The active v18 text restricts its novelty claim to results proved in the manuscript itself: the intrinsic regular-hypersurface coefficient

`I_Sigma = integral_Sigma a_0 v^2 / |grad w_0| d sigma`,

its independent-thinning likelihood/TV law with the failure atom retained, the determinant-one billiard whitening giving coefficient one for unequal contact curvatures and both parities, and the exact physical hierarchy between complete records, endpoint records and success indicators.

The text expressly does **not** claim priority for parameter-dependent support, the one-dimensional `alpha=2` change of rate, nonregular asymptotic normality, or Le Cam experiment methods in nonregular models.
