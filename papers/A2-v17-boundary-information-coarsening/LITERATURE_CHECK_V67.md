The equal-leading-data family in A2 compares nonlinear endpoint laws with the complete leading endpoint/count record within actual billiard tables. It is not an example of equal marked length spectra. Recovering the stable coordinates from the amplitudes likewise uses the laws and the marked quadratic reconstruction, not lengths alone.

These records support precise comparisons. They do not establish that no related mechanism exists elsewhere, nor do they prove exceptional significance. The paper asks for assessment of the combined relative physical law and actual boundary-response theorem on its own mathematical content.
