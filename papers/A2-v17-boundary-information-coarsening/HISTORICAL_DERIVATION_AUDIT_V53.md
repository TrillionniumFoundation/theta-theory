## Mathematical dependence of the new result

The referee supplied a functional quadratic-action calculation. The new section first proves that its leading coefficient depends only on the action Hessian, not on exact quadraticity. An explicit analytic support family supplies two genuine obstacle shapes with fixed lattice and gap. The actual half-line theorem computes their unequal Hessians and supplies even analytic actions and positive amplitudes. Positive recording probabilities fixed on a common collar then realize the functional calculation inside the billiard model.

The scalar statistic and triangular likelihood expansion give a matching two-point upper testing result and the critical optimal error. These are specified-pair conclusions, not an efficient estimator for the whole nuisance class. The proof explicitly uses exact pair means in the test threshold. Higher nonlinear action terms are left intact and enter the controlled remainder.

The physical comparison does not use weak convergence for a fixed window as a license to shrink that window with the sample size. It starts from a uniform interior density estimate from the relative forward theorem. The integration error is proportional to window area, as is the normalizer. Their quotient has no artificial inverse-area loss. Product error is bounded by `C n tau^J`, and an explicit joint choice of even `J` is made before data acquisition.

Finally the accepted mass is tracked separately: the extra recording/cropping probability has order `h^2`, and the original bridge success probability remains in the expected preparation count. Counts and waiting times are not asserted to be ancillary. This exhibits, within a realized family, the distinction among invariant geometry, conditional information and acquisition cost.
