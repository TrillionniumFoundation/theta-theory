## Uniformity and noise

All constants depend on the fixed period, marks, smoothness orders and geometric margins. The forward class bounds are stipulated through the finite order needed by the density lemma; they are not a derivative-error observation oracle. The two offsets and event/gate tags are exact. Endpoint readout errors are bounded after acceptance and may be dependent or adversarial within that bound. Unknown selection labels, noisy clocks, unbounded contamination, dependence along a single unreset trajectory, and uniform-in-period estimates are not included.

The density estimator can be signed and need not satisfy any exact billiard identity. Two-offset cancellation and smooth inversion are never applied to arbitrary estimates. The countable selection step and triangle comparison provide realizable arguments for those deterministic theorems. A bound for total variation alone is never promoted to a C^m derivative bound.

## Scope retained from previous versions

The contact conclusion is smooth, not analytic-only. The whole-obstacle continuation theorem, global registration/lattice conclusions and holomorphic norm inverse retain their separate hypotheses. Fixed-order stopping error remains `C_M(tau^N+epsilon)+L_M E_m`; no coefficient-one propagation or all-order unweighted norm is introduced. Earlier acquisition results are retained, but the new general fixed-period result is proved directly rather than inferred from their alternating special case.
