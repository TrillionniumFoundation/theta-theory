## What is not a premise of the smooth theorem

The bounded-holomorphic local inverse in the v65 module is an analytic branch of the actual-envelope argument, not an input to smooth flat separation. Whole-obstacle analytic continuation uses its own connectedness, placement/lattice and visitation hypotheses. Intrinsic alternating-channel matching, unknown-origin registration and lattice reconstruction have their own observation model and do not establish calibration for the general marked-polygon experiment by inference. Statistical coarsenings, moving-support experiments and the wider calibration catalogue are not needed to prove the fixed-marked smooth theorem.

The earlier finite-chain and alternating proofs remain available in the next part of both manuscripts. The v64 periodic construction gives its own Green/cofactor proof; its reduction to the alternating formulas is a comparison, not a circular derivation from a theorem later generalized by notation alone. The global curvature/stopping module has additional finite-jet algorithmic conclusions; only its positive two-point curvature inverse enters exact smooth uniqueness.

## The three interfaces that must not be collapsed

1. Relative mixed-twist error is established before conditioning. It is not obtained by dividing an absolute action error by an exponentially small twist.
2. The weighted inverse is between actual candidate graphs. In stability, unequal low jets are first removed by a controlled polynomial; neither arbitrary unequal jets nor formal series are put into an infinite weighted norm.
3. Empirical density estimates are not asserted to be physical limiting laws. Measurable realizable selection precedes geometric inversion; finite-flight bias remains visible and failures reduce the effective sample size.

## Quantitative scope

The real-profile result uses a class-dependent common collar and a finite, contraction-dependent derivative order. Its rate depends on the period, geometric margins and finite smoothness priors. Exact offsets, phase and gate tags are part of the experiment. Endpoint errors may be dependent or adversarial within their bound after acceptance. A single correlated trajectory, noisy selection and unknown registration are not included. The existence selector is not an efficient enumeration procedure, and no minimax optimality is claimed.

The actual flat family separates full laws from contact/action Taylor records. Its finite test requires the profile-error radius to be below one third of a fixed nonzero separation. It says neither equal normalized-density jets nor equal full marked length spectra, and it does not determine arbitrary unvisited smooth arcs.
