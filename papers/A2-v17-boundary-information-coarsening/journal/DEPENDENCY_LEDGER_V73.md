# Revision 73 — observation and proof interfaces

The operative mathematical title remains *Boundary laws and smooth contact rigidity of periodic dispersing billiards*. This ledger supplements, and does not replace, `DEPENDENCY_LEDGER_V72.md`.

## Parameter and observation

The parameter is an actual marked table–offset pair `(T,d)`. The fixed lattice, polygon, frames, physical momentum, phase tags, gates, returning lengths, resetting protocol and positive/smoothness margins remain specified. Each offset is a fixed unknown constant in a positive interval. The graph heights, curvatures, endpoint amplitudes, normalizing constants and free area are not supplied as data.

The exact conditional observation is the complete two-variable limiting distribution at each phase, with gate normalization specified. The exact charged observation retains the success mass and one failure symbol, not a rejected trajectory. The statistical observation is `B` independently reset preparations per phase: success/failure tags and noisy endpoint marks only on success, with bounded post-acceptance readout error and exact gates/tags.

## Exact versus limiting versus finite-sample statements

`pi_N f_N(0,0)=D_N d/(2 pi A)` is exact on the stated finite gate. The normalized scalar `lim pi_(nP)/D_(nP)` exists by the relative density limit. Neither statement grants density evaluation or an infinite limit as a finite-sample oracle. The finite theorem estimates a density from marks and a probability from counts, then fits one actual pair.

For fixed finite-density fitting, normalize on `Q_+` and restrict to `Q` without renormalization. For comparison of local experiment **germs**, a smaller gate is a newly specified experiment with its own conditional normalization. Mixing these conventions would corrupt the area anchor; the revision states them separately.

The area is output as `A(T_hat)` of the selected actual table. The log twist comparison is relative, and its `1+N` factor remains in the error. No arbitrary density is inverted as if it were physical, no negative or unrelated numerical area is appended, and no success marks from outside the charged record are used on the good count event.

## Scope retained

The complete two-offset, symmetric one-offset, smooth, analytic, multichannel, global registration, lattice, moving-family, coarsening and acquisition material remains in its previous native entry. Retention and compilation do not constitute a new full proof audit of those branches. The new theorem makes no same-data comparison with exterior scattering or marked/enriched length spectra, and no claim of determination of all remote smooth boundary arcs.
