# Literature boundary — revision 25

The bibliography retains the predecessor's classical and current finite-memory sources. The new controlled section additionally cites R. D. Smallwood and E. J. Sondik, *The Optimal Control of Partially Observable Markov Processes over a Finite Horizon*, Operations Research 21 (1973), 1071–1088, DOI 10.1287/opre.21.5.1071. The publisher abstract explicitly treats finite-horizon belief-state piecewise-linear convex values. Consequently neither Bellman recursion nor that general geometric property is claimed as an invention here.

Blackwell comparison, Sion minimax, Carathéodory support reduction, positive-part total-variation testing, and finite policy-tree convexification are classical mechanisms. The revision submits explicit controlled-validation theorems, marked-family saddle points and resource consequences for assessment, with these mechanisms identified.

M. A. Managoli and V. M. Prabhakaran, *Memory Constrained Adversarial Hypothesis Testing*, arXiv:2605.12063v1, submitted 12 May 2026, studies randomized time-invariant finite-state binary tests with adaptive adversarial distribution choices. The primary arXiv abstract was checked. This is adjacent work, but its adversarial and stationary formulation is not silently identified with one fixed private parameter and finite training–validation score. No broad priority claim for state savings from randomization is made.

The Hellman–Cover stationary formula remains attributed to the original finite-memory testing theory. The precision/time ledger is not counted as a new sharp stationary theorem. Müller–Montúfar and Putinar remain the references for adjacent polynomial stochastic-policy geometry and positivity certificates.

E. Norberg, *Comparison of statistical experiments with filtered probability spaces*, Statistics & Risk Modeling 20 (2002), 1–28, DOI 10.1524/strm.2002.20.14.1, remains relevant. An attempt to access the original publisher article did not yield the full proof. Therefore no full-proof comparison or non-overlap finding is claimed. The paper explicitly delimits its novelty claims to its stated formulas and constructions rather than claiming general filtered-comparison priority from unavailable text.

These checks establish bibliographic positioning, not exhaustive priority clearance for every theorem in the paper.
