# Primary-source literature audit — A2 v23

Date: 29 September 2026. This is a scoped comparison, not an exhaustive priority search. References in the article identify the historical works; immutable arXiv versions were consulted where available.

## Nearest inverse-geometry sources

Stefanov–Uhlmann–Vasy, *Local and global boundary rigidity and the geodesic X-ray transform in the normal gauge*, arXiv:1702.03638v3; Annals of Mathematics 194 (2021), 1–95, doi:10.4007/annals.2021.194.1.1. The primary PDF and the pages containing Theorems 1.1 and 1.3 were inspected. The local dimension-at-least-three metric result and convex-foliation global lens result differ from unknown reflecting arcs in a fixed Euclidean plane. Section 8 gives that comparison; no claim of subsumption in either direction is made.

Gurfinkel–Noakes–Stoyanov, *Travelling Times in Scattering by Obstacles in Curved Space*, arXiv:2003.12261v1; Journal of Differential Equations 269 (2020), 9508–9530, doi:10.1016/j.jde.2020.05.049. The primary PDF pages containing Theorems 1–2 and Lemma 7 were inspected. Exterior travelling-time data, flow conjugacy and non-positive-curvature obstacle uniqueness are distinguished from selected intrinsic three-impact records.

Noakes–Stoyanov, *Travelling times in scattering by obstacles*, arXiv:1404.4147v3; Journal of Mathematical Analysis and Applications 430 (2015), 703–717, doi:10.1016/j.jmaa.2015.05.013. The primary paper was consulted for the enclosing-boundary travelling-time setting and its constructive special cases. The retained local proof credits the direction information in derivatives of travel time.

The exact local information-equivalence proposition remains active. The new paper does not claim that its elementary affine elimination invents lens rigidity, or that the geometric producer follows from exterior travel-time theorems.

## Time-uniform probability and numerical conditioning

Howard–Ramdas–McAuliffe–Sekhon, *Time-uniform, nonparametric, nonasymptotic confidence sequences*, arXiv:1810.08240v9; Annals of Statistics 49 (2021), 1055–1080, doi:10.1214/20-AOS1991. Primary arXiv metadata and full HTML were consulted. The article cites the general time-uniform setting while proving its own elementary conditional error-spending argument. No new general concentration inequality is claimed.

Trefethen, *Quantifying the ill-conditioning of analytic continuation*, arXiv:1908.11097; BIT Numerical Mathematics 60 (2020), 901–915, doi:10.1007/s10543-020-00802-7. The primary publication metadata and abstract support the conditioning comparison. The cylinder-slit continuation argument is included in the retained active proof, and its exponent remains class dependent.

## Arithmetic, proximity and other billiard invariants

Kannan–Bachem, SIAM Journal on Computing 8 (1979), 499–507, doi:10.1137/0208040: the publisher source verifies the Smith/Hermite algorithm reference. Its integer-input complexity is not transferred to real-analytic body fitting or gate installation. Cohen's Chapter 2 is retained as background for integer normal forms.

The Toussaint, Jaromczyk–Toussaint and Pocchiola–Vegter entries are retained from the reviewed bibliography. They provide proximity/visibility context, not a claimed algorithm for the present physical producer. The complete obstruction-descent proof is written in the article rather than outsourced to those references.

De Simoi–Kaloshin–Leguil and Finamore–Leguil retain their distinct marked-length or enriched marked-length observation categories. Finamore–Leguil's primary arXiv abstract (2510.18983v1) was checked. The new paper does not identify endpoint laws with those spectra, claim a no-mark observer theorem, or assert exact analytic count-only rigidity.

## What this audit does not establish

No source listed here certifies the new sequential geometric construction or establishes its priority. The proofs, statistical upper bounds and access assumptions require independent mathematical review. Bibliographic checking is not proof verification or a journal decision.
