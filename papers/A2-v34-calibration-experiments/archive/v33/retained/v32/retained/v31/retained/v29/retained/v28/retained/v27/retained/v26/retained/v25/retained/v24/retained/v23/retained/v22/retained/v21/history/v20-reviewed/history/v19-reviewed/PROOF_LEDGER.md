## Boundaries retained

The unlabelled theorem does not remove local origins, itinerary selection or within-record grouping. It does not identify unvisited obstacles. Unique incidence recovery uses distinct congruence classes; unique period recovery uses a primitive geometric pair. The full finite-index list is a candidate upper bound; only candidates satisfying all physical and cycle constraints survive. The divisor-sum bound is not claimed sharp for physical tables.

The fixed statistical exponent is class-dependent and an upper bound. The estimator is a measurable admissible-candidate selection, not an efficient exhaustive-search implementation. The bounded-presentation loss compares actual complete images and an unmarked lattice; no unsupported metric axioms are used. Integer locking applies to physical candidates known to have integral indices, not raw noisy vectors.

Exact analytic count-only rigidity/nonrigidity is not inferred from the retained formal or smooth-Taylor fibers. Smooth local full-law equality is a function statement, while whole-image continuation uses analyticity.

## Finite diagnostics

`verify_v19.py` exercises exact quotient jets, curvature and foot identities, row-Hermite quotient groups through index 32, primitive and prime-index controls, incidence bookkeeping, cell reconstruction and rate algebra. Most counted checks are individual quotient membership tests; the total is not a count of independent theorems.
