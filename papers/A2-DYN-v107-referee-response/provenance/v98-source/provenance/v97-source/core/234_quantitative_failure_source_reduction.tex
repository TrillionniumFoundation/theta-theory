\section{Quantitative reduction of the old failure source}
\label{sec:v97-reduction}
Keep $q=\beta/D\le1/2$, use $H=\Pi$ in the capped clock, and use
$P$ from \eqref{eq:v97-protected}. Define a residual target set and
its original weighted source by
\begin{align}
 Y_{a,q,r}&=\Pi\cap\{c_r<q\}\cap P^c,\notag\\
 Y_{a,q,r}^\varepsilon(t)&=\int_{F=t}W^\varepsilon
                                    \one_{Y_{a,q,r}}\,d\mu_t.
                                      \label{eq:v97-residual}
\end{align}
No new measure is placed on the boundary or on a sampled orbit.
Every target is in exactly one of $\{c_r\ge q\}$,
$\{c_r<q\}\cap P$, and $Y_{a,q,r}$.

\begin{theorem}[An estimated part of every previous failure class]
\label{thm:v97-reduction}
At finite count the residual is a subsource of the old three-way
failure source and has the geometric support
\begin{align}
 0\le Y_{a,q,r}^\varepsilon&\le Z_{r,D,\beta}^\varepsilon,
                                      \label{eq:v97-contained}\\
 Y_{a,q,r}&\subset
 \{\tau^-\le4qr,\ z<a\}\ \cup\ \{\ell\le6qr\}.
                                      \label{eq:v97-support}
\end{align}
At almost every target roof,
\begin{align}
 b^\varepsilon(t)\le{}&Y_{a,q,r}^\varepsilon(t)
 +\frac1{qr}\int_{t-r}^{t+r}
               \{b^\varepsilon(u)+\kappa_\varepsilon(r)p(u)\}\,du
 +C\int_{t-2qr}^{t+2qr}v^\rho(u)\,du.
                                      \label{eq:v97-full-source}
\end{align}
The recovered part of the \emph{old} failure source obeys the actual
height estimate
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v97-paid-old-failure}
 \mathcal H_M(Z_{r,D,\beta}^\varepsilon-Y_{a,q,r}^\varepsilon)
 \le\frac Cq\{\varepsilon^{1/16}+\varepsilon^{-1}\sqrt r\}+Cqr.
\end{equation}
All parameters here are fixed before the collision limit. The constant
in \eqref{eq:v97-paid-old-failure} is independent of the positive $a$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Old admission implies $c_r\ge q$ by
Theorem~\ref{thm:v97-capped-anchor}. Thus $\{c_r<q\}\subset N$,
which proves \eqref{eq:v97-contained}. The complement of $P$ is
exactly
\[
 \{\tau^-\le4qr\}\cap\bigl(\{z<a\}\cup\{\tau^+\le2qr\}\bigr).
\]
In the second alternative $\ell=\tau^-+\tau^+\le6qr$, proving
\eqref{eq:v97-support}. Split the full source by the three disjoint
sets preceding the theorem. The capped anchor estimates the first;
Corollary~\ref{cor:v97-low-clock} estimates the second. This proves
\eqref{eq:v97-full-source} at finite count.

The same split restricted to $N$ gives a nonnegative recovered source
$Z-Y$, bounded by those same two paid terms. Apply the original
fixed-width estimates for $b^\varepsilon$ and $p$, and the audited
positive-cutoff estimate for $v^\rho$. This proves
\eqref{eq:v97-paid-old-failure}. The sum of the recovered parts of
$N_T,N_D,N_O$ is precisely $Z-Y$: it is not estimated by counting
three overlapping events or by subtracting signed boundary fluxes.
\end{proof}

The capped anchor admits some targets rejected by the old one-sided
or worst-time tests, and the running-loss theorem pays the remaining
low-clock targets on $P$. The theorem proves containment and a bound
on the recovered source. It does not assert a positive lower bound
for the amount recovered in every Lorentz fiber.

\begin{corollary}[A sharper original-source support at one legal schedule]
\label{cor:v97-schedule}
Choose $D=2$, $\beta=1/4$, $q=1/8$,
$r(B)=c\varepsilon(B)^4\le r_{\rm col}$, and
$a(B)=\sqrt{q r(B)}$, with fixed sufficiently small $c>0$.
Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v97-endpoint}
 \mathcal E_M\le\mathcal H_M(Y_{a(B),q,r(B)}^{\varepsilon(B)})
                                      +C_MB^{-1/192}.
\end{equation}
Moreover the recovered old failure in \eqref{eq:v97-paid-old-failure}
is $O(B^{-1/192})$. The residual contains only targets with
$z<\sqrt{qr}$ near an original entrance or total original residence
at most $6qr$. Its fixed-window mass satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v97-residual-mass}
 \limsup_m\sup_{R,n,k}\sup_{|J|=L}m^2\int_JY_{a,q,r}^\varepsilon(t)\,dt
 \le C L\{(qr)^{129/256}+(qr)^{1/32}\}.
\end{equation}
The last assertion is a mass estimate, not an essential-height estimate.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Insert \eqref{eq:v97-full-source} into the retained positive scalar
remainder. Its explicit errors have orders
$\varepsilon^{1/16}$, $\varepsilon^{-1}\sqrt r=O(\varepsilon)$,
and $qr=O(\varepsilon^4)$. With $\varepsilon=A_0B^{-1/12}$ the
slowest is $B^{-1/192}$. All widths, including
$\rho=\min(a,\sqrt{4\lambda_\circ qr})$, are fixed before the
collision limit; auxiliary bands are enlarged afterwards.
For \eqref{eq:v97-residual-mass}, use \eqref{eq:v97-support},
$W\le1$, the critical-volume bound at $a=\sqrt{qr}$, and
$\{\ell\le6qr\}\subset\{\tau^-\le6qr\}$. The two original
volume estimates give the displayed powers after the same legal limits.
\end{proof}

For completeness, the original pointwise theorem along this schedule
is equivalent to $\mathcal H_M(Y_{a(B),q,r(B)}^{\varepsilon(B)})\to0$.
Sufficiency follows from \eqref{eq:v97-endpoint}; necessity follows
from $Y\le b^\varepsilon$ and the positive remainder. This equivalence
is secondary to the estimated reduction \eqref{eq:v97-paid-old-failure}.
The remaining residual height is not proved to vanish.

The retained bidirectional signed forcing is formed after projection
and satisfies $0\le J_r^\varepsilon\le b^\varepsilon$. Thus
\eqref{eq:v97-full-source} bounds it without moving a minimum or a
positive part into a sheet integral. Since the schedule also has
$(1+\varepsilon^{-1})r\to0$, its retained scalar equivalence applies.
No joint-forcing-zero, unrestricted pointwise denominator, same-roof
ratio, essential-likelihood or arithmetic-zero normalization assertion
is promoted by the present reduction.
