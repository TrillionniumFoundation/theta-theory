\section{Physical continuation and exact-label occupation}
\label{sec:v96-continuation}
A maximal segment of an exact event can be short because of section
\emph{decisions}, while its physical word remains regular for a longer
roof interval. We use this distinction only for comparison. The original
stopped segments, endpoint traces, occupation index and source measure
are not changed.

Fix $p_*=19/20$ and put $\lambda_*=9\sqrt{39}/400$.
Let $\widehat\Omega_m$ be the actual regular $m$-flight collision
states with $|p_0|,|p_m|<p_*$ and $z>0$, before imposing any section
decisions. The physical word and displacement are fixed on a component.
A comparison trajectory stops at a true physical boundary, at the
collar boundary, or at a backward critical end. It does not stop at a
section-decision wall. Write $\widehat\Phi_s$ for this partial flow.
The original event $\Pi=\Pi_{n,k,m,R}$ is always tested at the sampled
state, including its initial and terminal tests and the exact value $n$.

\begin{lemma}[A comparison flow with the same physical density]
\label{lem:v96-flow}
On $\widehat\Omega_m$ the field
\[
 \widehat X=\frac{K^{-1}g}{g^{\mathsf T}K^{-1}g}
\]
is smooth on each physical component, with
$\lambda_*I\le K\le\Lambda_\circ I$, $\widehat XF=1$ and
$\widehat X\vartheta=0$. Its complete-physical-segment coordinates
$(\xi,u)$ have density
\[
 \widehat A_\xi(u)=\frac{\omega}{4\pi c_*\chi},\qquad
 d(c_*^{-1}\nu)=\widehat A_\xi(u)\,du\,d\xi.
\]
They restrict to the original coarea measure wherever $\Pi$ holds.
For a surviving comparison interval of roof length at most $r$,
\begin{align}
 |W^\varepsilon(\widehat\Phi_sx)-W^\varepsilon(x)|
 &\le\kappa_\varepsilon(r):=
       \min\{1,C_\Theta\varepsilon^{-1}\sqrt r\},
                                  \label{eq:v96-guard}\\
 |\partial_u\log\widehat A_\xi|
 &\le C_* I_m/z.                   \label{eq:v96-log-density}
\end{align}
All constants are independent of the collision count. The comparison
flow is not a continuation through a grazing or competing-hit boundary.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The general endpoint Hessian inequality, before the section restriction,
is
\[
 R\operatorname{diag}(c_0,c_m)\le K
 \le(2R_+^2/\tau_-+R_+)I.
\]
Its proof in retained module 174 uses the supporting polygonal action
and the positive internal Schur complement, not section membership.
On the collar, $c_0,c_m>\sqrt{39}/20$, giving the stated constants.
The identities for $F,\vartheta$ and $\chi$ follow by differentiating
$g/|g|$, exactly as for the original field. They give the displayed
area density, including both optical factors. The rational-roof Borel
transversal construction of module 196 applies to this open physical
domain. At any fixed roof the comparison map is injective on its
domain; two different starting states cannot meet under a smooth flow.
This remains true when the same orbit leaves and reenters $\Pi$.

The contact inverse of module 177 is independent of section decisions.
It bounds contact motion by $Cq_0^{d_j}$ times endpoint-curve length.
Radial growth with $\lambda_*$ bounds that length by $C\sqrt r$,
in either direction along a surviving interval. The original product
guard then gives \eqref{eq:v96-guard}; no monotonicity of $\Theta$ is
used. The optical log-sensitivity computation of module 182 requires
only nonzero internal incidences and a positive lower endpoint cosine.
On this fixed collar it gives $|\widehat X\log\omega|\le CI_m/z$.
The stationary cubic estimate of module 180 and $\chi\ge\lambda_*$
give $|\widehat X\log\chi|\le C/z$. Subtracting the two logarithmic
derivatives proves \eqref{eq:v96-log-density}. Thus the extension
uses the same local geometric formulas, not a spectral theorem outside
$\Pi$ or an assumed source bound on the comparison domain.
\end{proof}

Let $H$ be any Borel subset of the original exact event. The choices
$H=\Pi$, $H=\Pi\cap\{z<a\}$, $H=\Pi\cap\{\ell<\delta\}$,
and their original union are important. Here $\ell$ is always the
length of the \emph{original stopped exact-event segment}, recomputed
at each sampled state. Define
\[
 R_H^\varepsilon(t)=\int_{F=t}\one_H W^\varepsilon\,d\mu_t,
 \qquad U_H(t)=\int_{F=t}\one_H\,d\mu_t.
\]
For a sign $e\in\{-1,1\}$, a comparison interval is available when
$\widehat\Phi_{es}x$ exists for $0\le s\le r$. On such an interval put
\begin{align}
 d_{e,r}(x)&=\sup_{0\le s\le r}
 \frac{\widehat A(x)}{\widehat A(\widehat\Phi_{es}x)},\notag\\
 o_{H,e,r}(x)&=\frac1r\int_0^r
                         \one_H(\widehat\Phi_{es}x)\,ds.
                                      \label{eq:v96-tests}
\end{align}
Set $d_{e,r}=+\infty$ if the interval is unavailable. The ratio is
intrinsic: it is the inverse physical coarea Jacobian in the same
$(F,\vartheta)$ coordinates. Fix $D\ge1$ and $0<\beta\le1$.
A target in $H$ is \emph{admitted} if, for at least one sign,
$d_{e,r}\le D$ and $o_{H,e,r}\ge\beta$. Denote this Borel target
set by $H^{\rm adm}_{r,D,\beta}$. The supremum in $d$ can be taken
on rational times by continuity; the occupation integral is Borel by
Tonelli. Choose the positive sign when both signs are admissible.

\begin{theorem}[Exact-label occupation anchoring]
\label{thm:v96-anchor}
At every finite collision count and almost every target roof,
\begin{align}
 R_{H^{\rm adm}}^\varepsilon(t)
 &\le\frac{D}{\beta r}\int_{t-r}^{t+r}
        \bigl(R_H^\varepsilon(u)+\kappa_\varepsilon(r)U_H(u)\bigr)\,du,
                                                   \label{eq:v96-weighted-anchor}\\
 R_{H^{\rm adm}}^\varepsilon(t)
 &\le U_{H^{\rm adm}}(t)
 \le\frac{D}{\beta r}\int_{t-r}^{t+r}U_H(u)\,du.
                                                   \label{eq:v96-unweighted-anchor}
\end{align}
There is no lower gradient, incidence or original-residence cutoff.
There is no factor counting visits, words, or exact-event components.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix an admitted target and its chosen sign. At every sampled time
with $\widehat\Phi_{es}x\in H$, the density ratio and guard modulus give
\[
 \widehat A(x)W^\varepsilon(x)
 \le D\widehat A(\widehat\Phi_{es}x)
    \{W^\varepsilon(\widehat\Phi_{es}x)+\kappa_\varepsilon(r)\}.
\]
Integrate over these sampled times, whose total length is at least
$\beta r$. Then integrate over the target transverse coordinates.
For each fixed $s$ and each sign, flow injectivity and preservation of
$\vartheta$ identify the transported targets with a subfamily of the
\emph{same exact-label} fiber at $t+es$. Its density is exactly
$\widehat A(\widehat\Phi_{es}x)$; the inverse Jacobian has already
been paid by $D$. Adding the two disjoint target-sign classes yields
\eqref{eq:v96-weighted-anchor}. Apply the identical argument with
weight one to obtain \eqref{eq:v96-unweighted-anchor}.

Intermediate states outside $H$ supply no anchor mass. In particular
states with a different occupation value are never substituted. The
flow may reenter $H$ many times, but at any fixed sampled roof each
transported target is counted only once. Tonelli and the physical
Borel coordinates justify the integrals. As usual each fixed parameter
choice has its common almost-everywhere version; no common Lebesgue
set for uncountably many Borel selectors is asserted.
\end{proof}

A directly checkable sufficient distortion test is
\[
 C_*\int_0^r\frac{I_m(\widehat\Phi_{es}x)}
                      {z(\widehat\Phi_{es}x)}\,ds\le\log D.
\]
It follows from \eqref{eq:v96-log-density}. This is an integrated
physical test, not an incidence corridor. Its satisfaction on all
potential spike trajectories is not assumed.
