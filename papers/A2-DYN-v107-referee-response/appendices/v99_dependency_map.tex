\section{Dependencies and quantifiers of the active proof}
\label{app:v99-dependencies}
The original problem and physical conventions precede the new proofs.
The inherited analytic interfaces are stated in the article before
module 235: the finite-count critical-volume estimate, original
positive scalar remainder, full angular density, Borel segment map
and running-compression residual. They retain their exact parameter
and null-set conventions.

The new global uniqueness theorem uses only the physical reflection
law and convexity of the Cartesian sum of flight lengths. The optical
comparison uses the standard invariant cones and collision-area
Jacobian, with every change of coordinates displayed. The marked
pressure comparison uses both of these results, the lower endpoint
Hessian bound and the finite lifted-step alphabet. The external input
is the bounded fixed-table stable-Jacobian partition sum at parameter
one in \cite[Lemma 3.8, proof]{BDPressure}; its partition is specified
in \eqref{eq:v99-pressure-domination}. It is not cited as an endpoint
momentum-fiber theorem: that comparison is proved here.

The finite-block estimate takes the parameter supremum first. Its
strict condition (BC) must be verified at each of an unbounded sequence
of fixed auxiliary bands to imply the original uniform (SC). The
fixed-radius theorem instead takes the collision limsup with the
radius held fixed. It has no additional capacity hypothesis but does
not assert uniform convergence in radius. In either case the positive
paired short trace is a separate unresolved height estimate.

The complete preceding article, all 237 inherited cores, both v88
variants and their original source files remain in the repository and
archive. Their historical conditional statements are preserved rather
than rewritten as if they had used the present comparison. The active
claim authority is the new source manifest and this theorem sequence,
not a superseded historical status flag. No source receipt, finite
fixture or successful build is a continuum proof certificate.
