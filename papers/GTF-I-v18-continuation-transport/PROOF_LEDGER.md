## Scope controls that cannot be omitted

Continuation covers are simultaneous across times and input/output shifts; separate rank inequalities are only necessary. The erasure cut forbids an uncharged correlated input or recalled output after the cut. Independent selectors do not encode a preparation variable.

The audit reads completed marked reset trials, has a fixed candidate design, resets its per-run randomness and validates independently. It proves an expected signed score, not a specified-confidence one-shot test. The sharp exponent is a sampling statement for this interface, not a revealed-polynomial degree or bit lower bound.

The Gaussian theorem assumes a common preparation, bounded signals and fixed positive noise. Its path conclusions allow different flows but do not cover arbitrary singular changes of the latent law. The entropic terminal variable is its observable conditional certainty equivalent; local H2 integrand convergence is not silently replaced by a global unbounded-generator theorem.

The physical gap transduces a prepared variable through one collision. Its uniformity is in contact distance at fixed particle number and positive noise, not a particle-number-uniform or Boltzmann--Grad assertion. The no-collision experiment remains a control with zero deficiencies.
