\section{Absolute guard control and zero-center caustic height}
\label{sec:v69-absolute-guards}
We separate the angular certificate from the clearance guard. This
removes the relative guard condition, including its singularity at
zero center value, from the all-order collar theorem.

For a nonzero angular germ retain $r_{z,\lambda}^*$ and
$k_{z,\lambda}^{\rm a}$ from
\eqref{eq:v68-angular-relative-budget}, and define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v69-angular-persistence}
 \mathfrak p^{\rm a}_{z,\lambda}
  =\max\{(r_{z,\lambda}^*)^{-1},2k_{z,\lambda}^{\rm a}\}.
\end{equation}
For an identically zero germ choose a certified zero radius
$0<r_{z,\lambda}^{\rm zero}<r_\chi$ and set
$\mathfrak p^{\rm a}_{z,\lambda}=(r_{z,\lambda}^{\rm zero})^{-1}$.
This latter convention asserts zero source only within the certified
disk. It does not assign a finite birth order to a zero germ.
Let $\mathcal A_{m,R,\lambda}(K)$ consist of all selected charts
with $\mathfrak p^{\rm a}_{z,\lambda}\le K$. This family does not
depend on $H$ or on the value of the center guard. For a positive
center guard, the old persistence number satisfies
$\mathfrak p^{\rm a}_{z,\lambda}\le\mathfrak p_{z,\lambda}$.

\begin{lemma}[An annular comparison without a guard denominator]
\label{lem:v69-angular-annulus}
Fix $2H<h_\chi$ and $2K\sqrt H<1$. For every member of
$\mathcal A_{m,R,\lambda}(K)$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v69-angular-annulus}
 \operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{0<u<H}
         \widehat b_{z,\lambda}(t_z+u)
          \le2F_{\chi,K}(H)\widehat q_{z,2H;\lambda},
\end{equation}
where $F_{\chi,K}(H)$ is the factor in
\eqref{eq:v68-annular-factor}. Its constant is independent of every
finite angular birth order and of the center guard.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For a nonzero germ, \eqref{eq:v68-relative-angular-type} and
\eqref{eq:v69-angular-persistence} give
$(1-Kr)\eta r^d\le\ell(r)\le(1+Kr)\eta r^d$ for $r<K^{-1}$.
Insert this estimate in \eqref{eq:v69-unguarded-profile}.
On $H<v<2H$ the lower profile is at least
\[
 e^{-2K_\chi\sqrt H}(1-2K\sqrt H)J_z\eta(2H)^{d/2}.
\]
On $0<u<H$ its upper counterpart is at most
$e^{\sqrt2K_\chi\sqrt H}(1+\sqrt2K\sqrt H)
 J_z\eta(2H)^{d/2}$. Divide these two bounds and compare the
positive outer-annulus average to twice the full $2H$-collar
average. This proves the assertion with exactly the indicated $F$.
Only monotonicity of $r^d$, not a bound on $d$, has been used.
For a zero germ, $2\sqrt H<K^{-1}$ lies within its certified zero
disk, so both source integrals vanish. The guard has not entered
either case.
\end{proof}

For $0\le\delta\le1$ let
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v69-small-center-source}
 b^{\rm a,K,H;\le\delta}_{m;\lambda,R}(t)
 =\sum_{\substack{z\in\mathcal A_{m,R,\lambda}(K)\\g_z\le\delta}}
       \one_{\{0<t-t_z<H\}}
                   b_{z,\lambda}^{\varepsilon,\mathrm{clr}}(t).
\end{equation}
For $\delta=1$ write simply $b^{\rm a,K,H}$. These are restrictions
of the original first-clearance source, not of the comparison source.
Use the ordered height $\mathcal H$ defined before
\eqref{eq:v68-complete-clearance-budget}.

\begin{theorem}[Absolute-guard height on every angular stratum]
\label{thm:v69-absolute-guard-height}
Fix $\chi,\varepsilon,K,H,\delta$ before the collision limit, with
$2H<h_\chi$ and $2K\sqrt H<1$. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v69-small-center-height}
 \mathcal H(b^{\rm a,K,H;\le\delta})
 \le6CH F_{\chi,K}(H)
                    \min\{1,\delta+L_g\sqrt{2H}\}.
\end{equation}
In particular
\begin{align}
 \mathcal H(b^{\rm a,K,H})
            &\le6CH F_{\chi,K}(H),
                                      \label{eq:v69-all-center-height}\\
 \mathcal H(b^{\rm a,K,H;\le0})
            &\le6\sqrt2 C C_g\varepsilon^{-1}
                                H^{3/2}F_{\chi,K}(H).
                                      \label{eq:v69-zero-center-height}
\end{align}
Every finite angular birth order is included. Zero center guards,
including infinitely flat ones, and arbitrarily small positive
center guards require no further cutoff. A bounded measurable
insertion costs only its supremum norm in these bounds.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At a fixed roof $t$, contributing centers lie in $[t-H,t]$.
Lemma~\ref{lem:v69-positive-comparison}, followed by
Lemma~\ref{lem:v69-angular-annulus}, gives almost everywhere
\[
 b^{\rm a,K,H;\le\delta}_{m;\lambda,R}(t)
 \le 2F_{\chi,K}(H)\min\{1,\delta+L_g\sqrt{2H}\}
             \widehat{\mathcal Q}_{m,2H;\lambda,R}([t-H,t]),
\]
where the trace is restricted to the same chart subfamily. Apply
Theorem~\ref{thm:v69-comparison-collar} with interval length $H$
and collar width $2H$. Its bound is $3CH$, proving
\eqref{eq:v69-small-center-height}. The choices $\delta=1$ and
$\delta=0$ give the two displayed consequences. The common coarea
representative excludes a null set at each finite count; finite
unions over words and labels remain null. Positivity proves the
insertion assertion before pushforward. All birth orders are summed
at fixed count; no infinite scale sum is interchanged with a limsup.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Fixed-stratum shrinking collars]
\label{cor:v69-shrinking-collars}
At fixed $\chi,\varepsilon,K$, for every deterministic sequence
$H_m\downarrow0$,
\[
 \sup_{R,\lambda}m^2
        \|b^{\rm a,K,H_m}_{m;\lambda,R}\|_\infty\longrightarrow0.
\]
No quantitative rate in $m$ is asserted.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
For any fixed admissible $H$, eventually $H_m<H$. The chart family
is independent of the collar width, so the original positive
restrictions satisfy $b^{\rm a,K,H_m}\le b^{\rm a,K,H}$.
Apply \eqref{eq:v69-all-center-height} at that fixed $H$ and then
let $H\downarrow0$. This is a monotone comparison with fixed-width
estimates, not a spectral evaluation at $H_m$ or a choice $K=K_m$.
\end{proof}

The original relative-guard persistence theorem remains intact. Its
controlled source is contained in the present one at the same
$K,H$. The new theorem removes the guard part of the cutoff; it
neither proves tightness of $\mathfrak p^{\rm a}$ nor identifies it
with the old angular condition number $\mathfrak a$. Source outside
the certified angular disks has not been inferred small. We next
give a separate comparison which can be evaluated on that source
without using an angular germ at all.
