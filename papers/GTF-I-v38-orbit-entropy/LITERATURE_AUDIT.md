## Spectral certificate provenance

The six-gate constant is the analytic LPS Hecke/Ramanujan bound, not a numerical spectral calculation. Original LPS publication metadata was checked; the explicit normalization and p=5 construction were read in the above full coauthor account and Pisier's theorem. This audit does not claim a new proof of the arithmetic result or full reproduction of the original LPS articles. `GAP_CERTIFICATE.json` records the exact algebraic normalization and primary references. The finite verifier checks the rational rotation/qubit identification and resulting constants; it does not certify an infinite-dimensional gap by truncation.

The old five-gate gap remains non-evaluated. The general-q adjacent generators also retain a qualitative external gap, not a numerical estimate. These distinctions appear in the main article and response.

## Inherited context and comparison status

Polyanskiy–Wu's Gaussian-smoothed Wasserstein entropy continuity, positive-realization/invariant-cone mechanisms, regular branching-program Fourier growth and quantum stochastic-memory results remain cited. Their role is background and model comparison; this revision does not claim a fresh exhaustive proof audit of every source in those mature literatures. The newly formalized branching-program equivalence follows directly from the product of stochastic matrices and a Bernoulli acceptance row. Standard quantum reset and measurement identities are proved directly, not claimed new.

The proposed mathematical advance is the representation-valued occupation bound together with the all-spectra conjugation estimate and matching projective synthesis. Its comparison with prior coding theorems specifies source law, conditioning variable, output timing, distortion, generator restrictions and cost. This is stronger than a bibliography list, but independent novelty assessment is still needed.
