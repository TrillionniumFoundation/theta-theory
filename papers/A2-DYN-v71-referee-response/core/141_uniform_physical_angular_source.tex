\section{A count-uniform angular formula for the original boundary source}
\label{sec:v66-physical-angular}
We use the uniform selected-contact chart without linearizing a physical
boundary. Thus a small boundary gradient does not enter an error
constant. The resulting angular integral keeps the exact nonlinear
first-hit tests, the smooth physical guard and every section decision.
It is not a closed evaluation of all those angular sets.

Fix a $\chi$-incident critical word and the chart of
Theorem~\ref{thm:v66-uniform-morse}. Write $x_z(y)$ for its selected
initial state. Define $B_{z,n,k}(y)$ to be zero unless this selected
word is the actual physical word and both original section endpoint
tests hold. On that physical set impose its exact original return
label $(n,k,m)$. Occupation uses collisions $0,\ldots,m-1$ and
terminal membership is checked at $m$. Consequently
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v66-exact-label-partition}
 0\le\sum_{n=1}^m\sum_k B_{z,n,k}(y)\le1.
\end{equation}
These tests are made on the actual selected positions and velocities,
not on signs of first-order approximations. A point on the continuation
failing a physical test receives zero weight.

\subsection{The first-clearance multiplier on the common chart}
For $0<\varepsilon\le\chi/4$ all selected incidence guard factors
are one on this chart. Let $D_z^\varepsilon(y)$ be the sum of the
original first-clearance multipliers there. On the physical set it is
exactly $1-H_{m,R}^\varepsilon$. It may be evaluated as a function
of the selected contacts off that set, solely for the coordinate
formula. It is always multiplied by $B_{z,n,k}$ when defining a source.

\begin{lemma}[Uniform control of the actual clearance guard]
\label{lem:v66-uniform-guard}
There is $C_g$ independent of $m,R,z,\chi,\varepsilon$ such that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v66-guard-lipschitz}
 |D_z^\varepsilon(y)-D_z^\varepsilon(y')|
                \le C_g\varepsilon^{-1}|y-y'|
                  \quad (|y|,|y'|<r_\chi).
\end{equation}
If at least one original competing-disk clearance vanishes at $z$,
then on the physical part of $|y|<r_{\chi,\varepsilon}$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v66-seam-saturation}
 D_z^\varepsilon(y)=1,\qquad
 r_{\chi,\varepsilon}=
             \min(r_\chi,\varepsilon/C_g),
\end{equation}
after a fixed enlargement of $C_g$. This permits simultaneous seams,
arbitrarily small nonzero margins and section coincidences.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Theorem~\ref{thm:v66-contact-jets} and the bounded derivative of
$\Phi_z$ give a Lipschitz bound $Cq^{d'_j/2}$ for every flight-$j$
clearance as a function of $y$, where $d'_j=\min(j,m-1-j)$.
Distance from a segment to a fixed competing disk is Lipschitz in
its two endpoints, also when its closest point changes. A fixed finite
candidate list per flight suffices on the continuation, by the same
uniform bound on selected flight lengths. Taking a minimum preserves
this Lipschitz bound. Equivalently, on a near-clearance envelope one
uses the inherited signed margin at image collision $j+1$.

The original flight guard has width
$\varepsilon e'_j=\varepsilon q^{d'_j/4}$. Its Lipschitz bound is
therefore $C\varepsilon^{-1}q^{d'_j/4}$. For factors in $[0,1]$,
the difference of products is at most the sum of their differences.
The sum over $j$ is bounded by a geometric series independent of $m$.
This proves \eqref{eq:v66-guard-lipschitz}. There is no factor for
the number of competing disks, because the minimum is Lipschitz with
the maximum of the individual constants.

If a specified competing clearance is zero at $z$, its absolute value
on the disk of radius $\varepsilon/C_g$ is at most
$\varepsilon e'_j$. On the physical part it is nonnegative, so that
flight guard is zero. Every incidence factor remains one because
$c_j\ge\chi e_j/2\ge2\varepsilon e_j$. Hence the unchanged
first-defect telescoping sum consists entirely of clearance pieces
and equals one. No earlier clearance test or later incidence has been
deleted from its definition.
\end{proof}

Set
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v66-nonlinear-angular}
 A_{z,n,k}^{\varepsilon}(h)=
 \int_0^{2\pi} B_{z,n,k}(\sqrt{2h}\,\omega_\theta)
                  D_z^\varepsilon(\sqrt{2h}\,\omega_\theta)\,d\theta,
             \qquad 0<h<h_\chi.
\end{equation}
Then $0\le\sum_{n,k}A_{z,n,k}^{\varepsilon}(h)\le2\pi$.
The selected-coordinate source coefficient is not a probability;
the physical measure is supplied only by the indicators and the
original normalization $c_*^{-1}$.

\begin{theorem}[Uniform nonlinear physical profile]
\label{thm:v66-nonlinear-profile}
Let $b_{z,n,k}^{\varepsilon,\mathrm{clr}}$ be the roof density of
the original first-clearance source restricted to this Morse disk.
For every $m$, every $\chi$-incident critical word, every
$0<\varepsilon\le\chi/4$, and almost every $0<h<h_\chi$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v66-multiplicative-profile}
 e^{-K_\chi\sqrt{2h}}\frac{J_z}{2\pi}A_{z,n,k}^{\varepsilon}(h)
 \le b_{z,n,k}^{\varepsilon,\mathrm{clr}}(t_z+h)
 \le e^{K_\chi\sqrt{2h}}\frac{J_z}{2\pi}A_{z,n,k}^{\varepsilon}(h).
\end{equation}
In particular
\begin{align}\label{eq:v66-vector-profile-error}
 \sum_{n,k}\left|b_{z,n,k}^{\varepsilon,\mathrm{clr}}(t_z+h)
           -\frac{J_z}{2\pi}A_{z,n,k}^{\varepsilon}(h)\right|
 &\le (e^{K_\chi\sqrt{2h}}-1)
                 \frac{J_z}{2\pi}\sum_{n,k}A_{z,n,k}^{\varepsilon}(h)
 \notag\\
 &\le C K_\chi J_z\sqrt h.
\end{align}
The first bound is relative to the retained physical angular weight,
not to a sum of full-word coefficients on nonphysical directions.
There is no lower bound on any boundary gradient in these statements.
On a clearance seam, for $h<r_{\chi,\varepsilon}^2/2$, the angular
integral is simply the measure of the exact physical and exact-label
angular set, by \eqref{eq:v66-seam-saturation}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
On points where $B_{z,n,k}=1$, the selected initial-state map is the
original orbit map and its Jacobian density is $w_z$. Thus change of
variables, followed by polar coarea, gives exactly
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v66-exact-angular-coarea}
 b_{z,n,k}^{\varepsilon,\mathrm{clr}}(t_z+h)=
 \int_0^{2\pi}\widetilde w_z(\sqrt{2h}\,\omega_\theta)
 B_{z,n,k}(\sqrt{2h}\,\omega_\theta)
 D_z^\varepsilon(\sqrt{2h}\,\omega_\theta)\,d\theta.
\end{equation}
The factor $r$ in polar area cancels the derivative of $r^2/2$.
Insert the relative density bound of
Theorem~\ref{thm:v66-uniform-morse}. Positivity gives
\eqref{eq:v66-multiplicative-profile}. Summing absolute errors uses
\eqref{eq:v66-exact-label-partition}, not the number of labels.
The budget makes $K_\chi r_\chi$ bounded by a fixed constant, so
$e^{K_\chi\sqrt{2h}}-1\le C K_\chi\sqrt h$. This proves the last
line. The saturation assertion was proved on the physical source in
Lemma~\ref{lem:v66-uniform-guard}. All identities use common coarea
representatives, outside a null roof set at each finite count.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Positive coverage without deleting its complement}
For fixed $m,R,\chi$, take all the preceding critical words and restrict
each chart to its own physical itinerary. These restrictions are
disjoint: different words give disjoint regular itinerary events, and
a selected word has at most one normal critical point. The latter
also holds in a physical-side closure with positive selected incidences:
its contact gradient is strictly inward at every disk, so the convex
minimization argument of Lemma~\ref{lem:v44-endpoint-curvature}, now
including the two normal endpoints, gives uniqueness.

Let $b^{\varepsilon,\chi,\mathrm{chart}}_{n,k,m,R}$ be the sum of
the original first-clearance restrictions to these physical chart
pieces. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v66-complete-positive-partition}
 b^{\varepsilon,\mathrm{clr}}_{n,k,m,R}
 =b^{\varepsilon,\chi,\mathrm{chart}}_{n,k,m,R}
                  +b^{\varepsilon,\chi,\mathrm{outside}}_{n,k,m,R},
           \qquad b^{\varepsilon,\chi,\mathrm{outside}}_{n,k,m,R}\ge0.
\end{equation}
The outside term includes critical points below the tapered incidence
envelope, selected-grazing neighborhoods, noncritical roof-rank
components, and every physical state not in the specified disks.
If smaller disks are used, the removed annuli also belong to this term.
Bounded measurable insertions are dominated in variation by their
supremum times this same positive partition.

Theorem~\ref{thm:v66-nonlinear-profile} supplies count-uniform chart,
guard and relative-density constants at a fixed physical width. It
avoids the local affine-gradient denominators of
\eqref{eq:v65-jet-budget} by retaining the nonlinear angular sets.
It does not bound their concentration as the collision count grows,
and it does not assign a height estimate to the outside term.
