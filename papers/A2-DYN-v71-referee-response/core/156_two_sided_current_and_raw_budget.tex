\section{The signed two-sided current and the complete raw budget}
\label{sec:v71-two-sided-current}
For a receiving collar below part of the source interval, radial
births contribute to the deficit as well as radial deaths. We now
identify their exact signed combination. The positive part will be
taken only after the physical weights, the common-roof translations
and all charts of one exact record have been combined.

\subsection{A two-sided BV identity}
Let $0<j<h$, let $L\in BV(0,S)$ for every $S<h$, with finite
variation near zero, and suppose $0\le L\le1$. Write $\mu=DL$
and $\overline L=j^{-1}\int_0^jL(v)\,dv$. Define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v71-collar-distribution}
 F_j(s)=\min\{s/j,1\}\quad(0<s<h),\qquad
 k_j(u,s)=
 \begin{cases}
 F_j(s),&0<s<u,\\
 -(1-F_j(s)),&u<s<h.
 \end{cases}
\end{equation}
The value at $s=u$ is immaterial at continuity points of $L$.
For each $u<h$ the kernel is supported below $\max(u,j)<h$,
so its integral uses a finite signed measure on a fixed compact
radial range together with the finite-variation neighborhood of zero.
No assertion of finite total variation up to $h$ is needed.

\begin{lemma}[Signed collar balance]
\label{lem:v71-bv-balance}
For almost every $u\in(0,h)$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v71-bv-balance}
 L(u)-\overline L=\int_{(0,h)}k_j(u,s)\,d\mu(s).
\end{equation}
In particular
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v71-directed-jordan}
 (L(u)-\overline L)_+
 \le \int_{(0,u)}F_j(s)\,d\mu^+(s)
       +\int_{(u,h)}(1-F_j(s))\,d\mu^-(s).
\end{equation}
Both formulas retain atoms of $DL$. They introduce no endpoint
mass at $0$ or $h$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose a continuity point $u$, and then $S$ with
$\max(u,j)<S<h$. For almost every $v\in(0,j)$, the fundamental
theorem for the precise BV representative expresses $L(u)-L(v)$
as $\mu((v,u))$ if $v<u$, and as $-\mu((u,v))$ if $v>u$.
The omitted discontinuities are countable. Average these identities
in $v$. A point $s<u$ is traversed by a fraction
$|(0,j)\cap(0,s)|/j=F_j(s)$ of the receiving interval. A point
$s>u$ is traversed in the opposite direction by a fraction
$|(0,j)\cap(s,h)|/j=1-F_j(s)$. Fubini for the finite variation
measure on $(0,S)$ proves \eqref{eq:v71-bv-balance}. Its right
side is independent of $S$. The Jordan decomposition and the signs
of $k_j$ give \eqref{eq:v71-directed-jordan}.
\end{proof}

For a jump of size $a$ at $s_0$, its exact contribution is
$aF_j(s_0)$ when $s_0<u$, and $-a(1-F_j(s_0))$ when $s_0>u$.
Thus a positive-radius birth below $u$ has a positive cost. A death
above $u$ also has a positive cost. The reverse orientations give
credits rather than additional loss. In the isolated late-birth
example of Section~\ref{sec:v71-pooled-collar}, take the birth at
$s_0>j$. For $u>s_0$ the current is a positive unit atom,
$\mu^-=0$, but $L(u)-\overline L=1$. A bound using only negative
radial variation cannot control this whole-disk comparison. This does
not alter the valid inner-to-outer formula
\eqref{eq:v70-loss-current}, whose receiving geometry is different.

\subsection{The physically weighted net current}
Return to $h=h_\chi$, $j=h/2$ and the exact label $\lambda$.
Theorem~\ref{thm:v70-physical-current} applies to
$\mu_{z,\lambda}=DL_{z,\lambda}$ on every required radial
range and identifies it as
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v71-physical-current-input}
 \mu_{z,\lambda}
 =-\frac1{2\pi}\pi_\#
    \bigl(n_u\,\mathcal H^1|_{\partial^*E_{z,\lambda}}\bigr).
\end{equation}
Use the actual Boolean physical indicator and attribute a coincident
primitive interface once, as in Section~\ref{sec:v70-radial-current}.
Define the signed scalar function
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v71-net-current}
 \mathsf C_\lambda(t)=
 \sum_{z\in\mathcal Z_\lambda(t)}J_z
       \int_{(0,h)}k_j(t-t_z,s)\,d\mu_{z,\lambda}(s).
\end{equation}
This is a common-roof evaluation of signed radial currents. It is not
asserted to be the distributional derivative of the total source
as a function of $t$; no derivative of the moving active-chart set
is taken.

\begin{theorem}[Exact net-current identity and recovered loss]
\label{thm:v71-net-current}
On common coarea versions,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v71-net-capacity-identity}
 \mathsf C_\lambda(t)=\mathsf X_\lambda(t)-\mathsf Y_\lambda(t).
\end{equation}
Consequently, for every fixed $K\ge1$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v71-net-loss}
 d_{71,K}(t)\le E_\chi
       [\mathsf C_\lambda(t)-(K-1)\mathsf Y_\lambda(t)]_+.
\end{equation}
In particular this retains cancellation between births and deaths,
and between different selected charts of the same exact label. A
sufficient, generally larger, upper bound at $K=1$ is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v71-jordan-source-bound}
 \begin{split}
 d_{71,1}(t)\le E_\chi
 \sum_{z\in\mathcal Z_\lambda(t)}J_z\bigg[
       &\int_{(0,u_z(t))}F_j(s)\,d\mu_{z,\lambda}^+(s)\\
       +&\int_{(u_z(t),h)}(1-F_j(s))\,d\mu_{z,\lambda}^-(s)
                                                    \bigg].
 \end{split}
\end{equation}
The exact formula \eqref{eq:v71-net-loss}, not the sum of absolute
interface currents, is used in the endpoint budget.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply Lemma~\ref{lem:v71-bv-balance} to each active chart at
$u=t-t_z$, multiply by its actual $J_z$, and sum. There are
finitely many charts at a fixed count; their translated exceptional
sets have zero roof measure. The resulting identity is exactly
\eqref{eq:v71-net-capacity-identity}. Apply
\eqref{eq:v71-pooled-deficit} to obtain \eqref{eq:v71-net-loss}.
Finally the positive part of a finite sum is no larger than the sum
of its positive parts. Apply \eqref{eq:v71-directed-jordan} to
get \eqref{eq:v71-jordan-source-bound}. All signs and atoms in
\eqref{eq:v71-physical-current-input} are retained until these
explicit inequalities are taken.
\end{proof}

The common-roof translation is essential. Profiles with different
critical values are evaluated at different radial coordinates. Likewise
the physical weights cannot be suppressed when taking cancellation.
The ordered first-clearance witness and its next-collision mark remain
inside $s_{70},d_{70}$; the recovery does not redefine either witness.

\subsection{An ordered complete-source estimate}
For parameters fixed before the collision limit, put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v71-current-height}
 \mathcal N_{\chi}(K)=\limsup_{m\to\infty}\sup_{R,\lambda}
 m^2\bigl\|[\mathsf C_\lambda-(K-1)\mathsf Y_\lambda]_+
                                                        \bigr\|_\infty.
\end{equation}
Any dependence of the selected source on $\varepsilon$ is retained
at its fixed value; the subscript does not average over that parameter.
This extended nonnegative quantity uses essential height, not mass
or total variation. The current identity defines its finite-count
integrand but does not bound the ordered quantity. We have proved
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v71-complete-chart-height}
 \mathcal H(d_{71,K})\le E_\chi\mathcal N_\chi(K),\qquad
 \mathcal H(b^{\varepsilon,\chi,\mathrm{chart}})
                  \le C'K\chi^6+E_\chi\mathcal N_\chi(K).
\end{equation}
Since $d_{70}$ is a positive part of the complete selected source,
the second bound also bounds its ordered height. In particular an
ordered estimate for the pooled net current is a direct sufficient
estimate for the original angular-loss request, not a change of the
source whose height is sought.

\begin{corollary}[The original pointwise budget with recovered loss]
\label{cor:v71-raw-budget}
For sufficiently large fixed $B$, choose
\[
 \varepsilon(B)=A_0B^{-1/12},\qquad
 \chi(B)=\sqrt{\varepsilon(B)},\qquad K(B)\ge1.
\]
Every choice is made before the collision limsup. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v71-raw-budget}
 \begin{split}
 \mathcal E_M\le{}&C_MB^{-1/192}+C'K(B)B^{-1/4}
                    +C''\mathcal N_{\chi(B)}(K(B))\\
                 &+\mathcal H(b^{\varepsilon(B),\mathrm{inc}})
                    +\mathcal H(e_{70}).
 \end{split}
\end{equation}
Here $e_{70}$ is formed at these same fixed parameters. Thus the
unrestricted two-sided arithmetic raw-density theorem follows if
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v71-endpoint-hypotheses}
 \begin{gathered}
 K(B)B^{-1/4}\longrightarrow0,\qquad
 \mathcal N_{\chi(B)}(K(B))\longrightarrow0,\\
 \mathcal H(b^{\varepsilon(B),\mathrm{inc}})
                    +\mathcal H(e_{70})\longrightarrow0.
 \end{gathered}
\end{equation}
The finite arithmetic transition kernel and its zero classes are
unchanged.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Insert \eqref{eq:v71-complete-source} and the unchanged incidence
source into Theorem~\ref{thm:v51-positive-error}. Use
\eqref{eq:v71-pooled-height} and \eqref{eq:v71-net-loss}.
The parameter choices give $\chi^6=A_0^3B^{-1/4}$ and a
uniform bound for $E_\chi$. This proves \eqref{eq:v71-raw-budget}.
Only after obtaining the fixed-band limsup may $B$ tend to infinity.
Under \eqref{eq:v71-endpoint-hypotheses} every term vanishes.
\end{proof}

For instance $K=1$ retains the controlled $O(B^{-1/4})$ rate;
$K(B)=B^\eta$, $0<\eta<1/4$, trades it for
$O(B^{\eta-1/4})$ and tests only excess current above the larger
receiving reserve. Neither choice depends on $m$. The positive part
in \eqref{eq:v71-current-height} can be much smaller than the
physically weighted sum of Jordan variations. It need not be small
merely because each word has finite perimeter.

No unconditional estimate for the last three quantities in
\eqref{eq:v71-raw-budget} is proved here. The outside source still
contains failed taper, selected grazing, noncritical roof-rank pieces
and uncovered physical states. The bound $CA^m\varepsilon$ in
Corollary~\ref{cor:v61-incidence-height-gain} remains a finite-count
estimate. It cannot be substituted for its fixed-band ordered height.
No roof set is removed and no cutoff is chosen exponentially in $m$.

The same-roof conclusions retain their original numerator and
positive-reference hypotheses. Scalar closure would remove their
remaining scalar denominator obstruction, but capacity recovery alone
is not a new path-valued local law or a forward essential-likelihood
theorem. No conditional law is defined on a zero reference class.

The source-height argument also remains distinct from a mixing local
limit theorem tested against fixed intervals. In particular Definition
2.2 and Remark 2.5 of \cite{DN} concern continuous compactly supported
additive tests and suitable continuity sets. Those statements do not
bound \eqref{eq:v71-current-height}. The present proof uses the
already established positive physical collar and the BV current on the
same Lorentz record, rather than differentiating an interval theorem.
