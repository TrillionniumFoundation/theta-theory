\section{The strengthened mathematical objective}\label{sec:scope}
An experiment is specified by a deterministic mechanical program, a
preparation of its system and apparatus, a readout, and a class of admissible
commands. A probability law is the push-forward of that preparation. A
change of probability density is not automatically an admissible command.
Likewise, retaining all future tests gives a sufficient description, but not
an effective reduction of the description. We address both issues in one
explicit laboratory.

The review of English edition 2.0 accepted the local collision-tube
calculation under its stated assumptions, but identified a missing substantive
connection between that calculation, actual instruments, parameter-family
prediction, and resource control. Edition 3 supplied that connection through
a positive polynomial likelihood algebra. The present revision retains its
complete derivation and establishes structural attainability criteria,
quantitative approximation, and a strictly positive adaptive advantage.
It does not appeal to an unspecified collection of attainable laws or an
unverified many-collision derivative hierarchy.

\subsection{What the apparatus counts}
One unit of resource is one \emph{ambient placement attempt}, including an
unsuccessful attempt. Initially there are $N$ independent cartridges, each
with a uniformly prepared placement coordinate, a velocity angle, and two
uniform apparatus variables. Their preparation does not depend on the
unknown radius. A hard-contact placement guard checks whether the proposed
position is accessible. The guard is part of the physical interaction with
the table, not a numerical comparison with an unknown parameter supplied to
the controller. Subsequent cartridges use fresh prepared coordinates. The
experiment is thus a finite laboratory with repeated intervention, not one
uninterrupted billiard orbit. No mixing or free equilibrium reset is used to
justify independence.

The resource is the number of attempts, not an elapsed-time constraint,
entropy, mechanical work, or an inferred minimum energy. The commanded $T$
is the actual physical observation window, not a reparametrized collision clock.
The ideal preparation and detector are specified in Section~\ref{sec:lab}.
The theorem concerns their explicitly declared classical hybrid apparatus
class. Counting energy or constructing a quantum implementation would define
a different resource model.

\begin{theorem}[Operational positive-polynomial closure]\label{thm:main}
Fix the radius interval $I=[9/20,47/100]$, a compact mode set
\[
 \cA=[T_-,T_+]\times[0,E]\times\mathbb S^1,
 \qquad 0<T_-\le T_+<3/50,\qquad E(47/100)^2<1,
\]
a gate bound $0\le\eta<1/2$, a probability prior $\pi_0$ on $I$, and an
integer budget $N$. For the apparatus of Section~\ref{sec:lab}:
\begin{enumerate}
\item The raw one-cartridge readout has the positive cubic-polynomial
kernel \eqref{eq:raw-kernel}, including placement failure. Every measurable
gate $g$ with $\eta\le g\le1-\eta$ is implemented by the specified uniform
comparator. The resulting family is exactly \eqref{eq:gated-kernel}; the
same $g$ must work at every radius.
\item For every adaptive sequence of modes and gates, after $n\le N$
cartridges, at every history with nonzero likelihood, the full radius
likelihood, up to a radius-independent scalar,
is a positive polynomial of degree at most $3n$. It has a normalized
Bernstein coefficient state with $3n+1$ entries, and the update is the
positive convolution \eqref{eq:bern-product}. Rejected reports and
unsuccessful placements are included in this statement. When a mode with
$\epsilon>0$ is available, $3n$ is also a sharp lower bound on the number
of free coordinates in a continuous exact likelihood state.
\item This state and the remaining budget suffice for the terminal-decision
and observable-reward problem in Section~\ref{sec:control}. Optimization over
all measurable gates reduces exactly to the compact body in $\R^5$
\eqref{eq:gate-body}. Its support function is \eqref{eq:support}; every
feasible body point has a measurable apparatus realization. The Bellman
recursion \eqref{eq:bellman} attains its optimum with a Borel feedback rule.
\item For every fixed admissible adaptive controller, its length-$N$
readout law is strongly $C^r$ in signed-measure variation for every finite
$r$, with the explicit bounds in Theorem~\ref{thm:response}. Its density is
a polynomial of degree at most $3N$, so its derivatives above that degree
vanish. Likelihoods, failure posteriors, and control refer to precisely the
same readout channel.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Proposition~\ref{prop:raw} proves the mechanical kernel and its uniform
bounds. Theorem~\ref{thm:attainable} proves necessity, sufficiency, and
adaptive pasting for the apparatus class. Theorems~\ref{thm:filter} and
\ref{thm:general} give the positive likelihood reduction, and
Theorem~\ref{thm:dimension} proves its sharp continuous dimension, including the
explicit coefficient verification for this table. Theorem~\ref{thm:control}
proves the finite-coordinate optimization, measurable realization, and
attainment. Theorem~\ref{thm:response} proves the fixed-controller response.
Theorem~\ref{thm:boundary} includes the complete gate interval $[0,1]$,
without an artificial acceptance floor. All model estimates used by these
results are calculated below.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Structural, policy, and robust performance strengthening]\label{thm:strengthening}
The preceding apparatus and its likelihood construction have the following
additional properties.
\begin{enumerate}
\item For a general positive polynomial experiment, a positive-definite
coefficient Gram matrix is necessary and sufficient for an open attainable
failure family. An explicit right inverse gives a ball of physically
realizable coefficients, stable under quantified same-degree perturbations.
For degree $q$ this implies the sharp intrinsic continuous likelihood-state
dimension $qn$ at budget $n$.
\item Every finite-budget optimum has a Borel endpoint-gate realization.
For the last cartridge, if rewards are constant on each raw tag, the collision
acceptance set can be chosen as at most two arcs, even with a compact infinite
terminal-decision space. Under angular Lipschitz bounds, $J$ intervals per
circle suffice for the explicit $O(N e^{N|\lambda|B}/J)$ value error below.
\item Every uniformly positive smooth normalized collision-mark family has
a positive, normalized, physically realizable Bernstein surrogate. Its
policy-regret rates at fixed budget are $O(m^{-1})$ under a uniform
second-derivative bound and $O(m^{-1/2})$ under a Lipschitz bound; the exact
dependence on the budget and payoff oscillation is stated below.
Exact polynomial closure is not assumed of the true perturbed detector.
\item There is an explicitly specified instance with $N=2$, the full gate
class, nonzero-amplitude modes, and a full-support prior on $I$, for which
adaptive control exceeds every nonadaptive control by more than $11/10000$.
The conclusion includes arbitrary Borel gates and precommitted randomized
commands, not just a sampled finite menu.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Theorems~\ref{thm:rank-criterion} and \ref{thm:q-dimension} prove the first
claim; Theorems~\ref{thm:endpoint}, \ref{thm:two-arcs}, and
\ref{thm:lookup-rate} prove the second. Theorems~\ref{thm:model-stability}
and \ref{thm:smooth-surrogate} prove the third, and
Theorem~\ref{thm:adaptive-advantage} proves the fourth. The numerical inequality
in the last claim is enclosed by exact rational arithmetic after an analytic
reduction of the entire comparator class.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Contribution and closest established mechanisms}
The flux-to-phase-volume calculation is classical; Golse's free-flight
formula and Chernov's mean-free-path analysis are the relevant comparison
points \cite{Golse,Chernov}. The change of variables is reproved because its
normalization and coverage determine the apparatus kernel. It is not
presented as a new general principle of billiards.

Predictive representations based on action-conditional observations already
have a substantial theory \cite{LittmanSutton}. Our state is instead an
explicit algebraic representation of a continuous unknown-parameter
likelihood. Its dimension grows linearly with a prescribed finite budget;
it is not a horizon-independent finite-state model. A matching $3n$ local
dimension lower bound makes the continuous state count sharp for the
full command family, without claiming a minimal linear predictive rank.
The positivity-preserving representation and exact gate/failure update are
what make the reduction operational rather than an array of all tests.

Bernstein arithmetic, including polynomial multiplication, is established
\cite{FaroukiRajan}. The update in Theorem~\ref{thm:filter} uses that algebra;
it is not claimed as a new multiplication algorithm or a universal
floating-point conditioning guarantee. The beta-mixture connection is also
established: Petrone and Wasserman study Bernstein priors and posterior
consistency for density estimation \cite{PetroneWasserman}. That problem is
distinct from an exact likelihood for a single static physical radius.
Theorem~\ref{thm:rank-criterion} instead identifies when the permitted
physical gate family has full polynomial rank, and quantifies its stability.

The support function of a moment body is a classical convex-geometric
mechanism related to lift zonoids \cite{KulikTymoshkevych}. We do not claim
that mechanism as new. Its use here eliminates the infinite-dimensional
gate from an actually realizable feedback problem while retaining the
information in a rejected outcome. Adaptive censoring is itself established
\cite{RenEtAl}; the result here is not a new claim that censoring can be
adaptive. The endpoint argument in Theorem~\ref{thm:endpoint} credits the
current referee report. The two-arc result, implementation bound, and the
certified full-class adaptive separation supply the more specific control
content that existence alone did not provide.

Posterior continuity is a substantive issue in general partially observed
control \cite{FeinbergEtAl}. That reference treats expected total costs; it
is not imported as a proof for our multiplicative payoff. In this model
continuity follows from explicit positive polynomial coefficients and the
zero-evidence weighted-continuation argument. The proposed contribution is
now the structural and performance theorem with these verified apparatus
inputs, rather than the juxtaposition of classical identities. No exhaustive
priority or journal-acceptance certification is inferred from this targeted
comparison.
