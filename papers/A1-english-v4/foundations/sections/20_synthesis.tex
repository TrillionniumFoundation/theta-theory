\section{Synthesis and exact interfaces for the paper series}\label{sec:synthesis}
\begin{theorem}[One whole-preparation mechanical--information construction]\label{thm:synthesis}
Fix the triangular Lorentz family $R\in[9/20,47/100]$, natural unit-speed equilibrium preparation, and $0<T<3/50$. The following statements hold for this same mechanical experiment.
\begin{enumerate}
\item The complete zero-or-one-collision record has the positive, normalized assembly \eqref{eq:global-tube}; its strong finite-order distributional radius derivatives are given by Theorem~\ref{thm:global-response}.
\item The collision bit has probability $p_R=2RT/(A_\Lambda-\pi R^2)$, identifies $R$, and has positive finite Fisher information without added measurement noise.
\item The source $\xi C_R$ has exact pressure $\log(1-p_R+p_Re^\xi)$ and a unique unconstrained source for any target hit fraction in $(0,1)$.
\item Direction-only preparation has the nonzero deficit \eqref{eq:direction-gap} for $\xi\ne0$, while a parameter-independent, completed-bit acceptance protocol realizes the entire tilted family with the finite-trial failure bound of Theorem~\ref{thm:lorentz-selection}.
\item Deterministic finite readouts, or specified apparatus channels, produce chronological experiments to which the corresponding predictor, information, and resource theorems apply. Continuous prediction uses the separately verified hypotheses of Theorem~\ref{thm:continuous-state}, not merely the existence of a continuous-valued readout.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The first assertion combines the exact all-incidence tube substitution with strong differentiation of its fixed integration domains and moving disk term. The second follows from Corollary~\ref{cor:global-hit} and Theorem~\ref{thm:binary-info}. Since $C_R$ is binary, summing its two outcomes gives the pressure and its strictly positive Hessian; the logit formula in Section~\ref{sec:operational} gives the inverse. Proposition~\ref{prop:direction-prep} proves the restricted value, while Theorem~\ref{thm:lorentz-selection} constructs the postselected record law by a causal, radius-independent acceptance rule. Finally the path law is an instance of Theorem~\ref{thm:instrument-path}; subsequent state constructions are applied only with their stated alphabet, density, likelihood, and resource conditions. No step replaces the radius by an unrelated sensor parameter or changes the physical clock.
\end{proof}

\subsection{What is unconditional within a stated model, and what is an interface}
The finite preparation formulas, entropy identities, instrument composition, predictor constructions, regular-chamber response, prepared Lorentz event, and whole-preparation short-horizon Lorentz assembly are proved in this paper under their printed hypotheses. The exact whole-preparation calculation is a genuine model verification rather than an invocation of an unnamed spectral package.

The complete-preparation Cauchy theorem, experimental approximation estimates, and joint current--clock pressure formulas are transfer results. A new model must establish their inputs. In particular, the long-time response criterion is not verified here for the full many-collision Lorentz family; the general hard-sphere genealogy and hydrodynamic limits are not consequences of a one-collision geometry calculation.

\subsection{Conclusion}
The foundation is a causal mechanical experiment with explicitly attainable operations, not a pre-existing nonlinear expectation. Path statistics come from preparation. Selection and observation are distinct operations. Effective states retain future experimental responses and admissible resources. Collision derivatives require physical boundary assembly, not pointwise differentiation outside a null set. The whole-preparation Lorentz formula shows how a suitable geometric assembly can remove inverse-grazing singularities in a definite regime. Broader regimes require new estimates of the same actual objects, not a broader adjective attached to the setup.
