## Scope of the revision

The original monomial theorem still permits any fixed full-support prior and compact exponent chambers containing future additive collisions. The new circle theorem assumes Haar prior, exactly known contrast, a fixed horizon and a uniformly positive but sufficiently small contrast interval. At zero contrast its physical information state is constant. These are separate model hypotheses, not reductions of the original theorem.
