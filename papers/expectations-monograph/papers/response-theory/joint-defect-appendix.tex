\begin{corollary*}[Joint primitive-face and primitive-periodic split]
Fix regular periodic orbits
\(\mathcal O_1,\ldots,\mathcal O_N\) disjoint from the compact moving-face
subarc \(K\) and its two selected preimage tubes.  Let
\(\mathfrak P_{\mathscr F,\mathcal O}\) be the completion of the smooth core
in the norm
\[
 \|h\|_{\mathfrak P_{\mathscr F}}
 +\max_{1\le j\le N}\|h\|_{C^0(U_j)},
\]
where the mutually disjoint regular neighbourhoods \(U_j\) contain
\(\mathcal O_j\) and avoid the selected face tubes.  Define
\[
 \mathcal D^P_{\Gamma,K}h
 :=\dot G_0[\mathcal A_{+,0}h-\mathcal A_{-,0}h]|_K,
 \qquad
 \mathcal V_Nh
 :=\left(\sum_{z\in\mathcal O_j}h(z)\right)_{j=1}^N.
\]
Then
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:joint_primitive_face_periodic_map}
 \mathcal J^P_{\Gamma,K,N}
 :=(\mathcal D^P_{\Gamma,K},\mathcal V_N):
 \mathfrak P_{\mathscr F,\mathcal O}longrightarrow
 \mathcal T_K^r(I)\oplus\mathbb R^N
\end{equation}
is a split surjection.

Indeed, choose the tubular extension
\(E_{\Gamma,K}:\mathcal T_K^r(I)\to
\mathfrak P_{\mathscr F,\mathcal O}\) from
\eqref{eq:weighted_tubular_extension} with support disjoint from every
\(U_j\), including the inverse of \(\dot G_0\) and the nonvanishing branch
multiplier.  Thus
\[
 \mathcal D^P_{\Gamma,K}E_{\Gamma,K}=I,
 \qquad \mathcal V_NE_{\Gamma,K}=0.
\]
For each orbit choose a smooth bump supported in \(U_j\), equal to the
reciprocal of the orbit cardinality at its orbit points, and zero on all
other selected orbits.  These bumps define a bounded map
\(B_N^P:\mathbb R^N\to\mathfrak P_{\mathscr F,\mathcal O}\) with
\[
 \mathcal V_NB_N^P=I,
 \qquad \mathcal D^P_{\Gamma,K}B_N^P=0.
\]
Consequently
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:joint_primitive_face_periodic_right_inverse}
 (\phi,c)\longmapsto E_{\Gamma,K}\phi+B_N^Pc
\end{equation}
is a bounded right inverse of
\eqref{eq:joint_primitive_face_periodic_map}.  The common zero set is
complemented and its quotient contains
\(\mathcal T_K^r(I)\oplus\mathbb R^N\).

This is a statement about independently prescribed values of a primitive.
It is not a joint surjection for the orbit sums of its source.  If
\(g=(I-\Ltransfer_0)h\) and \(h\) is single-valued on a regular periodic
orbit, then
\[
 \sum_{z\in\mathcal O}g(z)=0
\]
identically.  Hence the earlier source-level face--periodic surjection would
contradict the Ward telescoping identity and is not claimed.
\end{corollary*}
