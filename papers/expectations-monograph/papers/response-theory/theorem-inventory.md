## Obstructions and deliberate non-claims

- The fixed-atlas block-flux display is an unnumbered graph-norm remark, not a
  chart-independent Banach-module theorem or S1.
- The isolated second radial letter leaves the nonintegrable `u^{-3}` flux
  coefficient; higher radial results must be complete-assembly specific.
- On that weighted primitive/Poisson-pullback domain, the localized moving-face gluing map
  is surjective onto smooth face profiles; graph-domain sources passing first
  order form an
  infinite-codimension, locally complemented kernel outside the symmetry
  class.  After recovery
  through order m-1, the admissible next source jets for the order-m face test
  form an affine infinite-codimension slice.
- The induced map is first split surjective on a Poisson-independent weighted
  local-potential class.  It is also split surjective on the pullback
  correlation graph class.  The latter passing set is closed, complemented, and
  infinite-codimensional, with open dense complement in the graph topology;
  the affine cascade
  repeats on every subsequent observable jet.
- The graph class also carries a rooted `W_r`-weighted source norm.  It
  controls `E phi-L E phi` through its depth-zero and depth-one roots and
  replaces the false unweighted branch norm of the transfer image; regular
  periodic evaluation is continuous in this topology.
- The split surjection persists after restriction to every irreducible
  finite-group isotypic space on a free face orbit, by the corresponding
  central idempotent.  Hence parity does
  not reduce the defect range; only a pointwise stabilizer identity could
  force it to vanish.
- No nontrivial finite physical torus isometry fixes an open regular grazing
  face pointwise: translations have no fixed point, rotations have only
  isolated fixed points, and a reflection-fixed flight meets a
  reflection-invariant strictly convex boundary normally rather than
  tangentially.  Thus the abstract stabilizer exception is absent physically.
- A continuous local Poisson/Ward primitive forces the source sum to vanish on
  every regular periodic orbit.  The evaluation map on any `N` disjoint
  periodic orbits is onto `R^N`, so local primitives face an independent
  unbounded-codimension constraint.  A conserved local charge is constant;
  its nonconserved increment belongs to the exact-current benchmark.
- The auxiliary primitive theorem proves that primitive face data and finitely
  many primitive-orbit values are onto their direct sum, using disjoint
  tubular and orbit-bump extensions.  Source-orbit sums are explicitly not
  claimed independent on a local-Ward domain; they telescope to zero.
- The local-Ward proposition additionally classifies finite-memory label
  candidates on a finite strongly connected regular itinerary graph: vanishing
  of every cycle sum forces the edge rule to be a vertex-potential difference,
  hence an exact cylinder current.
- Point atoms do not belong to the ordinary Demers--Zhang weak space, and a
  periodic atom prevents generic source decay.
- No nonconjugate radial prescribed correlation density is claimed beyond the
  exact-current and differentiated-invariance benchmarks.
- No moving-family flow graph domain, symbol theorem, or regularity budget is
  exported.
