## Closed referee-level correctness issues

- The dynamical/response topology mismatch is removed.  Every unconditional
  gap, Neumann series, strong primitive, Taylor remainder, and base reset is
  on the Demers--Zhang bundle `B_U`.  The positive flux-seed scale `D_U` is not
  claimed invariant.  A separate dynamically cut weighted primitive domain is
  anchored by `L^infinity`; the manuscript proves its closability,
  branch-cocycle-weighted one-step/iterated trace estimate,
  direct-limit refinement and chart equivalence, tubular-extension orbit
  estimate, and radial invariant-jet/fixed-length assembly membership.  It no
  longer claims transfer invariance of this domain.
- The radial reset no longer inherits arbitrary-seed R3.  The physical-cutoff
  multiplier is now defined on regular, face, point/jet, and terminal
  coefficients; it is bounded for each fixed cutoff, preserves the realization
  kernel, descends to the quotient, and is compatible with the countable
  completion.  Historical truncation and post-assembly cutoff are therefore
  connected by an exact commutation identity, and the scalar tail follows from
  the explicit radial strong current.
- The auxiliary block-flux statement has been downgraded to a fixed-atlas
  graph-norm remark.  It no longer claims chart independence, completion,
  transfer invariance, next-event closure, or S1.
- A nonzero physical correlation density is prescribed before its Poisson
  primitive for common rigid translations.  The exact conjugacy itself is the
  Banach-bundle trivialization, so the fixed-fiber operator, source, and
  primitive are constant: both Poisson sources, terminal data, and the strong
  remainder vanish identically.  The statement is now a consistency
  proposition, not a moving-face recovery theorem.
- Flow background is no longer interleaved with the collision/spectral proof.
  It is compressed and placed after the actual and conditional collision-map
  statements.
- The actual radial theorem now appears before the appendices; auxiliary no-go
  material is in Appendix A and the conditional S1--S3 propagation theorem is
  isolated in Appendix B.

## Remaining significance boundary

The prescribed source belongs to an exact-conjugacy symmetry class.  It is a
valid consistency example, but it does not solve the genuinely
nonconjugate radial prescribed-source problem.  Outside the symmetry class,
on the actual weighted primitive/Poisson-pullback domains, the localized moving-face gluing
map has arbitrary smooth trace range and
hence an infinite-codimension, locally complemented kernel.  The same affine
obstruction recurs for
the next source jet at every recovered finite order, while the isolated second
radial letter has a `u^{-3}` obstruction, and recursive source-specific
recovery is still open.
The obstruction first acts on a Poisson-independent weighted local-potential
class.  On the pullback correlation graph class the passing set is complemented and
infinite-codimensional with open dense complement in that graph topology, and
the affine cascade repeats on every graph-domain observable jet.  No Baire
claim is made for radial or all branchwise smooth observables.
The observable graph additionally carries a rooted `W_r`-weighted source
norm.  The two-root presentation of `E phi-L E phi` proves the right-inverse
bound without reviving the discarded unweighted high-strip estimate.
The same split range survives every irreducible finite-group isotypic
restriction on a free moving-face orbit, ruling out parity and higher
dimensional representation constraints as generic Ward mechanisms.
Strict convexity then rules out an open pointwise-fixed physical grazing face,
closing the only abstract finite-symmetry stabilizer exception.
Local Noether primitives are separately constrained by all periodic-orbit
sums; these evaluation maps have arbitrarily large finite rank, and a true
invariant charge gives only the zero current.
The auxiliary joint result is now correctly primitive-level: a face profile
and finitely many primitive-orbit values are simultaneously realizable by
disjoint bounded extensions.  It is not stated for source-orbit sums, since
those sums telescope to zero whenever the primitive is single-valued.
The same local-Ward proposition gives a self-contained graph-cohomology
classification for finite-memory label rules: all periodic sums vanish only
for cylinder coboundaries, which are already exact currents.

Accordingly the paper is strongest as a rigorous source-module, reset, and
obstruction theorem with one explicit symmetry consistency source and a
nonconjugate invariant-source radial model.  A referee can still regard the
absence of a nonconjugate prescribed radial correlation source as a
significance limitation.  The previous R3 dependency leak, cutoff-completion
gap, strong-transport overclaim, and graph-domain theorem overclaim have been
removed from the stated results.
