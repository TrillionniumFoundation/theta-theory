## Invariants and non-claims

- Artificial faces cancel before physical assembly.
- The radial reset proof does not call the R3 block-factorization lemma.
- A physical cutoff is common to incident labels; label-dependent strip
  truncations are not silently exchanged with assembly.
- The block-flux seminorm is a fixed-atlas remark only and is not S1.
- No weak trace source or point atom is sent through the ordinary strong
  density resolvent.
- The weighted primitive trace domain is anchored by `L^infinity`, so a
  sequence converging to zero in the bulk cannot retain a nonzero one-sided
  trace.  Backward singularity/homogeneity cuts are canonically labelled; the
  actual one-step `C^r` pullback norms form a multiplicative cocycle weight;
  artificial refinements enter an isometric direct limit.  The iterated
  weighted trace estimate, tubular-extension orbit estimate, and radial
  fixed-length memberships are proved.  Transfer invariance of this primitive
  domain is not asserted.
- No raw historical terminal state is identified with the regular reset.
- The prescribed correlation source is fixed before its canonical Poisson
  primitive is formed.
- The localized moving-face gluing map realizes every smooth face profile;
  the first-order admissible source kernel is therefore infinite-codimensional
  and locally complemented by an explicit bounded right inverse.
- The order-m face coefficient varies as m G delta g_(m-1), so the
  infinite-codimension obstruction recurs at every recovered finite order.
- Before introducing any Poisson inverse, the same defect operator is split
  surjective on the weighted local-potential class.  After composing with the
  centered correlation-density map, it is also split on the pullback graph
  class.  In that graph topology the first-order passing set has open dense complement,
  and the order-m variation is
  m H delta a_(m-1).
- The pullback graph is intersected with a rooted `W_r`-weighted source space.
  The observable right inverse is bounded through the explicit presentation
  `E phi-L E phi`; no uniform unweighted branch derivative bound is used.
- Finite geometric symmetry does not create a hidden cancellation: on any
  free face orbit, the central idempotent of every irreducible representation
  sends the bounded right inverse into the corresponding isotypic subspace
  while retaining split surjectivity.
- The possible pointwise-stabilizer exception is eliminated for physical
  finite torus isometries: a reflection-fixed trajectory meets an invariant
  strictly convex boundary normally, so it cannot form an open grazing face.
- A single-valued continuous local Ward primitive implies zero sum on every
  regular periodic orbit.  Independent bump functions show that `N` selected
  orbit sums have rank `N`, independently of the face-gluing conditions.
- At primitive level the joint map consisting of the localized face profile
  and `N` primitive-orbit values has an explicit bounded right inverse from
  disjoint extensions.  Source-orbit sums are not independently prescribed
  on the local-Ward subdomain; they vanish by telescoping.
- On any finite strongly connected regular itinerary higher-block graph, zero
  sums on all periodic words imply that a finite-memory label rule is the
  difference of a vertex potential at consecutive states.  This closes the
  finite-symbolic-rule route without invoking a Poisson inverse.
- The rigid-translation proposition uses its exact conjugacy as the Banach
  trivialization.  It asserts no strong differentiability of a general
  transported anisotropic primitive and is not a nonconjugate radial theorem.
- The isolated second radial letter has the explicit `u^{-3}` obstruction.
- No moving-family flow graph domain, symbol estimate, or regularity-loss
  theorem is exported.
- Finite-group, physical-stabilizer, periodic/Noether, joint-defect, and
  finite-memory proofs are collected in the auxiliary appendix rather than the
  main radial line.
