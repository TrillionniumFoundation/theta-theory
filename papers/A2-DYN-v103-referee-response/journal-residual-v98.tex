\subsection{The residual retained from the preceding revision}
The physical comparison flow $\widehat\Phi$ crosses section-decision
walls on the regular endpoint collar $|p_0|,|p_m|<19/20$, and stops
at true physical or backward critical ends. It has the same roof and
transverse coordinates as the original flow while it stays in the
original exact event. Its complete angular density is $\widehat A$.
Only comparison times returning to the identical exact event are
counted in the capped clock
\[
 c_r(x)=\frac1r\int_{(-r,r)\cap(-\widehat\tau^-,\widehat\tau^+)}
 \one_\Pi(\widehat\Phi_sx)
 \min\{1,\widehat A(\widehat\Phi_sx)/\widehat A(x)\}\,ds.
\]
For $0<q\le1/2$, $d=2qr$ and $a>0$, set
\[
 P=\{\tau^->2d\}\cup
     \{\tau^-\le2d,\ z\ge a,\ \tau^+>d\},\qquad
 Y=\Pi\cap\{c_r<q\}\cap P^c.
\]
The lifetimes in $P$ belong to the original stopped exact event,
not the enlarged comparison domain. Write
$Y^\varepsilon(t)=\int_{F=t}W^\varepsilon\one_Y\,d\mu_t$.

\begin{inputstatement}[Recovered failure source and scalar reduction]
\label{input:v98-recovered}
The capped-clock and running-compression theorems of retained modules
232--234 give, with every parameter fixed before the collision limit,
\[
 \mathcal H_M(b^\varepsilon-Y^\varepsilon)
 \le Cq^{-1}\{\varepsilon^{1/16}+\varepsilon^{-1}\sqrt r\}+Cqr.
\]
The support is
$Y\subset\{\tau^-\le4qr,z<a\}\cup\{\ell\le6qr\}$.
The collar has no exit at widths
$r\le r_{\rm col}=R_-^2/(800\Lambda_\circ)$.
For $q=1/8$, $r=c\varepsilon(B)^4\le r_{\rm col}$ and
$a=\sqrt{qr}$, the unchanged scalar objective satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v98-retained-reduction}
       \mathcal E_M\le\mathcal H_M(Y^{\varepsilon(B)})
                                      +C_MB^{-1/192}.
\end{equation}
No residual-height decay is part of this input.
\end{inputstatement}
The support and the low-clock condition will both be used below.
Dropping the latter would lose the density-decrease mechanism on the
long critical component.
