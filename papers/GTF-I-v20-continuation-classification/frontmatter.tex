\begin{abstract}
We study finite marked causal experiments with separately charged simulator
and auditor memory. At a training--validation cut, a decision continuation
spectrum characterizes the minimum retained alphabet for a strict expected
score, with a uniform finite-sample approximation. For the marked private
product family arising from a prepared two-sphere collision, its one-state
and two-state values are exactly $1/8$ and $3/8$. Three retained states are
necessary and sufficient to certify score greater than $2/5$ throughout
the stated physical parameter range. A single rational $402$-trial audit
attains this score; its informative training segment has an exact
simultaneous stochastic continuation profile and its complete scheduled
implementation uses fewer than $2^{16}$ states. A weighted separation
obstruction converts missing continuation states into distribution-sensitive
regret for finite-valued decisions. The same physical certificate and the
bounded entropic backward representation are transported along moving
collision paths by a common-preparation Gaussian comparison. The full
comparison, realization, sampling, effectiveness and conditional-transport
proofs are retained, with private, hidden and visible randomization
accounted for separately.
\end{abstract}
