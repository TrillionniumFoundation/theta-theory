## Exact contribution under review

The statistical novelty claim is specific: minimax support contact and stationarity restrict Bayes-indifferent response coordinates, producing a face obstruction for every positive realization of the optimal saddle set. In the marked family this yields exact decision width five and exact forward serial validation peak twelve. Neither number is obtained by ordinary rank or by minimizing the deterministic compiler of one selected rule. A three-event Bayes-optimal tie rule provides an explicit reason the contact constraints cannot be omitted.

The all-N empirical estimate is an inherited classical mechanism, already present in the repository's decision-spectrum theorem. The new statements localize every least-favorable prior, derive the marked closest-experiment set and quadratic growth directly, and give a W2 convergence bound for symmetric optimizing priors. The rates are upper bounds, not claimed sharp asymptotics.
