## 2. Substantive response: a noncommuting finite outcome alphabet

The new domain is the balanced body of ordered POVMs

\[
\mathfrak P_{d,k}=\{(E_j)_{j=1}^k:\ \sum_jE_j=I_d,\quad I_d/(2k)\preceq E_j\preceq3I_d/(2k)\},\qquad k\ge2.
\]

This is a full-dimensional family of real affine dimension `p=(k−1)d²`, not just simultaneously diagonalizable tuples. The device still consumes its input and returns only the ordered classical label. The complete binary-body results retain their original statements and wider ranges.

**Geometry.** `thm:multioutcomemetric82` proves

\[
\frac1{128}\min\{1,\sqrt N\max_j\|E_j-F_j\|_{op}\}
\le D_N^{na}\le D_N\le\min\{2,2k\sqrt N\max_j\|E_j-F_j\|_{op}\}.
\]

For each segment effect `A_j`, the ODE `dot X_j=X_j A_j^{-1}H_j/2` gives `X_j*X_j=A_j`, `X_j*dot X_j=H_j/2`, and total `W*dot W=0` because `sum H_j=0`. The proof cancels cross-slot derivatives for common coherent adaptive testers. A single-label coarse-graining supplies the nonadaptive lower and also permits arbitrary legal centres in the entropy converse. There is no shared-eigenbasis assumption.

**Entropy and finite exact coding.** `thm:multioutcomeentropy82`, `lem:multigrid82` and `thm:multioutcomecode82` prove the dimension-uniform order

\[
\log_2\mathcal C_{N,k}(\delta)=\frac p2\log_2N+p\log_2(1/\delta)+O(p\log(k+1)).
\]

Inward displacement before rounding, followed by defining the last effect as the exact residual, preserves both spectral bounds and normalization. A strict-separation greedy dictionary uses exact rational PSD predicates, with equality handled explicitly. Volume packing takes place in the genuine affine norm ball. The final word transmits an index into a public, target-independent dictionary; its enormous reconstruction work is not counted as payload.

**Joint learning and description.** `thm:multioutcomelearning82` gives

\[
 cN\delta^{-2}[d^2+d\log(1/\eta)]
 \le M^*_{d,k}
 \le Ck^3N\delta^{-2}[d^2+d\log(k/\eta)]
\]

for `d,N>=1`, `k>=2`, `0<delta<=2^-24`, `0<eta<=1/8`. Hence the dependence is sharp jointly in `d,N,delta,eta` for every fixed `k`; the growing-`k` upper factor is explicitly not called sharp. Public label randomization produces `I/4+E_j/2`, to which the inherited rational binary learner applies. A complete finite tuple search restores legality simultaneously. The good-event errors are `3delta/8` before coding and at most `5delta/8` after coding, with no further device calls. The same optimal-order index length is attained. The lower proof embeds the binary interior through split labels, controls the odd-alphabet erasure exactly on retained references, and transfers the full coherent adaptive lower bound, including its `d log(1/eta)` contribution.

**Finite certificate.** `thm:multicertificate82` transfers complete rational net inequalities with radius `a`, failure `alpha` to every real tuple with radius `a+r` and failure `alpha+Mk r/2`. Its proof uses the normalized multi-outcome Choi record and transfers one fixed set of good labels, rather than assuming continuity of the decision boundary. An independently specified physical output-law error must be added. The actual-control learning theorem uses the already budgeted inherited binary procedure.

The one-use tomography upper primitive is credited to Mele–Bittel. Their general theorem already covers finite-output channels. The new result is not advertised as the first learner of multi-outcome POVMs. The comparison also substitutes the appropriate future-loss accuracy into Zambrano–Ramos-Calderer–Kueng's stated estimator guarantee. The primary versions were rechecked: arXiv:2512.10214v3 (15 June 2026) and Quantum 10, 2162 (15 July 2026).
