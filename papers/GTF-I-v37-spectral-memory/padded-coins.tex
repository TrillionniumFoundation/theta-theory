\section{Fixed-duration finite-coin implementation}\label{sec:coins}
We make the timing repair explicit rather than assuming that duration is
unobservable. The environment supplies a command at a ready boundary.
Every completed update uses the same publicly specified number of
microsteps, independent of the private bits, old label and command.
No output or internal decision is visible during the update; only its
fixed-time ready boundary is visible. Threshold tables are nonuniform
read-only data. The bit position, input latch, old label and control
variables are counted. There is no uncharged microstep clock.

\begin{theorem}[Padded sparse-row compiler]\label{thm:compiler}
Let a finite transducer have peak $K$, common command rows and at most
$L\ge2$ successors per initialization, command or decoder row, with fixed
seed, command and query alphabets. For $\delta>0$, put
\[
 b=\max\left\{1,\left\lceil\log_2
              \frac{(N+2)(L-1)}\delta\right\rceil\right\},
 \qquad D=(L-1)b.
\]
It admits a fair-bit implementation with exactly $D$ private fair-bit
microsteps per macro update, terminal error increased by at most
$\delta$, and at most $C_{\mathcal A,X,J,L}K(b+1)$ persistent labels.
Its duration discloses no information about the private trajectory.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each categorical row, consider its conditional binary decision tree,
with at most $L-1$ internal nodes. Replace each conditional probability
by a lower dyadic approximation with $b$ bits, keeping probabilities zero
and one exact. Coupling decisions bounds the row total variation by
$(L-1)2^{-b}$. Zero-mass nodes are given arbitrary legal rows.

To draw a threshold $m/2^b$, compare $b$ private fair bits with its stored
binary digits. Until the first mismatch the sampled prefix is equal to
the table prefix, so no prefix integer is needed. Store the bit position
and the relation (equal, below or above); after a mismatch, continue
consuming the remaining bits of this comparison without changing the
relation. After $b$ steps, move to the selected child of the categorical
tree. Once a leaf has been selected, hold its new label and continue
consuming dummy bits until exactly $D$ steps have elapsed. Deterministic
thresholds and trees of shorter depth are padded in the same way.

Active states retain the old label, current input, tree node, relation
and counted progress; their number is at most a constant times $K(b+1)$
for fixed alphabets and $L$. After leaf selection the old label is
released; the sampler holds only the new label and counted progress.
It does not hold two independent $K$-valued labels. Ready, initialization
and query phases add only the same order. A final binary answer can be
held inside the padding states. All timing information is the deterministic
number $D$, so even a source observing ready times receives no private
coin information from duration. This observation does not enlarge the
main theorem into an adaptive-policy optimization result.

Each completed macro update has its rounded stochastic law regardless
of padding. Stochastic contraction and a coupling of the at most $N+2$
updates bound final error by $(N+2)(L-1)2^{-b}\le\delta$.
The construction changes no mathematical target to a cutpoint language.
\end{proof}

The comparison/tree construction is standard; the purpose is to price
its control and remove the early-stopping side channel noted for the
preceding self-paced compiler. It is not zero-error fair-bit sampling of
arbitrary irrational probabilities.

\begin{corollary}[Padded label-bit rate]\label{cor:coin-rate}
For every fixed positive tolerance in Theorem~\ref{thm:main}, the exact
spherical upper machine has a padded fair-bit approximation with
$O(N^{(d-1)/2}\log(N+1))$ labels. In this separately defined nonuniform
padded model, minimum persistent label bits obey
\[
 \frac{d-1}{2}\log_2N-O(1)
 \le b_N^{\rm pad}
 \le\frac{d-1}{2}\log_2N+O(\log\log(N+1)).
\]
For the five rational gates the leading coefficient is one.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Lemma~\ref{lem:net-upper} gives common rows with $L=d+1$ (enlarge $L$
for the binary output if necessary). Compile them with $\delta$ equal
to the desired fixed tolerance, so $b=O(\log(N+1))$. This proves the
upper label and bit bounds. At ready boundaries every padded machine
induces an atomic transducer with no more labels than its total control
alphabet. The sharp atomic lower bound therefore proves the lower bit
bound. Tables, their generation, and arithmetic remain uncharged in both
nonuniform measures. No exact order for the padded label count is inferred
from these leading-bit estimates.
\end{proof}
