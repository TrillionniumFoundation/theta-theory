The primary comparisons for the two 2013 active-testing papers included their original arXiv manuscripts, not only secondary descriptions. The 2026 imperfect-recall comparison used the original manuscript. These sources do not establish that no other work contains a special-case formula. The contribution claims are the explicit proved saddle family, finite-profile value separation, typed resource realizations, integer revelation statements and their integrated causal use.

## Norberg boundary

Norberg, *Comparison of statistical experiments with filtered probability spaces*, Statistics & Risk Modeling 20 (2002), 1–28, DOI 10.1524/strm.2002.20.14.1, remains directly relevant. A complete proof-level comparison of the original article was not available in this audit. The manuscript therefore makes no claim to originate general filtered-experiment comparison or to exclude overlap with that theory.
