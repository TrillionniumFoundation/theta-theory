\section{Expansion of the effective action}\label{sec:action41}
The stochastic experiment specifies matrices, not a presentation of a group.
Let $\mathcal A$ be its finite command alphabet, let $A_a\in O(V)$ be
its command matrices, and put
\[
 H=\overline{\langle A_a:a\in\mathcal A\rangle}\subset O(V).
\]
We assume that this compact Lie group is connected. A probability $p$ on
$\mathcal A$ is used only in a converse; the required numerical law remains
wordwise. All the quantities below are defined from these matrices and $p$.
For a nonzero invariant subspace $E$, write $\sigma_E$ for uniform sphere
probability and define operators on $L^2(\mathbb S(E),\sigma_E)$ by
\[
 P_E f(u)=\sum_a p(a)f(A_a^{-1}u),\qquad
 \Pi_E f(u)=\int_H f(h^{-1}u)\,dm_H(h).
\]
The squared \emph{action gap} is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:action-gap41}
 g_E=1-\|P_E-\Pi_E\|_{2\to2}^{\,2}.
\end{equation}
Thus $0\le g_E\le1$. When every function is invariant, the norm in this
formula is zero. Such a subspace has no positive-dimensional orbit and
is not used in an occupation lower bound.

\begin{proposition}[Presentation invariance and the relevant spectrum]
\label{prop:action41}
The action gap has the following properties.
\begin{enumerate}
\item It is unchanged by an orthogonal intertwining of the action, by
splitting a command into identical letters with the same total mass, or
by replacing a presenting group by any compact extension with the same
matrices and pushed-forward command law.
\item On $L^2(E,dz)$, with the same formulas for $P_E$ and $\Pi_E$, the
optimal inequality is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:action-lebesgue41}
 \|P_Ef\|_2^2\le\|\Pi_Ef\|_2^2+(1-g_E)\|f-\Pi_Ef\|_2^2.
\end{equation}
In particular, the sphere and Lebesgue action gaps are identical.
\item Let $\mathcal H_l(E)$ be the restrictions of homogeneous harmonic
polynomials of degree $l$, and let $P_{E,l},\Pi_{E,l}$ be the corresponding
finite-dimensional restrictions. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:harmonic-gap41}
 1-g_E=\sup_{l\ge0}\|P_{E,l}-\Pi_{E,l}\|_{2\to2}^{\,2}.
\end{equation}
No finite truncation of this supremum is an asserted gap certificate.
\item A mean-zero regular-representation norm bound $\sqrt{1-g}$ on $H$
implies $g_E\ge g$. It is a sufficient certificate, not the definition
of the hypothesis.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The operators are determined by the probability measure
$\sum_a p(a)\delta_{A_a|_E}$ and the effective compact image on $E$.
An orthogonal intertwiner induces a unitary conjugacy of both operators.
Haar probability pushes forward to Haar probability on a compact image.
This proves the first assertion, including extensions with a nontrivial
kernel.

Polar coordinates identify $L^2(E,dz)$ with
$L^2((0,\infty),r^{d-1}dr)\mathbin{\widehat\otimes}
L^2(\mathbb S(E),\sigma_E)$, up to an immaterial fixed normalization.
Both operators act as the identity on the radial factor. Consequently
$\|P_E-\Pi_E\|$ is the same in the two spaces. Haar invariance gives
$P_E\Pi_E=\Pi_EP_E=\Pi_E$, so the invariant and orthogonal components
separate, proving \eqref{eq:action-lebesgue41}. The usual orthogonal
harmonic decomposition of sphere functions is invariant under every
orthogonal matrix; the norm of a block-diagonal operator is the supremum
of its block norms. This gives \eqref{eq:harmonic-gap41}.

For the last assertion apply the group norm inequality, for fixed $u$,
to $F_u(h)=f(h^{-1}u)$ and integrate in $u$. Orthogonal invariance of
sphere measure identifies the terms with the corresponding action
norms. One may first take continuous $f$, and then use density. This is
the group-to-action transference argument, not an implication from a
gap in the defining representation alone.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Constituent action hypotheses suffice]\label{cor:action-budget41}
In the orbital occupation theorem below, the full presenting-group
assumption can be replaced by $g_E>0$ on the particular irreducible
space in which the conditional centroid is tracked. Its conclusion is
\[
 g_E\,\#\{0\le t<N:K_t\le k\}
 \le B_{d,A,s}\kappa^{-3-2/s}k^{2/s}.
\]
Other invariant summands need not have a positive action gap.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Equation \eqref{eq:action-lebesgue41} is exactly the analytic inequality
used in entropy production. Apply Theorem~\ref{thm:occupation} in $E$.
The register is still the actual whole-machine register; no constituent
identifier is supplied to it. The orbit estimate is that of
Theorem~\ref{thm:uniform-orbits} for the image on $E$.
\end{proof}

The distinction is substantive even after removing trivially acting
factors. Section~\ref{sec:mixed41} gives a faithful finite-alphabet
experiment with an effective circle factor and no full group norm gap,
yet with a matched numerical width law supplied by one dominant
expanding constituent. The older full-group results remain valid as
corollaries whenever their stronger hypotheses hold.
