
\subsection{Precision and enumeration size}
The power in Corollary~\ref{cor:v32-relative} is a choice of
certificate precision. It is not an intrinsic exponent of the experiment.
The latter is described by the full profile $\Xi_N$.

\begin{theorem}[Arbitrary precision and its finite cost]
\label{thm:v33-precision}
Fix the prescribed-command experiment of
Theorem~\ref{thm:resolution-main}. Suppose its read-only moments and
coefficients have certified enclosures as in
Theorem~\ref{thm:v32-certificates}. For any real $s>2$ and integer
$M\ge1$, choose command radius $\delta\le M^{-s}$, denominator
$b=\lceil M^{s+1}\rceil$, and objective enclosure width
$\tau\le M^{-s}$. There is a constant $C_1$ depending only on $T$
and $\kappa$ such that, for either common-controller criterion,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v33-precision}
 \operatorname{width}(I_M)\le C_1M^{-s},\qquad
 \frac{\operatorname{width}(I_M)}{\mathfrak R_{M,N}^{a}}
 \le\frac{C_1M^{-s}}{c\Xi_N(M,a)}
 \le\frac{C_1}{c}M^{2-s}.
\end{equation}
All bounds are uniform in $a\in K$.

If a common command grid has $C_\delta$ cells, the report alphabet
has $X$ elements, and checkpoint $t$ has $Q_t$ queries, the literal
net of all stage-state-report rows and decoder tables has cardinality
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v33-net-size}
 \binom{b+M-1}{M-1}^{\,TM C_\delta X}
                (b+1)^{M\sum_{t=1}^T Q_t}.
\end{equation}
Repeated or unreachable tables are counted. A cubical partition can
be chosen with
$C_\delta\le(\lceil(1-2\eta)/\delta\rceil+1)^J$.
A lower certificate by this construction requires coverage of the
whole net, not merely evaluation of a feasible table.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Theorem~\ref{thm:v32-certificates} bounds the mean interval width by
$\tau+[T(M-1)+2]/b+2T(1+2/\kappa)\delta$, and the worst-history
width by $\tau+[T(M-1)+2]/b+2T\delta/\kappa$. Since $b\ge M^{s+1}$,
both are at most
$[T+3+2T(1+2/\kappa)]M^{-s}$. The retained collision theorem bounds
each true risk below by $c\Xi_N(M,a)$, while its one-dimensional term
gives $\Xi_N(M,a)\ge M^{-2}$. This proves \eqref{eq:v33-precision}.
The argument does not identify a new statistical exponent.

A stochastic row on $M$ labels with denominator $b$ is a weak
composition of $b$ into $M$ parts, so there are
$\binom{b+M-1}{M-1}$ such rows. There are $TM C_\delta X$ row
positions, including the unused ones. Each of the $M\sum_t Q_t$
decoder entries has $b+1$ choices. Multiplication proves
\eqref{eq:v33-net-size}. Splitting each coordinate interval into
pieces of length at most $\delta$ gives the stated command count
(and radius at most $\delta$ in the sup norm). These are counts of
read-only programs in a global search, not extra persistent labels.
\end{proof}

For a prescribed absolute tolerance $\epsilon>0$, it also suffices to
choose $\tau\le\epsilon/3$,
$b\ge3[T(M-1)+2]/\epsilon$, and
$\delta\le\epsilon/[6T(1+2/\kappa)]$. The interval width is then
at most $\epsilon$ for both criteria. Increasing precision changes
the search size and the required oracle accuracy. It does not change
the physical horizon, the command law, the number of retained labels,
or the attainable dimensions and collision phases.
