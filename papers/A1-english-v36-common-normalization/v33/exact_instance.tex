\section{An exact continuous-command experiment}
\label{sec:v33-instance}

We now evaluate an instance of the original per-report model. The
commands range over a genuine cube, the prior has full support, and
the query is selected from a full attainable failure-factor basis.
The calculation covers all Borel stochastic encoders, not only a
finite table net.

Let $N=2$, $M=2$, $\mu$ be uniform on $[0,1]$, and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v33-example-cells}
 k_1(t)=\frac14+\frac t2,\qquad
 k_2(t)=\frac34-\frac t2,\qquad
 G=[9/20,11/20]^2.
\end{equation}
The acquisition command is uniform on $G$. The two equally weighted
future queries are failure at commands $(1/2,1/2)$ and
$(9/20,11/20)$, respectively. Their likelihoods are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v33-example-queries}
 H_1(t)=\frac12,\qquad H_2(t)=\frac{19}{40}+\frac t{20}.
\end{equation}
Both commands belong to $G$, and $H_1,H_2$ span $W_{\{0,1\}}$.
One acquisition report is followed by compression, query selection
and one future trial. Every trial is counted. All acquisition and
query report likelihoods are at least $9/80$.

Put $a_*=1/240$, let $U,V$ be independent uniform variables on
$[9/20,11/20]$, and define
\[
 J_* =\mathbb E\frac{(U-V)^2}{U+V}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Exact two-label value]\label{thm:v33-exact-example}
For \eqref{eq:v33-example-cells}--\eqref{eq:v33-example-queries},
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v33-example-value}
 \mathfrak R_{2,2}^{\mathrm{av}}
       =a_*^2\left(\frac1{12}+\frac{J_*}{4}\right).
\end{equation}
An optimal controller retains label $1$ precisely when the acquisition
report is accepted cell $1$, and label $0$ otherwise. It ignores the
command. Its predictions for $H_1$ are $1/2$ at both labels; for $H_2$
they are $121/240$ at label $1$ and $359/720$ at label $0$.
The formula is optimal among all Borel encoders with two labels and
fresh private randomization.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $Y$ be the full-history prediction for $H_2$, centered at $1/2$.
The accepted reports each have unconditional probability $1/4$.
Their posterior means of $t$ are $7/12$ and $5/12$, so they give
$Y=a_*$ and $Y=-a_*$. Write $u=1-g_1$, $v=1-g_2$. On failure the
report probability is $(u+v)/2$, and direct integration of the affine
likelihood gives
\[
  Y=a_*\frac{u-v}{u+v},\qquad |Y|\le a_*/10.
\]
The complete acquired law of $Y$ is symmetric. It has atoms of mass
$1/4$ at each of $\pm a_*$, and a central part of mass $1/2$ and mean
zero. This description includes the report evidence: the central
law is weighted by $(u+v)/2$, not by a command law conditioned
without its evidence. In particular,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v33-second-moment}
 \mathbb EY^2=a_*^2\left(\frac12+\frac{J_*}{2}\right).
\end{equation}

For any stochastic binary encoder write $Q(h)\in[0,1]$ for the
probability of label $1$, $p=\mathbb EQ$, and $v=\mathbb E(YQ)$.
The best centered decoder values are $v/p$ and $-v/(1-p)$, and
the explained variance is $v^2/[p(1-p)]$. The constant encoders
explain zero. Replacing $Q$ by its conditional expectation given
$Y$ preserves $p,v$, so no encoder information has been excluded.
Complementing the label and using symmetry of the law allow us to
bound the optimum with $0<p\le1/2$ and $v\ge0$.

For $p\le1/4$, $v\le a_*p$ gives
$v^2/[p(1-p)]\le a_*^2/3$. For $p=1/4+s$, $0\le s\le1/4$,
the integral of $YQ$ at fixed $p$ is maximized by an upper-tail
selection: moving any selected mass from a lower value of $Y$ to
an unselected higher value cannot decrease the integral. Such a
selection first takes the entire atom at $a_*$ and then $s$ mass
from the central part. With $\epsilon=1/10$ this gives
$v\le a_*(1/4+\epsilon s)$. The elementary identity
\[
 \begin{split}
 &(1/4+s)(3/4-s)-3(1/4+\epsilon s)^2\\
 &\hspace{12mm}=s\bigl[(1/2-3\epsilon/2)-(1+3\epsilon^2)s\bigr]
          \ \ge\ \frac{37}{400}s
 \end{split}
\]
therefore again bounds the explained variance by $a_*^2/3$.
The bound is attained by selecting only the positive atom, exactly
the controller in the statement. Its centered predictions are
$a_*$ and $-a_*/3$. Only $H_2$ contributes regret and has query
weight $1/2$. Subtracting the maximal explained variance from
\eqref{eq:v33-second-moment} and multiplying by $1/2$ proves
\eqref{eq:v33-example-value}, including the stated decoder values.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A finite rational enclosure with complete coverage}
For an explicit enclosure of the number in
\eqref{eq:v33-example-value}, put $U=1/2+X$ and $V=1/2+Z$.
Then $X,Z$ are independent uniform on $[-1/20,1/20]$. Simultaneous
sign symmetry gives
\[
 J_* =\mathbb E\frac{(X-Z)^2}{1-(X+Z)^2}.
\]
For an integer $K\ge0$ define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v33-rational-enclosure}
 \begin{split}
 J_K&=\sum_{k=0}^K\mathbb E\bigl[(X-Z)^2(X+Z)^{2k}\bigr],\\
 r_K&=\frac1{600}\frac{10^{-2K-2}}{1-10^{-2}},\\
 L_K&=a_*^2(1/12+J_K/4),\qquad
 U_K=L_K+a_*^2r_K/4.
 \end{split}
\end{equation}
All these numbers are rational. Each expectation is evaluated by the
binomial theorem and
$\mathbb EX^j=0$ for odd $j$, while
$\mathbb EX^{2j}=20^{-2j}/(2j+1)$.

\begin{corollary}[Executed certificate formula]\label{cor:v33-enclosure}
For every $K\ge0$,
\[
 L_K\le\mathfrak R_{2,2}^{\mathrm{av}}\le U_K,
 \qquad U_K-L_K=\frac{a_*^2}{2400}
                         \frac{10^{-2K-2}}{1-10^{-2}}.
\]
The controller in Theorem~\ref{thm:v33-exact-example} implements
the upper bound. The lower bound covers every admissible stochastic
encoder on the entire continuous command cube.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Expand $(1-(X+Z)^2)^{-1}$ as a geometric series. Every summand
is nonnegative. Since $|X+Z|\le1/10$ and
$\mathbb E(X-Z)^2=1/600$, the tail after $K$ is bounded by $r_K$.
Now apply the exact value theorem. Its upper-tail argument, rather
than enumeration of selected controllers, supplies the global
coverage needed for the lower endpoint.
\end{proof}

The accompanying rational-arithmetic program evaluates
\eqref{eq:v33-rational-enclosure} at $K=5$, records the implementing
controller, and checks its coefficient and decoder identities.
This is an executed enclosure of an original continuous-command
experiment. It is not an execution of the general controller net
in Theorem~\ref{thm:v32-certificates}, and its rational checks do
not replace the all-encoder proof above.
