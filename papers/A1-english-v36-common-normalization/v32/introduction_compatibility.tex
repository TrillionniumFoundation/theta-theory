\subsection{Causal compatibility and uniform certificates}
The common-filter restriction can also be imposed exactly, without first
passing to local distortion bounds. On a finite command alphabet, let
$\alpha_h(i)$ be the joint mass of history $h$ and retained label $i$.
A single collection of command and update rows satisfies
\[
 \alpha_{hcx}(j)=r_x(h,c)\sum_i\alpha_h(i)b_{t+1}(c,j\mid i,x).
\]
The same row is used at every history. Theorem~\ref{thm:v32-finite}
proves that these equations and their stochastic-row constraints describe
exactly the admissible unseeded controllers. The resulting optimization
is compact and polynomial, but generally nonconvex. It also describes
acquisition chosen from the retained state before each report; this is a
separate convention from prescribed uniform exploration.

For the original command cube, take finite partitions of sup-norm radius
$\delta$ and give each representative the probability of its cell. If
$T=N-1$ and every report likelihood is at least $\kappa$, then
Theorem~\ref{thm:v32-grid} gives
\[
 |V^{\mathrm{av}}_{\delta,M}-\mathfrak R_{M,N}^{a,\mathrm{av}}|
       \le T(1+2/\kappa)\delta,
 \qquad
 0\le\mathfrak R_{M,N}^{a,\max}-V^{\max}_{\delta,M}
       \le2T\delta/\kappa.
\]
These estimates hold for arbitrary Borel update kernels; they do not
assume that an optimal partition varies continuously with the data.
Positivity controls likelihood perturbations without division by a
collision gap. Rounding current commands implements the finite controller
with exactly the original retained-label resource.

An exhaustive rational net of feasible common controllers supplies both
a global lower certificate and an implementing upper certificate. With
command radius $M^{-3}$, transition denominator $M^4$, and objective
enclosures of width $M^{-3}$, the resulting risk interval has width
$O(M^{-3})$. The attained classification gives
$\mathfrak R_{M,N}^{a,\mathrm{av}}\ge c\Xi_N(M,a)\ge cM^{-2}$.
Thus the relative interval width is $O(M^{-1})$, uniformly through all
collision strata (Corollary~\ref{cor:v32-relative}). This is an exhaustive
finite construction with certified access to the finite prior-moment
list and fixed coefficients, not a polynomial-time assertion or an assumption that every prior
is computable.

Theorem~\ref{thm:v32-delayed} gives a separate exact two-checkpoint test.
Two-label early acquisition must retain competing later-relevant
coordinates, whereas just-in-time acquisition reads them when needed.
Both schedules are admitted and have the same positive local distortions,
total acquisition count, and retained-state and seed convention. Their
risk ratio diverges as the nuisance scale tends to zero. The proof
optimizes over all private randomized binary encoders and gives attaining
deterministic encoders; it does not rely on a public mixture of codebooks.
