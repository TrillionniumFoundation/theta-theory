This is a record of the limited comparisons actually used, not an exhaustive
novelty search or a claim that all inherited bibliography entries were newly

## Linear matroid intersection

Tatsuya Terao, *Faster Approximate Linear Matroid Intersection*, SWAT 2026,
LIPIcs 370, Article 39, 39:1–39:19, DOI 10.4230/LIPIcs.SWAT.2026.39.
The official Dagstuhl PDF was opened and printed page 39:2 was also viewed
as a rendered PDF page. It recalls the common-base criterion that the
symbolic determinant of M1 D M2^T is nonzero. The manuscript states and
proves its finite rectangular common-independent-set version by applying
Cauchy–Binet to every minor; it does not attribute original invention of
the symbolic method to this 2026 paper.

Primary source:
https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol370-swat2026/LIPIcs.SWAT.2026.39/LIPIcs.SWAT.2026.39.pdf

## Retained comparisons and limits

The Banaji–Pantea and Müller et al. universal-positive-diagonal comparisons
remain unchanged. They concern full rank for every positive weighting;
the finite matroid calculation concerns the maximum rank under some positive
weighting. Restricting the weights to H_mu U=0 changes the polynomial
relations and requires the quotient moment chart. The retained Fourier,
analytic-order, clustered-Vandermonde and finite-window approximation
comparisons keep their original stated scope and provenance in the earlier
literature records. The new geometry-to-causal theorem is presented as a
fully proved synthesis under explicit hypotheses, not as a claim that none
of its component mechanisms has precedents.
