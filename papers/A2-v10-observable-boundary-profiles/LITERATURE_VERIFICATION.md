# Focused literature check for E2

Checked on 2026-09-10 against primary sources.

Dai, Zhewei, and Patricia K. Lamm. *Local Regularization for the Nonlinear Inverse Autoconvolution Problem*. SIAM Journal on Numerical Analysis 46(2) (2008), 832–868. DOI: 10.1137/070679247. The publisher abstract and bibliographic record were inspected. The manuscript attributes causal local regularization and convergence for continuous and L2 data; it does not claim to have checked an applicable singular-kernel theorem in the paywalled full text.

Hofmann, Bernd, Frank Werner, and Yu Deng. *On uniqueness and ill-posedness for the deautoconvolution problem in the multi-dimensional case*. arXiv:2212.06534v1 (13 December 2022), with related publication DOI 10.1088/1361-6420/acd07b. The primary arXiv abstract and paper were consulted. The comparison uses its L2-supported-cube setting and full/limited-data distinction. The actual manuscript cites the inspected arXiv version rather than inventing a journal article number.

Section 10 separates these results from our L1, non-L2 kernel x^(-1/2)V, finite-collar normalization, and the C3-to-C0 theorem. The non-L2 observation is a direct computation from V(0)=1, not a theorem attributed to either paper. The new acquisition proof is supplied in full and is not claimed as an automatic consequence of a cited regularization theorem. No exhaustive priority search or general optimal regularization claim is made.
