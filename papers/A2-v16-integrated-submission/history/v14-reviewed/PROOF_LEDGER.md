## No circular dependency

The smooth relative theorem is proved by the retained determinant and Morse arguments before Section 8. The new scalar density theorem uses that established relative theorem; it is not its replacement proof. The Volterra compatibility proof uses the original half-line law, not the subsequent interpretation as a stable-action width. The width corollary can therefore invoke the compatibility theorem without a cycle.

## Fixed-order uniformity

Every differentiated statement is for each fixed finite order on the stated compact local family. Higher constants can depend on higher graph/chart norms. Polynomial factors in flight number caused by parameter derivatives are absorbed into a fixed strict exponential margin. Neither uniformity in jet order nor uniformity as hyperbolicity degenerates is asserted.
