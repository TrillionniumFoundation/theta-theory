\begin{abstract}
We establish nonlinear relative boundary laws for near-onset collision
records in smooth periodic dispersing billiards, without symmetry or
finite horizon assumptions. The stationary action and its normalized
mixed derivative separate into two half-line actions and relative
determinant amplitudes. Physical residual-time integration gives a
probability law on a common closed offset collar, with every fixed
geometric and offset derivative controlled uniformly in collision order.
The law yields rare-event-preserving comparison of endpoint experiments,
and a joint small-offset testing profile that permits unequal facing
curvatures. For unlabelled counts in an explicit analytic physical
family, four fixed windows invert the unknown gap and coalescing
curvature coordinates. Among bounded-flight binary designs in a fixed
local collar, joint curvature accuracy $\varepsilon$ and gap accuracy
$\delta$ have optimal preparation order
$(\varepsilon^{-6}+\delta^{-2})\log(1/\eta)$ at confidence $1-\eta$.
A separate smooth remainder experiment admits fully charged
count-only reconstruction without an exact finite-offset formula.
We also establish contact-jet inversion and analytic contact rigidity,
with complete geometric realizations, conditional acquisition bounds,
and collision-record response formulas.
\end{abstract}
