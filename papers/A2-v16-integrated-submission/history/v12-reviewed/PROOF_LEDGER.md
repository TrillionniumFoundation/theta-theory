## Inherited chain actually used

`v3/10_geometry_action.tex` supplies the alternating Jacobi reduction, finite Green kernel and relatively normalized cofactor formula. `v3/20_integration.tex` retains the full-phase residual-time measure and a common Morse domain. `v4/10_boundary_layers.tex` supplies the weighted half-line orbit, action and determinant amplitude. `v5/15_differentiated_operators.tex` controls fixed geometric derivatives in trace norm. `article/20_boundary_compatibility.tex` gives the actual normalized energy pushforward and Volterra identity. `article/21_abel_stability.tex` gives the two-sided linear Abel discrepancy. The full smooth pilot proof remains in `article/27_profile_calibration.tex`.

The old shared-contact result `v5/20_contact_rigidity.tex` is the comparison case, not the proof of the new antisymmetric direction. Restricting the new block to a shared jet at equal curvatures recovers its diagonal exactly. The new finite-window lower bound uses the newly constructed physical family; no abstract profile alternative is presented as a realized obstacle.

## Verification boundary

Finite diagnostics independently reproduce the block algebra and finite Dirichlet/ellipse-moment benchmarks and check the entire retained formal-block multiset. They do not prove infinite-dimensional differentiability, analytic continuation, concentration or geometric realization. Those arguments are in the paper. No source-retention count, successful compilation, or finite-check count is an editorial significance certificate.
