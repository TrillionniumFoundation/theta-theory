\begin{abstract}
We prove nonlinear relative laws for near-onset collision records in
smooth periodic dispersing billiards, without symmetry or finite horizon.
The normalized mixed endpoint derivative factors into half-line
determinant amplitudes on a nonshrinking collar. We identify these
amplitudes with the classical normalized scalar linearizing densities
of the physical stable returns. Physical residual-time integration preserves
the limit with every fixed mixed derivative. The two oriented even laws determine the complete
symmetrized boundary energy profiles and predict the odd law by a
Volterra compatibility identity. For two independent even contacts at
fixed labelled leading geometry, both the limiting laws and the two
oriented two-flight germs admit explicit block-triangular jet inverses,
including coincident curvatures. Their finite-jet coordinates are
analytically equivalent, although their law functions are different.
Independent jets are realized in analytic billiards with the selected
leading hierarchy fixed. Each locally full-rank physical finite-jet
family constructed here is observable through positive windows at two
flights. For general smooth
contacts, a weighted Abel coordinate and the two-derivative gain of
integrated flux yield a finite binary preparation bound for the whole
profiles. A separately charged smooth-class pilot removes the actual
gap, area and multiplier. Complete auxiliary inverse, calibration and
collision-record proofs are retained.
\end{abstract}
