\begin{abstract}
We prove uniform nonlinear relative laws for near-onset collision
records in smooth periodic dispersing billiards, without symmetry or
finite horizon. On a nonshrinking collar, the normalized mixed endpoint
derivative factors into half-line determinant amplitudes, and physical
residual-time integration preserves the limit with every fixed mixed
geometric and offset derivative. The amplitudes are identified with the
classical normalized scalar linearizing densities of the physical stable
returns. The two oriented even laws determine the complete symmetrized
boundary energy profiles and predict the odd law through a Volterra
compatibility identity. At fixed labelled leading geometry, two independent
even contacts admit explicit block-triangular jet inverses, both from the
limiting laws and from two-flight germs, including coincident curvatures.
Their finite-jet coordinates are analytically equivalent, and independent
jets are realized by analytic billiards preserving the selected leading
hierarchy. Each locally full-rank physical finite-jet family constructed
here is observable through positive two-flight windows. For general smooth
contacts, weighted Abel stability and the regularity of integrated flux
give a finite binary preparation bound for the full profiles, with a
separately charged pilot for the unknown gap, area and multiplier.
The relative comparison also controls complete adaptive, stopped
experiments by a success-weighted error budget while retaining every
failed preparation. An exact overlap functional describes their tangent
critical transition.
\end{abstract}
