\begin{proof}
The Young tower and its one-sided quotient have the same natural extension.
Doob normalization changes the invariant path measure but not the factor map
to $T_R$.  Conditional probabilities on the quotient reconstruct the natural
extension, whose shift is conjugate almost everywhere to the deterministic
billiard map.
\end{proof}

\section{Main theorem and scope}\label{sec:main}
\begin{theorem}[Periodic-Sinai first-principles ensemble theorem]\label{thm:main}
For the one-parameter family $Q_R$, the following objects belong to one compact
periodic finite-horizon specular billiard platform:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item natural Liouville collision measure;
\item actual lattice cell displacement $J_R$;
\item local current pressure and LDP;
\item the endogenous field $\theta_R(j)=I_R'(j)$;
\item quantitative central-window microcanonical-to-canonical conditioning;
\item a same-billiard driven equilibrium preparation;
\item a nonconjugate moving-radius family with explicit mean-roof response.
\end{enumerate}
No transition matrix, stochastic collision rule, or independent risk parameter
is inserted.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Combine \cref{thm:finite-horizon,prop:liouville,thm:nonconjugacy,prop:santalo,thm:spectral-packet,thm:ldp,thm:gibbs-conditioning,prop:no-noise}.
\end{proof}

\section*{Truth boundary}
The theorem is local in the canonical field $q$ and uses the standard spectral
packet for finite-horizon Lorentz maps.  It proves a family of compact periodic
Sinai tables with natural Liouville preparation and a nonconjugate radius
deformation.  It does not claim differentiability of every tilted spectral
coefficient with respect to $R$, nor external line-by-line certification.
