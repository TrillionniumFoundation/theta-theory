# Author response to Referee Round Twenty-Two — A2

The referee's two explicit counterexamples are accepted and used as regression tests in the Round-Twenty-Three source.

## A2.1 — lattice aperiodicity

The former determinant argument has been removed.  The active proof works with the annihilator of the closed subgroup generated by all periodic data.  A finite certificate contains:

1. three equal-period pairs whose lattice differences are the exact generators of `Z^3`;
2. two equal-period, zero-homology pairs with incommensurable roof differences;
3. two coprime periodic lengths.

The roof pairs first force the continuous Fourier frequency to vanish, the integer generators then force the three torus frequencies to vanish, and the coprime periods force the constant phase to vanish.  This is an integer/topological subgroup proof, not real linear algebra.

## A2.2 — high-frequency coordinates

The active coarea step uses `(r,phi)`, the two independent coordinates of one original collision chart.  Returned points are functions of these coordinates and are never promoted to independent variables.  Two phase functions have a uniformly nonzero full Jacobian with respect to `(r,phi)`; dual vector fields give two integrations by parts with boundary-flat partitions.

## A2.3 — branch complexity

The source now proves a weighted `C^4` branch sum.  Homogeneity-strip derivative growth is polynomial, strip widths are summable after choosing the strip exponent, and the remaining return-time polynomial is absorbed by the exponential return tail.  Integral branch mass alone is not used to control supremum derivative norms.

## A2.4 — local theorem

The small, medium, and unbounded-frequency regions are stated and proved separately.  The unbounded roof region has an integrable `(1+|b|)^{-2}` majorant.  Fourier inversion is explicitly three lattice variables plus one continuous roof variable and therefore has the `n^{-2}` prefactor.

The manuscript compares its operator inputs with the established Sinai-billiard and Lorentz-process literature and identifies the additional arithmetic and raw roof estimates proved here.  The positive joint local limit theorem is retained.
