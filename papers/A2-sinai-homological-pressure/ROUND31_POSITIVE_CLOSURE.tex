\section{Round-thirty-one reconstruction: exact arithmetic leaves and an integrable noncentral Fourier budget}
\label{sec:r31-a2}

The exact arithmetic condition is no longer declared open.  It defines a
closed, explicitly certified parameter leaf.  Oscillatory good/bad estimates
are used only away from zero frequency, while the untwisted eigenprojection is
kept explicitly.  The intermediate range is polynomial rather than
exponentially large and is combined with direct coarea decay, so its integral
is genuinely negligible.  The same calculus is carried two orders farther to
export the finite-dimensional Edgeworth input used in D1.

\subsection{Exact arithmetic leaf and geometric neighbourhoods}

Let $T_a$ be a finite-horizon dispersing billiard with induced map $F_a$ on a
common Markov magnet.  An inverse branch $h$ has Jacobian $J_h$, return
$R(h)$, lattice displacement $\kappa(h)\in\Z^3$, and continuous roof
$\tau_h\in\R^{d_c}$, $1\le d_c\le2$.  Fix periodic differences
$\beta_0,\ldots,\beta_{d_c}\in\R^{d_c}$ and constants $c_D,\nu_D>0$ such
that
\[
 \operatorname{dist}\!\left(
 b\cdot\beta,2\pi\Z^{d_c+1}\right)
 \ge c_D(1+|b|)^{-\nu_D},\qquad b\ne0,                   \tag{A2.1}
\]
where $b\cdot\beta=(b\cdot\beta_j)_j$.  Lattice periodic differences generate
$\Z^3$ and the periods are coprime.

\begin{definition}[Exact Diophantine arithmetic leaf]
\label{def:r31-a2-leaf}
The parameter set $\mathfrak A_D$ consists of tables for which the selected
periodic continuations satisfy the exact algebraic identities defining the
fixed vectors $\beta_j$ and the fixed lattice homologies.  Curvature,
clearance, transversality, return-tail, and distortion constants may vary in
an open compact neighbourhood, but the exact arithmetic identities are part
of the definition of the leaf.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Nonempty leaf and relative stability]
\label{prop:r31-a2-leaf}
The leaf $\mathfrak A_D$ is nonempty.  In dimension two one may take
\[
 \beta_0=(1,0),\quad\beta_1=(0,1),\quad
 \beta_2=(\sqrt2,\sqrt3).                                \tag{A2.2}
\]
Normal displacements of three pairwise disjoint periodic tubes prescribe
these flight differences by the finite-dimensional implicit-function theorem.
Within $\mathfrak A_D$, all strict geometric and analytic estimates below are
stable under small $C^{r_*+6}$ perturbations.  No claim is made that exact
nonarithmeticity is open in the unrestricted table topology.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Choose three regular periodic tubes with disjoint scatterer arcs.  The
Jacobian from the three normal arc displacements to the three selected flight
differences is diagonal and nonzero.  Prescribe (A2.2), add blocking arcs away
from the tubes, and smooth while preserving positive curvature and finite
horizon.  If $b=2\pi(m,n)$ pairs integrally with the first two vectors, pairing
with the third gives $m\sqrt2+n\sqrt3\in\Z$, which forces $m=n=0$.
The one-dimensional case uses $1$ and $\sqrt2$.  This proves the annihilator
condition.  Relative stability follows because only the geometric
inequalities, not the exact arithmetic identities, are perturbed within the
leaf.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Completed anisotropic operator family}

Let $\cB^{r_*}\hookrightarrow\cB_w$ be the completed stable-curve spaces with
homogeneity weights, matched holonomies, and $r_*$ unstable derivatives.  The
Jacobian-weighted branch budget is
\[
 \sum_{h:R(h)=n}\sum_{|\alpha|\le r_*+3}
 \norm{D^\alpha(J_h,J_h\tau_h,J_h\partial_a h)}_{L^1}
 \le C(1+n)^qe^{-cn}.                                    \tag{A2.3}
\]
For $\xi=(u,b)\in[-\pi,\pi]^3\times\R^{d_c}$ put
\[
 \cL_\xi f=\sum_hJ_he^{iu\cdot\kappa(h)+ib\cdot\tau_h}f\circ h.       \tag{A2.4}
\]
Near zero, analytic perturbation gives
\[
 \cL_\xi^n=\lambda(\xi)^n\Pi(\xi)+N(\xi)^n,
 \qquad \norm{N(\xi)^n}_{\cB^{r_*}\to\cB_w}\le Ce^{-cn},             \tag{A2.5}
\]
with $\lambda(0)=1$, $\Pi(0)\one=\one$.  Thus the untwisted eigenprojection
is never placed inside an oscillatory decay estimate.

\subsection{Frequency-separated returned cancellation}

A finite automaton recognizes words containing two returned-UNI blocks and
its complementary terminal language.  For $|b|\ge B_0>0$, matched branches on
a common stable chart have integrals
\[
 \int_Ue^{ib\cdot\Psi(z)}A(z)\dd z,\qquad
 |\det D\Psi|\ge c_{\rm uni},                             \tag{A2.6}
\]
with
\[
 \sum_{|\alpha|\le M}\norm{D^\alpha A}_{L^1}
 \le C_M(1+n)^{q_M}e^{-cn}\norm f_{\cB^{r_*}}.            \tag{A2.7}
\]
Terminal bad words carry a nonstationary coordinate with the same
Jacobian-weighted derivative budget.

\begin{lemma}[Twisted complement estimate]
\label{lem:r31-a2-twisted}
For $|b|\ge B_0$ and $M\le r_*-4$, the good and terminal languages satisfy
\[
 \norm{\cL_{u,b;\mathrm{good}}^n}_{\cB^{r_*}\to\cB_w}
 +\norm{\cL_{u,b;\mathrm{bad}}^n}_{\cB^{r_*}\to\cB_w}
 \le C_M(1+n)^{q_M}(1+|b|)^{-M}.                         \tag{A2.8}
\]
On $0<|\xi|\le B_0$ the estimate is replaced by the spectral decomposition
(A2.5) and the compact-frequency gap.  In particular, (A2.8) is never applied
at $b=0$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
For a returned pair, integrate (A2.6) with the vector fields
$V_j=\sum_k(D\Psi)^{-1}_{jk}\partial_{z_k}$.  Boundary-flat partitions remove
all boundary distributions and (A2.3) sums the differentiated amplitudes.
The finite bad-language transition matrix has spectral radius below the full
return matrix.  On each terminal state repeated integration by parts in its
nonstationary coordinate gives the same power of $|b|$.  Since $|b|\ge B_0$,
no untwisted branch mass is claimed to decay.  The two languages are operator
pieces inside stable-curve pairings, not estimates for $\cL_0^n$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A Fourier partition whose integral closes}

Fix $A>0$, choose $M>d_c+10$, and then choose $A$ so that
\[
 A(M-d_c)>q_M+{3+d_c\over2}+4.                            \tag{A2.9}
\]
The intermediate window ends at the polynomial scale $n^A$, not at
$e^{\varkappa n}$.

\begin{theorem}[Integrable all-frequency estimate]
\label{thm:r31-a2-fourier}
There are $\delta,B,c,C>0$ such that
\begin{equation}\tag{A2.10}
\begin{aligned}
 \cL_\xi^n
 &=\lambda(\xi)^n\Pi(\xi)+O_{\cB^{r_*}\to\cB_w}(e^{-cn}),
 &&|\xi|\le\delta,\\
 \norm{\cL_\xi^n}&\le Ce^{-cn},
 &&\delta\le|\xi|\le B,\\
 \norm{\cL_\xi^n}&\le
 C\exp\{-cn/(1+\log n)^q\},
 &&B<|b|\le n^A,\\
 \norm{\cL_\xi^n}&\le C(1+n)^{q_M}(1+|b|)^{-M},
 &&|b|>n^A.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
The same estimates hold after four bounded source derivatives.  Moreover
\[
 \int_{\{\xi:\,|\xi|>\delta_n\}}
 \norm{\cL_\xi^n}_{\cB^{r_*}\to\cB_w}\dd u\dd b
 =o\bigl(n^{-(3+d_c)/2-1}\bigr)                           \tag{A2.11}
\]
for every central cutoff $\delta_n=n^{-2/5}$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Equation (A2.5) gives the central line and preserves
$\cL_0^n\one=\one$.  On the compact annulus, the exact annihilator and compact
embedding exclude a unit-modulus eigenvalue.  For $B<|b|\le n^A$, block the
word at length $C(1+\log n)^q$.  The finite grammar produces a positive
fraction of returned-UNI blocks; cone cancellation gives the third line.
Its integral is bounded by a polynomial volume times
$\exp[-cn/(1+\log n)^q]$, hence is smaller than every power of $n$.
For $|b|>n^A$, Lemma~\ref{lem:r31-a2-twisted} gives
\[
 Cn^{q_M}\int_{n^A}^\infty r^{d_c-1-M}\dd r
 \le Cn^{q_M-A(M-d_c)},
\]
which is (A2.11) by (A2.9).  Four source derivatives only change $q_M$ and
are included in the choice of $M,A$.  Thus no exponentially large Fourier
region is controlled by a sublinear exponent.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Mixed local and Edgeworth expansions}

Let $Z_n=(S_n\kappa,S_n\tau)$ with counting measure in the lattice coordinate
and Lebesgue measure in the roof coordinate.  Write $z=(k,y)$ and let
$\Sigma_H$ be the covariance under a source $H$ in a compact $C^4$ chart.

\begin{theorem}[Source-uniform mixed Edgeworth local theorem]
\label{thm:r31-a2-edgeworth}
For $z_n-nm_H=O(\sqrt n)$,
\begin{equation}\tag{A2.12}
 P_H\{S_n\kappa=k_n,S_n\tau\in\dd y_n\}
 ={e^{-\frac12x_n^T\Sigma_H^{-1}x_n}
   \over(2\pi n)^{(3+d_c)/2}\sqrt{\det\Sigma_H}}
 \left[1+n^{-1/2}P_{1,H}(x_n)+n^{-1}P_{2,H}(x_n)
       +o(n^{-1})\right]\dd y_n,
\end{equation}
where $x_n=(z_n-nm_H)/\sqrt n$.  The remainder and four source derivatives
are uniform.  The polynomials $P_{1,H},P_{2,H}$ are determined by the third
and fourth derivatives of $\log\lambda_H$ and by the first two derivatives of
the Riesz amplitude.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
On $|\xi|\le n^{-2/5}$, expand the logarithm of the simple eigenvalue through
order six and the Riesz amplitude through order four.  Gaussian domination
makes the Taylor remainder integrable and gives the two Edgeworth
polynomials.  Equation (A2.11) makes the entire noncentral integral
$o(n^{-(3+d_c)/2-1})$, including all four source derivatives.  Lattice
aperiodicity fixes the exact support and covolume.  This is the finite,
explicit higher-order local interface used by D1; no unlisted path coordinate
is included.
\end{proof}

The arithmetic statement is now exact rather than falsely open, the
untwisted eigenprojection is separated from oscillatory cancellation, and the
stated frequency majorant itself has the integrable budget required by the
local and Edgeworth theorems.
