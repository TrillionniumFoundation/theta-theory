\begin{abstract}
Orbit births are handled by a renormalized physical current bundle whose norm scales the vanishing birth mode.  A finite family of periodic certificates controls compact frequencies, while a separate geometric nonlocal-integrability theorem supplies Dolgopyat cancellation.  The vector--roof local limit theorem is stated in sum variables and has the correct average-window scaling.
\end{abstract}
