\begin{abstract}
For the compact finite-horizon Lorentz family we construct a parent-branch moving transfer bundle, prove covariance damping at genuinely small frequencies, derive the lattice obstruction from returned-branch temporal nonintegrability and closing, and cover the complete Fourier line by an integrable moderate/very-high-frequency decomposition.  The resulting vector--collision-count--roof density theorem yields all local, central and saturated window regimes.
\end{abstract}
