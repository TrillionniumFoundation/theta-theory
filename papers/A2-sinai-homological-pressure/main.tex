\begin{abstract}
For a compact finite-horizon Sinai family we construct one induced
parent-fold Banach bundle carrying homology, return count, and physical roof.
Lattice rank and returned temporal non-integrability are proved on explicit
regular branch families.  High vertical frequencies are controlled directly
by branchwise physical integration by parts on matrix coefficients; no flat
trace, nuclear trace, or Fredholm determinant is invoked.  Fourier inversion
therefore yields an unsmoothed four-dimensional vector--return--roof density
local limit theorem and the corresponding ratio conditioning.
\end{abstract}
