\begin{abstract}
For a certified compact family of finite-horizon Sinai billiards we prove the
joint local theorem for two homology coordinates, return count, and physical
roof.  Arithmetic is decided by the closed subgroup of all periodic data: two
incommensurable zero-homology roof differences kill the continuous frequency,
exact integer generators kill the lattice frequencies, and coprime periods
kill the constant phase.  The raw high-frequency estimate integrates twice in
the independent collision coordinates of the original section, with
branchwise derivative norms summed by a weighted growth lemma.
\end{abstract}
