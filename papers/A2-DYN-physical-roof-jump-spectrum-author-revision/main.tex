\begin{abstract}
We study the original triangular Lorentz gas for
$R\in[9/20,47/100]$, retaining every physical collision, the
unit-speed clock, the prescribed section law and the exact
return, displacement and collision records. We continue the
complete native proof chain through its endpoint kernels and
exact-interval inversion. For the full smooth-guard and bounded
$C^1$ observation class, we identify the entire distributional
roof derivative as an integrable density plus a finite signed
sum of physical jumps at each collision count. The decomposition
keeps every original section side, unvisited boundary and
critical entrance. Its geometric support is common to the
observation class and has a bound uniform in radius at each
fixed count; no complexity bound uniform in the count is assumed.
We give the exact jump coefficient, including cancellations
at coincident roofs, with the inherited complete variation
budget and the original conditioning factor. An elementary
full-torus Fourier calculation then recovers the total squared
jump mass from the differentiated marked physical transform,
with an explicit finite-frequency error. The raw target forces
a corresponding collision-normalized central jump energy to
vanish. The previously proved zero-clearance critical-fan
payment transfers to this signed spectrum. The normalized
shallow comparison shortage and the alternative complete local
roof-variation condition remain open, as do the full raw
pointwise theorem and its four-coordinate inverse conclusion.
All inherited proof bodies and original arithmetic factors
are retained at their proved scopes.
