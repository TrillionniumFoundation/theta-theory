\begin{abstract}
We determine the maximal-collision onsets of full-phase equilibrium
probabilities for periodic planar configurations of separated smooth
strictly convex obstacles with positive curvature. The threshold
collar and every fixed derivative of the normalized remainder are
uniform in the number of flights. No finite-horizon or near-circle
hypothesis is required. A weighted Dirichlet construction controls the
stationary action, while a relative determinant estimate retains its
exponentially small physical flux. The leading count coefficient sees
the product of the two endpoint curvature factors. In contrast, two
centered Euclidean endpoint variances determine both contact curvatures
at any odd collision order, with inverse bounds uniform in the order
and at equal curvatures. We prove finite-offset and sampling estimates,
including the cost of the rare conditioning, and construct analytic
obstacles with identical leading count hierarchies which these records
distinguish. We also retain exact unlabelled identification on an
equal-gap family, its area and coalescence identities, marked source
response, and the complete circular specialization.
\end{abstract}
