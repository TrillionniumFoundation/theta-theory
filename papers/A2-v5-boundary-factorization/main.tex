\begin{abstract}
We prove relative two-boundary factorization for spatially varying
uniformly convex scalar twist chains. On a common endpoint
neighborhood, the stationary action and its normalized mixed derivative
converge exponentially to their half-line boundary counterparts, with
every fixed mixed derivative controlled independently of length. For
periodic planar dispersing billiards this gives collision-order-uniform
threshold laws and a nonlinear fixed-offset conditional limit under
full-phase equilibrium preparation, without a finite-horizon assumption.
An analytic fixed-area family has identical leading count and endpoint
metric data but distinct nonlinear probabilities. We also prove a sharp
pairwise one-third-H\"older inverse from four unlabelled threshold
amplitudes near a three-curvature collision. For channel-resolved records,
normalization by the mean first residual time removes the inverse-offset
factor in the timing sensitivity of an extrapolated curvature estimator;
its preparation cost is quantified. Exact inverse formulas, the sharp
circular-reference one-half exponent, marked response, and the complete
circular calculations accompany these results.
\end{abstract}
