\begin{abstract}
We study terminal stochastic realizations of compact-group experiments
with legal convex output constraints. For every fixed-point-free
finite-dimensional orthogonal representation of a compact connected Lie
group, the least dimension of a unit orbit is the optimal uniform
small-ball exponent. This supplies a width converse in every
conditional-centroid direction; a quadratic inner orbit polytope gives
a positive upper realization. For legal density matrices the exponents
agree up to a logarithmic loss. We give fixed rational $2\times2$ unitary
generators and a rational pure seed whose exact minimum profile is
$2\cdot3^t-1$, still necessary at errors at most $625^{-N}/16$, while
fixed positive subcritical error admits width $O(N)$ and requires
$\Omega(N/\log N)$. Elementary quaternion arithmetic proves freeness
and the trivial stabilizer. The noisy converse alone imports an
algebraic-generator spectral-gap theorem. A uniform rational compiler
constructs sparse rows and legal matrix decoders, with explicit finite
table and fair-bit bounds. The output is numerical, not quantum state
preparation, and the lower logarithmic gap is retained.
\end{abstract}
