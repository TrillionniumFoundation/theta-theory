\section{Conditional centroids at arbitrary cuts}
\begin{lemma}[Conditional-centroid contraction]\label{lem:centroid54}
Let $Z$ be a bounded random vector in a finite-dimensional Euclidean
space and $S$ the current hidden register. Choose an independent external
word $w$ whose physical action is orthogonal $R_w$. Suppose, for all
unit vectors $u,v_1,\ldots,v_k$,
\[
 \mathbb E_w\max_{i\le k}\langle v_i,R_wu\rangle\le1-a,
                                  \qquad 0<a<1.
\]
If the endpoint register $S'$ has at most $k$ labels, then
\[
 \mathbb E\|\mathbb E[R_wZ\mid S']\|
                  \le(1-a)\mathbb E\|\mathbb E[Z\mid S]\|.
\]
No restriction is imposed on intermediate widths. A fixed physically
identity word cannot increase the latter conditional norm.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Let $z_s=\mathbb E[Z\mid S=s]$ and $T_w(s,i)$ be the actual composite
stochastic row. The Markov property of the retained label gives
\[
 \Pr(S'=i)\mathbb E[R_wZ\mid S'=i]
       =\sum_s\Pr(S=s)\mathbb E_w T_w(s,i)R_wz_s.
\]
Pair with a unit vector in each nonzero endpoint centroid's direction,
sum in $i$, and use stochasticity to bound this by
$\sum_s\Pr(S=s)\mathbb E_w\max_i\langle v_i,R_wz_s\rangle$.
The hypothesized inequality gives the result. Zero masses and zero
centroids cause no difficulty. For a fixed physical identity word the
physical vector is unchanged, while the endpoint is a stochastic image
of the incoming label; conditional Jensen proves nonincrease. Hidden
rows on such a word need not be identity rows.
\end{proof}
