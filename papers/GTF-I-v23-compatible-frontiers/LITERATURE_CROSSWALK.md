# Primary-source crosswalk and attribution boundary — v23

## Newly checked comparisons

**Tsitsiklis (1993), Extremal properties of likelihood-ratio quantizers,
IEEE Transactions on Communications 41(4), 550–558.** The author's original
MIT PDF was inspected, including images of pp. 550 and 552 (Section III,
Proposition 3.1 and its likelihood-ratio proof mechanism). The affine
upper-envelope and threshold mechanisms in the new Bayesian and binary
channel proofs are classical. The manuscript supplies its full-profile
compatibility, attainable-channel recursion and exact stochastic minimax
evaluation; it does not claim to invent likelihood-ratio quantization.
Source: https://www.mit.edu/~jnt/Papers/J041-93-jnt-extrem.pdf
DOI: 10.1109/26.223779.

**Muller and Montufar (2022), The Geometry of Memoryless Stochastic Policy
Optimization in Infinite-Horizon POMDPs.** The original arXiv text and the
page containing Example 15 and Theorem 16 were inspected. Their feasible
occupation/frequency geometry and polynomial planning are prior art;
Example 15 already exhibits vanishing 2-by-2 minors. The new finite
protocol formulation explicitly handles cross-environment observable-row
classes, finite changing profiles, zero occupations and shared rows.
These distinctions do not establish that every generalization is novel.
The report's objection cannot be answered by calling rank-one constraints
new. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.07409 (Example 15, Theorem 16,
Remark 19; published at ICLR 2022).

**Putinar (1993), Positive Polynomials on Compact Semi-Algebraic Sets,
Indiana University Mathematics Journal 42(3), 969–984.** Publisher
bibliographic metadata was checked. The manuscript applies the classical
Archimedean positivity theorem after adding an explicit ball generator;
it does not propose a new positivity theorem.
Source: https://iumj.org/article/3638/cite/
DOI: 10.1512/iumj.1993.42.42045.

**Powers (2009), Rational certificates of positivity on compact
semialgebraic sets.** The original text's Theorem 6 was read. Its explicit
rational ball-generator condition is included in our feasible set.
Equalities are represented by both signs before collecting ideal
multipliers. Strict positivity gives finite-degree certificates for each
u>V; it does not imply an exact finite certificate at u=V.
Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/0911.1331.

## Norberg and priority status

Espen Norberg, Comparison of Statistical Experiments with Filtered
Probability Spaces, Statistics & Risk Modeling 20 (2002), 1–28,
DOI 10.1524/strm.2002.20.14.1. Publisher metadata was located again, and
an original PDF retrieval was attempted but did not deliver the proof
text. The original theorem/page comparison therefore remains incomplete.
No absence claim about Norberg's results is inferred from this failure.
Source: https://www.degruyterbrill.com/document/doi/10.1524/strm.2002.20.14.1/html

The new three-report formulas have supplied analytic proofs and exact
finite diagnostics. An exhaustive priority search for those formulas and
the binary-register recursion has not been certified. The resubmission
claims the stated mathematics, not an unsupported first-in-literature
status. No third-party full text is redistributed in the source archive.
