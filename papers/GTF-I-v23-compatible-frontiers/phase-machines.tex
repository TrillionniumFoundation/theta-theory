\section{Explicit phase transitions for the two-count certificate}
\label{sec:v20-phases}
For comparison with the new residual quotient, we give the complete
phase-state specification of Lemma~\ref{lem:v19-counter}. It realizes
the original $400$-training-trial rule exactly, not the majority rule of
Section~\ref{sec:v20-physical}. Let
\[
 C=(C_0,C_1)\in\{0,\ldots,250\}^2,
 \qquad \tau(x)=\min(x,250).
\]
The free clock records the training index, the within-trial observation
epoch, the validation phase, and deterministic processing epochs. It
contains no data-dependent information. At the start, $C=(0,0)$.

For a completed transcript $(x_1,x_2,w)$ define the deterministic update
\[
 T(C,x_1,x_2,w)=
 \bigl(\tau(C_0+\1_{\{(x_1,x_2)=(0,0)\}}),
       \tau(C_1+\1_{\{(x_1,x_2)=(1,1)\}})\bigr).
\]
The value of $w$ does not enter this training update. The phase transitions
are the following; $x$ denotes the current input, not an earlier unretained
report.
\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{p{0.22\textwidth}p{0.33\textwidth}p{0.34\textwidth}}
\toprule
Phase & Retained data & Next transition\\
\midrule
Training start & $C$ & Read $x_1$ and keep $(C,x_1)$.\\
Second report & $(C,x_1)$ & Read $x_2$ and keep $(C,x_1,x_2)$.\\
Terminal mark & $(C,x_1,x_2)$ & Read $w$ and keep $(C,x_1,x_2,w)$.\\
Count update & $(C,x_1,x_2,w)$ & Replace by $T(C,x_1,x_2,w)$; discard the transcript.\\
Event formation & $C$ & Replace by the event mask $A(C)$ defined below.\\
Candidate validation & $A$, partial transcript & Complete the transcript; replace it by $i=\1_A(x_1,x_2,w)$.\\
Target validation & $(A,i)$, partial transcript & Complete the transcript and emit $\1_A(y_1,y_2,v)-i$.\\
Score & A value in $\{-1,0,1\}$ & Terminate.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
The event formation map, including both threshold ties, is
\begin{align*}
 000\in A(C)&\Longleftrightarrow C_0<250,&
 001\in A(C)&\Longleftrightarrow C_0<150,\\
 110\in A(C)&\Longleftrightarrow C_1<150,&
 111\in A(C)&\Longleftrightarrow C_1<250.
\end{align*}
No other atom is included. For each report pair there are three choices:
both marked atoms, only its larger-target-mass atom, or neither. Thus
there are at most nine masks. Clipping at $250$ preserves every comparison,
including equality; no flag for a count exceeding the clipping threshold
is required.

At a training epoch the possible states are bounded by
$8\cdot251^2=504008$: the factor eight dominates every partial or complete
transcript buffer. At validation epochs the largest alphabet has at most
$9\cdot2\cdot8=144$ values, including the already computed
candidate-event indicator. Event formation and final emission need at
most nine and three states, respectively. Embed each phase alphabet into
$[504008]$ by a fixed injective encoding. Because the phase is supplied by
the free clock, these encodings need not have disjoint images. The next
row decodes the current phase's encoding and applies the displayed
transition. This gives formal deterministic rows with entries zero or
one at every epoch. The buffers, event mask, and validation indicator are
all charged. No unpriced data store is introduced by label reuse.
