\section{A third resource regime for the same physical task}
\label{sec:v22-physical}
The three-response obstruction, the short exact selector, and the
constant-width block audit concern the same positive-noise marked
collision family. A truncated absorbing comparison supplies a further
resource pair without changing that family or its threshold.

\begin{theorem}[Polynomial-horizon physical realization]
\label{thm:v22-physical}
Use the original row--event interface and the parameter range of
Theorem~\ref{thm:v21-physical}. Let $\beta=1/300$, $0<\theta<1/4$,
and $m,n\ge1$. There is a deterministic audit with $2n$ candidate
training trials and two validation trials whose expected signed score
is strictly greater than
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v22-physical-score}
 \sqrt2-1-\beta-\theta-2e_{m,n}(\theta).
\end{equation}
It uses at most $2m+2$ states at completed training-trial cuts,
$4m+4$ at intermediate report-bit cuts, three at the decision cut, and
twelve throughout validation. In particular, $\theta=1/200$, $m=400$
and $n=160000$ give score greater than $2/5$, with $320000$ training
trials and peak width at most $1604$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Write $a=(1-p)(1-q)$ and $c=pq$ for the actual blind product candidate
report probabilities, as in Theorem~\ref{thm:v21-physical}. On the
first $n$ reports apply Lemma~\ref{lem:v22-walk} to the indicator of
$00$. If the answer is positive retain $A_1$ and ignore the second
training phase. Otherwise use the next $n$ reports to compare the
indicator of $11$, retaining $A_0$ for a positive answer and $A_D$
for a negative answer. Conceptually generate the second phase even
when it is ignored. Independence of the reset preparations gives an
error probability at most $2e_{m,n}(\theta)$ for either comparison to
be wrong outside its indifference band.

On the complementary event the selected ideal payoff is within
$\theta$ of
$\max(1-a-c,1/2-a,1/2-c)$. Indeed $\min(a,c)\le1/4$, so at most one
coordinate is within $\theta$ of $1/2$. If neither is, the two
comparisons select the maximizing event; if one is, switching the
relevant events changes payoff by at most $\theta$. The three payoffs
have range at most one at each $(a,c)$. Their envelope is at least
$\sqrt2-1$ by Lemma~\ref{lem:v20-envelope}. Replacing the ideal target
by the physical target loses strictly less than $\beta$ and does not
change the candidate training law. This proves
\eqref{eq:v22-physical-score}.

The walk has $2m+1$ labels. The second phase adds the previously latched
$A_1$ label, giving $2m+2$. Retaining the first report bit doubles this
number; after the second bit, determine the relevant indicator and
discard the pair. The mark is read and discarded. A deterministic
clock supplies the phase and the within-phase time. At its end map the
remaining walk states to $A_0$ or $A_D$. The validation implementation
from Theorem~\ref{thm:v21-physical} uses at most twelve states, including
the event, first bit, and candidate-event value. No real-valued
randomization is needed by this construction.

For the stated integers, $4\theta m=2n\theta^2=8$, so
$e_{m,n}(\theta)=2e^{-8}<1/1000$. The rational estimates
$e>8/3$, $(8/3)^8>2000$, and $\sqrt2>707/500$ give
\[
 \sqrt2-1-\frac1{300}-\frac1{200}-\frac1{500}-\frac25
 >\frac{11}{3000}>0.
\]
This proves the strict physical margin, with the stated fixed training
count and peak width.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Three compatible resource bounds]
\label{cor:v22-physical-regimes}
The original physical task admits all three rows below, each followed
by two validations and having decision width three:
\[
\begin{array}{c|r|l}
 \text{training trials}&\text{peak width}&\text{training mechanism}\\\hline
 399&40602&\text{exact empirical selector}\\
 320000&1604&\text{truncated absorbing comparisons}\\
 12800\cdot2^{400}&12&\text{four-state block comparisons}.
\end{array}
\]
The all-auditor row-indexed decision optimum remains
$\kappa^{\rm rows}_{2/5}=3$ and the unrestricted finite-horizon
physical peak still satisfies $3\le W^{\rm phys}_{2/5}\le12$.
The table gives attainable points, not an optimal physical fixed-sample
frontier. In particular the $40602$ bound is not an all-auditor lower
bound for $399$ samples.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Combine Theorems~\ref{thm:v20-physical}, \ref{thm:v21-physical} and
\ref{thm:v22-physical}. The feedback-uniform two-state obstruction in
Theorem~\ref{thm:v21-physical} continues to apply to every audit in
this enlarged set of constructions.
\end{proof}

For a perturbed blind training source with per-active-trial defect
$\alpha$, the two-phase construction also has the bound
\[
 2\min\{1,2\alpha\min(n,m^2)\}
       +\beta_{\rm c}+\beta_{\rm t}
\]
on score change, by the proof of Corollary~\ref{cor:v22-active-training}.
The moving-collision parameter alone leaves that source unchanged, so
its training contribution is zero; the previous target transport and
bounded Gaussian/entropic consumer are retained without change. If
one makes the machine autonomous, an explicit bound is
$(6n+12)\max(4m+4,12)$ states, since the $2n+2$ trials each acquire two
bits and a mark, with the phase/decision boundaries included. The
clock multiplier is an implementation upper bound, not an optimized
autonomous-memory claim.
