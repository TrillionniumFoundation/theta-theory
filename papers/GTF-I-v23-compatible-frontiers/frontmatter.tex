\title[Compatible statistical experiments]{General Theta Foundations I:\\
Compatible Statistical Experiments and Resource Frontiers}
\author{Qian Qi}
\date{September 24, 2026}
\subjclass[2020]{Primary 62B05, 93E20; Secondary 60G44, 68Q45, 82C40}
\keywords{Statistical experiments, finite memory, causal realization,
minimax testing, likelihood-ratio quantization, polynomial certificates}
\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
We study statistical experiments subject to a register constraint at
each cut of a finite causal protocol. Their feasible occupation arrays
are characterized by linear flow equations and quadratic compatibility
equations, including at zero-probability histories. This yields
constructive realizations and a complete polynomial upper-certificate
hierarchy for the full-profile minimax value, without convexifying the
class of machines. For binary experiments we obtain an exact posterior
partition recursion for Bayesian decisions and an exact receiver-curve
recursion for minimax decisions with binary registers. In a three-report
experiment with overlapping binary noise, we compute the entire
register-profile frontier for both objectives; the resulting strict
gap shows that a free mixture of machines changes the minimax value.
The characterization is connected to finite-precision response-cover
auditors, acquired calibration, and positive-noise physical experiments,
with preparations, intermediate registers and clocks explicitly charged.
The marked continuation, residual, microscopic and Gaussian comparison
results are developed with their proofs and with their distinct
hypotheses retained.
\end{abstract}
