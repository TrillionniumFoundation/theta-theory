\section{Introduction and realization model}
This paper studies the stochastic label width needed to preserve a
terminal numerical experiment on a compact group. Two distinctions are
central. Conditional centroids need not lie on the target orbit, so the
converse requires estimates in every direction. Also, a legal pure output
is an extreme point, and exact correctness can impose a much larger
state requirement than any fixed positive error.

We prove that the least dimension of a unit orbit gives the optimal
uniform small-ball exponent of every fixed-point-free finite-dimensional
orthogonal representation of a compact connected Lie group. With a group
spectral gap this yields an all-profile width converse. A quadratic inner
approximation of the target orbit gives a positive common-row upper
construction. If $p_*$ is the least orbit dimension and $q$ the target
orbit dimension, their respective powers are $p_*/2$ and $q/2$; the
lower bound retains a logarithmic loss.

For full legal density-matrix output, the two dimensions are
$2(d-1)$. We then exhibit fixed rational $2\times2$ unitary generators
and a rational pure seed for which
\[
 W_{N,0}=2\cdot3^N-1,
 \qquad
 c_\varepsilon N/\log(N+2)\le W_{N,\varepsilon}
                          \le C_\varepsilon(N+1)
 \quad(0<\varepsilon<1/\sqrt2).
\]
The exponential profile persists for
$\varepsilon\le625^{-N}/16$. Freeness, the seed stabilizer, and this
robust exact regime are proved by elementary quaternion arithmetic
modulo five. The noisy upper bound has a uniform rational compiler with
sparse rows and explicit fair-bit budgets. Only the noisy converse uses
the imported spectral-gap theorem. No effective gap constant, quantum
state preparation, or scalar language-recognition separation is claimed.

\begin{definition}[Clocked numerical realization]\label{def:machine54}
Let $H$ be a compact group, $\mathcal A$ a finite nonempty command
alphabet, and $F_{s,j}:H\to Y_j$ continuous interfaces for finite seed
and query sets. Each $Y_j$ is a compact convex set in a finite-dimensional
normed space. At horizon $N$ choose finite nonempty registers
$S_0,\ldots,S_N$, initialization rows $E_s$, stochastic matrices
$T_{t,a}:S_{t-1}\to S_t$, and terminal decoder maps $D_j:S_N\to Y_j$.
The realized numerical output on $w=a_1\cdots a_N$ is
$E_sT_{1,a_1}\cdots T_{N,a_N}D_j$, and the physical product is
$u_w=u_{a_N}\cdots u_{a_1}$. Write $W_{N,\varepsilon}$ for the least
$\max_t|S_t|$ with worst-word, worst-seed and worst-query error at most
$\varepsilon$.
\end{definition}
This command width excludes a sampled answer register. Every declared
persistent label, including unused padding and retained private
information, is counted. The original invariant leaves the external
clock, horizon, arbitrary real tables, their construction and lookup,
exact arithmetic and exact atomic sampling uncharged. The uniform
compiler below prices the finite tables and fair-bit sampling separately.
A numerical density matrix is not a physically sampled quantum state.
A prescribed joint terminal law can be one query; online or adaptive
observations are not part of this model.

The proof chain is
\[
 \text{least infinitesimal orbit rank}
 \ \Longrightarrow\ \text{uniform caps}
 \ \Longrightarrow\ \text{executable centroid contraction},
\]
combined with inner orbit polytopes for the upper bound and disjoint
support or separated decoder caps at the extreme endpoint. The structural
companion concerns clock removal and finite physical actions. No result
from that companion, or from a predecessor manuscript, is required for
the proofs in this article.
