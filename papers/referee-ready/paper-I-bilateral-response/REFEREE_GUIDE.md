# Referee guide — Paper I, revision v4

## Central claims

The paper proves a sharp bilateral product-tail theorem and verifies it in an
explicit nonconjugate moving collision family.  It also proves arbitrary-source
all-order susceptibility, exact predictable innovations, and an analytic
moving open-billiard response theorem on a fixed symbolic space.

## Suggested audit order

1. **Crossed interpolation:** verify the equivalence between the strict rate
   determinant and simultaneous contraction of both time factors.
2. **Finite-DQ and totalization:** check that the common product envelope is
   independent of the finite difference and that common-refinement
   cancellations preserve occurrence UIDs.
3. **Symplectic collision model:** verify invertibility, area preservation,
   moving seams, and the unstable quotient transfer formula.
4. **Exact letters:** differentiate the transfer operator independently and
   check the loss `W^{r+k,1} -> W^{r,1}`.
5. **All-order words:** check centered contraction, normal convergence, and
   future insertion without a reset.
6. **Nonconjugacy:** check the two fixed points, their regularity, and the
   disjoint multiplier ranges.
7. **Arbitrary-source theorem:** verify that neither source nor test is assumed
   to be a coboundary or an invariant-density derivative.
8. **Exact innovations:** audit the conditional-uniform induction for a
   predictable parameter path.
9. **Symbolic desingularization:** check the uniform graph-transform
   contraction, analytic parameter dependence in a Hölder sequence norm, and
   the Ruelle perturbation argument.
10. **Specular nonconjugacy:** verify persistence and nonzero derivative of the
    isolated two-periodic flight length and the non-coboundary cylinder
    observable.

## High-risk proof locations

- analytic dependence of the infinite collision sequence in the no-eclipse
  family;
- uniform Hölder constants under parameter motion;
- normal convergence of differentiated arbitrary-source susceptibilities;
- equivalence between the physical open-billiard observable and its symbolic
  pullback.

## Permanent scope

The Markov collision theorem is an actual all-order moving-seam theorem.  The
open-billiard theorem is an actual analytic no-eclipse specular theorem.  The
paper does not infer the same analytic coding for arbitrary recurrent periodic
Sinai tables without a fixed symbolic nonwandering set.

A positive report should state separately whether the referee checked the
explicit collision calculation, the abstract bilateral theorem, and the open
billiard symbolic theorem.
