This manuscript restates the control-uniform homogenization input it needs from
Paper III.  Filtering, sequential lower/upper games, simultaneous relaxed
games, and pure-strategy games are treated as separate branches.

The phrase “pure Isaacs” in this manuscript means equality of the pure lower
and upper Hamiltonians, not mixed relaxed equality.  The weighted PDE result
also requires its separately stated comparison hypothesis.
