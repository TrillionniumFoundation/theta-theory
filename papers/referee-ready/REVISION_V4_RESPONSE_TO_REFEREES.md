## 2. The two new mathematical tools

### 2.1 Symbolic desingularization

For an analytic no-eclipse family of open dispersing billiards, the collision
itinerary belongs to one fixed subshift of finite type.  The parameter-dependent
collision sequence is constructed as the fixed point of a uniformly
contracting reflection graph transform.  The coding map, roof, unstable
Jacobian, and physical observables are analytic as Hölder functions on the
fixed symbolic space.  Thus moving physical singularities are removed before
differentiation, and the Ruelle operators form an analytic family on one
operator space.

This produces a genuine nonconjugate moving-scatterer theorem for arbitrary
Hölder correlation sources.  It is neither a static invariant-density identity
nor an exact coboundary.

### 2.2 Exact-innovation collision platform

The second tool is an explicit symplectic Markov collision map with four
full-cover branches and three moving seams.  Its one-dimensional unstable
quotient has exact transfer operator

```text
(L_a h)(y) = sum_i w_i(a) h(s_{i-1}(a)+w_i(a)y).
```

The same family has:

1. all-order moving-seam response on a fixed Sobolev--variation ladder;
2. arbitrary-source correlation susceptibility;
3. branchwise roof/displacement pressure and full-frequency resolvents;
4. exact predictable innovations under a history-dependent parameter path;
5. a nonautonomous martingale and rough limit;
6. an exact pointwise risk-sensitive DPP;
7. a controlled HJB and a two-player pure Isaacs branch;
8. microscopic exponential tangent laws converging to continuum tangent laws.

This makes the actual response, pressure, homogenization, HJB, game, and
representation chain a chain on one deterministic collision system rather
than a collage of unrelated examples.

## 4. Response and CM2 objections

### R1. The hard response theorem was assumed as S1--S3

The controlling Paper I does not use S1--S3.  For the Markov collision model,
all estimates follow from two displayed formulas:

```text
||Q_a^n||_{W^{r,1}_0 -> W^{r,1}_0} <= C_r rho^n,

partial_a^k L_a h
 = sum_i [k v_i r_i^{k-1} h^{(k-1)}(psi_i^a)
          + w_i r_i^k h^{(k)}(psi_i^a)].
```

They imply a product envelope for every insertion and every finite order.
For open dispersing billiards, the response theorem follows from the analytic
fixed-symbolic-space Ruelle family.

### R2. The positive theorem was only differentiated invariance

Paper I now proves response for independently prescribed centered
`F_a` and tests `G_a`:

```text
chi(a;F,G) = sum_{n>=0} <Q_a^n F_a,G_a>.
```

No identity `F_a=(I-L_a)H_a` is assumed.  In the open-billiard branch, a
locally constant cylinder observable is chosen with nonconstant periodic sums,
which excludes cohomology to a constant.

### R3. Static averages were confused with dynamical response

The all-order susceptibility theorem is an infinite dynamical correlation
series with normal convergence of every derivative.  Static flux
differentiation is not used in its proof.

### R4. Exact-current and exact-conjugacy examples were tautological

Neither is used as the main witness.  Nonconjugacy of the Markov collision
family is proved from two isolated regular fixed-point multipliers.  The open
billiard is nonconjugate because an isolated two-periodic flow length changes.

### R5. Assembly reset was not a closed source theory

No reset is used.  Every derivative letter maps one level of the invariant
ladder to a lower level, every centered transfer contracts on each level, and
every future insertion is a bounded composition on the same ladder.

### R6. Higher-order radial singularities blocked finite-order scope

The main all-order theorem is no longer a radial-invariance expansion.  The
explicit moving-seam map has exact letters of every order, and the analytic
open-billiard Ruelle family has all finite derivatives on one Hölder space.

### R7. Continuous-time moving-family response was absent

Paper II proves two actual full-frequency results:

1. a Diophantine branch-roof theorem for the same nonconjugate moving collision
   family, including parameter derivatives of the complete resolvent;
2. a moving open-billiard Dolgopyat theorem on the fixed symbolic space under a
   verified temporal-shear condition.

The oriented renewal theorem then converts these resolvents into suspension
correlation response.

### R8. The response modules were not canonical

The main actual collision theorem uses the canonical periodic Sobolev ladder
`W^{r,1}(T)` and its centered subspace.  The open-billiard theorem uses the
standard Hölder space of one fixed subshift.  No custom label quotient is a
load-bearing operator domain.

### R9. The conditional CM2 criterion had no actual application

In the finite occurrence atlas of the Markov collision family, source and
target contractions immediately give

```text
|<Q^n (partial_a^k L) Q^m g,f>| <= C rho^{m+n}.
```

Thus the product-time face estimate, finite-DQ convergence, higher-source
Cauchy theorem, and arbitrary-source susceptibility are all actual theorems.
The old interface forest is not imported.

## 6. Homogenization and HJB objections

### H1. Paper III imported a nonexistent response theorem

Paper III uses only the explicit coefficient theorem of Paper II.  The long-time
limit is proved separately from exact conditional innovations; response is not
used as a substitute for an invariance principle.

### H2. The microscopic problem was undefined

The exact one-step collision evaluation is

```text
T_h phi(x)
 = (1/theta) log sum_i w_i(Theta(x))
   exp(theta phi(x+h b(x)+sqrt(h) m_i(Theta(x)))).
```

Its finite-horizon recursion is the prelimit.  The controlled recursion is also
displayed.  Existence and stability are immediate from the finite branch
formula.

### H3. The residual lemma merely named the desired estimate

No anisotropic residual lemma is used.  A second-order Taylor expansion of the
finite branch formula gives the complete consistency operator and an explicit
third-moment remainder.

### H4. Contact selection was invalid

The monotone-scheme proof uses ordinary maxima of scalar slow-state functions.
There is no microscopic singular stratum in the viscosity contact problem.

### H5. Pairing smallness did not imply a pointwise inequality

No distributional pairing occurs in the HJB proof.  Monotonicity and pointwise
consistency give the sub- and supersolution inequalities.

### H6. Global comparison was unavailable

The actual coefficients are globally Lipschitz and uniformly elliptic.  The
uncontrolled entropic equation is linearized by Cole--Hopf.  The controlled
branch uses globally bounded structure conditions and standard uniformly
elliptic HJB comparison.

### H7. The correct rough second level was not fixed

The controlling lift contains

```text
sum_{i<j} M_i tensor M_j + (1/2) sum_i M_i tensor M_i,
```

so it is the canonical geometric lift.  Its symmetric part is exactly one half
of the first-level tensor square.

### H8. Full-sequence nonautonomous convergence lacked a rate

For the actual model, predictable characteristics give full-sequence
martingale convergence directly.  The block theorem separately states the
strict rate window and does not promote qualitative WIP to a full-sequence
claim.

### H9. The theta terminology lacked an intrinsic structure

Paper III introduces forward theta-independence and proves it for exact
collision innovations.  Paper V proves that probability tangents plus
covariance curvature characterize the entropic theta functional and that the
tangent cocycle characterizes its dynamic semigroup.

## 8. Representation objections

### V1. Semigroup differentiability was assumed

Paper V proves a Banach-space parabolic implicit-function theorem.  The
terminal solution map is `C^{k-1}` in a uniformly parabolic Hölder window, and
its derivative solves the tangent PDE.

### V2. Viscosity convergence was incorrectly used to infer derivative convergence

That argument is absent.  The actual entropic semigroup is analytic by its
logarithmic moment formula.  General degenerate limits are used only when
uniform tangent estimates are separately available.

### V3. The smooth diffusion representation was only standard postprocessing

The revised main result is not merely Feynman--Kac.  It is the tangent-law
characterization and the convergence of microscopic deterministic tangent laws
to the continuum tangent law.

### V4. `Z=sigma^T p` was inverted incorrectly

No nonlinear inversion occurs.  The tangent drift is written directly as
`b+theta a Du`; the quadratic BSDE uses the already established decoupling
field.

### V5. The FBSDE/BSDE identity was not a construction

The entropic quadratic BSDE is independently identified by exponentiation and
bounded-solution uniqueness.  Its tangent is a linear BSDE under the tilted
law.  The finite deterministic tangent laws converge to this law.

### V6. The alleged Girsanov theorem was algebraic

Paper V now constructs

```text
M_s = exp(theta u(s,X_s)-theta u(t,x))
    = stochastic exponential of integral theta Z dW,
```

proves it is a uniformly integrable density martingale, states the
Radon--Nikodym derivative, constructs the changed Brownian motion, and obtains
the changed state drift.  It includes both a Novikov route and a bounded-density
BMO route.

### V7. The linearity obstruction lacked precise hypotheses

It is stated for a probability kernel at every initial state and every bounded
continuous terminal payoff.

### V8. The representation paper lacked a new structural theorem

The static characterization proves that

```text
first derivative = probability tangent
second derivative = theta times tangent covariance
```

forces the logarithmic moment-generating functional.  The dynamic theorem adds
the tangent-kernel cocycle and characterizes the entropic theta-semigroup.

### V9. Path and second-order branches were labels without a DPP

The path entropic branch is defined by conditional exponential moments and
proves DPP, PPDE, and quadratic BSDE.  The volatility branch uses a family
stable under conditioning and pasting, proves its DPP, and invokes the typed
