## What remains to be judged on its mathematical content

The new statements connect actual-support exact task feasibility to the zero risk face, extend executable common-encoder risk compactness to coherent infinite prefixes with summable criteria, and transfer full microscopic observable approximations uniformly across all finite retained widths. The exact-profile certificate combines a finite decision gap with an independently established costed experiment comparison. Product compactness, convex minimax, finite-rank projection, compatible covers and Galerkin convergence have all been subtracted from any standalone novelty claim. Whether the resulting connection has the originality and depth required by the requested journal standard remains for independent refereeing.
