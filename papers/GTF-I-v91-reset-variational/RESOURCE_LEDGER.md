# Resource ledger — Revision 89

The unknown interface is ordered, memoryless, input-consuming and classical-output. D uses unhalved trace norm. The original measurement and learning resource ledgers are preserved in `predecessor-v88-audit` and earlier unchanged sections.

| Quantity | Exact convention |

| V | Sum n_r² of a fixed public profile, not its maximum component |

| Learning/description | Retained balanced-interior fixed-k query law and public index; no general polynomial dictionary claim |

The profile lower may discard unused slots but still charges the complete reservation. A high-cost policy may erase all information, so only a budget-class optimum has a matching lower. The feasibility interval s_* and sufficient allowance from the normalized realization do not compute the discrimination interval s_0. Zero tangents and unrestricted higher-order terms remain outside the nonzero-tangent law.

The reset-defect theorem is conditional: final separation changes by at most 2epsilon. Fixed calibration error need not preserve a signal tending to zero. Polynomial classical certificate arithmetic does not synthesize GHZ encodings, general recoveries, collective readouts or hardware. No finite experiment is represented as a physical implementation.
