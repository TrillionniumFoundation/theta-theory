# Retained shallow-cancellation attempt

At a fixed regular Gauss angle, one selected physical component with roof endpoints a<b has
D(A 1_(a,b)) = A' 1_(a,b) dt + A(a+) delta_a - A(b-) delta_b.
For a smooth scalar test psi, the paired endpoint term is bounded by
|A(a+) - A(b-)| ||psi||_infinity + A(b-) ||psi'||_infinity |b-a|.

This gives weak signed cancellation. It does not bound roof height: reconstruction at t uses a discontinuous tail indicator, and for a<t<b the two endpoint charges have not canceled, however small b-a is. Thus this shortcut cannot remove the shallow endpoint terms in328 by signed cancellation alone.

Native317 retains the nonzero-offset term -a_w/(g^T G^-1 g), whose current integrated turning bound gives no small-clearance factor. Native322 pays zero-clearance incoming critical coefficients. Native335 pays positive-width leading critical coefficients, not the uniform nonleading common-roof remainder. Native293's common-roof comparison requires actual guarded tubes and optical distortion, which do not cover the entire shallow strip. Native336 gives finite-count side decomposition without a uniform-in-count boundary-map sublevel rate.

A useful next theorem would bound the complete weighted mass of roofs lying between matched zero/δ crossings, including unmatched branches and critical remainders, at the original normalization and after the full physical sum. That statement is not proved here. This record preserves a failed implication, not a no-go result or a substitute research objective.
