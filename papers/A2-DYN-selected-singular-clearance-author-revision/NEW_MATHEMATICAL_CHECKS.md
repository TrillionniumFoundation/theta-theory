# New proof checks and their limits

The written continuous proof is core338. The additional program verify_selected_clearance_multiplier.py checks the optimized annular-cutoff constant symbolically, both physical block index formulas in 333,300 finite cases with 1≤m≤100, exact rational θ_r<q0^(1/4), the 47/3 incidence weight sum, linear-regime fractional exponents and the single-conditioning powers. These checks are regression diagnostics only, not proof of the continuum or all-count billiard kernel.

The proof itself treats every m≥1 and every interior index. If j<i, the block is 0..i; otherwise it is i..m. All selected constants use the same C_end of eq:ce-constants. The density starts from dα dp/(4π), not from the conditioned version of eq:ce-joint. The interior-to-roof step introduces c_*^-1 once; inherited physical boundary constants already contain theirs.

The endpoint-momentum kernel is not substituted for a boundary turning measure. The old outgoing tails θ_r^j+θ_r^(m−1−j) are retained. They become O(δ) only under the explicit finite-count hypothesis θ_r^d≤δ. The original guard satisfies this after a fixed-B depth threshold. Both finite-depth conditions are required before summing the physical union. The eventual logarithmic-depth substitution does not change ε(B), any physical window or the order of limits.

Full selected pth moments are claimed only for 1<p<4/3; the 4/3 endpoint remains weak. Shallow-source capacity abundance, full normalized local derivative charge, original conditional denominators and four-coordinate Jacobian are not checked by these finite programs and remain open as stated.

The six predecessor verification programs remain unchanged and are rerun alongside the new one. Source graph, archive integrity and TeX checks are separate execution stages. Independent expert reconstruction and journal readiness are not inferred from any stage.
