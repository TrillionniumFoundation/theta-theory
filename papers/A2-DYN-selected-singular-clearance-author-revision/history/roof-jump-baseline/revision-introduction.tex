\subsection*{Inherited results and the present additions}
The complete native chain through
Sections~\ref{sec:lc-length}--\ref{sec:ce-kernel} is retained.
It includes length-sensitive physical source transport,
normalized deep-clearance payment, all-label variation,
the conservative return tower, endpoint-tested operators,
the quantified original section, exact roof-interval inversion,
and the selected endpoint kernel. In particular, the critical
coefficient estimate of Theorem~\ref{thm:ce-critical} is an
unscaled tightness result. It is not an $m^{-2}$ local limit.

The new geometric result describes the actual sides of every
physical roof source at fixed collision count. It keeps the
first-hit inequalities, all visited reflections, all relevant
third-disk clearances, every original section decision, and
the exact terminal and occupation conventions. Closures are
taken at a fixed radius before the parameterized family is
decomposed. Lemma~\ref{lem:sbv-geometric-sides} produces a
finite family of genuine side maps, each constant in the
physical roof or strictly monotone on its open piece, with
a finite count bound uniform in radius for that fixed
collision count. No estimate on its growth with the count
is inferred from finite semialgebraic decomposition.

For the complete density vector
$f_\ell=f^{w;a,b,\Psi}_{\ell,m,R}$ of
Theorem~\ref{thm:wo-bv}, the new
Theorem~\ref{thm:sbv-physical} proves
\[
 Df_\ell=d_\ell(t)\,dt+
                  \sum_{u\in\mathcal S_{m,R}}a_\ell(u)\delta_u,
 \qquad
 \sum_\ell\left(\|d_\ell\|_1+\sum_u|a_\ell(u)|\right)
                  \le\mathcal V_m^w(a,b,\Psi).
\]
There is no singular-continuous derivative. The critical
incoming coefficient is exactly
$w_xV_x\omega(x)\beta_{\ell,y}(x)/
(2c_*\sqrt{\det G_x})$.
Constant-roof regular sides supply their actual signed fluxes.
All contributions at the same roof and label are combined
before taking an absolute value. Other labels are never
combined. Nonconstant sides can yield unbounded integrable
turning densities, which are retained in $d_\ell$.
This is a statement on the arbitrary smooth guard and
bounded $C^1$ tests already admitted by native 332; it does
not silently require those observations to be subanalytic.

Write $\mathcal F(\vartheta,s)$ for the original transform
with all three integer frequencies and the physical roof
frequency. Theorem~\ref{thm:js-spectrum} proves, at each
fixed $m,R$,
\[
 \lim_{S\to\infty}\frac1{2S(2\pi)^3}
  \int_{-S}^S\int_{[-\pi,\pi]^3}
          s^2|\mathcal F(\vartheta,s)|^2\,d\vartheta\,ds
             =\sum_{\ell,u}|a_\ell(u)|^2.
\]
The finite-frequency error in \eqref{eq:js-finite} retains
the separation of every pair of distinct physical roofs
and the transform of the complete integrable derivative.
It makes no uniform separation assumption and exchanges
no frequency limit with the radius supremum or collision
limit. This Fourier average is an identity for the
unchanged physical density, not a replacement of the
pointwise objective by an averaged limit theorem.

For a fixed central observation equal to one on a smaller
original central window, Theorem~\ref{thm:js-raw-necessary}
shows that the raw error $E_m(M_1)$ controls its jump energy:
\[
 \sup_R m^2\sum_{\ell,u}
        |\chi_{\ell,m,R}(u)a_\ell(u)|^2
                 \le2D_{\rm all}^{1}E_m(M_1).
\]
This is a necessary consequence, not a sufficient raw
criterion. Corollary~\ref{cor:js-paid-fans} places the
already normalized zero-clearance critical-fan payment
inside this signed identity, including coincident roofs
and cross terms. That old payment is not claimed anew.

The remaining original shortage keeps its full nongrazing
comparison pool. The alternative scalar bridge in
Proposition~\ref{prop:ui-inherited-bridge} already uses
the historical fixed-window theorem; it still needs the
complete normalized local roof-derivative charge to
vanish in the stated order. The new absence of a Cantor
part does not prove that charge small: both actual jumps
and narrow integrable derivative concentrations must
be controlled. Conditional microscopic-return and
four-coordinate inverse conclusions retain their actual
denominator, arithmetic and Jacobian hypotheses.
