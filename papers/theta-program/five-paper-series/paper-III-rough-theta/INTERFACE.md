## Optimal-rate scope

The test distance for `P3-RWIP-OPTIMAL-WETA-P` is generated by the regular
Stein--Dirichlet class `Sigma_(eta,p)`: Lipschitz rough functionals whose
signature pullbacks have the second-derivative regularity required by the
finite-dimensional Stein theorem.  A smooth midpoint-bridge cylindrical test
belongs uniformly to this class and supplies the matching lower bound.

The export does **not** assert an exact rate for the full bounded-Lipschitz
Kantorovich--Rubinstein distance on the entire rough Sobolev space.  Nor does it
assert a topology- or test-class-free optimal exponent.

The optimal typed rate gives the explicit full-scale block window

\[
\frac{2}{1+(1/2-\eta)}<\kappa<2,
\qquad m_\epsilon=\epsilon^{-\kappa}.
\]
