## R.1 Typed rough space and test distance

Fix

```text
p>6,
1/3<eta-1/p,
eta<1/2.
```

The state topology is the step-two fractional-Sobolev rough metric

\[
\|x-y\|_{W^{\eta,p}}
+
\|\mathbb x-\mathbb y\|_{W^{2\eta,p/2}}^{1/2}.
\]

The quantitative law distance is not the full Lipschitz--KR distance.  It is
the Stein--Dirichlet distance generated by the normalized class
`Sigma_(eta,p)`: rough-Lipschitz functionals whose signature pullbacks have the
second-derivative regularity required by the finite-dimensional Stein theorem.

## R.2 Matching rate in the declared distance

For the affine enriched four-branch random walk,

\[
cN^{-(1/2-\eta)}
\le
d_{SD}^{\eta,p}
(\mathbf W_N,\mathbf B_\Sigma)
\le CN^{-(1/2-\eta)}.
\]

The upper bound is the Stein--Dirichlet enriched Donsker estimate plus the
Brownian bridge interpolation error.

For the lower bound, a smooth cylindrical midpoint-defect functional

\[
F_N(\mathbf x)
=c_0N^{-3/2+\eta}
\sum_k
\phi(\sqrt N D_{k,N}(\pi_1\mathbf x))
\]

has uniformly bounded `Sigma_(eta,p)` norm.  It vanishes on every mesh-affine
walk and has Brownian expectation of order `N^{-(1/2-eta)}`.

## R.3 Permanent metric boundary

An exact rate for the full bounded-Lipschitz Kantorovich--Rubinstein distance is
not exported.  The cited Stein argument explicitly needs the regularity of the
pulled-back second derivative.  “Optimal” here always means optimal in the
declared Stein--Dirichlet rough test distance.

For smooth endpoint tests with nonzero third cumulant, the separate endpoint
rate is `N^(-1/2)`.

## R.4 Nonautonomous block window

Putting `delta=1/2-eta` and `m_epsilon=epsilon^(-kappa)`, accumulated block
error vanishes when

\[
\frac{2}{1+\delta}<\kappa<2.
\]

No rate is inferred from qualitative WIP alone, and no topology- or
test-class-free optimal exponent is exported.
