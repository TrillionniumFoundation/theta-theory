\[

m^2\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{t\in\mathcal C_{m,R,n,k}(M)}
\left(\mathsf U^{\rm low,end}_{B,m}(t)-K_BP^{\rm ng}_{r_B,m}(t)\right)_+=0

for every fixed central parameter (M). The source is native 329's original positive restriction; the comparison pool is the unchanged **full** original pool.
