\section{One-copy variation of the complete exact-label vector}
\label{sec:av-all-labels}

All collision states, section rectangles, labels and physical roofs in this
section are those of Section~\ref{sec:rv-records}.  Fix $m\ge1$ and
$R\in[R_-,R_+]$.  The initial section is counted in $A_{m,R}$ and
the terminal membership is tested separately, exactly as in
\eqref{eq:v76-event}.  Write $\ell=(n,k)\in\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z^2$;
components outside the actual label range are zero.  For $w=1$, or for
the original $w=W^\varepsilon$ with $\varepsilon>0$ fixed, retain
$p^w_{\ell,m,R}=p^w_{n,k,m,R}$ from \eqref{eq:rv-measures} and put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:av-all-constant}
 D_{\rm all}^w=D_{\rm base}^w+B_{\rm sec,end}+2B_{\rm sec,int}.
\end{equation}
Every constant on the right has already been defined on the original
physical family in \eqref{eq:rv-section-budget} and
\eqref{eq:rv-variation-constants}.  In particular there is only the
original conditioning factor $c_*^{-1}$.  The complete physical word
sum is part of those constants.

\begin{theorem}[Summed variation over every exact physical label]
\label{thm:av-all-bv}
For every $R,m$ and either specified weight,
\begin{align}
 \sum_{\ell}\|Dp^w_{\ell,m,R}\|_{\rm TV}
        &\le D_{\rm all}^w,                              \label{eq:av-all-tv}\\
 \sum_{\ell}\|p^w_{\ell,m,R}\|_1&\le1,\qquad
 \sum_{\ell}\|p^w_{\ell,m,R}\|_\infty
        \le\tfrac12D_{\rm all}^w,                       \label{eq:av-all-height}\\
 \sum_{\ell}\|p^w_{\ell,m,R}(\cdot+h)-p^w_{\ell,m,R}\|_1
        &\le D_{\rm all}^w|h|\qquad(h\in\mathbb R).
                                                               \label{eq:av-all-translation}
\end{align}
Every component has support in the actual interval
$[m\tau_-,m\tau_+]$.  The sums precede the suprema over the radius
and collision count.  No factor equal to the number of occupation or
lattice labels is present.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For clarity, use the unit vectors $e_\ell$ of the label space with its
$\ell^1$ norm.  On a regular physical word define
\[
 v(x)=\eta_R(x)\eta_R(T_R^mx)
              e_{(A_{m,R}(x),K^{\rm c}_{m,R}(x))}.
\]
This vector has norm zero or one.  Cut each maximal fixed-angle
physical interval by all the actual preimages of the original finite
section sides.  The analytic slicing and exhaustion in
Lemma~\ref{lem:rv-jumps} give complementary regular chambers on
which $v$ is constant.  The physical displacement is fixed on a
word.  Artificial chart seams with the same vector on both sides
cancel before absolute values.

Let $\mathscr A$ be the physical coarea coefficient in
Lemma~\ref{lem:rv-jumps}.  Inside a chamber, the derivative of
$w\mathscr A v$ has $\ell^1$ norm at most the absolute derivative
of $w\mathscr A$.  Thus the complete interior charge is bounded by
the interior part of $D_{\rm base}^w$.  At every genuine physical
endpoint, the vector has one-sided norm at most one.  The cap and
unvisited-disk charges, the incoming positive critical sectors and
the proved zero visited-grazing traces are exactly those in
Lemma~\ref{lem:rbv-endpoints}.  Multiplication by this vector does
not increase any positive one-sided charge.  Charges on different
physical words are kept in their already complete positive sum;
no cancellation between different words is assumed.  The boundary
part of $D_{\rm base}^w$ therefore pays all those endpoints.

At a regular interior visit crossing, the vector changes from one
unit vector to another if both endpoint tests hold, and remains zero
otherwise.  Its $\ell^1$ jump is at most two.  At the initial or
terminal section boundary it changes between zero and a single unit
vector, at cost at most one.  A change in the initial occupation is
included in that initial transition; no visit at index $m$ is added.
For coincident visit boundaries, order the affected visits and
telescope the vector changes.  The total is bounded by two for each
changed interior visit and one for each changed endpoint test.
All these sides, with their original finite side intervals, are
already included in \eqref{eq:rv-section-budget}.  Their complete
charge is at most $B_{\rm sec,end}+2B_{\rm sec,int}$.

One can express this calculation without any vector-valued
differentiation theorem.  Choose any finite collection of labels
and compactly supported $C^1$ scalar tests $\psi_\ell$, each with
$\|\psi_\ell\|_\infty\le1$.  Integrate
$\sum_\ell p^w_{\ell,m,R}\psi_\ell'$ by parts first on a finite
regular chamber exhaustion.  Each interior, physical endpoint and
section jump is bounded by precisely the positive charges above.
The source against the test derivatives is integrable.  The finite
total positive charge permits passage to the exhaustion, just as
in Theorem~\ref{thm:rv-exact-bv}.  Taking independent tests for each
component gives the sum of its distributional variation norms.
Increasing the finite label set proves \eqref{eq:av-all-tv}.
Equivalently, at fixed count only finitely many labels are attainable
by the finite physical horizon, but the bound does not use their
number.

The exact label events partition the original source with both
endpoint memberships.  Positivity and $0\le w\le1$ give
\[
 \sum_\ell\int p^w_{\ell,m,R}(t)\,dt
 =\int w\eta_R(T_R^mx)\,d\nu_R^*(x)\le1.
\]
Each nonnegative compactly supported BV component starts and ends
at zero.  Reaching a value $a$ at a Lebesgue point and returning
to zero costs variation at least $2a$.  Consequently
$2\|p^w_{\ell,m,R}\|_\infty\le\|Dp^w_{\ell,m,R}\|_{\rm TV}$.
Summing proves the height estimate.  Finally, the BV representative
satisfies
\[
 |p(t+h)-p(t)|\le |Dp|\bigl([\min(t,t+h),\max(t,t+h)]\bigr)
\]
outside irrelevant endpoint conventions.  Tonelli integration in
$t$, followed by the positive label sum, proves
\eqref{eq:av-all-translation}.  The support follows from the true
flight bounds.  Null sets are understood at the fixed radius, as in
the original target.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Bounded tests of the exact labels]
\label{cor:av-label-tests}
For any complex numbers $a_\ell$ with $|a_\ell|\le1$, the density
$\sum_\ell a_\ell p^w_{\ell,m,R}$ has $L^1$ norm at most one and
variation at most $D_{\rm all}^w$.  Its translations have $L^1$
error at most $D_{\rm all}^w|h|$, and its roof Fourier transform is
bounded by $\min\{1,D_{\rm all}^w/|s|\}$ for $s\ne0$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The summed $L^1$ and derivative norms give convergence and the
asserted bounds, first on finite label sums and then by their
absolute norm convergence.  Integrating the finite derivative
measure by parts on the compact physical roof support gives the
Fourier assertion.  The translation assertion follows from
\eqref{eq:av-all-translation}.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Information retained.}
This result adds a summed label budget to the separate bounds in Theorem~\ref{thm:rv-exact-bv};
it does not sum those separate bounds by counting labels.  The
coefficients in Corollary~\ref{cor:av-label-tests} are independent
of the roof and trajectory within one label.  An arbitrary Borel
trajectory selector has no asserted BV derivative.  Such selectors
will instead be handled by a positive trajectory-level error in the
next section.  All physical boundaries, the original guard when
present, and all original conditional and arithmetic conventions
remain unchanged.
