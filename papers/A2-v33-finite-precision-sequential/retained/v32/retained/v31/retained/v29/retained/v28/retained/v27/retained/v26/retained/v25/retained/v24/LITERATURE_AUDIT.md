- Stefanov–Uhlmann–Vasy, *Local and global boundary rigidity and the geodesic X-ray transform in the normal gauge*, arXiv:1702.03638. The primary arXiv abstract was retrieved: boundary distance determines the metric locally near a strictly convex boundary point; lens rigidity is also treated under geometric convexity assumptions. The present unknown is a periodic obstacle geometry under a different locally marked probability experiment, not their general metric.
- Gurfinkel–Noakes–Stoyanov, *Travelling Times in Scattering by Obstacles in Curved Space*, arXiv:2003.12261. The primary abstract was retrieved: exterior travelling times yield time-preserving conjugacy of nontrapping flows and, under nonpositive curvature and strictly convex obstacles, obstacle determination. The present local-probe registry is not a substitute for that data set.
- Noakes–Stoyanov, *Travelling times in scattering by obstacles*, arXiv:1404.4147. The primary abstract was retrieved: generic exterior travelling-time/flow-conjugacy results and constructive recovery for two planar convex obstacles. The comparison concerns unknowns, normalization and marking rather than a claim of subsumption.
- Trefethen, *Quantifying the ill-conditioning of analytic continuation*, arXiv:1908.11097; BIT 60 (2020), 901–915. The primary arXiv page was read. Uniform analytic bounds change an ill-posed continuation problem into a conditional, geometry-sensitive one. Our compactness and two-constants arguments explicitly retain this dependence.
