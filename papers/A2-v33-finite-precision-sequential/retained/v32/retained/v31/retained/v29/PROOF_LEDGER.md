## Limits that remain mathematical assumptions rather than evidence

Localized launch densities and attempted-failure accounting are part of the physical protocol. No apparatus has been executed. Position-output elimination does not eliminate the laboratory frame or preparation calibration. The period presentation is assumed bounded; periodicity is not inferred for an arbitrary aperiodic Delone set from one finite patch. Full lattice decisions use the patch gap. Exact rational relations do not mean exact real-valued lattice coordinates. Old uniformly prepared count-germ and passive spectral problems are not identified with the controlled experiment.
