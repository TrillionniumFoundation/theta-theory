\begin{abstract}
We study intrinsic endpoint-arclength laws of periodic dispersing billiards.
Two strictly active endpoint density functions recover a local stationary
action, both reflecting arcs and the absolute free-area normalization.
For analytic asymmetric and pairwise noncongruent obstacle representatives,
we give a completeness certificate for an arbitrary connected collection
of these records. The covolume of its recovered cycle group minus the
free area and the areas of the observed shapes equals the missing
obstacle area plus the defect of the period index. Its vanishing therefore
certifies both obstacle coverage and the full period group; neither is
assumed. On uniform analytic classes with shape, symmetry, geometric and
area margins, bounded-denominator reconstruction yields a reference-free
noisy certificate and a conditional whole-table estimate. No reference
table, witness-key neighborhoods or retained generating set is supplied.
Two growing endpoint histograms give a simultaneous statistical guarantee,
and a physical hidden-obstacle subexperiment has sharp area-detection
scale $N^{-1/2}$. The sensor remains a marked, absolutely normalized local
scattering law. The retained finite-aperture scanner is a sufficient
geometry-aware producer of records, not an unmarked-trajectory algorithm.
All earlier local, registered, catalogue, moment and relative-law results
are included without alteration in the supplementary source package.
\end{abstract}
