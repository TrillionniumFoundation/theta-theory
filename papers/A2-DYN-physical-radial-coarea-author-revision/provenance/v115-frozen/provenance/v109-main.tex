\begin{abstract}
We study the exact-label raw pointwise local limit problem for
actual returns in a triangular Lorentz gas. On the original physical
pairing sheets, we derive a positive optical denominator for the
endpoint action and a collision-uniform marked-incidence estimate.
A continuous Schur-complement formula and the convexity of the
reduced action give finite one-sided coarea traces, including at
visited-grazing and critical endpoints. The inherited regular-point
variation estimate therefore extends to each closed physical segment.
An actually visited grazing collision makes the original paired
minimum zero, whereas tangency to an unvisited disk need not do so.
We retain the full comparison pool and prove that deleting its
visited-grazing capacity changes the original microscopic shortage
by ordered essential height at most $C_MB^{-95/384}$. The remaining
shortage is supported on two-nongrazing-ended segments, with the
original section, arithmetic reference, exact labels, positive
weights and collision-limit order unchanged. The argument supplies
this physical part of the scarcity estimate; the collision-uniform
abundance estimate on the remaining microscopic source is not
inferred from finite-count exhaustion.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Introduction}
The raw pointwise problem requires control of a positive roof density
at the original target roof, uniformly in the collision count, radius,
and exact return labels. A bound on a fixed-window average is not
such a control. In the retained scalar reduction, all previously paid
physical comparisons leave an original microscopic paired source.
Its comparison capacity can be assembled at the target roof without
choosing angular source blocks. The remaining question is whether
that capacity is sufficiently abundant in the original Lorentz model.

This article addresses a physical part of that question. The new
argument begins with the actual broken-path action, not a selected
comparison certificate. Its second variation yields a positive
optical denominator and controls a full inverse-incidence insertion
on each trajectory. It also gives a continuous reduced Hessian when
visited interior collisions become grazing. Along a fixed Gauss
angle, the inverse gradient is integrable up to both physical ends.
These facts complete the endpoint trace and variation statements on
the original pairing space and distinguish two geometrically different
tangency mechanisms. They permit an exact source partition and a
quantitative payment for the entire comparison capacity of the
visited-grazing class.

The principal geometric statement is Theorem~\ref{thm:v109-traces};
the physical source and capacity estimates are
Theorems~\ref{thm:v109-grazing} and \ref{thm:v109-residual}.
The optical estimates are proved directly in
Section~\ref{sec:v109-action}. The retained analytic and scalar inputs
are stated in Section~\ref{sec:v109-inputs}, so their role does not
have to be reconstructed from the historical supplement. The original
pointwise assertion is \eqref{eq:v76-target}, without a change of
topology or a deleted arithmetic class. The full prior article and
all 259 inherited modules are retained in the separate historical
packet \cite{Q108}; the current exposition does not replace their
proofs by this shorter dependency statement.
