\begin{abstract}
For the original triangular Lorentz gas we continue the exact-record
raw pointwise local limit and four-coordinate inversion problem without
changing its physical clock, section or conditioning. The previously
proved all-time endpoint-momentum kernel and complete leading critical
coefficient sums are retained. We now obtain a nonlinear endpoint-momentum
metric with collision-uniform two-sided bounds, an exact radial coarea
formula and a complete synchronized source estimate. On actual physical
inverse arms reaching zero endpoint momentum, integration of this exact
identity gives uniform critical-band and inverse-gradient budgets at
every band width, with no Taylor remainder or tangent-sector hypothesis
on Borel cuts. On each physical fan the limiting roof is constant.
The proof includes all one-copy regular roots and permits interior
incidences tending to zero, without continuing the collision map through
tangency. The resulting source estimates are unscaled: they do not
establish the common-physical-roof $m^2L^\infty$ bound or the paired
positive-shortage limit required for the full raw local limit theorem.
An all-direction lattice-row proof also gives the explicit physical
free-flight bound $\tau_{\max}\le\sqrt{3847/124}<28/5$ and finite
enclosing candidate counts. The original spectral and normalization
obligations remain explicit.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Introduction}
A unit-speed particle moves among equal circular disks centered at the
triangular lattice and undergoes true specular collisions. The original
radius interval, physical clock, section, exact return--displacement--
collision record, and four-coordinate raw local inversion target are
unchanged. The raw pointwise assertion is \eqref{eq:v76-target}; its
remaining ordered shortage limit is \eqref{eq:v112-remaining}. Neither a
Markov replacement nor an averaged theorem is substituted for that target.

\subsection*{New quantitative geometry, without trajectory sampling}
Theorem~\ref{thm:eh-horizon} covers every direction by four primitive
lattice-row rectangles and proves
$\tau_{\max}\le\sqrt{3847/124}<28/5$. This removes the nonquantitative
compactness step for the finite-horizon constant. It yields the explicit
candidate bounds $N_f,N_\circ\le150$ and $N_{\rm pair}\le162$,
while retaining the original intrinsic meanings of those counts.
Neither candidate count is a physical-word multiplier.

\subsection*{New exact radial geometry and a complete physical band budget}
Write $\zeta=(p_0,p_m)$, $D=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1)$ and let
$G=D_q^2F$ be the actual reduced endpoint Hessian. On the regular
physical set $|\zeta|<\rho<1$, Lemma~\ref{lem:rc-metric} proves
\[
 K_\rho I\preceq\widehat G:=G/R^2\preceq U_m I,
 \quad K_\rho=\tfrac{100}{47}\sqrt{1-\rho^2},\quad
 U_1=\tfrac{320}{9},\quad U_m=\tfrac{170}{9}\ (m\ge2).
\]
These inequalities hold away from the critical point and without an
interior-incidence cutoff. For $\zeta=re_\theta$ on an actual inverse
branch, its physical roof satisfies the exact, rather than asymptotic,
identity
\[
 \frac{dL}{dr}=r h_x,\qquad
 h_x=e_\theta^{\mathsf T}D\widehat G^{-1}De_\theta,
 \qquad U_m^{-1}\le h_x\le K_\rho^{-1}.
\]
The factors of $r$ in polar collision measure and in the roof derivative
cancel. The exact density is
$u_x/(4\pi c_*h_x)$, where
$u_x=|\partial_{\alpha_0}p_m|^{-1}$.
Theorem~\ref{thm:rc-coarea} sums it at synchronized endpoint momenta
over the entire one-copy regular root family using the retained momentum
kernel. It does not synchronize different physical roofs.

For actual inverse arms regular all the way to zero momentum, the
limiting physical roof $a$ exists and
\[
 \frac{r^2}{2U_m}\le L-a\le\frac{r^2}{2K_\rho}.
\]
There is no omitted $O(r^3)$ term, third-derivative assumption or local
Taylor radius. Proposition~\ref{prop:rc-critical-roof-constancy} proves
that $a$ is constant on each connected physical fan. The original exact
record still uses $L$, never $L-a$. Arbitrary Borel guards and section
or pairing cuts remain selectors on these actual points, even when
no tangent-sector limit exists. Theorem~\ref{thm:rc-band} proves
\[
 \int_{E_s}\one_\Pi w\,d\nu_R^*
 \le\frac{C_{\rm mom}}{4c_*}\min\{\rho^2,2U_m s\},
 \qquad 0\le w\le1,
\]
and the corresponding $O(\sqrt{s})$ inverse-gradient budget. The entire
physical family is summed before the collision/radius/record suprema.
The class includes actual approaches to a visited tangency but makes no
claim about a collision derivative or a prescribed trace on that
singular endpoint. Arms terminating at positive momentum radius and the
paired endpoint measure are not silently included in this estimate.

\subsection*{The retained all-time physical source theorem}
The retained source is the coordinate fibre $p_0=a$ with measure $d\alpha_0$.
It is not the probability law conditioned on a smaller physical event.
The first actual collision transforms this initially horizontal graph
into an unstable curve. With $c=\sqrt{1-a^2}$, terminal incidence $d$,
physical flight $\tau$, and $A=c+\tau/R$, its exact source identities are
\[
 \frac{ds_1}{ds_0}=-A/d,\qquad
 \frac{d\varphi_1}{ds_1}=R^{-1}+d/(RA),\qquad
 \frac{d\alpha_0}{d\ell_1}
     =\frac{d}{RA\sqrt{1+(d\varphi_1/ds_1)^2}}.
\]
The terminal factor in this density pays the complete first-image
homogeneous grazing partition. The source curvature is uniformly
bounded by an explicit constant, and the preceding physical curve-growth
theorem applies after one fixed, paid enlargement of its class.

A direct terminal-coordinate coarea would introduce an unnecessary
inverse incidence. We instead reserve the last \emph{physical collision}
for the calculation. On every incoming unstable graph of slope $V$,
write $w=R^{-1}+V$ and $A=c+\tau w$. Exact contact differentiation gives
\[
 \left|\frac{dp_1}{d\ell}\right|
       =\frac{R^{-1}A+dw}{\sqrt{1+V^2}}
       \ge k_-t_*.
\]
This cancellation is valid at arbitrarily small positive incidences.
There are only a fixed finite number of physical next-hit branches on
each short incoming graph; no terminal homogeneous subdivision is needed.
Consequently Theorem~\ref{thm:mc-kernel} proves, for every $m\ge1$,
$R\in[R_-,R_+]$, and $a,v\in(-1,1)$,
\[
 \sum_{\substack{\alpha_0:\ p_m(\alpha_0,a)=v\\
                         \text{regular physical }m\text{-orbit}}}
       |\partial_{\alpha_0}p_m|^{-1}\le C_{\rm mom}.
\]
The sum is over unique physical roots before the suprema are taken.
The almost-everywhere density bound is upgraded at every regular root
value by a finite inverse-branch argument, not by choosing a favorable
version of the density. Arbitrary bounded exact-record and original-roof
selectors may be retained in its positive measure inequality. This is
an endpoint-momentum kernel, not an original-roof density estimate.

\subsection*{The retained leading critical budget without word pressure}
At a regular critical roof the retained first variations give
$p_0=p_m=0$. If $G$ is its reduced endpoint Hessian, $\Delta=\det G$,
and $u=|\partial_{\alpha_0}p_m|^{-1}$, its exact critical coefficient is
\[
 \mathfrak J=\frac{\sqrt\Delta}{2R^2c_*}\,u,
 \qquad \frac{\sqrt\Delta}{R^2}\le\frac{170}{9}.
\]
The uniform endpoint kernel at $(0,0)$ thus gives a complete uniform
sum of these coefficients without whole-cylinder pressure or an
exponential physical-word multiplier. This replaces the dependency of
the earlier coefficient estimate; the earlier proof itself is retained.

For an unvisited-disk tangency or a section boundary, the physical-side
trace must not be set to zero. We keep the smooth auxiliary action only
where every designated visited incidence is positive, and retain its
actual physical selector. The normalized endpoint coordinates
$y=G^{1/2}(q-q_x)$ and the physical momenta are related to first order by
$z=R^{-1}\operatorname{diag}(-1,1)G^{1/2}y$. Their angular Jacobian is
\[
 \frac{\sqrt{\det G}}{e_\theta^{\mathsf T}G e_\theta},
\]
not one. A physical-side joint-kernel domination, angular convergence for
finite $C^1$ cuts, and positive finite exhaustion prove
Theorem~\ref{thm:mc-critical-sectors}:
\[
 \sum_x\mathfrak J(x)\beta_y(x)
                    \le\frac{(320/9)C_{\rm mom}}{2c_*}.
\]
The conclusion includes all one-copy critical sectors of this stated
class at each collision count. It does not assert sector limits for
general Borel pairing selectors or continue the physical derivative
through a visited grazing collision.

\subsection*{Inherited results and the exact remaining target}
The immediate native predecessor is the complete momentum-critical
manuscript at commit

Its active proof bodies 260--285 and original model/interface files are
retained byte for byte; the new bodies are 286--287. The predecessor's main,
response, ledgers and source manifests are preserved separately.
The uniform-growth local manuscript and the earlier v114--v115
clearance results retain their own source identities. The latest
controlling assessment explicitly reviews v114--v115, not subsequent
277--287 proofs; no later expert certification is inferred from it.

The preceding coefficient theorem remains unscaled and keeps its
finite-$C^1$ critical-sector scope. The new radial theorem removes
Taylor remainders and sector regularity for its different, explicitly
stated physical-source budgets. It does not replace that preceding
trace theorem by an unjustified Borel trace assertion. At a common
physical roof $t$, each root has its own radius $r_x(t,\theta)$.
The synchronized momentum-kernel estimate does not bound that sum
without an additional theorem. Nor is the paired minimum of two
endpoint traces automatically a submeasure of the collision source.
These distinctions prevent a change of clock, topology or probability
from being hidden in the new coordinates.

The complete first-order physical-roof source still requires an
$m^{-2}$ exact-label bound with all critical, noncritical, visited-singular
and pairing contributions controlled in the original order of limits.
The incidence-only low-load source or sufficient nongrazing comparison
capacity must also be paid, with the retained spectral, arithmetic,
optical and conditional-denominator interfaces checked at their actual
quantifiers. The original $B$-dependent widths are fixed before the
collision limsup. A weak $O(\varepsilon^2)$ mark payment is not multiplied
by $m^2$ and declared small. The full raw pointwise LLT and final
four-coordinate inversion remain the research objective, not a claimed
consequence of this manuscript's new source theorems.
