# A2 v21 proof and information ledger

The primary is organized around Theorems 1.1–1.2, with complete proofs in Section 7 and the full v20 proofs retained in Sections 2–6 and 8. The topic is unchanged.

The geometric keys use a common-isometry quotient metric on two entire support functions and their marked contacts. All finite reflections and record permutations are canonicalized at the data level; final tables are compared modulo a common Euclidean isometry. Statistical fits remain in a physical prior, not in an arbitrary class of noisy curves.

The local Lipschitz inverse still requires the physical C3 and positive gap/area, curvature, twist, active-time and non-grazing bounds stated in the unchanged local proposition. Global analytic continuation uses a uniform complex strip; symmetry and shape-separation margins control matching. None of those inputs is removed by the witness argument.

The proof uses a finite set of possible relative copy types in a local presentation only to establish persistence and key separation. No such copy labels are included in the inverse data. The key neighborhoods are part of a local prior chart, not a claim of a global unlabelled discovery algorithm.

Finite tests check displayed algebra and finite graph/aperture models. They do not prove an infinite periodic statement, a uniform analytic estimate or the existence and consistency theorem for the statistical estimator. The manuscripts contain the mathematical arguments; the scripts are reproducibility diagnostics.
