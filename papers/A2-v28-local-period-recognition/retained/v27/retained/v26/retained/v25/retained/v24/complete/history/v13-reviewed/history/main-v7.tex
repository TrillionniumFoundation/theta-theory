\begin{abstract}
We prove collision-order-uniform relative boundary laws for near-onset
records in smooth periodic dispersing billiards, without symmetry or
finite horizon assumptions. A stationary bridge separates into two
half-line actions and relative determinant amplitudes; physical
residual-time integration gives a nonlinear probability law on a
common closed offset collar, with uniform differentiated control.
For endpoint and residual-time experiments we retain the rare-event
mass and prove a joint positive-offset testing limit. After common
whitening, the quadratic support separation is exactly
$(2/\pi)\arcsin(e^{-j\gamma})$, also for unequal facing curvatures.
Growing product experiments have an explicit erasure profile, with
distinct conditional-success and raw-preparation scales. We retain
uniform fixed-order contact-jet inversion, analytic contact rigidity,
and geometric realizations preserving a selected leading hierarchy.
Unlabelled amplitudes recover coalescing curvature triples with sharp
pairwise exponent $1/3$; three amplitudes suffice in the constrained
physical family and four permit an independent area normalizer.
A count-only procedure, now starting from a fixed local gap interval,
charges coarse search, fine calibration, and amplitude acquisition.
For fixed extrapolation order $m$ its sufficient curvature-accuracy
cost is $C_m\varepsilon^{-(6+6/m)}\log(C_m/\eta)$, with an all-history
preparation cap. A physical testing lower bound identifies the
corresponding obstruction for shrinking-offset Bernoulli designs.
\end{abstract}
