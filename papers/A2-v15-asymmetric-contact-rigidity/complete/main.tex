\begin{abstract}
This volume supplies the complete smooth relative determinant law,
its physical residual-time integration, the symmetrized energy-profile
inverse, and the auxiliary geometric and statistical arguments accompanying
\emph{Nonlinear boundary laws and asymmetric two-contact rigidity in
dispersing billiards}. It retains the probability-only even-contact
inverse and its physical image, the Volterra and Abel arguments, and
the complete calibration and acquisition proofs. Their original
hypotheses remain in force; the all-degree signed inverse is proved
separately in the main article. All derivative and jet orders are fixed
before uniform constants are chosen.
\end{abstract}

\subsection*{Reading convention}
The main article centers the asymmetric signed-moment inverse. This
companion retains every earlier mathematical input and proof, so that
no auxiliary argument has been replaced by a citation to an unavailable
version. In particular the evenness hypotheses in the probability-only
inverse are not removed here. The same-section chart convention is
specified explicitly in Section~\ref{sec:v14-normal-form}; positive
probability margins in Section~\ref{sec:v14-observability} are obtained
after the nodes and parameter ball are fixed. Source pins and the
historical acknowledgments are kept outside the mathematical exposition.
