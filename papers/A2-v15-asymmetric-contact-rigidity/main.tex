\begin{abstract}
We study geometric information in near-onset collision laws of periodic
dispersing billiards. At a labelled normal channel, with its gap and separate
curvatures supplied, two oriented two-flight probability germs and the
corresponding signed transverse endpoint first-moment germs determine both
analytic contact germs without reflection symmetry. An explicit triangular
inverse recovers every finite smooth contact jet. Its odd-degree blocks are
strictly nonsingular at all positive curvatures, including equal curvatures;
the even-degree blocks retain their two-flight form. Unknown free area is
recovered from the common leading coefficient. We realize independent jets
of every degree in analytic periodic tables, either at fixed area or with
area free. At each fixed jet order, finitely many positive-window means give
local bi-Lipschitz coordinates; a specified randomized binary compression
has the regular parametric risk order. The signed observation is strictly
richer than an unmarked count. The smooth relative boundary law, its complete
symmetrized energy profiles, and their Volterra and Abel inverse theory are
retained with full proofs in the companion volume. The analytic normal-form
comparison is separated from the geometric inverse, and no finite-horizon
assumption is imposed.
\end{abstract}
