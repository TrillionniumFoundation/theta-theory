## 6. Revision 105: fixed-positive-residence persistence

Module 253 considers the original selected positive source on segments with

\[
 h\le\ell=b-a<\Delta.
\]

If \(a<t<b\) and \(\ell\ge h\), write

\[
 d_-=t-a,\qquad d_+=b-t.
\]

Then

\[
 \int_{t-h}^{t+h}\mathbf 1_{\{a<u<b\}}\,du
 =\min(h,d_-)+\min(h,d_+)\ge h,
\]

because \(d_-+d_+=\ell\ge h\).

Multiplication by the nonnegative original weight and summation over physical sheets gives

\[
 T_{H,[h,\Delta)}^\varepsilon(t)
 \le {1\over h}\int_{t-h}^{t+h}
       \mathsf T_\Delta^\varepsilon(u)\,du
 =2A_h\mathsf T_\Delta^\varepsilon(t).
\]

This is a correct finite-count positive inequality. The selector stays at the starting roof. No comparison path, endpoint derivative or trace transport is used. There is no word-count multiplier.

Under the inherited paired-average estimate, the ordered height is bounded by

\[
 C\{\varepsilon^{1/16}+(1+\varepsilon^{-1})\Delta\},
\]

with a leading constant declared independent of the previously fixed positive \(h\). Thus for each outer \(B\), a threshold \(h_B>0\) chosen before the collision limit legitimately removes all source with \(\ell\ge h_B\).

This is useful. It covers fixed-positive-residence flat pulses, singular endpoint types and reach-deficient segments within the original paired source without requiring any local geometry beyond the segment itself.

The quantifier boundary is equally important. The theorem does not give the estimate at \(h=0\). It does not allow \(h\) to depend on collision count. Uniformity in previously fixed \(h\) does not identify or control a limiting positive source which concentrates on \(\ell\downarrow0\) as collision count increases. The manuscript states this correctly.

## 14. Moving nested roofs and attained levels

The nested-roof corollary assumes an actual angular arm on which the incoming roof is nonincreasing and the outgoing roof is nondecreasing. A source interval precedes a comparison interval, and the outgoing endpoint gains at least \(r\). These conditions indeed imply the product interval inclusion.

The attained-level proposition uses

\[
 a(x)=a_*-\eta\psi(x),
 \qquad
 b(x)=a_*+d+\psi(x),
 \qquad0\le\eta\le1,
\]

with actual levels \(\delta/2,2\delta,3\delta\) reached before a true boundary. The comparison residence remains below \(8\delta\), and the level-width estimate follows from integrating

\[
 x\psi'(x)/\psi(x)\le K.
\]

Together with the stated source and envelope trace bounds this verifies the capacity balance with cost

\[
 \kappa={LK\over l\log(3/2)}.
\]

This is a valid sufficient physical pattern. Polynomial examples demonstrate that both endpoints may move and may have zero velocity at the initial point. They do not prove that all Lorentz arms admit this monotone nesting, actual reach, trace ratio or uniform order. The article labels them diagnostic and does not overstate them.

## 15. Fractional capacity allocation

 \beta_j=\min\{1,\kappa P_j/C_j\}.
\]

Replacing the source weight by \(\beta_js_j\) makes the block satisfy the envelope balance. Therefore the recovered fraction obeys the two-ended height estimate, and the exact positive split is

\[
 T_j=eta_jT_j+(1-\beta_j)T_j.

Moreover

\[
 (1-\beta_j)C_j=(C_j-\kappa P_j)_+.

This is the correct shortage after the endpoint trace loss has already been paid. It improves the earlier one-ended capacity for the same family because \(p_\vee\) dominates either endpoint trace pointwise.

This distinction is central. The theorem proves how much source a proposed family pays. It does not prove that the complete microscopic source admits enough such capacity.

## 18. Flat pulses

Revision 105 resolves all flat pulses whose residence is at least a previously fixed \(h_B>0\). That is a meaningful improvement over revision 104.

It does not prove a uniform pointwise bound on a sequence of positive rectangular pulses whose widths tend to zero with collision count. A microscopic isolated flat sheet may have no comparison corridor and may carry its full source into \(\mathsf R_B\). The logarithmic criterion would control such a sequence only if its collision-uniform weighted source height were proved.

Accordingly, the manuscript is correct to leave microscopic flat-pulse decay uncertified. A future closure theorem must either prove a direct shrinking-width estimate for these pulses or include them in a comparison family with a schedule-compatible capacity budget.
