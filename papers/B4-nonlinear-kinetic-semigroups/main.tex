\begin{abstract}
We work on invariant kinetic shells with a propagated $(2+\delta)$ velocity
moment and the $2$-Wasserstein topology.  This removes escape of quadratic
energy and makes the product collision measure continuous.  Controlled
Boltzmann paths have compact action sublevels, a strongly continuous Nisio
semigroup, and a discounted resolvent satisfying the implicit nonlinear
resolvent identity.  A cylindrical core, superquadratic containment function,
Wasserstein comparison theorem, and collision-history BBGKY correctors yield
the finite-volume nonlinear semigroup limit.
\end{abstract}
