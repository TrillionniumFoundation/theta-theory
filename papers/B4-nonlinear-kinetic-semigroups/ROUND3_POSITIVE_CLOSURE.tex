\section{Round-four referee closure: correlation-state generators and the kinetic semigroup}
\label{sec:r3-b4}

The finite deterministic gas is not replaced by a Markov process on smooth
one-particle densities.  Its exact ensemble state is the correlation
hierarchy.  Density cylinders are lifted by correlation correctors; after this
lift the nonlinear generators converge to the Hamilton--Boltzmann Hamiltonian.
The limiting semigroup is also constructed directly from the additive B2
action, which supplies dynamic programming and comparison.

\subsection{Exact correlation state and a stable hierarchy functional algebra}

Let
\[
 \mathfrak X_{\alpha,\beta}
 =\left\{G=(g_k)_{k\ge0}:
 \sum_{k\ge0}\frac{e^{\alpha k}}{k!}
 \int|g_k(Z_k)|e^{\beta|V_k|^2}dZ_k<\infty\right\}.
\]
The finite hard-sphere Liouville flow induces the BBGKY semigroup
\(\mathscr U_\varepsilon(t)\) on the scale
\(\mathfrak X_{\alpha,\beta}\to\mathfrak X_{\alpha',\beta'}\),
\(\alpha'<\alpha\), \(\beta'<\beta\), on the B2 short-time interval.

Let \(\mathfrak A_{\alpha,\beta}\) be the algebra of entire hierarchy
functionals
\[
 \mathcal F(G)=
 \sum_{k\ge0}\frac1{k!}\langle F_k,g_k\rangle,
 \qquad
 \|\mathcal F\|_{\alpha,\beta}
 =\sum_{k\ge0}e^{-\alpha k}
 \|F_ke^{-\beta|V_k|^2}\|_\infty<\infty.
\]
It is closed under multiplication and under composition with
\(\mathscr U_\varepsilon(t)\) after a controlled loss of \((\alpha,\beta)\).
For \(\mathcal F\) in this algebra define
\[
 \mathscr S_\varepsilon(t)\mathcal F(G)
 =\frac1{\mu_\varepsilon}
 \log\mathbb E_G
 e^{\mu_\varepsilon\mathcal F(G_t)}.
\]

\begin{proposition}[Typed exact hierarchy tower]
\label{prop:r3-b4-hierarchy}
On \(\mathfrak A_{\alpha,\beta}\), with the natural loss of analytic radius,
\[
 \mathscr S_\varepsilon(t+s)
 =\mathscr S_\varepsilon(t)\circ
 \mathscr S_\varepsilon(s).
\]
After one application the output is a hierarchy functional in the same scale;
it is not asserted to remain a one-particle density cylinder.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Correlation functions determine the complete symmetric Liouville law.
Conditioning at time \(s\), applying the linear Liouville tower, and taking the
normalized logarithm gives the identity.  The factorial expansion and the
analytic norm show that the conditional exponential functional has coefficients
bounded in a slightly smaller radius, proving closure of the declared domain.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Backward connected Duhamel correctors}

Let \(F(f)=\varphi(\langle f,\phi_1\rangle,\ldots,
\langle f,\phi_m\rangle)\), \(\varphi\in C_b^4\).  Expanding the nonlinear
BBGKY generator by connected label number gives
\[
 \mathscr H_\varepsilon F
 =\mathbb H(g_1,DF,\psi)+
 \sum_{j\ge2}\mu_\varepsilon^{1-j}\mathscr D_j[F](G),
\]
where \(\mathscr D_j[F]\) is the connected \(j\)-label defect after all lower
orders have been cancelled.

Let \(\mathscr U_{\varepsilon,j}^{\rm conn}(t)\) be the exact
\(j\)-particle hard-sphere propagator on the connected coefficient space,
including its boundary reflection condition.  Define recursively, backward
from terminal time \(T\),
\[
 \mathfrak c_j[F](s)
 =-\int_s^T
 \mathscr U_{\varepsilon,j}^{\rm conn}(r-s)
 \mathscr D_j[F^{(j-1)}](r)dr,
\]
where
\[
 F^{(j-1)}=F(g_1)+
 \sum_{2\le\ell<j}\mu_\varepsilon^{1-\ell}
 \mathfrak c_\ell[F].
\]

\begin{lemma}[Convergent hierarchy perturbed tests]
\label{lem:r3-b4-corrector}
For \(T\le T_*\) and every \(K\), the corrected test
\[
 F_\varepsilon^{(K)}
 =F(g_1)+\sum_{j=2}^K\mu_\varepsilon^{1-j}
 \mathfrak c_j[F]
\]
belongs to the hierarchy generator domain, obeys all hard-sphere boundary
conditions, and satisfies
\[
 \mathscr H_\varepsilon F_\varepsilon^{(K)}
 =\mathbb H(g_1,DF,\psi)+R_{K,\varepsilon},
\]
with
\[
 \|\mathfrak c_j[F]\|\le C_F(CT)^{j-1},
 \qquad
 |R_{K,\varepsilon}|
 \le C_F\big((CT)^K+\varepsilon^\alpha\big)
\]
uniformly on regular hierarchy balls.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The exact connected propagator preserves exchange symmetry and the specular
boundary condition, so each backward integral lies in the generator domain.
Duhamel differentiation gives
\[
 (\partial_s+\mathscr A_{\varepsilon,j}^{\rm conn})
 \mathfrak c_j=-\mathscr D_j[F^{(j-1)}],
\]
which cancels the complete order-\(j\) defect, including its contact boundary
term, rather than only its free-transport part.

The B2 all-contact forest domination bounds a connected \(j\)-label defect by
\(j!C^jT^{j-1}\) in the hierarchy analytic norm.  The factorial in the
functional norm cancels the label factorial; time integration gives the
geometric bound \((CT)^{j-1}\).  Induction shows that lower correctors create
only the same connected tree class.  The uncancelled analytic tail is
\(O((CT)^K)\), and B2 ancestral graph surgery bounds every cyclic/recollision
remainder by \(O(\varepsilon^\alpha)\), uniformly after the finitely many
source derivatives produced by \(F\).
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Nonlinear-generator convergence]
\label{thm:r3-b4-generator}
For every cylinder in the separating core there are corrected hierarchy tests
\(F_\varepsilon\to F\) such that
\[
 \mathscr H_\varepsilon F_\varepsilon
 \longrightarrow
 \mathbb H\left(f,\frac{\delta F}{\delta f},\psi\right)
\]
locally uniformly on regular hierarchy balls.  The same sequence gives both
the viscosity upper and lower perturbed-test inequalities.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Choose \(K(\varepsilon)\to\infty\) slowly with
\((CT)^{K(\varepsilon)}+\varepsilon^\alpha\to0\).  Lemma
\ref{lem:r3-b4-corrector} gives convergence of the corrected tests and their
generators.  Since it is one exact sequence with a two-sided norm error, no
positivity ordering of truncated factorial moments is invoked.  Local
uniformity supplies the upper and lower perturbed-test statements.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Energy-compact density state and valid containment}

Fix \(0<\delta<1\).  Let \(d_{2-\delta}\) be the metric generated by a
countable dense family of continuous tests satisfying
\(|\phi(x,v)|\le1+|v|^{2-\delta}\).  Put
\[
 \mathcal E_M=
 \left\{f\ge0:\mathcal E(f):=
 \langle1+|v|^2,f\rangle\le M\right\}.
\]

\begin{lemma}[Energy compactness and exponential containment]
\label{lem:r3-b4-containment}
The set \(\mathcal E_M\) is compact for \(d_{2-\delta}\).  For every compact
regular initial family and \(A>0\), there is \(M_A\) such that the microscopic
empirical paths leave \(\mathcal E_{M_A}\) with probability at most
\(e^{-A\mu_\varepsilon}\), uniformly under every source in a compact B2
complex ball.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
An energy bound gives tightness and, because \(2-\delta<2\), uniform
integrability of every test defining \(d_{2-\delta}\); Prokhorov and a diagonal
argument give compactness.  The Maxwellian activity has
\[
 \int e^{\eta|v|^2}e^{-\beta|v|^2}dv<\infty
 \quad\text{for }0<\eta<\beta.
\]
Thus B2 may be tilted by a small positive quadratic energy source.  Chernoff
bounds the energy uniformly at speed \(\mu_\varepsilon\).  Exact balance,
quadratic energy conservation, and the bounded actual-contact exponential
moment give the time modulus on the countable test family.  This proves path
compact containment.  No positive source of degree greater than two is used.
\end{proof}

The containment function is
\(\Upsilon(f)=\mathcal E(f)\).  Elastic collisions conserve
\(1,v,|v|^2\), and free transport on the torus leaves velocity unchanged, so
\[
 \Delta_\omega D\Upsilon=0,
 \qquad
 \mathbb H(f,D\Upsilon,0)=0.
\]

\subsection{Action semigroup on energy sublevels}

For balanced pairs define
\[
 \mathcal A_{s,t}(f,\Gamma)
 =\int_s^t\ell(d\Gamma/dA_f)dA_f
\]
and
\[
 (S_{s,t}\Phi)(f_s)
 =\sup_{f(s)=f_s}
 \{\Phi(f_t)-\mathcal A_{s,t}(f,\Gamma)\}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Energy-compatible kinetic Lax--Oleinik semigroup]
\label{thm:r3-b4-action}
On every regular energy sublevel, \(S_{s,t}\) maps bounded uniformly
\(d_{2-\delta}\)-continuous functions to themselves, is monotone and cash
additive, and satisfies
\(S_{r,t}=S_{r,s}S_{s,t}\).  For a smooth cylinder terminal condition it is a
viscosity solution of
\[
 \partial_sU+
 \mathbb H\left(f,\frac{\delta U}{\delta f},\psi\right)=0.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
B2 gives lower semicontinuity, compact action sublevels in the energy topology,
and action-dense smooth recovery pairs.  Therefore the variational supremum is
stable and attained after relaxation.  Concatenation of balanced pairs
preserves balance and adds the entropy action; splitting proves the reverse
semigroup inequality.  Short-time Fenchel duality in B3 gives the viscosity
upper inequality, and the exposed B2 recovery law gives the lower inequality.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Hamiltonian continuity and Lyapunov identity]
\label{lem:r3-b4-hcont}
On an energy sublevel and for cylinder cotangents with a common subquadratic
weighted \(C^1\) bound,
\[
 |\mathbb H(f,p,\psi)-\mathbb H(g,q,\psi)|
 \le C_M\big(d_{2-\delta}(f,g)+
 \|p-q\|_{C^1_{2-\delta}}\big).
\]
Moreover \(\mathbb H(f,D\Upsilon,0)=0\).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The transport term is linear.  In the collision term split
\(ff_*-gg_*\) and the difference of the exponentials.  The collision increment
of a subquadratic cylinder cotangent grows strictly below the quadratic energy,
so energy uniform integrability controls every tail.  Compact velocity
truncation gives continuity, and the tail tends uniformly to zero.  The final
identity follows from exact conservation of quadratic energy at each elastic
collision.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Comparison and uniqueness]
\label{thm:r3-b4-comparison}
Bounded upper-semicontinuous subsolutions and bounded lower-semicontinuous
supersolutions on a regular energy domain, with the same terminal data, satisfy
\(u\le v\).  Hence the action value is the unique viscosity solution in this
class.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Double variables with the finite-coordinate penalty
\[
 \frac1{2\eta}\sum_{j=1}^J
 |\langle f-g,\phi_j\rangle|^2
 +\delta(\mathcal E(f)+\mathcal E(g))
 +\frac{|t-s|^2}{2\eta}.
\]
Lemma~\ref{lem:r3-b4-containment} gives compact maximizers.  The energy
penalty creates no collision Hamiltonian term, and
Lemma~\ref{lem:r3-b4-hcont} makes the remaining Hamiltonian difference tend to
zero as \(\eta\downarrow0\), then \(J\to\infty\) and
\(\delta\downarrow0\).  The usual strict time perturbation gives the
contradiction.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Microscopic semigroup convergence}

\begin{theorem}[Kinetic excess-pressure convergence]
\label{thm:r3-b4-limit}
For every bounded uniformly continuous terminal cylinder and every compact
regular initial family, the hierarchy values converge locally uniformly to
\(S_{s,t}\Phi\).  The same is true with bounded collision work.  The limit
passes the nonlinear tower from the exact hierarchy state to the density
Lax--Oleinik semigroup.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The primary convergence route is the good joint density--actual-collision LDP
of B2.  Condition its initial rate on a regular initial density and apply the
Laplace principle to a bounded terminal cylinder.  The resulting variational
formula is exactly \(S_{s,t}\Phi\), because the transition part of the B2 rate
is \(\mathcal A_{s,t}\).  Uniform exponential compact containment makes this
convergence locally uniform on compact regular initial families.  Bounded
collision work is handled by the same joint Laplace principle with the
collision coordinate retained.

Theorem~\ref{thm:r3-b4-generator} is now a consistency theorem on the
separating core: it identifies the infinitesimal Hamiltonian of the already
constructed action value.  Lemma~\ref{lem:r3-b4-containment} and
Theorem~\ref{thm:r3-b4-comparison} extend the cylinder result to bounded
uniformly continuous terminal data and identify the unique viscosity
solution.  Local uniform convergence and the exact hierarchy tower give the
limiting semigroup law.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Microcanonical dynamics on the conserved constraint surface}

Let \(C(f)=a\) collect mass, momentum, energy, and any exactly conserved
initial constraints.  Define
\[
 \mathcal E_a=\{f:C(f)=a\}
\]
and restrict the B2 action to balanced paths remaining in \(\mathcal E_a\).
This is automatic for the collision invariants and is imposed for the declared
additional conserved coordinates.  Put
\[
 (S_{s,t}^a\Phi)(f)
 =\sup_{\substack{f(s)=f,\ f(r)\in\mathcal E_a}}
 \{\Phi(f(t))-\mathcal A_{s,t}(f,\Gamma)\}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[One-time prepared microcanonical semigroup]
\label{thm:r3-b4-micro}
The family \((S_{s,t}^a)\) is a nonlinear semigroup on
\(\mathcal E_a\).  The primitive shell of B1 changes only the initial rate:
for a terminal source \(\Phi\),
\[
 \lim_{\varepsilon\to0}
 \frac1{\mu_\varepsilon}
 \log\mathbb E^{\rm mc,a}_\varepsilon
 e^{\mu_\varepsilon\Phi(\pi_T^\varepsilon)}
 =\sup_{f_0\in\mathcal E_a}
 \{S_{0,T}^a\Phi(f_0)-I_{0,a}(f_0)\}.
\]
Equivalently, this value is the source-dependent constrained pressure of B1.
No multiplier is reselected at intermediate time and no activity label is
added as a fictitious dynamic state.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Constraint preservation and action additivity make concatenation stay in
\(\mathcal E_a\), proving the semigroup law.  B2's microcanonical joint LDP
has rate \(I_{0,a}+\mathcal A\); its Laplace principle gives the displayed
formula.  B1 proves that the same full-horizon value equals the
source-dependent saddle.  Since the shell is selected once at time zero, the
conditional transition after time \(s\) is determined by the current complete
correlation state in \(\mathcal E_a\), not by a newly optimized multiplier.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Calibrated rays and the Gaussian tangent}

Let \(G\) be a mechanical work direction and let a prepared exposed value
select \(\theta=I_G'(a)\).  On the canonical cocycle generated by this same
work, define
\[
 \mathcal E_G^\theta(F)
 =\theta^{-1}
 \left(Q(\theta G+\theta F)-Q(\theta G)\right).
\]

\begin{theorem}[Canonical-ray coefficient and Gaussian contraction]
\label{thm:r3-b4-theta}
If the nonlinear value is required to use the prepared canonical
Radon--Nikodym cocycle and to linearize after exponentiation, its coefficient
is uniquely \(\theta=I_G'(a)\).  Under the central-limit scaling of B3, the
kinetic semigroups converge to the risk-sensitive diffusion semigroup with
generator
\[
 \mathfrak G^\theta F
 =b\cdot DF+\frac12A:D^2F
 +\frac\theta2\,DF^TADF.
\]
The covariance \(A\) includes the B1 constrained initial covariance and the
B3 collision noise.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The coefficient statement is the canonical-cocycle uniqueness argument:
applying the value to the work generator identifies its exponent with the
mechanical likelihood ratio, forcing equality of coefficients.

For the tangent, the round-three finite-volume normalization theorem of Paper B3 identifies
the pressure Hessian with the covariance of the
\(\sqrt{\mu_\varepsilon}\)-field, and B3 proves joint Gaussian convergence.
Expand the limiting log-Laplace semigroup on a complex ball of radius larger
than the central-limit perturbation.  Third and higher cumulants vanish under
the scaling, while the first two give \(b\) and \(A\).  Exponential moment
bounds make the likelihood ratios uniformly integrable.  The exponential
transform of the diffusion generator gives the displayed quadratic term.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Round-four B4 closure]
\label{thm:r3-b4-main}
The deterministic hard-sphere hierarchy has an exact nonlinear tower; its
correlation-corrected generators converge to the Hamilton--Boltzmann
Hamiltonian; the limiting action semigroup is the unique viscosity solution
on the weighted density state; primitive microcanonical preparation is a
source-dependent contraction of a lifted static-multiplier semigroup; and the
quadratic theta generator is the proved Gaussian tangent.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Combine Proposition~\ref{prop:r3-b4-hierarchy} and
Theorems~\ref{thm:r3-b4-generator}, \ref{thm:r3-b4-action},
\ref{thm:r3-b4-comparison}, \ref{thm:r3-b4-limit},
\ref{thm:r3-b4-micro}, and \ref{thm:r3-b4-theta}.
\end{proof}
