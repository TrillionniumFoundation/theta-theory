\section{A2: nonempty arithmetic and a checkable Fourier-inversion budget}
\label{sec:r33-a2}

This chapter proves the arithmetic assertion and the integration step independently of billiard geometry. It does not describe a six-dimensional periodic-data construction using three scalar parameters. The billiard transfer-operator bounds are retained as a precise application problem, rather than included in an allegedly verified certificate.

\subsection{A quantitative continuous-character estimate}
Put $\beta_0=(1,0)$, $\beta_1=(0,1)$ and $\beta_2=(\sqrt2,\sqrt3)$. The norm and distance in the next proposition are Euclidean.

\begin{proposition}[Exact large-frequency arithmetic]
\label{prop:r33-a2-arithmetic}
There exists $c>0$ such that, for every $b\in\mathbb R^2$ with $|b|\ge1$,
\[
 \operatorname{dist}\big((b\cdot\beta_j)_{j=0}^2,
                          2\pi\mathbb Z^3\big)
 \ge c(1+|b|)^{-3}.
\]
The annihilator is trivial. The condition is not asserted at arbitrarily small nonzero $b$ and is not open under unrestricted perturbations of the three vectors.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Let $\delta$ denote the distance and select closest integers $(m,n,k)$. If $\delta\ge1/10$, a sufficiently small $c$ proves the inequality. Otherwise $|b_1-2\pi m|,|b_2-2\pi n|\le\delta$ and
$|\sqrt2 b_1+\sqrt3 b_2-2\pi k|\le\delta$. Thus
\[
 |k-m\sqrt2-n\sqrt3|\le C\delta,\qquad
 |m|+|n|+|k|\le C(1+|b|).
\]
If $m=n=0$, the first two inequalities contradict $|b|\ge1$ and $\delta<1/10$. Otherwise $\alpha=k-m\sqrt2-n\sqrt3$ is a nonzero algebraic integer: rational independence of $1,\sqrt2,\sqrt3$ excludes zero. Its norm in $\mathbb Q(\sqrt2,\sqrt3)$ is a nonzero integer. The other three conjugates are bounded by $CH$, with $H=1+|m|+|n|+|k|$. Therefore $1\le|\mathrm{Norm}(\alpha)|\le|\alpha|C^3H^3$, giving $\delta\ge cH^{-3}$. The same argument with $\delta=0$ gives the trivial annihilator. Rational approximations of $(\sqrt2,\sqrt3)$ produce nonzero dual-lattice characters arbitrarily close to these data, proving that unrestricted openness would be false.
\end{proof}

Prescribing three vectors in $\mathbb R^2$ is six scalar conditions. An implicit-function construction needs a parameter map with rank six on the independent conditions, or explicit identities reducing their number. The arithmetic proposition proves neither the existence of such a billiard parameter map nor the returned-UNI geometry.

\subsection{A Fourier compiler with explicit hypotheses}
Let $d=d_Z+d_C$ and consider a centered mixed lattice--continuous characteristic family $\chi_N(u,b)$ on a fixed fundamental lattice torus times $\mathbb R^{d_C}$. Suppose the following bounds have been proved for the actual family, including every source derivative to be inverted:
\begin{align*}
 |\chi_N(\xi)|&\le C(e^{-cN|\xi|^2}+e^{-cN}), &&|\xi|\le\delta,\\
 |\chi_N(\xi)|&\le Ce^{-cN}, &&\delta\le|\xi|,\ |b|\le B,\\
 |\chi_N(u,b)|&\le C\exp[-cN/(1+\log N)^q],&&B<|b|\le N^A,\\
 |\chi_N(u,b)|&\le CN^v(1+|b|)^{-M},&&|b|>N^A.
\end{align*}
Here $M>d_C$ and $A(M-d_C)>v+d/2+1$. If centering differentiates the family, all resulting polynomial factors in $N$ are included in $v$ and in the central expansion; they are not discarded as merely marked vertices.

\begin{theorem}[Noncentral integral, not merely a pointwise bound]
\label{thm:r33-a2-integral}
Under these explicit bounds, with $\delta_N=N^{-2/5}$,
\[
 \int_{|\xi|>\delta_N}|\chi_N(\xi)|\,du\,db
     =o(N^{-d/2-1}).
\]
The same conclusion holds for any derivative family satisfying the same adjusted budget.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
In $\delta_N<|\xi|<\delta$, the Gaussian term is bounded by $Ce^{-cN^{1/5}}$ and the domain has bounded volume. The compact annulus contributes $O(e^{-cN})$. The intermediate region contributes at most
$CN^{Ad_C}\exp[-cN/(1+\log N)^q]$, which is smaller than every negative power of $N$. Finally polar integration in the continuous variables gives
\[
 CN^v\int_{N^A}^\infty r^{d_C-1-M}dr
 =\frac{C}{M-d_C}N^{v-A(M-d_C)}=o(N^{-d/2-1}).
\]
These estimates cover disjoint regions; there is no exponentially wide intermediate interval hidden in the notation.
\end{proof}

At zero frequency a normalized Markov transfer operator obeys $L_0^N1=1$. Its leading eigenprojection must therefore remain in the central expression. Return-time layer estimates, differences of matched branches, and full iterate estimates are three different objects. Only the last can be substituted into this Fourier compiler.

\subsection{What an Edgeworth input must actually say}
Suppose, in addition, the central characteristic family has an expansion
\[
 \chi_N(t/\sqrt N)=e^{-t^T\Sigma t/2}
 [1+N^{-1/2}A_1(it)+N^{-1}A_2(it)]+R_N(t)
\]
with $\int_{|t|\le N^{1/10}}|R_N(t)|dt=o(N^{-1})$, and the Gaussian-polynomial tails outside that ball have the same negligible order. Fourier inversion and the preceding theorem then give the corresponding two-term mixed density expansion with absolute error $o(N^{-d/2-1})$. On compact central $x$-sets where the leading Gaussian is bounded below, this is a relative $o(N^{-1})$ error. The polynomials include the Riesz amplitude, not only derivatives of an eigenvalue.

For example, if the logarithmic cumulants are $\kappa_3,\kappa_4$ and the normalized amplitude has coefficients $a_1,a_2$, then
\[
 A_1(it)=a_1(it)+\kappa_3(it)/6,
\]
\[
 A_2(it)=a_2(it)/2+\kappa_4(it)/24+
          \kappa_3(it)^2/72+a_1(it)\kappa_3(it)/6.
\]
The convention is that $a_j$ and $\kappa_j$ are homogeneous polynomials of the indicated order. This is an inversion theorem conditional on an $L^1$ central remainder estimate, not a proof that singular billiard spaces possess six differentiable spectral jets.

\subsection{Explicit nonempty probabilistic benchmark}
Take independent Bernoulli lattice increments with parameter $p$ in a compact subset of $(0,1)$ and independent standard Gaussian continuous increments. Their characteristic function is
\[
 (1-p+pe^{iu})^N e^{-N|b|^2/2}.
\]
Its modulus has a Gaussian bound near zero and a strict gap on every compact annulus of the lattice torus; the Gaussian factor controls all continuous tails. All fixed parameter derivatives contribute explicitly bounded powers of $N$. Taylor expansion of the elementary logarithm gives the central polynomials and their integrable remainder. This verifies the compiler on a real family without asserting it is a Sinai billiard.

\paragraph{Mechanical application still to be established.}
For the original Sinai programme, the complete stable-curve spaces, a concrete periodic-data parameter map, returned cancellation, bad-word estimates and source-differentiated $L^1$ remainder remain to be proved. Demers--Melbourne--Nicol, arXiv:1901.00131, establishes statistical limits for Holder observables of a Lorentz gas in an anisotropic framework; that result is not represented here as a four-source-derivative raw local Edgeworth theorem for the present family.
