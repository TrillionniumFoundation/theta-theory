\begin{abstract}
We determine the maximal-collision onsets of full-phase equilibrium
probabilities in smooth periodic planar dispersing billiards. A common
threshold collar and every fixed derivative of the relative remainder
are uniform in the number of flights; finite horizon is not required.
We then construct two semi-infinite boundary layers and prove exponential
factorization of the normalized nonlinear flux. This gives a fixed-offset
long-bridge law and a meromorphic continuation of its onset generating
function. The limiting law contains information absent from the entire
leading count and endpoint-metric hierarchy: an analytic, fixed-area
family has all these leading data in common, while an explicitly computed
nonlinear coefficient varies. Two physical endpoint variances recover the
contact curvatures uniformly at odd flight numbers. For unlabelled counts
we prove a sharp one-half-H\"older estimate relative to the circular
configuration, using a three-amplitude dispersion statistic. Fixed-order
multi-offset extrapolation gives quantitative acquisition bounds for the
conditional inverse, including timing error. The exact equal-gap inverse,
marked response, and complete circular specialization are retained.
\end{abstract}
