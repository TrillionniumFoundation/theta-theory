## E2 — Past-limited attainable geometry and general sequential compatibility

This is the principal mathematical addition, in `sections/06a_attainable_filtration.tex`.

For the ordered nonconstant future jet orders nu_i, let p=min(n(r−1),K_m−1). Theorem `thm:attainable-checkpoint` gives the uniform checkpoint law

    max_{1<=ell<=p} theta^{2(nu_1+...+nu_ell)/ell} M^{−2/ell}.

It does not assume the full-future inequality. Lemma `lem:attained-flag` proves that the actual binomial tangent is transverse to every initial resolution flag: each selected cluster contains complete initial jet blocks, so both the positive-parameter mixed pairing and the zero-parameter confluent pairing are surjective. The proof includes normalization, uniform local inverse bounds, complementary command integration and the actual all-failure evidence.

The global upper bound is a separate step. Lemma `lem:attainable-format` proves that the whole reachable scaled image is a bounded-format semialgebraic image of normalized factors, with dimension at most p. Prior integrals are real coefficients in a rational function of commands; neither a prior density nor semialgebraic dependence of those coefficients on calibration is assumed. Lemma `lem:tame-rectangle` uses real integral-geometric entropy and coefficient-independent section component bounds to truncate the covering sum at the actual image dimension. Its proof treats arbitrary sheet geometry, zero-width axes, actual integer M budgets, reachable centers and Borel ties.

Theorem `thm:general-uniform-streaming` then gives the maximum of these stage profiles as the sharp uniform streaming order for any fixed horizon in the affine class. It is not a checkpoint upper bound relabeled as online. The implemented state is a finite representative index. All updates are ratios of physical formal moments with denominator at least the positive report margin. No inverse power of theta appears; an explicit recurrence accumulates every requantization error. Lower bounds use prefixes of one common independent uniform acquisition law.

The seven-trial boundary is resolved as a consequence: `cor:seven-trial` gives max(M^(−1/3),theta^(1/2)M^(−1/4)). The peak has six strong and two weak attainable directions, not the ambient six-plus-three. The proof covers the full interval [0,1/2], explicitly allowing irrelevant positive collisions in longer sumsets. The general theorem itself chooses an interval without positive crossings in the relevant future sumsets. Neither distinction is hidden.
