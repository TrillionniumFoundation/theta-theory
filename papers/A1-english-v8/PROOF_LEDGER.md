## What is imported, and what is proved here

The real integral-geometric entropy inequality, bounded-format semialgebraic component theorem, real quantifier elimination, and standard facts about dimension under rational maps are classical inputs. The manuscript does not pretend to reprove all of real algebraic geometry. It states the entropy inequality precisely, attributes it, and proves the anisotropic consequence and its model-specific hypotheses. The all-prior attained flag, evidence minorization, dimension truncation and successive raw-update application are the written arguments to be checked here.

The zero-exponent cluster has only the constant. Positive-order jets therefore have positive limiting exponent and are bounded at t=0. Selected blocks are initial within every cluster, which is indispensable to the confluent pairing argument. A local flag cube is never substituted for a global flat posterior image. The global image is allowed to be curved and singular.
