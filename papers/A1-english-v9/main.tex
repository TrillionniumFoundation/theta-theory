\begin{abstract}
We determine the persistent finite-state memory of positive calibrated
monomial experiments uniformly across arbitrary additive exponent
collisions. For each $m$-step future, let $\mathcal V_{m,\ell}$ be the
largest absolute Vandermonde product of $\ell$ positive formal sum
exponents, scaled into a fixed unit interval. If
$p=\min\{n(r-1),q_m\}$, where $q_m$ counts nonconstant formal labels,
the optimal $M$-message checkpoint regret has order
\[
 \max_{1\le\ell\le p}
       (\mathcal V_{m,\ell}/M)^{2/\ell}.
\]
The constants are uniform on compact subsets of the one-step exponent
chamber and may depend on the fixed full-support prior and horizon.
No affine, analytic or one-parameter calibration assumption is needed.
A Newton flag is transverse to an attainable normalized-product tangent
even at repeated nodes; a bounded-format estimate controls the full
reachable image. For each known calibration and budget, one index-only
transducer realizes the maximum checkpoint law at every stage. Its raw
moment transitions contain no inverse exponent gaps. Pairwise collision
orders determine all pathwise resolution phases by a finite tree
allocation rule. A two-parameter collision arrangement exhibits three
regimes with arbitrarily separated crossover budgets along tangent paths.
The exact information dimension, the affine five- and seven-trial laws,
and the common-risk decision and mechanical comparisons are retained as
explicit subsidiary results.
\end{abstract}
