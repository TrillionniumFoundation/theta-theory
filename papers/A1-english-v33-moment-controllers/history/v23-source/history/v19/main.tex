\begin{abstract}
We study the geometry of prediction states acquired in positive finite
experiments. For likelihoods polynomial in the commands, on an arbitrary
compact latent space, an augmented moment matrix determines the sharp
finite-memory exponent for every prior. Its rank strata are algebraic
in finitely many prior moments, and one causal filter attains the maximal
checkpoint exponent. For positive affine acquisition we determine the
entire resolution profile: its scales are the singular values of the
physical acquisition--query cross-covariance, with constants uniform
through all rank losses. In the square pairing regime, a sign condition
on paired evaluation determinants is necessary and sufficient for full
acquisition rank under every full-support prior. A product-cone
alternative treats arbitrary rectangular pairings and realizes all
permitted kernel dimensions by full-support priors. One fixed positive
experiment realizes every small covariance matrix by bounded prior
tilts; along analytic arcs, determinantal orders consequently determine
all joint memory--degeneration exponents. For sparse monomial
experiments we further identify the exterior-volume profile uniformly
through arbitrary additive exponent collisions, with truncation by the
past and a gap-free causal realization. For a circular detector, acquired
and observed attenuation combine to give paired scales $\tau^{2j}$.
The complete integer-budget checkpoint curve determines the initial
scale products within these classified families. We also determine
monomial prior-ambiguity widths and the joint memory and common-moment
error law. Finite numerical realization and its separate resource bounds
are established for the monomial experiment.
\end{abstract}
