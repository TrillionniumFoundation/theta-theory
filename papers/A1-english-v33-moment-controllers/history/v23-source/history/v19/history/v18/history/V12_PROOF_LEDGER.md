## Three separate assurances

**Evaluation assurance:** a supplied finite-data tolerance bounds numerical
state/query error. It may be checked against independent truth in finite
fixtures, but neither the compiler nor a source hash proves an arbitrary oracle.

**Residual assurance:** `verify_program` bounds the trajectory error of the
particular table given to it. It remains valid for a poor or corrupted table,
and its bound may grow when that table changes. This proposition is retained.

**Construction assurance:** `inspect_construction` fixes numerical lists and
covering radii before checking the table. It checks every representative,
transition, tie and output entry without importing compiler helpers. A changed
target cannot enlarge that acceptance allowance. The mathematical theorem links
this construction to the covering scale. Its executable checker is not a formal
proof checker and relies on the stated fixed-evaluation and input-accuracy
contracts.

## Hand-audited delicate points

The common-advice theorem compares the true state to its true representative
history in each compatible model, so initial representation error is zero even
when the empty-history moment name is uncertain. At every visited scale,
`tau=max(h,rho)<=2h`; failure at the preceding scale yields both
`e_E<10(A+4)h` and `rho<2h`. This gives simultaneous scale control without any
monotonicity assumption on numerically computed radii.

The prior-envelope extension explicitly checks all prior-dependent parts of the
inherited local lower and global upper arguments. It does not infer uniformity
over arbitrary full-support measures. Compactness alone is used only after
continuity and positivity of the desingularized mixed-moment pairings are
established. A fixed arbitrary prior remains covered by taking a singleton
family.

The positive lower witness uses an entire positive-probability report event,
not an exact command from a continuous distribution. Given accepted cell 0,
the command-history law is identical under both priors. The probe is a genuine
rejection event with command `(1/2,3/8,5/8)`. Thus no discarded acquisition cost
or uncharged true-prior information enters the lower bound.

Preservation is checked by source hashes and theorem/proof inventories, not by
counting results as independent breakthroughs. The new statements require
further mathematical referee scrutiny; the execution receipts certify only the
listed finite checks and builds.
