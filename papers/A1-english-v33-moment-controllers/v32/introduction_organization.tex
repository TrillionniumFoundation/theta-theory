The compatibility, approximation and certificate results are proved in
Sections~\ref{sec:v32-compatibility}--\ref{sec:v32-certificates}; their
exact finite nonregenerative application is in
Section~\ref{sec:v32-delayed}. These sections follow the geometric
classification and precede the graph extensions. Only the relative
certificate invokes the attained lower bound; the exact finite equations
and their absolute-error approximation are proved independently of it.
Section~\ref{sec:v32-ledger} records the information conventions of the
principal models. All inherited proof modules remain active in the main
article or the complete companion under their original hypotheses.
