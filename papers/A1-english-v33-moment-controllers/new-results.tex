\section{Fixed-calibration menu stabilization}
For a finite graph, let $p_e$ be fixed edge caps, let $V_{e,0}=1$,
and let $V_{e,\ell}\ge0$ be the edge determinant volumes at calibration
$a$. Put
\[
 b_e(\varepsilon,a)=\max_{0\le\ell\le p_e}
 \{(\ell/2)\log_2(1/\varepsilon)+\log_2V_{e,\ell}(a)\},
\]
omitting zero positive volumes. For an order $\pi$, let $S_j^\pi$ be
its first $j$ vertices and put
\[
 W_\pi=\max_j\sum_{e\in\delta(S_j^\pi)}b_e,\quad
 W_G=\min_\pi W_\pi,\quad W_\Pi=\min_{\pi\in\Pi}W_\pi.
\]
Empty graph profiles are zero. For nonempty $\mathcal E\subset(0,1]$,
define
\[
 r_K(\mathcal E,a)=\min_{1\le|\Pi|\le K}
 \sup_{\varepsilon\in\mathcal E}(W_\Pi-W_G).
\]
