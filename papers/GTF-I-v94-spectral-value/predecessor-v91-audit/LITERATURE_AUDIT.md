# Primary-source comparison — General Theta Foundations I, Revision 91

This is a source comparison for R60, not independent specialist priority clearance. The inherited full bibliography and comparison remain active. The exact claims are a general two-call reset variational principle with finite realization and attained dual, and simultaneous equal-prior optimization of three strategy classes on a shared finite device family. We do not claim firstness for the underlying tools.

## Memory-constrained strategies

Ohst, Zhang, Nguyen, Plavala and Quintino, arXiv:2411.08110v2, especially Definition 23, equation (64), Theorem 24 and Section 6.2, formulate classical/quantum adaptive memory restrictions using constrained separability. Their complete-measure-and-reprepare effects are positive complete-call products. Our class definition retains their smaller classical class but allows an unmeasured old receiver after a complete fresh-preparation cut in the reset class. The new variational theorem treats that larger cut-defined class and explicitly realizes its common-barycenter decompositions. It is not a classification of every ancillary-dimension slice in their framework. Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2411.08110v2 .

Zonnios and Binder, Distinguishing quantum processes with bounded coherent memory, arXiv:2606.19511v1, Proposition 2, Theorem 1 and Corollary 1, study autonomous repeated-instrument machines for multi-time process discrimination and finite-time completeness with sufficient coherent memory and a counter. Our fresh-preparation cut, unrestricted old receiver, time-dependent classical rules and hard call-allocation costs are different resources. The new finite receiver bound is for attaining a two-call cut-constrained decision value, not a converse to their entire autonomous-memory hierarchy. Source: https://arxiv.org/html/2606.19511v1 .

## Envelopes and duality

Uhlmann, Roofs and convexity, Entropy 12 (2010), 1799–1832, surveys convex/concave roof constructions; the arXiv copy is 1108.3218. Our envelope is taken over all density matrices, not just pure states. Compact convexification, face-wise Caratheodory reduction and atom elimination are standard finite-dimensional tools. The theorem-specific step is their exact physical realization under a reset cut with a common barycenter across first labels. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.3218 .

Boyd and Vandenberghe, Convex Optimization, Cambridge University Press, 2004, Section 5.2.3 and the treatment of generalized inequalities, give Slater strong duality. The manuscript specifies its closed homogeneous hypograph cones and an explicit strictly feasible point; it does not merely invoke a formal Lagrangian. Source: https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/ .

## Design and measurement-comparison mechanisms

Gross, Audenaert and Eisert, Evenly distributed unitaries: On the structure of unitary designs, Journal of Mathematical Physics 48 (2007), 052104, arXiv:quant-ph/0611002, is retained for finite second-moment design antecedents. Our d^6+1 Caratheodory support bound is an existence reduction with real weights, not an efficient design algorithm. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0611002 .

Ziman, Heinosaari and Sedlak, Unambiguous comparison of quantum measurements, arXiv:0905.4445, is retained for antisymmetric comparison mechanisms. Neither use of a swap nor use of an antisymmetric test alone is a novelty claim. The present theorem asserts all three exact optimized equal-prior values and proves the reset upper with receiver feedback. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/0905.4445 .

Eid and Quintino, arXiv:2602.12258, study measurement discrimination with accessible post-measurement states in a Luders-instrument interface. Our unknown interface exposes only a classical outcome, not a residual device system; its receiver references are experimenter-supplied. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12258 .
