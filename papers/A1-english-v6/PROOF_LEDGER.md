## Analytic obligations not discharged by the test count

The all-budget transversality theorem, generalized Vandermonde sign argument,
invariance-of-domain lower bound, local subprobability minorization, randomized
coding quantifiers and general finite-state error propagation rely on the
written proofs. The finite diagnostics test their finite algebraic instances.
The 1,057 positive minors are one exact family, not a proof for every matrix size.
The streaming fixture is on a finite menu, whereas the asymptotic lower theorem
requires its explicitly continuous command stream.
