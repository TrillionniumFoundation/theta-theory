## Logical separations the proofs require

The full posterior dimension qn and observable-future quotient q min(n,m) solve different problems. Full support supplies Gram definiteness, not a uniform conditioning constant. The quantitative attainable ball depends on the actual calibration and prior moments. The finite-code theorem counts mutable checkpoint history storage, not total firmware bits, arithmetic precision, a streaming-quantization algorithm, or global policy-search time. Its fresh public seed is independent of the entire history. The common query index is revealed after encoding and does not leak the history. A fixed past-command family supplies the lower-bound section; its all-failure event is retained and has probability at least eta^n.

The old adaptive cost gap is not relabelled as a mark-induced gain. The new sign-erasure comparison changes the sharp law under the same score protocol, and the separate two-cartridge theorem gives positive free-memory value without cost saving. Same-degree calibration robustness does not imply exact polynomial rank for a nonpolynomial perturbation. The referee's two extensions are credited, and the classical quantization/topology/comparison mechanisms are not claimed as newly invented.
