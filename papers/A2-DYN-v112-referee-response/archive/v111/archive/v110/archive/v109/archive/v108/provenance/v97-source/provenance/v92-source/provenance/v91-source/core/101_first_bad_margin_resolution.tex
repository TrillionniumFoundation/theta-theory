\section{First bad margins after endpoint deconcentration}
\label{sec:v47-first-bad-margins}
The endpoint estimate pays a genuine part of the pointwise boundary
correction. We now separate it from the remaining incidence, clearance
and interior-decision sources. The split is positive, takes place
before pushforward, and preserves every original coefficient. A mass
estimate for the residual is recorded only in its own norm.

Set $\eta=\varepsilon$ in
\eqref{eq:v47-partial-guard} and \eqref{eq:v47-end-source}. Then the
original guard factors exactly as
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v47-guard-factorization}
 \Xi_{m,R}^{\varepsilon}
       =G_{m,R}^{\varepsilon,J}E_{m,R}^{\varepsilon,J},\qquad
 1-\Xi_{m,R}^{\varepsilon}
       =(1-G_{m,R}^{\varepsilon,J})
          +G_{m,R}^{\varepsilon,J}(1-E_{m,R}^{\varepsilon,J}).
\end{equation}
For $m\le2J+2$ take $\mathcal E_{m,J}=\{0,\ldots,m\}$, omitting
repeated indices. This defines the identities for every finite count;
it does not affect any fixed-$J$ collision limit.

List the factors of $G_{m,R}^{\varepsilon,J}$ as $w_1,\ldots,w_N$,
ordered by distance from the nearest endpoint and, at ties, by incidence,
clearance, section distance, then increasing physical index. Flight
distance means $\min(j,m-1-j)$. Fix this order once. Define disjoint
measurable sets
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v47-first-defect}
 A_i=\{w_i<1\}\cap\bigcap_{h<i}\{w_h=1\}.
\end{equation}
They partition $\{G<1\}$, not just the set where a factor vanishes.
A first defect includes the transition region of the smooth guard.
Let $\mathcal A_{\rm inc},\mathcal A_{\rm clr},\mathcal A_{\rm mid}$
be the unions corresponding to the three kinds of factor. Put
\begin{align}
 r^{\varepsilon,J,\mathrm a}_{n,k,m,R}(t)\,dt
  &=(T_{n,R})_*
    \bigl(\one_{\Pi_{n,k,m,R}}(1-G_{m,R}^{\varepsilon,J})
              \one_{\mathcal A_{\mathrm a}}\nu_R^*\bigr),
       &&\mathrm a\in\{\mathrm{inc},\mathrm{clr},\mathrm{mid}\},
                                                         \notag\\
 r^{\varepsilon,J}_{n,k,m,R}
  &=r^{\varepsilon,J,\mathrm{inc}}_{n,k,m,R}
    +r^{\varepsilon,J,\mathrm{clr}}_{n,k,m,R}
    +r^{\varepsilon,J,\mathrm{mid}}_{n,k,m,R}.
                                      \label{eq:v47-residual-densities}
\end{align}
All these densities are nonnegative and have their original
normalization. Together with $f^\varepsilon$ from
\eqref{eq:v46-protected-correction} and $d^{\varepsilon,\varepsilon,J}$
from \eqref{eq:v47-end-source}, they give the exact full density
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v47-full-source-split}
 p_{n,R}(k,m,\cdot)=f^\varepsilon_{n,k,m,R}
            +d^{\varepsilon,\varepsilon,J}_{n,k,m,R}
            +r^{\varepsilon,J}_{n,k,m,R}.
\end{equation}
This is not a mixture of differently conditioned probabilities.

\begin{proposition}[First-defect masses and their quantifiers]
\label{prop:v47-first-defect-masses}
There is an absolute constant $C$ such that, for every $m,J,R$,
\begin{align}
 \sum_{n,k}\|r^{\varepsilon,J,\mathrm{inc}}_{n,k,m,R}\|_1
     &\le C\varepsilon^2,\notag\\
 \sum_{n,k}\|r^{\varepsilon,J,\mathrm{clr}}_{n,k,m,R}\|_1
     &\le C\varepsilon,\notag\\
 \sum_{n,k}\|r^{\varepsilon,J,\mathrm{mid}}_{n,k,m,R}\|_1
     &\le C\varepsilon q^{(J+1)/4}.
                                      \label{eq:v47-defect-mass-bounds}
\end{align}
The last source is zero if there are no retained middle decisions.
For bounded insertions of modulus at most $M$, the variation-mass
bounds acquire only the factor $M$. Variation here has no factor
$1/2$. These are not pointwise density bounds.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
A first incidence defect at contact $j$ has
$c_j<2\varepsilon e_j$, a clearance defect has
$\Delta_j<2\varepsilon e'_j$, and a middle-decision defect has
$s_j<2\varepsilon e_j$ with $\min(j,m-j)>J$.
Drop the earlier-good conditions in \eqref{eq:v47-first-defect}, use
$1-G\le1$, and apply Lemma~\ref{lem:v46-margin-neighborhoods}
at the indicated collision or flight. Collision invariance and
$\nu_R^*\le\nu/c$ are sufficient. At most two indices have a given
endpoint distance, so the three sums are bounded respectively by
\[
 C\varepsilon^2\sum_{d\ge0}q^{d/2},\qquad
 C\varepsilon\sum_{d\ge0}q^{d/4},\qquad
 C\varepsilon\sum_{d>J}q^{d/4}.
\]
These prove \eqref{eq:v47-defect-mass-bounds}. At fixed $m$ the
original events $\Pi_{n,k,m,R}$ are disjoint, so the component mass
sum is at most the original restricted-source mass. Domination gives
the assertion for bounded complex insertions. No independence is used.
\end{proof}

\subsection{An explicit endpoint depth in the ordered bandwidth limit}
For a fixed sufficiently large $A$, choose
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v47-ordered-endpoint-depth}
 \varepsilon(B)=A B^{-1/12},\qquad
 J(B)=\lfloor B^{1/24}\rfloor,\qquad B\ge B_0.
\end{equation}
Increase $B_0$ so that $\varepsilon(B)<\varepsilon_0$ and choose
$A^{12}\ge C_2$ from Theorem~\ref{thm:v46-protected-correction}.
Both $\varepsilon(B)$ and $J(B)$ are fixed before the collision limit.
This is an explicit bandwidth-dependent depth, not a rate depending
on $m$.

\begin{theorem}[Removal of a growing endpoint decision layer]
\label{thm:v47-ordered-boundary-removal}
At the choices \eqref{eq:v47-ordered-endpoint-depth},
\begin{align}
 \limsup_{m\to\infty}\sup_{R,n,k}m^2
 \bigl\|\mathscr D_{B,n,R}(k,m,\cdot)
       -[r^{\varepsilon(B),J(B)}_{n,k,m,R}
                 -K_B*r^{\varepsilon(B),J(B)}_{n,k,m,R}]\bigr\|_\infty
                                  \le C B^{-1/24}.
                                      \label{eq:v47-paid-correction}
\end{align}
Here $\mathscr D_{B,n,R}=p_{n,R}-K_B*p_{n,R}$ is the same complete
coefficient-first correction as in
Theorem~\ref{thm:v43-arithmetic-raw-criterion}.
For the residual sources the explicit uniform mass estimates are
\begin{align}
 \sum_{n,k}\|r^{\varepsilon(B),J(B),\mathrm{inc}}\|_1
       &\le C B^{-1/6},\notag\\
 \sum_{n,k}\|r^{\varepsilon(B),J(B),\mathrm{clr}}\|_1
       &\le C B^{-1/12},\notag\\
 \sum_{n,k}\|r^{\varepsilon(B),J(B),\mathrm{mid}}\|_1
       &\le C B^{-1/12}q^{(\lfloor B^{1/24}\rfloor+1)/4}.
                                      \label{eq:v47-ordered-residual-masses}
\end{align}
The endpoint component removed in
\eqref{eq:v47-paid-correction} satisfies the same pointwise estimate,
uniformly for every bounded measurable insertion. The protected
weighted component retains the endpoint-derivative assumptions of
Theorem~\ref{thm:v46-protected-correction}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply $I-K_B*$ to \eqref{eq:v47-full-source-split}.
The protected part has normalized collision limsup at most
$CB^{-1/2}$, since $B\ge C_2\varepsilon(B)^{-12}$.
The endpoint part has bound $C(J(B)+1)\varepsilon(B)$ by
\eqref{eq:v47-endpoint-all-band}. This is at most $CB^{-1/24}$.
Their sum proves \eqref{eq:v47-paid-correction}, with no rate in the
collision count. The mass statements are direct substitutions in
\eqref{eq:v47-defect-mass-bounds}. The two weighted classes are exactly
those in their respective source estimates and are not conflated.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[The remaining arithmetic correction]
\label{cor:v47-arithmetic-residual}
On every central target set used in
Theorem~\ref{thm:v43-arithmetic-raw-criterion}, the full uniform
arithmetic pointwise law is equivalent to
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v47-residual-criterion}
 \lim_{B\to\infty}\limsup_{m\to\infty}\sup_{R,n,k}
 m^2\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{\text{central }t}
 \left|r^{\varepsilon(B),J(B)}_{n,k,m,R}(t)
              -(K_B*r^{\varepsilon(B),J(B)}_{n,k,m,R})(t)\right|=0.
\end{equation}
Its main term remains $\mathcal L_{m,R}$ uniformly in the radius,
and $c\mathfrak a_R(k,n,m)g_{\Omega_R}$ at fixed radius.
The three residual corrections are summed with their signs before
taking the absolute value in \eqref{eq:v47-residual-criterion}.
No separate vanishing assertion for each of them is needed for this
equivalence or proved here.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The triangle inequality and \eqref{eq:v47-paid-correction} show that
the ordered limsup of the full correction equals zero exactly when
that of the residual does. Apply the unchanged arithmetic criterion.
No arithmetic residue is replaced by one, and no exact coefficient
is replaced by a packet average.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Scope of the pointwise advance.}
Equation~\eqref{eq:v47-endpoint-density-smallness} is a density
estimate for an actual collapsing decision source; it does not follow
from \eqref{eq:v47-defect-mass-bounds}. It also controls all of that
component's signed inverse and its finite trace jumps, with arbitrary
bounded source insertions. The positive comparison crosses finitely
many section cuts, but the conclusion holds for the original return
index, roof and displacement.

Incidence and clearance margins are different: crossing them can
change the physical word and collision count, so the same flow cannot
be used there. Nor has a pointwise bound for the middle-decision source
been inferred from its exponentially small mass in $J$. These three
sources retain the signed correction in
\eqref{eq:v47-residual-criterion}. The full raw-return theorem and a
pointwise roof-conditioned bridge still require that analytical
estimate. A bandwidth-dependent choice in
\eqref{eq:v47-ordered-endpoint-depth} supplies no prescribed
count-dependent protection rate and no growing-band spectral bound.
