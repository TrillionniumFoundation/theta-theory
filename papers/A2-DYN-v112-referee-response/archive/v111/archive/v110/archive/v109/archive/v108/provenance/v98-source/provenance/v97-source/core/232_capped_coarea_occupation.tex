\section{Capped coarea occupation on the original event}
\label{sec:v97-capped}
A worst-time density ratio can fail at a time which supplies no
same-label anchor. Requiring a whole one-sided interval can also
reject two shorter intervals which together supply sufficient mass.
We retain all available times and cap their coarea contribution
before integrating. No state of a different label supplies mass.

\begin{lemma}[The collar is not a microscopic obstruction]
\label{lem:v97-collar}
Put $r_{\rm col}=R_-^2/(800\Lambda_\circ)$. A comparison trajectory
starting in $\Pi$ cannot reach the artificial collar boundary in
either direction within roof distance $r_{\rm col}$. This statement
does not assert continuation past a true physical or critical end.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The original section gives $|p_0|,|p_m|<17/20$. Indeed its largest
positive momentum is at most
$2(53/100)/(1+(53/100)^2)+1/200<17/20$; all other section
rectangles have smaller absolute momenta. Along the comparison flow,
$g$ has constant direction and its squared norm has derivative between
$2\lambda_*$ and $2\Lambda_\circ$. Therefore, for either sign of a
surviving time $s$,
\[
 |p_i(\widehat\Phi_sx)-p_i(x)|
 \le R_-^{-1}|g(\widehat\Phi_sx)-g(x)|
 \le R_-^{-1}\sqrt{2\Lambda_\circ|s|},\qquad i=0,m.
\]
At the stated radius this is at most $1/20$. Hence the momenta
remain below $9/10$, strictly inside the collar $19/20$.
The same estimate holds at a limiting first boundary time, so a
collar end cannot be the first obstruction, including a simultaneous
failure. A backward critical end is not excluded by this argument.
\end{proof}

For $x\in H\subset\Pi$, let
$T_r(x)=(-r,r)\cap(-\widehat\tau^-(x),\widehat\tau^+(x))$.
Define the dimensionless capped occupation
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v97-capped-clock}
 c_{H,r}(x)=\frac1r\int_{T_r(x)}
 \one_H(\widehat\Phi_sx)
 \min\left\{1,\frac{\widehat A(\widehat\Phi_sx)}{\widehat A(x)}\right\}\,ds.
\end{equation}
It belongs to $[0,2]$. It is a Borel function by the measurable
survivor graph and Tonelli. The density ratio is taken along the
same physical transverse coordinate; it is not a ratio of projected
roof densities. For $0<q\le1/2$ put $G_{H,r,q}=H\cap\{c_{H,r}\ge q\}$.

\begin{theorem}[An anchor using all available coarea mass]
\label{thm:v97-capped-anchor}
At finite collision count and almost every target roof,
\begin{align}
 R_{G_{H,r,q}}^\varepsilon(t)
 &\le\frac1{qr}\int_{t-r}^{t+r}
       \{R_H^\varepsilon(u)+\kappa_\varepsilon(r)U_H(u)\}\,du,
                                      \label{eq:v97-capped-anchor}\\
 U_{G_{H,r,q}}(t)&\le\frac1{qr}\int_{t-r}^{t+r}U_H(u)\,du.
                                      \label{eq:v97-capped-unweighted}
\end{align}
If $q=\beta/D\le1/2$, the previously admitted set
$H^{\rm adm}_{r,D,\beta}$ is contained in $G_{H,r,q}$.
No lower gradient or residence cutoff is used in this theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Write $A_0=\widehat A(x)$, $A_s=\widehat A(\widehat\Phi_sx)$
and $W_s=W^\varepsilon(\widehat\Phi_sx)$. On a sampled return to
$H$, the unchanged guard gives
\[
 \min(A_0,A_s)W_0\le A_s\{W_s+\kappa_\varepsilon(r)\}.
\]
Integrating this inequality and using $c_{H,r}(x)\ge q$ bounds
$A_0W_0$ by the right side divided by $qr$. Integrate over target
transverse coordinates. At each fixed signed time the comparison
flow is injective, preserves the transverse coordinate, and maps the
selected targets into the same exact $H$ fiber. Thus their $A_s$
mass is a submeasure of that original fiber. Tonelli proves
\eqref{eq:v97-capped-anchor}; taking weight one proves
\eqref{eq:v97-capped-unweighted}. The two signs are already the two
halves of the displayed roof integral. Multiple returns introduce
no multiplicity at a fixed sampled roof.

On a previously admitted one-sided interval, at least $\beta r$
times return to $H$, and $A_s/A_0\ge D^{-1}$ there. Their capped
contribution is at least $\beta/D=q$. This proves containment.
All identities use common kernels at each fixed choice of parameters;
no universal null set for uncountably many selectors is asserted.
\end{proof}

For $H=\Pi$, denote $c_{\Pi,r}$ by $c_r$. Let $N=N_T\cup N_D\cup N_O$
be the preceding disjoint failed-test set. The theorem already estimates
an actual part of that old failure source:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v97-first-recovery}
 \mathcal H_M\left(R_{N\cap\{c_r\ge q\}}^\varepsilon\right)
 \le\frac Cq\{\varepsilon^{1/16}+\varepsilon^{-1}\sqrt r\},
 \qquad q=\beta/D\le1/2.
\end{equation}
This follows from the two original fixed-width source estimates.
Unavailable times and times of a different label contribute zero to
\eqref{eq:v97-capped-clock}; a very large inverse ratio at one such
time does not by itself reject the target. This is a containment and
an estimated recovery, not a claim that every physical failure is recovered.
