\section{Retained inputs and conventions}
The next statements specify the prior analytic and geometric results
used here. They are part of the frozen proof archive, not new
assumptions silently substituted for the original event. Exact proof
locations are recorded in the accompanying dependency map. The new
argument below requires no path-remainder input.

\begin{inputstatement}[Physical optical geometry and common fibers]
\label{input:v93-geometry}
At fixed $m,R,n,k$, the regular exact event, with critical points
removed, has a Borel parametrization by its complete maximal segments
$(\sigma,u)$, $a_\sigma<u<b_\sigma$. The transverse measure $d\sigma$
is the disjoint angular measure. In endpoint angles,
\[
 F=L,\quad g=\nabla F=R(-p_0,p_m),\quad z=|g|,\quad
 K=D^2F,\quad \vartheta=\arg g,\quad
 X=\frac{K^{-1}g}{g^{\mathsf T}K^{-1}g}.
\]
Here $XF=1$, $X\vartheta=0$,
$\lambda_\circ I\le K\le\Lambda_\circ I$, and
$\lambda_\circ\le\chi:=|\det D(F,\vartheta)|\le\Lambda_\circ$,
where $\lambda_\circ=9\sqrt{19}/200$ and $\Lambda_\circ=47/6$.
The physical source and its common roof kernels are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v93-area-input}
 d\bar\nu=A_\sigma(u)\,du\,d\sigma,
 \qquad A_\sigma=\omega/(4\pi c_*\chi),\qquad
 \omega=|\partial_{p_0}\alpha_m|^{-1}.
\end{equation}
For every surviving segment and $s>0$,
\[
 2\lambda_\circ s\le z(u+s)^2-z(u)^2\le2\Lambda_\circ s,
 \qquad |\partial_u A_\sigma|\le C A_\sigma I_m/z,
 \quad I_m=1+\sum_{i=1}^{m-1}q_0^{d_i}/c_i.
\]
The complete original guard is locally Lipschitz on the physical graph
and satisfies $|\partial_uW^\varepsilon|\le C\varepsilon^{-1}/z$
almost everywhere. All constants depend only on the fixed physical
family and the original smooth $\Theta$.

For $V_i=2/(Rc_i)$ the positive optical recursion is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v93-recursion-input}
 y_1=\ell_0,\quad F_0=1,\quad
 F_i=F_{i-1}+V_i y_i,\quad y_{i+1}=y_i+\ell_iF_i,
 \qquad \omega=Rc_0c_m/y_m.
\end{equation}
The $F_i$ in this recurrence are optical variables, not the roof $F$.
The physical flights satisfy $\ell_i\ge\tau_-$.

The flow stops at actual physical and section-decision boundaries;
Borel chart seams are not ends. All contact data have one-sided
limits. A finite backward end is critical or is a true incidence,
clearance or section-decision end; there is no forward interior
critical termination. At a fixed count the unweighted physical-word
and endpoint graph is a finite definable family in rational circle
coordinates. No definability of $\Theta$ is assumed.
\end{inputstatement}
This is the endpoint-field, optical-recursion, all-contact-motion,
full-angular-variation and Borel-segment chain in the retained archive.
The formula for the angular density includes both the inverse twist
and angular Jacobian; it is not Lebesgue density in endpoint angles.

\begin{inputstatement}[The positive scalar and cutoff-source estimates]
\label{input:v93-analytic}
For every fixed $\varepsilon,r>0$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v93-bad-input}
 \limsup_m\sup_{R,n,k}m^2\|A_rb^\varepsilon\|_\infty
        \le C\varepsilon^{1/16},\qquad
 A_rf(t)=\frac1{2r}\int_{t-r}^{t+r}f(s)\,ds.
\end{equation}
For $\rho>0$ define the actual positive source
\[
 v^\rho(t)\,dt
       =F_\#(I_m z^{-1}\mathbf1_{\{z\ge\rho\}}\bar\nu).
\]
For every previously fixed $\rho,L>0$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v93-cutoff-input}
 \limsup_m\sup_{R,n,k}\sup_{|J|=L}m^2\int_J v^\rho\le C_0L,
          \qquad C_0\text{ independent of }\rho,L.
\end{equation}
This estimate is not stated at $\rho=0$. Its inherited finite-count
remainder contains a factor $\rho^{-1/2}$.

Writing $P=m^2p$, $G=\mathcal L$, the scalar decomposition is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v93-scalar-input}
 P-G=m^2b^{\varepsilon(B)}+e_B,\quad
 \varepsilon(B)=A_0B^{-1/12},\quad
 \limsup_m\sup_{R,n,k}\esssup_{\mathcal C(M)}|e_B|
                                  \le C_MB^{-1/192}.
\end{equation}
The original union density $R_{\zeta,\delta}^\varepsilon$ has the
ordered local mass estimate
\[
 \limsup_m\sup_{R,n,k}\sup_{|J|=L}m^2\int_J
 R_{\zeta,\delta}^\varepsilon
       \le C_L(\zeta^{129/128}+\delta^{1/32}).
\]
The complete unweakened weak-$L^{32/31}$ raw limit and original-union
estimate are retained; they are not required to justify the signed
balance. Every auxiliary band is fixed before its collision limit
and enlarged only afterwards. The reconstruction band $B$ is last.
\end{inputstatement}
The fixed-width bound is on the complete original source. The cutoff
estimate is the audited positive layer-cake theorem, not a theorem
for a boundary insertion. The one-sided lower law follows from
\eqref{eq:v93-scalar-input} and remains historically credited. The
present revision returns to the opposite, upper sign.

All original densities are common coarea versions for fixed
$m,R,n,k$. Signed identities are identities of finite measures at
fixed count. Pointwise conclusions are understood at their Lebesgue
points, with the permitted uniform order $\sup_R\esssup_t$.
The Borel segment formulas define common versions for all positive
selectors; we do not require one set of Lebesgue points for all
radii and every uncountable family of cutoffs simultaneously.
