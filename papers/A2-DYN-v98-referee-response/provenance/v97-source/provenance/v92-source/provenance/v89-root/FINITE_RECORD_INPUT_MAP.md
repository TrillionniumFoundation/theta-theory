# Finite-record input map: v16

The new external fact is the finite, coefficient-uniform bound for the zeroth Betti numbers of realizable sign conditions. Source: S. Basu, R. Pollack and M.-F. Roy, *An asymptotically tight bound on the number of semi-algebraically connected components of realizable sign conditions*, Combinatorica 29 (2009), 523--546; arXiv:math/0603256v3, Section 3.2, equation (3.3), printed page 5. The primary preprint and the author's publication list were consulted. The proof uses the displayed finite inequality, not the fixed-dimension leading asymptotic in the abstract.

For `s` polynomials of degree at most `d` in `k` variables, the finite bound used is `d(2d-1)^(k-1) sum_{i=0}^k binom(s,i) 4^i`. A Boolean union of sign conditions has at most the total number of their components. A continuous projection cannot increase the number of connected components. The manuscript applies these observations to a lifted graph with `s,k=O(L)` and bounded degree, allowing at most exponentially many label words. The deliberately safe budget is `exp(C L log(2+L))`.

The following are proved in the article, not imported from the component theorem: the polynomial encoding of the actual first flight and specular reflection; no-earlier-hit inequalities obtained by minimizing distance along a segment; bounded auxiliaries and uniqueness of the regular fiber; reverse-iterate encoding; angular rectangle membership as finite half-plane tests; superlevel slice projection; coarea passage to first variation; the smooth composition bound on the embedded collision cylinder; the exact age partition and top-defect identity; all approximation and regularity budgets.

The BV coarea, product and Lipschitz-chain facts are the same first-derivative facts already used in v15, referenced there to Ambrosio--Fusco--Pallara, Chapter 3. This argument imports no bound for second derivatives of pushforward densities, no quantitative complexity for inverse coarea weights, and no spectral realization of the unbounded induced twist.
