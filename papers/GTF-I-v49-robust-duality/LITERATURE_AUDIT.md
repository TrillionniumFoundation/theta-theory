The article's finite two-state theorem concerns the bounded product over seed, each controlled epoch and query. It includes zero thresholds, sign chambers and a complete multiplicative rejection alternative, followed by stochastic realization. The cubic positive-error field example is proved internally. Shitov's field-of-exact-NMF result is retained as a different comparison, not invoked to prove that example.

Inherited positive realization, weighted automata, Hankel, compact arithmetic, quantization and finite-bit citations remain and have their earlier source-audit provenance. The present audit does not pretend to have independently re-audited every inherited citation. The r30 critique is answered by the explicit comparison table in the paper and the new proofs. Targeted primary-source comparison is not exhaustive priority clearance or an independent expert report.
