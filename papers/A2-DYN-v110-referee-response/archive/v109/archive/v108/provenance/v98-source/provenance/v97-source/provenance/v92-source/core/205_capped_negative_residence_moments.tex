\section{A physical cap and negative residence moments}
\label{sec:v88-residence}
Throughout this section $\ell=\tau^-+\tau^+$ is the total residence
of the original maximal regular exact-event segment. It is not the
collision return time. Every point of $\Omega$ has $\ell>0$.
Write $\gamma_0=1/32$ and $\bar\nu=\nu_R^*|_\Omega$.
The following estimate integrates down to residence zero at finite
collision count; its proof does not sum fixed-scale limiting bounds.

\begin{lemma}[A count-independent physical cap]
\label{lem:v88-physical-cap}
There are $s_0,C>0$ such that, uniformly in the collision count,
radius and exact labels,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v88-physical-cap}
 \bar\nu\{\ell\le s\}\le \bar\nu\{\tau^+\le s\}
                                      \le C s^{1/2},\qquad 0<s<s_0.
\end{equation}
No interval in the roof or incidence corridor is imposed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use the actual starting layers in \eqref{eq:v83-layer-input}.
Under invariant collision measure, $\nu\{c<w\}=1-\sqrt{1-w^2}
\le w^2$. A $w$-neighborhood of the finitely many rectangular
section edges has measure at most $Cw$, uniformly in $R$.
For a flight-clearance layer use the image-collision envelope of
Supplement Lemma~\ref{supp-lem:v49-physical-envelope}, at collision
$j+1$. Here is the area estimate, in addition to its retained
stable-curve estimate. At fixed image angle $\alpha$, each candidate
disk in the finite set $\mathcal D$ has backward coordinates
$L_d,Z_d$. On $L_d>0$, $R\le |Z_d|\le R+w$, one has
$L_d\ge\ell_*>0$ and $\partial_\varphi Z_d=L_d$. The sinusoidal
level equations have a bounded number of components. Their total
$\varphi$ length is at most $Cw$. Integrating
$d\nu=(4\pi)^{-1}\cos\varphi\,d\alpha\,d\varphi$ proves that
this envelope has measure at most $Cw$. The finite disk set and
$\ell_*$ are uniform for the prescribed family.

Invariance permits these estimates at every actual marked collision.
There are at most two contact marks, and two flight marks, at each
depth. Thus the union bound at widths $C_Eq_0^d\sqrt s$ is bounded
by a constant times
$\sum_{d\ge0}(q_0^{2d}s+q_0^d\sqrt s)\le C\sqrt s$.
Dropping the initial and terminal section tests only on this positive
upper comparison costs $c_*^{-1}$ once. No exact labels are changed
in the source being estimated. Finally $\ell\le s$ implies
$\tau^+\le s$.
\end{proof}

The retained finite-count exit theorem, restated in
\eqref{eq:v88-exit-input}, and the physical cap give a stronger
residence estimate than either bound separately. Namely,
\begin{align}
 m^2\bar\nu\{F\in J,\ell\le s\}
 \le{}& Cs^{\gamma_0}(|J|+B_*^{-1})\notag\\
 &+C_{B_*}(1+B_*|J|)m^3
                         \min\{\rho_{B_*}^{m/2},s^{1/2}\}.
                                      \label{eq:v88-capped-residence}
\end{align}
This holds for every interval and $0<s<s_0$, with $B_*$ fixed.
Indeed $x\le A+D$ and $x\le V$ imply
$x\le A+\min(D,V)$. Enlarging the common constant and using
$m^2\le m^3$ gives exactly the displayed remainder. This step is
performed on the positive probability, not on an unsigned spectral
remainder claimed to have the physical cap by itself.

\begin{theorem}[Actual untruncated negative residence moments]
\label{thm:v88-negative-residence}
For $0<\upsilon<\gamma_0$ define the actual density
\[
 q_{\upsilon,s}(t)\,dt
 =F_\#(\ell^{-\upsilon}\one_{\{0<\ell\le s\}}\bar\nu).
\]
It is finite at every count. Uniformly in all the physical parameters,
intervals, $m\ge2$ and $0<s<s_0$, at each fixed auxiliary band,
\begin{align}
 m^2\int_Jq_{\upsilon,s}(t)\,dt
 \le{}&C_\upsilon s^{\gamma_0-\upsilon}(|J|+B_*^{-1})\notag\\
 &+C_{B_*,\upsilon}(1+B_*|J|)m^3
 \min\{s^{1/2-\upsilon},\rho_{B_*}^{m(1-2\upsilon)/2}\}.
                                      \label{eq:v88-negative-finite}
\end{align}
Consequently, for every previously fixed $L,s>0$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v88-negative-ordered}
 \limsup_m\sup_{R,n,k}\sup_{|J|=L}m^2\int_Jq_{\upsilon,s}
                               \le C_\upsilon Ls^{\gamma_0-\upsilon}.
\end{equation}
The source with weight $\min(\ell,1)^{-\upsilon}$ is also finite
and has the corresponding fixed-width bound with a constant right
side. There is no positive lower residence cutoff in these statements.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At the source level,
\[
 \ell^{-\upsilon}\one_{\{\ell\le s\}}
 =s^{-\upsilon}\one_{\{\ell\le s\}}
       +\upsilon\int_0^s v^{-\upsilon-1}\one_{\{\ell\le v\}}\,dv.
\]
Use positive lower truncations first and then monotone convergence.
The leading term of \eqref{eq:v88-capped-residence} integrates to
$\gamma_0s^{\gamma_0-\upsilon}/(\gamma_0-\upsilon)$.
For $D>0$ the capped remainder obeys the exact identity
\begin{align}
 &s^{-\upsilon}\min(D,s^{1/2})
   +\upsilon\int_0^s v^{-\upsilon-1}\min(D,v^{1/2})\,dv\notag\\
 &\hspace{16mm}=\frac1{1-2\upsilon}
                     \min\{s^{1/2-\upsilon},D^{1-2\upsilon}\}.
                                      \label{eq:v88-residence-layer-cake}
\end{align}
Split at $D^2$ when that point is less than $s$; the boundary terms
cancel. Substitution of $D=\rho_{B_*}^{m/2}$ proves
\eqref{eq:v88-negative-finite}. An uncapped error independent of
$v$ would not be integrable here.

The physical cap alone also proves finite total mass for every
$\upsilon<1/2$. Coarea on compact regular charts and positive
exhaustion give absolute continuity for the exponents used above;
the excluded critical set has zero source area. For the last
assertion split at $s_0/2$: on its complement the weight is bounded,
and the unweighted complete source estimate applies.
Finally fix $L,s,B_*$, take the collision limsup, and only then
enlarge $B_*$. The complete layer cake has already been evaluated
at finite count. This proves \eqref{eq:v88-negative-ordered}.
\end{proof}
