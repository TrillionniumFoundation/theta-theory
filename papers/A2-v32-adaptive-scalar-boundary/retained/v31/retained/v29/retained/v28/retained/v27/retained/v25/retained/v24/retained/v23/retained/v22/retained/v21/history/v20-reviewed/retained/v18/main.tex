\begin{abstract}
We study near-onset collision laws with intrinsic endpoint-arclength
marks in periodic dispersing billiards. Two strictly active windows for
one return orientation determine the stationary two-flight action by an
explicit quotient of their endpoint densities. Diagonal Hessian identities
then recover both smooth boundary germs, their gap and the free area,
without an analyticity or reflection-symmetry assumption. The local
inverse is Lipschitz in a two-derivative density norm. On uniformly
analytic classes separated from Euclidean symmetry, a finite onset pilot
and endpoint histograms give whole-table reconstruction with a fixed
conditional exponent, independent of contact-jet order. Complete images
synchronize independently reflected channel observations; rank-two
lattice-cycle data determine the marked periods. No cross-channel
contact registration is supplied in this asymmetric case. We also retain
the explicit all-parity inverse from a finite collection of exact moment
germs and limits, registered rigidity for symmetric tables, physical
finite-jet realization, count-only finite and formal fibers, and the
smooth relative boundary theory. All finite preparations include failure
outcomes, and no finite-horizon hypothesis is imposed.
\end{abstract}
