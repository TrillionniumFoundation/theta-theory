\begin{abstract}
We study acquisition and whole-table certification for periodic dispersing
billiards from intrinsically marked endpoint laws. On a compact analytic
class with quantitative asymmetry and separation of obstacle shapes, a
fixed finite contact jet distinguishes all bounded-length oriented bridge
classes occurring in the same table. This gives a finite local rule for
recognizing a record at every translated occurrence, without a supplied
period lattice, complete-body catalogue or periodic-gate installation
oracle. Under explicitly stated certified local contact-probe access,
independent bounded-time launches produce a saturated record witness with
uniform positive arrival probability. The resulting two endpoint
histograms per record retain absolute normalization under laboratory-square
preparation. We propagate both finite-square bias and contact-registration
error through density differentiation, analytic continuation and rational
period recovery. The completion defect then yields simultaneous validity
at all stopping epochs. Launch attempts, local probe work and apparatus
range are accounted for separately. The theorem is local-probe-relative,
not discovery from an unmarked passive trajectory. The full preceding
moment, registered, relative-law and geometric-oracle results are retained.
\end{abstract}
