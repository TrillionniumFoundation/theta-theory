## 3. Device interface and resource classes

The device is the memoryless, input-consuming, classical-output channel

```text
M_E(rho)=sum_j tr(E_j rho)|j><j|,
sum_j E_j=I.
```

The tester may use an external reference, quantum memory, adaptive controls, and bounded public stopping. The operational distance `D_N` is the supremum of unhalved final-state trace distance under one common strategy for the two hypotheses.

The new product class is narrower:

- one probe–reference pair per call;
- pairs may differ and may depend on the known hypotheses;
- no quantum correlation between different pairs;
- no feedback into later inputs;
- arbitrary joint processing after all calls;
- public randomization allowed; and
- no restriction to scalar measurements on each completed output.

Thus the paper distinguishes:

```text
independent product acquisition
< arbitrary entangled parallel acquisition
< fully adaptive acquisition,
```

but proves a converse only for the first class and a lower/upper law for the last. No ordering equality for the middle class is proved.

The coherent linear lower uses:

- known base `E` and direction `H`;
- known scale `s`;
- a pair-dependent code and recovery;
- one logical qubit carried between calls;
- an external reference of dimension at most `2d` per call;
- ideal trusted controls; and
- a pair-dependent final Helstrom readout.

These are discrimination resources, not uncharged advice for the common learner.

## 6. Detailed proof audit

### 6.1 Reference-uniform channel norm

For

```text
D_R(X)=sum_j tr(R_jX)|j><j|,
```

the amplified output is block diagonal. A dual block test has components of operator norm at most one. Its adjoint image is a sum of `R_j tensor Y_j`, whose operator norm is bounded by the sum of the component operator norms. This controls the trace norm on every amplified trace-class input, including nonpositive inputs.

The proof therefore avoids an unjustified assertion that a Hermiticity-preserving map's diamond norm may always be optimized over states. When the tuple sum is zero, the map is trace annihilating. I find the argument correct.

### 6.2 Necessity of the one-sided cone

For `v` in the kernel of `E_j`, the scalar function

```text
v*E_j(s)v
```

is nonnegative on a right neighborhood and vanishes at zero. Its right derivative is nonnegative. Polarization on the missing-support subspace gives `Q_jH_jQ_j>=0`.

This is the standard tangent-cone condition for the PSD cone. The measurement tangent cone is its product intersection with the zero-sum normalization constraint.

### 6.3 Sufficiency by Schur complement

For nonzero support, the `P_j` block of

```text
E_j+sH_j+c s^2I
```

is bounded below by half the smallest positive support eigenvalue for small `s`. The cross block is `sP_jH_jQ_j`. The inverse support block is bounded above by twice the reciprocal support eigenvalue. Hence the Schur complement is bounded below by

```text
sJ_j+s^2(c-2||H_j||^2/lambda_j)Q_j.
```

A common large `c` makes it positive for all outcomes. For a zero effect, the condition is simply `H_j>=0`. Dividing by `1+kcs^2` preserves positivity and makes the tuple sum exactly the identity.

The proof is sound. For arbitrary real input, the coefficient `c` need not itself be rational; the phrase “rational normalized curve” should be explained as a rational formula in the parameter or restricted to represented rational input with a chosen rational majorant.

### 6.4 Lineality and range

The one-sided cone contains a direction and its negative exactly when every missing block vanishes. This proves the lineality statement.

In the complete kernel pairing, the tuple mean disappears because the tangent sum is zero. The independent missing-support parameters give

```text
sum_j tr(J_j Z_j).
```

Orthogonality for all `Z_j` forces every `J_j=0`. The remaining support-commutator calculation gives exactly `-2tr(A Gamma)`. Since the covariance is self-adjoint in finite dimension, the range is the orthogonal complement of the entire kernel. The range criterion follows.

No closed-range or infinite-dimensional assumption is hidden here.

### 6.5 Finite normalized factors

The zero tangent sum gives

```text
A*B+B*A=0,
```

so the stacked Gram matrix of `A+sB` is `I+s^2B*B`. The inverse root is positive and analytic near zero. Expanding

```text
R_s(E_j+sH_j+s^2B_j*B_j)R_s
```

in the displayed order gives the submitted remainder bound. No matrix is commuted through `R_s`.

For horizontal `B`, the normalized derivative identities imply `A(s)*A'(s)=0`. In a purified adaptive tester, differentiated call slots are mutually orthogonal after later common isometries are cancelled. The derivative norm is therefore `sqrt(N)b`, not `Nb`; converting pure-vector variation to unhalved trace variation gives the factor two.

The discarded dilation environment is not made available to the tester.

### 6.6 Range branch of the tube theorem

Triangle comparison through the horizontal surrogate gives

```text
2b sqrt(N)s +(Lambda+K_b)Ns^2.
```

The conversion to `C min(1,sqrt(N)s)` is valid:

- if `sqrt(N)s<=1`, then `Ns^2<=(sqrt(N)s)`;
- otherwise use the absolute trace-distance cap two.

The lower uses one fixed component eigenvector of `H_j`. The allowed remainder changes its scalar probability by at most `Lambda s^2`, so a first-order gap remains. The finite Bernoulli lemma gives the square-root law uniformly over the tube.

The theorem is universal over legal pairs satisfying the tube condition. If no such pair exists for a specified `Lambda`, the statement is vacuous. The cone realization supplies nonempty tubes for sufficiently large allowances but the main theorem does not quantify the minimal allowance.

### 6.7 Support-opening branch

A nonzero positive missing block has a positive quadratic form. The corresponding state lies in the kernel of the base effect, so the chosen label is impossible under `E`. Under every tube member its probability is at least `lambda s/2` after reducing the interval. The event of observing that label at least once has unhalved separation twice its probability under the second hypothesis.

The witness does not depend on the unknown second-order remainder. It establishes both product and adaptive lower bounds and needs neither reference memory nor recovery.

### 6.8 Coherent tangential branch

When all missing blocks vanish and `Gamma` is outside the support span, the inherited code is correctable for the base measurement. The first-order factor calculation gives the corrected derivative `-i[Gamma_L,·]`. This calculation depends only on `E,H` and the completed recovery identities.

The tube bound supplies the actual logical-channel remainder `Lambda s^2`. The ideal logical exponential contributes at most `2||Gamma||^2s^2`. Thus the one-cycle comparison is uniform over the tube.

With logical gap `Delta>0`, choose

```text
m=min{N,floor(1/(s Delta))}.
```

On the stated interval the floor is at least two, and `x=msDelta` lies between one half of the truncated scale and one. The sine lower and accumulated quadratic loss leave a positive constant fraction of `min(1,NsDelta)`. The final readout may be chosen for the ideal logical pair and is independent of the allowed remainder.

### 6.9 Product upper

For all-missing-block-zero directions, canonical first-order factors exist but need not be horizontal. Their normalized surrogate has stacked factor displacement at most `3bs/2`. This supplies one common purification of the two one-call outputs and hence a Bures upper.

The surrogate-to-`F` channel error is `O(s^2)` in unhalved trace norm, so its Bures distance is `O(s)`. After the Bures triangle inequality,

```text
1-f_r <= a^2s^2/2
```

for every probe–reference state. For independent acquisitions, root fidelity multiplies. Therefore

```text
1-prod f_r^2 <= N a^2s^2,
```

and the unhalved trace distance is at most `2a sqrt(N)s`. Arbitrary final processing contracts trace norm. Public randomization is a direct-sum convex mixture and satisfies the same bound.

This argument is sound for independent product acquisition. It says nothing about a globally entangled parallel input.

### 6.10 Higher-order and mixed rates

The power-jet corollary merely substitutes `s=t^q`; it does not differentiate an inverse parameterization. Positivity of the family forces `H` into the one-sided cone after compression and division by `t^q`.

The `O(t^(2q))` remainder is a real hypothesis. Intermediate coefficients can change the law. The scalar example proves this by adding support leakage at order `b<2a`. The full classical iid record is Blackwell-more-informative than every stopped or processed experiment because the input dimension is one. The lower combines two coarse events, and the upper splits on support leakage and then uses a Bernoulli fidelity estimate.

The result is correct but does not constitute a general higher-jet classification.
