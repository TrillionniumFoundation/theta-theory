# Revision-26 abstract (unchanged historical text)

```tex
We study the joint lattice displacement, collision count and flight time
of genuine returns to a moving section in a triangular periodic Lorentz
gas, uniformly for radii in $[0.45,0.47]$. Bounded collision compensation
and exact stopping give Gaussian and functional laws, all fixed marked
moments and quantitative convergence of the actual covariances.
Measurable phase holonomy and finite physical covers prove joint
nondegeneracy. Fixed-order spectral jets and damped removal of smoothing
then give integrated Gaussian comparisons at a prescribed return count,
with a complex insertion at any one actual return.

We establish a weighted-volume criterion that extends these comparisons
to one nonrectangular region in all four coordinate directions, while
retaining the preceding isotropic and anisotropic regions and their
central rate. A finite dyadic cutoff controls the full scale sum without
growing expansion orders. For the raw density, finite-count
power--logarithm extraction gives an absolutely invertible residual.
An exact cancellation between the extracted-edge and residual changes
yields a quantitative transport theorem for the complete signed raw
correction under changes of cutoff. The comparison uses the same law,
weight and extraction throughout. The full complementary integral,
long-time derivative budget, absolute local-edge estimates and relative
physical-event comparison remain distinct requirements of the raw
mixed-density local limit problem.
```
