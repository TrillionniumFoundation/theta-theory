## Boundaries

Protection means exponentially relaxed incidence/section/clearance margins with fixed prefactor epsilon, not a uniform positive interior-incidence assumption. The results do not give an asymptotic threshold uniform as epsilon decreases with m. No complete bound for the omitted critical or boundary source, the remaining noncritical inverse, the full residual derivative budget, or a growing-band spectral family is asserted.

The full raw four-coordinate LLT, complete signed correction estimate, pointwise-roof bridge and unconditional residue triviality remain unproved in this revision. The existing arithmetic transition kernel and exact fixed-radius residue factor are retained. Existing completed Gaussian, physical singleton, compact-family, occupation, interval, bridge, posterior, full-source reconstruction and jump--cusp results remain in the full compiled paper.
