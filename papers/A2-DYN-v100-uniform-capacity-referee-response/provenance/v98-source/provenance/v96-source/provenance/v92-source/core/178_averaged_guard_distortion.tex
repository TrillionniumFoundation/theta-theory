\section{A complete-source estimate for guard distortion}
\label{sec:v78-averaged}
The preceding pointwise control of the guard can be used at the
collision scale without assuming a pointwise bound for the unweighted
roof density. The essential step is to average the translated target
before applying the fixed-width concentration estimate.

Fix one exact event and write $p(t)=p_{n,R}(k,m,t)$ for its
unweighted roof density. Let $S_h(t)$ be the surviving part of its
fiber under the explicit forward field, $J_h$ the area Jacobian
relative to the original collision source, and $E_h^\varepsilon(t)$
the original weighted mass killed before time $h$. Define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v78-geometric-defect}
 G_h^\varepsilon(t)=E_h^\varepsilon(t)
       +\int_{S_h(t)}W^\varepsilon(x)[1-J_h(x)]_+\,d\mu_t(x),
 \qquad \overline G_r^\varepsilon(t)=\frac1r\int_0^rG_h^\varepsilon(t)\,dh.
\end{equation}
The first term contains every true exit. The second contains the
loss of physical coarea volume, not the change of the guard.
In particular $0\le G_h^\varepsilon(t)\le b^\varepsilon(t)$.
These quantities are defined by the full physical source, not by
summing estimates of word cardinalities.

\begin{theorem}[Averaged separation of geometry and guard]
\label{thm:v78-separation}
For each fixed $r>0$ and almost every $t$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v78-averaged-persistence}
 b^\varepsilon(t)\le 2A_rb^\varepsilon(t)
       +\overline G_r^\varepsilon(t)
       +2K_\Theta\varepsilon^{-1}\sqrt r\,A_rp(t).
\end{equation}
The guard contribution on the right has the unconditional ordered
bound, relative to Input statement~\ref{input:v76-analytic},
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v78-guard-closure}
 \limsup_m\sup_{R,n,k}m^2\left\|
    2K_\Theta\varepsilon^{-1}\sqrt r\,A_rp\right\|_\infty
       \le C_\Theta\varepsilon^{-1}\sqrt r.
\end{equation}
All incidences, clearances, terminal tests and exact labels remain
present in this statement. No estimate of $m^2\|p\|_\infty$ is used.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The stopped physical graph couples source weight
$\min\{W(x),W(\Phi_hx)J_h(x)\}$ to the target fiber. For any
$w,w'\in[0,1]$ and $J\ge0$,
\[
 [w-w'J]_+\le w[1-J]_++J|w-w'|.
\]
Apply this identity on survivors and use
Corollary~\ref{cor:v78-guard}. Coarea covariance gives
\[
 \int_{S_h(t)}J_h(x)\,d\mu_t(x)
       =\mu_{t+h}(\Phi_hS_h(t))\le p(t+h).
\]
Here $\mu$ is the \emph{unweighted} coarea measure on the exact
event; hence its full mass is precisely $p$. The Jacobian is in
the target direction in this formula. Target domination of the
subcoupling now yields
\[
 b^\varepsilon(t)\le b^\varepsilon(t+h)+G_h^\varepsilon(t)
            +K_\Theta\varepsilon^{-1}\sqrt h\,p(t+h)
\]
for almost every $(t,h)$. Average over $0<h<r$, bound
$\sqrt h\le\sqrt r$, and bound each forward average by twice the
centered average of its nonnegative density. This proves
\eqref{eq:v78-averaged-persistence}.

The unweighted fixed-width estimate is already contained in the
analytic input as stated. Indeed with auxiliary guard parameter
one, the first incidence factor is $\Theta(c_0)=0$, so that
$H^1=0$, $W^1=1$, and $b^1=p$. Thus
\[
 \limsup_m\sup_{R,n,k}m^2\|A_rp\|_\infty\le C
\]
for each fixed $r$, with the same constant independent of that
previously fixed width. This application does not change the guard
$W^\varepsilon$ in the source being proved; it supplies only an
upper bound for its translated target. Substitution proves
\eqref{eq:v78-guard-closure}. Equivalently one may use the retained
complete-source fixed-width concentration theorem with weight one.
The averaging must precede the collision limit; replacing $A_rp$
by $p$ would be an unproved step.
\end{proof}

Set
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v78-ordered-geometry}
 \mathcal G_{\varepsilon,M}(r)=
   \limsup_m\sup_{R,n,k}m^2
   \esssup_{t\in\mathcal C_{m,R,n,k}(M)}
                      \overline G_r^\varepsilon(t).
\end{equation}
\begin{theorem}[The original endpoint with the guard term estimated]
\label{thm:v78-endpoint}
Under Input statement~\ref{input:v76-analytic}, for all sufficiently
large fixed $B$ and fixed $r>0$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v78-endpoint-bound}
 \mathcal E_M\le C_MB^{-1/192}
       +\mathcal G_{\varepsilon(B),M}(r)
       +C_\Theta\varepsilon(B)^{-1}\sqrt r.
\end{equation}
In particular the original pointwise assertion follows from
$\mathcal G_{\varepsilon,M}(r)\le A_M\varepsilon^{-q}r^\beta$
for finite $q\ge0$ and $\beta>0$. More precisely it is equivalent,
under the same analytic input, to
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v78-equivalent-geometry}
 \lim_{B\to\infty}\inf_{0<r<r_0}
   \left\{\mathcal G_{\varepsilon(B),M}(r)
                +\varepsilon(B)^{-1}\sqrt r\right\}=0
 \quad\hbox{for every fixed }r_0>0.
\end{equation}
Neither the geometric bound nor \eqref{eq:v78-equivalent-geometry}
is asserted as proved here. Unlike the guard contribution, these
terms still contain true exits and physical volume distortion.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Combine \eqref{eq:v78-averaged-persistence} with the positive
remainder, and take the collision limsup only after fixing
$B$, $\varepsilon(B)$, $r$ and the auxiliary spectral bands.
The fixed-width bad-source term is at most
$2C\varepsilon(B)^{1/16}=O(B^{-1/192})$.
Equation~\eqref{eq:v78-guard-closure} proves
\eqref{eq:v78-endpoint-bound}. Under the power bound choose
$r=c\varepsilon(B)^\gamma$, with
$\gamma>\max\{2,q/\beta\}$ and fixed sufficiently small $c>0$.
Both terms then vanish. The auxiliary spectral band is enlarged
after the collision limit and $B$ last.

Condition~\eqref{eq:v78-equivalent-geometry} is sufficient by
\eqref{eq:v78-endpoint-bound}; approximate the infimum with a
positive width fixed for each $B$ before taking the collision limit.
Conversely, if the original endpoint holds, the positive remainder
implies
\[
 \limsup_m\sup_{R,n,k}m^2
       \esssup_{\mathcal C(M)}b^{\varepsilon(B)}
                         \le C_MB^{-1/192}.
\]
Since $\overline G_r^\varepsilon\le b^\varepsilon$ for every $r$,
the same bound holds for $\mathcal G$ independently of $r$.
Taking the infimum over $r$ and then $B\to\infty$ proves necessity.
No collision-dependent Fourier band or density representative is
selected by this argument.
\end{proof}

The original angular recoupling remains available and can only
improve its own stopped-flow comparison. The estimate just proved
isolates and controls one complete contribution without claiming
that the angular current, the true boundary flux or the full optical
volume distortion has thereby been bounded. Scalar closure must
still precede every same-roof path normalization and every likelihood
conclusion, as in the retained path remainder theorem.
