The support-component rank theorem adds an all-stochastic lower bound that remains valid for generators outside the residual hull, plus a finite sufficient condition under which a compatible residual machine matches it. The binary-query theorem adds an explicit chain of enclosing simplices, not only a terminal positive factorization. Its strong-signal converse is a corner-cap argument, whose elementary convex mechanism is not claimed as new. The mathematical claims are the stated simultaneous classification and exact streaming regimes, with their full quantifiers.
