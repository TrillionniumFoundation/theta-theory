# Primary-source comparison — Revision 70

This is an author-side theorem comparison, not independent priority clearance. The predecessor audit is retained in predecessor-v68-audit/LITERATURE_AUDIT.md. Retrieved primary pages were checked on 3 October 2026.

## Direct replacer antecedent required by r45

Tom Cooney, Milán Mosonyi and Mark M. Wilde, *Strong converse exponents for a quantum channel discrimination problem and quantum-feedback-assisted communication*, arXiv:1408.3373v3, 26 February 2016. Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.3373 ; PDF https://arxiv.org/pdf/1408.3373 . The p. 5 two-replacer discussion and Sections 4.1–4.2 were inspected.

Their two-replacer reduction is a direct antecedent for using an input-erasing channel as a source of copies of a state. Their main theorems treat asymptotic binary discrimination and strong-converse exponents for a channel versus a replacer. We do not infer exact finite-use nonadaptive optimality for an arbitrary non-replacer/replacer pair. The retained finite-use equality for two preparation instruments and the new pointwise covering-centre retraction are different assertions. The latter uses a fixed maximally mixed-input test of an arbitrary centre and compares it against every preparation target; it also concerns rational centres and the covering cardinality, rather than error exponents.

## Coherent amplification

A. Acín, *Statistical Distinguishability between Unitary Operations*, Physical Review Letters 87 (2001), 177901. DOI https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.87.177901 . The primary publisher abstract identifies finite-use unitary discrimination and the qubit fidelity connection.

Runyao Duan, Yuan Feng and Mingsheng Ying, *Entanglement is Not Necessary for Perfect Discrimination between Unitary Operations*, Physical Review Letters 98 (2007), 100503. DOI https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.98.100503 . The primary publisher abstract identifies sequential perfect discrimination with interleaved known controls.

Unitary amplification, pair-dependent controls, and the real dimension d^2-1 are established antecedents, not inventions of this revision. The new proof states its own elementary finite bound with constants because the covering argument requires a uniform small-precision modulus and bounded stopping. The contribution claimed for review is the combination of this coherent modulus with rank-stratified preparation coding in one non-input-erasing family, a converse against arbitrary legal memoryless centres, and a legal finite rational atlas/codec with the matching joint coefficient u+v/2. It is not a new general discrimination protocol or a classification of all noisy unitary models.

## Inherited boundaries retained

The fixed-margin classical-programme estimate retains the metrological attribution to Demkowicz-Dobrzański–Kołodyński–Guţă. Quantum population compression, adaptive binary discrimination, channel tomography, and physical classical simulation remain separate from supplied matrix descriptions. The earlier positive realization, automata, entropy–transport, nonbacktracking/LPS and tail-capacity comparisons remain in the preserved audits and complete research edition. Their presence is provenance, not a claim to have independently eliminated every equivalent theorem formulation.
