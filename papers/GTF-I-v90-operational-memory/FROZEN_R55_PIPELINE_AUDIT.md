## 3. Device interface and resource model

The device is the memoryless classical-output channel

```text
M_E(rho)=sum_j tr(E_j rho)|j><j|,
sum_j E_j=I.
```

The input is consumed and the device returns no residual quantum system. A tester may use an external reference, retain memory, adapt controls to earlier labels, and stop at a public bounded stopping time no larger than `N`. The same control rule is used under both hypotheses.

The operational loss `D_N` is unhalved final-state trace norm, so `0<=D_N<=2`.

For the new linear lower:

- the base measurement, direction, target angle, and call number are known;
- the code and recovery may depend on the known pair;
- the recovery acts only on the available classical label and retained reference;
- a logical qubit is carried between calls;
- the per-call reference dimension is at most `2d`;
- ideal controls give the existential lower;
- the implemented-control theorem requires supplied deterministic diamond-error certificates;
- no general circuit synthesis or hardware execution is claimed.

The resource ledger correctly separates pair information, future calls, references, payload, public dictionaries, exact arithmetic, control synthesis, implementation error, and physical execution.

## 5. Claim-by-claim audit

### 5.1 Finite Bernoulli lemma

The proof uses the bounded statistic `exp(iu sum X_r)`. For `u=m^{-1/2}`, the expectation is `z(p)^m`. The modulus lower follows from

```text
|z(a)|^2 >= 1-1/(4m),
```

and Bernoulli's inequality. The argument derivative is exactly `sin(u)/|z(a)|^2`, giving a phase gap proportional to `sqrt(m)|p-q|`. The finite truncation handles the saturated regime, and one observation handles large `|p-q|`.

No asymptotic normal approximation is used. The lemma is valid at endpoints.

### 5.2 First-order realizability

The formula

```text
A_j^*B_j+B_j^*A_j
 = P_jH_j+H_jP_j-P_jH_jP_j
 = H_j
```

uses exactly `Q_jH_jQ_j=0`. Summing `A_j^*B_j` differs from half the commutator sum by `(1/2)sum_jH_j`, which vanishes. Thus the stacked normalization derivative is anti-Hermitian and the inverse-square-root normalization is legitimate.

This establishes sufficiency for a legal analytic curve. It does not assert that the given curve's canonical square roots are differentiable.

### 5.3 Horizontal identities

With `S=B^*B`, direct differentiation yields

```text
A'(t)=(B-tAS)(I+t^2S)^(-3/2),
A(t)^*A'(t)=0,
A'(t)^*A'(t)=S(I+t^2S)^(-2).
```

The second-derivative bound `7b^2` follows for `|t|b<=1`. No commutation between `A`, `B`, and `S` is assumed beyond the displayed right multiplication by functions of `S`.

### 5.4 Adaptive derivative cancellation

A Stinespring isometry is formed by retaining an environment copy of the classical label and Kraus row. Its derivative norms equal the stacked factor derivative norms. In the derivative of a purified adaptive tester, cross terms from different call slots vanish because the later differentiated slot contains `W^*W'=0` after common later isometries are cancelled.

This gives a `sqrt(N)` rather than `N` derivative bound in the horizontal branch.

### 5.5 Actual-versus-surrogate comparison

For a Hermitian zero-sum tuple `R`, the measurement-channel derivative is trace-annihilating and Hermiticity preserving. On a state with reference, each classical output block has trace norm at most `||R_j||op`; summing gives the diamond upper.

The actual curve has second derivative bounded by the stated effect-curvature quantity `L`. The surrogate Stinespring second derivative gives the `16b^2` channel bound. Taylor's integral remainder and hybrid telescoping produce the finite `Nt^2` term.

The notation would be more transparent if `L` were repeated in every theorem or lemma that uses it, but the mathematical dependency is sound.

### 5.6 Logical derivative

The recovery Kraus operators satisfy

```text
R_l L_mu J=c_lmu I.
```

Taking adjoints and summing with recovery completeness gives

```text
sum_l,mu conjugate(c_lmu)R_l dot(L_mu)J
 =J^*sum_mu L_mu^*dot(L_mu)J.
```

The first-order factor choice makes the right side `-iGamma_L`. The adjoint term gives the commutator derivative. The recovery is a genuine globally completed CPTP channel before differentiation.

### 5.7 Finite-angle integer choice

On the fixed angle interval, the floor is at least two. With

```text
x=m|t|Delta,
```

one has

```text
(1/2)min(1,N|t|Delta) <= x <=1.
```

The sine term is at least `2x/pi`, while the curvature loss is at most `x/(2pi)`. This leaves a positive constant times the truncated linear scale. The estimates are symmetric in the sign of `t`.

### 5.8 Control budget

The curvature error is paid on the nonzero-angle ideal channel. Implementation changes under each hypothesis are bounded by `e_0+e_f+sum nu_r`, so comparison of the two implemented laws pays twice that quantity. The sufficient conditions leave at least `z/(2pi)` separation.

Encoding and recovery errors may be added around the unchanged unknown measurement by contraction. This is a worst-case channel statement, not an average calibration statement.
