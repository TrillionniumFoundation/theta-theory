\begin{abstract}
We connect a counted mechanical preparation, a physically implemented
observation channel, exact parameter inference, and attained adaptive control
within one finite-budget experiment. Parameter-independent ambient placement
cartridges retain both unsuccessful insertion and censored reports. Whole
collision-tube integration gives a positive cubic radius likelihood for a
specified coordinate detector. A positive Bernstein state has $3n+1$
coefficients after $n$ trials, with a matching sharp $3n$ continuous-state
lower bound. We retain the complete operational closure, boundary-gate,
fixed-controller response, and corrected foundation proofs, and establish
three further levels of structure. A coefficient Gram criterion is necessary
and sufficient for a full-dimensional attainable polynomial failure family;
an explicit gate right inverse quantifies its robustness and gives the
general sharp intrinsic dimension $qn$. The control problem reduces to a
compact moment body in $\mathbb R^5$, admits optimal Borel endpoint gates,
and has at most two collision acceptance arcs on the last cartridge under
tagwise rewards. Explicit lookup errors quantify finite implementation.
Positive normalized Bernstein surrogates implement general smooth mark
families with degree--regret bounds while keeping the mechanical flag masses
exactly. Finally, an analytic reduction and exact rational interval
certificate establish a strictly positive adaptive advantage over every
nonadaptive Borel-gate controller; the gap remains greater than $11/10000$
for a nonzero-amplitude cubic mode family and a full-support prior. The
likelihood algebra is a designed observation property, not a consequence of
chaos alone. The earlier whole-equilibrium response theorem remains in the
preserved foundation companion, and no long-orbit assertion is inferred
from the short physical collision window.
\end{abstract}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} deterministic experiments; Lorentz gas; realizability;
positive polynomial likelihood; adaptive censoring; Bayesian control;
Bernstein representation; mechanical response; certified adaptive advantage.
