\section{Complete operational closure statements}\label{app:operational}
The following two statements collect the full circular-detector framework.
They also distinguish its arbitrary continuous parameter-dependent terminal
payoffs from the observable-future decision quotient of the main theorem.
All proofs and component statements remain in the body of the paper.

\begin{theorem}[Operational positive-polynomial closure]\label{thm:main}
Fix $I=[9/20,47/100]$, the compact mode family $\cA$ of
Section~\ref{sec:scope}, a gate bound $0\le\eta<1/2$, a probability prior
$\pi_0$ on $I$, and an integer budget $N$. For the apparatus of
Section~\ref{sec:lab}:
\begin{enumerate}
\item The raw readout has the positive cubic-polynomial kernel
\eqref{eq:raw-kernel}, including placement failure. The comparator realizes
exactly the family \eqref{eq:gated-kernel}, with the same radius-independent
gate at every radius.
\item At every positive-likelihood history after $n\le N$ cartridges the
full radius likelihood, up to a radius-independent scalar, is a positive
polynomial of degree at most $3n$. It has $3n+1$ positive normalized
Bernstein coefficients and the positive convolution update
\eqref{eq:bern-product}. A nonzero-amplitude mode makes $3n$ a sharp lower
bound on the free coordinates of a continuous exact likelihood state.
Both types of failure are included.
\item This state and the remaining budget suffice for the observable-reward
and arbitrary continuous positive terminal-payoff problem of
Section~\ref{sec:control}. Optimization over all Borel gates reduces to the
compact body in $\mathbb R^5$ in \eqref{eq:gate-body}, with support
\eqref{eq:support}. Every body point has a measurable comparator realization.
The recursion \eqref{eq:bellman} attains its optimum with a Borel feedback rule.
\item Every fixed admissible adaptive controller has a path law strongly
$C^r$ in signed-measure variation for every finite $r$, with the bounds of
Theorem~\ref{thm:response}. Its density is a polynomial of degree at most
$3N$; derivatives above that degree vanish. The same readout channel is
used for likelihood, failures, control and response.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Proposition~\ref{prop:raw} proves the kernel and uniform bounds.
Theorem~\ref{thm:attainable} proves common-gate necessity, sufficiency and
adaptive pasting. Theorems~\ref{thm:filter}, \ref{thm:general} and
\ref{thm:dimension} prove the exact positive filter and sharp dimension.
Theorem~\ref{thm:control} proves reduction, realization and attainment;
Theorem~\ref{thm:boundary} includes the full gate interval $[0,1]$ with
zero-evidence weighted continuations. Theorem~\ref{thm:response} proves
fixed-controller response. These proofs include their apparatus estimates.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Structural, policy and robust performance closure]\label{thm:strengthening}
The full circular apparatus additionally has the following properties.
\begin{enumerate}
\item In a general positive polynomial experiment, positive definiteness
of the coefficient Gram matrix is equivalent to an open attainable failure
family. Its explicit gate right inverse gives an attainable ball and
same-degree perturbation margins. For full polynomial degree $q$ this
gives sharp intrinsic likelihood dimension $qn$.
\item Every finite-budget optimum has a Borel endpoint-gate realization.
At the last cartridge, tagwise accepted rewards give a high-acceptance
collision set of at most two arcs, even for a compact infinite terminal
decision space. Under angular Lipschitz bounds the explicit endpoint-lookup
value error is $O(Ne^{N|\lambda|B}/J)$ for $J$ intervals per circle.
Theorem~\ref{thm:all-stage-arcs} extends the arc count to every finite stage.
\item Every positive smooth normalized collision-mark family has a
positive normalized implementable Bernstein surrogate. It preserves the
physical placement and hit masses exactly. At fixed budget its sufficient
policy-regret rates are $O(m^{-1})$ under a uniform second-derivative bound
and $O(m^{-1/2})$ under a Lipschitz bound, with the explicit budget and
payoff-oscillation factors below.
\item For the two-cartridge instance of Section~\ref{sec:advantage}, with
the full Borel-gate class, nonzero-amplitude modes and the specified
full-support prior, adaptive control exceeds every nonadaptive control by
more than $11/10000$, including independently randomized precommitted
commands. This is the retained acceptance-cost comparison, distinct from
Theorem~\ref{thm:zero-cost-gain}.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Theorems~\ref{thm:rank-criterion} and \ref{thm:q-dimension} prove the first
claim. Theorems~\ref{thm:endpoint}, \ref{thm:two-arcs} and
\ref{thm:lookup-rate} prove the second, with its full-horizon extension in
Theorem~\ref{thm:all-stage-arcs}. Theorems~\ref{thm:model-stability} and
\ref{thm:smooth-surrogate} prove the third. Theorem~\ref{thm:adaptive-advantage}
proves the fourth by an analytic reduction of the entire nominal comparator
class, exact rational enclosure, and uniform complete-policy transfer.
\end{proof}
