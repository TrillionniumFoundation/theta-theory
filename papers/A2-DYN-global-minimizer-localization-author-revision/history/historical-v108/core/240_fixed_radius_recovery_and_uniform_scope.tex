\section{Unconditional fixed-radius recovery and the uniform endpoint}
\label{sec:v99-recovery}
The preceding comparison discharges the spectral-capacity hypothesis
for each fixed physical radius. We state the resulting source estimates
with that quantifier visible. For a nonnegative family let
\[
 \mathcal H_{M,R}(f)=\limsup_m\sup_{n,k}m^2
       \operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{t\in\mathcal C_{m,R,n,k}(M)}f(t).
\]
It differs from $\mathcal H_M$ by placing the radius outside the
collision limsup.

\begin{theorem}[Uncut recovery with no capacity hypothesis at fixed radius]
\label{thm:v99-fixed-recovery}
For every fixed $R\in I$, every fixed interval length $L>0$, and
$0<b<b_0$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v99-fixed-insertion}
 \limsup_m\sup_{n,k}\sup_{|J|=L}m^2\int_J w_b
                  \le CLb^{1/128},\qquad
 \limsup_m\sup_{n,k}\sup_{|J|=L}m^2\int_J v^0\le CL.
\end{equation}
The leading constant is independent of $R$; the collision count
needed for a prescribed error is not asserted uniform in $R$.
For the disjoint residuals of module 236 and for every original Borel
short-segment selector $H$ of total residence at most $\delta$,
\begin{align}
 \mathcal H_{M,R}(\mathsf C^\varepsilon)&\le C(qr)^{257/256},
                                      \label{eq:v99-fixed-critical}\\
 \limsup_m\sup_{n,k}m^2
       \|R_H^\varepsilon-T_H^\varepsilon\|_\infty
                      &\le C(1+\varepsilon^{-1})\delta.
                                      \label{eq:v99-fixed-paired}
\end{align}
In particular \eqref{eq:v99-fixed-paired} bounds the entire selected
short source with a grazing physical endpoint, without any positive
initial-gradient or internal-incidence cutoff.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $K_R=\sup_m\mathcal K_m(R)<\infty$. The finite-count estimate
\eqref{eq:v98-uncut-insertion}, before taking any limit, has remainder
at most
\[
 C C_{B_*}^{2/3}(1+B_*L)^{2/3}
                     m^2K_R^{1/3}\theta_{B_*}^{2m/3}.
\]
It vanishes for each fixed $R,L,B_*$. Enlarge $B_*$ only after the
collision limit. The leading constants in the inherited local source
estimate and the new geometric comparison are uniform, proving
\eqref{eq:v99-fixed-insertion}. Adding $z\ge b_0$ uses the retained
positive-cutoff estimate, not a new zero-cutoff exchange.

The finite-count critical certificate
\eqref{eq:v98-critical-certificate} is at the target roof, and so
\eqref{eq:v99-fixed-insertion} on its interval of length $d=2qr$
proves \eqref{eq:v99-fixed-critical}. The two-ended finite-count
comparison \eqref{eq:v98-paired-finite}, with $L=2\delta$, proves
\eqref{eq:v99-fixed-paired}. Neither proof differentiates a Borel
target selector. At a grazing physical endpoint one original weighted
trace is zero, so its same-segment minimum is zero. A backward critical
trace is not deleted by that argument.
\end{proof}

For one fixed $R$ let $\mathcal E_{M,R}$ denote the collision limsup
of the original scalar error. With the retained schedule
$q=1/8$, $r=c\varepsilon(B)^4$, $a=\sqrt{qr}$ and $\delta=6qr$,
the preceding theorem gives, without (SC),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v99-fixed-endpoint}
 \mathcal E_{M,R}\le\mathcal H_{M,R}(T_{\mathsf S}^{\varepsilon(B)})
   +C_MB^{-1/192}+C(qr)^{257/256}
                       +C(1+\varepsilon(B)^{-1})qr.
\end{equation}
This follows by the original positive scalar decomposition and the
disjoint residual partition before their suprema are taken. The
positive paired trace on short nongrazing segments has not been
proved small, even at a fixed radius; \eqref{eq:v99-fixed-endpoint}
is not a claim of pointwise scalar closure.

For the original radius-uniform target, (BC) on an unbounded sequence
of fixed auxiliary bands implies the same displayed bound with
$\mathcal E_M$ and $\mathcal H_M$. This is
Theorem~\ref{thm:v98-endpoint} with its capacity condition supplied
by Theorem~\ref{thm:v99-block}. There are still two separate
family-uniform tasks: certify this pressure-growth margin, and prove
decay of the positive same-segment trace. The new comparison proves
the first at every fixed radius but does not turn that assertion into
$\limsup_m\sup_R$ by compactness alone. Nor does it identify the
paired minimum with the Jordan positive part of a projected current.

The original exact labels, guard, section and arithmetic reference
remain those of \eqref{eq:v76-target}. All original unconditional
finite-roof-norm and lower pointwise conclusions are retained; the
unrestricted upper pointwise half, and the downstream conclusions
requiring it, are not promoted. No zero arithmetic class is normalized.
