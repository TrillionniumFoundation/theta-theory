\section{A pointwise estimate of the positive paired part}
\label{sec:v102-transverse-height}
Write $\mathsf T_\Delta^\varepsilon$ for the complete paired source
on $0<\ell<\Delta$, with no target selection beyond the original
exact event. All incoming endpoint types are included. The preceding
uncut trace comparison and source estimates imply
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v102-paired-average}
 \limsup_m\sup_{R,n,k}m^2
       \|A_r\mathsf T_\Delta^\varepsilon\|_\infty
       \le C\{\varepsilon^{1/16}+(1+\varepsilon^{-1})\Delta\}
 \quad(r,\Delta,\varepsilon>0\text{ fixed}).
\end{equation}
The constant is independent of these previously fixed widths.
Indeed $\mathsf T_\Delta^\varepsilon\le b^\varepsilon+
C(1+\varepsilon^{-1})\int_{t-\Delta}^{t+\Delta}v^0(u)\,du$.
Apply the fixed-width bounds to $A_rb^\varepsilon$ and $A_rv^0$;
translation invariance of the latter supremum pays the convolution
with the factor $2\Delta$. This argument uses neither a pointwise
bound on $v^0$ nor a boundary spectral insertion.

Fix $0<\delta<\Delta$ and use $\Theta^\perp$ on
\[
 \mathcal D_\Delta^\varepsilon
  =\{0<\ell<\Delta,\ c^\varepsilon>0,\ v\ne0\}
\]
inside the regular pairing chambers. Stop at the boundary of this
comparison domain or at an actual loss of chamber regularity.
The original trace outside that domain is retained, not discarded.
For an interior starting pair $y$ define the capped transverse clock
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v102-clock}
 k_{r,\Delta}^\perp(y)
  =\frac1r\int_{-r}^r
       \one_{\{\Theta_h^\perp y\text{ exists}\}}
                        \min\{1,J_h^\perp(y)\}\,dh.
\end{equation}
It is zero on all other starting pairs. Its range is $[0,2]$ and
it is Borel. The trace weight already contains the original guard
at both endpoints; there is no extra guard mismatch in this clock.

For a Borel selector $H$ supported on $\ell\le\delta$ and
$0<q_\perp\le1$, let $T_{H,\mathrm{adm}}^\varepsilon$ and
$T_{H,\mathrm{fail}}^\varepsilon$ be its paired source restricted,
respectively, to $k_{r,\Delta}^\perp\ge q_\perp$ and
$k_{r,\Delta}^\perp<q_\perp$. Their nonnegative sum is exactly
$T_H^\varepsilon$, including every nonregular or zero-speed pair.

\begin{theorem}[Complete transverse-clock trace estimate]
\label{thm:v102-admitted-height}
At every finite count and almost every target roof,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v102-admitted-finite}
 T_{H,\mathrm{adm}}^\varepsilon(t)
       \le \frac{1}{q_\perp r}
              \int_{t-r}^{t+r}\mathsf T_\Delta^\varepsilon(u)\,du.
\end{equation}
Consequently, uniformly in the original radius and exact labels,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v102-admitted-height}
 \mathcal H_M(T_{H,\mathrm{adm}}^\varepsilon)
 \le \frac C{q_\perp}
          \{\varepsilon^{1/16}+(1+\varepsilon^{-1})\Delta\}.
\end{equation}
This is an essential-height estimate for the positive common trace
itself. It imposes no positive lower original residence, gradient
or internal incidence cutoff on its admitted part. No estimate of
an outgoing-minus-incoming difference is substituted for it.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let $\pi_t^\varepsilon$ be the paired kernels supplied by
Theorem~\ref{thm:v102-pair-coarea}. On admitted starting pairs,
$q_\perp\le k_{r,\Delta}^\perp$. Integrate this inequality with
the target selector held at $t$. For each fixed comparison time,
\[
 \int\one_H\one_{\mathrm{adm}}
 \one_{\mathrm{survives}\ h}\min(1,J_h^\perp)\,d\pi_t^\varepsilon
 \le\int\one_H\one_{\mathrm{adm}}
 \one_{\mathrm{survives}\ h}J_h^\perp\,d\pi_t^\varepsilon
 \le\mathsf T_\Delta^\varepsilon(t+h).
\]
The last inequality is target-direction coarea covariance on the
one-to-one graph. It is valid after all physical sheets are summed.
The transported target need not still satisfy $H$; it does belong
to the identical exact event and has total residence below $\Delta$.
Thus only the selector is enlarged in a positive upper comparison,
not the displacement, occupation, collision count or section tests.
Integrate over $-r<h<r$ and divide by $q_\perp r$.
Joint almost-everywhere covariance and Tonelli prove
\eqref{eq:v102-admitted-finite} without selecting a fiber version
separately at every time. Equation~\eqref{eq:v102-paired-average}
then proves \eqref{eq:v102-admitted-height}, with all parameters
fixed before the collision limit and auxiliary bands enlarged only
after it.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[What has and has not been averaged]
The left side of \eqref{eq:v102-admitted-finite} is at the original
target roof; only its upper comparison is averaged. The clock
changes angular direction while preserving relative position between
the same two physical ends. It is therefore different from the
original radial clock, whose trajectory can terminate inside a short
segment. The Jacobian is that of the positive paired measure, not
that of the original volume with the minimum suppressed. No word,
chamber-count or transverse-overlap constant is inserted. The
unadmitted part is a genuine positive remainder and is not asserted
small by this theorem.
\end{remark}
