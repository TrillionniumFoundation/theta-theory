\begin{abstract}
This complete research edition contains the structural and quantitative
stochastic-realization theory and all retained mathematical refinements.
Same-width, same-error stationarization under eventual approximate returns
and physical-equivariant purification give equality-inclusive finite
quotient criteria. The least orbit dimension supplies a uniform cap
exponent for all unit directions. A fixed rational qubit experiment has
an exponential exact profile, robust under exponentially small positive
error. Under a full action spectral gap, spectral entropy removes the logarithmic loss in the noisy
converse; least-orbit and target-orbit dimensions give sharp matching
laws when they agree. An additional rational Bloch experiment has
explicit spectral constants and exact profile $5^t$. Its positive upper realization is constructed by a uniform
rational compiler. The focused structural and quantitative articles are
independently complete; this edition preserves the combined development,
including arithmetic, profinite and existential higher-dimensional
examples. Repeatable probes additionally give a finite-response-quotient
classification for uniform causal process error and a constructive
rational approximation under adaptive control. Orthogonal covariance removes the quantitative return assumption and
gives joint subexponential-accuracy laws. A classical tail-capacity
argument sharpens the process minimum to every error below one.
The full spherical Ramanujan hypothesis further gives the entire
exponential accuracy rate. A separate uniform one-pass theorem proves
optimal bit-space for rational orthogonal actions with legal unit-ball
outputs under the stated action-gap and cap hypotheses, including the
six-rotation qubit experiment, by an explicit
integer algorithm. No independent analytic pipeline closure is asserted.
Positive fixed-denominator matrix rounding additionally gives
finite-bit simulation of disturbing rational quantum instruments
under adaptive control, and sharp density-matrix space laws in every
fixed dimension under explicit spectral and cap assumptions.
Reference-stable rational Choi rounding further gives explicit
instrument diamond-error bounds and common adaptive-tester guarantees
with arbitrary entangled references. Variable-description numerical
streaming charges matrix dimension and rational coefficient lengths;
stopped posterior guarantees remain probability weighted.
Intrinsic instrument coordinates additionally give the exact
high-resolution description exponent. On every fixed strictly positive
Choi body, a reusable rational description accurate against arbitrary
common adaptive testers for $N$ calls has optimal length
$\tfrac12d^2(mn^2-1)\log_2N+O(1)$ at fixed error and dimensions.
The joint description law is now uniform in horizon and accuracy.
For preparation instruments a rank-stratified rational factor code also
covers all permitted singular boundaries and preserves zero outcomes.
\end{abstract}
