# Literature and scope record

This is an attribution and interface record, not an exhaustive priority certificate.
The paper does not claim that statistical kernel categories, predictive states,
Blackwell comparison, filtering quantization, or entropy variational formulas were
invented in this project.

## Primary sources checked for this preparation

- Fritz, arXiv:1908.07021, author full HTML: kernel calculus, conditional structure,
  and sufficient statistics. These provide background, not the quantitative memory law.
- Fritz–Gonda–Perrone–Rischel, arXiv:2010.07416, author full HTML: comparison of
  experiments in categorical probability, with attention to parameter/prior distinctions.
- Shalizi–Crutchfield, cond-mat/9907176, author full HTML: minimal predictive/causal
  states for stochastic processes.
- Barnett–Crutchfield, arXiv:1412.2690, author full HTML: input/output processes and
  epsilon-transducers. The present controlled objects are not claimed to be the first
  extension of predictive-state ideas to inputs.
- Littman–Sutton–Singh, NeurIPS 2001 proceedings abstract: predictive representations
  of state. The full paper was not exhaustively audited for every resource variant.
- Pollard, arXiv:1107.3797 and IMS publisher record: preservation of Fisher information
  does not imply sufficient statistics. The paper proves the finite positive score identity;
  its QMD extension is attributed rather than silently re-proved.
- Pagès–Pham (2005), Bernoulli publisher abstract/record: nonlinear-filter quantization.
- Kalogerias–Petropulu, arXiv:1604.02631, author abstract: recursive grid filtering.
- Saldi–Yüksel–Linder, DOI 10.1287/moor.2016.0832, publisher record: finite approximation
  of Borel-space decision processes. Optimal control under arbitrary feedback and
  filtering along the actually selected actions remain distinct questions here.
- Kallenberg, third-edition Springer record (2021): standard probability-kernel and
  conditional-distribution background.
- Dupuis–Wang, DOI 10.1287/moor.1070.0266, publisher record (2007): exponential/entropy
  and dynamic importance-sampling background. This manuscript supplies its own bounded
  exponential-transform proof and does not import an importance-sampling theorem.
- Donoho–Liu II (1991), DOI 10.1214/aos/1176348114, bibliographic verification:
  classical geometry-of-rates background, not a completed priority search.
- Varadhan's 2010 author lecture notes, as identified in the preserved outline:
  classical contraction-principle interface. No Sinai or hard-sphere LDP is inferred from it.

## Results developed in this manuscript

The central construction joins representative quantization, numerical perturbations,
feedback-uniform path comparison, stopping, and charged simulator/controller state.
Its second main result derives a horizon-uniform, vanishing-strength memory law for a
specific refreshing hidden Markov experiment from its physical posterior Jacobian,
actual command/report density, invariant domain, and contractive update.

The lower bound permits arbitrary finite-label encoders and independent randomization,
not only the displayed grid method. The upper bound uses one fixed codebook at all times.
The model is deliberately explicit and strongly refreshing; it is not a theorem for all
hidden Markov chains. The command reparametrization tanh is not offered as a novelty claim.
Whether these quantitative statements merit a particular venue requires subsequent
independent mathematical review and a broader theorem-level priority comparison.
