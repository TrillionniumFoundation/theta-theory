# Author proof audit — Revision 73

This is an author-side audit of written arguments, not an independent referee report or formal proof-assistant certificate.

## Quantifiers

N is any positive integer; public visibility lambda lies in [0,1] and may depend on N and delta. The target coordinate is an ordered equatorial angle. The metric takes the supremum over one common adaptive reference-assisted tester with at most N invocations of the same memoryless instrument. Lower covering centres are arbitrary legal memoryless instruments of the same interface. The angular theorem has absolute constants on 0<delta<=2^-10, not on all errors below two.

## Upper mechanism

Effect differences have reference-assisted one-use distance 2a, a=lambda sin(h/2). A hybrid gives 2Na. At positive eta=1-lambda^2, write the Bloch-vector pair as m±a v and select common pure endpoints m±sqrt(eta+a^2)v. Their two programme biases are ±a/sqrt(eta+a^2). Product root fidelity gives the exact upper expression 2 sqrt(1-(eta/(eta+a^2))^N). All adaptive operations and initial entanglement are a common channel of the programme bits. Unused bits after a public stop are discarded.

The processor depends on the pair and is not the global ambient seizer of the v72 transfer theorem. At lambda=1 the hybrid bound is used rather than division by zero. At lambda=0 the pair is identical.

## Lower mechanism and constants

For k inputs in a GHZ state, the sign-product statistic has mean lambda^k cos(k theta-alpha). One phase alpha chosen from the pair produces opposite biases a_k=lambda^k |sin(kh/2)|. With m=floor(N/k) blocks, a finite majority-polynomial lemma gives unhalved distance at least (1/2)min(1,sqrt(m) a_k).

For T=min(N,1/eta), k0=floor(T), one has k0>=T/2 and lambda^k>=lambda/2 for 1<=k<=k0. The latter follows from log(1-eta)>=-eta/(1-eta), not a noise-independent assertion at arbitrary block size. Small angular differences use k0; k0=1 uses single-copy inputs throughout; for larger angles choose max(1,floor(1/h)). These cases give a universal lower constant at least 1/64. The finite binomial lemma includes r=1, bias zero and bias one and never uses a variance lower bound or a normal limit.

## Covering and endpoints

Angular separation 256delta/K gives pairwise d_N separation at least 4delta on the stated error interval. Triangle inequality excludes two packing points from one radius-delta ball, even for an off-family centre. If K/delta is small, the lower cardinality one supplies the needed uniform constant.

Two semicircle charts cover all angles, have angular speed at most two and are rational. Nearest grid rounding gives angular error <=1/B. A grid ceil(4K/delta) is determined by rational squaring and yields a legal codebook of at most 4B+2 centres. The chart sign and full index are charged. lambda=0 uses no angular digit. The real-lambda theorem is not described as rational matrix output unless lambda is rational.

## Conditional-state theorem

Discarding the conditional quantum output reveals the readout independently of the states. Feeding I/2 makes the label fair and gives the same programme state (rho_+/2) direct_sum (rho_-/2) at every angle and visibility. Rank-stratum product patches have dimension sum_y[r_y(2n-r_y)-1] with fixed trace constraints. Crossing the angular packing and the state packing is legitimate: different angular indices are separated independently of states, while equal angular indices have the state witness. No full product metric equality is assumed.

An angle error delta/2 and two state N-copy errors delta/4 compose by a common conditional processor. Rank nonincrease is inherited from the state factor code. CP/TP assembly is E_y^T tensor rho_y, with the input-first transpose required for complex entries. This result is uniform in visibility but dimension/rank dependent and restricted to small error.

## Implementation evidence, not proof

The new suite uses only exact integer/Fraction decisions. It checks all small chart words, many rational visibilities and horizons, GHZ tensor probabilities, the majority derivative, conditional assembly/ranks and strict target replay. Negative controls expose flag erasure, nonunit coordinate rounding, schema/budget/rank errors and altered nested objects. All nine inherited suites run unchanged in normal and optimized modes. Universal tester bounds, volume asymptotics and independent priority are not certified by these finite cases.
