# Entanglement-width and nonemptiness certificate — v87

Input schema: `gtf87.entanglement-width/1`. Required fields are schema, dimension, outcomes, effects, direction; the optional pair follows the inherited cone supplied-pair schema. Effects and directions are canonical Gaussian-rational matrices in the same representation as `cone_geometry.py`. All dimensions and caps must be genuine positive integers, not booleans. Unknown keys, illegal effects, non-Hermitian or nonzero-sum tangents, negative missing-support blocks and false pair allowances are rejected.

`block_geometry.py` embeds a freshly computed `cone_geometry.py` certificate. The branch selects regular, coherent tangential or support-opening orders. A zero tangent remains `higher_order_undetermined`, with no unjustified stationary rate. For a coherent tangent the block rate is `min(1,sqrt(N*b)*s)` and the parallel rate is `min(1,N*s)`; the product upper is not a parallel upper.

The nonemptiness object contains a rational tangent norm majorant, a rational common support floor, c, s_star and Lambda_star, plus explicit scope fields. Full PSD tests establish the support floor. Formula reconstruction establishes the sufficient curve and allowance. Both `--max-system-dimension` and `--max-halvings` are explicit resource caps; exhaustion rejects rather than returning partial success. `--verify` reconstructs the complete canonical certificate and compares it exactly, including scope fields. A supplied pair only certifies its component allowances at the supplied scale; it does not validate a whole unknown curve.

The sufficient realization can be evaluated by `realized_tuple` only on the certified interval. The interval is not the small-angle interval of discrimination. Lambda_star is not minimal. Exact represented-input polynomial complexity comes from rational support algebra, polynomially many support-floor halvings, and rational arithmetic, not from exhaustive grid search.

The new finite suite contains 12 complete parallel probability laws on explicit entangled qubit inputs; it does not execute the arbitrary-dimension corrected GHZ protocol or construct its general recovery. No certificate computes D_N, a best tester, discrimination constants, control synthesis or physical execution. Its continuous nonemptiness conclusion relies on Proposition nonempty87; tests alone are not continuum proofs.
