# Literature comparison — exact scope and retrieval record

Date: 23 September 2026. This is not a certificate of exhaustive priority or top-four novelty.

## Original filtered-comparison source actually inspected

H. Weisshaupt, *LeCam's Randomization Criterion in the Setting of Locally Convex Spaces*, EURANDOM report 2002-017, original report at https://www.eurandom.tue.nl/reports/2002/017-report.pdf. The introduction, the compact filtered-operator construction, Theorems 4–5 and the Theorem 5 proof were read; the theorem/proof pages were also inspected as rendered PDF pages.

Theorem 5 treats filtration-preserving generalized stochastic operators, a compact convex comparison class and bounded losses. Its proof applies the convex separation/randomization machinery of Theorem 4. The generalized operator formulation also has a representability question: it should not be paraphrased as automatically giving every requested finite-state executable kernel.

The two-time example is the exact separation relevant to this crosswalk: fixed test mixtures have minimum at most zero while the private deficiency is sqrt(2)-1. This distinguishes the stated private theorem from a direct application of a constant convex minimax. It does not prove that all possible nonlinear test formalisms in the literature have been exhausted.

## Classical subtraction

Blackwell/Le Cam comparison, convex separation and finite feedback enumeration are retained antecedents. The row parameterization is elementary stick breaking. Power-mean concentration, positive Bernstein bases, and degree elevation are classical; the paper proves precisely the estimates it uses and does not claim those ingredients as inventions. The novel claim submitted for review is their complete resource-sensitive causal application, with hidden/visible distinctions, strict rational certificate equivalence, a counterexample to constant convex testing, and the same-budget physical transfer.

The collective hard-sphere computation uses classical momentum conservation and Gaussian Fourier transforms. It is an explicit solvable mode, not a generic kinetic theorem. The new claim is the joint collision-domain/positive-deficiency realization, its controlled mean-function reduction, and the certified marked consequence. The sharp bounded exponential-transfer inequality is elementary once the marked TV comparison is available; its role is to make the exponential-scale accuracy obligation explicit.

The v15 witness-dimension theorem, exact task widths, finite-state automata comparison, common-information control and weak-star compactness keep their previous attribution. They are not silently rolled into the new theorem's originality count.

## Originals not retrieved at proof level

The Weisshaupt bibliography identifies E. Norberg's Oslo 1999 preprint on approximate comparison of experiments with filtered probability spaces. The historical preprint index `https://www.math.uio.no/eprint/stat_report/1999/rept_1999.html` and its current-site directory candidate returned access errors during this revision. The published DOI landing page also did not yield original text. No proof-level claim of absence of finite-state variants is made on the basis of those failures.

The IEEE landing page for Paull–Unger (`https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/5222697`) returned a JavaScript/robot check, not the original paper. The retained bibliography and incomplete-machine comparison remain relevant, but the original proof-level crosswalk is not certified here.

Accordingly E15.7 remains incomplete as to those two original texts. The manuscript states its positive theorem and its exact distinction from the original source actually inspected, rather than asserting an exhaustively established priority boundary.
