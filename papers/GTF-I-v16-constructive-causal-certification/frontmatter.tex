\begin{abstract}
We study causal comparison of marked experiments at fixed persistent-state
and randomization budgets. A polynomial testing dual detects the original
nonconvex private behavior image without free convexification: strict
lower bounds are equivalent to positive finite Bernstein certificates,
while upper witnesses remain executable at the original budget.
The construction distinguishes a two-time private deficiency $\sqrt2-1$
from its zero hidden-selector counterpart, which constant test mixtures
cannot separate. Combined with stateless finite-report and joint-prefix
rational presentations, it gives convergent certified intervals for
physical causal deficiency. For interacting hard spheres we construct a
nonconserved, collision-compatible position observable with explicit
Gaussian-moment graph data and a nonzero residual. Its controlled words
reduce from $2^k$ to $k+1$ distinct mean functions, yielding an explicit
convergent width-uniform approximation. The same physical acquisition has
an exact marked deficiency represented by an absolutely convergent series
and enclosed by rational endpoints. A sharp exponential transfer bound
quantifies the additional accuracy needed for logarithmic acquisition
values. The compact, endogenous-task, regenerative and graph-domain
theory is retained with its separate information and analytic hypotheses.
\end{abstract}
