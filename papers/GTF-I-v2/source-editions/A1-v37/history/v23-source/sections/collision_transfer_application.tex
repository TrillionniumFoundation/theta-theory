\subsection{Verification of the transfer hypotheses}
\label{sec:transfer-application}
We now apply Theorem~\ref{thm:causal-transfer} directly. The two
classification theorems in Appendix~\ref{app:complete-routes} give
complete alternative proofs; neither is used in the following argument.

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:resolution-main}]
At checkpoint $(n,m)$ use the nonconstant formal raw moment vector
\[
 v(h)=\bigl(\E[t^{Hx_i}\mid h]\bigr)_{i=1}^{q_m}.
\]
Let $S_{n,a}$ be its reachable image over all commands and report words.
It is compact. Expansion of the physical product queries gives an
affine map $b+Gv$ with uniformly bounded $G$ and a left inverse, by
the full-column-rank formal product matrix established above. Repeated
exponents give equal coordinates of $v$, not a change in this formal
coefficient matrix.

We verify (G) without inverting a vanishing Newton pivot. Write
$s$ for the number of distinct positive future nodes and decompose the
first $s$ columns of $L_a$ from Lemma~\ref{lem:leja-scales} as
$\binom{A_a}{B_a}$, where $A_a$ is the leading $s\times s$ block.
Both $A_a^{-1}$ and $B_a$ are uniformly bounded. In the Leja ordering set
\[
 T_a=\begin{pmatrix}
       A_a^{-1}&0\\-B_aA_a^{-1}&I_{q_m-s}
     \end{pmatrix},\qquad
 T_a^{-1}=\begin{pmatrix}A_a&0\\B_a&I_{q_m-s}\end{pmatrix}.
\]
When $s=q_m$ the empty blocks are omitted. Both matrices have uniformly
bounded norms. Since $v=L_aD_az$, $D_a=\operatorname{diag}(d_j)$,
we have $T_av=D_az$, including all zero coordinates after $s$.
The associated label permutation is orthogonal and can be absorbed in
$T_a$. Thus an invertible change on the \emph{ambient} raw space exists
even when the reachable set lies in a proper subspace. The sup-norm
bounds for $g_j^a$ in Lemma~\ref{lem:newton-attainment} put this image
in a rectangle of half-widths $B d_1,\ldots,B d_{q_m}$.

For each fixed calibration and report word, every raw moment has the
form $N_j(\boldsymbol u)/Z(\boldsymbol u)$, where $N_j,Z$ are
polynomials of bounded degree and $Z\ge\kappa^n$. Quantifier
elimination applied to this graph bounds the semialgebraic format of
its image independently of its real coefficients. A finite union over
report words preserves a uniform bound. Dividing each factor by its
positive integral against $\mu$ puts its coefficients in an affine
hyperplane of dimension $r-1$; this division changes neither the
posterior nor the normalized moment map. The dimension is consequently
at most $n(r-1)$ and at most $q_m$, hence at most $p_{n,m}$.
Application of $T_a$ does not alter this format conclusion: for a fixed
$a$, its entries are additional real coefficients. No semialgebraic
dependence of $a$, $\mu$ or divided differences on calibration is
required. This proves (G).

For (A), take the common experiment of independent uniform commands and
all its actual reports. Lemma~\ref{lem:newton-attainment} gives an
all-failure subprobability dominating a fixed positive multiple of
uniform measure on a translated $p_{n,m}$-cube in the first Newton
coordinates. Project to any $\ell\le p_{n,m}$ with $d_\ell>0$
and multiply by $d_1,\ldots,d_\ell$. This is precisely the required
rectangle in the first coordinates of $T_av$. Its mass constant is
unchanged. The probability of the report word has already been
included. The command and report laws for different $n$ are prefixes
of one experiment, even though different prefixes can be used to
establish different lower bounds.

For (C), retain the constant moment together with the raw vector.
For each next report factor $f(t)=\sum_i f_i t^{a_i}$, multiplication
sends the remaining tests of degree $m-1$ into those of degree $m$:
\[
 f(t)t^{\alpha\cdot a}=\sum_i f_i t^{(\alpha+e_i)\cdot a},
 \qquad |\alpha|=m-1.
\]
The coefficient vectors are uniformly bounded, and every likelihood
is at least $\kappa$. Lemma~\ref{lem:positive-remaining-update}
therefore gives a raw update with a gap-independent Lipschitz bound
and maps every reachable representative to an actually reachable next
state. No value of $d_j^{-1}$ is used in this recursion.

All the hypotheses of Theorem~\ref{thm:causal-transfer} now hold with
$s_{n,j}=d_j$ and $p_n=p_{n,m}$. Equation~\eqref{eq:leja-volume}
identifies its profile $Q_n$ with $\Psi_{n,m}$, within constants
depending only on the fixed dimensions. Equal stage budgets give
$\Xi_N$. The physical query norm converts the state bounds to the
stated squared prediction regrets. The theorem supplies the common-law
randomized lower bound and one stage-compatible deterministic upper
filter for every integer budget. All constants used above are uniform
on $K$, including collision configurations, which completes the proof.
\end{proof}

Unequal stage budgets are covered by the same verification: replacing
$M$ by $M_n$ at checkpoint $n$ gives the exact error propagation in
\eqref{eq:transfer-recurrence} and the sharp fixed-horizon maximum law
$\max_n\Psi_{n,N-n}(M_n,a)$. No separate acquisition assumption or
re-examination of an exact prefix is needed. The following consequences
return to a common budget at every checkpoint.
