\begin{abstract}
For positive finite-horizon monomial experiments with a fixed full-support
prior, we determine the information attainable from past reports and the
memory required for its causal retention. Maximal Vandermonde volumes of
the formal future
exponents give matching bounds for optimal prediction regret at every
integer label budget, uniformly through all additive collisions in a
compact exponent chamber. The constants may depend on the fixed prior,
detector and horizon. The lower bound comes from an attainable monomial
tangent and simultaneous confluent flags with positive mass under the
actual acquisition law. A whole-image covering estimate and reachable
raw-moment updates realize the upper bound by one transducer charged
after every report. The law determines persistent-bit requirements and
resolution phases at intersecting collision loci. We also give an exact
realization theory for prescribed acquisition in general finite-cell
experiments: the one-step coefficients form a Blackwell postprocessing
region, and conditional state laws close in finite cell moments.
This yields attained mean-risk formulas, deterministic realization
under atomless commands, and polyhedral updates for scalarized risks.
Complete finite-certificate constructions and an exact continuous-command
example accompany the classification.
\end{abstract}
