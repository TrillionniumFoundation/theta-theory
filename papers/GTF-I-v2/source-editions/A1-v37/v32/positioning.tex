\subsection{Theorem-level comparison}
\label{sec:v32-positioning}
The principal geometric result is the attained,
collision-uniform classification of
Theorem~\ref{thm:resolution-main}: the determinant volumes are truncated
by directions actually acquired under a positive experiment, and one
persistent-label transducer realizes the resulting scale at every
checkpoint. The new compatibility results determine how its operational
optimum can be bracketed without replacing a shared posterior by fresh
independent blocks.

Quantization theory, including its general and asymptotic foundations,
is developed by Graf and Luschgy~\cite{GrafLuschgy2000}. The extra issue
here is not the existence of a quantizer for a prescribed measure. It is
the acquired measure, its collision-dependent attainable directions and
the realization of a sequence of quantizers by one retained state.
Clustered Vandermonde estimates of Batenkov, Diederichs, Goldman and
Yomdin~\cite{V32Batenkov} concern spectral and conditioning properties
of linear matrices. They do not themselves give the positive acquired
mass or the nonlinear whole-history statistical image required here.
These are differences of hypotheses and conclusions, not an assertion
that the full comparison literature has been exhausted.

Wood, Linder and Y\"uksel~\cite{V32Wood} study zero-delay coding of
finite-state Markov sources using a stochastic-control formulation, with
stationary and finite-memory conclusions. A transmitted-symbol alphabet
and a decoder's communication history are not automatically the same
resource as the entire persistent state bounded here. Our new finite
formulation does not claim priority for expressing finite-state
controllers as nonlinear programs. Amato, Bernstein and
Zilberstein~\cite{V32Amato} give precisely such an approach for POMDP
controllers. In the present formulation the history--state compatibility,
the exclusion of a retained seed and the full-future prediction target
are made explicit; the uniform approximation applies to arbitrary Borel
transition kernels, without an inverse exponent gap. The exhaustive
certificate, unlike a local nonlinear solver, provides a global lower
bound by a complete controller net with a proved error.

Thus Theorem~\ref{thm:v32-finite} supplies an exact finite representation,
not a new linear-programming duality. Theorem~\ref{thm:v32-grid} supplies
the approximation theorem for the continuous positive experiment.
Corollary~\ref{cor:v32-relative} is their consequence for the same
nonregenerative decision problem as the collision classification.
Theorem~\ref{thm:v32-delayed} illustrates the surviving causal constraint
with two admissible schedules and a closed-form unseeded optimum.
The regenerative flow theorems remain exact in their own model and are
not promoted to a characterization of arbitrary shared-posterior memory.
