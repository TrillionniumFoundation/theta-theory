## Quantitative obligations retained

The finite extraction and its derivative sum are now genuine theorems for each fixed count packet. Their actual dependence on n,L,R and the weight is not yet a uniform long-time bound. The exact error formula names the remaining quantities: the central edge correction, the finite complementary residual integral, and the summed derivative norm divided by the chosen roof cutoff. The other spectral arcs, simultaneous mesh/time control, actual uncompressed powers, and the weighted exact-event comparison are not inferred from these structural results. No numerical check or successful build is used as a proof of those estimates.
