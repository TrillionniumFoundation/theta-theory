\begin{abstract}
We study finite-label realizations of compact group interfaces and
observation processes. For fresh nondisturbing classical probes, the
minimum horizon-dependent width converges, at every fixed
whole-transcript total-variation error below one, to the cardinality
of the future-response quotient. Every smaller width has a uniform
occupation bound, even with unrestricted intervening registers.
The proof combines a common diagnostic cycle, Markov tail-event
capacity and a compactness strong converse. For terminal numerical
interfaces, eventual approximate returns permit same-width,
same-error stationarization; stochastic purification identifies the
stationary minimum with a finite continuous physical action.
The resulting quotient-radius criteria include the critical boundary.
\end{abstract}
