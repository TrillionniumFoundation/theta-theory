The paper distinguishes controlled finite-horizon suffix tables, signed rational series, normalized positive state cardinality, stationary HMM laws and wordwise binary-TV approximation. The observed-space enclosure is a classical cone-type positive realization. The new rate comparison uses its all-length continuation and repeated packet loss; no reverse equality is asserted. The ordinary O(log N) space upper bound for finitely many commuting angles is elementary by retaining counts. The finite-bit compiler is assessed for compatible small clean registers, absence of per-label advice tables and charged evaluation, not for inventing that logarithmic upper bound.

No finite regression certifies a universal asymptotic theorem, spectral gap or priority boundary. The optional original-page LPS limitation of the archived material is unchanged and is not needed for the present new results.
