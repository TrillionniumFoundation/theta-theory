## New proofs and direct dependencies

**290 / `lem:gr-jacobian`.** Differentiates the directions of the two actual adjacent flights. Departure and arrival incidence signs give every interior and boundary entry. The factorization H_II = R diag(c_i) B is compared to the inherited exact second variation. No division by c_i is used.

**290 / `thm:gr-resolvent`.** Sign conjugation yields nonpositive off-diagonals. The nonnegative column-normalized matrix N has l1 norm at most 47/53. Its geometric series proves positivity of the inverse. The unweighted column margin is 2, the weighted margin for lambda=50/47 is 1. Multiplication by the positive inverse proves the stated norm and Green bounds. This is a finite Jacobian identity for the original dynamics, not a stochastic transition model. Simultaneous zero incidences are permitted algebraically. Row dominance is not assumed.

**290 / `lem:gr-angle-tensors`.** The local complex logarithm of a nonzero physical flight gives every mixed direction tensor through set partitions. Only R<=47/100 and flight length>=3/50 enter. The l1 and weighted-l1 flight sums do not introduce the number of collisions.

**290 / `thm:gr-jets`.** Implicit differentiation includes every set partition, including adjacent and coincident derivative directions. The unique one-block term is B times the unknown interior jet. All other blocks have strictly lower derivative order. Positive inverse estimates and the displayed finite recursions prove every fixed finite-order bound. Constants may grow with order, not with m. The theorem does not assert a common physical continuation radius or uniformly bounded forward collision-map derivatives.

**290 / `cor:gr-grazing-limit`.** For fixed m and fixed centers, convergent physical configurations retain positive flight lengths. The limiting reflection Jacobian is invertible even at simultaneous zero incidences. The ordinary parameter-dependent implicit function theorem gives a local formal continuation. Only its limits along the original physical branch are claimed; blockers, section cuts and physical itinerary changes are not crossed or removed.

**291 / `lem:gr-flight-four`.** Differentiation of the Euclidean norm through order four gives coefficients 1,1,3,15 and complete composition counts 1,7,6,1. Summing flight products uses one l1 sum and remaining suprema, with a factor at most 2, independent of m.

**291 / `thm:gr-fourth`.** Differentiates the retained exact third-order stationary identity. The three mixed terms involving D^2 alpha survive and are bounded using the new reflection resolvent, not a degenerate action-Hessian inverse. The alternative symmetric contraction identity is a check, not the grazing estimate. This proves F4 = 2D4 C1^4 + 6D3 C2 C1^2.

**291 / `cor:gr-taylor`.** Integral Taylor remainders on an entire straight segment inside the actual physical branch yield F error F4||h||_infinity^4/24 and Hessian error F4||h||_infinity^2. One-sided limits do not imply the existence of such a segment beyond a physical boundary.
