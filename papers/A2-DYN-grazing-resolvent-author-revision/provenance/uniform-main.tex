\begin{abstract}
We study the original exact-record raw pointwise local limit and
four-coordinate inversion problem for the triangular Lorentz gas. This
revision establishes uniform admissibility and growth of the physical
angular clearance source. For the actual circular collision map we derive
a grazing-uniform affine contraction of geometric curve curvature with an
explicit coefficient less than one half. We prove a one-sided first-hit
lemma, a complete homogeneous one-step expansion bound, and an all-time
one-third logarithmic distortion estimate. These verify the precise
hypotheses of the cited combinatorial curve-growth lemma, including the
source class and the singular-null passage. The previously conditional
complete cross-flight clearance kernel and one-copy root sum therefore
no longer require an unproved curve-growth premise or physical-word
pressure. The earlier source construction, geometric, optical, arithmetic,
critical-roof and exact-record proofs are all retained. This is a bound
in clearance, not in the original roof variable. The normalized
first-order roof source, complete critical and boundary budgets,
nongrazing capacity and the original raw pointwise inversion limit are
not proved by this revision.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Introduction}
A unit-speed particle moves among equal circular disks centered at the
triangular lattice, undergoing true specular collisions. We keep the
original radius interval, physical clock, section and occupation convention,
exact displacement labels, and the joint return--displacement--collision
record. The target remains the raw pointwise assertion
\eqref{eq:v76-target} and the original four-coordinate inverse problem.
Neither an integrated limit theorem nor a replacement stochastic process
is introduced.

\subsection*{The new result and the premise it removes}
The complete preceding native manuscript lifted an unvisited-clearance
angular source into an actual weighted unstable family. At the first true
collision after the marked foot it proved
\[
 \frac{ds}{d\vartheta}=-\frac ac,\qquad
 V=\frac{d\varphi}{ds}=R^{-1}+c/a,\qquad
 \rho=\frac{c}{a\sqrt{1+V^2}},
\]
with $a\ge\sqrt{1827}/200$. The vanishing incidence factor pays the
infinite homogeneous grazing partition. Those source identities, their
finite first-hit construction and all proofs in
Sections~\ref{sec:ag-source}--\ref{sec:ag-growth} are preserved; they
are not claimed as new contributions here. That manuscript explicitly
left membership in an invariant curve class, uniform expansion,
distortion and singular-null coverage as the premise
Input~\ref{input:ag-growth}.

Sections~\ref{sec:uc-curvature}--\ref{sec:uc-growth} now supply those
missing verifications. The geometric curvature $K$ of a collision graph
in physical $(s,\varphi)$ coordinates obeys, at every regular reflection,
\[
 |K_1|\le q_*|K|+C_*,\qquad
 q_*={946236091513673179721\over2005319667933288000000}<\tfrac12,
 \quad C_*={64061\over8100}.
\]
The inequality is uniform as either positive incidence approaches zero.
It concerns neither the optical wavefront curvature nor the Hessian of
a long roof. A sufficiently large fixed geometric-curvature class is
therefore invariant and contains the actual marked source.

Curvature invariance alone is not a growth theorem. We next prove that
on a uniformly short graph there is at most one non-grazing limiting
first-hit branch. All other homogeneous image branches carry a summable
$k^{-2}$ inverse-Jacobian factor. An exact adapted expansion yields a
one-step contraction sum strictly below one, after one fixed choice of
homogeneity threshold and length. We also compute the logarithmic
Jacobian derivative in the \emph{image} coordinate. Its only grazing
term is bounded by a constant times the inverse terminal incidence;
the $k^{-3}$ strip width converts this to a one-third distortion bound.
Adapted expansion sums the distortion over all collision counts.
These are direct physical proofs, not deductions from a finite ray test.

Theorem~\ref{thm:uc-growth} then applies exactly the combinatorial
curve-sum statement of \cite[Lemma 3.2(b)]{DZUniform}. Its curve,
expansion, distortion and null-set requirements are checked for the
constructed family, in physical arclength and uniformly in radius.
A varying-radius perturbation theorem on a common Banach space is not
needed. The result is
\[
 \sup_{R,l,C}\sum_{V\in\mathcal G_l^+(C)}
                  \sup_V J_V(T_R^{-l})<\infty.
\]
The one-copy physical partition is summed before any supremum over the
initial curves, radius or collision count. Time reversal is used only
to transfer a stable-curve lemma to unstable curves; the original section
event is not reversed. Together with the retained source and coarea
proofs, this discharges the curve-growth premise of the complete
cross-flight clearance kernel and root sum. Repeated observations on
different physical flights are included, and there is no independence
assumption or multiplier equal to the number of long words.

\subsection*{Scope, provenance and the original obstruction}
The active numbered proof bodies 260--280 and the model and interface
files remain byte-identical to the recovered complete angular-source
predecessor. Its full source and execution packet is frozen separately.
Modules 270--273, 274--275, 276, 277--278 and 279--280 have distinct
historical origins recorded in the source manifest. The new numbered
bodies are 281--283. Conditional statements in preserved bodies retain
their original formulation; Corollary~\ref{cor:uc-clearance} records
exactly which of their premises is now discharged. The inherited
whole-cylinder pressure, pairing and spectral inputs are not silently
upgraded.

A bound for the second clearance is not a bound for the physical roof.
The complete first-order marked source still has to be pushed to the
original roof with uniform $m^2$ normalization, including folds, critical
roofs, higher degeneracies, one-sided section cuts and pairing traces.
The original low-incidence shallow source and sufficient nongrazing
comparison capacity also remain. The precise ordered shortage limit is
\eqref{eq:v112-remaining}, restated without alteration in
\eqref{eq:uc-open-limit}. It is not established here. In particular an
$O(\varepsilon^2)$ total-variation error is not multiplied by $m^2$ at
fixed outer widths and declared negligible.

The response to the controlling report identifies the mathematical
advance, the exact remaining closure conditions and the limited scope of
computational evidence. The full original raw pointwise LLT and final
four-coordinate inversion remain the research objective, not an asserted
consequence of the present curve-growth theorem. No journal acceptance,
independent expert certification or formal proof certificate is claimed.
