\section{The complete physical comparison space}
\label{sec:v109-pool}
The comparison space is now specified without an unverified
endpoint-trace exclusion:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v109-admissible}
 \Theta_{\rm ad}=\Theta_{\rm phys}.
\end{equation}
More explicitly, it consists of all positive-length maximal regular
pairing segments of the original exact event, with their original
incident-component identities. Their endpoints may be critical,
visited-grazing, third-disk-tangent, or section-boundary limits.
Theorem~\ref{thm:v109-traces} supplies finite traces and the endpoint
variation inequality on this space. The underlying Borel pairing
construction and the averaged analytic estimates remain the inputs
in Section~\ref{sec:v109-inputs}. No singular trajectory is continued
past its physical boundary, and no new source supported at a
zero-length segment is introduced.

For fixed $0<r<\Delta$, define
\begin{align}
 q_+(t,\theta)&=\one_{\{0<\ell<\Delta,\ a<t,\ t+r<b\}},\notag\\
 q_-(t,\theta)&=(1-q_+)\one_{\{0<\ell<\Delta,\ a<t-r,\ t<b\}},
 \qquad Q_r=q_++q_-.
 \label{eq:v109-pool}
\end{align}
Thus $Q_r$ is Borel and at most one. Write
\[
 P_r(t)=\int Q_r p_\vee\,d\theta,
 \qquad D_r(t)=\int Q_r c^\varepsilon\,d\theta,
 \qquad E_r(t)=\int Q_r e_\partial\,d\theta.
\]
The full pool is retained even on a sheet whose paired source is zero.

\begin{theorem}[Capacity on the full original comparison space]
\label{thm:v109-pool}
At finite collision count, on the original common roof versions,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v109-pool-bound}
 P_r(t)\leq\frac1r\int_{t-r}^{t+r}\mathsf T_\Delta^\varepsilon(u)\,du
 +C(1+\varepsilon^{-1})\int_{t-\Delta}^{t+\Delta}v^0(u)\,du.
\end{equation}
Consequently, for every previously fixed $K>0$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v109-pool-height}
 \mathcal H_M(KP_r)\leq
 CK\{\varepsilon^{1/16}+(1+\varepsilon^{-1})\Delta\}.
\end{equation}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every positively oriented selected segment contains $[t,t+r]$;
every negatively oriented one contains $[t-r,t]$. Integrating its
constant paired minimum over the appropriate window gives $r$ times
that minimum. Tonelli and $Q_r\leq1$ therefore give
$D_r(t)\leq r^{-1}\int_{t-r}^{t+r}\mathsf T_\Delta^\varepsilon$.
Each selected segment lies inside $(t-\Delta,t+\Delta)$.
The completed variation inequality \eqref{eq:v109-completed-variation}
and the original one-copy physical coarea sum give
$E_r(t)\leq C(1+\varepsilon^{-1})\int_{t-\Delta}^{t+\Delta}v^0$.
Since $P_r=D_r+E_r$, this proves \eqref{eq:v109-pool-bound}.
Use \eqref{eq:v109-paired-average} and
\eqref{eq:v109-volume-average}, only on these previously fixed
windows, to obtain \eqref{eq:v109-pool-height}.
The weights are held at the original roof $t$ throughout; none is
differentiated or evaluated at the integration roof $u$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The physical grazing partition}
For each interior collision let $c_i^-$ and $c_i^+$ be its incidence
limits at the two ends of a pairing segment. Define the Borel sets
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v109-grazing-classes}
 \Gamma=\bigcup_{i=1}^{m-1}\{c_i^-=0\ \hbox{or}\ c_i^+=0\},
 \qquad\mathcal N=\Theta_{\rm phys}\setminus\Gamma.
\end{equation}
For $m=1$ the union is empty. The limits are Borel, for example by
evaluating the interior functions at $a+\ell/j$ and $b-\ell/j$ and
letting the integer $j$ tend to infinity. Set
\[
 P_r^{\rm gr}=\int_\Gamma Q_rp_\vee\,d\theta,
 \qquad P_r^{\rm ng}=\int_\mathcal N Q_rp_\vee\,d\theta,
 \qquad P_r=P_r^{\rm ng}+P_r^{\rm gr}.
\]
The superscripts refer to visited collision incidences in the
limiting finite itinerary, not to a lower incidence cutoff.

\begin{theorem}[Zero original grazing source and paid grazing capacity]
\label{thm:v109-grazing}
For every original Borel selector $0\leq H\leq1$, at finite count,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v109-grazing-zero}
 \int_\Gamma c^\varepsilon\one_{\{a<t<b\}}
                  H(\Psi(\theta,t))\,d\theta=0.
\end{equation}
Nevertheless $P_r^{\rm gr}$ need not vanish. It satisfies
\begin{align}
 P_r^{\rm gr}(t)&\leq C(1+\varepsilon^{-1})
                         \int_{t-\Delta}^{t+\Delta}v^0(u)\,du,
                         \label{eq:v109-grazing-volume}\\
 \mathcal H_M(KP_r^{\rm gr})&\leq
                       CK(1+\varepsilon^{-1})\Delta.
                         \label{eq:v109-grazing-height}
\end{align}
These estimates include the complete positive capacity on this
physical class, with no count of angular blocks or collision words.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Theorem~\ref{thm:v109-traces} gives $c^\varepsilon=0$ on $\Gamma$,
proving \eqref{eq:v109-grazing-zero} before the roof pushforward.
On that same class $p_\vee=e_\partial$, not necessarily zero.
Apply \eqref{eq:v109-completed-variation}, use the localization
$(a,b)\subset(t-\Delta,t+\Delta)$ on $\{Q_r=1\}$, and complete
the one-copy positive physical sum. This proves
\eqref{eq:v109-grazing-volume}. Equation
\eqref{eq:v109-volume-average} at the fixed width $\Delta$ proves
\eqref{eq:v109-grazing-height}.
\end{proof}

\section{The original microscopic shortage after grazing payment}
\label{sec:v109-shortage}
All allocations below are scalar allocations at the original roof;
they are not couplings of individual source and comparison sheets.
For any original source $U(t)$ define
\[
 V_{r,K}(t)=\min\{U(t),KP_r(t)\},\qquad
 R_{r,K}(t)=(U(t)-KP_r(t))_+.
\]
The multiplier is $\beta_{r,K}=\min\{1,KP_r/U\}$ on $U>0$ and
zero on $U=0$. Then $U=V_{r,K}+R_{r,K}$ at finite count and
$V_{r,K}$ is the original source multiplied by $\beta_{r,K}(t)$.
When a restricted comparison space is used, source outside it is
still in $U$ and may receive this same global fraction. It is not
therefore retained sheetwise with coefficient one.

Use the exact source \eqref{eq:v109-original-micro} and, for a concrete
outer choice, put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v109-explicit-widths}
 h_B=\delta(B)/2,\qquad r_B=\delta(B)/8,\qquad
 K_B=\max\{1,\varepsilon(B)^{-1/32}\}.
\end{equation}
For all sufficiently large $B$ these satisfy the inherited cost
budget. Define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v109-two-residuals}
 \begin{split}
 \mathsf R_B^{\rm full}&=
       (\mathsf U_B^{\rm micro}-K_BP_{r_B})_+,\\
 \mathsf R_B^{\rm ng}&=
       (\mathsf U_B^{\rm micro}-K_BP_{r_B}^{\rm ng})_+.
 \end{split}
\end{equation}
The full capacity pool includes any sheets previously excluded solely
for lack of a trace verification. On the complete comparison space
it is exactly the canonical pool; on a previously restricted space
it is an enlargement, not an identification of different capacities.

\begin{theorem}[Quantitative payment of the visited-grazing class]
\label{thm:v109-residual}
Under the retained physical-source and analytic inputs, for the
original event and every fixed $M$,
\begin{align}
 0\leq\mathsf R_B^{\rm ng}-\mathsf R_B^{\rm full}
     &\leq K_BP_{r_B}^{\rm gr},\label{eq:v109-residual-difference}\\
 \mathcal H_M(\mathsf R_B^{\rm ng}-\mathsf R_B^{\rm full})
     &\leq C_MB^{-95/384},\label{eq:v109-residual-rate}\\
 \mathcal E_M&\leq\mathcal H_M(\mathsf R_B^{\rm ng})
                                     +C_MB^{-1/384}.
                                     \label{eq:v109-final-reduction}
\end{align}
The unchanged pointwise assertion \eqref{eq:v76-target} is equivalent
to $\mathcal H_M(\mathsf R_B^{\rm ng})\to0$ for every fixed $M$.
The original microscopic source in these formulas is supported on
$\mathcal N$ without a change of its positive weight.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For $u,a,b\geq0$, the positive-part function gives
$0\leq(u-a)_+-(u-a-b)_+\leq b$.
Apply this with $a=K_BP_{r_B}^{\rm ng}$ and
$b=K_BP_{r_B}^{\rm gr}$ to prove
\eqref{eq:v109-residual-difference} at finite count.
Theorem~\ref{thm:v109-grazing}, $\Delta_B=O(\varepsilon^4)$ and
$K_B=O(\varepsilon^{-1/32})$ give
\[
 \mathcal H_M(K_BP_{r_B}^{\rm gr})
 \leq C\varepsilon^{-1/32}(1+\varepsilon^{-1})\varepsilon^4
 =O(\varepsilon^{95/32})=O(B^{-95/384}).
\]
This proves \eqref{eq:v109-residual-rate} in the prescribed order.
By Theorem~\ref{thm:v109-pool}, the accepted full-pool source has
height at most
\[
 C\varepsilon^{-1/32}\{
                 \varepsilon^{1/16}+O(\varepsilon^3)\}
 =O(B^{-1/384}).
\]
Insert its exact positive decomposition into
\eqref{eq:v109-scalar-input}. Since
$\mathsf R_B^{\rm full}\leq\mathsf R_B^{\rm ng}$, this proves
\eqref{eq:v109-final-reduction} and sufficiency of the displayed
residual limit. Necessity follows from Input
statement~\ref{input:v109-scalar} and
$0\leq\mathsf R_B^{\rm ng}\leq\mathsf U_B^{\rm micro}$.
Equation~\eqref{eq:v109-grazing-zero} proves the final assertion.
\end{proof}

This theorem supplies a physical component of the required scarcity
estimate, rather than a new certificate class. It removes an entire
visited-grazing source class exactly and pays the loss of all its
comparison capacity by a faster-decaying original optical volume.
It does not yet bound $\mathcal H_M(\mathsf R_B^{\rm ng})$ by a
quantity tending to zero. In $\mathcal N$ every visited endpoint
incidence is positive at each fixed count, but its infimum may tend
to zero with the count. Third-disk tangencies, nongrazing critical
entrances, section-boundary cuts, flat and short-reach segments, and
the original comparison shortages all remain with their full weight.
A collision-uniform estimate on that remaining source is still
required for \eqref{eq:v76-target}. Neither finite-count exhaustion
nor the per-itinerary exponential bound is used in its place.
