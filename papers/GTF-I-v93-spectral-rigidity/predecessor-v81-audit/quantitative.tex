\begin{abstract}
We study memoryless ordered binary quantum measurements that consume
their input and return a classical label.  A regularized midpoint
Sylvester form is comparable to the finite-use adaptive distance on
the complete effect body, with explicit dimension-dependent constants.
At fixed dimension $d$, the small-error covering number has order
\mbox{$N^{d^2/2}[\log(N+2)]^{\lfloor d/2\rfloor}\delta^{-d^2}$},
including every spectral boundary.
A common learner uses $Cd^4N\delta^{-2}\log(d/\eta)$ calls, and a
finite exact rational code attains the optimal reusable description
order.  With independent training blocks of at most $b\le N$
calls, the fixed-dimensional minimax order is
$\Theta_d((N^2/b)\delta^{-2}\log(1/\eta))$ for $d\ge2$.
Classical feedback and collective processing of completed outputs
are permitted; a conditional fidelity argument gives the converse.

On the fixed interior $I/4\preceq E\preceq3I/4$, the query order is
\mbox{$\Theta(N\delta^{-2}[d^2+d\log(1/\eta)])$},
with absolute constants jointly in dimension, horizon, accuracy and
confidence.  Independent one-call Choi acquisition attains the upper
bound; the lower permits arbitrary coherent adaptive training.
Weak-measurement information and diagonal relative-entropy bounds
give its two contributions.  The dimension-uniform interior
description length is
$(d^2/2)\log_2N+d^2\log_2(1/\delta)+O(d^2)$;
shared randomness and failure change the leading optimal expected
prefix length by the factor $1-\eta$.
A finite rational dictionary and an effective algebraic collective
readout attain the joint query and description orders simultaneously.
Besides an algebraic synthesis by real quantifier elimination, we
construct Gaussian-rational readouts with finite, exactly checkable
risk certificates.  A rational parameter net and a trace-norm bound
transfer their inequalities to every real effect; bounded-denominator
search attains the same query order.  Finite trusted controls preserve
the original operational loss and confidence.  Classical search and known-control costs are stated
separately from device calls and transmitted length.
The interior learning laws hold for $d,N\ge1$,
$0<\delta\le2^{-13}$ and $0<\eta\le1/8$; the effective
construction takes rational accuracy and confidence.
The full-body laws retain their stated small-error ranges and
separate scalar case.
\end{abstract}
