# Resource ledger

The principal model counts available persistent labels at each cut and the held two-label binary output. The alphabet, ambient dimension, seed set, signal and error are fixed before the horizon grows. One coordinate query arrives only at the end. The complete command word is external, not an action-selection policy.

The principal atomic model does not charge the external epoch, known horizon, horizon-specific design, exact row tables, their construction/lookup, or exact real arithmetic and sampling. Time-dependent command rows and horizon-dependent decoders are allowed. Positive-probability state counts under one converse law may strengthen that converse, but do not redefine available width.

For finite synchronization group L the explicit tracker has at most `max(2,|X||L|)` labels; at signal at most `1/d` a simplex tracker has at most `max(2,(d+1)|L|)`. Group and seed labels are charged. At epoch t the reference conjugation is incorporated into the prescribed row; it is not a hidden free transcript. The finite table may have `N|A||X||L|` deterministic rows when listed cut by cut. No claim of a succinct or uniform implementation is inferred.

The finite number-field decision uses exact input representation, not a spectral-gap oracle. Its worst-case enumeration bound is enormous; the group table and certificate may be correspondingly large. The finite-example verifier has a separately stated safety cap and is not the general decision algorithm.

Revision 44's table-free finite-bit compiler, included unchanged, charges arithmetic registers, counters, random words and parameter storage. Its work bits differ from its clean stochastic-label count. Exactly real atomic simulation and positive-error finite-bit simulation are distinct tasks. The inherited compiler does not turn the new general finite-group tracker into a uniform efficient implementation.
