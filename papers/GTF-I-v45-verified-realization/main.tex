\title[Synchronized actions and numerical realization]{General Theta Foundations I: A Boundedness Criterion for Numerical Realization}
\author{Qian Qi}
\date{26 September 2026; revision 45}
\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
A prescribed command length can make an infinite orthogonal action require only bounded stochastic memory: deterministic motion may be placed in the horizon-dependent decoder. We identify the remaining obstruction. On the invariant span of the seeds, let the synchronization subgroup be the closed normal subgroup generated by the multiplicative differences $U_a^{-1}U_b$ of command matrices. Exact numerical response probabilities admit uniformly bounded clocked positive-realization width if and only if this subgroup is finite. Otherwise the minimum width tends to infinity, also at every fixed error below an explicit seed-activation threshold. The converse permits arbitrary hidden states, time-dependent rows, and redesign at each horizon; it assumes neither commutativity nor a spectral gap. The criterion is decidable for matrices and seeds in a specified real number field, by exact finite-group enumeration with a proved stopping bound. We also give the exact finite-horizon continuation-polytope formulation and a comparison between optimized packet loss and common enclosure dilation. The previous metric Diophantine and Liouville theorems are retained with full proofs. The resource throughout is nonuniform clocked atomic-row label width, not table size or uniform computational space.
\end{abstract}
