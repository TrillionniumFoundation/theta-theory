\begin{abstract}
We study marked causal simulation at a prescribed persistent-state and
randomization budget. For active finite-horizon Gaussian acquisitions,
we construct stateless finite-report and rational marked presentations
and transfer finite nonconvex testing certificates to the original
physical experiment without increasing its register or selector budget.
Joint-prefix rationalization has an explicit feedback bound which does
not divide by rare-history probabilities. An enriched graph-path
estimator has certified errors tending to zero under effective graph
and integration hypotheses, giving convergent intervals for physical
intrinsic deficiency. Certified collision-chart exhaustion verifies
these hypotheses for fixed-particle hard spheres with algebraic kicks
and bounded computable inputs. We also bound testing-witness size by attainable
behavior dimension and relate positive-error task widths to marked
prediction packing and covering numbers. An active hard-sphere example
has exact continuous-report deficiency one quarter, and a localized
collision-compatible observable supplies nonzero rigorously evaluated
Gram residuals. The finite private, hidden-selector and visible-selector
theory, endogenous decision theorem and stationary regenerative theory
are retained with their separate resource and domain hypotheses.
\end{abstract}
