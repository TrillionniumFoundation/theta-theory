## Norberg and Paull–Unger: exact scope of the remaining audit

The v14 bibliography identifies Norberg's *Comparison of statistical experiments with filtered probability spaces* (2002) and Paull–Unger's *Minimizing the number of states in incompletely specified sequential switching functions* (1959). The journal/DOI and publisher endpoints were retried in this revision. The Norberg Oslo preprint index cited by Weisshaupt was also followed. They did not deliver the original full texts. Therefore the following eight comparisons remain **unverified against those originals**: precise hypotheses, decision class, filtration/control interface, hidden-versus-visible randomization, finite-state accounting, zero/positive error conclusions, existence hypotheses, and proof-level residual novelty. No theorem number or absence claim is invented for either inaccessible text.
