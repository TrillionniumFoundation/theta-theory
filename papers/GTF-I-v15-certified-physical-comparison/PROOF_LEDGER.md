## Unified dependency chain

The active microscopic group and its collision domain feed the new constructive graph-data theorem. Enriched defects give analytic error alpha. Actual report quantization gives beta. Weighted marked-prefix rationalization gives rho. The inherited finite same-budget certificate gives [L,U]. Stateless composition proves the main original-physical interval. The independent physical examples instantiate the analytic data and a positive deficiency. None of these arrows invokes the historical nonlinear B4 corrector as an assumption.

## Critical distinctions retained

The finite trial lookup state is part of the approximating physical instrument, not free persistent simulator memory. A mark is never independently resampled to match its marginal. Exposed selectors are compared jointly. Rationalized prefix masses generate kernels but are not asserted to be the generated prefix law. The old raw Galerkin residual sequence is not claimed convergent; the new estimator is proved convergent. The general effective proof uses certified full-measure covers, not unquantified almost-everywhere computability alone. Finite task covers do not imply causal updates or universal marked reconstruction without further hypotheses.

The core construction, packing, separation, subdivision, graph smoothing, variation of constants and real-algebraic elimination have classical ingredients. Full priority against the unavailable Norberg and Paull–Unger originals remains unverified. The paper does not claim the nonlinear kinetic/Boltzmann–Grad historical target from fixed-particle L2 bounds. Source and diagnostic verification is not analytic verification.
