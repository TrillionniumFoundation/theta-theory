\begin{abstract}
We determine the finite-use description geometry of noisy equatorial
qubit readouts uniformly in the noise, including complete erasure and
projective measurement. For public visibility $\lambda$, put
$K_{N,\lambda}=\lambda\min\{N,\sqrt{N/(1-\lambda^2)}\}$, with
$K_{N,1}=N$. The adaptive distance between angles separated by $h$
is comparable to $\min\{1,K_{N,\lambda}h\}$ with absolute constants.
For every sufficiently small error $\delta$, the optimal reusable
classical payload is therefore
$\log_2(1+K_{N,\lambda}/\delta)+O(1)$, uniformly in all three
parameters. The upper proof uses an exact two-point classical programme;
the lower proof uses finite entangled blocks and an elementary product
estimate. It does not infer a finite-distance law from Fisher
information. Exact rational circle charts give legal decoded effects.
Appending two rank-bounded conditional states contributes
$(V/2)\log_2N+V\log_2(1/\delta)$, where $V$ is their rank-stratum
dimension. These results quantify the approach from full-rank readout
to the projective boundary. We also retain the observable projective
flag, intrinsic instrument and uniformly seizable state-family coding
laws with their distinct hypotheses and error ranges. Error is the
unhalved trace norm maximized over common reference-assisted adaptive
testers with feedback and bounded stopping. Noise-crossover and
programme-state methods are established antecedents; the statements here
concern supplied finite-use descriptions and exact legal rational codes,
not unknown-device learning, mutable workspace or processor hardware.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textit{Operational interfaces.}
Description compilation returns a mathematical quantum instrument;
numerical streaming processes fully specified matrix data. Neither is a
physical classical device acting on an unknown quantum input, and
neither is merely a transcript-only simulation task. The new code lengths
charge the reusable description, with fixed public dimensions, rank bounds where applicable, horizon,
error and visibility. Visibility may depend on the horizon but is not an
unknown target coordinate charged in the new payload.
All joint-state errors
are unhalved trace norms; transcript total variation is half the classical
block trace norm.
