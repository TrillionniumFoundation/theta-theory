**Date:** 23 September 2026. This audit distinguishes inspected original theorem text, inherited inspection and unavailable full originals. It is not a universal priority certificate. Third-party articles are not copied into the repository.

## Direct filtered-comparison crosswalk

Heinz Weisshaupt, *LeCam's randomization criterion in the setting of locally convex spaces without lattice structure*, EURANDOM report 2002–017, dated 22 April 2002:
https://www.eurandom.tue.nl/reports/2002/017-report.pdf

The title page, filtered-operator definition, Lemma 1 and Theorem 5/proof passage (printed pp. 14–16) were inspected, including page images. Lemma 1 establishes compactness for filtered stochastic operators in a bounded-test weak topology. Theorem 5 uses a compact convex operator family and finite test inequalities to obtain a single filtered stochastic operator with the prescribed errors. Its target is a finitely additive measure space `ba`, with no prescribed persistent-state budget. This is a directly relevant prior filtered randomization theorem, not merely an abstract mentioning filtrations.

The v14 comparison is exact at the declared resource: the private image can be nonconvex, and the delayed rank-three channel distinguishes that image from its hidden convex hull. The local test-cover theorem certifies the private set without claiming that a single convex separator has this power. On finite carriers the distinction already persists, independently of any countable-versus-finite-additivity issue. The v14 theorem does not replace Weisshaupt's general locally convex result or claim its filtered compactness argument as new.

## Classical subtraction

Separate-affine vertex interpolation, dyadic subdivision, quantifier elimination, convex minimax, nonnegative factorization, geometric regeneration, graph-core approximation and variation of constants are not claimed as new general techniques. The precise combined assertion submitted for review is original-budget local marked-feedback testing completeness with an executable upper witness and explicit causal modulus, stable under priced causal comparison and supplied with an active microscopic a posteriori realization. Whether this exact result has top-four-scale priority and depth remains an independent scholarly judgment.
