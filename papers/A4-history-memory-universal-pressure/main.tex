\begin{abstract}
The complete-history kernel is controlled by distinct Lyapunov, distortion,
and bounded-metric scales. This yields a synchronized small set without
claiming that a Lyapunov tail is uniformly truncatable. Analytic
Feynman--Kac theory and the rough-path Poisson equation are developed on the
same weighted Lipschitz space. The exact memory equation is obtained from the
closed block generator: its kernel is
$PLQe^{tQLQ}QLP$, its transform has the generic leading term
$z^{-1}PLQLP$, and exponential decay follows from the orthogonal semigroup.
No residue at a regular point is used.
\end{abstract}
