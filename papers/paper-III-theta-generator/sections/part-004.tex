\section*{Truth boundary}
The exact DPP is canonical on the one-sided quotient state.  Finite
microcanonical conditioning supplies an asymptotic preparation, not an exact
finite Markov law.  The theorem uses an iterated or diagonal preparation limit
and is local in the current field.  External review remains pending.
