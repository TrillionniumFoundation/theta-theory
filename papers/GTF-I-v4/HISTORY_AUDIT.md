## How the programme changes the present mathematics

The programme's T07 identifies posterior quantization as a checkpoint question. T08 adds a causal update obligation and does not infer it from a static cover. This distinction motivates the new posterior-orbit sandwich, the `Nn+1` charged realization, the exponent theorem, and the exact autonomous shift-closure criterion. The new theorem does not assume a contracting reduced update: it constructs a finite update and pays for its depth.

G1 and G3 ask for actual acquired laws, causal transport, and simultaneous statistical/resource risk rather than independently postulated terms. The noisy torus experiment supplies a single likelihood and posterior, with an exact Bayes floor and matched finite-state excess profile. Independent renewal lengths are part of the model, not inferred from an unproved mixing statement. Nongeometric laws are allowed under the explicit residual-life bound; weak tail hypotheses are never promoted into exponential tails.

T10 and G2 ask for a physically specified noise law and derived singular modulus. Modern A2 v112's native contact and statistical sections distinguish actual score experiments, independent measurement errors, finite precision, and unknown calibration. The new root-moment pair makes these choices explicit. Its changing-rank inverse modulus follows from Newton identities and root counts, and its lower bound from moment-cancelling configurations. It is neither a substitute for A2's determinantal-geometry theorem nor a transfer of its local Poisson experiment without an experiment-comparison proof.

## Preservation and dependency discipline

The build hashes all inherited compilation inputs, checks the exact preserved legacy/A1/A2 Git trees in the remote checkout, verifies the byte-identical v2 quantitative body, and requires every retained mathematical label to resolve in the new PDF. The mathematical contribution of this round is proved in the new principal sections. It advances the programme's causal-realization and actual singular-statistical interfaces. It does not represent the unresolved historical spectral, LDP, kinetic, or operator statements as proved, and none is needed to validate the new proofs. A future closure of one of those model-specific gates must be established by its own argument, not by changing a status field in this audit.
