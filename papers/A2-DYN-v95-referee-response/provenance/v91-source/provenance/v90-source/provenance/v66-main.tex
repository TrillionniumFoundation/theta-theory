\begin{abstract}
We study the joint displacement, collision count and flight time of
actual returns in a triangular periodic Lorentz gas. The complete
arithmetic return law, its common Gaussian shape through changes of
arithmetic type and its coupled collision--return bridges are retained.
We develop the geometry needed for pointwise inversion on the same
physical source.

For normal-to-normal collision words we prove a count-uniform
selected-contact continuation under an exponentially tapered incidence
bound. Competing-hit and section boundaries may cross this chart.
An incidence-normalized tridiagonal inverse gives uniform contact jets,
Morse radii and relative source distortion. Keeping the nonlinear
physical decisions exactly, we obtain a multiplicative angular formula
for the original first-clearance density. Its coefficient is the
reversible square-root determinant of the original half-word.

A positive comparison with the two-endpoint local law gives an ordered
central-scale bound for the resulting finite-width physical collar
trace. This comparison permits coincident critical values and changing
exact labels, and uses no word-count multiplier. The zero-width
observable trace has the additional physical angular fraction. An exact
positive inequality separates it from the finite-width trace by a
specified angular-loss term. That term, the complete incidence height
and the positive source outside the uniform charts are not estimated
here. They remain distinct inputs to the unrestricted pointwise
arithmetic raw-density theorem; no order of limits is interchanged.
\end{abstract}

\paragraph{The record and the pointwise problem.}
Let $J_{n,R}=(K_{n,R},N_{n,R},T_{n,R})$ be the record of $n$ actual
returns of the circular triangular Lorentz gas to the original section
$Y_R^*$, under $\nu_R^*=c_*^{-1}\nu|_{Y_R^*}$, where
$c_*=91/(10000\pi)$ and $R\in[9/20,47/100]$. Its density
$p_{n,R}(k,m,t)$ uses counting measure in $(k,m)\in\Z^2\times\{n,n+1,\ldots\}$
and Lebesgue measure in the roof $t$. Neither a collision nor a return
is removed. The joint occupation convention counts $0,\ldots,m-1$;
terminal membership is checked separately at collision $m$.

The arithmetic transition kernel $\mathcal L_{m,R}$ retains its finite
phases and damped residues. The complete record theorem
(Theorem~\ref{thm:intro-v58-record}) concerns the untruncated law in
variation, with coupled path conclusions in the stated path-dual norm.
For each fixed $M$, put
$Z_{m,R}=(k_1,k_2,t-m\bar\tau_R,n-mc_*)/\sqrt m$.
The stronger pointwise target, as $m\to\infty$, is
\[
 \sup_{R,n,k}\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{t:\ |Z_{m,R}|\le M}
 \left|m^2p_{n,R}(k,m,t)-\mathcal L_{m,R}(k_1,k_2,t,n)\right|
 \longrightarrow0.
\]
The positive raw-error identity requires the complete ordered incidence
and clearance heights for this target. The present geometric results
apply directly to those original sources; they do not replace the
pointwise problem by a different observable.

\paragraph{A chart may cross a physical boundary.}
Put $q=47/53$, $d_j=\min(j,m-j)$ and $e_j=q^{d_j/4}$. A
normal-to-normal word $z$ in the physical-side closure is
$\chi$-incident when its selected incidence cosines satisfy
$c_j(z)\ge\chi e_j$, $0<\chi\le1/10$. No lower bound is imposed
on its clearance or section margins. Its selected contact equations
can therefore continue beyond the physical word. Every such nonphysical
point will have zero source weight. This distinction allows uniform
coordinates without replacing the physical orbit space by an overcover.

\begin{leadtheorem}[Uniform charts and the physical angular density]
\label{thm:intro-v66-profile}
There are constants $r_0,C>0$, independent of the collision count,
such that every $\chi$-incident word has a Morse chart on
$|y|<r_\chi=r_0\chi^3$ with
\[
 F_z=t_z+|y|^2/2,\qquad
 \left|\log\frac{2\pi\widetilde w_z(y)}{J_z}\right|
          \le K_\chi|y|,\qquad K_\chi=C\chi^{-2}.
\]
Here $\widetilde w_z$ is the original selected-coordinate density
before imposing physical restrictions, and
\[
 J_z=\frac{1}{R c_*\sqrt{|\det(I-SM_z^{-1}SM_z)|}},
       \qquad S=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1).
\]
The matrix $M_z$ is the original canonical half-word derivative,
or its physical-side branch value at a seam. There is no primitive-period
quotient, and no label is read from the doubled word.

For $0<\varepsilon\le\chi/4$ let
$A_{z,n,k}^{\varepsilon}(h)$ be the angular integral of the exact
physical word indicator, exact return label and unchanged first-clearance
multiplier on $|y|=\sqrt{2h}$, as in
\eqref{eq:v66-nonlinear-angular}. Then $\sum_{n,k}A_{z,n,k}^{\varepsilon}\le2\pi$
and the original restricted density satisfies, almost everywhere,
\[
 e^{-K_\chi\sqrt{2h}}\frac{J_z}{2\pi}A_{z,n,k}^{\varepsilon}(h)
 \le b_{z,n,k}^{\varepsilon,\mathrm{clr}}(t_z+h)
 \le e^{K_\chi\sqrt{2h}}\frac{J_z}{2\pi}A_{z,n,k}^{\varepsilon}(h),
 \quad 0<h<r_\chi^2/2.
\]
The constants require no boundary-gradient lower bound or angle between
boundary gradients. The nonlinear angular sets themselves are retained,
not replaced by a uniformly evaluated affine profile.
\end{leadtheorem}
\begin{proof}
The weighted inverse in Lemma~\ref{lem:v66-weighted-inverse} gives
Theorems~\ref{thm:v66-contact-jets} and~\ref{thm:v66-uniform-morse}.
Theorem~\ref{thm:v65-reversible-weight} identifies the coefficient.
Theorem~\ref{thm:v66-nonlinear-profile} applies positive polar coarea
with the exact source indicators.
\end{proof}

\begin{leadtheorem}[The ordered physical collar trace]
\label{thm:intro-v66-trace}
For a family of these charts, each counted once, define
\[
 \mathcal Q_{m,s;n,k,R}
 =\sum_z\left(\frac1s\int_0^s
           b_{z,n,k}^{\varepsilon,\mathrm{clr}}(t_z+u)\,du\right)\delta_{t_z}.
\]
For each fixed $\chi,\varepsilon$, $0<s<r_\chi^2/2$ and $h>0$,
\[
 \limsup_{m\to\infty}\sup_{R,n,k,t}m^2
          \mathcal Q_{m,s;n,k,R}([t-h,t+h])\le C(2h+s).
\]
The constant is independent of the permitted widths. The estimate
keeps the two exact integer labels and permits coincident critical
values and section-boundary coincidences. In particular the expression
with $h=s$ tends to zero when $s\downarrow0$ after the collision limit.
\end{leadtheorem}
\begin{proof}
Every contributing physical collar lies in the same exact-label event,
in a roof interval of length $2h+s$, and in two normal endpoint strips
of width $C\sqrt s$. The collars are disjoint. Their positive comparison
with Lemma~\ref{lem:v45-two-strip-local} gives
Theorem~\ref{thm:v66-ordered-collar-trace}.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{The zero-width distinction.}
At a clearance seam the fixed-count limiting coefficient is
$\beta_{z,n,k}=J_z\theta_{z,n,k}$, where $\theta_{z,n,k}$ is the
physical and exact-label angular fraction. The resulting atomic trace
$\mathcal B_m$ satisfies
\[
 \mathcal B_m(I)\le e^{K_\chi\sqrt{2s}}\mathcal Q_{m,s}(I)
                            +\mathcal D_{m,s}(I),
\]
where $\mathcal D$ is the positive loss of the actual angular set
relative to its tangent angular fraction, defined in
\eqref{eq:v66-angular-loss}. Proposition~\ref{prop:v66-atomic-collar-comparison}
states the additional uniform estimate needed to pass to $\mathcal B_m$.
That estimate is not supplied by bounded contact jets. Nor does
$\mathcal B_m$ equal the larger trace of all full coefficients $J_z$.
The complete first-incidence source and the positive clearance source
outside the charts remain in
\eqref{eq:v66-complete-positive-partition} and in the original raw-error
identity. In particular the two preceding theorems do not assert the
unrestricted pointwise local limit or its same-roof conditional laws.

\paragraph{Proof route and relation to the preceding article.}
The new proof route is
Sections~\ref{sec:v66-contact-continuation}--\ref{sec:v66-collar-trace}.
It uses the contact matrix and source normalization of
Sections~\ref{sec:v45-critical-collars} and~\ref{sec:v65-reversible-critical},
and the inherited two-endpoint local law in
Section~\ref{sec:v45-critical-deconcentration}. No periodic-orbit
counting theorem is substituted for that positive comparison. The
square-root weight retains the action--monodromy relation discussed
in \cite{BT65}; the new ingredients are the incidence-normalized
weighted inverse, uniform selected-contact jets across physical seams,
and the exact physical collar trace.

All preceding core proofs, the arithmetic return law, the independent
model realizations and their scopes remain in the article. The previous
abstract and leading statements are compiled verbatim in
Appendix~\ref{app:v66-retained-front}; the earlier appendices also remain.
The effective finite-count budgets of
Section~\ref{sec:v63-positive-localization} are retained but not used in
the new uniform argument. Their threshold
$4K_m\varepsilon\le(\chi h)^{E_m}$ fails eventually for fixed
$\varepsilon>0$ and $\chi,h\le1$. No choice of a growing reconstruction
band is made here.

\paragraph{The retained return-window and bridge route.}
The new local constraint is proved in
Sections~\ref{sec:v37-occupation-local} and~\ref{sec:v37-return-window}.
It uses the exact section geometry, the inherited compensated
covariance and its nondegeneracy, and the compact collision spectrum
of the preceding part. The return index is resolved on its diffusive scale;
single-index inversion and pointwise roof inversion remain distinct
from that theorem. Sections~\ref{sec:v38-four-dimensional-concentration}
and~\ref{sec:v38-return-resolution} then replace the diffusive window
by any diverging integer block, prove a single-index upper bound, and
locate the exact inverse on a fixed occupation torus. The new
Sections~\ref{sec:v40-occupation-powers}--\ref{sec:v40-fixed-count-local}
then give a full fixed-band spectral reduction and evaluate the
fixed-radius interval law with its arithmetic coefficient. Sections~
\ref{sec:v41-transition}--\ref{sec:v41-residue-filters} then evaluate
the uniform transition kernel and prove the exact-event return bridge. The
complete inherited arithmetic, raw edge and return-frequency analysis
continues on the same physical record.
