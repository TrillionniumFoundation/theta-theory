\begin{abstract}
We study exact displacement, collision count and flight time for
actual returns in a triangular periodic Lorentz gas. A positive
finite-scale angular-overlap construction separates radial growth
from angular loss on the original physical source. Its height is
controlled by one physical collar trace, without a radial-ratio
cutoff or a moment hypothesis. For angular occupation with no loss
to an outer annulus, this gives the complete inner-collar estimate,
including zero-center guards and later positive-radius births.
For each fixed word we identify the signed radial current of the
exact physical angular set and retain its jump contributions.
On the whole selected Morse disk the overlap part has ordered
height $O(\chi^6)$. We insert this bound into the original pointwise
arithmetic error identity, retaining explicitly the angular loss,
the uncovered physical source and the first-incidence source.
\end{abstract}

\paragraph{The original local problem.}
For $R\in[9/20,47/100]$ the actual return record is
$J_{n,R}=(K_{n,R},N_{n,R},T_{n,R})$ under
$\nu_R^*=c_*^{-1}\nu|_{Y_R^*}$, with $c_*=91/(10000\pi)$.
Its density $p_{n,R}(k,m,t)$ uses counting measure in $(k,m)$
and Lebesgue measure in $t$. Occupation counts collisions
$0,\ldots,m-1$; terminal membership is imposed at collision $m$.
The pointwise problem throughout this article is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:intro-v70-pointwise-target}
 \sup_{R,n,k}\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{t:\,|Z_{m,R}|\le M}
 \left|m^2p_{n,R}(k,m,t)-\mathcal L_{m,R}(k_1,k_2,t,n)\right|
 \longrightarrow0,
\end{equation}
where $Z_{m,R}=(k_1,k_2,t-m\bar\tau_R,n-mc_*)/\sqrt m$.
The finite arithmetic transition kernel in $\mathcal L_{m,R}$,
including its zero classes, is unchanged. The results below concern
the positive sources entering this exact pointwise problem.

\paragraph{Angular overlap.}
At a selected critical chart write $F_z=t_z+|y|^2/2$,
$r_\chi=r_0\chi^3$, $h_\chi=r_\chi^2/2$, and $K_\chi=C\chi^{-2}$.
For the complete physical indicator with exact label $\lambda=(n,k)$,
put
\[
 L_z(u)=\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}
                 B_{z,\lambda}(\sqrt{2u}\,\omega_\phi)\,d\phi.
\]
The guard is still $D_z^\varepsilon$, with center $g_z\in[0,1]$
and Lipschitz constant $C_g\varepsilon^{-1}$. Given a fixed receiving
interval $J\subset(0,h_\chi)$, form
\[
 a_{z,J}(u)=\frac1{|J|}\int_J\min\{1,L_z(v)/L_z(u)\}\,dv,
 \qquad
 \Delta_{z,J}(u)=\frac1{|J|}\int_J(L_z(u)-L_z(v))_+\,dv.
\]
The first expression is set to one when $L_z(u)=0$; the corresponding
source is then zero. Multiplication of the original clearance source
by $a_{z,J}$ and $1-a_{z,J}$ gives complementary positive weights.
No point is transported to a nonphysical continuation, and no section
decision is changed.

\begin{leadtheorem}[Full inner collar with no angular loss]
\label{thm:intro-v70-no-loss}
Fix $0<\varepsilon\le\chi/4$, $2H<h_\chi$ and $0\le\delta\le1$.
On any selected subfamily with $g_z\le\delta$ and
$L_z(u)\le L_z(v)$ for almost every $(u,v)\in(0,H)\times(H,2H)$,
the entire original clearance source on $0<F_z-t_z<H$ satisfies
\[
 \mathcal H(b)\le6CH e^{(2+\sqrt2)K_\chi\sqrt H}
       \min\{1,\delta+C_g\varepsilon^{-1}\sqrt{2H}\}.
\]
Here $\mathcal H$ is the ordered $m^2$ essential height. The bound
requires no analytic-germ radius, birth-order bound or radial moment.
At zero center guard its order is $\varepsilon^{-1}H^{3/2}$.
\end{leadtheorem}
\begin{proof}
The loss $\Delta_{z,(H,2H)}$ vanishes. The scalar overlap is bounded
by the outer angular average, which the relative physical Jacobian
compares with the unchanged positive collar trace. The contributing
centers occupy an interval of length $H$. This gives the displayed
constant by Corollary~\ref{cor:v70-no-loss-height}.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Loss and physical boundaries.}
The no-loss hypothesis is not imposed on all physical words. For an
arbitrary angular profile the same comparison controls its overlap
part, while the remaining positive density is bounded by its exact
$\Delta_{z,J}$. Theorem~\ref{thm:v70-physical-current} proves, at
every fixed word and on every compact radial interval, that $L_z$ is
of bounded variation and
\[
 DL_z=(2\pi)^{-1}\pi_\#D_u\one_{E_{z,\lambda}},
 \qquad E_{z,\lambda}=\{(u,\phi):B_{z,\lambda}(\sqrt{2u}\omega_\phi)=1\}.
\]
Its negative part bounds loss to an outer collar. Radial deaths are
retained as measure atoms, rather than omitted from a classical
derivative. Fixed-word finiteness does not supply an ordered weighted
tail estimate.

\begin{leadtheorem}[The complete selected disk]
\label{thm:intro-v70-whole-chart}
With $I=(0,h_\chi)$ and $J=(0,h_\chi/2)$, the original clearance
source has an exact positive weight decomposition
\[
 b^{\varepsilon,\mathrm{clr}}=s_{70}+d_{70}+e_{70},\qquad
 \mathcal H(s_{70})\le\frac32Ch_\chi
                   e^{(1+1/\sqrt2)K_\chi r_\chi}\le C'\chi^6.
\]
Here $s_{70}$ is the overlap part on every selected physical disk,
$d_{70}$ is its complementary angular-loss part, and $e_{70}$ is
the original source outside those disks. This allocation includes all
outer annuli of the selected disks, not merely their analytic-germ
neighborhoods.
\end{leadtheorem}
\begin{proof}
The complete interval comparison, with receiving width $h_\chi/2$,
has center-interval plus collar length $3h_\chi/2$. Sum the
complementary weights before adding the original outside event.
The full argument is Theorem~\ref{thm:v70-whole-chart-allocation}.
\end{proof}

For $\varepsilon(B)=A_0B^{-1/12}$ and
$\chi(B)=\sqrt{\varepsilon(B)}$, the controlled overlap height is
$O(B^{-1/4})$. These geometric choices are fixed before the collision
limsup. The complete endpoint estimate is
\eqref{eq:v70-ordered-raw-budget}; it retains the heights of $d_{70}$,
$e_{70}$ and the complete first-incidence source. Their vanishing,
and hence \eqref{eq:intro-v70-pointwise-target} and its unrestricted
same-roof consequences, are not asserted by the overlap theorem.
The new argument isolates angular loss rather than treating a large
radial ratio itself as an obstruction.

The principal proof is in
Sections~\ref{sec:v70-angular-overlap}--\ref{sec:v70-whole-chart}.
Its quantitative input is the unchanged physical collar trace of
Theorem~\ref{thm:v69-comparison-collar}, proved on the same exact
record. The signed-current identification additionally uses the
analytic physical decisions at a fixed word. All preceding mathematical
modules are retained below; the preceding opening statements appear
in Appendix~\ref{app:v70-retained-front}.

\paragraph{The retained return-window and bridge route.}
The new local constraint is proved in
Sections~\ref{sec:v37-occupation-local} and~\ref{sec:v37-return-window}.
It uses the exact section geometry, the inherited compensated
covariance and its nondegeneracy, and the compact collision spectrum
of the preceding part. The return index is resolved on its diffusive scale;
single-index inversion and pointwise roof inversion remain distinct
from that theorem. Sections~\ref{sec:v38-four-dimensional-concentration}
and~\ref{sec:v38-return-resolution} then replace the diffusive window
by any diverging integer block, prove a single-index upper bound, and
locate the exact inverse on a fixed occupation torus. The new
Sections~\ref{sec:v40-occupation-powers}--\ref{sec:v40-fixed-count-local}
then give a full fixed-band spectral reduction and evaluate the
fixed-radius interval law with its arithmetic coefficient. Sections~
\ref{sec:v41-transition}--\ref{sec:v41-residue-filters} then evaluate
the uniform transition kernel and prove the exact-event return bridge. The
complete inherited arithmetic, raw edge and return-frequency analysis
continues on the same physical record.
