\section{Recovery of every physical defect and a quantitative height bound}
\label{sec:v72-all-source-recovery}
We now apply a receiving capacity to the complete physical source,
not only to the selected disks. The preceding recovered source is
retained. The new allocation acts also on first incidence and on the
outside source, using only a bounded weight on the same original
exact-label event.

For a family of exact-label densities, the ordered height used
throughout this part is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v72-ordered-height-definition}
 \mathcal H(f)=\limsup_{m\to\infty}
   \sup_{\substack{R\in I,\,1\le n\le m\\k\in\Z^2}}
                          m^2\|f_{n,k,m,R}\|_{L^\infty(\R)},
 \qquad I=[9/20,47/100].
\end{equation}
Let $\mathcal E_M$ denote the collision limsup of the left side of
\eqref{eq:intro-v72-target}. Every density norm is an essential
supremum. Suppressed width and reserve parameters are fixed before
this collision limsup. The term $e_{70}$ always refers to the
original first-clearance source outside the selected disks; incidence
is retained separately, not counted in that term.

\subsection{A fixed-width capacity on the full source}
Let $b=b^\varepsilon_{n,k,m,R}$ be
\eqref{eq:v72-complete-source-density}, set $s=s_{71,1}$, and write
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v72-original-residual}
 r=b-s=d_{71,1}+b^{\varepsilon,\mathrm{inc}}+e_{70}\ge0.
\end{equation}
Fix $\delta>0$ and $K\ge1$ before the collision limit, and put
$q=A_\delta b$. At almost every roof define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v72-allocation}
 \alpha(t)=
 \begin{cases}
 \min\{1,(Kq(t)-s(t))_+/r(t)\},&r(t)>0,\\
 0,&r(t)=0,
 \end{cases}
 \qquad s_{72,K}=s+\alpha r,\quad r_{72,K}=(1-\alpha)r.
\end{equation}
The values on exceptional coarea levels are immaterial.

\begin{lemma}[Recovery preserving an already controlled source]
\label{lem:v72-preserved-recovery}
For nonnegative finite $b,s,q$ with $s\le b$, the preceding
allocation satisfies
\begin{align}
 s_{72,K}&=\min\{b,\max(s,Kq)\},
                  &s\le s_{72,K}\le s+Kq,
                                      \label{eq:v72-controlled-algebra}\\
 r_{72,K}&=[b-\max(s,Kq)]_+,
                  &0\le r_{72,K}\le\min\{r,[b-Kq]_+\}.
                                      \label{eq:v72-residual-algebra}
\end{align}
Their sum is exactly $b$. The controlled source is nondecreasing
in $K$ and the residual is nonincreasing. Where $r>0$, $\alpha$
is maximal among $a\in[0,1]$ for which
$s+ar\le\max(s,Kq)$. At $r=0$ its value is immaterial.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If $Kq\le s$, no residual is recovered. If $s<Kq<b$, the
recovered amount is $Kq-s$. If $Kq\ge b$, the entire residual
is recovered. These three cases prove both identities and all bounds.
They also prove monotonicity and the stated scalar maximality.
\end{proof}

The allocation is realized on the physical source. Let $W_s,W_r$
be its existing complementary weights, so that their pushforwards
have densities $s,r$. Replace them by
$W_s+\alpha(L_{m,R})W_r$ and
$(1-\alpha(L_{m,R}))W_r$. These are nonnegative complementary
weights with the same sum, the same section normalization and the
same exact labels. In particular
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v72-three-residuals}
 r_{72,K}=(1-\alpha)d_{71,1}
       +(1-\alpha)b^{\varepsilon,\mathrm{inc}}+(1-\alpha)e_{70}.
\end{equation}
Every original physical state is still accounted for. The three terms
on the right need not vanish. The allocation borrows no capacity from
a different $m,R,n,k$ and makes no transport of physical trajectories.
The interval average is an auxiliary upper capacity; the law being
estimated and the recovered source are not roof-window laws.

\begin{theorem}[Ordered recovery on the complete physical source]
\label{thm:v72-global-recovery}
For fixed $0<\varepsilon\le\chi/4$, $0<\chi\le1/10$,
$\delta>0$ and $K\ge1$, the sources in
\eqref{eq:v72-allocation} satisfy
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v72-global-controlled-height}
 \mathcal H(s_{72,K})\le C\chi^6+CK\varepsilon^{1/16}.
\end{equation}
The constants do not depend on $\delta$, the collision count, the
exact label, the number of words, or the number of first-defect
witnesses. Here $\varepsilon,\chi,\delta,K$ are fixed before
the collision limsup.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For each fixed auxiliary band $B_*$, apply
\eqref{eq:v49-physical-window-bound} with interval length $2\delta$
and divide by $2\delta$. It gives the finite-count bound
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v72-finite-average-height}
 \begin{split}
 \sup_{R,n,k}m^2\|q\|_\infty
 \le{}&C\varepsilon^{1/16}\left(1+\frac1{2\delta B_*}\right)\\
 &+C_{B_*}\frac{1+2\delta B_*}{2\delta}
                            m^3\rho_{B_*}^{m/2}.
 \end{split}
\end{equation}
First let $m\to\infty$ with $\delta,B_*,\varepsilon$ fixed.
Then let $B_*\to\infty$, still with $\delta$ fixed. This proves
$\mathcal H(q)\le C\varepsilon^{1/16}$. This is only a bound
for the average, not for $b$. Theorem~\ref{thm:v71-pooled-height}
with reserve one gives $\mathcal H(s)\le C\chi^6$. Finally use
$s_{72,K}\le s+Kq$ from
\eqref{eq:v72-controlled-algebra}. The averaging coefficient is
used only in a positive source comparison; no spectral norm of that
coefficient is required.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The complete excess as a global current}
The quantity
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v72-complete-current-excess}
 \mathcal U_{\varepsilon,\delta}(K)
 =\limsup_{m\to\infty}\sup_{R,n,k}m^2
   \left\|\left[\kappa_\delta*\mathfrak D^\varepsilon_{n,k,m,R}
                        -(K-1)A_\delta b\right]_+\right\|_\infty
\end{equation}
is well-defined as an extended nonnegative number. Its integrand
is $[b-KA_\delta b]_+$ by
Theorem~\ref{thm:v72-current-primitive}. Thus every signed physical
flux, including all moving boundaries, is combined at the same roof
before a positive part is taken. This definition is not a claim that
the ordered excess is small.

\begin{theorem}[A complete physical-current budget]
\label{thm:v72-complete-budget}
For every sufficiently large fixed $B$, choose
\[
 \varepsilon(B)=A_0B^{-1/12},\qquad
 \chi(B)=\sqrt{\varepsilon(B)},\qquad
 \delta(B)>0,\qquad K(B)\ge1.
\]
All four choices precede the collision limsup. Then the original
arithmetic raw error satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v72-complete-budget}
 \mathcal E_M\le C_MB^{-1/192}+CB^{-1/4}
       +CK(B)B^{-1/192}
       +\mathcal U_{\varepsilon(B),\delta(B)}(K(B)).
\end{equation}
Consequently the unrestricted two-sided arithmetic pointwise law
follows if $K(B)B^{-1/192}\to0$ and the last term tends to zero.
This is a sufficient condition on the complete original source,
not an assertion that the Lorentz family already satisfies it.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The exact positive-remainder representation
\eqref{eq:v51-positive-error} bounds the original error by
$C_MB^{-1/192}+\mathcal H(b)$. Use
$b=s_{72,K}+r_{72,K}$, Theorem~\ref{thm:v72-global-recovery},
and the pointwise inequality
$r_{72,K}\le[b-KA_\delta b]_+$. The stated substitutions give
$\chi^6=A_0^3B^{-1/4}$ and
$\varepsilon^{1/16}=A_0^{1/16}B^{-1/192}$. This proves the
budget. Only after this fixed-band limit may $B\to\infty$.
The original finite arithmetic transition kernel, including its zero
classes, has not been changed.
\end{proof}

There is no assertion that \eqref{eq:v72-complete-budget} is
numerically smaller than the three-term budget of
Corollary~\ref{cor:v71-raw-budget}. Its gain is different: it
recovers portions of all three physical remainders, preserves the
old controlled source, and gives one partition-independent current
on which cancellations can be studied. Taking $K=1$ retains the
$B^{-1/192}$ controlled rate. A growing reserve must now pay that
rate, not the selected-disk rate $B^{-1/4}$.

\subsection{A quantitative local-flux sufficient estimate}
Suppose, additionally, that $\mathfrak D^\varepsilon_{n,k,m,R}$
is a finite signed measure for each indicated finite count. Define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v72-directed-flux-height}
 \begin{split}
 \mathcal V_\varepsilon(\delta)=\limsup_m\sup_{R,n,k,t}m^2
 \bigl[& (\mathfrak D^\varepsilon)^+([t-\delta,t])\\
       &+(\mathfrak D^\varepsilon)^-([t,t+\delta])\bigr].
 \end{split}
\end{equation}
The Jordan parts here are those of the complete physically weighted
current, not sums of wordwise variations. The finite-measure
hypothesis is explicit and is not inferred from the distributional
construction.

\begin{proposition}[Local flux controls essential height]
\label{prop:v72-flux-height}
Under this finite-measure hypothesis,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v72-flux-height}
 \mathcal U_{\varepsilon,\delta}(K)
           \le\mathcal U_{\varepsilon,\delta}(1)
           \le\tfrac12\mathcal V_\varepsilon(\delta)
                  \qquad(K\ge1).
\end{equation}
If, for some $A<\infty$, $q\ge0$ and $\beta>0$, one proves
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v72-flux-power-hypothesis}
 \mathcal V_\varepsilon(\delta)
                  \le A\varepsilon^{-q}\delta^\beta
\end{equation}
for every sufficiently small fixed $\varepsilon,\delta$, then
for any $a>0$ the choice
$K(B)=1$, $\delta(B)=\varepsilon(B)^{(q+a)/\beta}$ gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v72-flux-power-consequence}
 \mathcal E_M\le C_MB^{-1/192}+CB^{-1/4}+C_aB^{-a/12}.
\end{equation}
The limit order remains fixed $B$, then $m\to\infty$, then
$B\to\infty$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
A finite measure $Db$ makes $b$ a $BV$ function. At its continuity
points, the convolution in \eqref{eq:v72-current-primitive} has
positive kernel on currents below $t$ and negative kernel on currents
above $t$. Both kernel magnitudes are at most $1/2$. The positive
part is therefore at most
\[
 \tfrac12\bigl[(Db)^+([t-\delta,t])+(Db)^-([t,t+\delta])\bigr].
\]
The excluded jump points are countable. The inequality thus bounds
the essential height; common versions are fixed before taking the
suprema. Subtracting the nonnegative $(K-1)A_\delta b$ can only
decrease the positive part. This proves \eqref{eq:v72-flux-height}.
Under \eqref{eq:v72-flux-power-hypothesis}, the proposed width gives
$\mathcal V_{\varepsilon(B)}(\delta(B))\le A\varepsilon(B)^a$.
Insert this bound in \eqref{eq:v72-complete-budget}.
\end{proof}

Neither the finite-measure hypothesis for the full source nor
\eqref{eq:v72-flux-power-hypothesis} is proved for the unrestricted
Lorentz family here. A bounded variation statement for each truncated
word does not supply either missing ordered estimate. For example,
the scalar densities
$m^{-2}\one_{(0,e^{-m})}$ have vanishing normalized mass on each
fixed window but normalized essential height one; their two boundary
atoms retain order-one normalized local variation. This is a test
against an invalid limit exchange, not a Lorentz counterexample.

Thus the complete first-incidence and outside-source requests remain
part of the same full problem. Their recovered portions now have the
unconditional height estimate \eqref{eq:v72-global-controlled-height};
their remaining positive portions are displayed in
\eqref{eq:v72-three-residuals}. No source is discarded and no
unrestricted height is declared proved. Same-roof bridges, forward
essential likelihoods and pointwise roof-conditioned path laws still
require the remaining scalar estimate and their inherited numerator
hypotheses. Conditioning on a zero arithmetic reference class remains
undefined. The full pointwise target is retained throughout.
