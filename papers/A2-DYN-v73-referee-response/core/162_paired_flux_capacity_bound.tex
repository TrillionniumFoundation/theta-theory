\section{Paired directed flux at a common roof}
\label{sec:v73-paired-flux}
A bound on the sum of directed Jordan masses charges an isolated
entrance atom even when the neighboring average already pays for
its height. A reserve of two permits a different estimate. It
requires incoming and outgoing flux near the same roof, and hence
does not demand that each genuine physical jump disappear.

\begin{lemma}[A two-sided averaging inequality]
\label{lem:v73-paired-flux}
Let $f\ge0$ be compactly supported and in $BV(\R)$, and let
$\delta>0$. Then for almost every $t$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v73-paired-flux}
 [f(t)-2A_\delta f(t)]_+
 \le\min\{(Df)^+([t-\delta,t]),(Df)^-([t,t+\delta])\}.
\end{equation}
The same upper bound applies to $[f-KA_\delta f]_+$ for $K\ge2$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use the usual BV representative and exclude its countable jump set.
Put
\[
 L_\delta f(t)=\delta^{-1}\int_{t-\delta}^t f(u)\,du,
 \qquad R_\delta f(t)=\delta^{-1}\int_t^{t+\delta}f(u)\,du.
\]
Integration of the increment formula for $f$ gives
\begin{align*}
 f(t)-L_\delta f(t)
   &=\int_{(t-\delta,t)}\frac{u-t+\delta}{\delta}\,Df(du),\\
 f(t)-R_\delta f(t)
   &=-\int_{(t,t+\delta)}\frac{t+\delta-u}{\delta}\,Df(du).
\end{align*}
The weights lie in $[0,1]$. Since $L_\delta f,R_\delta f\ge0$
and $2A_\delta f=L_\delta f+R_\delta f$, the positive part of
$f-2A_\delta f$ is at most either of the two positive discrepancies
on the left. Their respective bounds are the two Jordan masses in
\eqref{eq:v73-paired-flux}. Increasing $K$ only decreases the
positive part. The excluded set does not affect essential norms.
\end{proof}

For the complete current $\mathfrak D^\varepsilon=Db$ of
Theorem~\ref{thm:v73-full-source-bv}, define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v73-paired-height}
 \mathcal P_\varepsilon(\delta)
 =\limsup_{m\to\infty}\sup_{R,n,k}
   \operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{t\in\R}m^2
   \min\{(Db)^+([t-\delta,t]),(Db)^-([t,t+\delta])\}.
\end{equation}
The interval masses are measurable functions of $t$. Their measures
are the Jordan parts of the complete physically weighted current,
not sums of variations of words, witnesses or threshold sources.
The value is allowed to be infinite. Existence of these Jordan
parts is now a conclusion for the original Lorentz source, rather
than an additional finite-measure assumption.

\begin{theorem}[A full-source paired-flux budget]
\label{thm:v73-paired-budget}
For the original source and every fixed $\varepsilon,\delta>0$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v73-U-paired}
 \mathcal U_{\varepsilon,\delta}(K)
       \le\mathcal P_\varepsilon(\delta)
       \le\tfrac12\mathcal V_\varepsilon(\delta),\qquad K\ge2.
\end{equation}
For sufficiently large fixed $B$, put
$\varepsilon(B)=A_0B^{-1/12}$ and choose any $\delta(B)>0$
before the collision limsup. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v73-paired-budget}
 \mathcal E_M\le C_M B^{-1/192}
             +\mathcal P_{\varepsilon(B)}(\delta(B)).
\end{equation}
The arithmetic main term, including every zero class, is unchanged.
If one establishes, for some $q\ge0$ and $\beta>0$, the additional
quantitative bound
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v73-paired-power-premise}
 \mathcal P_\varepsilon(\delta)\le A\varepsilon^{-q}\delta^\beta,
\end{equation}
then for any $a>0$ the choice
$\delta(B)=\varepsilon(B)^{(q+a)/\beta}$ gives
$\mathcal E_M\le C_M B^{-1/192}+C_aB^{-a/12}$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply Lemma~\ref{lem:v73-paired-flux} to the full source, whose
finite variation was proved in the preceding section. Taking the
indicated essential suprema and collision limsup gives the first
inequality of \eqref{eq:v73-U-paired}. The second follows from
$\min(x,y)\le(x+y)/2$ and the definition of
$\mathcal V_\varepsilon(\delta)$ in
\eqref{eq:v72-directed-flux-height}.

For fixed $\delta$, the finite interval estimate
\eqref{eq:v72-finite-average-height}, first with fixed auxiliary
band $B_*$ and then with $B_*\to\infty$ after the collision limit,
gives $\mathcal H(A_\delta b)\le C\varepsilon^{1/16}$.
The pointwise inequality
$b\le2A_\delta b+[b-2A_\delta b]_+$ therefore yields
$\mathcal H(b)\le2C\varepsilon^{1/16}+
\mathcal P_\varepsilon(\delta)$. Insert it in the positive-remainder
error bound \eqref{eq:v51-positive-error}. This proves
\eqref{eq:v73-paired-budget}. Alternatively the source-preserving
allocation of Lemma~\ref{lem:v72-preserved-recovery}, with $K=2$,
retains $s_{71,1}$ and gives the same bound, since
$B^{-1/4}\le B^{-1/192}$ for $B\ge1$.
The substitution in \eqref{eq:v73-paired-power-premise} gives
$\mathcal P_{\varepsilon(B)}(\delta(B))\le A\varepsilon(B)^a$.
All choices depending on $B$ precede $m\to\infty$; only afterwards
is $B$ enlarged. No estimate is evaluated at a collision-dependent
spectral band.
\end{proof}

The pairing is consequential even at a fixed count. For
$f=h\one_{(a,b)}$, the two genuine boundary atoms are
$h\delta_a-h\delta_b$. If $b-a>2\delta$, the two Jordan intervals
in \eqref{eq:v73-paired-flux} cannot both contain their respective
atoms, except at irrelevant endpoints. Thus the paired bound is
zero although the directed sum has supremum at least $h$. If
$b-a<\delta$, a roof interval can see both edges and the paired
bound retains their height; at interior roofs
$[f-2A_\delta f]_+=h(1-(b-a)/\delta)$. The estimate cancels no
physical atom. It uses the positive averaging capacity to distinguish
an isolated edge from a narrow positive excursion.

The power bound \eqref{eq:v73-paired-power-premise} is a stated
sufficient estimate, not a proved collision-uniform Lorentz bound.
The constant $C_m$ of \eqref{eq:v73-full-bv-bound} does not establish
it. In particular finite variation and vanishing mass of a thin
source are not substituted for a bound on its normalized essential
height. The remaining problem is quantitative control of paired
physical flux at the original exact labels.
