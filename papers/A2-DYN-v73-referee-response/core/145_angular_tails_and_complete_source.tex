\section{Angular tails and the complete positive source}
\label{sec:v67-angular-tails}
The preceding result replaces a fixed-word dominated-convergence
argument by an ordered zero-width theorem on every finite-jet stratum.
Passing to all words still requires a dynamical estimate. We state it
in the original physical coefficient and do not replace it by an
unweighted word count.

\subsection{A positive tail sufficient for the full angular loss}
Define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v67-bad-trace}
 \mathcal E^K_{m;\lambda,R}
   =\sum_{\substack{z:\theta_{z,\lambda}>0\\
                         \mathfrak a_{z,\lambda}>K}}
                   \beta_{z,\lambda}\delta_{t_z},\qquad
 \mathcal B_m=\mathcal B^K_m+\mathcal E^K_m.
\end{equation}
Let
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v67-tail-functional}
 E(K)=\limsup_{m\to\infty}\sup_{R,\lambda,t}
                     m^2\mathcal E^K_m([t-1,t+1])\in[0,\infty].
\end{equation}
These are the physical weights $J_z\theta_{z,\lambda}$, with the
original half-word labels. They are not the full reversible trace.

\begin{proposition}[Quantified angular exhaustion]
\label{prop:v67-angular-exhaustion}
For fixed $K$ and $s<h_\chi$ with $K\sqrt{2s}<1$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v67-full-loss-bound}
 \limsup_m\sup_{R,\lambda,t}m^2\mathcal D_{m,s}([t-1,t+1])
                  \le\tfrac43 C K\sqrt{2s}+E(K).
\end{equation}
Consequently the additional estimate
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v67-physical-tail-input}
                         \lim_{K\to\infty}E(K)=0
\end{equation}
implies the full angular-loss conclusion
\eqref{eq:v66-angular-loss-input} for this seam-chart family, and
\[
 \limsup_m\sup_{R,\lambda,t}m^2\mathcal B_m([t-h,t+h])
                                      \le2Ch\quad(0<h\le1).
\]
Condition~\eqref{eq:v67-physical-tail-input} is a sufficient physical
weighted-tightness input, not an assertion proved for the full Lorentz
family in this article.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
On the complement of the stratum the loss of each label is at most
its tangent fraction, so
$\mathcal D_{m,s}\le\mathcal D^K_{m,s}+\mathcal E^K_m$.
Labels with zero tangent fraction contribute zero to the one-sided
loss even when their nonlinear profile is positive. Apply
\eqref{eq:v67-ordered-stratified-loss} to obtain
\eqref{eq:v67-full-loss-bound}. First let $s\downarrow0$ at fixed
$K$, and only then let $K\to\infty$. The nonnegative left side
therefore tends to zero under \eqref{eq:v67-physical-tail-input}.
For the atomic bound use \eqref{eq:v67-bad-trace}, the stratum bound,
and $[t-h,t+h]\subset[t-1,t+1]$; then let $K\to\infty$.
No count-dependent cutoff is inserted into the local law.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[A moment sufficient for weighted tightness]
\label{cor:v67-angular-moment}
If for some $p>0$ the independently verified bound
\[
 M_p:=\limsup_m\sup_{R,\lambda,t}m^2
       \sum_{z:\,|t_z-t|\le1}
                  \beta_{z,\lambda}\mathfrak a_{z,\lambda}^{p}<\infty
\]
holds, with zero coefficients omitted, then $E(K)\le M_pK^{-p}$.
For this hypothesis only, the ordered angular loss in
\eqref{eq:v67-full-loss-bound} is $O(s^{p/(2p+2)})$ as $s\downarrow0$.
No such moment bound or polynomial rate is asserted for the unrestricted
Lorentz source.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
On $\mathfrak a>K$, the indicator is at most
$\mathfrak a^pK^{-p}$. Positivity gives the tail bound. In
\eqref{eq:v67-full-loss-bound} choose
$K=s^{-1/(2p+2)}$. For each fixed $s$ this is a fixed cutoff before
the collision limit, and $K\sqrt{2s}\to0$. Both terms have the stated
power of $s$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The pointwise target retains every positive component}
For $K,H$ in Corollary~\ref{cor:v67-collar-height}, the source defining
$b^{K,H}$ is a disjoint restriction of the unchanged complete
first-clearance source. Thus there is the exact positive partition
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v67-complete-partition}
 b^{\varepsilon,\mathrm{clr}}
            =b^{K,H}+r^{\varepsilon,\chi,K,H},\qquad
                        r^{\varepsilon,\chi,K,H}\ge0.
\end{equation}
The second term includes the original outside source, tapered-incidence
violations, selected grazing, noncritical rank components, non-seam
critical centers, labels of zero tangent fraction, the large-condition
strata and all omitted chart annuli. This is a source partition, not
a cancellation or an equality inferred from small mass.

Write $\mathcal H(f)=\limsup_m\sup_{R,n,k}m^2\|f_{m;n,k,R}\|_\infty$.
For every fixed admissible $\varepsilon,\chi,K,H$, positivity gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v67-complete-height-budget}
 \mathcal H(b^{\varepsilon,\mathrm{clr}})
 \le C H e^{K_\chi\sqrt{2H}}(1+K\sqrt{2H})
                       +\mathcal H(r^{\varepsilon,\chi,K,H}).
\end{equation}
In particular, at each fixed $\varepsilon$, an arbitrarily small bound
on both terms, obtained by fixed choices of $\chi,K,H$ before the
collision limit, would close the clearance height at that width.
For the unrestricted theorem one needs the corresponding bounds tending
to zero as $\varepsilon\downarrow0$, as well as the complete incidence
height. A bound on $E(K)$ alone would not give the height of $r$.

The inherited positive-error representation
(Theorem~\ref{thm:v51-positive-error}) remains
\[
 \limsup_m\sup_{R,n,k}
 \|P-G-m^2(b^{\varepsilon(B),\mathrm{inc}}
                  +b^{\varepsilon(B),\mathrm{clr}})\|_\infty
                                      \le CB^{-1/192},
 \qquad\varepsilon(B)=A_0B^{-1/12}.
\]
Here $B$ is the fixed reconstruction band, unrelated to the atomic
measure $\mathcal B$. All new cutoffs are chosen with this band fixed
before the collision limit. The complete incidence estimate and the
height of the positive complement in \eqref{eq:v67-complete-partition}
are not supplied by the angular calculation. The unrestricted two-sided
pointwise arithmetic law and its same-roof consequences therefore
remain the unchanged target, not conclusions inferred from the stratum
theorem. Its finite arithmetic transition factor and zero classes are
retained.

\subsection{Relation to local limit results}
Sz\'asz--Varj\'u \cite[Theorem 1.1 and the following remark]{SV}
prove a mixed local limit in weak topology for a minimal nondegenerate
bounded H\"older observable over a Young system. The stated weak
conclusion is distinct from a local limit for densities. Demers--P\`ene--Zhang
\cite{DPZ} treat the cell index of randomly deforming billiards with
piecewise H\"older endpoint observables. Dolgopyat--N\'andori \cite{DN}
formulate local limit and mixing conditions for suspensions, including
finite-horizon Sinai billiard applications.

The comparison needed here is more specific than a change of observable:
Section~\ref{sec:v66-collar-trace} uses the already established
exact-occupation and displacement local law with two normal endpoint
strips of width $O(\sqrt s)$ and roof interval of length $2h+s$.
It keeps $s$ fixed before the collision limit. The new step in
Section~\ref{sec:v67-stratified-trace} is a relative geometric absorption
which yields a zero-width trace and then an essential-height bound on
specified strata. These are not obtained by differentiating a fixed-set
local limit. Nor do the cited local-limit statements, without an
additional hypothesis verification, estimate the weighted condition-number
tail \eqref{eq:v67-physical-tail-input} or the full positive remainder
\eqref{eq:v67-complete-partition}. We make no claim of a new general
local limit theorem for all singular hyperbolic systems.
