## Exact claims isolated in v28

The new analytic saddle extends the marked target to an explicit symmetric full-support rectangle with a root and response bounded uniformly away from the endpoints. Its exact physical identification uses the shared conditional Gaussian error, rather than an independent unconditional bit-error approximation. The actual Brownian model then has exact decision width five and minimum declared-clock peak twelve throughout its original positive-noise range. Contact-forced vertices/faces at all unavoidable schedule entrances characterize exactly the two minimizing orders. The ideal reverse-order exact count ten proves a support-boundary jump.

The general facial scheduling theorem is a reusable certificate theorem, not an unrestricted algebraic classification of all causal experiments. Its proof uses positive-factorization geometry and a bottleneck path recurrence, whose underlying mechanisms are classical. The novelty under consideration lies in the forced-channel certificates obtained from uniform saddle contact and the resulting exact physical/order/noise conclusions.

No assertion that an external source lacks these results is inferred merely from an abstract, a failed access attempt, or the absence of a matching title. The report's demand for a rigorous originality boundary is addressed by narrowing the claimed new statements to the explicit theorems actually proved, while retaining the broad program and all predecessor content.
