\section{Round-twenty-nine reconstruction: exact two-clock currents and overshoot-separated stopping}
\label{sec:r29-a3}

This revision starts from the exact finite-word telescoping identity.  It does
not replace that identity by a false continuum balance equation.  Collision
clock and physical clock are normalized by their own totals, and the renewal
overshoot is kept on its natural order-one scale instead of being inserted as
a nondegenerate square-root Gaussian coordinate.

\subsection{Legal completed excursions and a fractional moment kernel}

A legal excursion mark is
\[
 m=(R,\tau,\kappa,e^-,e^+,\mathcal G),                    \tag{A3.1}
\]
where $R\in\N$, $\tau>0$, $\kappa\in\Z^2$, $e^\pm$ are
one-sided section points, and $\mathcal G$ is the completed graph of the
position--velocity flight.  Completed graphs are continuous parametrized
graphs modulo increasing reparametrization, with flat time coordinate across
velocity jumps.  On bounded duration and variation sets the resulting
$M_1$-type graph metric is complete and separable.  Grazing limits remain
labelled boundary graphs; they are not deleted from the completion.

Let $E$ be the legal mark space and $\mathsf H=E^{\mathbb Z_-}$ the complete
past.  The exact stable disintegration is a kernel $K(h,dm)$ and
$U(h,m)$ appends $m$ and shifts the past.  Put
\[
 \chi(m)=1+R(m)+\tau(m)+|\kappa(m)|+
\norm{\mathcal G}_{{\rm var}}.
\]
Fix $0<\rho<1$ and
\[
 V(h)=1+\sum_{j\ge0}\rho^j\chi(m_{-j}),\qquad W(h)=e^{\epsilon V(h)}.
                                                                    \tag{A3.2}
\]

\begin{assumption}[Fractional conditional exponential moment]
\label{ass:r29-a3-fractional}
There are $\epsilon>0$, $0\le\zeta<1-\rho$, and $C<\infty$ such that
\[
 \int_Ee^{\epsilon\chi(m)}K(h,dm)\le C W(h)^\zeta          \tag{A3.3}
\]
for every complete past in the certified A2 family.
\end{assumption}

This is stronger and more precisely typed than an estimate with right side
$CW(h)$.  It is verified by the A2 Jacobian-weighted return tail after choosing
$\epsilon$ below the return exponent and $\rho$ above the stable distortion
rate.

\begin{lemma}[Derived power drift]
\label{lem:r29-a3-drift}
Under Assumption~\ref{ass:r29-a3-fractional},
\[
 PW(h)\le C W(h)^{\alpha},\qquad \alpha=\rho+\zeta<1.      \tag{A3.4}
\]
Hence $PW\le\lambda W+b$ for suitable $\lambda<1$ and $b<\infty$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The update identity is
$V(U(h,m))=1+\chi(m)+\rho(V(h)-1)$.  Therefore
\[
 PW(h)\le C e^{\epsilon\rho V(h)}W(h)^\zeta
       \le C'W(h)^{\rho+\zeta}.
\]
Since $\alpha<1$, the affine drift follows by splitting at a sufficiently
large $W$-level.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The exact discrete chronological complex}

For a stopped legal word $w=(m_1,\ldots,m_{\nu})$, set
\[
 h_k=U(h_{k-1},m_k),\qquad
 s_{0,N}=0,\qquad s_{k,N}=N^{-1}\sum_{j\le k}R(m_j).       \tag{A3.5}
\]
Define the transition flow and the holding occupation by
\[
 Q_N={1\over N}\sum_{k=1}^{\nu}
  \delta_{(s_{k,N},h_{k-1},m_k)},                         \tag{A3.6}
\]
\[
 L_N(ds,dh)=\sum_{k=1}^{\nu}
  1_{[s_{k-1,N},s_{k,N})}(s)\,ds\,\delta_{h_{k-1}}(dh).   \tag{A3.7}
\]
For a bounded $C^1$ cylindrical test $\varphi(s,h)$, write
$\Delta_m\varphi(s,h)=\varphi(s,U(h,m))-\varphi(s,h)$.

\begin{proposition}[Exact finite-word telescoping identity]
\label{prop:r29-a3-telescope}
Every legal word satisfies
\begin{equation}\tag{A3.8}
\begin{split}
 {\varphi(s_{\nu,N},h_\nu)-\varphi(0,h_0)\over N}
 ={}&{1\over N}\int\partial_s\varphi(s,h)\,L_N(ds,dh)\\
 &+\int\Delta_m\varphi(s,h)\,Q_N(ds,dh,dm)+\mathfrak e_N(\varphi),
\end{split}
\end{equation}
where
\[
 \abs{\mathfrak e_N(\varphi)}
 \le {\norm{\partial_s\Delta_m\varphi}_\infty\over N^2}
       \sum_{k\le\nu}R(m_k).                              \tag{A3.9}
\]
The identity includes the chronological increments $R(m_k)/N$, the holding
occupation, and both endpoint values.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For each $k$, insert and subtract
$\varphi(s_{k,N},h_{k-1})$:
\[
 \begin{split}
 \varphi(s_{k,N},h_k)-\varphi(s_{k-1,N},h_{k-1})
 ={}&\int_{s_{k-1,N}}^{s_{k,N}
       \partial_s\varphi(r,h_{k-1})\,dr\\
 &+\Delta_{m_k}\varphi(s_{k,N},h_{k-1}).
 \end{split}
\]
Summation telescopes.  Dividing by $N$ and replacing a transition time inside
its interval by the right endpoint produces exactly (A3.9).  No continuum
equation is asserted before this exact identity is passed to a limit.
\end{proof}

A limit state retains $(Q,L)$ together with the vanishing boundary defect in
(A3.8).  Its history constraint is the closed weak limit of (A3.8); in
particular, tests depending only on $s$ satisfy the ordinary endpoint
fundamental theorem and do not give a contradiction.

\subsection{Two graph clocks and bounded recession mass}

Let
\[
 C_N=\sum_{k\le\nu}R(m_k),\qquad
 T_N=\sum_{k\le\nu}\tau(m_k).                             \tag{A3.10}
\]
The physical completed graph is defined on $[0,T_N]$.  Its normalized path is
\[
 \Gamma_N^{\rm phys}(t)
 =\operatorname{Cat}(\mathcal G_{m_1},\ldots,\mathcal G_{m_\nu})(tT_N),
 \quad 0\le t\le1.                                       \tag{A3.11}
\]
The collision-clock graph is a separate concatenation in which each collision
edge has unit length; it is normalized by $C_N$:
\[
 \Gamma_N^{\rm col}(t)=\operatorname{Cat}_{\rm col}(m_1,\ldots,m_\nu)(tC_N).
                                                                    \tag{A3.12}
\]
Thus neither path is evaluated outside its natural domain.

For $r\in\mathbb Q_+$ define the recurrent and recession pieces
\[
 Q_N^{(r)}={1\over N}\sum_k1_{\{\chi(m_k)\le rN\}}
   \delta_{(s_{k,N},h_{k-1},m_k)},                        \tag{A3.13}
\]
\[
 \mathcal R_N^{(r)}={1\over N}\sum_k\chi(m_k)
  1_{\{\chi(m_k)>rN\}}\delta_{(s_{k,N},[m_k])},           \tag{A3.14}
\]
where $[m]$ is the radial compactification
$(m/(1+\chi(m)),\chi(m)/(1+\chi(m)))$.  The two pieces are disjoint by
definition and their compatibility under changing $r$ is recorded in the
countable projective system.

\begin{lemma}[Mass-budget compactification]
\label{lem:r29-a3-compact}
On every normalized entropy sublevel,
\[
 \sup_N E^q\left[{1\over N}\sum_{k\le\nu}\chi(m_k)\right]<\infty.        \tag{A3.15}
\]
Consequently every $\mathcal R_N^{(r)}$ has uniformly bounded total mass,
the projective recession space is compact on that sublevel, and the terminal
mark is tight in the same radial compactification.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Apply the entropy inequality to the fractional exponential moment (A3.3) one
conditional step at a time.  The accumulated logarithmic normalizer is
controlled by the power drift (A3.4), while the normalized relative entropy is
bounded.  This gives (A3.15).  Prokhorov compactness on the radial compactification
and diagonal extraction over rational $r$ prove the claim.  A single terminal
mark of order $N$ contributes finite radial mass instead of escaping the
state space.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Controlled Laplace principle}

A predictable controlled kernel is $q_{s,h}(dm)K(h,dm)$ with action
\[
 \mathcal A(q,L)=\int\!\bigl[q\log q-q+1\bigr]K(h,dm)L(ds,dh).            \tag{A3.16}
\]
Finite partitions use conditional-reference representatives
\[
 q^{\mathcal P}_{s,h}(dm)
 =\sum_C qK(h,C)\,{1_C(m)K(h,dm)\over K(h,C)},             \tag{A3.17}
\]
so every approximating mark remains legal.  A finite regular connector
library joins partition cells, and the number of inserted connectors is
$o(N)$.

\begin{theorem}[Two-clock chronological path LDP]
\label{thm:r29-a3-ldp}
The tuple
\[
 (Q_N,L_N,\Gamma_N^{\rm col},\Gamma_N^{\rm phys},
   C_N/N,T_N/N,(\mathcal R_N^{(r)})_{r\in\mathbb Q_+},[m_\nu])            \tag{A3.18}
\]
satisfies a good Laplace principle at speed $N$ on the closed state space
defined by Proposition~\ref{prop:r29-a3-telescope}, graph compatibility, and
projective recession consistency.  The rate is the infimum of (A3.16) over
predictable kernels producing the state.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The conditional entropy variational formula gives the upper bound for finite
cylindrical functionals.  Lemma~\ref{lem:r29-a3-compact} gives controlled
compactness in every state coordinate and upgrades the upper bound to bounded
continuous functionals.  For the lower bound, approximate the density
$dq/dK$ by (A3.17), approximate its past dependence by finite cylinders, and
join blocks with the regular connector library.  Conditional entropy increases
to (A3.16), while the connector cost is $o(N)$.  The exact identity (A3.8)
holds for every recovery word, so the limiting balance cannot acquire a
spurious term.  Radial recession coordinates capture all macroscopic marks.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Renewal stopping with a separate overshoot kernel}

Let
\[
 \nu_N=\inf\{k:S_kR\ge N\},\qquad O_N=S_{\nu_N}R-N.        \tag{A3.19}
\]
The Gaussian vector is
\[
 X_N=\left(\nu_N,{S_{\nu_N}\kappa},S_{\nu_N}\tau\right),                 \tag{A3.20}
\]
while $O_N$ remains unscaled.  The renewal operator acts on the enlarged state
$(h,r)$, where $r$ is the remaining return clock:
\[
 (\mathscr R_{\theta,z}F)(h,r)
 =\sum_{n\ge0}\mathcal L_{\theta,z}^n
   \left[1_{\{R(m)>r-S_nR\}}F(U(h,m),S_nR+R(m)-r)\right]. \tag{A3.21}
\]
Thus the terminal threshold is part of a typed renewal kernel, not a fixed
strong-space multiplier.

\begin{theorem}[Gaussian--overshoot stopped local theorem]
\label{thm:r29-a3-stopped}
For central values $x_N-NE X_N/N=O(\sqrt N)$ and every fixed overshoot
$o\in\N_0$,
\[
 P_H\{X_N=x_N,\ O_N=o\}
 =N^{-d/2}\,\phi_{\Sigma_H}
   \left({x_N-Nm_H\over\sqrt N}\right)
   \rho_H(o)\left(1+O(N^{-1/2})\right),                  \tag{A3.22}
\]
with counting/Lebesgue interpretation in the mixed coordinates.  The
overshoot law $\rho_H$ has an exponential tail and
$\sum_o\rho_H(o)=1$.  The estimate and two bounded finite-memory source
derivatives are uniform on the certified compact family.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Fourier--Laplace inversion of (A3.21) separates the simple renewal root from
the contracting complement.  The A2 local theorem applies to the genuinely
fluctuating coordinates in (A3.20).  The return coordinate is eliminated by
the renewal equation, and the terminal branch produces the order-one kernel
$\rho_H(o)$.  The derivative of the renewal denominator in the clock direction
is the positive mean return, while the covariance is taken only on the
transverse Gaussian coordinates.  A2's all-frequency estimate gives the
integrable majorant and the exponential return tail sums the overshoot.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Exact conditioned chronological principle]
\label{cor:r29-a3-conditioned}
Conditioning on regular lattice--continuous values of $X_N$ and, optionally,
on a fixed or integrated overshoot gives the Laplace principle obtained by
restricting the rate in Theorem~\ref{thm:r29-a3-ldp} to the corresponding
coarea fibre and subtracting its minimum.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Apply (A3.22) to numerator and denominator for finite-memory sources.  Their
mixed local factors and overshoot factors cancel.  Compactness from
Lemma~\ref{lem:r29-a3-compact} and a Stone--Weierstrass approximation on each
compact rate sublevel extend the result to bounded continuous path
functionals.
\end{proof}

The active state now has an exact balance law, valid clock domains, bounded
recession mass, a true power-drift export, and a renewal theorem that separates
Gaussian fluctuations from the pinned return-clock overshoot.
