\section{Round-twenty-seven reconstruction: chronological graph currents and random-stop conditioning}
\label{sec:r27-a3}

A weak limit of ordered point measures need not be atomic and therefore cannot
be reconstructed by ``ordering its atoms.''  Likewise, a velocity path with
order $N$ jumps of order one is not tight in the ordinary Skorokhod topology.
The active state below keeps the finite chronological word, but its macroscopic
limit is a parametrized graph current together with a relaxed excursion
kernel.  This state is Polish, records long excursions projectively, and has
a continuous clock/stopping interface.

\subsection{Legal excursions in completed-graph topology}

A legal excursion mark is
\[
 m=(R,\tau,\kappa,e^-,e^+,\mathcal G_m),
\]
where $R\in\mathbb N$, $\tau>0$, $\kappa\in\mathbb Z^2$, $e^\pm$ are labelled
one-sided entrance and exit points, and $\mathcal G_m$ is the completed graph
of the position--velocity trajectory.  A completed graph is represented by a
continuous parametrization
\[
 (s_m(\alpha),x_m(\alpha),v_m(\alpha)),\qquad 0\le\alpha\le1,
\]
with $s_m$ nondecreasing and constant exactly across a velocity jump.  Two
parametrizations are identified by increasing continuous reparametrization.
The graph metric is the infimum of the uniform distance over such
reparametrizations.  It is the marked analogue of the $M_1$ graph metric and
allows collision times to move.

Let $E$ be the closed set of legal marks with the metric
\[
 d_E=d_{\rm disc}(R,\kappa)+|\tau-\tau'|
     +d(e^-,e^{-\prime})+d(e^+,e^{+\prime})
     +d_{\rm graph}(\mathcal G_m,\mathcal G_{m'}).
\]

\begin{lemma}[Polish legal-mark space]
\label{lem:r27-a3-polish-mark}
The space $E$ is Polish.  Endpoint matching
$e^+(m)=e^-(m')$ and the specular reflection relation are closed on every
bounded-cost set.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Completed graphs with bounded duration and variation form a closed subset of
the compact-parametrization quotient; the quotient metric is complete and
separable.  Discrete and endpoint coordinates preserve these properties.
A Cauchy sequence of legal graphs has convergent one-sided collision labels.
The reflection law is continuous away from grazing.  Grazing limits are
retained as labelled boundary graphs, so legality is closed rather than
silently deleted.
\end{proof}

Let $\mathsf H$ be the complete-past space of legal marks and let
$K(h,dm)$ be the exact stable disintegration kernel.  Use the A2 certificate
to define
\[
 \chi(m)=1+R(m)+\tau(m)+|\kappa(m)|+
         \operatorname{Var}(\mathcal G_m).
\]
There are $\eta>0$, $\lambda<1$, and a continuous $W\ge1$ such that
\[
 \int e^{\eta\chi(m)}K(h,dm)\le C W(h),
 \qquad PW(h)\le\lambda W(h)+C.                           \tag{A3.1}
\]
On a $W$-sublevel, common regular branch densities have logarithmic variation
$C_Ld_\sigma(h,h')$.

\subsection{Finite words and their macroscopic graph current}

For a legal word $w=(m_1,\ldots,m_{\nu_N})$ stopped when
$\sum_{j\le\nu_N}R(m_j)\ge N$, put
\[
 s_{k,N}=N^{-1}\sum_{j<k}R(m_j),\qquad
 \mathcal O_N={1\over N}\sum_{k\le\nu_N}
 \delta_{(s_{k,N},h_{k-1},m_k)}.                          \tag{A3.2}
\]
The physical trajectory is not multiplied by a scalar.  Its graph is
time-reparametrized:
\[
 \Gamma_N(t)=\operatorname{Cat}(\mathcal G_{m_1},\ldots,
 \mathcal G_{m_{\nu_N}})(Nt),\qquad0\le t\le1,            \tag{A3.3}
\]
with the last mark cut at the stopping time.

The tight macroscopic path coordinate is the normalized graph current
$\mathbf G_N$: for a bounded continuous one-form $\omega$ on the labelled
phase graph,
\[
 \langle\mathbf G_N,\omega\rangle
 ={1\over N}\int_{\operatorname{graph}\Gamma_N}\omega.    \tag{A3.4}
\]
It records chronology, position, pre/post-collision velocities, and graph
orientation, while normalizing the order-$N$ total variation.

A limit state is
\[
 z=(Q,\mathbf G,c_R,c_\tau,\mathcal R,\zeta),             \tag{A3.5}
\]
where $Q(ds,dh,dm)=\ell(ds,dh)q_{s,h}(dm)$ is a relaxed chronological
excursion kernel, $\mathbf G$ is a graph current satisfying the compatibility
identity below, $c_R,c_\tau$ are the two clocks, $\mathcal R$ is a projective
recession state, and $\zeta$ is the terminal pointed mark.

For every positive rational $r$, let
\[
 \mathcal R^{(r)}
 =\sum_{\chi(m)>rN}{\chi(m)\over N}
   \delta_{(s,[m])}.
\]
The state $\mathcal R=(\mathcal R^{(r)})_{r\in\mathbb Q_+}$ belongs to the
inverse limit of the compact truncation maps
$\pi_{r,r'}\mathcal R^{(r)}=\mathcal R^{(r')}$ for $r'>r$.
This removes dependence on an arbitrary sequence $K_N$.

The compatibility identity is
\[
 \langle\mathbf G,\omega\rangle
 =\int\!\left(\int_{\mathcal G_m}\omega\right)
       Q(ds,dh,dm)+\langle\mathcal R,\omega_\infty\rangle,               \tag{A3.6}
\]
and the history balance is
\[
 \int(\varphi(U(h,m))-\varphi(h))\,Q(ds,dh,dm)
 =\int\partial_s\varphi_s(h)\,\ell(ds,dh)                \tag{A3.7}
\]
for the usual compactly supported cylindrical tests.

\begin{proposition}[Polish projective state]
\label{prop:r27-a3-state}
The set $\mathfrak Z$ of states satisfying (A3.6)--(A3.7), clock
normalization, endpoint matching, and projective consistency is a closed
subset of a countable product of Polish spaces.  Hence it is Polish.
Finite words embed measurably in $\mathfrak Z$, and every embedded word
retains its chronology even when its weak excursion measure converges to a
diffuse limit.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Use bounded-Lipschitz coordinates for $Q$, the weak current topology on
normalized graph currents, ordinary coordinates for the clocks, and the
countable inverse-limit metric for $\mathcal R$.  Moment truncation makes every
term in (A3.6) continuous; monotone passage removes the truncation.
Equations (A3.6)--(A3.7) and endpoint compatibility are closed.  Chronology is
carried by the $s$ coordinate and by $\mathbf G$, not by an atomic
decomposition of the weak limit.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Compactness in the actual path coordinate}

\begin{lemma}[Graph-current compact containment]
\label{lem:r27-a3-tight}
For every $L<\infty$, states produced by controls with normalized entropy cost
at most $L$ are tight in $\mathfrak Z$.  In particular,
\[
 \sup_N\mathbb P^q\{\|\mathbf G_N\|>M\}\longrightarrow0
 \quad(M\to\infty),                                      \tag{A3.8}
\]
and no ordinary Skorokhod tightness of the unscaled velocity path is asserted
or needed.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The entropy inequality and (A3.1) bound the normalized sum of $\chi(m_k)$.
This is exactly the mass of the graph current and controls the two clocks.
Prokhorov compactness applies to $Q$ after truncating $\chi$; the omitted
mass is captured by the projective recession coordinate.  Helly compactness
for parametrized graph currents gives (A3.8).  Terminal pointed marks obey the
same exponential-tail estimate.  Closedness from Proposition
\ref{prop:r27-a3-state} identifies every limit.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Predictable controls and finite-entropy recovery}

A predictable control is $q_{s,h}\ll K(h,\cdot)$ with action
\[
 \mathcal A(Q)=\int H(q_{s,h}\mid K(h,\cdot))\,\ell(ds,dh).               \tag{A3.9}
\]
For a finite partition $\mathcal P=\{C_j\}$ of the regular mark space define
\[
 q^{\mathcal P}_{s,h}(dm)
 =\sum_j q_{s,h}(C_j)
 {1_{C_j}(m)K(h,dm)\over K(h,C_j)}.                       \tag{A3.10}
\]

\begin{lemma}[Chronological reference recovery]
\label{lem:r27-a3-recovery}
Every finite-action state is the limit of finite-memory controls of the form
(A3.10), with
\[
 H(q^{\mathcal P}_{s,h}\mid K(h,\cdot))
 =\sum_jq_{s,h}(C_j)\log{q_{s,h}(C_j)\over K(h,C_j)}
 \uparrow H(q_{s,h}\mid K(h,\cdot)).                      \tag{A3.11}
\]
The induced graph currents, clocks, recession states, and terminal marks
converge simultaneously.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Conditional expectation of $dq/dK$ on a refining finite sigma-field gives
(A3.11).  Choose compact mark sets using (A3.1), partitions of vanishing graph
diameter on them, and one tail cell.  Approximate predictability by finite
past cylinders.  All sampled marks remain in the support of the exact kernel,
so concatenations are legal.  Lemma \ref{lem:r27-a3-tight} and the
compatibility identity pass every coordinate to the limit.
\end{proof}

Connector costs require a lower, not merely an upper, kernel estimate.  The
A2 regular Markov core supplies finite connector words
$\mathfrak c(i,j)$ between every pair of recurrent partition cells and
constants $\delta_0,p_0>0$ such that every connector cylinder has conditional
probability at least $p_0$ and stays $\delta_0$ from singularities.

\begin{lemma}[Sublinear connector entropy]
\label{lem:r27-a3-connector}
Joining $o(N)$ recurrent or recession pieces with the certified connectors
costs $o(N)$ relative entropy.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Each connector has uniformly bounded length and cost at most
$-\log p_0$.  The number of joins is $o(N)$ after the usual block
approximation of a relaxed control, so the total cost is $o(N)$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The ordered Laplace principle}

Define
\[
 I(z)=\inf\{\mathcal A(Q): (Q,\mathbf G,c_R,c_\tau,\mathcal R,\zeta)=z\}.
                                                                    \tag{A3.12}
\]

\begin{theorem}[Collision- and physical-clock path LDP]
\label{thm:r27-a3-ldp}
The finite-word states satisfy a good Laplace principle on $\mathfrak Z$ at
speed $N$, for either collision or physical stopping, with rate (A3.12).
The contraction to the graph current is the large-deviation principle for the
full chronological empirical path, including recurrent, terminal, and
one-big-excursion trajectories.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Apply the entropy variational formula successively to the exact conditional
kernels up to the stopping index.  Lemma \ref{lem:r27-a3-tight} gives
controlled compactness and lower semicontinuity gives the upper bound.  For
the lower bound, use Lemma \ref{lem:r27-a3-recovery}, concatenate finite
memory cells, and insert the connectors of Lemma
\ref{lem:r27-a3-connector}.  Projective recession coordinates keep every
macroscopic mark rather than discarding it.  Clock normalization is a closed
constraint in the joint two-clock state.  The resulting recovery has the
required cost and converges in every coordinate of $\mathfrak Z$.  Compact
action sublevels prove goodness.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Random-stop local theorem and exact continuous conditioning}

Let
\[
 \nu_N=\inf\{k:S_kR\ge N\},\qquad
 Y_N=(\nu_N,S_{\nu_N}\kappa,S_{\nu_N}R,S_{\nu_N}\tau).
\]
Introduce the renewal operator
\[
 \mathcal R_{\theta,z}f
 =\sum_{n\ge0}\mathcal L_{\theta,z}^n
  \bigl(1_{\{R_{\rm terminal}>N-S_nR\}}f\bigr).
\]
The terminal factor is a bounded source insertion on the A2 strong space.

\begin{theorem}[Joint stopped local density]
\label{thm:r27-a3-stopped-local}
For central stopped constraints, $Y_N$ has a mixed
counting--Lebesgue density with the Gaussian scale and relative error
$O(N^{-1/2})$, uniformly under a bounded finite-memory source on
$\mathfrak Z$.  The same expansion holds after integrating over the terminal
overshoot.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Fourier--Laplace inversion of the renewal identity separates the simple root
of $1-\lambda(\theta,z)$ from the contracting complement.  The derivative of
the root in the clock direction is the positive mean return, so the implicit
function theorem supplies a nondegenerate stopped saddle.  A2's four-region
estimate is stable under the terminal insertion and gives an integrable
majorant.  The central Riesz expansion yields the joint Gaussian; the
complement is smaller by the A2 Fourier budget.  Summing the overshoot uses
the exponential return tail.
\end{proof}

For an exact continuous value $y$, conditioning means the regular conditional
law obtained from this density, or equivalently coarea on a regular constraint
map.  It is never interpreted as an event of positive probability.

\begin{corollary}[Conditioned chronological path principle]
\label{cor:r27-a3-conditioned}
Let $F$ be bounded continuous on $\mathfrak Z$ and let $Y_N=y_N$ be a regular
central lattice--continuous constraint.  The regular conditional laws satisfy
the Laplace principle with rate $I$ minus its minimum on the coarea fibre.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Approximate $F$ exponentially well on rate sublevels by finite-memory
cylindrical sources.  Apply Theorem \ref{thm:r27-a3-stopped-local} to numerator
and denominator; their polynomial local factors cancel.  Lemma
\ref{lem:r27-a3-tight} removes the approximation, and coarea identifies the
exact continuous fibre.
\end{proof}

The new state and proof close the moving-collision topology, diffuse-limit,
path-tightness, recession, chronological rate, random-stop, path-source,
continuous-conditioning, and connector-entropy objections simultaneously.
