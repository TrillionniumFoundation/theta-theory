    \begin{abstract}
    We establish a full weak-topology empirical-path large-deviation principle for
one compact periodic finite-horizon Sinai billiard under its natural Liouville
collision law.  The proof passes through an exponential-tail Young
presentation, applies a countable-shift Gibbs empirical-measure LDP with its
good pressure/free-energy rate, and then proves a singularity-shielded
marked-renewal contraction theorem in collision and physical time.  The resulting physical rate functional is independent of the chosen
Young presentation.

All bounded continuous path potentials are recovered by pressure--rate
duality.  General finite-dimensional macro preparations admit information
projections; unique exposed preparations satisfy a random-root Gibbs-conditioning
principle, while nonunique minimizers are retained as phase-labelled
preparations.  Constants and path coboundaries are quotiented out, so the
conjugate variable is a genuine cotangent class on deterministic orbit space.
The homological v9 theory and all earlier scalar-current models are
contractions of this result.
    \end{abstract}

\section{Collision and physical orbit-path spaces}\label{sec:path}
Fix a radius \(R\in[9/20,47/100]\) in the triangular-lattice
finite-horizon Sinai family.  Let
\(T_R:M_R\dashrightarrow M_R\) be the collision map,
\(\mu_R^c\) its normalized Liouville cosine measure, and
\(\tau_R:M_R\to(0,\infty)\) the actual free-flight roof.  The singularity
set has \(\mu_R^c\)-measure zero, and finite horizon gives
\[
 0<\tau_*\le \tau_R\le \tau^*<\infty.
\]

The collision orbit space is
\[
 \Omega_R^c=
 \overline{\{(T_R^kz)_{k\in\Z}:z\text{ is nonsingular}\}}
 \subset \overline M_R^{\Z},
\]
with the product topology and left shift \(\Theta\).  Its Liouville orbit law
is
\[
 \Pp_R^c=(z\mapsto(T_R^kz)_{k\in\Z})_\#\mu_R^c.
\]

Let
\[
 \mathcal X_R=
 \{(z,u):z\in M_R,\ 0\le u<\tau_R(z)\}/
 (z,\tau_R(z))\sim(T_Rz,0)
\]
be the physical suspension phase space, with billiard flow \(\Phi_R^t\) and
normalized Liouville flow law \(\mu_R^p\).  The physical orbit-path space
\(\Omega_R^p\) is the closure of
\(t\mapsto\Phi_R^t x\) in the local Skorokhod path space
\(D_{\mathrm{loc}}(\R,\overline{\mathcal X}_R)\); it is Polish.  Time shift on
this space is denoted by \(\Theta_t\), and
\[
 \Pp_R^p=(x\mapsto(\Phi_R^t x)_{t\in\R})_\#\mu_R^p.
\]

For \(\omega\in\Omega_R^c\) define
\[
 L_n^c(\omega)=\frac1n\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}\delta_{\Theta^k\omega}.
\]
For \(\omega\in\Omega_R^p\) define
\[
 L_T^p(\omega)=\frac1T\int_0^T\delta_{\Theta_t\omega}\,dt.
\]

\begin{proposition}[Deterministic path/state equivalence]\label{prop:path-state}
Every invariant probability on either deterministic orbit support is uniquely
determined by its time-zero marginal.  Conversely, every invariant state
probability that gives zero mass to singular trajectories has a unique orbit
lift.  Any empirical-path rate constructed below is therefore infinite away
from deterministic billiard-path lifts.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
On collision paths, \(z_k=T_R^kz_0\); on physical paths,
\(\omega(t)=\Phi_R^t\omega(0)\).  Hence all finite-dimensional marginals are
push-forwards of the time-zero marginal.  The converse follows by applying the
orbit map outside the null singular set.  A contraction from the symbolic
presentation can only charge these deterministic lifts, which gives the last
statement.
\end{proof}

\section{Exponential Young presentation and a countable-shift LDP}\label{sec:tower}
A standard finite-horizon Sinai construction supplies a mixing Young tower
\((\Delta_R,F_R,\widehat\mu_R)\), an inducing domain \(Y_R\), a return time
\(r_R:Y_R\to\N\), and a factor map \(\pi_R:\Delta_R\to M_R\) such that
\[
 \widehat\mu_R(r_R>n)\le Ce^{-cn}.
\]
The induced return map is full-branch Gibbs--Markov with bounded distortion
\cite{Young1998,Chernov1999}.

Code each return excursion by one symbol together with its finite collision
mark.  The natural extension is a finitely primitive countable Markov shift
\((\widehat\Omega_R,\widehat\Theta)\).  Its stationary block law
\(\widehat\Pp_R\) is the Gibbs state of a finite-pressure potential
\(\widehat\phi_R\) with summable variations.  The return length and physical
duration have exponential moments.

\begin{theorem}[Return-block empirical-measure LDP]\label{thm:block-ldp}
The empirical block measure
\[
 \widehat L_N=\frac1N\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}
 \delta_{\widehat\Theta^k\widehat\omega}
\]
satisfies a full level-2 LDP in the weak topology on
\(\mathcal P(\widehat\Omega_R)\), with a good rate
\(\widehat\cI_R\).  The rate is infinite off the invariant probabilities and
is the lower-semicontinuous pressure/free-energy rate of
\((\widehat\phi_R,\widehat\Pp_R)\).  In particular, for every bounded
continuous block potential \(F\),
\[
 \widehat Q_R(F)
 =\lim_{N\to\infty}\frac1N
 \log\E_{\widehat\Pp_R}e^{N\widehat L_N(F)}
 =\sup_{\widehat\nu}
 \{\widehat\nu(F)-\widehat\cI_R(\widehat\nu)\}.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The induced full-branch code is a finitely primitive countable Markov shift,
and \(\widehat\Pp_R\) is a Bowen Gibbs state for a summable-variation,
finite-pressure potential.  The countable-shift level-2 theorem therefore
applies to the one-sided code \cite{Takahasi2019}.  Passing to the natural
extension identifies invariant one-sided and two-sided probabilities and
preserves the weak empirical-measure LDP.  Exponential return tails give the
exponential tightness needed for the marked observables.  The final display is
Varadhan duality for bounded continuous \(F\).
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Why no specific-entropy formula is asserted]
For a countable shift the entropy map need not be upper semicontinuous on the
whole probability space.  The controlling object is the good
pressure/free-energy rate \(\widehat\cI_R\), not an unsupported claim that the
rate always equals a naive specific relative entropy.  On finite-state or
uniformly Gibbs submodels the familiar entropy formula is recovered.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Imported analytic input]
External review must verify the selected exponential Young presentation, the
finitely primitive coding, summable variations, finite pressure, and the
uniform exponential mark moments.  These are the only tower-specific inputs
to \cref{thm:block-ldp}.
\end{remark}

\section{Singularity-shielded marked-renewal contraction}
For a block path \(\widehat\omega\), let \(r(\widehat\omega)\) be its
collision length and
\[
 \mathfrak t(\widehat\omega)
 =\sum_{j=0}^{r(\widehat\omega)-1}
 \tau_R(T_R^j\pi_R\widehat\omega)
\]
its physical duration.  Let \(\Gamma_R^c\widehat\omega\) be the expanded
collision path and \(\Gamma_R^p\widehat\omega\) its suspended physical path.

For a stationary block law \(\widehat\nu\) with finite positive mean mark,
define its collision Palm lift by
\[
 \int F\,d\mathscr S_c\widehat\nu
 =\frac1{\widehat\nu(r)}
 \int\sum_{j=0}^{r-1}
 F(\Theta^j\Gamma_R^c\widehat\omega)\,d\widehat\nu,
\]
and define \(\mathscr S_p\widehat\nu\) by integrating along the suspended
orbit segment and normalizing by \(\widehat\nu(\mathfrak t)\).

\begin{lemma}[Singularity-shielded Palm approximation]\label{lem:shield}
There are continuous truncated Palm maps
\(\mathscr S_{c,L,\delta}\) and \(\mathscr S_{p,L,\delta}\) such that, for
every weak metric \(d\) and every \(\varepsilon>0\),
\[
 \lim_{L\to\infty}\lim_{\delta\downarrow0}
 \limsup_{N\to\infty}\frac1N\log
 \widehat\Pp_R\!
 \left(
 d(\mathscr S_{\bullet}\widehat L_N,
   \mathscr S_{\bullet,L,\delta}\widehat L_N)>\varepsilon
 \right)=-\infty,
\]
for \(\bullet=c,p\), after the standard conversion from block speed to the
corresponding collision or physical speed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
First truncate blocks with \(r>L\).  Exponential moments of \(r\) and
\(\mathfrak t\), followed by Chernoff's inequality, show that the empirical
fraction of collision or physical time carried by truncated blocks has an
LDP cost tending to infinity with \(L\).

For the remaining finite blocks, remove a \(\delta\)-neighbourhood of the
finitely many singularity curves met in the path window that defines the first
finitely many test functions of the weak metric.  These neighbourhoods have
Liouville mass \(O(\delta^\alpha)\); bounded distortion of the Gibbs--Markov
return map gives an exponential frequency bound whose cost tends to infinity
as \(\delta\downarrow0\).  On the complement, the coding, finite collision
expansion, suspension, and path-window evaluation maps are uniformly
continuous.  Approximate the remaining tail of the weak metric by finitely
many test functions.  This constructs the continuous maps and proves the
exponentially good approximation.  This is the extended-contraction device
that prevents singular billiard boundaries from being treated as a continuous
factor map.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Marked-renewal contraction]\label{thm:renewal-contraction}
The collision empirical path measures satisfy a full weak LDP on
\(\mathcal P(\Omega_R^c)\) with good rate
\[
 \boxed{
 \cI_R^c(\nu)=
 \inf_{\mathscr S_c\widehat\nu=\nu}
 \frac{\widehat\cI_R(\widehat\nu)}{\widehat\nu(r)}.}
\]
The physical empirical path measures satisfy a full weak LDP on
\(\mathcal P(\Omega_R^p)\) with good rate
\[
 \boxed{
 \cI_R^p(\nu)=
 \inf_{\mathscr S_p\widehat\nu=\nu}
 \frac{\widehat\cI_R(\widehat\nu)}
      {\widehat\nu(\mathfrak t)}.}
\]
Both rates are independent of the admissible exponential Young presentation.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply \cref{thm:block-ldp} jointly with the empirical means of \(r\) and
\(\mathfrak t\).  For bounded marks the Palm maps are continuous and the
random-speed contraction gives the displayed rate divided by mean collision
or physical duration.  Lemma~\ref{lem:shield} and the extended contraction
principle remove bounded-mark and continuity restrictions.  Initial and
terminal incomplete renewal blocks are covered by the same exponential-tail
estimate.  A good LDP rate is unique for a fixed family of physical empirical
measures, proving presentation independence.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Full pressure--rate duality]\label{cor:duality}
For every bounded continuous physical path potential
\(F\in C_b(\Omega_R^p)\),
\[
 Q_R^p(F)=\lim_{T\to\infty}\frac1T
 \log\E_{\Pp_R^p}
 \exp\left\{\int_0^T F\circ\Theta_t\,dt\right\}
 =\sup_\nu\left\{\int F\,d\nu-\cI_R^p(\nu)\right\}.
\]
The collision analogue holds with \(\cI_R^c\).
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The full good LDP and boundedness of \(F\) give Varadhan's lemma.  No
compactness of the physical path space and no local simple-eigenvalue branch
is being assumed here.
\end{proof}

\section{Information projections and preparation limits}
Let \(C:\mathcal P(\Omega_R^p)\to\R^m\) be continuous and affine,
representing a finite collection of bounded continuous mechanical path
constraints.  For a prepared value \(a\), set
\[
 \cM_R(a)=\argmin\{\cI_R^p(\nu):C(\nu)=a\}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Existence and phase concentration]\label{thm:iprojection}
If the constraint set is nonempty, \(\cM_R(a)\) is compact and nonempty.  For
any neighbourhood \(U\) of \(\cM_R(a)\),
\[
 \lim_{\delta\downarrow0}\limsup_{T\to\infty}
 \frac1T\log
 \Pp_R^p\left(L_T^p\notin U\mid
 C(L_T^p)\in B_\delta(a)\right)<0.
\]
If there are several minimizers, a phase label or declared mixing weights are
part of the macro preparation; no unique canonical law is inferred.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Goodness gives existence and compactness.  Subtract the constrained minimum
from the LDP upper and lower bounds to obtain conditional exponential
concentration.  Multiple minimizers leave a compact phase set.
\end{proof}

Let \(b_T\to\infty\) with \(b_T/T\to0\), and, independently of the billiard
path, let \(U_T\) be uniform on \([b_T,T-b_T]\).

\begin{theorem}[Random-root Gibbs conditioning]\label{thm:random-root}
Condition on \(C(L_T^p)\in B_{\delta_T}(a)\), where
\(\delta_T\downarrow0\) sufficiently slowly.  Every subsequential law of the
randomly rooted path \(\Theta_{U_T}\omega\) is a mixture of elements of
\(\cM_R(a)\).  If \(\cM_R(a)=\{\nu_a\}\), then
\[
 \operatorname{Law}
 \left(\Theta_{U_T}\omega\mid
 C(L_T^p)\in B_{\delta_T}(a)\right)
 \Longrightarrow \nu_a.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For bounded continuous \(F\), conditional expectation over \(U_T\) is the
interior empirical average
\[
 \frac1{T-2b_T}\int_{b_T}^{T-b_T}F(\Theta_t\omega)\,dt.
\]
Its difference from \(L_T^p(F)\) is at most
\(4b_T\|F\|_\infty/T\).  Theorem~\ref{thm:iprojection} therefore forces every
limit of the random-root law to lie in the convex hull of the phase set; in
the unique case all test-function expectations converge to
\(\nu_a(F)\).
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Deterministic central windows]
A fixed central-window local law requires an additional ratio/local-limit or
specification estimate and does not follow from a level-2 LDP alone.  The v9
homological simple-spectral domain supplies such a stronger theorem.  The
universal v10 preparation theorem uses the mathematically sufficient and
coordinate-free random-root protocol.
\end{remark}

Let
\[
 \mathscr V_R=
 C_b(\Omega_R^p)/
 \bigl(\R+\overline{\{U-U\circ\Theta_t\}}\bigr)
\]
be the quotient of bounded continuous physical path potentials by constants
and time coboundaries.

\begin{theorem}[Path-space cotangent field]\label{thm:cotangent}
At an exposed differentiability point, a finite constraint preparation has a
Lagrange multiplier \(\lambda\in(\R^m)^*\), and the mechanically selected
conjugate object is
\[
 \boxed{\Psi_a=[C^*\lambda]\in\mathscr V_R.}
\]
It is invariant under coordinate changes and addition of path coboundaries.
The scalar \(\theta\) of v6--v8 and the homological covector of v9 are
finite-dimensional contractions of \(\Psi_a\).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Convex Lagrange duality gives \(C^*\lambda\).  Constants shift pressure by a
constant, while a time coboundary contributes only bounded endpoints and has
zero asymptotic rate.  Only the quotient class is identifiable.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Full path-space closure]\label{thm:main}
For the declared Sinai table, the natural Liouville orbit law produces full
collision and physical empirical-path rates, presentation-independent
pressure duality, all finite continuous mechanical information projections,
phase-resolved random-root conditioning, and a coordinate-free cotangent
field.  No finite observable is primitive at the rate-functional level.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Combine
\cref{thm:block-ldp,lem:shield,thm:renewal-contraction,cor:duality,thm:iprojection,thm:random-root,thm:cotangent}.
\end{proof}
