\begin{abstract}
We prove deterministic-clock empirical-path large-deviation principles
for collision and physical time by an entropy-control method on the legal
renewal edge shift.  The state records recurrent edge flow, singular
exposure, mesoscopic and recession profiles, and the pointed terminal
excursion.  Arbitrary predictable controls are handled by entropy relaxation,
not by assigning them the stationary spectral gap.  The full terminal branch
pays its full selection entropy; a prefix-only observation pays the exact
negative log probability of all legal completions.
\end{abstract}
