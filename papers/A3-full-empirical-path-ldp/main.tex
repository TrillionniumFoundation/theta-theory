\begin{abstract}
The inducing process is treated directly as a countable Markov-renewal process stopped at deterministic collision or physical time. An entropy-control representation yields full good recurrent--recession path LDPs including unbounded excursion profiles, singularity exposure, and the pointed terminal residual. No cemetery Markovization or random speed is used.
