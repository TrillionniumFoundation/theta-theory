## Executive assessment

The revision attempts to turn the previous proof program into a complete theorem by adding a finite-connector code, exponential mark estimates, a singularity-frequency ledger, terminal clock surgery, information projections, deterministic graph completion, and a closed cotangent quotient.

The central argument is still false. The active paper claims that the collision-time mass carried by return blocks longer than a cutoff is superexponentially negligible at block speed under a merely exponential return-time tail. A single excursion of length comparable with the observation horizon has an ordinary exponential cost, not a superexponential one. This is exactly the “one big excursion” obstruction identified by the repository’s own hostile audit. It invalidates the claimed exponential approximation, the pure Abramov-ratio rate, and the collision/physical-time LDP as stated.

A separate file, `revision/round3-rereview/A3_DIRECT_PATH_LDP.tex`, was subsequently written to introduce a defect/escape-pressure completion. It is not included in the controlling manuscript. The paper therefore asserts closure using the proof that the internal audit already rejected.

## Decisive counterexample to the exponential-mark lemma

The paper assumes an exponential return tail. Thus, schematically,

\[
\widehat{\mathbb P}(r>n)\asymp e^{-cn}
\]

on the relevant scale. Fix `epsilon>0`. For every fixed cutoff `L` and every sufficiently large `N`, the event

\[
r_0>\epsilon N
\]

implies

\[
\frac1N\sum_{j<N}r_j1_{\{r_j>L\}}>\epsilon.
\]

Its probability has logarithmic block-speed cost of order

\[
\frac1N\log\widehat{\mathbb P}(r_0>\epsilon N)
\longrightarrow-c\epsilon,
\]

which is finite and independent of `L`. Therefore the active assertion

\[
\lim_{L\to\infty}\limsup_{N\to\infty}
\frac1N\log\widehat{\mathbb P}\left(
N^{-1}\sum_{j<N}r_j1_{\{r_j>L\}}>\epsilon
\right)=-\infty
\]

is false.

The pressure argument in the proof exhibits the same error. Twisting by `s r 1_{r>L}` is available only for `s` below the exponential-moment radius. Chernoff then yields at best a finite cost `-s epsilon`, not a cost tending to infinity. One cannot send `s` to infinity.

## Consequences of the one-big-excursion defect

### 1. The block empirical measure is not an exponentially good complete state for collision time

A single return excursion can carry a macroscopic fraction of collision time while contributing only `1/N` to the ordinary empirical block measure. The block empirical measure loses the internal path distribution of that excursion. Hence its Palm image cannot approximate the full collision empirical path superexponentially.

### 2. The displayed pure ratio rate is incomplete

The formula

\[
\mathcal I^c(\nu)
=
\inf_{\mathscr S_c\widehat\nu=\nu}
\frac{\widehat{\mathcal I}(\widehat\nu)}
{\widehat\nu(r)}
\]

can describe phases with positive inducing-domain frequency and finite mean return. It does not account for zero-frequency or recession phases generated by macroscopic excursions avoiding the inducing set. Those phases require a direct collision-time pressure or a defect-completed inducing scheme.

### 3. The singularity shield and clock surgery do not repair the missing state

The shield truncates long blocks using the false estimate. The lower-bound surgery starts from an ergodic block law of finite exponential mark moment, so it never constructs the omitted one-big-excursion phases. Initial and terminal residual estimates cannot turn a macroscopic internal excursion into a sublinear endpoint error.

### 4. The physical-time theorem also loses its foundation

Finite horizon bounds physical duration by collision length, but the collision-time defect remains. Physical time cannot be obtained from the claimed block contraction until the collision path rate has been completed.

## Further major objections

### 1. Countable-code applicability remains abbreviated

The proof moves from finite connectors, summable variations, and an exponential return tail to a full good empirical-measure LDP through a custom weighted transfer norm. It does not establish all operator-domain, positive recurrence, compactness, and approximation hypotheses needed for the entire countable shift. In particular, “replace every discarded excursion by a connector with the same entrance and exit” does not automatically preserve invariance, entropy/free-energy cost, or the marked empirical law with the claimed convergence.

### 2. Presentation independence is not proved

The paper says that the first common return to a product magnet has exponential marks. Exponential tails for two individual inducing schemes do not automatically imply an exponential tail for their common refinement. The refinement can have slower recurrence. This must be proved for the chosen magnets, not inferred from uniqueness of the eventual LDP rate.

Uniqueness of a rate helps only after both presentations have independently produced full LDPs for exactly the same laws and topology.

### 3. The cotangent annihilator theorem uses the wrong dual argument

The Banach dual of a weighted dynamically Hölder algebra is not automatically the space of countably additive signed path measures. A Hahn–Banach separator can be a distributional or finitely additive functional. The proof then takes its “positive and negative Jordan parts” and normalizes them to invariant measures, an operation unavailable for a general continuous functional on this algebra.

A correct theorem needs a locally convex bounded-strict topology whose continuous dual is an explicitly identified Radon-measure space, or a separate cohomological theorem in the Hölder Banach dual. Rational-time averaging alone does not supply positivity or countable additivity.

### 4. The finite-rate support claim depends on the false frequency ledger

The proof that every finite-rate law gives zero mass to genuinely multivalued singular continuations invokes the assertion that every fixed positive singularity frequency has arbitrarily large cost. The bad-set pressure estimate is proved only after long-return truncation, whose superexponential removal is false. The graph-completed support conclusion is therefore not established globally.

### 5. The information-projection theorem is downstream of an unproved denominator estimate

The corrected “finite-rate feasible” hypothesis is necessary and welcome. Conditional concentration still requires the full LDP lower bound at the minimizer and control of the shrinking conditioning sets. Since the full path LDP is not proved, neither the denominator nor the random-root phase theorem is available.

### 6. The active paper is not standalone

As in the other round-three manuscripts, `main.tex` contains only a preamble and an input module. The module relies on the previously defined billiard, magnets, graph-completed path spaces, weighted topologies, and path maps without rebuilding their definitions. The controlling source is not a self-contained manuscript.

## Editorial recommendation

**Reject.** The one-big-excursion counterexample invalidates the main proof architecture, not merely an estimate. A serious reconstruction should begin with a direct collision-time pressure or a defect-completed inducing theorem that retains zero-frequency/recession phases, prove a full collision path LDP there, and only then pass to physical time using the bounded roof. The cotangent space must be rebuilt in a topology with a genuine measure dual. Until those tasks are completed in the controlling paper, A3 cannot serve as the parent rate for A4, C2, or D1.
