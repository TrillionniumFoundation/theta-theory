\section{Round-seventeen positive closure: entropy-controlled stopped renewal paths}
\label{sec:r17-a3}

The local limit theorem of A2 is not applied to controlled chains.  Predictable
controls enter only through an exact entropy variational representation.  The
augmented terminal branch is a microscopic random variable, and the physical
stopped path is obtained from it by an explicit contraction over compatible
completions.

\subsection{The legal branch process and entropy representation}

Let \(\mathcal G=(V,E)\) be the countable edge graph of the induced Sinai
return map.  An edge \(e\) has source \(s(e)\), target \(t(e)\), collision
length \(r(e)\), roof \(\tau(e)\), homology \(\kappa(e)\), and a complete
regular excursion \(\gamma_e\).  The reference transition kernel is
\(p(e\mid v)>0\) on legal edges and zero otherwise.  The induced Gibbs bounds
give, for some \(\eta>0\),
\[
 \sup_v\sum_{s(e)=v}p(e\mid v)
 e^{\eta(r(e)+\tau(e)+\mathfrak s(e))}<\infty,
\]
where \(\mathfrak s\) is the singularity/excursion compactness cost.

A predictable control is a sequence of kernels
\(q_i(\cdot\mid\mathcal F_{i-1})\) supported on legal outgoing edges.  For a
controlled branch word \(E_1,\ldots,E_n\), put
\[
 \mathcal R_n(q)=
 \sum_{i=1}^n
 H\bigl(q_i(\cdot\mid\mathcal F_{i-1})
       \mid p(\cdot\mid t(E_{i-1}))\bigr).
\]

\begin{theorem}[Exact entropy variational representation]
\label{thm:r17-a3-entropy}
For every bounded measurable functional \(F\) of the first \(n\) legal edges,
\[
 -\log\mathbb E_p e^{-F(E_1,\ldots,E_n)}
 =\inf_q\mathbb E_q\bigl[F+\mathcal R_n(q)\bigr].
\]
The identity holds for arbitrary predictable controls and uses no spectral,
nonlattice, or local-limit estimate for the controlled chain.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Disintegrate any probability \(Q\ll P\) on the legal word space into its
successive conditional kernels.  The chain rule gives
\[
 H(Q\mid P)=\mathbb E_Q\mathcal R_n(q).
\]
The Gibbs variational formula
\(-\log E_Pe^{-F}=\inf_Q(E_QF+H(Q\mid P))\) then gives the result.  Conversely
every predictable legal kernel defines a unique word law by Ionescu--Tulcea,
so no admissible measure is lost.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The augmented and physical stopped states}

For a collision horizon \(N\), let
\[
 \nu_N=\max\{m:S_mr\le N\},
 \qquad
 U_N=N-S_{\nu_N}r.
\]
The augmented stopped variable records:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item the edge empirical flow up to \(\nu_N\);
\item the expanded recurrent excursion path and its homology/roof measures;
\item all mesoscopic and recession excursion profiles;
\item the complete terminal edge \(E_{\nu_N+1}\) and the pointed prefix
      \(\gamma_{E_{\nu_N+1}}|_{[0,U_N]}\).
\end{enumerate}
Call this measurable map of the full word \(\Xi_N^{\rm aug}\).  The physical
stopped path is
\[
 \Xi_N^{\rm phys}=\pi_{\rm phys}\Xi_N^{\rm aug},
\]
where \(\pi_{\rm phys}\) forgets the unobserved part of the terminal edge.

Let \(\overline{\mathcal E}\) be the closure of the marked edge set for the
metric
\[
 d(e,e')=|r(e)^{-1}-r(e')^{-1}|
 +d_{J_1}^{\rm loc}(\widehat\gamma_e,\widehat\gamma_{e'})
 +d_V(s(e),s(e'))+d_V(t(e),t(e')),
\]
where \(\widehat\gamma_e\) is the unit-clock normalized excursion with its
singular one-sided germs.  Equip measures on this space with the weighted
weak topology generated by continuous functions dominated by
\(1+r+\tau+\mathfrak s\).  The full stopped state space \(\mathsf X_{\rm aug}\)
is the product of the local Skorokhod path space, these weighted measure
spaces, and the pointed terminal-edge space.

\begin{proposition}[Polish topology and compact coercive sets]
\label{prop:r17-a3-space}
The spaces \(\mathsf X_{\rm aug}\) and \(\mathsf X_{\rm phys}\) are Polish,
\(\pi_{\rm phys}\) is continuous, and the sets
\[
 \left\{x:\int(r+\tau+\mathfrak s)\,dm_x
       +\operatorname{osc}_{J_1}(x)+\mathcal T_{\rm term}(x)\le M\right\}
\]
are compact after taking their closed balance-compatible hull.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The graph completion of each normalized excursion is closed in the local
Skorokhod topology; adjoining the discrete source/target vertices preserves
Polishness.  Weighted Prokhorov gives compactness of the measure coordinates.
The clock bound excludes accumulation of infinitely many positive-length
returns in a compact interval, while the singularity cost and local
Skorokhod oscillation give compact containment of expanded paths.  A terminal
edge with unbounded length converges to a recession profile and retains its
pointed prefix, so the compatibility relation is closed.  Forgetting a
completion is continuous by construction.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Entropy compactness without controlled spectral theory}

\begin{lemma}[Entropy-exponential-moment inequality]
\label{lem:r17-a3-entropy-tight}
For every predictable control and every nonnegative word functional \(G\),
\[
 \mathbb E_qG
 \le {1\over\alpha}
 \left(\mathbb E_q\mathcal R_n(q)
 +\log\mathbb E_p e^{\alpha G}\right).
\]
Consequently a bound \(\mathbb E_q\mathcal R_n(q)\le Cn\) gives uniform
integrability of return, roof, singularity, and terminal-profile costs.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Apply the entropy inequality
\(\alpha E_QG\le H(Q\mid P)+\log E_Pe^{\alpha G}\) and the chain-rule identity
from Theorem~\ref{thm:r17-a3-entropy}.  The reference exponential moment is the
induced Gibbs moment displayed above.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Exponential tightness of stopped states]
\label{thm:r17-a3-tight}
The laws of \(\Xi_N^{\rm aug}\), at speed \(N\), are exponentially tight in
\(\mathsf X_{\rm aug}\).  The same is true in physical time and after the
physical contraction.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Choose a superlinear sequence of thresholds for return, roof, singularity
cost, path oscillation, and terminal length.  Chernoff bounds under the
reference law follow from the exponential Gibbs moment and the tower growth
lemma.  A union over the thresholds is summable and gives a compact set from
Proposition~\ref{prop:r17-a3-space} whose complement has probability
\(e^{-MN}\).  For entropy-controlled sequences, use
Lemma~\ref{lem:r17-a3-entropy-tight}; this is the compactness input in the
Laplace-principle proof.  Finite horizon gives equivalence of collision and
physical clock compactness on recurrent pieces, while the pointed recession
coordinate controls macroscopic terminal roofs.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Stationary relaxation and legal recovery}

For a probability edge flow \(Q(de)\), write \(Q_0\) and \(Q_1\) for its
source and target marginals.  It is balanced when \(Q_0=Q_1\).  Define
\[
 \mathfrak h(Q)=
 \begin{cases}
 H(Q\mid Q_0p),&Q_0=Q_1,\\
 +\infty,&\text{otherwise}.
 \end{cases}
\]

\begin{theorem}[Predictable controls relax to balanced edge flows]
\label{thm:r17-a3-relax}
For every sequence of predictable controls with bounded normalized entropy,
every occupation-flow limit is balanced and its entropy cost is at least
\(\mathfrak h(Q)\).  Conversely every balanced finite-cost flow, including
flows with finitely many prescribed recession profiles, has a recovery by
legal finite-memory Markov kernels and connector words, with convergence of
all marked clock and path coordinates and of the normalized entropy.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The endpoint imbalance of an \(n\)-edge word is the difference of two Dirac
masses divided by \(n\), hence vanishes.  Conditional Jensen applied to the
successive entropy terms yields
\[
 \liminf {1\over n}E\mathcal R_n(q)
 \ge H(Q\mid Q_0p).
\]
For recovery, first restrict to a finite edge set carrying all but
\(\varepsilon\) of the flow and weighted marks.  Add an
\(\varepsilon\)-amount of a fixed finite connector circulation, and solve the
finite-dimensional source--target balance equations; the correction is
\(O(\varepsilon)\).  The kernel \(Q(e)/Q_0(s(e))\) is then legal and
irreducible.  Its empirical flow and all bounded marked coordinates converge
by the ergodic theorem, and the entropy equals \(\mathfrak h(Q)\).

For a recession atom, choose an actual legal edge whose normalized profile
approximates the atom and whose length has the required scale.  Insert the
prescribed finite number of such edges between connector circulations.  Their
full branch log likelihood is paid once.  Since connector length is
sublinear, it does not change the target clocks or cost.  Diagonalizing in the
finite edge set, profile accuracy, and connector mass gives the general
recovery.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Terminal cost and physical contraction}

Let \(a\) be a terminal edge selected from a source vertex \(v\).  Its full
selection cost is \(-\log p(a\mid v)\).  If only the prefix \(\zeta\) is
observed, let
\[
 \mathcal C(v,\zeta)=
 \{a:s(a)=v,\ \gamma_a|_{[0,U]}=\zeta\}
\]
and define the prefix probability by summing the legal completions.

\begin{lemma}[Exact terminal contraction]
\label{lem:r17-a3-terminal}
The augmented terminal rate charges the complete selected edge in full.  The
physical terminal rate is
\[
 I_{\rm term}^{\rm phys}(v,\zeta)
 =-\log\sum_{a\in\mathcal C(v,\zeta)}p(a\mid v),
\]
with the analogous conditional integral when the completion set is
uncountable.  It is exactly the contraction of the augmented rate under
\(\pi_{\rm phys}\).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The entropy chain rule charges the conditional law of the selected edge before
the stopping operation.  Contracting a relative entropy over all measures
with a fixed prefix gives the negative logarithm of the reference prefix
event; this is the elementary entropy projection onto a measurable event.
No fraction of the branch log weight is used.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The collision and physical path LDPs}

Let \(\mathcal I^{\rm aug}_c\) be the lower-semicontinuous envelope of the
sum of the recurrent entropy \(\mathfrak h\), the exact costs of mesoscopic and
recession edges, and the terminal cost in
Lemma~\ref{lem:r17-a3-terminal}, subject to the deterministic collision-clock
and graph compatibility equations.

\begin{theorem}[Full augmented collision-clock LDP]
\label{thm:r17-a3-collision}
The augmented stopped variables \(\Xi_N^{\rm aug}\) satisfy a full LDP at
speed \(N\) with good rate \(\mathcal I_c^{\rm aug}\).  The physical stopped
paths satisfy a full LDP with good rate
\[
 \mathcal I_c^{\rm phys}(x)
 =\inf\{\mathcal I_c^{\rm aug}(y):\pi_{\rm phys}y=x\}.
\]
The proof uses no local limit theorem for predictable controlled kernels.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Apply the exact variational representation
Theorem~\ref{thm:r17-a3-entropy} to bounded continuous functionals of the
stopped state.  Controlled compactness is
Theorem~\ref{thm:r17-a3-tight}.  The liminf follows from
Theorem~\ref{thm:r17-a3-relax}, lower semicontinuity of relative entropy, and
closedness of the clock/graph relations.  The recovery half of that theorem,
with the exact terminal construction, gives the Laplace-principle limsup.
The rate is good by the compact coercive sets of
Proposition~\ref{prop:r17-a3-space}.  The contraction principle gives the
physical rate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Physical-clock LDP]
\label{thm:r17-a3-physical}
The same augmented construction stopped at deterministic physical time \(T\)
satisfies a full LDP at speed \(T\).  Its rate is obtained from
\(\mathcal I_c^{\rm aug}\) by the deterministic time-change equations,
including a macroscopic terminal roof.  The physical empirical path rate is
the contraction over terminal completions.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Finite horizon bounds recurrent collision and physical clocks in both
directions.  When a terminal or recession edge is macroscopic, its normalized
profile and pointed prefix are retained, so the time-change map remains
continuous on the augmented space.  Apply the extended contraction theorem.
The recovery in Theorem~\ref{thm:r17-a3-relax} selects the full terminal edge
before stopping and therefore realizes the exact physical horizon without an
unrecorded boundary cost.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Conditioning after the reference LLT]
\label{cor:r17-a3-conditioning}
When exact homology/return/roof conditioning is imposed, the denominator is
provided by the raw reference LLT of A2.  The controlled LDP proof itself is
unchanged and never applies that LLT to a controlled kernel.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
The conditioning ratio uses the reference probability only.  The numerator is
handled by the joint augmented LDP and the denominator by the A2 matrix-
coefficient Fourier theorem.  Predictable controls enter only through entropy
variational representation.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Round-seventeen A3 closure]
\label{thm:r17-a3-main}
The collision- and physical-time empirical paths have good stopped-path LDPs
on an explicit graph-completed topology.  Predictable controls require no
spectral theorem, terminal branch entropy is paid exactly, recession clocks
are exponentially tight, and the physical observable is a written
contraction of the augmented one.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Combine Theorems~\ref{thm:r17-a3-entropy},
\ref{thm:r17-a3-tight}, \ref{thm:r17-a3-relax},
\ref{thm:r17-a3-collision}, and \ref{thm:r17-a3-physical}, together with
Lemma~\ref{lem:r17-a3-terminal}.
\end{proof}
