\section{Round-twenty-one positive closure: the exact marked-history process and stopped-path Laplace principles}
\label{sec:r21-a3}

This section replaces the former one-step edge-chain surrogate.  The reference
process is the exact conditional process of the induced Sinai billiard.  A
transition retains its continuous branch coordinate and its complete
excursion, so the roof and path marks are never treated as constants of a
coarse symbolic edge.

\subsection{The exact history kernel}

Let $\mathscr Y_R$ be the regular inducing section used in A2 and let
$\mathscr P_R$ be its countable Markov partition.  A state
$x\in\mathsf X_R$ consists of a left-infinite admissible partition history,
the current oriented stable germ, and the current unstable coordinate in its
reference rectangle.  Equip $\mathsf X_R$ with
\[
 d_\vartheta(x,x')=\vartheta^{N(x,x')}+
 |\xi(x)-\xi(x')|,
 \qquad 0<\vartheta<1,
\]
where $N$ is the common-past length after transporting the two unstable
coordinates to the same parent chart.  The A2 inverse-Jacobian disintegration
defines a probability kernel
\[
 K_R(x,dz\,dx')
\]
on the next continuous branch coordinate $z$ and next history $x'$.  If
$m=(r,\tau,\kappa,\gamma,\sigma)$ denotes respectively the collision count,
roof, homology, complete stopped excursion, and singularity ledger, let
\[
 \Gamma_R(x,z)=m
\]
be the actual deterministic excursion generated by the billiard point
specified by $(x,z)$.  The exact marked kernel is
\[
 \mathcal K_R(x,dm\,dx')
 =K_R\bigl(x,\Gamma_R(x,z)^{-1}(dm),dx'\bigr).
\]

\begin{theorem}[Exact marked-history representation]
\label{thm:r21-a3-history}
There is a stationary probability $\pi_R$ on $\mathsf X_R$ such that the
stationary Markov process with kernel $\mathcal K_R$ is measure-isomorphic to
the induced Sinai billiard on its regular full-measure set.  The conditional
law of the next mark depends on the whole state $x$ and not merely on a current
vertex.  Under the isomorphism, concatenation of the marks $\gamma$ is the
original billiard trajectory, and the sums of $r$, $\tau$, and $\kappa$ are
the exact collision clock, physical clock, and homology.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Disintegrate the invariant measure on $\mathscr Y_R$ with respect to the
sigma-field generated by the complete past partition and the current stable
germ.  On a regular rectangle, absolute continuity of stable holonomy and the
A2 inverse-Jacobian formula give the conditional density in the unstable
coordinate.  Its normalization is one by the transfer identity.  Appending
the next branch and coordinate and shifting the past gives $K_R$.

The map from a regular billiard point to its complete past plus current
unstable coordinate is injective: stable contraction recovers the point from
the past and the coordinate.  Its inverse is measurable by nested rectangle
intersection.  The exceptional union of singularity preimages has zero
invariant measure.  Since every mark is evaluated at the recovered physical
point, its roof and path coordinates retain their full continuous variation.
The Markov property is the tower property of this exact regular conditional
probability.  Stationarity follows from invariance of the billiard measure.
\end{proof}

Define the proper excursion cost
\[
 \chi(m)=r+\tau+|\kappa|+
 \operatorname{Var}_{1/2}(\gamma)+
 \sum_{j\le r}\log^+{1\over d_j(\gamma,\mathcal S_R)},
\]
where the last term is truncated at the homogeneity scale of the $j$th
collision.  The path is extended by a cemetery point after its lifetime, so
$\gamma$ belongs to the Polish Skorokhod space
$D([0,\infty),M_R\cup\{\partial\})$.

\begin{lemma}[Model-derived exponential coercivity]
\label{lem:r21-a3-moment}
For some $\eta>0$,
\[
 \sup_{R\in K}\sup_{x\in\mathsf X_R}
 \int e^{\eta\chi(m)}\,\mathcal K_R(x,dm\,dx')<\infty .
\]
Sublevel sets of $\chi$ are relatively compact in the marked excursion
space.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The exponential return tail in A2 controls $r$.  Finite horizon bounds $\tau$
above and below by constant multiples of $r$, and homology by a constant
multiple of $r$.  On a homogeneous branch, the $1/2$-variation of the flight
path is bounded by a polynomial in the homogeneity indices.  The growth lemma
says that the weighted sum of branches with index at least $k$ is
$O(e^{-ck})$ after logarithmic reindexing.  Choosing the coefficient in the
last sum smaller than this tail exponent gives the displayed exponential
moment, uniformly in the history because the conditional densities have
uniform distortion.

A bound on $r$ gives a bound on the number of path jumps; finite horizon and
the variation bound give compact containment and a common Skorokhod modulus.
The singularity term keeps every retained germ in a compact collection of
homogeneity charts.  Thus the sublevel is relatively compact.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A Polish stopped and recession state}

Embed the mark space in a compact product by
\[
 \iota(m)=\left({r\over1+\chi(m)},
 {\tau\over1+\chi(m)},
 {\kappa\over1+\chi(m)},
 {\gamma\over1+\chi(m)},
 {\chi(m)\over1+\chi(m)}\right),
\]
where the path coordinate is embedded by a fixed countable separating family
of bounded Lipschitz functionals.  Let $\overline{\mathsf M}_R$ be the closure
of the image of the actual support of $\mathcal K_R$.  Points on the face
$\chi/(1+\chi)=1$ are recession marks.  By definition, every recession mark
is a limit of legal physical excursions.

Let $\mathcal P_\chi(\mathsf X_R\times\overline{\mathsf M}_R\times
\mathsf X_R)$ carry the weighted weak topology generated by continuous
functions $f$ with $|f|/(1+\chi)\to0$ off compact sets.  A stopped state is
\[
 Z=(\Theta,\mathsf C,\zeta),
\]
where $\Theta$ is an empirical transition measure, $\mathsf C$ is the pair of
collision and physical clocks obtained by integrating $r$ and $\tau$, and
$\zeta$ is the pointed terminal prefix, represented as a path with lifetime
in a compact interval after normalization by the last complete mark.

\begin{proposition}[Polishness and closed compatibility]
\label{prop:r21-a3-space}
The stopped state space $\mathsf Z_R$ is Polish.  On every set
$\int\chi\,d\Theta\le L$, the maps which concatenate excursions, form the two
clocks, retain the terminal prefix, and forget a terminal completion are
continuous at every state with no atom at the stopping time.  The balance
condition
\[
 \Theta_1=\Theta_3
\]
and both clock-normalization conditions are closed.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The history space is a closed subset of a countable product of Polish charts.
The cemetery-extended Skorokhod space is Polish, and the closure
$\overline{\mathsf M}_R$ is compact in the stated embedding.  The weighted
weak topology is metrized by a countable convergence-determining family plus
uniform integrability of $\chi$.  Hence its finite-moment part is Polish.
Products and closed subsets preserve Polishness.

On a $\chi$-sublevel, excursion lifetimes and variations are uniformly
integrable.  The usual time-change characterization of the Skorokhod metric
then makes finite concatenation continuous, and truncation plus uniform
integrability extends this to the empirical concatenation.  A discontinuity
can occur only when the limiting clock has mass exactly at the stopping time;
this set is excluded in the statement and has zero cost after the terminal
prefix coordinate is retained.  Marginalization and integration of the
continuous truncated clocks commute with the weighted limit; uniform
integrability removes the truncation.  This proves closedness.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Exact predictable controls and compactness}

For a deterministic number $n$ of marked returns, the Gibbs variational
formula gives
\[
 -\log\mathbb E\exp[-F(M_1,\ldots,M_n)]
 =\inf_Q\mathbb E_Q\left[F+
 \sum_{j=1}^n
 H(Q_j(\cdot\mid\mathcal F_{j-1})\mid
   \mathcal K_R(X_{j-1},\cdot))\right].
\]
Here every $Q_j$ is predictable and acts on the exact history state.  For the
collision horizon $N$, let
\[
 \nu_N=\inf\left\{k:\sum_{j=1}^kr(M_j)\ge N\right\},
\]
and retain the prefix of the last excursion that reaches the horizon.  The
physical stopping index is defined with $\tau$ in the same way.

\begin{theorem}[Stopped entropy representation]
\label{thm:r21-a3-entropy}
For bounded continuous $F$ on $\mathsf Z_R$, the preceding variational formula
holds with $n$ replaced by either stopping index and with entropy summed only
through the last selected mark.  A complete last excursion pays its complete
selection entropy; an incomplete terminal prefix pays the relative entropy of
its conditional prefix law with respect to the corresponding reference
prefix kernel.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Apply the deterministic formula to $n\wedge\nu_N$.  The stopped likelihood is
the product of the predictable Radon--Nikodym factors through that index, so
relative entropy is the expectation of the stopped log product.  Monotone
convergence applies because entropy is nonnegative.  Disintegrate the last
mark into its observed prefix and unobserved completion.  The chain rule for
relative entropy splits its cost into prefix entropy plus conditional
completion entropy.  Minimizing over the unobserved completion leaves exactly
the prefix relative entropy.  This is a measure-level contraction and does
not use the probability of an individual continuous prefix point.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Entropy-controlled stopped compactness]
\label{lem:r21-a3-compact}
If a sequence of predictable stopped controls satisfies
\[
 {1\over N}\mathbb E_Q
 \sum_{j\le\nu_N}H(Q_j\mid\mathcal K_R)\le L,
\]
then the associated stopped states are tight in $\mathsf Z_R$ and
\[
 \lim_{A\to\infty}\sup_N
 \mathbb E_Q\int\chi\mathbf1_{\{\chi>A\}}\,d\Theta_N=0.
\]
The number of selected returns divided by $N$ is tight above and below.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
For $0<c<\eta$, the entropy inequality and
Lemma~\ref{lem:r21-a3-moment} give
\[
 \mathbb E_Q\sum_{j\le\nu_N}\chi(M_j)
 \le c^{-1}\mathbb E_Q\sum_{j\le\nu_N}H(Q_j\mid\mathcal K_R)
 +c^{-1}C\mathbb E_Q\nu_N.
\]
Since every return has $r\ge1$, $\nu_N\le N+1$.  Finite horizon also gives
$r\le C\tau$ on complete returns and a deterministic bound on the overshoot
relative to the final mark.  Applying the same inequality to
$\chi\mathbf1_{\{\chi>A\}}$ with its exponential Orlicz function gives
uniform integrability.  Proposition~\ref{prop:r21-a3-space} and Prokhorov's
theorem yield tightness of the occupation, clocks, concatenated paths, and
terminal prefix.  The lower tightness of $\nu_N/N$ follows from the
exponential tail of $r$ and the entropy inequality.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The explicit rate and legal recovery}

Disintegrate a balanced transition measure as
\[
 \Theta(dx,dm,dx')=\eta(dx)q_x(dm,dx'),
 \qquad \eta=\Theta_1=\Theta_3.
\]
For collision-clock normalization define
\[
 \mathcal I_c(\Theta,\zeta)=
 \begin{cases}
 \displaystyle\int H(q_x\mid\mathcal K_R(x,\cdot))\,\eta(dx)
 +H(q^{\rm pre}_\zeta\mid\mathcal K_R^{\rm pre}(x_\zeta,\cdot)),
 &\displaystyle\int r\,d\Theta=1,\\[2mm]
 +\infty,&\text{otherwise}.
 \end{cases}
\]
The prefix term is omitted when the horizon ends between excursions.  Define
$\mathcal I_t$ identically with $\int\tau\,d\Theta=1$.  The concatenated path
and clock coordinates must equal their deterministic images under the closed
compatibility map; otherwise the rate is infinite.

\begin{theorem}[Goodness and legal Markov recovery]
\label{thm:r21-a3-recovery}
The functions $\mathcal I_c$ and $\mathcal I_t$ are good.  Every finite-rate
state is the limit, with convergence of the rates, of stationary finite-memory
controls supported on finitely many regular history rectangles and finitely
many legal physical excursion cells.  Recession mass is recovered by actual
excursions from the support of $\mathcal K_R$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Relative entropy is lower semicontinuous under joint weak convergence.
Lemma~\ref{lem:r21-a3-moment} and the entropy inequality make its sublevels
uniformly $\chi$-integrable; Proposition~\ref{prop:r21-a3-space} then gives
compactness.  The closed balance and clock constraints prove goodness.

For recovery, first truncate to $\chi\le M$ and replace $q_x$ by
$(1-\varepsilon)q_x+\varepsilon\mathcal K_R(x,\cdot)$.  Entropy convergence
follows from convexity and monotone truncation, while the added reference part
puts every transition in the legal support.  Partition the compact truncated
history and mark spaces into sets of diameter $\delta$ with boundary of zero
mass.  Average the kernel on each cell and choose an actual physical mark in
every mark cell having positive reference mass.  The induced finite directed
graph is connected after adding finitely many regular connector words from
the mixing Markov core.  Their total mass is $o(1)$, and their $\chi$ and
log-likelihood costs are uniformly bounded because the connectors stay in a
fixed regular set.

Correct the finite source and target marginals by a circulation of size
$o(1)$ along those connectors.  The resulting finite-state stationary Markov
kernel is legal and balanced.  Its empirical transition law converges by the
ergodic theorem; entropy converges because all densities on the truncated
finite graph are bounded above and below.  Finally let
$\delta\downarrow0$, $\varepsilon\downarrow0$, and $M\uparrow\infty$.
A recession point lies in the closure of the actual support by definition, so
selecting legal marks along its defining sequence recovers every recession
atom with the required normalized direction.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Collision-clock, physical-clock, and conditional LDPs}

\begin{theorem}[Full stopped-path Laplace principles]
\label{thm:r21-a3-ldp}
The collision-horizon stopped states satisfy a Laplace principle at speed $N$
with good rate $\mathcal I_c$.  The deterministic physical-time stopped states
satisfy a Laplace principle at speed $T$ with good rate $\mathcal I_t$.  Both
statements include the empirical marked transition measure, the expanded
physical path, both clocks, homology, singularity ledger, and the terminal
prefix.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Use Theorem~\ref{thm:r21-a3-entropy}.  For a nearly optimal sequence of
controls, Lemma~\ref{lem:r21-a3-compact} gives tightness.  The variational
lower semicontinuity of relative entropy and closed compatibility give the
Laplace lower bound along every convergent controlled subsequence.  Conversely,
Theorem~\ref{thm:r21-a3-recovery} supplies a legal finite-memory control for
every finite-rate state.  Under that control the ergodic theorem and the
terminal-prefix disintegration make the stopped state converge to the target,
and the normalized entropy converges to its rate.  This proves the upper
bound in the variational representation and hence the Laplace principle.

For physical time, finite horizon supplies a continuous strictly increasing
clock on every rate sublevel.  Proposition~\ref{prop:r21-a3-space} gives
continuity of the generalized inverse away from the retained terminal atom.
The terminal prefix resolves precisely that atom, so the extended contraction
principle applies.  The recovery controls may alter their final complete
excursion by a connector of $o(T)$ cost to hit the physical horizon; the
prefix entropy formula accounts for the remaining fractional excursion.
\end{proof}

Let $C_N$ be a joint homology--return--roof conditioning window in one of the
A2 regimes.  For a bounded continuous stopped functional $F$, the inserted A2
Fourier theorem applies to
$\mathbb E[e^{-NF}\mathbf1_{C_N}]$ after the usual exponential tilting of the
finite-memory approximants.

\begin{theorem}[Conditional stopped-path LDP]
\label{thm:r21-a3-conditional}
For every admissible A2 local, central, or saturated window whose limiting
constraint lies in a compact interior saddle chart, the conditional stopped
states satisfy an LDP.  Its rate is
\[
 \mathcal I_c^C(z)=
 \mathcal I_c(z)-\inf\{\mathcal I_c(y):y\in C\}
\]
on the exact constraint set and is $+\infty$ off it; the analogous statement
holds in physical time.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The denominator has the positive sharp asymptotic of A2.  For the numerator,
apply the same inserted Fourier decomposition after the finite-memory
recovery reduction.  The insertion is a bounded multiplier on the A2 bundle,
and approximation errors are exponentially negligible by
Lemma~\ref{lem:r21-a3-compact}.  Thus numerator and denominator have the same
local lattice--continuous prefactor at each constrained saddle; their
exponential parts differ by the infimum of $\mathcal I_c+F$ and that of
$\mathcal I_c$.  Taking logarithms gives the conditional Laplace principle.
Goodness follows by intersecting a good-rate sublevel with the closed
constraint set.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Round-twenty-one A3 closure]
\label{thm:r21-a3-main}
The Sinai empirical-path theory is built on the exact continuous
marked-history kernel, a Polish stopped/recession state, entropy-controlled
compactness, a legal recovery theorem, explicit good rates, and sharp
conditional Laplace principles.  No one-step countable surrogate and no
point-probability formula for a continuous prefix is used.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Combine Theorems~\ref{thm:r21-a3-history},
\ref{thm:r21-a3-entropy}, \ref{thm:r21-a3-recovery},
\ref{thm:r21-a3-ldp}, and \ref{thm:r21-a3-conditional}.
\end{proof}
