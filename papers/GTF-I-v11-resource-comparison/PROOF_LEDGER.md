## New-proof dependency spine

The simulation theorem uses the defined marked feedback channel and explicit finite product construction. It does not invoke a historical pipeline theorem. Complete-state fusion uses the v10 operational marked quotient and parameter-independent reverse-report factorization. The task theorem supplies an operational interpretation of completeness; it does not replace the simulator construction.

The conditional compactness theorem uses the v10 innovation-policy compact space and measurable representatives, and adds a proof of conditional-kernel representation/compactness and uniform risk continuity. Classical positive-functional, minimax and measurable-selection results are named as such.

The microscopic graph-core construction uses classical almost-everywhere hard-sphere flow existence and elementary strongly continuous group smoothing. Its acquisition corollary is the point where the new resource comparison is consumed. This topology is not the action-sublevel topology of the historical kinetic corrector.
