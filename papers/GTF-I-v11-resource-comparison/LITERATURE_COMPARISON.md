## Filtered experiments: an explicit unresolved original-text boundary

**Norberg (2002), “Comparison of Statistical Experiments with Filtered Probability Spaces,” Statistics & Risk Modeling 20, 1–28, DOI 10.1524/strm.2002.20.14.1.** The bibliographic identity and direct conceptual relevance were identified. The full original text was not successfully obtained. Therefore the present audit does not state which of its exact hypotheses or conclusions exclude finite retained-state signatures, common encoders or marked reconstruction. It would be unjustified to manufacture a theorem-level absence claim from its title or an abstract.

This means E10.5 is not certified fully closed. The report should be read as a substantiated crosswalk for the accessible originals and a clearly delimited outstanding comparison for Norberg, not as an exhaustive survey proving priority.

## What remains after subtracting the classical ingredients

The submitted connection has four parts. First, simulator state and seed cost are carried through an actual common-encoder implementation, not only through an order on unrestricted experiments. Second, complete-state fusion identifies a precise subcategory on which marked reconstruction costs no additional persistent state, and a temporal example shows why the distinction is necessary outside it. Third, exact finite-task compatibility and Brier completion describe which decision requirements force that completeness. Fourth, the resulting comparison is applied to genuine microscopic graph-core acquisition, with a uniform bound across all register widths.

Neither the elementary product-state construction, conditional kernel compactness, automaton span algebra, graph coloring, nor orbit smoothing is by itself claimed to be new. Whether their stated resource-sensitive connection and its consequences meet the requested journal threshold is left to the independent referee. The claims are not supported by bibliographic counts or source-build diagnostics.
