## Scope requiring particular referee attention

The finite-task compatible cover is not an unconditional causal width-profile theorem. Its causal Brier refinement states a strict-positivity hypothesis. The singular compactness theorem prices a physically repeated covariate as an observation interface, not as private memory; it does not assert closure for arbitrary filtered information structures. Exact complete-state fusion applies to complete consumers, not every finite task list.

The hard-sphere result concerns actual microscopic transport in a weighted Lp graph topology. It does not claim the historical action-sublevel BBGKY corrector, exponential-rate estimates, or the nonlinear kinetic limit. The original full text of Norberg's filtered-experiment paper was not obtained in this audit; the comparison document does not convert bibliographic identification into proof-level priority exclusion.
