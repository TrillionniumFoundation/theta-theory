\appendix
\section{Theorem dependencies and information conventions}
\label{sec:v17-dependencies}

The comparison conventions of Section~\ref{sec:v13-deficiency} supply
a compact original behavior image for each fixed signature.
Theorem~\ref{thm:v17-dual} uses only that image and its affine tests.
Lemma~\ref{lem:v17-chart} and the binomial estimate then give
Theorem~\ref{thm:v17-degree}. The strict row-cube verifier of
Theorem~\ref{thm:v16-dual} is a separate route for checking lower
bounds. Proposition~\ref{prop:v17-effective} combines finite strict
certificates, executable upper tables and stateless presentation
errors; it does not require an inverse constrained randomization
theorem.

The collision path has a second, independent finite calculation:
Theorem~\ref{thm:v17-markedgap} solves its marked gated reference
experiment, and Lemma~\ref{lem:v17-scattering} proves the physical
separation of incoming and outgoing detector values. The stateless
marked variation estimate joins these two arguments in
Theorem~\ref{thm:v17-physicalgap}.
Proposition~\ref{prop:v17-collisioncompute} verifies the effective
presentation input using a separated collision root. None of these
steps assumes the collective benchmark's conserved-mode formula.

For optional projections, Bayes' formula and maximal inequalities
first prove Theorem~\ref{thm:v17-optional}. Conditional Gaussian
change of measure then verifies its hypotheses in
Theorem~\ref{thm:v17-physicaloptional}. The same-latent maximal
coupling is used only for process convergence. Finite-register risk
transport is instead a data-processing consequence of the original
marked variation estimate.

The graph-domain, residual and finite-prefix sections supply the
broader physical presentation machinery. Their input is an actual
flow, a specified Hilbert realization, a prepared law and an observable
with the declared approximation properties. The bound in
Theorem~\ref{thm:v15-main} already expresses the same-budget transfer
principle. Theorem~\ref{thm:v16-main} retains it with the strict
polynomial lower module. The present adaptive testing and scattering
results add independent-test size control and a collision-generated
resource separation; they do not reclassify the transfer principle as
a new result each time a certificate module changes.

Throughout the article fresh private randomization has no memory.
A hidden persistent selector and a visible persistent selector are
separate signatures. A visible comparison is joint with its
independent target selector; marginalizing it changes the problem.
The computational chart, the revealed-candidate testing game and the
latent-preserving convergence coupling are proof constructions, not
unpriced channels available to the physical decision maker.
