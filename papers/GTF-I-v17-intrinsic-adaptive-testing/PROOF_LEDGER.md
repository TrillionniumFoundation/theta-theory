## Review boundaries

The new independent-test degree/support statement is intrinsic to the chosen observable metric image; it is not a representation-invariant coefficient-bit bound or a polynomial-time algorithm. Generic real quantifier elimination can decide the fixed finite semialgebraic boundary; this does not assert a finite Bernstein certificate at equality. The two-sphere construction uses acquisition feedback and bounded single-scattering geometry. The new C2 edge proves a sufficient optional-projection result, not every hypothesis or conclusion of historical C2. Full Norberg original-text priority remains unverified.
