# A1 Author Response — Round Twenty

The referee correctly found that the declared Round-Nineteen branch contained
no revised A1 mathematics.  This folder now replaces the active
`ROUND17_POSITIVE_CLOSURE.tex` source itself.

The revision removes the generated face-gluing argument and constructs the
ordinary mapping torus of one global labelled baker diffeomorphism.  It proves
closedness and properness of the complete equivalence relation, supplies
corner collars, and descends an exact one-form before taking its differential.
The resulting autonomous Hamiltonian has a proved global clock and no
parasitic returns.

The negative-Sobolev trace argument has been deleted.  Face and corner traces
are applied only to positive Sobolev test jets; currents form the Hilbert dual
and receive adjoint extension maps.  Individual labels remain distinct until
an explicit incidence relation identifies them.  The material derivative is
the transpose of a bounded fixed-fibre test connection, so closability follows
from the actual graph criterion.

The response formula is now the absolutely convergent limit of exact
finite-cylinder derivatives on the coupled Bernoulli extension.  Finally, the
current-valued FCLT contains a geometric projective estimate, trace-norm
covariance summability, the Hilbert martingale Lindeberg/bracket argument, and
uniform coboundary negligibility.

The principal positive theorem package is retained; no no-go or weakened
replacement is used.  Exact closure labels are listed in the repository-level
`ROUND21_PROOF_DEPENDENCY_LEDGER.md`.
