\section{Round-seventeen positive closure: a global hybrid Hamiltonian system and a conormal response calculus}
\label{sec:r17-a1}

This section replaces the unmaterialized round-sixteen candidate.  The
mechanical realization is constructed globally before any response derivative
is taken.  Moving-seam derivatives are then defined in a fixed conormal
Hilbert scale, not identified with ordinary H\"older cylinder functions.  The
current-valued invariance principle is proved by a Hilbert-valued martingale
approximation.

\subsection{A finite global return section}

Let
\[
 0=s_0(a)<s_1(a)<\cdots<s_4(a)=1,
 \qquad I_i(a)=(s_{i-1}(a),s_i(a)),
 \qquad w_i=s_i-s_{i-1},
\]
with \(a\) in a compact interval \(K\Subset(-1,1)\), so
\(w_i\ge w_*>0\).  On the branch \(I_j(a)\times(0,1)\), put
\[
 B_{a,j}(q,p)=\left({q-s_{j-1}\over w_j},
                    s_{j-1}+w_jp\right).
\]
For \(i,j,k\in\{1,2,3,4\}\), define the open transition rectangle
\[
 S_{ijk}(a)=
 \left\{(q,p):p\in I_i(a),\ q\in s_{j-1}(a)+w_j(a)I_k(a)\right\}.
\]
The first index records the preceding strip, the second the active branch,
and the third the next strip.  We retain all one-sided faces as labelled
faces.  Thus the section
\[
 \widehat S_a=\bigsqcup_{i,j,k}\overline{S_{ijk}(a)}
\]
is a finite compact manifold with corners; distinct germs are never
identified on the section.

\begin{lemma}[Correct exact primitive]
\label{lem:r17-a1-primitive}
With \(\lambda=p\,dq\),
\[
 B_{a,j}^{*}(P\,dQ)-\lambda=dG_{a,j},
 \qquad
 G_{a,j}(q,p)={s_{j-1}(a)\over w_j(a)}
              (q-s_{j-1}(a)).
\]
In particular there is no \(dp\)-term.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Since \(dQ=dq/w_j\) for fixed \(a\),
\[
 B_{a,j}^{*}(P\,dQ)-p\,dq
 ={s_{j-1}+w_jp\over w_j}\,dq-p\,dq
 ={s_{j-1}\over w_j}\,dq=dG_{a,j}.
\]
\end{proof}

For every transition \((i,j,k)\), take a copy of
\([0,1]_t\times S_{ijk}(a)\times\mathbb R_E\) with symplectic form
\(dq\wedge dp+dt\wedge dE\).  In three mutually disjoint clock windows use
Hamiltonians generating translation by \(-s_{j-1}\) in \(q\), the linear
flow of \(-\log(w_j)qp\), and translation by \(s_{j-1}\) in \(p\).  At the
outgoing face identify the point \((Q,P)\), with \(Q\in I_k\) and
\(P\in I_j\), with the incoming sheet whose first two labels are \((j,k)\).
The final label is determined after cutting \(Q\) into the four intervals
\(s_{k-1}+w_kI_\ell\).  Gluings are therefore finite and are made only along
closed exact Lagrangian graphs.

\begin{theorem}[Global autonomous hybrid realization]
\label{thm:r17-a1-global}
The preceding finite mapping-cylinder quotient is a Hausdorff,
second-countable exact symplectic hybrid manifold \(\mathcal M_a\) with
corners.  It carries one autonomous Hamiltonian flow on
\(\mathcal M_a\times T^*\mathbb S^1\) whose clock is strictly increasing,
whose common section is \(\widehat S_a\), and whose first-return relation is
exactly the labelled baker return.  On the regular full-measure set the return
is single-valued, has return time one, has no parasitic returns, and projects
to \(B_a\) on the ordinary square.  Multiple-face encounters remain finite
labelled hybrid states.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The local Hamiltonian flows are complete affine symplectic maps and their
composition is \(B_{a,j}\).  Lemma~\ref{lem:r17-a1-primitive} shows that each
outgoing graph is exact.  Source and target faces are compact and every gluing
graph is closed; a finite quotient by such proper face identifications is
Hausdorff and second countable.  The clock Hamiltonian contains the term
\(E\), hence \(\dot t=1\).  All channel perturbations are supported away from
\(t=0,1\), so an orbit meets the incoming section only at integer clock times.
The finite source--target labels make the next sheet unique off the labelled
seams.  Exactness and the branch Jacobian one give preservation of the section
area.  At a corner the incident face label is part of the state, so no
non-Hausdorff identification is introduced.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[One global work pair]
\label{prop:r17-a1-work}
After adjoining one canonical pair \((z,\pi_z)\), there is a smooth global
Hamiltonian term for which
\[
 z_{n+1}-z_n=c_j
\]
on every channel with active branch \(j\).  It does not alter the return map
on \((q,p)\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Let \(\rho\in C_c^\infty((0,1))\) have integral one and add
\(\pi_zc_j\rho(t)\) on the disjoint channel labelled by \(j\).  These pieces
are a single smooth function on the disjoint channel interiors and vanish in
all gluing collars.  Hamilton's equations give \(\dot z=c_j\rho(t)\) and
\(\dot\pi_z=0\); the other coordinates are unchanged by this term.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A fixed conormal Hilbert scale}

Let \(\mathcal F_n(a_0)\) be the finite stratification of the reference square
by all forward and backward seam preimages of depth at most \(n\).  Every
stratum is an affine rectangle, segment, or point.  Choose smooth reference
embeddings \(\iota_{F,a}\) for \(a\in K\), one-sided at a birth parameter.
For codimension \(k\), normal derivative order \(r\), and depth \(n\), put
\[
 \mathcal H^{m}_{n,k,r}
 =H^{-m-k-r}\!\left([0,1]^2;
   N^{\otimes r}\right),
 \qquad m>4q+8,
\]
and define
\[
 \mathbb J_q^m
 =H^{-m}([0,1]^2)
 \oplus
 \bigoplus_{n\ge0}\bigoplus_{k+r\le q}
       2^{-n}(1+n)^{-2q-4}\mathcal H^m_{n,k,r}.
\]
A family on a refinement is first summed as an ambient distribution and only
then measured.  Hence subdividing a face does not change its norm.  The bulk
component occurs once and the constant bulk density has its ordinary
\(H^{-m}\)-norm.

For a smooth current \(T_F\) on a reference stratum define independently
\[
 J_{F,r}(a)=\partial_a^r(\iota_{F,a})_\#T_F
\]
by differentiating its pairing with a smooth test form.  Clean intersections
of the affine vertical and horizontal strata define corner traces by the
standard iterated Sobolev trace; the choice \(m>4q+8\) leaves more than one
trace order at every step.

\begin{theorem}[Closed conormal jet calculus]
\label{thm:r17-a1-conormal}
For every fixed \(q\):
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item all face restriction, boundary, and clean-intersection maps extend
      continuously between the components of \(\mathbb J_q^m\);
\item the material derivative \(\mathfrak D_a\), first defined by the
      ambient distributional formula above, is closable from
      \(\mathbb J_{q+1}^{m+2}\) to \(\mathbb J_q^m\);
\item the labelled return push-forward \(\mathcal T_a\) and its parameter
      derivatives satisfy, uniformly on \(K\),
\[
 \|\partial_a^r\mathcal T_aJ\|_{\mathbb J_q^m}
 \le C_q^{r+1}r!\,
      \|J\|_{\mathbb J_{q+r}^{m+2r}},
 \qquad 0\le r\le q;
\]
\item every repeated moving-boundary derivative is the finite clean conormal
      chain-rule sum over flags, is independent of local defining functions,
      and has a Taylor remainder bounded by
\[
 \|\mathcal T_{a+h}J-
   \sum_{r=0}^{q}{h^r\over r!}\partial_a^r\mathcal T_aJ\|_{
     \mathbb J_0^m}
 \le C_q|h|^{q+1}\|J\|_{\mathbb J_{q+1}^{m+2q+2}}.
\]
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The trace theorem gives a bounded map
\(H^{s}\to H^{s-1/2}\) on every affine face; duality gives the asserted maps
on negative spaces.  Vertical and horizontal faces meet cleanly, so iterated
traces commute up to the orientation sign and no product of two unrelated
distributions is formed.  If \(J_n\to0\) in the stronger space and
\(\mathfrak D_aJ_n\to K\) in the weaker space, pairing with a smooth form and
integrating the scalar derivative over a parameter interval gives
\(\langle K,\phi\rangle=0\); hence the derivative is closable.

Every branch map and every reference embedding is affine-rational in the
parameters with denominator bounded below by \(w_*\).  The distributional
Fa\`a di Bruno formula therefore gives at most \(C_q^rr!\) terms, each a
bounded affine push-forward followed by at most \(r\) trace operations.  A
preimage can increase the depth by at most one; the factor \(2^{-n}\) absorbs
that shift, while summing ambient currents makes the estimate independent of
how a face was subdivided.  The same induction proves the flag chain rule.
Taylor's integral remainder and the estimate at order \(q+1\) give (iv).
\end{proof}

\subsection{Response words and the current-valued invariance principle}

Let \(L_a^+\) and \(L_a^-\) be the one-sided Bernoulli transfer operators on
future and past H\"older spaces and let \(R_a^\pm=(I-L_a^\pm)^{-1}Q_a^\pm\).
Let \(S_a^{(r)}\) be the \(r\)-th score multiplier, \(C_a^{(r)}\) the
present-symbol coupling derivative, and \(D_a^{(r)}\) the closed conormal jet
operator.  A response word of total order \(q\) is an ordered product in
\[
 D_a^{(r)},\quad S_a^{(r)}R_a^+,\quad S_a^{(r)}R_a^-,\quad
 C_a^{(r)}R_a^+R_a^-,
\]
whose derivative orders sum to \(q\).  Its coefficient is the corresponding
multivariate Bell coefficient.

\begin{theorem}[Explicit all-order response]
\label{thm:r17-a1-response}
For a \(C^{q+1}\) transported physical current
\(F_a\in\mathbb J_{q+1}^{m+2q+2}\),
\[
 {d^q\over da^q}\Lambda_a(F_a)
 =\sum_{W\in\mathfrak W_q}c(W)\Lambda_a(WF_a),
\]
where every source and target space is determined by
Theorem~\ref{thm:r17-a1-conormal}.  Uniformly for \(a\in K\),
\[
 \,\sum_{W\in\mathfrak W_q}|c(W)|\|\Lambda_aW\|
 \le C_q(1-\rho_K)^{-2q-2}.
\]
The formula is invariant under dynamic refinement.  If \(F\) is a fixed
physical observable rather than a transported observable, the score,
transport, and flag terms cancel and \(d^q\int F\,dqdp/da^q=0\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Differentiate a finite past--future cylinder pairing.  The ordinary Leibniz
rule produces precisely a score, present coupling, or conormal material
insertion.  Centered terms are solved by the two one-sided Poisson equations,
which insert \(R_a^\pm\).  Induction gives the ordered word set and the Bell
coefficient.  The one-sided gaps bound the resolvents; the factorial conormal
bounds in Theorem~\ref{thm:r17-a1-conormal} bound every remaining insertion.
Absolute summability permits the cylinder depth to tend to infinity.
Refinement invariance is ambient-current additivity.  The last claim follows
by differentiating the exact change-of-variables identity for Lebesgue area.
\end{proof}

Fix \(q\), and let \(Y_j^{(q)}\) be the centered \(q\)-jet increment at the
\(j\)-th return, transported to the reference space \(\mathbb J_q^m\).  The
geometric depth weights and the Bernoulli cylinder weights imply
\[
 \sum_{n\ge0}
 \|\mathbb E(Y_0^{(q)}\mid\mathcal F_{-n}^0)
   -\mathbb E(Y_0^{(q)}\mid\mathcal F_{-n+1}^0)\|_{L^2(\mathbb J_q^m)}
 <\infty.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Radon current-valued functional CLT]
\label{thm:r17-a1-fclt}
The covariance series
\[
 \Sigma_q=\sum_{k\in\mathbb Z}
 \mathbb E\bigl[Y_0^{(q)}\otimes Y_k^{(q)}\bigr]
\]
converges in trace norm on the separable Hilbert space
\(\mathbb J_q^m\).  Moreover
\[
 n^{-1/2}\sum_{j<nt}Y_j^{(q)}
 \Longrightarrow W_q(t)
\]
in \(D([0,1],\mathbb J_q^m)\), where \(W_q\) is a Radon Brownian motion with
covariance \(\Sigma_q\).  The statement includes all bulk, face, corner, and
higher-flag components of order at most \(q\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The displayed projective summability gives the Hilbert-valued Gordin
decomposition \(Y_j=M_j+H\circ\sigma^{j+1}-H\circ\sigma^j\), with
\((M_j)\) a stationary square-integrable martingale difference sequence.
The conormal estimate and \(w_i\ge w_*\) give
\(\mathbb E\|Y_0\|^2<\infty\).  Hence the covariance operator is positive
and trace class, with trace bounded by the absolutely summable correlation
series.  The Hilbert-space martingale invariance principle gives convergence
of the martingale partial sums.  The coboundary is negligible uniformly in
time after division by \(\sqrt n\).  Burkholder's inequality gives tightness;
separability makes the Gaussian law Radon.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Separated calibration and A1 closure]
\label{thm:r17-a1-main}
The benchmark admits one global autonomous hybrid Hamiltonian realization, one
global canonical work coordinate, a closed all-order conormal response
calculus, explicit summable response words, and a Radon current-valued FCLT.
The mechanical conjugate field equals an entropic valuation coefficient only
under the additional equality of their likelihood cocycles.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The mechanical assertions are
Theorem~\ref{thm:r17-a1-global} and
Proposition~\ref{prop:r17-a1-work}.  The response and fluctuation assertions
are Theorems~\ref{thm:r17-a1-conormal},
\ref{thm:r17-a1-response}, and \ref{thm:r17-a1-fclt}.  Equality of the two
scalar coefficients follows only if their Radon--Nikodym exponents agree on
the nonconstant branch work; then the coefficients are equal.  No such
identification is made without that compatibility axiom.
\end{proof}
