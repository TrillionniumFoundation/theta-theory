## 1. Scope and standard of review

I have reviewed the active Round-Seventeen source rather than treating the repository's build, theorem/proof-count, source-identity, or hostile-verifier files as mathematical certification. The standard applied here is that expected at a leading general mathematics journal: every new functional-analytic object must be defined as a genuine space, every quotient and flow must be constructed rather than named, and every invariance principle must have verifiable hypotheses.

The revision makes several real improvements. In particular, Lemma `lem:r17-a1-primitive` corrects the primitive and removes the spurious `dp` term; the paper no longer identifies moving-boundary currents with ordinary Hölder cylinder observables; and the final statement correctly separates a mechanical work coordinate from a risk-sensitive coefficient unless a likelihood-cocycle compatibility condition is imposed. These are useful repairs. They do not, however, close the central geometric, Sobolev, response, or probabilistic arguments.

## 2. Decisive mathematical objections

### 2.1. [FATAL] The global hybrid quotient is not shown to be a Hausdorff symplectic manifold

The proof of Theorem `thm:r17-a1-global` says that source and target faces are compact, each gluing graph is closed, and therefore a finite quotient by those face identifications is Hausdorff and second countable. This implication is not valid in the stated generality. What must be shown is that the **equivalence relation generated by all identifications** is closed and proper, and that every equivalence class has a neighborhood admitting a compatible manifold-with-corners chart. Closedness of the individual graphs does not establish closedness of their transitive closure, especially at multiple-face and corner classes.

The proof also does not establish any of the following:

1. that the symplectic forms on the channel pieces descend to one nondegenerate closed form on the quotient;
2. that the gluing maps satisfy the cocycle and corner-compatibility conditions needed for a smooth structure;
3. that the piecewise channel Hamiltonians fit to a single smooth autonomous Hamiltonian across the gluing collars;
4. that the energy/clock coordinates match under every reset and that the resulting Hamiltonian vector field is complete;
5. that the asserted section and first-return relation are well defined at all regular equivalence classes and have no additional intersections.

Calling the object an “exact symplectic hybrid manifold” does not supply these missing definitions and compatibility lemmas. Exactness of each branch graph is not, by itself, a gluing theorem. Theorem `thm:r17-a1-global`, and therefore the claimed autonomous realization and Proposition `prop:r17-a1-work`, remain unproved.

### 2.2. [FATAL] The Sobolev trace argument in Theorem `thm:r17-a1-conormal` is reversed

The proof states that the trace theorem, followed by duality, yields the asserted face restriction and clean-intersection maps on negative Sobolev spaces. The standard trace theorem gives

\[
H^s(\Omega)\longrightarrow H^{s-1/2}(\partial\Omega),\qquad s>1/2.
\]

Duality gives an **extension of boundary distributions into ambient negative Sobolev spaces**. It does not define the restriction of an arbitrary element of `H^{-m}(\Omega)` to a face. For a general distribution, restriction to a submanifold is controlled by a wave-front/transversality condition; merely making the Sobolev order more negative makes the element more singular, not more traceable.

This is not a technical omission. Item (i) of `thm:r17-a1-conormal` is the foundation for the proposed face, corner, and higher-flag calculus. Once arbitrary negative-Sobolev currents cannot be restricted in the claimed way, the iterated trace maps, material derivatives, flag chain rule, and Taylor remainder are not well typed. The phrase “no product of two unrelated distributions is formed” does not cure the absence of a restriction operator.

A viable repair would require one of the following: a conormal/Hörmander wave-front class with explicit transversality under every branch map; a bespoke space built as compatible tuples of bulk and independently specified stratum distributions with proved incidence maps; or a positive-regularity test-space formulation in which all traces are taken before dualization. None is provided here.

### 2.3. [FATAL] The proposed Hilbert scale is not actually defined as a Hilbert space of labelled currents

The displayed definition of `\mathbb J_q^m` contains a depth index, codimension and normal-order indices, but no explicit direct sum over the strata `F` themselves, no fixed normal-bundle realization, and no compatibility rule between the same geometric current appearing after different dynamical refinements. The assertion that a family is “first summed as an ambient distribution and only then measured” creates an additional problem: distinct labelled face contributions may cancel as ambient distributions. The resulting quantity can therefore fail to distinguish the labelled states which the hybrid construction was introduced to retain.

The weight `2^{-n}(1+n)^{-2q-4}` is then invoked to “absorb” a depth shift without a calculation of the operator norm of that shift, the growth in the number of depth-`n` strata, or the multiplicity with which one face is pulled back. A scalar geometric weight cannot by assertion absorb uncounted branch complexity. Completeness, separability, independence of the chosen embeddings, and boundedness of the branch push-forwards all require proofs.

### 2.4. [MAJOR, effectively fatal for the response theorem] Parameter regularity is assumed after the fact

At the start, the seams `s_i(a)` are only ordered and have positive widths. In the proof of `thm:r17-a1-conormal`, however, “every branch map and every reference embedding is affine-rational in the parameters,” and factorial derivative bounds are inferred. That is a new analytic hypothesis not contained in the construction. At birth parameters, one-sided embeddings are mentioned, but no stratified parameter atlas, compatibility at births, or uniform analytic radius is specified.

The response theorem requires these estimates at arbitrarily high order. They cannot follow from the stated monotonicity and compactness assumptions. The admissible parameter family must be written explicitly and its regularity propagated through every preimage stratum.

### 2.5. [FATAL] The closability proof does not prove closability

For a fixed parameter, closability of an unbounded operator `\mathfrak D_a` requires a fixed dense domain and the implication

\[
J_n\to0,\quad \mathfrak D_aJ_n\to K \quad\Longrightarrow\quad K=0.
\]

The submitted proof instead says to pair with a test form and integrate “the scalar derivative over a parameter interval.” A sequence converging in the graph spaces at one value of `a` need not be a sequence of parameter-dependent families on a common interval, much less one with uniformly integrable derivatives. The argument therefore uses structure not present in the definition of the operator. The domain, graph norm, and identification of fibres at different parameters must be supplied before this step can be made.

### 2.6. [FATAL] The all-order response formula is schematic rather than a theorem

The operators `S_a^{(r)}`, `C_a^{(r)}`, `D_a^{(r)}`, the functionals `\Lambda_a`, the projections `Q_a^\pm`, the word set `\mathfrak W_q`, and their exact source and target spaces are not constructed in the manuscript. The proof consists of saying that finite-cylinder differentiation produces the listed insertions, that Poisson equations insert resolvents, and that induction gives Bell coefficients. This does not establish:

- convergence from finite cylinders to the physical current;
- interchange of parameter derivatives with the infinite-past/future limits;
- uniform boundedness of every mixed word on the conormal scale;
- cancellation of score, transport, and flag terms for a fixed physical observable;
- the claimed refinement invariance.

Because the conormal calculus itself is unavailable, the operators in `thm:r17-a1-response` are not even known to be composable.

### 2.7. [FATAL] The Hilbert-valued FCLT rests on an asserted, not proved, projective criterion

Immediately before Theorem `thm:r17-a1-fclt`, the decisive projective summability estimate is simply displayed and attributed to “geometric depth weights and Bernoulli cylinder weights.” No conditional expectation is computed, no filtration is defined precisely, and no estimate connects seam-preimage complexity to the `\mathbb J_q^m` norm.

Even if one had a Gordin decomposition, the proof still skips the steps needed for the stated conclusion: absolute trace-norm convergence of the two-sided covariance series, verification of the Hilbert-space martingale invariance-principle hypotheses, and uniform negligibility of the coboundary in the `D([0,1],\mathbb J_q^m)` topology. Square integrability alone does not imply trace-norm summability of the full correlation series. The assertion that Burkholder's inequality gives tightness is not a substitute for the relevant increment/bracket estimates.

## 3. Consequences for the main claim

The corrected primitive and the conceptual separation of mechanical and valuation parameters survive this review as useful observations. The autonomous global realization, closed conormal calculus, all-order response, and current-valued FCLT do not. Since those four components are precisely the content advertised in the abstract and Theorem `thm:r17-a1-main`, the main result is not established.

A1 is listed as independent in the repository dependency graph, so this rejection does not by itself invalidate another paper's formal premise. It does invalidate every cross-paper statement that treats A1 as an available response/realization platform.

## 4. Minimum requirements for any future reconsideration

A new submission would need, at minimum:

1. a complete gluing theorem with a closed proper equivalence relation, local corner charts, descent of the exact symplectic form, and an explicitly global smooth Hamiltonian;
2. a genuinely defined conormal or wave-front-controlled current space, with proved restriction, push-forward, incidence, and refinement maps;
3. a fixed-fibre parameter construction and a correct closability/domain theorem;
4. a fully written response algebra with exact operator domains and convergence from finite cylinders;
5. a standalone proof of the Hilbert-valued projective criterion, covariance trace-norm summability, and path-space tightness.

These are not revisions of exposition. They constitute most of the mathematical work claimed by the paper.

## 5. Recommendation

**Reject.** The present six-page proof module contains several attractive ideas and one important correction, but the central geometric quotient, distributional calculus, response theorem, and functional CLT are unsupported. The gap is substantially larger than a normal major revision and requires a fundamentally reconstructed manuscript.
