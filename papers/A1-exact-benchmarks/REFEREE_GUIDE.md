# Referee guide

## Manuscript

**Deterministic Path Ensembles, Driven Collision Maps, and Endogenous Conjugate Parameters**

## Review boundary

Please distinguish:

- deterministic-map statements proved in the manuscript;
- finite-dimensional formulas checked by the repository verifier;
- imported standard limit/PDE results;
- stronger periodic-Sinai or general moving-scatterer scopes that are not claimed.

## Priority checks

1. theorem scope and platform typing;
2. no hidden insertion of a Markov law, Brownian noise, or free theta parameter;
3. microcanonical/canonical ensemble interface;
4. physical-clock normalization;
5. fixed-law versus controlled tangent typing.

## Round-two positive-closure checks

- verify the new addendum in the compiled PDF;
- check every new theorem against both report files;
- check cross-paper dependencies against `REVISION_MANIFEST.yaml`;
- do not treat successful compilation as mathematical certification.
