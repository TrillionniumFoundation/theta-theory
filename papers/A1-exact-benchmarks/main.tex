\begin{abstract}
We construct the exact labelled Hamiltonian suspension and a fixed
incidence-dual current fibre, then distinguish that fibre from measurable
current-valued observables on the symbolic natural extension.  Exponential
cylinder approximation is imposed by an explicit response-domain norm rather
than inferred from Hilbert membership.  Finite-cylinder differentiation
converges in the Bochner space, and a verified projective decomposition gives
a current-valued functional central limit theorem whose covariance is trace
class by an exact Parseval identity.
\end{abstract}
