\begin{abstract}
We construct a target-refined inverse-limit symplectic section for the generalized baker family, an exact autonomous Hamiltonian suspension on its smooth symplectic leaves, a global affine work connection, and a graded flag-current response calculus. The symbolic natural extension is the coupled map $(x,y)\mapsto(\sigma x,x_0y)$; no bilateral Koopman gap or independent past inverse is used.
\end{abstract}
