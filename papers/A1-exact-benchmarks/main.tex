\begin{abstract}
We construct a single finite-dimensional autonomous hybrid Hamiltonian
realization of the driven baker benchmark and separate it from its symbolic
natural extension.  Parameter derivatives are defined on a fixed
Sobolev--conormal Hilbert scale carrying bulk, face, corner, and higher flag
currents.  A closed all-order chain rule yields explicit response words with
uniform remainder bounds.  A Hilbert-valued Gordin decomposition then gives a
Radon current-valued functional central limit theorem with trace-class
covariance.  Mechanical work and risk calibration remain distinct typed
claims.
\end{abstract}
