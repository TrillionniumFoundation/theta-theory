## 2. Unresolved fatal defects

### 2.1. The global quotient is still not constructed

The proof of `thm:r17-a1-global` still infers Hausdorffness and second countability from compact source and target faces and closed individual gluing graphs. That does not prove that the equivalence relation generated by all face identifications is closed and proper, nor does it produce compatible manifold-with-corners charts at multiple-face classes. The manuscript still gives no cocycle theorem for the gluings, no proof that the local symplectic forms descend to a single nondegenerate closed form, and no construction showing that the piecewise channel Hamiltonians combine into one smooth autonomous Hamiltonian across every collar. Exactness of separate branch graphs is not a global symplectic gluing theorem.

### 2.2. The negative-Sobolev trace argument remains backwards

The proof of `thm:r17-a1-conormal` still invokes the positive-regularity trace map and says that duality supplies restrictions of arbitrary negative-Sobolev currents to faces. Duality supplies an adjoint extension of boundary distributions into an ambient negative space; it does not define restriction of a general ambient distribution to a submanifold. Such restriction requires wave-front transversality or an independently specified compatible stratum component. Making the Sobolev exponent more negative increases singularity and does not repair traceability. Hence the face, corner, flag, and iterated-material-derivative operators remain ill typed.

### 2.3. The proposed current scale and material derivative are still undefined

The displayed `\mathbb J_q^m` does not explicitly sum over individual strata, does not fix the normal-bundle realizations, and measures labelled contributions only after ambient summation, allowing distinct labels to cancel. No proof of completeness, embedding independence, refinement invariance, or bounded depth shift is supplied. The closability argument still integrates a parameter derivative over an interval although closability at a fixed parameter requires a fixed fibre, a fixed dense domain, and the graph-limit implication at that parameter. The needed cross-fibre family is absent from the operator definition.

### 2.4. Response and FCLT remain assertions

The response words, score/coupling/jet operators, projections, functionals, domains, and convergence from finite cylinders are not constructed. The decisive projective summability estimate for the Hilbert-valued Gordin decomposition is still merely displayed. No calculation establishes trace-norm convergence of the covariance series, path-space tightness, or uniform negligibility of the coboundary. Square integrability and a reference to Burkholder do not prove the stated Radon Hilbert-valued functional CLT.

## 3. Editorial consequence

A top-journal resubmission must contain an actual new source proving the quotient/gluing theorem, a legitimate conormal or wave-front-controlled current space, a fixed-fibre derivative theorem, and complete response and invariance-principle estimates. None has been submitted on the declared branch.

## 4. Recommendation

**Reject without reconsideration.** The manuscript is unchanged, so the previous rejection remains controlling in full.
