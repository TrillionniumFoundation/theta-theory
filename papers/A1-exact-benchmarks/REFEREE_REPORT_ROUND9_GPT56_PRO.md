## Executive assessment

Round nine correctly separates the ordinary two-dimensional baker section from its symbolic natural extension, abandons the Cantor graph as a purported symplectic section, restores exact full-port work, and states the mechanical/valuation identification only conditionally. Those are genuine corrections.

The new response theory nevertheless fails at its first functional-analytic lemma. There is an elementary cylinder-function counterexample to the announced directional Lasota–Yorke estimate. Consequently the claimed spectral gap, reduced resolvent, all-depth current response, and Kato-word formulas have no valid foundation. The “autonomous Hamiltonian impact realization” also remains a hybrid reset rule rather than a Hamiltonian flow generated by one autonomous Hamiltonian on the displayed phase space.

## Decisive mathematical objections

### 1. The directional Lasota–Yorke estimate is directly false

Let
\[
 f(\omega)=1_{\{\omega_0=i\}}-p_i(a).
\]
It is centered and has only the martingale block \(\Delta_{0,0}f=f\). With the paper's operator
\[
 \mathcal L_af=f\circ\sigma^{-1},
\]
one has
\[
 \mathcal L_a^nf(\omega)=1_{\{\omega_{-n}=i\}}-p_i(a).
\]
This has a nonzero block at past depth \((r,s)=(n,0)\). Hence, up to the fixed \(L^1\)-norm of \(f\),
\[
 \|\mathcal L_a^nf\|_+\ge \alpha^{-n}\|f\|_1.
\]
Because \(0<\alpha<1\), this grows exponentially.

The proposed right-hand side is
\[
 C(\beta/\alpha)^n\|f\|_+ + C\|f\|_-,
\]
which is bounded uniformly in \(n\). This is a contradiction. The sentence that blocks with \(s<n\) are “bounded by the weak norm” ignores the subsequent growth of their past depth in the *strong* norm. The translated-cylinder counterexample is not absorbed; it is precisely the counterexample above.

Thus Lemma `r9-a1-ly` is false.

### 2. The spectral theorem and response calculus collapse with Lemma `r9-a1-ly`

The proof of quasicompactness, peripheral simplicity, and boundedness of
\[
 (I-\mathcal L_a)^{-1}(I-\Pi_a)
\]
uses the false estimate. The inverse shift is an invertible Koopman-type operator, and an anisotropic space can produce resonances only after the stable and unstable regularities are assigned with a dynamically compatible sign and the operator is typed correctly. The present weights do the opposite for a block that has crossed from future to past.

Every later response formula uses this nonexistent reduced resolvent. Therefore the physical-response theorem is not a local gap that can be repaired independently.

### 3. The displayed impact system is not an autonomous Hamiltonian flow on \(\mathcal M_a\)

Between impacts the Hamiltonian is \(H=E\). At \(\tau=1\) the manuscript applies an additional branch-dependent canonical reset generated by \(S_i^a\). A symplectic reset map is not the time evolution of the displayed autonomous Hamiltonian. It defines a hybrid Hamiltonian-impact system only after one supplies a separate impact relation and proves its global well-posedness.

The paper then says that a “smooth regularization” follows by inserting thin canonical layers, but no global Hamiltonian is constructed. In particular, no argument shows simultaneous exact realization of all strip-to-strip maps, compatibility near moving seams, or equality—not merely convergence—of the regularized first-return map. The theorem must either be stated as a hybrid symplectic suspension or replaced by an actual smooth autonomous construction.

### 4. The all-depth physical-current space is not defined well enough to support operator products

The expression
\[
 D_a\mathcal L_a=(D_a\mathcal L_a)_{\rm bulk}
 +\sum_w c_w[\partial w]
\]
does not specify the source and target Banach spaces or the action of a line current on an input distribution. At higher order the proof invokes “products of compatible one-dimensional currents.” Products of distributions supported on intersecting cuts are not automatically defined, and compatibility under refinement does not supply a multiplication theorem.

There is also no proof that the current norm is compatible with the directional martingale norm or that a current insertion followed by the claimed resolvent is bounded. Merely obtaining exponential cylinder weights does not type the operator calculus.

### 5. The physical and symbolic invariant measures are mixed in the response argument

Lebesgue measure on the physical baker section is the same measure for all parameters. For an ordinary physical observable \(G_a\),
\[
 \frac d{da}\int G_a\,dm
\]
is governed by the material dependence of \(G_a\), not by a varying invariant measure. The symbolic Bernoulli measure varies because the coding itself varies. A response theorem must specify exactly which observable is fixed, which pullback changes, and how the moving coding is compared. The present proof moves between these viewpoints without a commutative, bounded identification.

### 6. Correct scoping leaves a benchmark, not a top-four theorem

The measure-theoretic Bernoulli coding, explicit exponential tilt, exact port work, and conditional CARA calibration are useful benchmark facts. Once the invalid physical response and smooth-Hamiltonian claims are removed, the content is far below the novelty and depth threshold of the four journals.

## Dependency and editorial consequences

A1 is independent of the Sinai and hard-sphere roots, but it should not be cited elsewhere as a completed physical-response theorem. It can support symbolic regression tests only after the distinction between the physical map, symbolic coding, and hybrid impact realization is kept explicit.

## Required reconstruction

A credible paper would need to:

1. replace the directional norms by a genuinely hyperbolic anisotropic scale and prove a correct operator estimate;
2. define the current insertions as bounded maps on that scale, including intersections;
3. choose between a hybrid impact theorem and a constructed smooth autonomous suspension;
4. state response for a precisely transported class of physical observables; and
5. substantially narrow the editorial claims.

## Recommendation

**Reject.** The first spectral estimate has an explicit counterexample, and all claimed physical response follows from that estimate. The repaired symbolic/work benchmark is not independently of top-four-journal scale.
