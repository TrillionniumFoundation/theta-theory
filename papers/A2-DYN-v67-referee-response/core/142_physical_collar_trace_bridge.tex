\section{The physical collar trace and its dynamical bound}
\label{sec:v66-collar-trace}
A full reversible determinant and a physically observable caustic
coefficient are different at a seam. The former has weight $J_z$;
the latter also contains the physical angular fraction and the exact
label decision. We construct a positive collar trace on the original
source, prove its ordered dynamical bound, and state explicitly what
is still needed to pass from this trace to zero-width coefficients.

Fix $\chi,\varepsilon$ as in the preceding section. Let $\mathcal Z$
be any subfamily of its critical charts, each counted once. Exact
labels below are imposed on the source points inside a chart; they
need not be constant on the chart or defined by a boundary convention
at its center. For $0<s<h_\chi$ put
\begin{align}\label{eq:v66-collar-trace-definition}
 M_{z,n,k}(s)&=\int_0^s
               b_{z,n,k}^{\varepsilon,\mathrm{clr}}(t_z+u)\,du,
 \notag\\
 \mathcal Q^{\chi,\varepsilon}_{m,s;n,k,R}
                &=\sum_{z\in\mathcal Z}s^{-1}M_{z,n,k}(s)\delta_{t_z}.
\end{align}
This is a positive, unnormalized trace obtained from a restriction of
the original Lorentz source. Its atom is placed at the chart's critical
value only as bookkeeping; $M_{z,n,k}(s)$ integrates the actual roof
$L_{m,R}$ and the unchanged return event.

\begin{theorem}[An ordered bound for the physical collar trace]
\label{thm:v66-ordered-collar-trace}
For every fixed $0<s<h_\chi$ and $h>0$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v66-ordered-collar-trace}
 \limsup_{m\to\infty}\sup_{R,n,k,t}m^2
 \mathcal Q^{\chi,\varepsilon}_{m,s;n,k,R}([t-h,t+h])
                       \le C(2h+s).
\end{equation}
The constant is independent of $\chi,\varepsilon,h,s$ in these ranges.
In particular
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v66-diagonal-collar-order}
 \lim_{s\downarrow0}\limsup_{m\to\infty}
 \sup_{R,n,k,t}m^2
 \mathcal Q^{\chi,\varepsilon}_{m,s;n,k,R}([t-s,t+s])=0.
\end{equation}
Both statements permit clearance seams, exact-label changes and
coincident critical values. They concern the stated finite-width trace,
not the full reversible atomic measure of
\eqref{eq:v65-reversible-atomic-measure}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
On a chart with $t_z\in[t-h,t+h]$, the part $0<F_z-t_z<s$ has
actual roof in $[t-h,t+h+s]$ and both endpoint momenta bounded by
$C_0\sqrt{s}$, by Theorem~\ref{thm:v66-uniform-morse}. The physical
chart pieces are disjoint. Their clearance multipliers are at most one,
and their initial and terminal section restrictions are exact. Hence
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v66-positive-trace-comparison}
 s\,\mathcal Q^{\chi,\varepsilon}_{m,s;n,k,R}([t-h,t+h])
 \le\nu_R^*\bigl(E^{(C_0\sqrt s)}_{n,k,m,R}
                                  (t;[-h,h+s])\bigr).
\end{equation}
Choose the fixed $r_0$ in \eqref{eq:v66-morse-budget} small enough
that $C_0\sqrt{h_\chi}<1/4$. For fixed $s,h$, the inherited
Lemma~\ref{lem:v45-two-strip-local} bounds the limsup of the right
side times $m^2$ by $C s(2h+s)$. Division by $s$ proves the first
assertion. Setting $h=s$ and then taking $s\downarrow0$ proves the
second. No spectral estimate is evaluated at a width depending on $m$.
The label supremum is outside a positive source comparison; it does
not average either integer label. No word-count factor or separation
of critical values occurs.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The zero-width observable coefficient}
Suppose now that $\mathcal Z$ consists of clearance seams and take
$s<r_{\chi,\varepsilon}^2/2$. At each fixed finite word the active
physical and section scalar margins have nonzero individual gradients
in the regular selected chart, as in
Lemma~\ref{lem:v65-margin-description}. No uniform gradient lower
bound is imposed. Let $\theta_{z,n,k}\in[0,1]$ be the angular fraction
assigned to the original label by the signs of their linear parts;
nonzero margins retain their constant signs. Then
$\sum_{n,k}\theta_{z,n,k}\le1$. Put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v66-observable-atomic-trace}
 \beta_{z,n,k}=J_z\theta_{z,n,k},\qquad
 \mathcal B^{\chi,\varepsilon}_{m;n,k,R}
                     =\sum_{z\in\mathcal Z}\beta_{z,n,k}\delta_{t_z}.
\end{equation}
A full physical circle has fraction one, a single strict seam one half,
and a compatible quadrant one quarter. The label is always the
original half-word label, never an index read from the doubled orbit.

\begin{proposition}[Exact fixed-count source realization]
\label{prop:v66-fixed-count-trace}
For each fixed $m,R,z,n,k$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v66-fixed-count-trace}
 \beta_{z,n,k}
   =\lim_{u\downarrow0}b_{z,n,k}^{\varepsilon,\mathrm{clr}}(t_z+u)
   =\lim_{s\downarrow0}s^{-1}M_{z,n,k}(s).
\end{equation}
These limits use the coarea representative. Thus $\mathcal Q_{m,s}$
converges to $\mathcal B_m$ against every bounded critical-value test
at this fixed finite count. In general neither trace equals the
unrestricted sum of full coefficients $J_z$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
On the saturated physical disk $D_z^\varepsilon=1$. For a fixed
finite list of active scalar margins, Taylor expansion shows that
their signs converge on every angular direction except the finite
set where a first derivative vanishes. Initial, occupation and
terminal decisions are evaluated separately by the same visit rule.
Dominated convergence therefore gives
$A_{z,n,k}^{\varepsilon}(u)/(2\pi)\to\theta_{z,n,k}$.
Equation~\eqref{eq:v66-multiplicative-profile} gives the first limit;
its Cesaro average gives the second. There are finitely many selected
critical words at fixed count, so summation commutes with this fixed-count
limit. No assertion about an interchange with $m\to\infty$ is used.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A quantified separation of the two limits}
Write $a_{z,n,k}(u)=A_{z,n,k}^{\varepsilon}(u)/(2\pi)$ and define
for an interval $I$ the nonnegative angular loss
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v66-angular-loss}
 \mathcal D_{m,s;n,k,R}(I)
 =\sum_{z:t_z\in I}\frac{J_z}{s}
           \int_0^s(\theta_{z,n,k}-a_{z,n,k}(u))_+\,du.
\end{equation}
It measures loss of the actual physical and exact-label angular set,
not an error in the normalizing density or a discarded source mass.

\begin{proposition}[A positive atomic-to-collar comparison]
\label{prop:v66-atomic-collar-comparison}
For every interval $I$ and $0<s<r_{\chi,\varepsilon}^2/2$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v66-atomic-collar-comparison}
 \mathcal B_{m;n,k,R}(I)
 \le e^{K_\chi\sqrt{2s}}\mathcal Q_{m,s;n,k,R}(I)
                           +\mathcal D_{m,s;n,k,R}(I).
\end{equation}
Consequently, if the further physical estimate
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v66-angular-loss-input}
 \lim_{s\downarrow0}\limsup_{m\to\infty}\sup_{R,n,k,t}
                 m^2\mathcal D_{m,s;n,k,R}([t-1,t+1])=0
\end{equation}
holds, then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v66-conditional-observable-concentration}
 \lim_{h\downarrow0}\limsup_{m\to\infty}\sup_{R,n,k,t}
                m^2\mathcal B_{m;n,k,R}([t-h,t+h])=0.
\end{equation}
The additional estimate \eqref{eq:v66-angular-loss-input} is not
proved here and is not implied by the count-uniform selected-contact
jets. The implication concerns the physically weighted trace
$\mathcal B$, not the larger full reversible trace.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For each $z$, average the elementary inequality
$\theta\le a(u)+(\theta-a(u))_+$ over $0<u<s$ and multiply by
$J_z$. The lower bound in
\eqref{eq:v66-multiplicative-profile} bounds $J_za(u)$ by
$e^{K_\chi\sqrt{2s}}b_{z,n,k}^{\varepsilon,\mathrm{clr}}(t_z+u)$.
Sum to prove \eqref{eq:v66-atomic-collar-comparison}. For fixed
$s,h<1$, Theorem~\ref{thm:v66-ordered-collar-trace} then bounds
the normalized limsup by
\[
 C e^{K_\chi\sqrt{2s}}(2h+s)
 +\limsup_m\sup_{R,n,k,t}m^2\mathcal D_{m,s;n,k,R}([t-1,t+1]).
\]
First let $h\downarrow0$ and then $s\downarrow0$, with
$\chi,\varepsilon$ fixed throughout. This proves the stated implication.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{The unchanged pointwise endpoint.}
The new uniform charts and finite-width trace estimate do not bound
the complete first-incidence source or the positive outside source in
\eqref{eq:v66-complete-positive-partition}. They also do not prove
\eqref{eq:v66-angular-loss-input}, full reversible-weight concentration,
or a uniform pointwise bound for the nonlinear angular profiles.
The complete ordered incidence and clearance heights remain the inputs
of the original positive raw-error identity. The unrestricted two-sided
arithmetic raw-density law, its same-roof conditional consequences and
forward essential likelihood are not inferred from a fixed-count limit
or a finite-width trace. The finite arithmetic factor and its zero
classes remain unchanged.
