\subsection*{The new physical source-height mechanism}
The present revision advances the positive-source problem in its
original pointwise roof topology. The earlier exponential
total-variation estimate alone could not do this: a small
neighborhood of a critical point can contain an entire small roof
ellipse. We now compare each roof coefficient with a complete
two-dimensional family of actual nearby trajectories and pay the
overlap of all such families before taking a roof supremum.

For a fixed $0<\varepsilon\le1/4$, let
$\mathcal G_{\varepsilon,m,R}$ consist of the actual regular records
with the original margins
$c_i\ge\varepsilon q_0^{d_i/4}$ and
$\Delta_i\ge\varepsilon q_0^{d'_i/4}$, and endpoint momenta
$|p_0|,|p_m|\le5/6$. The last cap contains the complete original
section; no distance to its boundary is imposed.
Theorem~\ref{thm:at-height} proves, for every Borel original-source
selector $0\le h\le1$,
\[
 \left\|\frac{d(L_{m,R})_*
    (\one_E\one_{\mathcal G_\varepsilon}\one_\Pi h\,d\nu_R^*)}{dt}
                                                    \right\|_\infty
 \le C_{\rm at}\,\nu(\operatorname{Sat}_\varepsilon(E)),
 \qquad C_{\rm at}=\frac{25U C_{\rm dist}^2}
                   {2c_*(\rho_\varepsilon^{\rm at})^2}.
\]
Here the saturation is a proved union of physical momentum tubes,
used only on the upper side, and
$\rho_\varepsilon^{\rm at}=g\delta_*\varepsilon/(128R_+)$.
It is not a new probability or microscopic roof window.
Global uniqueness at fixed endpoint momenta makes every tube
unambiguous for its physical word. A point's momentum can be
within two mesh spacings of at most five grid values in each
coordinate, giving the exact uniform multiplicity bound $25$.
The native physical continuation theorem verifies first hits and
every third obstacle on each whole tube before any optical weight
is compared. The argument covers the complete guarded source,
including its critical and noncritical parts, without the
whole-cylinder pressure input or a momentum-kernel estimate.

Theorem~\ref{thm:nh-bad-height} applies this mechanism to the
already defined $\mathcal E^{\rm obs}_{D,R,m}$.
Physical endpoint localization shows that if a tube meets this
source, its whole volume lies in an explicitly enlarged original
observation neighborhood. Invariance, exact clearance flux, the
incidence marginal and the exact section collar then pay its
volume without independence assumptions. For
$D_m=\lceil\theta m\rceil$, $0<\theta<1/2$, this proves
\[
 \sup_{R,n,k,h}m^2
 \left\|\frac{d(L_{m,R})_*
  (\one_{\mathcal G_\varepsilon}
   \one_{\mathcal E^{\rm obs}_{D_m,R,m}}\one_\Pi h\,d\nu_R^*)}{dt}
                                                     \right\|_\infty
 \longrightarrow0
\]
at every fixed positive margin. The complete positive word sum
precedes all suprema. The actual $I_m$ multiplier is paid by an
explicit $O(\varepsilon^{-1})$ factor.
Theorem~\ref{thm:nh-records} also removes the pressure premise
from the normalized central clock, phase and transverse cost
estimates on this same class, while retaining all marks and the
exact endpoint phase.

The inverse-gradient factor in the physical variation budget
requires a separate argument. Lemma~\ref{lem:nh-singular-volume}
splits its source at a fixed endpoint-momentum radius, constructs
disjoint true critical tubes for the inner part, and integrates
the exact radial coarea singularity. For a roof set of length
$\ell$, the bound is an explicit constant times
$\nu(E^+)(\ell+\sqrt\ell)$. This pays the actual
$I_m/|\nabla_qF|$ volume of the deep exceptional source after
collision normalization, on fixed roof windows and over its
entire physical roof support. It does not claim a bounded
inverse-gradient density at a critical roof or erase a separate
paired endpoint trace.

 30000^{-1}c_mj_m\le\omega\le30000c_mj_m,
