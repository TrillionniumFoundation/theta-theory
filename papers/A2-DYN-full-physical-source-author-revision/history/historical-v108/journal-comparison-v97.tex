\subsection{The retained physical comparison and the old failure source}
\begin{inputstatement}[Physical comparison at a fixed exact label]
\label{input:v97-comparison}
Before imposing section decisions, the regular physical collar
$|p_0|,|p_m|<19/20$, $z>0$, carries the partial comparison flow
\[
 \widehat X=K^{-1}g/(g^{\mathsf T}K^{-1}g),\qquad
 \widehat XF=1,\quad \widehat X\vartheta=0.
\]
It stops at true physical, collar and backward critical ends, but
crosses section-decision walls. On that collar
$\lambda_*I\le K\le\Lambda_\circ I$, where
$\lambda_*=9\sqrt{39}/400$. The complete physical segment density is
$\widehat A=\omega/(4\pi c_*\chi)$, and its restriction to $\Pi$
is the original density $A$. The signed-time survivor graph is Borel,
and each fixed-time flow is injective with common coarea kernels.
Along an available interval of roof length at most $r$,
\[
 |W^\varepsilon(\widehat\Phi_sx)-W^\varepsilon(x)|
 \le\kappa_\varepsilon(r)=\min\{1,C_\Theta\varepsilon^{-1}\sqrt r\}.
\]
Squared radial speed lies between $2\lambda_*$ and $2\Lambda_\circ$.
No spectral estimate outside the original exact event is assumed.
\end{inputstatement}
This is the retained comparison-flow lemma, labeled
\texttt{lem:v96-flow} in module 229; its proof uses the general
endpoint Hessian, the contact inverse, the cubic action estimate and
the Borel transversal from modules 174, 177, 180 and 196.
The original stopped lifetimes $\tau^\pm$ and total residence
$\ell=\tau^-+\tau^+$ are not the comparison lifetimes
$\widehat\tau^\pm$.

For $H\subset\Pi$, set
$R_H^\varepsilon(t)=\int_{F=t}\one_HW^\varepsilon\,d\mu_t$
and $U_H(t)=\int_{F=t}\one_H\,d\mu_t$. An old comparison direction
$e\in\{-1,1\}$ is available if it survives for all $0\le s\le r$.
On an available direction put
\[
 d_{e,r}(x)=\sup_{0\le s\le r}
 \frac{\widehat A(x)}{\widehat A(\widehat\Phi_{es}x)},\qquad
 o_{H,e,r}(x)=r^{-1}\int_0^r\one_H(\widehat\Phi_{es}x)\,ds.
\]
Old admission means that some direction has $d_{e,r}\le D$ and
$o_{H,e,r}\ge\beta$. For $H=\Pi$, $N_T$ means neither direction
is available; $N_D$ means availability but no available direction
has $d\le D$; $N_O$ means a bounded-ratio direction exists but none
of them has $o\ge\beta$. Their disjoint sum is $N$, and
\[
 Z_{r,D,\beta}^\varepsilon(t)=\int_{F=t}\one_NW^\varepsilon\,d\mu_t.
\]
The new proofs estimate a portion of this very source, not a source
obtained by changing its label or its stopping convention.
