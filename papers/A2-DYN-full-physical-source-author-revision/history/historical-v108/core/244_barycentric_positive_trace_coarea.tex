\section{A transverse coordinate for the positive paired source}
\label{sec:v102-pair-coarea}
Fix the collision count, radius and exact label. A maximal segment of
that original event has roof interval $(a(\theta),b(\theta))$, length
$\ell=b-a$, and physical density $A(u,\theta)$. Its paired weight is
\[
 c^\varepsilon(\theta)=\min\{f^\varepsilon(a+,\theta),
                                      f^\varepsilon(b-,\theta)\},
 \qquad f^\varepsilon=AW^\varepsilon.
\]
The minimum is taken on the same physical segment, before summation.
A zero trace contributes no paired mass. In particular genuinely
grazing ends contribute zero; a backward critical trace is retained.

\begin{lemma}[Regular pairing chambers]\label{lem:v102-chambers}
At each fixed count and radius, the original paired measure admits
angular chambers on which $a,b$ are $C^2$ and $c^\varepsilon$ is
locally Lipschitz. These chambers represent all paired mass except
for transverse-null strata. Their interiors are counted once.
There is no assertion of a collision-uniform bound on their number
or on their derivatives. At a backward critical entrance, $a$ may be
constant while $c^\varepsilon$ is nonzero.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use the finite physical graph and its exact section decisions, not a
partition of symbolic extensions. On each lifted itinerary the map
from endpoint angles to $g$ has positive Jacobian and is globally
injective by the retained momentum uniqueness theorem. On finitely
many polar charts with $z>0$, its image is a definable set. Along
one ray its connected components are the original maximal segments:
$\partial_zF=z e^{\mathsf T}K^{-1}e>0$. Definable cell decomposition
and one-variable piecewise differentiability therefore give finitely
many angular intervals on which the component endpoints, their
limiting contact data and their roof values are $C^2$, after further
subdivision if necessary \cite{Coste}. No estimate of the number of
pieces is used.

At a nongrazing endpoint the stationary word has a smooth local
extension; this is not physical continuation through a first-hit
boundary. The clearance is the minimum of finitely many smooth
functions locally. Thus the original smooth guard has locally
Lipschitz endpoint values. The two angular density factors have the
same property away from $g=0$. At a critical incoming end the
positive endpoint Hessian gives a local inverse in $g$ at zero.
The limiting angular factor is
$e_\perp^{\mathsf T}K(q_*)e_\perp$, bounded below by
$\lambda_\circ$, and the optical factor has its ordinary value at
$q_*$. The resulting trace is locally Lipschitz in the direction
$e$ and need not vanish. This also gives the assertion in that case.
Taking a minimum preserves local Lipschitz regularity.

The disjoint physical-segment parametrization assigns each component
once. The finitely many angular subdivision points have zero
transverse measure; their roof saturations have zero paired measure
by Tonelli. Original Borel seams are only assignments. Smoothly
compatible chamber charts can be continued across such seams.
Where pairing regularity actually ends, the transverse comparison
below is stopped, and its failure source is retained. No part of the
original event is removed by that stopping convention.
\end{proof}

On a chamber put $0<s<1$ and set
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v102-barycentric}
 \mathscr F(\theta,s)=a(\theta)+s\ell(\theta),\qquad
 v(\theta,s)=\partial_\theta\mathscr F
                       =(1-s)a'(\theta)+s b'(\theta).
\end{equation}
Thus $s$ is the relative position between the two original ends.
Define a positive measure on the chamber by
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v102-pair-measure}
 d\pi^\varepsilon=c^\varepsilon(\theta)\ell(\theta)
                                                    \,d\theta\,ds.
\end{equation}
It is a representation of the paired source, not a new collision
probability or a redefinition of the guard.

\begin{theorem}[Exact paired coarea and transverse Jacobian]
\label{thm:v102-pair-coarea}
For any original Borel target selector $H$, the roof pushforward of
$\one_H\pi^\varepsilon$ has density $T_H^\varepsilon$. Here $H$
is evaluated at $\Psi(\theta,\mathscr F(\theta,s))$.
On the open regular region $c^\varepsilon>0$, $v\ne0$, define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v102-transverse-field}
 V^\perp=v^{-1}\partial_\theta,\qquad
 \varrho^\varepsilon=\frac{c^\varepsilon\ell}{|v|}.
\end{equation}
The partial flow $\Theta_h^\perp$ obeys
$\mathscr F\circ\Theta_h^\perp=\mathscr F+h$ and preserves $s$.
Its Jacobian relative to \eqref{eq:v102-pair-measure} is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v102-transverse-jacobian}
 J_h^\perp(y)=
 \frac{\varrho^\varepsilon(\Theta_h^\perp y)}
      {\varrho^\varepsilon(y)}.
\end{equation}
Consequently its target-direction coarea covariance holds jointly
for almost every $(t,h)$ and all nonnegative Borel integrands, on
the same stopped survivor graph. Physical sheets are summed with
multiplicity one.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For any nonnegative test $\phi$ the change of variable
$u=a+s\ell$ gives
\[
 \sum\int\int_0^1\phi(\mathscr F)
 c^\varepsilon\ell\one_H\,ds\,d\theta
 =\sum\int\int_a^b\phi(u)c^\varepsilon\one_H(\Psi(\theta,u))
                                                       \,du\,d\theta.
\]
This is precisely the defining paired source, including the target
selector. It proves the first assertion also across $v=0$.

On $v\ne0$ use $(t,s)=(\mathscr F,s)$ as local coordinates.
The measure is $\varrho^\varepsilon(t,s)\,dt\,ds$ and the new
flow is translation in $t$. This proves
\eqref{eq:v102-transverse-jacobian}, including negative times.
Equivalently, at fixed $s$ the angular derivative of the flow is
$v(y)/v(\Theta_h^\perp y)$; multiplying by the ratio of the
weights $c^\varepsilon\ell$ gives the same positive Jacobian.
The absolute value on $v$ cannot be omitted.

Uniqueness for the $C^1$ field makes the stopped graph one-to-one.
Local changes of variables, a countable determining class and
Tonelli give joint coarea kernels and the stated covariance. The
same argument is valid for graph selectors depending on $(y,h)$.
Compatible coordinate seams do not create multiple copies of a
physical segment. The construction never moves a state to another
occupation or displacement class. It is a transverse comparison
between actual trajectories, not the original dynamical roof flow.
The set $v=0$ is assigned to comparison failure whenever it carries
paired mass; no null-set assertion about that entire set is needed.
\end{proof}
