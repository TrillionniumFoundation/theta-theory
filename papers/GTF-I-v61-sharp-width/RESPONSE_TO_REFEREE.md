## Principal mathematical response

The principal unresolved quantitative objection is answered by a different converse, not by suppressing the logarithm in notation. Theorem `thm:sharpoccupation61` proves a narrow-cut occupation bound of order `k^(2/p)`, under the same full spectral and all-direction cap hypotheses, even when intervening registers are unrestricted. The proof transports a **centroid-mass-weighted law of directions**, smooths it by compactly supported spherical kernels, obtains a fixed entropy defect from the full action spectral gap, and telescopes only over the selected narrow cuts. The additive logarithmic entropy range is absorbed into `k^(2/p)`; there is no multiplicative mixing-block logarithm.

Together with the existing inner-hull construction, the new converse gives a matching `Theta(N^(q/2))` law whenever least-orbit and target-orbit dimensions agree. In particular, the full legal density-matrix result is now `Theta(N^(d-1))`; the original rational-unitary example has `Theta(N)` width at every fixed positive subcritical error. Its exact and robust exponential profiles are unchanged. No numerical Bourgain–Gamburd constant is inferred for that alphabet.

A second explicit experiment, Theorem `thm:effectivelps61`, uses rational **Bloch rotations**, not rational unitary lifts. For the displayed six nonidentity rotations the classical LPS theorem gives the actual spherical operator norm `sqrt(5)/3`. Explicit kernel constants yield

```
(1-sqrt(2)*epsilon) N / 6500 <= W_(N,epsilon) <= 52 + 104 N/epsilon.
```

The exact cut profile is `5^t`, because the rational first-axis seed has a cyclic stabilizer in a free group of rank three. That profile persists for error at most `25^(-N)/16`. The common-row rational/dyadic upper machine is actually constructed by the generic compiler. The spectral theorem is a deep imported input, not a result of the finite checker.

Revision 59 had already supplied a distinct causal, whole-transcript classification for repeatable classical probes, an adaptive rational instrument, a generic CLI and final-head read-only verification. All those proofs and implementations are retained, not claimed as new v61 discoveries. Arbitrary irreversible systems, the unrestricted process-width exponent, exact leading constants and the complete joint error/horizon crossover are not asserted to have been classified.
