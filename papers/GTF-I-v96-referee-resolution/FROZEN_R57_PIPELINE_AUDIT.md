## 6. Detailed audit: explicit nonemptiness

Let `P_j=supp(E_j)`, `Q_j=I-P_j`, and suppose `Q_jH_jQ_j>=0`. Choose `a>0` with `||H_j||<=a` and a common `lambda>0` such that `E_j>=lambda P_j` for every nonzero support.

For

```text
c=1+2a^2/lambda,
s_* =min{1,lambda/(2a),1/a},
```

the support block of the numerator

```text
E_j+sH_j+c s^2 I
```

is at least `lambda P_j/2`. The cross block has norm at most `sa`. Its Schur correction is at most

```text
2 a^2 s^2/lambda.
```

The missing-support Schur complement is therefore at least

```text
s Q_jH_jQ_j + s^2 Q_j.
```

This is positive semidefinite. If `P_j=0`, then `E_j=0` and the cone condition is simply `H_j>=0`; positivity is direct.

The normalization denominator `1+kcs^2` is positive, and the numerator sum equals that scalar times the identity because `sum E_j=I` and `sum H_j=0`.

The exact remainder identity gives

```text
||F_j^c(s)-E_j-sH_j||
 <=c(1+2k)s^2
```

on the stated interval, and summation gives `Lambda_*=ck(1+2k)`.

No step assumes a constant support rank for positive `s`. The construction also treats zero effects without assigning them a fictitious positive eigenvalue.

The exact implementation chooses a rational norm majorant and obtains a rational support floor by exact PSD tests and repeated halving. The proof sketches a polynomial lower bound for nonzero rational eigenvalues; this argument is mathematical, while the finite `--max-halvings` option only enforces safe refusal.

Audit conclusion: **pass as a sufficient construction**.

## 7. Detailed audit: finite block upper

For directions in the cone lineality, v86 supplies canonical factors `A_j,B_j` and a normalized surrogate `G(s)` satisfying

```text
||W_s-W_0|| <=(3/2) beta_0 s,
||M_F-M_G(s)||_diamond <=r s^2,
r=Lambda+K.
```

### 7.1 Internal entanglement

For an arbitrary input on `n` probes and a reference, tensor telescoping of the two Stinespring isometries gives

```text
||W_s^(tensor n)-W_0^(tensor n)||
 <=(3/2) beta_0 n s.
```

Applying the isometries to a purification bounds the Bures distance of the corresponding output states by the same quantity. This remains valid for arbitrary entanglement within the block.

### 7.2 Actual tube member

Diamond telescoping gives

```text
||M_F^(tensor n)-M_G(s)^(tensor n)||_diamond
 <=n r s^2.
```

For normalized output states, the squared Bures distance is bounded by the unhalved trace distance. Hence the surrogate-to-actual Bures contribution is at most `s sqrt(rn)`.

### 7.3 Independent blocks

Let `beta_r` be the resulting Bures bound for block `r`. Before the final joint processing, different block outputs are products. If `f_r` are their root fidelities, then

```text
beta_total^2
 =2(1-prod f_r)
 <=sum_r 2(1-f_r)
 =sum_r beta_r^2.
```

The unhalved trace norm is at most `2 beta_total`. A final channel contracts trace norm.

### 7.4 Width reduction

Because `n_r>=1`,

```text
(1.5 beta_0 n_r+sqrt(r n_r))
 <=(1.5 beta_0+sqrt r)n_r.
```

The partition satisfies

```text
sum n_r^2 <=b sum n_r <=bN.
```

Therefore

```text
D_N^[b](E,F)
 <=2(1.5 beta_0+sqrt r)s sqrt(Nb),
```

capped at two.

No root-fidelity multiplication is used inside an entangled block. The only multiplicativity occurs across the explicitly independent groups.

Audit conclusion: **pass**.

on logical qubits, applies the tensor encoding before acquisition, sends all device inputs without feedback, and applies the tensor recovery after all outputs are available.

The event is fixed from `E,H,s,m`; it is independent of the unknown remainder.

## 9. Detailed audit: uniform block allocation

Shrink the scale interval so that

```text
s <=1/(4Delta),
s <=Delta/(pi kappa).
```

Set

```text
m=min{b,floor((2Delta s)^(-1))},
ell=floor(N/m),
x=mDelta s.
```

Then the pre-minimum floor is at least two. Since `m<=b<=N`, one has `ell>=1` and

```text
ell >=N/(2m).
```

Also `0<x<=1/2`. The one-block event gap is at least

```text
(1/2)sin x -(1/2)m kappa s^2
 >=x/pi -(kappa s/(2Delta))x
 >=x/(2pi).
```

Applying the finite Bernoulli lemma to `ell` independent blocks gives

```text
D_N^[b]
 >=(1/128)min{1,sqrt(ell)x/(2pi)}.
```

### Case 1: `m=b`

```text
sqrt(ell)x
 >=sqrt(N/(2b))*b Delta s
 =Delta s sqrt(Nb/2).
```

### Case 2: `m<b`

Then `m=floor((2Delta s)^(-1))`. Since the floor argument is at least two,

```text
m >=1/(4Delta s),
x >=1/4.
```

The Bernoulli lower is a fixed positive constant. The target `min{1,sqrt(Nb)s}` is at most one, so the same fixed value controls a fixed multiple of it in the saturated regime.

The number of used calls is `ell m<=N` on every record. Constants are independent of `N,b,F`.

Audit conclusion: **pass**.
