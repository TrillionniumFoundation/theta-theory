\begin{abstract}
This supplement retains the complete local tangent-tube, allocation-profile
and hard-budget theory, together with the binary, learning and auxiliary
proofs accompanying \emph{Finite-Use Discrimination Geometry of Ordered
Quantum Measurements}.  The primary article gives a self-contained finite
reset-experiment development.  Its operational definitions are stated
there; the local geometric results here have separate hypotheses and are
not premises of the finite spectral value formulas.  All relocated
mathematical sections retain their complete text.
\end{abstract}
