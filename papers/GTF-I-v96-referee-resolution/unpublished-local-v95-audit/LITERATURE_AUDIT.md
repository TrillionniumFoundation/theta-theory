## Direct model comparisons

Their work characterizes restrictions on classical/quantum adaptation and auxiliary dimensions through constrained tester separability. Complete-call product effects apply to the complete-measure-and-reprepare class, not every final effect of a fresh-preparation reset strategy with retained quantum receivers. **Zonnios and Binder**, *Distinguishing quantum processes with bounded coherent memory*, arXiv:2606.19511v1, Theorem 1 and Corollary 1. Their finite-time completeness concerns autonomous repeated-instrument machines for multi-time processes, using a counter to represent time-dependent protocols.
