# Current literature comparison — Revision 92

Primary sources were rechecked on 6 October 2026. This is an author-side comparison, not independent priority clearance.

## Ohst–Zhang–Nguyen–Plavala–Quintino

*Characterising memory in quantum channel discrimination via constrained separability problems*, Quantum 10 (2026), 1988; arXiv:2411.08110v2; DOI 10.22331/q-2026-01-28-1988. Primary text: https://arxiv.org/html/2411.08110v2 . Definition 23 / equation (64) supplies the complete measure-and-reprepare tester model; Sections 5–6 address memory-constrained adaptive optimization, and Theorem 24 distinguishes parallel and classically adaptive sets. Their work already provides systematic channel-memory formulations through separability with affine constraints. The new theorem does not claim that general idea. It gives the exact rank-envelope form at an old-output recording cut and a fresh-reference cut for classical-output reset experiments, with an explicit all-threshold spectrum on one shared-basis family. The initial first-call reference is not bounded by the old-output threshold, so it must not be equated to an auxiliary-dimension cap applying throughout the earlier call.

## Zonnios–Binder

*Distinguishing quantum processes with bounded coherent memory*, arXiv:2606.19511v1. Primary text: https://arxiv.org/html/2606.19511v1 . Proposition 2 states memory monotonicity; Theorem 1 and Corollary 1 compile arbitrary finite-time testers into a repeated instrument with a counter and show saturation of the strategy-norm benchmark. The present two-cut hierarchy is not that hierarchy: it keeps acquisition reset at every threshold and only its old retained receiver and fresh reference grow. Its saturated value can remain strictly below coherent acquisition. No equivalence between these resource parameters, no priority over bounded-memory hierarchies, and no general multi-time compilation theorem is inferred.
