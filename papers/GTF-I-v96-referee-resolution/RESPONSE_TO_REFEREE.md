## Mathematical and editorial response

We have reorganized the journal-facing article around the finite two-call decision programme. The complete reset normal form and general variational/dual theory now come first, followed by a new general no-message criterion, the solved shared-basis hierarchy, rank cuts, initial spectra and rigidity, exact values and finite attainment. The local tangent-tube, covariance, profile and hard-budget theory is moved intact into the current linked supplement, not deleted or made dependent on a historical PDF. The complete edition and independent structural article retain their respective archival and mathematical roles.

The chief new general statement is Theorem `messagecriterion96` in printed Primary Section 3. With the full old Schmidt support retained and a specified fresh-reference rank bound, receiver messages have no value at every prescribed pure first acquisition **if and only if** each labelwise continuation function is concave. Nonconcavity is witnessed by a binary receiver instrument after one first label at some initial acquisition. This is an exact criterion for arbitrary finite experiments in this interface, not a claim of strict gain in every problem, nor a two-outcome optimum for every preassigned initial state. Its operational premise is the named Heisenberg postponement lemma, which explicitly cannot bypass a smaller post-instrument old-memory bound.

The canonical water-filling rule in Section 8 is supplemented by a continuity and L1-nonexpansiveness lemma, including active-index ties. Section 9 proves the finite support-inverse construction with its at-most-d outcome count in place. A fully explicit family of 24 ordered qutrit bases gives a complete prescribed-spectrum example: for rho=diag(3/4,1/8,1/8), old dimension three and fresh dimension two, the exact biased scores are `4/7+sqrt(3)/56` and `4/7+sqrt(6)/112` with and without a receiver message. The same record instrument works after every first device label. The proof distinguishes Gram traces, physical record probabilities, and averaging over the once-selected latent basis.

The supplied local v95 equal-prior initial-spectrum proof is also retained in full as Primary Section 10. That local delivery was not a remote ancestor, is not the incomplete remote v95 spectral-decision branch, and was not reviewed by R62. Its inventory is reconciled in `LOCAL_V95_IMPORT.json`; old local build receipts are not qualification for this revision.
