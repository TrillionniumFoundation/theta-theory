\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction83}

A measurement used repeatedly is not determined, at a fixed accuracy,
by its one-use distance alone.  Its spectral noise controls how errors
accumulate, and normalization couples the effects of different outcomes.
We study these two features for ordered, memoryless measurements which
consume their input and return a classical label.  The tester may retain
an arbitrary reference and quantum memory, use common adaptive control,
and stop publicly after at most $N$ calls.  The resulting operational
distance $D_N$ is the supremum of the unhalved final-state trace norm.
Nonadaptive experiments may use entangled inputs and retained references;
they do not condition subsequent inputs on earlier outcomes.

\subsection{Finite-outcome interior geometry and learning}
For $d,N\ge1$ and $k\ge2$, consider the balanced interior
\[
 \mathfrak P_{d,k}=\{(E_j)_{j=1}^k:\ \sum_jE_j=I_d,
              \ I_d/(2k)\preceq E_j\preceq3I_d/(2k)\}.
\]
No common eigenbasis is assumed.  With
$r(E,F)=\max_j\|E_j-F_j\|_{\rm op}$ and $p=(k-1)d^2$,
Theorems~\ref{thm:multioutcomemetric82} and
\ref{thm:multioutcomeentropy82} give
\begin{align*}
 \tfrac1{128}\min\{1,\sqrt N r(E,F)\}
 &\le D_N^{\rm na}(E,F)\le D_N(E,F)
 \le\min\{2,2k\sqrt N r(E,F)\},\\
 \log_2\mathcal C_{N,k}(\delta)
 &=\tfrac p2\log_2N+p\log_2(1/\delta)+O(p\log(k+1)).
\end{align*}
The geometric comparison holds without an accuracy restriction.  The
covering statement assumes $0<\delta<1/128$, permits arbitrary legal
centres, and has an absolute implied constant in its logarithmic remainder.
A normalization-preserving rational grid yields a finite exact public
code at this fixed-length order.

The common learner in Theorem~\ref{thm:multioutcomelearning82} satisfies
\[
 M^*_{d,k}(N,\delta,\eta)
 =\Theta_k\!\left(N\delta^{-2}[d^2+d\log(1/\eta)]\right),
 \quad 0<\delta\le2^{-24},\quad0<\eta\le1/8.
\]
This is a joint law in $d,N,\delta,\eta$ for each \emph{fixed} outcome
count $k$.  Its explicit upper bound is
$Ck^3N\delta^{-2}[d^2+d\log(k/\eta)]$; the lower bound does not
contain a growing-$k$ factor.  The upper uses independent one-call
probe--reference acquisitions and collective processing of completed
outputs.  The lower permits coherent adaptive training and bounded
public stopping.  Every training record satisfies the call budget.
The eigenvectors used in pairwise lower witnesses are not inputs to
this common learner.

\subsection{A coupled covariance on the complete body}
Let $\mathcal P_{d,k}$ instead impose only $E_j\succeq0$ and
$\sum_jE_j=I_d$.  On zero-sum Hermitian tuples define
\[
 S_E(K)=\sum_jE_jK_j,\qquad
 (\mathcal C_EK)_j=\tfrac12(E_jK_j+K_jE_j-E_jS_E(K)-S_E(K)^*E_j).
\]
The operator is positive and concave in the tuple $E$.  Its quadratic
form is the variance
\[
 \langle K,\mathcal C_EK\rangle
 =\sum_j\tr(E_jK_j^2)-\Big\|\sum_jE_jK_j\Big\|_{\rm HS}^2.
\]
Put $H=F-E$, $M=(E+F)/2$, and
\[
 \mathcal Q_N(E,F)^2
 =N\langle H,(\mathcal C_M+N^{-1}\operatorname{Id})^{-1}H\rangle.
\]
Theorem~\ref{thm:closedcovariance83} proves the closed-body bound
\[
 D_N(E,F)\le\min\{2,2\sqrt{k+1}\,\mathcal Q_N(E,F)\},
\]
including zero effects, changes of rank, and noncommuting supports.
A regularized horizontal energy identity separates the tangent into a
part accumulating at rate $\sqrt N$ and a residual accumulating at rate
$N$.  Concavity then converts a path estimate to the displayed midpoint
bound.  For two outcomes the operator is exactly the binary Sylvester
variance, with $\mathcal Q_N^2=2Q_N^2$.

This covariance also gives a data-processing inequality for its
unregularized dual energy, and it vanishes identically precisely at
projection-valued measurements.  Under admixture of uniform outcome
noise of size $\epsilon$, Theorem~\ref{thm:noisecrossover83} obtains a
finite-use crossover proportional to
$N/\sqrt{1+N\epsilon}$, sharp up to fixed-$k$ constants on a rotating
projective family.  Proposition~\ref{prop:covarianceexact83} evaluates
the squared modulus by one exact rational linear system of dimension
$p$.  This is an upper certificate, not a formula for the exact optimal
tester or a matching classification of the whole multi-outcome boundary.

\subsection{Legalization, descriptions, and proof organization}
Theorem~\ref{thm:affinerepair83} replaces tuple-grid legalization by a
rational affine map with polynomial bit complexity.  The map preserves
normalization, satisfies both balanced spectral bounds, and increases
component error by at most a factor four.  Its guarded version is legal
on every record.  Corollary~\ref{cor:affinelearner83} preserves the joint
query and description orders.  This removes enumeration from
\emph{legalization}, not from dictionary construction or general
collective-readout synthesis.

Sections~\ref{sec:finiteoutcome82}--\ref{sec:affinerepair83} contain the
finite-outcome statements and the coupled closed-body extension.
Section~\ref{sec:priority83} gives quantitative comparisons with current
tomography, together with the distinct resource conventions.  The
appendices supply the complete binary boundary geometry, entropy,
learning, confidence, finite-control, and certificate proofs used by
these results.  In particular, no proof of a cited binary subprocedure
is left outside this article.  The stochastic-realization companion has
its own hypotheses and proofs and is a separate mathematical object.
