\subsection{The experiment scale and the role of the relative law}
The information in a one-flight nonlinear germ already suffices for
the identical-even contact inverse, and the count-only reconstruction
uses only three or four flight numbers. Neither fact is a motivation
for passing to an infinite bridge. The purpose of that passage is
different: it identifies a nonlinear physical probability model which
remains valid as the bridge and the number of observations grow
together. Positivity of an endpoint Hessian alone cannot justify this
replacement. Its mixed derivative is exponentially small, and even a
small absolute flux error could dominate both the success probability
and the comparison of the prepared experiments. The relative
trace-norm construction controls precisely this missing scale.

Theorem~\ref{thm:v7-critical} gives a further consequence at the level
of testing the finite law against its boundary model. Put
$q_j=e^{-j\gamma}$ and $p_{j,d}^0=d^2/[2A\sinh(j\gamma)]$.
For actual positive-offset endpoint--residual observations,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v7-intro-profile}
 \begin{split}
 k_jq_j\to b,\quad k_j\sqrt{d_j}\to0
 &\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \|\Pi_{j,d_j}^{\otimes k_j}-
       \Pi_{\partial,j,d_j}^{\otimes k_j}\|_{\rm TV}
                 \to1-e^{-2b/\pi},\\
 n_jp_{j,d_j}^0q_j\to b,\quad n_jp_{j,d_j}^0\sqrt{d_j}\to0
 &\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \|P_{j,d_j}^{\otimes n_j}-
       P_{\partial,j,d_j}^{\otimes n_j}\|_{\rm TV}
                 \to1-e^{-2b/\pi}.
 \end{split}
\end{equation}
Here $j\to\infty$, $b\in[0,\infty]$, the same table is used in the
two hypotheses, and the error on a failed preparation is not discarded.
For even facing graphs, which may differ, $\sqrt{d_j}$ can be replaced
by $d_j$. The exact quadratic overlap is
$1-(2/\pi)\arcsin(q_j)$ for every flight number, with unequal facing
curvatures and both parities. Its product experiment is an erasure
experiment, so the limiting equal-prior testing error is
$e^{-2b/\pi}/2$. The explicit joint-offset conditions in
\eqref{eq:v7-intro-profile} are essential. At fixed positive offset
the nonlinear comparison remains that of
Theorem~\ref{thm:v6-transfer}, not an unproved sharp-rate assertion.

Full embedded growing collision arrays retain the maximum-norm
bounded-Lipschitz conclusion, not the endpoint total-variation
conclusion in the first row. The contact-germ result is exact analytic
uniqueness, not stable global continuation. The count-only procedure
and the position procedure have different data, different inverse
singularities, and different accuracy exponents.

Theorem~\ref{thm:v7-lower} identifies a physical cubic
indistinguishability bound for the shrinking-offset count design,
including adaptive policies: bounded-flight binary queries with
$d_i\le L_mh$ require order $h^{-2}\varepsilon^{-6}$ preparations
for fixed-confidence matching accuracy $\varepsilon$. With fixed
$m$ and $h\asymp\varepsilon^{3/m}$ this matches the power in the
local acquisition bound. Theorem~\ref{thm:v7-fixed-bracket} supplies
a count search for the initially missing bracket and caps all waiting
times on failure paths; it retains the upper rate from a fixed local
interval. Its initial search uses wider offsets, so the narrower
lower bound is not a global optimality assertion for that larger
class. These results strengthen the statistical interpretation without
identifying one-flight inversion with long-bridge approximation.
