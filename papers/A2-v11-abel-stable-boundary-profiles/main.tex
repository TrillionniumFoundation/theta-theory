\begin{abstract}
We establish nonlinear relative boundary laws for near-onset collision
records in smooth periodic dispersing billiards, without symmetry or
finite horizon assumptions. Stationary actions and normalized mixed
derivatives separate into half-line actions and relative determinant
amplitudes. Physical residual-time integration preserves the relative
factorization on a common closed offset collar, with every fixed
geometric and offset derivative. The two oriented even laws determine
the complete normalized boundary energy profiles, and the odd law
satisfies an exact Volterra compatibility identity. A weighted Abel
transform of the integrated physical flux gives a two-sided Lipschitz
coordinate for this nonlinear invariant. We reconstruct both profiles
from finitely many binary collision reports using positive-node
regularization in this norm. The resulting preparation bound accounts
for rare events, finite-bridge bias and a half-order differentiation
loss. In the same smooth physical experiment, a separately charged
one- and two-flight pilot estimates the unknown gap, area and multiplier;
this removes exact calibration without increasing the uniform sufficient
accuracy exponent. Patch labels and common regularity bounds remain
supplied. We also compare endpoint experiments and establish a joint
small-offset testing profile for unequal contacts. Separate
finite-dimensional experiments give fixed-window inverses at coalescing
curvatures and sharp preparation orders with an unknown gap or smooth
remainder nuisance. Complete contact-jet inverses, geometric realizations,
acquisition bounds and collision-record response formulas are included.
\end{abstract}

experiment or turn abstract profile alternatives into billiard tables.
