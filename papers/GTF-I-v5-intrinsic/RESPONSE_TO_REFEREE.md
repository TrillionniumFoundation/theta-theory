## Summary of the mathematical response

`c G_M(q) <= e_M^2(Z_q) <= R_M <= C G_M(q)`.

The quantity G_M is generated by a finite greedy cylinder algorithm from actual branch derivatives and cylinder probabilities. Its codes are implementable: the entire full b-ary greedy tree is suffix closed and has exactly `(bL-1)/(b-1)` vertices for L leaves. All vertices, including the empty word, count as designated states. No logarithmic age multiplier is incurred.

The exponent is computed from two intrinsic pressure roots,

`P(s_g phi + 2 s_g psi)=0`,
`P(s_t phi)+s_t log(q)=0`,
`alpha=(1-max(s_g,s_t))/max(s_g,s_t)`.

It is therefore an output rather than a supplied functional-quantization exponent. A second theorem transfers the risk law to invariant acquisition kernels Q that retain hidden state, with a conditional minorization Q>=epsilon mu. ## E1 — compute the intrinsic invariant rather than transfer an unknown one

**Response:** Theorems `thm:v5-intrinsic` and `thm:v5-pressure` replace the unknown orbit quantization input by a finite cylinder profile and then derive its exponent from pressure. The proof of the profile uses physical cylinder separation, mass-weighted diameters, a balanced greedy tree and disjoint Hilbert tubes. Off-image centres and arbitrary randomized/time-dependent causal coders are covered by the lower bound.

The pressure proof is separate from the profile proof. The mixed partition sum is sandwiched between the endpoints and the convolution of a spatial and a survival partition sum. Summability above the larger root supplies the upper exponent; a tilted Gibbs measure at the root supplies the matching lower exponent. There is no premise asserting existence of a quantization dimension.

The realization improvement is exact at the combinatorial level: every suffix is already a vertex of the greedy tree. This is stronger than attaching an age register to an arbitrary orbit code. We do not assert exact finite-M stationary optimality; we prove a stationary deterministic machine matches the full competing class up to a constant.

## E2 — complete regeneration excludes persistent hidden state

**Response:** Theorem `thm:v5-dependent` admits a Markov transition Q from the pre-acquisition hidden state to the new state, followed by an observation K of that new state. The assumptions are `mu Q=mu`, `T_#mu=mu` and `Q(x,.)>=epsilon mu(.)`. The remainder of Q may retain the whole old state. In particular `Q=(1-epsilon) delta_{Tx}+epsilon mu` does not regenerate at every acquisition; its refresh coin is unobserved.

The lower bound conditions on the actual old hidden state and dominates the new joint law QK by epsilon muK. It never replaces the conditional law by muK. The upper bound uses invariant marginals and an observer which overwrites its state at acquisition; that observer has exactly the same expected one-time losses as in the independent reference experiment. Consequently the intrinsic pressure law survives partial refreshment (`cor:v5-dependentpressure`).

`cor:v5-dependentnoise` treats noisy wrapped-Gaussian acquisitions with persistent hidden memory. The reference B_0 is deliberately not identified with the full-history Bayes risk. An explicit two-observation rational diagnostic demonstrates that older observations may strictly reduce that risk.
