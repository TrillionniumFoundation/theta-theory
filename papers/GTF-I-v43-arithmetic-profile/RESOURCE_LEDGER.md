# Resource ledger — v43

The resource is the maximum **available persistent label count** over initialization and command cuts, with a held two-label final answer. Padding counts. The machine may be redesigned for every horizon; it is not an anytime machine. The actual command sequence is externally supplied, never optimized by the simulator. Exactly one final coordinate query is answered.

Free structure in the stated atomic-row model: external epoch, prescribed horizon, nonuniform row and decoder tables, construction/lookup of those tables, exact real arithmetic and exact sampling from each fixed row. No prior command, seed, private random tape or phase identifier survives outside the counted label.

Complete converse packets are independent of the prior process and each other. Letters inside a packet may be correlated. All B ordinary commands are processed; the count vector and packet law are proof-side, never runtime inputs. A selected law may depend on a proposed available width/profile, not on hidden private states.

A k-vertex D-dimensional compiler has |A|k common command rows, at most D+1 successors per row and D bounded terminal columns. A planar r-angle compiler has (r+1)q rows, at most three successors, and two final columns. Reflection adds q rows. A rational phase group of order h multiplies actual labels by h; its identity is retained and charged. Sparse rows do not imply short descriptions, fast construction or finite-bit samplers.

Fixed algebraic packet/dilation inputs admit real quantifier elimination; the phase program is finite second-order cone optimization with potentially exponentially many partitions. Neither is presented as an efficient optimizer for full hidden width. Fixed-B word lists may have |A|^B entries. For computable-real commands, finite numerical tests are only diagnostics unless validated entry intervals are supplied.

Planar asymptotics fix r, alpha, rho and epsilon before N grows; 0<rho<=1/10 and epsilon<rho/(2 sqrt(2)). The minimal-type theorem gives a logarithmic exponent, not a constant-factor law. The full-measure constants are not uniform in alpha/r. Liouville fluctuation results do not determine the exact upper logarithmic limit. Every upper bound stated as exact preserves the conditional numerical law at the named horizon, not all earlier stopping lengths.

Label bits are ceil(log2(labels)). Total table size, uniform computational space, autonomous state count, total branching-program size and finite-fair-bit workspace are different resources. No equality among them is inferred. The classical/quantum and calibration results of the predecessor retain their original separate conventions.
