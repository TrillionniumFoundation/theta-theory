## 5. New global derivations

### Occupation recovery without a supplied killing aperture

The new core/14_global_response.tex treats arbitrary nonempty locally finite unions of separated bounded convex bodies. Periodicity, finite species and boundary smoothness are not hypotheses. The launch density is stationary, supported on a convex footprint, and positive almost everywhere in its interior.

The reciprocal identity is g=(T-I)v. Expanded positive components have bounded diameter and mutual gaps exceeding a compass step. Stopping on exit gives a uniform expected exit-time bound. Positivity identifies occupation from the stopped payoff even though the component and footprint are not supplied. The positive-obstacle iteration yields a global inverse, geometric block-tail error and finite-field locality.

The martingale, stopping and Green principles are classical. The new use removes the supplied geometric killing aperture. The proof also gives a uniform sup-norm comparison of admissible occupation fields.

### Translation periods without a crystallinity prior

The inverse commutes with translations, so a response period is an occupation period. It permutes the separated expanded convex components. Support-function cancellation removes the common footprint and proves that it is a period of the obstacle configuration. Translation covariance gives the reverse implications.

The period group is discrete: a nonzero period cannot fix a compact component, and distinct components are separated. Rank-two crystallinity is therefore inferred from the exact response instead of assumed in a periodic presentation. The retained finite-window period theorem still uses its quantitative patch margin. Exact whole-field identification and a uniform finite certificate have different hypotheses.

### Homothety with independent setting translations

The global calibration extension retains the density-cancelling flux identity and removes the common origin and supplied component correspondence. An isolated component's width deficit is a footprint-width scalar independent of obstacle shape. These scalars identify the operating ratio.

Steiner centering removes setting translations from component shapes. The pointwise infimum of support functions for the complete collection acquires the same footprint addend at each setting. Subtracting the envelopes cancels the collection, including infinite collections with repeated or symmetric shapes. The envelope need not be a support function; physical data ensure that its recovered difference has the asserted support interpretation.

The residual common translation and period-offset freedom belong in the classification. Exact homothety, labelled settings, the physical collision law and separation remain assumptions. Exact recovery for distinct scales supplies no uniform stability as the scale gap tends to zero.

The common-density range converse in core/11_stationary_information.tex remains active with its complete proof.
