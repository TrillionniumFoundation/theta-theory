\section{Physical clocks, multiscale passages, and generator interfaces}\label{sec:limits}
\subsection{Separate the limits before exchanging them}
Physical horizon $T$, particle number $N$, geometric scale $\varepsilon$, source intensity, and observation resolution are independent variables. A fixed-$N,T$ response theorem does not establish uniformity for $N\to\infty$ or $T\to\infty$. The Lorentz full-preparation result uses $T<d_*$; replacing it by repeated short-time applications requires a proof that the propagated preparations stay in a controlled class, including their correlations and singularities.

\begin{theorem}[A quantitative diagonal for selection and conditioning]\label{thm:diagonal}
Suppose, on common report spaces, $d_{\TV}(P_k,\widetilde P_k)\le\epsilon_k$,
$\osc V_k\le M_k$, and $\norm{V_k-\widetilde V_k}_\infty\le\delta_k$. Then
\[
 d_{\TV}(P_k^{V_k},\widetilde P_k^{\widetilde V_k})
 \le2e^{M_k}\epsilon_k+e^{2\delta_k}-1.
\]
If both tilted laws give an event $A_k$ probability at least $p_k>0$, the event-conditioned TV distance is at most $2/p_k$ times the displayed bound. In particular,
\[
 \delta_k\to0,\qquad (e^{M_k}\epsilon_k+\delta_k)/p_k\to0
\]
is sufficient for a common conditioned limit.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
First change the reference measure at fixed source and apply Lemma~\ref{lem:normalization}. Then change the source at fixed new reference. The triangle inequality gives the first bound. Apply Proposition~\ref{prop:tv-budget} to the two tilted laws for the second. The final statement follows from $e^{2\delta}-1=O(\delta)$ at zero. These are sufficient budgets, not asserted optimal thresholds.
\end{proof}

For weak experimental distances use Theorem~\ref{thm:test-tilt} instead, with the actual weighted and event-restricted tests. For derivative passages, require the full jet errors of Theorem~\ref{thm:jet-error}. A small unweighted mass error cannot replace either requirement.

\subsection{Joint current--clock pressure}
Suppose a later model theorem proves a differentiable joint per-return pressure
\[
 \mathcal P_a(\xi,s)
 =\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac1n\log E\exp\{\xi\cdot S_n\kappa-sS_n\tau\},
\]
and proves the renewal relation identifying a physical-time growth rate $H_a$ by
$\mathcal P_a(\xi,H_a(\xi))=0$. Suppose also that its first derivatives are tilted means and second derivatives are the corresponding asymptotic covariance form. These are explicit analytic inputs, not consequences of the notation.

Implicit differentiation then gives
\[
 \partial_iH_a=\frac{E_{\mu_{a,\xi}}\kappa_i}{E_{\mu_{a,\xi}}\tau}=v_i.
\]
Differentiating a second time yields
\begin{equation}
 \partial_{ij}H_a
 =\frac{\mathcal P_{\xi_i\xi_j}+\mathcal P_{\xi_i s}v_j
 +\mathcal P_{s\xi_j}v_i+\mathcal P_{ss}v_iv_j}
 {-\mathcal P_s}.
 \label{eq:physical-Hessian}
\end{equation}
The numerator is the asymptotic covariance of
$\kappa_i-v_i\tau$ and $\kappa_j-v_j\tau$. Thus clock fluctuations and their correlations with current are retained. Merely dividing a collision covariance by an untilted mean roof is not the general formula.

\subsection{A bilateral response estimate with a proved algebraic implication}
A typical infinite response atom is
$A_{m,n}^\omega=\langle Q_a^nJ_{a,\omega}Q_a^mg,f\rangle$.
Suppose the \emph{same physical occurrence} has both bounds
\[
 |A_{m,n}^\omega|\le C^-_\omega\alpha^m\Gamma_+^n,
 \qquad |A_{m,n}^\omega|\le C^+_\omega\Gamma_-^m\beta^n,
\]
where $\alpha,\beta<1$, $\Gamma_\pm\ge1$. Set
$A=\log(1/\alpha)$, $B=\log(1/\beta)$, $C=\log\Gamma_+$, $D=\log\Gamma_-$.

\begin{proposition}[Bilateral product summability]\label{prop:bilateral}
If $AB>CD$, set $\lambda=(B+D)/(A+B+C+D)$ and
$\rho=\exp[(CD-AB)/(A+B+C+D)]<1$. Then
\[
 |A_{m,n}^\omega|\le(C^-_\omega)^\lambda(C^+_\omega)^{1-\lambda}\rho^{m+n}.
\]
If these interpolated constants are summable over $\omega$, the complete double-time series converges absolutely.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
A nonnegative quantity bounded by $X$ and $Y$ is bounded by $X^\lambda Y^{1-\lambda}$. The time exponents are $-A\lambda+D(1-\lambda)$ and $C\lambda-B(1-\lambda)$. The chosen $\lambda$ makes both equal $(CD-AB)/(A+B+C+D)<0$. Sum two geometric series and then the occurrence constants. Additive decay such as $\alpha^m+\beta^n$ would not have a finite double sum.
\end{proof}

This algebra preserves a useful historical response mechanism \cite{RepoCM2}. The present short-time Lorentz assembly does not prove its hypotheses for arbitrary long-time moving-table sources. Finite-difference arrays must also have a common summable envelope before their limits can be interchanged with the double sum.

\subsection{Effective nonlinear generators come after closure}
If an effective Markov semigroup $P_t$ has actually been derived and its diffusion generator is
$Lf=b\cdot\nabla f+\frac12a:D^2f$, then, on a common smooth domain,
\begin{equation}
 G_\eta f=\eta^{-1}e^{-\eta f}L(e^{\eta f})
 =b\cdot\nabla f+\tfrac12a:D^2f+\tfrac\eta2\nabla f^Ta\nabla f.
 \label{eq:generator}
\end{equation}
This follows by differentiating the exponential twice. For a pure deterministic first-order generator, the quadratic term is absent and $G_\eta=L$, agreeing with Proposition~\ref{prop:dirac-linear}. For a finite-rate jump kernel,
\[
 G_\eta f(x)=\eta^{-1}\int(e^{\eta[f(y)-f(x)]}-1)\nu(x,dy).
\]
A quadratic approximation of this full exponential expression requires an integrable Taylor remainder and an explicit scaling.

Deterministic homogenization results such as \cite{KellyMelbourne} provide examples of how effective stochastic limits can be proved without postulating microscopic stochastic dynamics. They are not substitutes for the moving-boundary, preparation, and conditional estimates required here. Likewise, the hard-sphere fluctuation and large-deviation results in \cite{BodineauEtAl} have a specified short-time regime, not a blanket arbitrary-time extension.

Fixed-reference linear-source pressure is convex because its Hessian is a covariance. A supremum of fixed affine payoff functionals is also convex. A more general nonconvex theta-generator must therefore be derived from its own predeclared mechanical feedback or game, with no reverse dependence of the microscopic setup on an unknown future value function. The present paper preserves that research target but does not manufacture it from \eqref{eq:generator}.
