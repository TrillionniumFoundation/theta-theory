\begin{abstract}
We retain the original triangular Lorentz gas, its physical clock and
its exact return, displacement and collision records. A positive
variational gap on the product of the actual disks controls the
fourth powers of all contact displacements by a single budget,
uniformly in the collision count, radius and every incidence. For
any configuration in the full physical-record length sublevel we prove
$\sum_i|Y_i-Q_i|^4\le10^8R(2-c_0-c_m)$, rather than a collision-count
multiple of a pointwise estimate. The proof pays transverse turning
work by disjoint contact gaps and a bounded-overlap family of physical
reflection blocks. It extends to fixed-word singular closures and
gives quantitative uniqueness of critical limiting polygons without
defining a flow through their singularities. The complete displacement
budget controls simultaneously all-center clearance changes and, for
actual physical reference records, all section discrepancies and joint
Fourier marks. Positive source inequalities keep the original roof and
conditional measure. The complete native variational, optical,
clearance, pressure and low-load source chain is retained. The new
geometric and observation estimates do not yet supply the required
collision-normalized source density; the original raw pointwise local
limit and four-coordinate inversion remain subject to the explicitly
unchanged shortage limit.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Introduction}
The problem is the joint raw pointwise local limit and inverse theorem
for a unit-speed particle reflecting at every actual first collision
among equal disks on the triangular lattice. We keep the original
family $R\in[9/20,47/100]$, the collision and physical clocks, the
section probability $c_*=91/(10000\pi)$, all lattice labels and
microscopic roof windows. The precise original event and target are
\eqref{eq:v76-event} and \eqref{eq:v76-target}. In particular no
conditional event is replaced by a more convenient event and no
unvisited disk is made into an extra reflection.

\subsection*{From a one-contact modulus to a complete-record budget}
The starting point is the actual regular record
$Q_0,\ldots,Q_m$ of ordered disk centers $O_0,\ldots,O_m$. For
comparison only, put $\mathcal K=\prod_i\overline B(O_i,R)$ and
$A(Y)=\sum_i|Y_{i+1}-Y_i|$. A point in this product can be
nonphysical. The inherited variational identity writes $A(Y)-D_Q$
as a sum of nonnegative flight and contact gaps and proves
$L(Q)-D_Q=E_R(Q):=R(2-c_0-c_m)$, where the two $c$'s are the
actual endpoint cosines. The inherited maximum bound is
$\max_i|Y_i-Q_i|\le22E_R(Q)^{1/4}$ on $A(Y)\le L(Q)$.
Those statements are not new contributions of this revision.

The new Theorem~\ref{thm:ss-quartic} proves
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ss-intro}
 \sup_{Y\in\mathcal K:\,A(Y)\le L(Q)}
 \sum_{i=0}^m|Y_i-Q_i|^4\le10^8 E_R(Q).
\end{equation}
Its constant does not grow with $m$ and has no inverse-incidence
factor. A naive sum of the preceding maximum bound would not give
this statement. The proof instead sums the actual positive contact
budget before estimating transverse turning. Consecutive physical
turning anchors are at most $94$ flights apart. Their associated
intervals overlap at most $94$ times, so every flight and contact
payment is reused a bounded number of times, independently of the
record length. The geometric anchor input is reconstructed in
Appendix~\ref{sec:sc-geometry}; it is inherited, not assumed anew.
The positive-gap statement also applies to every disk-product
configuration, with the gap and endpoint deficit separated.

\subsection*{Singular closure without a fictitious singular flow}
The same estimates hold on the closure of a fixed finite physical
word. Corollary~\ref{cor:ss-critical-closure} proves for a normal-endpoint
limiting reference, including zero internal incidences,
\[
 A(Y)-L(Q)\ge C_G^{-1}\sum_i|Y_i-Q_i|^4.
\]
Thus a critical limiting polygon is the unique global minimum and has
a quantitative approximate-minimizer modulus. The resulting
fixed-word compactification is unique even when physical continuation
intervals are disconnected. This does not define a billiard map at a
grazing point, assign section labels there, or erase singular paired
endpoint traces. None of the inherited inverse Jacobians is extended
by an unjustified continuity claim.

\subsection*{All-center clearance and exact physical observations}
Theorem~\ref{thm:ss-clearance-sum} converts \eqref{eq:ss-intro} into
one summable clearance budget, simultaneously for every unvisited
lattice center on every closed flight segment. For words whose
comparison minimum is itself an actual regular reference record,
Theorem~\ref{thm:ss-section} proves a complete phase-space fourth-power
budget and bounds section discrepancies by the original
section-boundary occupation plus a specified endpoint-deficit cost.
The occupation count still excludes the last state, while the
terminal section test is kept separately. Existence of a reference
alone does not transfer these labels.

Proposition~\ref{prop:ss-twists} retains both lattice coordinates,
the physical roof and the integer return count in its Fourier error.
Theorem~\ref{thm:ss-source} pushes a positive discrepancy inequality
forward by the \emph{original} roof at the original exact labels and
original conditional measure. No probability factorization, changed
clock or unrecorded Jacobian occurs. The complementary words and the
unestimated boundary and endpoint-deficit source densities are kept
explicitly. These results do not furnish a normalized comparison-pool
capacity or a high-frequency local limit theorem.

\subsection*{Retained architecture and the governing estimate}
All forty complete predecessor proof bodies 260--299 and both native
continuation bodies 300--301 are preserved byte for byte. They retain
the optical Jacobi and endpoint trace completion, marked incidence
pressure, low-load shortage, physical single and multiple clearance
marks, critical coarea and reversing orbits, homogeneous growth,
reflection resolvents, physical tubes, complete bending, radial clock
identities and global variational comparisons. The original model and
analytic interfaces remain unchanged. The archive also recovers the
historical native modules 1--291 in all 343 retained variants, rather
than silently replacing their arithmetic, induced-return, spectral,
conditional-measure and inversion chain by a new model.

The minimum remaining final condition is exactly the inherited
\eqref{eq:v112-remaining}, restated only for compatibility with the
previous continuation:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sc-original-obligation}
 \lim_{B\to\infty}\limsup_{m\to\infty}\sup_{R,n,k}
 m^2\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{t\in\mathcal C_{m,R,n,k}(M)}
 (\mathsf U^{\rm low}_{B,m}(t)-K_BP^{\rm ng}_{r_B,m}(t))_+=0.
\end{equation}
Here $M$ is fixed, all complete positive physical sums precede the
suprema, all outer widths are fixed before the collision limsup, and
$B$ is outermost. The new statements are uniform deterministic
estimates and exact positive-measure comparisons, not a proof of this
limit. The full raw pointwise LLT and four-coordinate inversion are
not claimed completed. The family-uniform pressure and paired-source
interfaces are retained at their declared hypotheses and have not
been independently recertified by finite computations.

The literature retains its actual strength. The perturbative
anisotropic framework of Demers--Zhang~\cite{DZUniform} requires
uniform dynamical bounds and compatible function spaces, including
coordinate handling under changing radius. The pressure theory of
Baladi--Demers~\cite{BDPressure} is restricted by a geometric-potential
pressure gap. The flow local limit result of
Dolgopyat--N\'andori~\cite{DNFlow} retains minimality, arithmetic and
test-observable hypotheses. None is identified with the unproved
$m^2L^\infty$ exact-source estimate. The new geometric arguments are
proved directly; general convex duality~\cite{BVNotes} is background,
not a claimed novelty. Exact command receipts and native compilation
are reproducibility evidence, not independent continuum proof
certificates.
