\section{Positive comparison without an endpoint velocity}
\label{sec:v104-block}
We work on the original disjoint space of physical pairing sheets.
An angular point on that space is denoted by $\xi$; its measure is
$d\theta$, its endpoint roofs are $a(\xi)<b(\xi)$, and
\[
 c(\xi)=\min\{f^\varepsilon(a+,\xi),f^\varepsilon(b-,\xi)\},
 \qquad \ell(\xi)=b(\xi)-a(\xi).
\]
The suppressed superscript on $c$ is always the original $\varepsilon$.
In these coordinates the positive paired measure is
$c(\xi)\,d\theta\,du$ on $a(\xi)<u<b(\xi)$. In particular
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v104-target}
 \mathsf T_\Delta^\varepsilon(t)
 =\int c(\xi)\one_{\{0<\ell(\xi)<\Delta\}}
                \one_{\{a(\xi)<t<b(\xi)\}}\,d\theta.
\end{equation}
All sheets carry the same fixed exact event. No occupation class is
pooled, and coincident physical faces retain their incident component.

Fix $0<\delta<\Delta$ and $r>0$. A comparison pair consists of
Borel angular sets $I_j,J_j$ on an actual physical sheet, and a sign
$\tau_j\in\{-1,1\}$. Its source has $0<\ell\le\delta$ on $I_j$;
its comparison set has $0<\ell<\Delta$ on $J_j$. For almost every
$(\xi,\zeta)\in I_j\times J_j$ assume the corresponding inclusion
\begin{align}
 \tau_j=1:&\qquad a(\zeta)\le a(\xi),\quad
                           b(\zeta)\ge b(\xi)+r,\notag\\
 \tau_j=-1:&\qquad a(\zeta)\le a(\xi)-r,\quad
                           b(\zeta)\ge b(\xi).
                                      \label{eq:v104-inclusion}
\end{align}
The sets $I_j$ are disjoint in the physical angular space. Suppose
also that the comparison sets have overlap at most $N$:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v104-overlap}
                 \sum_j\one_{J_j}(\xi)\le N
                 \quad\text{for almost every }\xi.
\end{equation}
This is a condition on physical angular sets, not on the number of
words. For disjoint angular arms it holds with $N=1$.
Write $C_j=\int_{I_j}c\,d\theta$ and $D_j=\int_{J_j}c\,d\theta$,
and assume these numbers are finite. Zero values are allowed.

\begin{theorem}[A positive angular-interval comparison]
\label{thm:v104-block}
Let $H(\xi,t)$ be any original Borel selector with $0\le H\le1$,
and let $\kappa>0$. Define
\[
 \alpha_j=\begin{cases}
 \min\{1,\kappa D_j/C_j\},&C_j>0,\\
 0,&C_j=0,
 \end{cases}
 \qquad
 T_{H,j}^\varepsilon(t)=\int_{I_j}c(\xi)H(\xi,t)
                         \one_{\{a(\xi)<t<b(\xi)\}}\,d\theta.
\]
At every finite collision count and for almost every target roof,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v104-block-height-finite}
 \sum_j\alpha_j T_{H,j}^\varepsilon(t)
 \le\frac{N\kappa}{r}\int_{t-r}^{t+r}
                          \mathsf T_\Delta^\varepsilon(u)\,du.
\end{equation}
The remaining source is exactly
$\sum_j(1-\alpha_j)T_{H,j}^\varepsilon$, and satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v104-deficit}
 0\le\sum_j(1-\alpha_j)T_{H,j}^\varepsilon(t)
 \le\sum_j(C_j-\kappa D_j)_+
        \one_{\{T_{1,j}^\varepsilon(t)>0\}}.
\end{equation}
If $C_j\le\kappa D_j$ whenever $C_j>0$, then the left side of
\eqref{eq:v104-block-height-finite} is the complete selected source
on the union of the $I_j$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Fix $j$ and a roof $t$ for which $T_{H,j}^\varepsilon(t)>0$.
The set of source angles with $a(\xi)<t<b(\xi)$ and positive
selected weight then has positive angular measure. By Fubini,
\eqref{eq:v104-inclusion} holds between some such source angle
and almost every comparison angle. If $\tau_j=1$, every
$u\in(t,t+r)$ belongs to the corresponding comparison segment;
if $\tau_j=-1$, the same assertion holds for $u\in(t-r,t)$.
Writing $T_{J_j}(u)=\int_{J_j}c\one_{\{a<u<b\}}\,d\theta$, we obtain
\[
 rD_j\le\int_{t+\tau_j(0,r)}T_{J_j}(u)\,du,
 \qquad
 \alpha_j T_{H,j}^\varepsilon(t)\le\alpha_j C_j\le\kappa D_j.
\]
When the selected source is zero the desired comparison is immediate.
Thus each summand is bounded by the corresponding one-sided integral
multiplied by $\kappa/r$. All such intervals lie in $(t-r,t+r)$.
Tonelli and \eqref{eq:v104-overlap} give
$\sum_jT_{J_j}(u)\le N\mathsf T_\Delta^\varepsilon(u)$ and prove
\eqref{eq:v104-block-height-finite}. The remaining source is positive
because $0\le\alpha_j\le1$. Since
$(1-\alpha_j)C_j=(C_j-\kappa D_j)_+$ for $C_j>0$, its displayed
upper bound follows. The case $C_j=0$ contributes nothing.
Countable sums follow from monotone convergence of nonnegative finite
sums. No collision limsup has been taken.
\end{proof}

This proof compares integrals of the original positive endpoint
minimum. It neither differentiates that minimum nor divides by
$a'$, $b'$ or $(1-s)a'+sb'$. There is no flow through a stationary
point and no assertion that two prescribed trajectories are joined
by an admissible clock. The selector remains at the starting roof;
only its positive comparison source is enlarged.

\begin{corollary}[The physically summed essential height]
\label{cor:v104-block-height}
Suppose $N$ and $\kappa$ are uniform in collision count, radius and
exact labels. Under \eqref{eq:v104-paired-average},
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v104-block-height}
 \mathcal H_M\!\left(\sum_j\alpha_jT_{H,j}^\varepsilon\right)
 \le C N\kappa\{\varepsilon^{1/16}
                             +(1+\varepsilon^{-1})\Delta\}.
\end{equation}
All widths and constants in this statement are fixed before the
collision limit. The estimate permits any number of source intervals
and comparison intervals, subject to the stated overlap.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The right side of \eqref{eq:v104-block-height-finite} is
$2N\kappa A_r\mathsf T_\Delta^\varepsilon(t)$. Apply the complete
paired-average estimate only after the finite-count sum and the
pointwise comparison have been formed.
\end{proof}
