\section{Dependencies of the uniform conclusion}\label{app:v100-dependencies}
The new proof separates the following four steps. The first is purely
geometric and has no exact-label spectral input. Uniform short-curve
growth is taken in the geometric family of Demers--Zhang, with the
common arclength identification explicitly allowed there. The
whole-cell distortion and long-cell reduction of Baladi--Demers are
used at potential parameter one. Lemma~\ref{lem:v100-geometric}
records the finite choices required for their uniformity. The finite
transverse testing and final-step multiplicity are proved in
Lemma~\ref{lem:v100-tests}. No uniform mixing rate is invoked.

Second, the exact optical-to-pressure bridge is
Theorem~\ref{thm:v99-pressure-comparison}, based on the global
fixed-itinerary uniqueness and the nonaccumulating stable-Jacobian
comparison in the preceding section. Thus the pressure theorem
controls the actual weighted momentum fiber, not just a partition
count. Third, the existing endpoint-momentum area formula and
critical-volume input are combined by the positive layer cake in
Theorem~\ref{thm:v100-uncut}; all cutoffs reach zero before the
collision limit. The finite-count error is displayed there.

Fourth, the existing target loss certificate and same-segment
comparison are applied before the roof supremum. Their common
coarea versions retain the one-sided incident components, both
physical endpoints and arbitrary Borel target selection. Theorem
\ref{thm:v100-recovery} changes the available uniformity, not these
finite-count identities. The final positive paired trace has not
been bounded. The conditional historical formulas in the archive
remain verbatim; the active uniform capacity authority is
Theorem~\ref{thm:v100-capacity}.
