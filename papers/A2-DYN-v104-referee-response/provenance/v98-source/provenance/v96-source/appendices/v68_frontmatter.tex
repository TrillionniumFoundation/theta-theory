\section{Retained revision-68 opening statements}
\label{app:v69-retained-front}
The abstract and introduction of the preceding revision follow. They
record the scope of its guarded-persistence theorem; the absolute-guard
and finite-scale extensions are proved in the opening sections of the
present article.
\paragraph{Revision-68 abstract.}
\begin{quotation}
We study the joint displacement, collision count and flight time of
actual returns in a triangular periodic Lorentz gas, with the complete
arithmetic record and the original pointwise inversion problem retained.
At a normal roof-critical word, the exact physical and section decisions
have analytic angular germs. We prove that every nonzero germ has a
positive leading radial coefficient, including labels with zero tangent
fraction and arbitrarily high finite order of birth. A relative estimate
retaining the original clearance guard converts this description into
radial persistence. One positive comparison with an outer annulus then
gives an ordered essential-height bound for the original nonlinear
caustic collars on explicit persistence strata. Its constant is
independent of the birth order; no full reversible-trace estimate or
zero-width coefficient for a born sector is used. Non-seam centers with
positive center guard are included. The complete incidence source and
the complementary clearance height remain explicit in the positive
identity governing the unrestricted pointwise arithmetic theorem.
\end{quotation}

\paragraph{The physical record and the pointwise theorem.}
Let $J_{n,R}=(K_{n,R},N_{n,R},T_{n,R})$ be the actual return record
to $Y_R^*$ under $\nu_R^*=c_*^{-1}\nu|_{Y_R^*}$, where
$c_*=91/(10000\pi)$ and $R\in[9/20,47/100]$.
The density $p_{n,R}(k,m,t)$ is with respect to counting measure in
$(k,m)$ and Lebesgue measure in $t$. Occupation counts collisions
$0,\ldots,m-1$, with terminal section membership at $m$ imposed
separately. The complete arithmetic record theorem and coupled path
laws are retained (Theorem~\ref{thm:intro-v58-record}). With
$Z_{m,R}=(k_1,k_2,t-m\bar\tau_R,n-mc_*)/\sqrt m$, the original
unrestricted pointwise target is, for every fixed $M$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:intro-v68-pointwise-target}
 \sup_{R,n,k}\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{t:\,|Z_{m,R}|\le M}
 \left|m^2p_{n,R}(k,m,t)-\mathcal L_{m,R}(k_1,k_2,t,n)\right|
                                     \longrightarrow0.
\end{equation}
The finite arithmetic transition kernel and its zero classes are not
replaced by an unmodulated Gaussian. We address a nonlinear source
which a zero-width tangent trace cannot see.

\paragraph{Nonlinear angular birth.}
Fix $0<\chi\le1/10$ and $0<\varepsilon\le\chi/4$. At a normal
critical word satisfying
$c_j\ge\chi(47/53)^{\min(j,m-j)/4}$, the selected-contact Morse
chart has $F_z=t_z+|y|^2/2$, radius $r_\chi=r_0\chi^3$, and
relative density constant $K_\chi=C\chi^{-2}$. All physical
first-hit and section tests remain exact masks on that continuation.
For $\lambda=(n,k)$ let $B_{z,\lambda}$ be their one-hot output and
put $\ell_{z,\lambda}(r)=(2\pi)^{-1}\int B_{z,\lambda}(r\omega_\phi)\,d\phi$.
Its value at zero is the tangent fraction $\theta_{z,\lambda}$.
The unchanged normalization is
\[
 J_z=\frac1{Rc_*\sqrt{|\det(I-SM_z^{-1}SM_z)|}},
 \qquad S=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1),
\]
where $M_z$ is the canonical derivative of the original half-word,
or its physical-side branch value. There is no primitive-period quotient.

\begin{leadtheorem}[The full angular germ]
\label{thm:intro-v68-germs}
At every fixed such word, each exact-label angular fraction is either
identically zero on a smaller disk or has the form
\[
 \ell_{z,\lambda}(r)=\eta_{z,\lambda}r^{d_{z,\lambda}}
                         (1+O_z(r)),\qquad
 \eta_{z,\lambda}>0,\quad d_{z,\lambda}\in\{0,1,2,\ldots\}.
\]
If $\theta_{z,\lambda}>0$, its order is zero and its leading
coefficient is $\theta_{z,\lambda}$. Every nonzero nonlinear germ
with zero tangent fraction has a positive finite order and is retained.
No uniform bound on that order or on the analytic radius is asserted.
\end{leadtheorem}
\begin{proof}
Every active analytic scalar margin has two simple angular roots.
Their analytic germs have a fixed cyclic order at sufficiently small
positive radius, with coincident germs kept as simultaneous tests.
The exact angular fraction is a finite sum of analytic arc lengths.
The complete argument is Theorem~\ref{thm:v68-angular-germ-classification}.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{A physical persistence cutoff.}
Let $g_z=D_z^\varepsilon(0)$ be the original clearance guard at the
center. For $g_z>0$ and a nonzero angular germ, a recorded analytic
radius and relative Taylor remainder, together with
$\operatorname{Lip}(D_z^\varepsilon)/g_z$, define the finite number
$\mathfrak p_{z,\lambda}$ in \eqref{eq:v68-persistence-number}.
On its stratum $\mathfrak p_{z,\lambda}\le K$, with
$r=\sqrt{2u}<K^{-1}$, the actual density satisfies
\[
 e^{-K_\chi r}(1-Kr)\gamma_{z,\lambda}r^{d_{z,\lambda}}
 \le b_{z,\lambda}^{\varepsilon,\mathrm{clr}}(t_z+u)
 \le e^{K_\chi r}(1+Kr)\gamma_{z,\lambda}r^{d_{z,\lambda}},
 \qquad \gamma_{z,\lambda}=J_zg_z\eta_{z,\lambda}.
\]
At a seam $g_z=1$. Non-seam centers with positive $g_z$ are also
admitted. For positive birth order, $\gamma$ is not a zero-width atom.

\begin{leadtheorem}[Nonlinear collar height, uniformly in the birth order]
\label{thm:intro-v68-height}
Let $b^{\mathrm p,K,H}$ be the original exact-label clearance source
on the persistence stratum, restricted to $0<F_z-t_z<H$. For fixed
$K,\chi,\varepsilon,H$ with $2H<h_\chi=r_\chi^2/2$ and
$2K\sqrt H<1$,
\[
 \limsup_{m\to\infty}\sup_{R,n,k}
          m^2\|b^{\mathrm p,K,H}_{m;n,k,R}\|_\infty
 \le6CH e^{(2+\sqrt2)K_\chi\sqrt H}
                     \frac{1+\sqrt2K\sqrt H}{1-2K\sqrt H}.
\]
The constant is independent of all finite birth orders and of the
number of words, labels and coincident critical values. This ordered
height tends to zero as $H\downarrow0$ at fixed $K,\chi,\varepsilon$.
\end{leadtheorem}
\begin{proof}
The value at any inner roof level is bounded by a fixed multiple of
the original source averaged over the outer annulus $H<u<2H$.
The factor is independent of the birth order because $r^d$ is
nondecreasing for every $d\ge0$. Positivity compares that annular
average with the inherited collar trace at width $2H$. Contributing
critical centers lie in $[t-H,t]$, so its local bound is $3CH$.
Proposition~\ref{prop:v68-annular-comparison} and
Theorem~\ref{thm:v68-all-order-height} give the constants and the
common coarea representative. All scales are fixed before the
collision limit.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{The complete source and the proof route.}
The source in the second theorem includes nonlinear zero-tangent
sectors of every finite order on the stated strata. The exact partition
is nevertheless
$b^{\varepsilon,\mathrm{clr}}=b^{\mathrm p,K,H}
+r_{68}^{\varepsilon,\chi,K,H}$ with $r_{68}\ge0$.
Large persistence numbers, zero-center-guard births, omitted annuli,
failed incidence taper, selected grazing and all noncritical or
uncovered components remain in this complement. Its height and the
complete first-incidence height are still required in
\eqref{eq:v68-canonical-endpoint-budget} to obtain
\eqref{eq:intro-v68-pointwise-target}. Fixed-word analytic exhaustion
is not a collision-limsup tail theorem. In particular the original
physical angular tail $E(K)$ is not declared controlled.

The new proof is Sections~\ref{sec:v68-angular-germs}--\ref{sec:v68-annular-height}.
Its load-bearing inherited inputs are the complete scalar decisions,
selected-contact Morse chart, relative physical density and positive
fixed-width collar law. The earlier zero-width stratum theorem remains
in Sections~\ref{sec:v67-angular-stability}--\ref{sec:v67-angular-tails};
it gives the sharper critical-value statement for its original
order-zero coefficient. Every preceding core proof and appendix is
compiled below, and the preceding abstract and introduction are retained
verbatim in Appendix~\ref{app:v68-retained-front}. The present argument
uses neither density differentiation of a local limit nor a uniform
bound on the analytic birth order.
