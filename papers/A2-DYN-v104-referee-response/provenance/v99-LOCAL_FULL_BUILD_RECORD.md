The qualified v98 source archive is the byte-pinned baseline. The full native article/archive and normal/optimized validation results are recorded by the build output and evidence files; those artifacts, not a guessed page count, determine the local result.

Local archive checks are not remote exact-SHA qualification. Remote runs must match the final author commit on both revision refs. Neither kind of execution certifies the continuum mathematics or the unproved uniform pressure margin and positive short-trace height.
