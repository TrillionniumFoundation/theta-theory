\section{Short excursions of the complete physical trace}
\label{sec:v75-physical-tail}
Apply Theorem~\ref{thm:v75-exact-gauss} with the original weight
$W=1-H^\varepsilon$ and write its complete trace as
$\Gamma^\varepsilon_{m,R,n,k}$. Thus
$b^\varepsilon(t)=\int\Gamma^\varepsilon(t,\theta)\,d\theta$.
This includes incidence, every competing-hit clearance, and the
first-clearance source outside the previously selected disks.
The factor $c_*^{-1}$ already occurs in \eqref{eq:v75-gauss-density}.

For a nonnegative trace $\Gamma$ and $r>0$ define
\begin{align}
 \omega_r^\pm\Gamma(t)
  &=\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{0<h<r}
      \int_0^{2\pi}[\Gamma(t,\theta)-\Gamma(t\pm h,\theta)]_+\,d\theta,
                                      \label{eq:v75-trace-moduli}\\
 \mathfrak D_r\Gamma(t)&=\min\{\omega_r^-\Gamma(t),\omega_r^+\Gamma(t)\}.
                                      \label{eq:v75-paired-trace}
\end{align}
The entire word sum is taken before the positive part and the two
essential suprema. No assertion that a short-excursion functional
commutes with a positive sum is needed.

\begin{lemma}[From a physical trace to its level-component tail]
\label{lem:v75-trace-tail}
If $f(t)=\int\Gamma(t,\theta)\,d\theta$ is nonnegative,
compactly supported and $BV$, then, for almost every $t$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v75-trace-tail}
       T_f(t,r)\le\mathfrak D_r\Gamma(t),\qquad
       S_\delta f(t)\le\frac1\delta\int_0^\delta
                                     \mathfrak D_r\Gamma(t)\,dr.
\end{equation}
Here $T_f(t,r)$ is the height measure of levels $0<u<f(t)$ whose
essential superlevel component through $t$ has length less than $r$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
At almost every such level the essential superlevel set is a finite
union of intervals. If its component $(a,b)$ through $t$ has length
less than $r$, its two endpoints lie in $(t-r,t+r)$. Immediately
beyond each endpoint there is a set of positive measure on which
$f\le u$; otherwise it is not an endpoint of that essential component.
Consequently
\[
 u\ge\max\left\{
  \operatorname*{ess\,inf}_{0<h<r}f(t-h),
  \operatorname*{ess\,inf}_{0<h<r}f(t+h)\right\}.
\]
The measure of all contributing heights is therefore at most the
minimum of the two essential suprema of $[f(t)-f(t\pm h)]_+$.
For each sign, the exact trace formula and positivity bound this
last difference by the integral in \eqref{eq:v75-trace-moduli}.
Fubini fixes the needed versions for almost every $(t,h)$.
This proves the first inequality. The second follows from the
inherited identity $S_\delta f=\delta^{-1}\int_0^\delta T_f(t,r)\,dr$.
All operations involve the complete scalar density, not components
formed separately on individual words.
\end{proof}

Set, with empty central targets contributing zero,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v75-ordered-trace}
 \mathcal D_{\varepsilon,M}(r)=
 \limsup_{m\to\infty}\sup_{R,n,k}m^2
 \operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{t\in\mathcal C_{m,R,n,k}(M)}
                  \mathfrak D_r\Gamma^\varepsilon_{m,R,n,k}(t).
\end{equation}
The trace in \eqref{eq:v75-trace-moduli} uses the full roof line;
it is not cut off at the boundary of the central target.

\begin{proposition}[A physical trace estimate sufficient for the endpoint]
\label{prop:v75-trace-closure}
Suppose the complete Lorentz traces obey, with $A<\infty$, $q\ge0$,
$\beta>0$ and fixed positive $\varepsilon_0,r_0$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v75-trace-premise}
 \mathcal D_{\varepsilon,M}(r)\le A\varepsilon^{-q}r^\beta
 \quad(0<\varepsilon<\varepsilon_0,\ 0<r<r_0).
\end{equation}
Then the original exact-label pointwise theorem follows from the
source-pinned analytic inputs in the introduction. In particular,
for large fixed $B$, $\varepsilon(B)=A_0B^{-1/12}$ and $\delta<r_0$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v75-trace-error}
 \mathcal E_M\le C_MB^{-1/192}
       +\frac{A}{\beta+1}\varepsilon(B)^{-q}\delta^\beta.
\end{equation}
Equation \eqref{eq:v75-trace-premise} is a further dynamical
hypothesis, not a conclusion of the wordwise coarea theorem.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Lemma~\ref{lem:v75-trace-tail} gives
$\mathcal T_{\varepsilon,M}(r)\le\mathcal D_{\varepsilon,M}(r)$.
For clarity the ordered integral step can also be done directly.
The function $\mathfrak D_r\Gamma$ is nondecreasing in $r$.
For every fixed integer $N$,
\[
 S_\delta b\le\frac1N\sum_{j=1}^N
                      \mathfrak D_{j\delta/N}\Gamma.
\]
Fix $B,\varepsilon(B),\delta,N$ and the auxiliary spectral band
before the collision limsup. The finite sum and
\eqref{eq:v75-trace-premise} bound the resulting limsup by
$A\varepsilon(B)^{-q}\delta^\beta N^{-1}\sum_{j=1}^N(j/N)^\beta$.
Only afterwards refine $N$, giving $1/(\beta+1)$.
The inherited positive-remainder and fixed-width estimates give
$\mathcal E_M\le\mathcal S_M(B,\delta)+C_MB^{-1/192}$, proving
\eqref{eq:v75-trace-error}. Choose
$\delta(B)=\varepsilon(B)^{(q+a)/\beta}$ for any $a>0$, before
$m\to\infty$, and finally let $B\to\infty$. The scalar error
vanishes. The inherited common positive path remainder then gives
the path-numerator conclusion; normalization is performed only on
positive arithmetic classes and the return-clock hypotheses are
retained. No growing-band spectral estimate is used.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The remaining weighted dynamical estimate}
Theorems~\ref{thm:v75-convex-envelope} and
\ref{thm:v75-exact-gauss} prove a collision-independent geometric
bound on each word, even at arbitrarily small positive incidence.
They also replace the exponential wordwise level-multiplicity bound
by one inverse per normal direction. They do not bound the sum of
inverse twists with the exact occupation and roof constraints, nor
its paired trace modulus in \eqref{eq:v75-ordered-trace}. In
particular \eqref{eq:v75-explicit-inverse-height} is still exponential
in $m$. It cannot be inserted as an $m^{-2}$ bound in
\eqref{eq:v75-trace-premise}.

The quantitative issue is now on a fully specified physical trace:
control the creation, disappearance and change of its exact-label
inverse-twist weights as the roof varies, after summing all physical
words. A cap change or a physical exit is represented by the
zero-extended term in \eqref{eq:v75-gauss-density}, not suppressed.
The supporting envelope contributes no substitute trajectory or
extra source. No collision-uniform short-excursion decay, full
pointwise raw law, or unconditional same-roof normalization is
claimed without that remaining estimate. The pointwise theorem
\eqref{eq:v75-original-target}, with all its original labels, remains
the governing assertion.
