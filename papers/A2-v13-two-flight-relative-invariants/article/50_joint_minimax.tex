\section{Joint curvature and onset accuracy}
\label{sec:v8-joint}
We specify the class of experiments before giving the lower bound.
Fix an integer $J\ge3$, a sufficiently small common collar $d_*>0$ for
flight numbers at most $J$, and the compact physical neighborhood $K$
of Theorem~\ref{thm:v8-four}, decreased if necessary. A query chooses a
physical time $t\ge0$ and $1\le j\le J$ and returns only
$\one_{\{N_t\ge j+1\}}$ from a fresh independent full-phase preparation.
The policy may use previous returned bits and independent randomization,
and may stop at a stopping time $T$. Its rules are the same for every
unknown parameter. On every possible history, every selected query
satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v8-design}
                         t-jg(\theta)\le d_*
                         \quad\hbox{for every }\theta\in K.
\end{equation}
Below-onset queries are allowed and are deterministic failures. We keep
$J,d_*,K$ fixed as the requested errors decrease. The four windows of
\eqref{eq:v8-windows} belong to this class after the preceding choices
are made small enough. A fixed known neighborhood is thus permitted;
the true gap is not revealed.

Let $\mathcal N_K(\varepsilon,\delta,\eta)$ be the infimum of
$\sup_{\theta\in K}\E_\theta T$ over these policies and Borel
estimators satisfying \eqref{eq:v8-joint-confidence}. All information
retained from each trajectory is its one binary report. In particular
an unrecorded lower collision count is not an extra observation.

\subsection{Testing the unknown onset}
The curvature splitting alternatives of Lemma~\ref{lem:v7-cubic}
have the same gap. To measure the price of timing, we need a second
pair of physical alternatives.

\begin{lemma}[One-sided entropy for a displacement of the gap]
\label{lem:v8-gap-entropy}
In the circular subfamily $e=0$, consider gaps $g_c$ and
$g_c+\Delta$ with $0<\Delta$ small. For any common query satisfying
\eqref{eq:v8-design}, denote its probabilities by $p_0,p_\Delta$.
Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v8-gap-single}
 D_{\rm KL}(\operatorname{Ber}(p_\Delta)\Vert
                         \operatorname{Ber}(p_0))\le C\Delta^2.
\end{equation}
For a stopped policy with $\E_\Delta T<\infty$, its complete
transcript laws satisfy
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v8-gap-stopped}
 D_{\rm KL}(\mathsf P_\Delta\Vert\mathsf P_0)
                                  \le C\Delta^2\E_\Delta T.
\end{equation}
The constant is independent of how near to onset the queries are.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Put $d=t-jg_c$ and $z=j\Delta$. For the circular subfamily the
uniform physical expansion, at these finitely many flight numbers,
can be written
\[
 p_0=d_+^2 H_j(g_c,d_+),\qquad
 p_\Delta=(d-z)_+^2H_j(g_c+\Delta,(d-z)_+).
\]
Here $H_j$ is positive, bounded above and below, and has bounded first
derivatives on the common closed collar. This follows from
Theorem~\ref{thm:g-stability}; the area and all curvatures vary smoothly
with $R=(1-g)/2$. Decrease $d_*$ so all nonzero probabilities are less
than $1/2$.

If $d\le0$, both reports are deterministic failures. If $0<d\le z$,
then $p_\Delta=0$ and $p_0\le Cd^2\le CJd\Delta$. If $d>z$, then
\[
 |(d-z)^2-d^2|\le2dz\le2Jd\Delta,
\]
and the mean-value bound for $H_j$ gives an additional difference at
most $Cd^2(\Delta+z)\le Cd\Delta$, since $d\le d_*$. Thus in both
positive-$d$ cases
\[
               |p_\Delta-p_0|\le Cd\Delta,
               \qquad p_0\ge c d^2.
\]
The Bernoulli entropy inequality used in
\eqref{eq:v7-kl-exposure}, including its continuous extension when
the first probability is zero, yields
\[
 D_{\rm KL}(\operatorname{Ber}(p_\Delta)\Vert
                         \operatorname{Ber}(p_0))
 \le\frac{(p_\Delta-p_0)^2}{p_0(1-p_0)}\le C\Delta^2.
\]
The direction of entropy is important: the higher-gap law has no
successes where the lower-gap law is identically zero, so no unsupported
absolute continuity assertion across the onset is used.

Conditioning on a common finite history and the parameter-independent
random seed selects the same query under the two alternatives. The
chain rule for the first $M$ positions of the transcript, padded with
stop symbols, gives $C\Delta^2\E_\Delta(T\wedge M)$. Relative entropy
on the increasing transcript sigma-fields converges to the entropy of
the full transcript, as in the truncation proof of
\eqref{eq:v7-kl-exposure}. Monotone convergence proves
\eqref{eq:v8-gap-stopped}. A Borel statistic of the transcript adds no
relative entropy.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Joint local binary minimax order]
\label{thm:v8-joint-minimax}
For fixed $J,d_*,K$ as above there are $c,C,\varepsilon_0,\delta_0>0$
such that, for $0<\varepsilon<\varepsilon_0$,
$0<\delta<\delta_0$, and $0<\eta<1/4$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v8-joint-minimax}
 c(\varepsilon^{-6}+\delta^{-2})\log(1/\eta)
 \le\mathcal N_K(\varepsilon,\delta,\eta)
 \le C(\varepsilon^{-6}+\delta^{-2})\log(1/\eta).
\end{equation}
The upper bound uses four fixed physical windows and a deterministic
preparation count. The lower bound permits adaptive queries and random
stopping. It is a statement for this exact physical family and this
bounded-flight, local binary experiment.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The upper bound is Corollary~\ref{cor:v8-fixed-sampling}; for
$\eta<1/4$ its logarithm is comparable to $\log(1/\eta)$.

We first record the confidence version of the testing step. If a test
chooses its first hypothesis on an event $B$ with $P(B)\ge1-\eta$ and
$Q(B)\le\eta$, the log-sum inequality and monotonicity of binary
relative entropy on this rectangle give
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v8-confidence-entropy}
 D_{\rm KL}(P\Vert Q)
 \ge(1-2\eta)\log\frac{1-\eta}{\eta}
 \ge\frac14\log(1/\eta),\qquad 0<\eta<1/4.
\end{equation}
For the last inequality use $1-2\eta\ge1/2$ and
$1-\eta\ge\sqrt\eta$. The rectangle monotonicity follows by
differentiating the binary relative entropy in its two arguments when
the first exceeds the second.

For the curvature lower bound, take the physical tables
$R=1/4$, $\alpha=\zeta=0$, $\beta=\pm s$ inside $K$.
Their matching curvature distance is at least $c_0|s|$, and
Lemma~\ref{lem:v7-cubic} together with the stopped entropy argument
gives
\[
 D_{\rm KL}(\mathsf P_s\Vert\mathsf P_{-s})
       \le Cs^6\E_s\sum_{i\le T}d_{i,+}^2
       \le Cd_*^2s^6\E_s T.
\]
Common below-onset queries contribute zero. Choose
$s=L\varepsilon$ with $L$ fixed large enough that the curvature
separation exceeds $2\varepsilon$. The nearest-alternative test based
on the estimated multiset has both errors at most $\eta$ by
\eqref{eq:v8-joint-confidence} and the triangle inequality for matching
distance. Applying \eqref{eq:v8-confidence-entropy} yields
\[
                     \sup_{\theta\in K}\E_\theta T
                      \ge c\varepsilon^{-6}\log(1/\eta).
\]
Small enough $\varepsilon_0$ keeps both alternatives in $K$.

For the gap lower bound use the circular tables of
Lemma~\ref{lem:v8-gap-entropy} with $\Delta=3\delta$. They remain in
$K$ for small $\delta_0$. Choosing the nearer gap to $\widehat g$
gives a test with both errors at most $\eta$. Apply
\eqref{eq:v8-confidence-entropy} in the higher-gap to lower-gap
direction and then \eqref{eq:v8-gap-stopped}. It follows that
\[
                     \sup_{\theta\in K}\E_\theta T
                      \ge c\delta^{-2}\log(1/\eta).
\]
A policy with infinite worst-case expected cost already satisfies the
lower bound; otherwise both stopped entropy arguments apply. Combining
the two bounds and using $\max\{u,v\}\ge(u+v)/2$ proves the lower
half of \eqref{eq:v8-joint-minimax}.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Curvature loss, finer timing, and shrinking designs]
\label{cor:v8-comparison}
In the fixed-collar class, curvature-only recovery has optimal order
$\varepsilon^{-6}\log(1/\eta)$. For each fixed $m\ge1$, the joint
loss with curvature tolerance $\varepsilon$ and gap tolerance
$\delta=\varepsilon^{3+3/m}$ instead has optimal order
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v8-joint-m}
                    \varepsilon^{-(6+6/m)}\log(1/\eta).
\end{equation}
Separately, any all-history design whose positive offsets are at most
$Hh$ and whose flight numbers are at most $J$, even with the exact gap
supplied, needs worst-case expected cost at least
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v8-shrinking-confidence}
                    c h^{-2}\varepsilon^{-6}\log(1/\eta)
\end{equation}
for curvature accuracy $\varepsilon$ on the fixed-gap splitting
subfamily. Consequently the supplied-bracket acquisition theorem has
matching upper and lower orders at $h\asymp\varepsilon^{3/m}$,
including the confidence logarithm.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The first assertion is the curvature part of the preceding proof and
Corollary~\ref{cor:v8-fixed-sampling}. Substitute the displayed
$\delta$ in \eqref{eq:v8-joint-minimax} to obtain
\eqref{eq:v8-joint-m}. For the shrinking class use instead
$\E_s\sum d_{i,+}^2\le H^2h^2\E_sT$ in the curvature entropy
argument; the alternatives have the same gap, so supplying it has no
effect. This proves \eqref{eq:v8-shrinking-confidence}.

In Theorem~\ref{thm:v6-count-only} the supplied bracket is
$|g-g_0|\le h/4$. Its pilot offsets are in
$[3h/4,(m+5/4)h]$, and all its second-stage offsets are in
$[(L-3J/4)h,(Lm+3J/4)h]$ with $L=J+1$, even on an unsuccessful
pilot history. They fit such a shrinking collar with a fixed $H_m$.
Its expected upper bound and \eqref{eq:v8-shrinking-confidence} agree
after substituting $h\asymp\varepsilon^{3/m}$.
\end{proof}

Thus the fixed-offset comparison improves the curvature-only benchmark,
without invalidating the finer joint timing result. Theorem~\ref{thm:v8-joint-minimax}
also identifies a timing obstruction in the larger fixed-collar class;
its proof does not use the shrinking-design restriction. At the joint
cost in \eqref{eq:v8-joint-m}, the exact four-window procedure may
recover curvature more accurately than requested, because it estimates
all symmetric coordinates to the finer gap tolerance. These statements
make no assertion about the computational cost of evaluating exact
finite-offset probabilities or about a uniform limit $m\to\infty$.
