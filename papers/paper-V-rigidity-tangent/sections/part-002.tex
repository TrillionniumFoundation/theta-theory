 \bigl(\eta(X_T),\zeta(X_T)\bigr).
\]
Higher derivatives are tilted cumulants multiplied by
$\theta^{n-1}$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The exponential Nemytskii map has an absolutely convergent power series on
bounded balls.  Expectation is bounded and strictly positive, and the logarithm
is analytic on its range.  Differentiate the normalized exponential ratio.
\end{proof}

\section{Tangent-kernel cocycle}\label{sec:cocycle}
For $s<t$, let $P_{s,t}$ be the physical diffusion kernel and define
\[
 K_{s,t}^{\phi}(x,dy)
 =\frac{e^{\theta\phi(y)}P_{s,t}(x,dy)}
 {P_{s,t}e^{\theta\phi}(x)}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Dynamic tangent cocycle]\label{thm:cocycle}
The nonlinear semigroup is time consistent and
\[
 K_{r,t}^{\phi}
 =K_{r,s}^{\cE_{s,t}[\phi]}K_{s,t}^{\phi}.
\]
Equivalently, the Fr\'echet derivatives satisfy the Banach chain-rule cocycle.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the Markov identity
$P_{r,t}e^{\theta\phi}=P_{r,s}
 e^{\theta\cE_{s,t}[\phi]}$.  The Radon--Nikodym factors multiply and the
intermediate normalization cancels.
\end{proof}

\section{Covariance-curvature characterization}\label{sec:characterization}
\begin{theorem}[Static characterization]\label{thm:characterization}
Let $\mathfrak F:L^\infty\to\R$ be twice Fr\'echet differentiable,
monotone, cash additive, and normalized by $\mathfrak F(0)=0$.  Suppose for
every $\phi$ there is a probability $Q_\phi$ such that
\[
 D\mathfrak F(\phi)f=Q_\phi(f)
\]
and
\[
 D^2\mathfrak F(\phi)(f,g)
 =\theta\{Q_\phi(fg)-Q_\phi(f)Q_\phi(g)\}
\]
with one fixed $\theta\ne0$.  Then
\[
 \mathfrak F(\phi)=\frac1\theta\log Q_0(e^{\theta\phi}),
\]
and $Q_\phi$ is the normalized exponential tilt of $Q_0$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Set $Q_t=Q_{t\phi}$.  Differentiation gives
\[
 \frac d{dt}Q_t(f)
 =\theta\{Q_t(f\phi)-Q_t(f)Q_t(\phi)\}.
\]
The unique solution is the exponential tilt of $Q_0$.  Integrate
$d\mathfrak F(t\phi)/dt=Q_t(\phi)$ from zero to one.
\end{proof}

\section{Microscopic Ruelle tangent laws}\label{sec:micro}
Let $\Pp_\varepsilon$ be the canonical path law of the microscopic recursion
of Paper III and $X_T^\varepsilon$ its terminal slow state.  Define
\[
 \frac{d\Qp_\varepsilon^\phi}{d\Pp_\varepsilon}
 =\frac{e^{\theta\phi(X_T^\varepsilon)}}
 {\E_{\Pp_\varepsilon}e^{\theta\phi(X_T^\varepsilon)}}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Microscopic tangent convergence]\label{thm:micro-convergence}
For bounded continuous $\phi,\eta,\zeta$, the tangent path measures converge
weakly to $\Qp^\phi$, and the first two terminal derivatives converge to the
expectation and covariance operators in \cref{thm:analytic}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Paper III proves weak path convergence under the fixed canonical preparation.
The exponential Radon--Nikodym factors are uniformly bounded above and below
for bounded $\phi$.  Numerators and denominators therefore pass to the limit,
as do products $\eta\zeta$.
\end{proof}

\section{Girsanov and BSDE representations}\label{sec:girsanov}
Let
\[
 u(t,x)=\frac1\theta\log\E_{t,x}e^{\theta\phi(X_T)}
\]
for smooth bounded data.  It solves
\[
 -u_t=bu_x+\frac D2u_{xx}+\frac{\theta D}2(u_x)^2.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Genuine tangent-law density]\label{thm:girsanov}
The density process
\[
 M_s=\frac{\E[e^{\theta\phi(X_T)}\mid\mathcal F_s]}
 {\E e^{\theta\phi(X_T)}}
\]
is the uniformly integrable stochastic exponential
\[
 M_s=\mathcal E\left(
 \int_t^s\theta\sqrt D\,u_x(r,X_r)\,dW_r
 \right).
\]
Under $\Qp^\phi$, the process
\[
 W_s^{\Qp}=W_s-\int_t^s\theta\sqrt D\,u_x(r,X_r)\,dr
\]
is Brownian and
\[
 dX_s=(b+\theta D u_x)(s,X_s)\,ds+\sqrt D\,dW_s^{\Qp}.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply It\^o's formula to $e^{\theta u(s,X_s)}$.  The PDE cancels the
drift, while the conditional-expectation representation makes the resulting
positive martingale uniformly integrable.  For bounded terminal data, the
standard BMO localization argument validates the Girsanov change of measure.
\end{proof}
