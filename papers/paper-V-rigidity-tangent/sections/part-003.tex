$\Qp^\phi$ and satisfies the corresponding linear BSDE.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Apply It\^o's formula to $u$ and then differentiate the terminal-data PDE.
Use \cref{thm:girsanov} to remove the tangent drift.  Standard quadratic BSDE
well-posedness is given in \cite{Kobylanski2000,PardouxPeng1990}.
\end{proof}

\section{Controlled values are not fixed-law entropic maps}\label{sec:controlled}
For a risk-sensitive saddle value, Paper IV proves
\[
 D^2V
 =\theta\Cov_{Q_\phi^*}
 -G_{\phi z}G_{zz}^{-1}G_{z\phi}.
\]
Thus the covariance-curvature characterization applies to the fixed-law and
fixed-policy branches only.  The controlled branch is typed by its additional
optimizer-response tensor.

\section{Main theorem and scope}\label{sec:main}
\begin{theorem}[Mechanical generation plus axiomatic uniqueness]\label{thm:main}
The periodic Sinai program separates two logically different statements:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item Liouville current conditioning and deterministic homogenization generate
$\theta_R(j)$, $D_{R,j}$, and the theta-semigroup;
\item cash-additive relevance and tangent covariance curvature identify the
entropic form and its tangent probability bundle.
\end{enumerate}
The resulting fixed-law semigroup has microscopic tangent convergence,
Girsanov, and quadratic/tangent BSDE representations.  Controlled values carry
the separate saddle-response correction.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Combine Papers I--IV with
\cref{thm:rigidity,cor:identification,thm:analytic,thm:cocycle,thm:characterization,thm:micro-convergence,thm:girsanov,cor:bsde}.
\end{proof}

\section*{Truth boundary}
The representations begin after the deterministic Sinai-to-diffusion limit and
do not serve as inputs to it.  The fixed-law tangent theorem is not silently
extended to optimized values.  External review of the functional-analytic and
BSDE regularity windows remains pending.
