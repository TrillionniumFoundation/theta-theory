\section{The raw correction after all decision layers have been removed}
\label{sec:v48-physical-remainder}
The new decomposition puts all physical defects in one source before
ordering them. This is necessary: the old first-bad-middle-decision
source can contain a later bad physical margin. We do not declare that
old source pointwise small merely by renaming it.

At $\eta=\varepsilon$, define the physical remainder by
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v48-physical-remainder}
 b^{\varepsilon,w}_{n,k,m,R}(t)\,dt
 =(T_{n,R})_*\bigl(\one_{\Pi_{n,k,m,R}}w
                         (1-H_{m,R}^{\varepsilon})\nu_R^*\bigr).
\end{equation}
For the unweighted source the exact positive identity is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v48-full-density-split}
 p_{n,R}(k,m,\cdot)=f^{\varepsilon}_{n,k,m,R}
                +d^{\varepsilon,\varepsilon}_{n,k,m,R}
                +b^{\varepsilon}_{n,k,m,R}.
\end{equation}
Here $f^\varepsilon$ is precisely the old smoothly protected source,
not a new approximation to it.

Order only the incidence and clearance factors of $H^\varepsilon$
by depth and the tie convention of Section~\ref{sec:v47-first-bad-margins}.
Partition $\{H^\varepsilon<1\}$ by the first factor strictly below
one, including its transition region. Group these disjoint sets by
physical type to obtain $b^{\varepsilon,\mathrm{inc}}$ and
$b^{\varepsilon,\mathrm{clr}}$. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v48-two-physical-types}
 b^\varepsilon=b^{\varepsilon,\mathrm{inc}}
                     +b^{\varepsilon,\mathrm{clr}},\qquad
 \sum_{n,k}\|b^{\varepsilon,\mathrm{inc}}\|_1\le C\varepsilon^2,
 \quad
 \sum_{n,k}\|b^{\varepsilon,\mathrm{clr}}\|_1\le C\varepsilon.
\end{equation}
The proof is the invariant one-flight neighborhood sum in
Proposition~\ref{prop:v47-first-defect-masses}, with no section factors
in the first-defect order. It gives only mass bounds for these two
sources. All identities and domination statements retain the exact
labels and the original source normalization.

\begin{theorem}[Only physical-word defects remain in the correction]
\label{thm:v48-two-source-reduction}
Choose $\varepsilon(B)=A B^{-1/12}$ with $A$ sufficiently large as in
Theorem~\ref{thm:v46-protected-correction}. For all sufficiently large
fixed $B$,
\begin{align}
 \limsup_{m\to\infty}\sup_{R,n,k}m^2
 \bigl\|\mathscr D_{B,n,R}(k,m,\cdot)
      -[b^{\varepsilon(B)}_{n,k,m,R}
                     -K_B*b^{\varepsilon(B)}_{n,k,m,R}]\bigr\|_\infty
                                  \le C B^{-1/192}.
                                      \label{eq:v48-paid-all-decisions}
\end{align}
Thus, on the central target sets of
Theorem~\ref{thm:v43-arithmetic-raw-criterion}, the full uniform
arithmetic pointwise law is equivalent to
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v48-physical-residual-criterion}
 \lim_{B\to\infty}\limsup_{m\to\infty}\sup_{R,n,k}
 m^2\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{\text{central }t}
 \bigl|b^{\varepsilon(B)}_{n,k,m,R}(t)
              -(K_B*b^{\varepsilon(B)}_{n,k,m,R})(t)\bigr|=0.
\end{equation}
The main term is still $\mathcal L_{m,R}$ uniformly through arithmetic
transitions, and $c\mathfrak a_R(k,n,m)g_{\Omega_R}$ at a fixed radius.
No zero-residue assertion is added.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Apply $I-K_B*$ to \eqref{eq:v48-full-density-split}.
The old protected contribution has normalized limsup $CB^{-1/2}$
when $B\ge C_2\varepsilon(B)^{-12}$; this follows by choosing
$A^{12}\ge C_2$. Corollary~\ref{cor:v48-decision-all-band} bounds
the full decision contribution by $C\varepsilon(B)^{1/16}
=CA^{1/16}B^{-1/192}$. Here the protection and the auxiliary spectral
band used to prove the decision theorem are fixed for each collision
limsup. Their possibly large constants do not enter the leading
limsup bound. This proves \eqref{eq:v48-paid-all-decisions}.
The triangle inequality and the unchanged arithmetic reconstruction
criterion prove the equivalence. The incidence and clearance
corrections in \eqref{eq:v48-two-physical-types} are summed with their
signs before taking the absolute value.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Exact scope.}
Theorem~\ref{thm:v48-all-decision-smallness} is a pointwise theorem
for every decision depth on a physically protected source, including
arbitrary bounded measurable insertions. It is stronger in source
scope than a fixed-depth endpoint theorem; the exponent $1/16$ is
not claimed optimal. In the old first-defect decomposition, a middle
decision followed by a bad physical margin is now assigned to
$b^\varepsilon$, not silently discarded. No uncontrolled depth tail
is left in $d^{\eta,\varepsilon}$.

The two physical margins are different. Crossing a grazing or a
competing-hit boundary can change the physical word and sometimes
the collision count. None of the flows above crosses such a boundary.
Equation~\eqref{eq:v48-physical-residual-criterion} is therefore a
remaining requirement, not a theorem of vanishing. The small masses
in \eqref{eq:v48-two-physical-types} do not control this signed
pointwise correction. In particular neither the full raw density
law nor the pointwise roof-conditioned bridge is asserted here.
The finite-count exponential height bound of
Theorem~\ref{thm:v44-complete-density-bound} retains exactly that
finite-count scope; it is not used as a central-scale estimate.

For weighted raw inversion, the new decision bound needs only
$|w|\le M$. The protected weighted estimate still needs the
endpoint-derivative budget in
Theorem~\ref{thm:v46-protected-correction}. A common weighted theorem
requires the physical remainder for that same insertion and roof
value. Source domination alone gives no Gaussian amplitude for an
arbitrary path selector and no missing denominator.
