\begin{abstract}
We study displacement, collision count and flight time of actual returns
in a triangular periodic Lorentz gas, retaining the complete arithmetic
record law and the original pointwise inversion problem. Count-uniform
selected-contact coordinates and exact nonlinear physical masks give a
positive finite-width collar trace at normal roof-critical words.
We pass from this trace to zero-width physical coefficients on explicit
finite-jet strata. The decisive estimate measures angular error relative
to the physical coefficient, so that it can be absorbed before taking the
collision limit; no concentration of the larger reversible trace is used.
The resulting critical-value bound has a constant independent of the
stratum cutoff and gives an ordered essential-height estimate for the
corresponding original-source caustic collars. An explicit weighted tail
of the angular condition number controls the extension to all seam charts.
That tail and the complete incidence and positive complementary clearance
heights remain separate requirements for the unrestricted pointwise
arithmetic law. Every collision, exact return label and arithmetic zero
class is retained.
\end{abstract}

\paragraph{The record and the pointwise problem.}
For the original section $Y_R^*$ write
$J_{n,R}=(K_{n,R},N_{n,R},T_{n,R})$, under
$\nu_R^*=c_*^{-1}\nu|_{Y_R^*}$, where
$c_*=91/(10000\pi)$ and $R\in[9/20,47/100]$.
The density $p_{n,R}(k,m,t)$ uses counting measure in $(k,m)$ and
Lebesgue measure in $t$. Occupation counts collisions $0,\ldots,m-1$;
terminal section membership at $m$ is a separate condition.
The complete arithmetic record theorem and coupled path laws retain
their scope (Theorem~\ref{thm:intro-v58-record}). Put
$Z_{m,R}=(k_1,k_2,t-m\bar\tau_R,n-mc_*)/\sqrt m$.
For each fixed $M$ the stronger target remains
\[
 \sup_{R,n,k}\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{t:\,|Z_{m,R}|\le M}
 \left|m^2p_{n,R}(k,m,t)-\mathcal L_{m,R}(k_1,k_2,t,n)\right|
                         \longrightarrow0.
\]
The finite arithmetic transition kernel $\mathcal L_{m,R}$ is not
replaced by an unmodulated Gaussian. We develop a new density-height
estimate on its original positive boundary source.

\paragraph{Physical coefficients at a normal critical word.}
Fix $\chi,\varepsilon$ with $0<\chi\le1/10$ and
$0<\varepsilon\le\chi/4$. The selected incidence cosines of a word
satisfy $c_j\ge\chi(47/53)^{\min(j,m-j)/4}$.
Its Morse chart has $F_z=t_z+|y|^2/2$, radius $r_\chi=r_0\chi^3$
and relative density distortion $K_\chi=C\chi^{-2}$. Put
$h_\chi=r_\chi^2/2$.
The chart is a coordinate continuation, not an assertion of physical
continuation across a first-hit seam. Every actual decision is kept
as an exact mask. With $S=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$ the coefficient is
\[
 J_z=\frac{1}{Rc_*\sqrt{|\det(I-SM_z^{-1}SM_z)|}}.
\]
Here $M_z$ is the canonical derivative of the original half-word, or
its physical-side branch value. There is no primitive-period quotient.
At a clearance seam the zero-width physical coefficient is
$\beta_{z,n,k}=J_z\theta_{z,n,k}$, where $\theta$ is the angular
fraction assigned by the original exact-label rule. In particular this
coefficient need not equal $J_z$.

\paragraph{The geometric cutoff.}
In a sufficiently small saturated disk, take every scalar physical and
section margin $f$, with $b_f=f(0)$, $A_f=|Df(0)|$ and
$L_f=\tfrac12\|D^2f\|_\infty$. Let $\rho_z$ be the explicit
inactive-sign radius in \eqref{eq:v67-inactive-radius}, including the
radius on which this complete Boolean description is valid. For
$\theta_{z,n,k}>0$ put
\[
 \mathfrak a_{z,n,k}
 =\max\left\{\rho_z^{-1},\frac{1}{\theta_{z,n,k}}
                   \sum_{b_f=0}\frac{L_f}{A_f}\right\}.
\]
At every fixed word this is finite; no all-count bound is assumed.
The finite-jet stratum consists of pairs $(z,(n,k))$ with
$\mathfrak a_{z,n,k}\le K$. It can contain simultaneous seams and
coincident or arbitrarily close tangent directions.

\begin{leadtheorem}[Zero-width physical trace and caustic height]
\label{thm:intro-v67-trace}
Let $\mathcal B^K_{m;n,k,R}$ sum the coefficients $\beta_{z,n,k}$
at $t_z$ on the finite-jet stratum, and let
$\mathcal Q^K_{m,s;n,k,R}$ be the corresponding averages of the actual
physical source over $0<F_z-t_z<s$. For fixed $K,\chi,\varepsilon$
and $K\sqrt{2s}<1$, $0<s<h_\chi$, one has, for every $h>0$,
\[
 \mathcal B^K_m(I)\le
       \frac{e^{K_\chi\sqrt{2s}}}{1-K\sqrt{2s}}\mathcal Q^K_{m,s}(I),
 \qquad
 \limsup_m\sup_{R,n,k,t}m^2\mathcal B^K_m([t-h,t+h])\le2Ch.
\]
The constant $C$ is independent of $K$. If $b^{K,H}$ is the same
original source restricted to $0<F_z-t_z<H$, then
\[
 \limsup_m\sup_{R,n,k}m^2\|b^{K,H}_{m;n,k,R}\|_\infty
 \le CH e^{K_\chi\sqrt{2H}}(1+K\sqrt{2H}),
 \quad K\sqrt{2H}<1,\quad H<h_\chi.
\]
All widths are fixed before the collision limit. No word-count factor,
label averaging or full reversible-trace bound occurs.
\end{leadtheorem}
\begin{proof}
A homogeneous sign estimate gives a relative angular error at most
$K\sqrt{2u}$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:v67-relative-angular}). Positivity
then gives Theorem~\ref{thm:v67-stratified-trace} from the inherited
two-endpoint collar law. Corollary~\ref{cor:v67-collar-height}
converts the critical-value bound to essential height using the same
nonlinear profile, not differentiation of an interval local limit.
\end{proof}

\begin{leadtheorem}[The positive cost of exhausting the strata]
\label{thm:intro-v67-tail}
Let $E(K)$ be the collision-limsup unit-window height of the atomic
physical coefficients with $\mathfrak a_{z,n,k}>K$, as in
\eqref{eq:v67-tail-functional}. The complete seam-chart angular loss
of \eqref{eq:v66-angular-loss}, for fixed $K,\chi,\varepsilon$ and
$0<s<h_\chi$ with $K\sqrt{2s}<1$, satisfies
\[
 \limsup_m\sup_{R,n,k,t}m^2\mathcal D_{m,s}([t-1,t+1])
                           \le\tfrac43 CK\sqrt{2s}+E(K).
\]
Thus $E(K)\to0$ is sufficient for the full seam-chart angular-loss
limit and the corresponding zero-width concentration theorem.
The bound on $E(K)$ is an additional physical input, not a conclusion
of uniform selected-contact jets.
\end{leadtheorem}
\begin{proof}
This is Proposition~\ref{prop:v67-angular-exhaustion}: the lost angular
fraction in a bad stratum is at most its entire physical tangent
fraction. The good part is estimated before taking either outer limit.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{The complete source and the proof route.}
The three new sections below prove these statements from the primitive
margin budgets, the exact nonlinear profile and the positive two-strip
comparison. The density $b^{K,H}$ is a restriction, and the complete
clearance source is $b^{K,H}+r^{\varepsilon,\chi,K,H}$ with $r\ge0$.
The latter retains selected grazing, failed incidence taper, noncritical
rank components, non-seam centers, zero-angular-fraction labels, bad
strata and omitted annuli. Neither its essential height nor the complete
first-incidence height is inferred from the new theorem. The positive
raw-error identity and the unrestricted same-roof consequences retain
exactly their prior logical requirements.

The proof spine is Sections~\ref{sec:v67-angular-stability}--\ref{sec:v67-angular-tails},
with the geometric and dynamical inputs in
Sections~\ref{sec:v66-contact-continuation}--\ref{sec:v66-collar-trace}.
The comparison with local-limit literature is made at the end of
Section~\ref{sec:v67-angular-tails}. All preceding core proofs and
appendices remain compiled. The preceding leading statements are
retained in Appendix~\ref{app:v67-retained-front}; historical wording
there describes the earlier stage of the argument.

\paragraph{The retained return-window and bridge route.}
The new local constraint is proved in
Sections~\ref{sec:v37-occupation-local} and~\ref{sec:v37-return-window}.
It uses the exact section geometry, the inherited compensated
covariance and its nondegeneracy, and the compact collision spectrum
of the preceding part. The return index is resolved on its diffusive scale;
single-index inversion and pointwise roof inversion remain distinct
from that theorem. Sections~\ref{sec:v38-four-dimensional-concentration}
and~\ref{sec:v38-return-resolution} then replace the diffusive window
by any diverging integer block, prove a single-index upper bound, and
locate the exact inverse on a fixed occupation torus. The new
Sections~\ref{sec:v40-occupation-powers}--\ref{sec:v40-fixed-count-local}
then give a full fixed-band spectral reduction and evaluate the
fixed-radius interval law with its arithmetic coefficient. Sections~
\ref{sec:v41-transition}--\ref{sec:v41-residue-filters} then evaluate
the uniform transition kernel and prove the exact-event return bridge. The
complete inherited arithmetic, raw edge and return-frequency analysis
continues on the same physical record.
