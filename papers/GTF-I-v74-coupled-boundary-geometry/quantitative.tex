\begin{abstract}
We determine the finite-use description geometry of the entire unbiased
binary qubit measurement ball, with both visibility and direction encoded.
For Bloch vectors $x,y$, the reference-assisted adaptive distance is
comparable, with absolute constants, to
$\min\{1,\sqrt{N/(1-s^2+N^{-1})}\,|x-y|\}$, where
$s=\min(|x|,|y|)$. A binomial support-cutoff estimate and a
fixed-visibility entangled-block bound give the comparison at every
horizon, including erasure and projective effects. For a compact
$k$-regular identifiable subfamily $X$, its small-error covering number
is comparable to
$\delta^{-k}\int_X[N/(1-|x|^2+N^{-1})]^{k/2}\,d\mu(x)$.
The lower estimate allows arbitrary legal memoryless centres.
The distribution of support deficit then determines interior,
logarithmically enhanced, and boundary-dominated resolution laws.
In particular, jointly describing the measurement disk and ball costs
respectively $\log_2N+\log_2\log(N+2)+2\log_2(1/\delta)+O(1)$ and
$2\log_2N+3\log_2(1/\delta)+O(1)$ bits.
Rational stereographic layers attain these orders by a legal exact code,
without computing an irrational target normalization. The code charges
one index for the visibility and direction together. We retain the
public-visibility circle, observable projective-readout, singular
preparation and uniformly seizable covering theorems with their distinct
hypotheses. Classical simulation and noisy coherent amplification are
antecedents; the conclusions concern finite-use geometry and supplied
descriptions, not unknown-device learning or physical classical simulation.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textit{Operational interfaces.}
Description compilation returns a mathematical quantum instrument;
numerical streaming processes fully specified matrix data. Neither is a
physical classical device acting on an unknown quantum input, and
neither is merely a transcript-only simulation task. The new joint code charges visibility as a target coordinate;
only dimension, horizon and requested accuracy are public. The retained
fixed-visibility theorem has its separate public-visibility convention.
Payload means the reusable index capacity, not the JSON envelope,
encoder workspace, expanded matrices or processor hardware.
All joint-state errors
are unhalved trace norms; transcript total variation is half the classical
block trace norm.
