# Common actual platform - `TL1-LIO-v1`

The lattice is generated by

\[
e_1=(1,0),\qquad e_2=(1/2,\sqrt3/2).
\]

One circular scatterer of radius

\[
R\in[9/20,47/100]\subset(\sqrt3/4,1/2)
\]

is placed in every lattice cell.  The quotient is a compact periodic
finite-horizon Sinai table with unit-speed free flight and equal-angle
specular reflection.

In angular boundary coordinates the natural collision law is

\[
d\mu=(4\pi)^{-1}\cos\varphi\,d\alpha\,d\varphi.
\]

The mechanical current is the first lattice coordinate of the actual cell
displacement cocycle.  A prepared current `j` determines

\[
\theta_R(j)=(\Lambda_R')^{-1}(j)=I_R'(j).
\]

The physical ballistic velocity and fluctuation coefficient are obtained from
the joint current/roof pressure.  The clock-centred current is

\[
G=J-v\tau,\qquad v=j/\bar\tau,
\]

and

\[
D_{R,j}=\frac{\operatorname{AVar}_{\mu_{R,\theta}}(G)}{\bar\tau}.
\]

The fixed-law physical theta-generator is

\[
\mathfrak G f=c+bf'+\frac{D_{R,j}}2f''
 +\frac{\theta_R(j)D_{R,j}}2(f')^2.
\]
