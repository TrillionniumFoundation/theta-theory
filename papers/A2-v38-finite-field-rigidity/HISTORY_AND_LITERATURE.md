## Primary-source positioning

Lawler and Limic, _Random Walk: A Modern Introduction_ (2010), supplies the
classical stopping and Green-operator background. Its author-hosted text was
consulted at https://www.math.uchicago.edu/~lawler/srwbook.pdf . The stopped
martingale itself is not a new claim of this paper.

Altman, _Constrained Markov Decision Processes_ (1999), supplies the occupation
measure and linear-programming framework for total-cost controlled chains.
An author-hosted text was consulted at
https://www-sop.inria.fr/members/Eitan.Altman/TEMP/h.pdf . The manuscript proves
the finite stopping-measure realization used here explicitly, rather than
requiring a general control theorem as a hidden hypothesis.

Schneider, _Convex Bodies: The Brunn--Minkowski Theory_, second edition (2014),
is the retained source for support addition, Steiner centering and Cauchy's
perimeter formula. The normalization needed for the isotropic collision law
is derived in full in the manuscript. No novelty is claimed for those convex
identities or for Minkowski envelope subtraction itself.

The retained comparisons to active learning, informative abstention, noisy
support estimation, query precision, random caps and blind morphological
reconstruction remain in `05_comparison.tex` and in the unchanged v37
literature audit. The present additions concern the specified active pooled
collision response, not passive billiard trajectories, marked lengths or
spectra. This focused source check is not an exhaustive priority search.
