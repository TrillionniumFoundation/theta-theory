\begin{abstract}
For positive sparse monomial experiments at a fixed horizon, we determine
optimal finite-memory prediction uniformly through all additive
collisions of the exponents. The error is given by maximal Vandermonde
products of the future exponents, truncated by the dimension acquired
by the past. The same profile governs every integer memory budget,
both under one unconditional acquisition law and over all admitted
histories, and is attained by one causal finite-state filter.
The proof establishes simultaneous acquisition of complete confluent
flags, a dimension-truncated cover of the whole posterior image, and
updates in raw moments with no reciprocal collision gap. A
four-cell experiment exhibits six-, eight- and nine-dimensional
regimes at intersecting collision loci, with two separated memory
crossovers along paths of arbitrary finite contact order.
\end{abstract}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:resolution-main}]
Theorem~\ref{thm:intrinsic-checkpoint} proves both checkpoint bounds
with reachable representatives for every integer budget.
Theorem~\ref{thm:intrinsic-streaming} proves their simultaneous causal
realization and the converse under the same acquisition law. There
are finitely many checkpoints, so their constants have a finite
maximum and a positive minimum. These constants are independent of
the calibration and the memory budget by the two theorems.
