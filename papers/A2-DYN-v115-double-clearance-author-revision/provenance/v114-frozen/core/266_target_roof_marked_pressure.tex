\section{A complete marked pressure bound at the target roof}
\label{sec:v111-pressure}
We now transfer the retained geometric pressure estimate to the
roof fibers in \eqref{eq:v111-roof-sum}. The previous momentum-fiber
bound alone would not justify this transfer: different words at the
same roof can have different endpoint momentum magnitudes.
The global injectivity just proved supplies the missing multiplicity
statement.

Let $j(x)=J^sT_R(x)$ on the full-area set $M_R'$ regular in both
infinite time directions, and let $j_l(x)=\prod_{v=0}^{l-1}j(T_R^vx)$,
with $j_0=1$. The lifted physical one-flight alphabet has at most
$D$ members, uniformly in $R$. Define
\[
 P_l(R)=\sum_{w\in\mathcal W_l(R)}
                      \sup_{x\in[w]_R\cap M_R'}j_l(x),\qquad P_0=1.
\]
\begin{inputstatement}[Uniform physical pressure and stable optical comparison]
\label{input:v112-geometric-pressure}
On the circular family $R\in[9/20,47/100]$, use the original
finite-horizon flight bounds, positive curvature bounds and the
original endpoint section cap. The stable Jacobian $j$ is computed
with Euclidean arclength along the true stable line in collision
coordinates $(s,\varphi)$, where $s=R\alpha$ and $p=\sin\varphi$.
It is restricted to $M_R'$, the full-area set regular in both
infinite directions. Its cocycle uses this same convention on every
block, including the empty block. The endpoint weight $\omega$ is
still the derivative in the original $(\alpha,p)$ coordinates of
\eqref{eq:v109-density}; all coordinate factors are included in the
uniform optical constant.

For the realizable lifted physical words just defined, the retained
geometric theorems state
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v111-pressure-input}
 \sup_{R,l}P_l(R)\le Q_*,\qquad
 \omega(x)\le C_0c_m(x)j_m(x),\qquad j(x)/c(x)\le C_1.
\end{equation}
All three constants are independent of $R,l$ and the original exact
labels. These statements concern positive, unconditioned physical
word sums; they are not a twisted or exact-label pressure theorem.
\end{inputstatement}
We take $Q_*\ge1$ and $C_0,C_1>0$. These are the uniform geometric
pressure theorem in historical module 241 and the stable optical
comparison in module 238, with the physical-word domination in
module 239. Their complete original sources are pinned in the proof
ledger and copied in the provenance directory. They involve no
occupation, displacement or roof test. Such tests are dropped only
on the upper side of the estimates below. No new pressure estimate
for a twisted or point-conditioned operator is assumed.

\begin{lemma}[Removal of several marked stable factors]
\label{lem:v111-remove}
For $S=\{i_1<\cdots<i_s\}\subset\{1,\ldots,m-1\}$, at every
regular trajectory in $M_R'$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v111-remove}
 \frac{\omega(x)}{\prod_{i\in S}c_i(x)}
   \le C_0C_1^s\prod_{v\in\{0,\ldots,m-1\}\setminus S}j(T_R^vx).
\end{equation}
Consequently, if at most one point $x_w$ is chosen from each
physical word domain, then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v111-selected-word-sum}
 \sum_w\frac{\omega(x_w)}{\prod_{i\in S}c_i(x_w)}
                 \le C_0C_1^s D^s Q_*^{s+1}.
\end{equation}
The choice of point may depend on the word and need not have the
same momentum target for different words.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use $c_m\le1$ in \eqref{eq:v111-pressure-input}, factor $j_m$ into
its individual steps, and apply $j(T_R^ix)/c_i(x)\le C_1$ at each
marked index. This gives \eqref{eq:v111-remove}.

Delete the $s$ marked steps of a word. The remaining blocks have
lengths
\[
 i_1,\quad i_2-i_1-1,\ \ldots,\ i_s-i_{s-1}-1,
                                      \quad m-i_s-1.
\]
For clarity, steps are numbered $0,\ldots,m-1$, whereas the
marks belong to $1,\ldots,m-1$. Their indexing is
\[
 \underbrace{0,\ldots,i_1-1}_{i_1}\ \big|\ i_1\ \big|\
 \underbrace{i_1+1,\ldots,i_2-1}_{i_2-i_1-1}\ \big|\ i_2
 \ \big|\ \cdots\ \big|\ i_s\ \big|\
 \underbrace{i_s+1,\ldots,m-1}_{m-i_s-1}.
\]
For example, $m=7$ and $S=\{1,2,6\}$ leave blocks of lengths
$(1,0,3,0)$ and delete three physical symbols. Thus adjacent marks
and a final mark are included, and every empty block contributes
$P_0=1$. Some lengths can be zero. The blocks, translated to the initial
fundamental scatterer, together with the $s$ deleted physical step
symbols determine the full lifted word uniquely. There are at most
$D^s$ choices for those symbols. Bound the remaining cocycle on each
block by its own supremum and sum over all block words. Positivity
permits adding combinations which do not produce a realizable full
word. The resulting upper bound is $C_0C_1^sD^s$ times the product
of the $s+1$ corresponding $P_l(R)$'s, at most
$C_0C_1^sD^sQ_*^{s+1}$. For $S=\varnothing$ it is simply
$C_0P_m(R)$. This argument counts physical step symbols, never the
infinitely many homogeneous strips at a removed incidence.
\end{proof}

Set
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v111-constants}
       C_\star=\frac{C_0Q_*}{2c_*\lambda_\circ},\qquad
       K_\star=C_1DQ_*.
\end{equation}
Neither constant depends on the collision count, radius, exact
labels, roof or marked indices.

\begin{theorem}[Target-roof multiple-incidence insertion]
\label{thm:v111-roof-pressure}
For every finite $m$, every original exact event and every
$S\subset\{1,\ldots,m-1\}$, the nonnegative measure
\[
 F_\#\left(\prod_{i\in S}c_i^{-1}\one_\Pi\nu_R^*\right)
\]
has a roof density $d_S$ satisfying
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v111-pointwise-marks}
                    \|d_S\|_{L^\infty(dt)}\le C_\star K_\star^{|S|}.
\end{equation}
The bound holds on the whole roof line. For arbitrary numbers
$a_1,\ldots,a_{m-1}\ge0$, the density $d_{\boldsymbol a}$ with
weight $\prod_{i=1}^{m-1}(1+a_i/c_i)$ satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v111-product-insertion}
 \|d_{\boldsymbol a}\|_\infty
 \le C_\star\prod_{i=1}^{m-1}(1+K_\star a_i)
 \le C_\star\exp\left(K_\star\sum_{i=1}^{m-1}a_i\right).
\end{equation}
In particular the complete inherited linear incidence weight obeys
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v111-linear}
 \left\|\frac{dF_\#(I_m\one_\Pi\nu_R^*)}{dt}\right\|_\infty
 \le C_\star\left(1+K_\star\frac{2q_0}{1-q_0}\right).
\end{equation}
These are finite-count unscaled height bounds, not $m^{-2}$ local
limit estimates.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At an almost-everywhere target $(t,\vartheta)$,
Theorem~\ref{thm:v111-unique} supplies at most one $x_w$ per word.
Apply \eqref{eq:v111-selected-word-sum} to precisely those points,
use $\chi\ge\lambda_\circ$, and integrate
\eqref{eq:v111-roof-sum} over the angle interval of length $2\pi$.
This gives \eqref{eq:v111-pointwise-marks} with the constants
\eqref{eq:v111-constants}. Proposition~\ref{prop:v112-exhaustion} first establishes the
nonnegative change-of-variables identity by truncation and monotone
exhaustion; the present bound then proves finiteness and the density. At finite $m$ the
roof is confined to a bounded interval.

Expand $\prod_i(1+a_i/c_i)$ into its finite sum over distinct marked
sets. Apply \eqref{eq:v111-pointwise-marks} to each term. The sum
of the upper bounds factors exactly as
$C_\star\prod_i(1+K_\star a_i)$. The exponential inequality uses
$1+x\le e^x$. Finally expand $I_m$ linearly and use
$\sum_iq_0^{d_i}\le2q_0/(1-q_0)$.
All original exact labels remain in the source throughout. Removing
their tests only enlarges the positive word upper sums in the proof;
it does not change the source normalization or infer a Gaussian
factor from an unconditioned pressure bound.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[A factorial bound for changing incidence witnesses]
\label{cor:v111-witness-moments}
Let $J\subset\{1,\ldots,m-1\}$ and let $\eta_i\ge0$ be arbitrary.
On the original event define
\[
 N_J(x)=\sum_{i\in J}\one_{\{c_i(x)<\eta_i\}},\qquad
                         A_J=\sum_{i\in J}\eta_i.
\]
For every integer $s\ge1$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v111-factorial}
 \left\|\frac{dF_\#(\one_{\{N_J\ge s\}}\one_\Pi\nu_R^*)}{dt}
                  \right\|_\infty
 \le C_\star K_\star^s e_s((\eta_i)_{i\in J})
 \le C_\star\frac{(K_\star A_J)^s}{s!},
\end{equation}
where $e_s=0$ for $s>|J|$. The witnesses may change with the target
roof and angle; no single witness is chosen for an entire segment.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
At every regular state,
\[
 \one_{\{N_J\ge s\}}\le {N_J\choose s}
   =\sum_{\substack{S\subset J\\|S|=s}}
                       \prod_{i\in S}\one_{\{c_i<\eta_i\}}
   \le\sum_{\substack{S\subset J\\|S|=s}}
                         \prod_{i\in S}\eta_i/c_i.
\]
Push this positive inequality through the original roof and apply
\eqref{eq:v111-pointwise-marks}. In the expansion of $A_J^s$ each
product with distinct indices occurs $s!$ times; all remaining terms
are nonnegative. This proves the last inequality. The argument is
finite-count and already completes the full physical sum, so it
introduces no collision-count multiplier for the possible witnesses.
\end{proof}
