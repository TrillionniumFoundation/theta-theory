\begin{abstract}
Weak convergence of joint laws does not make disintegration continuous.
We therefore keep a global weak belief state but prove Feller filtering on
model-derived dominated Sobolev charts that control unnormalized kernels and
their observation derivatives.  Atomic microscopic observations enter by an
exponentially good regularization.  The unnormalized positive kernel, its
evidence, and its projective zero-evidence direction form an exact Markov
belief state with measurable dynamic programming.  Tolerance-dependent
bounded-Lipschitz coordinates approximate the full value, and the exact
regular experiment is QMD, LAN, and locally Bernstein--von Mises.
\end{abstract}
