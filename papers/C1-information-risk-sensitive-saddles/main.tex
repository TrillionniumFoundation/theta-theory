\begin{abstract}
Hidden dynamics remain deterministic push-forwards and are never dominated by
a common state measure.  Only the stratified observation channel has a
density.  An integrated numerator identity proves Feller continuity of the
belief kernel at small and zero evidence even for infinite reference strata.
Finite-coordinate approximations reconstruct into the convex belief space.
For persistently excited adaptive policies, differentiated unnormalised
filters give a martingale LAN expansion, uniform tests, and a local
Bernstein--von Mises theorem.
\end{abstract}
