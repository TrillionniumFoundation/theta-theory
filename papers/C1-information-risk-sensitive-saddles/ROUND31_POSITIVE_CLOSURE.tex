\section{Round-thirty-one reconstruction: normalized stratum selection and observable-quotient filter jets}
\label{sec:r31-c1}

The observation experiment now contains an explicit probability of selecting
each stratum.  The Radon--Nikodym density therefore integrates to one on the
whole disjoint union, not once per stratum.  Deterministic hidden
push-forwards are differentiated through a projective cylindrical test scale,
which applies to histories and measure-valued states without assigning them a
finite manifold dimension or assuming invertibility.  Filter stability is
derived from a finite-window observability inequality for the induced
observation law, not from a false Hilbert-projective contraction of a
deterministic Bayes update.

\subsection{A normalized stratified observation experiment}

Let $X$ be a Polish hidden state, $A$ a compact action space, and
$\Theta\subset\R^d$ compact.  Hidden evolution is deterministic,
\[
 M_\vartheta^a(x,\dd x')=\delta_{\Phi_\vartheta^a(x)}(\dd x').           \tag{C1.1}
\]
The observation space is the disjoint union
$E=\bigsqcup_{s\in\cS}E_s$.  Let $m_s$ be the canonical sigma-finite measure
on $E_s$, choose a positive probability density $q_s$ with respect to $m_s$,
and choose fixed envelope weights $\alpha_s>0$ with
$\sum_s\alpha_s=1$.  Set
\[
 \nu=\sum_s\alpha_sq_sm_s.                              \tag{C1.2}
\]

For every $(\vartheta,a,x)$ let
$p_s(\vartheta,a,x)\ge0$ be the stratum-selection probabilities,
\[
 \sum_s p_s(\vartheta,a,x)=1.                             \tag{C1.3}
\]
Conditional on stratum $s$, a signal map
$\cO_{\vartheta,s}^a(x)$ and an independent channel density
$\rho_s(\cO_{\vartheta,s}^a(x),y)$ with respect to $m_s$ define the
observation.  Surface actions include their Jacobian and countable strata use
a stochastic matrix.  The density with respect to (C1.2) is
\[
 g_\vartheta^a(x,y)=
 {p_s(\vartheta,a,x)\rho_s(\cO_{\vartheta,s}^a(x),y)
  \over\alpha_s q_s(y)},\qquad y\in E_s.                 \tag{C1.4}
\]

\begin{proposition}[Whole-channel normalization]
\label{prop:r31-c1-normalization}
For every $(\vartheta,a,x)$,
\[
 \int_Eg_\vartheta^a(x,y)\nu(\dd y)=1.                   \tag{C1.5}
\]
If the selection probabilities, signal maps, and conditional channel
densities have three dominated parameter derivatives, then
$g_\vartheta^a(x,\cdot)$ is $L^1(\nu)$-continuous and quadratically mean
differentiable through order two.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
On stratum $s$, multiplication by $\alpha_sq_s$ cancels the denominator in
(C1.4) and integration gives $p_s(\vartheta,a,x)$.  Summing uses (C1.3).
Dominated differentiation is performed stratum by stratum; the derivative
majorants are summable after multiplication by $p_s$ and its derivatives.
Thus a countable union has total mass one rather than the number of active
strata.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Projective cylindrical derivatives on the actual hidden state}

Choose a countable cylinder algebra
$\cD=\bigcup_n\cD_n\subset C_b(X)$, where each $\cD_n$ depends on finitely
many history, graph-current, density, or kernel coordinates and is completed
in a Sobolev norm $\norm\cdot_{r,n}$.  The inclusions and conditional
projections are compatible.  Put
\[
 \mathbb X^{-r}=arprojlim_n(\cD_n,\norm\cdot_{r,n})'.     \tag{C1.6}
\]
Assume that for every $\varphi\in\cD_n$ the map
$\vartheta\mapsto\varphi(\Phi_\vartheta^a(x))$ is $C^3$ and
\[
 \norm{\partial_\vartheta^j(arphi\circ\Phi_\vartheta^a)}_{r,n}
 \le C_n\norm\varphi_{r+j,n'},\qquad j\le3,              \tag{C1.7}
\]
for a finite enlarged cylinder $n'$.  No inverse of $\Phi_\vartheta^a$ is
used.

\begin{lemma}[Distributional projective push-forward]
\label{lem:r31-c1-pushforward}
For a finite measure or projective distribution $\mu\in\mathbb X^{-r}$,
\[
 \partial_\vartheta^j(\Phi_\vartheta^a)_\#\mu
 \in\mathbb X^{-(r+j)},\qquad j\le3,                     \tag{C1.8}
\]
and
\[
 \ip{\partial_\vartheta^j(\Phi_\vartheta^a)_\#\mu}{\varphi}
 =\ip{\mu}{\partial_\vartheta^j(\varphi\circ\Phi_\vartheta^a)}.        \tag{C1.9}
\]
The statement applies to complete histories and measure-valued hidden states.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Equation (C1.7) makes the right side of (C1.9) a bounded functional on the
$j$-times stronger cylinder test level.  Compatibility of the cylinder
projections gives an element of the projective dual.  Difference quotients
converge on every cylinder and are uniformly bounded, so they converge in the
projective distribution topology.  Finite-dimensional Sobolev coordinates
are used only inside each cylinder; no global dimension of $X$ is required.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Feller filtering and derivative jets}

For a belief $\pi$, define
\[
 \pi^-=(\Phi_\vartheta^a)_\#\pi,\qquad
 r_\vartheta(\pi,a,y)=\int g_\vartheta^a(x,y)\pi^-(\dd x),              \tag{C1.10}
\]
\[
 B_\vartheta(\pi,a,y)(\dd x)
 ={g_\vartheta^a(x,y)\pi^-(\dd x)\over
   r_\vartheta(\pi,a,y)}.                                \tag{C1.11}
\]
The value on the zero-evidence set is arbitrary because it is multiplied by
zero in the integrated belief kernel.

\begin{theorem}[Integrated Feller and cylindrical derivative filter]
\label{thm:r31-c1-filter}
On every Lyapunov-reachable belief shell:

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item the integrated belief transition is Feller in bounded-Lipschitz
      topology;
\item for diagnostic channels whose evidence is uniformly positive, the
      belief and its first two parameter derivatives are well defined in
      $\mathbb X^{-r}\times\mathbb X^{-(r+1)}\times
       \mathbb X^{-(r+2)}$;
\item the derivative recursion is obtained by differentiating the
      unnormalized update and then normalizing.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Proposition~\ref{prop:r31-c1-normalization} gives $L^1(\nu)$ convergence of
unnormalized numerators and evidences.  Multiplying a posterior difference by
its evidence removes the small-denominator set and proves the integrated
Feller property.  For derivatives, apply
Lemma~\ref{lem:r31-c1-pushforward}, multiply by the cylinder-smooth likelihood,
and use the quotient rule only on diagnostic channels with positive evidence.
Every loss of one order is recorded in the projective scale.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Finite-window induced-law observability}

A deterministic Bayes update need not contract Hilbert projective distance.
Instead fix diagnostic action words
$\mathbf a^{(1)},\ldots,\mathbf a^{(J)}$ of length $m_0$.  For a belief
$\pi$ let
\[
 \mathsf P_{\vartheta,\pi}^{\mathbf a}
 =\mathcal L_{\vartheta,\pi}
   (Y_1,\ldots,Y_{m_0}\mid\mathbf a)                       \tag{C1.12}
\]
be the induced finite-window observation law.  Let
$T_{\vartheta,\pi}^{\mathbf a}$ be its derivative in a belief/parameter
tangent $z=(\dot\pi,h)$.

Define the observable tangent seminorm
\[
 \norm z_{\rm obs}^2=
 \sum_{j=1}^J\rho_j
 \norm{T_{\vartheta,\pi}^{\mathbf a^{(j)}}z}^2_{
 L^2(\mathsf P_{\vartheta,\pi}^{\mathbf a^{(j)}})}.       \tag{C1.13}
\]
The kernel consists of hidden perturbations invisible to every diagnostic
window and is retained as a separate latent coordinate.  The parameter
certificate is the induced-law inequality
\[
 \norm z_{\rm obs}^2\ge c\left(|h|^2+
 \norm{\dot\pi}_{\mathbb X^{-r}/\ker T}^2\right).        \tag{C1.14}
\]

\begin{theorem}[Observable-quotient derivative stability]
\label{thm:r31-c1-observable-stability}
Assume (C1.14), uniformly bounded third likelihood derivatives, and a policy
that selects diagnostic word $j$ conditionally with probability at least
$\rho_j>0$.  Then the normalized score/derivative recursion has a stable
Riccati estimate on the observable quotient:
\[
 E\norm{z_{k+m_0}}_{\rm obs}^2\mid\cF_k
 \le (1-c_0)\norm{z_k}_{\rm obs}^2+C|h_{k:k+m_0}|^2.      \tag{C1.15}
\]
No strict contraction is claimed for latent directions or for one
multiplication/permutation update.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Over one diagnostic window, the second derivative of the negative log
predictive likelihood is the Gram operator
$\sum_jp_jT_j^*T_j$ plus a uniformly bounded martingale remainder.
Conditional action lower bounds and (C1.14) make this operator coercive on the
observable quotient.  The normalized derivative recursion is the associated
information-filter/Riccati update; completing the square gives (C1.15).
The latent kernel is propagated separately by deterministic prediction and is
not falsely declared to contract.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Risk-sensitive control and global identification}

For bounded continuous running reward $c$ and terminal value $V$,
\[
 (\cT_\vartheta V)(\pi)=\inf_{a\in A}
 \int e^{c(\pi,a,y)}V(B_\vartheta(\pi,a,y))
 r_\vartheta(\pi,a,y)\nu(\dd y).                          \tag{C1.16}
\]
Feller continuity and compactness give measurable minimizers.  Finite belief
coordinates are chosen from a determining cylinder family and reconstructed
as convex combinations of reachable beliefs, so the approximation remains in
the proved shell.

Global identification is imposed on induced laws, not pointwise signals:
for every $\delta>0$,
\[
 \inf_{\substack{|\vartheta-\vartheta'|\ge\delta\\
                  \pi,\pi'\text{ reachable}}}
 \max_j H^2\left(
 \mathsf P_{\vartheta,\pi}^{\mathbf a^{(j)}},
 \mathsf P_{\vartheta',\pi'}^{\mathbf a^{(j)}}ight)
 \ge\eta_\delta>0.                                      \tag{C1.17}
\]
This rules out parameter compensation by a relabelling of hidden states.

\begin{theorem}[Adaptive LAN and Bernstein--von Mises]
\label{thm:r31-c1-bvm}
Under (C1.14), (C1.17), the randomized diagnostic lower bounds, and a prior
positive at $\vartheta_0$,
\[
 \log{\dd P_{\vartheta_0+h/\sqrt n}\over\dd P_{\vartheta_0}}
 =h^T\Delta_n-\tfrac12h^TI_nh+o_P(1)                     \tag{C1.18}
\]
uniformly for bounded $h$ and admissible policies.  Along subsequences with
$I_n\to I>0$, the posterior of
$\sqrt n(\vartheta-\vartheta_0)$ converges in total variation to
$N(I^{-1}\Delta_n,I^{-1})$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The cylindrical derivative filter and
Theorem~\ref{thm:r31-c1-observable-stability} give a recursively stable QMD
expansion with summable third-order remainder.  Conditional action lower
bounds and (C1.14) give $I_n\ge cI_d$ pathwise in conditional expectation; the
bounded score envelope gives Lindeberg and the martingale CLT.  For fixed
alternatives, (C1.17) yields exponential tests uniformly over reachable
beliefs.  Polynomial nets and the local information inequality control the
$M/\sqrt n$ annuli.  LAN handles the remaining local ball and proves the
Gaussian posterior limit.
\end{proof}

The revised experiment is a probability kernel on the full stratified space,
its derivatives are typed on the actual projective hidden state, and its
statistical stability comes from observable finite-window laws rather than an
invalid contraction of deterministic hidden densities.
