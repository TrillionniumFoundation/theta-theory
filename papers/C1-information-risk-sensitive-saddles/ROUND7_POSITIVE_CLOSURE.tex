\section{Round-seven reconstruction: coarea posterior currents, quenched block pressures, and belief-state sufficiency}
\label{sec:r7-c1}

This section replaces the round-six controlling module.  Exact noiseless
observations are retained.  Their posteriors are not forced into an interior
$L^1$ density class; they are represented by finite coarea currents in the
measure-valued hierarchy of B2.  Positive-density observation channels are a
special case.  Strategy-uniform block limits are proved by a ratio theorem on
reachable posterior-current compacta.

\subsection{Admissible observation channels and posterior currents}

On a finite hard-sphere level let the block observation be
\[
 Y_k=O_k^u(\Phi_{[t_k,t_{k+1}]}Z_{t_k}),
\]
where $u$ is the relaxed control.  An admissible noiseless channel is a
$C^2$ submersion on every regular chronological cell, with Jacobian
$J_O\ge j_*>0$ away from a B2 superexponentially small singularity shield.
A positive-density noisy channel with bounded $C^2$ density is also admissible.

If the predictive law has density $g$ and $O$ is noiseless, the posterior at
$Y=y$ is the coarea current
\[
 d\Pi_y(z)=
 \frac{g(z)J_O(z)^{-1}
       \,d\mathcal H^{D-r}|_{O^{-1}(y)}(z)}
      {\int_{O^{-1}(y)}gJ_O^{-1}d\mathcal H^{D-r}}.
\]
Its factorial coordinates are finite Radon measures and its collision traces
are intersections of the observation level set with incoming flux faces.
Let $\|\Pi\|_{\rm pc}$ be the sum of their weighted masses and tangential BV
currents over all codimensions generated by observations and collisions.

\begin{lemma}[Coarea posterior-current stability]
\label{lem:r7-c1-coarea}
For every admissible observation channel, the Bayes posterior is an element of
the posterior-current hierarchy.  On a compact control set and outside a
superexponentially small set of observations,
\[
 \|\Pi_{k+1}\|_{\rm pc}
 \le C\|\Pi_{k}^{\rm pred}\|_{\rm pc}
\]
with a constant uniform in the observed value.  For a positive-density channel
the posterior is the codimension-zero case.  The B2 measure-trace renewal
semigroup acts on every such current.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The coarea theorem gives the displayed regular conditional probability and
finite mass.  Transversality $J_O\ge j_*$ controls its density with respect to
Hausdorff measure.  Intersecting a $C^2$ observation level with a regular
collision face gives the corresponding incoming flux current; tangential BV
is controlled by the second fundamental forms and the predictive trace norm.
The bad nontransverse set is contained in the B2 grazing/singularity shield.
The B2 renewal operator was constructed on finite boundary measures and hence
propagates these currents without requiring an interior density.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Prediction followed by observed disintegration}

For a block control $u$, let
$\mathcal P_{k,u}^\varepsilon$ be the exact predictive pushforward/Feynman--Kac
operator on posterior currents.  Let
$\mathcal D_{k,u}^\varepsilon(\pi,y)$ be the coarea or density disintegration
kernel.  The controlled Bayes update is
\[
 \Pi_{k+1}^\varepsilon
 =\mathcal D_{k,u_k}^\varepsilon
  (\mathcal P_{k,u_k}^\varepsilon\Pi_k^\varepsilon,Y_{k+1}).
\]
This conditions on the new observation and uses the controlled posterior from
the preceding blocks.

\begin{theorem}[Feller belief transition]
\label{thm:r7-c1-belief}
On every compact posterior-current ball and compact relaxed-control set, the
joint kernel of $(Y_{k+1},\Pi_{k+1})$ is Feller in the weak current topology.
It has an analytic graph and admits measurable minimax selectors for finite
block games.  Ionescu--Tulcea therefore constructs one strategy-dependent law
for every nonanticipative relaxed strategy pair.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Predictive continuity is the B2 measure-trace semigroup continuity.  For a
noiseless channel, coarea integrals depend continuously on the current, control,
and regular value; the singular values have superexponentially small total
mass and are removed by a common compact exhaustion.  For a noisy channel this
is ordinary dominated convergence.  Compact relaxed controls and the Feller
kernel give lower/upper semicontinuity of the one-step value.  The measurable
maximum theorem and the analytic-graph selection theorem yield selectors.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Quenched conditional block pressure}

Let $H$ be a bounded particle/contact source on one block.  For a reachable
posterior current $\pi$ define
\[
 Q_{k,\varepsilon}^{\pi,u}(H)
 =\mu_\varepsilon^{-1}
  \log\int e^{\mu_\varepsilon H}
  \,d\mathcal P_{k,u}^\varepsilon\pi.
\]
The B2 operator proof is linear in the initial finite current, and its
constants depend only on the posterior-current ball.

\begin{theorem}[Strategy-uniform quenched block theorem]
\label{thm:r7-c1-quenched}
Uniformly over reachable posterior currents in a compact ball, regular
observations, and relaxed controls,
\[
 Q_{k,\varepsilon}^{\pi,u}(H)\longrightarrow Q_k^{\pi,u}(H)
\]
with all finite source derivatives.  Conditional on the observation, the same
statement holds as a ratio theorem: numerator and denominator have uniform
coarea asymptotics and their exponentially small exceptional sets are common.
Every regular limiting block pair has a unique normalized tilt and an
exponential pressure gap outside each projected neighborhood.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The B2 one-block tree expansion acts on the initial current by duality.  Its
cyclic gain and measure-trace norm are uniform on the compact posterior ball,
so the consistency error is uniform in $\pi,u$.  For a noiseless observation,
insert the coarea delta by Fourier inversion in the observed coordinate.
Submersion transversality supplies the uniform local coefficient; the same
coarea denominator appears in the untitled law.  Their ratio cancels the
surface normalization.  The singularity shield is uniform over strategies by
compactness.  Strict projected curvature is the B3 Lax--Milgram covariance,
which gives the pressure gap.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Belief contraction and asymptotic sufficiency}

Let $\mathfrak m(\pi)$ be the resolved one-particle density/contact mean of a
belief current and let $\mathfrak h_j(\pi)$ be its connected hierarchy
coordinates of order $j\ge2$.  The B2 connected renewal estimate gives, after
one kinetic block,
\[
 \|\mathfrak h_j(\Pi_{k+1})\|
 \le C\rho^j\mu_\varepsilon^{1-j}
 +e^{-c\mu_\varepsilon}\|\Pi_k\|_{\rm pc}.
\]
Observation reweighting preserves this estimate on regular coarea fibers.

\begin{theorem}[Posterior contraction and value sufficiency]
\label{thm:r7-c1-sufficiency}
For every fixed number of blocks, finite posterior marginals concentrate
exponentially around the unique kinetic hierarchy determined by the resolved
density/contact history.  Uniformly over admissible strategies,
\[
 \sup_{\pi:\mathfrak m(\pi)=f}
 |V_k^\varepsilon(h,\pi)-\overline V_k^\varepsilon(h,f)|
 \longrightarrow0.
\]
Thus the higher posterior correlations are asymptotically insufficient for the
controlled value, and the limiting dynamic program closes on the resolved
kinetic state.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Apply the pressure gap in
Theorem~\ref{thm:r7-c1-quenched} to a finite separating family and then use
exponential compactness of posterior-current balls.  The connected hierarchy
estimate makes the tail uniformly negligible.  Couple two beliefs with the
same resolved mean through the common normalized block tilt; their next-block
payoff and transition laws differ by the displayed vanishing hierarchy tail.
Backward induction over the fixed number of blocks proves value sufficiency.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The controlled kinetic game}

The exact finite information state is $(h,\pi)$, with the complete observed
history and posterior current.  The finite game separates one-time
preparation, reward-only control, and adaptive law control.  The limiting
Hamiltonian is the Isaacs envelope of the B2 kinetic Hamiltonian over relaxed
controls.

\begin{theorem}[Posterior dynamic programming and kinetic limit]
\label{thm:r7-c1-dpp}
The finite belief game has upper and lower values and satisfies the exact block
DPP.  Under the Isaacs condition these values converge locally uniformly to
the unique B4 viscosity solution on the reduced kinetic state.  The proof uses
Theorems~\ref{thm:r7-c1-belief},
\ref{thm:r7-c1-quenched}, and
\ref{thm:r7-c1-sufficiency}; it does not replace the posterior by a density
before proving sufficiency.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Feller belief transitions and measurable selectors give the finite DPP.
Uniform quenched block Laplace principles identify the relaxed upper and lower
generators.  Posterior-current compactness gives containment, and asymptotic
sufficiency removes higher belief coordinates.  B4 comparison identifies the
common limit under Isaacs.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Testing, LAN, and continuous-prior denominators}

Let $Q_\varepsilon(\theta)$ be a finite-dimensional canonical pressure family.
Define the exact score
\[
 \Delta_\varepsilon
 =\sqrt{\mu_\varepsilon}
  (X_\varepsilon-DQ_\varepsilon(\theta_0)).
\]
For a continuous prior with density $r_0$ positive at $\theta_0$, put
$B_\varepsilon=B(\theta_0,L/\sqrt{\mu_\varepsilon})$.  Uniform LAN on this
ball gives, with high probability,
\[
 \int_{B_\varepsilon}r_0(\theta)L_\theta d\theta
 \ge c\mu_\varepsilon^{-d/2}L_{\theta_0}
 e^{-C(1+|\Delta_\varepsilon|^2)}.
\]
This is the missing denominator lower bound.

\begin{theorem}[KL odds, Chernoff error, exact LAN, and BvM]
\label{thm:r7-c1-statistics}
Typical log posterior odds have the directed KL rate.  Expected posterior error
has the minimax Chernoff/testing exponent, with the local denominator above.
The local likelihood ratio satisfies
\[
 \log\frac{dP_{\theta_0+h/\sqrt\mu}}{dP_{\theta_0}}
 =h\cdot\Delta_\varepsilon
 -\frac12h^T\mathcal I h+o_{P}(1)
\]
uniformly on compact $h$-sets.  Under prior thickness and identifiability, the
rescaled posterior converges in total variation to the Gaussian with
information matrix $\mathcal I$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The B3 exact-centred Gaussian theorem gives the score CLT and uniform derivative
bounds give stochastic equicontinuity.  Pairwise fractional likelihood moments
give the Chernoff numerator estimate.  Divide by the integrated local-ball
lower bound, not by a nonexistent prior atom.  Uniform tests on fixed
complements and on the intermediate LAN annulus follow from the pressure gap
and the positive B3 covariance.  Taylor expansion at the exact finite mean
gives LAN, and the usual local Gaussian integration plus the two testing
regions gives total-variation BvM.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Round-seven C1 closure]
\label{thm:r7-c1-main}
Exact observations are represented by coarea posterior currents, reachable
beliefs remain in the B2 measure-trace domain, block pressures satisfy a
strategy-uniform quenched ratio theorem, belief transitions are Feller,
higher correlations are proved asymptotically insufficient, and continuous
prior testing uses an explicit local denominator.  Finite LAN centering remains
exact.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Combine Lemma~\ref{lem:r7-c1-coarea} and
Theorems~\ref{thm:r7-c1-belief},
\ref{thm:r7-c1-quenched},
\ref{thm:r7-c1-sufficiency},
\ref{thm:r7-c1-dpp}, and
\ref{thm:r7-c1-statistics}.
\end{proof}
