\section{Round-ten reconstruction: stratified evidence states and an exact filtering game}
\label{sec:r10-c1}

An exact observation need not have a uniformly positive density.  The state
therefore carries the unnormalized observation current and its evidence; only
after this pair is formed is a normalized posterior defined.  Exact control is
kept on the full belief state.  Density reduction is proved only for the
quantitatively chaotic belief cluster reachable from the declared preparation
and control class.

\subsection{Admissible stratified observation channels}

On every chronological hard-sphere cell, an observation channel
\(O_k^u\) is a smooth map of fixed rank on each member of a finite
stratification.  Critical strata and their images are retained as lower-rank
current components rather than identified with grazing collisions.  Repeated
observations and collision faces generate the tower
\[
 \mathfrak B=\bigoplus_{r\ge0}\mathcal M_W(\mathcal S_r),
\]
where \(\mathcal S_r\) is the union of codimension-\(r\) observation/contact
strata with its normal orientation and compatibility traces.

\begin{theorem}[Closed observation--collision current tower]
\label{thm:r10-c1-tower}
Free transport, specular reflection, actual-contact creation, prediction under
an admissible control, and coarea observation update extend to a positive
closed transition operator on \(\mathfrak B\).  If \(\Lambda^-\) is the
predicted unnormalized belief and \(y\) is observed, the update is the coarea
current
\[
 \mathcal O_{u,y}\Lambda^-
 =\sum_{S}
 1_{S\cap O_u^{-1}(y)}J_{O_u|S}^{-1}\Lambda^-|_S.
\]
Its evidence is
\(Z(u,y;\Lambda^-)=\langle1,\mathcal O_{u,y}\Lambda^-\rangle\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Start from smooth densities on transverse strata.  The coarea theorem gives
the oriented update and its next trace whenever a level set meets a collision
face.  The B2 Green graph controls transport and reflection of each normal
current; adjoining one observation normal raises codimension by one and the
factorial codimension weights make the tower sum bounded.  Constant-rank
charts cover regular strata, while critical strata are promoted to their own
lower-rank components.  Closing the graph gives the positive transition
operator.  No assertion that all observation currents are ordinary B2
interior/binary-flux pairs is made.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Evidence lift]
\label{lem:r10-c1-evidence}
The pair
\[
 \bigl(\mathcal O_{u,y}\Lambda^-,\ Z(u,y;\Lambda^-)\bigr)
\]
is continuous in the weighted-strict topology on each stratum chart, including
sequences with \(Z\downarrow0\).  The normalized posterior is used only on
\(\{Z>0\}\); risk-sensitive expectations are integrated against the evidence
measure \(Z(u,y;\Lambda^-)dy\).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Unnormalized coarea currents vary continuously under constant-rank
perturbations and weighted tightness.  Their total mass is the same continuous
pairing with one.  Keeping the pair avoids division by a small number.  Bayes'
formula integrates a posterior functional as
\(\int Z(y)F(\Lambda_y/Z(y))dy\), so zero-evidence observations contribute
zero and no uniform lower bound is required.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The exact belief transition and DPP}

A strategy is predictable with respect to the observation history.  Prediction
uses the B2 source-controlled augmented-law transition; update uses
Theorem~\ref{thm:r10-c1-tower}.

\begin{theorem}[Feller evidence kernel and exact belief DPP]
\label{thm:r10-c1-dpp}
Prediction followed by evidence-bearing observation defines a Feller kernel on
\[
 \widehat{\mathfrak B}
 =\{(\Lambda,z):\Lambda\in\mathfrak B_+,\ z\in\mathbb R,
   \langle1,\Lambda\rangle=e^z\}.
\]
For compact relaxed controls, the finite-horizon upper and lower values obey
the exact belief-state dynamic programming principle.  The state is the full
stratified belief/evidence pair, not its one-particle mean.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The B2 prediction kernel is Feller on energy/evidence sublevels.  Coarea
continuity from Lemma~\ref{lem:r10-c1-evidence} and dominated integration in
the evidence measure give Feller continuity of the update.  Ionescu--Tulcea
constructs the law for every predictable relaxed strategy.  Conditioning at
a block endpoint, then applying prediction and the new observation update,
gives the tower.  Compactness and measurable selection give the minimax DPP.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A joint coarea coefficient}

The declared observation family consists of finite-dimensional affine
statistics of the particle path and actual-contact measure on each
chronological cell.  Their derivatives, together with the B1 constraint mark,
have full affine span on a finite family of positive-activity blocks.

\begin{theorem}[Uniform coarea local coefficient]
\label{thm:r10-c1-coefficient}
On a compact regular source/control chart, the joint variable consisting of
B1 shell constraints, exact observations, and bounded path/contact sources
has a mixed lattice/continuous local coefficient at its exact finite saddle.
For observation values with finite rate,
\[
 \frac1{\mu_\varepsilon}\log Z_\varepsilon(u,y)
 \longrightarrow-\mathcal J_u(y)
\]
uniformly on compact rate sublevels, and numerator/denominator ratios converge
to the corresponding tilted stratified posterior kernel.  No lower bound on
\(Z_\varepsilon\) uniform over all \(y\) is asserted.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Adjoin the observation coordinates to the B1 good-block sum map.  The stated
affine-span condition gives a full-rank regenerative block family.  The B1
central, lattice minor-arc, and large-frequency estimates therefore extend to
the joint Fourier/coarea inversion, uniformly in relaxed controls.  Tilting at
the exact joint saddle gives the local coefficient and its exponential rate.
Applying the same coefficient to a bounded posterior numerator cancels the
common local prefactor and gives the ratio theorem.  Critical observation
strata are treated in their lower-rank chart, not hidden in a billiard
singularity shield.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Reachable quantitative chaos}

Let \(\mathfrak R_\varepsilon\) be the smallest belief cluster containing the
B1 prepared laws and closed under the declared prediction and observation
kernels.  Let \(\kappa_j(\Lambda)\) be its connected correlation currents.

\begin{lemma}[Propagation of the reachable cluster]
\label{lem:r10-c1-chaos}
For every fixed horizon and strategy,
\[
 \sup_{\Lambda\in\mathfrak R_\varepsilon}
 \|\kappa_j(\Lambda)\|_{\rm trace}
 \le j!C^j\mu_\varepsilon^{1-j}
\]
after the finite evidence exponent is included.  The estimate is stable under
exact observations from Theorem~\ref{thm:r10-c1-coefficient}.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
B1 preparation has the connected cluster estimate.  B2 prediction propagates
it by the trace renewal norm.  Exact observation is an exponential tilt by a
finite-dimensional affine statistic followed by a local coefficient ratio;
normal convergence of the joint source pressure preserves the connected
cumulant scaling.  Induction over finitely many observation blocks gives the
uniform estimate.  Arbitrary beliefs sharing the same one-particle marginal
are not included.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Reduced kinetic game and statistical tangent]
\label{thm:r10-c1-main}
The exact controlled value is the belief-state value of
Theorem~\ref{thm:r10-c1-dpp}.  On the reachable cluster, it converges to the
kinetic information-state game obtained from the one-particle density plus the
finite evidence/phase coordinates; the error is \(o(1)\) uniformly over
strategies.  At a regular phase, exact finite-centered likelihood ratios obey
LAN and the posterior satisfies the corresponding local Bernstein--von Mises
theorem.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Lemma~\ref{lem:r10-c1-chaos} makes all higher connected correlations
subleading in the B2 generator and in bounded contact rewards, uniformly over
reachable strategies.  Apply the B4 nonlinear semigroup limit block by block
inside the exact DPP; the evidence and phase coordinates are retained rather
than discarded.  For LAN, expand the exact finite joint pressure at its exact
mean.  B3 supplies the joint Gaussian process and positive quotient
covariance; the coarea coefficient supplies the local denominator.  Le Cam's
third lemma and Laplace expansion give the Gaussian posterior limit.
\end{proof}
