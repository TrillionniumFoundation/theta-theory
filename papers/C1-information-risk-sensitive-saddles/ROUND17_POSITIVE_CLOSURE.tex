\section{Round-seventeen positive closure: dominated filter charts, exact belief dynamics, and quadratic-mean statistics}
\label{sec:r17-c1}

Disintegration is not continuous for weak convergence of arbitrary joint laws.
Accordingly, the global belief state carries the weak topology, while Feller
continuity and local asymptotic statistics are proved on a model-derived
regular chart whose topology directly controls unnormalized observation
kernels and their derivatives.  Singular and zero-evidence strata remain in
the global state but are not silently imported into the regular chart.

\subsection{The global belief state and regular observation charts}

Let $E$ be the microscopic or path state and $Y$ the observation space.  A
belief is a probability $\pi\in\mathcal P_W(E)$ with finite Lyapunov moment.
For a control $a$ let
\[
 \Lambda^a_\pi(dx,dy)=\pi(dx)K^a(x,dy)
\]
be the unnormalized one-step joint kernel.  The exact next belief is a
Rokhlin conditional law of $x'$ given the observed $y$ under the predicted
joint law.  The global state stores the unnormalized coefficient measure and,
when its total mass is zero, a projective direction in the positive cone.

Fix a reference observation measure $\nu$.  A regular chart
$\mathfrak B_{M,c,C,s}$ consists of beliefs and controls for which the
predicted observation kernel has a density $k_\pi^a(x,y)$ satisfying
\[
 c\le k_\pi^a(x,y)\le C,
 \qquad
 \|k_\pi^a\|_{W^{s,1}_y(C^1_{W,x})}\le M,
 \qquad s>\dim Y.
\]
The chart metric is the weighted weak belief metric plus the
$W^{s,1}_y(L^1_W)$ distance of unnormalized kernels.  By Rellich compactness,
its bounded subsets are compact in the corresponding $L^1$ kernel topology.

\begin{theorem}[Model-derived regular filter chart]
\label{thm:r17-c1-chart}
For the finite-cylinder Sinai observations and the regular hard-sphere
particle/contact observations used in this series, every compact source and
control set has a chart $\mathfrak B_{M,c,C,s}$ on a fixed positive-evidence
region.  The microscopic joint kernels enter that chart after an exponentially
good smoothing at a scale tending to zero, and the smoothing error is
exponentially negligible at the governing LDP speed.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For Sinai observations, the unsmoothed vector--return--roof coefficient theorem
of A2 gives a density with uniform derivatives on compact interior
observation windows.  Convolution only regularizes the bounded local path
coordinates and its error is controlled by the singularity shield of A3.  For
hard spheres, B1's exact-number coefficient theorem and B2's positive
actual-contact source expansion give the same bounds on compact regular
windows.  In both cases the denominator has a uniform Gaussian-scale lower
bound, while Sobolev derivatives follow by differentiating the common source
kernel before inversion.  The complement of the regular event has an
arbitrarily large exponential cost after the chart is enlarged.  Atomic
finite-volume empirical observations are therefore represented by an
exponentially good regularized kernel, not asserted to lie literally in an
$L^1$ density topology.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Integrated filter stability}

If $J(dx,dy)=j(x,y)m(dx)\nu(dy)$ is in the chart, put
$e_J(y)=\int j(x,y)m(dx)$ and
\[
 \Pi_J(y)(dx)={j(x,y)m(dx)\over e_J(y)}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Dominated disintegration estimate]
\label{thm:r17-c1-stability}
For two joint kernels $J,\widetilde J$ in one regular chart,
\[
 \int W_1^W(\Pi_J(y),\Pi_{\widetilde J}(y))
      \bar e(y)\nu(dy)
 \le C_\mathfrak B
 \|j-\tilde j\|_{L^1_y(L^1_W)},
\]
where $\bar e=e_J\wedge e_{\widetilde J}$.  Consequently prediction
followed by observation is Feller on the regular chart.  The theorem makes no
continuity claim for arbitrary weakly convergent joint laws.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For every weighted Lipschitz test $\varphi$,
\[
 \left|{\int\varphi j\,dm\over e_J}
      -{\int\varphi\tilde j\,d\tilde m\over e_{\tilde J}}\right|
 \le {C\over c}
 \int W(x)|j(x,y)-\tilde j(x,y)|\,dm_0(x),
\]
after embedding both kernels in the fixed dominating measure $m_0$ of the
chart.  Take the supremum over the weighted Lipschitz unit ball and integrate
in $y$.  The positive lower bound on evidence and the fixed dominating
measure are precisely the hypotheses absent from weak disintegration.  The
prediction kernel is continuous in the chart norm by
Theorem~\ref{thm:r17-c1-chart}; composition proves Feller continuity.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Exact belief Markov state and dynamic programming}

Let $\mathcal Z$ be the disjoint union of the regular belief charts and the
blown-up zero/singular-evidence boundary.  The state records the unnormalized
positive coefficient measure, its mass, and a projective cone direction when
the mass is zero.

\begin{theorem}[Belief transition and posterior DPP]
\label{thm:r17-c1-dpp}
For each predictable control pair there is a Borel transition kernel on
$\mathcal Z$ obtained by prediction and Rokhlin disintegration.  On regular
charts it is Feller; on the full state it is universally measurable.  Compact
relaxed controls admit measurable selectors, and the finite-horizon
risk-sensitive value satisfies the exact posterior dynamic-programming
principle.  One-time preparation, reward-only feedback, and adaptive
likelihood control remain three distinct games.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The microscopic transition and observation kernels are Borel on standard
Borel spaces.  Rokhlin disintegration can be selected jointly measurably in
the joint law on a countably generated observation sigma-field.  Adjoin the
unnormalized mass and the projective zero-mass direction before normalization;
this makes the update Borel also at zero evidence.  The regular-chart Feller
property is Theorem~\ref{thm:r17-c1-stability}.  The compact relaxed-control
space and lower semicontinuity of the transition action give a measurable
minimum/maximum selector by the measurable maximum theorem.  Conditioning the
exact path likelihood at the next observation time and applying the tower
property yields the DPP.  Since the phase/preparation variable is fixed once
in the first game but the adaptive source is selected blockwise in the third,
their timing is not conflated.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Finite determining coordinates without a fictitious rate}

Let $K\subset\mathcal P_W(E)$ be a compact regular belief set.  For every
$\eta>0$, choose a finite family
$\Phi_\eta=(\phi_1,\ldots,\phi_{m(\eta)})$ from the
weighted bounded-Lipschitz unit ball that separates an $\eta$-net of $K$.
Let $R_\eta$ map the coordinate vector to a nearest point of the net.

\begin{proposition}[Tolerance-dependent belief reconstruction]
\label{prop:r17-c1-finite}
The families may be chosen measurably so that
\[
 \sup_{\pi\in K}
 W_1^W\bigl(\pi,
 R_\eta(\pi(\Phi_\eta))\bigr)
 \le3\eta.
\]
The finite-coordinate controlled values converge uniformly to the full belief
value as $\eta\downarrow0$.  No claim is made that the first $K$ members
of an arbitrary dense enumeration have a prescribed resolution rate.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Compactness gives a finite $\eta$-net.  For each pair of distinct net
points, Kantorovich duality supplies a bounded Lipschitz test separating them
by at least their distance minus $\eta$; collect finitely many such tests.
Nearest-net-point reconstruction is Borel after fixing a deterministic tie
rule and gives the metric estimate.  The regular filter kernel and reward are
uniformly continuous on $K$, so backward induction and the contraction of the
risk-sensitive Bellman operator give uniform convergence of values.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Quadratic-mean differentiability and LAN}

Let $\vartheta\mapsto p_\vartheta^\varepsilon$ be the exact
finite-volume density of the observed experiment on a regular chart, with
respect to the fixed dominating observation measure.  The source derivatives
come from the exact finite-volume B1/B2 or A2 coefficient before taking the
limit.

\begin{theorem}[Exact-experiment QMD and LAN]
\label{thm:r17-c1-lan}
On every compact identifiable regular chart,
\[
 \int\left(
 \sqrt{p_{\vartheta+h}^\varepsilon}
 -\sqrt{p_\vartheta^\varepsilon}
 -{1\over2}h^T\dot\ell_{\vartheta,\varepsilon}
       \sqrt{p_\vartheta^\varepsilon}
 \right)^2d\nu=o(|h|^2)
\]
uniformly for $|h|\to0$, and the score covariance converges to a positive
information matrix $I_\vartheta$.  Therefore, for
$h/\sqrt{\mu_\varepsilon}$,
\[
 \log{dP_{\vartheta+h/\sqrt{\mu_\varepsilon}}^\varepsilon
          \over dP_\vartheta^\varepsilon}
 =h^T\Delta_\varepsilon
 -{1\over2}h^TI_\vartheta h+o_{P_\vartheta}(1),
\]
with $\Delta_\varepsilon\Rightarrow N(0,I_\vartheta)$.
A prior with positive continuous density then satisfies a Bernstein--von Mises
theorem in the local chart, uniformly over the declared compact strategy
class.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The exact coefficient theorems provide two source derivatives of the density
in $L^1$ and a third derivative dominated in $L^1$ on the fixed chart.
Taylor's theorem for the square root, using the uniform lower and upper density
bounds, gives QMD.  The score is the centered derivative of the exact
finite-volume log normalizer; A2 or B3 gives its Gaussian limit and B1 supplies
the prepared Schur complement.  QMD plus the central limit theorem yields LAN
with exact finite-volume centering.  Uniform tests follow from compact
identifiability and the coefficient large-deviation bound; Laplace expansion
of numerator and denominator gives BvM.  All upstream inputs invoked here are
proved earlier in the dependency order.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Round-seventeen C1 closure]
\label{thm:r17-c1-main}
The exact belief process is Markov on an unnormalized positive-kernel state,
regular filter charts have model-derived compactness and Feller stability,
finite coordinates approximate the full value at a chosen tolerance, and the
exact regular experiment is QMD, LAN, and locally Bernstein--von Mises.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Combine Theorems~\ref{thm:r17-c1-chart},
\ref{thm:r17-c1-stability}, \ref{thm:r17-c1-dpp}, and
\ref{thm:r17-c1-lan}, together with
Proposition~\ref{prop:r17-c1-finite}.
\end{proof}
