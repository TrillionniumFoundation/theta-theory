\section{Round-twenty-one positive closure: exact dominated experiments, measurable belief dynamics, and triangular-array inference}
\label{sec:r21-c1}

This section replaces the former Round-Seventeen source.  The hidden-state
transition and observation kernels are typed separately.  Atomic, lattice,
and continuous observations are treated on one stratified dominating measure;
no artificial smoothing is used.  Local asymptotic normality is proved as a
uniform triangular-array likelihood expansion, and the Bernstein--von Mises
theorem includes a separate testing and posterior-concentration argument.

\subsection{The exact controlled experiment}

Let $E$ be the hidden state space and $Y$ the observation space.  For every
admissible action $a\in A$ and parameter $\vartheta\in\Theta\subset\mathbb R^p$,
let
\[
 M_{\vartheta}^{a}(x,dx')
\]
be the hidden transition and
\[
 G_{\vartheta}^{a}(x',dy)
\]
the observation kernel conditional on the new state.  Their joint one-step
kernel is
\[
 \Lambda_{\vartheta}^{a}(x,dx',dy)
 =M_{\vartheta}^{a}(x,dx')G_{\vartheta}^{a}(x',dy).
\]
For the Sinai model, $M$ is the exact A3 history transition; for hard spheres,
it is the finite-volume law transition and its B4 kinetic limit.  The
observation coordinates are finite collections of the lattice marks,
continuous roofs/coarea coordinates, and bounded path/contact cylinders.

Write $Y$ as the disjoint union of finitely many strata $Y_\alpha$, each of
which is a product of a countable lattice and a Euclidean manifold.  Let
$\nu_\alpha$ be counting measure times smooth volume on that stratum and set
\[
 \nu=\sum_\alpha2^{-|\alpha|}\nu_\alpha.
\]
On every reachable compact state/control/parameter chart, the A2 and B1
Fourier/coarea theorems give a density
\[
 G_{\vartheta}^{a}(x',dy)=g_{\vartheta}^{a}(x',y)\nu(dy)
\]
with its atomic masses represented as densities on zero-dimensional strata.

Fix a reference hidden probability $m_0$ with positive density on every
reachable regular component.  A belief belongs to the regular chart
$\mathcal K$ when
\[
 \pi(dx)=r(x)m_0(dx),
 \quad c\le r\le C,
 \quad \|r\|_{C_W^1}\le C,
\]
and the transition has density
$m_\vartheta^a(x,x')$ with respect to $m_0(dx')$ satisfying the analogous
bounds.  These are verified below for the forward reachable set from the
prepared initial family; they are not imposed on all of
$\mathcal P(E)$.

\begin{theorem}[Model-derived dominated regular chart]
\label{thm:r21-c1-chart}
For every finite horizon, compact parameter set, and compact relaxed-control
class, the beliefs reachable from the A3 or B1--B4 prepared initial family lie
in a forward-invariant set $\mathcal K$ on which:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item $M_\vartheta^a$ and $G_\vartheta^a$ have the common dominating measures
      $m_0$ and $\nu$ above;
\item their densities are bounded above and below on each reachable stratum;
\item for some $s>\dim Y_\alpha+2$ on every stratum,
\[
 \|m_\vartheta^a\|_{C_W^1}+
 \|g_\vartheta^a\|_{W_y^{s,1}(C_{W,x'}^1)}\le C;
\]
\item the same bounds hold for three parameter derivatives and two control
      derivatives;
\item $\mathcal K$ is compact in weighted $L^1(m_0)$ and the transition maps
      it continuously into itself.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For A3, the reference density is the normalized inverse Jacobian of the exact
history disintegration.  Exponential stable contraction and the A2 weighted
branch theorem give the $C_W^1$ state bound and a positive lower bound on each
finite reachable regular chart.  Fourier inversion with the A2
$(1+|b|)^{-2}$ tail may be repeated after inserting polynomial observation
weights; the full finite-dimensional observation is partitioned into charts
so that a fixed number of coarea variables yields $s$ derivatives.  Lattice
and atomic coordinates are zero-dimensional strata and require no fictitious
Lebesgue density.

For hard spheres, B1's fixed-size full-rank blocks give uniform
$W^{s,1}$ densities in all continuous observation directions, including
position constraints; B2's positive controlled evolution and B4's transfer
lemma give the common state domination on every prepared finite-horizon
reachable set.  Source derivatives in A2/B1/B2 are exactly derivatives of the
finite-volume likelihood kernel before normalization, and their global
Fourier majorants justify differentiation in $y$ and in $\vartheta$ under the
inverse transform.

Bayes multiplication and prediction preserve positive lower and upper bounds
on a finite horizon.  The derivative bounds propagate by the quotient rule.
Weighted tail control in $x$ and Rellich compactness on every observation
stratum give precompactness; the uniform positive bounds and the closed
transition identities give closedness.  Thus the reachable set, rather than
the whole belief space, is compact and forward invariant.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The exact belief update and null observations}

For $\pi\in\mathcal K$ define the predictive joint density
\[
 j_{\vartheta}^{a,\pi}(x',y)=
 g_{\vartheta}^{a}(x',y)
 \int r(x)m_{\vartheta}^{a}(x,x')m_0(dx)
\]
and evidence
\[
 e_{\vartheta}^{a,\pi}(y)=
 \int j_{\vartheta}^{a,\pi}(x',y)m_0(dx').
\]
For $e(y)>0$, the posterior is
\[
 \mathsf B_{\vartheta}^{a}(\pi,y)(dx')=
 {j_{\vartheta}^{a,\pi}(x',y)\over e_{\vartheta}^{a,\pi}(y)}m_0(dx').
\]
Adjoin one cemetery belief $\dagger$.  On the Borel set $\{e=0\}$ set the
posterior equal to $\dagger$ and make $\dagger$ absorbing.  This is a declared
null-observation convention; the zero measure is not assigned a nonexistent
projective direction.

Let
\[
 \mathsf P_{\vartheta}^{a}(\pi,d\pi')
 =\int\delta_{\mathsf B_\vartheta^a(\pi,y)}(d\pi')
       e_\vartheta^{a,\pi}(y)\nu(dy)
\]
with the cemetery convention.

\begin{theorem}[Feller belief kernel]
\label{thm:r21-c1-filter}
The space $\mathcal Z=\mathcal K\cup\{\dagger\}$ is compact metrizable and
standard Borel.  The map $(\vartheta,a,\pi,y)\mapsto
\mathsf B_\vartheta^a(\pi,y)$ is Borel everywhere and continuous on every
positive-evidence stratum.  The belief transition
$\mathsf P_\vartheta^a$ is Feller, and its restriction to the reachable
positive-evidence set leaves $\mathcal K$ invariant.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Weighted $L^1(m_0)$ metrizes the compact set $\mathcal K$ by
Theorem~\ref{thm:r21-c1-chart}; adding one isolated point preserves compact
metrizability.  The predictive density is jointly continuous in weighted
$L^1$ by common domination.  The evidence is therefore continuous.  On
$\{e>0\}$, division is continuous and the elementary estimate
\[
 \left\|{j\over e}-{\tilde j\over\tilde e}\right\|_{L^1_W}
 \le {\|j-\tilde j\|_{L^1_W}\over e}
 +{\|\tilde j\|_{L^1_W}|e-\tilde e|\over e\tilde e}
\]
applies.  On $\{e=0\}$ the explicit cemetery definition is Borel because the
set is closed.

For a continuous $F$ on $\mathcal Z$, split the observation integral into
$\{e\ge\delta\}$ and $\{e<\delta\}$.  The first part is continuous by dominated
convergence and the posterior estimate.  The second has total predictive mass
at most $\delta\nu(Y)$ on compact strata, with the tail controlled by the
uniform observation moment.  Let $\delta\downarrow0$.  This proves the Feller
property.  Forward invariance is part of the construction in
Theorem~\ref{thm:r21-c1-chart}.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Risk-sensitive partially observed control}

Let $\mathcal A(\pi)$ be a nonempty compact set of relaxed actions with closed
Borel graph, and let $c(\pi,a)$ and terminal reward $h(\pi)$ be bounded
continuous functions on each energy/source shell.  A policy is predictable
with respect to the observation filtration.  For risk parameter $\theta$, set
\[
 V_n(\pi)=\sup_\alpha {1\over\theta}
 \log\mathbb E_\pi^\alpha
 \exp\left\{\theta\left[
 \sum_{k=0}^{n-1}c(\Pi_k,A_k)+h(\Pi_n)
 \right]\right\}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Exact belief-state DPP and measurable selector]
\label{thm:r21-c1-dpp}
The posterior process on $\mathcal Z$ is a controlled Markov process and
\[
 V_{n+1}(\pi)={1\over\theta}
 \log\sup_{a\in\mathcal A(\pi)}
 \left[e^{\theta c(\pi,a)}
 \int e^{\theta V_n(\pi')}
       \mathsf P_\vartheta^a(\pi,d\pi')\right].
\]
The supremum has a universally measurable maximizing selector, and a Borel
selector exists when the control correspondence is continuous.  The same
statement holds in continuous time for the B4 relaxed kinetic controls on the
compact action sublevels.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Prediction by $M$ followed by observation by $G$ and Bayes' formula shows that
the conditional law of the next belief depends only on the present belief and
action; the timing is therefore explicit.  Conditional expectation and the
tower property give the displayed recursion.  The Feller theorem and
continuity of the cost make the maximand upper semicontinuous in a relaxed
action.  Compactness gives attainment, and the measurable maximum theorem
applies to the Borel graph.

For continuous time, use the B4 path-space relaxed controls.  Its compact
action sublevels, lower semicontinuous cost, and positive transfer stability
give the same measurable concatenation and selection properties.  Exponential
integrability follows from the fixed risk parameter and the action/energy
bounds.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Finite coordinates on the reachable compact set}

Choose a dense family $(\varphi_j)$ in the unit ball of the weighted continuous
hidden-state tests and define
$T_m(\pi)=(\pi\varphi_1,\ldots,\pi\varphi_m)$.

\begin{proposition}[Quantitative finite-coordinate value approximation]
\label{prop:r21-c1-finite}
For every finite horizon $n$ and $\varepsilon>0$, there is $m$ and a continuous
reconstruction $R_m:T_m(\mathcal K)\to\mathcal K$ such that the policy obtained
by applying an optimal belief policy to $R_m(T_m\pi)$ has value within
$\varepsilon$ uniformly over $\pi\in\mathcal K$.  The required $m$ is chosen on
one forward-invariant reachable compact set.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The coordinate maps separate points of the compact metric space
$\mathcal K$.  A finite subfamily therefore gives an embedding up to any
prescribed metric tolerance; a partition of unity on its compact image gives
a continuous approximate inverse.  The belief kernel, cost, and exponential
Bellman map are uniformly continuous on
$\mathcal K\times\mathcal A$.  Backward induction shows that one-step state
errors are amplified by at most a finite horizon-dependent constant
$C_{n,\theta}$, not by an unproved global contraction.  Choose the reconstruction
tolerance below $\varepsilon/C_{n,\theta}$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Uniform triangular-array likelihood expansion}

Let $P_{\vartheta}^{\varepsilon,\alpha}$ be an exact finite-volume observation
experiment under a policy $\alpha$ in a compact regular strategy class.  Its
effective information scale is $a_\varepsilon\to\infty$ (collision returns for
Sinai or $\mu_\varepsilon$ for hard spheres).  Common domination yields a
joint likelihood density $p_\vartheta^{\varepsilon,\alpha}$ on the complete
observation path.  Write
\[
 \dot\ell_{\vartheta}^{\varepsilon,\alpha}
 =\partial_\vartheta\log p_\vartheta^{\varepsilon,\alpha},
 \qquad
 \Delta_\varepsilon^\alpha=
 a_\varepsilon^{-1/2}\dot\ell_\vartheta^{\varepsilon,\alpha}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Uniform LAQ and LAN]
\label{thm:r21-c1-lan}
On compact parameter, initial-belief, and strategy sets, for every bounded
$h\in\mathbb R^p$,
\[
 \log {dP_{\vartheta+h/\sqrt{a_\varepsilon}}^{\varepsilon,\alpha}
           \over
           dP_{\vartheta}^{\varepsilon,\alpha}}
 =h^T\Delta_\varepsilon^\alpha
 -{1\over2}h^TI_\vartheta^\alpha h
 +r_\varepsilon^\alpha(h),
\]
where
\[
 \sup_{\alpha,|h|\le H}
 P_\vartheta^{\varepsilon,\alpha}
 (|r_\varepsilon^\alpha(h)|>\eta)\to0,
 \qquad
 \Delta_\varepsilon^\alpha
 \Longrightarrow N(0,I_\vartheta^\alpha),
\]
and $cI\le I_\vartheta^\alpha\le CI$.  The shifted experiments are contiguous.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Factor the likelihood into its predictive one-step densities generated by the
exact filter.  Taylor's formula with integral remainder gives, at
$u=h/\sqrt{a_\varepsilon}$,
\[
 \ell_{\vartheta+u}-\ell_\vartheta
 =u^T\dot\ell_\vartheta+
 {1\over2}u^T\ddot\ell_\vartheta u+
 {1\over6}\partial_\vartheta^3\ell_{\vartheta+tu}[u,u,u].
\]
Theorem~\ref{thm:r21-c1-chart} bounds the third derivative relative to the
predictive density on every stratum.  The sum of its conditional expectations
is $O(a_\varepsilon)$, so the total third-order remainder is
$O(|h|^3a_\varepsilon^{-1/2})$ uniformly.  The martingale identity for the
score gives zero conditional mean.  The conditional information averages
converge uniformly by the A4/B4 spectral or action-compactness estimates, and
the centered observed Hessian is negligible by the same martingale maximal
bound.

The score is an additive functional of the exact history/filter process in
the Sinai case and a density/contact functional in the hard-sphere case.
A4's martingale approximation and B3's process CLT give its uniform Gaussian
limit.  The periodic/observability nondegeneracy estimates give the common
information bounds.  The likelihood expansion and Le Cam's first lemma then
imply contiguity.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Uniform tests and Bernstein--von Mises}

Assume a prior density $\pi_0$ continuous and positive near the true parameter
and with exponentially integrable tails in the compact parameter chart.  Let
$\widehat\vartheta_\varepsilon$ be any efficient one-step estimator obtained
from the score and information.

\begin{lemma}[Uniform posterior concentration]
\label{lem:r21-c1-tests}
For every $M_\varepsilon\to\infty$,
\[
 \sup_\alpha
 P_\vartheta^{\varepsilon,\alpha}
 \left[\Pi^\varepsilon\left(
 \sqrt{a_\varepsilon}|\vartheta'-\vartheta|>M_\varepsilon
 \mid\mathcal Y\right)>\eta\right]\to0.
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Inside a fixed neighborhood, the uniform information lower bound and the LAN
expansion yield likelihood-ratio tests with errors bounded by
$Ce^{-cM^2}$ for alternatives at local distance $M$.  Cover each local annulus
by $O(M^p)$ balls and take the maximum test.  Outside that neighborhood,
compact identifiability gives a positive Kullback--Leibler separation.  The A2
or B2 source large-deviation bound, uniformly over the compact policy class,
then gives exponentially consistent tests.  Integrate the testing inequality
against the prior and use its local thickness to lower-bound the posterior
denominator.  Summing annuli proves the claim.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Strategy-uniform Bernstein--von Mises theorem]
\label{thm:r21-c1-bvm}
Uniformly over the compact regular strategy class, the posterior law of
\[
 \sqrt{a_\varepsilon}
 (\vartheta'-\widehat\vartheta_\varepsilon)
\]
converges in total variation, in
$P_\vartheta^{\varepsilon,\alpha}$-probability, to
$N(0,(I_\vartheta^\alpha)^{-1})$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Lemma~\ref{lem:r21-c1-tests} reduces the posterior integral to a growing local
ball.  On that ball, Theorem~\ref{thm:r21-c1-lan} is uniform and the prior ratio
$\pi_0(\vartheta+h/\sqrt a)/\pi_0(\vartheta)$ tends uniformly to one on every
fixed ball.  Complete the square in
$h^T\Delta-\frac12h^TIh$.  Gaussian tails and the exponentially small annular
tests make the truncation error vanish as the local ball grows.  The efficient
one-step estimator differs from
$artheta+I^{-1}\Delta/\sqrt a$ by
$o_P(a^{-1/2})$ uniformly.  Scheff\'e's lemma then gives total-variation
convergence, with all estimates uniform in the declared strategy class.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Round-twenty-one C1 closure]
\label{thm:r21-c1-main}
The information platform has a fully typed transition--observation model, a
model-derived stratified dominated chart, an explicit standard-Borel null
convention, a Feller belief kernel and risk-sensitive DPP, quantitative finite
coordinates on the reachable compact set, a uniform triangular-array LAN
theorem, and a separately proved strategy-uniform Bernstein--von Mises theorem.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Combine Theorems~\ref{thm:r21-c1-chart},
\ref{thm:r21-c1-filter}, \ref{thm:r21-c1-dpp},
\ref{thm:r21-c1-lan}, and \ref{thm:r21-c1-bvm} with
Proposition~\ref{prop:r21-c1-finite}.
\end{proof}
