\title[Adaptive identification of an infinite mechanical lattice]{Constructive Adaptive Identification of Local Damping and Stiffness in an Infinite Mechanical Lattice}
\author{Qian Qi}
\date{September 3, 2026 --- Round 35 working revision}
\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
We give a fully specified partially observed mechanical experiment on an infinite coupled oscillator chain. The unknown damping and stiffness act in the physical equations, the initial state is an infinite-dimensional nuisance, and only one noisy position coordinate is observed. Four randomized force-duration words produce a uniform global contrast and sensitivity-information bound without resetting the system. An energy calculation proves uniform semigroup and filter-jet bounds. These estimates give a policy-uniform local quadratic likelihood expansion, root-sample-size posterior concentration, and a total-variation Gaussian posterior approximation with the realized information matrix, without assuming that this matrix converges. We also compute a nonzero memory kernel of the same infinite bath, and derive a normalized evidence expansion, a current-state posterior approximation, and explicit random amplitudes for finitely many initial preparations. The proofs are on the infinite Hilbert state, not limits asserted from finite simulations. A separate appendix corrects the two local statement errors in the Round 34 report. General Sinai and hard-sphere applications remain distinct programme targets.
\end{abstract}
