## Executive assessment

Round eight correctly stops dividing every coarea posterior by a supposedly uniform positive evidence. It retains the unnormalized slice current and its evidence, and it restricts state reduction to beliefs reachable from the prepared system rather than all beliefs sharing one mean. These are sensible repairs.

The new theorem package is still unproved and contains a direct saddle error. In a conditional ratio

\[
\frac{E[e^{\mu H};Y=y]}{P(Y=y)},
\]

the numerator and denominator generally have different source-dependent saddles. The manuscript claims one common exact saddle and cancellation of the determinant and shell factor. That is false except in special source-orthogonal cases. The stratified-current state is not shown to be closed under repeated nontransverse observations, the evidence tail is merely asserted to be exponentially contained, and the posterior-chaos estimate is claimed uniformly over all reachable beliefs although its proof normalizes only on finite-evidence slices.

## Genuine repairs recognized

The following ideas should be retained:

- unnormalized posterior currents and evidence are separate coordinates;
- critical observation strata are not identified with billiard grazing strata;
- exact observations are allowed to create singular currents;
- state reduction is claimed only after a reachable-set chaos theorem;
- LAN remains centered at the exact finite-volume mean;
- a continuous-prior denominator is explicitly acknowledged.

## Major mathematical objections

### 1. The stratified tower is not constructed for repeated observations

The state uses a finite family of strata generated by collision faces and all observation level sets encountered so far. Repeated exact observations can produce nontransverse intersections, changing ranks, self-intersections, and singular sets requiring a Whitney-type stratification. There is no reason for the resulting family to remain finite or for every intersection current to have a finite transport derivative in the declared norm.

The proof starts with smooth transverse forms and invokes Stokes. That does not establish a closed Banach/current complex containing arbitrary critical coarea slices reached by the controlled dynamics.

### 2. Evidence is reference-density dependent

For a continuous observation, `Z(y)` is a density relative to the chosen coordinate/reference measure on observation space. Under a smooth reparameterization `y -> h(y)`,

\[
-\log Z(y)
\]

changes by the log Jacobian. Thus the evidence coordinate is not an intrinsic scalar unless the reference measure and its transformation rule are fixed as part of the model. The risk-sensitive value cannot use it as an invariant state coordinate without that typing.

### 3. Feller continuity at changing strata is not proved

Coarea restriction is continuous only under uniform transversality and stable geometry. At critical values, supports and dimensions can change discontinuously. Merely adding the critical set as another stratum does not prove continuity of the disintegration kernel across the transition.

The theorem also invokes “exponential evidence containment” without deriving it. Small-evidence observations can occur at precisely the exponential scale relevant to a risk-sensitive game.

### 4. The numerator and denominator do not have one common saddle

For a conditional exponential payoff, the numerator is

\[
E[e^{\mu H};Y=y],
\]

while the denominator is

\[
P(Y=y).
\]

The source `H` changes the cumulant and therefore changes the exact finite saddle. This is the same source-dependence that B1 had to introduce for microcanonical preparation. In general one needs two saddles,

\[
\lambda_{H,y,\varepsilon}
\quad\text{and}\quad
\lambda_{0,y,\varepsilon},
\]

and two determinant factors. They do not cancel merely because the observed level set is the same.

The statement in Theorem `r8-c1-ratio` that numerator and denominator have “one common exact saddle” is therefore false for a generic nonconstant block source.

### 5. B1 good-block regeneration does not automatically include the observation delta

An exact observation of a path block can be nonlinear and can couple many labels and collision times. Treating its Fourier variable as one additional independent continuous mark is valid only for a fixed additive observation with a verified joint rank condition.

Transversality of the global observation map does not imply that linearly many independent B1 blocks remain smooth after the observation is imposed. The joint characteristic-function theorem required here is not present in B1 or B2.

### 6. The posterior-chaos estimate is not uniform on the stated reachable set

The lemma claims a bound uniformly over all reachable posteriors. Its proof says to normalize only after restricting to a finite evidence slice, where

\[
Z(y)\ge e^{-L}.
\]

Outside such a slice, division by `Z(y)` can amplify a connected current by an arbitrarily large factor. Keeping the unnormalized current avoids that division only until a normalized belief is needed for rewards and predictions.

Thus the proof establishes, at most, a bound depending on `L`. It does not prove the displayed uniform estimate on the full reachable set or show that the large-evidence complement is negligible at the game scale.

### 7. Exact observations can create persistent order-one correlations

A reachable observation may select a phase, a collision pattern, or a global pair statistic. Even if the prior is chaotic, the normalized posterior can be a mixture or a highly correlated slice. A finite number of “marked coordinates” does not imply that all higher connected correlations remain `O(mu^{1-j})`.

The required result is a quenched Gibbs-conditioning/propagation-of-chaos theorem under the declared observation family and controls. It is not a consequence of the unconditioned B2 cluster estimate.

### 8. The sufficiency theorem assumes the expansion it needs to prove

The Bellman-step expansion in connected hierarchy coordinates is not constructed, and a bounded Lipschitz reward on belief space need not admit an absolutely summable hierarchy series. Equality of resolved means plus small connected coordinates does not by itself control total variation or all future controlled transition kernels.

The theorem therefore does not prove that the value closes on the kinetic state.

### 9. The risk-sensitive game and statistical theorem are compressed into one paragraph

Feller continuity on a noncompact stratified belief state, compactness of relaxed strategies, measurable minimax selectors, Isaacs equality, uniform LAN, testing outside local neighborhoods, and BvM total variation convergence are distinct theorems. They cannot be inferred from the preceding unproved ratio and chaos statements.

## Dependency assessment

C1 remains downstream of B2's unavailable trace/pressure theorem, B1's unavailable mixed coefficient, B3's unavailable covariance, and B4's unavailable comparison theorem. It also introduces a new independent blocker: exact observation does not preserve the asserted posterior-chaos class.

## Required reconstruction

A viable filtering paper should restrict the observation class and prove, for that class:

1. a closed stratified posterior-current state and reference-measure convention;
2. a two-saddle conditional coefficient theorem;
3. evidence-tail estimates at the large-deviation speed;
4. a quenched propagation-of-chaos/phase-selection theorem on reachable beliefs; and
5. a belief-state DPP before any kinetic-state reduction.

## Recommendation

**Reject.** Evidence lifting is the correct response to small coarea denominators, but the central ratio theorem uses a nonexistent common saddle, and the uniform posterior-chaos/sufficiency claims are not established.
