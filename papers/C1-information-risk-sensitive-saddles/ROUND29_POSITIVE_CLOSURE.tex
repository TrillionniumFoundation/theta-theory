\section{Round-twenty-nine reconstruction: exact observation envelopes and distributional belief jets}
\label{sec:r29-c1}

The observation density is now written with the exact Radon--Nikodym factor of
its fixed envelope.  Deterministic hidden push-forwards are differentiated in
a negative Sobolev scale, where translation of a Dirac mass is differentiable
with the expected loss of one order.  Persistent information is imposed and
verified pointwise in the current belief and randomized action, rather than
inferred from aggregate action frequencies under an arbitrary adaptive
policy.

\subsection{A normalized stratified observation channel}

Let $X$ be the complete prepared hidden state, $A$ a compact action space, and
$\Theta\subset\R^d$ compact.  The hidden transition is deterministic,
\[
 M_\vartheta^a(x,dx')=\delta_{\Phi_\vartheta^a(x)}(dx'),   \tag{C1.1}
\]
where $(\vartheta,a,x)\mapsto\Phi_\vartheta^a(x)$ is $C^4$ on each smooth
hidden-state chart and continuous across the declared strata.

The observation space is a countable disjoint union $E=\bigsqcup_sE_s$.
Each stratum carries a canonical sigma-finite measure $m_s$: Lebesgue measure
on Euclidean strata, Riemannian area on smooth surface strata, and counting
measure on countable strata.  Choose a strictly positive probability density
$q_s$ with respect to $m_s$ and put $\nu_s=q_sm_s$ and
$\nu=\sum_s2^{-|s|}\nu_s$.

On a Euclidean stratum, the signal map
$\mathcal O^a_{\vartheta,s}:X\to E_s$ is $C^4$ and the independent noise has
density $\rho_s$ with respect to $m_s$.  Therefore the density with respect to
$\nu$ is
\[
 g_\vartheta^a(x,y)=2^{|s|}
 {\rho_s(y-\mathcal O^a_{\vartheta,s}(x))\over q_s(y)},
 \qquad y\in E_s.                                        \tag{C1.2}
\]
On a surface stratum, translation is replaced by the specified smooth group
action and (C1.2) contains its Jacobian.  On a countable stratum it is the
corresponding confusion-matrix entry divided by $q_s(y)$.

\begin{proposition}[Exact envelope normalization]
\label{prop:r29-c1-envelope}
For every $(\vartheta,a,x)$,
\[
 \int_Eg_\vartheta^a(x,y)\nu(dy)=1.                       \tag{C1.3}
\]
If $\rho_s$ and the signal/action maps have four integrable derivatives, then
$g_\vartheta^a(x,\cdot)$ is $L^1(\nu)$-continuous and quadratically mean
differentiable through order two.  The fixed factor $q_s^{-1}$ is retained in
all formulas.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
On a Euclidean stratum,
\[
 \int {\rho_s(y-\mathcal O(x))\over q_s(y)}q_s(y)m_s(dy)
 =\int\rho_s(z)m_s(dz)=1.
\]
The surface statement follows from the change-of-variables Jacobian and the
countable statement from row normalization.  Translation or smooth group
action is continuous in $L^1(m_s)$; the fixed envelope cancels inside the
$L^1(\nu_s)$ norm.  Chain rule and the assumed derivative envelopes give QMD.
\end{proof}

For the diagnostic experiment, select compact-output channels (for example a
torus sensor or a finite/countable channel) whose densities satisfy globally
on their entire output stratum
\[
 0<c\le g_\vartheta^a(x,y)\le C,
 \qquad |\partial_\vartheta^\alpha\log g_\vartheta^a(x,y)|\le C,
 \quad |\alpha|\le3.                                     \tag{C1.4}
\]
Unbounded-output channels remain available for Feller prediction but are not
used to claim a global lower likelihood bound.

\subsection{Belief derivatives in a negative Sobolev scale}

On every smooth hidden chart choose $r>\dim X/2+4$ and let
$\mathbb H^{-r}$ be the dual of $H^r$.  Measures embed continuously in
$\mathbb H^{-r}$.  If
$b^a_\vartheta(x)=\partial_\vartheta\Phi^a_\vartheta
 ((\Phi^a_\vartheta)^{-1}x)$, then distributionally
\[
 \partial_\vartheta(\Phi^a_\vartheta)_\#\mu
 =-\operatorname{div}\bigl(b^a_\vartheta
     (\Phi^a_\vartheta)_\#\mu\bigr)\in\mathbb H^{-(r+1)}.               \tag{C1.5}
\]
Higher derivatives lose one Sobolev order each.

\begin{lemma}[Differentiable deterministic push-forward]
\label{lem:r29-c1-push}
For $j\le3$,
\[
 \partial_\vartheta^j(\Phi^a_\vartheta)_\#:
 \mathbb H^{-r}\longrightarrow\mathbb H^{-(r+j)}          \tag{C1.6}
\]
exists and is bounded uniformly on compact hidden-state shells.  In
particular, for $\Phi_\vartheta(x)=x+\vartheta$,
$\partial_\vartheta\delta_{x+\vartheta}= -\partial_x\delta_{x+\vartheta}$
in $H^{-(r+1)}$, although the map is not continuous in total variation.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Pair the push-forward with a test $\varphi\in H^{r+j}$ and differentiate
$\varphi(\Phi^a_\vartheta(x))$.  Each derivative consumes one test derivative
and has bounded coefficients by the $C^4$ chart hypothesis.  Duality gives
(C1.6), with (C1.5) at first order.
\end{proof}

For a belief $\pi$, set
\[
 \pi^-=(\Phi^a_\vartheta)_\#\pi,
 \quad r_\vartheta(\pi,a,y)=\int g_\vartheta^a(x,y)\pi^-(dx),            \tag{C1.7}
\]
\[
 B_\vartheta(\pi,a,y)(dx)
 ={g_\vartheta^a(x,y)\pi^-(dx)\over r_\vartheta(\pi,a,y)}.              \tag{C1.8}
\]
The belief kernel integrates (C1.8) against
$r_\vartheta(\pi,a,y)\nu(dy)$.

\begin{theorem}[Feller and derivative-filter theorem]
\label{thm:r29-c1-filter}
On every Lyapunov-reachable belief shell:

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item the integrated belief kernel is Feller in the bounded-Lipschitz
      topology;
\item on diagnostic channels satisfying (C1.4), the belief, evidence, and
      their first two parameter derivatives are stable in
      $\mathbb H^{-r}\times\mathbb H^{-(r+1)}\times\mathbb H^{-(r+2)}$;
\item over every block of $m_0$ diagnostic observations, the derivative error
      contracts by a factor $\kappa<1$ plus the new local score error.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For Feller continuity, unnormalized numerators and evidences converge in
$L^1(\nu)$ by Proposition~\ref{prop:r29-c1-envelope}; multiplying posterior
differences by their evidence removes the apparent singularity at small
evidence.  For derivatives, apply Lemma~\ref{lem:r29-c1-push}, multiply by the
$C^3$ likelihood, and differentiate normalization only after the unnormalized
update.  On diagnostic channels (C1.4), all denominators are bounded below.
The positive upper/lower likelihood bounds give a strict Hilbert-projective
contraction over $m_0$ steps.  The induced negative-Sobolev derivative
recursion has the same contracting homogeneous part and bounded score forcing,
proving (ii)--(iii).  Mere Feller continuity is not used as a derivative
contraction.
\end{proof}

The reachable shell is defined by a hidden Lyapunov bound and the image of the
positive diagnostic update.  The drift and the block contraction above prove
that it is closed, tight, and forward invariant; no unverified convex hull is
introduced.

\subsection{Risk-sensitive dynamic programming}

For bounded continuous running reward $c$ and terminal reward $\Psi$, define
\[
 (\mathcal TV)(\pi)=\inf_{a\in A}
 \int e^{c(\pi,a,y)}V(B_\vartheta(\pi,a,y))
 r_\vartheta(\pi,a,y)\nu(dy).                             \tag{C1.9}
\]
Compactness of $A$ and Theorem~\ref{thm:r29-c1-filter} give continuous values
and measurable minimizing selectors.  Finite-coordinate approximations use a
finite determining family on the proved invariant shell and a positive
barycentric reconstruction; their finite-horizon values converge uniformly.

\subsection{Pointwise persistent information}

Let $a_1,\ldots,a_J$ be diagnostic actions.  An admissible adaptive policy is
randomized with
\[
 P\{A_k=a_j\mid\mathcal F_{k-1}\}\ge\rho_j>0             \tag{C1.10}
\]
for every $k,j$.  Let
$\mathcal I_j(\pi,\vartheta)$ be the one-step conditional Fisher matrix after
action $a_j$.  The finite observability certificate is the pointwise bound
\[
 \sum_{j=1}^{J}\rho_j\mathcal I_j(\pi,\vartheta)\ge cI_d                 \tag{C1.11}
\]
for every $(\pi,\vartheta)$ in the invariant shell.

\begin{theorem}[Noncircular persistent excitation]
\label{thm:r29-c1-information}
Every policy satisfying (C1.10) obeys pathwise in conditional expectation
\[
 E[s_ks_k^T\mid\mathcal F_{k-1}]
 \ge cI_d,                                                \tag{C1.12}
\]
up to the fixed comparison constant between actual action probabilities and
$\rho_j$.  The score has a uniform third moment and satisfies the conditional
Lindeberg condition.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Condition on the current belief and sum the action-specific Fisher matrices
with the actual conditional action probabilities.  Equations (C1.10)--(C1.11)
give (C1.12) directly; no ergodic theorem for an arbitrary nonstationary
policy is needed.  The global diagnostic score bound (C1.4) gives all moment
and Lindeberg estimates.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Global identification and adaptive Bernstein--von Mises}

Assume the signal family is globally separating on the invariant shell:
for every $\delta>0$ there is $\eta_\delta>0$ such that
\[
 \max_j\norm{\mathcal O^{a_j}_{\vartheta}(x)-
                   \mathcal O^{a_j}_{\vartheta'}(x)}
 \ge\eta_\delta
 \quad\text{whenever }|\vartheta-\vartheta'|\ge\delta,    \tag{C1.13}
\]
uniformly in reachable $x$.  The diagnostic noise has nonvanishing Fourier
transform (or an invertible confusion matrix).  Compactness then gives a
uniform positive Hellinger gap for fixed-distance alternatives.

\begin{theorem}[Uniform adaptive LAN and Bernstein--von Mises]
\label{thm:r29-c1-bvm}
For every admissible policy and bounded $h$,
\[
 \log{dP_{\vartheta_0+h/\sqrt n}\over dP_{\vartheta_0}}
 =h^T\Delta_n-\tfrac12h^TI_nh+o_{P}(1),                  \tag{C1.14}
\]
where
$\Delta_n=n^{-1/2}\sum_{k\le n}s_k$ and
$I_n=n^{-1}\sum E[s_ks_k^T\mid\mathcal F_{k-1}]$.
The remainder is uniform over the policy class and bounded $h$-sets.  Along
subsequences with $I_n\to I$, a prior positive and continuous at
$\vartheta_0$ yields
\[
 \mathcal L\bigl(\sqrt n(\vartheta-\vartheta_0)
       \mid Y_{1:n}\bigr)
 \longrightarrow N(I^{-1}\Delta_n,I^{-1})                \tag{C1.15}
\]
in total variation, uniformly over admissible policies.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Theorem~\ref{thm:r29-c1-filter} gives recursive QMD with a summable third-order
remainder, and Theorem~\ref{thm:r29-c1-information} gives a uniform positive
information matrix and martingale Lindeberg condition.  This proves LAN and
the martingale Gaussian limit.  For posterior contraction, the Hellinger gap
from (C1.13) gives exponentially consistent fixed-distance tests.  On annuli
$M/\sqrt n\le|\vartheta-\vartheta_0|\le\delta$, a Euclidean net has polynomial
cardinality while the information bound gives error $e^{-cM^2}$; first let
$n\to\infty$ and then $M\to\infty$.  LAN handles the local ball and Gaussian
tails complete the total-variation conclusion.
\end{proof}

The observation model is now exactly normalized, hidden-state derivatives are
taken in a topology in which they exist, diagnostic contraction is distinct
from Feller continuity, and adaptive information is guaranteed at the current
belief rather than by an invalid global frequency argument.
