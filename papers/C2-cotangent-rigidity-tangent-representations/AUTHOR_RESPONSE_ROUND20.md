# C2 Author Response — Round Twenty

The weighted strict topology is now generated by explicit compact-support and
normalized-tail seminorms.  Its continuous dual is proved to be the measures
of finite weighted variation by compatible local Riesz representations and a
disjoint-annulus argument for the global moment bound.  Coboundary separation
is asserted only for the actual A4 and B4 platforms, where invariant weighted
probabilities have already been constructed.

The type-(B) section no longer presents an abstract conditional lemma as a
platform theorem.  It verifies the common form domain, coercivity, Hilbert
metric transport, and parameter/source derivatives separately for the A4
suspension and the B3/B4 tangent form.  The form-level Schur complement assumes
and verifies boundedness of the resolved projection on the form domain, solves
the complementary equation by Lax--Milgram, and differentiates all projection,
form, and inverse terms.

Pressure rigidity is model specific.  A constructive countable-history
Livsic theorem supplies a bounded right inverse and closed coboundary range for
Sinai.  The hard-sphere annihilator follows from B3's closed-range theorem and
includes balance gauges, collision invariants, endpoints, and conservation
constraints.

Changing-filtration convergence now contains joint finite-dimensional
conditional laws, compact containment, and an Aldous stopping-time modulus for
the optional-projection processes.  A positive optional projection is not
called a Cameron--Martin exponential automatically: the limiting observation
is first represented by its nondegenerate innovation Brownian motion, the
observation and innovation filtrations are proved equal, and only then is the
martingale representation theorem applied.  Bracket convergence and uniform
integrability yield the Girsanov and Lipschitz-BSDE stability statements.

Finally, a contraction morphism contains only concrete state, approximation,
source, and form data.  Rate, pressure, covariance, and memory commutation are
proved consequences, with A3, B2, and C1 supplying nontrivial examples.
