\begin{abstract}
Weighted strict duality is defined as the exact pullback of the standard
strict topology under division by the weight, giving the finite weighted
Radon dual and Mackey property.  The hard-sphere annihilator includes the full
linearized collision adjoint.  Equality of pressure functionals is connected
to periodic data by a separate equilibrium-localisation and Livsic theorem.
Optional projections require convergence of the prediction process and
uniform tightness of semimartingale characteristics, while Brownian likelihood
exponentials require strict positivity and equivalence.
\end{abstract}
