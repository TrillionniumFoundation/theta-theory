\begin{abstract}
We separate weighted strict Radon duality, strong spectral response, and
closed form realizations.  All varying Hilbert spaces are transported to a
fixed reference space and the metric connection is included in every
response derivative.  Dissipativity makes the finite-dimensional compressed
resolvent strictly accretive in the right half-plane; resolved vectors outside
the generator domain are handled by a form-level Schur complement.  We prove
model-specific pressure-functional rigidity, changing-filtration optional
projection convergence, and a category whose typed contractions preserve
rates, covariances, cotangents, and memory.
\end{abstract}
