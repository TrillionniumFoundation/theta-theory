\begin{abstract}We prove weighted mixed-strict duality on arbitrary Polish spaces without a compact exhaustion, identify the annihilator of the full density-contact tangent including cycle directions, and localize equilibria through finite-amplitude pressure charts. Prediction is placed in a fixed stopped-path space, with separate likelihood theorems for discrete, point-process, and Brownian observations.\end{abstract}
