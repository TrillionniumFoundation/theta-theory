- `references.bib`

## Requested specialist checks

1. Check the parabolic implicit-function theorem and its regularity window.
2. Check the static and dynamic tangent-law characterization.
3. Check convergence of exponentially tilted microscopic laws.
4. Check the BMO/uniform-integrability justification of Girsanov.
5. Check that PPDE, game, and 2BSDE branches are optional and platform typed.
