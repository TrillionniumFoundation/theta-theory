\section{Unbounded path observables and same-roof transport}
\label{sec:v53-same-roof-transport}
We strengthen the path topology without changing the roof conditioning.
The new exponential moment pays the tails of unbounded path tests.
It does not bound the height of a density in the roof coordinate.

For a path $x$ put $r(x)=\|x\|_\infty$. For $p\ge1$ let
$\mathcal F_p$ be the continuous real functions on path space with
\[
 |F(x)|\le1+r(x)^p,\qquad
 |F(x)-F(y)|\le
 (1+r(x)^{p-1}+r(y)^{p-1})\|x-y\|_\infty.
\]
When $p=1$ take $r^0=1$. Write
$\|\sigma\|_{p,*}=\sup_{F\in\mathcal F_p}|\sigma(F)|$ when the
variation has a finite $p$th moment. This is a weighted test norm,
not path-space total variation. A countable norming subclass is
obtained from the pointwise topology on a fixed countable dense set
of paths: the displayed value and difference bounds form a separable
metrizable subspace of a countable product. Continuity extends its
dense approximants to all paths, and the growth bound permits dominated
convergence. Thus the kernel norms used below are measurable and can
be assigned a common roof-null set, as for $\mathrm{BL}^*$.

\begin{lemma}[Exponential tails upgrade a local test norm]
\label{lem:v53-growth-upgrade}
Let $\sigma_u$ be a measurable signed-measure kernel and let $\pi$ be
a probability or a finite measure of uniformly bounded mass. Suppose
\[
 e=\int\|\sigma_u\|_{\mathrm{BL}^*}\,d\pi(u),\qquad
 \int\int e^{a r(x)}\,d|\sigma_u|(x)\,d\pi(u)\le M
\]
for fixed $a,M>0$. For $0<e\le1/2$ and each fixed $p\ge1$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v53-growth-upgrade}
 \int\|\sigma_u\|_{p,*}\,d\pi(u)
              \le C_{p,a,M}\,e(1+|\log e|^p).
\end{equation}
At $e=0$ the left side is zero. The same estimate holds for
jointly Borel functions $\Psi(u,x)$ whose path sections belong to
$\mathcal F_p$, including a selection made after $u$ is observed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose a Lipschitz cutoff $\chi_R(x)$ which equals one on $r\le R$,
vanishes on $r\ge2R$ and has Lipschitz constant at most $R^{-1}$,
where $R\ge1$. For every $F\in\mathcal F_p$, the product $F\chi_R$
has bounded-Lipschitz norm at most $C_pR^p$, uniformly in $F$.
The difference has absolute value at most
$(1+r^p)\one_{r>R}\le C_{p,a}e^{-aR/2}e^{ar}$.
Consequently the left side of \eqref{eq:v53-growth-upgrade} is at
most $C_pR^pe+C_{p,a}Me^{-aR/2}$. Take
$R=\max(1,2a^{-1}\log(1/e))$. This proves the estimate, without
choosing a test before the roof value. For $e=0$, the signed measure
is zero almost everywhere, since bounded-Lipschitz tests determine
finite measures. Joint Borel measurability and the countable norming
class justify evaluation and integration for the stated selectors.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Polynomial-growth path-valued raw local laws]
\label{thm:v53-unbounded-raw-paths}
For each fixed $h,M_0>0$ and $p\ge1$, uniformly on $|Z(t)|\le M_0$,
\begin{align}
 \frac1h\int_t^{t+h}
 \|P_{m,R}^{n,k}(u)\mathsf Q_{m,R}^{n,k,u}
                      -G_{m,R}^{n,k}(u)\mathsf W_R\|_{p,*}\,du&\to0,
                                      \label{eq:v53-unbounded-collision}\\
 \frac1h\int_t^{t+h}
 \|P_{m,R}^{n,k}(u)\mathsf R_{m,R}^{n,k,u}
                      -G_{m,R}^{n,k}(u)\mathsf V_R\|_{p,*}\,du&\to0.
                                      \label{eq:v53-unbounded-return}
\end{align}
No positive denominator is needed. Both conclusions are uniform for
jointly Borel roof-dependent selectors in $\mathcal F_p$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The bounded-Lipschitz errors tend uniformly to zero by
\eqref{eq:v52-path-local-variation} and
\eqref{eq:v52-return-path-variation}. For each difference measure,
its variation is bounded by the sum of its two positive terms,
using $|G|$ for the reference term. The first term has uniformly
bounded exponential moment on the translated roof interval by
Theorem~\ref{thm:v53-exponential-maximal}. The second does also:
$G$ is uniformly bounded, the covariance families are bounded, and
Gaussian bridges have a common exponential supremum moment. For the
last assertion one may repeat the dyadic argument above using the
Gaussian increment moments, whose factorial bounds are uniform on a
bounded covariance family. Apply
Lemma~\ref{lem:v53-growth-upgrade} with $du/h$ restricted to the
window. Its constants are uniform in all the indicated targets.

The central enlargement is explicit: for $u\in[t,t+h]$,
$|Z(u)|\le M_0+h/\sqrt m$. This pays the return-clock estimate with
one fixed enlarged compact set. The raw reference is the signed
$G$, not $G_+$; no conditional probability has been introduced.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A transport estimate for conditional kernels}
For probabilities $Q,W$ on path space define
\[
 W_p(Q,W)^p=\inf_{\gamma\in\operatorname{Cpl}(Q,W)}
           \int\|x-y\|_\infty^p\,d\gamma(x,y),\qquad p\ge1.
\]
This distance uses the original, unbounded supremum metric. We use
only bounded-cost Kantorovich duality from \cite[Chapters 5--6]{Villani},
and give the tail estimate explicitly.

\begin{lemma}[From mean bounded-Lipschitz error to mean transport cost]
\label{lem:v53-transport-upgrade}
Let $Q_u,W_u$ be Borel probability kernels, let $\pi$ be a probability,
and suppose
\[
 \int\{Q_u(e^{ar})+W_u(e^{ar})\}\,d\pi(u)\le M.
\]
Put $e=\int d_{\rm BL}(Q_u,W_u)\,d\pi(u)$. For every fixed finite
$p\ge1$ and $0<e\le1/2$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v53-transport-upgrade}
 \int W_p(Q_u,W_u)^p\,d\pi(u)
                         \le C_{p,a,M}e(1+|\log e|^p).
\end{equation}
The left side is zero when $e=0$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The bounded metric $\bar d(x,y)=\min(\|x-y\|_\infty,1)$ has
transport cost at most $d_{\rm BL}(Q_u,W_u)$. Indeed every
$1$-Lipschitz test for $\bar d$ has oscillation at most one; after
subtracting a constant it is in the bounded-Lipschitz class used here.
Bounded-cost duality yields the assertion.

Take an arbitrarily accurate coupling for this bounded cost. On
$\|x-y\|_\infty\le R$, $R\ge1$, the $p$th power of the distance
is bounded by $R^p\bar d(x,y)$. On its complement,
\[
 \|x-y\|_\infty^p\one_{\|x-y\|_\infty>R}
 \le C_{p,a}e^{-aR/4}e^{a\|x-y\|_\infty/2}
 \le \tfrac12C_{p,a}e^{-aR/4}(e^{ar(x)}+e^{ar(y)}).
\]
The second inequality uses the triangle inequality and the arithmetic--
geometric mean inequality. Taking the infimum for the unbounded cost
and then removing the bounded-cost approximation gives a pointwise
upper bound. Integrate it in $u$ and choose
$R=\max(1,4a^{-1}\log(1/e))$. This proves
\eqref{eq:v53-transport-upgrade}. No measurable choice of couplings
is needed: the pointwise inequality is integrated after taking its
infima. Wasserstein costs are Borel for Borel probability kernels
on a Polish space, with value infinity allowed before the moment
hypothesis is imposed. If $e=0$, the two kernels agree almost
everywhere and the conclusion follows directly.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Same-roof bridges in every finite transport order]
\label{thm:v53-same-roof-Wp}
Fix the target class \eqref{eq:v52-target-class}. For every finite
$p\ge1$, uniformly in that class,
\begin{align}
 \int_{I_t}W_p(\mathsf Q_{m,R}^{n,k,u},\mathsf W_R)^p\,\pi_m(du)&\to0,
                                      \label{eq:v53-collision-Wp}\\
 \int_{I_t}W_p(\mathsf R_{m,R}^{n,k,u},\mathsf V_R)^p\,\pi_m(du)&\to0.
                                      \label{eq:v53-return-Wp}
\end{align}
The original kernels retain the exact labels and their own roof value.
Their exponential path moments are uniformly bounded in mean under
$\pi_m$. If the stronger condition $G(u)\ge d_0>0$ holds almost
everywhere on $I_t$, both conclusions also hold under
$\widehat\pi_m$. No such reference-law transport assertion is made
under the integrated reference lower bound alone.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The true event has $F_m=\int_{I_t}P\ge dh/2$ eventually.
Dividing \eqref{eq:v53-return-exponential} by $F_m/h$ gives the
uniform exponential moment of the conditional path mixture under
$\pi_m$, and hence of the same-roof kernels in mean. Gaussian bridge
exponential moments are uniform as above. The two mean
bounded-Lipschitz errors tend uniformly to zero by
Theorem~\ref{thm:v52-same-roof-mean}. Apply
Lemma~\ref{lem:v53-transport-upgrade} separately to the two kernels.
It supplies the explicit modulus in the mean bounded-Lipschitz
error, but not a rate in $m$.

Under the additional pointwise reference floor,
Theorem~\ref{thm:v51-conditional-minorization} gives
$\pi_m\ge\alpha_m\widehat\pi_m$, $\alpha_m\to1$.
Thus the reference means of the nonnegative transport costs are at
most $\alpha_m^{-1}$ times their true-roof means. This proves the
last assertion without using total variation to integrate an
unbounded function. Under an integrated lower bound alone the roof
TV comparison only transfers bounded costs; we do not use it for
these transport costs. The extra floor also ensures that kernel
versions agree up to null sets for both roof measures.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Conditional covariance kernels and path maxima]
\label{cor:v53-conditional-moments}
Let $(Q^u,W,C_R)$ denote either
$(\mathsf Q^u,\mathsf W_R,\Omega_R)$ or
$(\mathsf R^u,\mathsf V_R,D_R)$, and put
$\mu^u(s)=\int x(s)\,dQ^u(x)$. On the preceding target class,
\begin{align*}
 \int\sup_s|\mu^u(s)|^2\,d\pi_m(u)&\to0,\\
 \int\sup_{s,t}\left\|
   \operatorname{Cov}_{Q^u}(x(s),x(t))-(s\wedge t-st)C_R
                         \right\|\,d\pi_m(u)&\to0.
\end{align*}
For every finite $p\ge1$,
\[
 \int\left|Q^u(r^p)-W(r^p)\right|\,d\pi_m(u)\to0.
\]
These conclusions may also be read after jointly Borel roof-dependent
choices of observation times. Under the pointwise reference floor
of Theorem~\ref{thm:v53-same-roof-Wp}, they hold under
$\widehat\pi_m$ as well.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
A path coupling bounds $\sup_s|\mu^u(s)|$ by its mean path distance,
and hence by $W_2(Q^u,W)$. The difference of second moment kernels,
uniformly in both times, is bounded by the coupling expectation of
$(r(x)+r(y))\|x-y\|_\infty$. Cauchy--Schwarz first in the coupling
and then in $u$, the uniform second moments, and
\eqref{eq:v53-collision-Wp}--\eqref{eq:v53-return-Wp} prove its
convergence. Subtracting $\mu^u(s)\otimes\mu^u(t)$ proves the
covariance assertion. For $r^p$ use the same coupling argument with
a higher finite moment, or truncate it as in
Lemma~\ref{lem:v53-growth-upgrade}. Its growth and local Lipschitz
bounds are uniform in the roof. The supremum over times already
precedes roof integration, so a jointly Borel time selection costs
nothing further. Domination transfers these nonnegative errors to
the reference law under the stated extra floor.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{The unresolved scalar height is unchanged.}
All the new bounds retain the actual incidence and clearance source.
They control large path amplitudes in the original microscopic event,
not concentration of its scalar mass in a narrow roof set.
Neither exponential path integrability nor any of
\eqref{eq:v53-unbounded-collision}--\eqref{eq:v53-return-Wp} bounds
$\|\mathfrak b_{B,m}\|_\infty$. The two-sided pointwise raw theorem
and the unconditional uniform pointwise bridge therefore still require
\eqref{eq:v51-positive-criterion}. The arithmetic transition kernel
and its fixed-radius residue remain in every raw main term. No
forward roof likelihood, entropy estimate, path-space total variation,
or assertion at every externally prescribed roof is inferred here.
