\section{Relative angular stability of the exact physical decisions}
\label{sec:v67-angular-stability}
We retain the original circular Lorentz table, section and exact labels.
Fix $\chi,\varepsilon$ as in Section~\ref{sec:v66-physical-angular}, and
restrict attention to its normal critical words with a competing-clearance
seam. The selected incidences satisfy the stated taper; selected grazing
is not included. Write $\lambda=(n,k)$, $\beta_{z,\lambda}=J_z\theta_{z,\lambda}$,
and $a_{z,\lambda}(u)=A_{z,n,k}^{\varepsilon}(u)/(2\pi)$.
The new estimate is relative to $\beta_{z,\lambda}$, not to the full
reversible coefficient $J_z$. This distinction permits a positive
absorption argument without a concentration theorem for the full
reversible trace.

\subsection{A budget from the primitive scalar margins}
At each fixed word choose a closed disk $|y|\le\bar r_z$, contained
strictly inside the Morse chart and inside the saturated disk of
Lemma~\ref{lem:v66-uniform-guard}. Reduce it, if necessary, so that the
finite Boolean description of Lemma~\ref{lem:v65-margin-description}
is valid throughout it. This radius is part of the data; it is not
asserted to be uniform in the word. In particular no competing disk,
section edge or inactive first-hit condition may be omitted unless its
constant sign has been verified on this disk.

Let $\mathscr F_z$ be the resulting finite list of $C^2$ scalar margins
in Morse coordinates, including all physical and section decisions.
For $f\in\mathscr F_z$ put
\[
 b_f=f(0),\qquad a_f=Df(0),\qquad A_f=|a_f|,\qquad
 L_f=\tfrac12\sup_{|y|\le\bar r_z}\|D^2f(y)\|.
\]
Every active margin, meaning $b_f=0$, has $A_f>0$ by the cited lemma.
Set
\begin{align}
 \rho_z&=\min\left\{\bar r_z,
       \min_{b_f\ne0}\frac{|b_f|}{2(A_f+L_f\bar r_z)}\right\},
                                                        \label{eq:v67-inactive-radius}\\
 \kappa_z&=\sum_{b_f=0}\frac{L_f}{A_f}.   \label{eq:v67-active-budget}
\end{align}
An empty minimum or a quotient with zero denominator in
\eqref{eq:v67-inactive-radius} is $+\infty$. Thus $\rho_z>0$ and
$\kappa_z<\infty$ at every fixed word. These budgets are unchanged
by a positive constant rescaling of an individual margin. The sum is
over the actual scalar tests, not over words or output labels. It is
not claimed to have a uniform bound for all collision counts.

For $\theta_{z,\lambda}>0$ define the angular condition number
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v67-condition-number}
 \mathfrak a_{z,\lambda}
       =\max\{\rho_z^{-1},\kappa_z/\theta_{z,\lambda}\}.
\end{equation}
Labels with $\theta_{z,\lambda}=0$ have zero atomic coefficient and
are not assigned a finite condition number. Their nonlinear physical
source need not vanish and will remain in the positive complement.
Exact affine active margins have $\kappa_z=0$; in that case arbitrarily
small positive angular fractions cause no loss in
\eqref{eq:v67-condition-number}.

\begin{lemma}[Homogeneous threshold estimate]
\label{lem:v67-homogeneous-threshold}
If $f(0)=0$, $|Df(0)|=A>0$ and
$|f(r\omega)-rDf(0)\cdot\omega|\le Lr^2$, the normalized angular
measure of the sign-disagreement set is at most
$\min(1,Lr/A)$. No angle between different margin gradients enters this
estimate. If $b_f\ne0$ and $r<\rho_z$, the sign of $f(r\omega)$ is
its constant sign at zero for every $\omega$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Sign disagreement for an active margin implies
$|Df(0)\cdot\omega|\le Lr$. After rotation the angular fraction is
at most $2\pi^{-1}\arcsin(\min(1,Lr/A))$, which is at most
$\min(1,Lr/A)$: convexity places $\arcsin x$ below its chord
$\pi x/2$ on $[0,1]$. For an inactive margin, Taylor's estimate gives
\[
 |f(r\omega)-b_f|\le A_fr+L_fr^2
       \le r(A_f+L_f\bar r_z)<|b_f|/2.
\]
This proves the second assertion. Strict or half-open conventions
alter only finitely many tangent directions in the active comparison.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Relative angular error with exact labels]
\label{thm:v67-relative-angular}
For $r=\sqrt{2u}<\rho_z$, the exact physical angular fractions satisfy
\begin{align}
 |a_{z,\lambda}(u)-\theta_{z,\lambda}|
       &\le\min(1,\kappa_zr),                    \label{eq:v67-absolute-angular}\\
 (\theta_{z,\lambda}-a_{z,\lambda}(u))_+
       &\le\min(\theta_{z,\lambda},\kappa_zr),  \label{eq:v67-one-sided-angular}\\
 \sum_\lambda|a_{z,\lambda}(u)-\theta_{z,\lambda}|
       &\le2\min(1,\kappa_zr).                  \label{eq:v67-vector-angular}
\end{align}
Consequently, for every fixed $K>0$, whenever
$\mathfrak a_{z,\lambda}\le K$ and $r<K^{-1}$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v67-relative-angular}
 (1-Kr)\theta_{z,\lambda}
       \le a_{z,\lambda}(u)
       \le(1+Kr)\theta_{z,\lambda}.
\end{equation}
These estimates concern the original nonlinear masks. The tangent
masks supply the reference fraction only; they are not substituted
for the physical source.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The inactive signs are fixed by Lemma~\ref{lem:v67-homogeneous-threshold}.
The union of active sign-disagreement sets has normalized angular
measure at most $\min(1,\kappa_zr)$. Outside it the entire Boolean
input vector agrees with its tangent vector. In particular the first-hit
choice, the initial section decision, all occupation bits at
$0,\ldots,m-1$, and the separate terminal decision at $m$ agree.
The original visit rule therefore gives the same exact output label.
On the disk under consideration the first-clearance multiplier equals
one wherever a physical label is assigned. Its image mark is still
$j+1$. Thus $a_{z,\lambda}$ is precisely the normalized measure of
this nonlinear output set.

Integrating the two indicators proves
\eqref{eq:v67-absolute-angular}; their nonnegativity improves the
one-sided estimate to \eqref{eq:v67-one-sided-angular}. At each angle
both output vectors are zero or a single unit vector. Their $\ell^1$
distance is at most two, proving \eqref{eq:v67-vector-angular} without
a label-cardinality factor. Finally $r<K^{-1}\le\rho_z$ and
$\kappa_z\le K\theta_{z,\lambda}$ give
\eqref{eq:v67-relative-angular}.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{The role of the offsets.}
The linear-in-$r$ estimate uses homogeneous active thresholds only.
Inactive thresholds are kept fixed by the explicit radius
\eqref{eq:v67-inactive-radius}; near-active offsets can make this
radius small. It would be incorrect to apply the homogeneous estimate
uniformly to arbitrary translated thresholds. The affine
$u^{1/4}$ bound of Section~\ref{sec:v65-affine-caustic} retains its
original, different scope. No lower bound on an individual gradient,
no separation of tangent directions and no collision-count bound on
$\mathfrak a$ has been introduced silently.
