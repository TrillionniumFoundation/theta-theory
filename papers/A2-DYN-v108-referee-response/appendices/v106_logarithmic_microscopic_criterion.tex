\section{A logarithmic microscopic-residence criterion}
\label{sec:v106-logarithmic}
The following criterion concerns the \emph{original positive paired measure},
not an auxiliary volume collar or an integrated surrogate. It isolates a
strictly weaker sufficient hypothesis than any positive inverse-power
residence insertion while retaining the order of the collision and
microscopic limits.

For a fixed outer band $B$ and fixed original exact labels, let
$d\pi_{m,R,n,k}^{\varepsilon,+}$ denote the nonnegative paired
physical source of the preceding theorem and let $\ell>0$ be its
actual maximal regular residence. We use exactly the same original
pushforward to the roof as in the definition of
$\mathsf U_B^{\rm micro}$. All integrals below are with respect to this
same source, including its physical normalization. Fix a deterministic
scale $h_0>0$, independent of the collision count, and define
\[
  L_{h_0}(\ell)=\log\!\bigl(eh_0/\ell\bigr)
       \quad(0<\ell\le h_0).
\]
Write $\mathsf W_{\gamma,h_0}$ for the roof pushforward of
$L_{h_0}(\ell)^\gamma\mathbf1_{\{0<\ell\le h_0\}}\,d\pi^{\varepsilon,+}$
restricted to the \emph{same unmatched original physical source}
as $\mathsf U_B^{\rm micro}$.

\begin{theorem}[Positive microscopic localization by logarithmic insertion]
\label{thm:v106-logarithmic}
For every $\gamma>0$ and every $0<h<h_0$ one has, at every finite
collision count and for every original exact label, the pointwise
inequality of positive roof densities
\[
  0\le \mathsf U_{B}^{\rm micro}(t;h)
    \le \bigl[\log(eh_0/h)\bigr]^{-\gamma}
          \mathsf W_{\gamma,h_0}(t).
\]
Here $\mathsf U_{B}^{\rm micro}(t;h)$ is the actual unmatched
positive source on $0<\ell<h$; the restriction is made before
the roof pushforward and no original test is dropped.
Consequently, if at the fixed $B,h_0,\gamma$ one establishes the
\emph{collision-uniform original-source insertion estimate}
\[
  K_{B,M,\gamma,h_0}:=
  \limsup_{m\to\infty}\sup_{R,n,k}m^2
    \operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{t\in\mathcal C_{m,R,n,k}(M)}
       \mathsf W_{\gamma,h_0}(t)<\infty ,
\]
then
\[
 \mathcal H_M(\mathsf U_B^{\rm micro}(\cdot;h))
 \le K_{B,M,\gamma,h_0}
           [\log(eh_0/h)]^{-\gamma}.
\]
In particular the ordered microscopic source height vanishes
as $h\downarrow0$ at fixed $B$, \emph{provided the displayed
insertion estimate is proved for the original Lorentz source}.
No such insertion estimate is asserted here.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
On $0<\ell<h$, monotonicity of the logarithm gives
$L_{h_0}(\ell)^\gamma\ge\log(eh_0/h)^\gamma$.
Multiply by the original nonnegative paired density and retain
every endpoint, section, occupation and displacement test. Pushing
forward the resulting inequality by the same measurable roof map
preserves order without any Jacobian substitution or count of
collision words. This establishes the first claim at finite count.
Essential supremum, original-label supremum and collision limsup
are monotone and positively homogeneous, proving the second claim.
The logarithmic factor vanishes only after this ordered bound, so
no collision-dependent cutoff or spectral band has been introduced.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Strict weakness and its limitation]
\label{rem:v106-power}
For every $\sigma>0$ and $\gamma>0$, the function
$x^\sigma[\log(e/x)]^\gamma$ is bounded on $(0,1]$.
Therefore, on $(0,h_0]$, an estimate for the genuine inverse-power
insertion $(h_0/\ell)^\sigma$ implies the logarithmic hypothesis,
up to a constant depending on $\sigma,\gamma$. The reverse
implication does not hold for arbitrary positive measures: a mass
concentrated at $\ell_j=h_0e^{-j}$ with weight proportional to
$j^{-\gamma-2}$ has a finite logarithmic $\gamma$-moment and
an infinite inverse-power $\sigma$-moment for every $\sigma>0$.
Thus the logarithmic criterion is a mathematically weaker route
to the same unaltered pointwise endpoint. Neither criterion is
proved for the complete original Lorentz source here.
\end{remark}
