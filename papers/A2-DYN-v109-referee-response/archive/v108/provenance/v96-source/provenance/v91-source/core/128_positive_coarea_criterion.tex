\section{A positive coarea criterion with exact labels}
\label{sec:v60-positive-coarea}
Pressure contact concerns a characteristic branch. A small positive
source requires a separate estimate at the density level. We isolate
such an estimate before applying it to the perturbed continuous roof.
All labels in the following proposition are retained under pushforward.
The criterion does not identify a mass estimate with a height estimate.

Fix a time $m$, a parameter $\lambda$, and an exact auxiliary label
$\iota$. Suppose the original source with label $\iota$ is disintegrated
into countably many words $w$, with masses $c_w\ge0$, a scalar coordinate
$y\in[0,1]$, and conditional densities $r_w(y)\le D$. On a word let
$q_w\in\Z^d$ be its further exact label and let its scalar observation be
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v60-abstract-roof}
 T_w(y)=\ell_\lambda(q_w)+u_w(y),\qquad
 |u_w|\le H,\qquad |u_w'|\ge\eta>0.
\end{equation}
Assume $u_w$ is continuously differentiable and monotone on its word.
An additive deterministic time shift may be included in $\ell_\lambda$.
All constants below are independent of $m,\lambda,\iota$.

\begin{proposition}[Positive source height from labelled coarea]
\label{prop:v60-coarea-height}
Let $a_m>0$ and $\delta_m\downarrow0$. The following two sufficient
criteria bound the height of a positive source at its original labels.

First, suppose an event $E_s$ is covered by a collection $\mathcal C_s$
of word cylinders and
\begin{align}
 \sum_{C\in\mathcal C_s}c(C)&\le K(s+\delta_m),\notag\\
 \sum_{\substack{w\subset C\\
       |\ell_\lambda(q_w)-t|\le H}}c_w
       &\le K a_m c(C)\quad(t\in\R),
                                                        \label{eq:v60-cylinder-height-input}
\end{align}
where $c(C)=\sum_{w\subset C}c_w$. Overlap is allowed in the cover.
Then its unnormalized density $p^{E_s}_{m,\lambda,\iota}$ satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v60-coarea-height-output}
 \|p^{E_s}_{m,\lambda,\iota}\|_\infty
                \le D\eta^{-1}K^2a_m(s+\delta_m).
\end{equation}

Alternatively suppose each word has a statistic $b_w\in[0,1]$, and
nonnegative envelopes $e_m(q)$ obey
\begin{align}
 \sum_{w:q_w=q,\ b_w\in J}c_w
     &\le K e_m(q)(|J\cap[0,1]|+\delta_m),\notag\\
 \sup_t\sum_{q:|\ell_\lambda(q)-t|\le H}e_m(q)&\le K a_m
                                                        \label{eq:v60-endpoint-height-input}
\end{align}
for every interval $J$. If $T_w(y)=t$ and $(w,y)\in E_s$ imply that
$b_w$ lies in a union of at most $K$ intervals of total length at most
$K(s+\delta_m)$, depending only on $(q_w,t,\lambda,\iota)$, then
\eqref{eq:v60-coarea-height-output} holds with a changed constant.
Every bounded insertion supported on $E_s$ is controlled by the same
bound times its supremum norm. A finite union of such sources is
controlled by the sum of the bounds.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For almost every $t$ the contribution of word $w$ is
\[
 c_w\frac{r_w(y_w(t))}{|u_w'(y_w(t))|}
                    \one_{\{t\in T_w([0,1])\}},
\]
where $y_w(t)$ is its unique inverse. The change-of-variables formula
on each word, followed by Tonelli, proves that the sum is the density
of the original pushforward, including all its positive components.
It is bounded above by $D\eta^{-1}$ times the sum of the word masses
which can meet the roof value. Such a word necessarily satisfies
$|\ell_\lambda(q_w)-t|\le H$.

For the first criterion, enlarge the source to its cylinder cover,
apply the second line of \eqref{eq:v60-cylinder-height-input} on each
cylinder, and then sum its first line. For the second, group the same
positive coarea sum by $q_w$, use the endpoint intervals and the first
line of \eqref{eq:v60-endpoint-height-input}, and sum the second line.
No signed error is used in either argument. Domination at the source
proves the insertion and union assertions. Countably many null sets
from the wordwise changes of variables have zero Lebesgue measure;
no exceptional roof set of positive measure is deleted.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Why the exact-label hypotheses matter.}
The cylinder bound in \eqref{eq:v60-cylinder-height-input} concerns the
remaining local constraint \emph{after} a cylinder has been fixed.
Its conclusion is not supplied by a bound on $c(C)$ alone. Similarly,
\eqref{eq:v60-endpoint-height-input} is an exact-label estimate for
intervals depending on the roof value, not an unconditioned marginal
bound for $b_w$. Uniformity in an auxiliary exact label must be proved,
not acquired by summing that label away.

For the Lorentz source in Corollary~\ref{cor:v51-positive-criterion},
a version of these hypotheses at scale $a_m=m^{-2}$, with the original
$(n,k,m,R)$ fixed and with the complete incidence and next-collision
clearance sources, would yield the two required heights. The results
on local variation in Sections~\ref{sec:v51-positive-remainder} and
\ref{sec:v59-stable-coarea} do not provide those hypotheses. In
particular, \eqref{eq:v60-abstract-roof} is not asserted at Lorentz
grazing or at a change of the first physical hit. The application below
proves every input on its stated original deterministic source.
