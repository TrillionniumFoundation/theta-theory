\section{A positive comparison trace on the unchanged physical record}
\label{sec:v69-comparison-trace}
The division by the center value of a clearance guard is unnecessary
for an upper height estimate. The appropriate comparison measure is
the original physical measure on the selected itinerary, with the
clearance multiplier omitted only in that upper comparison. In
particular, omitting this multiplier does not omit a first-hit test,
a section decision, or an exact integer label.

Fix $0<\chi\le1/10$ and $0<\varepsilon\le\chi/4$. Retain the
selected-contact chart, $r_\chi$, $h_\chi=r_\chi^2/2$, $K_\chi$,
$J_z$, and the one-hot physical indicator $B_{z,\lambda}$, where
$\lambda=(n,k)$, from Sections~\ref{sec:v66-physical-angular}
and~\ref{sec:v68-angular-germs}. Write
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v69-comparison-density}
 \widehat b_{z,\lambda}(t_z+u)=
 \int_0^{2\pi}\widetilde w_z(\sqrt{2u}\,\omega_\phi)
              B_{z,\lambda}(\sqrt{2u}\,\omega_\phi)\,d\phi,
 \qquad 0<u<h_\chi.
\end{equation}
This is an unnormalized density of an actual physical source. The
normalization in $\widetilde w_z$ already includes $c_*^{-1}$.
Nonphysical points on the selected continuation still have weight
zero. The density is not asserted to equal the first-clearance source.

\begin{lemma}[Physical comparison and normalization]
\label{lem:v69-positive-comparison}
On a common coarea representative, for almost every $0<u<h_\chi$,
with $r=\sqrt{2u}$,
\begin{align}
 0\le b_{z,\lambda}^{\varepsilon,\mathrm{clr}}(t_z+u)
       &\le\widehat b_{z,\lambda}(t_z+u),
                                      \label{eq:v69-source-domination}\\
 e^{-K_\chi r}J_z\ell_{z,\lambda}(r)
       \le\widehat b_{z,\lambda}(t_z+u)
       &\le e^{K_\chi r}J_z\ell_{z,\lambda}(r).
                                      \label{eq:v69-unguarded-profile}
\end{align}
More precisely, if $g_z=D_z^\varepsilon(0)$ and
$L_g=C_g\varepsilon^{-1}$, then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v69-absolute-guard-comparison}
 b_{z,\lambda}^{\varepsilon,\mathrm{clr}}(t_z+u)
 \le \min\{1,g_z+L_g\sqrt{2u}\}\,
                    \widehat b_{z,\lambda}(t_z+u).
\end{equation}
The assertions also hold when $g_z=0$ and when the guard is infinitely
flat at the center. They use no analytic property of the guard.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The exact coarea identity \eqref{eq:v66-exact-angular-coarea} contains
one additional factor $D_z^\varepsilon$ relative to
\eqref{eq:v69-comparison-density}. Its values lie in $[0,1]$.
The relative physical-density bound used in
Theorem~\ref{thm:v66-nonlinear-profile}, and
$\int B_{z,\lambda}\,d\phi=2\pi\ell_{z,\lambda}$, prove the first
two assertions. In polar coarea the arclength factor $r$ cancels the
derivative of $r^2/2$; no radial power is introduced. Finally
\eqref{eq:v66-guard-lipschitz} gives
$D_z^\varepsilon(r\omega)\le\min\{1,g_z+L_gr\}$ pointwise.
Multiply by the nonnegative physical indicator before integrating.
No division by $g_z$ occurs.
\end{proof}

For any subfamily $\mathcal Z$ of the disjoint physical charts put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v69-comparison-trace}
 \widehat q_{z,s;\lambda}=\frac1s\int_0^s
                       \widehat b_{z,\lambda}(t_z+u)\,du,
 \qquad
 \widehat{\mathcal Q}_{m,s;\lambda,R}
        =\sum_{z\in\mathcal Z}\widehat q_{z,s;\lambda}\delta_{t_z}.
\end{equation}
A subfamily may depend on $m,R,\lambda$ and on parameters fixed before
the collision limit. No regularity of its selection is required: the
following estimate is a positive comparison with a single physical
event, not a spectral estimate on the selecting multiplier.

\begin{theorem}[Ordered unguarded physical collar trace]
\label{thm:v69-comparison-collar}
For every fixed $0<s<h_\chi$ and every fixed interval length $w>0$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v69-comparison-collar}
 \limsup_{m\to\infty}\sup_{R,\lambda,a}m^2
       \widehat{\mathcal Q}_{m,s;\lambda,R}([a,a+w])
                       \le C(w+s).
\end{equation}
The constant is independent of the chart subfamily, the exact labels,
$s,\chi,\varepsilon$ and the number of coincident critical values.
The estimate remains true when the atoms are multiplied by arbitrary
numbers in $[0,1]$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Each physical chart with $t_z\in[a,a+w]$, restricted to
$0<F_z-t_z<s$, has actual roof in $[a,a+w+s]$. Both endpoint
momenta have modulus at most $C_0\sqrt s$ by
Theorem~\ref{thm:v66-uniform-morse}. Different physical itineraries
are disjoint; the uniqueness of the normal critical point in each
selected word is the one used in
\eqref{eq:v66-complete-positive-partition}. Consequently
\[
 s\widehat{\mathcal Q}_{m,s;\lambda,R}([a,a+w])
 \le\nu_R^*\bigl(E^{(C_0\sqrt s)}_{n,k,m,R}
                                     (a;[0,w+s])\bigr).
\]
The initial, occupation, terminal and displacement tests on the left
are precisely those on the right. The comparison adds physical
points but never assigns measure to nonphysical continuations.
The choice of $r_0$ already made in
Theorem~\ref{thm:v66-ordered-collar-trace} ensures
$C_0\sqrt s<1/4$. Apply
Lemma~\ref{lem:v45-two-strip-local} at these fixed widths. Its
bound is $Cs(w+s)$ after multiplication by $m^2$ and the ordered
limsup. Divide by $s$. Multipliers in $[0,1]$ only decrease the
positive trace. The same argument bounds every selected subfamily
by the same event and therefore introduces no counting multiplier.
\end{proof}

The trace $\widehat{\mathcal Q}$ differs from the guarded trace
$\mathcal Q$ in \eqref{eq:v66-collar-trace-definition}. It also
differs from the full reversible atomic measure: its atoms are
averages of the actual, exactly labelled physical source over a
positive-width collar. Neither the physical angular indicator nor
that width has been removed.
