\section{Exact microscopic remainder of the original positive source}
\label{sec:v105-endpoint}
Retain the complete original short-source selector $H_B$ of
\eqref{eq:v104-schedule}. Let $\mathscr C_B$ be a Borel union
of disjoint physical critical-arm source sets already paid by
Corollary \ref{cor:v104-whole-arm-recovery}, and let
$\mathscr D_B$ be disjoint source sets satisfying Theorem
\ref{thm:v105-reach}, after first removing any membership in
$\mathscr C_B$. Both families have the corresponding actual reach,
physical overlap and outer balance costs. Choose
$0<h_B<\delta(B)$ using only the outer parameter $B$.
Define the nonnegative \emph{original} microscopic restriction
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v105-micro}
\mathsf U_B^{\rm micro}(t)=
T_{H_B\mathbf1_{(\mathscr C_B\cup\mathscr D_B)^c}
       \mathbf1_{\{0<\ell<h_B\}}}^{\varepsilon(B)}(t).
\end{equation}
This retains all endpoint types, original section tests,
occupation and displacement labels, the actual roof, the guard
and the physical normalization.

\begin{theorem}[Exact positive scalar reduction at any fixed resolution]
\label{thm:v105-original}
Assume that the old and adaptive selected families meet their
actual physical hypotheses and that their uniform outer costs
are at most $C\varepsilon(B)^{-1/32}$. Then, at the unchanged
outer schedule, for every such $h_B$ fixed before collision limsup,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v105-error}
\mathcal E_M\le
 \mathcal H_M(\mathsf U_B^{\rm micro})+C_MB^{-1/384}.
\end{equation}
The unrestricted original exact-label raw pointwise theorem is
equivalent to
\[
\mathcal H_M(\mathsf U_B^{\rm micro})\longrightarrow0
\qquad (B\longrightarrow\infty).
\]
The microscopic term includes all flat pulses, true reach failures,
singular critical extensions, nonstationary sheets and unbalanced
comparison deficits which have not otherwise been paid; none
is deleted or relabeled as a signed boundary current.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At each finite collision count, disjointization by first physical
membership yields the exact nonnegative identity
\begin{align*}
\mathsf U_B^{\rm rem}
={}&T_{H_B\mathbf1_{\mathscr C_B}}^{\varepsilon(B)}
  +T_{H_B\mathbf1_{\mathscr D_B}}^{\varepsilon(B)}\\
&+T_{H_B\mathbf1_{(\mathscr C_B\cup\mathscr D_B)^c}
          \mathbf1_{\{\ell\ge h_B\}}}^{\varepsilon(B)}
  +\mathsf U_B^{\rm micro}.
\end{align*}
The first term is paid by the inherited complete critical-arm
comparison; the second by Theorem \ref{thm:v105-reach}.
The third is paid by the full-source persistence estimate
\ref{thm:v105-persistence}, irrespective of its physical endpoint
class, because $H_B$ already selects $\ell\le\delta(B)$.
The first two ordered heights are $O(B^{-1/384})$, and the
third is $O(B^{-1/192})$. Apply subadditivity and the inherited
scalar bound \eqref{eq:v104-old-remainder} to obtain
\eqref{eq:v105-error}. For necessity use the inherited scalar
equivalence and $0\le\mathsf U_B^{\rm micro}\le
\mathsf U_B^{\rm rem}$. All widths and costs are fixed before
collision limsup. No cutoff is removed at a later collision count.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[A sufficient untruncated residence insertion]
\label{prop:v105-insertion}
Suppose for some $\sigma>0$ and every fixed $M$, the
following \emph{additional, not presently verified} original-source
bound is available with a constant independent of $B$,
collision count, radius and exact labels:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v105-needed}
\sup_{B\ge B_0}\limsup_m\sup_{R,n,k}m^2
\operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{t\in\mathcal C_{m,R,n,k}(M)}
\int c^{\varepsilon(B)}(\xi)H_B(\xi,t)
 \ell(\xi)^{-\sigma}\,
 \mathbf1_{\{0<\ell\le\delta(B)\}}
 \mathbf1_{\{a<t<b\}}\,d\theta(\xi)<\infty.
\end{equation}
Then the unchanged exact-label raw pointwise theorem follows.
The proposition is an implication; no estimate of the form
\eqref{eq:v105-needed} is claimed to have been proved for the
full Lorentz source.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
On $0<\ell<h_B$, $1\le h_B^\sigma\ell^{-\sigma}$.
Consequently $\mathcal H_M(\mathsf U_B^{\rm micro})
\le C_Mh_B^\sigma$. Choose $h_B\downarrow0$ from
the outer schedule; then apply Theorem \ref{thm:v105-original}.
This is the correct finite-count source insertion, not a
post-limit interpolation of fixed-width bounds.
\end{proof}
The task still to be proved is the vanishing of the
original positive shrinking-residence height, or a genuine
uniform finite-count source insertion strong enough to imply it.
No unrestricted denominator, same-roof conditional path
or forward likelihood conclusion is promoted before that
scalar proof.
