\begin{abstract}
Two strictly active endpoint-arclength density functions of a local
return channel determine the stationary two-flight action, both smooth
reflecting arcs, their gap and free-area normalization. For an analytic
periodic table with asymmetric, pairwise noncongruent obstacle
representatives, we reconstruct the entire table from the complete
catalogue of short clear normal channels, unlabelled between records.
If the acquisition range exceeds twice the covering radius of the
obstacle union, the lifted channel graph is connected and its cycles
generate the full translational period group. Coverage and period
generation are therefore consequences of the range condition, rather
than assumptions on a designed primitive network. No observed pair of
cycles need be a lattice basis. An all-cycle determinantal index gives
an explicit saturation certificate and sharpens the retained finite
arithmetic list for partial covering collections. On compact uniformly
analytic classes with geometric, shape and symmetry margins and onset
brackets, two growing endpoint histograms per record give conditional
whole-table stability, including the saturated period structure.
The catalogue remains complete by acquisition; local contact origins,
selected return branches and within-record grouping remain marked.
We retain the exact finite-index physical ambiguities, moment inverses
and relative-law theory. No finite-horizon assumption is imposed.
\end{abstract}
