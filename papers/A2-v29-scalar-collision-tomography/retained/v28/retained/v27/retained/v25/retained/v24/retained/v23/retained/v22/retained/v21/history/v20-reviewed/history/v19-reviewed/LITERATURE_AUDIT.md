The exact local information equivalence is stated as Proposition 2.2 of the revision. It makes the richness of two density functions explicit instead of relying on a misleading analogy with two scalar windows. The paper claims neither that affine elimination is an abstract new lens principle nor that the cited exterior or metric theorems follow from its selected local data. Its theorem-level distinction is recovery of two unknown intrinsic arcs plus shape-separated incidence and covolume-calibrated period descent from records without global identities or integer labels.
