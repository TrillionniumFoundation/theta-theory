These intrinsic spectra compose and transport common endogenous decisions. The active microscopic/regenerative result gives a nonfinite physical consumer of the before-optimization comparison.

The explicit rank-three separation and the exact identity-channel width/error laws make that statement testable. They do not turn an incomplete literature audit into proof of originality. We have not claimed that no earlier work contains this conjunction.
