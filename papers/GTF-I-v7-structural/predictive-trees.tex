\section{A structural theorem for predictive trees}\label{sec:v7-trees}
\subsection{Local hypotheses and the finite profile}
Let $\mathcal L$ be a pruned factorial language on a finite alphabet of
size $b\ge2$. Thus prefixes and suffixes of legal words are legal, every
word has an infinite continuation, and only legal children occur in its
rooted tree. Write $[v]$ for a cylinder in the boundary $\Omega$ and
$\emptyw$ for the empty word. Assume that along any branch there are at
most $U$ consecutive vertices with only one legal successor. This
condition allows unary vertices but excludes an infinite deterministic
branch. Let $\mu$ be a stationary probability on $\Omega$, positive on
every legal cylinder, and put $p_v=\mu[v]$.

A predictive energy is a positive family $a_v$, with $a_{\emptyw}=1$.
Put $D_v=(a_v/p_v)^{1/2}$. The following are the hypotheses of the
structural theorem. There are constants $a_->0$, $\rho<1$, and
$c_0,C_0>0$ such that
\begin{align}
 a_-a_v\le a_{vi}\le\rho a_v,
 &\qquad\sum_{i:\,vi\in\mathcal L}a_{vi}\le\rho a_v,
                                                   \label{eq:v7-tree-child}\\
 a_{uv}<a_v\quad(u\ne\emptyw),
 &\qquad D_{vi}\le D_v.                             \label{eq:v7-tree-suffix}
\end{align}
These statements concern legal words only. A bounded Borel map $Z$ from
$\Omega$ into a separable Hilbert space satisfies
\begin{align}
 \diam Z([v])&\le C_0D_v,                            \label{eq:v7-tree-diameter}\\
 \|Z(\omega)-Z(\omega')\|&\ge c_0D_u
 \quad\text{if $u$ is their longest common prefix}.  \label{eq:v7-tree-separation}
\end{align}
The image may be infinite dimensional. Separation is an explicit
geometric hypothesis, not a consequence of the word notation.

Starting with the root, repeatedly split a leaf of maximal energy into
\emph{all} its legal children. Fix a tie rule. Let $\mathcal T_N$ be the
last tree in this sequence with at most $N$ leaves, $\mathcal P_N$ its
leaf cut, and
\begin{equation}
             G_N=\sum_{v\in\mathcal P_N}a_v.         \label{eq:v7-greedy}
\end{equation}
A split at a unary vertex is an actual step. Finiteness of $\mathcal T_N$
will follow below. The construction is a mathematical finite algorithm;
computable implementation of arbitrary real energies is an additional
input, not part of their Borel specification.

There are two causal interpretations of these data.

\smallskip\noindent
\textbf{Deletion model.} Independent acquisitions $\omega\sim\mu$ occur
at renewal times of independent positive integer length $L$, with
$\E L<\infty$. The acquired sequence is observed exactly. At age $k$
the desired output is $g(\sigma^k\omega)$, where $g$ is bounded and
Hilbert valued. Write
\[
 w_k=\Pp(L>k)/\E L,\quad W(n)=\sum_{k\ge n}w_k,
 \quad Z(\omega)=(\sqrt{w_k}g(\sigma^k\omega))_{k\ge0}.
\]
In addition to \eqref{eq:v7-tree-child}--\eqref{eq:v7-tree-separation},
assume the local oscillation bound
\begin{equation}
 \sum_{k<|v|}w_k\bigl(\osc_{[v_{k:}]}g\bigr)^2
       +4\|g\|_\infty^2W(|v|)\le C_1D_v^2.          \label{eq:v7-deletion-osc}
\end{equation}
A register sees the renewal event only during its update; the decoder
receives neither that event nor a stored raw observation for free.

\smallskip\noindent
\textbf{Insertion model.} Let $(Y_t)_{t\in\mathbb Z}$ be a stationary
finite-alphabet observed process whose reversed past
$\omega_t=(Y_t,Y_{t-1},\ldots)$ has law $\mu$ and language $\mathcal L$.
For a square-integrable stationary Hilbert target $U_t$, suppose
\begin{equation}
 m_t=\E[U_t\mid Y_s:s\le t]=Z(\omega_t),\qquad
 B=\E\|U_t-m_t\|^2.                                 \label{eq:v7-past-mean}
\end{equation}
The finite register may start at zero with a fixed state; it is not
initialized with the infinite past. Infinite stationary pasts here
specify the reference prediction and its law. A finite warm-up is
included in the limiting average. No renewal or independent-observation
assumption is imposed in this interpretation.

For either model let $R_M$ be the infimum, over all $M$-value registers
with causal standard Borel update and decoding kernels, of the upper
limiting expected average squared loss. Absolute time and independent
coding randomization are permitted. In the insertion model put
$E_M=R_M-B$.

\begin{theorem}[Predictive-tree realization in two orientations]
\label{thm:v7-tree}
Under \eqref{eq:v7-tree-child}--\eqref{eq:v7-tree-separation},
\begin{equation}
                 cG_M\le e_M^2(Z)\le CG_M.           \label{eq:v7-tree-static}
\end{equation}
Every complete greedy tree is suffix closed. A tree with $L$ leaves has
exactly $1+\sum_{v\,\mathrm{internal}}d^+(v)$ vertices, and at most
$(U+1)(2L-1)$ vertices; all of them may be used as designated states.
For every fixed $B_0\ge1$, $G_{\lceil B_0M\rceil}\asymp_{B_0}G_M$.

In the deletion model, with \eqref{eq:v7-deletion-osc},
\begin{equation}
              cG_M\le e_M^2(Z)\le R_M\le CG_M.       \label{eq:v7-tree-delete}
\end{equation}
In the insertion model,
\begin{equation}
              cG_M\le e_M^2(Z)\le E_M\le CG_M.       \label{eq:v7-tree-insert}
\end{equation}
The converses apply to the lower limiting average of each randomized,
time-dependent observer. The upper bounds are achieved in order by
stationary deterministic updates. In particular a logarithmic age or
history multiplier is not required. Constants depend on the displayed
structural hypotheses, not on the register budget.
\end{theorem}

\subsection{Balance, dilation and exact closure}
\begin{lemma}[Greedy balance]\label{lem:v7-balance}
For all sufficiently large $N$, $N/b\le |\mathcal P_N|\le N$. If
$\theta_N$ is the largest energy of a current leaf, then
\begin{equation}
        a_-\theta_N\le a_v\le\theta_N
                      \quad(v\in\mathcal P_N).       \label{eq:v7-balance}
\end{equation}
The trees are finite and their profiles have the fixed-dilation property
in Theorem~\ref{thm:v7-tree}.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Suppress unary vertices in any finite complete tree. A rooted tree with
$L$ leaves and no unary internal vertex has at most $2L-1$ vertices,
since its number of edges is the sum of its internal outdegrees. Each
vertex of the suppressed tree accounts for itself and at most $U$
unary vertices on its incoming path, including the initial root path.
Thus the original tree has at most $(U+1)(2L-1)$ vertices. An infinite
sequence of splits with bounded leaf number is impossible, proving
finiteness. The split following $\mathcal T_N$ crosses the budget and
adds at most $b-1$ leaves. Hence $|\mathcal P_N|>N-b+1$.

The maximal leaf energy never increases. Every present leaf was born
with energy at least $a_-$ times an earlier maximal energy. This proves
\eqref{eq:v7-balance}, including any unary splits. Set $\ell=U+1$.
Every vertex has at least two distinct descendants at depth $\ell$, and
at least $2^j$ at depth $\ell j$. Choose $j$ with $2^j>b(B_0+2)$.
If the later maximal energy at budget $\lceil B_0N\rceil$ were less
than $a_-^{\ell j+1}\theta_N$, every original leaf would have been split
through all these $\ell j$ levels: all intervening vertices have energy
at least that threshold. The resulting number of leaves would exceed
$\lceil B_0N\rceil$, a contradiction. Therefore the later maximum is
at least a fixed multiple of $\theta_N$. Applying
\eqref{eq:v7-balance} at the later budget gives
$G_{\lceil B_0N\rceil}\ge c_{B_0}N\theta_N\ge c_{B_0}G_N$.
Splitting decreases the energy sum by \eqref{eq:v7-tree-child}, giving
the reverse inequality. Adjusting constants covers the finite initial
range.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Two exact operations on one tree]\label{lem:v7-closure}
Every proper suffix of an internal greedy-tree vertex is internal, and
every suffix of any vertex is present. Consequently deletion of the
first letter stays in the tree. If $v$ is a leaf and $iv$ is legal, then
$iv$ has a leaf prefix in the same tree. It can therefore be updated
by prepending $i$ and reading only the letters of $v$ until that leaf is
reached.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Suppose an internal word $uv$, $u\ne\emptyw$, is about to be split.
If its suffix $v$ has not been split, some current leaf $t$ is a prefix
of $v$. Child decrease and strict prefix-attachment decrease give
$a_t\ge a_v>a_{uv}$, contrary to maximality. Thus the suffix is already
internal. For a leaf $i_1\cdots i_n$ with $n\ge2$, its parent is
internal; the suffix $i_2\cdots i_{n-1}$ of that parent is internal,
so its legal child $i_2\cdots i_n$ is present. The one-letter case
ends at the root. Iteration gives closure of every vertex.

If there were no leaf prefix of $iv$, the complete tree would contain
$iv$ as an internal vertex. Its suffix $v$ would then be internal by
the first assertion, contradicting that it is a leaf. This proves the
insertion assertion. Only legal children were used in either proof.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Static lower bound and the causal converse}
\begin{lemma}[Arbitrary centres]\label{lem:v7-static}
The bounds \eqref{eq:v7-tree-static} hold for arbitrary Hilbert-space
centres, not only centres on $Z(\Omega)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose one image point in each leaf cylinder to obtain
$e_N^2(Z)\le C_0^2G_N$. For the lower bound use $\mathcal P_{4bM}$,
which has at least $4M$ leaves for large $M$. The open tubes about
$Z([v])$ of radii $\delta D_v$ are disjoint for a sufficiently small
fixed $\delta>0$. Indeed for incomparable $v,v'$ their common prefix
$u$ has $D_u\ge\max(D_v,D_{v'})$, and
\eqref{eq:v7-tree-separation} separates the two images by at least
$c_0D_u$. Take $2\delta<c_0$.

At most $M$ of the tubes contain one of $M$ proposed centres. On every
unoccupied cylinder the squared error is at least $\delta^2D_v^2$.
Its integrated contribution is $\delta^2a_v$. By balance, the at least
three-quarters unoccupied leaves carry at least $3a_-/4$ of the total
energy sum. Thus $e_M^2(Z)\ge cG_{4bM}\ge c'G_M$ by
Lemma~\ref{lem:v7-balance}. The finite initial range is harmless:
\eqref{eq:v7-tree-separation} and positive cylinder masses at unbounded
branching depths give infinitely supported image law, so every fixed
finite-centre error is positive. Alternatively the same tube proof at
a larger fixed cut proves that positivity directly.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Renewal converse]\label{lem:v7-renewal-converse}
In the deletion experiment every randomized, nonstationary $M$-state
observer has lower limiting average loss at least $e_M^2(Z)$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
A standard Borel probability kernel is realized as a measurable function
of its input and an independent uniform random variable
\cite{Kallenberg2021}. Applying this at the countably many update and
decode times gives an independent coding sequence. A data-dependent
persistent position in such a sequence, if used, belongs to the charged
state; the representation itself supplies no history channel.

Condition on an acquisition at a fixed time, its pre-acquisition history,
and the independent coding variables. The acquired $\omega$ still has
law $\mu$. Along a hypothetical continuation with no new acquisition,
each of the at most $M$ post-acquisition labels determines one output
string. For a truncation at age $n$, the survival-weighted loss is
therefore at least
$\E L\,e_M^2(Z^{<n})$, where $Z^{<n}$ is the first $n$ coordinates
of the weighted orbit. At a fixed physical time, events specifying the
last acquisition are disjoint. Summing this bound over acquisition times
at least $n$ before the end of a horizon and using the Ces\`aro renewal
count gives lower limiting average at least $e_M^2(Z^{<n})$.
For completeness, the elementary renewal theorem gives expected renewals
per unit time tending to $1/\E L$; a fixed truncation removes at most
$n$ terminal time indices, each with bounded contribution. No stationary
coding rule is presumed.

Finally $e_M^2(Z^{<n})\uparrow e_M^2(Z)$. Monotonicity follows by
projection. The reverse limiting inequality follows by appending zero
tails to an arbitrarily good truncated code: the extra mean squared
norm is at most $\|g\|_\infty^2W(n)\to0$. This completes the proof.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:v7-tree}]
The static assertion, finite count, dilation and suffix closure are
proved above. In the deletion experiment retain every tree vertex as
a designated state. At acquisition store the unique leaf prefix of
$\omega$. Between acquisitions delete its first letter until the empty
word is reached, and then keep that word. Choose a representative in
each nonempty cylinder and output its $g$ value; use a fixed bounded
output at the empty word. For an acquired leaf $v$ the weighted error
is bounded by the left side of \eqref{eq:v7-deletion-osc}. Averaging
and applying renewal reward gives at most $C_1G_N$.
Lemma~\ref{lem:v7-renewal-converse} gives the unrestricted converse.

For insertion, after reaching a leaf use the prepend-and-parse rule of
Lemma~\ref{lem:v7-closure}. During initialization store the observed
reversed word while it is internal, or its first leaf prefix when one
appears. This uses only vertices of the same tree and after at most its
maximum depth gives the correct leaf prefix of the full observed past.
Decode a representative $Z$ value in that leaf cylinder. Its stationary
excess loss is at most $C_0^2G_N$ by
\eqref{eq:v7-tree-diameter} and conditional orthogonality. The finite
warm-up has zero limiting average contribution. The converse holds at
each time: freeze independent coding randomness, apply orthogonality
against $m_t$, and use the at most $M$ decoder values to lower bound
excess by $e_M^2(Z)$. Stationarity makes this the same number at every
time, even for time-dependent decoders.

In either orientation choose $N$ a fixed sufficiently small multiple
of $M$, so that $(U+1)(2|\mathcal P_N|-1)\le M$. Dilation converts
$G_N$ into $O(G_M)$ without a time-depth multiplier. One fixed state
handles the remaining bounded range of budgets. This proves the two
risk assertions.
\end{proof}

The static part of the proof uses familiar separated-cylinder
quantization ideas \cite{KZ2017,Roychowdhury2014}. Maximal-energy
prefix splitting is likewise a classical coding device
\cite{Tunstall1967}. The additional conclusion is that a strict
\emph{predictive} energy inequality makes the adaptive tree executable
in both orientations, with its whole persistent state counted.
