\begin{abstract}
We study finite-resource realizations of predictive quotients of causal
experiments. A structural theorem on admissible predictive trees identifies
when a static adaptive quantization profile is achievable by a finite
causal register at the same order. Strict decrease of predictive energy
under prefix attachment makes every greedy tree suffix closed. The same
finite tree supports both deletion after an acquisition and insertion of
successive observations; all its vertices, including unary vertices, are
charged. Consequently the theorem applies both to Markov-renewal orbit
prediction and to a continually observed hidden shift with an
infinite-coordinate posterior. We also construct finite recursive
approximations of contracting measure-valued filters, with numerical and
representation errors explicitly separated. Finally, a positive
second-order reconstruction and a decision-theoretic cardinality converse
give the sharp $M^{-2/r}$ finite-experiment approximation order for a
nondegenerate Gaussian shift. For a physical Poisson count-to-contact
experiment this order holds jointly for $M\lesssim N^{r/6}$. The
foundational statements specify the positive experiment, the future-test
quotient, the causal simulation, and the resource transformation used in
each application.
\end{abstract}
