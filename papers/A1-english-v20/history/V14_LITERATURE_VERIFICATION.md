# Primary-source verification for revision 14

The sources below were checked against public primary texts on 6 September
2026. These are checks of inputs, not an exhaustive novelty search. None is
claimed to prove the manuscript's positive-history attainment theorem or its
model-specific body realization.

1. Carl de Boor, *Divided Differences*, arXiv:math/0502036v1.
   https://arxiv.org/html/math/0502036v1
   Proposition 35 explicitly relates divided-difference prefixes to the
   corresponding complete derivative data, including repeated sites. Formula
   (52) gives the Genocchi--Hermite integral; PDF page 19 (printed page 64)
   was rendered and inspected at https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0502036.
   These support the classical confluent identities used in Sections 4--6,
   not the normalized-prior pullback or the probability lower bound.
2. Y. Zhang and J. Kileel, *Covering Number of Real Algebraic Varieties and
   Beyond: Improved Bounds and Applications*, arXiv:2311.05116v4.
   https://arxiv.org/html/2311.05116v4
   Lemma 2.18 supplies the coefficient-independent bounded-format
   semialgebraic regularity input. Its role is the global attainable-image
   cover, not a local-surjectivity assertion. No semialgebraic density of the
   prior or semialgebraicity of the calibration chamber is assumed from it.
3. G. Comte and I. Halupczok, *Motivic Vitushkin invariants*,
   arXiv:2206.15412v2. https://arxiv.org/html/2206.15412v2
   The introduction, equations (4)--(5), recalls the real section-component
   variations and real entropy inequality. Only that real inequality is used
   for the thin-rectangle argument; the paper's new nonarchimedean result is
   not substituted for a real theorem.

The complete prior bibliography remains in the manuscript. Earlier source
verification, including the corrected discounted-control theorem citation,
is preserved in `history/V13_LITERATURE_VERIFICATION.md` and older history
records. Those historical checks are not represented as a fresh independent
check of every reference in this turn. The new body/width proof is written
out; singular-value and ellipsoid-width comparisons are derived there rather
than imported as a purported new external theorem. No claim of universal
priority clearance or journal endorsement is made.
