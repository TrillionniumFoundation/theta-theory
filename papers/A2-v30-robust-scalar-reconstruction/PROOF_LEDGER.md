## Boundaries of the conclusions

Physical calibration and the all-attempt convention are experiment inputs. No device has been tested. Local scalar inversion does not require periodicity; the global extension assumes a bounded periodic presentation. The known patch margin is used only for uniform finite discrete decisions, not manufactured by numerical evidence. Exact rational labels are distinct from approximate real vector coordinates. The uniformly prepared analytic count-germ question, passive spectral rigidity, minimax lower bounds and bit-complexity optimality are not asserted.
