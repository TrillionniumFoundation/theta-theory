# Literature audit for A2 v22

Primary sources were checked on 29 September 2026. This is a focused theorem/input comparison, not an exhaustive priority search. The manuscript does not infer top-four significance from citations or numerical tests.

## Sources inspected anew

**Stefanov–Uhlmann–Vasy**, *Local and global boundary rigidity and the geodesic X-ray transform in the normal gauge*, Annals of Mathematics 194 (2021), 1–95; arXiv:1702.03638, version 3. The primary text's Theorems 1.1 and 1.3 concern dimension at least three, metric recovery modulo boundary-fixing diffeomorphisms, and their strict-convexity/foliation assumptions. The new paper has known Euclidean metric and unknown reflecting arcs and a period group. No claim of subsumption is made.

**Gurfinkel–Noakes–Stoyanov**, *Travelling Times in Scattering by Obstacles in Curved Space*, arXiv:2003.12261; J. Differential Equations 269 (2020), 9508–9530. The primary PDF's Theorems 1 and 2 and its travel-time differentiation discussion were inspected, including the displayed theorem page. The exterior travel-time data and non-trapping-flow conclusion differ from selected local endpoint records. The local information equivalence is proved directly rather than called a new general lens principle.

**Kannan–Bachem**, *Polynomial algorithms for computing the Smith and Hermite normal forms of an integer matrix*, SIAM J. Computing 8 (1979), 499–507, DOI 10.1137/0208040. The publisher's primary record states the integer-operation and operand-size scope. It supports credit for the integer stage, not a complexity claim for analytic-body fitting, congruence or physical scanning.

**Trefethen**, *Quantifying the ill-conditioning of analytic continuation*, BIT 60 (2020), 901–915, DOI 10.1007/s10543-020-00802-7. The publisher text was checked for bounded continuation and geometry-dependent instability. The new primary proves the two-constants estimate it needs and does not claim a universal or optimal continuation exponent.

## Retained comparisons

Noakes–Stoyanov's local endpoint derivative comparison, Santaló-type volume relations, marked-length billiard results, relative-neighborhood constructions, and visibility/free-bitangent comparisons remain in the exact retained source and its literature ledgers. The new primary keeps the applicable distinction of observations. Not every retained publication was re-read in full in this revision; a failed direct DOI fetch for Noakes–Stoyanov was not recorded as successful retrieval.

The new completion identity uses the existing absolute phase normalization and the elementary subgroup covolume formula. Its claim is an explicit data-based test for simultaneous missing types and missing periods under stated genericity, followed by a reference-free noisy realization. The hidden-area lower bound is proved directly through the Bernoulli reduction, KL chain rule and two-point testing inequality. It is not asserted to be a whole-table minimax result or an exact analytic count-only theorem.
