## Primary literature read and used

* **Herva–Kari**, *Periodicity and local complexity of Delone sets*, arXiv:2504.20709v1 (2025). Primary HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/2504.20709v1 . Sections 5–7 distinguish slow Meyer-set patch growth, annihilators, periodic decomposition and forced periodicity. Section 5 of the new manuscript uses only the comparison needed here: those results concern structural hypotheses forcing periods, while this paper assumes a bounded periodic presentation. A nonzero measured finite difference is not an annihilator hypothesis.
* **Dolbilin–Garber–Schulte–Senechal**, *Bounds for the Regularity Radius of Delone Sets*, Discrete Comput. Geom. 74 (2025), 78–94. Publisher: https://doi.org/10.1007/s00454-024-00666-6 . The local-cluster/regularity-radius question is distinct from recognizing full translations in an already periodic bounded presentation. No general conjectural radius bound is described as proved here.
* **Lagarias–Pleasants**, *Local Complexity of Delone Sets and Crystallinity*, arXiv:math/0105088v1. Primary record: https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0105088 . Cited for the patch-complexity/crystallinity line, not as an identical sensor inverse.
* **Galerne**, *Computation of the perimeter of measurable sets via their covariogram. Applications to random sets*, Image Anal. Stereol. 30 (2011), 39–51. Publisher: https://www.ias-iss.org/ojs/IAS/article/view/22 . Translation autocorrelations and their variation/perimeter identities differ from spatially weighted signed occupation differences. No covariogram injectivity conclusion is imported.

Versions and publisher bibliographic metadata were checked online. The comparison is not an exhaustive priority search and does not establish that an elementary finite-difference operation itself is novel. The claimed additional content is the physical scalar reversal identity combined with a geometric zero-witness inverse and its conditional finite experiment.
