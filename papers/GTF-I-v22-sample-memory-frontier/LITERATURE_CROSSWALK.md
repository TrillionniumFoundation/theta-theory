# Literature crosswalk and attribution boundary — v22

## Source correction and direct check

The v21 companion audit gave an incorrect title for EURANDOM report 2002-017. Its title page reads **Heinz Weisshaupt, “LeCam's randomization criterion in the setting of locally convex spaces without lattice structure,” 22 April 2002**. The manuscript bibliography already had that correct title. The current audit corrects the companion, without changing the old file.

The original report was revisited at the title page and pp. 14–16. Page 14 defines filtered positive operators through equality of restricted measures and proves compactness in Lemma 1. Theorem 5 on pp. 15–16 derives a filtered randomization conclusion from risk comparisons. Its variation norm for probability differences is twice our event-supremum TV. This is a comparison with the actual text, not an inference from its title.

The exact revelation formula is stated on its own assumptions; an exhaustive literature priority claim for that specialization has not been completed.
