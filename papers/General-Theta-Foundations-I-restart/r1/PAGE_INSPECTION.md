# Local PDF visual inspection

The native manuscript was rendered with Poppler at 100 dpi. All 16 pages were
inspected in two eight-page contact sheets; the central theorem page was also
inspected at full rendered resolution. No clipped formula, overlapping text,
missing mathematical glyph, or margin overflow was observed. The reference list
was moved to begin on its own final page rather than leaving a single entry at
the foot of the previous page. Compiler checks reject overfull boxes and unresolved
references/citations. Underfull bibliography-line warnings are nonfatal.

This is a layout check, not a mathematical correctness certificate. The PDF bytes
and page count of each actual build are identified by its generated receipt, not
by a self-referential hash stored in this source file.
