# Literature and priority comparison

Primary sources checked online on 2026-10-06; bibliographic/abstract inspection
unless a more specific reading scope is stated. This is not exhaustive priority
clearance. No long passages are reproduced.

| Source | Established background used or compared | Distinction in this submission |
|---|---|---|

The proposed new unit is the theorem linking actual acquired weights, physical
anisotropic scales, innovation-localized propagation, matching label-risk curves
and typed causal resource composition. Its components are elementary/classical in
several places. Whether their proved synthesis has the depth and novelty required
for the four leading general mathematics journals requires independent assessment.
The manuscript does not settle this by declaring itself a “general foundation.”
