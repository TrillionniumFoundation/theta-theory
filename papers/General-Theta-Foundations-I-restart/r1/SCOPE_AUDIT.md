## Genuine remaining mathematical obligations

1. Derive weighted forced second moments from broad recurrent nonuniform kernel
   assumptions allowing expansion on active events. Mere average-log contraction
   is insufficient; the present proof establishes only its stated mechanisms.
2. Derive uniform acquired charts/mass for singular laws beyond finitely many
   uniformly bi-Lipschitz component charts. Cusp-density and infinitely many
   accumulating-stratum regimes are not proved here.
3. Jointly learn an unknown kernel/calibration and prove optimal pilot acquisition,
   workspace and retained-description costs. Known calibration is used here.
4. Prove matching lower bounds in the complete resource vector when simulator,
   controller, internal clock, or program memory grows with N or m. The intrinsic
   predictive cut is not identical to total bit complexity.
5. Optimize acquisition strategies and decision controllers without the fixed-
   exploration/common-controller qualification. A richness and resource-preserving
   reverse comparison is required and is not supplied here.
6. Establish a single broad physical class simultaneously admitting the uniform
   four-term upper and all robust lower alternatives, beyond the explicitly
   certified subexperiments. The conditional class proposition is not that proof.

These are proof obligations, not impossibility statements. The mother G1/G3 target
is retained. This r1 does not certify that its entire strongest form is closed.
Independent expert novelty/priority review and external proof review remain open;
no journal approval or significance claim follows from a passing build.
