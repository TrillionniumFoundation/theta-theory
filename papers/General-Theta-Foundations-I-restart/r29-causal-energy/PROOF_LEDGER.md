# Proof ledger

Status means a full written proof is supplied and has been checked for the indicated logical issue; it does not mean independent formal verification or journal acceptance.

| Statement | Proof mechanism | Load-bearing scope check |
|---|---|---|
| Predictive quotient | Coordinate separation, finite continuation closure, Borel section | Only the declared one-audit future experiment; interface coordinates retained |
| Causal energy | Two exact conditional-square decompositions | Fresh randomness only; no full-history register; zero incoming means handled |
| Equality criterion | Equality in each nonnegative row/pool term | Necessary/sufficient for local product saturation, not all experiments |
| Homogeneous rates | Uniform orbital small-ball + cover correlation | Mass bound for all incoming directions, not just actual pure points |
| Circle static formula | Fractional cosine rearrangement + concavity | Euclidean chordal squared loss; centroids in disk; randomized cells included |
| Circle causal product | Common outgoing centroids in every incoming row | Equal row masses and same centroid, not isolated row optimality |
| Deadline and allocation | Positive-path support clock + concavity of log beta | Halt/readout depend only on labels; phase labels disjoint |
| Singular law | Haar mean zero + actual surviving probability | Atom not renormalized; inactive labels charged; equivalent phase laws stated |
| Higher quantum orbit | A uniform spectral gap, top-projection inequality, Haar graph density | All traceless incoming means; no uniform simple spectrum assumption |
| Fixed-start access | Tagged preparation and gated raw score disintegration | Gain centering is analytic; no unknown gain in runtime; scalar cancellation retained |
| Composition | Product state, total-variation contraction, coupling | Complete protected audit and deadlines, not terminal marginal alone |
| Finite-bit circle | Uniform angle boundary mass and arithmetic certificates | Only decision coupling; program/workspace/report bits/time separate |
| Actual deficiency | Stateless Haar fill upper, bounded audit correlation lower | Cannot replace audit; lower applies even to arbitrary classical simulator memory |

The continuum proofs are in the mathematical sources. Regression evaluates finite instances only. General nonlinear likelihoods, arbitrary nondominated kernels, arbitrary sensing designs and unmatched computational lower bounds are not marked closed.
