## Not asserted closed

1. A necessary and sufficient characterization of *all* recursively compressible causal experiments. The saturation criterion is exact for the displayed energy coefficient, not a universal quotient classification.
2. Matching online curves under arbitrary state-dependent noisy likelihoods, non-Haar/nonuniform report laws, or arbitrary learned/unknown kernels without certificates. Earlier common-program results remain separate.
3. A free choice of optimal acquisition/sensing design for a fixed static target. The new n is a prescribed evolving-state tracking exposure. Additional absolute measurements or repreparations change the experiment.
4. Exact product attainment on every higher-dimensional homogeneous orbit. Only rate matching is claimed there; the circle has exact compatibility.
5. Sharp joint optimality of program description, workspace, numerical precision, physical execution time and minimal simulator state. Allocation is efficiently solved for the circle; the finite-bit execution region is not a matching computational lower bound.
6. General unbounded stopping, infinite retained boundaries, nondominated compactness, LDP equivalence or unbounded-operator extensions.
7. Earliest-priority and top-four significance judgments. Literature is compared at the stated theorem/observation-interface level, not certified exhaustively.
