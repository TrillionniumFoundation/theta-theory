# Literature comparison (primary sources checked 10 October 2026)

| Source and primary identifier | Theorem-level object | Relation and non-claim |
|---|---|---|
| Graf--Luschgy, *Foundations of Quantization*, 2000, DOI 10.1007/BFb0103945 | Squared quantization and geometric rates | Standard tool; no priority claim for projection or small-ball argument |
| Rosenblatt--Roychowdhury, arXiv:1809.08364v4 | Exact uniform curve quantizers including circle | Static formula credited; R29 adds recursive centroid compatibility and full clock allocation |
| Linder--Yuksel, IEEE TIT 60 (2014), 5975-5991, DOI 10.1109/TIT.2014.2346780 | Structural/existence results for zero-delay coding; encoder sees current source | Relative-only report and counted finite observer do not have that source access |
| Ghomi--Linder--Yuksel, arXiv:2103.10810v3 | Stationary optimal linear-source coding, near-optimal finite memory, finite-horizon approximation | Different average-cost criterion and current-source observation; not subsumed by the tracking theorem |
| Venel--Ziliotto, SIAM JCO 54 (2016), 1983-2008, DOI 10.1137/15M1043340 | Pure strategies achieving strong uniform value over large horizons and long-run liminf payoff | Does not fix the complete finite state count or derive this exact deadline law |
| Chatterjee--Saona--Ziliotto, Math OR 47 (2022), 100-119, DOI 10.1287/moor.2020.1116 | Approximately optimal finite-memory strategies for long-run POMDP objectives | Memory may be chosen for accuracy; R29 fixes the finite resource at each horizon |
| Yu--Bertsekas, Math OR 33 (2008), 1-11, DOI 10.1287/moor.1070.0279 | Under constant optimal liminf average cost, epsilon-optimal FSC independent of initial distributions | Broader POMDP setting with explicit average-cost hypothesis, not a fixed-M resource formula |
| Yuksel, arXiv:2301.11244 | History-space existence, ergodicity and approximation without belief reduction | Not treated as equivalent to Haar relative transport |
| Demirci--Kara--Yuksel, SIAM JCO 62 (2024), 2859-2883, DOI 10.1137/24M1643736 | Filter contraction, average-cost optimality equation, quantized/finite-memory approximation | R29 has isometric full-history updates, but a narrower state-independent report interface |
| Yu, Math OR 49 (2024), 1734-1760, DOI 10.1287/moor.2022.0188 | Strategic measures, measurability and epsilon-optimal universally measurable policies; additional minimax assumptions | No claim that finite-state energy saturation proves this general existence theory |
| Leizarowitz, Math OR 28 (2003), 553-586, DOI 10.1287/moor.28.3.553.16388 | Finite-state compact-action multichain average-optimal/epsilon-optimal policy computation | Separate from exact arithmetic allocation within this raw interface |
| Shaw, J Approx Theory 94 (1998), 285-299, DOI 10.1006/jath.1998.3186 | K-functionals and nonoptimal ergodic convergence rates | Earlier source than retained 2005 note; no claim to earliest discrete K-functional theorem |
| Glynn--Infanger, Stochastic Systems 14 (2024), 47-68, DOI 10.1287/stsy.2022.0001 | Poisson representations, martingales and integrability/CLT conditions | Inherited marked physical-time converse is distinguished from these standard tools |

Primary publisher pages, arXiv author manuscripts and institutional author records were checked. Publication issue dates are distinguished from earlier online dates (e.g. the 2022 finite-memory POMDP issue appeared online in 2021; Yu's 2024 issue appeared online in 2023). The comparison does not claim full-text line-by-line re-auditing of every cited paper or exhaustive priority certification.
