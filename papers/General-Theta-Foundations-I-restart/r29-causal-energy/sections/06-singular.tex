\section{Singular acquisition with a genuine surviving mass}\label{sec:singular}
Consider the same relative transport, but at call $t$ independently suppress the rotation report with probability $1-\alpha_t$, $0\le\alpha_t\le1$. A suppressed report is the visible symbol $\bot$; the physical rotation is still performed and is not redrawn or undone. Conditional on $\bot$, its independent Haar average sends every entering test mean to zero. Subsequent reported rotations preserve this zero mean. Write
\[
 w_t=\prod_{j=1}^t\alpha_j.
\]
The actual full-history predictive law is therefore
\begin{equation}\label{eq:singular-law}
 \nu_t=w_t\sigma_d+(1-w_t)\delta_0.
\end{equation}
This is a probability with a $d$-dimensional stratum and a zero-dimensional stratum; the first stratum is not renormalized. The actual full-history baseline is $B_t=V-w_t$.

\begin{theorem}[Surviving-stratum causal law]\label{thm:singular}
For all phase widths $m_t\ge1$, every Borel randomized observer satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:singular-lower}
 \calR_{\rm seq}^{\rm er}(n;\boldsymbol m)-B_n
 \ge w_n\left(1-\prod_t\beta_d(m_t)\right).
\end{equation}
For $m_t\ge2$ there is an observer with one counted inactive label in each phase and at most $m_t-1$ active labels for which
\begin{equation}\label{eq:singular-upper}
 \calR_{\rm seq}^{\rm er}(n;\boldsymbol m)-B_n
 \le C_d w_n\min\left\{1,\sum_t(m_t-1)^{-2/d}\right\}.
\end{equation}
On the circle its excess is exactly $w_n(1-\prod_t\beta_{m_t-1})$ for this construction. Hence, for all $m_t\ge2$, the optimal excess is comparable to
$w_n\min(1,\sum_t m_t^{-2/d})$, uniformly even when $w_n\to0$.
The checkpoint excess lies between $w_nq_m(\sigma_d)$ and $w_nq_{m-1}(\sigma_d)$ for $m\ge2$. For an autonomous exact deadline, all inactive phase labels are charged too; balanced widths give the analogous matching rate for $M\ge2n+1$ provided the report laws at different phases are mutually equivalent (for example $0<\inf_t\alpha_t\le\sup_t\alpha_t<1$).
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For an entering observer mean $v_s$, the conditional new mean before its label update is $R_tv_s$ on a disclosed report and zero on $\bot$. Its energy is $\alpha_t\norm{v_s}^2$. The row distortion, for any $m_t$ centers, is at least the contribution of the disclosed part, namely $\alpha_t\norm{v_s}^2q_{m_t}(\sigma_d)$. The energy identity, with the additional point mass at zero, gives
$e_t\le\alpha_t\beta_d(m_t)e_{t-1}$.
Thus $e_n\le w_n\prod_t\beta_d(m_t)$ and risk $V-e_n$ proves~\eqref{eq:singular-lower}.

For the upper, move to the inactive label on the first $\bot$ and retain it thereafter, outputting zero. On the event of no suppression run the construction of Theorem~\ref{thm:sphere} with $m_t-1$ directions. Suppression is independent of the directions and the full active history, so this event has its actual mass $w_n$ and the original conditional law. The inactive part has zero predictive error. This proves the upper, including the circle formula. Since $m_t-1\ge m_t/2$, the two bounds match to constants. A checkpoint may add the origin to an $(m-1)$-center codebook; restriction to the spherical submeasure gives its lower. For the autonomous construction use disjoint phases including their inactive labels and the initial label; the lower clock argument extends from equal laws to mutually equivalent phase laws. Indeed the integrated transition matrices then have the same positive-edge graph. A positive-probability path from a label to a halt label has the same possible length from every phase at which that label is reached, so the two different required remaining lengths are impossible. The autonomous upper itself needs no such lower clock argument.
\end{proof}
The phase-law qualification in the last sentence matters. If a deterministic pattern of visible erasures encodes the phase, it supplies an external clock and the autonomous clock lower does not follow. The sequential and checkpoint statements hold for every $\alpha_t\in[0,1]$ without that qualification. Also the baseline rises by $1-w_n$ relative to the fully disclosed task: missing reports do not make the total risk smaller merely because the acquired sphere loses mass.

\begin{corollary}[Homogeneous singular extension]\label{cor:homogeneous-singular}
For the group experiments of Theorem~\ref{thm:homogeneous}, independent hidden reports give the actual law $w_n\mu+(1-w_n)\delta_0$. For $m_t\ge2$, the sequential excess over $V-w_n$ is comparable to
$w_n\min(1,\sum_t m_t^{-2/d})$.
With mutually equivalent phase report laws and $M\ge2n+1$, the autonomous excess is comparable to $w_n\min(1,n^{1+2/d}(M-1)^{-2/d})$. The checkpoint bounds retain the factor $w_n$ as above.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Haar averaging is zero on $V$ by (a). A hidden report thus annihilates the full-history test mean. For each incoming vector, the row distortion on the visible part is at least $\alpha_t c\norm v^2m_t^{-2/d}$ by (c). The energy identity gives the corresponding product lower. For the upper reserve one inactive label per phase and use the homogeneous recursive cover on the surviving event. Conditioning on that event recovers exactly the original group experiment, with mass $w_n$. The same phase-support and balanced-allocation arguments prove the autonomous assertion. No mass is renormalized in either direction.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[One-task calibration and disclosure loss]\label{cor:singular-calibration}
Append a terminal, environment-owned coordinate $s\delta$, where $s\in\{-1,1\}$ is fixed but never reported and $0\le\delta\le1$. Under the sum of the original squared loss and the squared loss for this coordinate, minimax over $s$ adds exactly $\delta^2$ to all the displayed values. In particular, in the comparable sequential range,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:joint-sharp}
 \calR^{\rm er}_\delta-B
 \asymp_d (1-w_n)+w_n\min\left(1,\sum_t m_t^{-2/d}\right)+\delta^2.
\end{equation}
This is a sharp selected resource region. It does not assert simultaneous optimality of program bits, workspace or execution time.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Use the equal prior on the two invisible signs for the lower: conditional on every legal history and random seed the conditional mean of $s\delta$ is zero, so that coordinate costs at least $\delta^2$. The identically zero response attains it without another label. The original terminal task and calibration coordinate are independent under this prior, so the losses add. Subtract $B=V-1$ rather than the erased baseline $B_n=V-w_n$, and use Theorem~\ref{thm:singular}.
\end{proof}
