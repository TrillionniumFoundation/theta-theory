\section{Resource-certified composition and implementation}\label{sec:composition}
A task-compatible causal morphism has a parameter-independent report map, a strategy lift, a counted internal state, a physical clock map and a readout map. The action ports and the report deadline must agree. The environment-owned final audit remains the same physical audit: it cannot be regenerated by the simulator. Total variation below uses $\TV(\mu,\nu)=\sup_A|\mu(A)-\nu(A)|$. For bounded loss in $[0,L]$, an error $\epsilon$ on the complete report--response--audit law changes its expectation by at most $L\epsilon$.

\begin{proposition}[Finite-horizon composition]\label{prop:composition}
Suppose a simulator with $S$ internal labels transforms a source experiment into a target at complete task-law discrepancy at most $\epsilon$, uniformly over the declared target strategies and fixed unknown parameters. A target observer with $M$ labels lifts to a source observer with at most $MS$ labels. If it uses at most $n$ virtual acquisitions, each using at most $c$ physical calls, the lifted count is at most $cn$ plus the explicitly charged setup and audit. Suppose an implemented report/readout mechanism is further coupled to the ideal one with probability of any decision mismatch at most $\zeta$, and its terminal readout error on the agreement event is at most $\gamma$. If $\norm Z\le K$ and ideal and implemented readouts lie in the radius-$A$ ball, then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:composition-risk}
 R_{\rm source}\le R_{\rm target}+(K+A)^2(\epsilon+\zeta)+2(K+A)\gamma+\gamma^2.
\end{equation}
The same comparison reverses only when a reverse task-compatible simulator is supplied. Iterated simulator errors add by the triangle inequality, while their retained states multiply. Program description, temporary workspace and execution time add with the stated call schedule rather than being identified with $\log_2(MS)$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Retain the pair of observer and simulator states. The causal strategy lift uses only this pair and the current source report; its joint transitions and readout give the target law up to $\epsilon$. Couple the further implementation with its ideal run until the first mismatch. On a mismatch the squared loss differs by at most $(K+A)^2$; on agreement use
$|\norm{Z-a-e}^2-\norm{Z-a}^2|\le2(K+A)\norm e+\norm e^2$.
Integration proves~\eqref{eq:composition-risk}. No infinite-horizon or unbounded-stopping limit is taken. Products of state sets and concatenation of programs prove the resource assertions.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[A charged finite-bit circle implementation]\label{prop:numerics}
For the circle program of Theorem~\ref{thm:clock-circle}, let $K=M-1=\sum_t m_t$, and suppose the acquisition device returns an angle approximation with circular error at most $h=2^{-p}$ at cost $T_{\rm rep}(p)$ and temporary space $W_{\rm rep}(p)$. All approximation routines and their worst-case error bounds are part of the program specification. There is a finite-bit implementation with the same $M$ persistent labels, program description
$O(M(p+\log M)+n\log M)$ bits, temporary workspace $O(p+\log M)+W_{\rm rep}(p)$ bits, and online time
$O(n(p+\log M)^2)+nT_{\rm rep}(p)$,
whose risk exceeds the ideal value by at most
\begin{equation}\label{eq:numeric-bound}
 C(K_Z+A)^2\min(1,(M-1)h)+C(K_Z+A)nh+C n^2h^2.
\end{equation}
Here $\norm Z\le K_Z$ and $A$ bounds the readouts. The construction is a finite-precision approximation, not an exact finite-bit evaluation of arbitrary real report boundaries.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Store rational approximations to each phase's regular-arc endpoints, output directions and coefficients $a_m$ to absolute accuracy at most a fixed multiple of $h$; fixed-precision arithmetic evaluates the phase's rotated old direction with angle error at most $C h$. Constants can be compiled by convergent sine/cosine series with certified remainders. Reading the table and multiplying/dividing integers of $O(p+\log M)$ bits has the displayed schoolbook bounds; the compilation cost is separate from online time and is finite under these explicit routines.

Couple exact and rounded executions by the same real reports. Until their first state mismatch the incoming label is identical. The new exact angle is uniform conditional on that label, so it falls within $C h$ of one of the $m_t$ boundaries with probability at most $C m_t h$. A union bound over the calls gives $\zeta\le\min(1,CKh)$. On the agreement event, the product readout coefficient and direction have error at most $Cnh$, since all exact factors lie in $[0,1]$ and approximations can be clipped there. Apply Proposition~\ref{prop:composition}. The phase index and the old direction are encoded by the retained label; workspace is erased between calls. Report precision is a paid input capability, and the audit is still unavailable until after commitment.
\end{proof}

We next relate actual disclosure loss to a causal deficiency; the audit condition is essential. Let $\epsilon_*(\boldsymbol\alpha)$ be the infimum, over all causal simulators with arbitrary internal memory but no extra physical acquisition, of the total variation between (i) the ideal disclosed rotation reports with the original terminal audit and (ii) simulated reports from the erased experiment with that same physical audit. Reports must be emitted at their original deadlines. The simulator may not change the prepared system, the physical rotations or the audit; it only transforms the visible reports using fresh randomness. This is a specified task-compatible deficiency, not an unrestricted statistical comparison permitting a new independent audit.

\begin{proposition}[Attained order of disclosure deficiency]\label{prop:deficiency}
In the classical realization with contrast $\eta$ and $k=d+1$, in the qubit realization with $k=3$ and contrast $r$, and in the projective realization with $k=D^2-1$, write $\chi=\eta$, $\chi=r$, or $\chi=r c_D$, respectively. For all $\alpha_t\in[0,1]$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:deficiency-order}
 \frac{\chi}{2k}(1-w_n)\le\epsilon_*(\boldsymbol\alpha)\le1-w_n.
\end{equation}
The upper uses a stateless report transformation which replaces each hidden report by a fresh Haar draw. Consequently, when phase report laws are mutually equivalent and $M\ge2n+1$, the internally clocked same-task minimax risk with the invisible calibration coordinate satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:full-resource-law}
 \calR_{\rm aut}^{\rm er}(N,M,\delta)-B
 \asymp_{d,\chi}
 \min\left(1,\frac{(N-2)^{1+2/d}}{(M-1)^{2/d}}\right)
 +\delta^2+\epsilon_*(\boldsymbol\alpha),\qquad N=n+2.
\end{equation}
The exact surviving mass remains in Theorem~\ref{thm:singular}; the comparable sum does not replace it in that theorem.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For the upper couple the physical system, all rotations, masks and audit. On the event of no erasure the report transformation equals the ideal transcript, an event of probability $w_n$. On its complement any two complete laws differ in total variation by at most its probability. Fresh Haar replacement respects deadlines and needs no persistent simulator label.

For the lower, let $\widetilde x$ be the unit point obtained by multiplying the simulated reported rotations into the known prepared point. Consider the bounded statistic $S\widetilde x_I\in[-1,1]$ of the reported transcript and the subsequently revealed audit. Under the ideal experiment its expectation is $\chi/k$. In the erased experiment, conditional on the entire allowed visible history before the audit, the predictive coordinate is zero on any hidden-transform history and has norm one on the surviving history. Simulator randomness is independent of the physical state conditional on that information. Since the query $I$ is drawn only at audit, its expectation under any causal simulator is at most $w_n\chi/k$. The expectation gap is at least $(1-w_n)\chi/k$, and the total-variation bound for a statistic in $[-1,1]$ is twice its total variation. This proves the lower even for unbounded simulator memory. In the quantum case the same argument uses the conditional density $I/D$ on the erased event, not an assumed hidden joint value of incompatible measurements.

For~\eqref{eq:full-resource-law} combine the homogeneous (or circle) clock bound, the singular upper using a counted inactive label in every phase, and the calibration corollary. In the range $M-1\ge2n$, balancing the widths leaves at least one active label per phase and changes the geometric rate by only dimension-dependent constants. The elementary inequality
$\tfrac12(a+1-w)\le 1-w+wa\le a+1-w$ for $0\le a,w\le1$ then replaces the surviving-mass expression by the comparable sum. Finally use~\eqref{eq:deficiency-order}. No allowed error tolerance is automatically treated as a necessary risk floor: the lower uses this actual erased interface and its protected audit.
\end{proof}
