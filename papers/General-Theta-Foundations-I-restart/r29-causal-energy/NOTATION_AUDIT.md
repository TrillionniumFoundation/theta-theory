# Notation audit

- p_t is the full legal-history test mean, v_(t,s) the mean carried by a retained label. They are not the same runtime object.
- e_t is retained conditional-mean energy; eta_t(m) is the row loss coefficient; kappa_t is the raw second-moment multiplier. beta_d(m)=1-q_m(sigma_d) only in the spherical statements.
- For general group orbits, the lower uses all normalized incoming directions. A pure prepared orbit alone is insufficient.
- d is the actual geometric exponent; k is the number of centered test coordinates; D is Hilbert-space dimension. The projective case has d=2(D-1), k=D^2-1.
- M is total autonomous labels; m_t is phase-local width. N=n+2 counts all physical preparation/report/audit calls. q_M is squared distortion, not root error.
- B=V-1 is the original same-task baseline; B_n=V-w_n is the erased full-history baseline. Calibration delta denotes the invisible physical sign shift. Numerical h=2^-p, gamma and zeta are separate.
- K_Z bounds audit norm; K=M-1 is used only in allocation. The final numerical bound uses K_Z, not an overloaded state count.
- epsilon_* is an infimum over the explicitly allowed audit-preserving causal simulators; 1-w is actual erasure probability. These are comparable, not asserted identical.
- In the retained physical-time proof, T is the joint duration-exit matrix; T_j^bd is a boundary time. C_N counts started/crossing excursions. The shifted K_N=C_N+1 is distinguished from the unrelated family modulus K_N(X).
- Every retained tail term is written B_N(X), or B_N(X times the test family). Norm arguments z,y are braced. The zero approximate-corrector price case is explicit.
- Stable theorem labels are compiled and printed numbers extracted from the actual auxiliary file. Native and Companion F labels are audited in separate documents.
