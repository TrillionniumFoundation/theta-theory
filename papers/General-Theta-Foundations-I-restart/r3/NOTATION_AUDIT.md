# Notation audit

- mu_t is an actual acquired forward measure; w_tj is its component mass. sigma_j is a verified conditional law in the raw-flow realization, not a substitute for mu_t.
- Q_N(m) in the new theorem is an explicit mass/cover profile infimum, while q_M in the one-probe identity denotes ordinary quantization. They are not conflated.
- r_j(k) is a length scale; squared distortion uses r_j(k)^2. Zero allocation is charged at a_j^2.
- v_tj are bounded recharge densities, independent of allocation; the scalar v_t in the raw-flow corollary is a refresh probability. Their roles and indices are local and explicit.
- xi_j is a local state-metric implementation allowance; xi_t in the inherited resolvent is an environment variable. Contexts are separate.
- rho_n in the singular construction is a contraction ratio; rho in the scalar probe is a thinning probability. Neither denotes complete causal deficiency epsilon_N.
- N=n+1 in the initialized recurrent realization; the selected terminal probe adds one raw call. The scalar-probe horizon N counts attempts, with a further terminal call.
- A_G=max((1+eta)G^2-1,0), C_b=1+1/eta for b_*=1. They are not unknown apparatus parameters.
- The seeded inherited recurrence uses tilde Z_0=S separately from Z_0=0; S corresponds to the paid time-zero innovation.
- All logarithms in the noisy information converse are natural. Persistent bits convert via log 2.
- TV is sup_A, one half of density L1. Root errors are added before squaring, not squared twice.
