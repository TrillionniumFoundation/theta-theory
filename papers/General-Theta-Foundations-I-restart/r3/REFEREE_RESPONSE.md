## Multiscale singular geometry (report sections 6.4 and 8.4)

(MG) replaces finite bi-Lipschitz boxes by global covers, actual small-ball bounds and relative scale separation on finitely or countably many components. The lower assigns arbitrary decoder centers to disjoint scale neighborhoods, and sums by monotone convergence. The upper uses a finite allocation and one shared anchor. A one-label regularity inequality reconciles the budgets without a constant depending on the number of strata.

`lem:moran-profile`, `thm:moran`, and `cor:no-dimension` verify these conditions for a computably described nonhomogeneous binary product measure.
