# Literature comparison and verification scope

Primary sources consulted for the additional comparisons:

- Ogura and Martin, SIAM J. Control Optim. 52 (2014), 1809--1831, doi:10.1137/130925177; primary SIAM article record and arXiv:1309.2720. It characterizes mean stability of positive semi-Markovian jump systems with state resets through a spectral-radius criterion. We do not claim novelty for positive stability methods. The new statement concerns forced finite error measures dominated by time-varying acquired laws, with actual-mass lower and causal finite-label upper.
- Zhu, arXiv:1802.03723, *Asymptotic uniformity of the quantization error for Moran measures on R^1*. It compares optimal Voronoi contributions and successive errors for Moran measures with uniformly positive contraction and probability parameters. The binary cylinder estimate here is an explicit verification tool, not a claim to replace Moran quantization theory. The additional conclusion is the nonstationary causal transfer on countably many acquired components.
- Saldi, Yüksel and Linder, arXiv:1710.07009, *Finite model approximations for partially observed Markov decision processes with discounted cost*. Its abstract and theorem-level comparison in the predecessor concern nearly optimal belief-MDP policies under weak continuity. The present fixed-exploration acquired-law lower does not assert that optimized-control theorem.
- Cover and Thomas, *Elements of Information Theory*, second edition, Wiley, 2006, doi:10.1002/047174882X. Fano's inequality and the Gaussian maximum-entropy bound are classical; their elementary proofs are supplied here to expose the exact retained-information cut.

The retained bibliography includes Shalizi--Crutchfield, controlled predictive representations, Blackwell, Le Cam, Torgersen, Graf--Luschgy, finite-filter approximation, behavioral metrics, Markov jump systems and categorical kernel sufficiency. Those comparison paragraphs are preserved and extended, not replaced by generic priority claims.

Primary-source metadata and abstracts were checked for the new references. This is not an exhaustive literature or priority clearance, and no claim is made that no neighboring theorem exists. No secondary summary is used as a premise of a mathematical proof. The manuscript provides self-contained proofs for its new claims.
