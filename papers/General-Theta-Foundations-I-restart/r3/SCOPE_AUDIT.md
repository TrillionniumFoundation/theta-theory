## Established in this revision

A general forward error-measure theorem for finite/countable scale-separated acquired components, a nonstationary raw refresh-flow criterion uniform through rare components and waiting, a complete singular recurrent realization with oscillatory multiscale profiles, a noisy continuation information converse, and a one-probe coupled acquisition/memory/calibration/finite-input minimax law. All inherited finite-chart, stationary-kernel, resource and realization results remain.

## Not claimed closed

- Arbitrary nonreset transfer of nonzero old error between different strata. The current raw partition criterion resets on a stratum change; a general matrix-valued transport classification is still missing.
- Arbitrary countable intersecting strata or arbitrary singular measures without the stated small-ball/cover/relative-separation data.
- A universal optimum in the full resource vector: transient workspace, program length, runtime, pilot and calibration acquisition below the feasible upper region still lack matching general converses.
- A sharp noisy-tag upper matching the information lower at every eta, M and anisotropy.
- A natural recurrent unknown-kernel model jointly optimizing exploration, calibration, compression and causal simulation. The one-probe theorem couples a scalar target and its actual acquisition, but is not that unrestricted problem.
- Unrestricted optimized control, unknown-kernel learning, arbitrary stopping selection, infinite-horizon transcript equivalence, LDP transfer from TV alone, or unbounded-operator closure.

These are mathematical scope boundaries, not statements that the mother problem is impossible and not recommendations to abandon its general target. No claim about top-four publication suitability is inferred from build results or the internal audit.
