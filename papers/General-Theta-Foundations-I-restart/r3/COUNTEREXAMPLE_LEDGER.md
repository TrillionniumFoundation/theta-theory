# Counterexample and boundary ledger

1. **Reachable envelope is not acquired mass.** A point mass and a uniform distribution share an interval envelope but have different finite-label distortions. The lower uses the actual forward law.
2. **Mean contraction is not terminal localization.** A positive unconditioned moment bound can coexist with forcing in a terminal component of arbitrarily small mass. The new theorem requires a forward measure inequality; the raw-flow corollary derives it without dividing by the mass.
3. **No refresh, expansive continuation.** In the proposed sufficient criterion, x>0,y=0 and (1+eta)G^2>1 make its recharge deficit negative. This refutes automatic satisfaction of the criterion, not the existence of every possible horizon-specific encoder.
4. **Holds do not inject error.** h=1,u=v=0 cancels both sides exactly. Adding a nonzero fresh forcing term destroys this identity and creates spurious waiting costs.
5. **Nonreset cross-component moves.** The scalar per-component induction cannot omit incoming old error from another component. Such a model is outside its hypotheses unless a full matrix balance is supplied.
6. **Conditional independence is necessary for the measure recursion.** A mark chosen using a retained error not represented by the factor cannot be conditioned as if it depended only on that factor. Enlarge the factor and charge any implemented memory, or verify a different bound.
7. **Countable overlapping components.** Arbitrary centers cannot be assigned once if relative-scale neighborhoods overlap. The countable lower does not cover that case; finite intersecting strata retain their separate theorem.
8. **Zero-probability reports.** No pointwise Bayes update is inferred from a null singleton. Declared versions or direct update verification remain necessary.
9. **Free shared randomness / clock leaks.** A free seed correlated with the raw path, a surviving workspace word or an observation-dependent clock invalidates a nominal M-state lower. All such retained information is charged.
10. **Coin thinning versus deficiency.** For N=5,s=1,rho=1/10,L=4, terminal packet deficiency (1-(9/10)^5)*3/4 exceeds rho. A single-call coin is not a complete causal defect.
11. **Optional numerical error.** Allowed inaccuracy does not force an optimized algorithm to incur it. The minimax packet/calibration floors arise from compulsory indistinguishable input interfaces.
12. **TV versus exponential laws.** delta_0 and (1-exp(-Nc))delta_0+exp(-Nc)delta_1 have vanishing TV and different rates at 1. No LDP equivalence is inferred.
13. **Stopping selection.** A deterministic-time conditional error bound need not survive a stopping rule that selects an atypically large error. No such extension is claimed without a stopped certificate.
