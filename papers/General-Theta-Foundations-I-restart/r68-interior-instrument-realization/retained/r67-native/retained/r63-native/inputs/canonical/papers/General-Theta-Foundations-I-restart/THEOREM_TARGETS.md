# Load-bearing theorem targets

## F1 — Causal acquired-geometry transfer

Prove a class theorem deriving matching checkpoint and online resource curves from verifiable experiment-level hypotheses: actual acquisition small-ball mass, global covering, update stability, report-kernel continuity and explicit simulator-state accounting.

## F2 — Resource--resolution--risk composition

Combine acquisition count, persistent-state budget, calibration error and causal deficiency in one common task metric. State both an implementable upper and a matched lower under explicit attainable alternatives.

## F3 — Singular extension

Establish at least one nontrivial changing-rank or nonuniformly stable class in which the same theorem survives through singular strata. A fixed designed finite ensemble alone does not satisfy this target.

## F4 — Realization theorem

Derive the hypotheses for at least two genuinely different experiments. The archived refreshing-HMM and ordered-measurement programmes are candidate realizations, not substitutes for F1--F3.
