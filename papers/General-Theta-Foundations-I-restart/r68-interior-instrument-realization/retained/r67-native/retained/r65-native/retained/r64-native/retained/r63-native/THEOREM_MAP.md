## Current central proof chain

1. Common finite physical interface, compact model carrier and continuous raw instruments precede the uniform dark-margin lemma. Modelwise absence of a dark subspace becomes a uniform cost-bounded fallback by compactness; fixed unknown models must not be replaced by a switching adversary.
2. Positive effect bounds and instrument normalization give occupation-sensitive finite-prefix comparisons. The recursion runs on actual source pre-call occupation. Bounded terminal-observable error is charged once; cost perturbation retains the remaining horizon.
3. Finite common model-cover plans give the risk lower. Completing that actual plan and mixing it with the counted fallback restores the **same** cost budget on every target model and every initial density matrix. One common dictionary is maintained.
4. The lower reserved-cost and upper completed-cost certificates converge to N_min. Their strict upper witnesses permit risk synthesis at every N>N_min, not only above a crude primitive fallback bound. Full-history cost attainment does not imply finite-memory boundary attainment.
5. For compact algebraic primitives, quantified polynomial finite-prefix programs and classical real-algebraic elimination produce a computable N_min and approximate same-budget algebraic policies. No polynomial runtime, bounded-bit sampler or universal geometric-profile characterization is asserted.
6. Two-sided optimal-risk robustness follows by transferring a near-optimal source policy in each direction. The boundary, horizon-cost, affine-vertex and switching examples mark exact limits of the hypotheses.

The geometric actual-history transfer, width-resolved loss, singular range, zero-risk atomic spectrum, finite synthesis and potential certificates, report-resolved reusable-task depth, reset/detector optima, mixed acquired geometry, calibrated composition and predictive quotient remain fully present. The unknown-model risk route assumes no predictive-quotient oracle; the quotient appendix retains its fixed-law scope. The compact theorem is complementary to, not a proof of universal necessity for, the geometric reference-profile certificate.
