This is an author audit, not an earliest-priority or journal-significance certificate. The new mathematical distinction is constructive report-resolved instrument realization with exact stopped-resource transfer. ## New primary-source comparison

Fanizza, Lumbreras and Winter, *Quantum Theory in Finite Dimension Cannot Explain Every General Process with Finite Memory*, Communications in Mathematical Physics 405, article 50 (2024), DOI 10.1007/s00220-023-04913-4, was read in its primary full HTML. Theorem 2 and Section 4.2 already give a noise-dependent worst-phase lower for classical realization of a noisy qubit-process family. The conclusion varies the phase and uses that process's noise parameters. R68's fixed-angle logarithmic exclusion estimate is weaker and has different quantifiers; it is not claimed to supersede or originate such bounds. Sections 2.1–2.2 also explicitly describe finite-rank quasi-realizations and invariant polyhedral-cone characterizations. Their prior status is stated in the native manuscript.

Monras and Winter, *Quantum Learning of Classical Stochastic Processes: The Completely-Positive Realization Problem*, LIPIcs TQC 2014, 99–109, DOI 10.4230/LIPIcs.TQC.2014.99, is retained as primary positive/quantum realization ancestry. Its publisher record and abstract were rechecked. Heller's criterion remains credited rather than relabelled as R68. Our finite hull is derived directly from the branch margin and proved to preserve all terminal effects and stopped laws. That constructive quantitative statement, not the mere existence of a positive cone, is the present claim for independent assessment.

The matching exponent d-1 pertains to hulls of actual density matrices. It is not asserted for unrestricted scalar hidden Markov response realizations. The matrix convexity, Haar-cap calculation and finite-dimensional algebraic tools used to prove it are elementary/established; their use does not itself establish novelty. A comprehensive comparison to every convex-approximation or realization theorem has not been certified.
