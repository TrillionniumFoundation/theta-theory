## Substantive mathematical advance in this round

The new `thm:primitive-risk` treats an arbitrary finite family of time-homogeneous finite-dimensional classical or quantum instruments, with globally legal finite commands/reports, positive bounded call costs, bounded prospective audit loss, and a fixed finite output dictionary. It does not require reset, commuting effects, a balancing group, a supplied least-favorable reference member, or a controller-stored posterior.

First, `thm:primitive-fallback` derives a single proper command-row fallback from the primitive absence of a common dark subspace in every model. The qualitative obstruction is credited to existing quantum reachability theory. The quantitative finite-depth margin supplies an actual common cost bound kappa. Next, a finite common sequence-form semidefinite problem gives an attained lower value q_T(N). Its open-path cost reserve prevents a cutoff from being declared free success. Completion with the derived fallback produces a real policy. For every N>kappa, a counted initial mixture restores the original budget, giving

`0 <= R_C(N)-q_T(N) <= L D_T + L e_T/(N-kappa+e_T)`,

where `D_T=min(1,(N/c_*-1)/T)` and `e_T=(kappa-c_*)D_T`. The upper is implemented at the same N, with an explicit sufficient finite-label bound. Compact decision nets preserve a prescribed output width. At N=kappa the proof claims only its stated enlarged-budget completion unless kappa equals the call-cost floor. Neither original tail-cost uniform integrability nor a free branch seed is assumed.

The additional `thm:interior-budget` gives monotone lower and upper finite cost problems converging, with an explicit gap, to the least feasible cost N_min. For every N>N_min the upper search eventually constructs a finite fallback with kappa<N. The same-budget risk conclusion therefore extends to every strictly interior feasible budget, not merely budgets above an arbitrary initial command row. No finite-memory optimum is asserted exactly at N_min.

This is a constructive characterization of the actual constrained risk on that finite interface, rather than only a verifier for a supplied balancing routine. It does not show that every such risk equals the geometric comparison profile Q. The geometric and primitive routes are complementary and have separate hypotheses. The new six-regime selected-mass calculation solves an explicit mixed-strata comparator, including two nonsmooth optimal intervals; it is not presented as an exact universal physical risk formula.

## Historical closures, new correction and remaining obligations

R59 expressly accepted the event/state semantics, actual-history lower, conditional hazard, unmatched cost constants and scope of the effective layer. Their complete valid proofs are retained. The original R56 M-B mistake is not reintroduced or repeatedly presented as an unresolved objection. The R61 atomic zero-risk refinement is retained as a genuine later correction, rather than removed merely because the R59 report accepted the earlier presentation.

During this revision, one R61 reset-control sentence was tightened: an initial row minimizing worst-initial-state cost is not necessarily pointwise optimal for every known initial state. The proof had established the robust minimax statement. The current sentence now says exactly that, and the supplement gives an orthogonal-detector counterexample to the stronger reading. The original source is preserved and the existing value formulas are unchanged.

The new theorem resolves a finite-model constructive direction requested in section 17 of the report, but does not derive the geometric reference for all causal families, learn an arbitrary unknown kernel, or give matching program/scratch/precision/time lower bounds. The proof's strict budget slack N>kappa is visible; its boundary is not hidden in a denominator. Invariance under arbitrary stateful simulation and minimal simulator memory remain separate research tasks. Ordered finite-use quantum measurements, two-stage reset communication, rank-constrained discrimination, spectral formulae and other original lines remain at their pinned independent sources and are not claimed to follow from the new theorem.
