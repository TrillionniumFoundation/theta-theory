\section{Arithmetic synthesis and diagnostic boundaries}\label{sec:supp-r71}
The main arithmetic-envelope theorem constructs a stochastic matrix, not merely a rational angle close to a target. If $a/q$ is the chosen approximation, pass to the explicitly counted multiple $l$ of $q$. The target eigenvalue belongs to the triangle with vertices zero and the two adjacent $l$th roots of unity. If its radial boundary is $r$, the two chord coefficients give that boundary point; scaling them by $\alpha/r$ and assigning the remaining weight to $J=\one\one^{\mathsf T}/l$ gives a stochastic circulant matrix. Its row from the prescribed initial vertex produces the complete response from time zero. This explains why passing to a multiple costs labels but no physical invocation.

The arithmetic envelope itself also admits finite certified bounds. For any integer $B\ge1$, write
\[
 A_B=\min_{1\le q\le B}\max\{3,q,e_x(q)/\epsilon\}.
\]
Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:supp-r71-enumeration}
 \min\{A_B,B+1\}\le\mathfrak A_x(\epsilon)\le A_B.
\end{equation}
Indeed every omitted integer denominator is at least $B+1$. In particular, once $B+1\ge A_B$, enumeration gives the exact envelope if its finitely many arithmetic comparisons are exact. For a computable irrational phase, rational interval enclosures instead give certified upper and lower values by replacing each error with its interval bounds. Precision is increased whenever a comparison or a polygon margin remains unresolved. A supplied strictly feasible polygon upper is a finite exact construction; this does not assert decidability of arbitrary equality cases from finite-precision phase samples.

The two noise sequences used in the main proof have different functions. For a convergent denominator $Q$ with error $e_x(Q)$, noise $e_x(Q)/Q$ is a low-memory scale. For the next denominator $Q'$, noise $1/[Q(Q')^2]$ is a crossover scale at which every candidate denominator below $Q'/2$ has excessive error. Both statements use all candidate rationals in their lower arguments. Restricting a numerical search to convergents alone is not the proof of the universal envelope or of the crossover exclusion.

\subsection{Finite computations supplied with this revision}
The R71 diagnostic program separately records exact rational checks and floating matrix probes. The exact part tests finite continued-fraction identities, all denominators below the tested convergent bound, the rational separation used at crossover scales, complete finite envelope enumeration and its certified stopping condition. Rational finite tails are deliberately identified as rational test data. Their finite check results are not used to infer an irrationality exponent of a limiting number.

The floating part independently constructs the full circulant transition matrix and compares its forward probabilities with raw qubit iteration and with the target scalar formula. It checks bounded decoders, row normalization, nonnegative mixture weights, spectral eigenvectors, the envelope constants and the power-sector obstruction on finite examples. A separate sequence of large partial quotients probes the two distinct noise scales in the fluctuation proof; it is not labeled a numerical proof of the limiting exponents. Exact special cases and deliberately invalid shortcuts provide negative controls.

\subsection{Resources and unresolved sharp constants}
The upper construction may use more states than a minimal stochastic realization by the universal factor in Theorem~\ref{thm:r71-envelope}. Its main purpose is a uniform matching law and exact realization, not a claim that the polygon is optimal at every phase. At algebraic eigenvalues, the inherited finite spectral-membership test can search the actual minimal size below a supplied upper. For general computable phases, finite interval arithmetic can certify strict feasible constructions without deciding all exact spectral boundary equalities.

The growth exponents concern $\log M/\log(1/\epsilon)$ for the least live-label count. In a finite mixture, taking a maximum before a lower limit is essential; no simultaneous Diophantine property is supplied by separate one-dimensional irrationality exponents. Classical rational approximation, stochastic spectral geometry, positive realization and the physical squared embedding retain their distinct roles in this proof chain. No formal proof-assistant check or independent human priority assessment is claimed by the finite diagnostics or the native build.
