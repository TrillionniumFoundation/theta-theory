## Necessity and implementation layers

The affine-square matrix order is necessary and sufficient for its specified rich localized test family. The width-defect theorem is exact for the narrower selected loss and a single global decoder. Geometric task transfer is sufficient under its all-competitor lower and common-program upper certificates. The inherited finite-obstruction hierarchy is necessary and sufficient for common bounded target synthesis within finite branching and positive costs; its compact limiting policy does not carry a finite-memory guarantee. Fixed-memory potential certificates have their own time-homogeneous finite/algebraic scope. The current primitive route derives fallback feasibility and computes the constrained optimal risk at all strictly interior feasible budgets without assuming the geometric reference profile.

The predictive quotient is required for the separate executable representation conclusion, not as an oracle in either risk proof. Nonintervening sensing is retained in the full companion; post-command and state-changing tasks are not advertised as nonintervening. Read-only program bits, workspace, precision, arithmetic and physical time require additional effective hypotheses; no label count proves their optimality.
