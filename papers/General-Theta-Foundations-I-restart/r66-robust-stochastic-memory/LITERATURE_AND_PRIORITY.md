The new mathematical assertions are the weighted cost-support rigidity bridge, its robust tied optimal face and exact stochastic implementation, the continuum-horizon calibration identity, exact arbitrary finite dimensions, and the globally sharp three-label qubit threshold with a finite minimal-synthesis procedure. Their proofs do not assume any unverified theorem from the cited sources. Compact minimax, matrix positive potentials, Cayley–Hamilton, analytic uniqueness, finite rank arguments and real-algebraic elimination remain classical methods.
