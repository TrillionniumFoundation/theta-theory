## Principal new chain

`branchwise interior margin -> projective net -> positive matrix hull -> sparse branch interpolation -> all-history matrix identity -> exact stopped law and cost -> same-policy risk equality`.

The state-positive vertex lower has its own positivity and all-terminal-effects quantifiers. The separate near-peripheral spectral bound concerns arbitrary stochastic realizations of one fixed scalar response; neither lower is substituted for the other.
