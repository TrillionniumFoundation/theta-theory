The mathematical lower for the stochastic qubit does not use finite tests to infer nonperiodicity. The analytic proof excludes every finite memory and arbitrary real transition probabilities. The exact two-label optimum is not inferred from sampled matrices: the proof reduces the full feasible square of transition parameters to an explicit global scalar maximization. Numerical probes only test its implementation.

The expected-risk series is a bounded terminal series with a geometric probability tail. No claim about uniform integrability of arbitrary remaining stopping costs is used. The protected quantum state is part of the physical experiment, not a free persistent classical register or a runtime phase input.

Only the new boundary theorem class and the mathematical arguments needed for its integration have been newly audited at proof level. All historical texts were scanned and preserved by identity, but every theorem in every old revision was not re-proved. The original R64 report's accepted closures remain inherited rather than repeatedly reopened.
