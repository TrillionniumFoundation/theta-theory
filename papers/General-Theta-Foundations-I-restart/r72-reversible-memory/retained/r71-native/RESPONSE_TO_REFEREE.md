## New mathematical advance

The new `thm:r71-envelope` gives a universal, matching constant-factor law for the exact stochastic response memory at **every nonreal phase**, with no badly-approximable hypothesis:

`A_x(epsilon)/2 <= m_x(epsilon) <= (1+4 pi^2) A_x(epsilon)`,

where `A_x(epsilon)=min_{q>=1} max(3,q,||qx||/(q epsilon))`. The lower tests arbitrary stochastic realizations through powers and the classical sector inequality. The upper constructs a counted pure-start circulant by passing to a suitable multiple of a rational approximation denominator. It is not merely another regular-polygon upper with an unmatched lower.

`thm:r71-exponents` determines both logarithmic growth exponents for every irrational phase of irrationality exponent mu: `1/(mu+1)` and `(mu-1)/(2mu-1)`, with limits zero and one-half when mu is infinite. The proof treats favorable approximation scales and the intervening convergent-denominator crossover scales separately. `thm:r71-cubic-rigidity` then proves that a uniform cubic-root law holds if and only if the phase is badly approximable. At every other irrational phase, the cubic-root-normalized memory has lower limit zero and upper limit infinity. Thus a logarithmic exponent of one-third need not imply uniform cubic-root constants.

`cor:r71-mixtures` gives the corresponding envelope for finite spectral mixtures and leaky responses, retaining every frequency's spectral obstruction, the complete response rank and all initial/mixture/zero-output live labels. It explicitly avoids commuting a lower limit with a maximum of different phases. The exact single-frequency leakage identity transfers both exponents and the uniform-law characterization independently of absolute leakage.

These results close a precisely stated fixed-phase matching question left open in the previous source. They do **not** solve the different general-M squared-sign risk frontier, arbitrary multiple-command boundary control, the numerical irrationality exponent of `(3+4i)/5`, or matched program/sampling/workspace complexity. The physical task is the existing fixed squared-loss experiment with its actual audit and original least budget, not a newly supplied loss oracle. The title and journal objective are not lowered in lieu of a stronger theorem.
