## What has changed mathematically

The present revision develops a complementary exact physical-realization theorem for several commands and report-dependent quantum dynamics in arbitrary finite dimension. It derives a finite positive-state hull from a primitive interiority margin, rather than assuming a finitely generated cone or a supplied candidate controller. Positive branch interpolation then proves exact equality of all adaptively controlled stopped laws and actual expected costs, including nontermination. It therefore preserves the original external observer's constrained risk at every budget and memory count without granting that observer the simulator's hidden state.

The hidden-state count is explicit and independent of the horizon, command count and report count, although table storage depends on the latter two. Its worst-case noise exponent has matching upper and lower bounds in the stated state-positive realization class. Several-step interiority is sufficient with a counted finite clock. Algebraic instrument inputs give a terminating sparse construction. Exact multi-command examples include noncommuting, nonreset, state-dependent-report instruments without a shared stationary state.

A separate spectral argument supplies a computable rational exclusion margin for every fixed classical memory size in the inherited fixed-angle qubit response. This improves its qualitative divergence to a non-sharp quantitative lower. It does not purport to solve the report's requested sharp general-M squared-sign frontier, and it is not stronger than the different varying-angle noisy-process lower already proved by Fanizza, Lumbreras and Winter.

## Additional proof and priority audit

The row-wise lower bound is imposed on every actual reported branch. An average depolarizing channel is insufficient: the recorded unitary example has infinitely many pure conditioned states and no finite state-positive all-audit realization. The finite-cap lower permits mixed density vertices, not only the pure vertices used for the construction. Its interpretation is expressly restricted; arbitrary scalar HMMs may use intermediate response functions with no positive density representation. The independent eigenvalue-cycle proof handles those scalar realizations and does not borrow the state-positive lower.

Heller's positive-realization criterion and Monras--Winter's quantum realization theory are prior machinery. Fanizza--Lumbreras--Winter (2024), Theorem 2 and Section 4.2, already prove noise-dependent worst-phase classical-memory bounds for a different noisy qubit process. We compare their quantifiers directly and do not advertise the fixed-angle bound here as the first or strongest such result. The projective cap and sparse convex representation steps are proved from elementary ingredients in the article, not hidden behind empirical diagnostics or an unverified external lemma. Earliest priority and top-four significance are not certified by this author audit.
