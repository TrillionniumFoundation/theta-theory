# R62 author proof audit

This is an author-side proof audit, not an independent referee report. The controlling report is R59, which accepted the main halt-semantics repair and actual-history proof structure. Those closures are inherited rather than re-opened cosmetically.

Inherited R61 continuation-span, zero-risk spectrum, finite-memory potentials, two-projector frontier, task-width defects, calibrated transfer, singular geometry, quotient and companion proofs remain available. No new missing step is replaced by a numerical check or an unproved external hypothesis. The new route derives its fallback but does not force an arbitrary finite instrument's optimal risk to equal the comparison profile Q.

Finite model uncertainty is not unknown-kernel learning. Restricted legal domains require their own preparation/support treatment and are not silently covered by the globally legal fallback theorem. Matching lower bounds for program bits, workspace, precision, computation time and general simulator memory remain open research obligations.
