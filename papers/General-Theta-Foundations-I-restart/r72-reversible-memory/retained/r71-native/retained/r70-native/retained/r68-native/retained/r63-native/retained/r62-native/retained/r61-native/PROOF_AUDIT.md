The principal new proof steps were checked directly from definitions. No key step is delegated to numerical tests. Classical compactness, separation, Bellman verification, positive linear maps and real-algebraic elimination are credited as methods, not presented as newly invented machinery.

Remaining research includes arbitrary nonfinite branching, intrinsic minimal memory under general stateful simulation, matching program/scratch/precision/time lower bounds, broader nonintervening sensing, and genuinely general pipeline adapters. The independent A1/A2, unbounded-operator and large-deviation programmes remain independent. A full line-by-line reproof of every historical v1–v96 document has not been completed; the full corpus scan is not described as such a reproof.

The report-resolved continuation criterion is further proved for standard Borel return instruments, with an existential finite measurable coarsening. Its bound d^2-r0 has a matching physical example at r0=1: a full-rank initial preparation and an algebraic strictly completely positive channel first violate squared-task balance after d^2-1 rejections. The existing Hermitian reachable-space distinction is credited explicitly to Fu et al. (2024), not claimed as new. Continuous measure identities are not asserted decidable from arbitrary numerical bins.
