\section{Two raw-kernel verifications with imperfect references}
\label{sec:calibrated-realizations}
We verify the same two-sided theorem in a classical controlled experiment and a noncommuting instrument. These examples extend the exact constructions of Section~\ref{sec:raw}; the physical audit is the post-command task in each. The nonintervening sensing result of Theorem~\ref{thm:directional} has a different task and is retained separately.

Use the two-port gate of Lemma~\ref{lem:gate}. All gate success probabilities are at least $p_0>0$. In the actual reference they are at most $p_1\le1$. The command supports of the two ports are disjoint, with no free port input. Thus $B_0=B=2$, and the same predictable count argument gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:imperfect-gate}
 \kappa_-=1+1/p_1,\qquad \kappa_+=1+1/p_0.
\end{equation}
Indeed each productive call needs a successful gate call. Under the reference, the expected number of gate successes is at most $p_1$ times the expected number of gate calls, so total cost is at least $(1+1/p_1)\E_*A_\tau$. The gate-first construction has conditional expected cost at most $1+1/p_0$. No exact gate parameter is needed. Gate reports can reveal physical information; all subsequent conditional certificates below must still hold after that information.

\begin{theorem}[A classical imperfect-reference realization]\label{thm:classical-calibrated}
Let $E\in\{-1,1\}$ be an arbitrary hidden sign at a productive call. Within that call the commands are identity and sign reversal, followed by a known nominal report--audit channel $J_E(dy,dz)$, $z\in H$. The declared comparison prepares a fair sign and has
$K_0=(J_1+J_{-1})/2$; its full report is its mark, with derived moments \eqref{eq:derived}.

Suppose the actual family contains a witness whose pre-command conditional sign bias, after every surviving history and legal productive command choice, has absolute value at most $\eta_r$. Each actual report--audit channel, conditional on either post-command sign and the full pre-call history, is within probability total variation $\xi_-$ of $J_E$ under this witness and within $\xi_+$ under every operating model. The common upper uses a fresh sign-reversal coin whose conditional law is within $\eta_u$ of a fair coin, independent of the current hidden sign given pre-call history. Assume the gate conditions above.

Then the lower row certificates hold with
\[
 a_-=\eta_r+\xi_-,\qquad e_-=L(\eta_r+\xi_-),
\]
and the common upper certificates hold with
\[
 a_+=\eta_u+\xi_+,\qquad e_+=L(\eta_u+\xi_+),
\]
whenever $a_+<1$. Exports are the identity, so $D_0=1$. The two-sided bounds of Theorem~\ref{thm:reference-stability} apply with actual witness effort \eqref{eq:imperfect-gate}. An exactly fair witness need not be present. In particular any nominal marked geometry verified from $(J_1+J_{-1})/2$ yields matching curves in the certified regime of Corollary~\ref{cor:reference-geometry}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For any probability weights on two signs, the variation distance between its mixture of $J_1,J_{-1}$ and their fair mixture is at most the distance of the sign weights from the fair weights. Identity or reversal preserves the absolute sign bias. Applying the actual channel instead of $J_E$ changes the full joint law by at most $\xi_-$, after integration over the sign. Therefore every chosen lower command and actual history has full report--audit distance at most $\eta_r+\xi_-$. This includes any auxiliary report through the declared channel; a report revealing a hidden audit coin would have to be included and satisfy this same bound. The bounded-loss argument gives $e_-$.

For the upper, write the hidden sign mean as $s\in[-1,1]$ and the sign of the independent applied command as $F$, with $|\E F|\le2\eta_u$. Then $|\E(EF)|\le2\eta_u$, so the post-command sign law is within $\eta_u$ of fairness, on every surviving history and for every hidden input distribution. Averaging the within-call command and using the channel error gives full task-law distance at most $\eta_u+\xi_+$. The algorithm accepts using the virtual report, not the discarded command coin. The audit is the post-command audit and its finite output is unchanged by export. Hence no command coin or hidden sign persists in the observer. The common controller has the two gate roles and the required output roles. All constants are independent of the chosen dictionary and budget. The gate argument already supplies effort and mandatory live roles; the general theorem now applies without using the realization to prove it.
\end{proof}

The reference condition can be verified, for example, by a genuinely biased preparation with parameter in $(0,\eta_r]$ at each productive call. The admitted witness is then not the fair comparison. More generally conditional biases and channel defects can depend on a hidden good/bad calibration mode. Convexity of variation gives the visible conditional defect bounds of Theorem~\ref{thm:metastable}. This realizes its nonuniform extension with actual correlated physical modes, not a change of the formal parameter dimension.

\begin{theorem}[A noncommuting imperfect-reference realization]\label{thm:quantum-calibrated}
Let the productive physical system be a qubit. Its commands include the four Pauli conjugations, followed by a nominal normalized positive quantum instrument and its fixed prospective audit, with classical joint law $\mathcal J(\rho)(dy,dz)$, $z\in H$. The map $\mathcal J$ is affine, completely positive before its classical output, and trace preserving when all reports and audit outcomes are included. Take comparison state $\omega=I/2$ and $K_0=\mathcal J(\omega)$.

Suppose an actual witness satisfies
$\frac12\|\rho-\omega\|_1\le\eta_r$ conditional on every surviving physical history before a chosen productive Pauli command. The conditional actual full instrument, as a map to joint report and audit probabilities, differs from $\mathcal J$ by at most $\xi_-$ in probability variation at this witness and at most $\xi_+$ uniformly on all states and histories in the operating family. Suppose the common upper's randomized Pauli channel $\mathcal T$ satisfies
\[
 \sup_{\rho}\tfrac12\|\mathcal T(\rho)-\omega\|_1\le\eta_u.
\]
Together with the same gate certificate, these raw conditions imply all the conclusions and constants of Theorem~\ref{thm:classical-calibrated}. No common eigenbasis, lower bound on posterior rank, or contraction of a normalized filter is required.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For a Hermitian trace-zero operator $A$, its positive and negative parts have equal trace $\|A\|_1/2$. A normalized positive instrument sends them to positive classical measures of those masses. Therefore
$\|\mathcal J(\rho)-\mathcal J(\sigma)\|_{\TV}\le\|\rho-\sigma\|_1/2$.
Pauli conjugation fixes $\omega$ and preserves trace norm. The witness bound and the primitive instrument error hence give lower full-joint distance at most $\eta_r+\xi_-$. For the upper, integrate the within-call Pauli choice first. Its average state is $\mathcal T(\rho)$, so contraction and the operating instrument error give distance at most $\eta_u+\xi_+$. Apply bounded squared loss to get the task defects. These comparisons concern unnormalized joint report--audit measures; they do not divide by a small outcome probability or claim continuity of a normalized state across a rank change.

Uniform Pauli weights give $\mathcal T(\rho)=I/2$. A weight vector at probability total-variation distance at most $\eta_u$ from uniform gives the displayed bound, because it is a convex combination of density matrices. The audit follows the physical command, so exports are the identity. Its controller stores no qubit: the qubit belongs to the physical experiment, while the observer is the finite classical machine with the two explicitly charged gate roles. The remaining checks are the common gate count and actual full-report measurability. This proves the theorem directly from the raw instrument.
\end{proof}

For a concrete noncommuting family take the equatorial two-outcome instruments of Theorem~\ref{thm:quantum}, with continuously varying axis and effects $[I\pm\eta n\cdot\sigma]/2$, including both $\eta=1$ and $0<\eta<1$. The already derived comparison marked law, including its actual continuous and atomic weights, is unchanged as a calibration object. Biased near-mixed preparations and imperfect randomizations supply the new witness and construction errors. A hidden two-state mode can select a different positive instrument in bad periods; convexity gives the mode charges in \eqref{eq:metastable-mass}--\eqref{eq:metastable-task}. This allows genuine rank-changing and metastable rows. The geometry of the actual operating support is not asserted to equal that of the comparison: it is connected through the proved occupation and task bounds.
