## Logical layers

Prepared kernels and explicit halt semantics precede the actual-history comparison and paid common upper. The geometric conclusions retain their full-report, effort, occupation, implementation and memory certificates. Their general-loss, singular-range, width, zero-spectrum, mixed-geometry and realization results remain fully proved in the current supplement.

For the primitive route, common sequence flow plus nonnegative costs with a positive open-call reserve gives compact feasibility. Bounded terminal tails, not expected remaining-cost tails, give boundary attainment and performed-law compactness. Fixed-dictionary convexity then yields a scalar resource price and complete-score separation; this does not convexify optimized hard width. Compact finite-prefix data and strict slack yield the sparse physical dual. Its common backward recursion requires separate primal feasibility; its cost and risk atoms need not share a model prior.

The new boundary route is logically separate from an interior fallback limit. A model-independent herald probability and predictable counting give the exact least cost and force first-herald commitment. Compact wordwise minimax plus positive word masses gives nonnegative slack. Unique decisions under an attained least-favorable measure allow determinization, hence the exact residual-class memory count. Single-model ties and robust ties are treated separately. Finite table compactness gives attained fixed-memory optima; finite distinguishing words give quantitative positive gaps.

The algebraic qubit combines the herald identity, rational cosine recurrence, root-of-unity obstruction and irrational orbit visits with that memory criterion. The uncertain-polarization corollary derives its own least-favorable model. A contraction and strict limiting decision gap instead produce an exact finite prefix-and-tail observer. Noisy rotation shows the gap cannot be dropped. The exact two-label law additionally uses the full two-state stochastic spectral decomposition and an explicitly optimized rational derivative, including all angle/damping boundary cases. The rational-channel algorithm uses actual matrix recursion and a geometric terminal tail, with no free runtime clock or phase.
