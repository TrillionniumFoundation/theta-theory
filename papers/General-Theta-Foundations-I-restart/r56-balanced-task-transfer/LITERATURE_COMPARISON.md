The inherited rectangle exponent, selected-law density cap and physical-port concerns are explicitly credited to the retained General Theta derivations. The claimed mathematical addition is the written raw joint-task-balance transfer and its complete paid minimax realization, including zero-floor classical/quantum verification and the specified finite-precision/stopped consequences. Whether these advances have general-journal importance requires independent mathematical evaluation. Bibliographic checking and abstract comparison are not a full literature-proof or priority audit.

Gaussian inheritance clarification: R52 already supplies a two-sided common threshold, the logarithmic waiting scale and an exact small-state curve for its positive-prior-floor class. R56 does not claim these tools anew. Its reference lower removes that prior floor for the declared larger uncertainty class and gives a ratio-one minimax asymptotic uniform over all M>=4; physical gate and task-interface changes are separately counted.
