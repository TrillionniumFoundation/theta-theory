# Adversarial proof audit

1. **Quantifiers.** The lower uses one admitted reference theta0 against ALL competitors. The upper uses one theta-independent program against ALL models and surviving histories. No minimax interchange or model-dependent dictionary is hidden.
2. **Actual law.** nu comes from the reference raw report/audit kernel. Terminal measure domination is written before its density. Gate count cost is proved, not inferred from a conditional successful law.
3. **Stopping selection.** The reference conditional audit law includes the entire current report, chosen command and accessible auxiliary information. The independent fresh stop coin cannot leak the audit. Potential audits are defined even on rejected calls.
4. **Joint balance.** Both report mass and unnormalized task loss are balanced. Known randomization coins are not conditioned back into a false posterior identity. An acceptance function depending on the balanced virtual mark is integrated cell by cell with its exported center.
5. **No hidden renewal.** Repeated attempt survival follows from its conditional hazard. Hidden states may change adversarially within the fixed model class; observer start-label reset is not a physical reset.
6. **States.** Every live port and terminal value is counted. Export multiplicity is charged. The same-model mandatory visit quantifier is repeated. Physical legal sets are exactly the sets attached to their contexts, not merely intersections over unvisited histories. No free contextual dispatcher or persistent program-selection seed.
7. **Topologies.** Compactness uses nets, not unjustified sequential weak-star compactness. Full Borel quotient realization requires a section and representative independence. The main proof remains on histories and does not grant an exact posterior register.
8. **Singularities.** Instrument estimates weight normalization by actual report mass. Atomic threshold and rank changes are explicit. Nonnegative kernels, pure states and likelihood zeros do not require division by q.
9. **Geometric range.** Large-b packing has fixed radius and separation thresholds. The bounded-b remainder is handled uniformly in arbitrary m, not by claiming that (b,m) is compact.
10. **Gaussian priority and asymptotics.** Conditional-variance rejection is standard. The new proof here additionally pays gate cost and all persistent states and compares every larger memory budget by the same D(b). Mills bounds retain the factor 4 exp(-2a^2/sigma^2). It is a large feasible rescaled-budget assertion for fixed known a,sigma.
11. **Finite precision.** Borel existence and finite description are separate. Approximation must preserve acceptance probability relative to 1/b; absolute error e gives rho=be. Numerical allowances are not risk lower bounds. The worked dyadic example gives exact rational upper constants and an independently derived ideal all-program lower.
12. **Composition.** TV compares the full stopped/scored task, not only a terminal marginal; time has separate drift/cost certificates. Simulator states multiply, root output errors add before squaring. The product-task delta/epsilon terms are achieved by explicit inaccessible alternatives.
13. **Borel flow.** A legal default selector is required to complete zero-occupation policies. Minimal Green occupation removes residual circulation, preserving terminal submeasure. Finite occupation is essential; no generic compactness or finite-memory realization is inferred.
14. **Independent status.** This is an author-side adversarial audit. Compilation, finite arithmetic checks and repeated builds do not independently certify these arguments, novelty or top-four significance.
