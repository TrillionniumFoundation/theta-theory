# Scope: what this revision proves and what it does not

## Written general result
A raw-kernel, same-task minimax transfer on the stated task-balance/reference/effort certificate class, with arbitrary hidden history dependence, complete autonomous state count, measurable attained Borel realization, effective finite-description approximation under additional certificates, and actual acquired geometry. It is not a finite common-primary-row theorem for all models. It has a regular matching law, exact atomic thresholds and rank-changing raw examples.

## Breadth restrictions kept visible
The uncertainty class must contain the fixed reference member, and the observer must have a parameter-independent balancing acquisition program. The scalar matched effort scale is explicitly proved in the gate examples. Arbitrary positive kernels need not have these properties. The finite moment test is necessary and sufficient only for the specified finite command family and task moments. It is not a near-necessary criterion for every causal algorithm.

Classical sign and quantum Pauli interventions are allowed physical primitives and score the post-command target. They are not a claim of learning an arbitrary pre-existing unknown state at no cost. The Gaussian application separately handles a nonintervening target with known sensor parameters and a guaranteed, possibly history-dependent gate rate.

## Remaining proof/research gaps
1. An intrinsic necessary/sufficient characterization encompassing all history-dependent acquisition and arbitrary unknown kernels, without a matching reference/balance certificate.
2. A general memory invariant under arbitrary stateful simulation, beyond the proved stateless physical-command recoding invariance and mandatory-context lower.
3. Efficient global synthesis and matched program-description, precision, workspace, physical-time and minimal-simulator-state lower bounds.
4. Unknown sensor-parameter learning, general regret rates, unbounded audits, arbitrary infinite physical dimension or non-a.s.-terminating protocols.
5. Uniform degeneration without any positive effort/gate guarantee; at fixed N, p0 approaching zero is genuinely infeasible.

No LDP equivalence from TV, unrestricted infinite-horizon transfer, universal smooth manifold or unbounded operator closure is asserted. Ordinary finite tests and compiler output do not resolve these gaps.

## Historical and editorial status
Old proofs are retained with their original hypotheses, not all re-certified. The native manuscript is the submission; historical companions are optional provenance. Neither an author-side proof audit nor successful CI establishes independent correctness, novelty, impact or top-four acceptance.
