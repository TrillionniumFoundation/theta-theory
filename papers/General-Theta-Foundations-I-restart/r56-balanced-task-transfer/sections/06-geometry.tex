\section{Actual marked geometry, singular supports and atoms}
The following calculation is inherited from the stopped-experiment and predictable-geometry results \cite{GTF52,GTF54}. It is included to make the sharp consequences independent of those manuscripts.

Let $\sigma=(f,\sqrt w)_\#\nu$, supported on $K\times[0,1]$ with compact $K\subset H$. Suppose there are $d,\alpha>0$, $0<r_0\le1$ and constants $c,C>0$ such that for every $x\in K$, $0<r\le r_0$ and $0<s\le1$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rectangle}
 c r^d s^\alpha\le\sigma(B(x,r)\times[0,s])\le C r^d s^\alpha.
\end{equation}
These are statements about the \emph{actual} report--task pushforward, not the volume of a reachable parameter set. They include Ahlfors-regular singular location measures of noninteger $d$ and a reliability coordinate with exponent $\alpha$.

\begin{proposition}[Marked acquired profile]\label{prop:geometry}
Under \eqref{eq:rectangle}, for all $b\ge1$ and $m\ge1$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:geometry}
 \Qm_m(b)\asymp
 \max\{b^{-2/\alpha},(bm)^{-2/(d+\alpha)}\},
\end{equation}
with constants depending only on the displayed geometric data and the bounded location set.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
A finite cover of $K$ by balls of radius $r_0/2$ gives
$\sigma(K\times[0,s])\le C_0s^\alpha$. Under any density cap $b$, the selected reliability obeys
$\Pp(\sqrt w\le s)\le bC_0s^\alpha$. Choose $s=(2C_0b)^{-1/\alpha}$, decreasing it by a fixed factor if necessary, to obtain the first lower bound.
For any dictionary of $m$ points, the sublevel $w+\operatorname{dist}(f,C)^2\le t^2$ is contained in $m$ rectangles of location radius $t$ and reliability at most $t$. Centers outside $K$ can be moved for this covering argument to a point of $K$ within $t$, increasing the radius to $2t$; empty balls are discarded. For $2t\le r_0$ its mass is at most $C_1mt^{d+\alpha}$. Taking $t=c_1(bm)^{-1/(d+\alpha)}$ with a sufficiently small fixed $c_1$ bounds the selected sublevel mass by $1/2$ and gives the second lower. This choice works for all $b,m\ge1$ after reducing $c_1$ once.

For the upper, the marginal $\sigma(\cdot\times[0,1])$ satisfies two-sided $d$-dimensional ball bounds. Maximal separated sets therefore provide at least $c_2r^{-d}$ centers at separation $4r$ for small $r$. Choose
\[
 j=\min\{m,\lfloor c_3b^{d/\alpha}\rfloor\},\qquad
 t=C_2(bj)^{-1/(d+\alpha)}.
\]
Take $c_3>0$ sufficiently small and $C_2$ sufficiently large, in that order with the packing inequality also enforced by decreasing $c_3$. For $b\ge b_0$, where
\[
 b_0=\max\{1,(2/c_3)^{\alpha/d},
            (C_2/\min(r_0/8,1))^{d+\alpha}\},
\]
we have $j\ge1$, $t\le\min(r_0/8,1)$, and $j$ disjoint rectangles
$B(x_i,t)\times[0,t]$ whose union has mass at least
$cjt^{d+\alpha}\ge1/b$.
Here the required packing condition $jt^d\le c_4$ follows from
$j\le c_3b^{d/\alpha}$. A density of mass one bounded by $b$ can be supported on this union (constant inverse union mass suffices), and its distortion to these centers is at most $2t^2$. Substituting $j$ gives the upper in \eqref{eq:geometry}. For $1\le b<b_0$, accept every report and use any fixed point in $H$. This gives a uniform upper while $b^{-2/\alpha}\ge b_0^{-2/\alpha}$ supplies a uniform positive comparison, independently of unbounded $m$. No compactness assertion about the integer $m$ is needed.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[An exact acquired atom transition]\label{prop:atom}
Suppose the marked law is
$\sigma=\lambda\delta_{(x_*,0)}+(1-\lambda)\sigma_0$, $0<\lambda<1$, where $\sigma_0$ is a probability law and has no atom at $(x_*,0)$. Write $\Qm_m^0$ for its profile. If $b\lambda\ge1$, $\Qm_m(b)=0$ for every $m\ge1$. If $r=1-b\lambda>0$, then for every $m\ge2$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:atom}
 r\Qm_m^0\!\left(\frac{b(1-\lambda)}r\right)
 \le\Qm_m(b)\le
 r\Qm_{m-1}^0\!\left(\frac{b(1-\lambda)}r\right).
\end{equation}
Consequently the exact case of Theorem~\ref{thm:main} retains the zero-risk threshold $N=\kappa/\lambda$, with no unknown mass renormalization.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Above threshold, select only the actual atom and output $x_*$. Below threshold, any selected probability has remainder mass $s\ge r$. With its fixed dictionary, scale its remainder submeasure by $r/s$. This yields a submeasure of mass $r$, still dominated by $b(1-\lambda)\sigma_0$, and with no larger nonnegative distortion than the original remainder part. Its minimum possible distortion over dictionaries of size $m$ is the left side of \eqref{eq:atom}. For the upper, select the full allowable atom mass $b\lambda$, allocate one center to $x_*$, and use an optimal $(m-1)$-point dictionary on a mass-$r$ submeasure of the remainder. The unnormalized factor $r$ is retained throughout.
\end{proof}

The atom result applies to any remainder, including a mixture of singular strata. It does not assert that arbitrary changing-rank geometries satisfy \eqref{eq:rectangle}. In the raw quantum construction below, rank changes can occur while the task law still satisfies these rectangle estimates; this is proved directly rather than inferred from ambient matrix dimension.
