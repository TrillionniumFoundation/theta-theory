# R76 author proof audit: conditional excursions

This is an author-team mathematical challenge record, not a referee report, a human expert assessment, or a proof-assistant certificate. It concerns the four new statements in `46-rare-branch-approximation.tex` and `47-physical-approximation-lower.tex`; the inherited R75 results are used at their stated hypotheses. The integrating author process read and challenged the complete proofs, including the lower-bound constants, the counted initializer, the stopped-law limit and the seven-seed rational upper. This is an author-team cross-check, not independent referee approval.

## Critical issues challenged and resolved

1. **Cost under the correct process.** All bounds use the target's expectation of the physical call count. No approximate-process or hybrid expected-duration bound is inserted. The finite-horizon coupling's error is bounded by a target exceptional event.
2. **No uncounted random initialization.** The mixed construction explicitly lists its initial seed mixture. The pure construction adds a counted label and executes the first actual command while entering the core. The initial terminal decoder is included.
3. **Quantifier over policies.** The finite transition tables are constructed once from the instrument and L. They are independent of the policy and B. Common policies may use all visible reports, randomization, and adaptive terminal POVMs. Policy storage is not supplied free by the simulator.
4. **Conditional versus average mixing.** The return-polytope hypothesis is explicit and is checked geometrically in the application. It is not inferred from the exactly depolarizing unobserved channel. The undamped continuation retains an irrational phase when that phase is chosen.
5. **Invariant real plane.** A triangle of real qubit states does not contain arbitrary complex qubit states. The general theorem permits an invariant convex state set. The application chooses the real symmetric density matrices, invariant because the unitary is real, the reset state is real, and depolarization preserves them.
6. **Overflow and coincident orbit states.** The abstract theorem can merge identical physical states and continues exactly whenever the next state remains in its dictionary. The explicit qubit table keeps depth/multi-index labels and may truncate at depth L even if a periodic phase would permit exact continuation. Its separate common-prefix argument remains valid and its count is an upper bound. Neither algorithm is claimed minimal.
7. **One count for all commands.** The same three seed states and all allowed multi-indices form one dictionary for the entire supplied finite alphabet. Separate per-command realizations are not confused with a common adaptive simulator. The continuum of arbitrary angles is not covered by the polylogarithmic count.
8. **Zero branches and duplicate states.** Zero-trace outputs have zero transition mass and require no normalized-state choice. Duplicate images may be merged in the abstract theorem. The word-count upper remains valid either way.
9. **Countable refinement is only proof scaffolding.** Its exact state identities establish the common positive contribution before overflow. The actual deliverable is the finite table. No infinite auxiliary label or hidden age is omitted from the stated finite count.
10. **Nontermination.** Finite stopped blocks are retained and unfinished paths are merged into one symbol. Their masses decrease to the nontermination masses; the full classical–quantum trace distance passes to the limit. The approximate process is not asserted almost surely terminating or to retain the target expected duration.
11. **Sharpness separated correctly.** The run-probability constant is optimal under its probabilistic assumptions. This does not show an optimal memory count, an optimal simulator error, or a matching unrestricted approximation lower bound.
12. **Classical ingredients separated from the new construction.** Polyhedral positive realization, coupling, stars-and-bars counting, and run-waiting potentials are not assigned novelty. The contribution is their explicit combination with return geometry and semigroup growth for a complete controlled stopped-law approximation, including the algebraic finite-angle qubit implementation.
13. **Restricted lower-bound class.** The lower permits arbitrary causal stochastic transitions, without assuming they intertwine the quantum branches on individual labels. It does require every label to carry one fixed physical density matrix and to use its Born probabilities for every terminal effect. Transitions cannot depend on a future terminal-measurement request. An unrestricted classical decoder need not satisfy this condition, so the theorem is not a lower for all hidden-state models.
14. **No unjustified conditioning on a rare prefix.** The early-abort policy starts with probability zeta=min(1,7B/8). Its target expected calls are at most zeta*8/7, uniformly in the requested prefix length. Two joint stopped-record events give the prefix mass error and the bad terminal-outcome mass, each at most delta. Only then is a conditional fidelity conclusion derived by division by the positive mass p_n-delta.
15. **Packing constants and degeneracy.** Under delta <= zeta/[8^J(1+J^2/c^2)], one label must lie within Bloch distance 2c/J of each target. Distinct targets have separation at least 4c/(n-m), which is strictly greater than 4c/J because 1 <= n-m < J. Hence even the closed balls cannot share a label. This strict inequality justifies the displayed constant without an additional safety factor.
16. **Pure-start lower and upper are distinguished.** Zero-call terminal tests force the pure initializer's physical state to lie within trace distance delta of I/2. Its fidelity is then too small to be any of the J high-fidelity labels. This proves the lower J+1. The construction gives K+L as an upper. No general approximate identity between pure and mixed minima is inferred.
17. **Leading constant and its quantifiers.** The asymptotic fixes both a badly approximable phase and B>0. The lower uses J=log_8(1/delta)-2 log_8 log_8(1/delta)+O(1); the upper uses L=log_8(1/delta)+O(1) plus a fixed orbit polygon. It is not a uniform two-parameter claim as B varies, nor a statement for every irrational phase.
18. **Orbit polygon and algebraic phase remain separate.** The upper polygon exists for every irrational phase and gives the exact count K+L-1 for the union of seed orbit intervals, plus one pure initializer. The golden phase has a fully proved c=1/3 and K=5. The original phase (3+4i)/5 has a seven-seed rational polygon and rational transition tables, but no badly-approximable certificate is claimed for it.

## Finite diagnostics actually executed

The active script is `diagnostics_r76_conditional.py`. Its original three-statement development version is preserved as `diagnostics_r76_excursions.py`, with the original reports `diagnostics-normal.json` and `diagnostics-optimized.json`; that preserved copy has 4,524 checks and is not a second active suite. The earlier TeX draft is preserved in `development/46-before-physical-lower.tex`. Only the active script is to be counted in the final verifier.

Ordinary Python and Python `-O` each produced the same final JSON report, with **4,843 exact checks and zero floating checks**, recorded as `diagnostics-final-normal.json` and `diagnostics-final-optimized.json`. The report bytes are identical. The algebra is rational arithmetic and exact quadratic-field arithmetic in Q(sqrt(3)) and Q(sqrt(5)); no numerical positivity tolerance is used.
