## Main boundaries

The reset theorem requires all initial density matrices, reported resetting, a positive conditional mean cost floor, a fixed observation port, and mandatory success stopping. It does not claim a general POMDP normal form. Deterministic routing does not purify arbitrary command rows. The explicit closed cost law is for the displayed family; strict inequalities, rather than its exact formula, survive the noncommuting perturbation.

The original general-M squared-sign decision-risk law, the unrestricted-decoder log-log/log approximation rate gap, nonreset and optional-stopping control, independent timing and A1/A2 objectives remain open at their stated scopes. The full reset frontier is a substantive result about a different precisely defined objective; it does not erase those questions.
