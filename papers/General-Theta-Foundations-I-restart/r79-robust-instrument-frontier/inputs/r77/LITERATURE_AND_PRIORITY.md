## Precise proposed contributions

**Shared preparation.** A common identity-word projection works for every preparation simultaneously under closed convex all-word constraints. The exact pure cost counts distinct mixed projected starts. The invariant-polytope formula accounts for nonextreme continuing profiles without double charging; the polytope may exceed the target span. Horizontal Fourier concatenation gives noncyclic multiplicities and a simultaneous positive decoder attains the complete permutation-module cover at small common contrast. The two growing C2 families separate individual, shared and initializer costs. These are the proposed additions; finite-group representation theory and positive cone geometry are ingredients.

**Tied reset optimality.** Bellman theory supplies a value and zero-residual sets. A least-favorable preparation with positive failure exposure forces any optimal controller to contain rows supported in those sets. This proves an exact transversal formula including reuse of its initial row. The attained residual minimization is positive exactly below that label threshold. The rational construction realizes every finite proper cover with explicit unrestricted optimum; the PSD example shows why exposure is needed. The variational certificate is not the full memory-constrained value frontier.

**Unrestricted approximate memory.** The robust factorization lemma is a quantitative adaptation of classical antichain counting. The operational theorem must control joint herald masses before conditional errors and deny the simulator knowledge of future test code. It then allows arbitrary decoder tables. Elementary integer numerators for `(3+4i)/5`, and algebraic norms for other nonroot algebraic unit phases, yield explicit finite thresholds and a log-log memory lower. The known logarithmic upper is retained. A matching unrestricted rate, an unrestricted extra pure initializer and the original general-M squared-sign risk law are not claimed.
