## High-risk interfaces audited explicitly

1. The main geometric partition need not coincide with the finite stability modes. The raw-reference proposition applies to any refinement, including a countable one. Analysis modes are not silently given to an encoder.
2. State-independent suspension is a real hypothesis. The energy mixture is not valid with arbitrary state-dependent waiting without further weighted-kernel work.
3. Occupation comparison can fail for disappearing strata. It is derived for the common-reference class, not asserted for every nonstationary kernel.
4. The singular full-report theorem and finite-read theorem are different experiments. Only the latter uses N_raw=B+T+2 and its explicit atomic acquired law.
5. Cylinder centers are not Cantor points. Their gaps use 1/6 instead of 1/3; this changes deletion gain 15 to 30 and the return prepend from four to six bits in the finite-read experiment. The two gain calculations are not interchanged.
6. The stored-prefix decoder is unbiased because bit origins depend on mode/operation history, not bit values. An arbitrary biased numeric decoder does not enjoy calibration cross-term cancellation.
7. Unbiased rational stochastic rounding has expected, not automatically worst-case bounded, random-bit cost. Truncation requires a charged tail defect.
8. The acquisition common-oracle proof keeps the actual posterior law. It does not declare a latent-state measure to be the acquired geometry.
