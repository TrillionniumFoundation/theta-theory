# Theorem-level literature comparison

The source retains the previous bibliography and adds Charalambous--Stavrou--Ahmed, *Nonanticipative Rate Distortion Function and Relations to Filtering Theory*, arXiv:1210.1266v3, related published DOI 10.1109/TAC.2013.2293403. Official abstract/version metadata were checked; the comparison is limited to its realizability-constrained reconstruction and information objective, not an unsupported claim of priority over all of its theorems.

| Literature / classical ingredient | Used or compared here | Distinction asserted by this manuscript |
|---|---|---|
| Shalizi--Crutchfield; Littman--Sutton--Singh | Future-test predictive state and minimality | Legal continuation, pointwise density-instrument versions and resource interface remain explicit |
| Graf--Luschgy; quantized nonlinear filtering (Pages--Pham, Sellami, Feuer--Goodwin) | Projection, quantization and filter approximation | Actual-law two-sided persistent-state risk plus executable nonreset transport; not a priority claim for quantization |
| Diaconis--Freedman, SIAM Review 41 (1999), DOI 10.1137/S0036144598338446 | Average contraction of random maps | Raw squared-gain forward weight, occupation and destination-dependent forcing; logarithmic average contraction alone is insufficient |
| Costa--Fragoso--Marques; Ogura--Martin | Positive moment/spectral stability | Standard matrix criterion is not claimed new; the acquisition/finite-machine comparison is the new theorem under stated conditions |
| Zhu, arXiv:1802.03723 (2018) | Moran quantization and cell-error theory | Elementary cylinder bounds are verified here; new application is nonreset acquired recurrence, not a replacement of optimal-cell asymptotics |
| Nonanticipative rate distortion | Causal reconstruction with information constraints | A directed-information/communication rate is not automatically cardinality of all retained computation state |
| Saldi--Yuksel--Linder; Ferns--Panangaden--Precup | Belief-model policies and behavioral metrics | Their controller optimization differs from the present fixed paid protocol; no unrestricted control result inferred |
| Blackwell; Le Cam; Torgersen; Fritz | Common-loss comparison, kernels and sufficiency | Explicit causal microsteps, state, clock and implementation ledger; TV data processing itself is standard |
| Cover--Thomas; automata-style continuation distinctions | Fano/entropy and state distinguishability | Fused retained information cuts, not a lower bound on every named program register |
