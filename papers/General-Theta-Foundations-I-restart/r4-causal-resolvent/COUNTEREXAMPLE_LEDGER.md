# Counterexamples and non-implications

1. **Formal support is not actual mass.** A point mass and a uniform law can share the same allowed interval but have different finite-state risks. Every Q/Phi in the new theorem uses actual prepared weights.
2. **Cross-stratum error is not reset error.** Setting every off-diagonal old-error coefficient to zero would erase the 0->1 deletion error in the new raw model. Both the proof and negative matrix controls retain this edge.
3. **Marginal contraction is insufficient.** A gain may correlate with destination and previous error. The pointwise marked-factor drift, not an unconditional average gain, is used.
4. **Occupation is substantive.** A component with positive seed/active forcing and zero terminal mass violates O. The common-reference class excludes that situation; the old reset-preserved theorem supplies a different class, not a universal fix.
5. **State-dependent waiting is not independent suspension.** Weighting the error selectively changes its distribution. The convex energy mixture requires the stated deterministic schedule; a stopped horizon can select large error.
6. **Unbounded overlap breaks assignment accounting.** Without finite Lambda one decoder center may be charged infinitely many times. No arbitrary overlapping countable-stratum lower is asserted.
7. **Atomic posterior laws do not obey all-scale non-atomic small-ball bounds.** The finite-read model uses actual acquisition variance plus its explicit atomic law. Hidden physical geometry is only a lower witness under the common oracle.
8. **Cantor endpoints and cylinder centers have different gaps.** The latter require deletion gain 30 and a six-bit return in the finite-read proof. Reusing gain 15 there would be unjustified.
9. **An infinite initialization report is not a finite packet.** The full-report and finite-read models are distinct. No TV equivalence is deduced by merely truncating the initial report.
10. **Biased rounding loses the exact calibration cancellation.** Unbiased conditional means (or unbiased independent rounding) are required for the exact delta^2 upper in the finite-read minimax result.
11. **A geometric representation index is not total complexity.** Mode, word length, simulator/controller/clock, temporary bits and program constants are explicitly distinguished.
12. **A forward simulator gives no automatic lower.** Its TV budget epsilon may be a loose certificate or zero for a task-insensitive defect. No universal epsilon or numeric floor follows.
13. **TV proximity does not imply LDP equivalence.** The retained exponentially rare-event counterexample remains in the manuscript.
14. **Positive-matrix necessity is not optimal-encoder necessity.** The spectral-radius equivalence concerns the selected comparison matrix only.
15. **Finite regression is not a continuum proof.** Adversarial identities catch implementation regressions, not missing measurable or infinite-parameter arguments.
