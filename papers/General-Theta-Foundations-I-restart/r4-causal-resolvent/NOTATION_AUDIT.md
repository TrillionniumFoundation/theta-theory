# Notation audit

- Z_t: exact raw Markov factor; s(Z_t): its predictive coordinate; S or a label denotes retained algorithm state. Factor and machine are not identified.
- D_j: factor component; s(D_j): its predictive image; S_T,j: terminal score image. Overlap is in score neighborhoods, not a claim that the factor partition overlaps.
- v: bounded forward gain Lyapunov weight. The retained reset bridge uses upsilon_t,j for recharge densities, separating it from refresh probability v_t there.
- T: initialized recurrence opportunities. N_raw: full paid acquisition count, including initialization and terminal task. In the finite-read realization N_raw=B+T+2.
- B_T: actual Bayes risk in the general theorem. B without subscript in the finite-read section: number of paid initial bit reads. A_B,T: unresolved score variance. Their roles are stated at introduction.
- H_t: full-history known prefix depth in the finite-read section, not the stable error constant H_*.
- r_j(k): certified geometric resolution; ell_n: exact cylinder scale; rho_n: its geometric contraction ratios. k_j counts allocated representatives, not phase-state bits.
- Lambda: finite overlap multiplicity; lambda: raw invariant reference measure. Actual laws remain mu_t and nu_B,T.
- delta: unobserved calibration-sign magnitude in the recurrent minimax class; xi_j: local metric allowance; zeta_T: score readout allowance; epsilon: complete common-task causal defect. No automatic equality of these error models is used.
- pi: invariant raw mode distribution in the symbolic realizations; inherited pi_t(h_t) is a posterior belief in the experiment section. Both are explicitly local.
- q_M(nu): quadratic quantization of the actual posterior law when stated; the ideal-score law appears only as a separately named lower witness in the acquisition proposition.
- L in the old soft-state theorem is a challenge alphabet, not the packet count in the separate single-probe model. Those sections retain their local definitions.

All labels and bibliography keys are mechanically unique; every included TeX source is reachable exactly once from main.tex. A label count is not a proof audit.
