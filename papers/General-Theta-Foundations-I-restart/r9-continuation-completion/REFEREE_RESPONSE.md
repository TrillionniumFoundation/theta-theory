## Main mathematical response

The exact one-cut functional-code problem is now displayed in `thm:cutduality`. It is explicitly credited as an ordinary one-shot functional-source-coding reduction. The additional theorem compares it from both sides with actual finite-measure Hilbert quantization under lower/upper common-channel domination, and proves a conditional version when global product domination vanishes. `lem:minorization` derives such certificates from common densities, finite packets and joint visible-block/hidden-state regeneration, with controller variables and actual success mass included.

The recursive problem is then addressed separately. `thm:chart` proves, for an explicitly defined class of sequentially acquired coordinates with arbitrary correlation, that the actual continuation width, the supremum of all valid cut lower bounds, and optimal online excess are all of order M^(-2/d). The upper is not an assumed one-cut optimizer: a global compander is applied to each actual incoming report, the complete prefix tuple remains within M at every cut, and the current suffix packet is erased after a final scalar quantization. Unbounded acquired tails are controlled by marginal moments. This is quantitative completeness on a stated class, not a universal characterization of all causal encoders.

`thm:noisy` verifies those conditions from a prepared Gaussian regression experiment with positive prefix and suffix noise and a continuous future query. An invertible nonorthogonal observation matrix permits correlated acquired reports. The one bounded Bernoulli prediction task has online excess of order M^(-2/d) and terminal checkpoint excess of order M^(-2), relative to exactly the same Bayes baseline. The earlier response geometry is therefore essential: terminal posterior density cannot supply the online exponent. Raw Gaussian square completion, a genuine acquired compact-event mass, normal-density minorization, a positive response derivative and a query frame provide every hypothesis. This is not an exact delayed bit query.

## Section 18: exceptional-significance objectives

We have pursued items 18.1, 18.3 and 18.6 mathematically: class-level quantitative completeness with an explicit recursive construction; a sharp noisy continuation separation; and reusable raw-kernel minorization with conditional localization. The new chart theorem is broader than one Gaussian model because its prefix law may be correlated and non-Gaussian, with any controlled marginal tails and the stated acquired response chart. Nevertheless its sequential coordinate interface and positive-mass co-Lipschitz chart are substantive hypotheses, and its constants are structural, not universal.

We have not proved the universal duality requested as one possible objective in 18.2, the arbitrary value-dependent nonproduct acquisition theorem in 18.4, or the full optimal computational region in 18.5. The manuscript and scope audit identify these remaining proof tasks without changing the General Theta mother problem, treating difficulty as a no-go argument, or describing repository work as mathematical novelty. Further independent scrutiny of correctness, priority, generality and significance is invited at the new fixed referee-ready head.
