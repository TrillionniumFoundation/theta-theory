# Scope audit

## F1

A general sufficient-class theorem now gives an all-strategy lower at one actual witness and a common all-model causal upper, with explicit effort ratios, full-report tests, actual acquired law and live/export state accounting. Global acquired mass and covering turn this into matching curves. The continuous-response compact subclass has a constructed Borel predictive quotient; raw positive instruments give report and mass-weighted update continuity. This is not restricted to one HMM, covariance geometry or quantum parameterization.

## F2

One affine theorem combines relative calibration and occupation-weighted absolute defects. Numerical approximation uses the same stopped squared-task metric and restores the actual budget. Simulator state factors multiply, complete scored-law defects add, and time remains separately certified. A same-task construction proves matching attained calibration/erasure contributions; finite command calibration now includes sample cost, confidence failure, a safe fallback and a counted guard. An arbitrary tolerated error does not automatically create an unavoidable lower bound.

## F3

New native results prove order on changing-rank measurable task ranges, an explicit coupled resource profile for finitely many different acquired dimensions/reliability exponents, and correct acquired-atom thresholds. The complete absolute-calibration metastable theory is retained, with actual initial bad-mode mass. General anisotropy is addressed by verified sublevel mass/cover bounds; an arbitrary anisotropic or infinitely stratified rate is not asserted without such estimates.

## F4

Positive finite classical channels and noncommuting finite-dimensional quantum instruments verify the same central hypotheses directly, including relative calibration, gate cost, finite state roles, acquired mass and singular posterior/task strata. They both concern post-command tasks. The unchanged companion's directional Gaussian theorem supplies a nonintervening continuous-target example; it remains inherited rather than being counted as a new R59 theorem.

## Remaining mathematical obligations

The new proofs are complete arguments for the explicitly stated class and await an external referee. They do not settle all-causal necessary balance, globally minimal simulator memory, optimal total bit/time complexity, arbitrary unknown-kernel calibration, common quotient realization for every Borel model family, or journal-level priority/significance. The archive has not been exhaustively re-proved in this revision. These are stated to delimit mathematical claims, not to stop work or substitute a narrower project.
