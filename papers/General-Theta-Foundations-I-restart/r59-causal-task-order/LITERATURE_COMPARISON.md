Primary publisher/arXiv metadata and relevant abstracts were checked during this revision. Some direct DOI endpoints did not load; available publisher/author-arXiv records and the previously pinned citation data were used without claiming a newly retrieved full text. The current comparison is targeted, not an exhaustive priority search of every 2026 paper. Every borrowed identity or method is either cited as standard or rederived. Recent citations appearing only in the byte-preserved companion are historical citations, not all independently reverified as part of the new native bibliography.
