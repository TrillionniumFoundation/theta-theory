# Mathematical scope and remaining proof objectives

## Statements supplied with proofs

General kernel/test realization, physical-law block transfer and common-task morphisms remain the foundations spine. R8 adds exact one-cut functional distortion and two-sided common-channel comparison, conditional localization and raw certificate production, class-level recursive continuation-width equivalence, and a sharp positive-noise continuous-query regression separation with a correlated-prefix extension. The matched progressive acquisition/calibration/attained-defect law and the full singular/nonuniform realization are retained.

## F1--F4

F1: actual acquired mass, global executable cover, predictive stability, report continuity and full state accounting are explicit. The block theorem yields checkpoint/online matching under its actual terminal witness. The sequential-chart theorem instead classifies online risk by earlier continuation width in its declared class and exhibits a strict noisy checkpoint separation. It does not assert that every cut-width family yields a block relation.

F2: the common N/M/delta/attained-epsilon profile remains proved for the progressive family. Root numerical errors and complete-law defects are composed in the same task. The noisy corollary gives an upper under declared perturbations, not new unavoidable delta/epsilon floors.

F3: arbitrary nonhomogeneous smooth/singular product scales, the full nonreset Cantor dynamics with expanding steps, and block-only Gaussian stability remain proved. The new correlated noisy prefix is a fixed finite acquisition class, not an unlimited nonuniform-dynamics theorem.

F4: sparse hidden-state filtering and nonreset singular dynamics independently verify the block theorem. Progressive sensing verifies a different exact-refinement mechanism. Noisy regression independently verifies the new chart theorem. All start at raw kernels.

## Genuine objectives not yet proved

1. A necessary-and-sufficient intrinsic classification of every causal encoder, or a universal derivation of a relation-preserving cover and block drift from all continuation widths.
2. A matching acquisition theorem for arbitrary correlated/nonproduct sources whose optimal next measurement depends on observed values, or for unknown-kernel learning with paid calibration.
3. A full optimal joint region for program length, scratch, bit operations, minimal clock, simulator state and controller state. The existing detailed accounts are feasible uppers except where an explicit lower is supplied.
4. Uniform chart constants through vanishing acquisition mass, response-rank collapse, arbitrary unbounded stopping or infinite-horizon complete transcripts. The proved singular models have their own explicit estimates; they do not imply these general extensions.
5. Unbounded-operator closure or exponential/LDP equivalence from finite-dimensional or finite-TV results.

These are remaining mathematical targets, not reasons to change the topic or delete existing results. No assertion is made that the new proofs have been independently accepted or that literature comparison certifies priority. Exhaustive re-reading of every historical v1--v96 source is not claimed; see HISTORY_COVERAGE.md.
