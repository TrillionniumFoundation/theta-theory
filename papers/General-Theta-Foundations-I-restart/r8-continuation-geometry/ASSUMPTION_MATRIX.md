Constants in the chart theorem depend on acquired mass p, domination alpha, co-Lipschitz modulus l and moment/global-modulus constants. They are not uniform through rank collapse or vanishing mass. Gaussian constants also depend on positive variances, G conditioning and the directional frame floor. F3 is provided by the retained full singular/nonuniform proofs, not by pretending these Gaussian constants are uniform at degeneracy.
