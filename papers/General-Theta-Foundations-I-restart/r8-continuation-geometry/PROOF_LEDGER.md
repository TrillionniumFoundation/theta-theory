## Logical separation

The exact one-cut identity alone has no recursive upper implication. Theorem thm:chart supplies a separate constructive implication using paid sequential coordinates. The noisy theorem invokes this general result after verifying all assumptions from raw kernels; it is not used to prove the general theorem. The R7/R6 models do not prove the foundations theorem backward. Conditions outside the declared classes are not silently inferred.
