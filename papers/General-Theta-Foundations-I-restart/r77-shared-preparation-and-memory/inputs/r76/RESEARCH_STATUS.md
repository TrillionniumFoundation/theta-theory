## Newly proved structure

These extend the existing mathematics rather than replacing the original scientific problem with one local example.

## Remaining research obligations

1. **Original general-M decision-risk frontier.** The squared-sign minimax quantity V_{p,alpha,M}-V_{p,alpha} still has no general exact formula or matching asymptotic lower. Exact response counts and stopped-law upper bounds do not close it.
2. **General nonrigid control.** Necessary and sufficient optimal-memory classification for arbitrary tied boundary problems with stopping and multiple commands, and broad boundary density beyond the proved classes, remain open. A simulator does not make its private state readable by the original observer.
3. **General high-contrast nonabelian realization.** Orbit convexity is characterized, and the growing S_n family now has a closed law at every feasible mean and signed contrast, with exact nontrivial boundary factors. The convex-specification theorem gives a finite attained optimum for a supplied group and target. Closed high-contrast formulas for arbitrary other finite groups, efficient global synthesis, and factorization of every individual off-boundary coefficient pair remain unresolved.
4. **Complete-instrument memory away from the solved sectors.** The lower stochastic spectral region and polygon upper need not be equal. Further exact damping transitions and equality classifications require proof.
5. **Sharp approximation resources outside the solved classes.** The finite-group uniform-error optimum is now reduced exactly to finite compact optimization, with a closed prime-group case and robust multiplicity certificates. The complete qubit instrument has a finite-alphabet polylogarithmic upper and a matching leading logarithmic constant for physical dictionaries at a fixed badly approximable phase and fixed B>0. The corresponding lower for unrestricted classical terminal decoders, matching continuum-command rates, joint B/delta asymptotics and general policy-dependent tradeoffs remain open. The new leading constant is not exported to those different problems.
6. **Intermediate timing.** The phase-type release/timing frontier remains separate from every qubit or group response law.
7. **Arithmetic and computation.** The golden phase has a fully proved badly-approximable constant; the original algebraic rotation has a rational seven-seed approximation construction. Sharp Diophantine constants and a certified exponent for that original algebraic rotation, joint favourable-scale questions, program-table bits, precision, workspace, exact sampling and uniform efficiency are not inferred from those results or from finite searches.
8. **Unknown models and the wider programme.** Recovering a predictive representation from data, independent A1/A2/B4/C2 construction goals and other Theta pipeline objectives retain their own assumptions and proof obligations.
