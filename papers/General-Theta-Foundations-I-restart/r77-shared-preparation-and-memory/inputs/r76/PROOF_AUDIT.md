Two-sided inverse averaging makes the sandwiched maps a group action, and closed convexity preserves the specification. | The original stochastic matrices need not obey group relations. | `thm:r76-robust-multiplicity` | Stacked Fourier perturbations have operator norm at most sqrt(2) times TV error; the binary scalar bound is delta. | `lem:r76-excursion-budget` | Every adapted continuation process with conditional continuation probability at most r<1 satisfies the sharp probability bound min(1,(1-r)B/(r^(-L)-1)) for completing L continuations before a policy with target expected calls at most B. | The nonnegative run-length potential is stopped at the earlier of the budget stop, the hitting time and a finite horizon. | `thm:r76-return-approximation` | A known finite-dimensional instrument on an invariant convex state set, a finite return polytope and a uniform continuation bound produce one finite physical dictionary for all visible adaptive command/stopping/terminal-POVM policies.
