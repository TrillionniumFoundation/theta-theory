# R76 targeted literature and priority record: rare-excursion approximation and physical dictionaries

## Actual primary-source checks

- **Shuo-Yen Robert Li (1980), _A Martingale Approach to the Study of Occurrence of Sequence Patterns in Repeated Experiments_, Annals of Probability 8(6), 1171–1176, DOI 10.1214/aop/1176994578.** The [Project Euclid primary record](https://projecteuclid.org/journals/annals-of-probability/volume-8/issue-6/A-Martingale-Approach-to-the-Study-of-Occurrence-of-Sequence/10.1214/aop/1176994578.full) and indexed title/author/year were retrieved. The direct full-text/PDF endpoint returned its anti-bot page; the DOI redirect was inaccessible through the web reader. This record is not described as a fresh full-text reading. The waiting-time calculation is proved completely in the new manuscript and does not rely on an inaccessible proof.
- **Vladimir Pozdnyakov, Joseph Glaz, Martin Kulldorff and J. Michael Steele (2005), _A Martingale Approach to Scan Statistics_, Annals of the Institute of Statistical Mathematics 57(1), 21–37.** The journal's original PDF was opened at https://www.ism.ac.jp/editsec/aism/pdf/057_1_0021.pdf. Its Section 2.1 credits Li's martingale method and reproduces the expected pattern-waiting formula. This confirms the classical provenance of the ingredient; neither the waiting-time formula nor the martingale method is presented as new here.
- **Alex Monràs and Andreas Winter, _Quantum learning of classical stochastic processes: The Completely-Positive Realization Problem_, [arXiv:1412.3634](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.3634), Journal of Mathematical Physics 57, 015219 (2016), DOI 10.1063/1.4936935.** The original arXiv record was read. Its positive/quasi-realization setting and the relation to quantum instruments supply historical context. The new return-polytope construction is proved directly instead of using that reference to conceal a new realization step.
- **Marco Fanizza, Josep Lumbreras and Andreas Winter (2024), _Quantum Theory in Finite Dimension Cannot Explain Every General Process with Finite Memory_, Communications in Mathematical Physics 405, 50, DOI 10.1007/s00220-023-04913-4.** The [publisher's full article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00220-023-04913-4) was read, particularly Theorem 2, Section 4.2 and the discussion following its noise bounds. Their inverse-square-root lower concerns exact positive realizations of perturbed quantum processes. The discussion distinguishes that problem from approximation. The present bounded-cost approximation theorem retains that distinction; the additional matching lower is expressly restricted to fixed physical dictionaries with Born terminal decoders.

The broad search also encountered empirical finite-Markov approximations in the 2025 arXiv article [_Neural networks leverage nominally quantum and post-quantum representations_](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.07432v1). Its Sections IV.3 and F.2.5 discuss finite-order Markov baselines and belief geometry. The original manuscript [_Learning finitely correlated states_](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.07516v3) was also opened: its reconstruction and error-propagation problem differs from minimizing a fixed physical dictionary for a known instrument under a target expected-call budget. Neither is needed as a theorem dependency, and neither is cited to assert novelty.

The current-source search additionally opened Yibin Wang's original [_Sequential Capacity of Quantum Processes with Finite Memory_](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.02068v2), submitted 1 October 2026 and revised 6 October 2026. Its sequential-capacity results concern families of multitime processes, and its tester allows private quantum workspace. The present theorem fixes one instrument and one representation class, uses visible classical policies without an additional entangled memory, and counts hidden physical dictionary states as the error tends to zero at fixed expected budget. The related use of convex channel dictionaries does not by itself identify these statements. This is a scope comparison from the source, not a claim of independent endorsement or a comprehensive exclusion of overlap.

## What is inherited, standard, or added

The physical instrument, all-angle noise simulator, exact stationary-initialization theorem, irrational-phase obstruction, and first-sector exact resource results are inherited from the authenticated R75 source. The triangle's inradius and barycentric identities are elementary geometry. Stars-and-bars counting of commuting products is standard. The coupling inequality and the use of a nonnegative stopped potential are standard probabilistic tools.

The R76 addition is a general constructive implication: a supplied finite return polytope and a uniform bound on continuation probability produce one finite simulator for all common visible adaptive stopping policies; its state count is controlled by the number of normalized continuation images. The explicit qubit application uses three fixed seed states and commutative rotations to turn this into a polylogarithmic count for a fixed finite angle alphabet, with a logarithmic bound for one angle. All report rows and terminal decoders are supplied. The sharp cost coefficient concerns the exceptional run probability only.

The fourth statement adds a matching leading-order law in the physical-dictionary class. The ingredients are elementary state-space packing, positivity of mixtures, the quadratic conjugate-norm certificate for a golden phase, and finite orbit polygons; none of those ingredients is assigned priority here. The substantive synthesis uses early-abort tests of uniformly bounded expected cost, joint-event rather than unproved conditional error bounds, and a shared orbit-seed polygon whose continuation union has only K+L-1 points. It proves a limiting ratio of one to log base 8 of 1/delta for fixed B>0 and a fixed badly approximable phase. The dictionary matrices are physical and all terminal decoders are Born probabilities; transitions may otherwise be arbitrary causal stochastic tables. It does not establish the corresponding lower for unrestricted decoder tables, a general two-parameter asymptotic, or a Diophantine certificate for the original algebraic phase (3+4i)/5. That original phase nevertheless receives an explicit seven-seed rational upper construction.

This targeted search did not locate an identical theorem with the same complete stopped classical–quantum law, target expected-cost quantifier, finite return geometry and continuation-growth count, or the same restricted leading-constant approximation statement. That observation is not an exhaustive priority clearance. It does not establish that no equivalent formulation exists elsewhere, and no independent expert endorsement is claimed.
