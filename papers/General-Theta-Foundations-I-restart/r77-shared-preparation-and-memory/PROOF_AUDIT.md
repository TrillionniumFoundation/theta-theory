## 1. Shared preparations: exact structure and initialization

The source is `sections/49-shared-preparation.tex`. A single device has common command transitions and a common terminal decoder. The selected preparation can affect its initial counted label or, under the explicitly different mixed convention, its externally prepared initial distribution. It cannot be revealed freely to the terminal decoder. Every command word, including the empty word, is subject to the specification.

| Statement | Proof mechanism | Delicate point checked |
|---|---|---|
| `thm:r77-shared-normal-form` | Apply the inherited identity-word averaging projection once to the common command matrices. Its recurrent classes give a single permutation core, and projecting each initial row gives the required simultaneous preparations. Distinct nonpoint projected rows require distinct original labels outside those classes. Conversely, one transient initializer per distinct nonpoint row reproduces both the empty-word law and the first actual command. | The projection does not depend on the preparation. Closed convex constraints are preserved separately for each preparation; no averaging of specifications is used. Several labels projecting to the same row are charged once; a transient label projecting to a point needs no extra charge. Group actions may have kernels. |
| `thm:r77-shared-polytope` | The label profiles generate a group-invariant polytope in the full product probability simplex. Vertex reduction gives the mixed formula. Vertex labels and transient nonvertex initializers attain it. | A nonvertex target that is already a continuing label consumes one of the nonvertex profile slots; it cannot be charged twice. The attained minimum is an integer minimum over a nonempty family, not a compactness assertion about an unspecified space of polytopes. |

For the common-mean synthesis, the proof explicitly sets `k=|X|`, takes an equivariant injection `j`, and writes `a_i=j(b_i-pi)`. The coordinate sum of every `a_i` is zero. If `F` is the equivariant left inverse on the embedded module, define `T(x,c)=F(e_x)(e,c)`. Then `T1=0` and the uniform row `p` satisfies `pT=0`. With `A=max_i ||a_i||_infinity`, `epsilon=1/(2kA)`, and `B=max_(x,c) |T(x,c)|/pi_c`, the choices

`lambda_i = p + epsilon a_i`,  `D_t = pi + (t/epsilon)T`,  `0<t<=min(1,epsilon/B)`

give nonnegative probability rows and the required common decoder. The constant `B` is positive when the centered module is nonzero: if evaluation at the identity vanished on that invariant module, equivariance would force every evaluation to vanish. The zero-module case is handled separately by a one-label constant response. The different-means counterexample has a four-element positive-diagonal fooling set and ordinary rank three; it correctly excludes an unconditional extension of the small-contrast equality.

## 2. Arbitrary classical decoders: finite quantitative lower bounds

The source is `sections/51-unrestricted-approximation.tex`. These results remove the fixed-density-matrix and Born-decoder restrictions from the lower certificate. They do not claim the stronger logarithmic lower previously proved under those restrictions.

### Robust factorization and causal exposure

In `lem:r77-robust-antichain`, probability rows `lambda_n` and arbitrary test columns `d_m` satisfy diagonal error at most `epsilon` and off-diagonal response at least `b`. Set `S_n={i:lambda_n(i)>=b/(2M)}`. For every ordered pair `n != m`, some summand of `lambda_n d_m` is at least `b/M`. Both factors lie in `[0,1]`, so the coordinate belongs to `S_n`, while the diagonal bound forces its `lambda_m` coordinate below `b/(2M)` whenever `epsilon<b^2/(2M^2)`. Thus the sets form an antichain, not merely a collection of different supports. The random-permutation proof of the central-binomial bound is included. The extremal equal-size-subset construction establishes sharpness of the abstract cardinality conclusion; it is not a chronological qubit simulator.

For `thm:r77-operational-antichain`, the target herald probabilities obey `p_n>=p_*>0`; the target test rejects its own prepared state with probability zero and the other prepared states with probability at least `s`. The terminal test is requested only after the herald. A simulator cannot inspect a future test identifier, the policy's future code, or a free uncounted copy of the preparation history. This causal quantifier is essential to obtain one conditional hidden-label row `lambda_n` shared by all later tests.

Full stopped-record TV gives two joint-event inequalities before conditioning:

`|q_n-p_n|<=delta`,  `|q_n lambda_n d_m-p_n f_nm|<=delta`.

For `delta<=p_* s^2/(32M^2)`, the herald mass is positive and

`lambda_n d_n <= 2delta/p_* <= s^2/(16M^2)`,  `lambda_n d_m >= s/2` for `m!=n`.

The diagonal bound is strictly smaller than `(s/2)^2/(2M^2)=s^2/(8M^2)`, so equality at the displayed operational error threshold causes no strictness problem. Therefore `J<=binom(M,floor(M/2))`. Writing `a(J)=min{m:binom(m,floor(m/2))>=J}`, the sufficient finite test condition `delta<=p_*s^2/(32J^2)` gives `M>=a(J)`. Both freely mixed and counted pure starts are covered. No assumption that rare-event conditioning preserves unconditional TV has been inserted.

### Exact phase and budget calculations

`thm:r77-unrestricted-qubit` uses the actual undamped instrument and its **Bloch rotation** `z=exp(i theta)`, not the square of a differently named unitary phase. Begin with probability `zeta=min(1,7B/8)`, then abort at the first mismatch from the prefix `1 0^n`. The target herald probability is `zeta 8^(-(n+1))` and the target expected call cost is `zeta sum_(j=0)^n 8^(-j)<=B`. After the herald the state is the pure Bloch orbit point `rho_n`; terminal rejection by `I-rho_m` has probability `(1-Re z^(n-m))/2`. The terminal test is free under the inherited cost convention. No simulator expected-cost bound is inferred from a target-cost bound.

For `J` preparations, put `s_J=min_(1<=q<J)(1-Re z^q)/2`. The general finite certificate is

`delta <= zeta 8^(-J) s_J^2/(32J^2)  =>  N_cl >= a(J)`.

For the original rotation `z=(3+4i)/5`, `z^q=(A_q+iB_q)/5^q` with integer numerators. The phase is not a root of unity, since a root of unity would make `z+z^(-1)=6/5` an algebraic integer. Consequently `5^q-A_q` is a positive integer and `s_J>=1/(2*5^(J-1))`. Substitution gives exactly

`delta <= 25 zeta/(128*200^J*J^2)  =>  N_cl >= a(J)`.

This supplies a quantitative lower for the original algebraic rotation without assuming that its angular frequency is badly approximable. Under the separate hypothesis `||q omega||>=c/q`, the elementary inequality `sin(pi x)>=2x` for `0<=x<=1/2` gives `s_J>=4c^2/J^2` and the threshold `delta<=zeta c^4/(2*8^J*J^6)`. These are different arithmetic hypotheses and are not interchanged.

In `cor:r77-algebraic-unrestricted`, let the algebraic unit phase have degree `D`, and choose an integer `A` making `Az` integral. The nonzero algebraic integer `A^q(z^q-1)` has nonzero integer norm. If

`H >= max(2, A^D product_(j=2)^D max(1,|z_j|))`,

bounding the other conjugates gives `|1-z^q|>=2^(1-D)H^(-q)`, hence `s_J>=4^(-D)H^(-2(J-1))`. The stated sufficient error is

`delta <= zeta H^4/[32*16^D*(8H^4)^J*J^2]`.

Exponential-in-`J` thresholds and `a(J)>=log_2 J` imply the stated lower leading coefficient one relative to `log_2 log_8(1/delta)`, at fixed `B>0`. The inherited orbit-seed construction still gives an `O(log(1/delta))` upper. The gap between these orders is open for unrestricted decoder tables. The proof does not add an unproved pure-start surcharge, prove a report-only approximate lower, give polynomial Diophantine separation for the original angle, or classify continuum command alphabets.

## 3. Tied optimal actions in reset experiments

The source is `sections/52-optimal-reset-memory.tex`. It uses the inherited reset instrument, positive call-cost floor, fixed observation port, success-implies-immediate-commitment rule, and finite Bellman/dual saddle theorem. Every failure effect is positive definite, and there is no closed failure set. Only cost/effect operators commute in the universality construction; the reset CP maps are not asserted to commute.

| Statement | Proof mechanism | Delicate point checked |

At a dual maximizing state, every first failure report has positive probability. Robust equality forces a continuing row supported in `A_i^*` for every report. Any one action in each such row hits all optimal sets served by that row.
