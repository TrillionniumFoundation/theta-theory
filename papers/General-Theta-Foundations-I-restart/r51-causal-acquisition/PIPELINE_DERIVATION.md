# Pipeline derivation — R51

## From the mother experiment to the theorem

Start with a prepared causal kernel and a protected terminal experiment. Fix legal actions, report access, fresh randomness, stopping, task readout and every resource boundary before evaluating a program. Construct the predictive quotient from executable future tests; a current task response is only a readout of that object unless full separation is proved.

For an actual history strategy, record action counts x_a and terminal submeasures μ_a. Conditional task regeneration gives μ_a≤x_aν_a. This is the precise link from the raw experiment to the geometry. It uses acquired mass and retains failures. It is not a guessed distribution on a reachable manifold.

The inverse is the principal constructive step: at a single live state choose action a with probability x_a/Σx, accept with probability h_a/x_a, and route to the common nearest codepoint. Per-call stop mass is 1/Σx. It realizes the complete occupation profile, including expected action counts, without keeping history or a clock. Thus optimization over history strategies and finite machines reduces to the same constrained acquired geometry with exactly one live-label difference.

Dualizing only the terminal mass and scalar cost yields one supporting price pair for all actions. Freezing conditional terminal components reduces an optimal mixture to two actions by an elementary extreme-point argument. Center choice stays outside this dualization. Neither step grants a separate codebook to each action.

## Quantitative extensions

Conditional envelopes make the inverse robust without pretending rejections are independent. Actual hazard and distortion contributions are summed along the executed trajectory. Global covers and small balls then produce matching regular rates. Atomic–regular laws require a different calculation: continuous terminal mass cannot be smaller than r=[1-N(1-b)]_+. Its normalized density cap is Nb/r, yielding r^(1+2/s), not a mass-free ambient dimension exponent.

Known regenerative perturbations permit an exact repair of one-call stopping mass by redirecting to an existing terminal label. That gives linear raw-error dependence at identical mean/state budgets. General stopped experiments do not have this constant-hazard inverse and retain the R49 square-root result.

## Beyond regeneration

For an informative Gaussian hidden chain, write the actual pre-report occupation first. With one live label its only decision variable is a Borel report-dependent stopping probability. Monotone likelihood ratio orders these rules at fixed negative-state stopping mass. The occupation derivatives show the same threshold simultaneously improves risk and cost, proving all-program optimality without a posterior oracle. The checkpoint construction is then evaluated at the same stopped experiment, while its unrestricted optimum remains distinct.

## Interfaces and realizations

The classical polynomial and quantum positive instruments verify the same conditional-envelope theorem independently. Their own acquired supports supply covering and mass. The generalized predictive quotient, complete-task simulators and computation remain explicit interfaces with proofs and hypotheses. Historical Bayesian decomposition and global occupation criteria continue to explain nonregenerative compatibility; they are not overwritten by the new exact sub-class.
