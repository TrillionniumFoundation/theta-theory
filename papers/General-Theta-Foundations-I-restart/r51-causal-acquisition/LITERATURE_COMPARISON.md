## Native contribution boundary

The claimed addition is the **conjunction**: action-indexed actual occupation, a common terminal codebook, exact one-live-state reconstruction of every feasible profile, full autonomous label pricing, same-budget stop-mass repair, and the stated singular and informative-filter consequences. Its priority and importance require independent assessment. The Gaussian theorem combines known likelihood-ratio ordering with explicitly derived raw occupation derivatives to obtain a new all-program finite-memory consequence; the ingredients are not relabelled as inventions.

## Bibliographic correction

The new bibliography uses the correct Varshney article **“Bayes risk error is a Bregman divergence”**, single author K.R. Varshney, for DOI 10.1109/TSP.2011.2159500. Some retained historical entries paired this DOI with a different title/coauthor. Those reviewed historical files are not silently overwritten; the correction is recorded here and applied in R51.

These comparisons do not settle top-four significance or claim exhaustive latest-publication coverage. Operational build facts remain outside the mathematical bibliography.
