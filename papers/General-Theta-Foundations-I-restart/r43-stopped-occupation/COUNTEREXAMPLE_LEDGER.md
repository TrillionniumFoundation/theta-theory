| Witness | Exact role | Not claimed |
|---|---|---|
| Identity two-label frozen chain | multiple invariant laws; wrong stationary initialization can be unreachable | a full-density realization |
| Periodic two-cycle | geometric terminal law differs from invariant and deterministic-call laws | observer mixing follows from raw overlap |
| Two singleton incoming rows, one global label | local zero distortion still produces global pooling loss 1/4 | local equalities characterize global recursion |
| First-positive-report stopping | selection destroys unconditional acquired row mixture | arbitrary stopping lower follows from geometric law |
| Gaussian a=10^-4 sensor | Hilbert same-report contraction but scalar curvature >1 | all stable experiments have Euclidean curvature <1 |
| Positive diagonal informative branch plus reset, r=1/8 | curvature <=1/2 but no strict same-report contraction in any Euclidean-equivalent metric on [0,1] | atomic row law satisfies main density hypotheses |
| Independent erasure bit protected by audit | exact causal deficiency a/2; same-task squared lower a/4 | arbitrary epsilon tolerance creates necessary risk |
| Invisible calibration sign | coexisting delta^2 minimax lower under product preparation | numerical upper tolerance automatically lower-bounds risk |
| Free time-varying decoder | would invalidate a label-only M-center converse | such a decoder is legal here |

The Gaussian interval bound is independently checked with exact rational exponential-tail inequalities in regression.py. Numerical witnesses remain checks of the displayed elementary inequalities, not a substitute for the general proofs.
