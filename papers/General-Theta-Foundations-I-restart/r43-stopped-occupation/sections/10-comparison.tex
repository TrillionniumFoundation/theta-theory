\section{Mathematical position and scope}\label{sec:comparison}
Belief-state reduction, discounted dynamic programming, positive measurement instruments and finite-state control are established tools \cite{DL1970,BMT2001,YB2008}. Value-directed belief compression \cite{PB2002} selects a predictive representation for task performance. Bayes-risk quantization is related to Bregman divergence \cite{Varshney2011}. The present conditional curvature is consistent with that interpretation; no priority is claimed for Jensen's inequality, perspective convexity, a Bayesian sufficient statistic or finite-controller approximation in isolation.

Modern filter-stability and finite-memory control results provide near-optimal policies through finite models or windows of past inputs and outputs \cite{KY2022,DKY2024}. Kernel-perturbation bounds \cite{DKY2025} analyze model error under discounted and average criteria. The policy-conditional comparison of \cite{Kim2026} evaluates finite-window belief mismatch in a fixed closed-loop execution; it is not an all-hard-state converse. Our lower quantifier covers every finite-label observer, while the implemented action table is compared to the full-history adaptive acquisition optimum. The total dynamic alphabet, the finite-window length, an offline belief grid and a free time-dependent controller are different resource objects. These distinctions establish the mathematical statement being compared; they do not by themselves establish historical priority over every theorem in those literatures.

The predictive quotient, normalized Bayesian row decomposition and nonsmooth acquired-curvature estimate are inherited mechanisms \cite{GTFBayesian,GTFControlled}. The finite-acquisition article \cite{GTFFinite} identifies an invariant calibrated table through a raw physical lift and controls entrance from a preparation for a uniform finite audit. The new step here is the preparation-injected fixed point and its positive resolvent: it identifies the \emph{first executed} geometric-stop ensemble, makes its entire regret one Jensen gap, and allows recurrent observer chains with no mixing certificate. The change of audit protocol is explicit. In particular, the removal of an entrance term is not asserted for the same finite-uniform-audit protocol, and one program is not claimed to work without recalibration for every preparation and every hazard.

The two principal raw verifications share the same general theorem. The quantum verification does not assume commuting effects; the Gaussian verification does not truncate reports. The marked extension preserves genuine acquired masses and dimensions, including atoms. These consequences connect the abstract construction to actual probability laws; they do not constitute a general classification of all causal experiments.

The finite affine closure, global legal cells, row-density bound and raw feedback overlap remain substantive sufficient conditions. General conditional expectation is not automatically a realizable finite predictive state. Arbitrary stopping selected from the hidden prediction error, unknown-kernel learning, efficient table synthesis, degenerate or infinitely many acquired strata, and matching joint lower bounds for program/workspace/time are not consequences of the theorem. The imposed independent hazard can be implemented internally, but its optimal choice and general optimal stopping are separate problems. No total-variation estimate is promoted to large-deviation equivalence, and no finite-dimensional instrument argument is promoted to an unbounded-operator closure.
