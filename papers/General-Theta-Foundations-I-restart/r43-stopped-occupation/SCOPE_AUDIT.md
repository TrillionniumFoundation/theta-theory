# Scope and remaining proof obligations

## Established in the native text
An exact preparation-injected finite-table construction and stopped Jensen regret identity; matching hard-label laws for actual-density/cover/raw-overlap classes; optimization over all full-history adaptive acquisition for the same prescribed hazard; internal geometric halt accounting; a separately proved hard-cap tail; finite marked acquired-strata law; two distinct raw controlled realizations; a scalar explicit code; complete-task typed simulation; attained bit/calibration law; and a conditional effective compiler with explicit numerical sensitivity.

## Gaps that are not claimed closed
1. A necessary or near-necessary raw characterization of recursive compressibility. Uniform actual-row geometry and feedback overlap remain sufficient hypotheses. The new proof removes observer mixing and stationary initialization, not every geometric/stability certificate.
2. Arbitrary observation-dependent or optimally chosen stopping. The independent hazard and its cap are prescribed protocol components. Mean count and hard count are different, as is a uniform finite audit task.
3. A preparation/hazard-independent single table with all the new uniform guarantees. The statistical constants are uniform, but the calibrated table depends on u0 and s.
4. Unknown-kernel learning, nondominated families, infinite predictive carriers and arbitrary degenerating or countably many strata.
5. Matching joint lower frontiers for program description, workspace, arithmetic precision, physical time and minimum simulator state. The compiler is finite but not efficient.
6. General informative nonsummable posterior-sensitivity lower curves and arbitrary free-decoder/resource architectures.

The native proof does not delegate one of its necessary steps to a realization or a numerical experiment. These open extensions are research boundaries, not a recommendation to abandon the mother problem or replace it by a framework. Whether the new theorem has top-four significance and independent novelty remains for external mathematical evaluation.
