# Derivation pipeline: preparation-injected stopped occupation

1. Start with raw positive kernels and executable coordinate tests. Affine unnormalized report expectations supply a finite ordered cone. The attained image is a Borel predictive quotient; the larger convex buffer is not asserted to be the actual acquired support.
2. Execute an independent hazard s after every genuine core call. The stopping law is an actual probability mixture s sum beta^(t-1) Law(X_t). It is neither a stationary replacement nor a conditional experiment in which failed calls disappear.
3. Optimize full-history acquisition via V=min_a K_a(sg+beta V). The perspective operation preserves concavity. Raw b-step overlap contracts the span of the block Bellman operator, giving a uniform Lipschitz bound for psi=g+beta V/s on a buffered compact domain.
4. Reserve an initial label for the known preparation. Its first action is Bellman-minimizing. The actual first-call cell ensemble is B0. Without this separate label, mixing initial and recurrent occupations would generally break the desired Bellman cancellation.
5. On the normalized product of compact cell cones apply Kakutani to Z -> s B0+beta P_Z Z, permitting all minimizing action mixtures at tied positive-mass rows. Positive likelihood and actual boundary nullity give the closed graph. Zero-mass rows contribute zero, so no discontinuous division at zero is used.
6. Freeze the table. Its physical recurrent map P is positive and mass preserving on the cone over K, even though its fixed codepoint tests need not match the true current posterior. Hence P is nonexpansive in base norm. The unique injected solution is s(I-beta P)^(-1) B0, exactly the ensemble at the FIRST stopped run. Invariant measures or an observer spectral gap are unnecessary.
7. The actual pooled row measure mu_s has cell masses w_j and means q_j. The Bellman equations give integral H dmu_s=s V(u0)+beta sum wV(q), while sum wH(q)=sR+beta sum wV(q). Their cancellation yields R-V=J(psi,mu_s).
8. Actual upper density and convex curvature measure give J <= C m^(-2/d). Conversely every stopped full-history law is an actual row mixture; a union-of-balls argument gives an M^(-2/d) readout lower against EVERY legal finite observer. The baseline is the full adaptive-acquisition optimum, not a same-controller comparison.
9. Internal halting duplicates recurrent labels; hard capping is coupled separately and pays beta^H. Finite marked strata use relative cells and original weights. These steps have their own proofs and resource counts.
10. Gaussian and noncommuting quantum raw kernels verify all certificates before any program is chosen. Numerical error accumulates with expected call count, so statistical uniformity is not a free finite-bit uniformity claim. Parameter-independent stopped simulators multiply retained state counts and add common-task errors.

The new table generally depends on s and u0. Exact geometric risk does not solve arbitrary optimal stopping or remove the old finite-uniform entrance term within the unchanged old task.
