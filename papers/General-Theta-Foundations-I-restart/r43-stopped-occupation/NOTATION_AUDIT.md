# Notation audit

- s in (0,1] is the imposed independent stopping hazard; beta=1-s. It is not a memory label.
- M is total dynamic labels. m is recurrent common-cell labels. An initial label and internal halt copies are included in the total.
- tau is genuine raw core acquisition count. H is a hard cap, H_s a Bellman potential; subscripts and context distinguish them. N=H+2 denotes a core hard physical-call cap, not automatically 1/s.
- L_a is a raw positive instrument; ell_a its likelihood, U_a its unnormalized test numerator, F_a its normalized map; c(u) is affine audit second moment, not a likelihood derivative.
- X_t is the full-history predictive state. q_i is a programmed conditional mean at the stopped call. There is no assertion q_i=E[X_t|S_t=i] at every t.
- B is the realizable convex base, K the compact normalized image domain, D a larger convex certificate buffer. None is by definition the actually acquired support. C is the positive cone; C_j is a cell.
- B0 is an actual first-call ensemble. B_s^pi is a scalar full-history risk. Z is the terminal ensemble; Y^aud is the protected audit random vector. They are different types.
- V_s is a scalar full-history optimal value; U_s=V_s/s. psi_s=(s g+beta V_s)/s is concave and has a uniform Lipschitz certificate under raw overlap.
- mu_s is the actual pooled row law; nu_s^pi is the full-history stopped predictive law. They are not interchangeable, but both inherit the required upper-density bound.
- epsilon in the class definition is raw overlap mass. varepsilon in the simulation/joint sections is causal deficiency. delta is invisible calibration magnitude; eta is per-call implementation mismatch; v is task readout error.
- d_j is acquired affine dimension; D_q quantum Hilbert dimension. Acquired rank change is not eigenvalue rank change.
- All readouts in the hard-state converse are fixed label-only readouts. Fresh randomization is independent and no private seed is retained unless encoded in the label.

Actual labels/cross-references/bibliography are independently enumerated from TeX inputs and compiled auxiliary data. No empirical count is used as a mathematical proof.
