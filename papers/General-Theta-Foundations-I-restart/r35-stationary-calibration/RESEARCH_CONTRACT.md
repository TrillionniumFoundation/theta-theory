## Mathematical target

Extend the acquired-overlap/Bellman-curvature mechanism to a genuinely time-homogeneous finite-state observer used indefinitely, without a free exact posterior, uncharged phase counter, or a compulsory physical reset. Prove existence of one globally calibrated stationary controller, not merely locally optimal conditional quantizers. Aim for an all-controller lower and a matching total-label upper against the full-history average predictive control optimum.

The proposed mechanism is a finite-dimensional fixed point for unnormalized label/preparation ensembles, combined with a small independently randomized observer-label erasure. Erasure is an internal algorithmic choice; no physical state is reset. A separately stated raw averaged-instrument minorization must justify convergence from arbitrary initial preparation. This is distinct from a renewal interface that forces both physical and observer resets.
