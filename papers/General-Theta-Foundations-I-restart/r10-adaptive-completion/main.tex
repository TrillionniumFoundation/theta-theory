\begin{abstract}
A prepared causal experiment acquires a geometry of executable future responses. We prove that, under uniform response refinement and actual-mass separation, optimal full-history acquisition followed by an $M$-point checkpoint code can be matched within constant factors by a single autonomous machine with at most $M$ states in total. The constants are independent of the observation budget and include the acquisition controller and stopping rule. The proof compares arbitrary quantizers with actual-mass tree frontiers and then compiles a depth-truncated frontier into a causal machine. A correlated singular preparation has a strictly observation-dependent optimal acquisition decision. Calibration, numerical precision and an attained causal defect compose in the same task with explicit resource charges. For overlapping noisy responses, a complementary continuation-cut theorem identifies the largest acquired cut exponent and gives matching sequential bounds under global update certificates; positive-noise regression verifies these at changing observable ranks. The finite acquired law is distinguished throughout from the preparation law, and neither ambient dimension nor an uncharged exact posterior is an input.
\end{abstract}
