## What changed mathematically

The center is **Theorem 2.1 (adaptive causal acquired-geometry completion)**. For prepared causal observation kernels with a fixed bounded executable audit, every legal refinement must have actual conditional mass bounded below, separated response supports, and child-to-parent scales bounded away from zero and one. These hypotheses are fixed before choosing any policy or budget. They allow correlated, nonstationary, singular and observed-value-dependent refinement.

Let `nu_n^pi` be the actual finite acquired law of the posterior response under policy pi, `A_n^pi` its remaining response variance, `A_n=inf_pi A_n^pi`, and `B_infinity` the irreducible audit risk. For `Psi(n,M)=inf_pi(A_n^pi+q_M(nu_n^pi))`, the theorem proves

```text
R_cp^ref(n,M) - B_infinity = Psi(n,M),
Psi(n,M) <= R_aut^ref(n,M) - B_infinity <= C Psi(n,M),
Psi(n,M) asymp A_n + q_M(mu).
```

Here `mu` is the preparation-response law, used only through a proved oracle comparison; it is not called the finite acquired law. The constant C is independent of n and M. The autonomous state alphabet includes every internal acquisition-controller node and every terminal stop/readout node. There is no external clock or exact uncharged posterior. The terminal readout must use only the retained tuple after scratch erasure; the last report, stopping time and program position are not extra channels.

The proof is a new completion chain relative to R9: uniform actual-mass frontier comparison (Lemma 2.2), bounded-depth causal pruning (Lemma 2.3), and a cardinality-stability argument that pays for all `2m-1` controller nodes rather than merely m leaf labels. It compiles a full-history acquisition minimizer into one finite autonomous machine. Conditional projection, finite Bellman recursion and tree counting are standard tools, not claimed new separately.

**Theorem 3.1** verifies the hypotheses from a two-state correlated graph-directed preparation. Every binary observation costs one call; the controller chooses the first coordinate of each pair and must pay separately for the remaining one. Actual next-coordinate probabilities switch between `(1/2,1/10)` and `(1/10,1/2)` with acquired parity. The optimal next coordinate genuinely depends on the acquired state. The proof gives exact rational moment systems, closed even/odd acquisition formulas and a strictly positive advantage over any fixed third-coordinate choice. A proved nonstationary extension permits history-dependent pair laws and scales with uniform floors, without a limiting dimension.

**Corollary 3.3** composes this feedback acquisition with a genuinely inaccessible calibration offset and an attained early erasure channel. For N>=3 and M>=7, in one direct-sum squared task,

```text
R_cp(N,M,delta,epsilon)-B_infinity
  asymp R_aut(N,M,delta,epsilon)-B_infinity
  asymp A_(N-3) + q_M(mu) + delta^2 + epsilon.
```

The channel's causal deficiency to its exact-bit target is exactly epsilon under compulsory early virtual-bit completion. Its internal status and acquisition controller use `1+3L<=M` states. Sign/erasure constructions are inherited mechanisms, not newly claimed inventions. Fixed-leaf numerical approximation has a proved upper, not a mandatory lower floor. **Corollary 2.4** separately charges finite primitive action descriptions, read-only program, scratch, output precision, planning and sequential execution; it is a feasible computational region, not a universal optimal complexity theorem.

## Actual remaining mathematical gaps

The new result does not yet classify arbitrary overlapping-noise adaptive acquisition, unknown kernels or interventions that change the audit law. It does not show that bare continuation widths alone always produce an executable recursion without refinement/global-cover certificates. An optimal joint region for all program, workspace, planning time, physical duration and minimal simulator resources remains beyond the feasible implementation theorem. Degenerating mass/separation/noise floors and unbounded stopping or infinite-horizon transcript equivalence need further proofs. No TV closeness is asserted to imply LDP equivalence or an unbounded-operator closure.

These are explicit remaining research obligations, not a change of the mother problem. The supplied new statements have full proofs, but their independent correctness, originality and top-four mathematical significance remain matters for the next referee. No journal acceptance or commissioned editorial decision is claimed.
