# Literature comparison and evidence scope

The existing thirty references and comparisons in R9 are retained. They include predictive sufficiency/experiment comparison, quantization, functional and decoder-side-information coding, filtering, POMDP geometry and finite controllers. Their theorems are not claimed proved anew.
