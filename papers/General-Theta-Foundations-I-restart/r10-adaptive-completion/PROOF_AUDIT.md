# Proof audit

**Quantifiers.** The response-refinement constants hold for every legal history/action before any choice of n, M or code. The response audit is fixed under sensing and conditionally independent of observation randomness. An arbitrary intervention changing the outcome is not silently admitted.

**Actual measures.** Each node carries its actual probability, including Markov dependence. `nu_n^pi` is finitely acquired and atomic. `mu` is the preparation-response law. The comparison between them is proved via Ψ, not assumed. No formal support replaces acquired probability mass.

**Lower bounds.** The 2L-leaf frontier supplies disjoint neighborhoods; L arbitrary centers miss at least L of them. Centers may lie outside the support. Randomized encoders are handled by conditioning on independent coins. Two constraint-dropping bounds are combined without exchanging policy infima.

**Depth and controller.** The eligible-node greedy order is the unrestricted order with forbidden depths removed. It refines the unrestricted frontier truncated at n. The internal-node count is essential: `m=floor((M+1)/2)` gives all `2m-1` states. The clock, stop decision and policy action are determined by the current node. Program heads reset; scratch is erased at every cut.

**Uniformity.** The new completion constant depends on p*, a*, b*, D, g, not on n or M. It may be large, and degenerating separation or probability floors are not covered uniformly. The retained serial/noisy theorem still has finite-horizon/nondegeneracy constants; it is not made horizon uniform by the new separated theorem.

**Perturbations.** Calibration and numerical representation errors on fixed leaf means add in the root task norm and are then squared; the conditional residual cross term vanishes. This is not a stability theorem for a corrupted acquisition policy. Policy degradation is the separate κ. Arbitrary perturbed report kernels require their own causal/morphism certificate.

**Realization audit.** Positive Markov cylinder probabilities establish support but are not treated as uniform masses. Within-pair conditional independence is used explicitly; cross-pair dependence remains. The next action changes with the acquired state and the strict difference is computed. Positive-noise Gaussian responses use the separate continuation theorem, not disjoint-support refinement.

**Joint nuisance and simulator.** The calibration sign is genuinely unobserved; the erasure is an attained channel. The fair nuisance prior provides a minimax lower. The early virtual bit must be completed before geometry/audit, ruling out future leakage. All product controller states are counted. A defect allowance is never promoted to an attained lower outside this construction.

**External certification.** This audit is a proof reading and dependency record, not a formal verification or independent editorial acceptance. Tests, compilation and visual inspection certify only their stated finite/source/layout properties.
