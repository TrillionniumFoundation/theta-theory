## Detail of the new proof chain

The audit projection is established directly at any admitted bounded stopping time using conditional independence of the audit given the preparation. There is no substitution of a random time into an unrelated deterministic estimate. The checkpoint optimization ranges over full legal policies. Its acquired laws are finite atomic posterior-mean distributions; they need not agree across policies.

The lower `max(A_n,q_M(mu))` is obtained twice, by dropping separate constraints. It is not an interchange of policy-dependent infima. The preparation-response law is used only in this proved oracle comparison. Under any fixed policy, node supports partition its actual response mass. Child energies decrease strictly and have a uniform ratio floor. This makes the global maximum-energy order ancestor-compatible and gives the doubling estimate used to pay for every internal controller node.

The implementer stores at most `2m-1` complete tree nodes, not just `m` leaves, and no external clock. It stops at depth at most n. A program/temporary workspace exists separately; arbitrary real constants are not declared a free finite-bit machine. The finite-bit corollary assumes supplied finite certificates and finite primitive action descriptions. The Markov realization has rational probability, squared-scale and moment calculations, so comparisons and rounded means are effectively available.

All new general statements have textual proofs; numerical or finite checks only guard transcription and implementation. Priority and correctness remain subject to independent mathematical review.
