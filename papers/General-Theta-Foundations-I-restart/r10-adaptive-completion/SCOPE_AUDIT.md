# Scope audit

## Established relative to the pinned source

A new general controlled response-refinement theorem matches optimal full-history acquisition/checkpoint risk with one finite autonomous implementation, uniformly in both budgets. The implementation counts the controller, stop status and all internal nodes. The functional is explicitly built from actual acquired posterior-mean laws. A proved oracle comparison uses the preparation-response law without conflating it with finite acquisition. A correlated graph-directed class verifies the assumptions and has a strictly observation-dependent acquisition optimum. The nonstationary singular extension does not require a limiting dimension. An attained early-channel/calibration extension gives a four-resource law in the same task. Numerical/program/scratch/time accounts are explicit feasible bounds.

## Retained rather than new

The R9 serial continuation-completion theorem and noisy rank/singular-query realizations, R8 one-cut comparison/localization, R7 dominated cuts and independent-tail allocation, and earlier block/filter/morphism/technical proofs are retained with their own scopes. They are not relabeled as discoveries of this revision.

## Actual unresolved mathematics

1. Optimal feedback acquisition and finite-state matching for arbitrary overlapping noisy posteriors, unknown kernels or interventions changing the audit distribution. The new separation class does not close this problem.
2. Bare continuation-width sufficiency without global recursive-cover or refinement certificates; raw derivation of such certificates for arbitrary prepared experiments.
3. A generally optimal joint region for program length, workspace, planning time, physical duration and minimum simulator/controller complexity, beyond the matched total-state curve and feasible table construction here.
4. Uniform limits as branch masses, separation, noise or observable frames degenerate; unbounded stopping and infinite-horizon transcript equivalence.
5. Independent proof verification and priority/significance assessment. This revision is not evidence of journal acceptance or a formal proof certificate.

No allowed TV budget is made a risk floor outside an attained construction. No TV proximity is made LDP equivalence. No finite-dimensional identity is promoted to unbounded-operator closure. No finite tests replace continuous-parameter proofs. These boundaries preserve the mother problem rather than replace it by a different topic.
