# Counterexample and boundary ledger

1. **Leaf labels are not the controller.** A full binary tree with four terminal leaves has seven nodes. Claiming four autonomous states by counting outputs alone is false. The proof allocates at most `floor((M+1)/2)` leaves and proves cardinality stability to pay for internal nodes.
2. **Preparation law is not acquired law.** Before any Markov observation the posterior response mean is one atom; its preparation-response law is non-atomic and singular. The equality in the theorem is with Ψ, and the oracle comparison is a separate proof.
3. **Independent fair tails are not used.** In the new Markov model the next coordinate probabilities change between (.5,.1) and (.1,.5) after an acquired parity. The old fair-product allocation theorem does not prove this feedback optimum.
4. **Small terminal width alone is insufficient.** Retained delayed-query and positive-noise rank examples have earlier continuation geometry wider than the terminal forecast. The separated-response assumptions cannot be dropped simply because checkpoint coding is easy.
5. **No free time.** A state reused at different depths cannot issue different actions from an uncounted wall clock. The new tree-node machine has no time argument. The retained serial theorem explicitly charges a stored phase.
6. **Unknown action descriptions are not log K bits.** The finite-bit table bound assumes a fixed finite global primitive instruction alphabet. Arbitrary history-specific real-valued action descriptions need separate coding; they are not covered by that bit bound.
7. **Numerical error is not a mandatory floor.** A permitted rounding tolerance may be unused. Only the constructed inaccessible sign and actual erasure produce matching nuisance lower terms. Fixed-leaf numerical perturbations have a proved upper only.
8. **Overlapping Gaussian posteriors are not separated cylinders.** They satisfy the retained cut/global-cover mechanism, not the new response-refinement hypotheses.
9. **Finite stopped theorem is not infinite horizon.** The audit projection is proved directly for stop≤n. No unbounded stopping, LDP equivalence or unbounded-operator closure follows.
10. **Uniform constants have floors.** Vanishing branch probability/separation or a child ratio tending to zero can destroy the doubling comparison that pays for the controller. No uniform theorem at such degeneration is asserted.

The retained R9/R8/R7/R6 counterexample ledgers remain intact in their own subtrees.
