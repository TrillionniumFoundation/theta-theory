# Pipeline derivation

## Mother line

Start from a prepared causal observation kernel and a single executable audit. Its response `Z(theta)` is obtained by integrating that audit kernel. Histories define conditional response laws and a test-based predictive quotient. In the controlled binary class histories form a countable measurable realization; full future-test equivalence gives the minimal quotient. Nothing starts from an assumed manifold or Fisher matrix.

## Actual acquisition to a uniform causal implementation

1. For every legal report edge, retain the actual conditional probability and the raw-verified remaining response support/scale. Under a fixed policy these give actual node masses `w_h` and energies `e_h=w_h ell_h^2`.
2. A maximum-energy frontier has comparable leaf energies. Positive child ratio and mass floors imply a cardinality-doubling lower. Strong support separation then compares arbitrary M-center distortion with this actual-mass frontier.
3. Optimize full-history acquisition by the finite Bellman recursion. This is over the declared feedback policies, not a fixed exploration mislabeled optimal control.
4. Prune the selected policy's tree under both a leaf budget and a maximum observation depth. The finite eligible split order refines the unrestricted frontier truncated at the depth budget.
5. Store all retained nodes, including internal acquisition decisions and terminal stop nodes. A binary tree with m leaves has 2m−1 nodes; cardinality stability proves the loss of this conversion is only a fixed risk factor.
6. Conditional audit projection proves the exact checkpoint functional `inf_pi(A_n^pi+q_M(nu_n^pi))`. Independent constraint-dropping bounds compare it with `A_n+q_M(mu)`. The preparation-response law is not passed off as the finite acquired law.
7. Fixed-leaf calibration/numerical errors are squared only after addition in the same task norm. A full-law causal morphism adds bounded-loss TV error and multiplies actual simulator/controller states. A concrete sign/erasure experiment attains the matching δ and ε terms.

## Two different verification paths

The new correlated singular kernels verify the response-refinement theorem; a Markov moment system and a strict observation-dependent choice establish genuine feedback content. The retained positive-noise kernels verify the continuation-cut/global-cover theorem, with actual observable rank rather than hidden dimension. Overlapping noise is not artificially squeezed into disjoint cylinder hypotheses. Both paths begin from raw kernels and contribute to the same foundations pipeline.
