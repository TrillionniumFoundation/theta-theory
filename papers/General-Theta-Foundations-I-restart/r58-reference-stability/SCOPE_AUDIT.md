- A general sufficient-class all-strategy lower and one-common-program upper when the prepared comparison law is not an exact operating model, using actual productive occupation and full current report/audit row certificates.
- Corrected acquired-mass cap and task error, both state and event stopping widths, and exact zero-error specialization.
- Matching two-scale acquired-geometry curves in an explicit two-sided error regime, not at arbitrarily fine scales with fixed nonzero error.
- A raw hidden-mode metastable extension retaining initial mass and inverse exit time.
- Classical controlled and noncommuting quantum primitive verifications of the same theorem, including approximate controls, singular marked laws and rank-changing outcomes.
- Same-task attained calibration/erasure composition, and a numerical-execution bound conditional on actual effective certificates.
- All retained exact role, common balance, directional Gaussian, stopped-task and causal-simulation results under their original conditions.

## Genuine remaining mathematical problems

A universal intrinsic characterization from arbitrary causal kernels; automatic construction of an optimal comparison preparation and balanced program; statistical learning of all-history calibration certificates with optimal sample cost; all support-changing non-dominated experiments; an optimal stateful-simulator invariant; efficient global program synthesis and joint optimal program/workspace/time complexity; and general unbounded losses, nontermination or unrestricted infinite-dimensional operators remain unproved. These are not reasons to replace the mother problem with a realization paper; they are the unsolved obligations beyond the present stated sufficient class.

The physical cost N in the core is not a free calibration oracle: external comparison preparation and certificate-acquisition resources must be added when they are performed. The quotient is minimal only under its executable-test and measurable-section assumptions. The nominal comparison geometry is not automatically the actual perturbed support. The common program's robust minimax risk is not unrestricted control of a different post-intervention task.

## F1–F4 mapping

F1: central theorem plus actual mass/covering corollary, explicit update-hazard stability, report/task continuity and state costs. F2: same-task product construction and numerical/simulator composition. F3: actual metastable occupation and retained singular/atomic geometry. F4: two raw imperfect-reference theorems verify the same central assumptions. This maps obligations to the proved class; it is not a claim to have classified all experiments meeting the mother question.
