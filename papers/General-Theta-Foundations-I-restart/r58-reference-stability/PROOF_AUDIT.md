# Proof audit

**Quantifiers.** One actual witness supplies both effort and discrepancy for every competing strategy. One common upper works for all actual models and surviving histories. The comparison rows are not themselves an adversary chosen after seeing the code. No minimax exchange is used.

**Measurability.** Standard Borel full report/command/history spaces, jointly measurable conditional row versions on actual reachable histories, Borel mark maps and conditional first/second moments are stated. Current random transitions may be represented with an independent uniform variable; the certificates extend by integration. A finite readout dictionary is fixed across histories and actual models.

**Mass.** Almost-sure exactly-once commitment gives actual terminal mass one. The replaced comparison submeasure need not have mass one or be executable. Density domination is proved before Radon–Nikodym normalization. The t=0 convention avoids evaluating Q at infinity; b<1 and empty legal classes are not legitimized by the bounds.

**Task.** Conditional moments are taken given the full current visible report, not only a coarse mark. Nonnegative squared loss allows scaling down mass. Error e_- controls every finite-image report-dependent readout, not only constant outputs. Post-command and nonintervening audits are separated.

**Stopping.** Expected positive cost bounds actual productive count. Proofs first truncate nonnegative sums and then use Tonelli/monotone convergence. The comparison need not terminate. The drift proof drops a nonnegative killed potential and never invokes an unverified uniform-integrability assertion.

**State counts.** Only state-stopped alphabets have disjoint live and terminal labels. Event commitment permits reuse. Mandatory B0 is evaluated at the actual witness. Current command coins are not persistent seeds. Simulator composition stores actual finite pairs before discarding certified terminal information.

**Geometry.** The acquired comparison law is physically specified, not a formal reachable set. Actual perturbed law is connected with errors, not declared to have the same dimension. The geometric lower after subtracting e_N needs an explicit smallness constant. Zero-dimensional atoms retain their mass thresholds.

**Nonuniformity.** Mode transitions and drift are uniform over actual histories/commands; observation-dependent selection does not make a stationary bound valid. Initial bad mass/v is retained. Potentials are proof objects, not free state.

**Raw realization.** Classical sign bias is conditioned on all pre-call information. Pauli unitary invariance and unnormalized complete-instrument contraction are proved from positive/negative trace parts. No posterior normalization at a zero-mass or rank-changing outcome is used. Both verifications prove the same general assumptions.

**Numerics and causal simulation.** Numerical certificates are uniform for the selected corrected design. Effective synthesis is not deduced from Borel existence. Full stopped scored-law TV and local task-row errors are different; expected waiting requires its own certificate; one-way TV does not imply a converse, LDP equivalence or an arbitrary infinite-horizon result.

**Unresolved independent assessment.** The audit records the above proof obligations and written arguments. It is not an independent proof assistant certificate, an exhaustive historical reproof, a priority clearance or a journal significance decision.
