# Assumption matrix

| Requirement | General role | Classical kernel check | Quantum kernel check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard Borel full histories/reports, bounded audit | disintegration, measurable acceptance, finite loss | signs, continuous/singular marked report and bounded audit | finite-dimensional positive instrument to classical report/audit |
| Prepared comparison law, full-report moments | defines actual calibration geometry, not a formal manifold | fair comparison mixture of J_+ and J_- | J(I/2), all outcome weights retained |
| Actual witness close at every legal productive row | all-strategy lower, not a preferred controller | conditional sign bias and full channel error | trace-distance-to-mixed witness and full instrument error |
| All-strategy actual effort | cap b_-; witness cannot change between inequalities | gate success <=p1 | same paid gate count |
| All-model common attempt/hazard | implementable upper under arbitrary hidden history | imperfect fresh flip coin | imperfect within-call Pauli channel |
| Global acquired covering and small-ball mass | matching powers, not required for bare interval | verify from declared mark/audit pushforward | same law from the complete positive instrument |
| Update stability | conditional hazard summation and actual defect occupation | independent fresh coin, not independent attempts | unnormalized positive map contraction, not posterior contraction |
| Report/task continuity | current full-law/moment certificates | total variation of complete J rows | trace-to-total-variation of full instruments |
| Singular/nonuniform extension | actual initial bad mass and residence time | hidden two-state calibration mode | same mode with rank-changing instruments |
| Persistent-state costs | stopping role convention and paid gate | B0=B=2; identity exports | B0=B=2; no free persistent quantum storage |
| Finite computation | additional partitions/predicates/integration certificates | stated separately, not inferred from Borel kernels | stated separately, not inferred from matrix dimension |
| Simulator | strategy/action/report/clock/readout compatibility | full stopped-law comparison | same common task; no unbounded-operator extension |

The comparison law need not be an admitted actual model, but it must be a declared preparable experiment and its full current report must not hide an informative auxiliary coordinate. Fixed nonzero errors can invalidate fine-scale matching. The lower remains an interval even then; universal rates are not asserted. Comparison preparation/calibration costs are additional physical resources when used, not free calls hidden in N.
