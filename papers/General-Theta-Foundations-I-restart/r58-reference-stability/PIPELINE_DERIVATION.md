# Derivation of the central theorem

1. Start from normalized positive prepared kernels, legal commands and a fixed bounded future audit. Include every actually visible current coordinate in the report.
2. Form the predictive test quotient only under the stated measurable realization hypotheses; the task mark is an evaluation, not automatically the full quotient.
3. Specify the physical stopped observer (event or state halt), count every intercall label and give the fixed decoder.
4. Fix a preparable comparison experiment and derive nu,f,w by disintegration; do not append an ambient covariance/Fisher manifold.
5. At one actual operating witness prove the all-strategy cost inequality and full productive-row mass/task certificates.
6. For an arbitrary competing observer retain its actual pre-call occupation. Replace only each current report–audit row; project its accepted finite-image readout to measures xi_c.
7. Prove xi<=E A nu, |xi(X)-1|<=a_N and actual loss>=sum_c integral d_c dxi_c-e_N. There is no comparison stopped path at this step.
8. Retain only t=(1-a_N)_+ mass, normalize with cap b_-/t, and minimize over the actual fixed dictionary. This gives the corrected lower.
9. Independently build one common approximate balancing attempt. At cap beta use acceptance h/beta; conditional hazard >=1/beta-a_+ gives budget kappa_+/(1/beta-a_+) and the analogous task bound.
10. Solve beta=b_+/(1+b_+a_+) before execution. Pack live and output roles under the declared halt semantics.
11. For nonuniform calibration use actual stopped drift; for bad-mode dynamics choose V=bad_charge*1_bad/v and retain pi_0/v and uN/v.
12. Apply global covering and actual calibration small-ball mass to the same profile. Impose the explicit two-sided error regime before asserting matched powers.
13. Verify the hypotheses directly on a classical sign channel and on a noncommuting positive instrument; do not use these conclusions to prove steps 6–12.
14. Compose the same scored task with explicit early erasure/calibration and finite numerical or typed simulation certificates. Keep one-way upper transfer separate from reverse lower transfer.

The logical entry point for a new experiment is a raw certificate at steps 4–5 and 9, not a realization-specific formula imported backwards. All failed or absent hypotheses remain visible in the scope audit.
