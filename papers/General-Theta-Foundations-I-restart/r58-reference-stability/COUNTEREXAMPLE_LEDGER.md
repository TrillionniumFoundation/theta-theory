# Counterexample and sharpness ledger

1. **Live labels reused on commitment.** Two gate/productive labels also hold two final outputs when commitment is an event. Mandatory live count alone does not yield M-B outputs. State-only halting must be an explicit primitive.
2. **Reference outside the operating family.** Selecting rare zero-variance marks can change minimax risk by b e when only one-step residual task measure differs by e. Both directions are proved in `prop:reference-sharpness`; no finite-width relaxation removes the issue.
3. **Mark-only calibration leaks audit.** A constant mark with a full report revealing the audit sign has zero two-output prediction error, although conditioning only on the constant mark gives unit variance. The central theorem conditions on the full report and all stopping inputs.
4. **False normalization of comparison mass.** The substituted accepted comparison submeasure may have total mass t!=1. Dividing by t without changing cap b to b/t gives an invalid lower. The proof scales only to (1-a_N)_+.
5. **Small gate error, long waiting.** Replacing rare mass 1/beta by 1/beta-a has exact waiting time 1/(1/beta-a). A terminal-law error does not bound expected cost. Retained R57 sharpness.
6. **Bad mode initially occupied.** At u small and v small, an initial bad state contributes order 1/v before exiting. Dropping pi_0/v in favor of a stationary ratio fails, including under adaptive stopping. Rational finite check provided.
7. **No first-moment balance.** Identical reports with audits 0,+1,-1 have reference profile zero but common minimax risk one. Retained all-program proof, not a failed finite mixture search.
8. **Free port or clock.** A dispatcher receiving a forbidden intercall physical port state is an extra resource. Stateful simulation needs actual live state and output-export accounting.
9. **Infinite accuracy from finite observations.** A finite data set alone does not certify all-history row bounds. The theorem takes a mathematical raw certificate; statistical acquisition is a separate problem with its own budget.
10. **Nominal support substituted for actual support.** Total variation can move small mass to a new singular stratum. R58 transfers risk with errors, not a universal dimension identity.

These examples delimit hypotheses without changing the General Theta mother problem or declaring a general impossibility theorem.
