# Literature comparison and source audit

The following are primary papers or publisher records, consulted on 8 October 2026. This targeted comparison is not an exhaustive priority search.

| Work / fixed version | Inspected result | Distinction from the native theorem |
|---|---|---|
| Grover–Dimitrakakis, arXiv:2104.07276v1 (2021) | depth-dependent belief discretization, Theorem 1 and Lemma 1; planning tree and memory bounds | Depth-adaptive covers are not new here. Native labels are calibrated under the deployed observer's actual law, and total internal clock states receive a converse. |
| Li–Hammar–Bertsekas, arXiv:2507.04646v1 (2025) | feature-based aggregation; Propositions 5–7 on approximation error, lower estimates and bias | Belief aggregation and representative states are not new here. The native exact Jensen loss, nonsmooth actual-mass estimate and hard exact-deadline state law are different quantified conclusions. |
| Lyu–Suen–Zhang, arXiv:2305.18020v5 (2025) | Theorem 1 and Proposition 1: conditional means, curvature-weighted finite information partitions | Conditional-mean/curvature architecture has a static antecedent. The native law is causal under endogenous acquired distributions and includes execution and clock lower bounds. |
| Kara–Yuksel, JMLR 23(11), 1–46 (2022) | finite-window near-optimality and filter-stability bounds, especially Theorem 12; also Theorem 17 | Exponential forgetting and finite-memory approximation are established tools. The native resource is the total labels of an internally stopped machine and gives a matched M-n law, not a window-length claim. |
| Demirci–Kara–Yuksel, SIAM JCO 62(6), 2859–2883 (2024), doi:10.1137/24M1643736 | publisher abstract and metadata: average-cost POMDP optimality under contraction, finite approximations | Wasserstein contraction in partially observed control is not new. No claim is made that the native terminal-risk theorem contains their average-cost theorem. |
| Blackwell (1953), doi:10.1214/aoms/1177729032; Le Cam (1986) | classical experiment comparison references | Deficiency contraction, bounded-risk comparison and statistical randomization are standard; new causal accounts specify memory, clock and scored-law interfaces. |
| Davies–Lewis (1970), doi:10.1007/BF01647093 | operational instrument reference | Positive quantum instruments and density-matrix linearity are standard. The raw acquired Jacobian and charged-clock application are the native verifications. |
| Kechris (1995), GTM 156 | standard Borel and compact-image background | Quotient measurability is proved only on the explicitly stated compact-piece class, not asserted as a universal new measurable theorem. |

The first three recent works requested by the external referee are now cited in the formal article and compared theorem by theorem. The native manuscript does not declare conditional expectation, concavity, Bellman recursion, quantization, contraction, or optimal depth allocation alone to be new discoveries. The claim is their proved coupling with actual acquisition, boundary-valid curvature, exact internal-clock necessity and two raw-kernel verifications.
