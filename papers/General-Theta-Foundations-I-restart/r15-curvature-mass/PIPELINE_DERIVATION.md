# Pipeline derivation — from experiments to the joint law

1. Fix prepared positive kernels, legal finite common actions, costs and a finite terminal audit. Build the actual controlled path law by iterated kernel integration. The mother problem starts here, not at a metric matrix.
2. Evaluate executable future tests. Countable determining tests and compact-image regularity give a measurable predictive quotient. A verified invariant finite linear test span gives the additional affine carrier; it is not required to be a multiplicative algebra.
3. Push each incoming state through the real report kernel to obtain Lambda_t(s,a). Every density, support and stratum assertion below refers to this actual measure. Verify the conditions for compressed mixtures as well as full histories.
4. Compute finite-horizon Bayes continuation values. Affine decision-tree risks give concavity; the Wasserstein certificate gives Lip(V_t) <= L_g product beta. This is valid for optimized feedback policies, not just the same controller on two filters.
5. Design the observer forwards. At each phase, select a Bellman action from its retained calibrated state, push the finite mixture through the physical instrument, globally partition the resulting actual law and pool every incoming edge. Each next label carries its true conditional predictive barycenter under the implemented law.
6. Telescope the cost of forgetting: total excess is exactly the sum of Bellman–Jensen defects. This inherited exact identity is more general than the eventual exponent.
7. Extend -V_t by all bounded supporting planes. Local submean/radial estimates yield h^(2-d) times actual cell mass times distributional curvature in an enlarged ball. Density rho and bounded overlap turn this into C rho Lip(V_t)L_t^(-2/d), without excluding boundary contact or action switches.
8. Allocate L_t=1+floor((m-1)a_t^gamma/A_n), m=M-n. This spends one unavoidable baseline label at every phase. Summable continuation sensitivities make the geometric term uniformly C m^(-2/d); no O(n^(1+2/d)) equal-allocation penalty remains.
9. Prove the converse separately. Equivalent report supports imply identical null events in finite future traces from a reused label. Such a label cannot occur at two cuts of an internally exact-n machine, so terminal labels are at most M-n. Strong task concavity and last-step actual density then give c(M-n)^(-2/d).
10. Verify two raw classes: Gaussian likelihood ratios give a lower-dimensional linear Gaussian image and a boundary-decaying logistic density; noncommuting positive effects give an exact smooth inverse on trace hyperplanes. The instrument coupling supplies both classes' block stability and report continuity.
11. Allow finite acquired strata and actual rank changes. Keep unnormalized component mass in the lower; permit early allocations to pool several types when only one state is affordable.
12. Attach numerical and causal interfaces with complete state, readout and clock accounting. A separately specified early-erasure/offset task realizes additive error lower terms on the same preparation. Do not promote arbitrary approximation allowances to lower bounds.

This sequence produces one center and applications, not a realization-specific result retrofitted as foundations. The unresolved next step is a weaker intrinsic criterion encompassing non-summable sensitivities, unknown kernels or infinite predictive carriers; it is not asserted by this derivation.
