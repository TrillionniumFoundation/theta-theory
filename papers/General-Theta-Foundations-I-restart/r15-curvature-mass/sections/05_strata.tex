\section{Singular acquisition and changing rank}\label{sec:strata}
A density on a full affine carrier is not needed for the exact causal identity. The following extension keeps track of the mass of each acquired component. Its constants may depend on the fixed number of strata and their geometry.

\begin{theorem}[Finite stratified transfer]\label{thm:strata}
Retain Definition~\ref{def:affine}, clock neutrality, the task hypotheses and the block Wasserstein bound. Replace the full-dimensional density condition by the following uniform statement for every incoming state and legal action. A type readable from the current report and incoming programmed state decomposes the actual post-report law into finitely many subprobability laws $\lambda_e$, supported on fixed compact convex affine carriers $K_e\subset K$. Carriers of dimension zero are singletons. A positive-dimensional carrier has dimension $r_e\le r$, and $\lambda_e$ has density at most $\rho_e$ in its own affine coordinates. Immediately before the final action and report, uniformly over every incoming state and legal action, one fixed $r$-dimensional component has mass at least $w>0$ and subdensity at most $\rho_*<\infty$. Here $r\ge1$.

Then the checkpoint and autonomous excess risks satisfy
\[
 R_\cp(n,M)-B_n^*\asymp M^{-2/r},\qquad
 R_\aut(n,M)-B_n^*\asymp(M-n)^{-2/r},\quad M\ge n+1,
\]
with constants independent of $n,M$. An admissible lower constant is proportional to $\alpha w^{1+2/r}\rho_*^{-2/r}$. All stored type identities are included in the label count.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At a phase with a sufficiently large allocation $L$, reserve one label for each singleton and split the remaining labels among the finitely many positive-dimensional types, assigning a fixed fraction to each. Conditional barycenters are computed separately for each type and cell, pooling all its incoming edges. They remain in $K_e$ by convexity and have the exact predictive meaning of Lemma~\ref{lem:telescope}. Intersecting a type with a grid cell produces a Borel next-label rule. Distinct types may have intersecting carriers, but the readable type is part of the rule, so no unobservable stratum oracle is needed.

The proof of Lemma~\ref{lem:curvature} in dimension $r_e$ works for a subprobability law without renormalizing it. Its contribution is at most $C_e\rho_e\Lip(V_t)L_e^{-2/r_e}$. Since $r_e\le r$ and the number of types is fixed, the sum is at most $C\Lip(V_t)L^{-2/r}$. For the finitely many smaller allocations, use a single pooled label: the Jensen gap is at most $\Lip(V_t)\operatorname{diam}(K)$. Increasing the fixed constant yields the same $CL^{-2/r}$ bound for every integer $L\ge1$. Thus there is not a mandatory number of type labels at every early phase. Lemma~\ref{lem:allocation}, with $p=2/r$, gives the upper with exactly the same $M-n$ resource count.

For the lower, mixing over the actual history immediately before the last action preserves the chosen component's mass lower $w$ and subdensity upper $\rho_*$. For any $L$ representatives in the ambient space, their radius-$u$ balls intersect that affine carrier in volume at most $L v_r u^r$. Choose $u=(w/(2\rho_*Lv_r))^{1/r}$. At least $w/2$ of the unnormalized component remains outside, giving squared distortion at least
\[
 \frac{w}{2}\left(\frac{w}{2\rho_*Lv_r}\right)^{2/r}.
\]
Strong concavity supplies the factor $\alpha/2$. Use $L=M$ for checkpoint and Lemma~\ref{lem:clock} to use $L\le M-n$ for autonomous risk. The result uses a genuine terminal acquired submeasure, not a normalized hypothetical stratum.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Validation mixed with noncommuting observation]\label{cor:validation}
Modify the raw instrument in \eqref{eq:quantumraw} as follows. Fix $0<\vartheta<1$ and a rank-one orthogonal resolution $P_1,\ldots,P_D$ of $I$. After the charged channel $\mathcal C_t^a$, perform with probability $\vartheta$ the projective instrument $q\mapsto P_jqP_j$, reporting its type and $j$; otherwise perform the continuous instrument \eqref{eq:quantumraw}. The same choice probability is used at every phase. Then Theorem~\ref{thm:strata} holds with $r=D^2-1$. Its terminal converse contains the factor $1-\vartheta$ rather than discarding that mass by conditioning.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The two instrument types have a common reference measure with positive weights on the continuous cube and every atomic report $j$. Uniform positivity of $q$ gives positive probability to every such atomic report. Consequently the complete report laws remain equivalent at every phase, including following a pure-state report. The post-report state is exactly $P_j$ on validation, and its other component has mass $1-\vartheta$ and subdensity at most $(1-\vartheta)\rho$. The ordinary instrument coupling in Lemma~\ref{lem:instrument} applies to the combined instrument, with the same $3\max_a\alpha_t^a$ bound. Repeated calls can thus move from an interior state to a pure-state atom and, after the next positive preparation channel, back to a full-dimensional continuously acquired state. The transitions are part of the raw experiment, not a static direct sum of unrelated tasks.

Theorem~\ref{thm:strata} applies with singleton carriers $\{P_j\}$ and the full density-matrix carrier for the continuous type. Substituting $w=1-\vartheta$ and $\rho_*=(1-\vartheta)\rho$ gives
\[
 w^{1+2/r}\rho_*^{-2/r}=(1-\vartheta)\rho^{-2/r}.
\]
The type is read from the report; any type retained across a cut consumes an ordinary label. If $\vartheta$ were allowed to vanish at selected phases, its report support could encode a clock, so that alteration would require a new clock analysis.
\end{proof}

The theorem allows boundary approach within each convex carrier, but not an arbitrary countable accumulation of strata. It also permits nonuniform one-step stability through the block condition. No dimension is assigned to a zero-mass reachable component. For a general singular law outside this finite class, \eqref{eq:masscurvature} remains a proved upper criterion; the matching rate requires additional mass and curvature information.
