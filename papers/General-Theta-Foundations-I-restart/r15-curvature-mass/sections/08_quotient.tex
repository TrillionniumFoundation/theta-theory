\section{Predictive quotients and measurable realization}\label{sec:quotient}
For completeness we state a sufficient measurable construction before discussing its relation to the finite affine carrier. It does not assert that every measurable equivalence relation has a standard Borel quotient. The measurable ingredients below are standard; see also \cite{kechris}.

Fix a phase. Suppose its admitted history space $\mathcal H$ is a countable union of compact metrizable pieces. There is a countable determining family of bounded executable tests, together with countably encoded legal-interface coordinates. After rescaling, their evaluation map is
\[
 E:\mathcal H\longrightarrow[-1,1]^{\mathbb N}.
\]
Assume $E$ is Borel on $\mathcal H$ and continuous on each compact piece, and that equality of these coordinates is exactly equality of all admitted future-test expectations and the legal interface. All raw report kernels and history concatenations are jointly Borel. Tests include first-report events in a countable generating algebra followed by every determining continuation test. This last closure specifies the conditional updating content of test equivalence.

\begin{proposition}[Realized minimal predictive quotient]\label{prop:quotient}
Under these hypotheses, $Q=E(\mathcal H)$ is a standard Borel space, $E$ realizes the predictive quotient, and $E$ has a Borel section. Report kernels and predictive updates descend to jointly Borel kernels on $Q$; update equality is asserted almost surely under the relevant report law. Every standard Borel sufficient state through which all the determining coordinates factor measurably has a unique measurable map to $Q$ on its attained image. No operational information is added by the choice of section.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The image of each compact piece is compact in the countable product. Their union $Q$ is Borel in this Polish product and hence is standard Borel. Equality of coordinates is precisely the defining equivalence, not a finer relation involving unused tests.

Here is an explicit section construction. Select the least compact piece whose image contains $q$. In that piece the fiber $E^{-1}(q)$ is nonempty and compact. Take a countable dense sequence and successively choose the first closed ball of radius $2^{-k}$ meeting the fiber and all previously selected balls. Nonempty intersections are detected by membership in the image under $E$ of a compact set, hence by a closed condition; at each step the countably many previous choices give a Borel partition of $Q$. The nested compact intersections have diameter at most $2^{1-k}$ and define one point of the fiber. Their centers converge to that point, so the selected point is Borel in $q$. This constructs a section $h(q)$.

Equality of first-report tests implies equality of report kernels at equivalent histories by the monotone class theorem. Equality of tests which first require a report in a generating event and then run a determining continuation test implies equality of its conditional continuation expectations almost everywhere under that common report law. The family is countable, so the entire evaluation of the updated history agrees almost everywhere. Define the quotient update by concatenating the report to $h(q)$ and evaluating $E$ at the next phase. Its joint measurability follows from the section and concatenation. For any other representative it gives the same actual update almost surely. Integrating these per-history null sets against any admitted history distribution preserves that assertion; a universal pointwise version on every null report is not claimed. Arbitrary Borel assignments off legal or positive-probability interfaces complete the kernel.

If $T:\mathcal H\to S$ is another sufficient state with each coordinate of $E$ a Borel function of $T$, the product of those coordinate functions gives a Borel map into $[-1,1]^{\mathbb N}$ and takes $T(\mathcal H)$ into $Q$. On that attained image it factors $E$ and is unique because the coordinates separate points of $Q$. This is the asserted minimality. If the attained image of $T$ is not known to be Borel, its trace sigma field is the stated domain; no additional Borel-image assertion is being inferred.
\end{proof}

For a finite affine realization, evaluate a separating finite basis of the invariant test span. All other test coordinates are affine functionals of that evaluation. The positive unnormalized instruments give the explicit report and normalized update maps. The compact convex carrier $K$ may contain mixture states or boundary limits not yet attained from one fixed preparation; it is a global legal extension on which the codebooks are executable. The actual laws $\nu_n^\pi$ and $\lambda_t$ remain the corresponding preparation-and-policy pushforwards. The density or stratum hypotheses, rather than the mere size of $K$, supply the lower bounds.

In the Gaussian realization, histories of a fixed action word with reports restricted to closed bounded boxes form compact pieces, and the positive Bayes formulas are continuous there. Gaussian mixture identifiability gives separation. In the quantum realization the report cube, finite validation alphabet and finite action words give compact pieces directly, and \eqref{eq:quantuminverse} and the separating report effects verify finite affine dimension. These explicit constructions avoid requiring a general quotient theorem for an arbitrary hidden-state or quantum process.
