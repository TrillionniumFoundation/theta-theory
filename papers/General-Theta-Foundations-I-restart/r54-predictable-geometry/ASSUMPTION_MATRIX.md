The main theorem does NOT require an exact finite primary driver or an averaged invariant report row. It also does not assert a universal recursive posterior approximation: calibration provides the declared task stability, and the implemented live labels execute the protocol without retaining the full posterior. Full-state stability and primitive report continuity are verified separately where their numerical consequences are used. This is a stronger conclusion than a fixed-controller comparison but still a restricted, explicitly certified class of causal experiments.
