## Internal theorem-level attribution

R46 and R51 already use conditional-envelope correlated rejection. R52 and R53 supply the marked profile, Gaussian-tail comparison, raw moment-calibration inspiration and finite-driver stopped completion. These remain independent complete sources. R54's claims of addition are the explicit history-dependent effort/navigator transfer (without exact finite primary closure), the task-fiber minimax-radius criterion, semantic physical-command count, and their raw/numerical/same-task consequences. The Gaussian tail exponent, basic rejection method, convexity, projection, positive-map contraction, ordinary stopping potentials and Markovization are not newly discovered here.

## Distinct mathematical questions

A stationary Borel Markovization need not be a finite-state observer. A finite-state observer need not have a finite program description. A complete-task TV simulation does not control expected duration. A matching internal theorem does not establish external novelty or top-four importance. The main paper proves the stated restricted-class results; the larger significance question remains with independent referees.
