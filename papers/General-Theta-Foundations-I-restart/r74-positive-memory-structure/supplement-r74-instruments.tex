\section{The size of a universal physical dictionary}
\label{sec:r74-dictionary-lower}

\begin{proposition}[A matching noise exponent for physical dictionaries]
\label{prop:r74-dictionary-lower}
Suppose $\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_K$ are density matrices and
$\mathcal D_{h,\eta}\subseteq\operatorname{conv}\{\sigma_i\}$.
Put $s=\eta(1-1/h)$. If $0<s<1/2$, then
\[
 K\ge\left(\frac{1-s}{s}\right)^{h-1}.
\]
Together with Lemma~\ref{lem:r74-state-dictionary}, this gives the exact
power $\eta^{-(h-1)}$ as $\eta\downarrow0$ for fixed $h$ in this
dictionary problem. It is not a lower bound for arbitrary hidden classical
realizations of a fixed instrument.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For each unit vector $\psi$, the matrix
$(1-\eta)|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|+\eta I/h$ belongs to the convex hull.
Pairing with $|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|$ shows that some $i$ obeys
$\langle\psi,\sigma_i\psi\rangle\ge1-s$.
If such an $i$ covers any vector at all, its largest eigenvalue is at least
$1-s>1/2$ and is simple. Write $u_i$ for a corresponding unit eigenvector.
All its other eigenvalues sum to at most $s$ and are individually at most
$s$. With $x=|\langle\psi,u_i\rangle|^2$, therefore,
\[
 \langle\psi,\sigma_i\psi\rangle\le x+s(1-x).
\]
The superlevel condition implies $1-x\le s/(1-s)$.
By the cap-measure calculation in Lemma~\ref{lem:r74-state-dictionary},
the invariant measure of the vectors covered by this $i$ is at most
$(s/(1-s))^{h-1}$. The $K$ superlevel sets cover projective space; the
union bound on their measures proves the result.
\end{proof}

This is an elementary estimate for an inner convex approximation using
physical density matrices as its dictionary. The broader approximation
theory, including sharper dependence on $h$, is classical
\cite{AubrunSzarek17}. The hypotheses do not require every abstract stochastic
hidden state to represent a physical quantum state. In particular this
proposition must not be substituted for a lower bound on the minimal
stochastic simulator in Theorem~\ref{thm:r74-instrument}.
