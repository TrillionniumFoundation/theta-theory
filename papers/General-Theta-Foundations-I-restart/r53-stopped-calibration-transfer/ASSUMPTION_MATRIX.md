F1 is a matching finite-protocol profile with actual occupation and paid controller; the gate law adds actual mass/global cover. F2 is the same-task attained alternative and separately certified mean-time numerical transfer. F3 is singular acquired geometry plus p-nonuniform waiting. F4 consists of two raw kernels verifying the same theorem. This is a stated nontrivial sufficient class, not closure of all original G1/G3 questions.
