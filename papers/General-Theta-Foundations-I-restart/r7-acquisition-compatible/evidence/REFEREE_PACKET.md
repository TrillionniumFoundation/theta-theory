## Principal mathematical changes

**General theorem, native Theorem 2.3.** A physical-law block-stability construction is combined with a same-task dominated-continuation-cut converse. At a cut, the actual joint prefix/suffix law must dominate a specified finite prefix measure times a suffix probability. Conditional terminal response functions then define an acquired law in the suffix L2 space. Its M-center quantization error lower-bounds every M-state tuple crossing that cut. The theorem combines this with the terminal checkpoint identity and a relation-preserving executable upper bound. Thus the online lower concerns actual continuation geometry, not only the terminal predictive marginal.

The central relation is `B_T + W_M <= R_on(T,M) <= B_T + (L_f E_M + v)^2`, while `R_cp(T,M) = B_T + q_M(nu_T)`. The widths use the same task and retain the actual submeasure mass. Corollary 2.4 gives a necessary compatibility condition for checkpoint/online matching. Example 2.5 supplies a delayed-query separation using the classical random-access entropy mechanism, which is explicitly credited rather than claimed as a new coding inequality.

**Adaptive acquisition, native Theorem 3.3.** For independent progressive binary-cylinder sensors with arbitrary coordinate- and level-dependent ratios in `[a,1/2]`, every legal adaptive allocation obeys the acquisition lower. A merged-list greedy scale `R_b` also controls physical small balls and finite-state covering. Optimizing the full declared adaptive sensing class gives matching checkpoint and online risk of order `R_min(N-2,floor(log2 M))^2 + delta^2`. The actual acquired law is an explicitly weighted finite adaptive leaf law; it is not replaced by the latent physical product law.

**One common four-coordinate law, native Theorem 3.4.** A designated early erasure interface has exactly attained causal deficiency epsilon to an exact-bit target, with a compulsory completion clock and no early access to the terminal audit. In the single weighted squared task, the optimized source risk is of order `R_min(N-3,floor(log2 M))^2 + delta^2 + epsilon`. This is a theorem for the specified experiment family, not a claim that any deficiency upper allowance produces a risk floor. The shared sign/erasure tools from R4 are acknowledged as antecedents.

**Finite implementation, native Theorem 3.5.** A certified finite schedule and dyadic coefficient table yield an actual binary/idle-input implementation. Retained data, scratch, read-only program, phase, precision, raw calls and bit-operation time are accounted separately. Numerical root error is `(K+1)2^(-p)`. A stored clock multiplies total persistent cardinality; the law does not silently identify M labels with total bit complexity. The construction proves a feasible computational upper region, not optimal scratch/program/time lower bounds.

**Predictive quotient.** Proposition 1.1 uses the closed linear span of the determining future tests and interface coordinates, so the evaluation fibers are exactly predictive equivalence classes. Joint action measurability is assumed and descended through a constructed Borel section; positive-density and null-report domains are separated. This avoids an arbitrary finer partition masquerading as the minimal predictive quotient.
