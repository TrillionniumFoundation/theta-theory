# Scope and unresolved mathematics

## Established within explicit hypotheses

- A measurable predictive realization certificate for continuous instruments, and direct raw realizations in the examples.
- A general actual-joint-law dominated continuation converse in the same task, integrated with the retained constructive block theorem.
- A necessary compatibility condition for checkpoint/online matching, with a classical delayed-query separation witness.
- Matching optimized adaptive acquisition and finite data-state curves for nonhomogeneous anisotropic progressive binary sensors, including smooth/singular mixtures.
- A single-task calibration/erasure profile attaining its complete deficiency term and an explicit finite-bit feasible implementation region.
- Matching ordinary categorical filtering excess for primitive sparse Gaussian experiments under the same-controller convention.
- Retention and rebuilding of the full nonreset singular, stopped, grid, calibration, typed morphism and tag mathematics of R6.

## Genuine unclosed issues

The dominated cut widths are not shown sufficient for recursive realization. There is no general construction of a globally relation-preserving cover from arbitrary local acquired geometry. Unknown-kernel learning, nonproduct progressive sources and arbitrary action-design optimization are outside the adaptive theorem. The natural metastable/nonuniform filter frontier beyond the displayed primitive class is not closed. Workspace, read-only program and computation time have feasible upper bounds, not a complete optimal region. Stored clock and minimal simulator state are not universally characterized. There is no unrestricted stopping, infinite-horizon transcript or LDP equivalence theorem.

These are not described as impossibility results and do not change the General Theta mother problem. They are explicit limits of what the written proofs establish. Top-four-journal significance and external correctness remain for independent referee judgment.

## Historical and build limits
