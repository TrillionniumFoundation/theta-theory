# Pipeline derivation

## From the original mother problem

1. Specify preparation, normalized causal kernels, action graph, visible costs, terminal task and legal finite stopping. Do not posit a manifold or Fisher matrix.
2. Form future-test equivalence with the continuation interface. Use a compact continuous-instrument quotient certificate or directly verify a standard Borel realization from the raw kernel.
3. Fix actual exploration where the fixed-experiment theorem is used. Form P=E[Y|history] and its acquired law. For a dominated continuation cut, form the actual joint prefix/suffix law, a product submeasure, and the conditional response-function image in L2 of the suffix measure.
4. Projection identifies checkpoint distortion. The same projection plus joint domination bounds every finite alphabet crossing an earlier cut, with no new task inserted. This yields a necessary online/checkpoint compatibility test.
5. For the upper, construct a static finite cover preserving a budget-independent relation. Prove physical-law block pair contraction and relation-robust candidate envelopes. Telescope local errors with later gains; no exact state or report block is retained.
6. For acquisition design, do not optimize a fixed-law theorem by relabeling its law. In the progressive class, prove unread-tail fairness under every adaptive policy, derive the per-history variance lower, and solve the deterministic scale allocation by merged decreasing lists.
7. Use the same greedy scale to construct a finite cylinder cover and a physical-oracle small-ball lower. Combine acquisition and memory by their maximum; the monotone common scale converts it into R_min(n,log M)^2. Construct an exact finite-prefix online machine achieving it.
8. Add calibration and a real erasure interface within one weighted squared task. Prove the parameter-uniform completion-constrained deficiency and lower, not merely an allowed epsilon.
9. Supply the coefficient/schedule description and a finite-bit implementation; price scratch, output precision, phase, all calls and time. Choose p so that its explicit Gamma is at the geometric order when desired.
10. Verify the block route from primitive sparse Gaussian kernels and from the retained completed nonreset singular kernel. Do not use their conclusions backward to prove the general theorem.
11. Transfer to another experiment only through causal report transformation, legal strategy lift, compatible stopping clock and common scored outcome, charging all retained registers. Compose root errors before squaring and complete TV afterward.

## Audit conclusion

The new proof route strengthens the general converse and closes adaptive acquisition/state matching for a substantial explicitly defined multiscale class. It does not close universal recursive attainability, arbitrary adaptive experimental design or the optimal full computational resource region. The construction/packing argument is mathematical; repository preservation, regression and PDF reproducibility are evidence of delivery, not mathematical breakthroughs.
