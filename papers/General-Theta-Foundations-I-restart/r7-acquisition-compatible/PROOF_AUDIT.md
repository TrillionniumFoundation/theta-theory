The full written arguments were checked for these specific failure modes before the build. The audit is not a claim of an independent external proof certificate.

**Quotient exactness and jointness.** The evaluation space is the closed linear span of the determining future tests and interface coordinates, not an arbitrary larger space; both directions of the equivalence are proved. Actionwise continuity is not substituted for joint measurability. The proposition explicitly assumes common action--report Borel versions and constructs a compact-fiber Borel section by nested finite closed covers to descend them jointly.

**Cut lower.** Domination is an inequality of the actual joint prefix/suffix measure, not a product assumption about the physical experiment. The conditional mean is the same terminal task; null-version changes vanish under the dominated product. Shared randomization is conditioned only when independent of the fixed exploration. The decoder receives at most its full counted cut tuple and the suffix.

**General upper.** Static relation is independent of M. All adapted relation-preserving candidates are in the drift quantifier. Local insertions receive all later pathwise gains. The exact shadow is not retained. Finite public fibers are specified; a data-dependent phase is charged in the lower.

**Optimized acquisition.** Random-depth prefix uniformity would be false and is not used. Unread-tail independence is conditional on the complete adaptive history. Greedy removes the largest remaining entries of monotone scale lists. At the chosen greedy partition, each active last child has width at least a times the current maximal width, which is the needed global small-ball estimate even at touching cylinders. Oracle physical-law quantization and actual acquired atomic quantization remain separate.

**Joint profile.** The sign/bit minimax lower uses one prior and one scored task. The three-state erasure construction is an upper overhead, not asserted minimal. Its virtual bit completion occurs before any permitted future audit. No generic defect allowance is called an error floor.

**Digital implementation.** The finite schedule and dyadic coefficients are supplied counted data; no exact comparison of arbitrary computable reals or unknown-kernel calibration algorithm is presumed. Pre-decision inputs are finite; the scored audit is strictly after output. Read-only data, scratch, padded phase and total state are not folded into K data bits. The bit-complexity region is feasible, not optimal.

**Gaussian realization.** Positivity comes from a primitive product, not one-step overlap. Missingness is an informative report. Good-block probability uses conditional iteration. Relation-induced component floors survive every rounding. The last action is fixed before the report used in the Jacobian. The submeasure density integrates with its actual mass; the terminal task is ordinary categorical squared loss.

**Stopping/composition.** A legal virtual continuation is required for the entered-block maximum argument but not physically executed after stopping. The baseline is B_tau, including tau=0. Preparation discrepancy appears in TV. Reverse comparison retains the target baseline and inflated target state budget.
