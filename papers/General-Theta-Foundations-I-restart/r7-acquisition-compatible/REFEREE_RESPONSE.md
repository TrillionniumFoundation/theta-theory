# Response to the R6 external top-four-journal report

We retain the General Theta mother problem and title. The report did not identify a fatal proof error in R6; its principal challenge was whether the paper had an intrinsic obstruction or a broad optimized acquisition result beyond a strong sufficient filter construction. The revision responds mathematically rather than changing the title to a specialized filtering paper. No acceptance recommendation is presumed.

## Major requests

1. **Exact novelty and scope.** Native Theorem `thm:transfer` combines the retained constructive block certificate with the dominated-continuation-cut lower. Lemma `lem:cut` uses a subproduct minorization of the actual prefix/suffix law and conditional response functions for the very same terminal task. Corollary `cor:necessary` is an explicit necessary compatibility condition; it is not a necessary-and-sufficient characterization. Exact tag separation, random-access entropy, erasure testing and standard quantization are identified as inherited or classical.

2. **Closest literature.** Section `sec:literature` now includes reachable-belief covering, heuristic planning, adaptive belief discretization, offline belief-metric coverage, finite-history policy learning and finite-state-controller synthesis/extraction, including published 2025/2026 work. Zhang--Hsu--Lee Theorem 1 is compared at theorem level. Existing AIS/filter-control and zero-delay coding comparisons are retained. No claim of exhaustive priority clearance is made.

3. **Conceptual status of the relation.** Relation `eq:filterrelation` is manufactured from positive-cone component domination and spread control, independent of M. The proof explicitly quantifies over all adapted admissible perturbations. Proposition `prop:repair` permits flagged probabilistic implementation violations while retaining an unconditional repaired reference. This does not prove that local covers always extend globally, or that every realizable geometry has such a relation.

4. **Standard task.** Theorem `thm:brier` gives matching excess for the ordinary categorical squared filtering loss, with full-history Brier baseline. The last-action-before-report Jacobian and actual positive submeasure are proved explicitly. The indexed probe results are not deleted; the complete R6 article remains in the companion.

5. **Finite-bit implementation.** Theorem `thm:digital` closes a concrete feasible implementation region for the new progressive-sensor class: binary/idle pre-decision alphabet, K retained data bits, separately charged deterministic phase, coefficient/schedule program, scratch, output precision and bit-operation time. Arbitrary real coefficients are not claimed computable. Gaussian clipping now has an explicit U(T,M,zeta) formula and a relation-preserving interval compander; an optimal full Gaussian bit-resource region is not claimed.

6. **Uniformity and interfaces.** Section 1 names finite measurable public interface fibers. Block assumptions quantify over true/candidate pairs and physical strategies; Gaussian actions are finite, support primitive, dimension and all parameter floors fixed. Numerical u is allowed only when the relation survives. The initial posterior is the specified uniform seed. Time/controller registers are not silently free.

7. **Stopping.** Proposition `prop:stopped` defines the actual stopped task, filtration and baseline, includes tau=0, specifies a legal virtual continuation for estimates, counts the clock at the terminal cut and gives the E ceil(tau/m) consequence with explicit constants. No deterministic-time bound is substituted at a stopping time.

8. **Morphisms.** Section 5 states causal realization, action compatibility, preparation, source completion stopping times, common scored outcome, class closure and resource tuples. Theorem `thm:joint` verifies a concrete parameter-uniform source/target pair attaining the linear defect in the same task. A reverse bound uses the target baseline unchanged.

9. **Foundations scope.** The general theorem comes before consequences. Gaussian, nonreset singular and progressive-sensor results verify or extend distinct parts; none is used backward to prove the foundational cut or block theorem. Ordered-measurement, streaming-space and action papers remain independent.

10. **Provenance.** Canonical first parent, pinned latest report, exact old subtrees, native sources, companion builds, manifests and final read-only receipts are separate. Historical coverage is described literally, not as a claim to have re-certified all v1--v96 proofs.

## Technical requests

- Positive report domain D_a is open in the relative product topology; a common Borel null-report extension is extra data.
- A post-encoding probe index selects a response vector/function, not a separately pre-encoded scalar; Example `ex:query` distinguishes those cuts.
- Candidate fibers and all data-dependent public registers are named and charged.
- The Gaussian block probability p0^m is proved by conditional iteration, not independence.
- The exponential interpolation inequality is displayed; the softmax determinant is derived by the determinant lemma.
- Actual good-block mass is retained in the small-ball lower.
- Preparation discrepancy epsilon_0 appears in complete stopped-law comparison.
- The old singular relation is the full completed candidate domain with w=1, not a vacuous trajectory-specific declaration.
- Morphism overheads are upper accounts unless a real continuation challenge or reverse certificate proves necessity.
- Defect allowances are not universal risk floors. The exact erasure pair is explicitly distinguished.
- D_H denotes posterior spread; C_ctr is controller cardinality, avoiding the old overloaded D.

## What remains genuinely unsettled

The revision proves a dominated-continuation obstruction and a fully optimized adaptive allocation law for an infinite class of nonhomogeneous product sensors. It does not prove that the obstruction is sufficient for all causal kernels, that every useful acquired quotient admits a robust block relation, or that its scratch/program/time region is optimal. Its adaptive result does not cover arbitrary intervention design, nonproduct unknown kernels or unrestricted infinite-horizon stopping. Those limitations are mathematical, not a replacement of the mother problem by a survey or a no-go claim.
