# Counterexamples and forbidden shortcuts

1. **Terminal atomicity does not determine online memory.** Delayed random index: terminal checkpoint has two answers; the earlier response-function cut is a d-bit cube. Example ex:query gives a positive entropy lower.
2. **Adaptive depth is not an unbiased sampling condition.** Read a first bit and continue only when it is zero. Conditional on stopping at depth one, the prefix is biased. Only unread-tail fairness conditional on the complete history is used.
3. **Actual atomic law is not the latent Cantor law.** Finite digit reads produce finitely many posterior centers, even when the physical law is continuous or singular. Policy identity eq:policyspecific retains both uncertainty and acquired quantization.
4. **Unweighted allocation ignores anisotropy.** Greedy selection must use sqrt(weight) times actual remaining scale; ambient coordinate count is insufficient.
5. **Local error size does not preserve a relation.** A small perturbation that violates component domination may destroy positivity accumulated through a sparse block. The numerical theorem requires the relation or pays a flagged-failure term.
6. **Root errors add before squaring.** Replacing (a+b)^2 by a^2+b^2 is not a general upper. Complete-law TV is instead added after bounded-loss comparison.
7. **Defect/calibration allowances are not lower floors.** An exact identity experiment also satisfies every larger upper allowance. The joint law uses an actually inaccessible sign and a physical erasure channel.
8. **Future audit leakage invalidates deficiency.** A simulator allowed to inspect z's terminal audit before producing its virtual bit would destroy the stated erasure lower. The completion clock forbids this.
9. **Free data-dependent clocks defeat label lower bounds.** Such a clock is another retained register; deterministic padded time is not the same thing.
10. **A block shadow is not a free persistent posterior.** It appears only in the proof; the implemented recursion retains one finite representative and its charged interface.
11. **Endogenous missingness is not an independent hold.** In the Gaussian class the missing symbol carries likelihood information and updates the posterior.
12. **Zero acquired mass does not imply a positive geometric lower.** Every small-ball witness retains its actual submeasure mass.
13. **Same-controller coupling is not optimized control.** No unrestricted control theorem follows by changing the controller after establishing a physical-law bound.
14. **Finite stopping is not infinite-path or LDP equivalence.** The stopped sum and clock remain explicit.
15. **Known real constants do not imply a finite program.** The digital theorem explicitly supplies a finite certified schedule and coefficient table.
16. **Passing all dominated cuts is not a proved sufficiency theorem.** An absent minorization may simply make this converse inconclusive.

Finite regression includes concrete witnesses for several items. The mathematical restrictions above are part of the theorem statements, not consequences of those finite tests.
