# Notation and cut audit

- X_t is a raw hidden state; X in Section 3 is the fixed prepared coordinate vector. Y_t is a report; Y or Y^tau is the separately named terminal scored task.
- S_t is a predictive realization, not an assumed parameter manifold. v is a named public interface fiber; output-error v in the transfer bound is local to that theorem and explicitly scalar. No data-dependent fiber is free.
- P_T is the full-history conditional mean; nu_T is its actual law. underline-mu is a finite prefix submeasure and xi_s its response-function image, with mass retained.
- q_M is defined for finite measures on separable Hilbert spaces. It includes actual atomic laws and unnormalized continuation witnesses.
- M denotes alphabet cardinality, not bits. log2 M gives retained data bits only. Q, R, C_ctr, W and L_prog are separate coordinates.
- D_H is posterior spread, d_H projective distance, and A is the admissibility relation. Controller size is C_ctr, not D.
- In the progressive section omega_j are task weights, r_jl contraction ratios, ell_jk remaining cylinder lengths, b_jl digit coefficients, R_b the merged-list scale, and b_M=floor(log2 M). They are not a preassigned covariance spectrum.
- delta is a fixed inaccessible task offset in the specified robust family; u is a certified local metric numerical error; p is finite coefficient/output precision; epsilon is a complete common-task defect.
- E_M is a root metric error. B_T and B_tau are risk baselines. Squaring and adding TV in the wrong order is forbidden.
- sigma=ceil(tau/m) in the stopping proposition is unrelated to Gaussian standard deviation sigma in Section 4; contexts are disjoint and both are explicitly defined.
