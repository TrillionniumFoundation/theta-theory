# Notation audit

- N is the real expected acquisition budget. It is not a deterministic horizon or uncharged clock.
- M counts all live and terminal states. k=M-1 counts the maximal live phases only in the singular frontier.
- delta0 is preparation base-norm error; eta is joint one-call induced base-norm error; alpha is terminal cost error; zeta=delta0+N eta; c_N=1-1/N. These are not all the same quantity as an external causal deficiency epsilon or a squared calibration offset delta^2.
- H_N,J_N,Delta_N are survival mean, replacement probability and budget margin. Temporary n=floor N, b=N-n, q=1-rho in that subsection have explicitly local meanings.
- In the singular experiment p is raw success probability, q=1-p, b is actual trapped preparation mass; h is raw mutation probability. They are fixed before the program is optimized.
- T0 is the absorption-call count under the all-failure report sequence, m its mean; tau is the actual stopped acquisition under the prepared experiment. b E T0 is actual occupation. F is a failure risk, not the full squared prediction risk.
- g_{k,p}(m) is the discrete phase frontier. W and the CTMC clock are proof-only variables, not executable resources.
- Phi is the implemented general occupation value; Q_m^nu is the inherited acquired mass-constrained geometric profile. Their equalities require their own explicitly stated hypotheses.
- Numerical theorem numbers are generated by LaTeX; source labels are the stable audit identifiers. All references, citations, labels and TeX inputs are checked mechanically, independently of proof correctness.
