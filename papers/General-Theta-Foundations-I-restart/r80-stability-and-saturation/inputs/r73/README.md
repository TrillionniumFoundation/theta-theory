# General Theta Foundations I — R73 author revision

**General Theta Foundations I: Acquired Geometry and Causal Resource Transfer**  
Qian Qi

This is the complete current author source for a further revision of the
existing paper. The central development is a permutation normal form for every
finite stochastic realization of a compactly reversible response, with the
initialization cost retained. It gives an exact stochastic-memory variational
principle, sharp group and rank consequences, arbitrary-dimensional
two-command families, a bounded-decoder obstruction, exact algebraic synthesis,
and finite certificates with a stable-memory application. The original causal,
predictive, finite-resource, quantum-measurement, reset, discrimination,
variational, spectral and geometric results remain preserved with their own
hypotheses and proof identities.
