## The mathematical advance and its limits

**The complete cost frontier replaces an optimum-only question by an all-budget theorem.** The inherited R77 cover theorem gave the exact memory required to attain the unrestricted optimum under positive exposure, and its `D_M` gave a first-residual lower certificate. The complete value `V_M` is then an attained minimum over M probability rows, a finite selector, and only s bounded scalar continuation potentials. A Bellman supersolution together with a Hermitian initial-cost inequality is necessary and sufficient for every budget up to an explicit one-label baseline. Failure effects may be positive semidefinite, optimal actions may be tied, and costs and failure probabilities may depend on the state. Exact algebraic data give exact finite synthesis. This solves the full constrained reset problem as a variational classification, not as a universal closed elementary formula or a polynomial-time algorithm.

**Exact formulas and matching resource bounds.** For every finite action-cover incidence system in the retained rational physical family, the new theorem solves the whole frontier by a finite formula. `V_M = 4 + 1/n + (n-1) max(n-M+1,0)/n^2`.

There are n consecutive strict improvements, and the full excess cost equals `2 D_M` in this family. Thus the old valid certificate is neither discarded nor silently reclassified as an exact value. Its mean costs remain between I and 4I; the demonstrated actual charge support is {1,9}, while actual costs in [1,4] belong to the unperturbed family. The exact closed law is not asserted for perturbed data.

**Priority and independent questions.** Bellman theory, fixed-size stochastic-controller nonlinear programs, real-algebraic elimination and covering combinatorics are classical. Amato–Bernstein–Zilberstein's original fixed-size NLP is explicitly cited and compared. The new claimed structure is removal of stochastic update variables with the same counted rows, the s-dimensional compact all-state certificate in the undiscounted positive-cost problem, and the exact family-wide resource law. The original squared-sign risk frontier, matching unrestricted qubit approximation rates, arbitrary nonreset or optional-stopping control, independent timing and A1/A2 objectives remain distinct.
