## Current additions and their proper classification

`sections/54-robust-instrument-frontier.tex` organizes arbitrary finite-dimensional failure instruments, positive costs, legal feedback and a fixed unknown compact model family. The reachability, fallback, cost-witness and attainment arguments are inherited foundations restated with complete proofs. The effective extension treats compact semialgebraic families with common controller variables and model-dependent reachable-support witnesses. It does not assume properness at inaccessible labels or a uniform bound on unregularized witnesses.

`sections/55-memory-regularization.tex` supplies the main new chain. An occasional return to the existing initial label changes only permitted controller updates, preserves M, and leaves the physical state untouched. Explicit initial/global cost bounds yield a perturbation estimate uniform over all finite label budgets. Stronger estimates under a stated uniform success floor have stronger hypotheses and use the classical resolvent mechanism. Finite-prefix approximation reuses the earlier completion method; its transfer of a uniform-in-M stability bound is a consequence of the new estimate.

`sections/56-exact-hidden-model.tex` proves the exact two-model frontier with values `2`, `(1+sqrt(5))/2`, and `3/2` from three labels onward, including its lower bounds and implementing controllers. This is a model-specific exact solution. The separate support-degeneration and positive-probability/nonreset examples in Section 54 demonstrate why the larger theorem's quantifiers matter; they do not replace the general result.

The current introduction, main input order, supplement wrapper, bibliography, response, proof audit, research status, dependency map, reading entry, verification and source records are refreshed. ## Preservation and scope are distinct

The current results prove nonreset mandatory-success control statements with a positive cost floor, a common controller for fixed models, and all physical initial states. They do not settle the separate general-M squared-sign minimax-risk problem, optional commitment, independent timing, the unrestricted-decoder asymptotic gap, or independent A1/A2 and larger Theta objectives. Local geometric results remain local; exact formulas retain their specified families. Archived open problems are not silently marked closed because the current center changed.

The branch is intended to add only this independent R79 source directory and, in a later publication commit, its sibling publication directory. Existing R78 and older papers, reports, branches and unrelated paper folders are preserved. Exact committed Git diffs and remote readback, recorded during final publication, establish the actual isolation; this document does not claim a future push has already succeeded.
