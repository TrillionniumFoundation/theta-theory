## Active statement-level map

### 1. Attainment of the module bound at small contrast

**Label:** `thm:r74-low-contrast`. **Source:** `sections/37-contrast-memory.tex` (main).

**Hypotheses.** Nonconstant compactly reversible finite-dimensional probability response with finite minimal observable group G; Arbitrary finite terminal outcome set; pi is the group average and b_t=pi+t(b-pi), 0<=t<=1; outputs with pi_c=0 are identically zero and may be restored as zero decoder columns.

**Conclusion.** For an explicit t0>0, kappa_G(b_t)=nu_G(V_b) and m(b_t)=min{N,1+nu_G(V_b)} for every 0<t<=t0. The observable group, module and residual count are unchanged for all t>0. At t=0 memory is one.

**Direct dependencies.** `eq:r73-module-bounds`, `thm:r73-memory-formula`.

**Proof chain.** Separate the invariant constant response from its zero-average module W. Embed W equivariantly into a minimum permutation module, project back using its invariant inner product, perturb a uniform preparation, and scale the decoder. epsilon=1/(2k||a||infty), B=max|T_xc|/pi_c and t0=min{1,epsilon/B} ensure positivity. An invertible equivariant residual map proves the unchanged invariants and matching lower.

**Classical ingredients and actual increment.** Averaging and invariant inner products for finite group representations; Positive factorization under mixing. Exact permutation-module saturation with one common command action and the inherited pure-start charge; not the invention of equivariance or mixing-based positivity. Citation keys: `GPT13`, `KPT10`, `BocciCarliniRapallo11`.

**Scope boundary.** Terminal readout contrast is not channel damping; t=0 is a separate degenerate observable quotient. No A1/A2 or general Theta completion follows.

### 2. Attained exact contrast thresholds

**Label:** `thm:r74-contrast-thresholds`. **Source:** `sections/37-contrast-memory.tex` (main).

**Hypotheses.** The finite-G contrast family and minimal observable group of thm:r74-low-contrast; Integer budget k>=1; Algebraic or rational data only for the respective effective arithmetic assertions.

**Conclusion.** The feasible contrasts for kappa<=k are exactly [0,tau_k], with attained endpoints, monotone budget thresholds, tau_N=1, tau_k=0 below nu and tau_nu>0. Only finitely many positive-contrast memory transitions occur. Algebraic data give computable algebraic endpoints; Dedekind groups give finitely many LPs and rational endpoints/controllers for rational data.

**Direct dependencies.** `thm:r74-low-contrast`, `thm:r73-memory-formula`, `sec:r73-algebraic`, `thm:r74-quotient-lp`, `thm:r74-quotient-dual`.

**Proof chain.** For each of finitely many actions of size at most k, probability parameters form a compact set with closed polynomial response constraints. Decoder mixing fills the initial interval and compactness retains its endpoint. The remaining arithmetic assertion is classical real QE, or the exact normal-subgroup LP reduction.

**Classical ingredients and actual increment.** Compactness; Convex mixing; Real quantifier elimination; Vertex arithmetic for linear programs. Structure of the exact resource thresholds for this group-response invariant. Citation keys: `Seidenberg54`, `BocciCarliniRapallo11`.

**Scope boundary.** No efficient general algorithm or executed general QE solve is asserted. Rationality of endpoints is not claimed for arbitrary nonabelian groups.

### 3. An exact qubit readout threshold

**Label:** `prop:r74-s3-contrast`. **Source:** `sections/37-contrast-memory.tex` (main).

**Hypotheses.** The retained prepared-qubit S3 response with u=(1,-1,0); beta_t(g)=1/2+(t/4)uP_gu^T and binary bounded decoder; 0<t<=1; prescribed pure start for m.

**Conclusion.** d=3 and N=6 throughout positive contrast. Core size is 3 up to and including t=2/3 and 6 above it; exact pure-start size is respectively 4 and 6. At zero contrast memory is one.

**Direct dependencies.** `prop:r73-decoder-obstruction`, `thm:r74-low-contrast`, `thm:r73-memory-formula`.

**Proof chain.** Every faithful S3-set of size<=5 has one natural orbit and only trivial/sign extras. Remove those coefficient components; four displayed matrices span End(R^2), forcing preparation and decoder contrasts parallel to u. Probability bounds imply t<=2/3. Explicit three-point decoder and the six-residual machine match both intervals.

**Classical ingredients and actual increment.** Elementary S3 representation and orbit structure; Rank-one bilinear functional equality. New exact contrast law for the retained model, not a new claim for its already established full-contrast obstruction.

**Scope boundary.** Special qubit/S3 family, not a universal nonabelian closed formula.

### 4. Absorption of within-orbit preparation

**Label:** `lem:r74-normal-orbits`. **Source:** `sections/38-quotient-memory.tex` (main).

**Hypotheses.** Finite G-set probability factorization b(g)=lambda P_gD; Every orbit stabilizer in this factorization is normal.

**Conclusion.** Without increasing size, preparation is supported at the identity coset of each quotient and there is at most one orbit of each subgroup type. Only a mixture across different quotient orbits remains.

**Direct dependencies.** No other numbered statement; its elementary proof is included.

**Proof chain.** Normality makes the local mixed-orbit response H-invariant, hence itself a legal quotient decoder. Merge repeated quotient types using their orbit masses and delete zero-mass orbits.

**Classical ingredients and actual increment.** Normal quotient group action; Convex mixture of probability rows. Size-preserving elimination of the within-orbit bilinearity in this resource problem. Citation keys: `GPT13`, `KPT10`.

**Scope boundary.** The lemma is not asserted for nonnormal stabilizers. A finite S3 negative control explicitly demonstrates the failed invariance in that case.

### 5. Exact quotient linear programs

**Label:** `thm:r74-quotient-lp`. **Source:** `sections/38-quotient-memory.tex` (main).

**Hypotheses.** Finite minimal observable Dedekind group G: every subgroup is normal; Arbitrary response probability table on G and finite output set; Coefficient field assumptions only for rational/algebraic realization claims.

**Conclusion.** kappa is the minimum sum of subgroup indices over distinct nonempty subgroup families satisfying the displayed nonnegative quotient-table linear equations. N=|G|, and m=min{|G|,1+kappa}. Rational tables have rational optimal controllers; data in a real algebraic number field admit optimal controllers in the same field.

**Direct dependencies.** `lem:r74-normal-orbits`, `thm:r73-memory-formula`.

**Proof chain.** Absorption proves necessity; quotient-point preparations and f_H/p_H decoders prove sufficiency. A normal initial-residual stabilizer fixes the whole observable span and is trivial. Every feasible bounded polyhedron has a vertex over its coefficient field.

**Classical ingredients and actual increment.** Orbit-stabilizer; Linear feasibility and vertex arithmetic. Exact linear reduction for normal quotient orbits with unchanged label resource. Citation keys: `GPT13`, `KPT10`, `GillisGlineur12`.

**Scope boundary.** Subgroup-family selection is finite but not claimed polynomial; arbitrary nonnormal G actions remain bilinear.

### 6. Payoff certificates and exact contrast endpoints

**Label:** `thm:r74-quotient-dual`. **Source:** `sections/38-quotient-memory.tex` (main).

**Hypotheses.** A nonempty distinct subgroup family in the finite Dedekind setting; Payoff pairing is the unnormalized sum over G times the finite output set; The endpoint formula uses nonconstant v=b-pi and pi equal to the group average.

**Conclusion.** Feasibility is equivalent to all displayed payoff support inequalities. Infeasibility has a strict rational witness and a norm-controlled perturbation margin. The exact contrast endpoint equals the attained primal LP and the displayed normalized support-function dual; rational input gives rational endpoints and controllers.

**Direct dependencies.** `thm:r74-quotient-lp`, `eq:r74-contrast`.

**Proof chain.** The feasible set is the convex hull of quotient probability polytopes. Compute its support function cosetwise. Closest-point separation supplies a strict witness, rational approximation preserves the gap, and the l1 bound controls perturbations. Affine-hull equations or a reached facet prove dual attainment even without strict feasibility.

**Classical ingredients and actual increment.** Support functions; Finite-dimensional convex separation; Polyhedral duality; LP vertex arithmetic. An explicit exact dual for the quotient memory optimization and its resource thresholds. Citation keys: `GPT13`.

**Scope boundary.** This is not a general black-box Farkas proof for arbitrary nonabelian factorizations; no general solver run is claimed.

### 7. The abelian module cover

**Label:** `cor:r74-abelian-cover`. **Source:** `sections/38-quotient-memory.tex` (main).

**Hypotheses.** Finite abelian observable G; Active complex character set consists of the nonzero vector Fourier coefficients of b, including the trivial mode and closed under conjugation.

**Conclusion.** nu is the least sum of subgroup indices whose annihilators cover all active characters. This equals kappa at every sufficiently small positive contrast, but can fail at full contrast.

**Direct dependencies.** `thm:r74-low-contrast`.

**Proof chain.** The cyclic abelian module has each active complex character once; quotient permutation modules have precisely the characters trivial on their stabilizer. Real conjugate pairs are counted as their two-dimensional real summands.

**Classical ingredients and actual increment.** Finite Fourier decomposition; Characters of quotient permutation modules. Classical representation cover identified with the exact low-contrast stochastic resource.

**Scope boundary.** No claim that the character-cover computation or abelian representation decomposition itself is new.

### 8. A sharp transition invisible to the observable module

**Label:** `prop:r74-circuit-transition`. **Source:** `sections/38-quotient-memory.tex` (main).

**Hypotheses.** G=(F2)^4 with coordinate generator commands; chi_5=chi_1 chi_2 chi_3 chi_4 and beta_t=3/8+(t/8)sum_{i=1}^5 chi_i; Binary response, 0<t<=1, bounded probability decoder.

**Conclusion.** The fixed invariants are d=6, N=16, nu=10. kappa is 10 for 0<t<=3/5 and 12 for 3/5<t<=1; pure-start memory is respectively 11 and 13. At zero contrast it is one.

**Direct dependencies.** `thm:r74-quotient-lp`, `thm:r74-quotient-dual`, `cor:r74-abelian-cover`, `thm:r73-memory-formula`.

**Proof chain.** Any four of the five characters are independent. All actions of budget<=11 must partition the active modes into singleton/pair quotient blocks. The explicit witness z=1-sum chi_i+sum_pair chi_i chi_j has nonnegative quotient averages but target pairing (3-5t)/8. Five binary quotient decoders and a four-plus-eight point indicator/majority construction match both regions.

**Classical ingredients and actual increment.** Fourier orthogonality; Linear dual certificates. New sharp positivity threshold with matching construction and unchanged observable invariants.

**Scope boundary.** Special sixteen-point group family; its physical realization is already classical, so no quantum advantage is claimed.

### 9. A robust finite-table obstruction

**Label:** `cor:r74-circuit-robust`. **Source:** `sections/38-quotient-memory.tex` (main).

**Hypotheses.** 3/5<t<=1; A binary probability table on the same G=(F2)^4 and the same command quotient; Sup norm distance from beta_t is strictly less than (5t-3)/18.

**Conclusion.** The perturbed table still requires kappa>=12 and prescribed-start memory>=13.

**Direct dependencies.** `prop:r74-circuit-transition`, `thm:r74-quotient-lp`, `thm:r74-quotient-dual`, `thm:r73-memory-formula`.

**Proof chain.** The perturbation is smaller than t/8, retaining all five active modes, faithfulness and N=16. All small actions have the same partition type. Their witnesses have normalized l1 norm at most 9/4, so the exact negative margin survives.

**Classical ingredients and actual increment.** Fourier coefficient stability; Dual-norm perturbation estimate. Quantified lower certificate from the exact circuit dual witnesses. Citation keys: `BocciCarliniRapallo11`.

**Scope boundary.** Sufficient radius only; not a sharp neighbourhood or a matching upper for arbitrary perturbed tables. No statement about perturbing the command dynamics outside this fixed quotient.

### 10. A common contracting realization

**Label:** `thm:r74-contraction`. **Source:** `sections/40-joint-contraction.tex` (main).

**Hypotheses.** Finite nonempty command and output sets; linear response b(w,c)=p_c+ell_c(x_w), x_{wa}=F_a x_w; All actual word responses are probabilities; p is a probability row and sum_c ell_c=0; A supplied common norm, 0<rho<1 and r>0 obey ||F_a x||<=rho||x|| and r||ell_c||_*<=p_c; Choose T>=1 with rho^(T-1)||x0||<=r.

**Conclusion.** One bounded-decoder stochastic machine from a prescribed pure start realizes every mixed command word on at most sum_{j=0}^{T-1}|A|^j+ceil((1+2/(1-rho))^n) labels. A supplied uniform exponentially decaying product bound implies such a common norm. A constant response needs one label.

**Direct dependencies.** No other numbered statement; its elementary proof is included.

**Proof chain.** A maximal norm-net produces rho B subset conv(V) subset B, making r conv(V) invariant for every command. Prefix-tree transient labels enter this common polytope at depth T. Barycentric transition rows preserve represented vectors and all legal decoder rows. The product-bound norm is an explicitly bounded supremum over words.

**Classical ingredients and actual increment.** Invariant polytope realization; Volumetric net bound; Common contraction norms. A quantified constructive consequence of the classical invariant-polytope mechanism: arbitrary finite mixed-command linear responses, a supplied norm and explicit transient/core counts. It is not a new positive-realization principle. Citation keys: `Heller65`, `MonrasWinter14`.

**Scope boundary.** Sufficient certificate and explicit upper only; no minimum, efficient net synthesis, joint spectral-radius decision procedure, or finite exact-sampling complexity assertion. The proof is a quantified corollary of the established invariant-polytope framework; no broad novelty claim is attached to existence alone.

**Comparison with retained results.**

- `thm:r67-polytope`: General anchored invariant-response-polytope criterion; the new proof explicitly constructs such a polytope from its norm certificate.
- `prop:contractive-boundary`: Only eventual constancy of the optimal decision under a strict limiting task gap; not exact preservation of every response probability.
- `thm:r70-background`: A specific unary leakage/background family and its sharper model-specific count.
- `thm:r68-instrument`: Physical CP maps with a posterior floor, not arbitrary abstract linear commands with only a legal decoder ball.

### 11. Exact memory in a uniformly positive one-dependent family

**Label:** `thm:r74-depolarizing-obstruction`. **Source:** `sections/41-conditional-memory.tex` (main).

**Hypotheses.** The three displayed qubit branches Psi0=Ad(Utheta)/8, Psi1=tr(EX)tau/4 and Psi2=D-Psi0-Psi1; E=diag(3/4,1/4), tau=|0><0|, D(X)=tr(X)I/2; sin(theta)!=0 and known initial state I/2; All classical candidate models use arbitrary nonnegative outcome matrices whose sum is row-stochastic.

**Conclusion.** The instrument is CP with uniformly positive outcome probabilities and exactly depolarizing average channel. Its stationary output is one-dependent and has Hankel rank three. For phase order q>=3 the exact free-mixed-start count is q and exact prescribed-pure-start count q+1; for non-root phase no finite model exists. Algebraic sin/cos give algebraic superoperators, including a rational-superoperator infinite example.

**Direct dependencies.** `prop:r70-relative-spectrum`.

**Proof chain.** A uniform Choi lower bound proves CP. The averaged reset proves one-dependence. The cylinders 1 0^n 1 have three nonzero exponential modes. Active-path inequalities bound the relevant nonnegative matrix radius; Perron normalization and the peripheral eigenvector equality force cycles divisible by q. Regular physical q-gons give the matching upper. At equality q, pure initialization forces a constant-weight q-cycle and constant outcome-one column, contradicting the oscillatory cylinder.

**Classical ingredients and actual increment.** Choi criterion; Vandermonde independence; Perron-Frobenius normalization and peripheral cyclicity; Invariant-polytope simulation. The simultaneous fixed-qubit, rank-three, three uniformly positive outcome, exactly depolarizing average and one-dependent properties are combined with the sharp prescribed-pure-start increment from q to q+1. Phase-order/infinite-memory phenomena and the peripheral-spectrum mechanism have the FRDN antecedents explicitly discussed in `Fanizza24`, Section 2.3. Citation keys: `MonrasWinter14`, `Okay26`, `HolroydLiggett16`, `Fanizza24`.

**Scope boundary.** No first-existence claim for one-dependent non-HMM processes or phase-order/infinite-memory dichotomies. The counterexample distinguishes average mixing from posterior mixing and does not weaken the inherited `thm:r68-instrument`.

### 12. Inherited complete four-period count

**Label:** `cor:r74-period-four-closed`. **Source:** `supplement-r74-period-four.tex` (supplement).

**Hypotheses.** b_alpha(n)=(1+alpha^n cos(pi n/2))/2 for n>=0 and 0<=alpha<=1; Prescribed pure initial label and bounded randomized decoder.

**Conclusion.** Exact memory is 2 at alpha=0, 3 for 0<alpha<=1/sqrt(3), and 4 for 1/sqrt(3)<alpha<=1.

**Direct dependencies.** `thm:r69-sector-phase`, `prop:r73-period-four`.

**Proof chain.** Apply the inherited first-sector theorem with M=3 and theta=pi/2, use the three-mode rank lower, and retain the damped four-cycle upper. At alpha=0 the initial probability differs from the constant tail, requiring two labels.

**Classical ingredients and actual increment.** Inherited spectral threshold and explicit cycle realization. Inherited logical closure, explicitly not counted as a new independent spectral theorem.

**Scope boundary.** Corrects the status of this family's first transition; other damping transitions and the finite-probe certificate remain different questions.
