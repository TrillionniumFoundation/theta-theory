## Mathematical directions supported by this audit

The present source has two concrete structural openings: (i) determine whether
the uniform-all-memory square-root primitive modulus is optimal under only the
current positive-cost/properness assumptions, including a fully physical
support-changing lower family; and (ii) replace mere convergence to the
history optimum by a necessary-and-sufficient exact finite-saturation theorem
in a nontrivial fixed-hidden-model class, with a counted realization and an
obstruction against all stochastic finite controllers. Those would strengthen
the active theorem chain. They must not be described as solving the original
noisy-qubit risk problem unless that separate objective is actually proved.
