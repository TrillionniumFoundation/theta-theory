# Counterexample and obstruction ledger

1. **TV versus expected length.** A discrepancy on probability e followed by length e^-2 changes bounded risk by O(e) while expected acquisition can diverge like e^-1. Retained Section errors contains this witness. It invalidates a TV-only mean claim, not the occupation-weighted resolvent estimate.
2. **Sensitivity needs a stopping denominator.** One live state with stop probability rho has mean 1/rho. Replacing rho by rho+u changes it by u/[rho(rho+u)]. Thus bounded terminal-law sensitivity and cost sensitivity are different obligations.
3. **Weak balance versus actual preparation.** An unreachable invariant live component may be added to a balance solution; retained phantom-removal proof subtracts it before implementation accounting.
4. **Separate-model versus common program.** The retained joint-support counterexample rejects pointwise model-wise feasible routes without a common route. Model summation must precede maximum.
5. **Zero margin at N=1.** The synthesis proof switches to exactly-one-call tables. It does not apply an interior-budget formula at this endpoint.
6. **Free clock versus mean stopping.** The fixed-horizon M-n law belongs to a different protocol; it is not a valid mean-stopping label count. Here live/terminal sets are charged and the observer may self-loop.
7. **Raw continuity versus posterior continuity.** Quantum effects may have a zero eigenvalue. Raw instrument continuity survives, but division by a vanishing report likelihood does not acquire a uniform modulus. The new theorem never divides by that likelihood.
8. **Finite grid upper versus global lower.** A failed numerical search is not a lower bound. Every Borel competitor must first be approximated inside the accepted grid using the paid reserve.
