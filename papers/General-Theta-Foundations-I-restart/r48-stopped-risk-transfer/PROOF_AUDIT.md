# Proof audit

## Quantifiers and legality
All algorithms are homogeneous, model-independent, finite-state and Borel randomized. They may select actions and stop from their charged labels/current reports. The parameter family is known; the true parameter is never given. The terminal task is fixed and uniformly bounded. The fallback terminal label already exists. Fresh regularization bits are not persistent hidden seeds. Competing algorithms have only the stated mean bound; the proof does not impose the construction's geometric stopping tail on them.

## Delicate steps checked
1. The killing coupling changes the physical audit time only on an event of probability <=rho(E tau-1); bounding *both* scores in [0,L] handles this change without assuming a static target.
2. The terminal hitting map Q~(I-P~)^-1 is positive and mass preserving under the target floor. Its norm is one, although the live resolvent norm is 1/rho. This is why bounded risk does not acquire the full cost sensitivity.
3. D_z uses actual positive occupation z, with norm E tau. An algebraic phantom component may not be substituted. Joint-map norm bounds imply D_z<=delta0+eta E tau.
4. For N>1, regularization buys rho(N-1) uniformly even when the original mean is less than N. The finite approximation spends at most one eighth of this reserve; evaluation upper uncertainty spends at most one quarter. Boundary feasibility never needs a decision oracle.
5. Finite report averaging is under the *common reference*, not the model-dependent likelihood. Sum of routing weights equals one, so all outgoing labels are controlled simultaneously. The implementation still sees the true native report cell.
6. The global lower is u-E, because every feasible Borel program has an accepted grid approximation. Merely enumerating feasible grid programs would give only an upper; the covering proof is essential.
7. N=1 is handled as a one-call problem. No limit of a formula containing 1/(N-1) is used.
8. Gaussian integrated error is controlled on a compact box plus the actual tail; zero transition entries do not require inversion. Quantum bounds use only finite matrix positivity and square-root continuity, not normalized posterior stability at null likelihoods.
9. Computable partitions/decoders are additional assumptions. No arbitrary real oracle or uncharged high-precision report selector is inferred from Borel measurability.

## Limits
The manuscript does not prove optimality of the square-root modulus, efficient synthesis, an explicit informative Gaussian mean-memory power law, or optimal program/workspace/time lower bounds. The two old stability-route separation examples requested in R32 remain outstanding. Compilation and finite checks cannot establish continuum correctness.
