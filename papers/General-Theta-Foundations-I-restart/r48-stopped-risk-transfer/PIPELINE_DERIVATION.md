# Pipeline derivation

1. Prepare the positive causal experiment and specify the executable future tests and legal interface before choosing a code.
2. Pass to a Borel predictive quotient via a finite separating invariant affine test closure. Physical finite dimension alone is not asserted sufficient for arbitrary tasks.
3. Express a finite causal observer through positive live and terminal ensemble maps. Retain one common action/routing/decoder program across the unknown fixed model.
4. Enforce the joint sum-before-max routing criterion. Remove invariant phantom occupation to obtain actual preparation-generated occupation and the same terminal law. This gives the inherited exact Phi(N,M).
5. A Borel witness is not yet finitely describable. Reroute live decisions to an existing terminal label with probability rho. Pay risk <= L rho(N-1), obtain mean reserve rho(N-1), add no persistent state.
6. Use target stopping floor only in the resolvent. D_z controls terminal mass with factor 1 and mean cost with factor 1/rho. Work against actual occupation, so raw defect cost is <=delta0+eta N.
7. Average all Borel routes on a common finite report partition. Round action and routing simplexes. The grid covers all competitors; a model-dependent partition is not used.
8. Reserve part of the strict mean margin for approximate execution and part for certified model-net/truncation/arithmetic evaluation. Accept only guaranteed feasible finite tables.
9. The least accepted risk upper u approximates every Borel competitor within E, proving u-E <= Phi <= u. This is constructive upper plus global information/decision-class lower at the same N,M.
10. Actual acquired law has further geometric content when report recoverability and prepared-reference comparison hold: stopped density bound + mass-constrained quantization + one-live rejection yield matching laws. Additional geometry is not invented for every effective informative model.
11. Compose task errors and simulator resources only through complete stopped/scored law and explicit expected-cost certificates. Gaussian/quantum raw verification follows the general proof, never precedes it logically.
