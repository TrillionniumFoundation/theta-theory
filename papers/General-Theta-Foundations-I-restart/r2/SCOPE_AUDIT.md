# Scope and remaining genuine proof responsibilities

## Established in this revision, subject to independent proof review

F1: General acquired-geometry transfer with full actual-mass/covering/local-update/report-continuity hypotheses. A structural K route derives forced energy from one-step raw Markov-factor Lyapunov data. Uniform matching is proved when the displayed acquired-weight normalization is uniformly bounded; one-chart recurrent classes need no extra normalization.

F2: Typed causal microstep composition in the complete resource ledger; common-loss defect transfer; a growing semantic-overhead total-memory converse; a single explicit joint robust minimax class with matching feasible-region finite-bit upper and lower. Erasure deficiency is proved optimal under the legal command order.

F3: Intersecting finite strata and rank collapse retained; recurrent active expansion added; independent exact suspension gives activity-uniform profiles. These are proved extensions, not interface declarations.

F4: Three structurally different raw-kernel verifications: Bayesian filtering; singular absorbing acquisition; recurrent fold/refresh. No realization is a premise of a general theorem.

## Not established by this manuscript

1. Uniform rare-component transport for every multi-chart nonstationary kernel. H/weight normalization is explicit and can diverge; sharper localized finite-horizon operators may be needed.
2. A lower bound for arbitrary total program/workspace/runtime budgets, or shortest/fastest implementation. The full-ledger minimax law has an explicit feasible region; program/runtime/workspace optimality below it is not proved.
3. A growing-overhead lower without a semantic continuation requirement, or with nonzero allowed challenge error. Arbitrarily naming a register cannot force it to be necessary.
4. Unknown-kernel learning and optimal calibration/pilot acquisition costs. Certified input/rational data are charged but their globally optimal acquisition is not solved.
5. Unrestricted optimal adaptive exploration. Fixed exploration or explicitly uniform strategy classes remain part of the hypotheses.
6. Arbitrary adaptive/state-dependent hold mechanisms, infinitely accumulating strata, general singular laws without verified mass estimates, infinite-horizon transcript equivalence, LDP equivalence or unbounded-generator closure.
7. Independent certification of all historical v1--v96 results, all historical referee responses, or journal priority/acceptance. Reading coverage is in HISTORY_COVERAGE.md.

These gaps are not hidden as achieved by source organization or test counts. The original mother problem is retained; the new positive theorems make progress on its specified classes without claiming a universal solution beyond their quantifiers.
