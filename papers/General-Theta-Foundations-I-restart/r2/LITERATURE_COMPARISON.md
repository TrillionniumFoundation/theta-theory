# Literature comparison and priority audit

The formal bibliography has fifteen cited items. Primary abstracts/publisher records were used for bibliographic checks; primary text was checked for the specific theorem-number comparisons below. This is not an exhaustive independent priority clearance.

| Area and source | Checked overlap | Difference actually proved here |
|---|---|---|
| Shalizi--Crutchfield, J Stat Phys 104 (2001), Thms 2--3 | Minimal/unique prescient causal states of stochastic processes | Controlled continuation/resources and continuous-report density-version descent; no priority claim for predictive equivalence |
| Littman--Sutton--Singh (2001) | Future action-observation predictive representations | Acquired-law geometry, forced finite-machine risk and full ledger are extra responsibilities |
| Ferns--Panangaden--Precup, UAI 2004 | Behavioral distances and value comparison for finite MDPs | Task-selected state/report metrics; no assertion of a universal bisimulation metric |
| Saldi--Yuksel--Linder, arXiv:1710.07009, Assumption 1/Thm 3 | Finite belief-model approximation and discounted-control near-optimality under continuity/moment hypotheses | Fixed-exploration, nonasymptotic acquired-mass lower and online matching; not unrestricted control |
| Graf--Luschgy; Pages--Pham; Sellami; Feuer--Goodwin | Quantization and nonlinear-filter approximation | Same acquired task lower, innovation localization and explicit separate resource accounts |
| Zhu arXiv:1708.07657, Thm 1.1 (v3) | Local error estimates for Ahlfors--David measures | Finite-budget anisotropic/rank-uniform finite-chart mixture; not a general singular-measure classification |
| Diaconis--Freedman; Costa--Fragoso--Marques | Random-iteration contraction and Markov jump moment stability | Reverse conditional forced profiles normalized by actual acquired mass; no novelty claim for spectral radius/Neumann resolvents |
| Blackwell; Le Cam; Torgersen | Randomization, deficiency and common bounded-decision losses | Causal microsteps, admitted lift classes and full cost maps; direct mandatory-state/robust converses |
| Fritz, Adv Math 370 (2020) | Kernels, conditional independence and sufficiency | Abstract sufficiency does not itself provide actual geometry or finite-machine transport bounds |

Torgersen's deficiency chapter and Le Cam's book are classical framework citations, not assertions that every proof in those books was re-audited. Markov-jump spectral stability is explicitly inherited background. New statements concern the assembled, acquisition-weighted forced process and its causal/resource implications; journal-level novelty assessment remains for independent review.
