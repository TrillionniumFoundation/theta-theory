# Notation and quantifier audit

- Raw action domains are written as action spaces; the multiplicative error gain is gamma, never A_t.
- p_t(s) is the weighted terminal-score vector. The filter subsection uses p for its scalar posterior and explicitly identifies this local specialization. General state distance d and Euclidean score norm are distinct.
- N is preforecast call count in the acquired-law theorem; N_raw is the complete ledger count including initializer/pilots/failures and the selected terminal probe. Robust family N_raw=n+3. Exact hold deletion is algebraic, not deletion from N_raw or tau.
- Every horizon-dependent chart may carry superscript (N); the body lists them explicitly and suppresses only within a fixed theorem. Geometric constants exclude N,m,positive widths,weights; separated cases retain the declared separation ratios.
- w_N is actual mass under the fixed exploration, not an auxiliary geometric measure. H is the kernel-derived energy matrix; h_j and V_jk are its first and mixed-second profiles. H_n in the robust section is the scalar failure probability with that explicit local definition.
- Zero widths are deleted; point components have psi(k)=0 for positive k; psi(0)=D0^2 is a discard cost. Matching is stated as two inequalities, including simultaneous zeros, not a quotient of zeros.
- Gamma_N is explicit. A zero acquired weight requires zero transport; K's stationary majorant cannot certify otherwise.
- v_N^num is a deterministic score-norm allowance; U_N is a random propagated state error measured in L2. Local numerical/calibration terms add before squaring. Causal/report-law defects add once after squared-loss conversion.
- Total variation is sup_A and half the density L1 norm. Only common-loss/oracle comparisons are made.
- Exact allocation requires exact comparable data; finite programs may use certified constant-factor allocations. Borel ties and approximate finite-bit comparisons are distinct.
- M denotes total persistent capacity; L in the tag theorem is tag cardinality, locally distinct from the Lipschitz constant L of G. R0,D0',Q0 are semantic token cardinalities, not automatic lower bounds for arbitrary registers.
- All displayed stopping comparisons are bounded. Controller classes are fixed or have explicitly uniform certificates. Public clocks are exogenous, not observation-dependent channels.
