# Scope boundaries for the restart

Foundations I must not be identified with any one of the following:

- a single strongly refreshing hidden-Markov model;
- a single finite measurement ensemble;
- Hilbert-space memory dimension alone;
- a covariance, Fisher matrix, rank locus or rate function supplied without an experiment;
- the existence of CI receipts or a long preservation archive; or
- closure of A2/B4/C2 or the whole historical programme.

These may be examples, derived structures or independent obligations. They are not the foundational object.
