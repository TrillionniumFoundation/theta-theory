# Counterexamples and negative controls

* A nearest grid point need not be a conditional posterior. Its nonzero first moment invalidates the exact pooling telescope. Regression checks a strict quadratic excess for rounding p=1/3 to 1/2.
* A singleton law may occupy a large formally reachable set but has zero M-center distortion. Lower geometry uses actual density or actual submass, never reachability alone.
* The one-hot validation posterior is atomic. It violates the full-dimensional smoothing definition and must use the stratified theorem, not be inserted into the continuous theorem by informal limiting language.
* A free phase counter would replace 1+sum L_t by max L_t. That is a different machine. Full phase counts are checked in every new bound.
* The two-state noisy sensor has positive posterior mass on both hidden states after every finite report sequence. Thus strong separated response refinements are not available, although fixed-horizon completion is proved.
* A lower-dimensional terminal query does not generally reveal all intermediate continuation information. The integral noisy-regression theorem retains online/checkpoint exponent separation; no general equality is claimed outside the new smoothing task.
* Allowing an exact-bit simulator to wait until the terminal audit destroys the stated early information deficit. The deadline is part of the experiment morphism.
* A permitted calibration or deficiency tolerance can be zero in an actually exact implementation. Only the constructed inaccessible-sign/erasure experiment gives an attained floor; generic tolerances have transport uppers.
* Exact infinite precision in a read-only programme is not finite total bit complexity. Digital certificates are separate.
