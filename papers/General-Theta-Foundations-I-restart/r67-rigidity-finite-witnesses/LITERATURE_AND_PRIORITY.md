# Literature and contribution audit — R67

No exhaustive priority certificate or human endorsement is claimed. Native citations and theorem-level distinctions are part of the manuscript; this is an author audit.

R66 already proved the weighted cost identity, robust tied optimal face, anchored convex realization criterion, Erlang memory hierarchy, damped-qubit positive-realization phases and supplied-response finite-size test. Those results are inherited verbatim. R67 adds the necessary-and-sufficient normalized-margin boundary condition, a common squared-loss physical replication instrument, a finite rational model witness with a positive lower-memory risk gap, and unknown robust risk optimization at fixed memory over compact semialgebraic families.
