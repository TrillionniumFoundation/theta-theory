# Notes clarifying the unchanged R22 companion

The R22 native source remains byte-for-byte unchanged. These explanatory notes respond to technical requests without overwriting reviewed history.

The exact 729-table example has beta=1/2, M=2, p in [1/4,1/2], report cells C0=[0,1/2), C1=[1/2,1], and readouts 7/16 and 9/16. For BOTH previous labels, its selected row on C0 is (1,0), and on C1 is (0,1). The initial label is 1. It has exact worst risk 63/256. The all-Borel analytic lower is separate from the rational finite enumeration; the generic interval without that extra analytic lower is [59/256,63/256]. This table is read directly from R22 `sections/07_certificate.tex`, not inferred from a test count.

The calibration offset in that old scalar target uses enlarged loss bounded by (1+2delta)^2 and is explicitly rescaled in its proof. Its p0 is the known endpoint of the declared parameter family, not the inaccessible realized p. The three-state irreversible-erasure construction is sufficient to attain an unrestricted-history lower, not a proved minimum threshold.

The moving-support logistic report calculation is a dominated compact-interval example with its actual interval support and endpoint-null convention. It is not a general assertion that deterministic parameter transitions are close in total variation; the new primitive-kernel route warns of that failure. The inherited discount–expansion theorem retains its consistent common-adaptive-action coupling and uniform score assumptions.

Old singular references are common Cantor/countable/tagged references, not arbitrary uncountably many singular translates. The old quantum payoff responses represent one performed audit at the specified score; the new regenerative marked-kernel formulation includes any repeatedly performed audit back-action explicitly, so it does not silently assume a joint classical path of incompatible audits.
