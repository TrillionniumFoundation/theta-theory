# Submission guide

Review the native article first. The central theorem is `thm:renewal`; its proof is self-contained in `lem:tensor` and Section 3. Review `thm:defect`, `lem:primitive`, `lem:cells`, `thm:compile` next for same-task resource composition. The geometric chain is `thm:geometry` → `cor:noncontractive` → `thm:hidden-geometry` / `thm:quantum-geometry`. `thm:holding` and `prop:joint` supply separate exact singular and sharpness results.

The contribution is not an unrestricted nonreset average-cost theorem. It is not inferred by substituting beta=1 in the discounted predecessor. The physical-plus-observer regeneration protocol and uniform actual cycle tail are stated before optimization. Full-history checkpoint geometry is compared only with the specified fixed-exploration online prediction class, whereas the average common-program theorem optimizes its complete declared stationary class.

Proofs and new assumptions are in the native article. Audit files and build records are separate. No manuscript or audit asserts editorial acceptance, independent correctness, or a priority certificate.
