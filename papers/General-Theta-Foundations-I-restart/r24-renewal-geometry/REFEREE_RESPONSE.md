## Principal mathematical response: report section 12.2

The new central theorem is `thm:renewal`: an attained robust **average** risk for one parameter-independent stationary M-state observer, with a finite-model value identity and uniform finite-acquisition / vanishing-discount comparisons at unchanged M. This is not a formal beta→1 inference from R22. The proof uses the actual cycle return tail and a finite-row compact topology, then proves an overshoot bound and an Abel comparison uniformly over every legal program and fixed model. Under a 1+p return moment it gives N^{-p} and (1-beta)^p orders; `prop:time-sharp` proves their sharpness over the declared moment class.

The strengthening has an explicit protocol boundary: physical preparation and **observer memory reset** both occur at regeneration and are charged. No persistent seed, clock or intercycle record survives. This is not an assertion about unrestricted nonreset controllers, nor a claim that physical mixing resets a controller for free. The model remains fixed throughout the whole execution. The native theorem treats within-cycle nonlinear, history-dependent, nonmixing and infinite-dimensional hidden experiments under finite-history product domination. Thus the general result is not a single refreshed HMM formula.
