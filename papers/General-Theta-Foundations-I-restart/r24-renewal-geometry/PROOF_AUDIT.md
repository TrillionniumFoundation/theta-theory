# Proof audit

1. The model theta is fixed throughout all cycles. The program is selected before the model supremum. Finite-family determination uses one compact controller space and the finite-intersection property; there is no inf–sup interchange.
2. Cycles are iid only because BOTH physical preparation and observer state are reset with no retained seed. Physical recurrence alone would not justify this proof. All preparation calls count in tau and N.
3. The main exact topology has finitely many weak-star rows and compact readouts. A Borel representative is chosen modulo common report references. Finite product domination validates repeated rows on distinct coordinates; the diagonal example excludes a false pointwise multiplication step.
4. Mean return time alone is insufficient. The actual uniform tail envelope is imposed on the entire legal class. Reward and mean-length truncations both have that error, so their ratio is continuous since mean length is at least one.
5. The finite-N proof uses K_N=min{k:T_k>N}, with K_N<=N+1. The event K_N>=j depends only on previous cycles, justifying the centered stopped sum. The residual score minus g times residual length has absolute value at most the overshoot. Renewal mass at an integer is at most one. These steps prove the explicit uniform bound without an implicit stationary initialization.
6. The moment refinement is proved before taking suprema: sum E(tau-s)+ <= E[tau min(tau,N+1)]. This avoids a spurious logarithm at p=1. Abel weights are positive and sum to one. Cluster-point optimality uses lower semicontinuity, not uniform convergence in theta of a sequence of optimizers.
7. Defect transfer compares both reward and length. It is not linear in arbitrary cycle TV. The 1+p Holder exponent is attained by rare long cycles. Causal simulator timing and task compatibility are stated; no reverse bound follows from a forward simulator alone.
8. Classical primitive comparison includes post-hidden state, flags and actual audit, not just report marginals. Quantum comparison is an unnormalized CP telescoping argument, not a fictitious coupling of quantum hidden states.
9. Report reconstruction retains joint physical/cell output and averages a row once. Its endpoint law equals an original legal cell program and so inherits the tail certificate. The selected program is a finite runtime table; proof-only conditional averages are not performed online.
10. Geometric projection uses the actual fixed-exploration occupation law, which may be a submeasure. Numerical updates must remain in the valid candidate domain. The comparison state is used only in the proof. The inherited whole-history controller is not given to the finite machine.
11. The first informative posterior law supplies an ACTUAL positive submeasure at average, finite N and discount. Full-dimensional lower constants retain its mixture weight. Global covers include all later candidates and the initial state. Nonexpansive propagation does not imply mixing.
12. The quantum audit is performed and its average back-action appears in the next predictive update. Rank-one and continuous load branches are actually mixed; no lower-dimensional stratum is normalized to unit mass. At continuous weight zero the full-dimensional lower is not claimed.
13. The joint resource lower averages fixed inaccessible parameters solely as a minimax lower. It does not redraw theta during execution. The simulator must issue the first target bit before later acquisitions and cannot consult audit marks. Finite-N error is signed, not a positive learning-rate term.

Unclosed extensions are itemized in `SCOPE_AUDIT.md`. This audit is an author-side check, not a formal or external correctness certificate.
