# Counterexample and sharpness ledger

- `rem:diagonal`: two reports equal one uniform variable; weak-star oscillating row converges to 1/2 but its square integrates to 1/2 rather than 1/4. Marginal domination does not supply product-history continuity.
- `prop:time-sharp`: zero-score two-call cycles mixed with probability L^(-1-p) with a preparation plus L unit-score calls. Uniform 1+p moment, but finite-N and discounted biases attain N^(-p) and (1-beta)^p orders by choosing L at the corresponding scale.
- Same proposition with probability 1/L: bounded mean lengths and convergent finite responses, but average reward tends to 1/3 while the limiting short experiment has reward zero. Uniform integrability is essential to this continuity route.
- Reset omission: an observer can retain information about a fixed theta across physically independent cycles. Its augmented cycles are not iid, so the renewal ratio proof cannot silently include it. This is an information-pattern distinction, not a claim that all nonreset average theory is impossible.
- `thm:holding` example L=ceil(V^(-alpha)): preparation near-zero mass is r while occupation mass is r^(1-alpha). Using preparation geometry would miss the actual length bias.
- `thm:quantum-geometry` at continuous mixture weight zero: the full-dimensional first-report lower disappears. No full-dimensional lower constant is claimed uniformly down to that endpoint.
- `prop:joint`: erasure probability e on a length-L cycle gives attained deficiency e/2 but average bit loss eL/(4mu). L~e^(-1/(1+p)) attains the recurrence-defect Holder exponent. A universally linear error bound would be false for the declared moment class.
- Deterministic parameter-dependent hidden transitions can be mutually singular despite continuous trajectories. Primitive operational-norm continuity must not be inferred from a state-space Lipschitz map alone.

All historical counterexamples remain intact in their original subtrees. This ledger does not delete or downgrade their independent mathematical roles.
