# Proof audit

The text was reviewed for quantifier and interface mistakes before building. Main stress points are the whole-cone remainder, robustness of quiet access under grid error, the invariant correspondence at zero mass, and the same-policy projection preceding optimization. Native proofs explicitly include them.

- The input row law is the likelihood-weighted pushforward of the actual raw experiment. No uniform distribution is substituted on a formal support.
- Cone order and base norm are specified on C_K; when subtracting programmed mass the intermediate remainder belongs only to C. Raw whole-base interiority is used at that precise point.
- The common ensemble Z_M^circ may have zero rows; a minorization need not assign positive mass to every cell. Contraction is on total zero-mass differences, not on coordinatewise centroids.
- Every programmed representative belongs to its fixed cell closure even when its invariant row mass is zero. A free choice anywhere in K at zero mass would invalidate the quiet-access induction.
- The fixed-point graph is checked with integrable raw-map domination and acquired-boundary nullity. Weak continuity alone is not used to pass discontinuous cell indicators.
- All action selectors are legal before their next report. The average comparator allows full adaptive sensing; the constructed observer is homogeneous and uses only one label.
- The actual invariant table is calibrated, while transient execution generally is not. Transient risk is transferred as a bounded affine ensemble functional, not by claiming false transient calibration.
- Numerical perturbations may be time dependent for the finite-time bound, but stationary attraction requires a homogeneous perturbed map. Nonphysical numerical maps cannot use the positive-map theorem.
- Benchmark q_* is defined by a proven strict contraction, not a decimal root. Exact Brier stationary risk is not stated as the globally optimal two-label risk.
- Finite tests do not validate density/Jacobian or continuum fixed-point arguments. Original historical claims are retained with original scope, not automatically re-certified.

Priority and editorial significance require external judgment. The manuscript does not equate a new lemma, Git cleanup or a successful CI job with a major mathematical breakthrough.
