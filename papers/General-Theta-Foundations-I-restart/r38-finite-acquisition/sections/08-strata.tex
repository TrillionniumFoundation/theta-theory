\section{Weighted acquired strata}\label{sec:strata}
\begin{theorem}[Finite-acquisition singular profile]\label{thm:strata}
Replace the full-dimensional row density by a finite visible decomposition: at each call the report mark $j$ has fixed actual probability $w_j>0$, independent of the incoming state and action. Given that mark, the acquired state lies in a compact convex affine set $K_j\subset K$ of dimension $d_j\ge1$, with relative density at most $\Lambda_j$ uniformly on $D$. Finitely many separately marked atom states are also allowed. Require the same raw interior image and robust accessibility, with the local overlapping measure now a probability on the marked acquired space. Relative grid boundaries have zero conditional mass. There is at least one positive-dimensional component.

For all $M$ above a fixed threshold, put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:xi}
 \Xi(M)=\sum_{j:d_j>0}w_jM^{-2/d_j}.
\end{equation}
Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:stratified-law}
 \calR_{\rm cp}(n,M)-J_*(n)\asymp\Xi(M),\qquad
 c\Xi(M)\le\calR_{\rm hom}(n,M)-J_*(n)
       \le C\bigl(\Xi(M)+n^{-1}\bigr).
\end{equation}
One calibrated homogeneous observer attains the upper for every $n$, has an $M$-independent joint mixing bound, and has stationary excess comparable to $\Xi(M)$. Thus finite-acquisition matching holds for $n\ge c_*\Xi(M)^{-1}$. Constants can depend on the fixed positive weights, affine components and accessibility certificates. No optimal allocation across varying weights is claimed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Projection of the marked overlap to its predictive coordinate gives the actual overlap needed for the Bellman bias. Reserve one label per atom and distribute the remaining labels in fixed positive proportions among the finitely many positive-dimensional marked grids. For sufficiently large $M$ their maximal ambient diameter is below $s_0$. In the calibration correspondence use, for each label, the cone over its own compact relative cell closure; an atom has its singleton cone. Every outgoing raw contribution is supported in that cell with its actual mark. Zero-row and action-tie arguments are unchanged, and relative boundaries are null.

The cone lift integrates $(1,x)$ against the marked subprobability witness and allocates it to the matching marked label. It is positive in $C_K$ regardless of the dimension of the cell. The robust quiet-report argument uses only ambient mesh error. It therefore gives the same budget-independent block minorization and attraction, without an erasure label. At stationarity apply Lemma~\ref{lem:curvature} in each positive-dimensional affine span to the restriction of $h$, whose Lipschitz constant is inherited from $K$. Each atom contributes zero cell gap. The total Jensen gap is at most
$C\sum_jw_jm_j^{-2/d_j}\le C\Xi(M)$, and entrance costs $C/n$.

For the lower, orthogonally project any $M$ global readouts into each component's affine span. Projection cannot increase distance. The conditional actual-density small-ball argument gives $c_jM^{-2/d_j}$. Multiply by the original $w_j$ and sum. The same conditional projection at the actual audit cut gives the lower relative to $J_*(n)$ for every acquisition policy and every observer, without a bias endpoint subtraction. Marked grids give the checkpoint upper. Weights have not been normalized away; overlapping affine spans and off-stratum readouts cause no difficulty.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Raw changing acquired rank]\label{cor:rank}
After the quantum instrument in Theorem~\ref{thm:quantum}, independently choose a visible mark $j$ with probability $w_j$ and apply a trace-preserving unital conditional expectation $\mathcal E_j$ onto a matrix subalgebra. Include the identity with positive weight. The completely depolarizing map may be an atom mark. Then Theorem~\ref{thm:strata} holds with $d_j$ the affine dimension of the trace-one part of the subalgebra.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The raw marked instrument is $w_j\mathcal E_jL_a(H)$. It is positive and normalized after summing marks. Conditional expectations fix $\tau$ and preserve the positive spectral floor, so the entire physical cone is still mapped to $C_K$. In Hilbert--Schmidt coordinates each expectation is an orthogonal projection. Projecting the full-dimensional bounded acquired density gives a bounded relative density by Fubini, with a bound equal to the original density constant times a maximal compact-fiber volume. The fixed audit chart only changes Jacobian constants. Depolarization gives exactly the atom $\tau$.

For the joint accessibility witness restrict the $k$ quiet reports and the last overlapping report to the identity mark. Their real subprobability mass is multiplied by $w_{\rm id}^{k+1}$. The local overlap is supported on the identity mark, and the quiet event probability at each step is at least $w_{\rm id}(1-\beta)s^d$. All other marks and their original weights still execute on the complement. No postselection changes the experiment. Relative grids give the remaining conditions.

The acquired predictive rank changes, for example, between the full density body and a diagonal body. Physical density matrices remain strictly positive because of the stated channel. This is not an assertion of physical eigenvalue-rank change or an extension to arbitrary pure-state boundaries; the separate pure-rank theorem retains its own hypotheses in the Bayesian companion.
\end{proof}
