# Literature comparison

Primary sources checked in this revision include arXiv:2312.14111v3 (Demirci–Kara–Yuksel average-cost control), arXiv:2601.03132v1 (Kim, submitted 6 January 2026), arXiv:1503.02244v3 (Saldi–Yuksel–Linder finite MDP approximations), and the original journal metadata/abstract of Yu–Bertsekas 2008, DOI 10.1287/moor.1070.0279. The native bibliography also retains original sequential quantization, fixed-point, value-directed compression and Bayes-risk/Bregman references. Inspection date: 12 October 2026. The checked Kim page exposed v1, not an assumed later version.

| Prior cluster | Inherited/standard content | Native distinction |
|---|---|---|
| Sequential vector quantization, Borkar–Mitter–Tatikonda | causal coding/quantization is established | actual instrument-level joint acquired component, active action rows and total finite labels |
| Yu–Bertsekas finite-state average control | near-optimal FSC existence is established | quantitative actual-geometry hard-state lower, continuous reports, uniform entrance/precision |
| Demirci–Kara–Yuksel average POMDP | Bellman equation from contraction is established | prove mixing of the executed quantized physical/label ensemble, not assume it from exact-filter stability |

| Poupart–Boutilier value-directed compression | task-relevant belief compression is established | all-controller converse and actual calibrated common-grid stationary execution |
| Varshney Bayes risk/Bregman | decision divergence and static curvature are established | conditional dynamic pooling, nonsmooth Bellman curvature with true acquired mass |
| Kim 2026 policy-conditional approximation | contractive compression and Wasserstein mismatch for fixed execution | comparator here ranges over all legal adaptive acquisition policies; joint observer gap is proved independently |
| R32/R34/R37 | quotient, perspective identity, acquired curvature, Bellman overlap, erasure-based invariant calibration | new lift removes vanishing erasure and its memory-dependent entrance/precision losses |

No claim of first finite-state controller, first belief compressor, new Kakutani theorem, new Dobrushin principle, or complete priority certification is made. No claimed solution of an established named open problem is inferred from the benchmark. Editorial importance remains for external assessment.
