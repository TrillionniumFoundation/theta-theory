Inherited claims remain valid at their original scopes: actual-history comparison and sufficient geometric transfer; distinct event/state roles; width-localized task defects; exact atomic zero-risk spectrum; finite common synthesis and reachable-support potentials; standard-Borel reusable-task spans and sharp continuation depth; primitive reset-control saddle and arbitrary-rank two-detector memory frontier; mixed-strata comparator; calibrated computation/composition; fixed-law predictive quotient. The baseline reset wording already distinguished robust minimax from pointwise optimality, and the atom necessary-threshold correction was already completed in R61. Neither is re-advertised as a new R63 discovery.

The round-2 report's accepted closures concern R59, not the correctness or significance of these new R63 theorems. Its 15 major and 36 technical comments receive separate responses. No independent human evaluator was invoked. Literature checks establish specific primary-source comparisons, not an exhaustive earliest-priority certificate.

Remaining problems are stated positively and explicitly: classify exactly when the geometric reference profile is realized by a broad causal experiment; intrinsic minimal memory under general stateful simulation; a common executable predictive quotient for substantial unknown-model classes; matching program/precision/workspace/time lower and upper bounds; and broader nonintervening sensing. The compact theorem is a new constrained-risk approximation theorem on its own finite interface, not a declaration that these other programmes are complete.
