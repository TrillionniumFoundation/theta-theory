\section{The two-sided causal transfer theorem}
\label{sec:reference-stability}
For an interface $\iota\in\{\cp,\evt,\st\}$ write $R_\iota(N,M)$ for the risks of Section~\ref{sec:experiment}. Suppose every terminating state-stopped competitor needs at least $B_0\ge1$ live labels at $\theta_*$. Let
\[
 m_-(M)=
 \begin{cases}M,&\iota=\cp,\evt,\\M-B_0,&\iota=\st,\end{cases}
 \quad
 m_+(M)=
 \begin{cases}\lfloor M/D_0\rfloor,&\iota=\cp,\evt,\\
 \lfloor(M-B)/D_0\rfloor,&\iota=\st.
 \end{cases}
\]
For the event upper additionally require $M\ge B$. If no stronger live lower is known, $B_0=1$ is always valid for a procedure initially waiting to make a physical call.

\begin{theorem}[Calibration-stable causal acquired-geometry transfer]
\label{thm:reference-stability}
Assume precisely the experiment, row, effort, occupation and construction certificates of Sections~\ref{sec:experiment}--\ref{sec:calibrated}; an exactly matching reference model in $\Theta$ is \emph{not} assumed. Fix $N$ and $M$ with $b_-=N/\kappa_-\ge1$ and $m_-(M)\ge1$. Put $t_N=(1-\mathfrak a_N)_+$. If $t_N>0$, then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:reference-lower}
 R_\iota(N,M)\ge
 \left[t_N\Qm_{m_-(M)}(b_-/t_N)-\mathfrak e_N\right]_+.
\end{equation}
When $t_N=0$, the asserted lower is zero, with no evaluation of $\Qm$ at infinity. An empty legal class has risk $+\infty$ and is not made feasible by this formula.

For $m_+(M)\ge1$, the stated event feasibility condition, and
$b_+=N/\kappa_+\ge(1-a_+)^{-1}$, one common Borel program at the \emph{same} budget $N$ attains an upper bound
\begin{equation}\label{eq:reference-upper}
 R_\iota(N,M)\le
 (1+b_+a_+)\Qm_{m_+(M)}\!\left(\frac{b_+}{1+b_+a_+}\right)+b_+e_+.
\end{equation}
It commits almost surely, with at most $B$ live and $D_0m_+(M)$ output roles. For $1\le b_+<(1-a_+)^{-1}$ replace the right side by $\Qm_{m_+(M)}(1)+e_+$ using first-attempt commitment.

In particular, with uniform lower defects $a_-,e_-$ and $b_-a_-<1$, the lower reads
\begin{equation}\label{eq:uniform-reference-lower}
 \left[(1-b_-a_-)\Qm_{m_-(M)}
       \!\left(\frac{b_-}{1-b_-a_-}\right)-b_-e_-\right]_+.
\end{equation}
At zero defects these inequalities recover the exact role-dependent identities of Theorem~\ref{thm:main} whenever effort, live counts and identity exports match. The lower ranges over all legal strategies; the upper is one program for the entire actual model family.
\end{theorem}

The proof uses comparison rows along \emph{actual} histories. A product coupling of whole stopped paths would require a separate termination and cost comparison and is not used.

\begin{lemma}[Accepted comparison submeasure]\label{lem:comparison-submeasure}
Fix a legal competing procedure with committed outputs in a fixed dictionary $C\subset H$, $|C|\le m$, and finite actual expected cost. Under the lower certificates, there are finite measures $\xi_c$ on $X$, $c\in C$, such that, for $\xi=\sum_c\xi_c$,
\begin{align}
 \xi&\le (\E_*A_\tau)\nu,\label{eq:phantom-cap}\\
 |\xi(X)-1|&\le\mathfrak a_N,\label{eq:phantom-mass}\\
 \E_*\|Z-\widehat r\|^2
 &\ge\sum_{c\in C}\int d_c(x)\xi_c(dx)-\mathfrak e_N.
 \label{eq:phantom-risk}
\end{align}
The measures need not be terminal laws of any comparison process.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Represent each current random transition by an independent uniform variable on $[0,1]$, using the standard Borel kernel representation. Absorb it into the current report on both sides. Fix a reached productive history and the chosen command. The transition supplies an acceptance probability $q_h(y)\in[0,1]$ and, on its acceptance event, a finite-image readout $r_h(y)\in C$. Equivalently one may use the acceptance indicator with the adjoined uniform variable. Complete $r_h$ arbitrarily on rejection. Conditioning also on current command coins causes no loss: \eqref{eq:row-mass}--\eqref{eq:row-loss} hold for every chosen legal command.

For Borel $D\subset X$, define
\[
 \xi_c(D)=\E_*\sum_{i\le\tau:\ i\ {\rm productive}}
  \int\one_{\{\pi_{H_{i-1},a_i}(y)\in D\}}
       q_{H_{i-1}}(y)\one_{\{r_{H_{i-1}}(y)=c\}}
       P^0_{H_{i-1},a_i}(dy,H).
\]
The expectation is over actual pre-call histories and commands; it does not replace their distribution. All integrands are jointly measurable, so Tonelli defines finite measures. Summing over $c$ and dropping $q_h\le1$ gives \eqref{eq:phantom-cap}, since every comparison row has mark law $\nu$. Actual commitment occurs exactly once almost surely. Its corresponding sum of actual conditional acceptance probabilities therefore has expectation one. Subtract the two sums, apply \eqref{eq:row-mass} termwise, and use \eqref{eq:defect-occupation}, obtaining \eqref{eq:phantom-mass}.

On the actual disjoint commitment events the expected terminal losses sum to the risk. Replace only the current report--audit row in each contribution. The sum of absolute errors is at most $\mathfrak e_N$. By \eqref{eq:row-moments}, the replaced conditional contribution is precisely $d_{r_h(y)}(\pi y)$. This proves \eqref{eq:phantom-risk}. One may first truncate the number of calls; nonnegativity and the finite accumulated defect bounds justify passage to the limit. The comparison process need neither stop under this program nor satisfy its budget.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:reference-stability}]
For the lower fix any admissible competitor. Its actual number of distinct outputs is at most $m_-(M)$: checkpoint and event-stopped procedures have at most $M$, whereas intrinsic state stopping leaves at most $M-B_0$ because its live and terminal alphabets are disjoint. This is a statement about its actual state labels, not about a fictitious comparison execution.

Use Lemma~\ref{lem:comparison-submeasure} with its fixed dictionary. If $t_N>0$, put $t=\xi(X)\ge t_N$ and $\xi'=(t_N/t)\xi$. Then $\xi'$ has mass $t_N$, and $\xi'\le b_-\nu$ by the actual effort certificate. Thus $h=(d\xi'/d\nu)/t_N$ is a probability density with cap $b_-/t_N$. Nonnegativity of squared loss and nearest-dictionary projection give
\[
 \sum_c\int d_c\,d\xi_c
 \ge\int\bigl[w+\operatorname{dist}(f,C)^2\bigr]d\xi
 \ge t_N\Qm_{m_-(M)}(b_-/t_N).
\]
Combine this with \eqref{eq:phantom-risk}, use nonnegativity, take the worst case over actual models, and then the infimum over all competitors. No minimax interchange or comparison-model membership is involved.

For the upper take an attained dictionary and density at cap
$\beta=b_+/(1+b_+a_+)\ge1$, and accept the virtual mark with probability $h/\beta$. Its conditional actual acceptance probability on every reached attempt is at least $p=1/\beta-a_+>0$. Its conditional unnormalized loss is at most $\Qm_{m_+(M)}(\beta)/\beta+e_+$. Iterating the conditional acceptance bound gives probability at most $(1-p)^j$ of reaching attempt $j+1$. Summing conditional costs gives $\kappa_+/p=N$; summing the disjoint terminal loss contributions gives \eqref{eq:reference-upper}. This argument needs only \eqref{eq:upper-row-mass}--\eqref{eq:upper-row-loss}, not a reference-model membership assumption. The packing construction in Lemma~\ref{lem:packing} uses $B+D_0m$ labels for state stopping and $\max\{B,D_0m\}$ for event stopping. A checkpoint counts only the latter output width. The first-attempt rule has acceptance one and needs no denominator. Uniform defects in \eqref{eq:defect-occupation} give \eqref{eq:uniform-reference-lower} directly. This proves every assertion.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Matching curves under two-sided calibration]\label{cor:reference-geometry}
Suppose Proposition~\ref{prop:geometry} holds for the actually prepared comparison law, with fixed constants, and put
$F(b,m)=\max\{b^{-2/\alpha},(bm)^{-2/(d+\alpha)}\}$.
Suppose $b_-/b_+$ and the positive widths $m_-/m_+$ remain bounded above and below. On a set of feasible budgets assume
\[
 \mathfrak a_N\le a_0<1,\qquad b_+a_+\le A_0<\infty,
 \qquad \mathfrak e_N\le c_0F(b_-,m_-),\quad
 b_+e_+\le C_0F(b_-,m_-).
\]
For sufficiently small $c_0>0$, depending only on the stated geometry, $a_0$ and the bounded effort and width ratios, one has
\[
 R_\iota(N,M)\asymp F(b_-,m_-).
\]
The comparison need not be an actual operating model. At a fixed nonzero calibration error the conditions need not persist to arbitrarily fine resolution; no exact dimension identity between perturbed and comparison supports is asserted.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Let $t_0=1-a_0$. The lower geometry bound gives
\[t_N\Qm_{m_-}(b_-/t_N)\ge c\min\{t_0^{1+2/\alpha},t_0^{1+2/(d+\alpha)}\}F(b_-,m_-).\]
Take $c_0$ at most half this coefficient. The upper cap and width differ from $b_-,m_-$ only by bounded positive factors. Apply the upper geometry bound to \eqref{eq:reference-upper} and absorb the two error bounds. This proves constants uniform over the specified budgets.
\end{proof}
