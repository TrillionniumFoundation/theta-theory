# R71 theorem and dependency map

The main article and current supplement form one paired submission. Numbers and pages below are taken from the actual native build, not inferred from version names. The independent inherited maps remain in `retained/r70-native/`.

## New load-bearing chain

`thm:r69-spectral` + `lem:r69-arithmetic-tools` → universal envelope → two exact logarithmic exponents and uniform-law rigidity. The classical continued-fraction identities are proved in `lem:r71-cf`. Counted spectral mixtures and the visible transient-core theorem then yield the multi-frequency/leakage conclusions. The actual squared-task interpretation additionally uses `thm:r67-embedding` in the current supplement.
