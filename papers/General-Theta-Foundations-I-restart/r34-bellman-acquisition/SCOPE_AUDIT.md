# Scope audit and remaining mathematical problems

## Established within declared assumptions

A known finite-horizon positive controlled experiment with finite executable affine closure, actual row density, global legal grid and buffer, feedback-uniform acquired overlap and common report supports has matching checkpoint and fully clocked adaptive-control resource curves. Both optimizations refer to the same physical task. Gaussian and quantum raw classes verify these inputs and exhibit genuinely report-dependent optimal actions. Finite visible changing-rank affine strata retain actual weights. Typed complete-law simulation, certified numerical execution and an attained post-control calibration/deficiency extension are proved separately.

## Not established

1. The overlap/buffer/density assumptions are sufficient, not necessary or an intrinsic classification. General nonsummable informative likelihoods do not have a proved matching weighted lower in this article.
2. Kernels are known. Uniformity on compact parameter subfamilies does not mean one unknown-kernel programme, statistical learning/regret, or an automatically computable controller.
3. The finite affine executable test closure and Borel realization sections are substantive hypotheses, not derivations for every physical experiment.
4. Stopping is an exact finite internal deadline. Arbitrary unbounded stopping, infinite horizon and LDP transfer are not inferred. Older results remain with their original scope.
5. The singular theorem covers finitely many visible compact affine strata with fixed weights and rank constants. It does not subsume every pure quantum orbit, vanishing mass, arbitrary stratification or nondominated support.
6. Programme description, workspace, numerical precision, planning cost, CPU time and simulator state are charged but their full joint optimal frontier is not proved. Finite-bit existence requires the stated computational certificate.
7. Positive-parameter constants may deteriorate near zero determinant, zero acquired overlap, loss of buffer or loss of positivity. Horizon-uniformity is not uniformity at those degeneracies.
8. The Gaussian benchmark's constants are conservative, not exact optimum coefficients. The quantitative expansive/switch witnesses are explicit theorem consequences, not a claim to settle an independently named longstanding problem.
9. No literature search or build certifies mathematical priority or top-four journal significance. The new proof is a submission for independent examination, not an external acceptance judgment.

These are genuine remaining research questions. They are not a change of the General Theta mother problem or a decision to replace it with a survey. Preserved nonlinear, temporal, orbit and unknown-model results remain independent where their hypotheses differ.
