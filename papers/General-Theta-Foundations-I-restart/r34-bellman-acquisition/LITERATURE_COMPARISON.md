# Primary-source literature comparison and access audit

## Theorem-level distinctions

| Prior line | Object / guarantee | R34 relation, not a claim of subsumption |
|---|---|---|
| Poupart–Boutilier, UAI 2000/2001 and NeurIPS 2002 | Value-directed belief approximation/compression, reward-relevant subspaces and policy value | Finite executable affine test closure has direct conceptual proximity. R34 counts all autonomous labels/phases, constructs a controlled code and proves an all-Borel same-task lower. Finite affine closure itself is not a new invention. |
| Varshney 2011 | Bayes risk error as Bregman divergence | Static prior quantization and risk geometry are acknowledged. Conditional Bellman/Jensen defects are not new convex principles; actual incoming-edge pooling and executable causal allocation distinguish the dynamic theorem. |
| Kara–Yuksel 2022 | Near-optimal finite-memory feedback, filter stability | Genuine control results predate this paper. History-window memory differs from a fixed total state alphabet and internal exact halt; neither notion can be substituted without accounting. |
| Demirci–Kara–Yuksel 2024 | W1 filter contraction, average-cost optimality and approximations | The new experiment-generated common-mass route supplies Bellman sensitivity even when the one-step W1 coefficient expands. It does not claim a new average-cost theorem. |
| Kim 2026 | Finite-memory belief approximation with policy-conditional Wasserstein analysis and subsequent value comparisons | Compare memory object, transition mismatch, task and policy quantifiers separately. R34 is not described as the first finite-memory control result. |
| Diaz-Infante Velasco–Garcia–Minjarez-Sosa 2026 | Finite partition observations and recursive/control filtering | The primary abstract announces class-restricted normalized filters and finite partitions. This is not the same as the hard total-state all-controller converse. Full technical proof was not available during this inspection. |
| Sequential/finite-state quantization; recursive marginal quantization; nonanticipative rate-distortion | Causal coding, finite-state encoders, recursive marginal grids, information-rate budgets | Actual acquired quantization is a shared tool. Hard total configurations, feedback sensing actions and autonomous deadline are distinct resources. No priority claim over the entire field is made. |
| Dobrushin common-mass coefficient | Oscillation contraction by overlapping probability mass | Classical principle, not new. The new certificate is its derivation on controlled acquired posterior laws and its connection through nonsmooth Bellman curvature to fully charged matching curves. |
| R15 / R18 / R32 | Controlled curvature and clock, allocation, normalized Bayesian row/pooling | Retained and explicitly credited. The new source re-proves required arguments and does not count the exponent 2/d or M-n shift as newly discovered. |

## Primary sources actually checked

- Poupart–Boutilier 2002: official NeurIPS full paper, including the exact Bellman-preservation and invariant-subspace statements; PDF theorem page inspected. UAI 2000/2001 publication metadata and archived versions checked.
- Kara–Yuksel 2022: JMLR official article/PDF and arXiv version; finite-memory control and filter-stability comparison checked.
- Demirci–Kara–Yuksel 2024: official SIAM publication metadata, DOI `10.1137/24M1643736`, and authors' arXiv `2312.14111`.
- Kim: primary arXiv `2601.03132`, full HTML; fixed-policy comparison and later control/value claims distinguished, not collapsed into a blanket “no control result.”
- Varshney: author/institution publication entry and DOI `10.1109/TSP.2011.2159500`; direct Bayes-risk/Bregman context.
- Dobrushin: official SIAM entry, Theory of Probability and its Applications 1(1), 65–80, DOI `10.1137/1101006`; common-mass ergodic coefficient is classical.
- Diaz-Infante Velasco, Garcia and Minjarez-Sosa: primary arXiv `2608.13825` abstract/metadata. Full HTML/PDF fetches were unavailable in this inspection. The manuscript therefore states only the abstract-level distinction, not a detailed unverified theorem comparison.
- Classical convex analysis, filter stability, quantization and scoring references: retained bibliography checked against their stated roles; not claimed as newly re-proved external results.

Full identifiers and years are in `references.tex`. URLs/DOIs are scholarly references, not an endorsement that any prior author agrees with the new theorem. This finite search is not a priority certificate, an exhaustive bibliography, or a top-four significance judgment.
