# Proof ledger

Status meanings: **proved in source** means a complete mathematical argument is submitted, not independent or formal certification; **conditional** means explicitly listed hypotheses are indispensable; **open** means not asserted.

| Obligation | Status and exact resolution |
|---|---|
| G1 predictive realization | Conditional on finite executable closure and Borel image/sections; Lemma 2.1 proves equivalence, minimality and joint update descent. These are verified in both raw classes. |
| Controlled comparison | Proved in `prop:identity` and `thm:main` against `V_0`, the infimum over all full-history adaptive strategies of the same physical task. No replacement by same-controller risk. |
| Uniform acquired geometry | Actual rows, not reachable volume, carry the density certificate. Their mixtures remain bounded for arbitrary randomized policies and retained barycenters. |
| Primitive continuation certificate | `prop:corridor` integrates unsuccessful and successful paths at their actual weights. `lem:span` takes a common interval before policy minimization. |
| Nonsmooth decision error | `lem:curvature` extends the convex potential, controls its Laplacian mass and cancels affine terms at actual conditional centroids. No differentiable Bellman action assumption. |
| Recursive executability | `lem:implementation` pools all incoming edges. It stores only a finite label; exact centroid/optimal-action computation is programme data, with extra finite-bit assumptions separately stated. |
| Matching autonomous lower | Last Jensen defect plus support-based phase disjointness. All legal Borel randomized controllers included. |
| Boundary and minimal budget | Density proof includes Gaussian tail/boundary. Integer phases remain nonempty at `M=n+1`; coarse small-budget constructions cover marked and post-control extensions. |
| F3 singular geometry | `thm:strata` and `cor:rank-raw` retain every positive branch weight, use relative dimensions and allow phase cells to merge when width is too small to store every mark. |
| F4 classical | `thm:gaussian` verifies full affine response, density, cover, continuity, common acquired mass and task. Proposition 5.2 supplies a strict action switch and W1-expansive step. |
| F4 quantum | `thm:quantum` verifies the same objects; the block corridor is uniform over all adaptive unitary choices. Proposition 6.3 supplies strict sign-dependent feedback. |
| G3 same-task lower | `thm:joint`: a bit prepared after all control prevents physical encoding by actions. Common product prior makes core, bit and invisible-sign losses add. |
| Calibration vs numerical error | Attained invisible offset has a matching lower; finite numerical tolerances only have the stated perturbation upper. They are not conflated. |
| Simulator | Parameter independent, action/clock/task compatible and complete-law; states multiply and errors add. No reverse lower without reverse simulation. |
| Full intrinsic characterization | Open; acquired-overlap is sufficient, not necessary. |
| Unknown kernels / nonsummable converse | Open outside retained special laws. No universal rate is asserted. |
| Finite programme/workspace/time optimality | Open. The finite-bit upper requires a computation certificate; exact Borel existence alone is not that certificate. |
| Unbounded stopping/infinite horizon | Open for this new theorem. Old stopped results retain their original conditions. |

Finite regression cases check rational identities, integer costs and floating raw formulas. They do not establish any of the continuum rows above. Independent external verification of the submitted arguments remains necessary.
