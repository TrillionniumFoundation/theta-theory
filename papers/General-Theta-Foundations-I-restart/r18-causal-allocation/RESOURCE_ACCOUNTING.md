## Product-task convention

The early bit call is followed by n engine calls. The predictor then halts and fixes its remaining decisions. A scoring authority makes one final paid physical audit, so N=n+2 physical acquisitions and N-1 predictor calls are both explicit. The early bit estimate is emitted to a write-only score port during its call; it cannot be read back as a free register. Only the early action is legal initially and only engine actions afterwards, so one distinct early label is paid. The engine uses at most M-1 labels. The offset is independent and never observed.

The regular matched term is (M-N+1)^(-2/d). The blind exact term is q_floor((M-2)/(N-2))(mu). The actual erasure contributes epsilon/2 in squared bit loss and the offset delta^2; these additive terms share one product task. A different audit protocol or terminal-recall bit requires a different state account and is not silently covered.

## Error composition

Exact action slack and Jensen defects add under the same deployed law. A simulator's complete-law TV adds bounded loss times its defect. Root readout errors must be combined in their stated norm before squaring; they are not arbitrarily added as squared risks. Finite numerical first-mismatch bounds require certified ideal-prefix events or uniform conditional coupling, not a selected successful-trajectory density. Reverse risk comparison requires a reverse certificate covering every policy in the converse class.
