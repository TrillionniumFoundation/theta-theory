# Scope audit — positive claims and remaining mathematical boundary

## Proved in the native manuscript, subject to independent verification

An exact necessary-and-sufficient causal allocation criterion for the compact predictive class is stated and proved, with different tests for external and stationary internal interfaces. It yields equal optimal-risk variational formulas, finite witnesses against impossible proposed allocations, an action-slack/Jensen identity and zero/epsilon completion. For external widths, compactness gives an attained optimum and global finite-test obstruction below it.

The general criterion admits singular/atomic laws, noncontracting updates and arbitrary continuous affine terminal tasks without full curvature. A raw compact-kernel quotient theorem supplies measurable realization and minimality under explicit test-closure assumptions. A separate weak-Feller assumption is retained where needed.

Gaussian categorical filtering and a finite noncommuting IC-measurement forecast verify it from raw kernels. Finite readable changing-rank strata retain actual mass. A separate blind-transport theorem gives an exact noncontracting law for every compactly supported actual acquisition measure, with proved fractal and countable-atomic rates.

Typed morphisms and execution certificates transfer common-task risk with explicit label/call/error accounting. Finite rational experiments admit a proved effective compiler and support-preserving finite-bit implementation. A single product task attains selected acquisition, memory, calibration and deficiency coordinates.

## Not proved or not asserted

The compact quotient assumptions are not automatically satisfied by an arbitrary standard-Borel experiment. The law-valued criterion is not a general computable closed formula, and the finite-test theorem does not give a uniform effective witness size. Arbitrary stationary-kernel global attainment is not asserted. General unknown-kernel frequentist minimax learning is not solved by embedding a Bayes parameter into the hidden state.

No theorem here gives a universal optimal joint region for programme length, workspace, planning time, arithmetic precision, reset count, physical time or minimal simulator state. The finite rational compiler is decidable but can be computationally prohibitive. Exact-real measurable source programmes can lie outside its scope.

Finite-horizon identities are not automatically unbounded-stopping or infinite-horizon results. TV proximity is not LDP equivalence. Finite-dimensional quantum positivity/inversion does not prove an unbounded-operator closure. The separate algebraic-contact, ordered-measurement, streaming, physical-action, pressure and LDP realizations remain independent where no theorem-level connection is proved.

The new allocation criterion is a general theorem over a nontrivial class, not a relabelled Gaussian, quantum or Cantor theorem. Whether its intrinsic reach, proof and proposed novelty meet a leading general mathematics journal's standard is for independent assessment. No acceptance, priority certificate or complete archive re-certification is claimed.
