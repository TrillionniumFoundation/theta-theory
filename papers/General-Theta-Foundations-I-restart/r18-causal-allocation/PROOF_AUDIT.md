# Native proof audit

## Quantifiers checked in the written proofs

The preparation and scoring task are fixed before policy optimization. Legal phase action sets are given independently of a particular candidate controller. The instrument identities hold for mixtures, including compressed states. The continuous value/compact-exhaustion arguments explicitly require weak-Feller acquisition laws. No measurable quotient is asserted merely from an arbitrary equivalence relation.

The external criterion uses actual finite retained laws and their own acquisition mixtures. The autonomous criterion retains one action kernel and one report kernel, tests their simultaneous moments, and charges the initial and halt states. Conditional q_t,z may vary with phase but cannot be read by the runtime machine. Report randomness and fresh controller coins do not provide a free persistent tape.

Finite-coordinate separation is applied to compact convex images of weak-star allocation simplexes. The global external obstruction is a separate compactness argument over all candidate flows; it does not assert that one fixed affine witness certifies all bad programmes. Infimum and attainment are distinguished for the autonomous problem and for zero-risk feasibility.

Matching regular rates use a policy-uniform actual-law upper density, not a reachable-set packing. Norms, volume, task curvature and the explicit block constants are visible. Singular atoms can be split randomly by the exact criterion. The finite-stratum lower keeps unnormalized mass. Blind transport lower uses information at every cut and the actual acquisition law, with exact floor accounting at small budgets.

Numerical and simulator propositions compare the complete scored law in a common task. Error tolerances do not automatically generate lower bounds. The finite rational compiler preserves zero transition support, hence does not turn a near-correct deadline into an inadmissible one. Product physical audit timing differs from predictor stopping and is stated in both theorem and proof.

## What this audit does not certify

This is an author-side audit of the written arguments, not a formal proof-assistant certificate, independent referee approval or novelty certificate. Functional analytic separation, disintegration and real-closed-field decision are standard external tools explicitly attributed. Finite regression only checks selected exact/numerical instances and implementation invariants.

The proposed general theorem covers compact metrizable predictive carriers with affine unnormalized instruments, not all Borel models. It supplies a law-valued necessary-and-sufficient frontier, not an automatically evaluable scalar rate formula. General stationary global-attainment/computable-witness bounds, unknown-kernel frequentist minimax learning, unbounded stopping and the optimal complete computational-resource region remain unproved here. This is a scope boundary of concrete theorems, not a deletion or relabeling of the mother problem.
