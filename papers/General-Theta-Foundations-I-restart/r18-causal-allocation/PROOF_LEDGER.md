# Proof ledger

Every native formal result has a proof in the native text; standard tools are named, not treated as novelty. This ledger records obligations rather than asserting independent formal verification.

## General carrier and allocation

A.1: raw compact state plus continuous future-test closure gives a compact convex image of preparations; a countable family separates exactly the declared tests and legal interfaces. The Borel section is constructed through nested finite closed-ball covers. Joint conditional coordinates use standard-Borel disintegration. Equality of unnormalized test/report measures descends the predictive update. Mixtures are physical mixtures of preparations, not a claim that every carrier point is reached by the selected policy. Weak Feller of the normalized acquisition-law map is an additional hypothesis, separately verified in applications.

4.1: weak-star compact simplex in finite products of L-infinity; continuous moment image in a product of affine coordinates; finite-coordinate separation; Borel maximizing index. Atomic input can require a randomized split. Convex order is neither deterministic pointwise reachability nor an uncharged information source.

4.2 and 3.1(a): all incoming labels/actions/reports are mixed before conditional barycenters are computed. Forward construction avoids circularity. Duplicate predictive atoms may be merged using conditional action randomization. The constructed exact programme can be noncomputable in the general measurable class; its label cardinality alone is what is bounded.

5.1 and 3.1(b): use one dominating measure per incoming label/action, summed over phases, and its Radon–Nikodym weights. A single simplex kernel is optimized for all phase moments. The maximum must remain outside the phase sum. The induction certifies actual occupancies and legal exact stopping. Zero-mass states and null report events are completed measurably without changing the law.

3.1(d): the one-step identity separates action slack from loss under barycentric contraction. Each is nonnegative by Bellman minimality/concavity. Telescoping is under the deployed law, not a shadow controller or unretained filter. Corollary 3.2 distinguishes exact existence from zero infimum.

4.4: the candidate set uses measures on K_t x A_t with marginal support at most m_t. It is compact, even when atoms collide. Finite actions and weak-Feller acquisition make every constraint continuous. A countable rational finite-coordinate polyhedral family tests all continuous convex functions. Nested nonempty compact feasible relaxations give increasing minima with the true limit; finite intersection gives a finite family excluding every candidate below a strict target. No computable exhaustion speed or global stationary compactness is asserted.

## Quantitative and raw realization proofs

5.3: equivalence of actual/reference finite trace laws uses the same action and next-label programme and erased randomness. Mixtures over conditional physical states preserve null sets. A reused positive label would force early stopping. Exact conversion follows by disjoint phase supports and the reverse phase-label construction. A known time stamp invalidates only this converse, not 3.1(b).

6.2: the supporting-plane supremum extends the negative continuation value to a globally convex Lipschitz function. Centers may be outside K. Submean/divergence bounds, mollification and enlarged closed balls control curvature at boundaries. A compact cutoff gives total positive Laplacian mass; acquired density and bounded grid overlap yield the square rate. This entire mechanism is inherited from R15 and re-proved.

6.3/6.1: each phase receives one baseline label; m=1 and zero weights treated separately. Incomplete blocks cost max(1,B^(b-1)). Norm-comparison constants are fixed first. The lower conditions on the actual final action/report under any policy; randomized terminal labels have at most their alphabet count of barycenters. Strong task curvature and small-ball mass are separate requirements.

6.4: finite report-readable affine types; no normalized conditional-law shortcut. If there are J types, use the coarse one-cell estimate for L<2J, and for L>=2J reserve one cell per type and divide the rest in fixed fractions among positive-dimensional types. Constants depend on J and the type carriers; the terminal r-dimensional submass contributes kappa*w^(1+2/r)*rho_*^(-2/r). This statement is not a general countable-stratum theorem.

7.2: Gaussian contrast covariance nondegeneracy, transition floor and compact parameter bounds give global posterior-density bounds through the logit/softmax Jacobian, including faces approached through unbounded reports. Distinct exponential contrasts and one invertible transition separate predictive states. No clipping or conditioning cost is removed.

7.3: normalized positive congruence has an explicit positive inverse, full differential and one-to-one report parametrization. Compact positivity bounds its Jacobian away from zero. Hilbert–Schmidt affine volume, null cube boundary, finite dimension and spanning effects are fixed. Standard informationally complete outcome prediction supplies strong task curvature.

## Singular and resource proofs

8.1: after capture the conditional law of the terminal output given any retained label is generated without additional X-information. Thus X -> Z_t -> output at every cut and every intermediate alphabet has the q_L lower. For the internal model the capture-only initial action prohibits initial-label reuse; blind later reports and exact stopping prohibit reuse of any later cut label. Quantize once and retain copies to attain the lower. The carrier is P(E), not a formally prescribed finite manifold.

8.2: separated Cantor prefixes give covering and actual small-ball estimates. Countably atomic x_j=2^(-j) have disjoint x_j/8 balls; L centers miss one of the first L+1 balls. Exact first L-1 atoms plus a tail label prove the matching exponential upper. Constants depend on the specified preparation parameters.

9.1/9.2: complete stopped/scored-law TV plus bounded loss; product state count. First mismatch bounds use the ideal acquired law before conditioning on success of earlier comparisons. Coordinate boundary probabilities are capped at one; output error has its own modulus. Reverse lower requires a reverse comparison covering every relevant policy.

9.3: enumerate finite discrete machine components and express every legal/exact-halt path restriction as a nonnegative rational polynomial. Compact feasible set and standard real-algebraic decision yield an algebraic minimizer. Rounding within each row's original support cannot activate an impossible path. Row TV error, finite-horizon coupling, programme storage, temporary counter and rational-coin sampling are separately counted. No efficient or optimal-bit theorem follows from decidability.

9.4: early bit and offset are independent of the engine and their losses add under one task. Early output is write-only. The final audit is a charged scorer call after predictor halt, hence N physical vs N-1 predictor transitions. Deficiency exactly epsilon follows from two-point TV contraction/triangle and fair guessing. The offset is inaccessible, not a learned unknown kernel.
