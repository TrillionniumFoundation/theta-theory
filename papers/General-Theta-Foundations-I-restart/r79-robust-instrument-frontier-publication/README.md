## What changed mathematically

The central additional argument is **controller-label regularization at the same memory budget**. With a fixed hidden instrument model and an all-initial-state expected-cost bound, a small chance of returning only the controller label to its existing initial label yields globally bounded cost witnesses without resetting the physical system or adding labels. This leads to an explicit perturbation bound for optimal cost, simultaneously for every finite label budget, even when reachable supports change. The theorem states its positive cost floor, cost ceiling, model-pairing quantifiers and small-error domain. The unrestricted-history infimum is reached as a limit of the finite-label values, with a quantified prefix-completion bound.

Under the additional hypothesis that every command and model has success effect at least `kappa I`, a separate resolvent argument gives Lipschitz dependence `zeta/kappa + c_max eta/kappa^2`, as well as an exponentially small truncation error in history horizon. Scalar experiments establish the local sharpness of the displayed Lipschitz coefficients. They do not prove that the general square-root modulus is sharp.

A separate exact two-hidden-model experiment has values `V_1 = 2`, `V_2 = (1 + sqrt(5))/2` and `V_M = 3/2` for `M >= 3`. Its lower bound permits arbitrary randomized label updates and charges the pure initial label. This is an exact special model, not a universal frontier. One model has an unreachable dark label, illustrating why the support-sensitive certificates must not be replaced by full-space transience of the original policy.

The support, cost-potential, dark-subspace, finite-model semialgebraic and related verification results are explicitly identified as inherited or classical where applicable. They are reproduced with their correct quantifier order and combined with the new regularization chain. The nonreset setting alone is not presented as a new result. The article retains the prior reset, optimal discrimination, variational/dual, communication, rank, shared-preparation, approximation, spectral, geometric and causal-history results at their actual scope. No independent A1/A2 or larger Theta programme is declared complete.
