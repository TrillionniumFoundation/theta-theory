\begin{abstract}
We prove an acquired-geometry transfer theorem for positive causal instruments with state-dependent likelihoods and finite executable test closure. Bayesian normalization replaces the transported quadratic energy by a convex perspective potential. The loss of predictive energy has an exact decomposition into its conditional Jensen defect, within-row quantization, and the additional defect of pooling incompatible predecessor centroids. This gives a converse for every Borel randomized finite-state observer. Actual conditional acquisition mass and a global covering certificate, together with either integrated-curvature stability or stable posterior transport, give matching checkpoint and online laws. With an internally enforced deadline of $n$ acquisitions and $M$ total states, the excess is comparable to $(M-n)^{-2/d}$, uniformly in both budgets; the checkpoint excess is comparable to $M^{-2/d}$. The dimension is verified from the acquired report map. Positive Gaussian hidden-state filters and informative noncommuting quantum instruments verify the same theorem, without Haar report laws. Periodically positivity-improving quantum instruments need not contract at each step. A full-rank/pure-state instrument mixture yields a rank-changing, actual-mass resource profile. We also prove a matched calibration--deficiency extension in which retaining an independently acquired bit changes the internal-clock charge, and give separate finite-precision and causal-simulator certificates.
\end{abstract}
