# Proof audit

## Adversarial checks of the arguments

1. **Bayes weighting:** conditional law of the current report is ell(y,E[X|S]), not a report law frozen independently of X. The proof integrates U and ell before normalization. The scalar informative-bit example would detect the incorrect neutral-transport substitution.
2. **Three defects:** the Jensen term is exactly the residual conditioned on old label plus current report; row and pooling terms are separately nonnegative. No subtraction of incomparable full-history baselines occurs.
3. **Randomization:** all updates may use fresh coins. Conditional nearest-center minimization includes fractional cells. Persistent seeds and decoder transcripts are not free. Halt status is a declared property of a state; randomness may enter its transition.
4. **Global implementation:** optimal row codebooks are not assumed mutually compatible. C uses common cells and bounds row+pool together; P stores an actual codepoint label and follows the true report path.
5. **Clock:** equivalent supports, not equal report probabilities, imply phase-independent possible edges. Reports continue until the machine halts. The proof is for a prescribed exact horizon, not arbitrary stopping.
6. **Kernel geometry:** Gaussian log odds and the quantum report Jacobian derive dimensions from actual conditional laws. Lower constants retain positivity/invertibility/rank dependencies. No Gaussian tail is conditioned away.
7. **Cone metric domains:** Gaussian post-transition K has an eigen/component floor; quantum post-improvement K has a spectral floor. Finite bounded-gap nonimproving phases preserve a uniform smaller floor. Congruence is an isometry, not a contraction by assertion.
8. **Changing rank:** in the singular instrument the channel comes before the informative/pure branch; the C proof is separate from P. The pure branch's potential is affine while its actual mass is nonzero. P is not applied at pure boundary states.
9. **Retained bit:** core physical evolution is passive and independent of U. At a singleton phase no physical feedback has stored U elsewhere, and no future report/audit before commitment can recover it. Action compatibility separates the initial bit state from later filter states. This is essential to the 2n+1 count.
10. **Errors:** arbitrary allowed calibration/deficiency budgets provide upper comparisons only. The matched lower uses an actual erased bit and inaccessible offset; the protected audit cannot be simulated away. TV convention and bounded-loss range are explicit.
11. **Effective implementation:** exact Borel constants are not finite bit programs. Boundary, tail and certified arithmetic conditions are separate. Numerical tests never replace these inequalities.
12. **Provenance:** R11/R15/R29 tools are credited. The same exponent is not counted as new merely because it is rederived. New passive-protocol results are not promoted to the earlier adaptive-control quantifiers.
