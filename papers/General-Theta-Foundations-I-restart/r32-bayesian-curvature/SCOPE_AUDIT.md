# Scope audit

## Claimed on stated hypotheses

- Exact likelihood-aware conditional Jensen/row/pooling decomposition for positive raw instruments with finite executable affine test closure, including all Borel randomized updates.
- Matching actual checkpoint and full internal-clock finite-memory curves under explicitly declared actual mass, global cover and either curvature-product or same-report stability.
- Passive Gaussian hidden-state forecasting for every positive invertible transition; noncommuting weak effects followed by every strictly improving stated channel; bounded-gap noncontracting phases.
- A proved rank-changing acquired measure with actual full/pure mixture weights and its weighted resource law.
- Same-task complete-law simulation and conditional-boundary precision upper; an actual erased-bit/offset alternative with matching retained-bit phase count.

## Not claimed

- Every positive experiment has a finite-dimensional Borel predictive quotient or global stability.
- A fixed passive-protocol theorem is unrestricted optimal control, unknown-kernel learning or optimal at-most-N static experimental design.
- Every local row optimizer has a compatible global recursion, or a global cover is necessary.
- The same exponent is new simply because it is repeated from R15; inherited tools are credited.
- The nonsummable Bayesian case has a matching weighted lower, or the new stable theorem subsumes the exact Haar circle accumulation law.
- Uniform constants survive zero transition probabilities, singular likelihood rank, lambda -> 1, or vanishing physical contrast.
- A quantum system is free classical-observer memory; a finite-dimensional identity is an unbounded-operator theorem.
- Finite bit workspace/program/time optima follow from hard-label lower bounds.
- TV implies LDP, fixed horizon implies arbitrary stopping, or a one-way simulator gives a reverse minimax comparison.
- Build/regression/reconstruction equals proof certification, full historical re-refereeing, novelty or top-four acceptance.

These exclusions are proof boundaries, not a change of the General Theta mother problem. The native article remains a theorem-and-proof research manuscript rather than a framework or roadmap.
