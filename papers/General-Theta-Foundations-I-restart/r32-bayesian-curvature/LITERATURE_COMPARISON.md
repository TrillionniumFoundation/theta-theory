# Literature comparison and novelty audit

Search date: 11 October 2026. Primary publisher, author, and institutional sources were checked. The absence of an exact search match is not a priority certificate; publication significance remains an independent referee question. The dated bibliography uses the published Ghomi--Linder--Yuksel (2022) and Rosenblatt--Roychowdhury (2023) versions. SIAM historical online dates are not substituted for original volume years.

## Exact credit boundaries

Standard tools are conditional expectation/projection, quadratic perspectives, Bregman identities, finite quantization, global grids, projective contraction, Gaussian density change of variables, and elementary coupling.

Internal inheritance is separately stronger than an external-literature citation: R11 already proves affine posterior pooling; R15 already contains a state-dependent curvature/pooling resource theorem, the full-tail Gaussian density argument, and the positive-congruence inverse. R29 provides the state-independent row/pooling energy identity and exact circle clock law. The current revision does not relabel their conclusions as new.

The newly written mathematical connection is the normalized raw-instrument conditional Jensen identity with explicit likelihood-weighted Hessian, the distinct row and merging defects, and the same-report alternative which avoids the old sufficient `3*kappa<1` condition. The all-observer matching proof is written for its declared stable class. Weighted changing-rank occupation and the counted retained-bit joint task provide additional consequences. The old matched exponent by itself is not the claimed novelty, and neither is the matrix inverse or filter contraction by itself.

No proof here classifies every causal experiment, settles an independently verified named open problem, establishes first priority, or proves that a journal will accept the paper.

## Verified primary records

### Graf--Luschgy (2000)

Foundations of Quantization for Probability Distributions. publisher monograph.

https://doi.org/10.1007/BFb0103945

Static fixed-cardinality squared-error quantization; this paper credits the standard centroid and small-ball tools, not a new quantization theory.

### Boyd--Vandenberghe (2004)

Convex Optimization. author monograph site.

https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/

The quadratic perspective is a standard convex construction. The experiment-level conditional identity uses it, rather than claiming perspective convexity anew.

### Gneiting--Raftery (2007)

Strictly proper scoring rules, prediction, and estimation. journal.

https://doi.org/10.1198/016214506000001437

Proper-score and entropy/Bregman connections are standard; the new decomposition is specialized to an actually executed finite observer and its raw likelihood.

### Borkar--Mitter--Tatikonda (2001)

Optimal Sequential Vector Quantization of Markov Sources. SIAM journal.

https://doi.org/10.1137/S0363012999365261

Sequential vector quantization of a stationary Markov source is cast as partially observed stochastic control and analyzed by dynamic programming. This is a direct methodological neighbor; our observer receives a raw report, not the hidden source point, and the converse is a hard-total-state terminal task bound.

### Foster--Gray--Dunham (1985)

Finite-state vector quantization for waveform coding. IEEE journal.

https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.1985.1057035

Finite-state vector quantization already uses state-selected codebooks and recursive next-state rules. We do not claim the finite-state coding architecture is new. The likelihood reweighting and merged-row conditional centroids are the specific comparison objects.

### Pages--Sagna (2015)

Recursive marginal quantization of the Euler scheme of a diffusion process. journal and author preprint arXiv:1304.2531.

https://doi.org/10.1080/1350486X.2015.1091741

Recursive marginal quantization controls error propagation for approximated Markov dynamics. Our common-cover upper is adjacent; the all-Borel converse, physical likelihood law and internal halt-state count are separate assertions.

### Stavrou--Kourtellaris--Charalambous (2014)

Information Nonanticipative Rate Distortion Function and Its Applications. author research preprint.

https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.1593

Nonanticipative rate-distortion theory charges an information quantity on causal reconstruction laws, rather than every persistent label in one autonomous machine. No equivalence of those budgets is asserted.

### Stavrou--Charalambous--Charalambous--Loyka (2018)

Optimal estimation via nonanticipative rate distortion function and applications to time-varying Gauss--Markov processes. SIAM journal.

https://doi.org/10.1137/17M1116349

Finite-horizon causal filtering and nonanticipative distortion allocations are directly adjacent. The manuscript distinguishes directed-information or rate constraints from a total number of phase, update and halt labels.

### Tanaka--Kim--Parrilo--Mitter (2017)

Semidefinite programming approach to Gaussian sequential rate-distortion trade-offs. author preprint; published IEEE TAC DOI 10.1109/TAC.2016.2601148.

https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.7632

Gaussian sequential rate-distortion has semidefinite formulations. Our Gaussian realization is a finite hidden-state model with continuous Gaussian likelihoods, not a replacement for Gaussian-source rate-distortion results.

### Atar--Zeitouni (1997)

Exponential stability for nonlinear filtering. journal archive.

https://www.numdam.org/item/AIHPB_1997__33_6_697_0/

Projective/cone contraction is an established filter-stability method. The manuscript supplies elementary estimates and combines them with actual row mass and a counted finite observer; stability alone is not claimed new.

### Kara--Yuksel (2022)

Near Optimality of Finite Memory Feedback Policies in Partially Observed Markov Decision Processes. journal.

https://www.jmlr.org/beta/papers/v23/20-1152.html

Finite-window feedback approximation and POMDP control are established. Our passive fixed-protocol theorem does not supersede adaptive feedback optimality or turn a counted-label budget into a window-length budget.

### Ghomi--Linder--Yuksel (2022)

Zero-delay lossy coding of linear vector Markov sources: optimality of stationary codes and near optimality of finite memory codes. IEEE journal; arXiv:2103.10810.

https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.2021.3138769

The published 2022 article, not merely its preprint, is cited. Its stationary/finite-memory zero-delay coding conclusions have different observations, horizon and resource variables.

### Rosenblatt--Roychowdhury (2023)

Uniform distributions on curves and quantization. author institutional archive linking published DOI 10.4134/CKMS.c210434.

https://scholarworks.utrgv.edu/mss_fac/408/

The static uniform-circle coefficient is credited to existing exact quantization work. The retained recursive product and internal deadline law are separate inherited results, not new static geometry.
