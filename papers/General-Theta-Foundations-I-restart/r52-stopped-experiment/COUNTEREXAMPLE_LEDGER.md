# Counterexample / nonimplication ledger

**Missing residual-risk mark.** A fair hidden sign and an uninformative constant report have zero proxy quantization loss at one center, but conditional squared risk one. A zero-mark profile is invalid unless recovery/calibration is proved.

**No model-uniform variance lower without fresh uncertainty.** Let q=0 and keep one hidden sign forever with repeated informative Gaussian reports. Full histories can accumulate evidence; on histories with a very concentrated posterior, the conditional variance after a bounded current report is arbitrarily smaller than a fixed current-report mark. The history-uniform c_v certificate fails. This is an assumption witness, not a no-go theorem for a different acquired-history geometry.

**Memory cannot erase acquisition uncertainty.** In the regular marked class with fixed N, conditional low-variance mass still limits risk by a positive multiple of N^(-2/alpha), regardless of terminal dictionary size. Using only the Nm term is false.

**Critical mass cannot be rescaled away.** In the revealing Gaussian experiment, exact zero risk occurs at beta N=1 for two-output/three-state observers. Replacing N by an envelope multiple can cross this phase transition. Hence the central comparison keeps explicit rescaled costs and the critical theorem has its own exact mass proof.

**Two states are not two outputs.** For beta>0 and symmetric q, one live plus one terminal has zero-risk cost 2/beta+kappa; one live plus two terminal labels has cost 1/beta. A free controller would erase a genuine resource distinction.

**Task TV does not bound expected stopping time.** Identical constant scored output may accompany time one, or time one except on a probability 1/k event of length k^3. Task laws agree while expected times diverge. A mean-time certificate is independent.

**Proxy versus quotient.** Hidden-state/quantum histories with the same current Y,T,V can have different posterior residuals and therefore different future tests. They share a proxy, not the full predictive class. The risk comparison uses an invariant envelope, not false sufficiency.

**Inherited local/global and stability witnesses.** R51 appendices A and C retain the fair-bit/random-query obstruction and both quantitative failures of the older curvature/pathwise certificates. R52 does not rederive them as new results, remove them, or confuse their hypotheses with marked mass regularity.
