The exact continuous certificate uses a deterministic two-state overwrite table and rational forecasts 7/16,9/16. Its geometric scoring convention has expected two calls, not a two-call internal stopping algorithm. The quantum system belongs to the physical experiment; no additional quantum observer memory is silently included.

A scored quantum run performs exactly one terminal audit at its selected scoring call. The common physical continuing instrument determines earlier reports. Unperformed incompatible audits are not treated as jointly observable outcomes; an audit actually affecting later state must be included and charged in the physical kernel.
