# Proof audit

## Critical checks made in this revision

- The controlling hypotheses are kernel-level positivity/normalization, continuing legality and finite-history product domination, not a supplied Fisher/covariance matrix.
- Product domination is for every finite action word and theta; one-time marginal domination is insufficient. The tensor argument uses different coordinates and a common-null-set Borel completion.
- Action/readout/update rows are fixed before both time and parameter optimization. The parameter is not reselected by an adversary at each call.
- Finite hidden histories under adaptive actions are generated by the same raw kernels; the open-loop-word expansion is an exact change-of-measure identity, not an unrelated acquisition law.
- Compactness applies to Borel row equivalence classes; representatives are selected on one common reference-null set. The exact result has no finite syntactic-program promise.
- Infinite discounted risk is a uniform limit for each model. Robust lsc attainment and finite-intersection model witnesses do not require exchanging inf and sup.
- Effective lower controls every Borel controller through a complete approximation comparison. Report and row errors subtract on the lower side; the model-net modulus belongs on the upper side. N truncates score analysis, not the deployed machine.
- Hidden-state domination is proved by conditional disintegration, including report-dependent causal transitions; no false conditional independence of the complete hidden path is used.
- The unstable-dynamics proof assumes a consistent causal coupling and a matched-report, uniform-in-decision score bound. An unconditional fixed-readout loss bound would be insufficient.
- A scored quantum prefix has one actually executable terminal audit. No joint classical path of incompatible audit outcomes and no non-disturbing repeated measurement are assumed; any audit physically performed before another call must enter the raw instrument.
- Quantum estimates are made on unnormalized finite-dimensional positive instruments. Projective zeros do not create an inverse denominator; no unbounded operator claim is made.
- Blind minimax lower uses the actual family of preparation laws and common centers. Its nondominated clause has a direct proof, not an illicit application of the product-domination theorem.
- Score-prefix simulation includes the target outcome, task readout, clock and action compatibility. The physical-time and virtual-call indices remain distinct.
- The continuous certificate's generic report error and special value-rounding result are not conflated. Q=2 does not give a small generic row-coupling bound. The exact lower is independently analytic.

## Results not certified by this audit

This is an author-side mathematical audit, not formal verification or an independent priority/journal certificate. The claimed proofs may still be challenged by external review. It does not close arbitrary nondominated control, average cost, unknown-kernel learning, or full multi-resource optimality. Historical preservation and repeated builds do not re-prove all previous manuscripts.
