# Literature and priority comparison

The native article cites published versions where verified: Witsenhausen (MCSS 1988, doi:10.1007/BF02551232); Yüksel--Saldi (SICON 2017, doi:10.1137/15M1049129); Yüksel (SICON 2020, doi:10.1137/18M1221382); Hogeboom-Burr--Yüksel (AMO 2023, doi:10.1007/s00245-023-09965-5); Cubuktepe et al. (AAAI 2021, doi:10.1609/aaai.v35i13.17401); Andriushchenko et al. (PMLR 180, 2022); Kara--Yüksel (JMLR 23, 2022, no.11); Yu--Bertsekas (MOR 2008, doi:10.1287/moor.1070.0279).

The independent-reference response expansion is closely related to Witsenhausen static reduction. Compact strategic-measure/controller methods and existence are established. Robust finite-state control and fixed model-family common controllers are established. Finite-intersection compactness, discount tails, simplex rounding, finite enumeration and scalar quantization are standard tools, not individually claimed new.

The candidate contribution is the explicit combination: one fixed reused row, arbitrary fixed-model robust family, product-history-dominated continuous reports, no finite-dimensional hidden presentation, attained discounted value and finite-model identity, plus a lower comparison against all Borel rows at unchanged M and a finite implementing table under supplied response moduli. The article proves this combination instead of citing a general strategic-measure theorem as if it automatically preserved stationary row reuse.

The natural nonlinear consequence is a controller-uniform discount/expansion modulus with critical and Hölder regimes, verified for logistic dynamics with moving interval support. It is not a claim to settle a recognized general nonlinear-filter memory problem. Average-cost and increasing-window approximation results concern different hypotheses and comparison classes and are acknowledged explicitly.

This is a targeted primary-literature comparison, not an exhaustive novelty search or an independent priority certificate. The earlier native bibliography remains intact in companion A; retaining it does not require duplicating all 29 entries in the new article.
