\begin{abstract}
A finite observer of an uncertain causal experiment must use the same update rule at every time and at every inaccessible model parameter. We prove a discounted infinite-horizon transfer theorem for this common controller, starting from the report and payoff laws of finite interventions. Finite-history product domination makes the response of every fixed-cardinality controller continuous in a compact topology, even with infinite-dimensional hidden dynamics and changing report supports. Consequently the robust optimum is attained, finite model families determine its value, and finite report tables give two-sided certificates at the unchanged persistent-state budget under effective response data. No finite-dimensional predictive carrier, filter contraction, or equivalent active report supports is required for this continuing interface. Nonlinear hidden-state kernels and noncommuting projective instruments verify the theorem independently. A quantitative consequence gives sharp discount--expansion moduli for unstable hidden dynamics. Singular acquired laws yield exact blind-memory curves, including a nondominated preparation class. A continuous-report example supplies a complete rational certificate. Calibration, numerical error, causal deficiency, persistent labels, program description, workspace, acquisitions and physical time are accounted for separately. Discounted continuation is not an exact internal deadline or an undiscounted average-cost assertion.
\end{abstract}
