There are no reverse arrows from a realization to the general theorem. Compact predictive-quotient closure, finite-horizon allocation, regular M-power laws and exact-deadline clock laws remain complete in companion A, with their own hypotheses. They are not invoked to prove raw-response compactness. Geometric discount is an external scoring convention, not a construction of an internal free clock.
