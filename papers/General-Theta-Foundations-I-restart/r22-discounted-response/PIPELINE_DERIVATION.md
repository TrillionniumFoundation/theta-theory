# Pipeline derivation

1. Fix prepared causal kernels, all legal continuing actions, score timing, controller memory and hidden fixed parameter. Separate readout from feedback.
2. For each finite open-loop action word derive its report law and positive payoff submeasure. The finite action-word expansion reconstructs the actual adaptive law of every fixed reused controller.
3. Prove product-history domination and L1 continuity of payoff in the compact decision. Do not replace this by marginal domination or by a finite-dimensional belief assumption.
4. Put one row per controller-state/action in a weak-star simplex. Finite sums of rectangle indicators prove tensor continuity at distinct report coordinates, even for repeated copies of the same row.
5. Uniformly bound discounted tails by beta^N. Compactness gives robust attainment for an arbitrary fixed-model set. Closed common-controller risk sublevel sets give finite-model value equality without a minimax interchange.
6. Average each row once under a common finite partition, using the actual raw payoff responses. Group by final output to obtain the explicit E_N bound.
7. Round stochastic rows and common readouts. Since all actions remain legal forever, no equal-support certificate is needed for legality. Count the entire finite list at the same M.
8. Certified evaluations on a model net give global lower and implementing upper; distinguish report, row, model, evaluation and temporal errors. Keep all offline costs explicit.
9. Verify nonlinear observation kernels and noncommuting instruments independently. Use raw couplings for unstable dynamics and raw positive-map differences for rank-changing quantum reports.
10. Extract genuine acquired-law geometry where extra mass/cover assumptions hold. A direct blind theorem covers arbitrary actual preparation families, including nondominated ones; do not infer nondominated general compactness from it.
11. Compose typed scored-prefix simulators and numerical errors by discounted coupling. Preserve state products and source-call costs. Prove actual erasure and calibration alternatives for matching error terms.
12. Instantiate all finite certificate entries for a continuous family and separately prove its all-Borel quantization lower. Keep ordinary source, build artifacts and final read-only evidence at distinct immutable commits.
