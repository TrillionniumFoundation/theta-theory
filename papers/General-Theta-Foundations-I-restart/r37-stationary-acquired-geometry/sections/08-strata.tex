\section{Visible strata and changing predictive rank}\label{sec:strata}
A predictive carrier can have full affine dimension while its actual acquisitions occupy smaller pieces. The weights of those pieces belong to the experiment, not to a normalized geometric surrogate.

\begin{theorem}[Weighted stationary acquired profile]\label{thm:strata}
Replace the full-dimensional row-density condition by the following finite visible decomposition. Each report has a visible mark $j\in\{1,\ldots,J\}$ with a fixed actual probability $w_j>0$, $\sum_jw_j=1$, independent of the incoming predictive state and action. Conditional on $j$, its acquired law is supported on a compact convex $K_j\subset K$ in an affine subspace of dimension $d_j\ge1$, with relative density at most $\Lambda_j$, uniformly over $u\in D,a\in A$. Allow additionally finitely many marked atom states. Assume the same acquired overlap, interior buffer, raw minorization and effective boundary-free relative grids. Put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:xi}
 \Xi(M)=\sum_{j:d_j>0}w_j M^{-2/d_j}.
\end{equation}
Assume at least one positive-dimensional component. For all $M$ above a fixed threshold depending on the finite decomposition, the stationary optimal excess and checkpoint excess are comparable to $\Xi(M)$. There is one homogeneous observer with finite-acquisition upper
\[
 R_n-J_*(n)\le C\left(\Xi(M)+\frac1{n\Xi(M)}\right),
\]
and every $M$-label observer has lower $c\Xi(M)-C/n$. The upper matches the lower for $n\ge C_*\Xi(M)^{-2}$. Actual weights are retained; constants may depend on the fixed stratum certificates but not on $n,M$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The overlap proof of the Bellman bias does not use full-dimensional density. Strong concavity and a common Lipschitz bound on $K$ remain valid, as does raw ensemble contraction. For the upper reserve one erasure label, one label for each atom, and divide the remaining labels among the finitely many positive-dimensional marked grids. Each gets a fixed positive fraction of $M$ once $M$ exceeds the threshold. Apply the fixed-point correspondence with these marked grids. Every positive-mass cell centroid lies in its convex $K_j$. The corresponding indicator integrals have closed graphs because the conditional row charges no relative cell boundary; labels on different marks are kept distinct. The stationary Jensen gap is bounded by
\[
 C\sum_{d_j>0}w_j m_j^{-2/d_j}+\alpha H\le C\Xi(M)+\alpha H.
\]
Atoms have zero cell gap and cause no division by $d_j=0$. Choose a dyadic erasure rate comparable to $\min\{1/2,\Xi(M)\}$. Ensemble contraction and the preceding proof give the entrance term.

For the converse, any global set of $M$ reconstruction points has conditional distortion at least $c_jM^{-2/d_j}$ on component $j$. Orthogonal projection of these points into its affine span can only decrease distances, so the relative small-ball proof applies even to off-stratum codepoints. Multiply by the actual $w_j$ and sum. Strong Bellman concavity and \eqref{eq:bellman-loss} give the dynamic lower. The same argument at the acquired random cut proves the checkpoint lower; marked grids give its upper. Mixture weights are neither discarded in a conditioning step nor replaced by weights of a formal reachable union.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[A raw quantum rank-changing realization]\label{cor:rank}
After the instrument in Theorem~\ref{thm:quantum}, independently choose a visible mark $j$ with probability $w_j$ and apply a trace-preserving unital conditional expectation $\mathcal E_j$ onto a matrix subalgebra. Include the identity map with positive weight; the completely depolarizing map may be one mark. Theorem~\ref{thm:strata} applies with $d_j$ the affine dimension of the trace-one part of that subalgebra, and with an atom for the completely depolarizing mark.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The raw marked instrument is $w_j\mathcal E_jL_a(H)$ and is completely positive and normalized after summing marks. Each conditional expectation fixes $\tau$ and is an orthogonal projection in Hilbert--Schmidt coordinates. It maps $K$ into the compact convex affine section $K_j$. Projecting the full-dimensional bounded row density of Theorem~\ref{thm:quantum}, Fubini's theorem bounds the relative density by that upper bound times the maximal volume of the compact fibers. A fixed injective audit chart changes only finite Jacobian constants. The rank-zero map gives exactly $\tau$. Thus each genuine branch mass remains $w_j$.

The raw minorization is preserved because $\mathcal E_j\tau=\tau$. Restricting to the event that all $b$ marks in the quiet-report overlap proof are the identity gives an actual common mass multiplied by $w_{\rm id}^{\,b}>0$. This event is used as a lower-mass witness, not as an uncharged conditioned experiment. All reports and all marks execute on every run. Relative grids have zero conditional boundary mass. The predictive acquired rank changes between, for example, the full density body and a dephased diagonal body; the physical matrices here remain strictly positive. This does not claim a physical eigenvalue-rank change or extend the theorem to arbitrary pure-state boundaries. The separate pure-rank result is preserved, with its original assumptions, in the Bayesian companion.
\end{proof}
