\section{A globally calibrated invariant ensemble}\label{sec:calibration}
\begin{lemma}[Stationary calibration with charged erasure]\label{lem:fixedpoint}
Fix a grid $\mathcal P_m=\{C_1,\ldots,C_m\}$ as in Lemma~\ref{lem:curvature} and $0<\alpha\le1/2$. There is one Borel homogeneous observer with labels $0,1,\ldots,m$ and an invariant normalized ensemble $(z_s)$ such that its positive-mass labels have their actual conditional predictive means $q_s$, its action distributions are supported on $\arg\min_aK_ah(q_s)$, and it erases to label $0$ independently with probability $\alpha$ after each report. Nonerased updates send $F_a(y,q_s)$ to its common grid cell. Zero-mass labels require neither a conditional mean nor a division by zero.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $C_K=\{w(1,q):w\ge0,q\in K\}$. The set
\[
 \mathcal E=\{(z_0,\ldots,z_m)\in C_K^{m+1}:\sum_se(z_s)=1\}
\]
is compact and convex. At $z_s=w_s(1,q_s)$ with $w_s>0$, allow every probability vector $p_s$ supported on the finite minimizing set $\arg\min_aK_ah(q_s)$. At $w_s=0$, allow every $p_s$ and use any $q_s\in K$; the contribution of this row is defined to be zero. For fixed $z$ and an allowed choice $p$, set
\begin{align}
 \mathcal F_0(z,p)&=\alpha\sum_{s,a}p_{sa}\bar L_az_s,\label{eq:fixed0}\\
 \mathcal F_j(z,p)&=(1-\alpha)\sum_{s,a}p_{sa}
 \int\one_{\{F_a(y,q_s)\in C_j\}}L_a(y)z_s\,d\lambda_a(y),
 \quad 1\le j\le m.\label{eq:fixedj}
\end{align}
These vectors lie in $C_K$, and their masses sum to one. Thus $\mathcal F$ is a correspondence from $\mathcal E$ to itself. For fixed $z$, the allowed set of action probabilities is a nonempty product of simplices. The displayed map is linear in these probabilities, so its image is convex and compact. This use of all tied minimizers, rather than a discontinuous selected action, is essential.

We verify closedness of the graph. Suppose $z^{(k)}\to z$ and pass to a subsequence on which all action probabilities converge. At positive limiting mass, $q_s^{(k)}\to q_s$. Since $K_ah$ is continuous, a limiting action with positive probability is still minimizing. Outside a common $\lambda_a$-null set, the positive linear instrument makes $F_a(y,q_s^{(k)})\to F_a(y,q_s)$. The acquired law of the latter point charges no grid boundary. Positivity of its likelihood makes the boundary preimage $\lambda_a$-null as well. Dominated convergence applies to \eqref{eq:fixedj}, using the integrable operator norm of $L_a$ and compactness of $K$. At a zero-mass limiting row, every integrated contribution has norm bounded by a constant times $w_s^{(k)}\to0$, uniformly in its arbitrary coordinates and actions. Thus no continuity of normalization at zero mass is needed. The graph is closed. A compact-valued closed-graph correspondence on a compact domain is upper semicontinuous.

The finite-dimensional Kakutani theorem \cite{Kakutani1941} gives $z^*\in\mathcal F(z^*)$. Fix one witnessing set of action rows $p_s$. Equations \eqref{eq:fixed0}--\eqref{eq:fixedj} are exactly the invariant equations of the positive instrument obtained by executing those rows and those report updates. They are not moment constraints detached from an implementable kernel. The prepared label mixture with unnormalized states $z_s^*$ is realizable by the mixture hypothesis. After an update it reproduces the same ensemble. Consequently, at every positive-mass output label its programmed $q_j$ equals the actual conditional predictive mean. The erasure label has the mean of all outgoing physical states, not a prescribed physical reset state. Arbitrary legal codepoints and action probabilities complete rows that have zero invariant mass, making the program defined from every allowed initial label.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Invariant risk and attraction]\label{prop:invariant}
The observer of Lemma~\ref{lem:fixedpoint} obeys \eqref{eq:stationary-bound} and \eqref{eq:entrance}. Its invariant ensemble is unique as an ensemble of the finite executable test coordinates. Convergence of this ensemble is not asserted to be total-variation convergence of an unrepresented microscopic state.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
At stationarity put $Q=q_S$, choose $A$ from $p_S$, and let $W=F_A(Y,Q)$. Its law is the actual acquired mixture
\[
 \mu=\sum_{s,a}w_sp_{sa}\mu_{a,q_s},\qquad \mu\le\Lambda\,\mathrm{vol}_d.
\]
Write $Q'$ for the predictive mean of the next label. Summing \eqref{eq:acoe} over the calibrated incoming ensemble, using minimizing actions and stationarity, gives the exact identity
\begin{equation}\label{eq:stationary-jensen}
 R_\infty-\rho=\E h(Q')-\E h(W)
 =(1-\alpha)\mathcal J_{\mathcal P_m}(h,\mu)
  +\alpha\left[h\left(\int x\,d\mu\right)-\int h\,d\mu\right].
\end{equation}
Here $\mathcal J$ is the cell-centroid gap in \eqref{eq:curvature}. Conditional expectation is justified by the unnormalized instrument equations, including all incoming labels and action rows. The curvature estimate and $\osc_Kh\le H$ prove the upper. Since $h+\norm{\cdot}^2$ is concave,
\[
 \E h(Q')-\E h(W)\ge\E\norm{W-Q'}^2\ge q_{m+1}(\mu).
\]
The last inequality includes randomized encoders. Lemma~\ref{lem:smallball} proves the lower.

For the fixed table, let $P_\gamma$ be its positive linear map on the direct sum of $m+1$ label cones. The erase row and \eqref{eq:minorization} imply, for every nonnegative ensemble $Z$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ensemble-minor}
 P_\gamma Z\ge\alpha\eta_* e_{\rm tot}(Z) Z_*,
\end{equation}
where $Z_*$ places $z_*$ in label $0$. Thus
$P_\gamma=\gamma_*R+(1-\gamma_*)Q_\gamma$,
where $\gamma_*=\alpha\eta_*$, $RZ=e_{\rm tot}(Z)Z_*$, and $Q_\gamma$ is positive and normalization preserving. If $\gamma_*=1$, convergence is immediate; otherwise this formula defines $Q_\gamma$. Positive mass-preserving maps contract base norm. On differences with zero total mass the $R$ term vanishes, proving
\[
 \norm{P_\gamma^t Z-z^*}_{\oplus B}\le2(1-\gamma_*)^t.
\]
This proof decomposition does not prescribe that the physical device actually reset at a random time.

With the readouts fixed, expected audit loss is a bounded affine functional of the physical/label ensemble. It lies in $[0,L_0]$ on normalized positive ensembles. The difference of its expectations is at most $L_0$ times the sum base norm. Summing the geometric estimate over $t=1,\ldots,n$ gives an error at most $2L_0/(n\alpha\eta_*)$. This proves the entrance bound from an arbitrary admissible physical preparation and arbitrary initial label, even if that initial label is not calibrated.
\end{proof}

The fixed point solves a global recursion, not just the equality case of a one-step row quantization. The codepoints at which actions are chosen are exactly the centroids of the invariant executed law. Calibration need not hold during entrance; the separate contraction estimate controls that period. A report that enters the erasure row is still an acquired report and is paid for.
