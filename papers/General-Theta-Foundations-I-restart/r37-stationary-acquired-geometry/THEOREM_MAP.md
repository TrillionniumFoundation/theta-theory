Labels are stable source anchors; displayed numbers are recorded from the actual LaTeX build.

```

  -> lem:quotient -> raw/row representation
feedback-uniform acquired overlap -> lem:span -> h, rho, strong concavity, interior Lipschitz

  -> lem:fixedpoint (Kakutani, including zero masses)
raw averaged minorization + charged label erasure
  -> prop:invariant (actual calibration, all-initial attraction, invariant risk)
raw affinity -> lem:barycenter -> prop:all-observer (all-Borel converse)
these branches -> thm:main -> cor:checkpoint
independent raw Gaussian and quantum verification -> thm:gaussian / thm:quantum
relative acquired density + marked grids -> thm:strata -> cor:rank
complete task compatibility -> prop:morphism
positive ensemble contraction -> prop:perturbation -> prop:compilation
core theorem + fresh post-control bit + unobservable calibration alternatives -> cor:joint
```

The calibration alternatives are unobservable and the bit deficiency is attained by the explicit erasure experiment; an error ceiling is not a risk floor.

Neither realization is an antecedent of the general theorem. Classical span contraction, convexity, conditional expectation, Kakutani and positive-cone contraction are standard tools. Curvature and finite-horizon overlap mechanisms are inherited and reproved. Global stationary calibration, all-initial ensemble attraction with paid erasure and its quantitative assembly are the present addition.
