# Proof audit and critical checks

1. **Zero mass:** normalization is used only for positive label mass. Vanishing-row output is bounded by its mass, uniformly over codepoints/actions. This closes the graph at zero.
2. **Ties:** all minimizing action distributions are allowed. The correspondence has convex values; a discontinuous first-minimizer selection is not used for the fixed point.
3. **Actual calibration:** output unnormalized vectors are the integrated raw instrument, not prescribed separate conditional means. The fixed point realizes all incoming rows at once.
4. **No free posterior:** the actual full-history predictive process and conditional means in the converse are proof objects. Execution stores only a label and reads its programmed codepoint.
5. **Two distinct overlaps:** feedback-uniform predictive overlap gives Bellman span contraction. Raw averaged positive-cone minorization plus erasure gives attraction of the fixed finite observer. Neither implies the other by definition.
6. **Task curvature:** the average bias satisfies h+norm² concave in the actual audit chart. Constant orthogonal audit noise is retained in the affine second moment.
7. **Boundary:** density/Jensen proof uses global convex extension, not trimming. Grid boundaries have zero mass for every allowed incoming barycenter/action, not only an exact trajectory.
8. **Gaussian minorizing state:** use P times the uniform prior in K=P Delta, not a generally unavailable uniform state in K. The entrywise bound gives a valid cone minorization.
9. **Quantum inverse:** the positive congruence inverse is on trace-D positive effects, with compact positive spectral floors. Finite-dimensional Jacobians do not imply unbounded-operator statements.
10. **Clock:** the protected uniform request is sampled outside the observer, its counter paid separately. Potential audits on the reference chain are not physical intermediate measurements.
11. **Control:** the Bellman comparator includes every legal feedback policy in the stated finite action set; the fixed observer chooses from its own label. No stationary action-switch claim is inferred from a finite-terminal example.
12. **Deficiency:** the bit is prepared after controlled acquisitions freeze. Its exact erased-report deficiency is computed before audit access. Calibration alternatives are actually unobservable.
13. **Compilation:** arbitrary table perturbations are compared as raw ensemble maps, with 1/(alpha eta) amplification. Small-mass readout labels use weighted error, not unsafe division. Fixedpoint existence is not called finite computation.
14. **Unmatched region:** no claim that M^(2/d)/n is an unavoidable entrance cost, or that n~M^(4/d) is necessary. The long-run order is matched.

The native statements are submitted as proved on these assumptions, but remain subject to independent mathematical review. The audit records checks and remaining scope, not a formal proof certificate.
