# Counterexample and sharpness ledger

1. **Duration–exit dependence:** `ex:marked` has exits to two absorbing classes with durations 1 and 3. Its transient gain bias is -3/2, not -1. T must not be replaced by D or DP.
2. **Unbounded recurrence, valid task:** `thm:diagnostic` has h(P_t) of order t^(-k), exact task bias t^(1-k), but gain zero and approximate price a^(1-k). It validates the strictly weaker criterion.
3. **Visible degeneration:** `ex:discontinuity` on the one-sided slow chain has fixed positive incorrect-label reward. Gain jumps; no uniform convergence is claimed.
4. **Infinite-model obstruction to finite H:** theta_j=1/j in the same two-state chain gives an individual retained program with sup_theta h(P_theta)=infinity. Finite-family exhaustion alone cannot certify this family.
5. **Periodic but regular:** the two-state swap has bounded group inverse. Periodicity alone is not the obstruction.
6. **Pooled duration error:** two absorbing classes of different rewards/durations require separate ratios before absorption mixing. The original R25 witness remains unchanged in Companion D.
7. **False all-control geometry:** a lower mass witness for one exploration does not hold for a different controller that never acquires that mass. The optimized theorem states universal lower quantifiers explicitly.
8. **Blind threshold under positive defect:** lambda_e=(t^k-e)_+ may make gain discontinuous. The matched error law is direct, not a falsely regular endpoint application.
9. **Score feedback changes the problem:** revealing diagnostic losses would leak information beyond the stipulated report; the two-sign lower would require a different proof. Audits are expressly not controller feedback.
10. **Finite precision is not labels:** a codebook of J real vectors is not a J-bit program. Description/space/time and acquisition resolution are separate.
11. **Source artifact integrity is not proof:** exact finite diagnostics, valid PDF builds and cross-environment rendering cannot certify arbitrary-Borel compactness or priority.
