# Adversarial proof audit

## Central stopped-cycle identity
Use T_ij=E_i[tau 1{exit=j}], d=T1, b=r-Tg. The use of r-Dg in a transient state would be wrong when holding time and destination correlate. The three-state example and exact finite recurrence suite check this distinction. The crossing index K_N is bounded by N because physical durations are positive integers. Its summation indicators are known before the next excursion. The coefficient S_k of the harmonic martingale is below N on the stopped event. Thus the optional/predictable sums are integrable without an unproved uniform hitting-time assumption.

The overshoot and omitted reward each lie between zero and the same overshoot. Their difference costs one overshoot, not two. The labelwise moment estimate explicitly pays m; only a common dominating lifetime removes that factor.

## Equivalence, not a uniform group-inverse assumption
Gain continuity makes b continuous and centered. For the averages A_n b, choose a small local finite block, use stochastic contraction to repeat it, and cover compact X by finitely many such neighborhoods. This proves a uniform approximate coboundary at every tolerance; it does not claim exact bias is bounded. Every limiting projection E has E Pi_S=E, so E c=E g; the fixed-vector condition is precisely the obstruction. The theorem treats all entering labels, not just one accidental initial mixture.

## Quantifiers and computational assumptions
The legal program is selected once, before the fixed unknown model. Robust attainment applies only if the task criterion holds across that whole compact class. Finite-model determination is a compact-intersection conclusion, not a minimax exchange. The algebraic theorem is restricted to finite algebraic instruments with continuous primitive task/response coefficients and certified uniform termination; the general QE algorithm is specified mathematically, not implemented as a new fast solver. The recurrence schedule's common-support condition is additional and fails in the singular rare-herald example, which instead uses task compatibility.

## Geometry and matched lower
Quantization uses the actual scored submeasure, with all calls in the denominator. In a robust problem a modelwise optimal codebook is only a lower bound unless a common implementation is supplied. Candidate stability applies to every approximate path in the budget-independent domain. The QJ construction has no shadow posterior register. Optimized matching requires a lower witness for every legal control and a fixed-size attaining exploration. The four-call construction verifies those quantifiers directly because marks are fresh and action independent, while the diagnostic lower holds against full history.

## Error, singularity and implementation boundaries
The diagnostic task's losses are not observed feedback. At a blind threshold the erased experiment's gain can be discontinuous; the same-task matched law is proved directly, not by applying a two-endpoint continuity theorem incorrectly. Deficiency is bracketed e/2<=epsilon_*<=e, not asserted exactly e. The quantum POVM is fixed and its standard origin is credited. Numerical risk is the square of the displayed root bound. Finite bit programs, physical report resolution and random samplers require their own certificates. No test substitutes for any continuum proof.
