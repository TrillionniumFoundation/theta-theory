# Scope and remaining mathematical obligations

The article preserves the General Theta mother problem. Its center is a genuinely proved sufficient-class transfer theorem with actual acquired-law converses, common causal implementations, two explicit stopping resources, singular raw verifications and separate resource/error accounting. It is not an all-positive-kernel classification.

New claims are: exact role packing and risk widths under both semantics; state/history-dependent approximate task balance with exact budget correction and attained sharp error scales; resource-aware live/terminal simulator composition; nonintervening directional Gaussian sensing with a joint waiting–memory law, finite-output obstruction and sharp asymptotic noise constant. The underlying trimming functional, conditional projection, symmetry averaging, posterior formula, classical indistinguishability, Gaussian tails and elementary sphere covering are not new in isolation.

Still open in this work:
1. An intrinsic necessary-and-sufficient reference/balance characterization over all legal causal programmes, rather than the stated witness/program certificates and finite-command tests.
2. Presentation-independent globally minimal memory under arbitrary stateful simulation, and matching lower bounds for program description, workspace, precision, execution time and minimal simulator state.
3. General unknown-sensor/kernel learning, unrestricted intervention design with changing tasks, infinite-dimensional unbounded operators or audits, and nonterminating protocols without explicit integrability.
4. Universal approximate-balance necessity or error floors from tolerances alone. Sharpness is shown by attained experiments, not asserted for each member.
5. External priority and the level of importance required by a top-four mathematics journal. The new natural application is not described as resolving a recognized open problem without evidence.

State stopping and event stopping are both fully specified; neither is silently privileged as the uniquely correct memory model. The old R56 three-label upper survives, while its untyped subtraction is corrected. No old review, realization or frozen archive is modified. Retained results remain independently meaningful under their own hypotheses.
