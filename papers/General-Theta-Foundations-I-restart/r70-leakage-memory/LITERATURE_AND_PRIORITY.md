Every new load-bearing positive-matrix or interpolation step is proved in the manuscript. The source comparison does not substitute an abstract hypothesis for the missing step. The formal language of positive realizations is classical; the current claimed contributions are the exact pure-start bounded-decoder leakage law, the strictly mixing physical realization, its finite rational squared-test obstruction, and the explicit positive-background construction at the same cost.

The background logarithmic upper is not claimed sharp, the finite-model family is not fixed independently of the tested memory bound, and the scalar response is not the full output process of a general controlled instrument. General multi-command optimal control, general unbounded-size realization decidability and matching bit complexity remain distinct. A full theorem-by-theorem comparison with every historical positive-realization publication has not been completed, so earliest priority and top-four editorial significance are not asserted.
