\section{The role-aware acquired-geometry theorem}
\begin{theorem}[Reference--balance transfer with explicit stopping roles]\label{thm:main}
Assume the raw reference/effort and common task-balancing program conditions of Section~\ref{sec:exactconditions}. The reference and effort certificate use the same fixed admitted model. Write $b_-=N/\kappa_-$ and $b_+=N/\kappa_+$.

For every feasible lower budget,
\begin{align}
 \Qm_m(b_-)&\le R_\cp^\Theta(N,m),\label{eq:cp-lower}\\
 \Qm_M(b_-)&\le R_\evt^\Theta(N,M),\qquad
 \Qm_{M-1}(b_-)\le R_\st^\Theta(N,M).\label{eq:aut-lower}
\end{align}
For every $b_+\ge1$ and $m\ge1$, one common Borel program gives
\begin{align}
 R_\cp^\Theta(N,D_0m)&\le\Qm_m(b_+),\label{eq:upper}\\
 R_\st^\Theta(N,B+D_0m)&\le\Qm_m(b_+),\qquad
 R_\evt^\Theta(N,\max\{B,D_0m\})\le\Qm_m(b_+).\label{eq:role-upper}
\end{align}
The latter program has at most $B$ live roles and $D_0m$ output roles. If every legal terminating observer at the same reference model needs at least $B_0$ live labels, then the state-stopped lower improves to $\Qm_{M-B_0}(b_-)$. The event-stopped lower remains $\Qm_M(b_-)$.

In particular, assume $\kappa_- =\kappa_+=\kappa$, $B_0=B$, and \emph{identity exports} $D_0=1$. Then the following attained equalities hold on the indicated feasible budgets:
\begin{align}
 R_\cp^\Theta(N,m)&=\Qm_m(N/\kappa) &&(m\ge1),\label{eq:exact-cp}\\
 R_\st^\Theta(N,M)&=\Qm_{M-B}(N/\kappa) &&(M\ge B+1),\label{eq:exact-state}\\
 R_\evt^\Theta(N,M)&=\Qm_M(N/\kappa) &&(M\ge B).\label{eq:exact-event}
\end{align}
Here $N\ge\kappa$. Below $\kappa$ every class is empty; the state-stopped class is empty for $M\le B$, and the event-stopped class for $M<B$. With nonidentity exports the width shifts in \eqref{eq:upper}--\eqref{eq:role-upper} remain necessary in the statement. An optimal dictionary invariant under all exports gives its own unexpanded upper, but such invariance is an additional hypothesis, not automatic.
\end{theorem}

The lower uses all competing strategies at one fixed model; the upper uses one program for all models and histories. The program need not simulate the full predictive state. Unequal effort constants leave a genuine interval $\Qm(b_-)\le R\le\Qm(b_+)$ at the appropriate widths; they do not give an exact budget identity. The role law does not assert an intrinsic minimal controller for an arbitrary physical interface. It gives exact minimax widths when the live lower and the constructed live upper match.

\begin{corollary}[Regular orders under both stopping interfaces]\label{cor:order}
Suppose the actual marked geometry obeys Proposition~\ref{prop:geometry}, effort is matched and the mandatory live count is $B$. For fixed export multiplicity $D_0$, define
\[
 F(b,m)=\max\{b^{-2/\alpha},(bm)^{-2/(d+\alpha)}\}.
\]
For state-stopped observers with $M-B\ge D_0$, risk is comparable to $F(N/\kappa,M-B)$. For event-stopped observers with $M\ge\max\{B,D_0\}$, it is comparable to $F(N/\kappa,M)$. For checkpoint observers with $m\ge D_0$, it is comparable to $F(N/\kappa,m)$. Constants are independent of $N,M,m$ and depend only on the stated geometry and export multiplicity.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The state-stopped lower width is $M-B$ and the virtual upper width is $\lfloor(M-B)/D_0\rfloor$. The latter is at least $(M-B)/(2D_0)$. The event-stopped widths are $M$ and $\lfloor M/D_0\rfloor$, since their live roles can overlap the output roles. Apply Proposition~\ref{prop:geometry}; the checkpoint case is the same. Fixed unequal positive effort constants give analogous order bounds but not the exact equalities of the theorem.
\end{proof}

Atoms and zero-risk strata must be retained separately; Proposition~\ref{prop:atom} gives their exact mass threshold. None of these conclusions substitutes the dimension of a formal parameter set for an acquired covering or small-ball estimate.
