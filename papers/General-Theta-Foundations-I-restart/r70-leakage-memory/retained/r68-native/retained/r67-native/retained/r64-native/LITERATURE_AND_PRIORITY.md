# R64 literature and priority audit

Primary literature was checked on 16 October 2026. This is an author-side comparison, not a certificate of earliest priority. Native mathematical proofs do not depend on unverified novelty claims. The full prior R63 ledger remains in `retained/r63-native/`.

The existing Blackwell, trimming, causal rejection sampling, sequential experiment order, quantum reachability, predictive representation, two-subspace and detector references are retained. The prior ledger's unsuccessful full-text retrieval for Liu–Verdú is not rewritten as successful. Complete full-text comparison against all historical and current neighboring literature has not been achieved.

The exact finite-score representation uses classical convex separation; its paper-specific work is uniform-TV compactness of budget-feasible performed laws and same-budget finite-label approximation. The sparse k+1 certificate uses classical semi-infinite geometry; its physical and resource interpretation, raw Bellman slack identity, distinct-prior and hard-width counterexamples are stated separately. The delayed-release result is an exact scoped application, not a claim of resolving a known long-open problem. Whether this collection supplies top-four conceptual significance remains an independent mathematical/editorial question.
