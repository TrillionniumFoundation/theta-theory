\section{Calibration measures on actual histories}
\label{sec:calibrated}
The data in this section replace, rather than supplement, an exact admitted reference model. Fix a known positive prepared comparison experiment. Its productive report--audit law, in a declared mark coordinate, is a probability $K_0(dx,dz)$ on $X\times H$. Its mark marginal is $\nu$. The comparison experiment is genuinely preparable, but need not belong to the operating family $\Theta$. Its preparation, calibration observations and stored specification are separate resources; no uniform certificate below is inferred from a finite data sample without an additional statistical theorem.

Disintegration on standard Borel spaces gives Borel versions
\begin{equation}\label{eq:derived}
 f(x)=\int zK_0(dz\mid x),\quad
 w(x)=\int\|z-f(x)\|^2K_0(dz\mid x),\quad
 d_r(x)=w(x)+\|f(x)-r\|^2.
\end{equation}
They may be completed arbitrarily on a $\nu$-null set, with $f\in H$. Put $L=\operatorname{diam}(H)^2$ and assume $H$ is nonempty. For $b\ge1$ and integer $m\ge1$ define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:profile}
 \Qm_m(b)=\min_{\substack{C\subset H,\ 1\le|C|\le m\\
                    0\le h\le b,\ \int h\,d\nu=1}}
 \int\bigl[w(x)+\operatorname{dist}(f(x),C)^2\bigr]h(x)\nu(dx).
\end{equation}
Set this value to $+\infty$ when $b<1$ or $m<1$. Lemma~\ref{lem:attain} proves attainment. The case $L=0$ has zero risk whenever a legal program exists and may be omitted from nonzero-rate assertions. The law $\nu$ is a comparison preparation law, not the law of reports accepted under an erroneous operating experiment. The transfer estimates quantify this difference.

\subsection*{A reference certificate without an exact reference model}
Choose a single actual model $\theta_*\in\Theta$. For each productive pre-call history $h$ and legal command $a$, let $P_{h,a}(dy,dz)$ be its conditional law of the \emph{full visible current report} and the prospective audit. On the same report space let $P^0_{h,a}$ be a row of the declared comparison experiment, with a Borel mark $x=\pi_{h,a}(y)$. Its mark law is $\nu$ at every such row, and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:row-moments}
 \E^0[Z\mid y]=f(\pi_{h,a}y),\qquad
 \E^0[\|Z\|^2\mid y]=w(\pi_{h,a}y)+\|f(\pi_{h,a}y)\|^2.
\end{equation}
The conditional moments are given the full report, not merely the mark. All specified rows and defects are measurable in history and command. Versions are required on every legal history reachable under the actual witness, not only almost everywhere under an unrelated comparison path law. An independent uniform coin may be adjoined to $y$ on both sides; the certificates below are stable under this adjunction by integration. Thus they cover randomized transitions and stopping.

Suppose nonnegative predictable bounds $a_-(h,a),e_-(h,a)$ satisfy, for every Borel $q:y\mapsto[0,1]$ and every finite-image Borel $r:y\mapsto H$,
\begin{align}
 \left|\int q(y)(P_{h,a}-P^0_{h,a})(dy,H)\right|
      &\le a_-(h,a),\label{eq:row-mass}\\
 \left|\int q(y)\|z-r(y)\|^2(P_{h,a}-P^0_{h,a})(dy,dz)\right|
      &\le e_-(h,a).\label{eq:row-loss}
\end{align}
These compare unnormalized task measures. In particular a joint probability total-variation bound $a_-$ implies $e_-=La_-$. Bounds on order-zero, first and squared-norm measures on the \emph{full report space}, as in Proposition~\ref{prop:approx-moments}, give a sometimes smaller $e_-$. Comparing the mark marginal alone is insufficient when the observer sees other informative components.

For every competing legal procedure under this \emph{same actual witness}, require
\begin{equation}\label{eq:reference-effort}
 \E_*C_\tau\ge\kappa_-\E_*A_\tau,
 \qquad \kappa_->0.
\end{equation}
The number $A_\tau$ counts all productive calls through commitment. For each declared budget $N$ let $\mathfrak a_N,\mathfrak e_N$ be certified bounds, uniformly over every competing procedure with robust cost at most $N$, on
\begin{equation}\label{eq:defect-occupation}
 \E_*\sum_{i\le\tau:\ i\ {\rm productive}}a_-(H_{i-1},a_i),\qquad
 \E_*\sum_{i\le\tau:\ i\ {\rm productive}}e_-(H_{i-1},a_i),
\end{equation}
respectively. These are bounds on actual pre-call occupation, not on the occupation of a preferred algorithm. Uniform row bounds $a_-,e_-$ give $\mathfrak a_N=(N/\kappa_-)a_-$ and $\mathfrak e_N=(N/\kappa_-)e_-$. Proposition~\ref{prop:potential} gives a less restrictive raw drift certificate. Mandatory live-state bounds used below are likewise bounds under the actual witness, not under the comparison experiment.

\subsection*{An approximately balanced common construction}
Independently, suppose one parameter-independent acquisition program has $B$ live roles and conditional expected cost at most $\kappa_+$ per attempt, on every surviving history in every actual model. It obtains one productive report, and returns to its start routing label after rejection. Within the attempt it forms a virtual mark $x$ and a fresh choice $J$ with exports $T_J:H\to H$. For every finite $C\subset H$ assume
\begin{equation}\label{eq:calibrated-export}
 |\{T_jr:j\in J,r\in C\}|\le D_0|C|,
 \qquad D_0\in\mathbb N.
\end{equation}
All persistent routing for this operation is counted in $B$. Assume constants $0\le a_+<1$, $e_+\ge0$, uniformly over every surviving history and actual model, such that
\begin{align}
 \|\law(x\mid h)-\nu\|_{\TV}&\le a_+,\label{eq:upper-row-mass}\\
 \left|\E[q(x)\|Z-T_Jr(x)\|^2\mid h]
          -\int q(x)d_{r(x)}(x)\nu(dx)\right|&\le e_+\label{eq:upper-row-loss}
\end{align}
for all Borel $q\in[0,1]$ and finite-image Borel $r$. Total variation of probabilities is $\sup_D|\mu(D)-\nu(D)|$. The averaging includes the within-attempt coins and hidden history; no such coin may be retained for free. These are a construction certificate, not a restriction on competing strategies. Neither independent attempts nor a normalized-filter contraction is assumed.
