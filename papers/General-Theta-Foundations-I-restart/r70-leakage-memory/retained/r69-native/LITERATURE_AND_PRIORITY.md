The current claim is not a new full spectral-region classification. It is the exact equality between prescribed-start stochastic response memory and spectral membership, the exact worst-phase size for the declared response, the matching cubic-root size at every fixed globally badly-approximable phase, and the corresponding actual squared-task implementations. The power-sector and directional-polygon proofs are given completely. The scope does not include an exact general-M risk formula for the older squared-sign prediction task.

Searches including stochastic eigenvalues, positive realization, Karpelevic regions and Diophantine/badly-approximable phases did not locate an identical fixed-phase statement during this audit. This negative search result is not proof of novelty. The whole literature has not been exhaustively checked, and no independent expert priority assessment is asserted. Earlier direct rejection-sampling, Blackwell, positive realization, POMDP, absorbing-occupation and predictive-state comparisons remain current in the predecessor audit and native bibliography.
