# Literature comparison and consulted primary sources

The new claim is a uniform same-cardinality, support-preserving completion of the exact causal frontier for positive instruments, not the invention of finite-state controllers, conditional means, report quantization, convex order or real algebraic decidability.

| Work | Existing result relevant here | R19 distinction |
|---|---|---|
| Strassen; Leskela–Vihola (Bernoulli 23, 2784–2807, 2017) | Martingale/conditional convex-order coupling and measurable kernel results | Serial actual acquisition and one common reused programme; no new one-step martingale-coupling claim |
| Poupart–Boutilier, NIPS 16 (2003) | Bounded stochastic finite-state controller optimization | Explicit fixed-proposal dual and same-M all-Borel certified risk frontier, not a claim that bounded controllers are new |
