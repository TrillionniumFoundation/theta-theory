# Proof audit

1. The central novelty claim is the uniform same-M completion, not merely finite enumeration. Its lower must compare against all Borel controllers. Lemma report_reduction does this before programme discretization.
2. Reconstruction is one measure per action/cell, independent of phase and physical state. A phase-specific posterior-weighted reconstruction would silently give multiple stationary programmes and is not used.
3. Use unnormalized positive instruments and the direct-sum base norm. No classical hidden trajectory is invented for quantum states, and no normalized-filter Lipschitz bound is needed.
4. Exact stopping is protected by support equivalence for every incoming positive state. Small total variation alone would not protect a probability-one deadline. Numerical cell errors must preserve support signatures if exact stopping is claimed.
5. Rounding only removes original edges. It may drop tiny probabilities; that is harmless to legality. Probability-one stopping is checked only to n, not via infinite recurrence.
6. min_g l_g <= min risk <= min_g u_g and min u-min l <= eta are justified with an index attaining min l. The risk certificate lower subtracts E+beta, while the upper programme runs directly on the original experiment.
7. Computable instrument integrals plus effective moduli, support tables and decision nets are input certificates. Exact equality tests on arbitrary computable reals are not used by graph legality.
8. Gaussian tails are retained as an extra cell. The tail error is twice actual mass, never a renormalized conditional law. No transition positivity is needed for the new effective theorem.
9. Quantum effect lower bound concerns E_y, not the evolving state's rank. CPTP outputs of pure inputs are legitimate; the norm estimate remains uniform.
10. Cantor and flagged atomic masses remain actual acquired masses. A singular reference alone is not asserted to imply every later posterior distribution is fractal.
11. Finite shared-row example proves the randomized lower by label types and row composition, not by claiming an exhaustive deterministic test covers all randomized rules.
12. All earlier exact/geometric/resource arguments remain with their original assumptions. No externally clocked rule is promoted to a free internal clock.

Unclosed: arbitrary Borel effectiveness, generally optimal search complexity and resource-description lower bounds, unknown-kernel learning and infinite-horizon extensions. Correctness and priority are subject to independent review; neither CI nor this audit certifies them.
