## Remaining research obligations

1. **Original general-M decision-risk frontier.** The squared-sign minimax quantity V_{p,alpha,M}-V_{p,alpha} still has no general exact formula or matching asymptotic lower. Exact response counts and stopped-law upper bounds do not close it.
2. **General nonrigid control.** Necessary and sufficient optimal-memory classification for arbitrary tied boundary problems with stopping and multiple commands, and broad boundary density beyond the proved classes, remain open. A simulator does not make its private state readable by the original observer.
3. **General high-contrast nonabelian realization.** Orbit convexity is characterized, and the growing S_n family has a closed law at every contrast with exact transition rigidity. High-contrast formulas for arbitrary other finite groups and an efficient general positive-factorization solution remain unresolved.
4. **Complete-instrument memory away from the solved sectors.** The lower stochastic spectral region and polygon upper need not be equal. Further exact damping transitions and equality classifications require proof.
5. **Sharp approximation resources.** The new bounded-cost simulator gives a common constructive upper. Matching lower bounds for arbitrary approximating classical models, exact leading constants and optimal policy-dependent tradeoffs are not established.
6. **Intermediate timing.** The phase-type release/timing frontier remains separate from every qubit or group response law.
7. **Arithmetic and computation.** Sharp Diophantine constants, a certified exponent for the particular algebraic rotation, joint favourable-scale questions, program-table bits, precision, workspace, exact sampling and uniform efficiency are not inferred from finite searches or real-valued tables.
8. **Unknown models and the wider programme.** Recovering a predictive representation from data, independent A1/A2/B4/C2 construction goals and other Theta pipeline objectives retain their own assumptions and proof obligations.

These are explicit research questions, not assumptions inserted into a theorem. The current positive results are proved without pretending these questions have been resolved.
