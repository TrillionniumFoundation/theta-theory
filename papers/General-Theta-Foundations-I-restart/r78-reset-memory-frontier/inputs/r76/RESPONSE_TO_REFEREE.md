## What has changed mathematically

**General closed-convex specifications.** `thm:r76-convex-normal-form` replaces exact response equality by arbitrary closed convex allowed-output sets indexed by a supplied finite group. Every word in the corresponding fiber must satisfy its constraint. An arbitrary stochastic controller admits a permutation core at no greater mixed-start cost, or with the precise counted-pure initializer charge. The original transitions need not obey group relations; the reduced action need not be faithful. The proof uses a closed-class stationary projection and inverse sandwiches, not an unjustified identity between hidden transition matrices. `thm:r76-minimax-normal-form` gives an attained finite optimization for the optimal uniform all-word error, Lipschitz target dependence and a genuine positive gap below the exact-memory minimum. `thm:r76-robust-multiplicity` supplies Fourier singular-value lower certificates and the residual-separation initializer charge. `cor:r76-prime-error` gives an exact all-or-nothing error law below the prime cyclic orbit size. These statements strengthen the realization problem beyond a larger collection of exact examples.

**A sharp synthesis boundary and complete mean–contrast law.** For every `n>=5`, `lem:r76-simultaneous-positivity` proves a sharp weighted inequality under both output constraints and classifies equality. `thm:r76-bounded-coefficients` characterizes exactly the parameter pairs `(p,a)` for which a probability column `u` and matrix `A` satisfy `0<=A<=u 1^T`, total mass `np`, `PAP=aP` and `rank[A|u]<n`. The allowed positive contrast is `a<=F_n(p)`, with three explicit pieces and transition means `1/(n-sqrt(n))` and `2/n`. Some pair at every feasible parameter has common positive factor width `n-1`; the theorem does not assert this for every given feasible pair. The curved boundary coefficient pair is unique up to simultaneous relabeling, and its normalized factors are classified. The new middle segment comes from simultaneous preparation and decoder positivity and cannot be obtained by rescaling the old one-output bound.

For `beta(g)=p+a(chi_n(g)-1)`, `thm:r76-mean-contrast` then gives the complete `S_n` memory law for every feasible mean and both signs of contrast. For positive contrast the mixed core is `n(n-1)` exactly when `a<=F_n(p)`, and otherwise `n^2`; pure start adds exactly one. Negative contrast exchanges outputs and replaces `p` by `1-p`; zero contrast has one label. The group, `n!` residuals and predictive dimension `1+(n-1)^2` remain fixed for nonzero contrast. Here `p` is a group-average response, not a statistical prior. Explicit optimal tables and a physical realization attain the lower bounds.

**Adaptive stopping, return geometry and a matching restricted approximation law.** `lem:r76-excursion-budget` proves the sharp bound `(1-r)B/(r^{-L}-1)` on reaching `L` consecutive continuing reports before a stopping rule of expected calls at most `B`. `thm:r76-return-approximation` converts a verified physical return polytope and continuation-orbit growth into a single finite simulator for all visible adaptive stopped command/report laws and terminal POVMs, also in classical–quantum trace distance. It neither exposes the simulator label nor infers branch contraction from average mixing. `cor:r76-qubit-polylog` supplies explicit branch tables for every fixed finite angle alphabet, with `1+3 binomial(L+a-1,a)` pure labels and error at most `7B/[8(8^L-1)]`. One angle has logarithmic rather than square-root label growth.

Within the explicit class of fixed physical density dictionaries and Born terminal decoders, `thm:r76-physical-logarithm` proves a matching fixed-`B`, fixed-badly-approximable-phase law: the minimum label count divided by `log_8(1/delta)` tends to one, for both start conventions. The lower derives high fidelity from joint rare-prefix events and separates the required physical states; zero-call tests also exclude the pure initializer. A finite prefix of the same rotation orbit attains the leading constant. The golden-ratio phase has explicit constants `c=1/3`, `K=5`. The original algebraic rotation `(3+4i)/5` has a rational-table upper with `K=7`; a matching Diophantine lower is not asserted for it. Arbitrary classical decoder tables are outside this lower-bound class.
