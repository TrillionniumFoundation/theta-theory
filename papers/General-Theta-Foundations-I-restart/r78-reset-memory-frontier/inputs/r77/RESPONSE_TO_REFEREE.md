## The substantive mathematical response

**A common device for multiple preparations.** The previous single-response
cyclic theory is extended to a family of independently selectable
preparations sharing one decoder and one command program. For arbitrary
finite groups and closed convex all-word constraints,
`thm:r77-shared-normal-form` gives a common permutation core and the exact
charge for distinct mixed preparation rows. `thm:r77-shared-polytope`
gives necessary and sufficient geometric resource formulas: the least
vertex count for mixed starts, and that count plus the number of distinct
prescribed nonvertex responses for pure starts. Redundant continuing
profiles are accounted for, so a label serving another preparation is not
charged twice.

The joint module need not be cyclic. Horizontal concatenation of the
stacked Fourier blocks gives its irreducible multiplicities, which can
exceed the irreducible degree. `thm:r77-shared-low-contrast` proves exact
attainment of the complete joint multiplicity cover for every finite
group when the preparation means coincide and the common contrast is
sufficiently small. The positivity construction uses one decoder for all
preparations. A rank-three/nonnegative-rank-four constant-family
counterexample explains why unequal means cannot be silently included.
Weighted singular-value certificates retain explicit robustness.
Two sharp families separate the resources: each preparation individually
needs two labels, while a shared device needs exactly `2q` labels in one
family; in another, the mixed core is two but the pure-start minimum is
exactly `r+1`. These are arbitrary-size families, not extrapolations from
one numerical instance.

**Optimal control with ties derived from reset primitives.**
`thm:r77-reset-cover` concerns an optimal feedback problem, not merely a
supplied output specification. For finite-command reset instruments with
strictly positive failure effects, positive bounded call costs and no
closed failure set, it derives the optimal continuation action sets from
the Bellman vector and the robust initial minimax problem. The exact
pure-start live memory is one plus a minimum transversal number after
accounting for states that can reuse the initial command row. This is a
necessary and sufficient formula over arbitrary stochastic autonomous
controllers; ties and randomized initial command rows are allowed. The
proof does not assume a unique wordwise optimal action.

`thm:r77-reset-gap` gives an attained positive variational certificate
exactly below the optimal-memory threshold. It is not a formula for the
entire memory-constrained risk frontier. `thm:r77-reset-universality`
realizes every finite nonempty proper action-cover system by an explicit
rational reset experiment with commuting cost and effect operators, and proves NP-hardness of optimal
memory even when the unrestricted value is explicit. The positive-
semidefinite boundary example `ex:r77-reset-exposure` shows that strict
reachability cannot simply be omitted: an inaccessible failure can hide
a locally nonoptimal continuation below the robust optimum.

**Approximation lower bounds for unrestricted classical decoders.**
The Born-decoder restriction in the inherited sharp physical-dictionary
law is now removed for a different, weaker lower bound.
`thm:r77-operational-antichain` obtains a robust antichain certificate
from a finite family of joint herald-and-test records. Conditional errors
are derived only after herald masses are controlled. The terminal test
is requested after the preparation, so the same counted-label distribution
must support all later tests. Its decoder columns are otherwise arbitrary.

For the original algebraic phase `(3+4i)/5`, and more generally every
non-root algebraic unit-circle phase, the new results give explicit
finite error thresholds forcing unbounded classical memory, with lower
order `log log(1/delta)`. The original phase requires no unproved
bad-approximability premise. The inherited one-angle constructions give
an `O(log(1/delta))` upper. This leaves an explicit rate gap. The leading
logarithmic equality remains confined to the proved physical-dictionary
class and its stated phase hypothesis.

All inherited causal comparison, predictive-state compression,
finite-resource, finite-order quantum measurement, two-stage reset,
memory/communication, rank-constrained discrimination, variational/dual,
spectral, local-geometric and optimal-implementation results remain
traceable at their individual scopes. Classical residual automata,
invariant cones, equivariant nonnegative factorizations, representation
theory, antichains and set-cover hardness are credited as ingredients.
Their use does not itself settle independent priority or the journals'
assessment of significance.
