# Counterexample and sharpness ledger

| Witness | Excludes | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Two-state boundary P_eta, eta down to zero | uniform average continuity from compact program space alone; horizon error independent of recurrence | exact formula in `prop:boundarysharp`; finite rational diagnostic |
| Periodic swap chain | identifying periodicity with failure of Cesaro regularity | included, not a negative theorem |
| Transient split to classes with lengths 2 and 4 and costs 0 and 1 | ratio of pooled reward and duration | true gain 1/2, pooled ratio 2/3 |
| One changed rare transition in a slowly communicating chain | average defect modulus independent of recurrence scale | stationary change t/[2(2 eta+t)] |
| Different conditional lifetime laws at entering labels | removing the finite-label factor without a common dominating lifetime | proof audit distinguishes moment and envelope inputs |
| New edge under generic row rounding | automatic preservation of closed-class structure and H | compiler states graph support/floor architecture |
| Fixed hidden bit revealed on short cycles | importing compulsory-reset sharpness into retained learning | old result preserved under its own interface; new bit is fresh |
| Simulator replacing the audit wire | calling a different statistical task a deficiency lower | new deficiency explicitly fixes the task wire and deadline |
| One-state rare long cycle | better uniform N^-p or (1-beta)^p under only 1+p moment | proof in `prop:tailsharp`; class sharp, not instancewise lower |
| Same reachable set, different lifetime weighting | preparation geometry replacing acquired occupation | exact length-biased law in `prop:holding` |
| Diagonal finite-report law | tensor continuity from one-step marginal references | excluded by finite-product hypothesis; historical companion retains explicit construction |

These witnesses refine hypotheses. They are not used to rebrand the paper as an impossibility or terminology paper.
