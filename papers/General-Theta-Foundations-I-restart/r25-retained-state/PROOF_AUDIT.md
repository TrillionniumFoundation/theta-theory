# Proof audit — actual issues checked

The principal audit is mathematical, separate from TeX and arithmetic checks.

## Corrections made before source binding

1. A retained Markov-renewal cycle has different conditional lifetime laws at different entering labels. The raw 1+p moment specialization therefore pays M in the finite-N and discount overshoot bound. A sum of suprema was not silently interchanged with an expectation. The queue's common stochastic dominating busy period legitimately removes that extra factor in its special explicit constant.
2. Physical-time multichain gain is the mixture of classwise reward/duration ratios, represented by the time-changed stochastic matrix S. A three-state counterexample excludes the pooled ratio.
3. A known-prior predictor initialization resets only the auxiliary predictor coordinate. The control label is retained. The product chain group inverse is proved to have norm at most H+2; recurrence is not assumed for free.
4. An arbitrary spectral-sublevel program cannot safely be rounded across zero transition entries. The finite compiler is restricted to a declared support-stable architecture, for which averaging and rounding preserve exact forbidden zeros, end-row floors, closed classes, tails and recurrence.
5. A previously revealed fixed hidden bit can be remembered across cycles. The new joint lower uses a fresh per-cycle physical bit and an unchanged environment-owned audit wire. It is an attained task-preserving causal deficiency, not unrestricted report-and-audit resimulation.
6. A finite task factor attached to an unbounded queue is not the queue's full predictive quotient. Full geometric raw verification is supplied separately for hidden substochastic instruments and noncommuting quantum instruments; the queue factor is explicitly labeled as such.
7. A drift tail formula and a sharper mean envelope are combined by a minimum, preserving the required monotonicity of a(T).

## Proof boundaries

The compactness proof uses finite-response product domination, not marginal domination or arbitrary nondominated path laws. Exact average existence is pointwise for every finite boundary chain; uniformity and robust attainment are asserted on bounded-H classes. Finite-family unrestricted exhaustion is not an exchange with N or beta limits. Geometry first fixes exploration; optimized matching requires the displayed uniform lower and a common attaining controller. Full numerical resource optimality is not proved. No testing or rendering claim substitutes for these arguments.

## Remaining scrutiny requested

The next referee should check the root stopping proof in reducible chains, the M factor in the conditional moment bound, closure of the common recurrence-constrained program class, support-preserving all-Borel coverage, the product-chain identity, and the task-preserving rather than unrestricted interpretation of the fresh-bit deficiency. These are the places where a change of quantifier or interface would change the theorem.
