# Scope audit

## Proved claims

- One parameter-independent stationary M-state Borel program can retain its state across physical regenerations. Arbitrary standard Borel physical states are allowed under finite-product response domination.
- On the actual boundary recurrence sublevel, robust average risk is attained and finitely determined, with explicit same-memory finite-acquisition/discount errors. Periodic, transient and multichain boundary matrices are included.
- For finite model families, unrestricted fixed-M average value is exactly exhausted by increasing recurrence bounds. This is a value statement, not unrestricted attainment.
- Complete conditional marked-cycle errors have a recurrence-sensitive modulus; simulator and predictor private states are counted.
- A support-stable raw graph architecture has same-M all-Borel lower and implemented upper certificates under explicit computable data.
- Class-weighted acquired occupation gives checkpoint quantization, a retained online lower, a counted recursive upper, and a joint-control sandwich. Optimized matching has additional uniform mass/baseline/attaining-controller assumptions.
- Two different raw families verify retained transfer; both hidden and quantum endogenous instruments also verify the geometric hypotheses. The queue supplies a natural unbounded-state application with an explicit uniform constant.
- Nonuniform holding permits direct nondominated preparation laws. The joint erasure slice is sharp in a task-preserving, fixed audit-wire interface and uses fresh physical bits.

## Not proved

No unrestricted uniform average-risk theorem follows by letting H grow without bound. For infinite parameter families an individual program need not have a finite uniform H. No broad nondominated adaptive compactness, universal acquired-dimension classification, unknown-kernel learning/regret rate, efficient general synthesis, or full matched program/workspace/precision/physical-time/simulator-state region is established. No observed fixed-policy power law is asserted to solve arbitrary optimal control. No TV-to-LDP transfer or unbounded-operator closure is asserted.

The mother problem remains General Theta rather than a queue, HMM or quantum paper. The precise breadth and significance of the new theorem, its independent correctness and priority, remain external mathematical review questions. No journal acceptance or formal proof certification is claimed.
