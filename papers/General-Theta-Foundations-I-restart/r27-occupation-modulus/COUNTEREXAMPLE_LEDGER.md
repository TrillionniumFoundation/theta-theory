# Counterexample ledger

1. Periodic terminal cancellation: swap P, tau=1, scores (0,1). J_N=g at every even N but k_N=Delta_N=1/(2N). `rem:prefix-tail` prevents dropping the prefix maximum.
2. Within-cycle front loading: one label, tau=4, scores (1,1,0,0). b=k_N=0 but J_1-g=1/2. Physical return remainder is necessary.
3. Duration/exit covariance: the inherited three-label example has g=(1/2,0,1) and b_1=-3/2, not the bias obtained from D P g. `ex:marked` retains joint marks.
4. Wrong initial-law lower: an unused entering label can have large dual witness while the prescribed start never reaches it. `thm:modulus` states an all-row norm, not a fixed-start decision lower. Separate accessibility/all-history witnesses are required.
5. Resolution collapse: uniform measure versus its M midpoint image has transport O(1/M), positive continuous q_M=1/(12M^2), but atomic q_M=0. `prop:resolution-sharp` calibrates the mass-transfer scale.
6. Biased last mark: deleting one unfinished mark gives only an additive measure error, not rho times a missing-count coefficient. `eq:load-first-count` uses predictable early starts instead.
7. Invisible slow mode: lambda=t^k, a=t has unbounded exact group inverse and task bias but finite approximate price and vanishing risk. `thm:profile` includes logarithmic rates as well.
8. Blind erased gain: lambda_e=0 at positive t with a(t)>0 does not have gain zero from both labels. The finite-horizon no-revelation proof is used directly; no misuse of the compact task criterion.
9. Non-doubling acquisition/quantization: retain `eq:profile-sandwich` rather than simplifying to one rate without A_*(2e) and q_floor(M/2) control.
10. Formal support versus acquired submass: arbitrary low-mass strata cannot be normalized for the lower. Every w remains visible in `thm:resolution`.
11. Free controller/clock/readout: Q times J and simulator product states are charged; raw load and scored service are separate calls. A prefix evaluator is not an internal stopping clock.
12. General nondominated or nonregenerative experiments: no automatic finite-row compactification, predictive section, or physical-return decomposition is claimed.
