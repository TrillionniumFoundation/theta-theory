# Proof audit

1. Re-derived the physical first-excursion equation from real call prefixes and the full conditional exit/duration marks. The gain is P-harmonic, not assumed stationary at the start. The terminal strip contains min(tau,N) terms; bound uses the conditional mean, not a fake factorized kernel.
2. Proved finite LP primal attainment modulo constants and the exact signed dual. All-row dual certificates are not claims about an inaccessible initial state. A supplied fixed response certificate can be checked exactly; deriving that response/gain from an arbitrary raw kernel is separate.
3. Verified both necessity examples: periodic terminal cancellation and deterministic front-loaded physical scores. Removing either the prefix qualifier or the physical remainder gives a false theorem.
4. The actual occupation family must itself be a conditional excursion task; an unrecorded nonreset posterior cannot be smuggled across a preparation. Conditional-mean risk projection uses the same visible history and fixed readout budget.
5. Localized lower uses phi=min(dist^2,r^2), with Lipschitz constant 2r. It is uniform over centers and retains the actual witness mass. Controller optimization has its own all-law and baseline assumptions.
6. During derivation of controlled-load mass, an attempted subtraction of one unfinished mark was recognized as invalid for measure domination: the unfinished event can depend on V. The manuscript instead counts starts before N-2, where a scored call is guaranteed, and proves the explicit constant. No invalid draft theorem was published.
7. For the joint law, erasure can create positive-amplitude blind parameters. Only the finite-horizon history lower survives there; the gain-zero K identity is asserted only for the original positive-off-zero profile. Independent signs and fresh marks make the lower additive in one task.
8. Powers and logarithmic profile formulas are proved by monotonicity on either side of the acquisition threshold. Full raw continuum proofs are not inferred from sampled parameters.
9. Two raw geometric realizations verify action-uniform lifetime control and stable delayed mark prediction. They do not pretend to characterize full workload/quantum predictive geometry. Generic report-continuity/compactness results apply only when separately verified from continuous primitive data.
10. Classical approximation theory, LP duality, quantization, Poisson martingales and unambiguous discrimination are credited. No independent novelty or top-four importance certificate is claimed.

The written proofs are complete for their stated hypotheses; independent correctness remains a task for the next referee. The wider mother problem is not declared closed merely because these classes and scopes are proved.
