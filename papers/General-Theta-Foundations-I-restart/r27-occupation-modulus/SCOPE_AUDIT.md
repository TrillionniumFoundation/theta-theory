# Scope audit

The new center is general for marked finite-boundary physical experiments, not a single queue or quantum model. It is a two-sided quantitative prefix theorem; it is not a necessary-and-sufficient spatial recursive classification. A finite observer can coexist with infinite physical state.

F1: actual occupation gets a radius-localized finite-acquisition small-ball lower; matching causal upper uses explicit global cover/stability/common-init certificates. The controlled-load theorem derives all-controller actual mass directly from raw physical timing and conditional return bounds. No actual measure is replaced by a formal reachable set.
F2: same-task exact sandwich and matching profile law combine physical calls, labels, a real invisible calibration sign and actual report deficiency. Numerical approximation, program, workspace and simulator products are separately accounted. No arbitrary tolerance becomes an unavoidable lower bound.
F3: nonalgebraic logarithmic task profile, recurrent-rank degeneration, quantum preparation-rank changes, Cantor/product/mixture acquired laws, and endogenous survival are included. General arbitrary support degeneration is not.
F4: queue drift and unnormalized noncommuting quantum survival verify both temporal and loaded-task geometric results. Their finite mark is an explicit task factor, not the full physical quotient. No unrestricted POMDP, workload filtering or arbitrary quantum-memory optimum is claimed.

Remaining genuine gaps: nonregenerative arbitrary histories; general nondominated common-program compactness; automatic near-necessary recursive cover construction; all-kernel optimal acquisition/control curves; efficient global synthesis and the optimal joint program/workspace/precision/time/minimal-simulator region. Task compatibility can still fail on an unrestricted class. General data-dependent unbounded stopping and unbounded operators are not covered.

No mathematical statement in the new source is intentionally left with an omitted proof. This is an author-side proof audit, not independent correctness, priority or journal-acceptance certification. Historical preservation and successful builds do not resolve the gaps above.
