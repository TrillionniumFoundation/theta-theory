# Scope audit and remaining proof obligations

## Established in this manuscript
A sufficient raw-kernel inward-cover theorem constructs a recursive encoder without assuming the encoder or terminal occupation domination. Two realizations verify it from their raw kernels: a finite-dimensional intermittently mixing filter and a finite-read nonreset singular process with time-varying mode masses. Actual submass and common-oracle arguments provide matching lower bounds. Selected label/output/calibration and acquisition/memory/calibration regions share one task. Typed causal morphisms compose bounded common risk and explicit resource accounts.

## Genuine obligations not declared closed
1. A necessary-and-sufficient, or near-necessary intrinsic characterization of matching checkpoint/online laws. All-pair moment/envelope/inward-cover hypotheses are sufficient and may exclude predictable experiments.
2. Matching laws for optimized adaptive acquisition, kernel learning, unknown transition/observation parameters, and pilot design. Both realized protocols are fixed and their known calibration is explicit.
3. A full optimal region for workspace, program description, runtime, internal numerical precision, minimal simulator state, and optimal causal deficiency. The current product/resource map is constructive; selected lower witnesses do not establish every coordinate.
4. State-dependent waiting, error-selected stopping, and arbitrary infinite horizons. The path comparison is finite horizon and fixed controller; the prediction theorem only includes the specified independent exact suspension.
5. Broad overlapping-strata allocation laws without either an occupation certificate or this alternative global inward geometry. Preservation of the earlier weighted theorem is not a proof of this stronger generalization.
6. Exhaustive priority comparison and independent specialist/human proof review. The manuscript records consulted theorem-level precedents and does not claim journal acceptance or formal verification.

The original mother problem and title are retained. These are explicit research obligations, not a decision to replace foundations by a survey, a realization paper, or a terminology interface.
