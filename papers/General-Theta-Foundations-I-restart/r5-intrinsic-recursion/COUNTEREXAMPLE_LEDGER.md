# Counterexample and scope controls

1. A static cover alone does not supply stable online approximation: rounding innovations can be expanded at each recurrence. The raw true-law pair moment is indispensable to this sufficient theorem; tests deliberately reject unstable gain choices.
2. A drift under reports sampled from an approximate state need not hold under physical reports. The envelope quantifies every true/candidate pair. The conditioning filtration excludes unobserved physical states.
3. Informative identity-transition filtering calls are not exact holds. Charging their true observations is necessary; the constants may diverge as p tends to zero. Independent holds instead mix squared energies without fictitious new rounding.
4. A finite prefix yields an atomic acquired law. Treating its latent Cantor measure as that acquired law gives an incorrect lower below the finite-acquisition scale. The exact oracle identity retains both terms.
5. Deletion on finite cylinder centers has gain bound 30, not the physical Cantor-point bound 15. A two-bit rather than six-bit return with the completed-domain gain does not certify the displayed matrix. Both are negative regression controls.
6. A stationary reference comparison cannot be inferred from arbitrary initial mode masses. The new proof does not use one; its uniform all-pair raw bound is checked separately.
7. Arbitrary biased output rounding does not preserve the exact calibration cancellation. The two-sign expansion retains 2 delta |E(P-a)|.
8. Complete causal defect is stronger than one terminal marginal bound. A fixed-controller estimate is not unrestricted optimal control; finite-horizon TV is not infinite-path or LDP equivalence.
9. The state product MRDQ is not universally necessary: a simulator may have redundant private states. Only an executable continuation challenge or another explicit separation witness proves a lower.
10. Exact Borel evaluation does not imply bounded finite workspace or finite program description. Certified enclosure/table costs are separate; no universal lower on these coordinates is claimed.

These controls delimit mathematical assertions. They do not replace positive proofs or alter the mother problem.
