## Nonuniformity of constants

`1/(1-sqrt(kappa))`, `1/(1-a)`, the block telescoping sum, and the small-ball mass enter the general constant. Sparse filtering also depends on fixed d, primitive length m, s, q_-, 1-q_+, Gaussian mean/variance bounds, the chosen cube, b0 and eta. As these degenerate, neither horizon-uniform risk nor the exponent formula supplies a parameter-uniform constant. Numerical error must preserve the same relation; arbitrary rounding is not covered. Stopping adds `1/(1-gamma)` and an actual expected block-count term. No reciprocal terminal mode mass occurs in the singular upper; its lower chooses an actually occupied mode of mass at least one half.
