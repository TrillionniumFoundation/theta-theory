## Principal mathematical response

The central theorem now permits strict contraction only across a raw block, while the implemented encoder still quantizes after every individual report. The new obstacle is real: ordinary inward rounding can destroy the positivity that a sparse product would otherwise acquire. A fixed, budget-independent relation is introduced to preserve sufficient coordinate mass. Its robust envelope condition is verified for arbitrary adapted selections in that relation, not assumed as the error bound of a supplied quantizer. The proof combines an exact shadow used only for analysis with the actual finitely retained trajectory.

This mechanism is verified for a primitive sparse hidden Markov class, with Gaussian likelihood ratios unbounded on the report domain and state-dependent observation loss. A four-state lazy directed cycle witnesses a genuine absence of strict one-step projective contraction. The acquired lower comes from a positive-probability block event and an explicit Jacobian/density calculation, not a prescribed posterior density. The exponent remains the dimension of this verified acquired submeasure. This is a broader familiar raw experiment class; we do not claim an independent priority determination or that its memory law was previously unresolved.

## Technical comments 12.1–12.20

The compact quotient proof now identifies the positive-density domain and the closed quotient map after product with the report space. Exact suspension uses indicator-form expectations, including zero activity. The seed error remains in every general radius. The sparse compander includes smallest budgets; its mass-preserving quantizer is different from an arbitrary inward cover. The lower displays the score density bound `eq:actualdensity`, and the dyadic lower displays the max-to-sum comparison. Dimension-dependent uniformity is stated explicitly.

The singular proof retains finite centers as predictive, not physical states, labels source and destination matrix indices, distinguishes ball diameter from cylinder gap, and places the sign-averaging lower next to the unbiased-decoder upper. All waits, initial reads and final probes are counted. Total variation is consistently `sup_A |P(A)-Q(A)|`. The serial `MRDQ` count is an upper unless an executable continuation challenge or reverse morphism gives a lower. Native-subtree source verification is never described as a complete repository audit. Published Wood/Ghomi/JMLR records are identified; version-specific preprint comparisons are labelled as such.

## What has and has not changed

The revision supplies a new block generalization, a mass-preserving compander, a raw sparse Gaussian class verification and a separately proved stopped transfer. It retains the singular acquisition theorem and earlier r4/r5 mathematics with their original scopes. It does not claim that more build tests establish top-four significance. No theorem here characterizes every finite-memory predictable experiment or optimizes the full resource vector.
