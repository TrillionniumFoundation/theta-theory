## Audit of strengthened claims

An inward cover alone is not enough for a block theorem: permutation toward a different vertex preserves projective spread but can remove a coordinate needed by a sparse transition. The mass relation is therefore explicitly stronger and independently verified. The conditional good-block probability is obtained by iteration, not by an independence claim. Candidate drift is always integrated under the physical state law. The finite Gaussian packet result pays its out-of-box event and does not transplant analogue acquired density to a digital law.

The manuscript contains no proposed use of TV proximity as LDP equivalence, no unrestricted optimal-control inference from a same-controller coupling, and no finite-dimensional identity promoted to an unbounded-operator result. Remaining objectives are listed in `SCOPE_AUDIT.md` rather than labelled proved.
