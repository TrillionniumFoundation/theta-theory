# Theorem-level literature comparison

Primary versions inspected include the JMLR publisher records/PDFs for Subramanian–Sinha–Seraj–Mahajan (2022, 23(12), Theorem 9) and Kara–Yuksel (2022, 23(11), Theorem 16); the authors' arXiv versions of Ghomi–Linder–Yuksel (2103.10810v3), Wood–Linder–Yuksel (1606.09135v2) and Kara (2505.14879v1, Theorem 1). The final Wood/Ghomi publication metadata were cross-checked on the authors' Queen's research page. Kara's version-specific comparison remains identified as the consulted preprint; a publisher citation index also lists a CDC 2025 proceedings version, but its direct DOI record was inaccessible in this session.

| Work / object | Hypotheses and conclusion relevant here | Difference from this paper |
|---|---|---|
| AIS, JMLR 2022, Thm 9 | Approximate reward/prediction state; backward value-error recursion and a policy suboptimality bound | A planning guarantee, not an actual-law M-center converse or hard-alphabet implementation of an inward block cover. |
| Kara–Yuksel, JMLR 2022, Thm 16 | Finite observation/action windows, filter stability and discount/regularity assumptions; near-optimal control | Window length is not M persistent states for continuous reports. Our exploration prediction task is narrower than optimized control; no unconditional implication is asserted. |
| Ghomi–Linder–Yuksel, TIT 2022 | Linear vector Markov source zero-delay coding, stationary optimality and finite-memory approximation | Encoder/decoder coding state and time-average distortion are different cuts. Their theorem is not replaced by a checkpoint geometric argument. |
| Wood–Linder–Yuksel, TIT 2017 | Irreducible aperiodic finite Markov source, stationary deterministic coding and O(1/T) finite-horizon approach | The horizon T and communication/coding alphabets are not the same as the retained prediction alphabet M. |
| Kara, arXiv:2505.14879v1, Thm 1 | Linear basis policy evaluation under stochastic-approximation assumptions; projected Bellman convergence | Unknown-model learning objective and approximation assumptions differ from our known raw kernels and explicit acquired geometric lower. |
| Pages–Pham / Sellami | Quantized nonlinear filtering and error propagation | Recursive quantization is inherited background, not claimed new. Our comparison concerns block relation preservation plus a hard-label actual-law converse. |
| Nonanticipative rate distortion | Causal reconstruction kernels and information objective | Mutual information or rate is not a pointwise bound on each retained alphabet. |
| Blackwell / Le Cam / Torgersen | Randomized experiment comparison and bounded-loss risk order | The present typed theorem charges finite causal simulator/controller/clock state and common stopped task; the TV inequalities themselves are standard. |
