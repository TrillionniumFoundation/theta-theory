Retaining their exact trees and consulting a later history ledger are not equivalent to doing that. No claim of exhaustive historical proof certification is made.

Thus the r5 intermittent bounded-density filter, the earlier weighted-allocation/terminal-bridge/reset-preserved/single-probe/soft-information material, and all their original hypotheses remain available.
