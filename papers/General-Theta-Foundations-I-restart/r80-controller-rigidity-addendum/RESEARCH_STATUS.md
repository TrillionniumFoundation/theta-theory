# Current research status and minimum remaining closure conditions

The exact R80 value asymptotic and finite-search saturation theorem are inherited. The new proved closure is the if-and-only-if first-order optimizer profile for one fixed two-model/two-label scalar family. Every exact optimizer is included; arbitrary discontinuous selections and every admissible stochastic update are included. The result strengthens local optimal-program rigidity, not the general value exponent or the finite-search classification.

The following remain genuine distinct mathematical or external obligations; none is deleted or hidden in a new assumption:

1. General-M noisy-qubit squared-sign risk: obtain a lower certificate valid for every stochastic controller and an attaining family for that same original risk objective.
2. Unrestricted-decoder approximation: close the retained log-log versus log rate gap without reinstating a physical-state/Born dictionary assumption.
3. General exact finite saturation: characterize arbitrary instruments with model-dependent supports, back-action and ties; the search and reset subclasses do not decide the general case.
4. Optional commitment: preserve voluntary terminal loss and memory accounting in a constructive extension, rather than relabeling terminal decisions as compulsory success.
5. Primitive degeneracy classification: give intrinsic conditions distinguishing Lipschitz, fractional-power and other frontiers. The present first-order classification is one fully analyzed boundary, not the universal theorem.
6. Intrinsic causal transfer/common unknown-model prediction: necessary-and-sufficient primitive information conditions and matching construction complexity remain open beyond supplied continuation certificates.
7. Intermediate phase-type timing, global changing-support geometry and arbitrary-pair spectral values retain their separate obligations.
8. Effective resources: exact finite algebraic existence does not bound program bits, numerical precision, temporary computation or synthesis complexity.
9. Independent A1/A2 and wider Theta constructions require their own proofs and are not modified here.
10. External specialist priority and editorial significance remain unverified; no computation or internal proof read settles acceptance at the four target journals.

The complete inherited [R80 research status](../r80-stability-and-saturation/RESEARCH_STATUS.md) retains finer distinctions. These are open research conditions, not missing permissions, compilation failures or excuses to lower the original target.
