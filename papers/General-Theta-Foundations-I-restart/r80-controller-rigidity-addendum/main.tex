\begin{abstract}
We study finite positive memory in prepared causal experiments and the
cost frontier obtained by optimizing over every controller of a given
size.  For arbitrary finite-dimensional quantum failure instruments,
a fixed hidden model in a compact family and positive call costs,
regularization by returns to the counted initial label gives bounded
global cost witnesses without adding memory or resetting the physical
state.  It yields a primitive continuity modulus uniform in all memory
budgets, including changes of reachable support.  We prove the
square-root exponent optimal by an exact leading asymptotic for two
scalar models and two live labels; the converse covers all stochastic
controllers.  We also characterize exact finite-memory attainment of
the history optimum in imperfect search with any finite number of
locations and arbitrary positive call costs.  Such attainment occurs
if and only if all logarithmic failure ratios are rational.  Otherwise
every finite budget has an attained strict gap.  The necessity covers
all stochastic controllers, including partially commensurate families,
through rigidity of their projected output.  Explicit least-favourable
prior bounds yield exponential approximation in the label count and
an effective finite synthesis cap for commensurate rational data.
Classical search ordering, saddle and cyclic-search ingredients are
proved in full and explicitly attributed.
Exact support certificates, attained variational principles and
algebraic synthesis organize the general frontier.  Reported reset
instruments admit a stronger scalar normal form and arbitrarily long
exact resource hierarchies.  Shared preparations give common
permutation forms and joint Fourier memory laws.  The causal comparison,
predictive compression, finite-order measurement, two-stage reset,
communication, rank-constrained discrimination, duality and local
spectral geometry retain their separate hypotheses and complete proofs.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope and further mathematical questions}
\label{sec:r73-scope}\label{sec:r74-scope}\label{sec:r75-scope}\label{sec:r76-scope}\label{sec:r77-scope}\label{sec:r78-scope}\label{sec:r79-scope}\label{sec:r80-scope}
The general stability theorem concerns finite-dimensional instruments
with mandatory success stopping, a fixed hidden model, a common positive
mean cost floor, and a cost guarantee for every initial density matrix.
It allows noncommuting failure maps and changing reachable supports.
The restarted controller has the same command rows and label budget;
its physical state is not reset.  The square-root exponent is optimal on this general class by
Theorem~\ref{thm:r80-sharp-modulus}; the exact leading coefficient
belongs to its displayed scalar family.  No optimality of the numerical
constant in the general upper bound is inferred.  The exact support
certificates, finite-model synthesis and compactness argument reuse
inherited theory.  The resource-preserving perturbation argument extends the complete
cost frontier uniformly across all finite budgets.  Its sharpness proof
uses a positive-polynomial boundary inequality, quantitative rigidity and
a matched upper and lower asymptotic over all six controller parameters.
Neither the golden-ratio example nor semialgebraic effectivity is a
closed classification of every instrument.  The unrestricted-history
value is the limit of finite-memory values; attainment of that limit
at finite memory is characterized here for every finite number of
imperfect search locations with arbitrary positive costs.  That characterization
does not extend by assertion to arbitrary instruments.


For finite-location imperfect search, rational commensurability of all
failure logarithms completely distinguishes finite saturation from
strict gaps at every finite memory.  The greedy ordering, interior
preparation saddle, consistent-priority mixtures and common-power
cycles are classical search facts.  The projected class-word
obstruction rules out every stochastic finite controller when more
than one commensurability class is present; it does not infer rational
frequencies for general finite Markov chains.  Counted pure-start
initialization, a primitive least-favourable prior bound, an exponential
memory upper rate and an effective rational synthesis cap complete the
implementation chain.  Arbitrary positive command costs are allowed.
The supplement retains sharper normalized two-location constants.
Neither rate is a matching lower bound or a minimal-label formula.
The arithmetic criterion concerns exact supplied primitives; finite
decimal orbit samples cannot decide it.  Positive command costs and
strictly imperfect detection are actual assumptions of this search
classification.  Rational nonsaturating examples do not imply
transcendence of the unrestricted optimum.

The shared-preparation normal form applies to every finite group, every
finite preparation and outcome alphabet, and closed convex wordwise
specifications.  The input controller need not satisfy group relations.
The invariant polytope may extend beyond the target response span.
The exact low-contrast formula requires a common mean law; the displayed
nonnegative-rank counterexample explains why this hypothesis cannot
simply be removed.  These are shared-device results with one fixed
decoder, not a claim that independently optimized devices can always
share their memory at no cost.

The reset frontier now allows positive semidefinite failure effects
and classifies every live-label budget, with arbitrary ties.  Its normal
form uses the all-initial-state quantifier, a fixed row program, reported
resetting, a positive cost floor, and mandatory success stopping.  It
does not apply unchanged to a restricted initial prior, nonreset
back-action, extra observation ports or optional commitment.  Positive
exposure remains necessary for the separate simple transversal formula;
the general frontier correctly includes its semidefinite counterexample.
The old first-residual certificate remains a lower bound, and the exact
hierarchy exhibits a strict factor-two difference from the full cost gap.
Algebraic computability is not an efficient algorithm, and the inherited
covering reduction prevents a general polynomial-time memory-synthesis
claim unless the usual complexity obstruction is resolved.

The approximation lower permits arbitrary classical terminal
decoder tables but requires delayed terminal test requests and no
uncounted access to the preparation history.  At fixed positive target
budget and the original algebraic phase it leaves a gap between
logarithmic-in-logarithm and logarithmic memory growth.  The sharper
matching logarithmic physical-dictionary law retains its Born-readout
and badly-approximable hypotheses.  Neither law is the original
general-$M$ squared-sign minimax-risk frontier.  The original squared-sign risk frontier and the intermediate
phase-type timing frontier remain distinct mathematical problems.
The present general cost theorem admits nonreset feedback and fixed
unknown models, but does not supply a closed risk formula, optional
commitment, or a finite-memory saturation theorem for every experiment.

The inherited mean--contrast law is exact for its displayed growing
symmetric-group family, and the general finite-group error principle
is an attained finite optimization.  No closed formula for every group
or universal polynomial-time synthesis is inferred.  The return-polytope
construction concerns explicitly verified conditional branches, not
average mixing.  Its target-cost bound does not control the simulator's
unbounded expected duration.  The stationary-initialization theorem
concerns exact predictively stationary laws; an approximate truncation
does not automatically inherit its extra-label lower bound.

All original causal, predictive-state, information-compression, finite
resource, finite-order measurement, two-stage reset, rank-constrained
discrimination, communication, dual, spectral and geometric developments
retain their hypotheses.  Local geometry remains local.  Independent
A1, A2 and further Theta construction problems require their own
arguments.  Program bits, arithmetic precision, working storage and
sampling work are not silently identified with persistent label counts.
The paired supplement contains complete technical and independent
proofs; the separately compilable R58 companion retains its own scope.
