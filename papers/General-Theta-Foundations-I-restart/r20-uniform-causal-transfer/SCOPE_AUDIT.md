# Scope audit

## Established in the submitted proofs
The exact common-programme criterion covers a jointly dominated family of compact affine predictive experiments with a fixed finite horizon/actions and shared readouts. Its dual couples parameter and phase. On dominated finite-dimensional positive physical instruments, a common worst-risk optimizer exists and finite parameter models exclude any strict lower target. These are distinct from arbitrary compact-carrier attainment.

The effective result assumes a supplied computably compact model family, common support-resolving report reconstruction, uniform raw preparation/instrument/loss moduli and effective integration/execution certificates. It computes two-sided risk intervals relative to every admissible Borel programme at the original M, not only the best sampled controller. Gaussian and quantum families are nontrivial and may lack normalized-filter contraction. Singular and rank-flag extensions carry actual masses.

## Not established
A universal unknown-kernel identification or regret rate from unrestricted data; a parameter-free formula in ambient dimension; arbitrary nondominated/noncompact predictive quotients; general infinite-dimensional physical compactness; arbitrary changing-support singular filters; an infinite-horizon or unbounded-stopping limit; LDP equivalence from TV; unbounded operator closure; unrestricted quantum-memory synthesis; and an optimal joint region for programme description, scratch, precision, planning time, physical time or simulator state.

No parameterwise oracle Bayes benchmark is silently subtracted from robust risk. The finite calibration law is a sharp witness, not a generic learning curve. Effective finite search need not be efficient. A finite-model obstruction without moduli does not give an effective witness size. The broad classification and all-coordinate matching problems remain genuine mathematical work; they are not replaced by repository operations.

The paper remains a foundations theorem paper. Its general statements precede verifications, and the complete inherited mathematics is preserved. Independent correctness, priority and top-four-journal importance remain matters for external assessment, not claims certified by the author-side proof ledger.

For the erased calibration extension only the exact risk for M at least seven is asserted. Smaller-label optima are not inferred from the binary-report common-null-set theorem, since the erasure flag changes that hypothesis.
