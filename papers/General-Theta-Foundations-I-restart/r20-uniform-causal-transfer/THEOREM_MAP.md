The main theorem may be stated before its constituent lemmas. No proof edge points from a realization back to the general theorem. No edge from a regression or build receipt is a mathematical dependency. The robust risk-valued Phi is not the singleton excess-valued Phi with its baseline omitted.

`cor:uniform_erasure` follows from the explicit calibration experiment, data processing, a conditional-variance lower bound and one seven-state construction. It proves attained delta-square and epsilon coordinates in the same fixed-parameter task; it is a consequence, not the mother theorem.
