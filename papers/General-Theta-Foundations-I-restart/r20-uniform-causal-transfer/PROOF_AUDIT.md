## New compactness point
The argument is not that products of arbitrary weak-star-convergent pointwise kernels converge. In a finite-dimensional physical instrument, each integrated operator coefficient is a continuous moment of the row. A finite execution is a finite product of those scalar/matrix moments. Legality, no early stopping and total terminal mass are closed finite-horizon constraints for each parameter. Intersecting them over parameters is compact. Each parameter risk is continuous, so the worst risk is lower semicontinuous. This proves attainment and global finite-model exclusion under the extra physical hypothesis, not on all compact predictive carriers.
