\section{The finite stochastic model}\label{sec:model34}
State distributions are row vectors. Let
$\Delta_k=\{e\in\R^k:e\ge0,\ e\one=1\}$.

\begin{definition}[Clocked transducer and approximation]
\label{def:machine34}
A transducer for a prescribed $N$ has initialization rows
$E_x\in\Delta_{K_0}$, row-stochastic matrices
$T_{t,c}\in\R_+^{K_t\times K_{t+1}}$ for $0\le t<N$, $c\in\{0,1\}$,
and binary decoder means $d_j\in[-1,1]^{K_N}$ for $j\in\{1,2\}$.
Its mean after a word is
\[
 E_xT_{0,c_1}\cdots T_{N-1,c_N}d_j.
\]
Its uniform row error is the maximum total variation between this binary
law and \eqref{eq:array34}, over all seeds, command words and queries.
The available-label profile is $(K_t)$ and its peak is $\max_t K_t$.
$W_{N,\varepsilon}$ minimizes this peak over transducers of row error at
most $\varepsilon$. At error zero the defining product must equal the
specified mean on every word.
\end{definition}

Only the current state survives each update. All retained randomness,
past inputs and protocol distinctions belong to that state. The seed and
query are supplied once as atomic symbols. The command is an external
input; it is not a hidden parameter chosen by the model. The epoch is an
external clock, so different cuts can have different alphabets and rows.
All rows, including unreachable rows, are normalized. Available labels
are counted; padding is allowed. A retained final binary answer uses two
labels at its own output cut. This does not enlarge any exact peak here,
which is at least three.

Exact stochastic rows, their read-only descriptions, arithmetic, and
row-construction time are not charged by this positive-realization width.
A rational row can be compiled into fair-bit rejection sampling with extra
phase, old-state, current-input and sampler-prefix storage, as in
\cite{GTF31}. No equality between that implementation cost and the atomic
width is asserted. Irrational rows below are exact mathematical kernels,
not a finite fair-bit implementation claim.

\begin{proposition}[The separate positive minimum remains three]
\label{prop:separate34}
For exact realization of \eqref{eq:array34}, every internal cut has
normalized residual rank three and separate positive minimum three.
The separate minimum allows arbitrary finite registers at all other cuts.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
A prefix has a predictive vector $R_\alpha^a x/10$. Its complete future
array is affine in that vector. Four idle prefixes span an affine plane,
and two idle-suffix coordinate queries separate it. Normalization adds
the constant direction, so the residual rank is three.
The triangle with vertices
\[
 (1/2,0),\quad(-1/2,1/2),\quad(-1/2,-1/2)
\]
contains the disk of radius $\sqrt2/10$. Indeed its supporting-line
distances from zero are $1/2,1/(2\sqrt5),1/(2\sqrt5)$, all larger
than that radius. Its vertices have norm at most $1/\sqrt2$, so every
rotated continuation from a vertex gives legal binary answers.
At the selected cut decompose the actual predictive vector in this
triangle. Before that cut store the finite raw history; after it store
the chosen vertex label and remaining command count. This is a complete
finite realization with three states at the selected cut. Rank proves
minimality. The construction is inherited from \cite{GTF32,GTF33}; the
new result concerns simultaneous, not separate, width.
\end{proof}

The lower argument below uses only the stochastic update rule and the
correct terminal correlation. It does not project hidden basis states
into this predictive plane. In particular, it covers the simplex-section
lifts in \cite{GTF33}, including lifts with unobservable directions.
