# Version-specific primary-source verification

Access date: 6 September 2026. This is a targeted comparison, not exhaustive
priority clearance or a representation of journal endorsement.

**Saldi–Yüksel–Linder.** *On the Asymptotic Optimality of Finite Approximations
to Markov Decision Processes with Borel Spaces*, Mathematics of Operations
Research 42 (2017), 945–978. The exact version linked in this manuscript is
https://arxiv.org/pdf/1503.02244v3 . Its printed page 32 (PDF index 31) states
**Theorem 5.2**, the discounted `O(n^(-1/d))` result under the displayed
regularity and `beta K_2<1` assumptions. Printed page 35 (PDF index 34) states
**Theorem 5.3**, in the average-cost subsection, with the additional time
parameter. Both pages were retrieved and visually inspected. The two source
files `sections/comparison.tex` and `references.tex` now use Theorem 5.2 with
this explicit version pin. No claim is made about numbering in a different
unexamined final typesetting.

**Kara–Yüksel.** *Near Optimality of Finite Memory Feedback Policies in Partially
Observed Markov Decision Processes*, JMLR 23(11) (2022), 1–46.
https://jmlr.org/papers/volume23/20-1152/20-1152.pdf . Theorem 12 was checked at
printed page 15 / PDF index 14. It gives a discounted finite-window conclusion
after a warm-up period with its stability and discount hypotheses. This does
not supply the same label-budget covering theorem or the new common-advice
minimax statement.

**Subramanian–Sinha–Seraj–Mahajan.** *Approximate Information State for
Approximate Planning and Reinforcement Learning in Partially Observed Systems*,
JMLR 23(12) (2022), 1–83.
https://jmlr.org/papers/volume23/20-1165/20-1165.pdf . Theorem 9 was checked at
printed page 16 / PDF index 15, including the recursive error allowance and
factor-two policy bound. An error recurrence is not claimed as new here.

The mathematical comparison in the manuscript is deliberately narrower than a
priority assertion: the interval proposition distinguishes a valid residual
bound from a prescribed-budget construction guarantee. The non-routine input
claimed for the main program remains uniform physical attainment through
collisions and a matching global cover. The common-advice result is an
additional proved consequence. Whether the combined advance has the requested
journal significance remains an independent editorial judgment.
