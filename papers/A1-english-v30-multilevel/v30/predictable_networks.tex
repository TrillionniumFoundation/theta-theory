\section{Predictable acquisition on regenerative networks}
\label{sec:v30-predictable}
The initial commitment in the preceding section can be removed when
regeneration is imposed at every visited vertex. The appropriate
additional information is then available before each acquisition, not
only before the first one. We prove an exact statistical flow formula
under this hypothesis, allowing the next vertex to be chosen from the
revealed history. The public vertex descriptor and the charged label
are kept separate throughout.

\subsection{Protocol and the conditional acquired law}
Let $\mathscr D$ be a finite layered directed acyclic graph as in
Section~\ref{sec:v29-occupation}, with levels
$\mathscr V_1,\ldots,\mathscr V_J$. Before acquiring the block at level
$j$, the scheduler chooses the next vertex $w\in\mathscr V_j$ along
an admitted arc. It may use all previously revealed blocks and query
outcomes in a converse relaxation. It cannot inspect any part of the
new block before this choice. Given the chosen vertex and the entire
past, including the independent seed, the new acquisition block has a
fixed prescribed law $P_w$. Its query-mean vector is $q_w(H_w)$,
and its finite query distribution is $p_w$. In particular, the full
history conditional mean of the scored outcome is this same vector.
These are conditional regeneration hypotheses, not assumptions of
independence after selecting on the current block.

The query is selected after compression. The decoder receives the
current vertex as its finite query descriptor, the query, the independent
seed, and one of $M$ labels. It receives no further order prefix or
history. An admissible upper construction must use one causal state
transition system with that same label set. The graph and read-only
clock are fixed programs. If the vertex descriptor is to be charged
instead, it can be encoded together with the label at cost at most
$M\max_j|\mathscr V_j|$ states at a boundary. The exact equality below
uses the explicitly public-descriptor convention; it does not identify
this different charged-descriptor optimum.

Let $d_w(M)$ be the optimal $M$-center query distortion of $q_w(H_w)$
under $P_w$, defined as in \eqref{eq:v30-one-block}. Let $\mathcal F$
be the vertex occupations of unit source--sink flows, with no additional
terminal restrictions. Put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v30-predictable-value}
 T_M=\min_{x\in\mathcal F}\max_{1\le j\le J}
                         \sum_{w\in\mathscr V_j}x_w d_w(M).
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[Exact causal flow formula under regeneration]
\label{thm:v30-predictable-exact}
For every integer $M\ge1$, the minimum of the maximum expected
checkpoint excess in this protocol is exactly $T_M$. The same value
is obtained if the converse scheduler is allowed its entire revealed
history. Moreover,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v30-predictable-dual}
 T_M=\max_{\lambda\in\Delta_J}
       \min_{P:o\rightsquigarrow d}
                \sum_{w\in P}\lambda_{j(w)}d_w(M).
\end{equation}
A distribution on at most $J+1$ deterministic paths, sampled before
the first acquisition, and one causal reset quantizer attain $T_M$.
All extrema are attained.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Condition on the complete history just before the next block is
acquired, including the independent seed and the chosen vertex $w$.
The block still has law $P_w$ by hypothesis. At this fixed seed and
vertex the decoder has at most $M$ possible vectors. The pointwise
nearest-center inequality used in
Theorem~\ref{thm:v30-regenerative} therefore gives conditional expected
loss at least $d_w(M)$. The same assertion would remain true even if
this finite codebook had been chosen predictably from the past; no
such additional decoder information is required or granted in the
upper construction.

Let $x_w$ be the unconditional probability that $w$ is visited. The
conditional bound, integrated over the histories choosing $w$, is
$x_wd_w(M)$. Summing at a fixed level proves
$\E L_j\ge\sum_{w\in\mathscr V_j}x_wd_w(M)$. The expected arc
incidences of any adaptive schedule form a unit flow. Consequently
$x\in\mathcal F$, and taking the maximum followed by the infimum
proves the lower bound $T_M$, also for the full-history scheduler.

Conversely, decompose a minimizing flow into a probability distribution
on source--sink paths. Such a decomposition exists by the finite
acyclic flow argument in Section~\ref{sec:v29-occupation}. Sample a
path using the independent seed before any block is revealed. Follow
it regardless of the data. At a visited vertex, replace the old label
by the nearest-center index of a minimizing codebook for $P_w$.
The decoder returns the corresponding query coordinate. This uses
only the current acquisition block, a single label, the vertex and
the independent seed. Conditional regeneration ensures that its loss
at $w$ is exactly $d_w(M)$. It therefore attains every level cost
of the minimizing flow. No full-history scheduling memory is needed.

Each path has a risk vector in $\R^J$, with coordinate $j$ equal to
$d_w(M)$ at its level-$j$ vertex. The flow risk vectors are exactly
the convex hull of this finite family. The supporting-hyperplane
argument in Theorem~\ref{thm:v30-regenerative} gives
\eqref{eq:v30-predictable-dual}. Compactness of the flow polytope
and the simplex gives attainment. Finally every vector in this
convex hull is a combination of at most $J+1$ path vectors: if more
vectors occur with positive coefficients, their augmented vectors
$(v,1)\in\R^{J+1}$ are linearly dependent. Move their coefficients
along a nonzero dependence until one becomes zero, keeping all
nonnegative. Iterate. Applying this elementary reduction to an
optimal risk vector proves the support bound.
\end{proof}

For fixed $\lambda$, the inner problem in
\eqref{eq:v30-predictable-dual} is a shortest-path computation on the
acyclic graph. A value from this dual is a risk lower bound. A
nonoptimal feasible flow in \eqref{eq:v30-predictable-value} has the
opposite direction. The shortest-path description here has no omitted
node caps: $\mathcal F$ is precisely the unconstrained unit-flow
polytope permitted by this regenerative protocol.

\subsection{Predictable small-ball bounds}
Suppose that before a visited block is revealed, for every admitted
predictable $M$-center codebook its conditional loss satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v30-predictable-capacity}
 \Pr(L_j\le t\mid\mathcal H_j^-,w)\le c_w(t),
                       \qquad 0\le t\le1,
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal H_j^-$ includes the entire preceding history and the
independent seed. The nondecreasing right-continuous capacities have
$c_w(1)=1$. The inequality is required only for histories of positive
conditional relevance, in the usual almost-sure sense; no pointwise
regular conditional density on a null history is asserted. Set
\[
 \ell_w=\int_0^1[1-c_w(t)]\,dt,\qquad
 \Gamma_{\rm pred}=
      \min_{x\in\mathcal F}\max_j\sum_{w\in\mathscr V_j}x_w\ell_w.
\]

\begin{proposition}[Predictable occupation refinement]
\label{prop:v30-predictable-bound}
Every common controller satisfying
\eqref{eq:v30-predictable-capacity} obeys
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v30-predictable-bound}
       \max_j\E L_j\ge\Gamma_{\rm pred}
                      \ge\Gamma_{\rm pre}(c)\ge\Gamma(c).
\end{equation}
The last two values use event mass one and the local functions $c_w$
in the earlier occupation definitions. Thus information available
before every acquisition can strengthen the first-acquisition
refinement without changing the risk criterion.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Conditional layer cake gives conditional expected loss at least
$\ell_w$ at a chosen vertex. Averaging and using the actual common
occupation flow proves the first inequality, as above. Since
$c_w(1)=1$, every unit flow and every anchored path is feasible in
the earlier local relaxation. Also $\phi_w(0)=0$ and convexity gives
$\phi_w(z)\le z\phi_w(1)=z\ell_w$ for $0\le z\le1$.
Given any $x\in\mathcal F$, decompose it into a path law and group
the paths by their first vertex. This produces anchored flows $x^u$
with masses $\alpha_u$ and $x=\sum_u\alpha_ux^u$. At each level,
\[
 \sum_u\alpha_u\sum_{w\in\mathscr V_j}\phi_w(x^u_w)
       \le\sum_{w\in\mathscr V_j}x_w\ell_w.
\]
Taking the maximum and then minimizing the right side proves
$\Gamma_{\rm pre}\le\Gamma_{\rm pred}$. The inequality
$\Gamma_{\rm pre}\ge\Gamma$ was proved in
Theorem~\ref{thm:v29-pre}. The conditional data in
\eqref{eq:v30-predictable-capacity} are essential: the unconditional
local inequalities alone do not imply the first linear cost.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[A network-uniform statistical relaxation gap]
\label{cor:v30-network-gap}
At every vertex put an independent copy of the positive two-trial
experiment \eqref{eq:v30-law}, with a fixed gain $s_w\in(0,1]$ as in
\eqref{eq:v30-attenuation}. Require the next vertex to be selected
before that new block is revealed, but allow data-dependent choices
among admitted arcs. Define
\[
 V_{\mathscr D}(a)=\min_{x\in\mathcal F}\max_j
                       \sum_{w\in\mathscr V_j}x_w a_w,
                      \qquad a_w=s_w^2.
\]
Then for every $M\ge1$ the actual statistical optimum satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v30-network-gap}
 \mathcal R_{\mathscr D,M}^{\rm reg}
      =\frac{D_M(\nu)}{128}V_{\mathscr D}(a),\qquad
 \Gamma_{\rm pred}
      =\frac{V_{\mathscr D}(a)}{8306688M^2}.
\end{equation}
Consequently
\[
 1\le\frac{\mathcal R_{\mathscr D,M}^{\rm reg}}{\Gamma_{\rm pred}}
     =\frac{8306688}{128}M^2D_M(\nu)
     \le\frac{8306688}{64800}<129,
\]
and the ratio tends to $8306688\kappa_{\rm ex}\in(4.05,4.06)$,
uniformly in the finite network, its horizon and its positive gains.
One distribution on deterministic paths is optimal for all budgets,
with the scalar predictive codebook reoptimized at each budget.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The scalar projection argument gives
$d_w(M)=a_wD_M(\nu)/128$. Substitution in
Theorem~\ref{thm:v30-predictable-exact} proves the first formula.
Given the preceding history and the selected vertex, the acquired
law is still the full mixed law $\nu$. The proof of
Lemma~\ref{lem:v30-atom-capacity} therefore applies conditionally,
with $b_w=2HLM/s_w$ and the atom mass $1/2$ unchanged. Its integral
at unit occupation is $\ell_w=a_w/(8306688M^2)$, proving the
second formula and its converse direction by
Proposition~\ref{prop:v30-predictable-bound}. The remaining bounds
and uniform limit follow exactly as in
Theorem~\ref{thm:v30-controlled-gap}; the ratio contains no network
parameter. An optimal flow for $V_{\mathscr D}(a)$ and its path
law do not depend on $M$.
\end{proof}

The disjoint-route family of
Corollary~\ref{cor:v30-checkpoint-gap} is a special case and retains
its unbounded separate-checkpoint penalty. The present theorem also
permits branching and recombination and full-history adaptive
scheduling in the converse. Its decisive hypothesis is conditional
regeneration before each new block. Correlated latent edge experiments
with an evolving shared posterior need not satisfy it. The network
formula therefore complements, rather than replaces or weakens, the
collision-uniform and nonregenerative graph classifications.
