\subsection{An alternative transfer proof for observation algebras}
\label{sec:algebra-transfer-alternative}
For comparison with the nonsaturated monomial case, we give the complete
transfer proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:algebra-multistep}. The direct proof
in Section~\ref{sec:algebra-multistep} requires neither the bounded-format
part of the product-chart lemma nor Lemma~\ref{lem:tame-rectangle}.
The following proof verifies those stronger hypotheses as well.

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:algebra-multistep}]
Use the reachable raw moment set $S_n$ and initial state $m$.
The weighted query vector is $b_n+G_nv$, where $b_n$ has every entry
$2^{-(N-n)}/\sqrt{d+1}$ and
\[
 G_n=\frac{2^{-(N-n)}\delta}{\sqrt{d+1}}
                           \begin{pmatrix}0_{1\times d}\\ I_d\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Both $G_n$ and a left inverse have bounds depending only on
$d,\delta,N$, even when the attainable state has dependent coordinates.

Choose an orthogonal $U_\Sigma$ diagonalizing $\Sigma$ in decreasing
eigenvalue order. Lemma~\ref{lem:algebra-product-chart} gives
\[
 U_\Sigma^t(v_n-m)=\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_i)U_\Sigma^t\theta_n,
             \qquad\|\theta_n\|\le R.
\]
Together with its whole-image semialgebraic statement, this is (G) with
$p_n=q_n=d$, $T_n=U_\Sigma^t$ and scales $s_{n,i}=\lambda_i$.
Translations absorb the prior mean. All orthogonal matrices and their
inverses have norm one; there is no division by a zero eigenvalue.

For (A), Lemma~\ref{lem:algebra-acquired-density} gives a density lower
bound on a ball before multiplication by $\Sigma$. Rotation preserves
that ball and its Lebesgue density. For every $\ell\le d$, integration
over the last $d-\ell$ coordinates gives a density lower bound on
$[-r/(2\sqrt d),r/(2\sqrt d)]^\ell$: the remaining fiber contains
a ball of a fixed positive radius, with zero-dimensional volume one
when $\ell=d$. Multiplying the first $\ell$ coordinates by the
positive $\lambda_i$ gives a rectangle with proportional widths and
fixed dominated probability mass. This proves (A) for every nonzero
prefix. The preceding density lemma has already included the report
probability and used one common exploration law.

Lemma~\ref{lem:algebra-update} gives (C), with the same raw-state
metric used by the queries. Theorem~\ref{thm:causal-transfer} now applies
with profile \eqref{eq:algebra-profile}. It gives the integer-budget
checkpoint bounds, the accumulated error \eqref{eq:algebra-accumulation},
and \eqref{eq:algebra-causal-law}. When all eigenvalues vanish,
\eqref{eq:algebra-chart} makes every state equal to $m$, so one label
is exact. All constants in the verification have the stated uniformity.
\end{proof}
