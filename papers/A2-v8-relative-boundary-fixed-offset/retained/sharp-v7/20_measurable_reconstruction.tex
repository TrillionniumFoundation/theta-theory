\section{A measurable reconstruction convention}
\label{sec:v7-measurable}
The inverse estimates above are implemented by fitting finite data over a compact admissible parameter set. Nonuniqueness of a fit need not introduce a measurability qualification. We specify an exact tie-breaking rule that applies to every such fit in this article, including the compact fallbacks after an unsuccessful pilot.

\begin{proposition}[Borel selection of an exact compact fit]
\label{prop:v7-measurable}
Let $K\subset\R^r$ be nonempty and compact, let $F:K\to\R^q$ be continuous, and fix a norm on the data space. There is a Borel map $\widehat\theta:\R^q\to K$ such that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v7-argmin}
 \|x-F(\widehat\theta(x))\|=\min_{\theta\in K}\|x-F(\theta)\|
 \qquad\text{for every }x\in\R^q.
\end{equation}
It can be chosen by the fixed nested-cube rule in the proof. In particular, if $\theta_0\in K$ and $\|x-F(\theta_0)\|\le\delta$, then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:v7-fit-error}
             \|F(\widehat\theta(x))-F(\theta_0)\|\le2\delta.
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Choose a fixed closed cube containing $K$. Repeatedly bisect every coordinate of a cube into two closed intervals and order its $2^r$ closed children once and for all, lexicographically by their lower corners. Overlaps on cube boundaries are permitted. For any fixed descendant cube $C$ with $K\cap C\ne\varnothing$, put
\[
 s_C(x)=\min_{\theta\in K\cap C}\|x-F(\theta)\|,
 \qquad s(x)=\min_{\theta\in K}\|x-F(\theta)\|.
\]
Each is 1-Lipschitz in $x$, by the triangle inequality and compact attainment. Hence $\{x:s_C(x)=s(x)\}$ is a closed set. Skip cubes with empty intersection with $K$.

Starting from the initial cube, select at each level the first child $C$ for which $s_C(x)=s(x)$. At least one exists, since the parent contains a global minimizer and its children cover it. For each fixed level, the selected cube is a Borel function of $x$: this follows inductively from finitely many closed membership tests and the fixed child order. Its center is therefore a Borel function as well. The selected closed cubes are nested and their diameters tend to zero. Each contains an element of the compact minimizer set. Their unique common limit consequently belongs to $K$ and attains $s(x)$. Define it to be $\widehat\theta(x)$. As the pointwise limit of the Borel center maps, it is Borel, proving \eqref{eq:v7-argmin}. Finally the fitted residual is at most $\delta$, so the triangle inequality proves \eqref{eq:v7-fit-error}.
\end{proof}

For the three-amplitude reconstruction, take $F$ to consist of the spacing and three amplitudes on a small compact physical family. For the ambient four-amplitude reconstruction, use the continuous map of the gap and four amplitudes on its compact admissible coefficient set. Sorted real roots, their multiplicities represented by repeated coordinates, and the area are continuous functions on the respective physical sets in the matching topology used above. Their composition with the selected fit is measurable. Any fixed compact fallback is measurable. Conditioning the second-stage law on the pilot is therefore an ordinary conditioning on recorded data, not on a nonmeasurable choice of a minimizer. This convention leaves the factor $2\delta$ and all existing inverse rates unchanged. It asserts a well-defined decision rule; it does not assert an efficient numerical algorithm for computing a global minimum.
