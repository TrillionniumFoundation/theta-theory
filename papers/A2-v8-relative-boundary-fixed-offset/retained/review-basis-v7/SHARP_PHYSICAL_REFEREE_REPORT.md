## 2. Audit of the new mathematics

### 2.1 Exact overlap and products

**Locator:** `v7/10_sharp_experiments.tex`, `prop:v7-overlap`.

In the identical-even class the finite Hessian is

\[
 H_j=a\begin{pmatrix}\coth(j\gamma)&-\operatorname{csch}(j\gamma)\\
 -\operatorname{csch}(j\gamma)&\coth(j\gamma)\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad H_\infty=aI,
\]

with equal determinants. The quadratic endpoint/residual laws have equal constant densities on their respective sublevel sets. The common transformation to a disk and an equal-area ellipse gives

\[
 \delta_j={2\over\pi}\arcsin(e^{-j\gamma}).
\]

The intersection integral and the trigonometric identity are correct. Equality of densities on the overlap is the reason the product distance is exactly \(1-(1-\delta_j)^k\), not merely bounded above by \(k\delta_j\). With the common tangent success mass \(p_j^0=d^2/[2A\sinh(j\gamma)]\), the raw product distance is exactly \(1-(1-p_j^0\delta_j)^n\). Restoring failures is therefore mathematically essential, not a reporting detail.

### 2.2 Positive-offset error and the supercritical projection

**Locators:** `lem:v7-tangent`, `thm:v7-phase`.

The even graphs imply simultaneous endpoint sign symmetry of the stationary action and normalized twist. Uniform fourth action derivatives and second twist derivatives give

\[
 E_j(Y)=\tfrac12Y^TH_jY+O(|Y|^4),\qquad b_j(Y)=1+O(|Y|^2),
\]

with constants independent of j. Coercivity bounds the scaled endpoint domain; the residual fibers share their lower endpoint. The positive-part map therefore converts action error into a support symmetric-difference bound. Integrating, normalizing, and restoring failure masses prove conditional error \(Cd\) and raw error \(Cp_j^0d\). This supplies the uniform remainder that the preceding tangent-only calculation did not provide.

For finite effective sample size the product estimate and \(d_j/e^{-j\gamma}\to0\) make the accumulated tangent error vanish. The proof for infinite effective sample size correctly uses projection to a finite critical subsample and then sends its effective size to infinity. This is stronger than simply assuming the full-sample error \(k_jd_j\) or \(n_jp_j^0d_j\) tends to zero. In particular, such a full-sample remainder need not vanish above criticality; the projection argument avoids that trap. The necessity argument for vanishing total variation is also appropriately by subsequences and projections.

The result is nevertheless a tangent-regime statement. At a finite nonzero critical value the entire nonlinear experiment is approximated by its quadratic experiment with vanishing error. It does not prove that the general nonlinear fixed-offset transfer rate is sharp, and the source does not assert that. The surviving distinction between these statements is a significance issue, not a false theorem.

### 2.3 Physical count lower bound, high confidence, and stopping

**Locator:** `v7/30_count_design_lower_bound.tex`.

The two physical alternatives have gap 1/2, radii \((R+36s,R-18s,R-18s)\) and \((R-36s,R+18s,R+18s)\), and matching curvature separation comparable to |s|. The order-three spatial symmetry kills the linear differential of the full normalized finite-offset count probability. Bounded third derivatives then give \(|p_s-p_{-s}|\le Cd^2|s|^3\), with \(p_{\pm s}\asymp d^2\). The radius-to-curvature matching formula is valid for sufficiently small s; no separated-root assumption is silently introduced.

The elementary Bernoulli divergence bound gives \(D_{\rm KL}\le Cd^2s^6\) per query. Under a common recorded history, a randomized policy has the same next query and stop rule under the two hypotheses. The padded finite-history chain rule, followed by the entropy limit, yields the stated stopped-transcript bound. Finite expected cost gives almost-sure finite stopping; an infinite expectation already satisfies the lower bound.

An estimator accurate within epsilon at both alternatives yields a test with both errors at most eta. Binary data processing gives

\[
 D_{\rm KL}(\mathsf P_s\Vert\mathsf P_{-s})
 \ge(1-2\eta)\log((1-\eta)/\eta)
 \ge\tfrac14\log(1/\eta),\quad 0<\eta<1/4.
\]

This justifies the confidence logarithm rather than inferring it from a constant-confidence statement. Combining it with all-history offsets at most Hh gives the printed \(h^{-2}\varepsilon^{-6}\log(1/\eta)\) lower bound. The pilot clipping and enlarged second-stage windows obey such a fixed H_m bound. Therefore the matched upper and lower orders really concern the same shrinking-offset indicator experiment.

They do not concern arbitrary complete count values, positions, unbounded flight numbers, nonshrinking offsets, or uniform m tending to infinity. Those exclusions are printed and must be respected in both favorable and unfavorable interpretations.

### 2.4 Measurable reconstruction and calibration

**Locators:** `v7/20_measurable_reconstruction.tex`; inherited `v5/50_self_calibration.tex`, `v6/40_count_only_acquisition.tex`.

The nested closed-cube rule is sound. Each fixed compact-subset minimum residual is 1-Lipschitz in the data; its equality to the global minimum is a closed test. Choosing the first admissible child at each finite stage is Borel, nested diameters tend to zero, and the limit belongs to the compact minimizer set. The two-residual fit inequality is unchanged. This is a definition of a statistical decision rule, not an efficient optimization algorithm.

The inherited square-root pilot estimates a zero using polynomial coefficients and positive nodal probabilities; its proof does not require a negative-offset physical observation. The clipped estimate gives an all-history timing margin when the supplied coarse bracket is valid. Fresh second-stage preparations permit conditioning on the pilot, and the normalized timing error is explicitly charged.

This branch still assumes \(|g-g_0|\le h/4\) as supplied information and explicitly does not charge its acquisition. It would be inaccurate to credit it with the fixed-initial-interval search and caps present only in the parallel critical-boundary revision. Conversely, that declared local assumption is not an unacknowledged mathematical gap in this theorem.

## 3. Inherited core and the independently checked comparison

The principal geometric, alternating-Jacobi, trace-norm relative-flux, half-line factorization, physical integration, and experiment-transfer dependencies were examined during the paired review. The positive endpoint Hessian and exponentially small mixed derivative are correctly distinguished. The common residual-fiber argument justifies total variation for endpoints and residual time, not for different embedded full collision arrays. No fatal defect was identified in those inspected chains under their stated local and compact-family assumptions.

The explicit physical support-family source and three-amplitude inverse were compared across the two frozen revisions: their Git blob identities are, respectively, `dcd0cc453a9279f9979a3105c285216b668e8a5c` and `b67d4b0ba98ba98b8a901d41592d2b1549a783ac`. The count-acquisition source has blob `0c660bc399d9690060effdd8c0dd965ff6f34a07`. The fixed-offset comparison therefore uses mathematics actually present in this revision, not a sibling-only inverse.

Here is its decisive calculation. Write e for the three symmetric radius coefficients and \(g_*\) for the known center of the fixed local family. Query at

\[
 (1,g_*+a),\quad(1,g_*+2a),\quad(2,2g_*+a),\quad(3,3g_*+a),
\]

where a is one small fixed positive constant. Full finite-offset count probabilities in this analytic family are invariant under all radius permutations: the triangular lattice supplies both a cyclic rotation and a reflection. Fixed-domain analytic integration and analytic descent across the cubic discriminant give \(F_j(g,e,d)=C_j(g,e)+dB_j(g,e,d)\) in a full coefficient neighborhood. That descent is proved in the companion calculation; it is not presumed from labelled-root continuity.

A row difference separates gap information from the first-flight amplitude. After scaling the gap column by a, the limiting determinant is \(C_1\det D_e(C_1,C_2,C_3)\), which is nonzero by the printed three-amplitude theorem. An exact finite-offset compact fit is consequently Lipschitz in gap and coefficient coordinates, then cube-root stable in matching curvature distance. Four Bernoulli sample means yield the \(\varepsilon^{-6}\log(1/\eta)\) curvature benchmark, with a deterministic preparation count and an unknown true gap.

This comparison uses the known formula of the finite-dimensional analytic model; it is not a result for unspecified smooth nuisance remainders. At that budget its gap and area errors are O(epsilon cubed), not the extrapolation theorem's finer O(epsilon to the power 3+3/m) gap target. It neither invalidates the joint theorem nor its forced-shrinking-design optimum. It is a necessary comparative benchmark for the narrower claim about curvature acquisition cost in the exact physical family.

## 4. Requirements and severity

**S-R1 — Major comparative positioning.** Distinguish curvature loss in the exact known parametric family from a stronger joint timing target and from robustness to unknown higher-order smooth terms. Address the fixed-offset comparison by proving it, identifying a specific mathematical failure, or explaining a precisely stated nuisance model which excludes it. Repeating the design restriction is correct but does not replace this comparison. No result needs to be deleted to make it.

**S-R2 — Major significance assessment.** The positive-offset testing transition is a valid realization of the tangent support benchmark, including the correct supercritical proof. It does not by itself establish exceptional significance of the nonlinear fixed-offset determinant law. The latter needs a focused importance case relative to predecessor hyperbolic/action-Hessian mechanisms. A new global rigidity or arbitrary-itinerary theorem is not imposed as a moving acceptance condition. Nor is rejection a theorem that the program cannot succeed.

**S-R3 — Exposition and canonical submission.** The active main file includes both a new introduction and the inherited introduction before a large sequence of retained results. A conceptual hierarchy should distinguish the relative analytical theorem, its tangent testing corollary, and finite-dimensional inverse experiments. Keeping historical source bytes in Git does not force chronological accumulation in the active article. Preserve proofs while reorganizing the exposition. Designate a canonical next revision rather than letting “v7” refer to two complementary manuscripts.

**S-R4 — Delivery evidence, not mathematical falsity.** `DELIVERY_STATUS_V7.md` explicitly says no new PDF build completed, no new PDFs were delivered, and cross-reference/page/render validation remains uncompleted for this branch. That candor is appropriate. Do not reuse the sibling's author-reported build or inherited v6 build as verification of this source tree. A reproducible build of the designated final manuscript remains a delivery requirement; the present absent build is not a counterexample to a theorem and is not the principal reason for this adverse venue recommendation.

The exact support formula and constant-confidence cubic testing mechanism were already in the controlling referee memorandum and are acknowledged here. The projection, positive-offset, confidence, and measurability additions should be credited for what they prove, not inflated into independent foundational breakthroughs merely because each has a theorem label.

Primary records were checked online in this paired review. Bolotin and Treschev's *Hill's formula* concerns action-Hessian/monodromy relations for Lagrangian systems [1]. Zelditch's inverse work uses localized wave-trace information and specified analytic symmetry classes [2]. These references are relevant comparisons, not evidence that the entire submitted relative probability theorem has already been proved. The new fixed-offset comparison is derived from the printed family and Jacobian, not attributed to an external paper or claimed as a previously published result.

## 7. Final disposition

This is a real and mathematically meaningful v7 response. Its declared shrinking-design optimum, confidence dependence, and supercritical argument survive the targeted audit. The supplied-bracket assumption and absent PDF build are stated honestly. It would be unfair to treat either as a concealed theorem failure, or to repeat already-resolved v5/v6 criticisms.

I nevertheless withhold the requested top-four recommendation. No promise of acceptance follows from a checklist, and no impossibility conclusion follows from this rejection.
