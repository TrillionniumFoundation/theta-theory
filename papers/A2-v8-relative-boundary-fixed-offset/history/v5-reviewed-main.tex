\begin{abstract}
We prove collision-order-uniform threshold laws for periodic dispersing
billiards under normalized phase-volume preparation. A nonlinear
Dirichlet construction controls the exponentially small endpoint twist
relatively, and a trace-class comparison factors its limit into two
half-line boundary amplitudes. This yields smooth fixed-offset limiting
probabilities and conditional endpoint laws. For identical even facing
graphs, their offset coefficients determine all even contact jets by an
explicit triangular inverse; analytic boundaries are thereby determined
in their contact frames. Arbitrarily many finite nonlinear statistical
coordinates are realized by analytic obstacles with unchanged gap, area,
and complete leading count and endpoint-metric data. The corresponding
one-flight inverse is also given. For three unlabelled equal-gap channels,
the first four amplitudes recover the area and curvature multiset near
coalescence with a sharp pairwise one-third-H\"older bound. Finally, a
count-based local calibration procedure includes fine timing in the
preparation cost of high-order conditional curvature recovery. The
uniform boundary-law results require neither finite horizon nor symmetry;
the additional assumptions for each inverse problem are stated separately.
\end{abstract}
