\section{Deep clearance at the original normalization and the same comparison pool}
\label{sec:lc-normalized}

We apply the physical selected roof height, rather than an integrated
clearance mass, to the original persistent positive source.  The original
radius family, collision map, section and return tests, unit-speed clock,
all joint labels, and conditioning are retained.  At the outer stage fix
$B$ and all its original parameters
$\varepsilon(B),h_B,r_B,\gamma_B,K_B$; in particular
$\gamma_B\ge1$.  No parameter is chosen from a spectral estimate at a
collision-dependent window.

Let
\begin{equation}\label{eq:lc-original-depths}
 q_0=47/53,\qquad d'_j=\min(j,m-1-j),\qquad
 \delta_{j,B}=2\varepsilon(B)q_0^{d'_j/4},\qquad 0\le j<m.
\end{equation}
These are exactly the clearance thresholds of the original guard.
For an integer $D\ge0$ define the physical union
\begin{equation}\label{eq:lc-deep-event}
 \mathcal E^{\rm clr}_{B,m}(D)
   =\bigcup_{\substack{0\le j<m\\d'_j\ge D}}
          \{x:N_{\delta_{j,B},R}(T_R^jx)\ge1\}.
\end{equation}
If the index range is empty, the union is empty.  At a regular target
point this is the event that at least one actual original clearance
witness occurs in the indicated interior flight range.  Different
witnesses may occur at different roofs of the same paired segment;
no common witness on the whole segment is required.

\begin{theorem}[Complete deep-clearance height and its collision payment]
\label{thm:lc-deep-payment}
For every finite $m,B,D$ satisfying
$2\varepsilon(B)q_0^{D/4}\le\delta_*$, every original exact event
$\Pi_{n,k,m,R}$, and every original Borel selector $0\le H\le1$,
the pushforward
\[
 (L_{m,R})_*\bigl(\one_{\Pi_{n,k,m,R}}H
                \one_{\mathcal E^{\rm clr}_{B,m}(D)}\,d\nu_R^*\bigr)
\]
has a density of essential height at most
\begin{equation}\label{eq:lc-deep-finite}
 Q_{\varepsilon(B)}(D)
 =2C_{\rm clr,roof}\left\{
   \frac{(2\varepsilon(B))^{1/4}q_0^{D/16}}{1-q_0^{1/16}}
                   +\frac{\theta_{\rm r}^{D}}{1-\theta_{\rm r}}
                    \right\}.
\end{equation}
The same bound holds for the unchanged persistent source restricted
at the target roof to $\mathcal E^{\rm clr}_{B,m}(D)$, and for any
positive subsource of that persistent source.

Fix $\eta>0$, and put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:lc-log-depth}
 q_{\rm c}=\max\{q_0^{1/16},\theta_{\rm r}\}<1,\qquad
 D_m=\left\lceil\frac{(2+\eta)\log(m+1)}{|\log q_{\rm c}|}\right\rceil.
\end{equation}
For every fixed outer $B$, the size condition preceding
\eqref{eq:lc-deep-finite} holds at all sufficiently large $m$, and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:lc-normalized-rate}
 \sup_{R,n,k}m^2
 \|\mathsf U_{B,m}^{\rm per}[\mathcal E^{\rm clr}_{B,m}(D_m)]\|_\infty
 \le A_B(m+1)^{-\eta},
\end{equation}
where
\begin{equation}\label{eq:lc-AB}
 A_B=2C_{\rm clr,roof}\left\{
       \frac{(2\varepsilon(B))^{1/4}}{1-q_0^{1/16}}
                              +\frac1{1-\theta_{\rm r}}\right\}.
\end{equation}
All norms are essential suprema on the complete original roof line.
The asserted inner suprema follow after summing all physical words,
all marks, both impact signs and every relevant flight index.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
By positivity, the selected union measure is at most the sum of the
single-flight measures in \eqref{eq:lc-selected-roof}.  Every width
in the index range is at most
$2\varepsilon(B)q_0^{D/4}\le\delta_*$, so
Theorem~\ref{thm:lc-selected-roof-height} applies to each term.
There are at most two flight indices of each nonnegative depth.  Thus
\[
 \sum_{d'_j\ge D}\delta_{j,B}^{1/4}
 \le\frac{2(2\varepsilon(B))^{1/4}q_0^{D/16}}
                                                {1-q_0^{1/16}}.
\]
Also the two endpoint tails are separate subseries:
\[
 \sum_{D\le j\le m-1-D}
       (\theta_{\rm r}^j+\theta_{\rm r}^{m-1-j})
                  \le\frac{2\theta_{\rm r}^D}{1-\theta_{\rm r}}.
\]
These exact finite-index bounds prove \eqref{eq:lc-deep-finite}.
Double witnesses enlarge the positive upper sum but are counted once
in the union itself.  The original section and full joint label are
restored through the positive domination
\eqref{eq:rbv-domination}; no new conditioning factor is introduced.

For the persistent source use only the elementary coefficient
inequality \eqref{eq:v111-domination}:
\[
       0\le c_{\rm per}^{\varepsilon,\gamma}
                         \le f^\varepsilon(t,\theta)\le A(t,\theta).
\]
Multiply this inequality by the unchanged target selector and
$\one_{\mathcal E^{\rm clr}_{B,m}(D)}$, then perform the existing
one-copy roof--angle integration.  This proves domination by the
same physical selected majorant at the target roof.  A minimum at
unrelated segment endpoints is not estimated by a small bulk set.
This use of the elementary coefficient inequality does not invoke
the whole-word incidence pressure theorem or assume a uniform
comparison-capacity abundance statement.

The definition \eqref{eq:lc-log-depth} gives
\[
 q_0^{D_m/16}\le q_{\rm c}^{D_m}
          \le(m+1)^{-(2+\eta)},\qquad
 \theta_{\rm r}^{D_m}\le(m+1)^{-(2+\eta)}.
\]
In particular
$2\varepsilon(B)q_0^{D_m/4}
 \le2\varepsilon(B)(m+1)^{-4(2+\eta)}$.
At each fixed $B$ this is eventually at most $\delta_*$, without
any restriction on its original positive width beyond finiteness.
Insert these inequalities into \eqref{eq:lc-deep-finite} and use
$m^2\le(m+1)^2$ to obtain \eqref{eq:lc-normalized-rate}.
The finitely many earlier collision counts do not affect a limsup.

The selected depth is only a substitution in a finite-count bound
valid for every $D$.  It is not a shrinking physical roof, section,
residence or comparison window.  Moreover $D_m=O(\log m)=o(m)$,
so the interior range contains $m-O(\log m)$ actual flights and
is nonempty for every sufficiently large count.  The statement is
therefore not an empty-event payment.  At fixed $B$ its rate is
uniform in the original radius and exact labels, since all constants
were fixed before these suprema.  This proves the theorem in the
prescribed order.
\end{proof}

\subsection{An exact shortage equivalence with the original full pool}

Keep the existing source $\mathsf U_{B,m}^{\rm low}$ in
\eqref{eq:v112-new-sources}, with precisely its old incidence-load,
section and pairing selectors.  Define two positive restrictions,
with no renormalization, by
\begin{equation}\label{eq:lc-positive-split}
 \begin{split}
 \mathsf U_{B,m}^{\rm low,deep}
    &=\mathsf U_{B,m}^{\rm low}
                         [\mathcal E^{\rm clr}_{B,m}(D_m)],\\
 \mathsf U_{B,m}^{\rm low,end}
    &=\mathsf U_{B,m}^{\rm low}
                         [\mathcal E^{\rm clr}_{B,m}(D_m)^c].
 \end{split}
\end{equation}
The notation $\mathrm{end}$ describes the location of possible
clearance witnesses on this complement: every such witness lies
in one of the two layers $d'_j<D_m$.  It makes no assertion that
this remaining positive source is small or has enough comparison
capacity.  Define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:lc-same-pool}
 \mathsf R_{B,m}^{\rm end}
       =(\mathsf U_{B,m}^{\rm low,end}
                            -K_BP_{r_B,m}^{\rm ng})_+.
\end{equation}
The comparison pool $P_{r_B,m}^{\rm ng}$ is the same full original
nongrazing pool as in \eqref{eq:v112-new-sources}.  It is not
restricted to the new source class, and its multiplier and its
microscopic width are unchanged.

\begin{theorem}[Original-height equivalence of the two shortages]
\label{thm:lc-same-pool}
At every finite count there is the exact positive identity and bound
\begin{align}
 \mathsf U_{B,m}^{\rm low}
    &=\mathsf U_{B,m}^{\rm low,deep}
                             +\mathsf U_{B,m}^{\rm low,end},
                                                 \label{eq:lc-split-identity}\\
 0\le\mathsf R_{B,m}^{\rm low}-\mathsf R_{B,m}^{\rm end}
    &\le\mathsf U_{B,m}^{\rm low,deep}.
                                                 \label{eq:lc-shortage-difference}
\end{align}
For every fixed $B,M$, the difference has zero original ordered
height:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:lc-zero-ordered-height}
 \limsup_{m\to\infty}\sup_{R,n,k}m^2
 \esssup_{t\in\mathcal C_{m,R,n,k}(M)}
 (\mathsf R_{B,m}^{\rm low}(t)-\mathsf R_{B,m}^{\rm end}(t))=0.
\end{equation}
Consequently the old shortage obligation \eqref{eq:v112-remaining}
is equivalent, with exactly the same order of limits, to
\begin{equation}\label{eq:lc-final-shortage}
 \lim_{B\to\infty}\limsup_{m\to\infty}\sup_{R,n,k}m^2
 \esssup_{t\in\mathcal C_{m,R,n,k}(M)}
 (\mathsf U_{B,m}^{\rm low,end}(t)-K_BP_{r_B,m}^{\rm ng}(t))_+=0
 \qquad\hbox{for every fixed }M.
\end{equation}
This equivalence itself uses the new physical height theorem and
the elementary persistent domination, without importing an
unproved pressure or pool-abundance conclusion.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The event in \eqref{eq:lc-deep-event} and its complement partition
the same original target source.  This proves
\eqref{eq:lc-split-identity} before and after pushforward.  For
$u,v,c\ge0$,
\[
                  0\le(u+v-c)_+-(u-c)_+\le v.
\]
Take $u=\mathsf U^{\rm low,end}$,
$v=\mathsf U^{\rm low,deep}$ and
$c=K_BP_{r_B}^{\rm ng}$.  The same value of $c$ is essential for
this inequality to compare the specified shortages.  It proves
\eqref{eq:lc-shortage-difference} pointwise at almost every roof.
The low-load deep subsource is a positive subsource of the full
persistent deep source.  Theorem~\ref{thm:lc-deep-payment} therefore
bounds its complete-roof normalized height by
$A_B(m+1)^{-\eta}$ for all sufficiently large $m$.  Restriction
to the original central set only decreases the essential supremum.
This proves \eqref{eq:lc-zero-ordered-height}.

At every fixed $B$ the two nonnegative normalized shortage heights
thus have the same collision limsup, including when that value is
infinite: each is bounded above by the other plus a nonnegative
sequence tending uniformly to zero.  Sending $B$ to infinity last
proves the exact equivalence of the two displayed obligations.
The original $\varepsilon(B),h_B,r_B,\gamma_B,K_B$ stay fixed
through the collision limsup, and all label and radius suprema
remain inside it.  No roof supremum is interchanged with a
limit or replaced by an averaged norm.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Proved payment and the remaining minimal closure condition.}
The new conclusion pays a complete class of actual clearance witnesses
in the original $m^2L^\infty$ roof topology.  Its estimate covers every
physical word and every mark in the middle $m-O(\log m)$ flights,
including zero-incidence-load source, and retains its original
persistent coefficient and all conditioning factors.  The logarithmic
layers are the result of a proved uniform geometric tail and singular
multiplier bound; the mass outside them was not declared negligible by
a source partition alone.

Equation~\eqref{eq:lc-final-shortage} is still a substantive mathematical
obligation.  The present proof supplies no same-roof lower bound for
$P_{r_B}^{\rm ng}$ on the remaining positive source.  A guard witnessed
only by shallow clearance can still have zero incidence load and a
positive coefficient.  Shallow incidences, nongrazing critical entrances,
original section and pairing cuts, and short or capacity-deficient
segments are not removed by the new decomposition.  Establishing
\eqref{eq:lc-final-shortage} requires a physical comparison or decay
mechanism strong enough for its full suprema and original ordered
limits; a fixed unscaled endpoint-layer bound does not suffice.

Under the inherited analytic scalar input and the independently valid
previous incidence/load payments, the retained implication
\eqref{eq:v112-scalar} makes \eqref{eq:lc-final-shortage} the remaining
positive-source condition for the unchanged scalar raw pointwise
assertion.  If a previous pressure or spectral input has not been
independently established for the actual model, that dependency remains
in addition to this shortage condition.  The present equivalence does
not certify it.  Exact arithmetic, conditional return/path transfer,
and the four-coordinate local inverse retain their existing separate
dependencies and scope.  No new unconditional raw local limit or
completed inverse theorem is asserted by this section.
