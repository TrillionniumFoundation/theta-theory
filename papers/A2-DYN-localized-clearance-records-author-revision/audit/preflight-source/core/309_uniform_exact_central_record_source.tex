\section{An exponentially small source for exact central physical records}
\label{sec:ec-exact-source}
The anchor contraction gives a uniform positive-source theorem
without a word-count prefactor.  It controls all global minima
simultaneously and fixes the original section observations on their
physical central records.  The source is defined entirely by the
actual reference trajectory.  No exceptional event is inserted into
the original guard or into the original conditional return event.

Keep $R\in[R_-,r]$, $N=94$, $\tau_-=3/50$,
$q_{\rm a}$ and $j(d)$ from \eqref{eq:ae-contraction} and
\eqref{eq:ae-j-and-B}.  All probabilities in this section use
$\nu=d\alpha\,dp/(4\pi)$ or its unchanged section conditioning
$\nu_R^*=c_*^{-1}\nu|_{Y_R^*}$.  The original record is regular
for its $m$ flights; the estimates do not define a singular flow.
Bounded bulk sources, rather than singular paired traces, are under
consideration.

\begin{lemma}[The exact original section has a uniform collar bound]
\label{lem:ec-collar}
For $0<\rho\le h_0=1/200$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ec-collar}
 \sup_R\nu\{\operatorname{dist}_\infty(x,\partial Y_R^*)\le\rho\}
                                      \le\frac{\rho}{2\pi}.
\end{equation}
The bound refers to the five original rectangles in the collision
circle--momentum coordinates.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
For a rectangle of width $w$ and height $h$, with
$2\rho\le\min(w,h)$, its closed boundary collar has area at most
\[
 (w+2\rho)(h+2\rho)-(w-2\rho)(h-2\rho)
                                      =4\rho(w+h).
\]
On the circle this can be calculated on a lift; overlap at the
coordinate seam can only reduce the projected area.  Intersection
with $-1<p<1$ also cannot increase it.  The boundary of a finite
union is contained in the union of the component boundaries.
The four squares have $w=h=1/100$, and $U$ has $w=3/25$ and
$h=3/10$.  Therefore
$\sum(w+h)=4(2/100)+3/25+3/10=1/2$.  The total area is at most
$2\rho$, and division by $4\pi$ proves the claim.  The radius
changes the centers of two rectangles but not these widths.
\end{proof}

Put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ec-amplitudes}
 \begin{gathered}
 K_\phi=\frac{128}{\tau_-}+\frac{24C}{R_-^4},\\
 A_\Gamma=(2rB_\perp)^{1/2},\qquad
 A_c=(2r/\tau_-)^{1/2},\qquad
 A_\phi=(2rK_\phi)^{1/4}.
 \end{gathered}
\end{equation}

\begin{lemma}[A phase estimate at every active comparison contact]
\label{lem:ec-active-phase}
Let $i$ be an interior contact of depth $d_i=\min(i,m-i)\ge1$,
active in a global minimum.  Give that contact its circle coordinate
and the outgoing tangential component determined by its two adjacent
comparison directions.  Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ec-point-phase}
 d_\infty(x_i,\widehat x_i)^4
 \le K_\phi E_R(Q)q_{\rm a}^{j(d_i)}
 \le A_\phi^4q_{\rm a}^{j(d_i)}.
\end{equation}
This is a geometric collision-state estimate before the comparison
flights have been proved clear.  Inactivity is not assigned an
artificial collision state.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
At an active minimum its nonzero Cartesian gradient is a negative
multiple of the outward disk normal.  Its two unit directions
therefore obey the specular reflection law with strictly positive
normal incidence.  In particular their incoming and outgoing
tangential components agree.  The elementary circle and momentum
argument used in Theorem~\ref{thm:cp-phase}, now using the incoming
edge, gives
\[
 d_\infty(x_i,\widehat x_i)^4
 \le8|e_{i-1}|^4+24R_-^{-4}|\delta_i|^4 .
\]
That incoming edge has depth at least $d_i-1$.  Since
$|e_{i-1}|^4\le4|e_{i-1}|^2\le4F_{i-1}/\tau_-$, the flight
estimate \eqref{eq:ae-flight} gives
\[
 |e_{i-1}|^4\le
 \frac{8E_R(Q)}{\tau_-}
                     q_{\rm a}^{\lfloor(d_i-1)/N\rfloor}.
\]
Also \eqref{eq:ae-position} bounds $|\delta_i|^4$ by
$CE_R(Q)q_{\rm a}^{j(d_i)}$.
For every integer $d_i\ge1$,
$\lfloor(d_i-1)/N\rfloor\ge j(d_i)$.  The resulting direction
coefficient $64/\tau_-$ is at most the deliberately enlarged
$128/\tau_-$ in $K_\phi$.  This proves the first inequality; use
$E_R(Q)\le2r$ for the second.  The argument retains the incoming
edge even when the target consists of one central contact.
\end{proof}

Let $\delta_{\rm flux}=3/200$.  Define the finite constants

 \begin{split}
 J_{\rm obs}=1+\Biggl\lceil\frac{1}{-\log q_{\rm a}}\max\Bigl\{&
 0,\ 2\log(2A_\Gamma/\delta_{\rm flux}),\\[-2pt]
 &2\log(2A_c),\ 4\log(A_\phi/h_0)\Bigr\}\Biggr\rceil,

\end{equation}
For $D\ge D_{\rm obs}$, the following inequalities hold:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ec-small-widths}

 \quad A_cq_{\rm a}^{j(D)/2}\le1/2,

These are conservative constants fixed before every collision limit.
They do not modify the original guard widths

Every quantity in this finite union is an actual original observable.
In particular the clearance is the infimum over all third lattice

\begin{theorem}[A uniform exact-record source and its full positive mass]
\label{thm:ec-uniform-source}
Define
\begin{align}\label{eq:ec-source-bound}
 \epsilon_D={}&
 \frac{2NA_\Gamma}{R_-c_*}
       \frac{q_{\rm a}^{j(D)/2}}{1-q_{\rm a}^{1/2}}
 +\frac{2NA_c^2}{c_*}
       \frac{q_{\rm a}^{j(D)}}{1-q_{\rm a}}
 +\frac{NA_\phi}{\pi c_*}
       \frac{q_{\rm a}^{j(D)/4}}{1-q_{\rm a}^{1/4}} .
\end{align}
For every $D\ge D_{\rm obs}$, every $m,R,n,k$, every Borel
$0\le h\le1$ and every Borel roof set $I$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ec-positive-source}
 0\le\mu^{\rm obs}_{D,R,m,n,k,h}(I)\le\epsilon_D,\qquad
 \sup_{m,R,n,k,h}\|\mu^{\rm obs}_{D,R,m,n,k,h}\|_{\rm TV}
                               \le\epsilon_D\longrightarrow0
                          \quad(D\longrightarrow\infty).
\end{equation}
The complete physical word sum is already included in these
inequalities.  No word-count factor or independence assumption occurs.

If $Q\notin\mathcal E^{\rm obs}_{D,R,m}$ and $m>2D$, then
\emph{every} global minimum has an actual regular physical central
record $Y_D,\ldots,Y_{m-D}$ and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ec-exact-labels}
 \eta_R(\widehat x_i)=\eta_R(x_i)
                         \qquad(D\le i\le m-D).
\end{equation}
All those central contacts and states agree between global minima.
Their central displacement, number of flights, occupation, and two
endpoint section tests agree exactly with the original central
record.  The terminal indicator in \eqref{eq:ec-exact-labels} is
tested separately from its occupation count.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The exact one-flight physical clearance flux gives, for
$0<u<\delta_{\rm flux}$,
\[
 \nu\{\Gamma_0(Q)\le u\}
 \le\int N_u\,d\nu=\frac uR\le\frac u{R_-}.
\]
At equality boundaries, use the same estimate at $u+\varepsilon$
and pass down by continuity of a finite measure.  Invariance of the
actual collision map gives the same bound at each flight time.
The original incidence distribution is exact:
\[
 \nu\{c_i\le u\}
   =1-\sqrt{1-u^2}\le u^2\qquad(0\le u\le1).
\]
Lemma~\ref{lem:ec-collar} and invariance likewise give the collar
bound $\rho/(2\pi)$ at any contact time.  The hypotheses of all
three bounds hold at every included depth by
\eqref{eq:ec-small-widths}.  No assertion about independence of
different times is used.

At most two contacts or flights have a specified depth $d$.  For
any $a>0$ the elementary block sum gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ec-depth-sum}
 \sum_{d=D}^\infty q_{\rm a}^{a j(d)}
 \le N\,\frac{q_{\rm a}^{a j(D)}}{1-q_{\rm a}^{a}}
                         \qquad(D\ge D_{\rm obs}).
\end{equation}
Indeed $j(d)$ is constant on blocks of $N$ consecutive depths;
the first, possibly partial, block is enlarged to a whole block.
Apply the union bound to \eqref{eq:ec-bad-source}, the preceding
three one-time estimates, and \eqref{eq:ec-depth-sum} with
$a=1/2,1,1/4$.  Restriction to the original initial section costs
exactly $c_*^{-1}$.  Removing $\one_\Pi h\le1$ only on the upper
side then gives \eqref{eq:ec-source-bound}.  The positive
pushforward has total variation equal to its total mass; the roof
test can only decrease it.  Countable physical word cells form a
partition of the regular source, so this is already their complete
positive sum.  The geometric series tends to zero uniformly in all
the displayed variables, which proves \eqref{eq:ec-positive-source}.

Now fix an original record outside the source and any global
minimum.  At every included contact the original incidence is
strictly larger than $A_cq_{\rm a}^{j(d_i)/2}$.
Corollary~\ref{cor:ae-witnesses}, $E_R\le2r$, and
$\lfloor(d_i-1)/N\rfloor\ge j(d_i)$ show that inactivity is
impossible.  At each included flight,
\[
 \beta_i\le A_\Gamma q_{\rm a}^{j(d'_i)/2}
                  <\min\{\Gamma_i(Q),\tau_-/2\}.
\]
The strip lemma therefore gives strictly positive clearance from
every third disk, with both incident disks retained.

At every active vertex the Cartesian minimum condition gives the
specular law with positive incidence, as in the preceding lemma.
An outward edge from a circle and an inward edge ending at the next
circle stay outside their respective incident disks until the
prescribed contacts, by convexity and strict normal incidence.
Together with positive third-disk clearance, this proves that every
included flight is the actual first-hit physical flight to the next
specified disk.  Thus the whole central record is regular and no
collision has been hidden.

At a central contact, \eqref{eq:ec-point-phase} bounds the state
distance by $A_\phi q_{\rm a}^{j(d_i)/4}$, whereas the original
state is strictly farther than that from the actual section
boundary.  A shortest circle--momentum path cannot cross the
boundary, so the two indicators agree.  This argument includes both
central endpoint tests and every occupation state.  Equality of
centers gives the central lattice displacement and flight count
exactly.  Finally Lemma~\ref{lem:ae-common-minima} gives common
active vertices and unit directions for all minima.  There was no
measurable selection, parameter continuation, or minimum multiplicity
payment in this proof.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Exponential exact-label costs on the unchanged full roof]
\label{cor:ec-normalized}
Let $D_{\rm obs}\le D\le d$, $2d<m$, and restrict the measures
\eqref{eq:nc-measures} to
$Q\notin\mathcal E^{\rm obs}_{D,R,m}$.  Then
\begin{align}\label{eq:ec-exponential-costs}
 \mathsf T_{{\rm clock},d}
     &\le E_R(Q)q_{\rm a}^{\lfloor d/N\rfloor},\notag\\
 \mathsf T_{{\rm phase},d}
     &\le K_\phi E_R(Q)q_{\rm a}^{j(d)},\\
 \mathsf T_{\perp,d}
     &\le B_\perp E_R(Q)q_{\rm a}^{j(d)}.\notag
\end{align}
Consequently, under precisely
Input~\ref{input:v112-geometric-pressure}, their original full-roof
densities are bounded respectively by
\[
 2rC_\star q_{\rm a}^{\lfloor d/N\rfloor},\qquad
 2rC_\star K_\phi q_{\rm a}^{j(d)},\qquad
 2rC_\star B_\perp q_{\rm a}^{j(d)}.
\]
These assertions also apply to the dominated persistent low-load
interior source of Corollary~\ref{cor:nc-low-source}.

For fixed $0<\theta<1/2$ and $d_m=\lceil\theta m\rceil$, all three
collision-normalized heights are $O(m^2e^{-\lambda_0m})$, uniformly
in the original radius, exact labels and roof, where
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ec-lambda}
 \lambda_0=\frac{\theta|\log q_{\rm a}|}{N}>0.
\end{equation}
On this same source the section error in
\eqref{eq:cp-exact-twist} is exactly zero.  After retaining its
endpoint phase $e^{isW_d}$, the remaining nonnegative Fourier error
majorant is $|s|\mathsf T_{{\rm clock},d}$.
For $0<\lambda<\lambda_0$, its collision-normalized original-roof
height tends to zero uniformly for $|s|\le e^{\lambda m}$ and for
all lattice and return twists.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The clock identity \eqref{eq:cp-clock} is bounded by
$\mathcal E_d$, so the first estimate follows from the anchor
contraction.  To sum the phase distances use the proof of
Theorem~\ref{thm:cp-phase}, including its repeated terminal edge:
\[
 \mathsf T_{{\rm phase},d}
 \le\frac{64}{\tau_-}\sum_{i=d}^{m-d-1}F_i
                +24R_-^{-4}\sum_{i=d}^{m-d}|\delta_i|^4.
\]
Insert \eqref{eq:ae-flight} and \eqref{eq:ae-position}, and use
$\lfloor d/N\rfloor\ge j(d)$, to obtain exactly $K_\phi$.
The transverse estimate is \eqref{eq:ae-transverse}.
All three statements hold pointwise before integration.
Positive ordering on the unchanged original full roof, as in
Theorem~\ref{thm:nc-normalized}, and $E_R(Q)\le2r$ give the
density assertions under the named input.

For large $m$, $d_m$ is admissible, and
$j(d_m)\ge\theta m/N-2$.  Thus
$q_{\rm a}^{j(d_m)}\le q_{\rm a}^{-2}e^{-\lambda_0m}$.
The density bounds give the normalized rate.  The exact section
identity was proved for every central contact and both endpoint
tests, so the occupation term in the twist comparison vanishes.
Finally $m^2e^{\lambda m}e^{-\lambda_0m}\to0$ when
$\lambda<\lambda_0$, proving the stated frequency-range estimate.
This bounds the positive error majorant on the original roof; it
does not assert an interchange with an unbounded Fourier integral.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[The retained order of limits and the remaining full source]
\label{rem:ec-original-limit}
Theorem~\ref{thm:ec-uniform-source} proves
$\lim_{D\to\infty}\sup_{m,R,n,k,h}
 \|\mu^{\rm obs}_{D,R,m,n,k,h}\|_{\rm TV}=0$
on the entire original family.  Corollary~\ref{cor:ec-normalized}
proves a stronger roof topology for specified exponentially small
costs on its complement, under a precisely named inherited input.
Total variation of the exceptional source is not its roof essential
height.  In particular these two statements do not imply that its
$m^2L^\infty$ height vanishes.

The original $\varepsilon(B),h_B,r_B,\gamma_B,K_B$, microscopic
roof windows and guard $q_0$ remain fixed before the collision
limsup, and $B$ remains outermost in
\eqref{eq:sc-original-obligation}.  The positive shallow source,
all required physical multipliers, critical and singular paired
traces, roofwise pool capacity, full conditioning denominator and
the complete spectral/arithmetic inversion errors still require
their actual original-source bounds.  The exponentially accurate
central comparison does not discard the remaining end portions or
change the full event.  The final raw pointwise local limit theorem
and four-coordinate inversion are therefore not claimed complete.
\end{remark}
