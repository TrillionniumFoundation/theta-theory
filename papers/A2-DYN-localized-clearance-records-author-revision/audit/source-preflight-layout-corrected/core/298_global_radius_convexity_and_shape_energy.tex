\section{Global radius order and a positive physical shape energy}
\label{sec:gv-radius}
The radius--roof identities of Sections~\ref{sec:cc-clock} and
\ref{sec:rc-complete} are inherited. Here a global variational value
allows critical comparisons between disconnected physical continuation
intervals, and its second variation has a nonnegative, geometrically
explicit form. Nothing is continued as a true trajectory through a
forbidden collision or an unvisited disk.

\begin{theorem}[Global radius convexity of the disk-word value]
\label{thm:gv-convex-radius}
For each fixed word of Section~\ref{sec:gv-duality}, $R\mapsto\ell_w(R)$
is convex and strictly decreasing on $[R_-,R_+]$. For $R_1<R_2$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gv-global-slope}
 2(R_2-R_1)\le\ell_w(R_1)-\ell_w(R_2)\le2m(R_2-R_1).
\end{equation}
This statement concerns the geometric value even where its minimizer
is not physical. If regular physical critical records of the same word
exist at both $R_1,R_2$, with true roofs $a_1,a_2$, then the stronger bound
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gv-physical-global}
 \kappa_0m(R_2-R_1)\le a_1-a_2\le2m(R_2-R_1),
 \qquad\kappa_0=1/210560,
\end{equation}
holds without requiring a physical continuation on $(R_1,R_2)$.
Thus a fixed word has at most one regular physical critical record
with a prescribed physical roof over the \emph{whole} radius interval.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The dual formula \eqref{eq:gv-duality} is a maximum of affine functions
$C_w(V)-RT(V)$ over the same compact vector set for all $R$.
It proves convexity. At each $R_j$, choose the optimal unit-edge
vector $V_j$ furnished by the primal minimizer. Its affine function
supports $\ell_w$ from below and $2\le T(V_j)\le2m$. Therefore
\[
 T(V_2)(R_2-R_1)
 \le\ell_w(R_1)-\ell_w(R_2)
 \le T(V_1)(R_2-R_1).
\]
This proves \eqref{eq:gv-global-slope}, without differentiability of
$\ell_w$ or a common minimizing configuration.

At a regular physical critical point, Corollary~\ref{cor:gv-critical}
identifies its true roof with $\ell_w$. Its actual velocity vector
is an optimal dual vector and
$T(V)=2+2\sum_{i=1}^{m-1}c_i\ge\kappa_0m$ by the already proved
complete-bending bound \eqref{eq:cc-slope}. Use that vector at $R_2$
in the lower supporting inequality and the vector at $R_1$ in the
upper inequality. This proves \eqref{eq:gv-physical-global} across
any nonphysical holes. At a fixed radius the critical point is unique
by the inherited physical uniqueness theorem; distinct radii have
strictly ordered roofs by the new global comparison. No physical
orbit in a hole has been asserted or counted.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Transfer of complete physical bending to every minimizing dual]
\label{thm:gv-all-record-bending}
Let $Q$ be any regular physical record of $w$ at $R$, without normal
endpoint or clearance assumptions. Let $Y$ be any minimizer of $S$
on $K_w(R)$, let $V$ and $\widehat V$ be their respective unit flight
vectors, and write $E=R(2-c_0-c_m)$. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gv-velocity-energy}
 \frac12\sum_{i=0}^{m-1}|Y_{i+1}-Y_i|\,|\widehat v_i-v_i|^2\le E,
 \qquad
 T(\widehat V)\ge 2+2\sum_{i=1}^{m-1}c_i
                       -2\sqrt{2mE/\tau_-}.
\end{equation}
In particular, with $C_{\rm bend}=4\sqrt{R_+/\tau_-}$, every such
minimizing dual satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gv-minimum-bending}
 T(\widehat V)\ge b_m:=\kappa_0m-C_{\rm bend}\sqrt m,
 \qquad \kappa_0=1/210560.
\end{equation}
The comparison holds even when $Y$ is nonphysical and contains inactive
contacts. No inverse incidence occurs.

If $R_1<R_2$ both admit arbitrary regular physical records of this
same word, with actual roofs $L_1,L_2$, then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gv-all-record-radius}
 b_m(R_2-R_1)-2R_+
       \le L_1-L_2
       \le 2m(R_2-R_1)+2R_+.
\end{equation}
For $b_m>0$, all radii admitting an actual record of the word with
its true roof in any specified interval $I$ of length $h$ therefore
have diameter at most $(h+2R_+)/b_m$, even when that set is disconnected.
For $m\ge\lceil64R_+/(\tau_-\kappa_0^2)\rceil$, $b_m\ge\kappa_0m/2$.
This is a deterministic support bound across the original radius family,
not a radius-averaged substitute for the fixed-radius raw theorem.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use the actual velocity vector $V$ of $Q$ in the nonnegative gap
\eqref{eq:gv-gap} at the minimizing $Y$. Its total gap is
$\ell_w-D_{w,R}(V)\le L-D_{w,R}(V)=E$.
Its flight terms are exactly those in the first part of
\eqref{eq:gv-velocity-energy}. Every comparison edge has length at
least $\tau_-$, so
$\sum_i|\widehat v_i-v_i|^2\le2E/\tau_-$.
The two endpoint contributions to $T$ are one for both unit-vector
families. The reverse triangle inequality for each interior difference
and Cauchy--Schwarz give
\[
 |T(\widehat V)-T(V)|
 \le2\sum_i|\widehat v_i-v_i|
 \le2\sqrt{2mE/\tau_-}.
\]
For the actual physical velocities
$T(V)=2+2\sum c_i$. The complete-block incidence estimate in
Section~\ref{sec:cb-bending} gives
$2+2\sum c_i\ge\kappa_0m$ for every such record: this uses only
the same full-block counting as \eqref{eq:cc-slope}, not its critical
endpoint hypothesis. Since $E\le2R_+$, the stated $C_{\rm bend}$ follows.
This is an energy comparison with the actual trajectory, not a claim
that a minimizing polygon obeys the bending theorem physically.

Apply the supporting affine vector of a minimizer at $R_2$ to
$\ell_w(R_1)$. The preceding lower bound on its $T$ gives
$\ell_w(R_1)-\ell_w(R_2)\ge b_m(R_2-R_1)$.
The global upper bound remains $2m(R_2-R_1)$. Writing
$L_j=\ell_w(R_j)+e_j$ with $0\le e_j\le2R_+$ proves
\eqref{eq:gv-all-record-radius}. If both actual roofs lie in $I$,
$L_1-L_2\le h$, which yields the diameter bound for every pair of
admitted radii and thus for its entire, possibly disconnected, set.
The final integer threshold is the exact rearrangement of
$C_{\rm bend}\sqrt m\le\kappa_0m/2$. It is not claimed to be
sharp or to have been reached by the finite simulations.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Exact nonnegative curvature of the true critical clock]
\label{thm:gv-shape-energy}
On a connected interval of regular physical critical continuation of
one fixed word, write $Q_i(R)=O_i+Rn_i(R)$,
$X_i=Q_{i+1}-Q_i=\tau_i v_i$, and let a prime denote its genuine
radius derivative. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gv-shape-energy}
 \begin{split}
 a''(R)
 &=\sum_{i=0}^{m-1}\frac{|(I-v_iv_i^{\mathsf T})
                          (Q'_{i+1}-Q'_i)|^2}{\tau_i}
       +R\sum_{i=0}^m\lambda_i|n_i'|^2\\
 &=\sum_{i=0}^{m-1}\tau_i|v_i'|^2
       +R\left(|n_0'|^2+|n_m'|^2
                  +2\sum_{i=1}^{m-1}c_i|n_i'|^2\right)\ge0.
 \end{split}
\end{equation}
Every incidence and flight in this formula is physical. In particular
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gv-incidence-monotone}
 \frac d{dR}\sum_{i=1}^{m-1}c_i=-\frac12a''(R)\le0.
\end{equation}
At a regular physical point equality $a''(R)=0$ occurs precisely
for a normal two-disk bouncing record (possibly stopped after any
positive number of flights). Otherwise $a''(R)>0$.
No positive lower curvature constant uniform over all words is asserted.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The full Cartesian gradient of $S$ at a critical record is
$\nabla_iS=-\lambda_i n_i$, with the coefficients of
Corollary~\ref{cor:gv-critical}. Twice differentiating the sum of
actual lengths gives
\[
 a''=\sum_i\frac{|(I-v_iv_i^{\mathsf T})(Q'_{i+1}-Q'_i)|^2}{\tau_i}
             +\sum_i\nabla_iS\cdot Q_i''.
\]
Now $Q_i''=2n_i'+Rn_i''$, $n_i\cdot n_i'=0$, and
$n_i\cdot n_i''=-|n_i'|^2$. Substitution yields exactly
\eqref{eq:gv-shape-energy}, including its positive sign and both
endpoint factors. Since
$v_i'=(I-v_iv_i^{\mathsf T})(Q'_{i+1}-Q'_i)/\tau_i$, the second
display is identical to the first. The common formal reflection
construction supplies the needed smooth derivatives on each physical
interval, but no pressure or probability argument is used.
Differentiating the retained identity
$a'=-2-2\sum_{i=1}^{m-1}c_i$ proves
\eqref{eq:gv-incidence-monotone}.

If $a''=0$, every coefficient $R\lambda_i$ is positive, so all
$n_i'=0$, and all flight projections in \eqref{eq:gv-shape-energy}
vanish. Consequently $n_{i+1}-n_i$ is parallel to $v_i$.
At the initial normal contact $v_0=n_0$. The next normal must
therefore be parallel to $v_0$, and its negative incoming incidence
forces $n_1=-v_0$. The first reflection is normal and reverses the
flight. Repeating the argument gives normal incidence and reversal
at every contact. Since these are true first-hit flights, reversal
returns to the very disk just departed, not an invented farther
center. Hence the word is a repeated two-disk bounce. Conversely
that record has fixed contact normals and flight directions while
$R$ varies, and $a(R)=m(|O_1-O_0|-2R)$; its curvature is zero.
This also covers $m=1$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[A word-indexed critical radius--roof coarea]
\label{cor:gv-word-coarea}
In \eqref{eq:cc-coarea}, normalize $O_0=0$ as before and write
$\mathscr W_m$ for the countable set of all length-$m$ lattice words.
For each $t,w$, either no regular physical critical record of $w$
has roof $t$, or there is exactly one pair $(R_w(t),x_w(t))$.
For every nonnegative Borel weight $f$ and test $\phi$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gv-word-coarea}
 \int_{R_-}^{R_+}\sum_{x\in\mathscr K_m(R)}f(R,x)\phi(L(x))\,dR
 =\int_{\R}\phi(t)\sum_{w\in\mathscr W_m}
 \mathbf1_{\{(R_w,x_w)\ {\rm exists}\}}
 \frac{f(R_w(t),x_w(t))}{|a_w'(R_w(t))|}\,dt.
\end{equation}
The sum counts a word at most once at each roof, even when its set of
physical radii is disconnected. It allows infinite values and retains
arbitrary original section/return selectors. It is not a fixed-radius
LLT and does not bound the complete sum of the displayed weights.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Use the countable regular local charts and first-chart Borel allocation
of Corollary~\ref{cor:cc-coarea}. Each chart is locally invertible in
radius and roof. The new global order proves that charts of the same
word cannot yield distinct physical points at the same roof. Their
inverse graphs therefore agree on every overlap representing a point,
and combine into the indicated Borel partial graph. Nonnegative finite
chart substitution and monotone convergence give the identity, after
regrouping by word. This removes coordinate and disconnected-component
multiplicity, not any physical record. The reciprocal slope lies between
$1/(2m)$ and $1/(\kappa_0m)$ at every admitted term, as previously
proved. No radius average is exchanged with a radius supremum.
\end{proof}
