\section{Comparison costs on the original roof and their normalization}
\label{sec:nc-original-roof}
A deterministic small comparison cost and the density of the
exceptional physical source are different quantities.  Here positive
ordering pays the former on the unchanged physical roof.  The
unnormalized roof-height input is stated explicitly; none of the
geometric theorems just proved depends on it.

Fix $R,m,n,k$ and $d\ge2N$, $2d<m$.  On any Borel class
$\mathcal P_d$ of original records with a physical central minimum
as in Section~\ref{sec:cp-central}, choose one such global minimum
per physical disk word.  At fixed radius there are only countably
many finite words.  A chosen minimum is constant on its word, so
this choice gives Borel comparison coordinates on their physical
domains.  Alternatively, by Lemma~\ref{lem:ae-common-minima}, the
active central vertices of all such minima coincide.  No joint
measurability in the radius is needed for the original order
$\sup_R\esssup_t$.

Let $0\le h\le1$ be any Borel physical source selector; it may depend
on the whole record and on the fixed labels.  With $\Pi$ exactly as
in \eqref{eq:v76-event}, define the positive measures
\begin{equation}\label{eq:nc-measures}
 \mathcal M_{h,d}[f]
    =(L_{m,R})_*
       \bigl(\one_{\mathcal P_d}\one_\Pi h f\,d\nu_R^*\bigr),
 \qquad
 \mu_0=(L_{m,R})_*(\one_\Pi\,d\nu_R^*).
\end{equation}
Every pushforward is by the original full physical roof, even when
$f$ is a central comparison cost.  Let
$\mathsf T_{\perp,d}=\sum_{i=d}^{m-d-1}\beta_i^2$.

\begin{theorem}[Positive cost domination and its collision normalization]
\label{thm:nc-normalized}
For every Borel roof set $I\subset\mathbb R$ and
$f=\mathsf T_{{\rm clock},d},\mathsf T_{{\rm phase},d},
\mathsf T_{\perp,d}$, respectively,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:nc-positive-order}
 \mathcal M_{h,d}[f](I)\le K_f d^{-3}\mu_0(I),
 \qquad
 (K_f)=(K_{\rm clock},K_{\rm phase},K_\perp).
\end{equation}
This measure assertion is unconditional on the pressure input.

Under precisely Input~\ref{input:v112-geometric-pressure}, including
its physical stable arclength and optical-coordinate conventions,
these measures have densities on the whole original roof line, and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:nc-roof-cost}
 \left\|\frac{d\mathcal M_{h,d}[f]}{dt}\right\|_\infty
                                      \le C_\star K_f d^{-3}.
\end{equation}
In particular, for every fixed $0<\theta<1/2$ and
$d_m=\lceil\theta m\rceil$, for all sufficiently large $m$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:nc-normalization}
 \sup_{R,n,k,h}
 m^2\left\|\frac{d\mathcal M_{h,d_m}[f]}{dt}\right\|_\infty
 \le C_\star K_f\theta^{-3}m^{-1}\longrightarrow0.
\end{equation}
The same bound holds on each original microscopic roof interval and
on the original central target.  The norm on the whole line is the
stronger assertion.  The input constants are uniform in all the
displayed variables.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Theorems~\ref{thm:cp-clock}, \ref{thm:cp-phase}, and
\ref{thm:tb-budget} give $0\le f\le K_fd^{-3}$ pointwise on the
stated source class.  Multiply by
$\one_{\mathcal P_d}\one_\Pi h\,\one_I(L_{m,R})$ and integrate
against exactly $\nu_R^*$.  Removing only the upper-bound selectors
$\one_{\mathcal P_d}h\le1$ gives \eqref{eq:nc-positive-order}.
Equivalently the same proof works against every nonnegative Borel
test of the original roof.  Tonelli permits the complete positive
physical word sum before this comparison; there is no sum of a
separate error constant for each word.

For $S=\varnothing$, Theorem~\ref{thm:v111-roof-pressure}, under its
named input, gives $d\mu_0/dt\le C_\star$ almost everywhere, where
$C_\star=C_0Q_*/(2c_*\lambda_\circ)$.
The order property of positive measures first gives absolute
continuity of $\mathcal M_{h,d}[f]$ and then the bound on its
Radon--Nikodym derivative.  This uses the original inverse optical
Jacobian and section factor already in $C_\star$; it inserts no new
Jacobian and pays $c_*^{-1}$ only once.  Finally
$d_m\ge\theta m$ and $m^2/d_m^3\le\theta^{-3}/m$.
All constants were fixed before the collision limsup.  Restricting
a density norm to a roof interval cannot enlarge it.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[The dominated persistent low-load interior source]
\label{cor:nc-low-source}
For $\gamma\ge1$, the same positive-cost and conditional normalized
estimates apply to every Borel restriction of the inherited
persistent low-load interior coefficient which is dominated by the
original uncut roof measure as in Lemma~\ref{lem:v111-domination}.
This includes its original low-load selectors, all bounded by one.
It does not assert the corresponding domination for singular paired
endpoint traces or for an arbitrary full endpoint coefficient.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The inherited inequality is
$0\le c_{\rm per}^{\varepsilon,\gamma}
       \le f^\varepsilon(u,\vartheta)\le A(u,\vartheta)$
on each actual physical roof segment.  Multiplication by its
nonnegative low-load selector and by $f$ preserves the inequality.
Sum the actual source coefficients at their original roof and use
the positive original-measure comparison of that lemma.  The
result is a measure dominated by the right side of
\eqref{eq:nc-positive-order}; the preceding proof applies verbatim.
This argument invokes that specific domination theorem and its
existing physical multiplicities, not an assertion that a boundary
trace has a bulk density.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Central observation costs with a stated frequency range]
\label{cor:nc-fourier}
On the same source, after retaining $e^{isW_d}$ in
\eqref{eq:cp-exact-twist}, the nonnegative error majorant is
\[
 |s|\mathsf T_{{\rm clock},d}
 +2|\sin(\zeta/2)|
       \bigl(B_{\rho,d}+\rho^{-4}\mathsf T_{{\rm phase},d}\bigr).
\]
For fixed $\rho>0$, the two comparison-cost terms have
collision-normalized original-roof height tending to zero when
$d=d_m$ and $|s|=o(m)$, under the stated pressure input, uniformly
in the two lattice twists and the return twist.  The positive source
weighted by $B_{\rho,d}$ remains an explicit separate term.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Combine the exact twist estimate with the section payment and
Theorem~\ref{thm:nc-normalized}.  The lattice phase has modulus one,
and $2|\sin(\zeta/2)|\le2$ for all $\zeta$.  The clock coefficient
cost is bounded after multiplication by $m^2$ by a constant times
$|s|/m$.  At fixed $\rho$ the other comparison term is $O(m^{-1})$.
No estimate of $\mathcal M_{h,d}[B_{\rho,d}]$ was proved by this
argument, so it remains on its own side of the inequality.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[The exact scope of the normalization]\label{rem:nc-scope}
The estimates above are for specified small geometric costs on a
proved physical central comparison class.  They do not pay the
indicator of its complementary source, the original shallow end
portions, the singular one-sided or paired boundary traces, a
physical multiplier not dominated by the declared source, or a
shortage of roofwise comparison capacity.  Nor does an estimate
uniform on an increasing bounded frequency range by itself justify
the full unbounded Fourier integral.  In particular the measure
contraction in Theorem~\ref{thm:tb-uniform-source} alone is not an
$m^2L^\infty$ source estimate.  All these distinctions persist when
the sharper exponential costs below replace the algebraic bounds.
\end{remark}
