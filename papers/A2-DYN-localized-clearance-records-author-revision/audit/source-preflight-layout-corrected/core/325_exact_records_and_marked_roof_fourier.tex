\section{Exact marked records and a uniform physical-roof Fourier bound}
\label{sec:rv-records}

The original section $Y_R^*$ is the disjoint union of its four
specified squares and the specified rectangle $U$. In particular it
has $N_{\rm hor}=10$ horizontal sides and $N_{\rm ver}=10$ vertical
sides, counted with their actual coordinate levels and finite side
intervals. A square crossing the circular coordinate seam is glued
there; the seam is not another side. We use precisely these rectangles,
the original occupation convention and the original conditional measure.
No differentiability is assigned to an arbitrary measurable selector.

The constants of Theorem~\ref{thm:cs-charge} give
\begin{align}
 B_{\rm sec,int}
   &=\frac{2(N_{\rm hor}C_{\rm hor}
                          +N_{\rm ver}C_{\rm ver})}{1-\sigma},
                                                        \label{eq:rv-section-budget}\\
 B_{\rm sec,end}
   &=2(N_{\rm hor}E_{\rm hor}+N_{\rm ver}E_{\rm ver}).\notag
\end{align}
All collision indices $1,\ldots,m-1$ are included in the first
constant; both actual endpoint indices $0,m$ are included in the
second. When $m=1$ the interior sum is empty and its displayed
bound remains valid. Retaining the individual factors
$\sqrt{1-b^2}$ on horizontal sides can only improve this coarse
common constant.

Write $\eta_R=\one_{Y_R^*}$. At a fixed actual collision count
$m\ge1$, let
\[
 K=K^{\rm c}_{m,R}\in\mathbb Z^2,\qquad
 A=A_{m,R}=\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}\eta_R\circ T_R^j,\qquad
 L=L_{m,R}=\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}\tau_R\circ T_R^j.
\]
The initial visit is counted in $A$ and the terminal visit is tested
separately. The roof $L$ is the original unit-speed physical time.
For $n,k$ use the unchanged event $\Pi_{n,k,m,R}$ of
\eqref{eq:v76-event}. For real frequencies
$\xi\in\mathbb R^2$, $\zeta,\upsilon\in\mathbb R$, define
\begin{align}
 Q_{n,k,m}(x)
  &=\eta_R(x)\eta_R(T_R^mx)
                         \one_{\{K=k,A=n\}},\notag\\
 Q_m^{\xi,\zeta,\upsilon}(x)
  &=\eta_R(x)\eta_R(T_R^mx)
             \exp\{i(\xi\cdot K+\zeta A+\upsilon m)\}.
                                                        \label{eq:rv-weights}
\end{align}
The harmless collision-count phase $e^{i\upsilon m}$ makes the
fixed collision mark explicit. The other three discrete frequency
coordinates retain the two lattice labels and the exact occupation
count. The fourth nontrivial collision-observable frequency below is
the real roof frequency $s$. Thus $(\xi_1,\xi_2,\zeta,s)$
are exactly the original four transform coordinates at fixed $m$;
$\upsilon m$ records only that fixed collision tag and introduces
no fifth target coordinate or new inversion problem.

Let $w$ be either $1$ or the original $W^\varepsilon$, with
$\varepsilon>0$ fixed before any collision limit. Define the
positive or complex physical-roof measures
\begin{align}
 \mu_{n,k,m,R}^{w}
      &=L_*\bigl(w Q_{n,k,m}\,d\nu/c_*\bigr)
        =L_*\bigl(w\one_{\Pi_{n,k,m,R}}\,d\nu_R^*\bigr),
                                                        \label{eq:rv-measures}\\
 \mu_{m,R}^{w;\xi,\zeta,\upsilon}
      &=L_*\bigl(w Q_m^{\xi,\zeta,\upsilon}\,d\nu/c_*\bigr).
                                                        \notag
\end{align}
Set $D_{\rm base}^{1}=D_{\rm roof}$ and
$D_{\rm base}^{W^\varepsilon}=D_{\rm roof}^\varepsilon$ from
\eqref{eq:rbv-constants} and \eqref{eq:rbv-guard-constant}, and put
\begin{align}
 D_{\rm exact}^{w}
    &=D_{\rm base}^{w}+B_{\rm sec,end}+B_{\rm sec,int},
                                                        \label{eq:rv-variation-constants}\\
 D_{\rm twist}^{w}(\zeta)
    &=D_{\rm base}^{w}+B_{\rm sec,end}
                          +2|\sin(\zeta/2)|B_{\rm sec,int},\notag\\
 D_{\rm twist}^{w}
    &=D_{\rm base}^{w}+B_{\rm sec,end}+2B_{\rm sec,int}.\notag
\end{align}
Here and below a superscript $w$ names one of these two physical
weights, not an additional source assumption.

\begin{lemma}[Exact jump accounting for the original finite records]
\label{lem:rv-jumps}
On each regular physical word the weights
\eqref{eq:rv-weights} are locally constant away from the preimages
of the twenty original section sides. Differentiation in the roof
coordinate at fixed Gauss angle adds total section-boundary charge
at most $B_{\rm sec,end}+B_{\rm sec,int}$ for an exact record,
and at most
$B_{\rm sec,end}+2|\sin(\zeta/2)|B_{\rm sec,int}$ for the
unit-modulus marked twist. These bounds include all actual words
and all possible occupation labels. They do not depend on the
number of components selected by one exact label.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
A fixed lifted physical word fixes $K$, and all collision states
vary analytically on its regular endpoint chart. Every membership
test in $Y_R^*$ is a finite Boolean combination of its original
coordinate inequalities. Consequently an exact-record indicator
or its marked phase is constant in each complementary chamber.
On a compact regular fixed-angle interval, a nonconstant analytic
side function has only finitely many zeros. A slice on which one
such function is identically zero is exceptional for angular
integration unless the function vanishes on a neighborhood; the
latter is impossible for a genuine canonical coordinate side since
$T_R^i$ is a local diffeomorphism. To verify the exceptional-set
assertion, use a countable local analytic chart cover: an accumulating
sequence of constant-zero angular slices forces every coefficient
of the local power series to vanish. Thus the exceptional angular
levels form a null set. Exhaustion treats endpoints and any
accumulation at a physical singular boundary.

On a regular interior side where only the visit at index $i$ changes,
$A$ changes by $1$ or $-1$. An exact indicator jumps by at most one;
the marked phase jumps in modulus by
$|e^{i\zeta}-1|=2|\sin(\zeta/2)|$. If several visit boundaries
coincide, order the affected visits and telescope their changes.
The same upper bound is paid once for each changed visit, all of
which are present in \eqref{eq:rv-section-budget}. For an exact
indicator the bound one per such surface is still valid. The
endpoint factors $\eta_R(x)$ and $\eta_R(T_R^mx)$ have jump
at most one in modulus, even for the twisted weight. The initial
occupation contribution is part of its endpoint value, and the
terminal test creates no fictitious occupation at index $m$.

The exact canonical flux identity in Theorem~\ref{thm:bf-flux}
expresses the contribution of a section side as its one-copy charge
$\mathscr A|d\theta|$ times the absolute jump, where
$\mathscr A=\omega/(4\pi c_*\chi)$ is the coarea coefficient
(and not the occupation count $A$). The incident regular pieces
are combined into this jump before absolute values. Hence an
artificial subdivision does not pay twice for the same unchanged
weight. Apply Theorem~\ref{thm:cs-charge} to every actual horizontal
or vertical side at each indicated collision index. Restricting to
its finite rectangle-side interval is a positive restriction of
that complete source. Summing the geometric depth bounds over
$1\le i\le m-1$ and then the two endpoint bounds proves exactly
\eqref{eq:rv-section-budget} and the asserted jump charges.
Section corners or transverse intersections have zero angular-image
length. Coincident regular boundary arcs are treated by the jump
or the telescoping assignment just given, not duplicated as new
trajectories. This proof uses the geometry of the original finite
rectangles, not a variation claim for an arbitrary Borel set.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform bounded variation for exact physical records]
\label{thm:rv-exact-bv}
For every $R\in[R_-,R_+]$, every $m\ge1$, all original labels
$n,k$, and either specified weight $w$, the first measure in
\eqref{eq:rv-measures} has a nonnegative density
$p_{n,k,m,R}^{w}\in BV(\mathbb R)$ satisfying
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rv-exact-bv}
 \|Dp_{n,k,m,R}^{w}\|_{\rm TV}\le D_{\rm exact}^{w},
 \qquad \|p_{n,k,m,R}^{w}\|_1\le1,
 \qquad \|p_{n,k,m,R}^{w}\|_\infty\le C_{\rm roof}.
\end{equation}
For $w=1$ this is the original density $p_{n,R}(k,m,t)$.
The twisted measure has a complex density
$p_{m,R}^{w;\xi,\zeta,\upsilon}\in BV(\mathbb R;\mathbb C)$
with
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rv-twist-bv}
 \|Dp_{m,R}^{w;\xi,\zeta,\upsilon}\|_{\rm TV}
                         \le D_{\rm twist}^{w}(\zeta)
                         \le D_{\rm twist}^{w},
 \quad \|p_{m,R}^{w;\xi,\zeta,\upsilon}\|_1\le1,
 \quad |p_{m,R}^{w;\xi,\zeta,\upsilon}|
                         \le\overline p_{m,R}\quad\hbox{a.e.}
\end{equation}
All densities have support in $[m\tau_-,m\tau_+]$. The
variation bounds precede the supremum over $R,m,n,k$ and all real
discrete-mark frequencies. As throughout the original problem,
exceptional density sets may depend on the fixed radius.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Every original section state has $|p|<5/6$, as in the endpoint cap
of Section~\ref{sec:rbv-complete}. Therefore both weights in
\eqref{eq:rv-weights} vanish outside the source of its full positive
majorant. Their absolute values are at most one. The exact coarea
formula consequently gives integrable densities dominated in modulus
by $\overline p_{m,R}$, and hence their stated height bounds.
Their $L^1$ norms are at most one, rather than only $c_*^{-1}$,
because the initial factor has been restored and
$c_*^{-1}\int\eta_R\,d\nu=1$. Their roof and support are the
actual ones.

We verify the derivative, since domination alone would not prove it.
Use the maximal regular fixed-angle physical intervals of
Lemma~\ref{lem:rbv-endpoints}. On each chamber cut by the original
section sides, multiply the exact coarea coefficient by the constant
record weight. Its interior derivative has absolute value bounded
by the unweighted derivative of \eqref{eq:rbv-interior-pointwise},
or by the unchanged guarded derivative in
Corollary~\ref{cor:rbv-guard}. Hence the sum of interior derivatives
is bounded by precisely the interior part of $D_{\rm base}^{w}$.
All artificial seams and length subdivisions cancel before taking
absolute values.

The physical interval endpoints have the complete accounting already
proved in Lemma~\ref{lem:rbv-endpoints}. At a cap or an unvisited-disk
boundary, multiplying a one-sided trace by a number of modulus at most
one cannot increase its positive charge. All actual words are retained,
so no cancellation across different words is needed for this upper
bound. At a visited grazing limit the inherited optical trace is
zero, still after the bounded multiplier. At a critical endpoint the
finite original section inequalities have one-sided angular sector
limits: in a fixed local analytic chart, the first nonzero homogeneous
term of each active inequality has only finitely many zero rays.
Off those rays its sign is constant along a sufficiently short ray.
The finite Boolean record and its phase therefore have a limit of
modulus at most one on almost every incoming ray. Their incoming
charge is bounded by the complete positive critical sector charge of
Lemma~\ref{lem:rbv-endpoints}. No critical sector has been replaced
by zero merely because its source has area zero.

All these physical endpoint contributions, including the full
critical fan, are therefore bounded by the boundary part of
$D_{\rm base}^{w}$. The remaining section jumps have exactly the
bounds of Lemma~\ref{lem:rv-jumps}. Integrate by parts against a
compactly supported $C^1$ test function on a finite exhaustion of
the regular chambers. The positive absolute sums of interior,
physical endpoint and section-jump measures are finite after angular
integration. The source integral against the derivative of the test
function is integrable as well. Exhaustion thus produces a finite
signed, or complex, distributional derivative, with total variation
at most the constants in \eqref{eq:rv-variation-constants}.
This proves \eqref{eq:rv-exact-bv}--\eqref{eq:rv-twist-bv}.

This construction treats the entire finite set of original section
tests before the collision-uniform bound. It does not count the
components of a selected event, assume independent visits, discard a
collision, or fill a physically missing roof interval. The local
limits are taken at each fixed $R,m$ before applying the displayed
uniform estimates.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Uniform roof Fourier tail with exact marks]
\label{cor:rv-fourier}
For either density in Theorem~\ref{thm:rv-exact-bv}, let $D$ denote
its corresponding variation constant. For every $s\ne0$ and
$h\in\mathbb R$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rv-fourier}
 \left|\int_{\mathbb R}e^{ist}p(t)\,dt\right|
                 \le\min\{1,D/|s|\},\qquad
 \|p(\cdot+h)-p\|_1\le D|h|.
\end{equation}
In particular, uniformly in $m,R,\xi,\zeta,\upsilon$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rv-joint-characteristic}
 \left|\int w\,\eta_R(T_R^mx)
       e^{i\{\xi\cdot K^{\rm c}_{m,R}(x)+\zeta A_{m,R}(x)
                  +\upsilon m+sL_{m,R}(x)\}}\,d\nu_R^*(x)\right|
              \le\min\{1,D_{\rm twist}^{w}/|s|\}.
\end{equation}
No lattice or occupation frequency, including an arithmetic zero
class or a nonzero spectral peak, has been omitted. If desired,
the same uniform estimate gives the quantitative squared tail
\[
     \int_{|s|>S}|\widehat p(s)|^2\,ds\le2D^2/S
                         \qquad(S>0).
\]
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The densities have compact physical-roof support. Integration by
parts in the distributional identity for their finite derivative
measure gives $|\widehat p(s)|\le\|Dp\|_{\rm TV}/|s|$.
Their $L^1$ bounds give the other term. Integration of the absolute
variation measure between $t$ and $t+h$, followed by Tonelli, gives
the translation estimate also for the complex density. The
characteristic expression is exactly the Fourier transform of the
specified twisted roof measure, with the original conditional source
and terminal event. Integrating $D^2s^{-2}$ on the two tails proves
the last assertion.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Scope relative to the raw local limit.}
The new estimate differentiates every original finite section and
occupation test and retains the complete physical displacement and
collision marks. It supplies a uniform high-roof-frequency bound
for those exact measures and their unit-modulus joint twists. An
arbitrary Borel selector $H$ still has only the height domination of
Theorem~\ref{thm:rbv-height}; no Fourier or variation estimate for
such an $H$ follows from this result. Nor is the $|s|^{-1}$ majorant
integrable over the whole high-frequency line, or small enough by
itself to prove the original collision-normalized $m^2$ pointwise
local limit. The remaining joint twisted spectral estimates,
normalization payments and original-order limiting obligations must
be supplied at their stated strengths. No return event, microscopic
window, probability quantifier, physical clock or arithmetic factor
has been changed to obtain the present bound.
