\begin{abstract}
We study exact return, displacement, collision and unit-speed
physical-time records in the triangular Lorentz gas for
$R\in[9/20,47/100]$, with the original section conditioning.
We prove a uniform sum of inverse momentum-derivative suprema
on every genuine marked half-ray partition. A countable interval
overlay combines the two actual halves and controls the complete
marked inverse endpoint weight, without a collision-word multiplier.
Absolute homogeneous logarithmic variation then bounds the total
turning of the physical endpoint-momentum curve. Together with
the exact optical boundary identity, this proves a full regular
marked-boundary budget at every critical offset, including the
nonzero offsets previously unestimated. The complete sum over marks
of proportional endpoint depth vanishes after collision normalization.
A physical outgoing-angle foliation also gives a complete capacity
moment over all actual word/strip classes and pays each unweighted subfamily with a fixed positive capacity threshold, including assigned strip-boundary faces.
A positive-sector transport argument also pays the leading incoming
marked critical fans with positive visited incidences, including
collapsed zero-clearance slices and every shallow mark.
The source, its one-sided zero-clearance trace, all actual collisions,
original labels and physical roof remain unchanged.
The retained proof chain includes geometry, arithmetic, homogeneous
growth, critical coarea, variational localization, physical source
regularity and exact transverse cocycle comparison.
These results advance the raw pointwise local limit and four-coordinate
inverse problem. The final roofwise positive-source comparison,
complete singular boundary and uniform spectral/conditional interfaces
retain their exact unresolved obligations.
\end{abstract}
