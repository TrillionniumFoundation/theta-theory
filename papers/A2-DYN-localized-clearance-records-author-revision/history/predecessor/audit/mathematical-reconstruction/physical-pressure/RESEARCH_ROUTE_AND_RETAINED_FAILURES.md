# Pressure research route and retained unsuccessful approaches

This is an author-revision working record, not a referee report. It distinguishes inherited results, unused exploratory routes, and the new proof.

## Inherited material retained

Native 318 proves the all-component same-real-word distortion estimate. Native 321 proves the complete outgoing-angle capacity moment and large-capacity sum. Both are inherited contributions and are not claimed anew. Their remaining interface was the unweighted physical-word supremum sum for arbitrarily small-capacity classes.

The exact original pressure is coarser than a sum over every connected homogeneous two-dimensional cell. The new proof preserves this distinction: one open homogeneous class is selected for an almost-maximizer of each physical word, and that word is charged once.

## Initial route not used as a proof

I first investigated deriving a uniform full-cell short-descendant renewal directly from the inherited one-dimensional curve-growth theorem. At regular intersections, two backward homogeneity boundaries of different ages do have an ordered slope: the older boundary has positive cone slope at the younger boundary's horizontal birth time, and the exact projective slope map is strictly increasing. This pointwise observation does not, by itself, assign every disconnected or short planar cell to a prescribed curve, nor prove a full-cell renewal bound. It was not promoted into a theorem.

A proposed compactness argument for parameter-uniform mixing rectangles was also not completed. A long homogeneous curve is bounded away from grazing, and a finite strict proper-crossing witness would be open in the parameter and curve. This alone does not establish every persistence and full-cell assignment constant needed for a radius-uniform pressure result.

The capacity moment itself cannot erase the short-class remainder. The inherited abstract countercheck demonstrating this nonimplication remains valid. No finite simulation or area-smallness argument was used in its place.

## External theorem check and its actual role

The primary text checked was Baladi–Demers, *Thermodynamic formalism for dispersing billiards*, arXiv:2009.10936v3, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2009.10936 . Its Lemma 3.6 compares the full partition pressure with long elements through a short-descendant and most-recent-long-ancestor argument; later pressure comparison invokes additional geometric/mixing constants. This author did not infer radius-uniform constants from the fixed-table statement.

Native 326 does not invoke that theorem or its partition, exponent, or function-space conventions. Its new boundary-source stopping argument uses the native physical cocycles and sources throughout.

## Route that yields new mathematics

The successful mechanism is a finite physical boundary stopping construction. Along the intrinsic affine stable fiber, hold the complete actual word and all original past strips fixed, then stop at the first interval endpoint. The exit is terminal grazing, a finite past homogeneity level, or an actual unvisited tangency. The interval itself yields actual representatives at every small positive interior source level.

The capless exact matrix identity relates the affine inverse Jacobian to \(c_0u_x\). New 324 supplies horizontal collision source suprema with the decisive central incidence \(c_i\). Native 319 supplies all positive-clearance marked source suprema. Interval overlays pay each selected physical word once; original strip incidences and stopping ages are geometrically summable.

This mechanism never extends a derivative through a singular boundary, inserts a tangent collision, assumes connectedness of a word domain, or requires a positive capacity lower bound.
