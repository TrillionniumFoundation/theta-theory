\section{All physical margins in the original roof measure}
\label{sec:fr-full-roof}
The physical atlas has a fixed-margin height cost proportional to
$\varepsilon^{-2}$.  We now combine that cost with the exact distribution
of the original incidence and clearance margins.  The result includes
arbitrarily small positive margins and every actual collision word.
It is a quantitative regularity theorem for the original full-roof
measure, not an exchange of the fixed-margin and collision limits.
Its function-space conclusion is stated explicitly; it does not assert
the unrestricted pointwise height required by the final local theorem.

Keep $q_0=47/53$, $r_g=q_0^{1/4}$, and let
$\mathcal C_{m,R}$ be the regular physical $m$-flight records with
$|p_0|,|p_m|\le5/6$.  This cap contains the complete original exact
event, by Section~\ref{sec:at-atlas}.  For a Borel physical source $E$
and a Borel function $0\le H\le1$, put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fr-measures}
 \mu_{E,H}=(L_{m,R})_*
       (\one_E\one_{\mathcal C_{m,R}}H\,d\nu_R^*),\qquad
 \mu^I_{E,H}=(L_{m,R})_*
       (I_m\one_E\one_{\mathcal C_{m,R}}H\,d\nu_R^*).
\end{equation}
In particular $H=\one_{\Pi_{n,k,m,R}}h$ retains all original return,
displacement, collision, section and terminal labels and every selector
$0\le h\le1$.  The choice $H=1$ is a positive majorant used in estimates,
not a replacement of the original conditioning event.  Throughout,
$\nu_R^*=\one_{Y_R^*}\nu/c_*$ and $\nu(M_R)=1$.

Use the following fixed constants:
\begin{align}\label{eq:fr-constants}
 \varepsilon_\sharp&=1/200,&
 B_\Delta&=\frac{2}{R_-(1-r_g)},&
 B_c&=\frac{2r_g^2}{1-r_g^2},\notag\\
 B_\sharp&=\frac{B_\Delta+B_c\varepsilon_\sharp}{c_*},&
 \mathfrak A&=\frac{25U C_{\rm dist}^2}{2c_*}
        \left(\frac{128R_+}{g\delta_*}\right)^2,&
 J_\sharp&=1+\frac{4q_0}{1-q_0}=\frac{97}{3}.
\end{align}
Here $\delta_*$ is precisely the endpoint continuation constant in
\eqref{eq:pt-tube-constant}, $g=9/40$, and $U=320/9$.
The auxiliary upper margin $\varepsilon_\sharp$ is smaller than the
strict clearance-flux range $3/200$; it is not an outer microscopic
width.  In this notation $C_{\rm at}=\mathfrak A\varepsilon^{-2}$.

\begin{lemma}[Uniform physical margin and incidence-insertion tails]
\label{lem:fr-margin-tail}
For every $m\ge1$, every physical radius, and
$0<\varepsilon\le\varepsilon_\sharp$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fr-margin-tail}
 \nu(\mathcal C_{m,R}\setminus\mathcal G_{\varepsilon,m,R})
       \le B_\Delta\varepsilon+B_c\varepsilon^2.
\end{equation}
For every Borel $0\le h\le1$, with $a=\int h\,d\nu$,
the actual unchanged insertion satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fr-insertion-tail}
 \int hI_m\,d\nu\le J_\sharp\sqrt a,
 \qquad
 I_m=1+\sum_{i=1}^{m-1}\frac{q_0^{d_i}}{c_i}.
\end{equation}
These estimates include all depths and need no independence of
collisions, clearances or section visits.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
On the stated endpoint cap, $c_0,c_m\ge\sqrt{11}/6>1/4$.
Thus failure of an incidence condition of $\mathcal G_\varepsilon$
can occur only at an interior contact.  At most two interior contacts
have any depth $d\ge1$, and at most two physical flights have any
depth $d\ge0$.  The exact invariant incidence marginal gives
$\nu\{c_i<u\}=1-\sqrt{1-u^2}\le u^2$ for $0\le u\le1$.
Theorem~\ref{thm:cf-flux} gives
$\nu\{\Delta_i<u\}\le u/R_-$ for $0<u<3/200$.
Apply these statements to $u=\varepsilon r_g^{d_i}$ and
$u=\varepsilon r_g^{d'_i}$ and sum the two geometric series.
This proves \eqref{eq:fr-margin-tail}.  The clearance event uses
every third disk on the whole closed physical flight, including disks
visited at other times.  Exact tangencies and visited grazing belong
to collision-area null sets; invariance gives the same assertion at
each of the finitely many actual indices.  No positive pairing trace
is identified with such a bulk-null set.

For the insertion, layer cake and $0\le h\le1$ give, at each
interior index,
\[
 \int\frac{h}{c_i}\,d\nu
  =\int_0^\infty\int h\one_{\{c_i^{-1}>s\}}\,d\nu\,ds
  \le\int_0^\infty\min\{a,1,s^{-2}\}\,ds=2\sqrt a.
\]
For $a=0$ this follows directly; for $0<a\le1$ split the integral
at $a^{-1/2}$.  The bound $s^{-2}$ for $s\ge1$ is exactly the
preceding invariant incidence marginal with $u=1/s$; for $s<1$,
the bound by one suffices.  As
\[
 \sum_{i=1}^{m-1}q_0^{d_i}
       \le2\sum_{d=1}^\infty q_0^d=\frac{47}{3},
\]
we obtain $\int hI_m\le a+(94/3)\sqrt a\le(97/3)\sqrt a$.
For $m=1$ the interior sum is empty and the same inequality holds.
\end{proof}

For a nonnegative measurable roof function $f$, define the exact weak
norm
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fr-weak-norm}
 \|f\|_{q,\infty}
   =\sup_{\lambda>0}\lambda
       |\{t\in\mathbb R:f(t)>\lambda\}|^{1/q}.
\end{equation}
Lebesgue measure on the original physical roof line is used here.
No averaging width or rescaled time is part of this definition.

\begin{lemma}[Quantitative exhaustion after the physical coarea sum]
\label{lem:fr-exhaustion}
Suppose a source $E$ in \eqref{eq:fr-measures} satisfies, for all
$0<\varepsilon\le\varepsilon_\sharp$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fr-premises}
 \left\|\frac{d\mu_{E,H}[\mathcal G_\varepsilon]}{dt}\right\|_\infty
       \le A_E\varepsilon^{-2},\qquad
 \mu_{E,H}(\mathbb R)\le M_E\le1.
\end{equation}
Here the first bound is a completed positive physical sum, uniform
in $H$, not an estimate on a chosen word.  Then the full measure
$\mu_{E,H}$ is absolutely continuous, with density $f_{E,H}$, and
\begin{align}\label{eq:fr-full-weak}
 \|f_{E,H}\|_{3/2,\infty}&\le W_E,\notag\\
 W_E&=\left[\sqrt{2A_E}
                 \max\{2B_\sharp,M_E/\varepsilon_\sharp\}\right]^{2/3}.
\end{align}
For every Borel roof set $S$ of finite measure,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fr-all-borel}
 \mu_{E,H}(S)\le\min\{M_E,3W_E|S|^{1/3}\}.
\end{equation}
The statements are also valid when $A_E=0$, in which case
$\mu_{E,H}=0$ and $W_E=0$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The exact physical partition into $\mathcal G_\varepsilon$ and its
complement, together with \eqref{eq:fr-margin-tail}, gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fr-finite-exhaustion}
 \mu_{E,H}(S)
 \le A_E\varepsilon^{-2}|S|+B_\sharp\varepsilon
 \quad(0<\varepsilon\le\varepsilon_\sharp).
\end{equation}
There is exactly one section-normalization factor in the second term:
the complementary mass is at most
$c_*^{-1}\nu(\mathcal C\setminus\mathcal G_\varepsilon)$.
If $S$ is a Lebesgue-null set, let $\varepsilon$ decrease to zero
in this finite-count inequality.  This proves absolute continuity
of the full measure, including all arbitrarily small margins.
If $A_E=0$, the same argument with $S=\mathbb R$, using the zero
guarded measure, proves that the whole measure vanishes.

Suppose $A_E>0$.  Write $f_{E,H}=f_\varepsilon+g_\varepsilon$
for the densities of the two exact nonnegative restrictions.
The proof of absolute continuity just given applies to both, or
equivalently they are submeasures of the full absolutely continuous
measure.  We have
$0\le f_\varepsilon\le A_E\varepsilon^{-2}$ almost everywhere
and $\|g_\varepsilon\|_1\le B_\sharp\varepsilon$.
For $\lambda\ge2A_E\varepsilon_\sharp^{-2}$ take
$\varepsilon=(2A_E/\lambda)^{1/2}$.  Then
\[
 |\{f_{E,H}>\lambda\}|
 \le |\{g_\varepsilon>\lambda/2\}|
 \le 2B_\sharp\sqrt{2A_E}\lambda^{-3/2}.
\]
For $0<\lambda<2A_E\varepsilon_\sharp^{-2}$, positivity and
the total mass instead give
\[
 \lambda^{3/2}|\{f_{E,H}>\lambda\}|
 \le M_E\sqrt\lambda
 \le M_E\sqrt{2A_E}/\varepsilon_\sharp.
\]
These two ranges prove \eqref{eq:fr-full-weak} on the entire
physical roof line, including arbitrarily large or small superlevel
sets.  No count of words, labels or roof components occurs.
Finally layer cake gives, for $\ell=|S|$,
\[
 \int_S f_{E,H}\,dt
 \le\int_0^\infty\min\{\ell,(W_E/s)^{3/2}\}\,ds
       =3W_E\ell^{1/3}.
\]
The case $\ell=0$ follows from absolute continuity.  Together with
the mass bound this proves \eqref{eq:fr-all-borel}.
All margin choices were made inside inequalities valid at every
finite collision count.  No collision limsup was interchanged with
an exhaustion or an essential supremum.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Full physical roof densities and the actual incidence factor]
\label{thm:fr-full-source}
The measures \eqref{eq:fr-measures}, for $E=M_R$, have full original
roof densities $f_H,f_H^I$.  They satisfy
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fr-full-source}
 \|f_H\|_{3/2,\infty}\le W_* ,\qquad
 \|f_H^I\|_{6/5,\infty}
                  \le J_\sharp\sqrt{3W_*/c_*},
\end{equation}
where $W_*$ is \eqref{eq:fr-full-weak} with
$A_E=\mathfrak A$ and $M_E=1$.
The constants are uniform in $m,R$ and all original labels and
selectors.  Neither measure retains a positive lower incidence or
clearance margin.  More generally, every source covered by
Lemma~\ref{lem:fr-exhaustion} satisfies
\begin{align}\label{eq:fr-weighted-full}
 \mu^I_{E,H}(S)&\le\frac{J_\sharp}{\sqrt{c_*}}
                           \sqrt{\mu_{E,H}(S)},\notag\\
 \mu^I_{E,H}(\mathbb R)&\le\frac{J_\sharp}{\sqrt{c_*}}\sqrt{M_E},\notag\\
 \|f^I_{E,H}\|_{6/5,\infty}
                  &\le J_\sharp\sqrt{3W_E/c_*}.
\end{align}
In particular the insertion is the actual $I_m$, with the unchanged
$q_0$ and collision indices, throughout the complete source.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For $E=M_R$, Theorem~\ref{thm:at-height} gives the first premise of
\eqref{eq:fr-premises} with $A_E=\mathfrak A$.
Its proof applies verbatim with an arbitrary $0\le H\le1$ in place
of $\one_\Pi h$: the exact-event factor is retained in coarea and
then bounded by one.  The physical tubes, their multiplicity, their
unconditioned lower volume, and the single factor $c_*^{-1}$ are
unaltered.  The total source is a subprobability since $\nu_R^*$
is the original conditional probability.  Lemma~\ref{lem:fr-exhaustion}
therefore proves the unweighted assertion.

For a roof set $S$, apply \eqref{eq:fr-insertion-tail} to the actual
function
\[
 h=\one_{Y_R^*}\one_E\one_{\mathcal C_{m,R}}H
                    \one_{\{L_{m,R}\in S\}}.
\]
Its unconditioned integral is precisely
$a=c_*\mu_{E,H}(S)$, so division by $c_*$ gives the first inequality
of \eqref{eq:fr-weighted-full}.  This displays the normalization
without creating a second conditioning.  It proves finiteness and
absolute continuity of the weighted measure, since both properties
hold for $\mu_{E,H}$.
Combining it with \eqref{eq:fr-all-borel} gives
\[
 \mu^I_{E,H}(S)
    \le J_\sharp\sqrt{3W_E/c_*}\,|S|^{1/6}.
\]
For $S=\{f^I_{E,H}>\lambda\}$, which has finite measure by
integrability, $\lambda|S|\le\mu^I_{E,H}(S)$ proves the weak
$L^{6/5}$ bound.  If $|S|=0$ the assertion is immediate.
This argument uses the distribution of the real incidence insertion
on the same physical roof preimage; it neither assumes independence
from the roof nor bounds $I_m$ by a fixed-margin surrogate.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The strong norms determined by mass and weak norm]
\label{lem:fr-strong}
If $f\ge0$, $\|f\|_1\le M$, $\|f\|_{q,\infty}\le W$, and
$1<p<q$, then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fr-strong}
 \|f\|_p^p\le
 \frac{p(q-1)}{(p-1)(q-p)}
 M^{(q-p)/(q-1)}W^{q(p-1)/(q-1)}.
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
If $M=0$ or $W=0$ then $f=0$ almost everywhere.
Otherwise its distribution is at most
$\min\{M/s,(W/s)^q\}$.
Split $p\int_0^\infty s^{p-1}|\{f>s\}|\,ds$ at
$s_0=(W^q/M)^{1/(q-1)}$ and evaluate the two integrals.
They converge precisely in the stated range and sum to
\eqref{eq:fr-strong}.  This proof applies on the whole roof line;
its support length may grow with $m$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The complete deep exceptional source, without a remaining guard}
Let $E_D=\mathcal E^{\rm obs}_{D,R,m}$ be precisely the actual
clearance, incidence and original-section collar union of
Theorem~\ref{thm:ec-uniform-source}.  In particular the original
thresholds and $q_{\rm a}=3263680/3263681$ are unchanged.
For $D\ge D_{\rm cv}$ let $V_\varepsilon(D)$ be the six-term
budget \eqref{eq:nh-budget}, including its squared-incidence factor
$16$, and define
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fr-deep-constants}
 A_D=\mathfrak A V_{\varepsilon_\sharp}(D),\qquad
 M_D=\min\{1,V_0(D)/c_*\},\qquad
 W_D=\left[\sqrt{2A_D}
              \max\{2B_\sharp,M_D/\varepsilon_\sharp\}\right]^{2/3}.
\end{equation}
Here $V_0$ denotes the explicit expression obtained by setting
$a_\varepsilon=0$ in that finite six-term budget.  It is an upper
bound for the actual unconditioned source mass, not a definition of
a zero-margin tube.

\begin{theorem}[All-margin normalized control of the exact deep source]
\label{thm:fr-deep-source}
For every $D\ge D_{\rm cv}$, all $m,R$ and every Borel
$0\le H\le1$, the full measures $\mu_{E_D,H}$ and
$\mu^I_{E_D,H}$ have densities satisfying
\begin{align}\label{eq:fr-deep-norms}
 \|f_{D,H}\|_1&\le M_D,&
 \|f_{D,H}\|_{3/2,\infty}&\le W_D,\notag\\
 \|f^I_{D,H}\|_1&\le J_\sharp\sqrt{M_D/c_*},&
 \|f^I_{D,H}\|_{6/5,\infty}&\le J_\sharp\sqrt{3W_D/c_*}.
\end{align}
There is no factor $\one_{\mathcal G_\varepsilon}$ in these
four measures.  They contain the complete actual exceptional source,
including its arbitrarily small margins and all relevant words.

Fix $0<\theta<1/2$, put $D_m=\lceil\theta m\rceil$, and retain
$\lambda_b>0$ from \eqref{eq:nh-rates}.  Uniformly in the physical
radius, exact labels and selectors, the bounds imply
\begin{align}\label{eq:fr-deep-rates}
 \|f_{D_m,H}\|_{3/2,\infty}
                   &=O(e^{-\lambda_bm/3}),&
 \|f^I_{D_m,H}\|_{6/5,\infty}
                   &=O(e^{-\lambda_bm/6}),\notag\\
 \|f_{D_m,H}\|_p
                   &=O(e^{-\lambda_bm(2/p-1)}),
                         &&1<p<3/2,\notag\\
 \|f^I_{D_m,H}\|_p
                   &=O(e^{-\lambda_bm(2/p-3/2)}),
                         &&1<p<6/5.
\end{align}
The respective $L^1$ rates are $O(e^{-\lambda_bm})$ and
$O(e^{-\lambda_bm/2})$.  Multiplication of any displayed norm by
$m^2$ still gives a limit of zero.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For $0<\varepsilon\le\varepsilon_\sharp$, the physical saturation
estimate of Theorem~\ref{thm:nh-bad-height} gives
\[
 \|d\mu_{E_D,H}[\mathcal G_\varepsilon]/dt\|_\infty
 \le\mathfrak A\varepsilon^{-2}V_\varepsilon(D)
 \le A_D\varepsilon^{-2}.
\]
Its proof keeps every source point and every physical word; the
extension to arbitrary $H$ is the same positive bound as in
Theorem~\ref{thm:fr-full-source}.  The depth $D_{\rm cv}$ ensures
the strict flux and collar regimes simultaneously for every such
$\varepsilon$.  Setting the widening to zero in the actual invariant
union estimate gives $\nu(E_D)\le V_0(D)$; the squared-incidence
coefficient in $V_0$ is a harmless upper bound also without widening.
Dividing once by $c_*$ and using the subprobability bound gives $M_D$.
Lemmas~\ref{lem:fr-margin-tail} and \ref{lem:fr-exhaustion}, and
Theorem~\ref{thm:fr-full-source}, prove \eqref{eq:fr-deep-norms}.

Since $j(D_m)\ge\theta m/N-2$, $N=94$, the already established
geometric series give
$A_{D_m}=O(e^{-\lambda_bm})$ and
$M_{D_m}=O(e^{-\lambda_bm})$.  The maximum in
\eqref{eq:fr-deep-constants} is bounded independently of $m$.
Its square-root and $2/3$ powers give the first two rates.
Apply \eqref{eq:fr-strong} with $q=3/2$ and then $q=6/5$ to
obtain the stated strong-norm exponents.  The $L^1$ rates are
already explicit in \eqref{eq:fr-deep-norms}.  All exponents are
strictly positive in their specified ranges.  Empty designated
index sets contribute zero, and the finitely many counts with
$D_m<D_{\rm cv}$ do not affect the conclusions.
\end{proof}

For example, for every fixed $a>0$ the original normalized densities
satisfy the quantitative roof-spike estimates
\begin{align}\label{eq:fr-spikes}
 |\{t:m^2f_{D_m,H}(t)>a\}|
   &\le a^{-3/2}m^3 W_{D_m}^{3/2}
           =O_a(m^3e^{-\lambda_bm/2}),\notag\\
 |\{t:m^2f^I_{D_m,H}(t)>a\}|
   &\le a^{-6/5}m^{12/5}
           [J_\sharp\sqrt{3W_{D_m}/c_*}]^{6/5}
           =O_a(m^{12/5}e^{-\lambda_bm/5}).
\end{align}
These are estimates for the possible roof spikes, not their
elimination from an essential supremum.  The sets are Borel after
choosing measurable density versions.  The constants are uniform
in all original labels and the radius; no common null set across
uncountably many radii is required.

\subsection{Exact marked comparison on the full source}
Use the actual central phases $\Phi_Q$ and $\Phi_Y$, the endpoint
work $W_d$, and the comparison coefficient $\widehat\Phi$ from
Theorem~\ref{thm:nh-records}.  On $E_d$ the comparison coefficient
is zero; outside $E_d$ it is $e^{-isW_d}\Phi_Y$, using the common
physical central record of every global disk-word minimum.
This construction is available on the complete actual source by
Theorem~\ref{thm:ec-uniform-source}, without an atlas margin.

\begin{corollary}[Full-source marked comparison in the proved roof spaces]
\label{cor:fr-full-comparison}
Fix $0<\theta<1/2$, $d=\lceil\theta m\rceil$, and restrict to
the sufficiently large counts with $d\ge1$ and $2d<m$.
For the exact signed measure
\[
 \mathfrak D_H=(L_{m,R}(Q))_*
       (\one_{\mathcal C_{m,R}}H(\Phi_Q-\widehat\Phi)\,d\nu_R^*)
\]
and its actual $I_m$ insertion, positive domination gives
\begin{align}\label{eq:fr-comparison}
 \left\|d\mathfrak D_H/dt\right\|_p
   &\le\|f_{d,1}\|_p
       +2R_+|s|q_{\rm a}^{\lfloor d/N\rfloor}\|f_1\|_p,
                      &&1<p<3/2,\notag\\
 \left\|d\mathfrak D_H^I/dt\right\|_p
   &\le\|f^I_{d,1}\|_p
       +2R_+|s|q_{\rm a}^{\lfloor d/N\rfloor}\|f^I_1\|_p,
                      &&1<p<6/5.
\end{align}
Every norm is taken on the unchanged full physical roof line.
For $\lambda_c=\theta|\log q_{\rm a}|/N$ and any fixed
$0<\lambda<\lambda_c$, their $m^2$-normalized values tend to zero
uniformly for all lattice and return twists and $|s|\le e^{\lambda m}$.
The same assertions hold for $p=1$ with the stated $L^1$ bounds.
All original exact labels and terminal tests can be included in $H$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Every integer mark and both separate endpoint tests agree on the
actual good central comparison.  Its true clock identity is
$L_d(Y)-L_d(Q)=W_d+\mathsf T_{{\rm clock},d}$ with
$0\le\mathsf T_{{\rm clock},d}
       \le2R_+q_{\rm a}^{\lfloor d/N\rfloor}$.
The minus sign in $e^{-isW_d}$ therefore gives
\[
 |\Phi_Q-\widehat\Phi|
  \le\one_{E_d}+2R_+|s|q_{\rm a}^{\lfloor d/N\rfloor}
\]
on the complete actual source.  The bound is independent of the
lattice and return twists.  Its pushforward dominates the total
variation of the signed measure, and the same is true after the
nonnegative actual $I_m$ insertion.  Theorem~\ref{thm:fr-full-source}
and Lemma~\ref{lem:fr-strong} give uniform strong bounds for the
unexceptional full-source factors.  Apply
Theorem~\ref{thm:fr-deep-source} to the first factors and use
$\lfloor d/N\rfloor\ge\theta m/N-1$ for the second.
Minkowski's inequality, or ordinary total variation for $p=1$,
proves \eqref{eq:fr-comparison} and its limits.
\end{proof}

These conclusions concern the exact physical finite-record measures,
with every original clock, collision and observation retained.
They do not constitute an averaged replacement for a local limit
theorem.  They supply a new full-source regularity and integrability
input, while the original raw pointwise target remains unchanged.
In particular weak $L^{3/2}$ or $L^{6/5}$ control permits positive
spikes on roof sets of small Lebesgue measure, so
\eqref{eq:fr-spikes} cannot be promoted to an $L^\infty$ estimate.
The actual mixed multiplier $I_m/|\nabla_qF|$ requires more than
the separate marginal argument above; its complete bulk estimate
is proved in Section~\ref{sec:sm-singular-multiplier} by intersecting
two actual physical momentum kernels.  Full singular paired traces,
short physical segments and the original shallow comparison capacity
still require their own geometric estimates.  The unrestricted
pressure input and the spectral, arithmetic and conditional inversion
interfaces are not inferred from these roof spaces.  Every outer
$B$ and its original microscopic widths remain fixed before the
collision limit, with $B$ outermost; no outer width was varied in
\eqref{eq:fr-finite-exhaustion}.
