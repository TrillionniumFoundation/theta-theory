\section{Normalized marked source, the physical singular multiplier, and pressure}
\label{sec:nh-normalized}
We now apply the physical atlas to the precise exceptional source
of Section~\ref{sec:ec-exact-source}.  This gives its
collision-normalized roof-height payment on the entire original
guarded class, including trajectories with no physical critical
continuation.  We also treat the actual multiplier
$I_m/|\nabla_qF|$ in the original variation budget, in its
appropriate roof-integral topology.  These deductions use the
physical geometry and the explicit optical formulas, not the
whole-cylinder pressure premise.

Let $\mathcal G_\varepsilon=\mathcal G_{\varepsilon,m,R}$,
$a_\varepsilon,\rho=\rho_\varepsilon^{\rm at},C_{\rm at}$ be
as in Section~\ref{sec:at-atlas}.  Keep the original $I_m$ and put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:nh-incidence}
 H_\varepsilon=1+\frac4\varepsilon
                  \frac{q_0^{3/4}}{1-q_0^{3/4}}.
\end{equation}
This deliberately uses the half-guard bound of
\eqref{eq:cv-I}; on $\mathcal G_\varepsilon$ itself a smaller
constant would suffice.  In particular it is valid on every
physical comparison tube used below.  It pays the original
incidence multiplier, not a substitute weight.

For $D\ge D_{\rm cv}$ and $s>0$ write
\begin{equation}\label{eq:nh-series}
 S_s(D)=\frac{Nq_{\rm a}^{s j(D)}}{1-q_{\rm a}^s},\qquad
 T_s(D)=\frac{\sigma^{sD}}{1-\sigma^s},\qquad N=94,
\end{equation}
and define
\begin{align}\label{eq:nh-budget}
 V_\varepsilon(D)={}&
 \frac{2A_\Gamma}{R_-}S_{1/2}(D)
 +\frac{4C_\Delta a_\varepsilon}{R_-}T_1(D)
 +4A_c^2 S_1(D)\notag\\
 &+16C_c^2a_\varepsilon^2 T_2(D)
 +\frac{A_\phi}{\pi}S_{1/4}(D)
 +\frac{2C_c a_\varepsilon}{\pi}T_1(D).
\end{align}
All amplitudes are those of the already proved actual-source
theorem.  The original guard parameter $q_0$ and the anchor
contraction $q_{\rm a}$ remain different fixed constants.

\begin{theorem}[The complete guarded exceptional source has vanishing height]
\label{thm:nh-bad-height}
For every $0<\varepsilon\le1/4$, $D\ge D_{\rm cv}$, all
$m,R,n,k$, and every Borel selector $0\le h\le1$, the original
full-roof measures with respective weights
\[
 \one_{\mathcal G_\varepsilon}
   \one_{\mathcal E^{\rm obs}_{D,R,m}}\one_{\Pi_{n,k,m,R}}h,
 \qquad
 I_m\one_{\mathcal G_\varepsilon}
   \one_{\mathcal E^{\rm obs}_{D,R,m}}\one_{\Pi_{n,k,m,R}}h
\]
against $\nu_R^*$ have densities $b_D$ and $b_D^I$ satisfying
\begin{equation}\label{eq:nh-height}
 \|b_D\|_\infty\le C_{\rm at}V_\varepsilon(D),\qquad
 \|b_D^I\|_\infty
                    \le H_\varepsilon C_{\rm at}V_\varepsilon(D).
\end{equation}
Consequently, for every fixed $\varepsilon>0$ and
$0<\theta<1/2$, with $D_m=\lceil\theta m\rceil$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:nh-zero-height}
 \lim_{m\to\infty}\sup_{R,n,k,h}
       m^2\bigl(\|b_{D_m}\|_\infty+\|b^I_{D_m}\|_\infty\bigr)=0.
\end{equation}
This is a complete positive-source limit in the required
pointwise roof topology on the stated class.  No conditioning
event or roof interval has been enlarged.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Use Corollary~\ref{cor:at-observables} with the three widths
defining $\mathcal E^{\rm obs}_{D,R,m}$.  Its widening constants
$2C_\Delta a_\varepsilon$ and $2C_c a_\varepsilon$ are smaller
than $L_\Gamma,L_c=L_\phi$ in \eqref{eq:cv-L}, since
$a_\varepsilon=s_\varepsilon/8$.  The rational depth
$D_{\rm cv}$ therefore establishes all required width regimes,
uniformly in $\varepsilon$.  At most two true contacts or
flights have each depth.  Apply the inherited block sum
\eqref{eq:ec-depth-sum}, the geometric series for $\sigma^d$,
and $(u+v)^2\le2u^2+2v^2$.  This gives exactly
$\nu(E^+)\le V_\varepsilon(D)$, including the factor $16$ in
the squared incidence widening.  Theorem~\ref{thm:at-height}
proves the first estimate.  The pointwise bound
$I_m\le H_\varepsilon$ proves the second.

Put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:nh-rates}
 \lambda_b=\theta\min\left\{
                 \frac{|\log q_{\rm a}|}{4N},|\log\sigma|\right\}>0,
 \qquad \lambda_c=\frac{\theta|\log q_{\rm a}|}{N}>0.
\end{equation}
Since $j(D_m)\ge\theta m/N-2$, the six explicit terms in
$V_\varepsilon(D_m)$ are $O_\varepsilon(e^{-\lambda_bm})$.
Thus the two normalized heights are
$O_\varepsilon(m^2e^{-\lambda_bm})$, uniformly after the full
physical sum.  The finitely many counts with $D_m<D_{\rm cv}$
do not affect the limit.  This substitution is into a theorem
valid at every finite $m,D$, not into a fixed-window asymptotic.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Exact central marks and their normalized Fourier comparison]
\label{thm:nh-records}
Fix the same $\varepsilon,\theta$ and let $d=D_m$, $b=m-d$.
On $\mathcal G_\varepsilon\setminus\mathcal E^{\rm obs}_{d,R,m}$,
for all sufficiently large $m$, every global disk-word minimum
has the actual first-hit central record $Y_d,\ldots,Y_b$.
All central collision states agree between minima.  Its two
lattice labels, $b-d$ actual flights, all original section
indicators, occupation on $[d,b)$, and the two separate endpoint
tests agree exactly with those of $Q_d,\ldots,Q_b$.
The unchanged central physical clocks obey
\begin{equation}\label{eq:nh-clock}
 L_d(Y)-L_d(Q)=W_d(Q,Y)+\mathsf T_{{\rm clock},d},\qquad
 0\le\mathsf T_{{\rm clock},d}
                 \le2R_+q_{\rm a}^{\lfloor d/N\rfloor}.
\end{equation}
The phase and transverse costs satisfy the corresponding pointwise
estimates \eqref{eq:ec-exponential-costs}.
Their original full-roof densities on this class, with any
selector $0\le h\le1$, are bounded by
\begin{equation}\label{eq:nh-costs}
 2R_+ C_{\rm at}q_{\rm a}^{\lfloor d/N\rfloor},\quad
 2R_+ C_{\rm at}K_\phi q_{\rm a}^{j(d)},\quad
 2R_+ C_{\rm at}B_\perp q_{\rm a}^{j(d)},
\end{equation}
respectively, without the pressure input.  Multiplication by
$I_m$ costs only $H_\varepsilon$.

More precisely define the central marked phase, for
$\xi\in\mathbb R^2$, $s,\zeta\in\mathbb R$, by
\[
 \Phi_Q=
 \eta_R(x_d)\eta_R(x_b)
 \exp\{i[\xi\cdot K_d(Q)+sL_d(Q)+\zeta A_d(Q)]\},
 \quad A_d(Q)=\sum_{i=d}^{b-1}\eta_R(x_i).
\]
On the good class put $\widehat\Phi=e^{-isW_d}\Phi_Y$;
on its exceptional class set $\widehat\Phi=0$ as a comparison
coefficient, without assigning it a fictitious trajectory.
For the signed measure
\[
 \mathfrak D=(L_{m,R}(Q))_*
  \bigl(\one_{\mathcal G_\varepsilon}\one_{\Pi_{n,k,m,R}}
                  h(\Phi_Q-\widehat\Phi)\,d\nu_R^*\bigr)
\]
one has
\begin{equation}\label{eq:nh-fourier}
 \left\|\frac{d\mathfrak D}{dt}\right\|_\infty
 \le C_{\rm at}
       \{V_\varepsilon(d)+2R_+|s|q_{\rm a}^{\lfloor d/N\rfloor}\}.
\end{equation}
Thus its $m^2$-normalized height tends to zero uniformly for
all lattice and return twists and $|s|\le e^{\lambda m}$,
for every fixed $0<\lambda<\lambda_c$.  The same conclusion
holds with the actual $I_m$ multiplier.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The physicality and exact observations, for every minimum, are
the already proved conclusion of
Theorem~\ref{thm:ec-uniform-source}; none is newly assumed.
The identity for the signed endpoint work $W_d$ is
\eqref{eq:cp-clock}, with the bound from the anchor contraction.
Apply the full guarded source bound in
Theorem~\ref{thm:at-height} to the nonnegative pointwise costs.
This proves \eqref{eq:nh-costs} on the original full roof.
Their rates are $O_\varepsilon(m^2e^{-\lambda_cm})$ after
normalization.  This discharges the former pressure dependence
of these cost estimates on the present full guarded class.

On the good class every integer mark and endpoint test in
$\Phi_Q$ equals its counterpart in $\Phi_Y$.
Equation~\eqref{eq:nh-clock} gives
$|\Phi_Q-e^{-isW_d}\Phi_Y|
 \le |s|\mathsf T_{{\rm clock},d}$.
On the exceptional class the difference has modulus at most one.
The absolute value of the signed pushforward is therefore
dominated by these two positive original-roof measures.  Apply
\eqref{eq:nh-height} and \eqref{eq:nh-costs} to prove
\eqref{eq:nh-fourier}.  The exceptional term is independent of
all twists, and the clock term tends to zero after multiplication
by $m^2e^{\lambda m}$ when $\lambda<\lambda_c$.
For $I_m$ use its actual pointwise bound.

The common active vertices make the central comparison
coefficient unambiguous; alternatively one may choose one
minimum for each of the finitely many words at fixed $R,m$.
The end portions of the full physical record are retained in
$L_{m,R}(Q)$ and in $\Pi$ throughout.  Multiplication by their
exact phases, or any Borel coefficient of modulus at most one,
preserves the same positive domination.  The statement is not
an integration over an unbounded Fourier range, nor a
replacement of the original return event by a central event.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[A complete physical inverse-gradient volume bound]
\label{lem:nh-singular-volume}
Let $E$ be any of the observable unions of
Corollary~\ref{cor:at-observables}, with widened event $E^+$.
For every Borel roof set $I$ of finite Lebesgue measure $|I|$,
the actual positive source satisfies
\begin{align}\label{eq:nh-singular-volume}
 &\int \one_{\mathcal G_\varepsilon}\one_E\one_\Pi h
        \one_{\{L_{m,R}\in I\}}
                      \frac{I_m}{|\nabla_qF|}\,d\nu_R^*\notag\\
 &\quad\le H_\varepsilon\nu(E^+)
 \left\{\frac{C_{\rm at}}{R_-\rho}|I|
       +\frac{U C_{\rm dist}^2\sqrt{R_+}}
                   {c_*R_-\rho^2}\sqrt{|I|}\right\}.
\end{align}
The source at the finitely many regular critical points may be
assigned any value; the nonnegative integral is defined by
truncation off those zero-area points.  The resulting roof
measure is absolutely continuous.  No bounded pointwise
density for $I_m/|\nabla_qF|$ at a critical roof is asserted.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write $\zeta=(p_0,p_m)$.  Since $|\nabla_qF|=R|\zeta|$,
the part with $|\zeta|\ge\rho$ is bounded by
$H_\varepsilon/(R_-\rho)$ times the measure in
Theorem~\ref{thm:at-height}.  Its saturation is contained in
$E^+$, giving the first term.

For every word with a point of
$\mathcal G_\varepsilon\cap E\cap\{|\zeta|<\rho\}$ choose
one such point $x$.  Lemma~\ref{lem:at-ball} supplies a physical
inverse on $B(\zeta_x,4\rho)$, which contains the whole disk
$B(0,2\rho)$.  Thus this word has a true regular critical
center, and a physical tube parametrized by that whole disk.
Every other selected point of this word with $|\zeta|<\rho$
agrees with this inverse by global wordwise momentum uniqueness.
Distinct words give disjoint tubes, since a physical source
point has only one lifted word.  The two-sided distortion bound
and the original witness transport, on the chosen endpoint
ball, give
\[
  \sum_{\text{selected words}}u_w(0)
                 \le\frac{C_{\rm dist}}{\rho^2}\nu(E^+).
\]
Indeed every point of such a tube is within the displayed
widened witness of its selected original point; the volume
argument is the one in \eqref{eq:at-weight}, now with
multiplicity one.  No initial-section indicator is put into
this lower tube volume.

Let $a_w$ be the original full physical roof of that critical
center.  On its disk, radial coarea and
\eqref{eq:at-roof-jacobian} give
\[
 0<L_w(re_\theta)-a_w\le R_+r^2,\qquad
 \frac1{Rr}\le
     \frac{\sqrt{R_+}}{R_-\sqrt{t-a_w}}\quad(t>a_w).
\]
The source density in $(t,\theta)$ is at most
$U C_{\rm dist}u_w(0)/(4\pi c_*)$ before the inverse-gradient
and incidence weights are inserted.  After integrating the
angle, the positive roof density is consequently bounded by
\[
 \frac{H_\varepsilon U C_{\rm dist}\sqrt{R_+}}{2c_*R_-}
       \sum_w u_w(0)(t-a_w)^{-1/2}\one_{\{t>a_w\}}.
\]
The actual radii chosen by a common roof may differ between
words; the displayed two-sided tube comparison has already
paid that difference.
For any Borel set of measure $\ell<\infty$, layer cake gives
\[
 \int_I(t-a)_+^{-1/2}\one_{\{t>a\}}\,dt
 \le\int_0^\infty\min\{\ell,v^{-2}\}\,dv
 =2\sqrt\ell.
\]
Inserting the complete coefficient sum proves the second term
of \eqref{eq:nh-singular-volume}, with its exact factor two.
Truncate the nonnegative multiplier, use local coarea on
regular charts, then monotone convergence.  The displayed
locally integrable bound proves finiteness on every finite
roof set and absolute continuity of the completed measure.
It does not set a separate singular paired trace to zero.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Normalized payment of the actual singular volume]
\label{cor:nh-singular-payment}
For $E=\mathcal E^{\rm obs}_{D_m,R,m}$, the right side of
\eqref{eq:nh-singular-volume} is bounded by the same expression
with $\nu(E^+)$ replaced by $V_\varepsilon(D_m)$.
After multiplication by $m^2$ it tends to zero, uniformly in
$R,n,k,h$ and the location of every roof interval of a fixed
finite length.  The full total singular volume also has zero
normalized limit on this source.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Use \eqref{eq:nh-budget} and the exponential estimate in
Theorem~\ref{thm:nh-bad-height}.  For the whole volume the
original full roof lies in $[m\tau_-,m\tau_+]$, so the
Lebesgue measure of its support is at most
$m(28/5-3/50)$.  The factor $|I|+\sqrt{|I|}$ is therefore
$O(m+\sqrt m)$ and is absorbed by the same exponential decay.
These are estimates for the actual singular positive volume
used in the physical variation budget.  A selected paired
boundary contribution can use them only after its original
variation/coarea inequality has been shown to charge this
particular interior source.  No such charge is inferred solely
from the boundary being a bulk-null set.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[A genuine guarded physical-word pressure estimate]
\label{cor:nh-guarded-pressure}
Let $M_R'$ and $j_m$ have precisely the true stable-arclength
meaning in Input~\ref{input:v112-geometric-pressure}.
Using the explicit optical theorem
\ref{thm:eo-optical}, the restricted physical-word pressure obeys
\begin{equation}\label{eq:nh-guarded-pressure}
 \sum_{w\in\mathcal W_m(R)}
       \sup_{x\in[w]_R\cap M_R'\cap\mathcal G_\varepsilon}j_m(x)
 \le \frac{1500000 R_+^2U^2C_{\rm dist}^2}{\rho^2}
                         \quad(m\ge1).
\end{equation}
An empty supremum contributes zero.  The estimate includes all
realizable words and the actual stable norm; its constant is
independent of $R,m$.  Its restriction to
$\mathcal G_\varepsilon$, including its endpoint cap, is
explicit and does not discharge the unrestricted premise $Q_*$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The two exact endpoint Jacobians give
$u=R^2\omega/\det G$.
The endpoint Schur bound gives
$\det G\le R^4U^2$, hence $\omega\le R_+^2U^2u$.
Theorem~\ref{thm:eo-optical} gives
$j_m\le30000\omega/c_m\le60000R_+^2U^2u$ on this class.
For a fixed word, cover all its guarded points by the actual
grid tubes of Lemma~\ref{lem:at-cover}.  Their upper distortion
bounds show that its supremum is at most
$60000R_+^2U^2C_{\rm dist}$ times the sum of its tube-center
weights.  This holds even if the supremum is not attained.
The complete center sum is at most
$25C_{\rm dist}\nu(M_R)/\rho^2=25C_{\rm dist}/\rho^2$.
Multiplication yields \eqref{eq:nh-guarded-pressure}.
There is no physical-word cardinality or accumulated
collision-dependent distortion factor.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The original ordered shortage and the proved new payment}
For the inherited persistent low-load \emph{interior} source,
Lemma~\ref{lem:v111-domination} gives the exact positive
domination $c_{\rm per}\le f^\varepsilon\le A$.
At every fixed outer stage $B$, choose any fixed positive atlas
margin $\varepsilon_B\le1/4$.  The restriction of that same
source to
$\mathcal G_{\varepsilon_B,m,R}\cap
 \mathcal E^{\rm obs}_{D_m,R,m}$ has zero $m^2$-normalized
roof height by \eqref{eq:nh-zero-height}, uniformly in the
original labels and central roof set.  The source-side estimate
is unconditional on pressure; its identification with the
inherited pairing coefficient uses precisely that retained
one-copy construction and domination.

All original positive source is preserved by the literal
identity, at the source level,
\[
 \one=\one_{\mathcal G_{\varepsilon_B}\cap\mathcal E^{\rm obs}_{D_m}}
      +\one_{\mathcal G_{\varepsilon_B}\setminus\mathcal E^{\rm obs}_{D_m}}
      +\one_{\mathcal G_{\varepsilon_B}^{\,c}}.
\]
Only the first term has just received this new payment.  On the
second term the full normalized central comparison, including
all marks and its endpoint clock work, is proved above; the
original shallow portions and roofwise comparison capacity
still have their actual obligations.  The third term is not
declared negligible.  The constant $\rho^{-2}$ diverges when
the fixed atlas margin tends to zero, so exhaustion of regular
trajectories does not extend the payment to that third term.
The full nongrazing pool $P_{r_B}^{\rm ng}$ is unchanged.

The new pointwise, singular-volume and guarded-pressure estimates
therefore reduce concrete missing work without redefining the
remaining target \eqref{eq:v112-remaining}.  All outer
$\varepsilon(B),h_B,r_B,\gamma_B,K_B$, and the optional fixed
$\varepsilon_B$, precede the collision limsup; $B$ remains
outermost.  Uniform pressure without the stated guard and cap,
the uncovered positive boundary source and its actual capacity,
the complete paired/singular budgets, and the uniform spectral,
arithmetic, conditioned microscopic and inversion inputs remain
separate obligations.  No full raw pointwise local limit or
four-coordinate inverse theorem is claimed from these payments.
