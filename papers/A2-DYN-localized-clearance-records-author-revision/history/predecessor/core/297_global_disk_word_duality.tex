\section{A global variational certificate for every physical word}
\label{sec:gv-duality}
This section supplies a comparison valid on the whole original physical
source, without requiring a common clearance margin or a physical critical
center. It extends, rather than reclaims as new, the Cartesian support
argument of Lemma~\ref{lem:v111-support} and the fixed-endpoint minimizing
property in Lemma~\ref{lem:v111-roof-range}.
The new ingredients are a complete disk-word duality formula, an exact
endpoint defect, and quantitative certificates with nonnegative terms.

Fix $m\ge1$, $R\in[R_-,R_+]$, and an ordered lifted center word
$w=(O_0,\ldots,O_m)$, with $O_0=0$ and $O_{i+1}\ne O_i$.
A word of an actual trajectory always satisfies this last condition.
Set
\[
 K_w(R)=\prod_{i=0}^m\overline B(O_i,R),\qquad
 S(Y)=\sum_{i=0}^{m-1}|Y_{i+1}-Y_i|,\qquad
 \ell_w(R)=\min_{Y\in K_w(R)}S(Y).
\]
The disks here are the original geometric disks, including their interiors
only for this auxiliary minimization. An arbitrary minimizer is \emph{not}
called a Lorentz trajectory: it may have an interior vertex, a tangency,
or a segment intersecting an unlisted disk. Neither $\ell_w$ nor the
minimization defines a replacement dynamics, a roof, a probability law,
or a new conditioning event. The actual clock throughout remains $L=S(Q)$
on the original first-hit trajectory $Q$.
Successive disks are separated by at least $1-2R_+=3/50$, so $S$ is
continuously differentiable on a neighborhood of $K_w(R)$.

For vectors $V=(v_0,\ldots,v_{m-1})$, $|v_i|\le1$, put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gv-dual-def}
 \begin{split}
 d_0&=-v_0,\quad d_i=v_{i-1}-v_i\ (0<i<m),\quad d_m=v_{m-1},\\
 C_w(V)&=\sum_{i=0}^{m-1}v_i\cdot(O_{i+1}-O_i),\qquad
 T(V)=\sum_{i=0}^m|d_i|,\qquad
 D_{w,R}(V)=C_w(V)-RT(V).
 \end{split}
\end{equation}
All lengths in these expressions have the original physical units.

\begin{theorem}[Exact disk-word duality and a nonnegative gap]
\label{thm:gv-duality}
For every word and radius just specified,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gv-duality}
 \ell_w(R)=\max_{|v_i|\le1}D_{w,R}(V).
\end{equation}
Both extrema are attained. For every $Y\in K_w(R)$ and every feasible $V$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gv-gap}
 \begin{split}
 S(Y)-D_{w,R}(V)
 &=\sum_{i=0}^{m-1}\bigl(|Y_{i+1}-Y_i|-v_i\cdot(Y_{i+1}-Y_i)\bigr)\\
 &\quad+\sum_{i=0}^m\bigl(d_i\cdot(Y_i-O_i)+R|d_i|\bigr).
 \end{split}
\end{equation}
Every summand is nonnegative. At any minimizing $Y$, the unit edge
vectors $v_i=(Y_{i+1}-Y_i)/|Y_{i+1}-Y_i|$ attain the dual maximum.
In particular an optimal dual vector can always be chosen with
$|v_0|=|v_{m-1}|=1$ and $2\le T(V)\le2m$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Expand $\sum_i v_i\cdot(Y_{i+1}-Y_i)=\sum_i d_i\cdot Y_i$ and
$C_w(V)=\sum_i d_i\cdot O_i$. Subtraction proves
\eqref{eq:gv-gap}. The first sum is nonnegative by $|v_i|\le1$;
the second is nonnegative by $|Y_i-O_i|\le R$. This proves weak
duality without a minimax interchange.

Compactness gives a primal minimizer $Y$. Convexity and differentiability
of $S$ show that its first-order inequality on the convex product
$K_w(R)$ is necessary and sufficient for minimality. With the displayed
unit edge vectors its $i$th gradient is exactly $d_i$. Varying one vertex
at a time therefore shows that $Y_i$ minimizes $d_i\cdot Z$ on
$\overline B(O_i,R)$. If $d_i\ne0$, this minimizer is
$O_i-Rd_i/|d_i|$; if $d_i=0$, the corresponding gap is identically
zero. Thus every term on the right of \eqref{eq:gv-gap} vanishes at
this $Y,V$. This proves equality, dual attainment and the asserted
choice of dual vectors. At the two endpoints $|d_0|=|d_m|=1$;
each of the $m-1$ interior differences has norm at most two, giving
$2\le T(V)\le2m$. The case $m=1$ uses just the two endpoint terms.
Nonconsecutive repetitions of a disk cause no difficulty: the vertices
are separate variables in the stated deterministic comparison.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The exact endpoint deficit of an arbitrary true record]
\label{thm:gv-endpoint}
Let $Q_i=O_i+Rn_i$ be \emph{any} regular physical $m$-flight record,
with its actual unit velocities $v_i$, endpoint momenta $p_0,p_m$,
and incidences $c_i>0$. All original section and exact-label tests
may be retained. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gv-endpoint-exact}
 L-D_{w,R}(V)=E_R(Q):=R(2-c_0-c_m),
\end{equation}
whence, uniformly in $m,R,w$ and all intermediate incidences and clearances,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gv-endpoint-bound}
 0\le L-\ell_w(R)\le E_R(Q)
 =R\left(\frac{p_0^2}{1+c_0}+\frac{p_m^2}{1+c_m}\right)\le2R.
\end{equation}
Consequently the range of the true roof on a physical word is at most
$2R$, improving the still valid $4R$ enclosure of
Lemma~\ref{lem:v111-roof-range}. No collision is deleted in this bound.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For actual velocities the flight terms of \eqref{eq:gv-gap} vanish.
The true reflection law gives $d_i=-2c_i n_i$ for $0<i<m$,
so every interior contact term also vanishes. At departure,
$d_0=-v_0$ and $v_0\cdot n_0=c_0$; at arrival,
$d_m=v_{m-1}$ and $v_{m-1}\cdot n_m=-c_m$.
The two remaining contact terms are exactly $R(1-c_0)$ and
$R(1-c_m)$. This proves \eqref{eq:gv-endpoint-exact}.
Weak duality and feasibility of $Q$ sandwich $\ell_w$ between
$D_{w,R}(V)$ and $L$. The identity
$1-c=p^2/(1+c)$ proves the second expression. Every true roof of
the word lies in the same interval $[\ell_w,\ell_w+2R]$.
This proof uses only real flight vectors and the exact specular law;
it introduces no independence, pressure hypothesis or enlarged
observational window. The interval is a deterministic enclosure,
not the event on which a local limit theorem is asserted.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[A globally certified regular critical record]
\label{cor:gv-critical}
If $p_0=p_m=0$, then $L=\ell_w(R)$ and $Q$ is the unique minimizer
of $S$ on $K_w(R)$. For any competitor $Y\in K_w(R)$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:gv-critical-gap}
 S(Y)-L\ge
 \frac1{2R}\sum_{i=0}^m\lambda_i|Y_i-Q_i|^2
 +\frac12\sum_{i=0}^{m-1}|Y_{i+1}-Y_i|
       |\widehat v_i-v_i|^2,
 \quad \lambda_0=\lambda_m=1,\quad\lambda_i=2c_i,
\end{equation}
where $\widehat v_i$ is the unit vector of the competitor edge.
This global minimizing certificate is new here; the uniqueness among
regular physical critical records was already proved in
Section~\ref{sec:v111-global} and is not counted again as a new result.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Equation~\eqref{eq:gv-endpoint-bound} gives equality and all
$d_i=-\lambda_i n_i$, with $\lambda_i>0$.
For $z=Y_i-O_i$ in the radius-$R$ disk,
\[
 R-n_i\cdot z
 =\frac{|z-Rn_i|^2+R^2-|z|^2}{2R}
 \ge\frac{|Y_i-Q_i|^2}{2R}.
\]
For the flight term, unit length of $v_i$ gives
$|e|-v_i\cdot e=|e|\,|e/|e|-v_i|^2/2$.
Insert these expressions into \eqref{eq:gv-gap}. Equality of lengths
forces every $Y_i=Q_i$, proving uniqueness even against nonphysical
competitors. This does not assert uniqueness of an arbitrary relaxed
minimizer at a word with inactive contacts.
\end{proof}
