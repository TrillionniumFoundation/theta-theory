The new lower-bound proof uses a fully written normalized-fraction identity
and the elementary two-point squared-loss calculation. Neither is advertised
as a new general statistical method. The new comparison concerns the precise
common-name minimax problem and its full collision-sensitive memory profile.

address such questions is made. The manuscript's original references and
version pins remain unchanged; the new theorem is distinguished by its stated
quantifiers and proof, not by an unverified priority claim.
