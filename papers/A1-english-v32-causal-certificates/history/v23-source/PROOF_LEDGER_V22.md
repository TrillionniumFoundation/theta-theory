# Proof dependencies for v22

The principal monomial route remains `thm:resolution-main` via mixed positivity, acquired Newton flags, the whole-image bound and `thm:causal-transfer`. The consequence proofs now cite that theorem directly. The exact-kernel route remains unchanged.

## The new algebra route

| Result | Inputs and output |
|---|---|
| `lem:algebra-product-chart` | Bounded coefficient multiplication and positive likelihoods represent **every** product, give exact `v=m+Sigma theta`, a uniform theta bound, and a bounded-format whole image. |
| `lem:algebra-acquired-density` | The first-command derivative equals the identity before applying Sigma. A quantitative inverse maps a fixed ball; its Jacobian is bounded; the complementary commands are integrated with actual report evidence. Constants are prior-independent. |
| `lem:algebra-update` | The multiplication coefficients give a raw-state Bayes quotient with denominator at least 3/4. This is a reachable-state Lipschitz map with no inverse covariance. |
| `thm:algebra-multistep` | The three preceding lemmas verify (G), (A), (C) for the physical delayed-query map. The existing transfer theorem gives the sharp integer-budget checkpoint and single-filter laws, including accumulated stage errors. |
| `prop:finite-observable-quotient` | Polynomial annihilation makes each generator finite-valued; separating polynomials produce the partition indicators. This identifies the exact scope of the function-algebra hypothesis without using partition coordinates in quantitative bounds. |
| `cor:algebra-attenuation` | Bounded diagonal-indicator multiplication, the explicit covariance matrix, and quadratic-form eigenvalue bounds give joint acquisition/query phase scales. The all-prior theorem uses the exact covariance; attenuation-only comparisons require the stated lower bounds on prior weights. |
| `cor:algebra-operational-inverse` | The forward law and an explicit real witness budget, rounded upward by at most two, recover every exterior-power product from the entire integer causal risk curve. Zero-rank and zero-product cases are handled separately. |

The new theorem is not proved by iterating the one-step risk statement, assuming independence of posterior coordinates, ignoring past quantization errors, whitening by a small eigenvalue, or assigning positive mass to a fixed command slice. Those substitutions would not establish the printed result.

`PRESERVATION_REPORT.json` records actual formal-block comparisons. Its counts measure inclusion and explicitly registered edits, not mathematical truth. The diagnostic scripts test exact finite identities; the lemmas contain the analytic arguments.
