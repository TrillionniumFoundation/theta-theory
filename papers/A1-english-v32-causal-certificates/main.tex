\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:resolution-main}]
Theorem~\ref{thm:intrinsic-checkpoint} proves both checkpoint bounds
with reachable representatives for every integer budget.
Theorem~\ref{thm:intrinsic-streaming} proves their simultaneous causal
realization and the converse under the same acquisition law. There
are finitely many checkpoints, so their constants have a finite
maximum and a positive minimum. These constants are independent of
the calibration and the memory budget by the two theorems.
\end{proof}
