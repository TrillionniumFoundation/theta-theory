[13] \begin{abstract}
[14] For positive finite-horizon monomial experiments with a fixed full-support
[15] prior, we determine the information attainable from past reports and the
[16] memory required for its causal retention. Maximal Vandermonde volumes of
[17] the formal future
[18] exponents give matching bounds for optimal prediction regret at every
[19] integer label budget, uniformly through all additive collisions in a
[20] compact exponent chamber. The constants may depend on the fixed prior,
[21] detector and horizon. The lower bound comes from an attainable monomial
[22] tangent and simultaneous confluent flags with positive mass under the
[23] actual acquisition law. A whole-image covering estimate and reachable
[24] raw-moment updates realize the upper bound by one transducer charged
[25] after every report. The law determines persistent-bit requirements and
[26] resolution phases at intersecting collision loci. We also give an exact
[27] realization theory for prescribed acquisition in general finite-cell
[28] experiments: the one-step coefficients form a Blackwell postprocessing
[29] region, and conditional state laws close in finite cell moments.
[30] This yields attained mean-risk formulas, deterministic realization
[31] under atomless commands, and polyhedral updates for scalarized risks.
[32] Complete finite-certificate constructions and an exact continuous-command
[33] example accompany the classification.
[34] \end{abstract}
