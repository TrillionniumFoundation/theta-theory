[11] \subsection{The global past kernel}
[12] 
[13] Let \(\Omega^-\) be the space of legal left-infinite return-edge histories
[14] with the current suspension residual.  Put
[15] \[
[16]  d_\beta(x,y)=\sum_{j\ge0}e^{-\beta j}
[17]    \bigl(1\wedge d_E(x_{-j},y_{-j})\bigr),
[18]  \qquad
[19]  W(x)=\exp\{\eta(r(x_0)+\tau(x_0)+\mathfrak s(x_0))\}.
[20] \]
[21] The reference Gibbs conditional probability of the next edge is denoted
[22] \(g(e\mid x)\).  Summable variations give
[23] \[
[24]  \sum_{n\ge1}\operatorname{var}_n(\log g)<\infty.
[25] \]
[26] Appending a legal edge and shifting the past defines one global Markov kernel
[27] \(P\) on \(\Omega^-\).
[28] 
[29] \begin{lemma}[Derived one-step coupling]
[30] \label{lem:r17-a4-coupling}
[31] There are \(C,c>0\) such that, if two histories agree through depth \(N\),
[32] then their next-edge kernels have total variation distance at most
[33] \(Ce^{-cN}\).  Under a maximal coupling, a successful match multiplies
[34] \(d_\beta\) by \(e^{-\beta}\); a failed match has probability at most
[35] \(Cd_\beta(x,y)^\alpha\) for some \(\alpha>0\).
[36] \end{lemma}
[37] 
[38] \begin{proof}
[39] The Gibbs ratio of two next-edge probabilities is the exponential of the
[40] variation of the induced potential on histories sharing their last \(N\)
[41] edges.  Summable variation bounds the logarithmic ratio by \(Ce^{-cN}\), and
[42] Pinsker's elementary two-measure estimate gives the total variation bound.
[43] Choose \(N\) comparable with \(|\log d_\beta|\).  If the next edges match, all
[44] old discrepancies shift one coordinate into the past, giving the factor
[45] \(e^{-\beta}\).
[46] \end{proof}

[48] \begin{lemma}[Lyapunov drift and a common small set]
[49] \label{lem:r17-a4-drift}
[50] For sufficiently small \(\eta>0\),
[51] \[
[52]  PW\le\lambda W+C,
[53]  \qquad \lambda<1.
[54] \]
[55] For every \(R<\infty\), the set \(\{W\le R\}\) is \(n_R\)-small: there are a
[56] probability \(\nu_R\) and \(\epsilon_R>0\) such that
[57] \[
[58]  P^{n_R}(x,\cdot)\ge\epsilon_R\nu_R(\cdot)
[59]  \quad\text{for all }W(x)\le R.
[60] \]
[61] \end{lemma}
[62] 
[63] \begin{proof}
[64] The exponential return/roof/singularity moment of the induced Gibbs kernel
[65] implies a Foster--Lyapunov estimate after reducing \(\eta\); the current edge
[66] is shifted out and the new exponential moment is uniformly bounded.  On a
[67] Lyapunov sublevel only finitely many edge cylinders carry all but a fixed
[68] small probability.  The finite connector property supplies a common legal
[69] word from each such cylinder to one fixed magnet cylinder.  Bounded Gibbs
[70] distortion gives a uniform positive lower bound on the probability of that
[71] word and on a common terminal subcylinder, proving minorization.
[72] \end{proof}

[81] \begin{theorem}[Global weak-Harris spectral theorem]
[82] \label{thm:r17-a4-harris}
[83] For suitable \(\epsilon,\delta\) there are \(n_0\) and \(\rho<1\) such that
[84] \[
[85]  \mathcal W_{\widetilde d}(P^{n_0}(x,\cdot),P^{n_0}(y,\cdot))
[86]  \le\rho\widetilde d(x,y).
[87] \]
[88] Consequently \(P\) has a unique invariant probability \(\nu\), a spectral gap
[89] on the weighted Lipschitz space
[90] \[
[91]  \mathcal B=\{f:\|f\|_W+\operatorname{Lip}_{\widetilde d}f<\infty\},
[92] \]
[93] and exponentially summable correlations.  This kernel is the original global
[94] Doob/history kernel; no chart mixture has been introduced.
[95] \end{theorem}
[96] 
[97] \begin{proof}
[98] For small \(d_\epsilon\), use the coupling of
[99] Lemma~\ref{lem:r17-a4-coupling}; the failed-match term is absorbed by choosing
[100] \(\epsilon\) small.  For large distance but bounded \(W\), use the common
[101] small-set coupling from Lemma~\ref{lem:r17-a4-drift}.  Outside that set, the
[102] Lyapunov drift contracts the weight factor.  The three estimates are exactly
[103] the weak-Harris contraction in the patched distance.  Iteration gives the
[104] spectral gap and correlation bound on \(\mathcal B\).
[105] \end{proof}
