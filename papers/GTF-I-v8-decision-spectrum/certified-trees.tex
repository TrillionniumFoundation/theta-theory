\section{Finite-prefix, finite-description realization}
\label{sec:v8-certified}
The recursive partition theorem isolates a necessity condition.
Predictive-energy trees provide a verifiable sufficient construction.
The full tree theorem and its geometric hypotheses are proved in
Section~\ref{sec:v7-trees} below. We now strengthen its implementation:
exact ordering of nearly tied real energies is unnecessary, and the raw
acquisition need not expose an infinite word.

Use the factorial legal language, stationary cylinder masses $p_v$,
energies $a_v$, scales $D_v=(a_v/p_v)^{1/2}$, and constants
$a_-,\rho,U,C_0,c_0$ of that theorem. In particular $a_\emptyw=1$,
$a_{vi}\le\rho a_v$ with $\rho<1$, $a_{vi}\ge a_-a_v$,
$D_{vi}\le D_v$, and $a_{uv}<a_v$ for nonempty $u$.
Legality and the child list are decidable. Suppose an oracle returns a
positive rational upper estimate $u_v$ satisfying, for a fixed
$\kappa\ge1$,
\begin{equation}
                         a_v\le u_v\le\kappa a_v.     \label{eq:v8-oracle}
\end{equation}
No uniform cost for this oracle is hidden in a cardinality assertion.
Set
\begin{equation}
 \overline a_v=\min\{u_w:w\text{ is a contiguous factor of }v,
                              \text{ including }\emptyw\}.      \label{eq:v8-factor-envelope}
\end{equation}
For $0<\eta<1$, split precisely the legal vertices for which
$\overline a_v>\eta$. Retain all their legal children. Denote the resulting
full finite tree by $\mathcal T_\eta$, its leaf cut by $\mathcal C_\eta$,
and its number of leaves by $l_\eta$.

\begin{theorem}[Robust tree realization with charged acquisition]
\label{thm:v8-certified}
The rational-threshold construction is finite, suffix closed, and
supports both deletion and prepend-and-parse insertion. Its depth and
state count satisfy
\begin{align}
 D_\eta&\le1+\left\lceil
                    \frac{\log(\kappa/\eta)}{|\log\rho|}\right\rceil,
                                                        \label{eq:v8-depth}\\
 V_\eta&=1+\sum_{v\text{ internal}}\deg^+(v)
                      \le (U+1)(2l_\eta-1).             \label{eq:v8-vertices}
\end{align}
For constants depending only on the displayed structural bounds and
$\kappa$,
\begin{equation}
 \sum_{v\in\mathcal C_\eta}a_v
                 \asymp e_{l_\eta}^2(Z)\asymp G_{l_\eta}.
                                                        \label{eq:v8-threshold-optimal}
\end{equation}
In the deletion experiment, at most $D_\eta$ symbols of a snapshot
acquisition suffice; no later access to that snapshot is used. The
same average-risk order as the exact greedy construction follows.
For every sufficiently large state budget $S$, a dyadic threshold can
be selected with $V_\eta\le S$, $l_\eta\ge cS$, and
$D_\eta=O(\log(S+1))$.

If the online readouts are in a fixed bounded subset of $\R^d$ and
representatives can be computed to prescribed accuracy, the selected
machine has a finite rational presentation. Readout precision
$p=O(\log(S+1))$ suffices to retain the risk order. A word list, transition
table and readout table have total description
\begin{equation}
 P=O\bigl(S\log(S+1)+Sd\,p\bigr)                     \label{eq:v8-description}
\end{equation}
for a fixed alphabet. Its online persistent storage is one of at most
$S$ states; a transition-table implementation uses transient storage
$O(\log(S+1)+dp)$ bits. An acquisition uses $O(\log(S+1))$ symbol queries.
Online table accesses can be implemented in word-RAM time proportional
to these query counts; a sequential bit-table implementation gives the
conservative bound $O(S(\log(S+1)+dp)\log(S+1))$ bit operations per
acquisition. Offline oracle time is separate. The Hilbert-space
cardinality assertion does not, by itself, encode an infinite-dimensional
readout in finitely many bits.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
If $w$ is a factor of $v$, removing a prefix and then a suffix cannot
decrease energy. Consequently
\begin{equation}
 a_v\le\overline a_v\le\kappa a_v,
 \qquad \overline a_{iv},\overline a_{vi}\le\overline a_v.
                                                        \label{eq:v8-envelopebounds}
\end{equation}
The second assertion follows by inclusion of factor sets and requires
no accurate comparison between two distinct true energies. Thus the
internal vertices form a prefix-closed and suffix-closed set. Each
internal vertex is split into all its legal children. A proper suffix
of a leaf is a legal child of a suffix of its parent, hence is present;
repeating this argument proves full suffix closure. If $iv$ had no
leaf prefix when $v$ is a leaf, it would be internal, forcing its suffix
$v$ to be internal, a contradiction. These are exactly the two update
operations required.

At depth $n$, $\overline a_v\le\kappa\rho^n$, which proves finiteness
and \eqref{eq:v8-depth}. Every nonroot leaf has an internal parent, so
\begin{equation}
                  a_-\eta/\kappa<a_v\le\eta.          \label{eq:v8-threshold-balance}
\end{equation}
The exact vertex count follows by counting edges; contracting unary
chains gives \eqref{eq:v8-vertices} as in the full-tree lemma below.

Here is a lower bound not depending on exact greedy ordering. Refine
all leaves by $h=3(U+1)$ further legal levels. Each leaf has at least
$2^3$ descendants at that level, because there are at most $U$
consecutive unary vertices. The resulting incomparable cylinders
number at least $8l_\eta$. Every one has energy at least
$a_-^{h+1}\eta/\kappa$. The separation hypothesis and monotonicity of
$D$ make the open tubes of radii $\delta D_w$ about their images
disjoint for $2\delta<c_0$. At most $l_\eta$ tubes can contain one of
$l_\eta$ arbitrary proposed Hilbert centres. Each unoccupied cylinder
contributes at least $\delta^2a_w$ to the squared error. Hence
$e_{l_\eta}^2(Z)\ge c l_\eta\eta$. One representative per original leaf
gives $e_{l_\eta}^2(Z)\le C_0^2\sum a_v$, and
\eqref{eq:v8-threshold-balance} proves the first comparison in
\eqref{eq:v8-threshold-optimal}. The second is the static conclusion of
Theorem~\ref{thm:v7-tree}, whose proof does not use the present theorem.
Its oscillation estimate in the deletion orientation, or its cylinder
diameter estimate in the insertion orientation, also proves the stated
risk bounds for this cut.

A prefix parser stops on reaching a leaf, after at most $D_\eta$
queries. It never reads or stores the rest of the snapshot. This is a
finite-latency acquisition convention: the snapshot is available during
that update and the prediction deadline permits these microsteps. No
claim is made for a moving physical state observed after an unmodelled
read delay. Insertion and deletion then use the already proved closure.

For budget selection, use $\eta_j=2^{-j}$ and take the last threshold
with $V_{\eta_j}\le S$. There is a constant bound on
$V_{\eta_{j+1}}/V_{\eta_j}$. In fact, beneath a leaf at threshold $\eta$,
all descendants at depth $k$ have estimated energy at most
$\kappa\rho^k\eta$, and so the next threshold adds at most $k$ levels
once $\kappa\rho^k\le1/2$. The ratio is bounded by a fixed geometric
sum of powers of the alphabet size. It follows that the chosen
$V_\eta$ is at least a fixed multiple of $S$, and then
\eqref{eq:v8-vertices} gives $l_\eta\ge cS$.
For completeness, a leaf at depth $n$ has $a_v\ge a_-^n$, so
$n\ge\log(1/\eta)/|\log a_-|$. Every legal path branches at least once
in $U+1$ levels. The complete cut therefore has at least
$2^{\lfloor\log(1/\eta)/((U+1)|\log a_-|)\rfloor}$ leaves.
Since $l_\eta\le S$, this bounds $\log(1/\eta)$ by $O(\log(S+1))$.
Combine with \eqref{eq:v8-depth}. Fixed-dilation comparison for $G$
then converts \eqref{eq:v8-threshold-optimal} into the same-$S$ order.

Finally replace each finite-dimensional readout by a rational vector
within Euclidean distance $\delta$. Pointwise squared loss is at most
twice its previous value plus $2\delta^2$. Take $\delta^2\le\eta$ and
use $\sum a_v\ge a_-l_\eta\eta/\kappa$. Bounded readouts require
$p=O(\log(1/\eta)+\log(d+1))$ binary digits per coordinate. Store all
vertex words, their legal child/deletion/insertion transitions, and these
rational vectors. There are $O(S)$ transitions for a fixed alphabet,
each state index costs $O(\log(S+1))$ bits, and the word-list depth is
$O(\log(S+1))$. This proves \eqref{eq:v8-description}. A streaming
parser uses only its current vertex index during the acquisition;
no prefix string survives the update. The stated time bounds follow
by random access, or by a full sequential scan for each table access.
The number and precision of the offline queries can be listed, but
\eqref{eq:v8-oracle} alone gives no uniform running-time bound for
answering them. This is why offline time is not absorbed into
\eqref{eq:v8-description}.
\end{proof}

The use of all factors in \eqref{eq:v8-factor-envelope} is essential.
Independent relative approximations $u_v$ need not respect suffix
monotonicity, even when the true energies do. Thresholding the raw
approximations can therefore produce a tree that cannot execute its own
suffix update. The envelope repairs that defect while changing energies
by at most the fixed factor $\kappa$.
