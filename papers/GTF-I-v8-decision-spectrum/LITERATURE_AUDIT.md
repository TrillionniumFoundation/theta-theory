## Exact boundary of the mathematical claims

The common-encoder spectrum deliberately includes its loss/output type, policy class and resource transformer. Merely naming an attainable-risk function is not asserted to establish a new universal dimension. The structural content is the proved compatibility characterization, the saturated causal reconstruction with its storage cost, and the quantified consequences.

The finite duality criterion is classical and is presented with proof to avoid unverified generality in the statistical/causal bridge. The finite-history partition formula is closely related to classical automaton state congruences; no claim that finite-state coding or minimal sufficiency was introduced here is made. The finite-precision tree argument and all-priors nonlinear law-presentation lower are explicitly stated with their own assumptions. The sequential Gaussian exponent is a consequence of a retained finite-experiment theorem and a genuine causal register construction, not an assertion of an unprecedented Gaussian quantization principle.

An independent referee may find additional relevant antecedents or overlaps. The records above distinguish that open bibliographic evaluation from the validity of the displayed proofs. Neither publication metadata nor successful retrieval proves originality.
