`OB3-MG-v1` is the three-disk no-eclipse specular open billiard used in
Papers I–III.

It carries, on one system:

- a non-similarity moving obstacle;
- fixed obstacle coding of the trapped set;
- a specified non-reversible Markov-Gibbs invariant law;
- a nonzero Hölder suspension source;
- the actual nonconstant free-flight roof;
- correlated collision increments with nonzero rough area;
- the microscopic collision DPP;
- the physical-time theta-HJB limit.

The declared probability is not an SRB or Liouville law for a periodic Sinai
table.  That stronger scope is not claimed.
