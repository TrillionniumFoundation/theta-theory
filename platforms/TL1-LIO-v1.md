# TL1-LIO-v1

A compact periodic finite-horizon Sinai billiard on the triangular lattice
with one circular scatterer of radius `R in [9/20,47/100]` per cell.

- microscopic law: deterministic unit-speed specular reflection;
- natural preparation: normalized Liouville cosine measure;
- mechanical path observable: first lattice coordinate of actual cell displacement;
- physical clock: actual free-flight time;
- moving family: radial scatterer deformation with fixed lattice;
- nonconjugacy witness: marked periods at centre separations `1` and `sqrt(3)`;
- theta: local current conjugate field `theta_R(j)=I_R'(j)`;
- physical fluctuation coefficient: pressure-root variance of `J-v tau`.
