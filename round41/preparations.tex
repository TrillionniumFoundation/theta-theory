
\section{Finite preparation mixtures and verification scope}
\label{sec:preparations}

The homogeneous lattice may begin from one of finitely many latent
preparation laws.  This section states the resulting mixture theorem with the
latent-variable and policy assumptions made explicit.

Let \(J<\infty\), let \(L\in\{1,\ldots,J\}\) have prior probabilities
\(w_j>0\), and, conditional on \(L=j\), let the working initial-state law be
\(\nu_j\), supported in the radius-\(R\) Hilbert ball.  The parameter prior is
common to all labels.  The policy is also common: it is a measurable function
of the observed record and a fresh randomizer, and it is not told the latent
label.  Under the external truth, the initial state may be fixed or sampled
from any one specified preparation; the result below concerns the working
finite mixture and its frequentist evidence asymptotics.

For one fixed infinite admissible policy, the summable nuisance series of
Lemma~\ref{lem:nuisance-budget} has a \(C^r\) limit
\[
 R_\infty^{\nu_j}(\vartheta)
 =\lim_{n\to\infty}R_n^{\nu_j}(\vartheta)
\]
almost surely.  Define
\begin{equation}
 \rho_{j,\infty}
 =\frac{w_j\exp R_\infty^{\nu_j}(\vartheta_0)}
        {\sum_{l=1}^Jw_l\exp R_\infty^{\nu_l}(\vartheta_0)}.
 \label{eq:preparation-amplitude}
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[Finite-preparation evidence mixture]
\label{thm:preparation-mixture}
Under one fixed infinite prefix-balanced policy, the posterior label probabilities
converge in probability to the vector in
\eqref{eq:preparation-amplitude}.  Jointly with the rescaled physical
parameter, the posterior is at total-variation distance \(o_P(1)\) from
\begin{equation}
 \left(\sum_{j=1}^J\rho_{j,\infty}\delta_j\right)
 \otimes N(I_n^{-1}\Delta_n,I_n^{-1}).
 \label{eq:preparation-product}
\end{equation}
For a triangular sequence of horizon-dependent policies, the same conclusion
holds with finite-horizon weights proportional to
\(w_j\exp R_n^{\nu_j}(\vartheta_0)\); no common almost-sure limit across those
different policies is asserted.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Apply the relative evidence expansion
\eqref{eq:five-evidence} to each of the finitely many initial-state priors.
At \(\vartheta_0\),
\[
 L_n^{\nu_j}(\vartheta_0)
 =L_n^0(\vartheta_0)e^{R_n^{\nu_j}(\vartheta_0)}.
\]
All remaining Laplace factors are common up to a relative \(o_P(1)\) error,
uniformly over the finite label set.  Normalization therefore yields the
finite-horizon weights.  Summability gives their almost-sure limits under one
fixed infinite policy.  Conditional on every label, Theorem~\ref{thm:lattice-bvm}
gives the same Gaussian approximation.  Total variation of a finite mixture
is bounded by the sum of the component distances and the \(\ell^1\) error of
the weights, proving \eqref{eq:preparation-product}.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
The amplitudes are finite-component Bayes factors generated by a bounded
amount of early transient information.  They are not called phases, and no
thermodynamic phase transition is inferred from this theorem.
\end{remark}
