# Literature interfaces and limits of use

The proofs in the reconstruction chapters state their mathematical inputs. The sources below are not blanket certificates for the original mechanical programme.

1. D. Finkelshtein, *Around Ovsyannikov's method*, Methods of Functional Analysis and Topology 21 (2015), 134–150; arXiv:1412.8628. Relevant to linear evolution in compatible Banach scales. B2 gives the equal-radius proof actually used, rather than citing an integrable Volterra kernel that is not present.
2. C. Cuny and F. Merlevède, *On martingale approximations and the quenched weak invariance principle*, arXiv:1202.2964. Relevant to Hilbert-valued projective martingale approximation. A1 works with a stronger explicit bounded-cylinder hypothesis and proves its own bounded remainder.
3. M. Demers, I. Melbourne, M. Nicol, *Martingale approximations and anisotropic Banach spaces with an application to the time-one map of a Lorentz gas*, arXiv:1901.00131. An anisotropic statistical-limit interface, not a source-uniform singular-family raw Edgeworth theorem.
4. M. Hairer, J. C. Mattingly, M. Scheutzow, *Asymptotic coupling and a general form of Harris' theorem with applications to stochastic delay equations*, Probability Theory and Related Fields 149 (2011), 223–259; DOI:10.1007/s00440-009-0250-6. The coupling cost and the function norm must match. It does not supply an arbitrary L2 compressed gap.
5. G. Lumer and R. S. Phillips, *Dissipative operators in a Banach space*, Pacific Journal of Mathematics 11 (1961), 679–698; DOI:10.2140/pjm.1961.11.679. Used in A4 only after dense domain, closedness, dissipativity and the range condition are established.
6. T. Bodineau, I. Gallagher, L. Saint-Raymond, S. Simonella, *Statistical dynamics of a hard sphere gas: fluctuating Boltzmann equation and large deviations*, Annals of Mathematics 198 (2023), 1047–1201; DOI:10.4007/annals.2023.198.3.3; arXiv:2008.10403. The publisher describes short-time fluctuation limits and large deviations under regularity assumptions. A theorem for the stronger joint collision-history topology in this repository is not imported without further verification.
7. The same authors, *Cluster expansion for a dilute hard sphere gas dynamics*, arXiv:2205.04110. Relevant to a genuine real-trajectory cluster construction, distinct from a logarithm of time-composition kernels.
8. R. Kraaij, *A Banach–Dieudonné theorem for the space of bounded continuous functions on a separable metric space with the strict topology*, Topology and its Applications 209 (2016), 181–188; arXiv:1602.01587. **There is a 2019 corrigendum** (volume 252, 198–199). No Banach–Dieudonné, hypercompleteness, or open-mapping conclusion from that paper is imported here. C2 supplies its own proof of the classical Radon dual representation via c0 sums, Hahn–Banach and the compact Riesz theorem.

Primary metadata were checked during this revision. The reconstruction is not a literature novelty claim, and the available abstracts do not verify additional technical hypotheses for this repository's models.
