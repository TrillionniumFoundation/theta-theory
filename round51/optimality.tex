\section{All-policy comparison and logarithmic depth order}
\label{sec:optimality}
Choose two admissible coefficients differing only in the physical diagonal
$b_J$ by $\delta\in(0,b_+-b_-)$. Give both the zero initial state, an allowed
subexperiment of the uniform initial-state problem. Write
$q_k=(e_k,0)$, $v_k=(0,e_k)$ and $m_0=2J+1$.

\begin{lemma}[Two-way propagation]
\label{lem:two-way}
For all $t\ge0$,
\[
 |g_\beta(t)-g_\gamma(t)|\le
 \delta e^{\Lambda t}\Lambda^{4J+2}t^{4J+3}/(4J+3)!.
\]
The analogous step-response bound is
$\delta e^{\Lambda t}\Lambda^{4J+2}t^{4J+4}/(4J+4)!$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
A generator word from $v_0$ to $q_J$, or from $v_J$ to $q_0$, needs one
velocity-to-position step and two steps per edge. Its first possible degree
is $m_0$; damping loops cannot shorten it. Each propagator is bounded by
$e^{\Lambda t}(\Lambda t)^{m_0}/m_0!$. The difference generator is
$\pm\delta v_Jq_J^*$. Duhamel is the convolution of these two propagators,
and
$\int_0^t(t-s)^{m_0}s^{m_0}\,ds=(m_0!)^2t^{2m_0+1}/(2m_0+1)!$.
Integration gives the step-response bound.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[All-time, all-policy information upper bound]
\label{thm:global}
Let
\[
 \vartheta=\min\{\log2,\lambda_* /(8e\Lambda)\},\qquad
 D_0=(8e\Lambda)^{-1}+C_*/\lambda_*.
\]
For every parameter-independent chronological policy with force bounded by
$U$ and $n$ Gaussian boundary readouts at selected finite times,
\begin{equation}
 \KL(P_\beta^{\pi,n},P_\gamma^{\pi,n})
 \le\frac{nU^2\delta^2D_0^4}{2\sigma^2}
       e^{-4\vartheta(2J+1)}=:K_n.
 \label{eq:kl}
\end{equation}
Every estimator has maximal two-point probability of $d_J$ error at least
$\delta/2$ at least $\max\{0,(1-\sqrt{K_n/2})/2\}$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Split the $L^1$ norm of each off-boundary propagator at
$S=m_0/(8e\Lambda)$. The factorial bound on the short part is at most
$[m_0/(8e\Lambda)]4^{-m_0}\le(8e\Lambda)^{-1}2^{-m_0}$.
The stable tail is at most $(C_*/\lambda_*)e^{-\lambda_*S}$.
Thus its $L^1$ norm is at most $D_0e^{-\vartheta m_0}$.
Duhamel convolution yields
$\|g_\beta-g_\gamma\|_1\le\delta D_0^2e^{-2\vartheta m_0}$.
At a common recorded history, the candidates prescribe the same bounded
input and readout time. Their Gaussian conditional mean difference is bounded
by $U$ times this norm. The chronological KL chain rule, with the action
kernels canceled, proves \eqref{eq:kl}. Testing the disjoint strict error
balls and applying Pinsker gives the risk bound.
\end{proof}

For fixed positive accuracy, $K_n\to0$ when $J_n/\log n\to\infty$.
Theorems~\ref{thm:block-posterior} and \ref{thm:response-estimator} provide
the attaining bounded-probe design. The lower-bound policy class is larger
than the attaining block-committed class, which is legitimate for an attainable
order comparison. At $J_n=\lfloor r\log n\rfloor$, a pair separated by
$4n^{-s}$ gives $K_n\le Cn^{1-2s-8\vartheta r}$. Thus
$8\vartheta r+2s>1$ is an outer obstruction, whereas $C_br+p_bs<1$ is an
inner sufficient region. They are not asserted to be the same sharp curve.
The fixed positive gap and the deterministically linear upper clock give the
same logarithmic order in elapsed time. Neither an arbitrary coarse clock nor
an optimal leading constant is inferred from this order statement.
