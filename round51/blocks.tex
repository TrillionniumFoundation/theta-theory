\section{Retained-state block information and the main posterior theorem}
\label{sec:blocks}
Call a block successful when all its durations equal $t_0$. It is enough to
count, at each stage, either a nonexploration or an exploration selecting the
designated atom. The conditional probability of this event is
$\pi_m=p_*^m$, with $p_*=1-\rho+\rho w$; counting coincident labeled atoms
can only increase it. The event is determined by independent action randomizers,
not by readouts. Those randomizers may be revealed in advance in the analysis
without changing the implemented chronological likelihood.

\begin{lemma}[All-sign energy and complete-record information]
\label{lem:block-floor}
On a successful block, conditional on its starting history and committed
profiles, put $d_k=b_{k,\beta}-b_{k,\beta_0}$. Then
\begin{equation}
 \E_S\sum_{k=0}^{m-1}f_{b,k}(\beta)^2
 =\sum_{k=0}^{m-1}P_{b,k}(\beta)^2
  +a^2\sum_{k=0}^{m-1}(m-k)d_k^2.
 \label{eq:sign-energy}
\end{equation}
Consequently
\begin{equation}
 \E[\sum_{i\in G_b}f_i(\beta)^2\mid\mathcal F_{b,-}]
 \ge a^2p_*^m\sum_{k=0}^{m-1}(m-k)d_k^2.
 \label{eq:block-floor}
\end{equation}
On $d_J(\beta,\beta_0)\ge\delta$, this is at least $a^2\kappa_W\ge a^2\kappa$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Linearity along the common realized input gives
$f_{b,k}=P_{b,k}+a\sum_{j=0}^k S_{b,j}d_{k-j}$.
The pre-block trajectory and the committed baseline are independent of every
block sign under this conditioning. The orthogonality
$\E S_{b,j}=0$, $\E S_{b,j}S_{b,l}=\mathbf1_{j=l}$ eliminates all cross terms.
Each $d_k^2$ occurs $m-k$ times, proving the identity. Multiply the nonnegative
part by $p_*^m$ and keep the nonnegative contribution of other action patterns.
Equations~\eqref{eq:jet}, \eqref{eq:sampled-weighted} and
$L\mathcal E\le\delta/2$ give the separation claim. This is an information
lower bound, not a likelihood restricted to the success event.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite-sample and asymptotic complete posterior]
\label{thm:block-posterior}
For $n'=Bm$, Theorem~\ref{thm:posterior} applies to
$F=\{d_J\ge\delta\}$ with $q=a^2\kappa_W/m$ or the simpler
$q=a^2\kappa/m$. In particular \eqref{eq:posterior-budget} is a finite
readout budget for the original complete working posterior. The posterior,
estimator and deterministic-time claims of Theorem~\ref{thm:main} follow.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Summing \eqref{eq:block-floor} gives $V_{n'}\ge Ba^2\kappa_W=n'q$ on $F$.
No other hypothesis of the likelihood theorem changes.
For $J_n=\lfloor r\log n\rfloor$, $\delta_n=n^{-s}$, put
$\gamma=C_br+p_bs<1$. Proposition~\ref{prop:envelope} yields
$m_n=O(\log n)$ and $\kappa\ge e^{-C_b}n^{-\gamma}$; also $n'/n\to1$.
The direct formula gives $\log(1/q)=O(\log n)$ and
\[
 n'q\ge c n^{1-\gamma}/\log n,\qquad
 n'q/C_{m_n}\ge c n^{1-\gamma}/(\log n)^2.
\]
These dominate the $O((\log n)^2)$ entropy and prior costs, and the nuisance
threshold diverges. Thus both the posterior upper bound and its failure
probability vanish uniformly. If more than half the posterior is in
$d_J<\delta$, every coordinatewise prefix median is within $\delta$ of
its true coordinate. The product box admits a completion of these medians.
Finally each used stage lasts $t_0+t_i\le t_0+T$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{What the enlarged event changes}
When $\rho<1$ and $w<1$, $p_*^m>(\rho w)^m$. This improves the information
certificate without changing a single implemented action. At $w=1$,
$p_*=1$ and $p_b=3$: artificial exploration rarity has disappeared from the
certificate. The stricter geometric remainder in \eqref{eq:sharp-tail}
provides a second, independent improvement. The weighted coefficient budget
$\mathcal W$ is a third improvement at finite depth; the closed-form exponent
region deliberately uses its conservative upper bound $\mathcal A^2$.
