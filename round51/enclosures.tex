\section{Exact-rational outer certificates with five separate budgets}
\label{sec:enclosures}
Let the statistical reference band be
$I_q=[\widehat y_q-R_q,\widehat y_q+R_q]$. Its supplied rational representation
must obey, for a separately supplied $\epsilon_q\ge0$,
\begin{equation}
 I_q\subset I_q^{\rm rat}\subset
 [\widehat y_q-R_q-\epsilon_q,\widehat y_q+R_q+\epsilon_q].
 \label{eq:representation}
\end{equation}
A nonrational original band must first be enclosed by a certified rational
reference band, with that first error included in the declared representation
budget. Exact arithmetic on a subsequently supplied band cannot retrospectively
certify a rounding step that was not recorded.

An observable has the form $y_q(\beta)=\sum_l\lambda_{ql}h_\beta(t_{ql})$
with rational coefficients and nonnegative rational times. This includes a
pulse difference with coefficients $1,-1$. Put $d_q=\sum_l|\lambda_{ql}|$
and $T_c=\max_{q,l}t_{ql}>0$. Take a rational mesh through site $K\ge J+1$,
of maximum coordinate half-width $r$, and Taylor order $P\ge0$. For each full
mesh box, evaluate its cut-chain center exactly:
\[
 H_q=\sum_l\lambda_{ql}\ell\sum_{k=0}^P A_K^kB\,
                  t_{ql}^{k+1}/(k+1)!.
\]
The full variables are $c,b_0,\ldots,b_K,a_0,\ldots,a_{K-1}$, including
$a_J$. With $E_0=3^{\lceil\Lambda T_c\rceil}$, use rational bounds
\begin{equation}
 \begin{split}
 E_{\rm tail}&=2T_cE_0(\Lambda T_c)^K/K!,\qquad
 E_{\rm mesh}=2rT_c^2E_0,\\
 E_{\rm num}&=T_cE_0(\Lambda T_c)^{P+1}/(P+1)!,\qquad
 E_q=d_q(E_{\rm tail}+E_{\rm mesh}+E_{\rm num}).
 \end{split}\label{eq:outer-errors}
\end{equation}
Retain a full box when every interval $[H_q-E_q,H_q+E_q]$ intersects
$I_q^{\rm rat}$. Return its prefix projection \emph{and} its full witness box.

\begin{theorem}[Outer coverage and diameter]
\label{thm:outer}
The returned union contains every originally feasible prefix. Every admissible
completion of a retained full box satisfies
\begin{equation}
 |y_q(\beta)-\widehat y_q|\le R_q+\epsilon_q+2E_q.
 \label{eq:outer-radius}
\end{equation}
On the sampled-pulse grid, if
$\max_q(R_q+\epsilon_q+2E_q)\le\delta/(4L\mathcal A)$, its prefix diameter
is at most $\delta$. A projected prefix has an existential witnessing full
box; the assertion does not quantify over arbitrary tails attached only to
that projection.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The full chain and cut center agree on boundary words through order $K-1$,
so their tail contribution is bounded by $E_{\rm tail}$. A coordinate change
at most $r$ changes the generator by at most $4r$. Integrating the Duhamel
bound $4rt e^{\Lambda t}$ gives $E_{\rm mesh}$. The exponential Taylor
remainder gives $E_{\rm num}$; $e^{\Lambda T_c}\le E_0$.
Summing with absolute observable weights gives $E_q$ uniformly in all
admissible completions of that full box. Any feasible parameter therefore
has its box retained. At an intersection point, its distance from $H_q$ is
at most $E_q$ and from $\widehat y_q$ at most $R_q+\epsilon_q$; another
$E_q$ reaches any completion, proving \eqref{eq:outer-radius}.
For two retained prefixes choose their full witnesses and apply
Theorem~\ref{thm:transfer}; the distance uses only the prefix.
\end{proof}

Increasing $K,P$ makes the factorial errors small; subsequently refining the
finite mesh controls $E_{\rm mesh}$. The algorithm terminates for finite
unrestricted resources. It checks the complete box count before enumeration
and raises \texttt{ResourceLimit} rather than returning a partial union as a
complete result. This is a finite exhaustive certificate, not a polynomial-time
claim. For example, replacing $[-10^{-7},10^{-7}]$ by $[-1,1]$ requires
$\epsilon\ge1-10^{-7}$, independently of every other error term. The executable
validator rejects zero-budget declarations for this input. The statistical,
representation, spatial-tail, mesh and Taylor errors are always reported
separately, even when the sufficient diameter condition fails.
