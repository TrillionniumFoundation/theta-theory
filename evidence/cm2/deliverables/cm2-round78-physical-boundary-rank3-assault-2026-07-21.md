1. replace all 65 interval carrier tubes by actual fixed-`s=0` physical

2. construct the first actual fixed-`s=0` finite rank-three registry and test

## 3. Why the complete depth-two quotient is not yet claimed

The atlas is not yet a full intersection subdivision.  Among the `244`
same-source tangency-curve pairs, exact bounding boxes discharge `68` pairs
as strictly disjoint, while `176` pairs retain overlapping boxes.  Those
pairs still require two-equation interval-Krawczyk intersection/exclusion,
followed by intersections with the R1/R2 and stationary boundaries and an
open-cell adjacency reconstruction.

Therefore:

- complete physical boundary-carrier atlas: **certified**;
- complete depth-two physical quotient: **not certified**; and
- Gate-4 commuting square: **not promoted**.
