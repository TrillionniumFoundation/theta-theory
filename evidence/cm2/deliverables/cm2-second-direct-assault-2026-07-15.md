For any finite or countable full-branch reverse kernel with a non-atomic SRB

`S_n(x)` is that support, then


maximal at every finite time.  Thus the TV uniform ergodicity and physical
Doeblin minorisation required by the proposed CDKM shortcut are rigorously
impossible; retaining the full countable tail does not help.
