## Verifier independence

The Round130 verifier does not import or execute the Round130 producer.  The
producer is only checked against its frozen byte hash.  The verifier:

- invokes the byte-pinned independent Round129 validation layer;
- independently revalidates the frozen Round129 certificate;
- uses the byte-pinned independent Round122 Jet2 mathematics;
- rebuilds the widened stage-three and F17 physical geometry;
- reconstructs the actual F1–F18 slot namespace without producer code.

The 4096-bit replay first checks the canonical lambda key at

```text

u = 1/2

```

including the terminating dyadic convention and the dedicated upper-endpoint
case.  The derived `b` enclosures are strictly ordered, and no independent
`b` key is consumed.
