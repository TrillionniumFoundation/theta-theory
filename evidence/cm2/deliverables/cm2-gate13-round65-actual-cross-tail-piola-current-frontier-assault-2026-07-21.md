## 3. Gate 3 — one material-coordinate operator calculus

### 3.1 Exact material conjugation

Let `Y_s` be the direct sum of physical branch spaces at parameter `s` and
let

```text
J_s:Y_0 -> Y_s
```

be a branchwise material transport/Piola isomorphism on one common
finite-`s` atlas.  For

```text
R_s:X->Y_s,       P_s:Y_s->Y_s,       Q_s:Y_s->Z,
T_s=Q_s P_s R_s,
```

define fixed-space maps

```text
Rhat_s=J_s^-1 R_s,
Phat_s=J_s^-1 P_s J_s,
Qhat_s=Q_s J_s.                                      (3.1)
```

Then the identity

```text
T_s=Qhat_s Phat_s Rhat_s                            (3.2)
```

is exact, and whenever the three fixed-space maps are differentiable in the
declared operator scale,

```text
DT=(DQhat)Phat Rhat+Qhat(DPhat)Rhat+Qhat Phat(DRhat). (3.3)
```

If the material atlas transports every unchanged regular source cell
exactly, `R_s=J_s R_0`, hence `Rhat_s=R_0` and the third term in (3.3)
vanishes.  The regular moving-boundary current has not disappeared: it has
been moved into `DQhat/DPhat`, where the landing and Piola generator live.
This prevents double charging.

A single `J_s` cannot absorb a branch birth/death, a mismatched incidence,
a change of immutable time tag, or cemetery arrival.  Those topology rows
remain the dyadic-clock/physical-face/cemetery trace ledger from Round 64.

### 3.2 Exact trace criterion for strong moving restriction

For one moving cut `e(s)` and a function with two one-sided traces,

```text
R_s f=(1_[a,e(s)]f,1_[e(s),b]f),
DR_0 f=e'(0)(gamma_e^-(f)delta_e,-gamma_e^+(f)delta_e). (3.4)
```

On a carrier retaining the two side tags, the two atoms are disjoint.
Therefore the derivative is bounded from a Banach source `X` to the
current-completed direct sum if and only if the two-sided trace map is
bounded.  For countably many tagged faces the exact
condition is boundedness of

```text
Gamma:X->ell^1,
Gamma f=(|e_k'| gamma_k^- f,|e_k'| gamma_k^+ f)_k,    (3.5)
```

with norm equal to the tagged face-current norm (up to the declared two-side
normalisation).  This is a necessity as well as a sufficiency because the
tags prevent cancellation.

Weak `L^1` restriction gives no such trace map.  On `[0,1]`, put

```text
f_N(x)=2N(1-Nx)_+.
```

Then `||f_N||_1=1`, while `f_N(0+)=2N`; the derivative of a cut leaving zero
has current coefficient `2N`.  The executable replay uses
`N=1,2,4,8,16,32`.  Thus the frozen norm-one weak restriction cannot imply a
bounded strong moving restriction derivative.

### 3.3 Piola differentiation loses one further uniform derivative

Round 62 gives the correct pointwise weak generator

```text
G_V K: psi -> integral[Dpsi V dot dK+psi dot DV dK].  (3.6)
```

Existence of this generator on each fixed vector is not operator-norm
differentiability of the transport family.  The distinction already occurs
for scalar translations, an area-preserving Piola subcase.

Let

```text
phi(t)=(1-|t|)_+,
f_h(x)=h phi(x/h),
tau_h f(x)=f(x+h).
```

Direct piecewise integration gives

```text
||f_h||_1=h^2,
||f_h'||_1=2h,
||(tau_h f_h-f_h)/h-f_h'||_1=2h.                     (3.7)
```

Hence the quotient by the `W^{1,1}` norm is

```text
2/(h+2) ->1,                                         (3.8)
```

so `tau_h` is not operator-norm differentiable
`W^{1,1}->L^1`, although it is differentiable in `L^1` for every fixed
`W^{1,1}` function.  By contrast, for `f in W^{2,1}`,

```text
||(tau_s f-f)/s-f'||_1 <= |s| ||f''||_1/2.           (3.9)
```

Thus a bounded first generator is not enough: one needs an additional
uniform remainder modulus, commonly furnished by one more strong derivative
or a billiard-specific anisotropic scale estimate.  No frozen artifact
contains that estimate for the moving-scatterer branch Piola family.

### 3.4 Stopped `MT_DQ` needs a depth-weighted derivative ledger

On fixed spaces, once `DT` exists,

```text
D(T^n)=sum_(k=0)^(n-1)T^(n-1-k)(DT)T^k.              (3.10)
```

If `||T||<=M` and `||DT||<=L`, the direct bound is

```text
||D(T^n)||<=n M^(n-1)L.                               (3.11)
```

For disjoint stopped-depth tags, absolute assembly therefore needs the
corresponding depth-weighted series, in addition to the face and cemetery
ledgers.  Finite stopped mass is insufficient: the exact law

```text

```

has total mass one but

```text
sum_n n p_n=sum_n 1/(n+1)=infinity.                  (3.12)
```

This does not assert that the physical stopped law has this tail.  It proves
that weak stopped mass and fixed-depth differentiability do not supply
`MT_DQ` without a same-law depth-weighted derivative estimate.
