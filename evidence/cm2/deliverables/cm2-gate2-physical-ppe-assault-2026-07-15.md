## 1. Executive result

This audit separates three statements that had been too easy to conflate.

1. **Actual branch weights are not an independent mystery once a physical
   full-branch quotient has been built.**  On a one-state unstable quotient
   with invariant SRB conditional density `rho`, the only actual reverse
   kernel is

   ```text
   p_a(x) = rho(h_a x) |h_a'(x)| / rho(x).
   ```

   It is normalized by invariance, telescopes on words, and gives a uniform
   arbitrary-history lower bound for every fixed clean word.  This closes an
   algebraic/measure-theoretic bridge, not its pilot instantiation: the
   required common magnet, reference leaf, full-return partition, and two
   transported branches have not been selected and certified.

2. **The Bernoulli benchmark admits a genuine stopped-parent extension.**
   A history-dependent binary kernel with uniformly positive weights and
   uniformly separated cylinder intervals satisfies a finite-time Frostman
   bound on every stopped parent; i.i.d. or stationarity is unnecessary.
   However, the two certified pilot matrices define only an artificial
   binary subsystem.  They have not been shown to be a full-mass refinement
   of the physical SRB conditional.  Conditioning on survival in their
   fixed subsystem would lose exponentially most physical mass and is
   forbidden by the negative-pressure obstruction.

3. **The endpoint/projective formula is algebraically exact but not typed.**
   For actual endpoint covectors `dX,dY` and the actual tangent direction
   `(1,z)`, their ratio is automatically a Moebius function of `z`, with
   derivative equal to the endpoint wedge divided by the squared
   denominator.  What is missing is the pointwise identification of the
   projective state generated by the transported billiard cocycle with that
   same tangent direction on the same labelled endpoint carrier.

The irreducible remaining conjunction is therefore:

```text
physical full-mass stopped quotient
  + every-parent projective Frostman
  + exhaustive actual amplitude registry and normalized reverse-Holder
  + same-carrier endpoint/cocycle identification
  + retained buffer/continuation and parameter uniformity.
```

No inspected theorem supplies this conjunction.

## 3. Actual SRB/Gibbs kernel bridge

### Proposition 3.1 (actual quotient kernel and arbitrary-history hazard)

Let `F : union_a I_a -> I` be a one-state full-branch unstable quotient of a
clean billiard return rectangle.  Let `h_a : I -> I_a` be its inverse
branches.  Suppose the invariant quotient of the collision-SRB law is

```text
d mu^u = rho(x) dx,       0 < rho_- <= rho <= rho_+ < infinity.
```

Then, almost everywhere,

```text
p_a(x) := rho(h_a x) |h_a'(x)| / rho(x)
```

is a normalized positive kernel.  For a finite word `w`,

```text
p_w(x) = rho(h_w x) |h_w'(x)| / rho(x).
```

Consequently every fixed clean full word satisfies

```text
inf_x p_w(x) >= (rho_- / rho_+) inf_x |h_w'(x)| =: eta_w > 0.
```

The same lower bound holds after an arbitrary admissible past prefix, or
after conditioning on any past cylinder, because it is pointwise in the
remaining base coordinate.  If the rectangle, density bounds, and branch
derivative bounds persist on a compact parameter interval, so does `eta_w`.

#### Proof

Invariance of `rho dx` is the Perron--Frobenius identity

```text
rho(x) = sum_a rho(h_a x) |h_a'(x)|.
```

Division by `rho(x)` gives `sum_a p_a(x)=1`.  Multiplying successive
kernels cancels every intermediate density and gives the word formula.  The
displayed lower bound follows immediately.  Integrating a pointwise lower
bound against any conditional base law preserves it.  Compact parameter
uniformity follows from the stated uniform bounds.  QED.

### Consequence and limitation

This proposition removes freedom to insert artificial Bernoulli weights.
Once the physical quotient is fixed, the Gibbs weights are fixed as well.
It also shows that a positive conditional weight for a selected clean word
is not a separate probabilistic theorem after the common full-cross branch
and bounded SRB density have been produced.

It does **not** construct the quotient.  Lima--Obata--Poletti Lemmas 5.2--5.3
identify the correct equilibrium potential and a countable coding; they do
not choose the one-state homogeneous return base required above, prove its
pilot return/strip registry, or identify the two transported loop matrices.
Thus the pilot still has no numerical or symbolic `eta_w` attached to its
certified matrices.

## 4. A stopped-parent Frostman theorem beyond i.i.d. Bernoulli

### Proposition 4.1 (place-dependent binary stopped-parent Frostman)

Fix a stopped parent.  Suppose its retained tree is a binary interval tree.
At every node `v`:

- the two children carry all parent mass and have conditional weights
  `p_v,0,p_v,1` in `[eta,1-eta]`, where `0<eta<=1/2`;
- their interval supports are disjoint;
- for constants `g>0` and `0<lambda_-<1`, any two level-`k` cylinders are
  separated by at least `g lambda_-^(k-1)`.

The last property follows, for example, from a uniform first-level gap `g`
and inverse branches whose derivatives are bounded below by `lambda_-`.
Put

```text
q = 1-eta,       kappa = log(q)/log(lambda_-) > 0.
```

For the depth-`N` projective law `nu_N,y`, uniformly in the stopped parent,
the complete history, and the frozen variable `y`,

```text
nu_N,y(I) <= C_g ( |I|^kappa + q^N )
```

for every interval `I`.  If a named cemetery has mass at most `epsilon_N`,
the right side gains `epsilon_N`.

#### Proof

Every level-`k` cylinder has mass at most `q^k`, without independence,
because this is the product of history-dependent conditional weights each
bounded by `q`.  If

```text
g lambda_-^k <= |I| < g lambda_-^(k-1),
```

then `I` meets at most one level-`k` cylinder (at most two if closed endpoint
conventions are retained).  For `k<=N` its mass is at most `2q^k`, which is
at most a fixed multiple of `|I|^kappa`.  For `k>N`, use the maximum
depth-`N` cylinder mass `q^N`.  Large intervals are absorbed in the constant,
and a cemetery is added by the union bound.  QED.

### Why this does not yet certify the pilot

This proposition strictly strengthens the existing exact Bernoulli
benchmark: place-dependent weights and arbitrary stopped histories are
allowed.  It would feed the projective terminal criterion in v52.

But the physical hypothesis is absent.  The projective certificate proves
separation and derivative bounds for the two algebraic maps on `[5,7]`; it
does not prove that every actual SRB stopped parent has a **mass-preserving
binary refinement** by those two physical return loops.  Merely restricting
to the two loops produces a fixed clean subsystem of negative relative
pressure.  Its depth-`N` survival mass is exponentially small, so
normalizing that subsystem cannot yield a full physical cover.  This is
exactly the obstruction in v52 Lemma “Negative-pressure trial
obstruction.”

For a complete countable physical alphabet, two special positive-weight
branches also do not by themselves imply Proposition 4.1: the remaining
branches can overlap a target interval.  One must either:

1. prove an exhaustive separated grouping/full-mass binary refinement; or
2. prove a uniform countable/place-dependent projective Frostman theorem
   for the complete actual kernel.

Neither has been done.

## 5. Physical endpoint identity: exact algebra and missing typing

Let `X,Y` be the two actual scalar endpoint maps on one smooth two-dimensional
carrier, and write their covectors in a carrier chart as

```text
dX = (X_1,X_2),       dY = (Y_1,Y_2).
```

For the actual tangent direction `v=(1,z)`, wherever `dX(v)` is nonzero,

```text
dY(v)/dX(v) = (Y_1 + Y_2 z)/(X_1 + X_2 z).
```

Moreover,

```text
d/dz [dY(v)/dX(v)]
  = (Y_2 X_1 - Y_1 X_2)/(X_1 + X_2 z)^2.
```

Thus the transversality determinant in the projective terminal criterion is
the physical endpoint wedge `dX wedge dY`, up to the fixed orientation
convention.  A common reparametrization of the one-dimensional carrier
cancels from `Y'/X'`.

This proves the algebraic endpoint identity; no distributional argument is
needed.  The unresolved pilot statement is pointwise and typed:

```text
the z evolved by the transported common-vertex derivative cocycle
  = the tangent coordinate of the very same labelled BR_sec carrier
on which the actual X,Y endpoints and PPE amplitude are evaluated.
```

The matrices `J_g,J_w,J_B` are currently computed in periodic-orbit
trivializations.  No certified entrance/exit holonomies transport them to
the endpoint carrier, and no record map identifies their projective state
with the tangent used by `X,Y`.  Equality in law would not repair this.

## 6. The normalized-amplitude obstruction is genuine

For a stopped parent let `E` have conditional mass `delta`, and take
`A=1_E`.  Then, for every `p>1`,

```text
E[A^p | parent] = delta <= 1,
Z_A = delta,
E[(A/Z_A)^p | parent] = delta^(1-p),
mu^A(E) = 1.
```

Hence a raw `L^p(A)` estimate, a global tree moment, or ordinary
`psi`-mixing cannot give the normalized parentwise reverse-Holder estimate
required by PPE2.  The exact certificate uses `delta=1/64` and obtains raw
`L^2=1/64`, normalized `L^2=64`, and tilted event mass one.

There is a second exact quantifier obstruction.  Let `Y` be uniform on a
grid of `2^N` parent labels and let the child be deterministically equal to
its parent.  The child marginal obeys

```text
nu_N(I) <= |I| + 2^(-N),
```

but every frozen-parent conditional is a point mass.  Therefore a stationary
or marginal Frostman law cannot be disintegrated into the uniform
every-parent statement required by `PPE_N` without a separate kernel
theorem.

## 7. Exact literature audit through 2026-07-15

### Collision SRB and coding

- Yuri Lima, Davi Obata, Mauricio Poletti, *Measures of maximal entropy for
  non-uniformly hyperbolic maps*, arXiv:2405.04676v2.
  Lemma 5.2 proves pressure zero and the SRB equilibrium identity for the
  geometric potential.  Lemma 5.3 gives a locally compact countable Markov
  coding with Holder lifted potential.  These results identify the correct
  measure/potential, not the stopped projective kernel above.

### Furstenberg/projective results

- Tom Rush, *Frostman dimension of Furstenberg measure for
  `SL(2,R)` random matrix products*, arXiv:2601.14061v1.
  The standing assumptions are one compactly supported **i.i.d.** probability
  measure on `SL(2,R)` satisfying SIP.  Corollary 1.8 identifies the
  Frostman dimension of its stationary Furstenberg measure.  It gives no
  finite-depth estimate uniform over arbitrary stopped physical parents, no
  place-dependent/countable Gibbs kernel, and no amplitude tilt.

- Kiho Park and Mark Piraino, *Transfer operators and limit laws for typical
  cocycles*, arXiv:2007.02349.  Theorems A--B prove CLT and large deviations
  for 1-typical fiber-bunched cocycles over a finite irreducible subshift with
  a Holder equilibrium state.  They do not prove a Frostman or small-ball
  estimate, much less the stopped-parent version.

- Reza Mohammadpour and Paulo Varandas, *Statistical properties of
  equilibrium states for fiber-bunched matrix cocycles and applications*,
  arXiv:2508.05771v1.  Theorem A gives unique Gibbs equilibrium states for
  1-typical fiber-bunched cocycles in the stated parameter range; Theorem C
  gives `psi`-mixing/quasi-Bernoulli.  Neither theorem gives projective
  Frostman regularity or normalized rare-parent amplitudes.

### Standard families and billiard Banach spaces

- Giovanni Canestrari, *Linear response for Sinai billiards with small
  holes*, arXiv:2604.19671v2.  Theorem 2.1 is the one-sided response for a
  shrinking vertical hole.  Proposition 4.4 preserves regular standard
  families under conditional evolution; Theorem 4.7 and Corollary 4.8 give
  exponential convergence after the source is already an initial regular
  standard family.  They do not construct the Gate-2 projective variable,
  an all-parent native stopping antichain, or PPE2.  The introduction also
  explicitly notes that current general piecewise-hyperbolic Banach spaces
  do not contain the required standard-pair curve measures.

- Mark Demers and Carlangelo Liverani, *Recent progress in the application
  of transfer operators to dispersing billiards*, arXiv:2606.10155.
  Theorem 6.1 proves equivalence between the stated anisotropic Banach norm
  and a cone-order norm under its two-dimensional density hypotheses.  It
  does not turn a one-dimensional stopped curve source into the needed
  Banach element and supplies no projective Frostman/PPE theorem.

A targeted arXiv search on 2026-07-15 found no post-Rush theorem extending
Furstenberg Frostman regularity to the required conjunction of countable,
place-dependent, arbitrary-stopped-parent kernels with physical endpoint
typing and normalized amplitudes.
