# Restart charter — General Theta Foundations I

## Canonical mother problem

Given a prepared, intervenable causal experiment, determine:

1. which future tests are fixed by an observed history;
2. the minimal predictive quotient carrying those tests;
3. which target experiments can be implemented by parameter-independent causal simulators;
4. how simulator state, acquisition count, persistent labels, calibration error and approximation defect transfer prediction and decision risk; and
5. when actually acquired geometry, rather than a formal parameter manifold, yields matching resource--resolution laws.

## Required central theorem

Foundations I is reopened around a genuine G1/G3 theorem. Its assumptions must begin at the experiment/kernel level and verify actual acquisition mass, global covering, stable predictive updates and a resource-certified causal implementation. Its conclusion must give a matching resource--resolution--risk law over a class of experiments, not only one designed finite ensemble or one uniformly contracting example.

## Derived layers, not axioms

Fisher information, covariance, singular strata, pressure, LDP, spectral memory and Hilbert-space rank enter only after the experiment and task are fixed. A realization may instantiate one or more layers; it does not redefine the foundations tree.

## Preservation rule

No mathematical result from v1--v96 is deleted. Results that are model-specific or interface-specific live in realization branches and the frozen archive. Promotion back into Foundations I requires a theorem-level generalization with the original causal/resource quantifiers.
