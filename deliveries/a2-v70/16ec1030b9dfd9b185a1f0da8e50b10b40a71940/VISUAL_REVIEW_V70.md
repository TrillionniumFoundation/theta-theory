## Separate whole-document mechanical check

For all 167 principal, 343 complete and seven companion pages, extracted text and same-renderer 72-dpi RGB arrays of the downloaded native PDFs were compared with the clean local PDFs. Every page agrees. PDF byte hashes differ, so this is not byte-for-byte PDF identity. The renderer versions and product hashes are in `AUTHOR_VALIDATION_V70.json`.

All-page rendering agreement is not all-page visual inspection. Neither inspection, build success, finite rational checks nor source hashing establishes correctness of all retained theorems or exceptional significance at a journal.
