## Scope and fail-closed rule

The audit covered the canonical recursive/CM2 corpus, the v83 control index,
the dependency-closure branch, and the Round Three through Round Nine proof
packets already present in the repository.  A historical status word such as
`PROVED`, `CLOSED`, `PASS`, a compiler name, or an interface theorem does not
receive theorem credit unless the same source contains a typed statement,
proof body, and closed dependency chain for the present model.

The Round-Nine reports review the checksum-pinned payload

They supply direct counterexamples to several first lemmas.  Consequently no
Round-Nine certificate or queued publication workflow may be reused as
mathematical evidence.

## Control result

No previous derivation document closes the Round-Nine referee list as a
package.  Each Round-Ten controlling module is therefore self-contained at its
new load-bearing gate and imports only earlier items in the dependency order
below.  In particular:

1. A1 no longer uses a bilateral inverse-shift spectral gap.
2. A2 separates arithmetic aperiodicity from geometric UNI and the
   very-high-frequency Fourier tail.
3. B3 constructs covariance before identifying the action tangent.
4. B4 obtains microscopic values from the prepared LDP/Laplace principle and
   does not call a deterministic law push-forward a nonlinear generator.
5. D1 proves an actual common-space phase disintegration before contraction.

## No downgrade and no no-go substitution

All eleven manuscripts remain active standalone papers.  The revision
replaces false intermediate mechanisms while retaining positive theorems in
properly typed state spaces.  Editorial recommendations in the referee
reports are not converted into mathematical no-go conclusions.  Repository
verification remains internal and does not claim external journal acceptance.
