# Round-Twenty-Three third-pass technical audit

**Branch:** `revision/round23-referee-positive-closure-11paper-2026-09-02`  
**Scope:** defects found only after the first referee reconstruction and second-pass type/domain audit.  This ledger records why “the sources compile” was not accepted as a proof-closure criterion.

## A2: a two-dimensional Fourier tail needs more than two integrations by parts

### Defect

The continuous local-limit transform has two real Fourier coordinates.  A bound of order `|b|^{-2}` is not integrable on `R^2`; it leaves a logarithmic divergence.  A generic Dolgopyat contraction of the form `1-c|b|^{-kappa}` also cannot simply be added as a global ultra-high-frequency bound, because it approaches one as `|b|` grows.

### Repair

A2 now fixes a finite differentiability order `r_*` before the proof, assumes `C^{r_*+3}` billiard geometry and a `C^{r_*+2}` current, and proves one weighted branch estimate through every order up to `r_*`.  Returned-phase charts are controlled through the same order.

The Fourier domain is divided into four disjoint regimes:

1. perturbative Gaussian frequencies;
2. a compact annulus with a uniform spectral gap;
3. intermediate frequencies, where a quantitative Diophantine/Dolgopyat contraction is iterated; and
4. ultra-high frequencies, where direct repeated coarea integration by parts gives `n^{C_0}|b|^{-M}`.

The explicit gate `A2-INTEGRABLE-TAIL-BUDGET` chooses `M` so that the two-dimensional tail, including two source derivatives, is `o(n^{-2})`.  The finite deformation rank is also promoted to the exact data object `A2-SUBMERSION-CERTIFICATE` rather than being inferred from a numerical rank test.

## B1: full-frequency block damping and exact continuous constraints

### Defects

The earlier conditional block estimate weakened to `1-c|xi|^{-2}` at high frequency.  Multiplying such factors does not yield the claimed integrable majorant uniformly over the whole Fourier line.  In addition, conditioning on a fixed-width continuous cell is not the same object as an exact momentum/energy microcanonical fibre.

### Repair

B1 now uses three conditional block regimes on deterministic fresh label blocks:

- `1-c|theta|^2` near zero;
- a uniform `1-c` gap on compact annuli; and
- `C_M(1+|v|)^{-M}` after boundary-flat chart integration by parts, with `M>d_R+4`.

A finite `joint covariance certificate` provides positive-probability legal block increments spanning the conservation quotient and a nonzero continuous Jacobian minor.  Sequential conditional expectation then gives a genuinely integrable global characteristic-function majorant.

The mixed theorem is stated for the counting-times-Lebesgue density.  Completing the joint Gaussian square yields the conditional mean and Schur covariance.  Exact continuous conditioning is obtained from that density by regular coarea disintegration, so exact particle number, momentum, and energy are not conflated with a fixed-width window.

## B4: containment must not modify the microscopic rate

### Defect

An auxiliary high-velocity quantity had been placed additively in the contracted action.  That would define a different rate function and a different Hamiltonian rather than prove compactness for the microscopic relative-entropy action supplied by B2.

### Repair

B4 keeps

```text
A_T(f,g) = H(f_0 | f_ref) + int ell(g) dA_f
```

as the only dynamic rate.  Truncated superquadratic moment increments are controlled by the entropy Young inequality and the B2 exponential collision-source estimate.  After time localization and monotone convergence this gives `B4-ACTION-COERCIVE-CONTAINMENT`: the original action controls the superquadratic moment and its nonnegative Povzner dissipation.

The contracted rate is again `I_T(f)=inf_g A_T(f,g)`.  The Nisio cost and Hamiltonian contain no artificial moment penalty.  Strong collision products still pass through the positive strong core and the balance-preserving recovery compiler.

## C2: platform states are Polish, not generally locally compact

### Defect

Path, probability-law, and belief spaces used by A3, B4, and C1 are generally infinite-dimensional Polish spaces.  Compact action sublevels do not make the entire platform state locally compact.  A strict-dual theorem written only for locally compact `X` therefore did not match the exported interfaces.

### Repair

C2 now uses the mixed strict topology on `C_b(X)` for an arbitrary Polish `X`, generated by compact sequences and coefficients tending to zero.  Pullback under `f -> f/w` defines the weighted topology.  Its dual is the weighted finite signed Radon measures; equicontinuity is bounded weighted mass plus uniform weighted tightness.

This formulation uses exactly the compact-containment information proved by A3/B4/C1 and does not infer local compactness of the full state.  All kinetic, history, belief, optional-projection, and rigidity arguments are restated on this Polish weighted dual.

## Permanent gates added

The Round-Twenty-Three verifier is updated to reject:

- a merely `|b|^{-2}` two-dimensional A2 tail;
- the obsolete B1 high-frequency factor approaching one;
- continuous “exact conditioning” without a density/coarea interface;
- any B4 contracted rate containing an added containment penalty; and
- a C2 strict-dual theorem restricted to locally compact platform states.

The audit does not certify the long proofs externally.  It records that these four newly discovered contradictions were removed from the active revision before the next referee round and that the downstream imports were rechecked against the corrected interfaces.
