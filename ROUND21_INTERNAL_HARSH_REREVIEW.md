## Tests applied

### A1

- **Transitive-closure attack:** the quotient proof was tested against a cycle
  of three face identifications.  The active construction uses the mapping
  torus of one global labelled diffeomorphism, whose equivalence classes have
  at most two cylinder representatives; it does not infer closedness from
  separate face graphs.
- **Distribution restriction attack:** a Dirac mass conormal to a face was
  inserted into the old negative Sobolev claim.  The old restriction fails.
  The active construction never applies it: traces act on positive test jets
  and currents receive only adjoint extensions.
- **Label cancellation attack:** equal ambient distributions with opposite
  labels remain orthogonal dual coordinates unless an explicit internal-face
  relation identifies them.
- **Fixed-fibre attack:** the closability proof now pairs at one parameter with
  a bounded test connection; it does not integrate an unprovided family over
  a parameter interval.
- **FCLT attack:** covariance trace norms and the projective differences both
  have an explicit geometric tail `(d0 beta)^(n/2)`.

- **Rapid derivative-growth attack:** the raw tail uses one integration in each
  of two fixed regular blocks.  No integer `M` is chosen after an unknown
  constant `c_M`.
- **Exceptional-word attack:** words with fewer than two regular blocks are

  assigned imaginary universal coordinates.
- **Dimension attack:** Fourier inversion is on three lattice coordinates and
  one continuous roof coordinate, giving `n^-2`.
