## New mathematical results

For every quadratic pencil in dimension n>=3, its intrinsic multiplication-failure neighbourhood at order d=n^2+2n-4 recovers the pencil up to projective equivalence. Every lower-order neighbourhood is independent of it. The nilradical multiplication kernel at the first relation degree reconstructs the entire normal-cone algebra.

In fixed tensor coordinates the first relation spaces give a closed immersion of the full pencil Grassmannian, including singular pencils and arbitrary nonreduced complex bases. The resulting finite normal algebras form a finite locally free family. For hyperelliptic curve classes of genus g>=2, the exact detection order is 4g^2+12g+4.

The intrinsic theorem concerns a neighbourhood of the entire projective socle Grassmannian; the coefficient-family theorem retains tensor coordinates. Neither is an assertion of equivalence of unmarked moduli stacks. The new section adds to, rather than replaces, all predecessor mathematics.
