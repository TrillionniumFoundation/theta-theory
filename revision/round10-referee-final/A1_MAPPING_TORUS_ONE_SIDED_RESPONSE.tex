\section{Round-ten reconstruction: a genuine mapping-torus impact system and a one-sided response calculus}
\label{sec:r10-a1}

Let \(\mathcal A=\{1,2,3,4\}\), let
\(p_i(a)>0\), \(\sum_i p_i(a)=1\), and assume the branch weights are
\(C^{m+2}\) on the compact parameter interval under consideration.  Put
\(s_i(a)=\sum_{j\le i}p_j(a)\) and
\(I_i(a)=[s_{i-1}(a),s_i(a))\).  On the regular square define
\[
 B_a(q,p)=\left(
 \frac{q-s_{i-1}(a)}{p_i(a)},
 s_{i-1}(a)+p_i(a)p
 \right),\qquad q\in I_i(a).
\]
The seam set and all of its iterates have Lebesgue measure zero.  We never
identify the symbolic natural extension with a symplectic section; it is only
a coding of the ordinary two-dimensional section.

\subsection{The physical section and its two one-sided factors}

Let \(\Sigma^+=\mathcal A^{\mathbb N_0}\) and
\(\Sigma^-=\mathcal A^{\mathbb N}\).  For
\(\omega^+\in\Sigma^+\), \(\omega^-\in\Sigma^-\), define
\[
 q_a(\omega^+)=s_{\omega^+_0-1}(a)
 +p_{\omega^+_0}(a)s_{\omega^+_1-1}(a)
 +p_{\omega^+_0}(a)p_{\omega^+_1}(a)s_{\omega^+_2-1}(a)+\cdots
\]
and the analogous reverse expansion \(p_a(\omega^-)\).  Let
\(\nu_a^+\) and \(\nu_a^-\) be the Bernoulli laws with weights \(p(a)\).

\begin{theorem}[Physical--symbolic factor without a Cantor section]
\label{thm:r10-a1-factor}
Outside the countable union of iterated seams, the map
\[
 \mathfrak c_a:(q,p)\longmapsto(\omega^+(q),\omega^-(p))
\]
is a measurable bijection between the regular physical square and a
full-measure subset of \(\Sigma^+\times\Sigma^-\).  It satisfies
\[
 (\mathfrak c_a)_\#(dq\,dp)=\nu_a^+\otimes\nu_a^-,
 \qquad
 \mathfrak c_a\circ B_a=(\sigma_+\times\sigma_-^{-1})\circ\mathfrak c_a.
\]
In particular Lebesgue measure is invariant for every \(a\), whereas the
Bernoulli law varies because the coding map varies.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For a future cylinder \([i_0\ldots i_r]\), the inverse image under
\(q_a\) is the half-open interval of length
\(p_{i_0}\cdots p_{i_r}\); the reverse construction gives the same formula
for past cylinders.  Product cylinders therefore have Lebesgue measure equal
to their Bernoulli product mass.  Nested cylinder intervals shrink because
\(\max_i p_i<1\) on the compact parameter set.  Ambiguity occurs only at
endpoints, hence on the iterated seam set.  Substitution in the affine branch
formula proves the conjugacy.  The Jacobian of every branch is one, so the
factor identity also proves invariance.
\end{proof}

\subsection{An exact autonomous mapping-torus realization}

Refine each source strip by the four one-step target strips
\[
 C_{ij}(a)=\{(q,p):q\in I_i(a),\ Q_i(q)\in I_j(a)\}.
\]
For every nonempty \(C_{ij}\), take a copy of
\([0,1]_\tau\times C_{ij}\times\mathbb R_E\), with symplectic form
\[
 \Omega=dq\wedge dp+d\tau\wedge dE.
\]
Identify its outgoing face \(\tau=1\) with the incoming face of the unique
copy containing \(B_a(q,p)\), using the exact symplectic map \(B_a\) and the
identity in \(E\).  The quotient is denoted \(\mathcal M_a\).  It is a smooth
symplectic manifold on every open stratum and a finite branched symplectic
manifold with corners along the physical seam strata.

\begin{theorem}[Exact autonomous Hamiltonian suspension]
\label{thm:r10-a1-suspension}
The autonomous Hamiltonian
\[
 H_a(q,p,\tau,E)=E
\]
on \((\mathcal M_a,\Omega)\) has unit return time to the physical incoming
section \(\{\tau=0\}\).  Its first-return map is exactly \(B_a\) on the
regular square.  Liouville measure induces \(dq\,dp\) on the section.  No
branch-dependent reset is applied after the Hamiltonian evolution: the branch
map is part of the symplectic mapping-torus gluing.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Hamilton's equations are \(\dot\tau=1\) and
\(\dot q=\dot p=\dot E=0\) inside every cylinder.  At \(\tau=1\), the quotient
identification sends the point to the incoming representative
\((B_a(q,p),0,E)\).  Since the gluing map preserves \(dq\wedge dp\), the
forms agree on overlaps and define \(\Omega\) on the quotient strata.  Thus
the orbit is a genuine Hamiltonian orbit on \(\mathcal M_a\), not a flow
followed by an external reset.  The return and Liouville assertions are
immediate.  Seam strata have zero Liouville measure, and their oriented
branched completion gives the unique one-sided continuation required for
boundary tests.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Two one-sided spectral gaps}

For \(0<\theta<1\), let \(\mathscr H_\theta^+\) be the Banach space of bounded
future functions with norm
\[
 \|F\|_\theta=\|F\|_\infty+
 \sup_{n\ge0}\theta^{-n}\operatorname{var}_n(F),
\]
and define \(\mathscr H_\theta^-\) analogously on past sequences.  Define
\[
 (P_a^+F)(x)=\sum_{i=1}^4p_i(a)F(ix),
 \qquad
 (P_a^-G)(y)=\sum_{i=1}^4p_i(a)G(iy).
\]

\begin{lemma}[Correct one-sided Lasota--Yorke estimate]
\label{lem:r10-a1-one-sided}
For either sign,
\[
 \operatorname{var}_n(P_a^\pm F)
 \le \operatorname{var}_{n+1}(F),
 \qquad
 \|(P_a^\pm)^kQ_a^\pm F\|_\theta
 \le C\theta^k\|F\|_\theta,
\]
where \(Q_a^\pm F=F-\nu_a^\pm(F)\).  The constants and all parameter
derivatives through order \(m+1\) are uniform on the compact parameter set.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Prepending one symbol moves every first disagreement one coordinate farther
from the origin, which proves the variation estimate.  Coupling two copies
with the same newly prepended symbols gives contraction by \(\theta^k\) of
the oscillation after \(k\) steps.  The constant part is removed by \(Q_a\).
Parameter derivatives differentiate only the finite weights and therefore
obey the same estimate with a finite combinatorial constant.  This argument
never applies a bilateral inverse shift to a symmetric depth norm, so the
translated-cylinder counterexample does not arise.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Reduced one-sided resolvents]
\label{cor:r10-a1-resolvent}
The resolvents
\[
 S_a^\pm=(I-P_a^\pm)^{-1}Q_a^\pm
 =\sum_{n\ge0}(P_a^\pm)^nQ_a^\pm
\]
are bounded and \(C^{m+1}\) as operators on
\(\mathscr H_\theta^\pm\).  Finite products of their derivatives are bounded
with the corresponding geometric-series constants.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
This is the norm-convergent geometric series supplied by
Lemma~\ref{lem:r10-a1-one-sided}; differentiation is justified by uniform
normal convergence.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A typed cut-current calculus}

For a finite future/past cylinder rectangle \(R_w(a)\), define its order-zero
functional by integration over the physical rectangle.  Its material
derivative is defined by the exact one-dimensional Hadamard formula on each
moving endpoint.  At order \(k\), iterate the formula on the finite
stratification generated by the endpoints appearing in the word \(w\).
Corner terms are evaluations of traces, never products of unrelated
distributions.  Let \(\mathscr C_{\theta}^{r}\) be the completion of cylinder
functions for the norm
\[
 \|F\|_{\mathscr C_\theta^r}
 =\sum_w\theta^{|w|}
 \sum_{j\le r}\|D_a^jF_w\|_{C^1(R_w)}.
\]

\begin{lemma}[Bounded material current insertions]
\label{lem:r10-a1-currents}
For every \(k\le m\), the \(k\)-th material derivative of physical cylinder
integration extends to a bounded multilinear map
\[
 \mathfrak D_a^k:
 \mathscr C_\theta^{k+1}\longrightarrow\mathbb R.
\]
The definition is independent of cylinder refinement, and the sum over all
future and past cut depths converges absolutely.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
At fixed word length there are finitely many endpoint and corner strata.
Hadamard's formula gives a sum of bulk derivatives and oriented traces, with
coefficients made from derivatives of the endpoints \(s_i(a)\).  If a
rectangle is refined, internal faces occur twice with opposite orientations
and cancel.  The number of strata grows at most exponentially with word
length, while the declared \(\theta^{|w|}\) coefficient is chosen smaller than
the inverse growth constant.  Hence the refinement-compatible series is
absolutely summable.  Since no multiplication of currents is used, all
higher-order intersections are ordinary traces on the finite polyhedral
stratification.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite-order physical response for transported observables]
\label{thm:r10-a1-response}
Let \(F_a\in\mathscr C_\theta^{m+1}\) be a physical observable transported by
the coding convention of Theorem~\ref{thm:r10-a1-factor}.  Then
\(a\mapsto\int F_a\,dq\,dp\) is \(C^m\).  Every derivative is the sum of:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item the material bulk and cut-current terms of
      Lemma~\ref{lem:r10-a1-currents}; and
\item absolutely convergent future and past response words built from
      \(D_a^jP_a^\pm\) and \(S_a^\pm\).
\end{enumerate}
The formula equals the derivative obtained from the common-root quantile
coupling of the Bernoulli coordinates.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Pull the integral to \(\Sigma^+\times\Sigma^-\).  Differentiate finite
cylinders directly.  Weight derivatives give the usual centered score terms;
movement of the coding rectangles gives the Hadamard currents.  Conditional
expectations of the score tails solve the two one-sided Poisson equations,
producing the resolvent words.  Lemma~\ref{lem:r10-a1-one-sided} and
Lemma~\ref{lem:r10-a1-currents} give a common summable majorant, so cylinder
truncation may be removed and derivatives may be iterated.  In the common-root
coupling, crossing a quantile threshold produces exactly the same oriented
endpoint term, while differentiation away from thresholds produces the bulk
term.  Hence the two formulas coincide.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Full-port work and conditional calibration}

On every channel cylinder put the closed work one-form
\(\eta=c_i\,d\tau\).  Its integral over one return is \(c_i\), including the
whole incoming port.

\begin{theorem}[Mechanical work and conditional coefficient]
\label{thm:r10-a1-work}
The accumulated work over \(n\) returns is the symbolic additive current
\(A_n=\sum_{j<n}c_{\omega_j}\).  Its canonical tilt is the Bernoulli member
with weights proportional to \(p_i(a_0)e^{\vartheta c_i}\).  If a
cash-additive, time-consistent nonlinear valuation is required to use this
same mechanical Radon--Nikodym cocycle, its entropic coefficient equals the
mechanical conjugate field.  Without that compatibility axiom the valuation
coefficient remains external.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The work identity follows by integrating \(c_i\,d\tau\) from zero to one.
The path likelihood factorizes over returns, giving the tilted branch weights.
An entropic valuation has likelihood exponent equal to its coefficient times
\(A_n\).  Equality with the mechanical likelihood for a nonconstant current
forces equality of coefficients.  No preference axiom alone is used to infer
the mechanical field.
\end{proof}
