\subsection{Game III: block-normalized canonical law control}

An adaptive law control changes the canonical weight of deterministic paths;
it does not turn actual hard-sphere contacts into a Poisson random measure.
Let \(q^{u,v}\) be a compact family of positive bounded collision multipliers,
with
\[
 0<q_-\le q^{u,v}\le q_+<\infty,
\]
and with the exchange and pre/post symmetries.  Controls are predictable and
piecewise constant on a partition
\(0=t_0<\cdots<t_m=T\).

For the complete finite-volume correlation history \(\mathcal F_{t_j}\),
define the exact conditional block normalizer
\[
 K_{\varepsilon,j}(\psi)
 =\frac1{\mu_\varepsilon}
 \log\mathbb E\left[
 
 exp\left\{
 \mu_\varepsilon\langle\psi,
 \Gamma_{(t_j,t_{j+1}]}^\varepsilon\rangle
 \right\}\middle|\mathcal F_{t_j}\right].
\]
The controlled block density increment is
\[
 Z_{j+1}^{\varepsilon}/Z_j^{\varepsilon}
 =\exp\left\{
 \mu_\varepsilon
 \langle\log q^{u_j,v_j},
 \Gamma_{(t_j,t_{j+1}]}^\varepsilon\rangle
 -\mu_\varepsilon
 K_{\varepsilon,j}(\log q^{u_j,v_j})
 \right\}.
\]
By construction, \((Z_j^\varepsilon)\) is a positive mean-one martingale.
The hard-sphere trajectory and elastic reflection rule are unchanged.

\begin{theorem}[Adaptive canonical-law game]
\label{thm:r3-c1-adaptive}
For nonanticipative Elliott--Kalton strategies and an Isaacs family of compact
controls, the finite block-normalized games converge, as first
\(\varepsilon\to0\) and then the mesh tends to zero, to the unique bounded
viscosity solution of
\[
 \partial_tU+\sup_u\inf_v
 \mathbb H^{u,v}\left(f,\frac{\delta U}{\delta f}\right)=0,
\]
where
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathbb H^{u,v}(f,p)
 ={}&\langle f,v\cdot\nabla_xp\rangle\\
 &+\int q^{u,v}
 \left(e^{\Delta_\omega p}-1\right)dA_f
 +c(f,u,v).
 \end{aligned}
\]
The controlled dynamic action is
\[
 \int
 \ell\left(\frac{d\Gamma}{q^{u,v}dA_f}\right)
 q^{u,v}dA_f.
\]
No stochastic compensator is postulated at finite \(\varepsilon\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Each block density is exactly normalized by conditional expectation, so its
product defines a probability law for the deterministic paths under every
strategy.  B2 bounded-source continuation applies uniformly because
\(\log q^{u,v}\) ranges over a compact source set.  Its conditional pressure
increment converges to
\[
 \mathbb H(f,p,\log q^{u,v})-
 \mathbb H(f,0,\log q^{u,v})
 =\langle f,v\cdot\nabla p\rangle
 +\int q^{u,v}(e^{\Delta p}-1)dA_f.
\]
Thus the block dynamic-programming recursion is consistent with the displayed
Hamiltonian.  The B2 joint LDP under the normalized block tilt has reference
collision intensity \(q^{u,v}A_f\); pointwise exponential/entropy duality
gives the action.  Uniform compact containment, continuity on B4 moment
balls, and the B4 doubled-variable comparison argument pass the mesh to zero.
Isaacs equality identifies the upper and lower values.
\end{proof}
