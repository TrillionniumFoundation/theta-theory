\section{Round-twelve positive closure: exact trace graphs, summed Jacobi transversality, and global marked LDP}
\label{sec:r12-b2}

The first-surplus estimate is derived from the actual linearized hard-sphere
collision map.  No rigid subtree translation, QR reset, or genealogy-uniform
\L{}ojasiewicz exponent is used.  Fixed-genealogy transversality is summed by
dominated convergence against the factorial cluster weights.

\subsection{The space--time trace graph}

For every hierarchy level let
$\mathcal J_k=(g_k,V_kg_k)$ be the space--time divergence-measure current on
$[0,T]\times\Omega_k^\varepsilon$.  Its normal trace on a collision face is
the signed flux measure $\gamma_{k,ij}$ itself.

\begin{theorem}[Closed compatible trace generator]
\label{thm:r12-b2-trace}
The graph consisting of $(g_k,\operatorname{div}\mathcal J_k,
\gamma_{k,ij})_{k\ge0}$, with incoming/outgoing traces related by elastic
reflection, is closed in the weighted weak topology.  For every graph test
$\phi$,
\[
 \int\phi\,d(\operatorname{div}\mathcal J_k)
 +\int\mathcal J_k\cdot d(D\phi)
 =\sum_{i<j}\int_{\Gamma_{ij}}\phi\,d\gamma_{k,ij}
   +\text{time-endpoint traces}.
\]
The triangular free-flight/creation operator generates an Ovsyannikov
evolution on radii $\alpha(t)=\alpha_0-Ct$ and preserves the trace graph.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The Gauss--Green theorem for divergence-measure fields defines the normal
trace as one measure, so no second velocity factor occurs.  Reflection is a
measure-preserving involution on incoming and outgoing faces.  Uniform total
variation and divergence bounds give weak compactness; testing the Green
identity and reflection relation passes them to the limit, including endpoint
traces.  Collision-face intersections have zero flux under the weighted graph
norm and are included through their oriented iterated traces.  The creation
operator loses $Cdt$ of label radius on a time interval $dt$; Picard iteration
on the decreasing radius scale gives the stated evolution and graph
preservation.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The actual Jacobi map}

Fix a connected creation tree and its first surplus contact.  Cut the tree at
the youngest edge separating the two surplus labels.  Use two chronological
root variables before that edge and solve the creation constraints by the
implicit function theorem.  Let $J_\lambda$ be the resulting two-dimensional
transverse derivative of the surplus relative position.  It includes normal
variation, reflected-velocity derivatives, flight-time derivatives, and every
intermediate collision.

\begin{lemma}[Fixed-genealogy physical transversality]
\label{lem:r12-b2-fixed}
For every fixed regular genealogy $\lambda$, $\det J_\lambda$ is a nonzero
real-analytic function of the chronological variables off grazing and
multiple-collision strata.  Consequently there are $\beta_\lambda>0$ and
$C_\lambda<\infty$ such that
\[
 \mathfrak m_\lambda\{s_{\min}(J_\lambda)<\rho\}
 \le C_\lambda\rho^{\beta_\lambda}
\]
after integration with the actual incoming flux weights.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Differentiate free flight and the reflection law
$v'=v-((v-v_*)\cdot\omega)\omega$ without freezing $\omega$.  The resulting
Jacobi recursion is analytic on every homogeneous chronology.  To show that a
minor is not identically zero, choose the two root positions so that the two
subtrees approach the youngest connecting collision with transverse relative
translations.  The implicit creation equations have full rank there, and the
symplectic collision derivative sends the two translations to independent
transverse Jacobi fields at the surplus time.  Analytic sublevel estimates on
the compact homogeneous chart, followed by integration of the local inverse
cosine against incoming flux, give the claim.  The constants may depend on the
fixed genealogy; no uniform exponent is asserted.
\end{proof}

Let $a_m(T)$ be the total absolute cluster weight of all $m$-label connected
genealogies.  The tree estimate gives
$\sum_m a_m(T)<\infty$ for $T\le T_*$.

\begin{theorem}[Summed first-surplus modulus]
\label{thm:r12-b2-summed}
There is a deterministic modulus $\omega_T(\varepsilon)\downarrow0$ such that
the total absolute weight of every connected cluster containing a surplus
contact is at most
\[
 h\,\omega_T(\varepsilon)
\]
on a time block of length $h$.  The estimate is uniform on every bounded
particle/contact source ball and after every fixed number of source
derivatives.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For a fixed genealogy, coarea on the two transverse variables and
Lemma~\ref{lem:r12-b2-fixed} show that its surplus-contact weight tends to zero
as $\varepsilon\to0$.  It is bounded by the ordinary absolute cluster weight.
Summing genealogies and using $\sum_m a_m(T)<\infty$, dominated convergence
gives a modulus independent of genealogy depth.  The first surplus time is
integrated over the block, yielding the factor $h$.  Bounded source marks only
change the summable majorant.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Source sewing and grand-canonical pressure}

\begin{theorem}[Partition-stable marked source sewing]
\label{thm:r12-b2-sewing}
For every partition $\pi$ of $[0,T]$ with mesh $|\pi|$ and every bounded
predictable source, the block product differs from the exact marked
trajectory evolution by
\[
 C_T\bigl(\omega_T(\varepsilon)+|\pi|\bigr).
\]
The analytic radius at time $t$ is $\alpha_0-Ct$, independent of the number of
blocks.  Two arbitrary partition sequences with mesh tending to zero have the
same limit.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The noncyclic boundary error on a block is $O(h^2)$ and the cyclic sector is
$h\omega_T(\varepsilon)$ by Theorem~\ref{thm:r12-b2-summed}.  Summing blocks
gives $T\omega_T(\varepsilon)+CT|\pi|$.  The generator estimate loses $Ch$ of
label radius, so losses add to $CT$ rather than one fixed amount per block.
For two partitions use their union; its mesh is the larger of the two meshes
and satisfies the same estimate.  This proves partition independence.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[All-contact grand-canonical pressure]
\label{thm:r12-b2-gc}
For $T\le T_*$ the exact hard-sphere density and actual-contact fields have a
normally convergent marked real-trajectory pressure on every bounded complex
source ball.  Its limit is the Hamilton--Boltzmann pressure with collision
factor $e^{\Delta p+\psi}-1$.  All fixed source derivatives converge, and the
surplus-contact sector vanishes with modulus $\omega_T(\varepsilon)$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Creation trees are absolutely summable by the time simplex and label weights.
Theorem~\ref{thm:r12-b2-summed} removes all cyclic genealogies after summation,
while Theorem~\ref{thm:r12-b2-sewing} gives the source-uniform chronological
limit.  Collision coarea on the remaining trees produces the Boltzmann
collision factor.  Normal convergence justifies all fixed source derivatives.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Balance-preserving recovery}

Let $M$ be a positive Maxwellian reference.  Linearize its collision operator
as $\mathcal L_M$ on the invariant-orthogonal subspace.

\begin{lemma}[Continuous kinetic Hodge repair]
\label{lem:r12-b2-repair}
For every smooth compactly supported balance defect $d$ orthogonal to mass,
momentum, and energy, there is a pair $(u,\Xi)$ satisfying
\[
 (\partial_t+v\cdot\nabla_x)u-\Delta^*\Xi=d,
 \qquad
 \|u\|_X+\|\Xi\|_Y\le C\|d\|_Z,
\]
with zero prescribed endpoint moments, pre/post symmetry, and
$\Xi\ll A_M$.  The right inverse is continuous under velocity truncation and
space--time smoothing.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Solve the nonautonomous linear Boltzmann equation
$(\partial_t+v\cdot\nabla_x-\mathcal L_M)u=d$ by Duhamel on the
invariant-orthogonal subspace, using the classical Maxwellian collision gap
and transport energy estimate.  Represent $\mathcal L_Mu$ by its symmetric
binary-collision flux to define $\Xi$.  Correct the five endpoint moments by
finite smooth homogeneous solutions.  This gives the estimate and all
symmetries.  Since the construction is integral and not cellwise, its norm is
uniform under mesh refinement.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Grand-canonical and microcanonical joint LDP]
\label{thm:r12-b2-ldp}
The density--actual-contact pair satisfies a full good short-time LDP under
the grand-canonical law, with action
\[
 I_0^{\rm gc}(f_0)+\int\ell(d\Gamma/dA_f)\,dA_f
\]
after imposing the exact weak balance.  After the B1 source-dependent shell
transfer, the same dynamic action holds with constrained initial rate
$I_{0,a}$.  Every finite-action pair has a positive smooth tilted recovery.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Theorem~\ref{thm:r12-b2-gc} on increasing bounded real-source balls gives the
upper bound; exponential moment estimates give compact containment.  For a
smooth positive balanced pair use the exact contact tilt
$\psi=\log(d\Gamma/dA_f)-\Delta p$ and the normal pressure derivatives to
obtain concentration and the entropy cost.  Approximate an arbitrary
finite-action pair by truncation and smoothing, add a vanishing Maxwellian
background, and use Lemma~\ref{lem:r12-b2-repair} to restore balance exactly;
its norm and entropy costs vanish.  This proves the lower bound and goodness.
B1 transfers every source and recovery law to the primitive shell, changing
only the initial rate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Round-twelve B2 closure]
\label{thm:r12-b2-main}
Actual hard-sphere contacts admit a closed trace evolution, a source-uniform
all-contact trajectory pressure, and the full joint LDP of
Theorem~\ref{thm:r12-b2-ldp}.  Recollisions are controlled by summed physical
Jacobi transversality, without a false depth-uniform analytic exponent.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Combine Theorems~\ref{thm:r12-b2-trace},
\ref{thm:r12-b2-summed}, \ref{thm:r12-b2-gc}, and
\ref{thm:r12-b2-ldp}.
\end{proof}
