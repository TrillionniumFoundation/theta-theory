\section{Round-thirteen positive closure: the physical impact suspension and the complete flag complex}
\label{sec:r13-a1}

The physical symplectic section and the symbolic natural extension are no
longer identified topologically.  The former is the ordinary square with a
finite piecewise-smooth impact structure; the latter is a zero-dimensional
coding space.  This separation removes the false leaf claim while preserving
an exact mechanical realization, the work cocycle, and the full response
calculus.

\subsection{Physical and symbolic objects}

Let
\[
 Q=\mathbb T^2,\qquad D_i(a)=I_i(a)\times\mathbb T,
 \qquad H_i(a)=\mathbb T\times I_i(a),
\]
and on the regular part of $D_i(a)$ put
\[
 B_{a,i}(q,p)=
 \left({q-s_{i-1}(a)\over p_i(a)},
 s_{i-1}(a)+p_i(a)p\right).
\]
The branch map is exact symplectic and maps $D_i(a)$ bijectively onto
$H_i(a)$.  The current vertical seam set and its forward/backward images are
denoted by $\mathscr S_a$.

Let $\Sigma^+=\mathcal A^{\mathbb N_0}$ and
$\Sigma^-=\mathcal A^{\mathbb N}$.  The natural-extension map is
\[
 \widehat\sigma(x,y)=(\sigma x,x_0y).
\]

\begin{theorem}[Correct physical--symbolic factor]
\label{thm:r13-a1-factor}
There is a measurable coding map
$\mathfrak c_a:Q\setminus\mathscr S_a\to\Sigma^+\times\Sigma^-$ such that
\[
 \mathfrak c_a\circ B_a=\widehat\sigma\circ\mathfrak c_a.
\]
It is one-to-one modulo the complete seam orbit and sends Lebesgue measure to
the Bernoulli natural-extension law with weights $p_i(a)$.  The symbolic
space is used only as a coding space; no tangent bundle or symplectic form is
assigned to it.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The future word records the successive vertical strips.  The past word is
read from the successive horizontal strips of the inverse branches.  After
one application of $B_a$, the used future symbol becomes the newest past
symbol, which is exactly $(x,y)\mapsto(\sigma x,x_0y)$.  A finite two-sided
cylinder pulls back to a rectangle of area equal to the product of its branch
weights.  Monotone convergence over cylinders proves the measure statement.
Only points on an iterated seam have more than one coding germ.
\end{proof}

\subsection{An exact autonomous Hamiltonian impact system}

For each branch let $S_{a,i}$ be the type-two generating function
\[
 S_{a,i}(q,P)=
 {\bigl(q-s_{i-1}(a)\bigr)
  \bigl(P-s_{i-1}(a)\bigr)\over p_i(a)}.
\]
Let $W_i=D_i(a)\times[0,1]_\tau\times\mathbb R_E$ with symplectic form
$dq\wedge dp+d\tau\wedge dE$.  The incoming and outgoing ports are kept as
separate boundary faces.  At the outgoing face the canonical impact relation
is the graph of $B_{a,i}$, which is exact Lagrangian because
$B_{a,i}^*(p\,dq)-p\,dq=dS_{a,i}$.

\begin{theorem}[Autonomous Hamiltonian impact realization]
\label{thm:r13-a1-impact}
There is a finite-dimensional autonomous Hamiltonian impact system
$(\mathcal W_a,\Omega_a,\mathscr H_a)$ with the following properties.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item Between ports the flow is smooth Hamiltonian and satisfies
      $\dot\tau=1$.
\item Every impact is the canonical exact relation generated by $S_{a,i}$;
      no stochastic reset is added.
\item The regular first-return map on the physical section $Q$ is exactly
      $B_a$, with return time one.
\item The Liouville incoming flux of port $i$ is $p_i(a)$.
\end{enumerate}
The impact system is a smooth symplectic manifold with boundary on each
flight chamber and a standard exact symplectic impact relation at the finite
seam set.  No claim of a smooth flow through a discontinuity is made or
needed.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The affine exact map $B_{a,i}$ is the time-one map of a quadratic
Hamiltonian after the two canonical translations encoded by $S_{a,i}$.
Autonomization adds the clock pair $(\tau,E)$ and the Hamiltonian
$E+K_{a,i}(\tau,q,p)$.  The branch chambers are disjoint before impact, and
the outgoing exact Lagrangian graph identifies their flux with the common
physical section.  Standard composition of exact canonical relations gives
a deterministic symplectic impact flow.  Since $\dot\tau=1$, every regular
flight returns after one unit, and the generating equations give precisely
$B_{a,i}$.  The port flux is its $q$-width.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Canonical work family}

Fix nonconstant branch works $c_i$.  Starting from one reference weight vector
$p^0$, define the canonical family
\[
 p_i(\vartheta)=
 {p_i^0e^{\vartheta c_i}\over
  \sum_jp_j^0e^{\vartheta c_j}},
 \qquad
 a(\vartheta)=\sum_i c_i p_i(\vartheta).
\]
On every compact interval on which
$\operatorname{Var}_{p(\vartheta)}c>0$, the map
$\vartheta\mapsto a$ is a diffeomorphism.  The parameter $a$ used below is
this mean-work parametrization.

\begin{proposition}[Exact work holonomy and closure]
\label{prop:r13-a1-work}
Attach to the $i$th chamber the affine work coordinate
$u(1)=u(0)+c_i$.  Its $n$-return holonomy is
$A_n=\sum_{k<n}c_{x_k}$.  Canonical tilting of the reference path law by
$\exp\{\vartheta A_n\}$ gives exactly the member $p(\vartheta)$ of the
same family, and the conjugate field is
$\vartheta=I'(a)$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The affine coordinate adds once at every exact impact, so the holonomy is the
sum of the branch works.  The Radon--Nikodym derivative factorizes into
\[
 \prod_{k<n}{p_{x_k}(\vartheta)\over p_{x_k}^0}
 =\exp\{\vartheta A_n-n\Lambda(\vartheta)\}.
\]
Strict positivity of the work variance gives the Legendre inverse
$\vartheta=I'(a)$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The refinement-independent flag-current complex}

Let $\mathscr P^{m,n}_a$ be the finite physical partition generated by seams
at times $-m,\ldots,n$.  A presentation of a current consists of a smooth
bulk density on every cell and a smooth density on every oriented flag of
codimension $k\le r$.  Two presentations are equivalent when they agree on a
common refinement after cancellation of oppositely oriented interior flags.
For the equivalence class $[U]$ define
\[
 \|[U]\|_{r;\rho_-,\rho_+,\kappa}
 :=\inf_{U'\sim U}
 \sum_{C\in\mathscr P^{m,n}_a}
 \rho_-^{m}\rho_+^{n}
 \sum_{k=0}^{r}\kappa^k
 \sum_{F\in\mathfrak F_k(C)}
 \|U'_{C,F}\|_{C^{r-k}}.
\]
Choose $4\rho_-<1$, $4\rho_+<1$, and $M\kappa<1$, where $M$ bounds the
number of new incident faces created in one refinement.

\begin{theorem}[Complete graded flag complex]
\label{thm:r13-a1-flags}
The Hausdorff completion of the quotient above is a Banach complex
$\mathfrak C_a^r$.  Common refinement, the physical push-forward by $B_a$,
the flag boundary, and parameter transport are bounded on the indicated
levels.  More precisely,
\[
 D_a:\mathfrak C_a^r\longrightarrow\mathfrak C_a^{r-1}
\]

after one derivative, and
$D_a^q:\mathfrak C_a^r\to\mathfrak C_a^{r-q}$ contains exactly the flags of
codimension at most $q$.  The definition is independent of past/future
presentation because the norm is an infimum over all common refinements.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Refining one future or past level creates at most four descendants, while a
codimension-$k$ flag creates at most $M$ incident flags.  The three geometric
weights therefore make every refinement map summable.  The infimum is a
seminorm on the algebraic quotient; a zero-norm class has zero pairing with
every compatible test form and is therefore zero.  This proves Hausdorffness.
The coupled shift changes $(m,n)$ by at most one and hence has bounded norm
with factor depending only on $\rho_-/\rho_+$.  Reynolds' formula on each
polyhedron gives a bulk derivative plus its oriented face current.  Iteration
gives the standard chain of corner and higher flag terms, all already present
in the complex.  Closedness follows by completing the graph of the boundary
operator.
\end{proof}

\subsection{All-order physical response}

A transported observable is part of the theorem data: it is a $C^r$ family
$F_a$ represented by a section of $\mathfrak C_a^r$.  Let $P_a^+$ be the
future Ruelle operator, $P_a^-$ the conditional past operator, and
$J_af(x,y)=f(\sigma x,x_0y)$ the present-symbol coupling.

\begin{theorem}[Typed response and cancellation]
\label{thm:r13-a1-response}
For every fixed $q<r$, the derivative
$\partial_a^q\int_QF_a\,dq\,dp$ exists.  It is the absolutely convergent sum
of Bernoulli score insertions, the reduced one-sided resolvents joined by
$J_a$, and the bulk/flag Hadamard insertions from
Theorem~\ref{thm:r13-a1-flags}.  Mixed future--past cylinders are evaluated
with the projective tensor norm and are not replaced by independent dynamics.
If $F_a$ is one fixed physical function under the identity transport, the
whole sum is zero.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
On a finite two-sided refinement, differentiation is ordinary finite
calculus.  Conditioning successively on future and past gives the two
one-sided Poisson equations, while $J_a$ inserts the common present symbol.
Reynolds' theorem gives all flag terms.  The one-sided spectral gaps sum time
insertions, and the three geometric weights sum the refinement and flag
insertions.  Dominated convergence passes to the completed complex.  For an
identity-transported physical function, Lebesgue measure is independent of
$a$; differentiating its change-of-variables identity proves exact
cancellation.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Round-thirteen A1 closure]
\label{thm:r13-a1-main}
The generalized baker family admits an exact autonomous Hamiltonian impact
realization on the ordinary physical symplectic section, a correctly coupled
symbolic natural extension, a canonically closed work family, and a
refinement-independent all-order flag-current response calculus.  No
zero-dimensional symbolic inverse limit is presented as a symplectic leaf.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Combine Theorems~\ref{thm:r13-a1-factor},
\ref{thm:r13-a1-impact}, \ref{thm:r13-a1-flags}, and
\ref{thm:r13-a1-response}, with Proposition~\ref{prop:r13-a1-work}.
\end{proof}
