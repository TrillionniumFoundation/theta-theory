\section{Round-seven reconstruction: a forward terminal-value corrector, an augmented law state, and bounded-cotangent comparison}
\label{sec:r7-b4}

This section replaces the round-six controlling module.  The corrector solves
a time-dependent terminal-value Poisson equation and uses only the forward
hierarchy semigroup.  The exact state carries the cumulative contact ledger.
The generator theorem is first proved on an analytic law-coordinate algebra;
nonanalytic cylinders are reached by graph-norm approximation on compact
containment sets.  Comparison uses a bounded-gradient Tataru penalty, so the
collision increment never leaves the exponential Hamiltonian core.

\subsection{The augmented exact law state}

Let
\[
 \widehat Z_t^\varepsilon=(Z_t^\varepsilon,A_t^\varepsilon)
\]
where $Z_t^\varepsilon$ is the current hard-sphere microstate and
$A_t^\varepsilon$ is the cumulative marked actual-contact measure up to time
$t$.  The deterministic flow on the augmented state is Markov and invertible
between impacts.  Its exact law is
$\widehat\rho_t^\varepsilon$.  The hierarchy coordinates consist of all
current factorial correlations together with all mixed correlations having
one or more ledger marks.  Thus a density/contact path cylinder is a function
of the present augmented law, not an undeclared past functional.

Let $\mathfrak A_{\alpha,\beta}^{\rm an}$ be the completion of finite
polynomials in those coordinates under the absolute coefficient graph norm
\[
 \|F\|_{\rm gr}=\sum_J|c_J|w_J+
 \sum_J|c_J|\|\mathbb A_\varepsilon M_J\|w_J,
\]
where $\mathbb A_\varepsilon$ is the full augmented BBGKY generator.  The
weights include the factorial label radius and the B2 measure-trace norm.

\begin{lemma}[Analytic law algebra]
\label{lem:r7-b4-algebra}
The space $\mathfrak A_{\alpha,\beta}^{\rm an}$ is a Banach algebra, is stable
under exponentiation of its bounded elements, and is contained in the graph
domain of $\mathbb A_\varepsilon$.  The generator acts termwise and normally
on compact measure-trace balls.  No generic $C_b^3$ cylinder is declared to be
analytic merely because it is smooth.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The product of two hierarchy monomials is their shuffle product.  Factorial
weights and the binomial identity bound the shuffle coefficient by the product
of the two absolute coefficient norms.  The B2 generator raises label and
ledger degree by at most one, and its graph image is already included in the
weight.  Hence products and generator images converge absolutely.  The
exponential series of a bounded algebra element is normally convergent in the
same graph norm.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The forward terminal-value corrector}

Let $A(t)$ be the limiting triangular hierarchy generator along a smooth test
path and $U(t,s)$ its forward evolution for $t\ge s$.  Suppose the uncancelled
order-$j$ defect is $D_j(t)$.  Define
\[
 c_j(t)=-\int_t^T U(s,t)D_j(s)\,ds,
 \qquad c_j(T)=0.
\]
Then
\[
 \partial_t c_j(t)+A(t)c_j(t)=-D_j(t).
\]
The terminal boundary term is part of the perturbed test and is exactly zero.

\begin{theorem}[Full triangular forward corrector]
\label{thm:r7-b4-corrector}
For every bounded analytic cylinder $F$ and every finite horizon, the
triangular family $c_j$ is normally summable in the absolute graph norm and the
perturbed test
\[
 F_\varepsilon(t)=F(t)+\sum_{j\ge1}\mu_\varepsilon^{-j}c_j(t)
\]
belongs to the full augmented generator domain.  Its logarithmic generator
satisfies
\[
 \mathcal H_\varepsilon F_\varepsilon
 =\mathcal H F+o(1)
\]
locally uniformly on measure-trace compact sets.  No negative-time BBGKY
operator and no finite-time pseudo-inverse are used.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Differentiate the Duhamel integral.  Since
$\partial_tU(s,t)=-U(s,t)A(t)$, the identity
$\partial_tc_j+A(t)c_j=-D_j$ follows with no remainder.  The B2 forward
semigroup estimate gives
\[
 \|c_j(t)\|_{\rm gr}
 \le\int_t^Te^{C(s-t)}\|D_j(s)\|_{\rm gr}ds.
\]
Connected coefficient bounds yield
$\|D_j\|_{\rm gr}\le C\rho^j$ with $\rho<\mu_\varepsilon$ on the chosen label
ball, so the series of both $c_j$ and $\mathbb A_\varepsilon c_j$ converges.
Substitution in the logarithmic generator cancels every triangular defect at
the same order.  The remaining B2 consistency and cyclic errors tend to zero.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Approximation of nonanalytic cylinders}

Let $K_M$ be a compact containment set in the weak law topology with bounded
energy and action.  A $C_b^3$ cylinder depends on finitely many bounded
coordinates in a compact box.  Multivariate Bernstein polynomials approximate
it together with first and second derivatives on that box.  Multiply by an
entire cutoff equal to one on the coordinate projection of $K_M$.

\begin{lemma}[Graph-core approximation]
\label{lem:r7-b4-approx}
For every finite-coordinate $C_b^3$ cylinder $F$ there are
$F^{(n)}\in\mathfrak A_{\alpha,\beta}^{\rm an}$ such that, on $K_M$,
\[
 F^{(n)}\to F,
 \qquad DF^{(n)}\to DF,
 \qquad D^2F^{(n)}\to D^2F,
\]
and their collision increments are bounded uniformly in $n$.  The corresponding
Hamiltonians converge uniformly on $K_M$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Bernstein approximation gives uniform convergence of derivatives on the
finite coordinate box.  The entire cutoff prevents growth outside a slightly
larger box while leaving $K_M$ unchanged.  The coordinate tests are chosen
from the bounded collision-increment core, so a uniform derivative bound gives
a uniform bound on every $\Delta p+\psi$.  Dominated convergence applies to the
exponential collision Hamiltonian.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Generator convergence}

Let the kinetic Hamiltonian be
\[
 \mathcal H(f;p,\psi)=
 \langle p,-v\cdot\nabla_xf\rangle
 +\int\left(e^{\Delta p+\psi}-1\right)dA_f.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Perturbed-test generator convergence]
\label{thm:r7-b4-generator}
For every analytic core test there is one exact forward-corrected sequence
satisfying both relaxed upper and lower generator inequalities.  By
Lemma~\ref{lem:r7-b4-approx} the result extends to finite-coordinate $C_b^3$
tests with bounded collision increments.  The convergence is local in the
weak-energy topology and uniform on action compact sets.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Theorem~\ref{thm:r7-b4-corrector} gives equality up to $o(1)$ for analytic
core tests, hence both relaxed inequalities.  Approximate a nonanalytic test on
the compact containment set by Lemma~\ref{lem:r7-b4-approx}, first pass to the
microscopic limit for fixed approximation, and then send the approximation
index to infinity.  Uniform bounded collision increments control the
exponential nonlinearity.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Action compactness}

Let
\[
 \mathcal I(f,\Gamma)=I_0(f_0)+
 \int_0^T\int\ell(d\Gamma/dA_f)dA_fdt.
\]
The topology is ordinary weak convergence of time marginals and contact
measures; energy is a lower-semicontinuous containment function, not part of
the convergence definition.

\begin{lemma}[Compact action sublevels]
\label{lem:r7-b4-compact}
Sets with bounded mass, energy, and $\mathcal I$ are compact in the declared
path topology.  The balance equation is closed there, and concatenation at a
common marginal preserves the action additively.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Energy gives velocity tightness at every time.  The entropy inequality with a
bounded indicator source bounds contact mass on short time intervals by
$C|I|+o(1)$ uniformly on an action sublevel, because $A_f(I)$ is controlled by
mass and energy.  The balance equation then gives equicontinuity for a
countable convergence-determining family of bounded tests.  Prokhorov and
Arzela--Ascoli give compactness.  Lower semicontinuity of perspective entropy
and weak continuity of $A_f$ on energy-tight sets close the balance relation.
Concatenation adds the integrals.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Bounded-gradient comparison}

Choose bounded core coordinates $\phi_j$ and define
\[
 d_B(f,g)=\sum_{j\ge1}2^{-j}
 \vartheta(\langle f-g,\phi_j\rangle),
 \qquad
 \vartheta(r)=\sqrt{1+r^2}-1.
\]
For $\eta>0$ use the Tataru-type penalty
\[
 \Psi_\eta(f,g)=\eta^{-1}\omega(\eta d_B(f,g)),
\]
where $\omega'(r)\le1$ and $\omega(r)=r$ near zero.  Its derivative
coefficients are bounded by $2^{-j}$ independently of $\eta$; the number of
active coordinates grows, but no coefficient blows up.

\begin{theorem}[Comparison in the bounded Hamiltonian core]
\label{thm:r7-b4-comparison}
Bounded upper-semicontinuous subsolutions and bounded lower-semicontinuous
supersolutions of the kinetic Hamilton--Jacobi equation satisfy comparison on
every energy containment set.  Every doubled-variable cotangent generated by
$\Psi_\eta$ has a collision increment bounded uniformly in $\eta$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
At a doubled maximum, differentiate the bounded-gradient penalty.  Since
$|\vartheta'|\le1$ and $|\omega'|\le1$, the source coefficient of $\phi_j$ is
at most $2^{-j}$.  Summability and the bounded increments of $\phi_j$ give a
uniform bound for $\Delta p$.  The Hamiltonian continuity lemma therefore
applies without an $\eta^{-1/2}$ escape.  Send the finite-coordinate cutoff to
infinity, then the containment penalty to zero, and finally $\eta$ to zero.
The standard doubling argument yields the contradiction to a positive maximum.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The nonlinear semigroup and microscopic convergence}

Define the Lax--Oleinik value
\[
 (V_tF)(f)=\sup_{(g,\Gamma):g_0=f}
 \{F(g_t)-\mathcal I_{[0,t]}(g,\Gamma)\}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Kinetic semigroup limit]
\label{thm:r7-b4-semigroup}
The action value is attained, satisfies the semigroup law, and is the unique
viscosity solution generated by $\mathcal H$.  The microscopic augmented-law
log-Laplace semigroups converge locally uniformly on compact containment sets
to $V_t$.  The lower recovery uses the upstream B2 exact projected tilt and
balance-preserving regularization, not any B4 conclusion.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Lemma~\ref{lem:r7-b4-compact} gives attainment and compact concatenation.
Dynamic programming gives the semigroup law.  Comparison from
Theorem~\ref{thm:r7-b4-comparison} gives uniqueness.  The nonlinear-generator
convergence of Theorem~\ref{thm:r7-b4-generator}, compact containment, and the
standard half-relaxed-limit theorem give microscopic convergence.  For the
lower inequality use the B2 normalized finite-dimensional tilt followed by
its exact feasible regularization; this input is already proved upstream.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Round-seven B4 closure]
\label{thm:r7-b4-main}
The exact state contains the contact ledger; the hierarchy corrector solves a
forward terminal-value equation with zero terminal remainder; the infinite
series converges in graph norm; nonanalytic cylinders are approximated on
action compacta; doubled cotangents stay in a uniformly bounded exponential
core; and the nonlinear kinetic semigroup follows without a negative-time
hierarchy or circular recovery argument.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Combine Lemmas~\ref{lem:r7-b4-algebra},
\ref{lem:r7-b4-approx}, and
\ref{lem:r7-b4-compact} with
Theorems~\ref{thm:r7-b4-corrector},
\ref{thm:r7-b4-generator},
\ref{thm:r7-b4-comparison}, and
\ref{thm:r7-b4-semigroup}.
\end{proof}
