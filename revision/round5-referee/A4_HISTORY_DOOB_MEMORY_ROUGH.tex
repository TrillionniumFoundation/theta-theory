\section{Round-five referee closure: history Doob towers, Volterra memory, and anomalous rough limits}
\label{sec:r5-a4}

All finite-scale conditional expectations are taken on the complete resolved
history.  The nonlinear evolution is a genuine two-parameter Doob cocycle,
not a fixed-denominator logarithmic transform.

\subsection{The exact history kernel}

Let
\[
 {\mathsf H}=D(( -\infty,0],E)
\]
be the graph-completed history space and let
\({\mathcal F}^{\mathsf H}_t\) be the sigma-field generated by the shifted
history up to time \(t\).  Disintegration of a prepared invariant path law
\(\nu\) gives a Borel transition kernel
\(P^\nu_{s,t}(h,dh')\).

\begin{theorem}[Typed Ray realization and spectral realization]
\label{thm:r5-a4-history}
For every prepared law, the complete history process has a Polish Ray
realization with a right-continuous Markov kernel \(P^\nu_{s,t}\).  If \(\nu\)
belongs to an exposed A2--A3 spectral phase, the Ray realization is the
weighted Skorokhod history space and
\[
 \|P^\nu_{s,t}F-P^\nu_{s,t}G\|_{\rm Lip,\beta}
 \le Ce^{-\gamma(t-s-m)_+}\|F-G\|_{\rm Lip,\beta}
\]
for cylinders depending on the last \(m\) units of history.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Take the countable cone generated by bounded history cylinders and all their
rational resolvents.  The resolvent identity and positivity make its
evaluation completion a Polish Ray space with the same completed measurable
sets.  Conditional disintegration defines the kernel there and
right-continuity follows from right-continuity of graph-completed paths.  In
an exposed phase, the A2 spectral gap and the A3 terminal connector couple
two futures after the first common magnet return.  The uncoupled probability
is bounded by \(Ce^{-\gamma(t-s-m)_+}\); bounded distortion controls the
Radon--Nikodym density on the coupled part.  This gives the displayed
weighted Lipschitz estimate and identifies the explicit Skorokhod
realization.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Space-time harmonic normalization}

Let \(W_{s,t}\) be an additive bounded history work:
\[
 W_{r,t}=W_{r,s}+W_{s,t}\circ\Theta_s.
\]
Define the Feynman--Kac kernel
\[
 P^W_{s,t}F(h)
 =\mathbb E_h^\nu[e^{W_{s,t}}F(H_t)].
\]
Fix a positive terminal function \(g_t\) and put
\[
 g_s=P^W_{s,t}g_t.
\]
The Doob kernel is
\[
 {\mathsf P}^{W,g}_{s,t}F
 =g_s^{-1}P^W_{s,t}(g_tF).
\]

\begin{proposition}[Doob cocycle and nonlinear tower]
\label{prop:r5-a4-doob}
The kernels \({\mathsf P}^{W,g}_{s,t}\) are Markov and satisfy
\[
 {\mathsf P}^{W,g}_{r,t}
 ={\mathsf P}^{W,g}_{r,s}{\mathsf P}^{W,g}_{s,t}.
\]
Consequently
\[
 {\mathcal E}^{W,g}_{s,t}F
 =\log{\mathsf P}^{W,g}_{s,t}e^F
\]
satisfies the exact nonlinear tower
\[
 {\mathcal E}^{W,g}_{r,t}F
 ={\mathcal E}^{W,g}_{r,s}
   \bigl({\mathcal E}^{W,g}_{s,t}F\bigr).
\]
Its derivative at \(F=0\) is the same Doob kernel:
\[
 D{\mathcal E}^{W,g}_{s,t}(0)F
 ={\mathsf P}^{W,g}_{s,t}F.
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Additivity of \(W\) gives
\(P^W_{r,t}=P^W_{r,s}P^W_{s,t}\), and the harmonic relation gives
\(g_r=P^W_{r,s}g_s\).  Substitution cancels the intermediate factors \(g_s\)
and proves the Markov cocycle.  Applying it to \(e^F\) proves the logarithmic
tower.  Differentiation at zero is legitimate by bounded convergence and
gives the final identity.  No state-independent denominator appears.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Compressed resolvent memory}

Let \(L\) be the generator of the Doob history semigroup on an exposed phase,
let \(P\) be a finite-rank resolved projection, and put \(Q=I-P\).  On the
centered subspace remove the finite-dimensional conserved projector
\(\Pi_0\).  Define
\[
 K(t)=PLQ\,e^{tQLQ}QLP,\qquad
 G(z)=P(z-L)^{-1}P.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Time-domain Volterra memory]
\label{thm:r5-a4-memory}
The corrected A2 spectral packet implies
\[
 \|e^{tQLQ}(I-\Pi_0)\|\le Ce^{-\gamma t}.
\]
Hence \(K\in L^1(e^{\gamma' t}dt)\) for every \(\gamma'<\gamma\), and
\[
 \widehat K(z)
 =zP-PLP-G(z)^{-1}
\]
on the resolved range.  Every resolved observable \(x(t)=Pe^{tL}x_0\)
satisfies the exact equation
\[
 \dot x(t)=PLP\,x(t)+\int_0^tK(t-s)x(s)\,ds
           +PLQe^{tQLQ}Qx_0.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The history kernel in Theorem~\ref{thm:r5-a4-history} is obtained from the
same twisted A2 transfer operator by a bounded Doob conjugacy, so the
spectral gap passes to \(L\).  Finite-rank \(P\) and the removal of
\(\Pi_0\) leave an exponentially stable \(Q\)-semigroup.  Duhamel's formula
for \(Qe^{tL}\), substituted into the derivative of \(Pe^{tL}\), gives the
Volterra equation.  Laplace transformation and the Schur complement of
\(z-L\) give the compressed-resolvent identity.  Exponential stability
justifies every transform and inversion.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Filtration-typed rough tangent}

Let \(A:E\to\mathbb R^d\) be a centered resolved additive functional.  The
enhanced path is
\[
 {\bf X}^{(n)}
 =\left(
 n^{-1/2}S_{\lfloor nt\rfloor}A,\,
 n^{-1}\sum_{i<j\le\lfloor nt\rfloor}
 A_i\otimes A_j
 \right).
\]
Write \(\mathbb A\) for the antisymmetric part of the second level.

\begin{theorem}[Prepared rough limit with area anomaly]
\label{thm:r5-a4-rough}
On the complete-history filtration,
\({\bf X}^{(n)}\) converges to
\[
 \left(B_t,\,
 \int_0^tB_s\otimes\circ dB_s+t\Gamma\right),
\]
where \(B\) is Brownian motion with Green--Kubo covariance and
\[
 \Gamma
 =\frac12\sum_{k\in\mathbb Z}
 \mathbb E_\nu[A_0\otimes A_k-A_k\otimes A_0]
\]
is an absolutely convergent antisymmetric matrix.  For a smooth driven
equation \(dY=V(Y)\,d{\bf X}\), the limiting drift contains
\[
 \sum_{i<j}\Gamma_{ij}[V_i,V_j].
\]
Under the Doob law of Proposition~\ref{prop:r5-a4-doob}, the covariance and
\(\Gamma\) are obtained from the same tilted history kernel.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The exponential history coupling gives summable correlations and a
Gordin decomposition \(A=M+G-G\circ\Theta\) in the complete-history
filtration.  The martingale rough invariance principle gives the Brownian
Stratonovich lift.  The coboundary terms telescope at first level but their
ordered cross-products converge to the absolutely summable antisymmetric
series displayed above.  The rough-path continuity theorem then inserts
\(\Gamma\) through the Lie brackets.  Doob conjugacy preserves the argument
and merely changes the stationary law and correlation sums.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Pressure and memory differentiation}

For a bounded source \(J\), let \(L_J\) be the Doob generator and let
\(K_J(t)\) be its compressed memory kernel.

\begin{corollary}[History-pressure/memory commutation]
\label{cor:r5-a4-commute}
On a common complex source neighbourhood,
\[
 D_J\widehat K_J(z)[H]
 =
 -P(z-L_J)^{-1}(D_JL_J[H])
   (z-L_J)^{-1}P
\]
inserted into the Schur-complement formula.  Equivalently, differentiating
the nonlinear history pressure first and then taking the Laplace transform
gives the same operator as differentiating the time-domain memory kernel.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Theorem~\ref{thm:r5-a4-memory} gives a common exponentially integrable
majorant.  The A2 complex spectral neighbourhood gives analytic dependence
of the resolvent and Doob eigenfunction.  The resolvent identity yields the
displayed Duhamel insertion, while dominated convergence moves the derivative
through the Laplace integral.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Round-five A4 closure]
\label{thm:r5-a4-main}
The exact finite-scale state is complete history; the nonlinear value is a
genuine Doob cocycle; compressed memory has an exponentially integrable
time-domain kernel; and the rough tangent includes the deterministic area
anomaly.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Combine Theorem~\ref{thm:r5-a4-history},
Proposition~\ref{prop:r5-a4-doob},
Theorems~\ref{thm:r5-a4-memory} and \ref{thm:r5-a4-rough}, and
Corollary~\ref{cor:r5-a4-commute}.
\end{proof}
