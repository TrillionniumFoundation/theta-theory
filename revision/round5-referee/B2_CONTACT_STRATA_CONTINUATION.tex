\section{Round-five referee closure: contact-coordinate strata, all-contact cumulants, and finite-source continuation}
\label{sec:r5-b2}

No collision is deleted from a deterministic trajectory.  Recollision
smallness is obtained by integrating the full trajectory on contact-coordinate
charts and exposing one transverse surplus constraint.  Later contacts remain
in the same trajectory and are summed by an ordered factorial-moment
inequality.

\subsection{Contact-coordinate charts}

A labelled hard-sphere pseudo-trajectory with \(k\) labels is described by
root positions and velocities, \(k-1\) creation contacts, and all later actual
contacts.  For every chronological combinatorial type \({\mathfrak a}\), let
\[
 \Xi_{\mathfrak a}
 =(x_{\rm root},v_{\rm root},t_1,\omega_1,\ldots,t_m,\omega_m)
\]
be the contact-coordinate chart obtained by collision coarea at every regular
binary contact.  Grazing, simultaneous, and multiple contacts are excluded
from the regular chart and placed in the singular strata.

\begin{lemma}[Regular contact atlas and singular codimension]
\label{lem:r5-b2-atlas}
For fixed \(k,m\) and an energy cutoff \({\mathbb V}\), the trajectory space
is the disjoint union, up to Liouville-null sets, of finitely many regular
contact charts and singular strata.  On each regular chart the hard-sphere
flow, all contact times, and post-collisional velocities are \(C^1\), and the
coarea density is
\[
 \prod_{\ell=1}^m
 ((v_{i_\ell}-v_{j_\ell})\cdot\omega_\ell)_+.
\]
The \(\delta\)-neighbourhood of the singular strata has weighted measure at
most
\[
 C^{k+m}(1+{\mathbb V})^{C(k+m)}\delta^\beta
\]
for one dimension-dependent \(\beta>0\).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Between contacts the flow is affine.  At a non-grazing binary contact the
implicit equation
\[
 |x_i(t)-x_j(t)|^2=\varepsilon^2
\]
has time derivative
\(2\varepsilon(v_i-v_j)\cdot\omega\ne0\), so the implicit-function theorem
gives a \(C^1\) contact time and the specular map is smooth.  Iterating gives
the regular charts and the product coarea density.  Grazing imposes one
additional scalar equation, while a simultaneous independent contact imposes
at least two.  Angular and time variables are smooth on the energy cutoff;
Fubini and the elementary sublevel estimate for a nonzero affine contact
derivative give a \(\delta^\beta\) bound.  The number of chronological
label/contact types is at most \(C^{k+m}(k+m)!\), which is absorbed by the
ordered time simplex in the estimates below.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The pivotal surplus constraint}

For a connected trajectory, mark the first chronological contact whose
endpoints are already connected by earlier contacts.  This is the first
surplus contact.  Keep the complete trajectory, including that contact and
every later collision.  In a regular chart, expose the last tree-contact
normal or root-relative translation that lies on the unique ancestral path
between the two endpoints and has not been fixed by earlier coarea equations;
call it \(\zeta\).

\begin{theorem}[Pivotal contact-coordinate estimate]
\label{thm:r5-b2-pivot}
There are \(\alpha>0\) and \(C<\infty\) such that, conditionally on all chart
variables except \(\zeta\), the set of \(\zeta\) producing the marked surplus
contact has measure at most
\[
 C^{k+m}(1+T+{\mathbb V})^{C(k+m)}
 \varepsilon^\alpha
\]
relative to the tree-contact measure.  The bound includes the singular
strata and is uniform in the chronological type.  It does not identify the
trajectory with a collision-deleted future.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
On a regular chart, immediately before the surplus contact the relative
position has the form
\[
 r(\zeta)=r_0+A\zeta+{\mathcal R}(\zeta),
\]
where \(A\) is the derivative transmitted along the two ancestral branches.
Choose \(\zeta\) at the last branch separation.  Before the branches meet
again, a rigid translation of one ancestral component changes their relative
position with unit transverse derivative; subsequent specular derivatives
are orthogonal reflections plus rank-one flight terms.  Hence either a
two-dimensional minor of \(A\) is at least \(\delta\), or the chart lies in
the \(\delta\)-neighbourhood of the singular/degenerate set of
Lemma~\ref{lem:r5-b2-atlas}.  In the first case,
\(|r(\zeta)|=\varepsilon\) confines \(\zeta\) to a tube of relative measure
\(C\varepsilon/\delta\).  In the second case the measure is
\(C\delta^\beta\).  Taking
\(\delta=\varepsilon^{1/(1+\beta)}\) gives
\(\alpha=\beta/(1+\beta)\).  The polynomial chart constants and the finite
number of transmitted derivatives give the displayed
\(C^{k+m}(1+T+{\mathbb V})^{C(k+m)}\) factor.  Since no collision is removed,
all later velocities and contacts are the actual deterministic ones.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Ordered all-contact generating inequality}

Let \(M(\lambda)\) be the number of actual contacts in a connected labelled
trajectory cluster and let \(d{\mathfrak m}_{k}^{\varepsilon}\) be its signed
connected activity.  For a particle source \(h\) and contact source \(\psi\),
put
\[
 {\mathcal W}_{h,\psi}(\lambda)
 =\exp\left\{\sum_i h(z_i(\cdot))
             +\sum_{c\in{\mathcal C}(\lambda)}\psi(c)\right\}.
\]

\begin{lemma}[Factorial contact recursion]
\label{lem:r5-b2-factorial}
For every finite source radius \(R\), there are \(T_R>0\) and \(C_R\) such
that
\[
 \sum_{m\ge0}\frac{u^m}{m!}
 \int 1_{\{M(\lambda)=m\}}
 |d{\mathfrak m}_{k}^{\varepsilon}(\lambda)|
 |{\mathcal W}_{h,\psi}(\lambda)|
 \le
 k!C_R^k(T+\varepsilon)^{k-1}
 e^{C_RT e^u}
\]
for \(\|h\|+\|\psi\|\le R\), \(T\le T_R\), and \(u\) in a fixed complex
neighbourhood of the origin.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Order contacts by time.  A creation contact introduces one new label; its
coarea integral is bounded by \(C_Re^R\,dt\) after using energy conservation
and the Gaussian activity.  A contact between existing labels inserts the
same boundary-flux operator but no new label.  Conditioning on the complete
history before that contact and integrating its time and normal gives another
factor \(C_Re^u\,dt\).  The ordered \(m\)-time simplex has volume \(T^m/m!\).
Tree summation contributes \(k!C_R^k(T+\varepsilon)^{k-1}\).  Iterating the
conditional boundary-flux inequality gives the exponential in
\(C_RT e^u\).  Every chronological contact appears once because the chart
uses its actual ordered time and unordered particle pair.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[All-contact connected cumulant theorem]
\label{thm:r5-b2-cumulant}
Under the conditions of Lemma~\ref{lem:r5-b2-factorial}, the connected
trajectory expansion is normally convergent on a complex source
neighbourhood.  The total sector containing at least one surplus contact is
bounded by
\[
 \varepsilon^\alpha
 k!C_R^k(T+\varepsilon)^{k-1}e^{C_RT e^u}.
\]
The estimate remains valid after any fixed number of particle or actual-contact
source derivatives.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
In each cyclic chart mark its first surplus contact and apply
Theorem~\ref{thm:r5-b2-pivot}.  The polynomial chart factor is absorbed by
shrinking \(T_R\) and increasing \(C_R\); singular strata are already included
in the same \(\varepsilon^\alpha\) bound.  All later contacts are left in the
trajectory and summed by Lemma~\ref{lem:r5-b2-factorial}.  Thus only one
geometric gain is claimed for the whole cyclic sector.  Normal convergence
follows after summing \(k\), and Cauchy's formula permits fixed source
derivatives.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Continuation to arbitrary finite sources}

Let \(R<\infty\).  The local time \(T_R\) may decrease with \(R\), but a fixed
macroscopic horizon \(T\) is covered by
\(m_R=\lceil T/T_R\rceil\) blocks.  The exact state passed between blocks is
the complete labelled correlation hierarchy; no one-particle Markov closure
is used.

\begin{theorem}[Finite-source block continuation]
\label{thm:r5-b2-continuation}
For every finite \(R\) and finite \(T\), the joint particle/actual-contact
log-Laplace functional is analytic on the source ball of radius \(R\).
It is obtained by composing \(m_R\) short-block hierarchy kernels, and its
derivatives are independent of the chosen partition.  The cyclic error is
\(O(m_R\varepsilon^\alpha)\).  Consequently the limiting pressure is defined
on the union of all finite source balls.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
On each block Theorem~\ref{thm:r5-b2-cumulant} gives a normally convergent
kernel on the complete correlation hierarchy with uniform moment loss.
The endpoint hierarchy of one block is the exact initial hierarchy of the
next.  Composition is therefore an identity at finite \(\varepsilon\).
Associativity shows partition independence.  The product of finitely many
normal families is normal on a slightly smaller complex ball; subdividing
once more restores the original radius by analytic continuation along the
real segment from zero to the source.  A cycle belongs to a first block in
which it closes, so the union bound gives \(m_R\varepsilon^\alpha\).
Overlapping source balls agree by uniqueness of analytic continuation.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Joint LDP and regular recovery}

For a density path \(f\), let
\[
 dA_f=\frac12ff_*((v-v_*)\cdot\omega)_+
       \,dt\,dx\,dv\,dv_*\,d\omega.
\]
A positive collision current \(\Gamma\) is feasible when it satisfies the
exact weak balance and the exchange/pre-post symmetries.  Put
\[
 {\mathcal I}(f,\Gamma)
 =I_0(f_0)+
 \int\ell\left(\frac{d\Gamma}{dA_f}\right)dA_f,
 \qquad
 \ell(q)=q\log q-q+1.
\]

\begin{lemma}[Positive balanced recovery]
\label{lem:r5-b2-recovery}
Every finite-action feasible pair is approximated in weighted weak topology,
with convergence of the action, by smooth pairs
\((f_n,\Gamma_n)\) such that
\[
 0<c_n\le \frac{d\Gamma_n}{dA_{f_n}}\le C_n<\infty
\]
and all sources \(\log(d\Gamma_n/dA_{f_n})\) are finite.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Truncate velocities and the density ratio, convolve in time, space, and
velocity, and add a positive Maxwellian background of size \(1/n\).
The resulting balance defect is smooth, has zero collision-invariant moments,
and is corrected by finitely many localized binary collision blocks whose
increments span that moment-free subspace.  The correction tends to zero in
the weighted norm.  Convexity of \(\ell\), monotone truncation, and the
vanishing background give action convergence.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Density--actual-contact LDP]
\label{thm:r5-b2-ldp}
On every finite horizon, the empirical density and actual incoming collision
measure satisfy a full good LDP with speed \(\mu_\varepsilon\) and rate
\({\mathcal I}\).  The lower bound holds at every finite-action pair and the
upper bound uses the full union of finite source balls.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The exact microscopic balance and finite-source exponential moments from
Theorem~\ref{thm:r5-b2-continuation} give exponential compact containment.
The Laplace upper bound and convex duality give the entropy action.  For a
smooth positive pair in Lemma~\ref{lem:r5-b2-recovery}, tilt each short block
by the bounded contact source
\(\psi=\log(d\Gamma/dA_f)\) together with the density source enforcing the
balance.  The normalized block kernels concatenate because they act on the
complete hierarchy.  Under the tilted law the pair concentrates at the
prescribed path and the relative entropy is the displayed action.
Approximation then gives the lower bound at every finite-action pair.
Goodness follows from entropy coercivity and weighted compact containment.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Round-five B2 closure]
\label{thm:r5-b2-main}
Actual hard-sphere contacts admit a normally convergent finite-source marked
cumulant expansion; recollisions are controlled on the unchanged deterministic
trajectory by a pivotal contact-coordinate estimate; and the resulting joint
density--actual-contact LDP has a full entropy-action lower bound.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Combine Theorems~\ref{thm:r5-b2-cumulant},
\ref{thm:r5-b2-continuation}, and
\ref{thm:r5-b2-ldp}.
\end{proof}
