\section{Round-five positive closure: typed strict duals, history likelihoods, and Doob--memory intertwining}
\label{sec:r5-c2}

Different microscopic platforms remain disjoint.  Before homogenization the
slow observable is not assumed Markov; likelihoods and optional projections
are taken in the full resolved-history filtration.  The pressure tangent is
the genuine Doob semigroup, and the memory generator is transformed by the
same eigenfunction intertwiner.

\subsection{Platform-wise weighted strict topologies}

Let \(\mathfrak A\) index the constructed platforms.  For platform \(\alpha\),
let \(\Omega_\alpha\) be its graph-completed path space and choose a continuous
coercive weight \(W_\alpha\ge1\).  Put
\[
 C_{W_\alpha}(\Omega_\alpha)=
 \{F\in C(\Omega_\alpha):F/W_\alpha\text{ vanishes at weighted infinity}\}.
\]
Its weighted bounded-strict topology \(\beta_{W_\alpha}\) is generated by
compact-uniform convergence on every set bounded in
\(\|F/W_\alpha\|_\infty\).  The coproduct source space is the locally convex
direct sum
\[
 \mathfrak C_W=igoplus_{\alpha\in\mathfrak A}
 (C_{W_\alpha},\beta_{W_\alpha}).
\]

\begin{theorem}[Typed strict dual]
\label{thm:r5-c2-strict}
The continuous dual of \(\mathfrak C_W\) is the product of the countably
additive weighted Radon spaces
\[
 \mathfrak C_W'=\prod_{\alpha\in\mathfrak A}
 \mathcal M_{W_\alpha}(\Omega_\alpha).
\]
For a good platform rate \(I_\alpha\),
\[
 Q_\alpha(F)=\sup_\nu\{\nu(F)-I_\alpha(\nu)\}
\]
is continuous on bounded source sets and
\[
 \partial Q_\alpha(F)=
 \operatorname*{argmax}_\nu\{\nu(F)-I_\alpha(\nu)\}.
\]
Every tangent is a countably additive probability on the same labelled
platform.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
On one platform, restriction of a continuous functional to compact weight
sublevels gives compatible Radon measures.  Bounded-set continuity makes their
weighted total variations summable, so they paste to one member of
\(\mathcal M_{W_\alpha}\); the converse is dominated convergence.  The dual of
a locally convex direct sum is the product of the component duals.  Goodness
and weight coercivity make the pressure supremum attained on a compact rate
sublevel.  Fenchel equality identifies maximizers and subgradients.  Constants
and positive tests show that every subgradient has mass one and is positive.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Typed contractions}

Let \(C_\alpha:\Omega_\alpha\to E_\alpha\) be continuous in the weighted
path topology, or let \(C_{\alpha,n}\) be continuous and exponentially good:
\[
 \lim_{n\to\infty}\limsup_\varepsilon
 \frac1{s_\varepsilon}
 \log\mathbb P_arepsilon
 \{d(C_{\alpha,n},C_\alpha)>\delta\}=-\infty.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Platform-labelled contraction]
\label{thm:r5-c2-contraction}
On platform \(\alpha\),
\[
 I_{\alpha,C}(y)=
 \inf\{I_\alpha(\nu):C_\alpha\nu=y\}.
\]
A conclusion between different labels requires a separately proved scaling
map between their microscopic laws.  The Sinai path, hard-sphere density--
contact, history, slow-memory and finite-observable contractions used in the
series satisfy the stated continuity or exponential-approximation condition.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The first assertion is the ordinary or extended contraction theorem on one
open-and-closed coproduct component.  For Sinai collision observables, truncate
return depth and singular distance and use A3's diagonal shield.  For physical
clock observables, use the bounded roof and terminal surgery.  Hard-sphere
linear density/contact maps are continuous in the B2 weighted topology.
History cylinders are continuous on the A4 Ray realization, and the memory
map is continuous on the finite resolved graph norm.  These constructions
provide the required approximants without deleting a platform label.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Exact finite history likelihoods}

Let \(X_T^\varepsilon\) be a terminal slow observable and let
\(\mathcal F_t^{\rm hist}\) be the complete resolved-history filtration.
For bounded \(\Phi\), define
\[
 M_t^\varepsilon=
 \frac{\mathbb E[
 e^{\theta\Phi(X_T^\varepsilon)}
 \mid\mathcal F_t^{\rm hist}]}
 {\mathbb E e^{\theta\Phi(X_T^\varepsilon)}}.
\]

\begin{lemma}[Exact likelihood martingale]
\label{lem:r5-c2-martingale}
The process \(M_t^\varepsilon\) is a positive uniformly integrable martingale
with mean one.  It is defined in the history filtration even when
\(X_t^\varepsilon\) alone is non-Markov.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
It is the normalized conditional expectation of one integrable terminal
random variable.  Positivity, the tower property and mean one are immediate;
bounded \(\Phi\) gives uniform integrability.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Asymptotic Markovization of optional projections}

A4 supplies exposed-phase history kernels
\(K_t^\varepsilon(h,\cdot)\), a limiting diffusion kernel
\(P_t^{\rm diff}(x,\cdot)\), and an exponential coupling estimate.  Let
\(x(h)\) be the present resolved coordinate.

\begin{theorem}[Conditional-kernel Markovization]
\label{thm:r5-c2-markovization}
For every compact regular history set \(K\), bounded Lipschitz terminal
functional \(F\), and \(0\le t<T\),
\[
 \sup_{h\in K}
 \left|
  K_{T-t}^\varepsilon F(h)
  -P_{T-t}^{\rm diff}F(x(h))
 \right|\longrightarrow0.
\]
The same convergence holds jointly for a finite family of optional
projections and their products.  Consequently the finite history likelihoods
of Lemma~\ref{lem:r5-c2-martingale} converge jointly with the enhanced slow
path to the diffusion likelihood.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Choose \(m\) and replace a history by one with the same last \(m\) units and a
fixed remote past.  A4's history coupling changes every future cylinder
expectation by at most \(Ce^{-\gamma m}\), uniformly on \(K\).  On the fixed
finite history cylinder, the A4 enhanced invariance principle and its
conditional-kernel theorem give uniform convergence to the diffusion kernel.
Let \(\varepsilon\to0\), then \(m\to\infty\).  Apply the argument on the
product history space to obtain joint convergence.  Uniform exponential
moments give uniform integrability and pass the normalized terminal
likelihoods to the limit.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The true Doob pressure tangent}

Let \(P_t^\Psi\) be the tilted A4 history Feynman--Kac semigroup and let
\((\lambda_\Psi,h_\Psi)\) be its positive eigenpair.  Define
\[
 \widetilde P_t^\Psi F=
 e^{-\lambda_\Psi t}h_\Psi^{-1}P_t^\Psi(h_\Psi F),
\]
\[
 \mathcal E_t^{\Psi,\theta}F=
 \theta^{-1}\log\widetilde P_t^\Psi e^{\theta F}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Pressure tangent equals the Doob semigroup]
\label{thm:r5-c2-doob-tangent}
The nonlinear history pressure satisfies the exact tower and
\[
 D\mathcal E_t^{\Psi,\theta}(0)F=\widetilde P_t^\Psi F.
\]
The derivative is not replaced by
\(P_t^\Psi F/P_t^\Psi\mathbf1\) unless the eigenfunction is constant.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The eigenfunction equation makes \(\widetilde P_t^\Psi\) Markov and gives its
semigroup property.  Differentiate the logarithmic exponential transform at
zero.  The ratio with \(P_t^\Psi\mathbf1\) would correspond to a statewise
normalization and equals the Doob transform only in the constant-eigenfunction
case.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Doob--memory intertwining}

Let \(L_\Psi\) be the tilted history generator and
\[
 L_\Psi^{\rm D}F=h_\Psi^{-1}L_\Psi(h_\Psi F)-\lambda_\Psi F
\]
its Doob generator.  Let
\(U_\Psi F=h_\Psi F\).  Then
\[
 z-L_\Psi^{\rm D}
 =U_\Psi^{-1}(z+\lambda_\Psi-L_\Psi)U_\Psi.
\]
For a resolved subspace \(V\), use its transformed version
\(V^{\rm D}=U_\Psi^{-1}V\) and projection
\(P^{\rm D}=U_\Psi^{-1}PU_\Psi\) in the equivalent eigenmeasure Hilbert
norm.

\begin{theorem}[Compressed resolvent and memory commutation]
\label{thm:r5-c2-memory}
For resolved \(A,B\),
\[
 \int_0^\infty e^{-zt}
 D\mathcal E_t^{\Psi,\theta}(0)[A](B)\,dt
 =\langle B,
 P^{\rm D}(z-L_\Psi^{\rm D})^{-1}P^{\rm D}A\rangle_{\nu_\Psi^{\rm D}}.
\]
Moreover
\[
 \widehat K_\Psi^{\rm D}(z)=
 zP^{\rm D}-P^{\rm D}L_\Psi^{\rm D}P^{\rm D}
 -[P^{\rm D}(z-L_\Psi^{\rm D})^{-1}P^{\rm D}]^{-1}
\]
is exactly the \(U_\Psi\)-transform of the A4 compressed-memory kernel with
the spectral parameter shifted by \(\lambda_\Psi\).  Linearization,
projection and memory formation therefore commute when all three use the same
Doob generator and transformed resolved subspace.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Theorem~\ref{thm:r5-c2-doob-tangent} gives the Doob semigroup; Laplace
transformation gives its resolvent.  The displayed similarity identity gives
\[
 (z-L_\Psi^{\rm D})^{-1}
 =U_\Psi^{-1}(z+\lambda_\Psi-L_\Psi)^{-1}U_\Psi.
\]
Compress with the transformed projection and take the Schur-complement
formula defining memory.  Similarity commutes with products and inverses on
the finite resolved range, proving the assertion.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Diffusion likelihood, Girsanov and BSDE}

Let the A4 corrected slow limit be
\[
 dX_t=b_\Gamma(X_t)dt+\sigma(X_t)dW_t,
\]
where \(b_\Gamma\) includes the rough-area Lie-bracket drift.  For bounded
\(C^3\) terminal \(\Phi\), set
\[
 u(t,x)=\theta^{-1}\log
 \mathbb E_{t,x}e^{\theta\Phi(X_T)}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Likelihood and BSDE diagram]
\label{thm:r5-c2-girsanov}
The martingales \(M_t^\varepsilon\) converge jointly with the enhanced slow
path to
\[
 M_t=\mathcal E\left(
 \int_0^t\theta\sigma^TDu(s,X_s)dW_s
 \right).
\]
Under the tilted law,
\[
 dX_t=(b_\Gamma+\theta\sigma\sigma^TDu)(t,X_t)dt
      +\sigma(t,X_t)dW_t^\theta.
\]
Furthermore
\((Y_t,Z_t)=(u(t,X_t),\sigma^TDu(t,X_t))\) is the unique bounded solution of
\[
 Y_t=\Phi(X_T)+\int_t^T\frac\theta2|Z_s|^2ds
 -\int_t^TZ_s\,dW_s.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Theorem~\ref{thm:r5-c2-markovization} gives joint optional-projection
convergence and uniform integrability.  The diffusion Feynman--Kac formula
makes \(u\) solve
\[
 \partial_tu+b_\Gamma\cdot Du+	frac12A:D^2u
 +\tfrac\theta2Du^TADu=0.
\]
It\^o's formula gives the stochastic exponential and Girsanov drift.  Applying
the same formula to \(u(t,X_t)\) gives the BSDE; boundedness and exponential
transformation give uniqueness.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Operational calibration}

A monotone, cash-additive, strictly relevant and strongly time-consistent
certainty equivalent generated by one \(C^2\) utility is affine or entropic
with a time-independent coefficient \(\vartheta\).  The axioms alone do not
select it.

\begin{theorem}[Canonical-cocycle calibration]
\label{thm:r5-c2-calibration}
If the valuation likelihood cocycle is additionally required to equal a
mechanically prepared canonical cocycle generated by \(\theta G\) in the same
work units, then \(\vartheta=\theta\).  Without this operational compatibility,
\(\vartheta\) remains an external preference parameter.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Cash additivity gives constant absolute risk aversion and hence the affine or
exponential form.  Equality of the two normalized likelihoods on the
nonconstant generator \(G\) forces equality of their exponential
coefficients.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Round-five C2 closure]
\label{thm:r5-c2-main}
The series has platform-wise weighted-strict Radon duals and typed
contractions; finite likelihoods are exact history martingales; conditional
kernel convergence proves asymptotic Markovization; and pressure tangents,
compressed memory, diffusion likelihoods and BSDEs are linked through one
explicit Doob eigenfunction intertwiner.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Combine Theorems~\ref{thm:r5-c2-strict},
\ref{thm:r5-c2-contraction}, \ref{thm:r5-c2-markovization},
\ref{thm:r5-c2-doob-tangent}, \ref{thm:r5-c2-memory},
\ref{thm:r5-c2-girsanov}, and \ref{thm:r5-c2-calibration}.
\end{proof}
