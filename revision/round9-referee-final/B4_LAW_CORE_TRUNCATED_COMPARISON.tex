\section{Round-nine reconstruction: an exact law state, a resolvent graph core, and entropy-truncated comparison}
\label{sec:r9-b4}

The exact finite system evolves on probability laws over the complete
hard-sphere configuration and contact ledger.  Its generator acts on
observables on that law space.  Static preparation is charged once at the
initial boundary; every transition action contains only the dynamic contact
entropy.  The comparison theorem is proved first for bounded collision-control
ratios and then by monotone entropy truncation, so large doubled-variable
slopes never enter an uncontrolled exponential Hamiltonian.

\subsection{The exact augmented law state}

Let $\mathsf E_\varepsilon$ be the graph-completed hard-sphere configuration
space augmented by the cumulative marked contact measure up to the current
time.  Let
\[
 \mathscr P_\varepsilon=\mathcal P_{W}(\mathsf E_\varepsilon)
\]
with the weighted weak topology.  Liouville transport and deterministic update
of the contact ledger define a linear Markov semigroup
$\mathscr U_\varepsilon(t)$ on laws.  On bounded cylindrical observables
$\Phi(\rho)$ its generator is
\[
 \mathbb A_\varepsilon\Phi(\rho)
 =\lim_{t\downarrow0}
  t^{-1}\bigl(\Phi(\mathscr U_\varepsilon(t)\rho)-\Phi(\rho)\bigr).
\]

\begin{theorem}[Exact augmented law semigroup]
\label{thm:r9-b4-law}
The family $\mathscr U_\varepsilon(t)$ is a positive mass-preserving strongly
continuous semigroup on $\mathscr P_\varepsilon$.  The factorial correlation
hierarchy and every ledger moment are compatible coordinates of a law, but are
not themselves identified with the law state.  The nonlinear log-Laplace
semigroup
\[
 \mathscr V_\varepsilon(t)F(\rho)
 =\mu_\varepsilon^{-1}
  \log\int e^{\mu_\varepsilon F(\eta)}
  \,d(\mathscr U_\varepsilon(t)\rho)(\eta)
\]
satisfies the exact tower on the complete law state.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The hard-sphere flow is measurable and measure preserving on the
full-measure regular graph-completed phase space.  The contact ledger is an
additive deterministic functional, so augmenting the state makes its update
Markov.  Push-forward gives the linear semigroup and continuity follows from
the B2 Green-graph flow on weighted laws.  The log-Laplace tower is the linear
tower after exponentiation.  Correlations determine a law only on the declared
moment-determinate class and are used there solely as coordinates.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A genuine observable graph core}

For $\lambda>0$, let
\[
 R_{\lambda,\varepsilon}=\lambda\int_0^\infty e^{-\lambda t}
  \mathscr U_\varepsilon(t)dt
\]
be the law-semigroup resolvent.  Let $\mathscr C_\varepsilon$ be the algebra
generated by
\[
 R_{\lambda,\varepsilon}\Phi,
 \qquad
 \Phi(\rho)=\varphi(\rho(F_1),\ldots,\rho(F_m)),
 \quad \varphi\in C_c^\infty,
\]
where the $F_j$ are smooth weighted configuration/contact cylinders.

\begin{lemma}[Resolvent graph core]
\label{lem:r9-b4-core}
The algebra $\mathscr C_\varepsilon$ is contained in
$D(\mathbb A_\varepsilon)$, is stable under smooth bounded functional
calculus, and is dense in the generator graph norm on every compact
containment set.  Its gradients and collision increments are bounded by
constants determined by the chosen compact set and resolvent parameters.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The resolvent identity gives
$\mathbb A_\varepsilon R_{\lambda,\varepsilon}\Phi
=\lambda(R_{\lambda,\varepsilon}\Phi-\Phi)$, so every generator image is
explicit.  The Markov chain rule for bounded smooth functions gives stability
under functional calculus.  Yosida approximations
$\lambda R_{\lambda,\varepsilon}F$ converge to $F$ and their generator images
converge whenever $F$ is in the graph domain.  Approximate the bounded
cylinders uniformly on a compact containment set by smooth finite-coordinate
cylinders; the Yosida identity upgrades this to graph-norm convergence.  No
analytic cutoff equal to one on an open set is used.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Finite-generator correlation correctors}

Let $F$ be a density/contact cylinder in the limiting core.  Lift it to the
augmented law state and expand its finite logarithmic generator in connected
factorial coordinates.  Write $D_j^\varepsilon(t)$ for the uncancelled
$j$-label defect after the lower orders have been removed.  Let
$V_{j,\varepsilon}(t,s)$ be the backward observable propagator generated by
the actual finite triangular BBGKY/ledger generator at order $j$.

\begin{lemma}[Finite graph-domain corrector]
\label{lem:r9-b4-corrector}
Define
\[
 c_{j,\varepsilon}(t)
 =\int_t^T V_{j,\varepsilon}(t,s)D_j^\varepsilon(s)ds.
\]
Then $c_{j,\varepsilon}(T)=0$ and
\[
 \partial_tc_{j,\varepsilon}
 +A_{j,\varepsilon}(t)c_{j,\varepsilon}
 =-D_j^\varepsilon(t)
\]
in the exact observable graph domain.  On a slightly smaller hierarchy
weight,
\[
 \sum_{j\ge2}
 \mu_\varepsilon^{1-j}
 \bigl(
  \|c_{j,\varepsilon}\|_{
m gr}
  +\|\mathbb A_\varepsilon c_{j,\varepsilon}\|_{
m gr}
 \bigr)<\infty
\]
uniformly on the kinetic horizon.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The B2 positive boundary-renewal hierarchy constructs
$V_{j,\varepsilon}$ by forward Picard iteration in the remaining time and
preserves the exact trace graph.  Differentiating the Duhamel integral gives
the terminal equation.  A connected $j$-label defect has norm
$j!C^jT^{j-1}$; the factorial law-coordinate weight and
$\mu^{1-j}$ give a geometric majorant after one label-weight loss.  The same
bound applies after the finite generator because the Duhamel equation replaces
its graph image by $D_j$ and a time derivative.  Thus the sum may be
interchanged with the generator.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Microscopic nonlinear-generator convergence]
\label{thm:r9-b4-generator}
For every limiting core cylinder $F$ there are corrected finite observables
$F_\varepsilon\in D(\mathbb A_\varepsilon)$ with
$F_\varepsilon\to F$ locally uniformly such that
\[
 \frac1{\mu_\varepsilon}
 e^{-\mu_\varepsilon F_\varepsilon}
 \mathbb A_\varepsilon e^{\mu_\varepsilon F_\varepsilon}
 \longrightarrow
 \mathbb H\left(f,\frac{\delta F}{\delta f},\psi_F\right)
\]
locally uniformly on regular containment sets.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Sum the correctors from Lemma~\ref{lem:r9-b4-corrector}.  Their finite graph
images cancel every connected correlation defect.  The remaining creation
trees converge by the B2 source-uniform one-block theorem to the binary
Hamilton--Boltzmann exponential.  Cycles are $O(\varepsilon^\alpha)$.
Normal graph convergence justifies exponentiation, differentiation, and
interchange of the series with $\mathbb A_\varepsilon$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Dynamic action and entropy truncation}

For a balanced path on $[s,t]$ set
\[
 \mathcal A_{s,t}(f,\Gamma)
 =\int_s^t\!\int
 \ell\left(\frac{d\Gamma}{dA_f}
 \right)dA_fdr.
\]
It contains no $I_0(f_s)$.  Static grand-canonical or microcanonical
preparation is a boundary functional applied once at the initial time.

\begin{theorem}[Additive kinetic transition semigroup]
\label{thm:r9-b4-action}
For bounded uniformly continuous terminal data,
\[
 (S_{s,t}\Phi)(f_s)
 =\sup_{(f,\Gamma):f(s)=f_s}
 \{\Phi(f_t)-\mathcal A_{s,t}(f,\Gamma)\}
\]
is finite on weighted moment sublevels, attains its relaxed supremum, and
satisfies
\[
 S_{r,t}=S_{r,s}S_{s,t}.
\]
The full prepared value is
$\sup_{f_0}\{S_{0,T}\Phi(f_0)-I_{0,a}(f_0)\}$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
B2 rate-sublevel compactness gives attainment.  Splitting a balanced pair at
$s$ preserves the intermediate density/contact trace and adds the two dynamic
entropy integrals; concatenation gives the reverse inequality.  Since no new
initial rate is charged on the second leg, there is no double counting.  The
last formula inserts preparation once.
\end{proof}

For $M>0$ restrict the contact ratio to
$e^{-M}\le q\le e^M$.  Its Hamiltonian is
\[
 \mathbb H_M(f,p)
 =\langle f,v\cdot\nabla p\rangle
 +\int\sup_{e^{-M}\le q\le e^M}
 \{q\Delta p-\ell(q)\}\,dA_f.
\]
It is globally Lipschitz in the collision increment with constant $e^M$.

\begin{lemma}[Comparison at fixed entropy truncation]
\label{lem:r9-b4-comparisonM}
Bounded upper and lower viscosity solutions of
\[
 \partial_tU+\mathbb H_M(f,DU)=0
\]
on a common moment sublevel satisfy comparison.  The doubled-variable
penalty can be chosen from finite weighted test coordinates and forces the
diagonal while its cotangent remains in the bounded-increment core.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Use
\[
 \frac1{2\eta}\sum_{j=1}^{J}
 |\langle f-g,\phi_j\rangle|^2
 +\delta(\Upsilon(f)+\Upsilon(g)).
\]
At a positive maximum, the penalty itself is bounded; hence the coordinate
distance is $O(\sqrt\eta)$ and tends to zero.  For fixed $M$ the Hamiltonian
continuity constant is finite, so the usual subtraction of the two viscosity
inequalities closes after $arepsilon\downarrow0$, then $J\to\infty$ and
$\delta\downarrow0$.  Smooth saturation of each coordinate derivative keeps
all collision increments in the fixed core without changing the penalty near
its maximizing pair.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Untruncated comparison and nonlinear limit]
\label{thm:r9-b4-main}
The semigroups $S^M$ increase locally uniformly to $S$, and $S$ is the unique
bounded viscosity solution of the full Hamilton--Boltzmann equation in the
entropy-coercive class.  The exact microscopic log-Laplace semigroups converge
locally uniformly to $S$.  Microcanonical preparation is inserted only at the
initial boundary.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Perspective entropy makes paths with $q$ outside
$[e^{-M},e^M]$ uniformly expensive on action sublevels.  Truncating $q$ and
repairing the balance with the B2 conservative finite-cell correction gives
$\mathcal A^M\downarrow\mathcal A$, hence $S^M\uparrow S$ locally uniformly.
Comparison for every $M$ and monotone stability give comparison for $S$.
Theorem~\ref{thm:r9-b4-generator} gives upper and lower generator limits on
the resolvent core, while B2 compact containment prevents escape.  Half-
relaxed limits are respectively sub- and supersolutions and are identified by
comparison.  B1 supplies the one-time prepared boundary functional.
\end{proof}
