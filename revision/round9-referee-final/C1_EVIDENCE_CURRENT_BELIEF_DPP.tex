\section{Round-nine reconstruction: evidence-bearing stratified beliefs and a two-saddle filtering theorem}
\label{sec:r9-c1}

Exact observations are retained.  Their posteriors are represented by
unnormalized currents on a finite stratified observation tower together with
the logarithmic evidence.  This avoids a false uniform lower bound on the
observation density.  The exact belief state, including the phase weights and
the finite correlation skeleton produced by conditioning, is Markov.  A
reduction to density/control coordinates is made only on a quantitatively
reachable chaotic class and after the full belief dynamic programming theorem
has been proved.

\subsection{Admissible exact observation channels}

Fix a finite observation schedule.  An admissible channel on a chronological
hard-sphere cell is a $C^{r+3}$ map
\[
 O_k^u:\mathsf E_\varepsilon\longrightarrow\mathbb R^{r_k}
\]
chosen from a compact definable/Whitney-stratified family.  On every stratum,
$O_k^u$ has constant rank; intersections of observation level sets with
collision faces are clean and have uniformly bounded second fundamental form.
Critical strata are included as separate lower-dimensional channels rather
than declared part of the grazing set.

Let $\mathfrak C^{(c)}$ be the weighted normal-current space of codimension
$c$.  Boundary/collision tracing maps
$\mathfrak C^{(c)}$ to $\mathfrak C^{(c+1)}$ at a clean intersection.  Put
\[
 \mathfrak B=\bigoplus_{c=0}^{c_*}\mathfrak C^{(c)},
\]
where $c_*$ is the finite total observation/collision codimension over the
fixed schedule.

\begin{theorem}[Closed observation--collision current tower]
\label{thm:r9-c1-tower}
The B2 Green-graph propagator, every admissible coarea observation map, and
specular collision tracing act continuously between the declared levels of
$\mathfrak B$.  For a prediction current $G^-$ and an observed value $y$, the
unnormalized posterior current is
\[
 \mathcal O_{k,y}^uG^-
 =\delta_{O_k^u=y}G^-,
\]
interpreted by the stratified coarea formula.  It satisfies the exact Bayes
identity and remains in $\mathfrak B$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
On one constant-rank stratum, choose normal coordinates for $O_k^u$ and apply
the coarea formula to the divergence-measure current.  Clean intersection with
a collision face makes the two normal traces commute up to the oriented
intersection sign.  Uniform Whitney estimates bound the resulting current
norm.  Sum over the finite stratification and use the B2 closed Green graph to
pass to the completion.  The coarea disintegration theorem gives the Bayes
identity without dividing by the evidence.
\end{proof}

Choose once and for all a smooth positive reference volume $dy$ on each
observation stratum.  The evidence density and its logarithm are
\[
 Z_k^u(y;G^-)=\langle1,\mathcal O_{k,y}^uG^-\rangle,
 \qquad e_k=\begin{cases}\log Z_k^u(y;G^-),&Z_k^u>0,\\-\infty,&Z_k^u=0.
 \end{cases}
\]
The normalized posterior is stored only when $Z_k^u>0$; the state always
retains $(\mathcal O_{k,y}^uG^-,e_k)$, so small evidence does not enlarge its
unnormalized current norm.

\begin{lemma}[Reference covariance of evidence]
\label{lem:r9-c1-evidence}
Replacing $dy$ by $r(y)dy$, with $r$ smooth and positive, changes
$e_k$ by $-\log r(y)$ and leaves the normalized posterior and every path
likelihood ratio invariant.  Thus the pair ``unnormalized current plus evidence
section'' is an intrinsic density section of the observation line bundle.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Both statements follow directly from the Radon--Nikodym transformation rule
for the coarea marginal.  In all ratios the same reference factor cancels.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The exact belief transition}

Let $\Pi_k$ be a probability measure on the augmented microscopic state or,
equivalently on the moment-determinate reachable class, its complete current
hierarchy in $\mathfrak B$.  Given a control, first predict by the exact B2 law
semigroup; then update by the observed current and its evidence.

\begin{theorem}[Feller belief/evidence kernel and exact DPP]
\label{thm:r9-c1-belief}
On compact reachable current sublevels the map
\[
 (\Pi_k,u,y)
 \longmapsto
 \left(
 \frac{\mathcal O_{k,y}^u\Pi_k^-}
      {Z_k^u(y;\Pi_k^-)},
 \log Z_k^u(y;\Pi_k^-)
 \right)
\]
is continuous wherever $Z>0$, and its unnormalized extension is Feller
through $Z=0$.  The controlled sequence of complete beliefs is a Markov state
under nonanticipative strategies.  The finite-horizon risk-sensitive value
satisfies the exact belief-state dynamic programming recursion.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Prediction is Feller by the B2 augmented law semigroup.  On each Whitney
stratum, coarea currents depend continuously on the state, control, and
observation in the weighted weak topology; the finite frontier condition
handles changes of stratum.  Keeping the unnormalized current removes the
singularity at $Z=0$.  Ionescu--Tulcea applied to the prediction and
observation kernels constructs the strategy-dependent law.  Conditioning at
the next observation time gives the dynamic programming identity.  Measurable
selection follows from compact controls and the Feller kernel.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A two-saddle exact observation coefficient}

Let $H$ be the path/contact source accumulated before the observation and let
$\zeta\in\mathbb R^{r_k}$ be the Fourier source for the observed variable.
The joint full B2/B1 pressure is
\[
 Q_\varepsilon(H,\lambda,\zeta)
 =\mu_\varepsilon^{-1}\log
 \mathbb E
 e^{\mu_\varepsilon\langle H,\mathcal X_\varepsilon\rangle
 +\lambda C_\varepsilon+i\mu_\varepsilon\zeta\cdot O_k^u}.
\]
Admissibility includes a joint affine-span/constant-rank condition for the
constraint and observation marks.

\begin{theorem}[Joint coarea local coefficient]
\label{thm:r9-c1-coefficient}
For a regular observation value $y$ and a compact source/control chart, the
numerator and denominator of the conditional source expectation have
separate exact finite saddles
\[
 (\lambda^N_{H,y,\varepsilon},\zeta^N_{H,y,\varepsilon}),
 \qquad
 (\lambda^D_{y,\varepsilon},\zeta^D_{y,\varepsilon}).
\]
Their coarea densities satisfy uniform saddle expansions with their own
Hessians and determinants.  Consequently
\[
 \mathbb E\left[e^{\mu_\varepsilon\langle H,
 \mathcal X_\varepsilon\rangle}
 \mid O_k^u=y,\mathcal S_\varepsilon\right]
 =\exp\{\mu_\varepsilon(\mathcal Q_N(H,y)-\mathcal Q_D(y))+o(\mu_\varepsilon)\}
 \frac{d_N(H,y)}{d_D(y)}(1+o(1)),
\]
where the two rate terms and the two determinant coefficients are evaluated at
their respective saddles.  No common-saddle cancellation is assumed.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Insert the exact coarea delta through Fourier inversion in the observation
variable and the B1 Fourier representation of the shell.  The regenerative
full-rank blocks are chosen for the combined constraint--observation mark, so
the joint characteristic function has the central, minor-arc, and
high-frequency bounds of B1 uniformly over the admissible observation family.
Apply steepest descent separately to the source numerator and zero-source
denominator.  Their critical equations differ when $H\ne0$, which produces
the two saddles and determinant ratio.  The stratified coarea Jacobian is
included in each amplitude.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Evidence-tail large deviations]
\label{cor:r9-c1-evidence-tail}
For every $M$ there is a compact reachable belief/evidence set such that the
probability, uniformly over admissible strategies, of leaving it is at most
$e^{-M\mu_\varepsilon}$.  In particular observations with exponentially small
evidence are retained with their correct risk-sensitive cost rather than
excluded by a false deterministic lower bound.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Theorem~\ref{thm:r9-c1-coefficient} gives the observation marginal rate and
uniform prefactor on every stratum.  Combine it with B2 exponential
containment of the prediction current and take a finite cover of the compact
control/stratum family.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Reachable phase-chaotic beliefs}

An exact observation may create order-one correlations.  The reduced state is
therefore not just the one-particle mean.  Let
\[
 \mathbf b=(f,\rho,\kappa)
\]
contain the density $f$, the compact phase weight vector $\rho$, and a finite
correlation skeleton $\kappa$ consisting of all observation/collision strata
which survive at order one.  A belief is in the reachable chaotic class
$\mathfrak R_T$ if its remaining connected correlations obey the B2 tree norm
\[
 \|h_j\|\le C^j\mu_\varepsilon^{1-j}
\]
after conditioning on $(f,\rho,\kappa)$.

\begin{lemma}[Quenched propagation of the reachable cluster]
\label{lem:r9-c1-chaos}
The prediction and admissible observation updates map
$\mathfrak R_T$ into itself.  Uniformly over strategies, all correlation
coordinates outside $(f,\rho,\kappa)$ change bounded dynamic rewards and
transition pressures by $o(1)$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
B2 source-uniform tree estimates propagate the connected hierarchy between
observations.  At an observation, differentiate the joint coarea pressure of
Theorem~\ref{thm:r9-c1-coefficient}; the finite set of order-one strata is
placed in $\kappa$, while each additional connected label pays the usual
$\mu^{1-j}$ factor.  Normal convergence makes the tail uniformly summable.
Every bounded reward/source derivative against the tail is therefore $o(1)$.
The statement is only for the strategy-reachable class, not for all singular
beliefs sharing one mean.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Reduced kinetic game and statistical tangent]
\label{thm:r9-c1-main}
The exact belief DPP of Theorem~\ref{thm:r9-c1-belief}, restricted to
$\mathfrak R_T$, converges locally uniformly to the DPP on the reduced state
$(f,\rho,\kappa)$ with transition Hamiltonians supplied by B4 and observation
costs supplied by Theorem~\ref{thm:r9-c1-coefficient}.  At a regular identified
phase, the finite experiment is LAN when centred at the exact finite score;
with a prior positive at that phase, the posterior has the corresponding
Bernstein--von Mises Gaussian limit.  At coexistence the finite phase weights
remain part of the limit state.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Lemma~\ref{lem:r9-c1-chaos} gives an exponentially contained, uniformly good
approximation of the complete belief transition by the reduced transition.
Apply dynamic programming backwards over the fixed observation schedule and
use B4 comparison on every prediction interval.  For LAN, Taylor-expand the
joint source/observation pressure at its exact finite saddle; B3 gives the
joint Gaussian score process and Theorem~\ref{thm:r9-c1-coefficient} gives the
local likelihood ratio.  Standard finite-dimensional Laplace expansion gives
BvM on an identified chart.  If several phases have the same leading cost,
their separate evidence coefficients update $\rho$ rather than being
collapsed.
\end{proof}
