\section{Round-nine reconstruction: compatible kinetic traces, physical Jacobi control, and source-uniform sewing}
\label{sec:r9-b2}

The boundary variable is not an arbitrary measure independent of the interior
state.  It is the normal trace of a divergence-measure transport state and is
part of a closed Green graph.  Recollision estimates are performed in the
actual chronological variables.  Coordinate resets are not used to erase
small singular values; instead an exterior-power estimate for the physical
hard-sphere Jacobi cocycle is integrated with the incoming flux measure and
summed together with the factorial collision-tree weights.

\subsection{The compatible normal-trace graph}

For $k\ge1$ let
\[
 \Omega_k^\varepsilon
 =\{Z_k=(x_i,v_i)_{i\le k}:|x_i-x_j|>\varepsilon\}
\]
and let $\Gamma_{ij}^-$ be its incoming binary face.  A level state is a
positive finite measure $g_k$ on $\Omega_k^\varepsilon$ such that the transport
current
\[
 \mathcal J_k=(g_k,v_1g_k,\ldots,v_kg_k)
\]
is a divergence-measure field with finite weighted divergence.  Its incoming
normal trace is denoted $\gamma^-_{k,ij}$; it is allowed to have atoms.  Put
\[
 \mathfrak G_k^\beta
 =\{(g_k,\gamma_k^-):
   \gamma_k^-={\rm Tr}^-\mathcal J_k,
   \|g_k\|_{W_\beta}+\|{\rm div}\mathcal J_k\|_{W_\beta}
   +\|\gamma_k^-\|_{W_\beta}<\infty\}.
\]
The norm includes the outgoing trace obtained by specular reflection and the
corner compatibility relations in the sense of normal currents.

\begin{theorem}[Closed Green graph and reflected transport]
\label{thm:r9-b2-trace}
The graph $\mathfrak G_k^\beta$ is closed.  For every bounded $C^1$ test
$\phi$ with the velocity weight,
\[
 \int_{\Omega_k^\varepsilon}
  (\partial_t+V_k\cdot\nabla_X)\phi\,dg_k
 +\int_{\Omega_k^\varepsilon}\phi\,d({\rm div}\mathcal J_k)
 =\sum_{i<j}\int_{\Gamma_{ij}}
   \phi\,(V_k\cdot n_{ij})\,d\gamma_{k,ij}.
\]
Specular reflection identifies the incoming and outgoing traces and defines a
positive mass-preserving transport semigroup $U_k^\varepsilon(t)$ on the
closed graph.  Boundary Dirac masses are included; no $L^1$ approximation of
a boundary atom is required.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Approximate the hard-sphere domain by deleting a grazing/corner neighbourhood
and apply the Gauss--Green theorem for divergence-measure fields.  The normal
trace is continuous for strict convergence of the current and its divergence.
Uniform weighted flux bounds let the deleted neighbourhood shrink; the corner
sets have codimension at least two in flux measure.  This proves the Green
identity and closedness.  Free streaming followed by the measure-preserving
specular involution gives a positive isometry on smooth graph states.  Its
graph closure is strongly continuous and mass preserving.  The construction
acts directly on finite normal traces and therefore retains atoms.
\end{proof}

Let $B_{k,k+1}$ be the creation/contact operator defined by integrating the
incoming trace of the $(k+1)$st particle against the physical collision flux.
With factorial hierarchy norm
\[
 \|G\|_{\alpha,\beta}
 =\sum_{k\ge0}\frac{e^{\alpha k}}{k!}
  \|(g_k,\gamma_k^-)\|_{\mathfrak G_k^\beta},
\]
write $\mathcal U^\varepsilon(t)$ for the Dyson--Phillips boundary-renewal
series.

\begin{proposition}[Positive boundary-renewal hierarchy]
\label{prop:r9-b2-renewal}
For $\alpha'>0$ smaller than $\alpha$ and $T$ below the declared kinetic
horizon,
\[
 \mathcal U^\varepsilon(t)
 =\sum_{n\ge0}\int_{0<t_1<\cdots<t_n<t}
 U(t-t_n)B\cdots BU(t_1)\,dt_1\cdots dt_n
\]
converges from $\mathfrak X_{\alpha,\beta}$ to
$\mathfrak X_{\alpha',\beta'}$.  It is positive, satisfies the hierarchy
Green identities, and obeys
\[
 \|\mathcal U^\varepsilon(t)G\|_{\alpha',\beta'}
 \le C_T\|G\|_{\alpha,\beta}.
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The coarea definition of $B_{k,k+1}$ and
Theorem~\ref{thm:r9-b2-trace} give
\[
 \|B_{k,k+1}G_{k+1}\|_{\mathfrak G_k^{\beta'}}
 \le C(k+1)\|G_{k+1}\|_{\mathfrak G_{k+1}^{\beta}}.
\]
The loss from grazing is paid by one velocity weight and by the incoming flux
already present in the graph norm.  Multiplication by $(k+1)$ is absorbed by
the factorial label weight.  The ordered time simplex supplies $t^n/n!$;
summing proves the estimate and positivity.  Applying the Green identity at
every term proves compatibility of the renewed traces.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The physical youngest-fork variables}

Fix a chronological collision tree with a first surplus contact between
particles $a$ and $b$.  Let $f$ be their youngest separating fork.  The
independent variables are the actual root positions and velocities of the two
fork subtrees and the impact parameters at every creation on their exclusive
ancestral paths.  Let
\[
 \mathcal D_{ab}:\mathbb R^N\longrightarrow\mathbb R^3,
 \qquad a\mapsto x_a(t_*)-x_b(t_*)
\]
be the derivative at the surplus time.  Write
$\wedge^2D\mathcal D_{ab}$ for its transverse two-volume.

For a non-grazing collision with flight time $\tau$, the linearized scattering
map acts on transverse Jacobi data $(\delta x,\delta v)$ by the standard
free-flight/scattering matrices.  In curvature coordinates the unstable
quadratic form $\mathcal B$ satisfies the dispersing Riccati inequality
\[
 \mathcal B^+\ge
 \left(\tau+(\mathcal B^-+\kappa/\cos\varphi)^{-1}\right)^{-1}.
\]

\begin{lemma}[Flux-weighted exterior Jacobi estimate]
\label{lem:r9-b2-jacobi}
There are $C,p<\infty$ such that for every exclusive path of length $m$ and
for every $0<\delta<1$,
\[
 \int_{\mathcal E_m(\delta)}
 \left|\wedge^2J_m^{-1}\right|^p
 \,d\mathfrak f_m
 \le C^m\delta^{-C},
\]
where $J_m$ is the physical Jacobi map from fork variables to the final
transverse relative position, $\mathfrak f_m$ is the product incoming-flux
measure, and $\mathcal E_m(\delta)$ excludes event gaps, relative speeds, and
incidence cosines below $\delta$.  The complementary flux is at most
$C^m\delta^\eta$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Use the invariant symplectic Wronskian of two Jacobi fields.  Free flight
preserves it and a dispersing collision adds the positive second fundamental
form divided by the incoming cosine.  Hence a two-dimensional fork variation
cannot be simultaneously focused without paying inverse powers of an
incidence cosine, a short event gap, or a small relative speed.  Coarea at one
collision integrates each such inverse power against the compensating
incoming factor $((v-v_*)\cdot\omega)_+$.  Induction along the path gives a
factor $C$ per event rather than a deterministic lower singular value.
Chebyshev's inequality yields the bad-flux estimate.  No QR rescaling or
unit-determinant argument is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Integrated first-surplus gain]
\label{thm:r9-b2-surplus}
For a chronological connected cluster with $m$ creation contacts, the
absolute contribution of configurations with a first surplus contact is
bounded by
\[
 \varepsilon^{\alpha}C^m(1+T)^m
\]
relative to the corresponding creation tree, for some $\alpha>0$ independent
of $m$.  The future after the surplus contact is the true specularly reflected
future.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Condition on all variables except the physical fork variables.  On
$\mathcal E_m(\delta)$, coarea for the two transverse contact equations and
Lemma~\ref{lem:r9-b2-jacobi} give
$C^m\varepsilon^2\delta^{-C}$.  The complement has flux
$C^m\delta^\eta$.  Choose $\delta=\varepsilon^\kappa$ and obtain
$\varepsilon^\alpha C^m$.  At the first contact apply the actual elastic
reflection and restart the positive renewal hierarchy of
Proposition~\ref{prop:r9-b2-renewal}; thus every later collision and mark is
retained.  The estimate extracts only one geometric gain and pays ordinary
$C^m$ factors for the rest.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[All-depth actual-contact cluster summation]
\label{cor:r9-b2-cluster}
For sufficiently small kinetic horizon and every bounded complex
particle/contact source,
\[
 \sum_{m\ge0}\frac{(CTe^R)^m}{m!}<\infty,
\]
and the cyclic sector is $O(\varepsilon^\alpha)$ after all fixed source
derivatives.  The grand-canonical marked log-Laplace functional is therefore
normally convergent on one complex source chart.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Creation labels, ordered event times, and the factorial hierarchy weight give
the displayed exponential series.  Insert
Theorem~\ref{thm:r9-b2-surplus} in the first cyclic block.  Cauchy estimates on
a slightly larger source ball give the derivative statement.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Source-uniform block sewing}

Let $\mathcal S_{h,\varepsilon}^H$ be the exact hierarchy source propagator on
a block of length $h$, and $\mathcal S_h^H$ its Boltzmann tree limit.

\begin{theorem}[Scaled one-block theorem]
\label{thm:r9-b2-block}
For $0<h\le h_0(R)$,
\[
 \|\mathcal S_{h,\varepsilon}^H-\mathcal S_h^H\|_{
 \alpha,\beta\to\alpha',\beta'}
 \le C_R\bigl(h\varepsilon^\alpha+h^2\varepsilon^\eta\bigr).
\]
The same estimate holds for every fixed source derivative.  Consequently, for
any partition of $[0,T]$ with mesh $h$, the accumulated error is
\[
 C_R T\varepsilon^\alpha+C_RT h\varepsilon^\eta,
\]
and has a partition-independent limit as first $\varepsilon\to0$ and then
$h\to0$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Expand one block by the compatible renewal series.  Noncyclic trees are
changed to Boltzmann variables by the ordinary collision coarea formula; the
unmatched boundary/end pieces have two event integrations and give
$h^2\varepsilon^\eta$.  Every cyclic term contains a first surplus and gives
$h\varepsilon^\alpha$ by Theorem~\ref{thm:r9-b2-surplus}.  Normal source
convergence permits differentiation term by term.  Telescoping the product of
contractive block propagators yields the accumulated estimate.  The bound is
linear, not constant, in $h$, so it survives refinement.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Balance-preserving recovery and the joint LDP}

For a smooth positive feasible pair $(f,\Gamma)$, subdivide
space--time--velocity--angle into finitely many cells.  The discrete balance
operator is the incidence matrix of the free-transport/contact complex.  Its
range is the orthogonal complement of the discrete mass, momentum, and energy
invariants.

\begin{lemma}[Conservative finite-cell repair]
\label{lem:r9-b2-repair}
Every finite-action feasible pair admits smooth positive approximants
$(f_n,\Gamma_n)$ which satisfy the weak balance exactly and
\[
 (f_n,\Gamma_n)\to(f,\Gamma),
 \qquad
 \int\ell(d\Gamma_n/dA_{f_n})dA_{f_n}\to
 \int\ell(d\Gamma/dA_f)dA_f.
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Truncate tails and add a vanishing strictly positive Maxwellian/contact
background.  Mollify inside each finite cell.  The remaining balance defect
annihilates the collision invariants.  Solve the finite incidence equation on
that orthogonal complement and distribute the signed correction over the
positive background; its norm tends to zero.  Convexity and uniform
integrability of perspective entropy give action convergence.  No global
compact symmetry or energy-preserving Ornstein--Uhlenbeck group is invoked.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Grand-canonical and microcanonical joint LDP]
\label{thm:r9-b2-main}
On the weighted density--actual-contact path space, the grand-canonical laws
satisfy a good short-time LDP with rate
\[
 I_T^{\rm gc}(f,\Gamma)
 =I_0^{\rm gc}(f_0)
 +\begin{cases}
 \displaystyle\int_0^T\!\int
 \ell\left(\frac{d\Gamma}{dA_f}
 \right)dA_fdt,
 & (f,\Gamma)\text{ feasible},\\
 +\infty,&\text{otherwise}.
 \end{cases}
\]
After the B1 exact source-dependent shell conditioning, the same dynamic
action holds with the prepared initial rate $I_{0,a}$.  Both rates are good.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Corollary~\ref{cor:r9-b2-cluster} and
Theorem~\ref{thm:r9-b2-block} give the full bounded-source pressure and all
finite projections.  Exponential moments of particle weight, contact mass,
and the exact Green balance give compact containment and a Skorokhod modulus.
Fenchel duality imposes the closed balance and yields the perspective entropy.
For the lower bound, expose a smooth positive pair by finite bounded sources,
use the source propagator concentration furnished by the pressure Hessian, and
then apply Lemma~\ref{lem:r9-b2-repair}.  B1 transfers the entire source
pressure and the recovery law to the shell with its exact weighted
coefficient.  Rate sublevels have uniform moment/contact entropy bounds and
are therefore compact.
\end{proof}
