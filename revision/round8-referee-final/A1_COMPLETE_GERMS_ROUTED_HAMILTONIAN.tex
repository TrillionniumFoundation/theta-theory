\section{Round-eight reconstruction: complete dynamic germs and routed Hamiltonian suspension}
\label{sec:r8-a1}

The finite biseam complex used in the preceding revision did not remember the
oriented side of the branch preimages of the next vertical seam.  We replace it
by the complete symbolic germ surface.  This simultaneously records every
forward and backward singularity cut and turns the return map into an honest
bijection before any transfer or response argument is made.

\subsection{The complete germ surface}

Let $p_i(a)>0$, $\sum_{i=1}^dp_i(a)=1$, and
$s_i(a)=\sum_{j\le i}p_j(a)$.  Put $\mathcal A=\{1,\ldots,d\}$ and
$\Omega=\mathcal A^{\mathbb Z}$ with shift $\sigma$.  For
$\omega\in\Omega$ define
\[
 q_a(\omega)=\sum_{n\ge0}p_{\omega_0}(a)\cdots p_{\omega_{n-1}}(a)
 s_{\omega_n-1}(a),
\]
\[
 p_a(\omega)=\sum_{n\ge1}p_{\omega_{-1}}(a)\cdots p_{\omega_{-n+1}}(a)
 s_{\omega_{-n}-1}(a).
\]
Empty products are one.  At an endpoint the two symbolic expansions are not
identified.  The \emph{complete dynamic germ surface} is the graph
\[
 \widehat\Sigma_a^\infty
 =\{(q_a(\omega),p_a(\omega),\omega):\omega\in\Omega\}
\]
with the topology transported from $\Omega$.  Its physical projection is
$\pi_a(q,p,\omega)=(q,p)$.

\begin{lemma}[All dynamic seams are present]
\label{lem:r8-a1-all-seams}
For every $N\ge0$, the partition of $\widehat\Sigma_a^\infty$ into cylinders
$[\omega_{-N},\ldots,\omega_N]$ refines
\[
 \bigvee_{|j|\le N}\pi_a^{-1}F_a^{-j}\mathcal P_a,
\]
where $\mathcal P_a$ is the physical branch partition.  In particular the
one-step cuts $q=s_{i-1}+p_i s_j$, all of their iterated preimages, and all
oriented sides at their intersections are distinct strata of the cylinder
system.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The coordinate $\omega_n$ is exactly the branch of $F_a^n\pi_a(\omega)$.
Therefore a cylinder fixes the side of each seam encountered for
$-N\le n\le N$.  Conversely, away from endpoints those branch labels determine
the cylinder.  At endpoints the symbolic expansion is retained rather than
identified, so both oriented sides remain distinct.  This proves the claimed
refinement at every depth.
\end{proof}

Define
\[
 \widehat F_a(q_a(\omega),p_a(\omega),\omega)
 =(q_a(\sigma\omega),p_a(\sigma\omega),\sigma\omega).
\]

\begin{theorem}[Complete-germ baker bijection]
\label{thm:r8-a1-bijection}
The map $\widehat F_a$ is a homeomorphism of
$\widehat\Sigma_a^\infty$, with inverse induced by $\sigma^{-1}$.  Its physical
projection is the generalized baker formula
\[
 q'=\frac{q-s_{i-1}}{p_i},\qquad p'=s_{i-1}+p_i p,
 \qquad i=\omega_0,
\]
on every symbolic germ.  Thus the formula is single valued on every preimage
seam and every multiple seam crossing.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The series defining $q_a,p_a$ converge uniformly because
$\max_i p_i\le1-(d-1)p_*$ on compact parameter sets.  Substitution after a
shift gives the displayed affine formula.  The old branch is $\omega_0$ and
the new branch is $\omega_1$, including both endpoint expansions.  Shift and
inverse shift are continuous in the product topology, proving the assertion.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A depth-weighted current scale}

For a finite word $w=\omega_{-r}\ldots\omega_s$, let $[w]$ be its cylinder and
$\partial^\alpha[w]$ the oriented parameter derivative current of order
$|\alpha|$ obtained by differentiating the moving coding map $\pi_a$ on that
cylinder.  Put $\ell(w)=r+s+1$.  Fix numbers
\[
 0<\vartheta<\vartheta_+<1,
 \qquad d\vartheta_+<1,
\]
and define
\[
 \|u\|_{m,+}=\sum_{|\alpha|\le m}
 \sum_w \vartheta_+^{\ell(w)}
 \bigl|\langle u,\partial^\alpha[w]\rangle\bigr|,
\qquad
 \|u\|_{m,-}=\sum_{|\alpha|\le m-1}
 \sum_w \vartheta^{\ell(w)}
 \bigl|\langle u,\partial^\alpha[w]\rangle\bigr|.
\]
The cylinder relations are quotiented before completion, so the same current
is not counted under two refinements.  Denote the completions by
$\mathscr B_{a,m}^+$ and $\mathscr B_{a,m}^-$.  The transfer operator is
\[
 \mathcal L_a u=u\circ\widehat F_a^{-1}
\]
with Bernoulli Jacobian weights when acting on densities.

\begin{lemma}[Dynamic-cut Lasota--Yorke estimate]
\label{lem:r8-a1-ly}
For every finite $m$ there are $C<\infty$ and $0<\rho<1$, uniform on a compact
parameter interval, such that
\[
 \|\mathcal L_a^n u\|_{m,+}
 \le C\rho^n\|u\|_{m,+}+C\|u\|_{m,-}.
\]
The inclusion $\mathscr B_{a,m}^+\hookrightarrow\mathscr B_{a,m}^-$ is compact.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Shift removes one future symbol and adds one past symbol.  After quotienting
cylinder refinements, every depth-$k$ coefficient has at most $d$ children.
The choice $d\vartheta_+<1$ absorbs this complexity.  A parameter derivative
creates only the two endpoint currents of the affected cylinder and lowers the
available derivative order by one; these terms are placed in the weak norm.
Iteration gives the displayed inequality.  For compactness, truncate the
cylinder depth at $N$ and the derivative order at $m-1$.  The truncated range
is finite dimensional, while the tail is bounded by
$C(\vartheta/\vartheta_+)^N$.  Hence the inclusion is a norm limit of finite
rank maps.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Spectrum and finite-order response on all generated cuts]
\label{thm:r8-a1-response}
The eigenvalue one of $\mathcal L_a$ is simple and the remainder of the
spectrum on $\mathscr B_{a,m}^+$ lies in a disk of radius less than one.  If
$G_a$ has $m$ parameter derivatives in the depth-weighted current scale, then
$a\mapsto\nu_a(G_a)$ is $C^m$.  Every derivative is an absolutely convergent
sum of cylinder interior terms and oriented currents on all iterated seams.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Write a function as the sum of its martingale differences with respect to the
future cylinders.  On the mean-zero subspace, the shift removes the first
martingale difference and the depth weight gives a strict contraction.  A
unit-modulus eigenvector has all martingale differences zero in both time
directions and is constant.  The compact embedding in
Lemma~\ref{lem:r8-a1-ly} then gives a spectral gap.

In moving symbolic coordinates only the Bernoulli weights and the coding map
depend on $a$.  Differentiating a depth-$N$ cylinder expectation gives finitely
many interior derivatives and endpoint currents.  The number of depth-$N$
terms grows at most like $d^N$, whereas their norm is bounded by
$\vartheta_+^N$ and the resolvent contribution by $\rho^N$.  The chosen weights
make the differentiated series normally convergent through order $m$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A symplectic router and an autonomous realization}

The previous attempt incorrectly required branch motions with overlapping
source and target regions to have disjoint support in one physical plane.  We
separate them in an auxiliary canonical routing coordinate.  Let
$(y,\eta)$ be canonical coordinates and choose disjoint routing tubes
$\mathcal T_i$ centered at $y=i$.  A compactly supported Hamiltonian lift first
moves the $i$th incoming strip into $\mathcal T_i$, a second Hamiltonian applies
the affine exact symplectic branch map there, and a third lowers it to the
$i$th outgoing horizontal port.  The three stages occupy disjoint clock
intervals, while the branch tubes are disjoint in $(y,\eta)$ even when their
physical projections overlap.

\begin{lemma}[Exact symplectic routing]
\label{lem:r8-a1-router}
There is a smooth periodic Hamiltonian
$K_{a,\tau}(q,p,y,\eta)$ on a four-dimensional exact symplectic routing manifold
such that its time-one map on the incoming section is the complete-germ baker
map and its routing coordinate returns to $(y,\eta)=(0,0)$.  The construction
is $C^{m+1}$ in $a$ and has matching flat jets on every symbolic germ.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The lift and lowering stages are translations generated by cutoff multiples of
$\eta$.  Inside tube $i$, the affine branch has generating function
\[
 S_i(q,P)=p_i^{-1}(q-s_{i-1})(P-s_{i-1}),
\]
which generates $Q=(q-s_{i-1})/p_i$ and
$p=(P-s_{i-1})/p_i$.  Interpolating $S_i$ to the identity with a clock cutoff
gives an exact symplectic isotopy.  All branch isotopies are disjoint in the
routing coordinate.  Flat cutoffs make their jets agree at tube boundaries.
The final lowering is the inverse of the lift, so the auxiliary coordinates
close exactly.
\end{proof}

On the extended phase space with clock pair $(\tau,E)$ set
\[
 H_a=E+K_{a,\tau}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Autonomous routed suspension]
\label{thm:r8-a1-suspension}
The Hamiltonian flow of $H_a$ has exact first return time one to
$\{\tau=0,y=\eta=0\}$ and return map $\widehat F_a$.  It preserves Liouville
flux and realizes every dynamic germ, not merely a positive-measure core.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Hamilton's equation gives $\dot\tau=1$.  During one clock period the lift,
branch generating-function isotopy, and lowering occur in that order.  The
router lemma identifies their composition with $\widehat F_a$ and returns the
auxiliary pair to zero.  Each stage is exact symplectic, hence the section flux
is preserved.  Endpoint expansions occupy distinct routed germs and therefore
never require an ambiguous choice.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Work, calibration, and universality}

Place a normalized work form $\epsilon_i\rho_i(\tau)d\tau$ in the incoming
routing tube before the branch motion.

\begin{proposition}[Full-port work]
\label{prop:r8-a1-work}
For every return segment with incoming symbol $i$,
\[
 \int_0^1\epsilon_i\rho_i(\tau)\dot\tau\,dt=\epsilon_i.
\]
Thus the work is the additive cylinder cocycle on the complete germ surface,
including every seam germ.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The symbol is constant during the work stage and $\dot\tau=1$.  The integral of
$\rho_i$ is one.  Concatenation proves the cocycle identity.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite-alphabet universality and conditional calibration]
\label{thm:r8-a1-universality}
Every smoothly parameterized finite Bernoulli shift with positive weights and
every finite additive port cocycle admits the complete-germ routed Hamiltonian
realization above.  A cash-additive law-invariant time-consistent certainty
equivalent has the same coefficient as the mechanical exponential likelihood
exactly when equality of the two normalized likelihood cocycles is imposed.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The coding, depth-weighted transfer scale, and router construction used only
finiteness and positivity of the alphabet weights.  For calibration, cash
additivity and time consistency give constant absolute risk aversion.  Equality
of normalized likelihoods on a nonconstant port cocycle forces equality of
the two exponential coefficients; without that compatibility axiom no such
identification follows.
\end{proof}
