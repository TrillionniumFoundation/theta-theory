\section{Round-eight reconstruction: evidence-lifted stratified filtering and reachable-state reduction}
\label{sec:r8-c1}

Exact observations are retained.  Because their coarea normalizers may be
arbitrarily small, the filtering state carries the unnormalized current and
its evidence separately.  Repeated observation and collision intersections
are represented by a closed tower of compatible currents of every reached
codimension.

\subsection{The stratified current tower}

For a chronological cell let $\mathfrak S$ be the finite family generated by
collision faces and the level sets of the observation maps used up to the
current decision time.  A state is a current chain
\[
 \mathbf J=(J_S)_{S\in\mathfrak S}
\]
with $J_S$ a finite measure on the stratum $S$ and incidence compatibility
\[
 \partial J_S=\sum_{S'\prec S}\epsilon(S,S')J_{S'}.
\]
Set
\[
 \|\mathbf J\|_{\rm str}
 =\sum_{S\in\mathfrak S}\omega^{\operatorname{codim}S}
 \bigl(\|J_S\|_{\mathcal M_w}+
 \|T_SJ_S\|_{\mathcal M_w}\bigr).
\]

\begin{lemma}[Closed stratified trace complex]
\label{lem:r8-c1-tower}
The B2 transport, reflection, contact creation, and coarea restriction operators
act triangularly on the completion of compatible current chains.  The boundary
operator squares to zero, and every new observation of rank $r$ raises
codimension by $r$ while preserving the incidence identities.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Start with smooth differential forms on every transverse stratum.  Stokes'
formula gives the incidence relation and $\partial^2=0$.  B2 transport maps a
stratum to its flow image; reflection and contact maps preserve incidence
orientation.  Coarea restriction is intersection with the level-set current,
and transverse intersection commutes with boundary up to the standard signed
incidence rule.  The weighted triangular norm absorbs the finite number of
new lower strata.  Passing to the graph completion proves the assertion.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Evidence-lifted Bayes updates}

For an observation $O^u$ and an unnormalized predicted current $\bar\Pi^-$,
define
\[
 \bar\Pi^y=\delta_{O^u=y}\bar\Pi^-
\]
by coarea intersection and let
\[
 Z(y)=\bar\Pi^y(1).
\]
The normalized posterior is $\Pi^y=\bar\Pi^y/Z(y)$ when $Z(y)>0$, but the state
used by the dynamic program is
\[
 (\bar\Pi^y,\ell^y),\qquad \ell^y=-\log Z(y).
\]

\begin{lemma}[Bayes update without a uniform denominator]
\label{lem:r8-c1-evidence}
The map $(\bar\Pi^-,y)\mapsto(\bar\Pi^y,\ell^y)$ is continuous in the projective
stratified topology on every finite evidence slice $\ell^y\le L$.  Values with
small $Z(y)$ are retained with their exact evidence cost; no uniform positive
lower bound for $Z(y)$ is asserted.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
On a transverse observation chart, coarea intersection is continuous in the
stratified graph norm.  Projective normalization is continuous when
$Z(y)\ge e^{-L}$.  If $Z(y)$ is smaller, the unnormalized current still varies
continuously and the diverging log evidence is an explicit state coordinate
rather than a hidden norm blowup.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Feller evidence-filter kernel]
\label{thm:r8-c1-feller}
Assume the observation maps are uniformly transverse on each declared chart
and their critical strata are included explicitly in $\mathfrak S$.  Prediction
by the B2 graph semigroup followed by the evidence-lifted update defines a
Feller kernel on the compactly contained evidence state space.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Prediction is Feller by the B2 graph semigroup.  On each transverse chart apply
Lemma~\ref{lem:r8-c1-evidence}.  The critical set is not identified with the
billiard grazing set; it is a separate finite current stratum and is propagated
by Lemma~\ref{lem:r8-c1-tower}.  Exponential evidence containment removes the
large-$\ell$ tail uniformly, so the finite chart pieces paste to a Feller
kernel.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Quenched coarea coefficients}

A noiseless observation inserts the Fourier representation of its level-set
current.  The observation frequency is therefore an additional continuous
frequency in the B2/B1 coefficient theorem.

\begin{theorem}[Uniform coarea local-limit ratio]
\label{thm:r8-c1-ratio}
On every strategy-reachable compact source and evidence set, numerator and
denominator coarea coefficients have one common exact saddle and satisfy
uniform mixed local-limit expansions.  Their ratio converges to the conditional
block pressure, while the finite log-evidence term is retained explicitly.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Use Fourier inversion for the collision/lattice constraints together with the
observation delta.  Transversality gives a full-rank observation block; the B1
good-block regeneration gives high-frequency decay, and B2 source sewing gives
uniform bounded-source derivatives.  Major-arc Gaussian coefficients are
computed at the same saddle.  Dividing cancels the common determinant and shell
factor, not the evidence itself.  The remaining $-\log Z(y)$ is precisely the
state coordinate from Lemma~\ref{lem:r8-c1-evidence}.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Reachable chaotic beliefs and state reduction}

Let $\mathfrak R_\varepsilon$ be the set of posterior currents reachable from
the prepared initial family under admissible strategies and the declared
observation channels.  For $j\ge2$ let $\mathfrak h_j(\Pi)$ be the connected
$j$-particle hierarchy coordinate.

\begin{lemma}[Posterior chaos on the reachable set]
\label{lem:r8-c1-chaos}
There are $C,\rho<1$ such that, uniformly over
$\Pi\in\mathfrak R_\varepsilon$ and finite decision horizons,
\[
 \|\mathfrak h_j(\Pi)\|_{\rm str}
 \le C^j j!\mu_\varepsilon^{1-j}+C\rho^j\varepsilon^\eta.
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The prepared law has the cluster bound by B1.  B2 graph propagation maps a
connected $j$-cluster either to a connected kinetic cluster or to a recollision
sector; the latter carries $\varepsilon^\eta$.  The coarea update marks only
finitely many coordinates and its evidence-lifted form does not divide the
unnormalized connected current by a small $Z$.  Normalize only after restricting
to a finite evidence slice and then use the same ratio theorem.  Induction over
decision times gives the estimate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Asymptotic sufficiency on strategy-reachable beliefs]
\label{thm:r8-c1-sufficiency}
For bounded Lipschitz rewards and finite horizons, the value difference between
two reachable beliefs with the same resolved kinetic state and evidence ledger
is $o(1)$ uniformly over strategies.  The limiting dynamic program is therefore
posed on the resolved kinetic state.  No equivalence is claimed for arbitrary
correlated beliefs sharing only a one-particle mean.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Expand one Bellman step in connected hierarchy coordinates.  The zeroth and
first coordinates are the resolved state and evidence.  Every remaining term
is bounded by the reward Lipschitz constant times the series in
Lemma~\ref{lem:r8-c1-chaos}, which tends to zero uniformly.  Backward induction
over the finite number of decisions proves the result.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Risk-sensitive games and local statistics}

\begin{theorem}[Risk-sensitive saddle and exact-centered LAN]
\label{thm:r8-c1-game-lan}
The evidence-filter kernel and quenched ratio theorem yield the finite-horizon
risk-sensitive minimax recursion with measurable selectors.  For a continuous
prior with positive local density, the posterior has the LAN expansion centered
at the exact finite-volume mean, and the local denominator is bounded below by
the prior mass of a shrinking regular ball times the Gaussian coefficient.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Feller continuity and compact containment give measurable minimax selectors.
Theorem~\ref{thm:r8-c1-ratio} passes each block payoff and transition kernel to
the limit.  Differentiate the exact finite log likelihood at its own mean; the
linear score is exactly centered.  Integrating the local quadratic likelihood
over a ball on which the prior density has a positive minimum gives the
explicit denominator.  B3 supplies the limiting covariance on the quotient.
\end{proof}
