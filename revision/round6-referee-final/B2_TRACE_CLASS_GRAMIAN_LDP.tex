\section{Round-six positive closure: trace-regular actual contacts and the joint dynamic LDP}
\label{sec:r6-b2}

The grand-canonical construction is logically prior to B1.  Collision
exponential moments are proved only on the actual initial and tilted hierarchy
class, which carries an incoming-boundary trace norm.  This excludes the
boundary-layer counterexample for arbitrary interior \(L^1\) densities.  The
first surplus contact is controlled by a full genealogical Gramian containing
root translations as well as creation variables; after that contact the true
post-collisional future is retained and propagated by the trace-class
semigroup.

\subsection{Orientation and exact balance}

For \(i<j\) at contact, put
\[
\omega=\frac{x_j-x_i}{\varepsilon},
\qquad (v_i-v_j)\cdot\omega>0
\]
for the incoming orientation, and use the elastic reflection
\[
v_i'=v_i-((v_i-v_j)\cdot\omega)\omega,
\qquad
v_j'=v_j+((v_i-v_j)\cdot\omega)\omega.
\]
Every unordered contact is counted once in \(\Gamma_T^\varepsilon\), on the
quotient \((v,v_*,\omega)\sim(v_*,v,-\omega)\).

\begin{proposition}[Microscopic density--contact balance]
\label{prop:r6-b2-balance}
For every weighted smooth test \(p\),
\[
\langle p_T,\pi_T^\varepsilon\rangle-
\langle p_0,\pi_0^\varepsilon\rangle-
\int_0^T\langle(\partial_t+v\cdot\nabla_x)p,
\pi_t^\varepsilon\rangle dt
=\int\Delta_\omega p\,d\Gamma_T^\varepsilon.
\]
The identity is invariant under the exchange quotient.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Integrate each particle test on free-flight intervals.  Interior endpoints
telescope.  The jump of the two colliding particle tests is exactly
\(\Delta_\omega p\).  Grazing and simultaneous multiple contacts form a
Liouville-null set.  Exchanging the labels and replacing \(\omega\) by
\(-\omega\) leaves the unordered jump unchanged.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A trace-regular hierarchy class}

Let \(g_k\) be the rescaled \(k\)-particle correlation density on the
hard-core domain \(D_k^\varepsilon\).  On an incoming face
\(\partial^-_{ij}D_k^\varepsilon\), define the flux trace
\[
\mathsf T^-_{ij}g_k=
((v_i-v_j)\cdot\omega)_+\operatorname{Tr}^-_{ij}g_k.
\]
For \(\beta>0\) and label radius \(\alpha\), put
\[
\begin{aligned}
\|G\|_{\alpha,\beta}^{\rm tr}
={}&\sum_{k\ge0}\frac{e^{\alpha k}}{k!}
\int_{D_k^\varepsilon}|g_k|e^{\beta|V_k|^2}dZ_k\\
&+\sum_{k\ge2}\frac{e^{\alpha k}}{k!}
\sum_{i<j}
\int_{\partial^-_{ij}D_k^\varepsilon}
\left(|\mathsf T^-_{ij}g_k|+
\tau_*|\nabla_{\tan}\mathsf T^-_{ij}g_k|ight)
 e^{\beta|V_k|^2}d\sigma.
\end{aligned}
\]
The tangential term is needed only on the regular contact charts; grazing
pieces are placed in the weighted bad-set ledger.

\begin{lemma}[Initial and tilted trace regularity]
\label{lem:r6-b2-trace-class}
The hard-core grand-canonical Maxwellian hierarchy belongs to
\(\mathfrak X_{\alpha,\beta}^{\rm tr}\) for every finite \(\alpha\) and every
\(\beta\) below the Maxwellian exponent.  For a bounded particle/contact
source of size \(R\), the exact Feynman--Kac BBGKY evolution maps
\[
\mathfrak X_{\alpha,\beta}^{\rm tr}
\longrightarrow
\mathfrak X_{\alpha-Cte^{CR},\beta'}^{\rm tr}
\]
for \(0\le t\le T\), with \(0<\beta'<\beta\), and
\[
\|\mathscr U_{\varepsilon,R}(t)G\|_{\alpha-Cte^{CR},\beta'}^{\rm tr}
\le e^{Cte^{CR}}\|G\|_{\alpha,\beta}^{\rm tr}.
\]
The constants are uniform in \(\varepsilon\).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The Maxwellian hierarchy has factorial correlations bounded by
\(C^ke^{-\beta_0|V_k|^2}\).  The hard-core boundary trace is its one-sided
restriction multiplied by the incoming flux, which is integrable after
reducing the Gaussian exponent.  Tangential derivatives obey the same bound.

For the evolution, integrate the BBGKY equations on each hard-core domain.
Free transport preserves the interior weighted mass.  The Green identity
pairs the outgoing and incoming traces through the elastic reflection, whose
velocity Jacobian and energy are one.  A contact source multiplies an incoming
trace by at most \(e^R\); a particle source costs the same factor per created
label.  Summing the boundary operators with the factorial weight loses
\(Cte^{CR}\) in the label radius and a fixed amount in the Gaussian exponent.
The tangential trace equation has the same triangular form plus bounded
curvature coefficients on regular charts.  Gronwall's inequality gives the
estimate.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Correct contact exponential ledger]
\label{cor:r6-b2-ledger}
For \(G\in\mathfrak X_{\alpha,\beta}^{\rm tr}\),
\[
\mathbb E_G e^{zN_t}
\le
\exp\{Cte^{Cz}\}
\|G\|_{\alpha,\beta}^{\rm tr}
\]
after normalization by the initial mass, uniformly for bounded \(z\).  The
same estimate holds after a stopping contact for the reflected outgoing trace
state.  No corresponding estimate is claimed for arbitrary interior
\(L^1\) densities without trace control.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Insert the constant contact source \(z\) in
Lemma~\ref{lem:r6-b2-trace-class}.  At a stopping contact, elastic reflection
maps the incoming trace isometrically to an outgoing trace, which is an
admissible initial boundary coordinate for the remaining semigroup.  The
trace norm, rather than the interior mass alone, controls the next contact.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The full genealogical controllability Gramian}

Fix a creation forest and its regular chronological cell.  For a proposed
first surplus contact between labels \(a,b\), let \(\mathcal P(a,b)\) be the
union of their two exclusive ancestral paths up to their first common
ancestor.  The control variables are:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item the three root-position translations of each independent root
component;
\item every creation time and the two local impact-normal coordinates on
\(\mathcal P(a,b)\);
\item the root velocity coordinates not fixed by the energy cutoff.
\end{enumerate}
Let \(J_e(s)\) be the derivative of the relative position
\(x_a(s)-x_b(s)\) with respect to control \(e\), transported through every
actual reflection.  With \(\Pi_{ab}^\perp\) the projection onto the plane
orthogonal to the relative velocity, define
\[
\mathcal G_{ab}(s)=
\sum_{e\in\mathcal U(a,b)}
\Pi_{ab}^\perp J_e(s)J_e(s)^T\Pi_{ab}^\perp.
\]

\begin{lemma}[Quantitative genealogical observability]
\label{lem:r6-b2-gramian}
On the regular set where all relative speeds are at least \(\delta\), all
incidence cosines are at least \(\delta\), and chronological gaps are at least
\(\delta\),
\[
\lambda_{\min}\bigl(\mathcal G_{ab}(s)|_{(v_a-v_b)^\perp}\bigr)
\ge c\,\delta^{C(1+m)}(1+m)^{-C},
\]
where \(m=|\mathcal P(a,b)|\).  The complement of the regular set has
Gaussian-weighted activity at most
\[
C^m\left(m\delta+\delta^c\right).
\]
The constants do not rely on a uniform analytic-sublevel exponent.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Write the linearized free flight as
\((\delta x,\delta v)\mapsto(\delta x+t\delta v,\delta v)\).  At a
non-grazing reflection, the linearized scattering matrix and its inverse have
norm at most \(C\delta^{-C}\).  Suppose a transverse covector \(\ell\)
annihilates every column of the control matrix.  Propagate \(\ell\) backward
through the inverse scattering matrices.  Orthogonality to the two impact
normal controls at each creation edge removes the two transverse components
introduced there.  At the terminal roots, orthogonality to the explicitly
included independent root translations removes the remaining transverse
components.  Thus \(\ell=0\), proving full rank.

For the quantitative estimate, normalize \(|\ell|=1\) and iterate the inverse
matrix bounds.  At some control column the pairing is at least
\(c\delta^{C(1+m)}(1+m)^{-C}\); otherwise the backward recursion would leave a
nonzero root component while all root translations are annihilated.  Squaring
and summing gives the Gramian bound.  Small-speed and grazing sets have direct
Gaussian/angular measure \(O(\delta^c)\); a gap shorter than \(\delta\) has
time-simplex measure \(O(m\delta)\).  A union bound over the chronological
cell gives the bad-set estimate, with no appeal to orders of vanishing of
composed analytic minors.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[First-surplus contact tube]
\label{lem:r6-b2-tube}
Condition on every variable before the first surplus contact and on all
controls except a two-dimensional subfamily selected by
Lemma~\ref{lem:r6-b2-gramian}.  The set of controls producing the contact has
relative activity at most
\[
C^m(1+\mathbb V)^C(1+T)^C\varepsilon^\alpha
\]
for one \(\alpha>0\), after optimizing \(\delta=\varepsilon^\kappa\).
The estimate is unchanged by bounded particle and actual-contact marks.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
On the regular set, the map from the selected controls to the transverse
relative position has a two-dimensional Jacobian bounded below by the Gramian
estimate.  Coarea bounds the preimage of the contact disk of radius
\(\varepsilon\) by
\(C^m\varepsilon^2\delta^{-C(1+m)}\).  The chronological tree weight supplies
one small factor per additional ancestral edge, so after summing in \(m\) the
powers of \(\delta^{-Cm}\) are absorbed by choosing the trace/label radius and
then \(\delta=\varepsilon^\kappa\) with fixed small \(\kappa\).  The bad set
from Lemma~\ref{lem:r6-b2-gramian} contributes
\(C^m(m\delta+\delta^c)\).  Taking the smaller resulting power gives
\(\alpha>0\).  Bounded marks multiply the integrand by a uniform factor and
do not change the coarea map.
\end{proof}

\subsection{All later contacts on the true future}

After the first surplus contact, keep the reflected velocities and every
subsequent actual contact.  The post-contact state is an outgoing boundary
trace, transported by the exact reflected trace semigroup.

\begin{theorem}[Future-preserving cyclic-sector estimate]
\label{thm:r6-b2-cyclic}
For a connected \(k\)-label cluster, the total marked activity of histories
containing at least one surplus contact is bounded by
\[
\varepsilon^\alpha k!C^k(T+\varepsilon)^{k-1}
\exp\{CTe^{R+U}\},
\]
where \(R\) bounds the sources and \(U\) is the exponential-generating
variable for marked actual contacts.  Only the first surplus contact supplies
the geometric \(\varepsilon^\alpha\) factor; the complete deterministic future
is controlled by Corollary~\ref{cor:r6-b2-ledger} and is never altered or
deleted.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Sum the creation forests by collision coarea, giving
\(k!C^k(T+\varepsilon)^{k-1}\).  Select the chronologically first surplus
contact and apply Lemma~\ref{lem:r6-b2-tube}.  Its post-collisional state is
retained exactly.  Conditional on that trace state, the exponential generating
function of every later actual contact is bounded by
Corollary~\ref{cor:r6-b2-ledger}.  Chronological integration yields the
exponential factor.  Summing the ancestral depth is geometric in the hierarchy
label radius.  No map to a collision-deleted pseudo-trajectory is used.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Grand-canonical marked pressure on fixed horizons}

Let
\[
Q_{\varepsilon,T}^{\rm gc}(h,\psi)=
\frac1{\mu_\varepsilon}\log
\mathbb E_\varepsilon^{\rm gc}
\exp\left\{
\mu_\varepsilon
(\langle h,\Pi_T^\varepsilon\rangle+
 \langle\psi,\Gamma_T^\varepsilon\rangle)
\right\}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Actual-contact cluster pressure]
\label{thm:r6-b2-pressure}
For one fixed short physical horizon \(T>0\) and every bounded real source
ball of radius \(R\), the pressure above has a normally convergent
real-trajectory expansion, locally uniform with all finite source derivatives.
It converges to a differentiable pressure \(Q_T^{\rm gc}\).  On a complex
neighborhood of every compact real subball the limit is analytic.  Every
\(\psi\)-derivative marks an actual microscopic contact, and cyclic clusters
vanish at rate \(O(\varepsilon^\alpha)\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For a fixed \(R\), choose the initial label radius
\(\alpha_R>CTe^{CR}\).  The grand-canonical Maxwellian hierarchy belongs to
that trace class by Lemma~\ref{lem:r6-b2-trace-class}.  Creation trees are
summed by the usual factorial tree estimate, while
Theorem~\ref{thm:r6-b2-cyclic} controls every cyclic history.  These bounds
are normally summable and remain so after source differentiation.

To keep the physical horizon fixed as \(R\) grows, divide it into a finite
number \(N_R\) of blocks.  Exact finite-volume Feynman--Kac composition acts on
the complete trace hierarchy.  Lemma~\ref{lem:r6-b2-trace-class} supplies a
uniform stability bound on each block and consumes only a finite amount of
label radius.  For fixed \(R\), \(N_R\) is finite; the local
Boltzmann--Grad consistency error is uniform on the reached trace ball, so the
sum of the block errors tends to zero with \(\varepsilon\).  This yields the
fixed-horizon pressure on every bounded source ball.  Cauchy estimates give
source derivatives, and an actual-contact derivative follows directly from
the microscopic exponent.
\end{proof}

Deleting the last collision of a limiting creation tree splits it into two
rooted trees.  Therefore the limiting Hamiltonian is
\[
\mathbb H(f,p,\psi)=
\langle f,v\cdot\nabla_xp\rangle+
\frac12\int ff_*((v-v_*)\cdot\omega)_+
\left(e^{\Delta_\omega p+\psi}-1\right).
\]
Normal convergence justifies the marked Hamilton--Jacobi equation and all
first and second source derivatives.

\subsection{Exponential compactness}

Use the state topology generated by weighted bounded-Lipschitz density tests,
bounded continuous contact tests, and one Maxwellian Lyapunov moment
\(e^{\beta'|v|^2}\), \(0<\beta'<\beta\).

\begin{lemma}[Joint exponential compact containment]
\label{lem:r6-b2-compact}
For every \(M\) there is a compact set \(K_M\) in the density--contact path
space such that
\[
\limsup_{\varepsilon\to0}\frac1{\mu_\varepsilon}
\log\mathbb P_arepsilon^{\rm gc}
\{(\pi^\varepsilon,\Gamma^\varepsilon)\notin K_M\}
\le-M.
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
A sufficiently small positive Maxwellian particle source lies in the B2 source
chart and gives exponential control of the Lyapunov moment.  A positive
constant contact source gives exponential control of total contact mass by
Theorem~\ref{thm:r6-b2-pressure}.  The exact balance in
Proposition~\ref{prop:r6-b2-balance} controls time increments of a countable
dense family of smooth weighted tests.  Weighted Prokhorov compactness and the
Skorokhod modulus criterion yield \(K_M\).
\end{proof}

\subsection{Upper and lower bounds}

For a density path \(f\), define
\[
dA_f=\frac12ff_*((v-v_*)\cdot\omega)_+
\,dt\,dx\,dv\,dv_*\,d\omega.
\]
A pair \((f,\Gamma)\) is feasible when it satisfies the closed weak balance,
exchange symmetry, and finite Lyapunov moment.

\begin{lemma}[Conservative action-dense regularization]
\label{lem:r6-b2-regularize}
Every finite-action feasible pair can be approximated by smooth pairs
\((f_h,\Gamma_h)\) with
\[
0<c_h\le\frac{d\Gamma_h}{dA_{f_h}}\le C_h<\infty,
\]
which satisfy the balance exactly and whose entropy actions converge.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
First truncate velocities and mollify in space, time, and velocity away from
endpoints.  The resulting balance defect is \(O(h^M)\) in a negative Sobolev
norm for arbitrarily large \(M\), because the original balance is exact and
the mollifier has matching moments.  Partition each compact energy--momentum
shell into cells of diameter \(h\).  The actual binary-collision incidence
matrix spans the orthogonal complement of mass, momentum, and energy.  Its
right inverse has norm at most \(Ch^{-\kappa}\), obtained from paths of
admissible cell collisions of length \(O(h^{-\kappa})\).  Solve for a signed
collision correction of size \(O(h^{M-\kappa})\).

Add beforehand a positive reference collision background of size
\(\eta_h\), with
\(h^{M-\kappa}=o(\eta_h)\) and \(\eta_h\to0\).  The corrected collision
density remains positive.  The relative-entropy cost of the background and
correction tends to zero by the quadratic expansion of \(q\log q-q+1\) on
compact positive intervals, followed by monotone truncation.  Endpoint
corrections are inserted in vanishing time layers.  This proves exact balance
and action convergence.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Grand-canonical joint actual-contact LDP]
\label{thm:r6-b2-gc-ldp}
The pair \((\pi^\varepsilon,\Gamma^\varepsilon)\) satisfies a full good LDP
with speed \(\mu_\varepsilon\) and rate
\[
I_T^{\rm gc}(f,\Gamma)=I_0^{\rm gc}(f_0)+
\begin{cases}
\displaystyle\int\ell\left(\frac{d\Gamma}{dA_f}\right)dA_f,
&(f,\Gamma)\text{ feasible and }\Gamma\ll A_f,\\[2ex]
+\infty,&\text{otherwise},
\end{cases}
\]
where \(\ell(q)=q\log q-q+1\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
On each bounded source ball, Theorem~\ref{thm:r6-b2-pressure} gives the
limiting cumulant.  The union of bounded particle/contact cylinder sources is
separating.  Lemma~\ref{lem:r6-b2-compact} upgrades finite-dimensional upper
bounds to the full closed-set upper bound.  Dualizing the density source
imposes the balance; dualizing the global union of bounded contact sources and
then taking monotone truncations gives the integral conjugate \(\ell\),
including infinite cost for singular current.

For the lower bound, first take a smooth positive pair from
Lemma~\ref{lem:r6-b2-regularize}.  Solve the backward balance equation for a
bounded test \(p\) and put
\(\psi=\log(d\Gamma/dA_f)-\Delta p\).  Define the exact normalized
finite-volume Feynman--Kac tilt
\[
\frac{d\mathbb P_\varepsilon^{p,\psi}}
{d\mathbb P_\varepsilon^{\rm gc}}
=\exp\left\{
\mu_\varepsilon\langle(p,\psi),X_\varepsilon\rangle
-\mu_\varepsilon Q_{\varepsilon,T}^{\rm gc}(p,\psi)
\right\}.
\]
It is a mean-one density without introducing a contact compensator.
Differentiability of the sewn pressure makes its mean converge to
\((f,\Gamma)\); the second derivative and exponential compactness give
concentration under the tilt.  The logarithmic density converges to the
relative-entropy action by pointwise conjugacy.  This proves the lower bound
for regular pairs.  Lemma~\ref{lem:r6-b2-regularize} and lower semicontinuity
extend it to every finite-action pair.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Microcanonical transfer}

B1 supplies the exact finite-volume source-dependent saddle and a
subexponential mixed shell coefficient for every bounded density/contact
source.

\begin{theorem}[Microcanonical joint LDP]
\label{thm:r6-b2-mc-ldp}
Under the primitive microcanonical shell, the same pair satisfies the good LDP
with rate
\[
I_T^{\rm mc,a}(f,\Gamma)=I_{0,a}(f_0)+
\int\ell\left(\frac{d\Gamma}{dA_f}\right)dA_f
\]
on feasible absolutely continuous pairs and \(+\infty\) otherwise.  The
zero-cost pair is the prepared Boltzmann solution and its actual limiting
contact intensity.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Apply the B1 finite-volume saddle change of measure to every source used in the
grand-canonical upper and lower bounds.  The shell has subexponential
probability under the exact tilted law, uniformly on bounded source balls.
Only the initial rate changes from \(I_0^{\rm gc}\) to \(I_{0,a}\); the dynamic
contact action and all compactness estimates are unchanged.  At zero cost,
\(d\Gamma/dA_f=1\) and the weak balance is the Boltzmann equation.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Round-six B2 closure]
\label{thm:r6-b2-main}
Every actual deterministic hard-sphere contact is marked in a source-uniform
fixed-horizon pressure theorem on a trace-regular hierarchy class.  The first
surplus contact is controlled by a full root-inclusive genealogical Gramian;
all later contacts follow the unchanged true trajectory.  The resulting
grand-canonical and microcanonical density--actual-contact pairs satisfy the
full good LDPs above.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Combine Theorems~\ref{thm:r6-b2-cyclic},
\ref{thm:r6-b2-pressure}, \ref{thm:r6-b2-gc-ldp}, and
\ref{thm:r6-b2-mc-ldp}, together with
Proposition~\ref{prop:r6-b2-balance}.
\end{proof}
