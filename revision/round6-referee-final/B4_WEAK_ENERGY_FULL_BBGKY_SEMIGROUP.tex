\section{Round-six positive closure: full-law microscopic values and weak-energy kinetic semigroups}
\label{sec:r6-b4}

The exact finite-volume object is the complete probability law on hard-sphere
microstates.  Correlation hierarchies are coordinates of that law, not random
states and not a linear algebra.  The microscopic logarithmic values are
transported through the full law and converge, by the B2 joint LDP and an
independent full-BBGKY perturbed-test construction, to the density--contact
Lax--Oleinik semigroup.  The kinetic state uses the ordinary weighted weak
topology with energy as a lower-semicontinuous containment function; it does
not require convergence of second moments.

\subsection{Exact finite-volume law and observable algebra}

Let \(\Omega_\varepsilon\) be the disjoint union of all hard-core
finite-particle phase spaces and let \(\Phi_t^\varepsilon\) be the Liouville
flow.  The exact state space is
\[
\mathfrak P_\varepsilon=\mathcal P(\Omega_\varepsilon),
\qquad
\mathcal U_\varepsilon(t)\rho=(\Phi_t^\varepsilon)_\#\rho.
\]
For a law \(\rho\), denote its complete factorial correlation hierarchy by
\(G(\rho)=(g_k)_{k\ge0}\).  This is a one-to-one coordinate map on the
exponential label class, but it is deterministic once \(\rho\) is fixed.

Let \(\mathfrak A_{\alpha,\beta}\) be the completion of finite polynomials in
factorial moment coordinates
\[
\rho\longmapsto
P\left(
\langle F_1,g_{k_1}(\rho)\rangle,\ldots,
\langle F_m,g_{k_m}(\rho)\rangle
\right)
\]
for the norm obtained by summing absolute polynomial coefficients against the
factorial hierarchy norm.  This is a genuine commutative algebra because the
product is the product of polynomials on law space; no false identity
\(g_{k+l}=g_k\otimes g_l\) is used.

\begin{lemma}[Law-coordinate algebra]
\label{lem:r6-b4-algebra}
The algebra \(\mathfrak A_{\alpha,\beta}\) is closed under multiplication and
under exponentiation of bounded density/contact cylinders.  Composition with
\(\mathcal U_\varepsilon(t)\) is a strongly continuous algebra homomorphism
from \(\mathfrak A_{\alpha,\beta}\) to
\(\mathfrak A_{\alpha-Ct,\beta'}\) on the B2 short horizon.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Polynomial multiplication is the Cauchy product of coefficient arrays, which
is bounded by the defining absolute coefficient norm.  A bounded cylinder has
an absolutely convergent exponential power series in that Banach algebra.
The full BBGKY semigroup transports every hierarchy coordinate of the exact
law, with the factorial label-radius estimate proved in B2; substituting the
transported coordinates into a polynomial preserves products.  Density of
finite polynomials and the hierarchy estimate extend the map and give strong
continuity.
\end{proof}

For a bounded microscopic terminal observable \(F_\varepsilon\), define the
exact logarithmic value at a law state
\[
\mathcal V_{s,t}^\varepsilon(F_\varepsilon;\rho)
=\frac1{\mu_\varepsilon}
\log\int
e^{\mu_\varepsilon F_\varepsilon(\Phi_{t-s}^\varepsilon Z)}\rho(dZ).
\]
This is an exact log-Laplace functional on the complete law.  It is not called
a Markov semigroup on one-particle density.  Conditional towers, when needed,
are taken on the complete posterior law/history state.

\label{thm:r6-b4-generator}

\begin{proof}

corrector series.  At each label number, the Duhamel equation cancels the full
incoming defect, including the coupling to the next marginal.  The only
uncancelled first-order terms are free transport and the binary collision
boundary.  Exponentiation of the empirical cylinder changes the two-particle
boundary value by
\(e^{\Delta p+\psi}\), which yields the displayed Hamiltonian.  B2's cyclic
sector contributes \(O(\varepsilon^\alpha)\), and the geometric corrector tail
contributes \(O(\rho^K)\) before sending \(K\to\infty\).  The absolute graph
norm permits the generator and limit to pass through the infinite series.
The same exact corrected sequence yields both half-relaxed inequalities, so
no order-preserving hierarchy truncation is used.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The compatible weak-energy state topology}

Let \(\mathcal E\) be the set of nonnegative densities with fixed mass and
finite energy.  Give it the ordinary weighted weak topology generated by
bounded continuous tests and compactly supported smooth tests; energy is not
required to converge.  Put
\[
\Upsilon(f)=\int(1+|v|^2)f(dx,dv).
\]
Then
\[
\mathcal E_M=\{f:\Upsilon(f)\le M\}
\]
is weakly compact by tightness and lower semicontinuity.  The referee's
sequence with disappearing second moment converges in this topology and hence
is not a counterexample to compactness.

Let \(\mathscr D_b\) be the cylinder algebra generated by
\(\phi\in C_c^1\) and collision invariants, with the requirement
\(\|\Delta\phi\|_\infty<\infty\).  This class is convergence determining for
the weak topology.  Every finite-coordinate doubling penalty built from it
has bounded collision increment.  Elasticity gives
\(\Delta|v|^2=0\), so the containment function is also compatible with the
Hamiltonian.

\begin{lemma}[Hamiltonian finiteness and continuity]
\label{lem:r6-b4-hamiltonian}
For \(f,g\in\mathcal E_M\), bounded \(\psi\), and
\(p,q\in\mathscr D_b\) in one bounded core set,
\[
|\mathbb H(f,p,\psi)-\mathbb H(g,q,\psi)|
\le\omega_M(d_w(f,g))+
C_M\|p-q\|_{C^1,\Delta},
\]
where \(\omega_M(r)\downarrow0\) and
\(\|p\|_{C^1,\Delta}\) controls the transport derivative and bounded collision
increment.  Moreover
\[
\mathbb H(f,D\Upsilon,0)=0
\]
for the collision part and has the declared transport bound.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Bounded \(\Delta p+\psi\) controls the exponential uniformly.  The collision
kernel grows only linearly in \(|v|+|v_*|\).  A uniform second-moment bound
makes first moments uniformly integrable:
\[
\int_{|v|>R}|v|f(dv)\le M/R.
\]
On a compact velocity ball, weak convergence makes
\(ff_*B(e^{\Delta p+\psi}-1)\) continuous; the tail estimate gives the
uniform modulus.  Transport is handled identically.  Energy is conserved at
each elastic collision, giving the final assertion.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The additive action and kinetic semigroup}

For balanced density--contact paths put
\[
\mathcal A_{s,t}(f,\Gamma)=
\int_s^t\ell(d\Gamma/dA_f)dA_f,
\]
with infinite value off the feasible set.  Define
\[
(S_{s,t}\Phi)(f_s)=
\sup_{(f,\Gamma):f(s)=f_s}
\{\Phi(f_t)-\mathcal A_{s,t}(f,\Gamma)\}.
\]
Static preparation costs are not included in this transition action.

\begin{lemma}[Compact action sublevels]
\label{lem:r6-b4-action-compact}
On an initial set with bounded mass and energy, every action sublevel is
compact in the density weak-path/contact weak-measure topology.  Concatenation
preserves feasibility and adds the action.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Energy is conserved by free transport and elastic contact balance, so all
density marginals remain in one weakly compact \(\mathcal E_M\).  Entropy
coercivity controls total contact mass and makes contact measures uniformly
integrable relative to \(A_f\).  The balance equation bounds the time modulus
of every test in a countable dense subset of \(\mathscr D_b\).  Prokhorov and
Arzela--Ascoli/Jakubowski compactness give a convergent subsequence.  B3's
closed balance theorem and lower semicontinuity of integral entropy preserve
feasibility and the action.  Splitting or joining a balanced path preserves
all boundary traces and makes the entropy integral additive.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Kinetic Lax--Oleinik semigroup]
\label{thm:r6-b4-semigroup}
The operators \(S_{s,t}\) are monotone, cash additive, preserve bounded
uniformly continuous functions on energy sublevels, and satisfy
\[
S_{r,t}=S_{r,s}S_{s,t}.
\]
For \(\Phi\in\mathscr D_b\), the value is a viscosity solution of
\[
\partial_sU+
\mathbb H\left(f,\frac{\delta U}{\delta f},0\right)=0,
\qquad U(T,f)=\Phi(f).
\]
A bounded actual-contact reward enters as the \(\psi\)-argument.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Lemma~\ref{lem:r6-b4-action-compact} makes the variational supremum stable and
attained after relaxation.  Concatenation and splitting give dynamic
programming.  On a short interval, B3 Fenchel duality gives the viscosity upper
inequality.  The B2 normalized regular tilt supplies a recovery path for the
lower inequality.  Bounded contact work is already part of the same dual
Hamiltonian.  Stability under initial weak convergence follows from compact
sublevels and regular recovery.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Comparison on the bounded-increment core]
\label{thm:r6-b4-comparison}
Bounded upper-semicontinuous subsolutions and bounded lower-semicontinuous
supersolutions on an energy sublevel, tested by \(\mathscr D_b\) and the
containment function \(\Upsilon\), satisfy comparison.  Hence the value in
Theorem~\ref{thm:r6-b4-semigroup} is the unique viscosity solution in this
class.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Choose a convergence-determining sequence
\((\phi_j)\subset C_c^1\) with bounded collision increments.  Double variables
with
\[
\frac1{2\eta}\sum_{j=1}^J2^{-j}
|\langle f-g,\phi_j\rangle|^2
+\delta(\Upsilon(f)+\Upsilon(g))
+\frac{|t-s|^2}{2\eta}.
\]
Every derivative of the finite-coordinate penalty is a finite linear
combination of the \(\phi_j\) and stays in \(\mathscr D_b\).  The energy
penalty has zero collision increment.  Compactness of \(\mathcal E_M\) gives
maximizers.  Apply the two viscosity inequalities and use
Lemma~\ref{lem:r6-b4-hamiltonian}; first let \(\eta\downarrow0\), then
\(J\to\infty\) and \(\delta\downarrow0\).  A standard strict time
perturbation contradicts a positive maximum.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Microscopic convergence}

Let \(F_\varepsilon\) be a bounded density/contact cylinder and let the
initial laws lie in one B2 exponentially compact trace-hierarchy class.

\begin{theorem}[Microscopic log-Laplace convergence]
\label{thm:r6-b4-limit}
The exact finite-volume logarithmic values
\[
\frac1{\mu_\varepsilon}\log
\mathbb E_{\rho_\varepsilon}
\exp\{\mu_\varepsilon F_\varepsilon(\pi_t^\varepsilon,
\Gamma_t^\varepsilon)\}
\]
converge locally uniformly over regular initial densities to the variational
kinetic value generated by \(S_{s,t}\).  The same limit is obtained by the
full-BBGKY perturbed-test method of
Theorem~\ref{thm:r6-b4-generator}.  Thus the density semigroup is a proved
large-deviation limit, not an exact finite-density Markov closure.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The B2 good joint LDP and compact action sublevels give the Laplace principle,
which is exactly the Lax--Oleinik variational value.  Uniform exponential
compactness makes the convergence local uniform on regular initial sets.
Independently, Theorem~\ref{thm:r6-b4-generator}, containment, and
Theorem~\ref{thm:r6-b4-comparison} identify the upper and lower half-relaxed
limits with the same unique viscosity solution.  Cylinder approximation
extends the result to bounded uniformly continuous terminal values.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Static microcanonical preparation and Gaussian tangent}

Nonconserved preparation moments constrain only the initial law.  Actual mass,
momentum, and energy invariants may additionally label dynamic components.
Retain the B1 multiplier \(\lambda\) as a static coordinate of an augmented
semigroup and optimize it once at the initial boundary; it is not reselected
at every time block.

kinetic observables, has drift \(b\) and covariance \(A\).  Its entropic

\begin{theorem}[Round-six B4 closure]
\label{thm:r6-b4-main}
The complete finite hard-sphere law has a well-typed observable algebra and
full-BBGKY corrector calculus.  Its logarithmic density/contact values
converge to the unique weak-energy Lax--Oleinik semigroup on the
bounded-collision-increment core.  Initial preparation is performed once
through the B1 static multiplier, and the risk-sensitive diffusion generator
is the finite-dimensional contraction of the B3 exactly centred Gaussian
process.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The microscopic algebra and generator are
Lemma~\ref{lem:r6-b4-algebra} and
Theorem~\ref{thm:r6-b4-generator}.  The kinetic semigroup and uniqueness are
Theorems~\ref{thm:r6-b4-semigroup} and
\ref{thm:r6-b4-comparison}; microscopic convergence is
Theorem~\ref{thm:r6-b4-limit}.  The B1 source-dependent conditioning formula
and B3 Gaussian contraction give the final two assertions.
\end{proof}
