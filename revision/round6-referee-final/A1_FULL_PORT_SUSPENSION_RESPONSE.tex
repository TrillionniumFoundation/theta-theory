\section{Round-six positive closure: full-port suspension, exact work, and physical response}
\label{sec:r6-a1}

The construction below uses one graph-completed self-return section from the
outset.  In particular, no outgoing rectangle is identified with a boundary
of the wrong type, and the work layer is traversed while the four incoming
ports are genuinely disjoint.  Thus the mechanical work equals the branch
current on the whole regular Liouville section, not only on a smaller port
core.

\subsection{The graph-completed port section}

Let
\[
0=s_0(a)<s_1(a)<\cdots<s_4(a)=1,
\qquad I_i(a)=(s_{i-1}(a),s_i(a)),
\]
with widths \(p_i(a)>0\).  Put
\[
\widehat\Sigma_a=igsqcup_{i=1}^4
\overline{I_i(a)}^{\,g}\times[0,1],
\]
where every endpoint of an interval has its two oriented graph-completion
copies.  A point of \(\widehat\Sigma_a\) therefore carries its incoming port
label even at a seam.  On the interior of port \(i\), define
\[
Q_i(q,p)=\frac{q-s_{i-1}(a)}{p_i(a)},
\qquad
P_i(q,p)=s_{i-1}(a)+p_i(a)p.
\]
Let \(j=j(Q_i)\) be the unique next-port label for which
\(Q_i\in I_j(a)\), with the corresponding one-sided label on the graph
completion, and set
\[
F_a(i,q,p)=\bigl(j,Q_i(q,p),P_i(q,p)\bigr).
\]

\begin{lemma}[One-section exact symplectic recutting]
\label{lem:r6-a1-section}
The map \(F_a:\widehat\Sigma_a\to\widehat\Sigma_a\) is a bijective,
stratum-preserving piecewise \(C^\infty\) diffeomorphism.  On each stratum it
preserves \(dq\wedge dp\) and has exact primitive
\[
S_{a,i}(q,P)=
\frac{(q-s_{i-1}(a))(P-s_{i-1}(a))}{p_i(a)}.
\]
In affine fixed-port coordinates it depends \(C^m\) on \(a\) whenever the
widths do.  Its symbolic factor is the full four-symbol shift.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
On each rectangle
\(I_i(a)\cap Q_i^{-1}(I_j(a))\times(0,1)\), the formula is affine and its
Jacobian matrix is
\[
\begin{pmatrix}p_i(a)^{-1}&0\\0&p_i(a)\end{pmatrix},
\]
so the determinant is one and the symplectic form is preserved.  The inverse
is obtained by reading the previous horizontal strip label from the second
coordinate and applying the inverse affine formula.  Oriented endpoint copies
make both label assignments single valued at every seam, hence the branch
maps paste to a bijection of the graph completion.  The displayed generating
function gives \(Q=\partial_PS_{a,i}\) and
\(p=\partial_qS_{a,i}\).  After the affine changes
\(u=(q-s_{i-1})/p_i\), \(v=p\), every branch has fixed domain and its
parameter dependence is carried by smooth coefficients.  Since \(Q_i\)
ranges over \((0,1)\), every next label occurs on a nonempty open rectangle.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Autonomous Hamiltonian suspension}

Define the mapping torus
\[
\mathcal M_a=
\bigl(\widehat\Sigma_a\times[0,1]_\tau\times\mathbb R_E\bigr)
\big/igl((x,1,E)\sim(F_ax,0,E)\bigr).
\]
On every corner stratum set
\[
\Omega_a=dq\wedge dp+dE\wedge d\tau,
\qquad H_a=E.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Full graph-completed Hamiltonian return flow]
\label{thm:r6-a1-suspension}
The quotient \(\mathcal M_a\) is a Hausdorff symplectic manifold with
corners.  Its Hamiltonian flow is complete, has return time one on
\(\widehat\Sigma_a\times\{0\}\times\{E_0\}\), and has first-return map
exactly \(F_a\).  Moreover it admits a smooth autonomous impact-network
realization obtained by replacing each affine branch by a compactly supported
Hamiltonian suspension in a disjoint port cobordism; the replacement is
symplectically conjugate to the mapping-torus flow near the return section and
preserves section flux and return time.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The graph of the stratum-preserving homeomorphism \(F_a\) is closed in the
compact graph-completed section.  Therefore the equivalence relation defining
the mapping torus is closed and the quotient is Hausdorff.  Corner charts are
mapping cylinders of smooth diffeomorphisms between finite orthants.  Since
\(F_a^*(dq\wedge dp)=dq\wedge dp\), the two boundary traces of \(\Omega_a\)
agree and the form descends.  It is nondegenerate on every stratum.  The
Hamiltonian vector field of \(E\) is \(\partial_\tau\), so one unit of time
reaches the glued boundary and applies \(F_a\).

For the smooth network realization, use the exact branch primitive from
Lemma~\ref{lem:r6-a1-section}.  On a disjoint Darboux copy of each incoming
port, the translation, diagonal symplectic dilation, and target translation
are time-one maps of explicit linear Hamiltonians.  Concatenate them in three
disjoint time windows.  Because the port components are disjoint, the four
Hamiltonians have disjoint spatial support.  A relative extension across the
collar is obtained by integrating the compactly supported exact difference of
the branch primitives; the resulting Hamiltonian vector field vanishes on
both collar boundaries.  The recutting hub is the coordinate identity on the
graph completion and is inserted only after the branch cobordisms.  Standard
autonomization with the pair \((\tau,E)\) gives an autonomous flow.  The
conjugacy is the identity on the return section, hence preserves the return
map, time, and Liouville flux.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Exact mechanical work on every port point}

Split one flight into a work layer \(0<\tau<1/3\), a branch layer
\(1/3<\tau<2/3\), and a recutting layer \(2/3<\tau<1\).  In the work layer
the phase space is the disjoint union of the four incoming port cylinders.
Choose \(\rho\in C_c^\infty((0,1/3))\) with
\(\int_0^1\rho(\tau)d\tau=1\).  Define
\[
\alpha_a|_{\{i\}\times\overline{I_i}^{\,g}\times[0,1]}
=\epsilon_i\rho(\tau)d\tau.
\]
Extend it by zero through the branch and recutting layers.

\begin{proposition}[Full-port work form]
\label{prop:r6-a1-work}
The one-form \(\alpha_a\) is globally smooth on the port cobordism and
descends to the autonomous suspension.  For every regular section point in
port \(i\), including points arbitrarily close to a seam,
\[
\int_0^1\alpha_a(X_{H_a})\,dt=\epsilon_i.
\]
Consequently the mechanical work of the first \(n\) returns is exactly
\[
W_n(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}\epsilon_{\iota(F_a^kx)}
\]
on the full regular Liouville path law.  The equality is not restricted to a
port core and remains true when consecutive labels differ.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Before the branch and recutting layers, the four port components are disjoint
closed manifolds with corners.  A constant coefficient on each component is
therefore smooth; no cutoff in the section coordinate is required.  The form
vanishes near the temporal interfaces, so its traces agree under every later
gluing.  Along the work layer, \(X_{H_a}=\partial_\tau\) and the port label is
constant.  Integration gives \(\epsilon_i\int\rho=\epsilon_i\).  Concatenating
return segments proves the additive formula.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Physical trace spaces and all-order shape response}

Let \(\mathcal C_a\) be the finite cell decomposition of
\(\widehat\Sigma_a\) by the current and next-port labels.  For integers
\(r\ge0\), \(m\ge0\), and \(0<\eta<1\), define
\[
\mathscr B_a^{m,r,\eta}=
\bigoplus_{C\in\mathcal C_a}C^{m+r,\eta}(\overline C)
\oplus
\bigoplus_{\gamma\in\partial\mathcal C_a}
\bigoplus_{\ell=0}^{r}C^{m+r-\ell,\eta}(\gamma),
\]
with the compatibility relation that the boundary coordinates are the
oriented one-sided trace jets of the cell functions.  Affine fixed-cell maps
identify these spaces with one Banach space \(\mathscr B_*^{m,r,\eta}\).
The physical transfer operator \(\mathcal L_a\) acts by inverse branches on
cell coordinates and by the induced triangular trace-jet action on boundary
coordinates.

\begin{lemma}[Bounded seam flux and shape derivative]
\label{lem:r6-a1-shape}
For \(r\ge m\), the conjugated operators
\(J_a\mathcal L_aJ_a^{-1}\) are \(C^m\) in
\(\mathcal L(\mathscr B_*^{m,r,\eta},\mathscr B_*^{0,r-m,\eta})\).
Every seam flux and every derivative of a seam flux up to order \(m\) is a
bounded functional on the corresponding trace coordinate.  The first
physical derivative is
\[
D_a\langle G,\nu_a\rangle
=\langle D_a^{\rm bulk}G,\nu_a\rangle
+\langle (D_a\mathcal L_a)S_aQ_aG,\nu_a\rangle
+\sum_\gamma \dot s_\gamma(a)
\langle [G]_{\gamma},\mathfrak j_{\gamma}\rangle.
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
In fixed-cell coordinates the branch maps and Jacobians are smooth rational
functions of the positive widths \(p_i(a)\).  A parameter derivative either
differentiates a bulk coefficient or moves one face.  The distributional
shape derivative of a moved face is its oriented trace; subsequent derivatives
produce the higher trace jets already included in the definition of
\(\mathscr B_a^{m,r,\eta}\).  Because the cell complex is finite, the sum of
all bulk and trace blocks is bounded uniformly on compact parameter sets.
The spectral gap on the finite-branch expanding factor gives the reduced
resolvent \(S_a=(I-\mathcal L_a)^{-1}Q_a\).  Differentiating the invariant
pairing and applying the Reynolds transport formula yields the displayed
identity.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[All-order physical and symbolic response]
\label{thm:r6-a1-response}
For every \(m\), observables in
\(\mathscr B_a^{m,m,\eta}\) have parameter derivatives through order \(m\).
Each derivative is an absolutely convergent finite sum of words in the bulk
operator derivatives, reduced resolvents, projectors, and trace-jet fluxes.
The same quantities are obtained from centered finite differences under the
common uniform-root coupling.  The symbolic Bernoulli response is the factor
of this physical formula obtained by discarding zero jump traces.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Iterate Lemma~\ref{lem:r6-a1-shape}.  At derivative order \(k\), at most
\(k\) trace derivatives are created, all of which are coordinates of
\(\mathscr B_a^{m,m,\eta}\).  Between insertions, the centered transfer
operator contracts by a uniform factor, so every time-index sum is geometric.
Taylor's formula in the fixed-cell Banach space gives convergence of centered
finite differences.  Under the common-root coupling, crossing a moving seam
produces precisely the oriented trace term, while points not crossing a seam
contribute the bulk derivative.  Dominated convergence identifies the two
forms of the response.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Preparation and conditional calibration}

The multinomial current preparation on the common symbolic factor has
canonical likelihood
\[
\frac{d\mathbb P_\theta}{d\mathbb P_0}
=\exp\{\theta W_n-n[Q(\theta)-Q(0)]\}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Conditional mechanical--valuation identification]
\label{thm:r6-a1-calibration}
A smooth cash-additive, law-invariant, strongly time-consistent certainty
equivalent generated by one utility is affine or entropic with a constant
coefficient \(\vartheta\).  It has \(\vartheta=\theta\) exactly when its
likelihood cocycle is required to equal the canonical mechanical work cocycle
in the same units.  Mechanics alone does not select the coefficient of an
unrelated preference functional.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Cash additivity gives the translation functional equation for the utility;
two differentiations make absolute risk aversion constant.  Strong time
consistency makes that constant independent of the time split.  Equality of
the two normalized likelihoods on the nonconstant random variable \(W_n\)
forces \((\vartheta-\theta)W_n\) to be constant, hence
\(\vartheta=\theta\).  The converse is immediate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Round-six A1 closure]
\label{thm:r6-a1-main}
The four-port family possesses one well-defined graph-completed symplectic
return map, an autonomous Hamiltonian suspension, a smooth full-port work form
whose integral is exactly the branch current on the complete regular
Liouville section, and a fully typed all-order physical response calculus.
The valuation identification is precisely the conditional compatibility
statement of Theorem~\ref{thm:r6-a1-calibration}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Combine Lemma~\ref{lem:r6-a1-section},
Theorem~\ref{thm:r6-a1-suspension},
Proposition~\ref{prop:r6-a1-work}, and
Theorems~\ref{thm:r6-a1-response} and
\ref{thm:r6-a1-calibration}.
\end{proof}
