\section{Round-six positive closure: ambient trace fields and the complete vector--roof packet}
\label{sec:r6-a2}

The parameter dependence is placed on one fixed ambient distribution space.
Geometric components which are absent at a particular radius are represented
by zero realization coefficients, not by replacing a nonzero Banach summand
with the zero space.  Parameter differentiation is closed by retaining trace
jets through the required order.  The local-limit statement is then derived
from one Fourier theorem and is separated into local, central, and saturated
window regimes.

\subsection{A fixed all-depth ambient field}

Let \(\mathfrak G\) be the countable set of triples
\(\gamma=(w,\sigma,\ell)\), where \(w\) is a finite collision word,
\(\sigma\) records a singularity or homogeneity face and its side, and
\(\ell\in\{0,1,2\}\) is the normal trace-jet order.  Write
\(d(\gamma)=|w|\).  Every label is assigned a fixed reference interval
\(J_\gamma=[-1,1]\), whether or not the corresponding geometric face is
present for a given radius.  For \(0<\zeta<1\), define
\[
\mathbb B^{(2)}=
\mathcal B^{\rm bulk}\oplus
\bigoplus_{\gamma\in\mathfrak G}^{\ell^1}
\zeta^{d(\gamma)}C^{1-\ell/2}(J_\gamma),
\]
and its weak companion \(\mathbb B_w^{(2)}\) by lowering every bulk and trace
regularity exponent once.  The bulk space is the standard stable-curve
anisotropic completion with fixed homogeneity coordinates.

For each radius \(R\), a geometric realization map
\(E_R:\mathbb B^{(2)}\to\mathscr D'(M_R)\) sends a trace coordinate to the
corresponding oriented current multiplied by a smooth incidence amplitude
\(a_\gamma(R)\).  The amplitude is zero when the component is absent and
vanishes to second order at a birth or death.  Near a tangency, the normal
coordinate is the signed square-root coordinate, so the continued incidence
map and the amplitude are \(C^2\) in \(R\).

\begin{lemma}[Fixed ambient realization]
\label{lem:r6-a2-ambient}
The maps \(R\mapsto E_R\) are \(C^2\) from the compact radius interval into
\(\mathcal L(\mathbb B^{(2)},\mathscr D')\).  Their physical ranges are
closed and the physical distribution represented by an ambient vector is
independent of the chosen local continuation chart.  At a birth or death no
invertibility between a zero and a nonzero trace space is asserted or needed.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
At every finite depth, singularity curves are finite unions of graph arcs
between tangencies.  Continue each graph in the signed square-root normal
coordinate and multiply it by a fixed cutoff in that coordinate.  When a
component is absent, its incidence amplitude is identically zero; when it is
born, the square of the signed normal displacement supplies an amplitude with
zero first and second incompatible traces.  Thus every label remains in the
same ambient direct sum and only its realization coefficient changes.

A depth-\(n\) family has at most \(C_0C_1^n\) members, while the stable-curve
growth lemma supplies a current norm at most \(C_2\vartheta^n\).  Choose
\(\zeta\) so that \(C_1\vartheta\zeta<1\).  The realization series and its
first two parameter derivatives then converge absolutely.  On chart overlaps
the two continuations represent the same oriented one-sided distribution, so
the realized physical current agrees.  Closedness follows from completeness
of the weighted direct sum and continuity of the realization map on each
finite-depth projection, followed by the vanishing weighted tail.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The complete ambient transfer operator}

The collision transfer operator lifts to
\[
\mathbb L_R=
\begin{pmatrix}
L_R^{bb}&0\\
L_R^{cb}&L_R^{cc}
\end{pmatrix}
\]
on \(\mathbb B^{(2)}\).  The block \(L_R^{cb}\) records newly created
oriented traces, and \(L_R^{cc}\) transports all trace jets, including the
normal derivative generated by a second parameter differentiation.  For a
vector displacement \(\kappa_R\) and roof \(\tau_R\), multiplication by
\(e^{\langle\xi,\kappa_R\rangle-s\tau_R}\) acts on every bulk coordinate and
on each one-sided trace by its corresponding trace jet.

\begin{theorem}[Uniform ambient spectral bundle]
\label{thm:r6-a2-spectrum}
There are \(\delta>0\), \(C<\infty\), and \(0<\rho<1\) such that, uniformly
in \(R\), the twisted operator
\[
\mathbb L_{R,\xi,s}
=\mathbb L_RM_{R,\xi,s}
\]
is a \(C^2\) family in \(R\), analytic in
\((\xi,s)\in\mathbb C^2\times\mathbb C\) for
\(|\xi|+|s|<\delta\), and has
\[
\mathbb L_{R,\xi,s}^n
=e^{nP_R(\xi,s)}\Pi_{R,\xi,s}+N_{R,\xi,s}^n,
\qquad
\|N_{R,\xi,s}^n\|_{\mathbb B^{(2)}\to\mathbb B_w^{(2)}}
\le C\rho^ne^{n\Re P_R(\xi,s)}.
\]
The physical range of \(E_R\) is invariant, and the leading spectral data on
that range agree with the physical billiard transfer operator.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
On homogeneous stable curves the usual one-step expansion and distortion
estimates give
\[
\|L_R^nh\|_s\le C\rho_0^n\|h\|_s+C\|h\|_w.
\]
A newly cut trace has one factor of inverse expansion.  A depth-\(d\) trace
therefore carries the bound \(C\vartheta^d\), and the choice in
Lemma~\ref{lem:r6-a2-ambient} makes the sum over all descendants geometric.
The trace-to-trace blocks are triangular in depth and jet order; a second
parameter derivative can increase the jet order by at most two, which is why
\(\ell=0,1,2\) was retained.  These estimates yield a uniform strong/weak
Lasota--Yorke inequality and compact embedding of the strong unit ball into
the weak topology: truncate the depth, use compactness on finitely many bulk
and trace coordinates, and then let the geometric tail vanish.

The displacement is branchwise constant and the roof has the same weighted
one-sided derivatives as the Jacobian.  Hence the multiplier and its trace
jets are bounded and analytic.  Uniform quasicompactness and mixing make the
unit eigenvalue simple.  A common resolvent contour gives the Riesz projector
and its first two \(R\)-derivatives.  Finally, the definitions of
\(L^{cb}\) and \(L^{cc}\) are precisely the distributional boundary terms in
\(E_R\mathbb L_R=L_RE_R\); this proves invariance and spectral agreement.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Arithmetic and temporal certificates}

Choose one finite-connector Young magnet.  On its stable quotient, retain the
two triangular return words \(w_+\) and \(w_-\) and four admissible periodic
return words \(v_1,\ldots,v_4\).  Let
\[
\mathbf c_R(v)=\bigl(S_{|v|}\kappa_R(v),S_{|v|}\tau_R(v),|v|\bigr).
\]
The words are chosen so that the three differences
\(\mathbf c_R(v_j)-\mathbf c_R(v_1)\), \(j=2,3,4\), form a matrix whose
determinant is bounded away from zero for all radii.  Direct differentiation
of the reduced polygonal length of \(w_+\) and \(w_-\) gives a temporal
distance \(D_R\) on a common quotient interval with
\[
\inf_R\inf_x|D_R'(x)|\ge c_{\rm UNI}>0.
\]

\begin{lemma}[Joint arithmetic certificate]
\label{lem:r6-a2-arithmetic}
If
\(u\cdot\kappa_R-t\tau_R\) is a measurable coboundary modulo a constant on
the quotient, with \(u\in[-\pi,\pi]^2\), then \((u,t)=(0,0)\).  The joint
asymptotic covariance of \((\kappa_R,\tau_R)\) is uniformly positive definite
on every compact real tilted chart.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
A coboundary has zero sum on every periodic word.  The four-word determinant
therefore forces its three coefficients \((u_1,u_2,t)\) to vanish.  In
particular no nonzero lattice character survives.  For covariance, the
spectral projector gives the Gordin decomposition in the ambient strong/weak
pair.  Zero asymptotic variance makes the martingale part vanish, so the
observable is an \(L^2\) coboundary.  The quotient transfer equation upgrades
it to a dynamically Hölder coboundary; the one-sided trace jets match because
the ambient equation holds coordinatewise.  The periodic certificate then
forces the coefficient vector to vanish.  Continuity of the Hessian and
compactness of the parameter/source set give a uniform lower eigenvalue.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The complete Dolgopyat estimate}

For \(|t|\ge1\), use the frequency-adapted norm
\[
\|h\|_{(t)}=\|h\|_w+|t|^{-1}\|h\|_s
\]
on the quotient bulk and the identical depth-weighted norm on trace jets.
Let \(\mathscr C_t\) be the cone of positive envelopes with logarithmic
oscillation at most \(C|t|\) on quotient intervals of length
\(c|t|^{-1}\).

\begin{lemma}[Return and non-concentration]
\label{lem:r6-a2-return}
There are \(n_t\le C\log(2+|t|)\), \(c_*>0\), and one finite family of
standard subintervals such that every normalized standard family has at least
mass \(c_*\) returning to the UNI interval after \(n_t\) iterates.  The mass
within distance \(r\) of all cutoff endpoints is at most \(Cr^\alpha\),
uniformly in \(R\).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Iterate the growth lemma until all long unstable components have length at
least the magnet scale; this requires at most \(C\log(2+|t|)\) iterates when
the initial scale is \(|t|^{-1}\).  Finite primitivity of the connector words
sends a fixed fraction of each long component into the UNI base.  Bounded
distortion makes the fraction uniform.  Homogeneity-strip distortion and the
one-step expansion estimate give the power bound for neighborhoods of the
finite cutoff family; summing descendants is geometric in the ambient depth
weight.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Paired cancellation operator]
\label{lem:r6-a2-cancel}
There are cutoff functions \(\chi_t\), constant on trace-compatible one-sided
cells, and positive operators \(\mathcal N_t\) preserving \(\mathscr C_t\)
such that
\[
|\mathbb L_{R,iu,it}^{n_t}h|\le\mathcal N_tH
\quad\text{whenever }|h|\le H\in\mathscr C_t,
\]
and
\[
\int(\mathcal N_tH)^2\,dm
\le(1-c_0)\int H^2\,dm.
\]
The same inequality holds for the depth-weighted sum of all trace jets.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
On every returned UNI interval, pair the inverse branches generated by
\(w_+\) and \(w_-\).  Their phase difference has derivative at least
\(c_{\rm UNI}|t|\).  Subdivide into intervals of length \(c|t|^{-1}\), choose
\(\chi_t\) to reduce the larger branch on alternating subintervals, and use
the elementary two-vector inequality
\[
|z_1+z_2|^2\le(1-c)|z_1|^2+(1-c)|z_2|^2
\]
whenever their phase separation is bounded away from zero.  The cutoff is
constant on the two one-sided traces of each retained cell, so creation and
transport of trace jets commute with it.  Lemma~\ref{lem:r6-a2-return}
controls the cutoff boundary loss.  Outside the paired set use the positive
untwisted envelope.  The returned mass \(c_*\) yields one fixed total
\(L^2\) loss.  Distortion and the frequency-adapted logarithmic cone bounds
show that \(\mathcal N_tH\) remains in \(\mathscr C_t\).
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform high-frequency contraction]
\label{thm:r6-a2-dolgopyat}
There are \(A,c,C>0\) such that for all \(|t|\ge1\), all
\(u\in[-\pi,\pi]^2\), and all \(n\),
\[
\|\mathbb L_{R,iu,it}^n\|_{\mathbb B^{(2)}\to\mathbb B_w^{(2)}}
\le C(1+|t|)^A
\exp\left\{-\frac{cn}{\log(2+|t|)}\right\}.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Apply Lemma~\ref{lem:r6-a2-cancel} every \(n_t\) iterates.  Cone recovery
between cancellation blocks follows from the strong/weak Lasota--Yorke
inequality; non-concentrated cutoff losses are absorbed by decreasing
\(c_0\).  Thus each block contracts the weak envelope by a fixed factor.
There are at least \(n/(C\log(2+|t|))-1\) complete blocks.  Conversion between
the frequency-adapted and ordinary norms contributes only a polynomial factor
in \(|t|\).  The triangular trace blocks obey the same recurrence and their
depth sums are geometric.  This gives the displayed estimate.  Compact
nonzero frequency annuli are handled by Lemma~\ref{lem:r6-a2-arithmetic} and
upper semicontinuity of the spectral radius.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A master vector--roof Fourier theorem}

Let \(K_n=S_n\kappa_R\in\mathbb Z^2\) and
\(T_n=S_n\tau_R\).  On a real tilted chart write the covariance in block
form
\[
\Sigma=
\begin{pmatrix}\Sigma_{KK}&\Sigma_{KT}\\
\Sigma_{TK}&\Sigma_{TT}\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\sigma_c^2=\Sigma_{TT}-\Sigma_{TK}\Sigma_{KK}^{-1}\Sigma_{KT}>0.
\]
For \(k_n-na=O(\sqrt n)\), define the conditional roof center
\[
m_n=nb+\Sigma_{TK}\Sigma_{KK}^{-1}(k_n-na)
\]
and
\[
G_n=\Phi\left(\frac{t_n+b_n-m_n}{\sqrt n\sigma_c}\right)
-\Phi\left(\frac{t_n-b_n-m_n}{\sqrt n\sigma_c}\right).
\]

\begin{theorem}[Uniform master local-limit estimate]
\label{thm:r6-a2-master-llt}
Let \(F\) be a fixed central cylinder.  Uniformly on compact radius and tilt
sets, for \(|t_n-m_n|=O(\sqrt n)\) and \(0<b_n=o(n)\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\mathbb E_0[F;K_n=k_n,\ |T_n-t_n|\le b_n]\\
&\quad=e^{-nI(k_n/n,t_n/n)}
\frac{e^{-\frac12x_n^T\Sigma_{KK}^{-1}x_n}}
{2\pi n\sqrt{\det\Sigma_{KK}}}
\left(\nu_{\xi,s}(F)G_n+\mathcal R_n\right),
\end{aligned}
\]
where \(x_n=(k_n-na)/\sqrt n\) and
\[
|\mathcal R_n|\le Cn^{-1/2}(1+|x_n|^3)+o(1)
\]
with the first term replaced by \(Cn^{-1/2}G_n\) whenever the interval is
smoothed on a scale \(\omega_n\) satisfying
\(1\ll\omega_n\ll b_n\).  Consequently the displayed formula is a relative
asymptotic whenever \(G_n\gg n^{-1/2}\); for smaller windows the same Fourier
argument gives the corresponding smoothed density local limit rather than an
invalid uniform relative statement.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Fourier inversion is over \([ -\pi,\pi]^2\times\mathbb R\).  In the ball
\(|(u,t)|\le n^{-2/5}\), Theorem~\ref{thm:r6-a2-spectrum} gives the cubic
Taylor expansion of the leading eigenvalue and uniform expansions of both
projectors around the central insertion.  Integrating the Gaussian in the
roof frequency over the interval transform gives exactly \(G_n\).  The cubic
remainder and projector derivatives give the stated central error.

On the intermediate region, positivity from
Lemma~\ref{lem:r6-a2-arithmetic} gives
\(e^{-cn|(u,t)|^2}\).  On compact minor arcs the spectral radius is uniformly
below one, and on unbounded roof frequencies use
Theorem~\ref{thm:r6-a2-dolgopyat}.  Smooth upper and lower interval
approximations have Fourier tails controlled by powers of
\((\omega_n|t|)^{-1}\).  The trace-jet extension of a central cylinder is
finite rank, so the same estimates hold with the insertion.  Combining the
regions proves the formula and the two forms of the error estimate.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[The three correct roof-window regimes]
\label{cor:r6-a2-regimes}
Under the hypotheses of Theorem~\ref{thm:r6-a2-master-llt}:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item if \(b_n\to\infty\) and \(b_n=o(\sqrt n)\), then
\[
G_n\sim\frac{2b_n}{\sqrt{2\pi n}\sigma_c}
\exp\left[-\frac{(t_n-m_n)^2}{2n\sigma_c^2}\right];
\]
\item if \(b_n/\sqrt n\to\beta\in(0,\infty)\), then \(G_n\) converges to
the corresponding nondegenerate Gaussian interval probability;
\item if \(b_n/\sqrt n\to\infty\) and
\(|t_n-m_n|=o(b_n)\), then \(G_n\to1\), so the total order is the
\(n^{-1}\) two-dimensional lattice local-limit order.
\end{enumerate}
The conditional central-window law converges to the stationary tilted path
law whenever the denominator falls in one of these relative-error regimes.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
These are the three elementary asymptotics of the Gaussian interval bracket.
Apply the master estimate to the numerator with \(F\) and to the denominator
with \(F=1\); the common lattice and exponential factors cancel.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Physical pressure root}

Since \(\partial_sP_R(0,0)=-\nu_R(\tau_R)<0\), the equation
\(P_R(\xi,\mathcal H_R(\xi))=0\) defines the physical pressure root.

\begin{theorem}[Round-six A2 closure]
\label{thm:r6-a2-main}
The compact Sinai radius family has a coherent fixed ambient all-depth
bulk/trace field, a twice parameter-differentiable vector--roof spectral
packet, a complete uniform Dolgopyat estimate, a positive joint covariance,
and the master local-limit theorem with its local, central, and saturated
window regimes.  Higher-block insertions and ratio conditioning are valid in
the relative-error regimes stated above.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Combine Theorems~\ref{thm:r6-a2-spectrum},
\ref{thm:r6-a2-dolgopyat}, and
\ref{thm:r6-a2-master-llt}, together with
Lemma~\ref{lem:r6-a2-arithmetic} and
Corollary~\ref{cor:r6-a2-regimes}.  The implicit-function theorem applied to
the simple pressure eigenvalue supplies the physical root and its response
formulas.
\end{proof}
