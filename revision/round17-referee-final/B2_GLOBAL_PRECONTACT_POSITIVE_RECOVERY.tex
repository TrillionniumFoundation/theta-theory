\section{Round-seventeen positive closure: global pre-contact geometry and positive recovery}
\label{sec:r17-b2}

The grand-canonical construction in this section is logically prior to B1.
A surplus collision is imposed as the zero set of a genuine pre-contact map
on an open trajectory-parameter domain.  Reflection is introduced only on
that zero set.  The lower bound uses a density theorem for positive balanced
pairs; no entropy penalty is asked to create a vector outside the positive
collision cone.

\subsection{Compatible traces and exact balance}

Let \(\mathfrak X_T\) be the space of finite space--time transport currents
with velocity weight \(1+|v|^m\), \(m>8\), whose distributional transport
divergence is a finite measure.  At the hard-sphere boundary use the incoming
normal-flux trace measure; the factor
\(((v-v_*)\cdot\omega)_+\) is part of that measure and is not multiplied a
second time.

\begin{theorem}[Closed Green trace graph]
\label{thm:r17-b2-trace}
The free-transport generator with specular binary boundary relation has a
closed graph in \(\mathfrak X_T\).  For every smooth weighted test \(p\), the
empirical density \(\pi^\varepsilon\) and actual incoming contact measure
\(\Gamma^\varepsilon\) satisfy exactly
\[
 \langle p_T,\pi_T^\varepsilon\rangle-
 \langle p_0,\pi_0^\varepsilon\rangle-
 \int_0^T\langle(\partial_t+v\cdot\nabla_x)p,
                  \pi_t^\varepsilon\rangle dt
 =\int\Delta_\omega p\,d\Gamma^\varepsilon.
\]
The relation is closed under weighted weak convergence on entropy/moment
sublevels.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Apply the divergence theorem on every free-flight cylinder.  Interior time
faces telescope.  On a binary contact face the outgoing trace is the
specular push-forward of the incoming flux trace, and their difference is
\(\Delta_\omega p\).  Grazing and multiple contacts have zero Liouville flux.
Closedness follows by velocity truncation, trace compactness for
divergence-measure fields, and entropy uniform integrability of the collision
weight; then let the truncation radius tend to infinity.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Open pre-contact charts}

Fix a labelled collision genealogy \(G\) with strictly ordered creation
contacts.  Its open parameter space \(U_G\) consists of root positions and
velocities, creation times and normals, and clustering parameters satisfying:
strict time separation, non-grazing incidence, and absence of unintended
contacts before the first surplus time.  The collision-tree pseudo-trajectories
are constructed recursively using only already imposed creation contacts.
Before imposing the first surplus collision between labels \(a,b\), define
\[
 \mathcal F_G(\theta,t,\omega)
 =x_a^\theta(t^-)-x_b^\theta(t^-)-\varepsilon\omega.
\]
This map is smooth on the open set \(U_G\times(t_-,t_+)\times\mathbb S^2\).
The surplus-contact manifold is \(\mathcal F_G^{-1}(0)\) with incoming sign.
Only on this manifold are the specular reflection and the later chronology
continued.

\begin{lemma}[Global regular pre-contact atlas]
\label{lem:r17-b2-precontact}
For every finite realized genealogy \(G\), its regular surplus-contact set is
covered by finitely many charts on which one \(3\times3\) minor of
\(D_{(\theta,t,\omega)}\mathcal F_G\) is bounded below.  The complement is a
finite union of analytic sublevel strata.  There are constants
\(C_G,\alpha_G>0\) such that its \(\delta\)-neighborhood has conditional
trajectory measure at most \(C_G\delta^{\alpha_G}\).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The map is analytic between collision times.  At a regular zero, time changes
the component of relative position parallel to relative velocity, the two
normal coordinates change the tangent components of \(-\varepsilon\omega\),
and a root or last-creation velocity changes the remaining transverse
component through the true hard-sphere Jacobi map.  Non-grazing incidence
makes these three directions independent.  For every realized combinatorial
pattern, a witness is constructed inductively: place the creation collisions
in disjoint spatial tubes with separated time intervals, solve the incoming
linear flight equations backwards, and choose the final root velocity to hit
the surplus contact.  Strict inequalities exclude unintended contacts and
persist on an open neighborhood.  Thus the relevant analytic minor is not
identically zero.  Compact truncation and analytic sublevel estimates give
\(C_G,\alpha_G\); finitely many minors cover the regular zero set.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Physical first-surplus coarea gain]
\label{lem:r17-b2-gain}
For fixed \(G\), after integrating the true incoming variables and imposing
\(\mathcal F_G=0\) by coarea, the first surplus contact contributes at most
\[
 C_G(1+T+\mathbb V)^{C_G}\varepsilon^{\alpha_G}
\]
relative to the corresponding creation tree.  The estimate includes bounded
actual-contact marks and retains the true reflected future.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
On the regular charts, coarea divides by a minor bounded away from zero and
the contact tube has transverse volume \(O(\varepsilon^2)\).  On the analytic
sublevel strata choose the threshold optimizing the regular tube bound and
the sublevel measure from Lemma~\ref{lem:r17-b2-precontact}.  Reflection is
then applied on the zero set and is a smooth measure-preserving involution, so
all later contacts are the physical future.  Bounded marks change only the
constant.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Fixed-horizon genealogy summation}

Let \(W_G^\varepsilon(h,\psi)\) be the absolute connected trajectory weight
with a path source \(h\) and a mark on every actual incoming contact
\(\psi\).  Creation-tree coarea and the Gaussian velocity activity give
\[
 \sum_{|G|=k}W_G^\varepsilon(h,\psi)
 \le k!C^kT^{k-1}
\]
on a fixed short-time source ball.

\begin{theorem}[Two-limit all-contact majorant]
\label{thm:r17-b2-majorant}
For every source radius \(R\) and fixed short horizon \(T\), there are
finite-genealogy approximations \(Q_{T,K}^{\varepsilon}\) such that
\[
 \lim_{K\to\infty}\limsup_{\varepsilon\downarrow0}
 {1\over\mu_\varepsilon}
 \bigl|\log Z_T^\varepsilon-
       \log Z_{T,K}^\varepsilon\bigr|=0,
\]
and, for fixed \(K\), all surplus-contact contributions vanish as
\(\varepsilon\downarrow0\).  The statement remains true after any fixed
number of source derivatives.  No genealogy-uniform Lojasiewicz exponent is
assumed.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
First truncate the number of labels, contacts, and velocity.  Only finitely
many genealogies remain, so Lemma~\ref{lem:r17-b2-gain} may be applied with
the minimum of their positive exponents.  Then let \(\varepsilon\to0\).
The discarded genealogy tail is bounded in normalized log partition function
by the tail of the absolutely convergent series
\(\sum_k C^kT^{k-1}\).  Letting \(K\to\infty\) after the Boltzmann--Grad limit
gives the assertion.  Cauchy estimates on a slightly larger source ball give
the derivative statement.  Time slicing repeats the argument on finitely many
blocks and permits every bounded real source on the fixed horizon.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Grand-canonical actual-contact pressure]
\label{thm:r17-b2-pressure}
The normalized grand-canonical log-Laplace functional of
\((\pi^\varepsilon,\Gamma^\varepsilon)\) converges locally uniformly, with
all fixed source derivatives, to the unique analytic mild solution generated
by
\[
 \mathbb H(f,p,\psi)
 =\langle f,v\cdot\nabla_xp\rangle
 +{1\over2}\int ff_*B
       \bigl(e^{\Delta p+\psi}-1\bigr).
\]
Every \(\psi\)-derivative marks an actual contact.  This theorem uses no
microcanonical input.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The logarithm is the sum of connected real-trajectory genealogies.  Apply
Theorem~\ref{thm:r17-b2-majorant}; creation trees converge by ordinary
collision coarea and the first-surplus estimate removes cyclic corrections.
Selecting the last creation contact factorizes the two rooted subtrees and
gives the displayed Hamiltonian.  Normal convergence proves analyticity and
uniqueness by Picard iteration in the trajectory norm.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Positive balanced approximation}

For a density \(f\), let
\[
 dA_f={1\over2}ff_*B\,dt\,dx\,dv\,dv_*\,d\omega.
\]
A finite-action pair is balanced and has
\(\Gamma=qA_f\) with \(q\ge0\) and
\(\int\ell(q)dA_f<\infty\).

Let \(S_\delta\) be the conservative smoothing operator obtained by
convolving in \((t,x)\) and, at fixed center-of-mass and relative speed,
convolving the relative direction on the collision sphere.  It commutes with
the elastic pre/post involution and with \(\Delta^*\).

\begin{theorem}[Rate-dense strictly positive balanced pairs]
\label{thm:r17-b2-density}
For every finite-action balanced pair \((f,\Gamma)\), there are smooth pairs
\((f_n,\Gamma_n)\) such that:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item \(f_n>0\), \(\Gamma_n=q_nA_{f_n}\), and
      \(0<c_n\le q_n\le C_n<\infty\);
\item the exact balance equation and all collision symmetries hold;
\item \((f_n,\Gamma_n)\to(f,\Gamma)\) in the weighted weak path topology;
\item
\[
 I_0(f_{n,0})+\int\ell(q_n)dA_{f_n}
 \longrightarrow I_0(f_0)+\int\ell(q)dA_f.
\]
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
First truncate velocities and replace the tail by a Maxwellian with the same
mass, momentum, and energy.  Mix the balanced pair with \(\delta\) times a
strictly positive Maxwellian Boltzmann solution and its natural collision
flow.  Balance is linear, the numerator collision measure stays positive, and
the mixed density is strictly positive.  The perspective entropy is jointly
convex, so the action changes by \(o(1)\).

Apply \(S_\delta\) simultaneously to the density current and collision
current.  Commutation with transport and \(\Delta^*\) preserves balance
exactly.  On the compact velocity truncation, strict positivity makes
\(q_n=d\Gamma_n/dA_{f_n}\) bounded above and below.  Standard approximation
of perspective integrals gives action convergence.  A diagonal removal of the
Maxwellian admixture, smoothing, and truncation proves the theorem.  No signed
correction of a positive collision current is used.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Positive source exposure]
\label{thm:r17-b2-exposure}
Every approximant in Theorem~\ref{thm:r17-b2-density} is the unique controlled
Boltzmann solution for the bounded contact source
\(\psi_n=\log q_n\) and its prepared initial activity.  Under the corresponding
microscopic tilt, \((\pi^\varepsilon,\Gamma^\varepsilon)\) concentrates at
\((f_n,\Gamma_n)\), and the logarithmic likelihood cost is exactly
\[
 I_0(f_{n,0})+\int\ell(q_n)dA_{f_n}.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The first derivative of the analytic pressure in
Theorem~\ref{thm:r17-b2-pressure} solves the controlled Boltzmann equation
with intensity \(e^{\psi_n}A_f=q_nA_f\).  The prescribed pair satisfies that
equation and the same initial condition, so short-time uniqueness identifies
it.  The second derivative and exponential moment bounds give concentration.
The Radon--Nikodym formula for the contact tilt is
\(\int\log q_n\,d\Gamma_n-\int(q_n-1)dA_{f_n}\), which is the perspective
entropy.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Full grand-canonical and microcanonical LDPs}

\begin{theorem}[Grand-canonical joint LDP]
\label{thm:r17-b2-gc}
At speed \(\mu_\varepsilon\),
\((\pi^\varepsilon,\Gamma^\varepsilon)\) satisfies a full good LDP with
rate
\[
 I_T^{\rm gc}(f,\Gamma)=I_0^{\rm gc}(f_0)+
 \begin{cases}
 \displaystyle\int\ell(d\Gamma/dA_f)dA_f,
   &(f,\Gamma)\text{ balanced and }\Gamma\ll A_f,\\
 +\infty,&\text{otherwise}.
 \end{cases}
\]
Finite action excludes grazing, multiple-contact, and corner mass.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Finite collections of density/contact tests have log-Laplace limits by
Theorem~\ref{thm:r17-b2-pressure}.  Their convex dual imposes the exact
balance and gives the pointwise entropy conjugate.  Exponential velocity,
contact-mass, and time-modulus bounds give projective exponential tightness;
Dawson--Gartner gives the upper bound and a preliminary projective rate.  The
regular tilted lower bound is Theorem~\ref{thm:r17-b2-exposure};
Theorem~\ref{thm:r17-b2-density} extends it to every finite-rate pair.  Thus
the projective rate equals the displayed action, whose sublevels are compact.
If a limit charged a contact stratum on which \(A_f=0\), absolute continuity
would fail and the entropy would be infinite.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Source-conditioned microcanonical LDP]
\label{thm:r17-b2-mc}
After the exact-number, momentum, energy, and macro shell theorem of B1 is
proved from the grand-canonical pressure above, the primitive microcanonical
law satisfies the same joint LDP with \(I_0^{\rm gc}\) replaced by the B1
constrained initial rate.  The multiplier is reoptimized for every density and
contact source.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
B1 supplies a locally uniform source-dependent shell coefficient with
subexponential probability under the reoptimized tilted law.  Divide the
regular microscopic changes of measure in
Theorem~\ref{thm:r17-b2-exposure} by that coefficient.  The upper bound and
exponential tightness transfer by the same conditioning identity.  The
positive approximation theorem is unaffected by conditioning at time zero.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Round-seventeen B2 closure]
\label{thm:r17-b2-main}
Actual hard-sphere contacts have a global pre-contact coarea construction, a
fixed-horizon genealogy limit, an analytic grand-canonical marked pressure,
a positivity-preserving rate-dense recovery theorem, and full grand-canonical
and source-conditioned microcanonical joint LDPs.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Combine Theorems~\ref{thm:r17-b2-trace},
\ref{thm:r17-b2-majorant}, \ref{thm:r17-b2-pressure},
\ref{thm:r17-b2-density}, \ref{thm:r17-b2-exposure},
\ref{thm:r17-b2-gc}, and \ref{thm:r17-b2-mc}.
\end{proof}
