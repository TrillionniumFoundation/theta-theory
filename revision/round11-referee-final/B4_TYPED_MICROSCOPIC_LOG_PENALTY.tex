\section{Round-eleven positive closure: typed microscopic values and logarithmic comparison}
\label{sec:r11-b4}

Five objects are kept distinct: the microscopic flow, its Koopman semigroup on
observables, the push-forward semigroup on laws, endpoint values obtained by
integrating an observable against a law, and the limiting kinetic action
semigroup.

\begin{theorem}[Exact microscopic tower on the correct domain]
\label{thm:r11-b4-tower}
Let $\Phi_t^\varepsilon$ be the deterministic hard-sphere flow and
$U_t^\varepsilon F=F\circ\Phi_t^\varepsilon$ its Koopman semigroup on the
weighted microscopic observable space.  For an initial law $\rho$,
\[
 \mathcal V_{s,t}^\varepsilon(F;\rho)
 =\mu_\varepsilon^{-1}\log
   \int e^{\mu_\varepsilon U_{t-s}^\varepsilon F(x)}\rho(dx).
\]
The exact tower is obtained by composing the Koopman terminal observable
before integrating.  No Markov closure on the kinetic macrostate is asserted
at finite volume.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The deterministic flow gives $U_{t-r}=U_{s-r}U_{t-s}$.  Substitute this
identity inside the single initial-law integral.  The inner object remains a
microscopic observable, so the domain does not change.  Push-forward on laws
is merely the dual identity.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Observable resolvent core]
\label{thm:r11-b4-core}
On the weighted continuous microscopic observable space, smooth cylinders
satisfying specular matching are dense.  Reflection unfolding near every
binary collision face, grazing cutoffs with vanishing graph norm, and ordinary
mollification give a core for the closed Koopman generator.  Equivalently, the
observable resolvent maps this dense cylinder class to a graph core.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Away from collision faces use weighted Stone--Weierstrass and mollification.
Near a regular face, unfold the two reflected copies and mollify an even
extension; this preserves specular matching.  The flux trace estimate of B2
shows that a cutoff removing a grazing layer has vanishing function and
generator norm.  Triple collisions are codimension at least two and are
removed by a second cutoff.  Thus cylinders approximate every graph-domain
observable, and applying the observable, not state, resolvent preserves graph
convergence.
\end{proof}

Use the exponential-label hierarchy norm
\[
 \|C\|_\alpha=\sum_{j\ge0}\frac{e^{\alpha j}}{j!}\|C_j\|_{\rm gr}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Full triangular corrector]
\label{thm:r11-b4-corrector}
The finite-label terminal-value correctors solve the full triangular BBGKY
observable equation.  If the connected order-$j$ defect is bounded by
$j!C^jT^{j-1}$, then the factor $1/j!$ in the hierarchy norm and the
Boltzmann--Grad coefficient make the complete corrector and its generator
image normally summable on a smaller label radius.  The corrected nonlinear
generator converges on the cylinder core to the Hamilton--Boltzmann
Hamiltonian.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Solve every finite triangular system backward from zero terminal condition by
Duhamel's formula.  Divide its graph estimate by $j!$ before summing; the
remaining geometric series converges after the label-radius loss.  The same
bound holds for the time derivative and generator image, so closedness permits
interchange with the sum.  B2 tree convergence identifies the Hamiltonian and
its cycle estimate removes the remainder.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Kinetic action semigroup]
\label{thm:r11-b4-action}
The good B2 dynamic action, with no repeated initial preparation term, defines
a monotone cash-additive Lax--Oleinik semigroup on weighted uniformly
continuous density/contact terminal data.  It satisfies exact dynamic
programming and has the Hamilton--Boltzmann equation as its viscosity equation
on the bounded exponential cylinder core.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Closed balance and compact action sublevels give attainment.  Concatenation
preserves balance and adds only the dynamic entropy, proving the tower.
Fenchel duality on a short interval gives the subsolution inequality; B2
smooth tilted recovery gives the supersolution inequality.
\end{proof}

For $r=d(f,g)$, use the logarithmic Tataru penalty
\[
 \Phi_\epsilon(r)=
 \kappa\int_0^r\log\left(1+\frac{s}{\epsilon}\right)ds,
 \qquad 0<\kappa<C_H^{-1},
\]
where $C_H$ is the exponential jet-growth constant of the Hamiltonian.

\begin{theorem}[Logarithmic-jet comparison]
\label{thm:r11-b4-comparison}
At a doubled maximum, $r_\epsilon\to0$, the common cotangent has size
$O(\log(1+r_\epsilon/\epsilon))$, and
\[
 r_\epsilon\exp(C_H|p_\epsilon|)\longrightarrow0.
\]
This balances diagonal coercivity with the exponential Hamiltonian and yields
comparison for the full, untruncated Hamilton--Boltzmann equation.  Entropy
truncations converge locally uniformly on these jets and balance repairs have
vanishing cost.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Off every fixed diagonal neighborhood, $\Phi_\epsilon\to\infty$, so the
maximizing distance tends to zero.  Its derivative is
$\kappa\log(1+r/\epsilon)$, and the choice $\kappa C_H<1$ gives
$r e^{C_H|p|}\le r(1+r/\epsilon)^{\kappa C_H}\to0$ by the maximizing
inequality.  The Hamiltonian state difference is bounded by this expression
on moment sublevels.  Clipping the collision ratio and applying B2's exact
repair gives monotone local convergence, completing the viscosity argument.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Microscopic convergence and preparation]
\label{thm:r11-b4-limit}
The endpoint values of Theorem~\ref{thm:r11-b4-tower}, after contraction to
the empirical kinetic state, converge locally uniformly to the unique action
semigroup of Theorem~\ref{thm:r11-b4-action}.  Microcanonical preparation is
the source-dependent B1 contraction at the initial boundary only.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Theorem~\ref{thm:r11-b4-corrector} gives upper and lower generator limits on a
core, B2 gives compact containment, and
Theorem~\ref{thm:r11-b4-comparison} gives uniqueness of half-relaxed limits.
B1's exact shell change of measure supplies the initial contraction without
altering any transition action.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Round-eleven B4 closure]
\label{thm:r11-b4-main}
The finite deterministic model, observable core, hierarchy corrector, dynamic
action, viscosity comparison, and microcanonical interface are now typed on
their correct spaces.  The comparison uses a coercive logarithmic penalty
whose exponentially growing jets remain controllable.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Combine Theorems~\ref{thm:r11-b4-tower},
\ref{thm:r11-b4-core}, \ref{thm:r11-b4-corrector},
\ref{thm:r11-b4-action}, \ref{thm:r11-b4-comparison}, and
\ref{thm:r11-b4-limit}.
\end{proof}
