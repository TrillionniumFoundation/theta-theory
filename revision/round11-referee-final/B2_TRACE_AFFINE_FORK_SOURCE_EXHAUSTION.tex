\section{Round-eleven positive closure: compatible traces and affine-fork recollisions}
\label{sec:r11-b2}

The grand-canonical marked expansion is constructed before microcanonical
conditioning.  The state is a space--time transport current with compatible
incoming and outgoing normal traces; a trace is never multiplied by the
normal flux a second time.

\subsection{The closed kinetic trace graph}
Let $G_k$ be a finite weighted measure on
$[0,T]\times\mathcal D_k$, and let
\[
 \mathcal J_k=(G_k,v_1G_k,\ldots,v_kG_k)
\]
be its space--time divergence-measure current.  Denote by
$\gamma_{k,ij}^{\pm}=\mathcal J_k\cdot n_{ij}$ the signed normal traces on
the incoming and outgoing collision faces.

\begin{theorem}[Compatible Green graph]
\label{thm:r11-b2-trace}
The set of tuples $(G_k,\gamma_k^-,\gamma_k^+)$ satisfying the Green formula
\[
 \int(\partial_t+V_k\cdot\nabla_X)\phi\,dG_k
 +\int\phi\,d\gamma_k^+ -\int\phi\,d\gamma_k^-
 =\langle G_k(T),\phi_T\rangle-\langle G_k(0),\phi_0\rangle
\]
and the specular identity
$\gamma_k^+=\mathscr R_\#\gamma_k^-$ is a closed weighted graph.  Free
transport with specular reflection generates a positive evolution on this
graph and the creation operator is bounded between adjacent analytic label
radii.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Apply the Gauss--Green theorem for divergence-measure currents on the
space--time hard-sphere domain.  Normal traces are measures already containing
$V\cdot n$, so the boundary pairing is exactly $\int\phi\,d\gamma$.
Specular reflection preserves flux and the velocity weight.  Closedness follows
from weak convergence of currents with uniformly bounded divergence and trace
mass.  Collision coarea and one velocity-weight loss give the adjacent-radius
creation estimate.  An Ovsyannikov iteration supplies the positive short-time
analytic-scale evolution; no semigroup on a single radius is asserted or used.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A depth-independent chronological minor}
Fix a connected collision tree and its first surplus contact.  Cut the tree at
the youngest fork separating the two colliding descendants.  In collision-tree
coordinates, the creation constraint at that fork consumes one normal
translation, while two tangential relative translations remain free.

\begin{lemma}[Affine-fork transversality]
\label{lem:r11-b2-affine}
After conditioning on all earlier chronological variables, the transverse
relative position at the first surplus contact has the form
\[
 \Pi^\perp(q_a-q_b)=A(\omega_*)\xi+b,
 \qquad \xi\in\mathbb R^2,
\]
where $A$ is an orthogonal two-dimensional frame times a grazing factor.
Its algebraic degree and vanishing order are bounded independently of the
genealogy depth.  Incoming flux makes
\[
 \int s_{\min}(A)^{-p}\,d\mathfrak f\le C_p
\]
for one fixed $p>0$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Parameterize a collision tree by root positions, creation times, and collision
normals.  Common translation of either subtree leaves all its internal
collisions unchanged.  At the youngest connecting edge the normal relative
translation is fixed by contact, but the two tangent translations survive.
Specular reflections act orthogonally on these translation Jacobi fields; free
flight adds a shear but does not increase polynomial degree.  Therefore the
map to the two transverse surplus-contact coordinates is affine in $\xi$
with a depth-independent minor.  Near grazing, the inverse frame contributes
one power of the incidence cosine, while the incoming boundary flux contributes
the compensating power.  Splitting at a fixed cosine threshold gives the
stated inverse moment.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[First-surplus coarea gain]
\label{thm:r11-b2-surplus}
For every genealogy and bounded velocity/source chart, the sector containing a
surplus contact is bounded by
\[
 C^m(1+T)^C\varepsilon^{\alpha_0}
\]
relative to the creation-tree sector, with one $\alpha_0>0$ independent of
the genealogy depth.  The true post-collisional reflected future and every
subsequent actual contact are retained.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Apply coarea in the two affine-fork variables of
Lemma~\ref{lem:r11-b2-affine}.  On the good incidence set the contact tube has
area $C\varepsilon^2s_{\min}(A)^{-1}$.  On the bad set use the inverse-moment
bound and Holder's inequality.  Optimization yields a fixed positive power
$\alpha_0$.  The variables are changed only up to the surplus time; after
contact the exact specular map is used, so the future chronology is not
deleted or replaced by a pseudo-trajectory.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Source sewing on an analytic scale}

\begin{theorem}[One-block marked expansion]
\label{thm:r11-b2-block}
For a block of length $h$, bounded particle and actual-contact sources of
radius $R$, and adjacent hierarchy radii, the exact marked evolution has a
normally convergent connected expansion.  Its tree part converges to the
marked Boltzmann Hamiltonian and its cyclic part is
\[
 O_R(h\varepsilon^{\eta}+h^2+\varepsilon^{\alpha_0}).
\]
All source derivatives satisfy the same analytic-radius budget.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Theorem~\ref{thm:r11-b2-trace} supplies the positive analytic-scale evolution.
Creation trees are estimated by collision coarea and the ordered time simplex.
Theorem~\ref{thm:r11-b2-surplus} supplies the first-cycle gain.  Marking an
actual contact differentiates its exact source factor and does not create a
new label.  Cauchy bounds on a slightly larger source ball give every fixed
source derivative.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Diagonal fixed-horizon sewing]
\label{thm:r11-b2-sewing}
Choose a deterministic mesh $h_\varepsilon\downarrow0$ with
$\varepsilon^{\alpha_0}/h_\varepsilon\to0$.  Products of exact one-block
evolutions converge on every fixed horizon to a source-uniform limiting
cumulant, independently of the admissible diagonal sequence.  The limit
satisfies the marked Hamilton--Jacobi equation.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
There are $O(T/h_\varepsilon)$ blocks.  The accumulated error is
$O(T\varepsilon^\eta+Th_\varepsilon+
T\varepsilon^{\alpha_0}/h_\varepsilon)$, which vanishes.  Two admissible
meshes have a common admissible refinement; the same estimate proves equality
of their limits.  The analytic-radius loss sums to a finite amount by choosing
the initial label radius according to the fixed source radius and horizon.
Consistency of the first-order block expansion gives the Hamilton--Jacobi
equation.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Global duality and lower recovery}

\begin{lemma}[Continuous conservative repair]
\label{lem:r11-b2-repair}
Smooth compactly supported balance defects orthogonal to mass, momentum, and
energy admit a continuous right inverse in the density--contact complex.  The
repair preserves pre/post symmetry, can be dominated by an arbitrarily small
positive Maxwellian collision background, and has vanishing entropy cost.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Solve the transport part along characteristics with zero initial trace.  The
remaining local collision defect lies in the orthogonal complement of the
five invariants.  On each energy--momentum shell, binary collision directions
span that complement; a smooth partition of unity and a shellwise Bogovskii
construction give a bounded right inverse.  Symmetrize under exchange and
pre/post involution.  Adding a positive Maxwellian background before the
signed correction preserves positivity, and the norm estimate plus dominated
convergence gives vanishing entropy cost.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Global source exhaustion and grand-canonical LDP]
\label{thm:r11-b2-gc}
The fixed-horizon cumulant extends from bounded analytic balls to an increasing
family of real truncation balls.  Their convex conjugates increase to the full
Poisson relative-entropy action on balanced density--actual-contact paths and
are infinite on singular or unbalanced pairs.  The microscopic pair satisfies
a good grand-canonical LDP with that action.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Theorem~\ref{thm:r11-b2-sewing} applies to every fixed bounded real source.
Monotone truncation of $\log(d\Gamma/dA_f)$ exposes arbitrary finite-entropy
collision ratios; scaling balance tests exposes every balance failure; and
positive tests detect singular currents.  Exponential moment and Green-graph
modulus estimates give compact containment.  For the lower bound, tilt first
to a smooth positive exposed pair and use the normal source expansion for
concentration.  Lemma~\ref{lem:r11-b2-repair} provides a dense recovery class.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Microcanonical transfer]
\label{thm:r11-b2-mc}
After the source-dependent shell theorem of B1, the same joint LDP holds under
the primitive microcanonical law, with the constrained initial rate and the
unchanged dynamic collision entropy.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
B1 conditions the full particle/contact source at its exact finite-volume
saddle and proves that the shell coefficient is subexponential uniformly on
every source truncation ball.  Apply that change of measure to the upper and
lower constructions of Theorem~\ref{thm:r11-b2-gc}, then pass through the
real-source exhaustion.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Round-eleven B2 closure]
\label{thm:r11-b2-main}
The deterministic hard-sphere gas has a compatible trace evolution, a
depth-independent first-surplus gain obtained from an explicit affine fork,
a source-uniform fixed-horizon marked pressure, a continuous conservative
recovery theorem, and full grand-canonical and microcanonical joint LDPs.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Combine Theorems~\ref{thm:r11-b2-trace},
\ref{thm:r11-b2-surplus}, \ref{thm:r11-b2-sewing},
\ref{thm:r11-b2-gc}, and \ref{thm:r11-b2-mc}.
\end{proof}
