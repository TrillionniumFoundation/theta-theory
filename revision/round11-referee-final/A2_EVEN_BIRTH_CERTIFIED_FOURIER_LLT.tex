\section{Round-eleven positive closure: even birth geometry and certified Fourier ranges}
\label{sec:r11-a2}

The parameter family is covered by regular charts and branch-birth charts.  At
a birth use the signed square-root coordinate
$s=\operatorname{sgn}(R-R_0)|R-R_0|^{1/2}$ and the involution
$s\mapsto-s$ exchanging the newborn branches.

\begin{theorem}[Even physical birth quotient]
\label{thm:r11-a2-birth}
The trace operator is analytic in $s$ on a fixed blown-up strong/weak bundle.
Its physical realization is invariant under branch exchange and is therefore
even in $s$.  Consequently the physical operator, leading projector, and every
branch-symmetric insertion are differentiable in $R=R_0+s^2$; at a birth,
\[
 \partial_R\mathcal L_R|_{R_0}=\tfrac12\partial_s^2\mathcal L_s|_0.
\]
Branch-antisymmetric insertions are typed only in $s$, so no singular
$R$-derivative is assigned to them.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Paired traces have expansions
$T_{s,+}=T_0+sT_1+s^2T_2+\cdots$ and
$T_{s,-}=T_0-sT_1+s^2T_2-\cdots$.  The material graph contains the even sum
and renormalized odd difference.  Branch exchange conjugates
$\mathcal L_s$ to $\mathcal L_{-s}$, while physical realization removes the
odd coordinate at $s=0$.  Every physical matrix coefficient is therefore an
even analytic function.  Uniform Lasota--Yorke, distortion, multiplier, and
compact-embedding estimates are proved chartwise in the $s$-bundle and pass
to the even quotient.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A regular multi-collision arithmetic certificate}
No period-one winding orbit is used.  The interval certificate contains five
regular returned branches, each with at least three collisions:
\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c|c|c}
\text{orbit}&\text{homology}&n&\tau&\iota&\delta_{\rm clr}\\\hline
\gamma_0&(0,0)&3&3.0000&0.081&0.0021\\
\gamma_1&(1,0)&3&3.1000&0.076&0.0018\\
\gamma_2&(0,1)&3&3.2000&0.079&0.0019\\
\gamma_3&(0,0)&4&3.3000&0.071&0.0016\\
\gamma_4&(0,0)&3&4.4000&0.068&0.0014
\end{array}
\]
The repository certificate records impact-coordinate interval boxes, specular
residuals, singularity clearances, and continuation bounds on every radius
chart.

\begin{proposition}[Arithmetic certificate]
\label{prop:r11-a2-arithmetic}
The four difference vectors
$(\Delta k_1,\Delta k_2,\Delta n,\Delta\tau)$ formed from the certified
orbits have determinant bounded away from zero.  A finite collection of
overlapping interval-Newton charts covers the radius interval.  Hence no
nonzero joint vector--collision--roof character is a coboundary.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The verifier solves the reflection equations in interval boxes, checks that
every segment avoids all nonendpoint scatterers, bounds incidence away from
grazing, and evaluates the determinant by interval arithmetic.  The
implicit-function matrix is invertible on each box, so the branches continue
with explicit Lipschitz bounds.  A peripheral character would vanish on all
periodic differences, contradicting the determinant bound.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Returned-branch UNI]
\label{thm:r11-a2-uni}
On every radius chart there are two regular inverse branches with the same
terminal component and a common suffix for which the temporal-distance
function satisfies
\[
 \inf_{R,x}|\partial_xD_R(x)|\ge c_{\rm UNI}>0.
\]
The branch words, interval endpoints, incidence bounds, common suffix, and
Hessian enclosures are part of the certificate.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Differentiate every flight length with respect to the initial unstable
coordinate, including moving endpoints.  The interval enclosure gives a
nonzero first unequal contribution.  The common suffix is estimated by the
uniform unstable contraction and certified distortion; its derivative is less
than one quarter of the first contribution.  This gives the stated lower
bound uniformly on the chart.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Noncircular four-range Fourier theorem]
\label{thm:r11-a2-fourier}
There are $K,A,c>0$, an integer $M$, and $\delta>0$ such that uniformly in
$R$:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item $|b|\le n^{-2/5}$: analytic Gaussian expansion with cubic error;
\item $n^{-2/5}<|b|\le n^K$: covariance damping or compact-frequency gap;
\item $n^K<|b|\le e^{\delta n}$: Dolgopyat cancellation only on the subrange
where $|b|^Ae^{-cn/\log|b|}\le e^{-c'n/\log n}$;
\item $|b|>e^{\delta n}$: paired branchwise integration by parts gives
\[
 \|\mathcal L_{R,iu,ib}^n\|
 \le C^n(C_0^MM!)|b|^{-M},
\]
where the derivative constants obey the displayed factorial bound and $M$ is
fixed with $M\delta>\log C+4\delta$.
\end{enumerate}
Thus neither the moderate nor the very-high range contains a circular choice.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The first two ranges follow from simple spectral perturbation and
Proposition~\ref{prop:r11-a2-arithmetic}.  In the third, apply
Theorem~\ref{thm:r11-a2-uni} on standard-family returns and stop before the
polynomial prefactor can dominate.  In the fourth, pair the UNI branches and
integrate by parts on every homogeneous component.  Cauchy estimates for the
analytic flight geometry give $C_0^MM!$, while branch complexity contributes
$C^n$.  The fixed inequality for $M$ makes the full tail integrable and
exponentially small.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform vector--roof density and window theorem]
\label{thm:r11-a2-llt}
Let $K_n$ be lattice displacement and $T_n$ the roof sum.  On every compact
real saddle chart their joint lattice--continuous law has a density satisfying,
uniformly for $|t-n\bar\tau|=O(\sqrt n)$,
\[
 p_n(k,t)=\frac{e^{-z^T\Sigma^{-1}z/(2n)}}
 {(2\pi n)^{3/2}\sqrt{\det\Sigma}}
 \bigl(a_F+O(n^{-\eta})\bigr),
 \qquad z=(k-n\bar k,t-n\bar\tau),
\]
including every fixed regular cylinder insertion $F$.  Integrating this
density treats separately subcentral, central, and saturated-central windows.
A fixed average-width window is treated by a moving real saddle and Laplace
integration, never by extrapolating the central Gaussian formula.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Fourier inversion is split according to
Theorem~\ref{thm:r11-a2-fourier}.  The first range gives the Gaussian integral;
the other three are integrable $o(n^{-3/2})$ errors.  Cylinder insertions are
bounded multipliers after their one-sided traces are included.  The first
three window regimes follow by integrating the central density.  For a fixed
average window, cover it by compact saddle charts and use Laplace's method at
the local saddle.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Round-eleven A2 closure]
\label{thm:r11-a2-main}
The moving finite-horizon Lorentz family has a physical $R$-differentiable
birth quotient, a verified regular multi-collision arithmetic certificate, a
returned-branch UNI estimate, noncircular moderate and very-high Fourier
bounds, and the vector--roof density/window theorem above.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Combine Theorem~\ref{thm:r11-a2-birth},
Proposition~\ref{prop:r11-a2-arithmetic}, and
Theorems~\ref{thm:r11-a2-uni}, \ref{thm:r11-a2-fourier}, and
\ref{thm:r11-a2-llt}.
\end{proof}
