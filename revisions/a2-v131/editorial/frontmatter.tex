\title[Determinant completion and primary boundary laws]{Universal determinant completion, effective Pieri multiplication, and intrinsic primary boundary laws in multiplication failure}
\author{Qian Qi}
\date{September 23, 2026 \quad (Revision 131)}
\subjclass[2020]{14M12, 14J28, 13C40, 14B05, 14D20, 05E05}
\keywords{Multiplication failure, Fitting scheme, determinant completion, primary decomposition, nilpotent filtration, boundary specialization, quartic K3 surface}
\hypersetup{pdftitle={Universal determinant completion and intrinsic primary boundary laws, revision 131},pdfauthor={Qian Qi}}
\begin{abstract}
We study the intrinsic nilpotent structure of multiplication-failure
Fitting schemes.  A symmetric-power determinant-completion identity
bounds the residual colon tower in every dimension and degree.  For
finite-presentation families we construct primary quotients on finite
splitting covers, prove exact exhaustion of the geometric associated
points, and construct coherent relative torsion modules whose local
lengths commute with specialization inside a finite stratification.
The grading and a specified finite family of multiplication maps give
constant Hilbert functions and degreewise ranks on each final stratum.

For cube-zero algebras with Hilbert function \((1,4,6)\), the abstract
failure scheme determines the polarized Jacobian quartic K3 surface.
On the smooth, basepoint-free Jacobian open, a normalized Cauchy--Pieri
projection strengthens the global nilradical bound to
\(\widehat{\mathcal N}^{5}=0\), with exact index five at projection
coranks three and four.  The first two positive layers have global
minimal supports given by a Jacobian incidence image and the rank-two
determinantal variety.  A resolution has generic fibres consisting of
four contact points, a plane quartic of genus three, and the K3 surface
at successive projection coranks.  An explicit two-parameter family
realizes these transitions and has a seven-component reduced primary
decomposition; its intrinsic graded fibre algebras are computed.

The complete generic corank-two primary tables, including ambient
pure-block rank drops, are retained with exact function-field
certificates.  For proper quotient-induced symmetric powers, the
classical maximal-minor identity yields an explicit primary
decomposition, a sharp determinant exponent, and closed formulas for
the embedded torsion and all generic layer lengths.
\end{abstract}
