\subsection{A finite rational outer construction, not an implicit oracle}
\label{subsec:algorithm}
For computational construction assume rational box endpoints, rational sample
times, and rational outward enclosures of the response-band endpoints. This
assumption concerns certified input representation, not the probabilistic
experiment: any real-valued data may first be enclosed with a declared error.
Let $T_c$ bound the finitely many selected times. Choose an integer $K\ge J+1$,
a rational mesh whose coordinate half-widths are at most $r$, and a Taylor
order $P$. A finite chain on sites $0,\ldots,K$ has variables
$c,b_0,\ldots,b_K,a_0,\ldots,a_{K-1}$; the edge out of site $K$ is removed.
Use its cell centers and compute, in rational arithmetic,
\[
 H_q=\ell\sum_{l=0}^{P}A_K^l B\,\frac{t_q^{l+1}}{(l+1)!}.
\]
Let $E_0=3^{\lceil\Lambda T_c\rceil}$ and set
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 E_{\rm tail}&=2T_cE_0(\Lambda T_c)^K/K!,&
 E_{\rm mesh}&=2rT_c^2E_0,\\
 E_{\rm num}&=T_cE_0(\Lambda T_c)^{P+1}/(P+1)!,&
 E&=E_{\rm tail}+E_{\rm mesh}+E_{\rm num}.
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:outer-errors}
\end{equation}
All these are rational certified bounds, since $e^x\le3^{\lceil x\rceil}$
for $x\ge0$ (including $x=0$). Retain a mesh box exactly when every interval
$[H_q-E,H_q+E]$ intersects the observed band at that node. Output the union of
its projections to $c,a_0,b_0,\ldots,a_J,b_J$.

\begin{proposition}[Correctness and termination of the outer construction]
\label{prop:outer}
The finite union just constructed contains the projection of $\mathcal C_n$.
Every admissible completion of every retained box satisfies the response bands
with radii enlarged by $2E$. Thus if $\max_q(R_{q,n}+2E)\le\delta/(4LA)$
on a certificate grid, the union has $d_J$ diameter at most $\delta$.
For any positive prescribed $E$ tolerance the truncation, mesh, and Taylor
orders can be selected in finitely many steps. Exhaustive enumeration then
terminates. No polynomial-time or practical large-depth complexity is claimed.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The cut chain and any completion agree on boundary words through degree $K-1$.
Both generators have norm at most $\Lambda$, even though the cut edge is zero.
Their exponential series give the first bound in \eqref{eq:outer-errors}.
A coordinate change bounded by $r$ has generator norm at most $4r$; integrating
the Duhamel estimate $4rt e^{\Lambda t}$ gives the mesh bound. The exponential
series tail after degree $P$ gives the numerical bound. Therefore every
completion represented by a box has response within $E$ of $H_q$.
A true feasible completion forces its box to be retained. Conversely,
intersection of a computed interval and an observed band implies that each
completion of the retained box is within radius $R_{q,n}+2E$ of the band
center. Apply the proof of \eqref{eq:diameter} to the enlarged bands.
Factorial tails tend to zero for fixed $T_c$; then a sufficiently fine finite
mesh makes the remaining error small. This proves termination. The supplied
implementation raises an explicit resource-limit exception before enumeration
when its declared box cap is exceeded; it never reports an incomplete search
as an empty or certified confidence set.
\end{proof}
