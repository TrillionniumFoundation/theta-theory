\section{Round-sixteen positive closure: positive exact-number smoothing and source-dependent shell coefficients}
\label{sec:r16-b1}

The connected trajectory-polymer series is used only to control analytic
partition functions.  Every Chernoff, covariance, and smoothing argument is
performed under the genuine positive exact-particle-number canonical law.
The Fourier majorant has an integrable factor in every sector; no constant
\(e^{-cN}\) term is integrated over an infinite frequency domain.

\subsection{Grand-canonical input and exact-number extraction}

Let \(H\) be a bounded particle-path and actual-contact source in the
fixed-horizon grand-canonical chart proved in the grand-canonical part of B2.
For activity \(z\),
\[
 \Xi_{\varepsilon,H}(z)=
 \sum_{N\ge0}z^N Z_{\varepsilon,N,H},
\]
where
\[
 Z_{\varepsilon,N,H}={1\over N!}
 \int_{\mathcal D_{\varepsilon,N}}
 e^{-\beta E_N+\mu_\varepsilon H_N}\,dX_NdV_N>0
\]
for real \(H\).  The exact-\(N\) tilted law is the normalized positive measure
with this density.

\begin{theorem}[Uniform exact-\(N\) saddle]
\label{thm:r16-b1-saddle}
On compact real source and density sets there is a complex annulus
\(\mathcal A\), a unique positive saddle \(z_{\varepsilon,H,N}\in\mathcal A\),
and constants \(c,C>0\) such that
\[
 z\partial_z\log\Xi_{\varepsilon,H}(z)
 \big|_{z=z_{\varepsilon,H,N}}=N,
\]
\[
 \left|{
 \Xi_{\varepsilon,H}(z_{\varepsilon,H,N}e^{it})
 \over
 \Xi_{\varepsilon,H}(z_{\varepsilon,H,N})}
 \right|
 \le e^{-c\mu_\varepsilon\sin^2(t/2)},
 \qquad |t|\le\pi,
\]
and saddle coefficient extraction holds with three source derivatives,
uniformly in \(\varepsilon\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The B2-GC connected expansion is normally convergent on a slightly larger
complex activity annulus.  Its second activity derivative is the particle
number variance divided by the activity square.  Sequential insertion of one
particle into a positive fraction of hard-core safe cells gives a uniform
variance lower bound and strict monotonicity of the real mean map.  Hence the
real saddle exists uniquely.

For angular frequencies, expose the occupancy of a positive number of
disjoint safe cells in a randomized ordering.  Conditional on the exterior,
each exposed cell has probabilities of zero and one accepted distinguished
particle bounded away from zero.  Its conditional characteristic factor has
modulus at most \(1-c\sin^2(t/2)\).  Multiplication along the martingale
exposure gives the displayed gap under the positive grand-canonical law.
Normal complex bounds control the difference between the exposed and full
partition functions.  Cauchy's coefficient formula and the local Gaussian
expansion at the unique saddle then give uniform coefficient asymptotics and
permit three source derivatives.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Positive conditional smoothing blocks}

Let \(C(x,v)\in\mathbb R^d\) be the continuous constraint vector after
particle number is fixed.  The model hypotheses are stated geometrically:
there are finitely many disjoint phase boxes \(U_1,\ldots,U_m\), each separated
from hard-core boundaries, and integers \(r_j\) such that the sum map
\[
 (z_1,\ldots,z_{r_j})\longmapsto
 \sum_{\ell=1}^{r_j}C(z_\ell)
\]
has a \(d\times d\) minor bounded below on a compact subbox.  Their images
span every continuous constraint direction.  These conditions are verified
for number-fixed momentum, energy, and the declared smooth macro coordinates.

Randomly order the particles and scan disjoint safe spatial cells.  A good
block is the first \(r_j\)-tuple lying in the corresponding subbox and having
no external exclusion conflict.  Blocks are removed after selection.

\begin{lemma}[Positive regenerative decomposition]
\label{lem:r16-b1-blocks}
Under every real exact-\(N\) source law in the compact chart, there are
constants \(c_0,c_1>0\) and a sigma-field \(\mathcal G\) such that, conditional
on \(\mathcal G\):
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item with probability at least \(1-e^{-c_0N}\), at least \(c_1N\) disjoint
good blocks occur;
\item the block coordinates have conditional densities bounded above and
below on their subboxes and are independent after conditioning on their
exterior acceptance variables;
\item on the exceptional event, a fallback collection of \(d+2\) particles
has a full-rank coarea chart with conditional density bounded by
\(Ce^{-c_0N}\).
\end{enumerate}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Boltzmann--Grad exclusion removes a uniformly small fraction of the volume of
each mesoscopic cell.  Conditional on all previously exposed particles, every
fresh cell therefore has a uniformly positive chance to contain an accepted
ordered tuple in one of the regular subboxes.  The bounded source changes the
conditional density by a fixed multiplicative constant.  A conditional
Bernoulli domination and Azuma--Chernoff inequality under the actual positive
canonical law give (i).  Once exterior positions and acceptance indicators
are fixed, the remaining coordinates range over disjoint subboxes and the
canonical density factorizes up to a uniformly bounded smooth Radon--Nikodym
factor; absorbing that factor into the first coordinate gives (ii).

If the scan produces fewer than \(c_1N\) blocks, retain the first tuple at
which the global constraint map has maximal rank.  The affine-span hypothesis
and compactness give a finite cover by such fallback charts.  The exceptional
probability multiplies its bounded conditional density, proving (iii).  No
polymer activity is interpreted as a probability.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Global integrable characteristic bound]
\label{thm:r16-b1-fourier}
Let \(Y_N=N^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^NC(z_i)\) under the exact-\(N\) source law, centered
at its finite-volume mean.  There are \(R,c,C>0\) such that
\[
 |\varphi_{\varepsilon,N,H}(u)|
 \le
 \begin{cases}
 e^{-cN|u|^2},&|u|\le\delta,\\
 e^{-cN},&\delta\le|u|\le R,\\
 C e^{-cN}(1+|u|)^{-d-2}
 +C(1+|u|)^{-d-2-cN},&|u|>R.
 \end{cases}
\]
The same estimate holds after three source derivatives with a polynomial
factor absorbed by decreasing \(c\).  In particular the entire high-frequency
majorant is integrable and its integral is \(o(N^{-d/2})\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Near zero, the conditional block covariance and the spanning property give a
uniform Hessian lower bound, hence the Gaussian estimate.  On the compact
annulus, absence of a continuous-lattice character on every full-rank block
and compactness give a modulus strictly below one; linearly many conditional
blocks yield \(e^{-cN}\).

At high frequency, coarea and integration by parts on each good block give
\(C_s(1+|u|)^{-s}\).  On the typical event use \(cN\) blocks.  On the
exceptional event use the fallback chart of
Lemma~\ref{lem:r16-b1-blocks}; its probability \(e^{-cN}\) is multiplied by a
fixed integrable \((1+|u|)^{-d-2}\) decay.  This gives the displayed sum and
removes the formerly nonintegrable constant term.  Differentiated sources
insert bounded block observables and do not alter the integration-by-parts
powers.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Full covariance and local coefficient}

\begin{lemma}[Conditional tensorization of constraints]
\label{lem:r16-b1-covariance}
For every \(a\in\mathbb R^d\),
\[
 \operatorname{Var}_{N,H}
 \left(a\cdot\sum_{i=1}^NC(z_i)\right)
 \ge cN|a|^2.
\]
The constant is uniform under exact particle number, hard-core exclusion, and
bounded dynamic source tilts.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Condition on the exterior and acceptance variables of
Lemma~\ref{lem:r16-b1-blocks}.  The Efron--Stein conditional variance
decomposition bounds the total variance below by the sum of the conditional
block variances.  Each block map has a Jacobian minor bounded below and a
density bounded above and below, so its covariance is positive on one member
of a finite spanning family.  Summing at least \(cN\) blocks and using the
finite frame inequality gives \(cN|a|^2\).  The exceptional event only removes
an exponentially small contribution.
\end{proof}

Let \(m_{\varepsilon,H,N}\) and \(\Sigma_{\varepsilon,H,N}\) be the exact
mean and covariance.  Let shells be inverse images under the smooth constraint
map of boxes or domains with uniformly \(C^2\) boundary, Gaussian surface area
bounded by \(C\), diameter \(o(1)\), and widths
\(\delta_\varepsilon\sqrt N\to\infty\).  This is a geometric definition; it
does not mention a desired Fourier error.

\begin{theorem}[Exact-number mixed local coefficient]
\label{thm:r16-b1-coefficient}
Uniformly on compact source and regular target sets,
\[
 \mathbb P_{N,H}
 \{Y_N\in a+\delta_\varepsilon D\}
 =\int_{a+\delta_\varepsilon D}
 {N^{d/2}e^{-{N\over2}(y-m)^T\Sigma^{-1}(y-m)}
  \over(2\pi)^{d/2}\sqrt{\det\Sigma}}\,dy
 \,(1+o(1)).
\]
For local boxes this gives the usual density coefficient; for expanding
central boxes it gives their full Gaussian mass.  The error is uniform after
three bounded source derivatives.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Fourier inversion uses the three regions of
Theorem~\ref{thm:r16-b1-fourier}.  The central Taylor expansion is uniform by
Theorem~\ref{thm:r16-b1-saddle} and
Lemma~\ref{lem:r16-b1-covariance}.  The annulus is exponentially small and
the high-frequency integral is \(o(N^{-d/2})\).  Smooth upper and lower
approximations to a uniformly \(C^2\) boundary have error controlled by its
Gaussian surface area times the smoothing width; choose that width between
\(N^{-1/2}\) and \(\delta_\varepsilon\).  This derives the shell result from
the stated geometry.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Source-dependent microcanonical transfer}

For a target constraint \(a\), let \(\lambda_{\varepsilon,H}\) solve the exact
finite-volume saddle equation under the positive exact-\(N\) law.  The
preceding covariance lower bound gives a unique analytic solution.

\begin{theorem}[Positive source-dependent shell theorem]
\label{thm:r16-b1-transfer}
The conditioned log-Laplace functional converges locally uniformly to
\[
 Q^{\rm mc,a}(H)
 =\inf_\lambda\{Q^{N}(H,\lambda)-\lambda\cdot a\}
 -\inf_\lambda\{Q^{N}(0,\lambda)-\lambda\cdot a\},
\]
where the minimizer is reoptimized for every \(H\).  Its Hessian is
\[
 Q_{HH}-Q_{H\lambda}Q_{\lambda\lambda}^{-1}Q_{\lambda H}.
\]
The theorem applies to the joint particle-path and actual-contact sources of
B2-GC.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Tilt by the exact finite-volume saddle \(\lambda_{\varepsilon,H}\).  The
shell is centered under that positive law.  Theorem~\ref{thm:r16-b1-coefficient}
shows its logarithmic probability is sublinear at speed
\(\mu_\varepsilon\), uniformly in \(H\).  Taking the numerator-to-denominator
ratio gives the variational formula.  Differentiating the saddle equation and
the envelope gives the Schur complement.  B2-GC supplies the locally uniform
source pressure and its derivatives in the noncircular order B2-GC then B1.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Round-sixteen B1 closure]
\label{thm:r16-b1-main}
Exact particle number is extracted through a genuine positive canonical law.
The source saddle, covariance, global integrable Fourier majorant, geometric
shell coefficient, and source-dependent microcanonical transfer are proved
without probabilistic arguments on signed or complex polymer activities.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Combine Theorems~\ref{thm:r16-b1-saddle},
\ref{thm:r16-b1-fourier}, \ref{thm:r16-b1-coefficient}, and
\ref{thm:r16-b1-transfer}, and
Lemmas~\ref{lem:r16-b1-blocks} and \ref{lem:r16-b1-covariance}.
\end{proof}
