\section{Round-sixteen positive closure: hybrid exact mechanics and an independently generated jet-current calculus}
\label{sec:r16-a1}

The physical impact system, its symbolic coding, and a risk-sensitive
certainty equivalent are three different structures.  This section constructs
the first in the exact hybrid symplectic category, uses the second only as a
measurable coding, and identifies the third with the mechanical field only
under an explicit cocycle-compatibility axiom.  Parameter jets are defined
before the response norm, so no graph space is allowed to contain the operator
whose closability it is meant to prove.

\subsection{Correct exact branch primitive}

Let \(I_i(a)=(s_{i-1}(a),s_i(a))\), \(w_i=s_i-s_{i-1}>0\), and write
\[
 Q={q-s_{i-1}\over w_i},\qquad P=s_{i-1}+w_i p .
\]
The branch map \(B_{a,i}(q,p)=(Q,P)\) preserves \(dq\wedge dp\).

\begin{lemma}[Exact affine branch]
\label{lem:r16-a1-primitive}
With \(\lambda=p\,dq\),
\[
 B_{a,i}^{*}(P\,dQ)-\lambda=dG_{a,i},
 \qquad
 G_{a,i}(q,p)={s_{i-1}(a)\over w_i(a)}
                 (q-s_{i-1}(a)).
\]
In particular the primitive has no \(p\)-term.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Since \(dQ=dq/w_i\),
\[
 B_{a,i}^{*}(P\,dQ)-p\,dq
 ={s_{i-1}+w_ip\over w_i}\,dq-p\,dq
 ={s_{i-1}\over w_i}\,dq=dG_{a,i}.
\]
The parameter is fixed during the exterior derivative, so this is the exact
identity on the branch.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A Hausdorff hybrid Poincar\'e section}

For each branch let \(C_{a,i}=\overline I_i(a)\times[0,1]\) be a separate
copy, with its four boundary faces retained.  The hybrid section is the finite
disjoint union
\[
 \widehat S_a=\bigsqcup_{i=1}^{4}C_{a,i}.
\]
Its interior points project one-to-one to the corresponding branch of the
square.  A boundary point carries the incoming face label; distinct one-sided
germs are never identified.  The outgoing face of the channel over branch
\(i\) is identified with the appropriate incoming branch sheet only after its
image point is known.  Thus the quotient uses a closed graph of paired faces,
not the complement of all forward and backward seam iterates.

\begin{theorem}[Exact hybrid suspension]
\label{thm:r16-a1-hybrid}
For \(a\) in every compact subinterval of \((-1,1)\) there is a Hausdorff
second-countable hybrid exact-symplectic manifold
\(\mathcal M_a\) with corners, a complete autonomous Hamiltonian flow away
from its impact faces, and a transverse hybrid section \(\widehat S_a\) such
that:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item each open channel is a smooth exact symplectic mapping cylinder;
\item the impact relation is a closed exact Lagrangian graph;
\item the first return on sheet interiors is \(B_{a,i}\);
\item the face-labelled return relation is single-valued and reversible; and
\item projection to the ordinary square recovers \(B_a\) outside the
      one-step seams and preserves Lebesgue measure.
\end{enumerate}
The maximal regular flow is complete in hybrid time; an orbit reaching a
multiple-face corner is retained as a labelled corner state rather than being
silently deleted.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Choose three time windows in \((0,1)\).  The first translates \(q\) by
\(-s_{i-1}\), the second is the time-one flow of
\(-\log(w_i)qp\), and the third translates \(p\) by \(s_{i-1}\).
Their composition is \((q,p)\mapsto(Q,P)\).  Adjoining a clock pair
\((t,E)\) makes the construction autonomous.  By
Lemma~\ref{lem:r16-a1-primitive}, the primitives on the incoming and outgoing
faces differ by the exact function \(G_{a,i}\); hence the face graph is exact
Lagrangian.

The disjoint-sheet topology is locally a product at an interior point, a
half-product at a face, and a finite orthant at a corner.  Face identifications
are proper because source and target faces are compact and the graph is
closed.  The quotient is therefore Hausdorff and second countable.  The clock
increases at unit speed between impacts.  At a labelled face the exact graph
specifies the unique outgoing state; at a corner the finite set of incident
labels is retained, so hybrid continuation is defined by the declared label.
The return time is one, and the branch formula gives (iii)--(v).
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Global canonical work coordinate]
\label{prop:r16-a1-work}
On \(\mathcal M_a\times T^*\mathbb R_z\) there is a smooth autonomous
Hamiltonian whose return increments satisfy
\[
 z_{n+1}-z_n=c_i
\]
on branch \(i\).  The construction uses one global canonical pair
\((z,\pi_z)\); no branchwise one-form is required to descend through the
quotient.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Choose a partition of unity \(\chi_i\) supported in the interior of the four
channel mapping cylinders and equal to one on the middle third of channel
\(i\).  Add \(\pi_zc_i\chi_i(t)\) with the clock normalization
\(\int_0^1\chi_i(t)dt=1\).  Hamilton's equation gives
\(\dot z=c_i\chi_i(t)\), while \(\pi_z\) is constant.  The supports avoid the
face gluings, so the Hamiltonian is globally defined and the integral over one
return is \(c_i\).
\end{proof}

\subsection{Jets before norms}

Fix a reference parameter \(a_0\).  Every finite dynamic partition face
\(F\) has a reference manifold with corners \(\bar F\) and a \(C^\infty\)
embedding \(\iota_{F,a}:\bar F\to[0,1]^2\), defined one-sidedly at a birth or
death parameter.  If \(T_F\) is a finite normal current on \(\bar F\), define
its \(r\)-jet in the ambient distribution space by
\[
 J_{F}^{[r]}(a)
 =\partial_a^r(\iota_{F,a})_\#T_F,
 \qquad 0\le r\le q,
\]
where the derivative is taken against compactly supported smooth forms.  This
definition precedes every completion.

Let \(\mathscr J_a^q\) be the completion of compatible finite families
\((J_F^{[r]})_{\operatorname{codim}F+r\le q}\) for
\[
 \|J\|_{q}
 =\sum_{k+r\le q}\gamma^{k+r}
   \sum_{\operatorname{codim}F=k}
   \bigl(\mathbf M(J_F^{[r]})+
         \mathbf M(\partial J_F^{[r]})\bigr).
\]
Internal faces are identified with opposite orientation, and refinement means
restriction followed by summation.

\begin{theorem}[Closed independently generated jet complex]
\label{thm:r16-a1-jets}
The preceding expression is a norm on the compatible quotient.  Refinement is
contractive, the constant bulk current has norm one, and the operator initially
defined on smooth finite families by
\[
 \mathfrak D_aJ_F^{[r]}=J_F^{[r+1]}
\]
is closable from \(\mathscr J_a^{q+1}\) to \(\mathscr J_a^q\).  Its closure
has graph norm equivalent to the displayed \((q+1)\)-jet norm.  Consequently
\(\mathscr J_a^\infty=\bigcap_q\mathscr J_a^q\) supports every finite
material derivative without a circular definition.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Jets are already distributions on the fixed ambient square.  Additivity of
mass under disjoint restriction and cancellation of opposite internal
orientations show that a nonzero quotient class has positive mass in at least
one component.  For a smooth family, differentiation commutes with testing
and shifts the jet index.  If \(J_n\to0\) in \(\mathscr J^{q+1}\) and
\(\mathfrak D_aJ_n\to K\) in \(\mathscr J^q\), every ambient test form pairs
with \(K\) as the limit of the derivative of a sequence converging with its
first jet to zero; hence the pairing vanishes and \(K=0\).  This proves
closability.  The two graph norms contain the same finite list of masses, up
to the fixed powers of \(\gamma\).
\end{proof}

\subsection{An explicit response word algebra}

Let \(R_a^+\) and \(R_a^-\) be the reduced one-sided transfer resolvents on
the future and past H\"older spaces.  Let \(S_a^{(r)}\) denote multiplication
by the \(r\)th derivative of the centered Bernoulli score, \(C_a^{(r)}\) the
\(r\)th derivative of the present-symbol coupling, and
\(D_a^{(r)}\) the closed jet operator on \(\mathscr J_a^\infty\).
Define word sets recursively by
\[
 \mathfrak W_0=\{I\},
 \qquad
 \mathfrak W_{q+1}
 =\{D_a^{(1)}W,\ S_a^{(r)}R_a^\pm W,\ C_a^{(r)}R_a^+R_a^-W:
       W\in\mathfrak W_{q+1-r},\ 1\le r\le q+1\}.
\]
Repeated identical insertions carry the standard Bell-polynomial coefficient
\(q!/(m_1!\cdots m_q!(1!)^{m_1}\cdots(q!)^{m_q})\).

\begin{theorem}[All-order response with domains and summability]
\label{thm:r16-a1-response}
For every \(q\ge0\) and every \(C^{q+1}\) transported physical current
\(F_a\in\mathscr J_a^{q+1}\),
\[
 {d^q\over da^q}\int F_a\,dq\,dp
 =\sum_{W\in\mathfrak W_q}c(W)\,\Lambda_a(WF_a),
\]
where \(\Lambda_a\) is the invariant future--past pairing and \(c(W)\) is the
explicit Bell coefficient above.  Every word has a declared source and target
space, and on compact parameter intervals
\[
 \sum_{W\in\mathfrak W_q}|c(W)|\,
   \|\Lambda_aW\|_{\mathscr J_a^{q+1}\to\mathbb R}
 \le C_q(1-\rho)^{-2q-2}.
\]
The formula is independent of dynamic refinement.  For a fixed physical
observable the complete score, transport, and face-current sum cancels, so the
derivative of its Lebesgue integral is zero.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
On a finite bi-cylinder, differentiate the exact rectangle integral.  The
Leibniz rule produces one jet insertion, one score insertion, or one derivative
of the present-symbol coupling.  Solving the centered future and past Poisson
equations inserts \(R_a^+\) and \(R_a^-\).  Induction gives precisely the
recursive word set; grouping equal derivative orders yields the Bell
coefficients.  The one-sided spectral gaps give
\(\|R_a^\pm\|\le(1-\rho)^{-1}\), while
Theorem~\ref{thm:r16-a1-jets} bounds every jet insertion.  The number of words
at fixed order is finite, proving the displayed bound.  Refinement
compatibility follows from current restriction and internal-face cancellation.
The final statement is differentiation of the exact change-of-variables
identity on the fixed square.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Separated calibration statement]
\label{thm:r16-a1-calibration}
The mechanical family realizes the canonical field conjugate to the chosen
branch work.  A scalar entropic valuation coefficient equals that field only
when its conditional likelihood cocycle is required to be the same canonical
mechanical cocycle.  No universal preference coefficient is inferred from the
Hamiltonian construction alone.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The return likelihood ratio of the engineered exponential family is
\(\exp\{\theta A_n-n\Lambda(\theta)\}\).  An entropic certainty equivalent
with coefficient \(\vartheta\) has the same cocycle only if these exponents
agree on the nonconstant current, which forces \(\vartheta=\theta\).  Without
that compatibility axiom the two scalars belong to different structures.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Round-sixteen A1 closure]
\label{thm:r16-a1-main}
The benchmark now has a correct exact primitive, a complete Hausdorff hybrid
Hamiltonian suspension, a global canonical work coordinate, an independently
generated closed jet-current complex, and an explicit summable all-order
response algebra.  Symbolic coding, mechanics, and risk calibration remain
typed as separate claims.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Combine Lemma~\ref{lem:r16-a1-primitive},
Theorems~\ref{thm:r16-a1-hybrid}, \ref{thm:r16-a1-jets},
\ref{thm:r16-a1-response}, and \ref{thm:r16-a1-calibration}, and
Proposition~\ref{prop:r16-a1-work}.
\end{proof}
