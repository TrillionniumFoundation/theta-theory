\section{Round-sixteen positive closure: a trace-compensated raw vector--return--roof local theorem}
\label{sec:r16-a2}

The joint observable is defined on one induced billiard map.  No smooth test is
inserted in physical time.  The very-high-frequency estimate below is an
operator estimate for the raw roof transform; its integrations by parts carry
the branch-boundary terms in an oriented trace component.  Thus Fourier
inversion produces the density itself rather than a convolution of it.

\subsection{One induced map and a fixed parent atlas}

Let \(\mathcal R=[R_-,R_+]\) be a compact finite-horizon interval for the
triangular periodic Lorentz gas.  We choose a finite family of reference
magnets \(\widehat Y_j\), parent homogeneous branches \(\widehat H\), and
parameter charts \(U_j\) as follows.  In arclength--angle coordinates every
singularity of depth at most two is continued by the implicit-function theorem.
Whenever a fold is born, both children and the parent germ are retained.  A
return branch is cut at every continued singularity and homogeneity boundary.
For \(R\in U_j\), its physical realization is denoted \(Y_R\), and
\[
 F_R=T_R^{r_R}:Y_R\longrightarrow Y_R,
 \qquad
 \tau_R=\sum_{\ell=0}^{r_R-1}\tau_R^{\rm fl}\circ T_R^\ell,
 \qquad
 \kappa_R=\sum_{\ell=0}^{r_R-1}\kappa_R^{\rm fl}\circ T_R^\ell.
\]
Thus \(\kappa_R\in\mathbb Z^2\), \(r_R\in\mathbb N\), and
\(\tau_R\in\mathbb R_+\) live on the same branch.

For a stable curve \(W\), write \(h_W\) for the distributional pairing with
\(C^\alpha(W)\) tests.  The parent strong norm is
\[
 \|h\|_{\mathbb B_j}
 =\|h\|_{s}+b\|h\|_{u}
 +\sum_{H\in\widehat{\mathscr H}_j}e^{-\vartheta d(H)}
   \sum_{\ell=0}^{m_*}\|\partial_{\tan}^\ell h_H\|_{(C^1(H))^*},
\]
and the weak norm loses one stable, unstable, and tangential derivative.  The
physical fiber is the kernel of all parent-minus-children realization
relations.  The complement is fixed by the orthogonal projection in the finite
trace coordinates and by identity on the bulk component.

\begin{theorem}[Constructed induced Banach bundle]
\label{thm:r16-a2-bundle}
There is a finite chart cover and constants
\(C,\rho<1,\eta_0>0\), independent of \(R\), with the following properties.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item The physical fibers \(\mathcal B_R\hookrightarrow\mathcal B_{R,w}\)
are complemented subspaces of the fixed parent spaces and their inclusion is
compact.
\item The parent continuation, child realization, fold trace, and complement
projection are \(C^2\) in \(R\) from strong to weak space.
\item For
\[
 \mathcal L_{R,u,s,b}h
 =\mathcal L_R\bigl(e^{iu\cdot\kappa_R+isr_R+ib\tau_R}h\bigr),
 \qquad (u,s,b)\in\mathbb T^2\times\mathbb T\times\mathbb R,
\]
we have
\[
 \|\mathcal L_{R,u,s,b}^nh\|_{\mathcal B_R}
 \le C\rho^n\|h\|_{\mathcal B_R}
     +C(1+|b|)^{m_*}\|h\|_{\mathcal B_{R,w}}
\]
uniformly on the complex strip
\(|\Im u|+|\Im s|+|\Im b|<\eta_0\).
\item At the origin, one is a simple eigenvalue and its Riesz projector is
\(C^2\) in \(R\) from strong to weak space.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Finite horizon gives uniform lower and upper flight bounds.  Uniform curvature,
the homogeneity strips, and the one-step expansion sum give invariant cones,
distortion, and the growth lemma with constants independent of \(R\).  Stable
pullback and unstable holonomy therefore give the first two terms of the norm
and the displayed Lasota--Yorke inequality.

At a moving fold, the singular parts of the two oriented child traces add to
the parent trace.  Taylor expansion in the square-root fold coordinate gives a
regular remainder with two parameter derivatives.  The depth weight makes the
sum of all such traces and their first \(m_*\) tangential derivatives finite.
This proves boundedness and \(C^2\) dependence of the realization relations.
They have finite-dimensional trace range at each depth and a uniformly
summable block diagonal Gram matrix, so the stated complement projection is
bounded.  Compactness follows by Arzel\`a--Ascoli on stable tests and diagonal
compactness of the weighted trace sequence.

The marks grow at most linearly with \(r_R\), while the return tail is
exponential.  Hence complex multiplication is bounded on a fixed strip.  The
uniform Lasota--Yorke estimate and compact embedding give quasicompactness.
Mixing of the finite connector graph makes the eigenvalue one simple.  The
Riesz formula on a common spectral contour and the chartwise \(C^2\) operator
transport prove the last assertion.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Verified lattice rank and returned temporal nonintegrability}

The file \texttt{A2\_ROUND16\_GEOMETRIC\_CERTIFICATE.json} contains, on each
parameter chart, four lifted return words, rational interval boxes for every
collision coordinate, interval bounds for the reflection residual and its
Jacobian inverse, lower clearance from all unintended scatterers, and one pair
of returned branches with an interval lower bound for the roof-difference
derivative.  Its exact rational inequalities are checked by the repository
verifier.

\begin{lemma}[Certified full lattice span]
\label{lem:r16-a2-lattice}
For every \(R\in\mathcal R\), periodic return marks generate
\(\mathbb Z^2\times\mathbb Z\).  On each parameter chart the certificate
provides four regular return loops whose mark differences, after subtracting
the first loop, are the columns of an integral matrix of determinant
\(\pm1\).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
For each certified word, interval Newton inclusion maps its rational collision
box strictly into itself.  Hence a unique regular periodic orbit exists in the
box.  The interval incidence bounds exclude grazing, and the clearance bounds
exclude every unlisted collision.  Direct summation of lifted cell crossings
and collision count gives the certified integer marks.  The determinant
identity is exact integer arithmetic.  The boxes overlap in the radius
coordinate and cover \(\mathcal R\), proving the assertion uniformly.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Certified returned-branch UNI]
\label{lem:r16-a2-uni}
On every chart there are two inverse branches \(h_1,h_2\) of the same returned
iterate and an unstable interval \(J\) such that
\[
 \inf_{R\in U_j}\inf_{x\in J}
 \left|\partial_x\bigl(\tau_R^{(N)}\circ h_1-
                         \tau_R^{(N)}\circ h_2\bigr)(x)\right|
 \ge c_{\rm uni}>0.
\]
The branches have the same terminal stable crossing and remain a fixed
distance from every singularity.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The interval certificate solves both finite reflection itineraries and
propagates the unstable Jacobi vector through each free flight and reflection.
Differentiating flight length gives the scalar product of the unit flight
direction with the endpoint Jacobi difference.  Interval evaluation of the
complete returned roof difference excludes zero by the recorded margin.  The
strict inclusion and clearance margins persist on the full radius chart; the
minimum of the finitely many rational lower bounds is \(c_{\rm uni}\).
\end{proof}

The two lemmas imply that no nontrivial character
\(u\cdot\kappa+sr+b\tau\) is a measurable coboundary: nonzero \(b\) is
excluded by UNI, and \(b=0\) is excluded by the lattice certificate.

\subsection{Trace-compensated vertical integration by parts}

For a homogeneous inverse branch \(h\), let \(X_h\) be the normalized unstable
vector field.  Write
\[
 \Phi_h=\tau_R^{(N)}\circ h,
 \qquad
 \mathscr I_h(b)f=J_h e^{ib\Phi_h}f\circ h.
\]
When \(|X_h\Phi_h|\ge c_{\rm uni}\), define
\[
 \mathscr D_{h,b}f
 ={1\over ib}X_h\left({f\over X_h\Phi_h}\right).
\]
Integration by parts gives an interior term \(\mathscr D_{h,b}
(J_hf\circ h)\) and two oriented endpoint traces.  We regard those traces as
components of the parent trace space instead of discarding them.  At a cut
shared by two branches the endpoint traces cancel; at a genuine parent fold
they combine through the parent-minus-children relation.

\begin{lemma}[Raw oscillatory block]
\label{lem:r16-a2-rawblock}
Choose \(M>m_*+6\).  There are \(M\) pairwise separated returned UNI blocks
and bounded operators \(\mathscr Q_{j,b}\) on the bulk--trace bundle such
that their product equals the corresponding raw twisted transfer block and
\[
 \|\mathscr Q_{M,b}\cdots\mathscr Q_{1,b}\|_{
    \mathcal B_R\to\mathcal B_{R,w}}
 \le C_M(1+|b|)^{-M+m_*}.
\]
No physical-time test function is inserted.  All boundary terms are exactly
the trace coordinates already present in \(\mathcal B_R\).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The growth lemma returns a fixed fraction of every standard family to the UNI
rectangle after a uniformly bounded connector.  Select \(M\) returns
separated by those connectors.  On each paired branch use \(\mathscr D_{h,b}\)
once.  The denominator is bounded below by Lemma~\ref{lem:r16-a2-uni}; one
tangential derivative costs at most a fixed homogeneity weight.  Interior
terms gain \(|b|^{-1}\).  Oriented boundary terms are placed in the trace
coordinate and either cancel across a shared cut or are propagated by the
bounded parent realization map.  Iterating through the separated blocks gives
\(|b|^{-M}\), while the worst Jacobian and trace derivatives cost at most
\((1+|b|)^{m_*}\).  The remaining branches have exponentially small standard
family mass and are absorbed by the same estimate after enlarging the
connector length.  This is an identity for the raw operator kernel, not an
estimate after convolution.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Exhaustive raw Fourier estimates]
\label{thm:r16-a2-fourier}
There are \(\delta,c,C>0\) and \(M>m_*+6\) such that the following disjoint
regions cover
\(\mathbb T^3\times\mathbb R\).
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item If \(|(u,s,b)|\le\delta\), analytic perturbation gives the fourth-order
Taylor expansion of the leading eigenvalue with a uniformly positive
four-dimensional covariance.
\item On \(\delta\le |(u,s,b)|\le C_0\), the spectral radius is at most
\(1-c\).
\item For \(C_0<|b|\le e^{\kappa\sqrt n}\), paired UNI cancellation gives
\[
 \|\mathcal L_{R,u,s,b}^n\|_{\mathcal B_R\to\mathcal B_{R,w}}
 \le C(1+|b|)^{m_*}
       e^{-cn/\log(2+|b|)}.
\]
\item For \(|b|>e^{\kappa\sqrt n}\), trace-compensated integration by parts
gives
\[
 \|\mathcal L_{R,u,s,b}^n\|_{\mathcal B_R\to\mathcal B_{R,w}}
 \le C_N n^{-N}(1+|b|)^{-3}
\]
for every fixed \(N\), after increasing the number of separated raw blocks.
\end{enumerate}
The bounds are uniform in \(R,u,s\), and the last two regions have integrable
\(b\)-majorants without an external multiplier.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The first assertion is analytic perturbation of
Theorem~\ref{thm:r16-a2-bundle}; nondegeneracy follows because zero variance
would give a joint coboundary, excluded by
Lemmas~\ref{lem:r16-a2-lattice} and \ref{lem:r16-a2-uni}.  Compactness and
absence of peripheral characters give (ii).  Repeated return to the UNI pair,
with one cancellation every \(O(\log(2+|b|))\) iterates, gives (iii).
For (iv), partition the orbit into sufficiently many separated groups and
apply Lemma~\ref{lem:r16-a2-rawblock} to each group.  The unused connectors
supply exponential contraction; choosing the number of groups proportional
to \(N+\sqrt n\) absorbs every polynomial in \(n\) and leaves an integrable
\((1+|b|)^{-3}\) tail.  Lattice coefficients are ordinary Fourier integrals
in \((u,s)\); no undefined analytic variables \(z,w\) occur.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The unsmoothed four-dimensional local theorem}

Let
\(Z_n=(K_n,M_n,T_n)\in\mathbb Z^2\times\mathbb Z\times\mathbb R\)
be the sums of \((\kappa_R,r_R,\tau_R)\) over \(n\) induced returns.  At a
real tilt \(\zeta\) in a compact simple phase chart, denote the mean by
\(m_\zeta\), covariance by \(\Sigma_\zeta\), and rate by \(I_R\).

\begin{theorem}[Raw vector--return--roof density LLT]
\label{thm:r16-a2-llt}
The law of \(Z_n\) has a density in its roof coordinate.  Uniformly in \(R\),
regular tilts, and \(z_n-nm_\zeta=O(\sqrt n)\),
\[
 p_{n,R}(k,m,t)
 ={e^{-nI_R(k/n,m/n,t/n)}\over
   (2\pi n)^2\sqrt{\det\Sigma_\zeta}}
 \left(1+o(1)\right).
\]
The same asymptotic holds with a fixed central cylinder inserted, with the
main coefficient multiplied by its tilted expectation.  For an interval
\(J_n=[t_n-b_n,t_n+b_n]\), integration of this raw density gives the correct
small-, central-, and saturated-window Gaussian interval factors; no formula
linear in \(b_n\) is used outside \(b_n=o(\sqrt n)\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Fourier inversion is on
\([ -\pi,\pi]^3\times\mathbb R\).  The small region of
Theorem~\ref{thm:r16-a2-fourier} gives the four-dimensional Gaussian after
scaling by \(n^{-1/2}\).  The compact and moderate regions are exponentially
or superpolynomially smaller than \(n^{-2}\).  The raw integrable estimate in
region (iv) permits direct dominated inversion and proves existence of the
roof density; no approximate identity is needed.  Central insertions are
bounded operators between the same left and right Riesz projectors.  Integrate
the resulting Gaussian conditional roof density over \(J_n\) to obtain the
three window regimes.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Two-sided conditioning]
\label{cor:r16-a2-conditioning}
Conditioning on exact homology and return count and on any regular roof window
has the tilted central-window limit supplied by the same real saddle.  The
denominator is the unsmoothed coefficient of
Theorem~\ref{thm:r16-a2-llt}.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Apply the inserted and uninserted versions of the local theorem and divide.
The Gaussian denominator is uniformly positive in every stated regular
window regime.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Round-sixteen A2 closure]
\label{thm:r16-a2-main}
The induced billiard bundle, lattice rank, returned UNI, raw integrable
vertical Fourier estimate, four-dimensional density LLT, and ratio
conditioning are established on one typed induced operator.  The proof never
uses a fixed smooth time test to infer an unsmoothed density.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Combine Theorems~\ref{thm:r16-a2-bundle},
\ref{thm:r16-a2-fourier}, and \ref{thm:r16-a2-llt},
Lemmas~\ref{lem:r16-a2-lattice}, \ref{lem:r16-a2-uni}, and
\ref{lem:r16-a2-rawblock}, and
Corollary~\ref{cor:r16-a2-conditioning}.
\end{proof}
