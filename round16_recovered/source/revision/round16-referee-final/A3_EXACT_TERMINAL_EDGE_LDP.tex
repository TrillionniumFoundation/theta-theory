\section{Round-sixteen positive closure: exact terminal entropy and legal stopped renewal large deviations}
\label{sec:r16-a3}

The stopped state is defined as a measurable image of the complete induced
edge sequence.  Its rate is the contraction of the exact predictable relative
entropy; in particular the terminal branch-selection cost is derived, not
postulated.  All approximations stay on the legal edge shift and preserve
vertex balance.

\subsection{The legal marked edge process}

Let \(G=(V,E)\) be the countable directed graph of homogeneous first-return
branches of the A2 induced billiard.  An edge \(e\) records its source and
target vertices, collision length \(r(e)\), physical roof \(\tau(e)\), lifted
homology \(\kappa(e)\), collision-path profile \(\iota_c(e)\), physical-path
profile \(\iota_p(e)\), and all one-sided singular germs.  The reference
conditional edge probability is \(w(e\mid\xi)\), where \(\xi\) is the complete
past.  A predictable control is a family
\(q_j(e\mid\mathcal F_{j-1})\) supported on the outgoing edges of the current
vertex.

For a finite controlled word \(e_1,\\dots,e_N\), the exact logarithmic
likelihood ratio is
\[
 \sum_{j=1}^N
 \log {q_j(e_j\mid\mathcal F_{j-1})
       \over w(e_j\mid\mathcal F_{j-1})}.
\]
Every sampled edge is paid once, including the edge on which a deterministic
clock stops.

\begin{theorem}[Predictable entropy identity]
\label{thm:r16-a3-entropy}
For every predictable legal control and every bounded stopping time \(N\),
\[
 H(Q|P)_{\mathcal F_N}
 =\mathbb E_Q\sum_{j=1}^{N}
 H\bigl(q_j(\cdot\mid\mathcal F_{j-1})
       \mid w(\cdot\mid\mathcal F_{j-1})\bigr).
\]
The identity remains valid for the collision and physical stopping indices by
monotone convergence under the exponential return/roof moment of A2.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The controlled density is the product of the one-step conditional density
ratios.  Taking its logarithm, conditioning term by term, and using the tower
property gives the displayed chain rule for bounded \(N\).  Stop at
\(N\wedge m\) and let \(m\to\infty\).  The exponential moment controls the
overshoot and makes the entropy sums uniformly integrable on every finite-cost
sublevel.
\end{proof}

\subsection{An explicit stopped state}

Let \(\overline{\mathcal E}\) be the disjoint union of the legal edge profiles
and their graph-completed singular limits.  Give an edge profile the metric
\[
 d_E(e,e')=1_{(s(e),t(e))\ne(s(e'),t(e'))}
 +|r(e)^{-1}-r(e')^{-1}|
 +d_{J_1}^{\rm loc}(\iota_c(e),\iota_c(e'))
 +d_{J_1}^{\rm loc}(\iota_p(e),\iota_p(e'))
 +\sum_{m\ge1}2^{-m}(1\wedge d_m^{\rm germ}).
\]
The boundary \(r^{-1}=0\) contains only limits of actual legal branches.  For a
collision horizon \(n\), let
\[
 N_c(n)=\min\left\{N:\sum_{j=1}^{N}r(e_j)>n\right\},
 \qquad
 \ell_c=n-\sum_{j<N_c(n)}r(e_j).
\]
The terminal coordinate is either
\[
 \mathfrak t_c^{\rm full}=(e_{N_c(n)},\ell_c)
\]
when the full edge label is observed, or the prefix path of length \(\ell_c\)
when only the prefix is observed.  Physical stopping is defined analogously.
The stopped empirical state records the invariant edge flow, all bounded
profile tests, the length-weighted mesoscopic/recession measures, singular
exposure, and the terminal coordinate.

\begin{proposition}[Polish stopped-state space]
\label{prop:r16-a3-space}
There is a complete separable metric \(d_{\rm stop}\) on the preceding state
such that:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item evaluation of every bounded local path test is continuous away from its
explicit graph-completed singular germ;
\item source/target balance and legality are closed;
\item rate sublevels with the A2 exponential mark moment are compact; and
\item the collision and physical stopped-state maps from the full edge sequence
are Borel measurable.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Use a countable determining family of bounded local profile tests and the
product of their weak metrics, together with the discrete vertex metric and
\(r^{-1}\).  Complete the set of actual legal edge profiles rather than an
abstract product; this makes the boundary closed by construction.  Flow
balance is a countable family of linear equalities.  Entropy coercivity plus
the exponential mark moment gives uniform integrability of \(r,\tau\) and
tightness of all local profile coordinates.  Prokhorov and diagonal compactness
then give compact rate sublevels.  Stopping indices and prefixes are measurable
functions of the mark sequence.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The exact terminal contraction}

Let \(\mathfrak S_c\) be the stopped-state map on a complete controlled edge
sequence.  Define
\[
 I_c(z)=\inf\left\{
  \mathcal H(Q|P):\mathfrak S_c(Q)=z
 \right\},
\]
where \(\mathcal H\) is the predictable entropy per deterministic collision
clock obtained from Theorem~\ref{thm:r16-a3-entropy}.  The physical rate is the
analogous contraction per physical clock.

\begin{lemma}[Terminal branch cost]
\label{lem:r16-a3-terminal}
If the stopped state contains the full terminal edge \(e\), the terminal
contribution is the complete conditional branch entropy
\[
 -\log w(e\mid\xi)
\]
(or its controlled relative-entropy analogue), paid exactly once.  If the
state contains only a prefix \(\pi\), its terminal contribution is
\[
 -\log\sum_{e:\,\operatorname{prefix}_{\ell}(e)=\pi}
       w(e\mid\xi),
\]
the negative logarithm of the legal completion event.  No valid contraction
contains the fractional expression
\(\ell r(e)^{-1}[-\log w(e\mid\xi)]\).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
For the full-edge state, the stopping map retains the atom \(e\); contraction
of the one-step conditional law therefore leaves its full log density.  For a
prefix state, the fiber of the stopping map is exactly the set of legal edges
with that prefix.  Summing their probabilities before taking the logarithm
gives the second formula.  Both statements are direct contractions of the
chain-rule entropy in Theorem~\ref{thm:r16-a3-entropy}.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Legal finite-memory relaxation}

For an invariant controlled edge law \(Q\), let \(Q^{[k]}\) be the conditional
expectation of its edge kernel given the last \(k\) vertices and edges.  This is
still supported on the actual outgoing edges of the current vertex.  Its
finite graph truncation is balanced by solving the finite incidence equation
for the retained edge flow and routing the discarded imbalance through a
fixed finite connector family.

\begin{theorem}[Entropy-preserving edge Markovization]
\label{thm:r16-a3-markovization}
For every invariant legal controlled law of finite rate there are finite-state,
irreducible, legal \(k\)-step Markov laws \(Q_{k,L,\varepsilon}\) such that:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item their source and target marginals agree exactly;
\item every transition is an edge or connector word in the original graph;
\item all prescribed bounded profile coordinates and truncated mark moments
converge;
\item
\(\mathcal H(Q_{k,L,\varepsilon}|P)	o\mathcal H(Q|P)\); and
\item the total connector length and entropy are \(o\) of either deterministic
clock.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Conditional expectation lowers relative entropy and preserves the current
vertex, hence legality.  Truncate to a finite set carrying all but
\(\varepsilon\) of the probability, return, roof, and entropy mass.  The
truncated edge flow has a finite divergence vector of total mass zero.  On
each communicating component route its positive and negative parts along the
fixed legal connector paths; the correction has mass bounded by the discarded
flow.  Add \(\varepsilon\) times a fixed irreducible circulation on the same
finite graph.  Normalize row by row.  Convexity of entropy and the exponential
reference moment give convergence of entropy and marks, while the connector
contribution tends to zero in both clocks.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Full stopped lower and upper bounds}

\begin{theorem}[Collision-clock stopped LDP]
\label{thm:r16-a3-collision}
The collision-clock stopped empirical state satisfies a full LDP with speed
\(n\) and good rate \(I_c\).  The rate includes recurrent edge flow,
mesoscopic profiles at every scale, recession mass, singular exposure, and the
terminal cost of Lemma~\ref{lem:r16-a3-terminal}.  Finite rate is possible only
for closed legal balanced states in
Proposition~\ref{prop:r16-a3-space}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For a finite Markov approximation, the empirical edge flow and terminal edge
have the ordinary finite-state Markov type LDP; its entropy is exactly the
chain-rule entropy including the final transition.  Exponential return moments
make truncation of long marks exponentially good.  A diagonal projective limit
over local profile tests, mark truncations, singular-germ neighborhoods, and
mesoscopic scales gives the upper bound and exponential tightness.

For the lower bound, begin with a finite-rate state and choose a controlled law
within \(\varepsilon\) of its contraction infimum.  Apply
Theorem~\ref{thm:r16-a3-markovization}.  Generate a typical legal Markov word,
insert actual legal long excursions whose empirical profiles approximate each
recession coordinate, and route every insertion through the connector
circulation.  Choose disjoint scales successively so later insertions alter
earlier coordinates by at most \(2^{-j}\varepsilon\).  Stop inside the last
sampled branch and retain its complete terminal coordinate.  The exact Markov
type count pays every selected branch once; connector and terminal errors are
sublinear.  Diagonalization proves the lower bound for every finite-rate
state.  Compactness of rate sublevels follows from
Proposition~\ref{prop:r16-a3-space}.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Physical-clock LDP and clock surgery]
\label{thm:r16-a3-physical}
The physical stopped empirical state satisfies a full LDP with speed \(T\) and
rate
\[
 I_p(z)=\inf_{\mathfrak S_p(Q)=z}
 {\mathcal H(Q|P)\over \mathbb E_Q\tau}.
\]
The lower bound remains valid when a macroscopic terminal branch is present;
its branch entropy is paid in full and its physical prefix is retained.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Partition finite-rate laws by their mean roof.  On each bin the number of
complete branches is \(T/\mathbb E_Q\tau+o(T)\), and the collision-clock LDP
gives the displayed exponent.  For the lower bound, use the legal Markov
recovery above, concatenate complete typical cycles, and choose the last actual
edge so that its roof crosses \(T\).  The overshoot and the pointed prefix are
kept in the state; no deletion or fractional entropy adjustment occurs.
Exponential roof moments control the upper tail and permit passage between
bins.  Taking the projective limit gives the full physical-clock LDP.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Information projections]
\label{cor:r16-a3-projection}
Every continuous finite-dimensional macro constraint has a compact minimizer
set on either stopped state.  At a unique exposed minimizer, conditioning on
shrinking regular neighborhoods concentrates exponentially on its uniformly
rooted central path law.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Goodness gives compact attainment.  A unique exposed minimizer has a positive
rate gap outside every neighborhood.  Apply the upper bound to the numerator
and the recovery lower bound to the denominator; uniform rooting changes only
a vanishing endpoint fraction.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Round-sixteen A3 closure]
\label{thm:r16-a3-main}
The predictable controlled renewal process has explicit Polish stopped states,
legal entropy-preserving Markov approximations, exact full-branch or
prefix-event terminal costs, and full collision- and physical-clock LDPs.  No
branch-selection entropy is diluted by the observed fraction of its path.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Combine Theorems~\ref{thm:r16-a3-entropy},
\ref{thm:r16-a3-markovization}, \ref{thm:r16-a3-collision}, and
\ref{thm:r16-a3-physical}, Proposition~\ref{prop:r16-a3-space},
Lemma~\ref{lem:r16-a3-terminal}, and
Corollary~\ref{cor:r16-a3-projection}.
\end{proof}
