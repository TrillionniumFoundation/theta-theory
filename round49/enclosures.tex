\section{Certified outer sets with an explicit input-representation budget}
\label{sec:enclosures}
A statistical band and its represented input interval are separate objects.
Let $I_q=[\widehat y_q-R_q,\widehat y_q+R_q]$ and suppose a certified rational
input satisfies
\begin{equation}
 I_q\subset I_q^{\rm rat}\subset
 [\widehat y_q-R_q-\epsilon_q,\widehat y_q+R_q+\epsilon_q].
 \label{eq:representation}
\end{equation}
Here $\epsilon_q\ge0$ includes outward rounding of the center and endpoints;
it is not a tail or Taylor error. The implementation accepts exact rational
reference bands and verifies \eqref{eq:representation} against a separately
supplied rational budget. For nonrational original bands, first form a certified
reference band enclosing them and carry that first enclosure error as part of
$\epsilon_q$. It must not be silently declared zero.

We allow an observable
$y_q(\beta)=\sum_l\lambda_{ql}h_\beta(t_{ql})$ with rational coefficients and
nonnegative rational times. A step cell has one coefficient $1$; a lagged pulse
has coefficients $1,-1$ at $k\Delta+t_0,k\Delta$. Let
$d_q=\sum_l|\lambda_{ql}|$ and $T_c=\max_{q,l}t_{ql}>0$.
For rational box endpoints choose $K\ge J+1$, a finite coordinate mesh of
half-width at most $r$, and Taylor order $P$. Cut the chain at site $K$.
Its variables are $c,b_0,\ldots,b_K,a_0,\ldots,a_{K-1}$; the edge out is removed.
At each mesh center compute exactly
\[
 H_q=\sum_l\lambda_{ql}\ell\sum_{k=0}^P A_K^kB\,
                         t_{ql}^{k+1}/(k+1)!.
\]
With $E_0=3^{\lceil\Lambda T_c\rceil}$ set the rational component bounds
\begin{equation}
 \begin{aligned}
 E_{\rm tail}&=2T_cE_0(\Lambda T_c)^K/K!,&
 E_{\rm mesh}&=2rT_c^2E_0,\\
 E_{\rm num}&=T_cE_0(\Lambda T_c)^{P+1}/(P+1)!,&
 E_q&=d_q(E_{\rm tail}+E_{\rm mesh}+E_{\rm num}).
 \end{aligned}
 \label{eq:outer-errors}
\end{equation}
Retain a full mesh box when every computed interval $[H_q-E_q,H_q+E_q]$
intersects $I_q^{\rm rat}$. Return its requested prefix projection together
with the retained full boxes and the separate error components.

\begin{theorem}[Coverage-preserving outer construction and corrected diameter]
\label{thm:outer}
The returned union contains the prefix projection of the original feasible
confidence set. Every admissible completion of a retained \emph{full} $K$-box
satisfies
\begin{equation}
 |y_q(\beta)-\widehat y_q|\le R_q+\epsilon_q+2E_q.
 \label{eq:outer-radius}
\end{equation}
On a step certificate grid, the condition
$\max_q(R_q+\epsilon_q+2E_q)\le\delta/(4LA)$ certifies $d_J$ diameter at most
$\delta$; on the sampled-pulse grid replace $A$ by $\mathcal A_{N,R}$.
The statement about projected prefixes is existential: each retained prefix
has a witnessing retained full box. Arbitrarily appending a different tail
to that prefix need not preserve the bands and is not claimed to do so.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The cut chain and a full completion agree on boundary words through order
$K-1$; exponential tails give $E_{\rm tail}$. Finite-coordinate error at most
$r$ changes the generator by at most $4r$; integrating
$4rt e^{\Lambda t}$ gives $E_{\rm mesh}$. The exponential-series remainder
gives $E_{\rm num}$. All generators have norm at most $\Lambda$, including the
cut chain, and $e^{\Lambda T_c}\le E_0$. The triangle inequality for the
observable coefficients gives the error $E_q$ uniformly on each full box.
Every originally feasible parameter therefore has its box retained.
If the two input intervals intersect, a point in the intersection is within
$E_q$ of $H_q$ and within $R_q+\epsilon_q$ of $\widehat y_q$. Another $E_q$
reaches any full completion, proving \eqref{eq:outer-radius}.
For each pair of projected prefixes choose their witnessing full boxes and
admissible infinite completions, then apply the step or pulse diameter argument.
The bound concerns only the prefix and so is independent of the witnesses.
\end{proof}

For every positive target error, increasing $K,P$ makes both factorial tails
small, and a sufficiently fine finite mesh makes the remaining error small.
The finite enumeration terminates with unrestricted finite resources. The
implementation checks the total box count before enumeration and raises
\texttt{ResourceLimit} rather than returning a partial union as complete.
There is no polynomial-time or practical large-depth claim. The original
statistical, representation, tail, mesh, and numerical budgets are reported
separately, including when the sufficient diameter test fails.

For example, the Round 48 witness uses the reference band
$[-10^{-7},10^{-7}]$ and represented interval $[-1,1]$. It requires
$\epsilon\ge1-10^{-7}$; declaring $\epsilon=0$ is rejected by the new input
validator. The coarse interval can legitimately retain a box, but no diameter
claim based only on $10^{-7}+2E_q$ is then issued. Refining the parameter mesh
cannot eliminate this independently specified input error.
