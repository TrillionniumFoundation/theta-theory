\section{Retained adaptive limits and earlier mathematical results}
For a fixed infinite policy, or any chosen coupling of horizon-dependent rows,
let
\[
 Q_n(\beta)=(2n\sigma^2)^{-1}\sum_{i\le n}
                \E[f_i(\beta)^2\mid\mathcal F_{i-1}].
\]
This is the original \emph{stagewise} predictable information, not the grouped
quantity used for finite-block contraction. For any fixed resolution $t>0$,
Gaussian score exponential bounds and bounded-square martingale bounds at a
deterministic metric net, followed by interpolation as in
Theorem~\ref{thm:variance}, give a bound of the form
$4\exp\{\overline{\mathcal H}(e_t)-cnt^2\}+e^{-n}$ for the centered fields.
Here $e_t>0$ is fixed and $c>0$ is common to the policy class. This bound is
summable. Together with Lemma~\ref{lem:remainder}, Borel--Cantelli at countably
many $t\downarrow0$ gives
\[
 \sup_\beta\left|n^{-1}\log
 [L_n^{\zeta_n}(\beta)/L_n^{\zeta_n}(\beta_0)]+Q_n(\beta)\right|\to0
 \quad\text{almost surely}.
\]
The probability-one assertion is for each chosen policy sequence and coupling;
it is not a simultaneous intersection over uncountably many policies.

\begin{proposition}[Actual-information Laplace tracking and cluster LDPs]
\label{prop:ldp}
For a fixed full-support prior on $\Bcal$, on that same event, for every
continuous $\varphi$,
\[
 -n^{-1}\log\int e^{-n\varphi(\beta)}\Pi_n^{\zeta_n}(d\beta)
       -\inf_\beta\{\varphi(\beta)+Q_n(\beta)\}\to0.
\]
The information functions are uniformly precompact. Any uniformly convergent
subsequence has a full good posterior LDP with its limit as rate function.
Without convergence, the unconditional upper exponent for closed sets is
$-\rho a^2\inf_FD_\chi/(2\sigma^2)$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The functions $Q_n$ are uniformly bounded, vanish at the truth, and have common
Lipschitz constant $M/\sigma^2$ in $d$. Arzela--Ascoli gives precompactness.
Full support and a finite cover imply $m(s)=\inf_\beta\Pi(B_d(\beta,s))>0$.
Integration over an $s$-ball about a minimizer shows
\[
 \inf(\varphi+Q_n)\le-n^{-1}\log\int e^{-n(\varphi+Q_n)}d\Pi
 \le\inf(\varphi+Q_n)+\omega_\varphi(s)+Ms/\sigma^2-n^{-1}\log m(s).
\]
Apply the uniform likelihood limit, normalize using $\varphi=0$, and let
$n\to\infty$, then $s\downarrow0$. On a uniformly convergent subsequence,
integrating over an open neighborhood and bounding over a closed compact set
gives the two LDP inequalities. This deterministic argument applies to every
continuous function and every Borel-set inequality on the same event.
The stagewise sign floor gives the final upper bound; it need not equal the
actual rate function.
\end{proof}
