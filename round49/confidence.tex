\section{Honest confidence with distinct sampling and allocation weights}
\label{sec:confidence}
Write $w_q=\chi_q$ for a sampling probability and let $\eta_q>0$ be any
confidence-allocation weights with $\sum_q\eta_q=1$. These are different
quantities throughout. Count exploration visits only. At $l=N_q(n)>0$, let
$\widehat h_q=(al)^{-1}\sum_{i\text{ visits }q}S_iY_i$ and set
\begin{equation}
 R_{q,n}=\frac1a\sqrt{\frac{2v_*}{l}
            \log\frac{2l(l+1)}{\alpha\eta_q}},
 \qquad v_*=\sigma^2+B_*^2,
 \label{eq:bands}
\end{equation}
with $B_*$ as in Section~\ref{sec:blocks}. A zero count imposes no constraint.

\begin{theorem}[Countably simultaneous confidence and coefficient diameter]
\label{thm:confidence}
For a fixed infinite admissible policy, the bands \eqref{eq:bands} cover all
$h_{\beta_0}(t_q)$ simultaneously in $q,n$ with probability at least $1-\alpha$.
At a fixed horizon this coverage is uniform over the permitted horizon-dependent
policies. A data-selected finite set of cells may therefore be used to form
\[
 \mathcal C_n=\{\beta:|h_\beta(t_q)-\widehat h_q|\le R_{q,n}
                   \text{ on the selected cells}\}.
\]
On either certificate grid of Proposition~\ref{prop:separation}, if
$\max_q R_{q,n}\le\delta/(4LA)$, then $\diam_{d_J}\mathcal C_n\le\delta$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At a visit, $Y_i=aS_i h_{\beta_0}(t_q)+B_i^0+\xi_i$, with a pre-sign
intercept of magnitude at most $B_*$. Conditional exponential moments of
$S_i(B_i^0+\xi_i)$ are at most
$e^{\sigma^2\lambda^2/2}\cosh(\lambda B_i^0)\le e^{v_*\lambda^2/2}$.
Stop the resulting nonnegative supermartingale at the $l$th visit, first with
a deterministic horizon cutoff. The two-sided threshold in \eqref{eq:bands}
has error at most $\alpha\eta_q/[l(l+1)]$. Sum in $l,q$ and then remove the
cutoff. This does not condition a selected sum on its terminal count and
call it Gaussian. Coverage is a single event, so data-selected collections
inherit it. Two candidates in $\mathcal C_n$ differ on the grid by at most
$2\max R$; apply \eqref{eq:grid}, $LAE\le\delta/2$, and \eqref{eq:jet}.
The known origin is not sampled. Empty sets have diameter zero and are excluded
on the coverage event.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Corrected explicit visit budget]
\label{prop:visits}
Fix a predetermined grid $G$ with $g$ labels. Suppose each label's conditional
visit probability is at least $\pi>0$. For equal exploration mass $w$ one may
take $\pi=\rho w$; for unequal masses take
$\pi=\rho\min_{q\in G}w_q$. Set
$\eta_{\min}=\min_{q\in G}\eta_q$ and fix confidence and count error levels
$\alpha_c,\alpha_v\in(0,1)$. For a desired response radius $r_*>0$, a variance
proxy $v>0$, and a total deterministic residual budget $R_b\ge0$, define
\begin{equation}
 B_v=\max\{1,16v/(a^2\pi r_*^2)\},\quad
 C_v=4/(\alpha_c\eta_{\min}),\quad L_v=\log(eC_v(16B_v)^2).
 \label{eq:visit-constants}
\end{equation}
It suffices to take
\begin{equation}
 n\ge\left\lceil\max\{3,16B_vL_v,
 4R_b/(a\pi r_*),\ (8/\pi)\log(g/\alpha_v)\}\right\rceil
 \label{eq:visit-budget}
\end{equation}
to have all grid radii at most $r_*$ with count failure at most $\alpha_v$,
and coverage and the associated diameter guarantee together with probability
at least $1-\alpha_c-\alpha_v$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For indicators with conditional success probability at least $\pi$, their
conditional Laplace transform gives
$\Pp\{N_q(n)<n\pi/2\}\le e^{-n\pi/8}$. This follows, for example, by using
$\lambda=\log2$ in the product bound
$\E e^{-\lambda N_q(n)}\le(1-\pi+\pi e^{-\lambda})^n$.
Union over $g$ labels proves the count bound. On its complement, using
$N_q(n)\le n$ and $\eta_q\ge\eta_{\min}$, each radius including residual
bias is at most
\[
 \sqrt{4v\log(C_vn^2)/(a^2n\pi)}+2R_b/(an\pi).
\]
At $n_0=16B_vL_v$,
$\log(C_vn_0^2)=L_v-1+2\log L_v\le3L_v\le16L_v$.
Since $C_v\ge4$, $\log(C_vn^2)/n$ decreases for $n\ge3$; thus
$n\ge B_v\log(C_vn^2)$ holds for every $n$ in \eqref{eq:visit-budget}.
It bounds the square-root term by $r_*/2$, and the bias term by $r_*/2$.
The confidence logarithm uses $\eta_{\min}$, while visit counts use $\pi$.
Neither is substituted for the other.
\end{proof}

In particular, on either step grid with sampling mass $w=p$,
$\kappa=p\delta^2/(4L^2A^2)$ and $r_*^2=\delta^2/(16L^2A^2)$ give the exact
identity
\[
 \frac{16v}{a^2\rho p r_*^2}=\frac{64v}{a^2\rho\kappa}.
\]
Hence, when $\eta_q=w_q$ or the allocation logarithms are of the same order,
confidence diameters have inverse-$\kappa$ cost up to logarithmic factors.
With error levels tending to zero polynomially, the sufficient regions are
$C_c r+12s<1$ and $C_\infty r+8s<1$ in the respective scales of
Corollary~\ref{cor:stage-rates}. Arbitrarily tiny allocations may increase the
budget; the theorem expressly retains that dependence. A grid selected after
the data retains coverage, but the predetermined-grid count bound is not
silently transferred to it.
