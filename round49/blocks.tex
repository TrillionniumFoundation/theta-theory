\section{Retained-state blocks: exact posterior and finite-sample confidence}
\label{sec:blocks}
Choose $\tau_g=t_0$ from \eqref{eq:fast-atom}, and make every baseline gap exactly
$t_0$. Partition stages into blocks of length $m=N+1$ from
\eqref{eq:block-certificate}. Before each block, choose a bounded baseline
profile, or a sequence of stage profiles, based on earlier blocks only.
Its dependence on the block's durations may be specified in advance; it must
not depend on any sign or readout within that block. Zero baseline is an
admissible example. Draw the original independent exploration flags, and on
exploration draw $\omega$ independently at every stage. Nonexploration uses
$q_0$. Signs remain independent at all stages. This is a subclass of the
original chronological experiment, with block-committed control and no hidden
observations, reset, or added long probe.

A block is called successful if every stage explores and selects $q_0$.
Conditional on its starting history, this event has probability
$\pi_N=(\rho w_0)^m$ and is independent of all signs and readout noises within
that block. Such success events can be determined by revealing the independent
action randomizers in advance for the analysis; the implemented chronological
law remains the same. On success all endpoint spacings within the block are
$\Delta$.

\begin{lemma}[A block-conditional information floor on the complete record]
\label{lem:block-floor}
For a block $G_b$ and its starting history,
\begin{equation}
 \E\left[\sum_{i\in G_b}f_i(\beta)^2\mid\mathcal F_{b,-}\right]
 \ge a^2\pi_N\sum_{k=0}^N(b_{k,\beta}-b_{k,\beta_0})^2.
 \label{eq:block-floor}
\end{equation}
In particular, on $\{d_J(\beta,\beta_0)\ge\delta\}$ this is at least
$a^2\kappa_b$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Condition on success, the committed profiles, and all signs other than the
first sign $S_{b,0}$. At lag $k$ in a successful block, linearity of the complete
candidate trajectories gives
\[
 f_{b,k}(\beta)=P_{b,k}(\beta)
       +aS_{b,0}(b_{k,\beta}-b_{k,\beta_0}).
\]
The first term includes the complete pre-block input, the baseline, and all
other signed pulses, but not $S_{b,0}$. Block commitment is what makes it
independent of that sign under this conditioning. Averaging its square over
$S_{b,0}$ leaves the nonnegative intercept square and the displayed pulse square.
Average other actions and multiply by $\pi_N$; the contribution of unsuccessful
blocks is nonnegative. Proposition~\ref{prop:block-certificate} proves the last
claim. No likelihood has been conditioned on success or had data discarded.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Exact bounded-probe posterior recovery and resource bound]
\label{thm:block-posterior}
For $n'=Bm$ readouts, use
\begin{equation}
 q_b=a^2\kappa_b/m
 \label{eq:block-q}
\end{equation}
in Theorem~\ref{thm:posterior}, with group-size cap $m$. This gives a finite
posterior guarantee and the explicit budget \eqref{eq:new-budget} for the
complete likelihood of all $n'$ readouts. The clock satisfies
$\mathsf T_{n'}\le(t_0+T)n'$ deterministically.
Consequently all positive conclusions in Theorem~\ref{thm:main} hold.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Lemma~\ref{lem:block-floor} gives $V_{n'}\ge Ba^2\kappa_b=n'q_b$ on the
separated set. The common full-input metric, Gaussian chronology, product prior,
and initial-state envelope have already been verified in
Section~\ref{sec:likelihood}. Therefore Theorem~\ref{thm:posterior} applies
without a change of likelihood.
For an observation budget $n$, choose $J_n,\delta_n$ as in the main theorem,
compute $m_n$, and use $n'=m_n\lfloor n/m_n\rfloor$ readouts. If
$\gamma=C_b r+p_b s<1$, then $m_n=O(\log n)$ and
$\kappa_b\ge e^{-C_b}n^{-\gamma}$, whereas $n'/n\to1$.
Since $C_{m_n}=O(m_n)$,
\[
 n'q_b/C_{m_n}\ge c n^{1-\gamma}/(\log n)^2,
 \qquad n'q_b\ge c n^{1-\gamma}/\log n.
\]
The direct certificate also gives $\log(1/q_b)=O(\log n)$, so entropy and
negative log prior mass are $O((\log n)^2)$. All failure probabilities vanish
and the posterior exponent diverges. The bound is uniform over the external
initial state, coefficient truth, working initial-state law, and chosen
block-committed profiles. If more than half the posterior lies in
$d_J<\delta$, each finite-prefix coordinate median lies within $\delta$ of
that truth. The product box admits a completion of those medians, giving an
estimator without an unspecified selection oracle. Every stage lasts $t_0+t_i$
with $t_i\le T$, proving the clock statement.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Direct pulse confidence from successful blocks}
For a fixed selected $(J,\delta)$, let $M_B$ count successful blocks among the
first $B$. For each lag $k\le N$, define
\[
 \widehat b_k=(aM_B)^{-1}\sum_{b\text{ successful}}S_{b,0}Y_{b,k}
 \quad(M_B>0),\qquad
 v_* =\sigma^2+B_*^2,\quad B_*=C_*(R_0+U_*/\lambda_*).
\]
Choose positive allocation weights $\eta_k$ summing to one over $0\le k\le N$.
At $M_B=l>0$ the simultaneous radii are
\begin{equation}
 r_{k,l}=a^{-1}\sqrt{2v_*\log(2l(l+1)/(\alpha\eta_k))/l}.
 \label{eq:pulse-bands}
\end{equation}
At no successful blocks impose no constraint.

\begin{proposition}[Pulse confidence, diameter, and finite budget]
\label{prop:pulse-confidence}
With probability at least $1-\alpha$, every pulse response is inside its band
for all block counts $B$. On this event the corresponding confidence set is
nonempty. Its $d_J$ diameter is at most $\delta$ whenever
$\max_k r_{k,M_B}\le r_*:=\delta/(4L\mathcal A_{N,R})$.
The visit budget of Proposition~\ref{prop:visits} below applies with stages
replaced by blocks, $\pi=\pi_N$, $\eta_{\min}=\min_k\eta_k$, zero residual
bias, and count factor $N+1$. Thus a finite number of bounded-duration stages
suffices with prescribed coverage and diameter error probabilities.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
On a successful block,
$Y_{b,k}=aS_{b,0}b_{k,\beta_0}+B_{b,k}^0+\xi_{b,k}$.
Conditioning on the committed profiles, success, and the other signs,
$B_{b,k}^0$ does not depend on $S_{b,0}$ and has magnitude at most $B_*$.
Its conditional exponential moment after multiplication by that sign is
bounded by $e^{v_*\lambda^2/2}$. This conditioning does not include readout
noises; they do not affect within-block actions. For each fixed lag, the
selected block increments therefore have a nonnegative exponential
supermartingale with variance proxy $v_*M_B$. Stop at the $l$th success and
allocate errors $\alpha\eta_k/[l(l+1)]$; summing proves the single coverage
event. There is no claim that these correlated lag statistics are independent.
For two elements of the set, pulse differences are at most $2\max r$.
Use \eqref{eq:sampled-jet}, $L\mathcal E\le\delta/2$ and \eqref{eq:jet}.
Finally $\Pp\{M_B<B\pi_N/2\}\le e^{-B\pi_N/8}$; using the looser
$(N+1)e^{-B\pi_N/8}$ in the cited budget is valid even though all lags
share the same count.
\end{proof}
The pulse confidence estimator is a separate, constructive consequence.
The exact posterior theorem above uses all blocks, including unsuccessful ones.
