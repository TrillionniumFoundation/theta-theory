
\section{Finite preparation mixtures and verification scope}
\label{sec:preparations}

The homogeneous lattice may begin from one of finitely many latent
preparation laws.  This section states the resulting mixture theorem with the
latent-variable and policy assumptions made explicit.

Let \(J<\infty\), let \(L\in\{1,\ldots,J\}\) have prior probabilities
\(w_j>0\), and, conditional on \(L=j\), let the working initial-state law be
\(\nu_j\), supported in the radius-\(R\) Hilbert ball.  The parameter prior is
common to all labels.  The policy is also common: it is a measurable function
of the observed record and a fresh randomizer, and it is not told the latent
label.  Under the external truth, the initial state may be fixed or sampled
from any one specified preparation; the result below concerns the working
finite mixture and its frequentist evidence asymptotics.

For one fixed infinite admissible policy, the summable nuisance series of
Lemma~\ref{lem:nuisance-budget} has a \(C^r\) limit
\[
 R_\infty^{\nu_j}(\vartheta)
 =\lim_{n\to\infty}R_n^{\nu_j}(\vartheta)
\]
almost surely.  Define
\begin{equation}
 \rho_{j,\infty}
 =\frac{w_j\exp R_\infty^{\nu_j}(\vartheta_0)}
        {\sum_{l=1}^Jw_l\exp R_\infty^{\nu_l}(\vartheta_0)}.
 \label{eq:preparation-amplitude}
\end{equation}

\begin{theorem}[Finite-preparation evidence mixture]
\label{thm:preparation-mixture}
Under one fixed infinite balanced policy, the posterior label probabilities
converge in probability to the vector in
\eqref{eq:preparation-amplitude}.  Jointly with the rescaled physical
parameter, the posterior is at total-variation distance \(o_P(1)\) from
\begin{equation}
 \left(\sum_{j=1}^J\rho_{j,\infty}\delta_j\right)
 \otimes N(I_n^{-1}\Delta_n,I_n^{-1}).
 \label{eq:preparation-product}
\end{equation}
For a triangular sequence of horizon-dependent policies, the same conclusion
holds with finite-horizon weights proportional to
\(w_j\exp R_n^{\nu_j}(\vartheta_0)\); no common almost-sure limit across those
different policies is asserted.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Apply the relative evidence expansion
\eqref{eq:five-evidence} to each of the finitely many initial-state priors.
At \(\vartheta_0\),
\[
 L_n^{\nu_j}(\vartheta_0)
 =L_n^0(\vartheta_0)e^{R_n^{\nu_j}(\vartheta_0)}.
\]
All remaining Laplace factors are common up to a relative \(o_P(1)\) error,
uniformly over the finite label set.  Normalization therefore yields the
finite-horizon weights.  Summability gives their almost-sure limits under one
fixed infinite policy.  Conditional on every label, Theorem~\ref{thm:lattice-bvm}
gives the same Gaussian approximation.  Total variation of a finite mixture
is bounded by the sum of the component distances and the \(\ell^1\) error of
the weights, proving \eqref{eq:preparation-product}.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
The amplitudes are finite-component Bayes factors generated by a bounded
amount of early transient information.  They are not called phases, and no
thermodynamic phase transition is inferred from this theorem.
\end{remark}

\section{Reproducibility and the exact scope of computation}
\label{sec:verification}

The repository contains an executable script that checks the following finite
claims.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Exact rational applications of a sufficiently long finite Jacobi chain
reproduce \(r_0,\ldots,r_7\) in \eqref{eq:eight-jets}.  Banded locality makes
these coefficients equal to the half-line coefficients.
\item The triangular formulas
\eqref{eq:jet-inverse-one}--\eqref{eq:jet-inverse-three} recover five rational
test parameters exactly whenever the chosen square root is rational.
\item The generalized six-duration Vandermonde matrix is nonsingular by an
exact rational determinant calculation.
\item A finite Jacobi Schur recursion agrees symbolically with direct matrix
inversion at rational spectral points.
\item Every theorem label, source file, and recorded SHA--256 digest in the
Round 39 source manifest is present.
\item The document builds twice with no undefined references or citations.
\end{enumerate}
The checks do not establish the empirical-process proof, the random-information
Laplace theorem, strong Banach-dual differentiability, inverse spectral
uniqueness, or posterior consistency.  Those are analytic proofs in this
manuscript.  No formal proof assistant is claimed.

The local execution record is generated by
\texttt{tools/verify\_round39.py}; the GitHub Actions workflow runs the same
standard-library tests and the two-pass \LaTeX\ build on a clean runner.  A
workflow result is reported as successful only after GitHub records that
conclusion for the immutable revision head.

\section{What this revision closes and what it does not}
\label{sec:scope}

The present paper addresses every mathematical proof obligation in the Round
38 report concerning the focused mechanical manuscript:
\begin{itemize}
\item the triangular experiment and both filtrations are explicit;
\item the empirical net includes the true parameter, radial coordinate,
direction, and tangent boundary;
\item uniform stochastic orders are defined over the complete experiment
class;
\item the random-information relative \(L^1\) Laplace lemma is standalone;
\item misspecified initial-state priors are identified as a quasi-Bayesian
model and the pseudo-true parameter is controlled;
\item strong filter derivatives are proved in a specified dual norm with
measurability;
\item the state image is labelled finite rank;
\item preparation labels are a finite-mixture theorem;
\item the finite computational certificate is assigned its exact scope; and
\item a clean remote workflow is supplied without presuming its outcome.
\end{itemize}

The positive mathematical strengthening is substantive: the homogeneous
experiment now identifies five physical coefficients, infers the full bath
memory kernel, and uses balanced calibration rather than atomwise compulsory
exploration; the second experiment identifies and learns a complete infinite
Jacobi coefficient sequence.  These claims replace neither the assumptions
nor the dynamics of unrelated Sinai-billiard or hard-sphere programmes.  No
closure of those separate programme obligations is asserted here.
