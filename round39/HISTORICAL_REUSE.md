## New mathematical tools

### Triangular response-jet inversion

The five unknown homogeneous coefficients are recovered successively from
\((r_2,r_3,r_5,r_6,r_7)\).  The positive coupling lower bound selects the
square-root branch and makes both divisions by \(\epsilon^2\) uniform.

### Six-duration generalized Vandermonde transform

The response at \(\tau,\ldots,6\tau\), after removing \(t^2/2\), is inverted
against the powers \(t^3,\ldots,t^8\).  A uniform \(C^1\) tail bound makes
the reconstructed finite-time map a small perturbation of the triangular jet
inverse.

### Balanced-window contrast

A fresh sign removes arbitrary pre-existing state at each calibration slot.
Using all six durations once per window converts the response embedding into
conditional contrast.  A net over
\((\theta_0,r,v)\) gives simultaneous global contrast and tangent information.

### Relative random-information Laplace principle

The proof compares unnormalized local posterior densities with the moving
Gaussian kernel in relative \(L^1\), on events with bounded score and
uniformly elliptic random information.  A coercive global envelope supplies a
common integrable tail.  This closes normalization without assuming an
information limit.

### Strong concentrated-push-forward lemma

Uniform Taylor remainders over the unit ball of \(C_b^{r+1}\), combined with
total-variation derivatives of the normalized likelihood weight, give strong
dual differentiability and measurable derivative norms.

### Infinite Jacobi reconstruction and statistical separation

The damped response yields the boundary Weyl function.  Its Schur recursion
recovers one diagonal, one off-diagonal and the next tail function at every
step.  On the compact coefficient product, countably many response values
give a continuous separating metric.  Increasing washout turns persistent
state effects into a uniformly summable likelihood perturbation; compact
separation and a finite-net strong law then give posterior consistency.
