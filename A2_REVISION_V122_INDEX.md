## Scope and documentary status

The new primary theorem covers **all projection-corank-at-most-one planes for every algebra in the stated open class**. It does not claim the omitted lower-rank locus, recovery of the input algebra or its polarization, or realization of every quartic K3 surface. All ideal-power statements concern ordinary powers of the failure ideal, not extra multiplication degrees.

The complete Ballico 1993 theorem text has not been obtained; **RII-120.1 / E120.1 remains uncompleted**. The response and manuscript do not substitute metadata or computations for that comparison. No journal acceptance, proof certification, or comprehensive priority certification is claimed.
