# Current primary-source baseline

Checked September 8, 2026. The checks below concern primary abstracts, bibliographic records and the scopes expressly described there. They are not complete re-proofs of external papers.

1. Yu Deng, Zaher Hani and Xiao Ma, *Long time derivation of the Boltzmann equation from hard sphere dynamics*, arXiv:2408.07818. The Annals record lists acceptance on 2025-10-31, revised on 2025-10-14, and forthcoming publication. The stated derivation holds for arbitrarily long times within the lifespan of a regular Boltzmann solution. Its long-time cumulant ansatz and history-cutting argument must not be omitted from the B2 baseline. https://annals.math.princeton.edu/articles/22284
2. Thierry Bodineau, Isabelle Gallagher, Laure Saint-Raymond and Sergio Simonella, *Statistical dynamics of a hard sphere gas: fluctuating Boltzmann equation and large deviations*, Annals 198 (2023), 1047--1201. Its fluctuation/LDP problem and short-time source estimates are different from merely deriving the kinetic equation. The former are not automatically supplied by a long-time law-of-large-numbers result. https://annals.math.princeton.edu/2023/198-3/p03
3. Jens Marklof, *Entry and return times for semi-flows*, arXiv:1605.02715. Used as context for stationary/Palm flow counting; the finite suspension count identity used in the new A2 note is proved directly. https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.02715
4. Mark Demers and Hong-Kun Zhang, *A functional analytic approach to perturbations of the Lorentz gas*, arXiv:1210.1261. Spectral stability under moving/deforming scatterers is relevant, but is not represented as a source of every higher-order moving-test response. https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.1261
5. Bernhard Altaner and Juergen Vollmer, *A microscopic perspective on stochastic thermodynamics*, arXiv:1212.4728. Relevant multibaker context for the dynamical manuscript; the construction is not claimed as a newly invented representation of finite Markov chains. https://arxiv.org/abs/1212.4728

No worldwide priority conclusion is drawn. The next B2 theorem must state its added source, response, exact conditioning or fluctuation conclusion relative to these actual baselines.
