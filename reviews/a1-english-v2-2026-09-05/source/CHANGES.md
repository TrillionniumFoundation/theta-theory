# Replacement and proof-scope changes

## From Chinese edition 1.0 to English edition 2.0

The previous edition supplied finite-time path identities, finite-alphabet
predictive states, local collision sensitivity, and an explicitly prepared
Lorentz beam. The new manuscript retains and rewrites these derivations in
English, repairs equation-layout details during compilation, and substantially
extends the results.

1. **Selection is now constrained by an attainable law class.** The Gibbs
   deficit is calculated exactly; coarse preparation and finite amplification
   caps have fully solved optimizers. Approximation in entropy is not mislabeled
   exact attainment.
2. **The apparatus has a joint state--readout law.** A Hamiltonian pointer shear
   produces both an observed coordinate and momentum back-action. Preparing a
   randomizer, resetting a trial, and applying a force are separate operations.
3. **Predictors qualify the entire parameter experiment.** Nominal equality
   does not imply equality of response jets. Past likelihood information is
   retained when a prior is to be updated.
4. **Continuous readouts receive a separate theorem.** The proof uses positive
   continuous densities, a countable determining family, a compact quotient,
   and pointwise causal versions. It does not divide by zero point probabilities.
5. **Resources cannot be reset inside Bellman recursion.** Finite and compact
   continuous-action results name legal pasting and feasibility conditions.
   Information-priced kernel replacement has its own objective and fixed update.
6. **Weak mechanical approximations use relevant tests.** Exponential source,
   evidence and response tests are charged separately; TV is only one sufficient
   route.
7. **A new whole-preparation mechanical result is proved.** The incoming-tube
   substitution covers every collision within a horizon shorter than the
   minimum intercollision flight. Its fixed coordinates remove inverse-grazing
   denominators from the complete assembly. Equilibrium and smooth non-equilibrium
   preparations are handled, with the normalization response retained.
8. **No Gaussian sensor is needed for the positive information example.** The
   collision bit already has a strictly monotone radius probability, positive
   Fisher information, and QMD. Added noise is an explicitly separate channel.
9. **A restricted-preparation loss and a realizable postselection are computed
   on the same complete Lorentz experiment.** Direction-only preparation cannot
   change its equilibrium short-time hit fraction. Completed-record acceptance
   produces the desired tilted record with a known trial failure probability.

## Scope not promoted

The new whole-preparation theorem is not a claim about arbitrary long times.
It has a concrete `T < 3/50` condition in the stated dimensionless cell. A
single tagged record determines the physical trajectory after the radius is
specified; radius differentiation of sampled physical observables includes the
parameter-dependent decoder. No generic trajectory-space smoothness is inferred.

The complete-preparation Cauchy theorem is a transfer statement whose hypotheses
must be verified for each additional model. Its presence does not certify all
multiple-collision or hard-sphere genealogies. The general framework permits
many preparations and instruments; concrete regularity theorems keep their
separate assumptions.
