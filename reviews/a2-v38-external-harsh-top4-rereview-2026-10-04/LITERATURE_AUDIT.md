# Focused literature audit for A2 v38

This is a focused comparison of mechanisms and information categories, not an exhaustive priority search. The purpose is to distinguish the manuscript's collision-specific contributions from the classical tools used after the reciprocal reduction.

## 1. Optimal stopping and occupation measures

The manuscript's global occupation inverse and fixed-stencil polytope belong to classical stopped Poisson and total-cost Markov decision theory.

- Lawler and Limic, *Random Walk: A Modern Introduction* (2010), provide standard random-walk stopping and Green-operator background.
- Altman, *Constrained Markov Decision Processes* (1999), provides the occupation-measure and linear-programming framework for controlled chains.

The v38 manuscript does not merely cite a black box: it proves the finite flow realization, Green bounds, duality specialization, and physical attaining rule needed for its sensor. The collision-specific point is that the unknown containing-component exit attains the stopping value while nonzero occupation may remain on the computational boundary.

## 2. Mathematical morphology and unknown probes

Villarrubia's scanned-probe reconstruction work is a close information-category comparison. Unknown probe imaging is represented through morphological dilation, and blind reconstruction seeks a consistent probe from multiple image features.

The present manuscript has a different sensor and adds two ingredients not supplied by that literature:

1. reciprocal collision means recover the occupation positivity components;
2. integrated collision deficits determine footprint scale before centered support-envelope cancellation.

Minkowski addition, support functions, Steiner centering, perimeter addition, and Cauchy's perimeter formula are classical convex geometry, appropriately attributed to Schneider.

## 3. Active boundary estimation and informative rare events

Castro--Nowak and Locatelli--Carpentier--Kpotufe are appropriate antecedents for active line search, interpolation, and smooth-boundary packing. Yan--Chaudhuri--Javidi provide a relevant statistical analogy in which an informative rare or abstention event can have inverse-probability rather than inverse-squared-signal cost.

The manuscript's new step is sensor-specific: translated pooled collision commands create an exterior event of exactly zero probability and an interior event with cap-mass probability, despite the unobserved compass direction. The subsequent batch calculation is elementary once this geometric event is established.

Brunel's random-polytope cap estimates and Brunel--Klusowski--Yang's noisy convex-support work are relevant to the cap-mass exponent and to support recovery without first estimating a full nuisance density. Their observations and losses differ from the current collision experiment.

## 4. Information-theoretic lower bounds

The stopped chain rule, Fano decoding, and Hellinger information-radius comparison are classical information tools. The manuscript's contribution is the geometric response contraction for all short commands, including lengths tending to zero, on a physical billiard packing. That contraction is what places the active collision experiment inside the information bound.

The matching statement should be positioned precisely: it identifies the polynomial minimax power for one fixed known uniform-disk experiment and the stated centered `C^2` loss. It does not settle logarithmic factors or every stationary density class.

## 5. Query precision

Goldberg--Kwek are a useful antecedent for treating query-coordinate precision as a separate resource. The manuscript similarly separates attempted bits, center-labelled batches, distinct sites, coordinate descriptions, numerical arithmetic, apparatus travel, and physical metrology.

## 6. Novelty assessment

The focused search and the manuscript's own references do not reveal a predecessor containing the complete combination of:

- scalar reciprocal collision balance;
- global occupation recovery without a supplied killing domain;
- equality of full response and obstacle translation-period groups;
- blind homothetic footprint registration through collision deficits and centered support envelopes;
- a physical short-command Hellinger contraction with matching stationary polynomial power;
- a fixed finite-stencil certified occupation inverse;
- an isotropic perimeter normalization.

That combination appears genuinely manuscript-specific. This observation is not a priority certificate. Each abstract component has clear classical antecedents, and the editorial question is whether their collision-specific synthesis reaches the conceptual level of the requested four journals.

## 7. Scope boundary

The work concerns actively selected collision-response fields and finite controlled experiments. It does not prove rigidity from passive trajectories, a marked length spectrum, a scattering relation, uniformly prepared count germs, or a spectral invariant. Literature from those categories should not be presented as directly equivalent data, and the current theorem should not be marketed as resolving those inverse problems.
