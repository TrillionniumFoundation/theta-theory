## Primary sources used

Allman, Matias and Rhodes, *Identifiability of parameters in latent structure models with many observed variables*, Annals of Statistics 37 (2009), 3099–3132; the inherited spectral literature discussion and bibliography are preserved.

Bauer and Fike, *Norms and exclusion theorems*, Numerische Mathematik 2 (1960), 137–141, DOI `10.1007/BF01386217`. The publisher record fixes the author initials C. T. Fike and publication details. The elementary spectral lemma used in v87 is proved in full.

Bonhomme, Jochmans and Robin, *Estimating multivariate latent-structure models*, Annals of Statistics 44 (2016), 540–563; DOI `10.1214/15-AOS1376`, arXiv `1603.09141`. The located abstract and the inherited repository discussion establish the diagonalization/latent-factor comparison; no uninspected theorem is said to be ruled out by a new priority search.

Li, Lee and Bresler, *A unified framework for identifiability analysis in bilinear inverse problems with applications to subspace and sparsity models*, arXiv `1501.06120`. The comparison concerns identifiability up to transformations, not a claim that this source proves a finite-record singular action modulus.

Donoho, *Statistical estimation and optimal recovery*, Annals of Statistics 22 (1994), 238–270; DOI `10.1214/aos/1176325367`. Cai and Low, *An adaptation theory for nonparametric confidence intervals*, Annals of Statistics 32 (2004), 1805–1840; DOI `10.1214/009053604000000049`. These are credited as modulus/inference predecessors rather than positioned as competitors over the same nonlinear model.

Dufour, *Some impossibility theorems in econometrics with applications to structural and dynamic models*, Econometrica 65 (1997), 1365–1389. The bounded/unbounded target distinction is retained from v86.

Ho and Nguyen, *Singularity structures and impacts on parameter estimation in finite mixtures of distributions*, SIAM Journal on Mathematics of Data Science 1 (2019), 730–758; DOI `10.1137/18M122947X`; arXiv `1609.02655v4`. The introduction, singularity framework and distinction between density and parameter convergence in that primary text were consulted. The v87 bibliography uses the publisher's journal/volume/pages, not the incorrect Electronic Journal of Statistics entry in an initial working draft.

## Preserved regimes and priority scope

The compact-surface, rational, polynomial and boundary-rigidity sections retain their original sources and sufficient-versus-sharp distinctions. They are not the new novelty claim of the principal article. A universal optimal meromorphic sampling invariant and a new geometric rigidity mechanism are not inferred from this audit. Their full theorem statements and hypotheses remain available for scrutiny in the companion.
