## 1. Recommendation

**Recommendation at the requested four-journal benchmark: reject.**

Version 23 is a genuine and mathematically substantive response to the v22 report. It does not merely place the fixed-list completion certificate inside a sequential wrapper. The revision adds three distinct pieces:

1. a laboratory-square preparation whose accepted free launches approximate the unknown quotient Liouville law without supplying a fundamental cell;
2. an explicit growing-catalogue model, with conditional discovery hazards and a randomized realization using the retained geometric scanner; and
3. an anytime-valid stopping theorem that remeasures the adaptively accumulated genuine records with fresh data, propagates the finite-square preparation bias through the endpoint histograms, and combines the resulting accuracy event with the reference-free completion defect.

These additions close the most concrete operational gaps identified in the preceding report **within the newly stated access model**. On the new v23 arguments audited in detail, I found no fatal counterexample to the boundary-layer preparation estimate, the biased histogram bound, the logarithmic witness extraction, the adaptive-arrival tail, the geometric-producer probability, the anytime soundness argument, or the displayed preparation and launch-cost bounds. Independent exact diagnostics accompanying this report support the finite inequalities and algebra. I therefore do not base the negative recommendation on a known false central theorem.

The negative recommendation is instead a top-four editorial judgment. The result still begins with an unusually rich selected local-scattering sensor: each record uses two complete two-dimensional endpoint-arclength densities at known absolute times, a coherent local origin and branch mark, and a periodic gate recognizing every translated occurrence of that branch. The concrete producer that guarantees discovery is given complete-body, shortest-pair, blocker-clearance, local-chart and periodic-gate installation access. Thus the new theorem proves oracle-relative sequential certification and stopping, not discovery from an ordinary unmarked billiard trajectory or a standard spectral invariant.

The probabilistic layer is carefully executed but conceptually elementary: summable conditional error spending is combined with a coupon/hazard bound and the deterministic completion certificate. The laboratory preparation is a useful periodic boundary-layer argument, but it does not remove the strong producer access, analytic continuation, asymmetry, shape separation, positive area/covolume gaps, and compact-class margins that drive the global conclusion. In my judgment the package is serious specialist-journal mathematics, but it does not deliver the exceptional naturality, breadth, or conceptual transformation expected by the four journals named above.

I would encourage a focused specialist submission after a fresh human proof review, repair of the failed exact-source full-package qualification, and further compression around the completion/discovery theorem. I would not recommend another open-ended revision cycle at the requested top-four benchmark.

## 3. Information category and theorem package

A record selects an isolated clear normal bridge and a three-impact return branch. At two known absolute times, the observer records the full subprobability densities of the first and last intrinsic boundary arclengths on a strictly active rectangle. Failure and successful endpoints outside the rectangle remain outcomes. One simultaneous reversal of both endpoint coordinates is allowed within a record, but origins and signs are not registered across records.

The ideal preparation uses the normalized quotient phase measure

\[
\frac{dq\,d\vartheta}{2\pi A},
\]

where `A` is the free area of the complete periodic cell, including obstacles that may not yet have appeared in any record. This absolute normalization is indispensable: conditioning on success or normalizing to a local aperture would erase the missing-area signal used by the completion defect.

For one record the retained local inverse gives

\[
W=\frac{T_1f_2-T_2f_1}{f_2-f_1},
\qquad
A=\frac{(T_2-T_1)(-W_{st})}{2\pi(f_2-f_1)}.
\]

The action and its diagonal derivatives reconstruct both reflecting arcs in their relative placement. Analytic continuation then reconstructs the complete obstacle images on the global class.

For any connected observed component, congruence of complete recovered images identifies the visible body types. A spanning tree places representatives; non-tree records produce physical cycle vectors. Their integer span is a subgroup `Gamma` of the true period lattice `Lambda`. If `I` is the set of visible types, the exact completion defect is

\[
\mathcal D=\operatorname{covol}\Gamma-A-
              \sum_{i\in I}\operatorname{area}(C_i)
 =([\Lambda:\Gamma]-1)\operatorname{covol}\Lambda
   +\sum_{i\notin I}\operatorname{area}(C_i).
\]

It is zero exactly when the component contains every obstacle type and generates the full period lattice. The reference-free noisy theorem first recovers shape incidence and the exact rational subgroup, then applies a positive defect gap.

Version 23 adds a growing list. A predictable producer proposes either a replayable genuine record or no record before each validation epoch. The learner keeps all records, remeasures them with fresh preparations, and stops only when a component passes the physical completion test. Under divergent cumulative hazards for some unknown finite saturated witness, stopping is almost sure. Under a uniform hazard `p`, the paper gives

\[
\Pr\{T_\xi>k\}\le \frac{\delta}{8(k+1)^6}+we^{-pk}
\]

beyond the deterministic precision threshold.

## 4. Correctness audit of the unknown-cell preparation

### 4.1 Periodic square sampling

The apparatus samples a position uniformly in the laboratory square

\[
\Omega_L=[-L,L]^2,
\]

rejects positions inside a solid obstacle, and chooses a uniform direction after a free launch has been accepted. It keeps every gate outcome, including failure. This is not conditioning on a successful return.

Let a fundamental parallelogram have diameter at most `b`. The union `U` of complete fundamental tiles contained in the square covers the inner square `[-L+b,L-b]^2`, so

\[
|\Omega_L\setminus U|\le 8Lb,
\qquad |U|\ge 4(L-b)^2.
\]

Conditional on a free launch inside `U`, projection modulo the lattice is exactly the quotient free-position law. The remaining free part is an arbitrary contamination whose weight is at most

\[
\frac{8Lb}{(A_0/V_1)4(L-b)^2}
 \,\le\,\frac{8V_1b}{A_0L}
\]

when `L>=2b`. A measurable periodic gate cannot increase total variation. This proves the displayed `O(L^{-1})` preparation error.

The free-launch probability is bounded below by

\[
\frac{A_0}{V_1}(1-b/L)^2\ge \frac{A_0}{4V_1}.
\]

The negative-binomial expectation and the stated Chernoff launch budget are consistent with this lower bound. I found no defect in these estimates.

### 4.2 The periodic-gate hypothesis is essential

The square argument works only after postprocessing by a **periodic** gate: translating a launch and trajectory by a true lattice vector must leave its output unchanged. A detector attached to one physical copy is not covered. This is not a minor implementation detail. It is the mechanism that lets the apparatus average complete cells without knowing their coordinates.

The manuscript states this condition, but every headline description of “unknown-cell sampling” or “sequential discovery” must keep it adjacent. The preparation theorem does not construct a periodic gate from raw trajectory data.

### 4.3 Propagation through endpoint histograms

For one cell, the finite-square bias contributes at most `tau_L` to the cell probability. Bernstein's inequality gives sampling error

\[
C\{h\sqrt{r_N}+\sqrt{\tau_Lr_N}+r_N\}.
\]

After adding the deterministic cell bias and using

\[
\sqrt{\tau_Lr_N}\le (\tau_L+r_N)/2,
\]

the cell error is bounded by

\[
C\{h\sqrt{r_N}+r_N+\tau_L\}.
\]

Converting cell masses to average densities costs `h^{-2}`, and taking two derivatives costs another `h^{-2}`. Together with the `C^{2,beta}` Taylor bias this yields

\[
C\{h^\beta+\sqrt{r_N}h^{-3}+(r_N+\tau_L)h^{-4}\}.
\]

The choice

\[
h=r_N^{1/(2\beta+6)},
\qquad
L\gtrsim r_N^{-(\beta+4)/(2\beta+6)}
\]

makes `tau_L h^{-4}` of the same order as `h^beta`, and the linear Bernstein term is smaller. The rate algebra is correct. Importantly, the proof does not illegitimately infer a `C^2` density bound directly from total variation; it first passes through finite cells.

## 5. Correctness audit of record discovery

### 5.1 A finite saturated witness

A saturated covering record graph contains a spanning tree using at most `r_0-1` records. Keep two independent non-tree records. Their cycle subgroup has index `n<=Q`. Whenever the current subgroup is proper, adjoining a fundamental cycle outside it replaces the index by a proper divisor. Every proper divisor of an integer `n>1` is at most `n/2`. Thus at most `floor(log_2 Q)` additional records are needed.

The resulting bound

\[
w_0=r_0+1+\lfloor\log_2Q\rfloor
\]

is correct and does not assume that any recorded cycle pair is primitive. The familiar `2,3,5` minor example is therefore covered.

### 5.2 Adaptive hazards

If witness record `ell` is still absent before epoch `j`, and its next-proposal probability is at least `p_{j ell}`, iterated conditioning gives

\[
\Pr\{\ell\text{ absent through }k\}
 \le \prod_{j\le k}(1-p_{j\ell})
 \le \exp\left(-\sum_{j\le k}p_{j\ell}\right).
\]

A union bound over the finite witness proves the displayed arrival tail. No independence between proposal epochs or witness records is needed. Divergent cumulative hazards imply almost-sure eventual appearance; a uniform positive lower bound gives the exponential tail. This argument is standard but correct.

### 5.3 The concrete producer

The concrete realization uses a strong geometric oracle. On a bounded disk it:

- lists the physical obstacle copies meeting the disk;
- represents their complete boundaries;
- computes shortest pairs;
- tests every possible blocker;
- verifies local physical margins; and
- installs a periodic local gate for every translated occurrence of the retained pair.

The finite-aperture connectivity argument is coherent. Since a fundamental parallelogram has diameter at most `b`, the obstacle union has covering radius at most `b`. For `R>2b`, overlapping neighborhoods produce a connected short-pair graph. If a shortest segment is blocked, the blocker yields strictly shorter gaps; induction over the finite list of sub-`R` gap values modulo periods replaces it by a clear path. Path lifting then shows that quotient cycles generate the period lattice.

A midpoint representative of every required translation class lies in the aperture. Endpoint and blocker bodies meet the padded disk. Under a positive body-separation prior, packing gives a finite list. Uniform choice among ordered pairs assigns every witness pair probability at least

\[
p=\{M_0(M_0-1)\}^{-1}.
\]

I found this proof internally coherent under its explicit oracle assumptions.

Those assumptions are also the main editorial limitation. The producer has access to essentially the complete geometry needed to identify candidate channels and install a lattice-periodic detector. Its boundary maps are hidden from the statistical learner, but they are still available to the acquisition system. This is not record discovery from the endpoint-law sensor alone, still less from unmarked trajectories.

## 6. Correctness audit of anytime certification

At epoch `k` the procedure proposes one record, retains all previous records, and uses fresh preparations to validate the current finite list. It chooses

\[
\delta_k=\frac{\delta}{8(k+1)^6},
\qquad N_k=(k+1)^2,
\]

with the corresponding bandwidth and laboratory square. Conditional on the proposal history, the list is fixed and has at most `k` entries, so the finite-list density bound applies with conditional failure probability at most `delta_k`. Iterated conditioning gives the same unconditional bound.

On the simultaneous accuracy event, the deterministic reference-free theorem applies to every connected component and every deletion of the list. Therefore data-dependent component selection and stopping require no extra graph union bound. Incomplete components have defect at least `g_0`, while complete saturated components have zero defect. The acceptance threshold separates them once the continuation and arithmetic error is below `g_0/4`.

For the stopping tail, if the witness has appeared by epoch `k`, the deterministic precision threshold has been reached, and the current epoch is accurate, acceptance has occurred by `k`. Hence

\[
\{T_\xi>k\}\subset
 \{K_{\rm cov}>k\}\cup E_k^c,
\]

which explains why the current-epoch error `delta_k`, rather than the sum of all previous errors, appears in the tail. Almost-sure stopping follows from eventual witness appearance and the first Borel--Cantelli lemma applied to the summable validation failures.

The preparation count through epoch `m` is

\[
\sum_{k\le m} O(kN_k)=O(m^4).
\]

The additional logarithmic launch budget sums to the stated

\[
O\{m^4+m\log(m/\delta)\}.
\]

Under a uniform positive hazard, multiplying the epoch cost `O(k^3)` by the `k^{-6}+e^{-pk}` stopping tail gives a finite expectation. I found the probability and resource algebra correct.

Two presentation points should be repaired:

1. the indexing should state explicitly that epochs begin at `k=1` when the manuscript invokes `sum_k delta_k<delta/8`;
2. every informal finite-expected-cost statement should visibly retain the uniform positive-hazard hypothesis. The pointwise margin-exhaustion theorem deliberately has neither a uniform rate nor a finite expected-cost conclusion.

## 7. What v23 substantively fixes

Relative to the v22 report, the revision makes four real improvements.

First, it no longer treats exact unknown-cell Liouville preparation as a free primitive. The laboratory-square theorem provides an operational approximation, counts rejected solid launches, and propagates the boundary error through the two-derivative histogram reconstruction.

Second, it no longer begins the principal statistical theorem with one fixed record list. A genuine-record producer may adapt to the past, and the list may grow for an unbounded number of epochs.

Third, it supplies time-uniform validity at the actual stopping epoch by fresh conditional validation and summable error spending. The v22 fixed-list confidence statement alone did not justify repeatedly testing and stopping.

Fourth, it gives a concrete oracle-relative producer with an explicit witness-size and proposal-probability bound. The theorem no longer simply assumes that a useful record collection has appeared.

These are substantive answers, not verbal qualifications. They should be recognized as such.

## 8. Qualifications required for any resubmission

### 8.1 Use “oracle-relative” in the headline account

The word “discovery” can suggest that the record branches are found from the same endpoint-law or trajectory observations used in the inverse. The proved concrete theorem instead grants complete-body listing, exact shortest-pair and blocker tests, local validity certification, and periodic-gate installation. The title, abstract, and first theorem should call the construction **geometric-oracle-relative sequential discovery** or use equally explicit language.

### 8.2 Separate gate installation from statistical observation

The gate is not a finite label alone. It is a periodic evaluation rule recognizing all lattice translates of one local branch, although the lattice itself is not given to the learner. The paper should specify more operationally whether this is an infinite periodic apparatus, an oracle callback, or a rule computed from the complete-body catalogue. The mathematical assumption is acceptable, but its physical and computational content should not be left implicit.

### 8.3 Keep all resource measures visible

The displayed preparation count excludes:

- complete-boundary representation;
- exact congruence and translation-class recognition;
- shortest-pair and blocker computations;
- periodic-gate installation;
- apparatus range `L_k` and travel/setup cost;
- compact analytic fitting and continuation complexity.

The manuscript acknowledges this. The same separation should remain in every theorem summary so that `O(m^4)` is not read as end-to-end acquisition complexity.

### 8.4 Strong global priors remain decisive

The reference-free theorem still requires bounded obstacle count and lattice presentation, positive body-area and covolume gaps, analytic strip bounds, pairwise noncongruence, quantitative asymmetry, separation and curvature bounds, and uniform active/twist/non-grazing/time/onset margins. The pointwise exhaustion removes a supplied common local witness margin, but not the global analytic and genericity assumptions.

### 8.5 The hosted exact-source qualification currently fails

The local source-content run reports 7,230 new finite diagnostics, 3,635 selected retained checks, identical normal/optimized output, 90 nonlinear curvature comparisons, and a warning-free 23-page primary. It explicitly does not authenticate a Git checkout or rebuild every retained volume.

## 9. Top-four significance assessment

The positive case is substantial:

- unknown-cell absolute normalization is now approximated operationally;
- finite-square bias is carried through the actual histogram inverse;
- the fixed-list certificate has become an anytime stopping rule;
- adaptive record arrivals are handled without independence;
- a finite saturated witness has an explicit logarithmic size bound;
- a concrete geometric producer gives explicit arrival hazards;
- omissions remain false-positive safe at every epoch;
- the nearest inverse-geometry and time-uniform probability comparisons are stated honestly;
- the previous local inverse, completion identity, and rational subgroup recovery remain active and source-pinned.

The negative case remains decisive at the requested benchmark:

1. **The observation is highly informative and purpose-built.** Each record consists of two full endpoint-density functions with absolute global normalization, local branch marks, and a periodic gate.
2. **The discovery theorem transfers the hard branch-identification problem to a geometric oracle.** The oracle sees complete unknown bodies and performs exact geometric tests. It is not a theorem from the standard billiard flow or unmarked trajectories.
3. **The new probabilistic mechanism is not conceptually new.** It is a careful application of error spending, fresh conditional samples, coupon/hazard tails, and Borel--Cantelli.
4. **The global inverse remains strongly generic and analytic.** Analytic continuation, asymmetry, and pairwise shape separation are the synchronization mechanism.
5. **There is no end-to-end efficient algorithm or minimax theory.** The integer stage is finite, but analytic fitting, gate production, oracle operations, and whole-table lower bounds remain outside the result.
6. **Natural lower-information problems remain open.** Exact analytic count-only rigidity/nonrigidity, conventional marked-length rigidity without the present marks and priors, and unmarked-trajectory discovery are not proved.
7. **The submission is still a multi-volume programme.** The focused 23-page primary relies on a large retained hierarchy for the full advertised body of results.

Version 23 successfully answers the concrete v22 objections in its chosen access model. What remains is not a small missing lemma; it is the editorial question whether that access model reveals a sufficiently universal phenomenon. In my judgment it does not meet the exceptional threshold of *Annals*, *Acta*, *Inventiones*, or *JAMS*.
