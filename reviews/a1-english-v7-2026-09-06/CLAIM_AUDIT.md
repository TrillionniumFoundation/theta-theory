## Operational definitions and unnumbered assertions

**Definition 2.2 (`def:future`, Section 2, lines 61–75).** The future-only continuation has the same task data, cannot read old prefixes or prefix-dependent seeds, and uses only post-checkpoint reports/commands. This closes the otherwise invalid equivalence quantifier. A separate payoff automaton remains task memory.

**Definition 6.1 (`def:finite-state`, Section 6, lines 8–25).** The only persistent mutable resource is the index. A known clock, exact read-only real constants, transition functions, temporary arithmetic and computation time are not charged. This is an explicit finite-state transducer existence model, not a Turing-space or effective synthesis theorem. The implementation uses the prior index and current command/report, not the exact historical data.

**Mechanical realization (Section 9, lines 7–77).** The collision flux, four cells and coefficient determinant are consistent with the counted ambient preparation and the one-collision time window. The exact-coordinate apparatus is an explicit idealization. No many-collision or physical measurement-complexity conclusion follows.

**Rare-probe and finite-command caveats (Section 6, lines 261–287).** The one-state zero forecast and calibration/horizon-dependent constants remain visible. Finite command alphabets do not support the same all-budget asymptotic lower bound, because they have finitely many finite-horizon histories.

**Scope and historical preservation (Section 10).** Past-limited uniform confluence, arbitrary factor spaces, optimal exploration, horizon-uniform estimates and fresh approval of legacy derivations are not claimed. The seven-trial calculation in this review illustrates a declared boundary, rather than supplying a counterexample to the submission.
