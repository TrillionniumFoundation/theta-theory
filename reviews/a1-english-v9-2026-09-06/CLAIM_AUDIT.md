## 4. Essential quantifier and scope ledger

The uniform constants are for a fixed positive coefficient realization, command cube, prior, horizon, and compact calibration set inside the open one-step chamber. For each known calibration and each M there exists a stage-compatible transducer. The same comparison constants work over that calibration set; the codebooks need not be jointly measurable, computable, or calibration-blind.

The lower experiment supplies continuous commands independently of the parameter and filtering randomness. A query is selected independently after the index is formed. Only one checkpoint query is executed in a run, and its remaining trials are counted. Fresh coding randomization is not a free retained command-generation seed.

The full-support prior need not have a density. Strict rank for every such prior does not imply a uniform conditioning constant over all such priors. Fixed-horizon state counting does not charge read-only exact-real program data or arithmetic workspace. Finite-input diagnostic codebooks cannot demonstrate the continuum lower asymptotic, because fixed finite alphabets and a fixed horizon have only finitely many histories.

The history erasure T_theta, raw mechanical sign erasure, and arbitrary optimal four-dimensional compression are three different objects. The submitted comparisons keep them separate. Future reviews should do the same.
