\begin{abstract}
We construct a controlled mechanical experiment in which the observable law,
its attainable modifications, a finite-dimensional inference state, and a
budgeted control problem close on the same apparatus. A parameter-independent
placement cartridge attempts to prepare a unit-speed particle in a triangular
Lorentz table. Unsuccessful placement consumes a cartridge and remains an
outcome. A collision-coordinate detector has an explicit deterministic
uniform-seed implementation. Integrating the whole collision tube gives a
strictly positive cubic-polynomial observation density in the unknown radius.
An arbitrary measurable acceptance gate in the entire interval $[0,1]$
may censor the readout, but its
failure symbol is retained. We characterize the entire attainable family of
such instruments and prove legal adaptive composition from the product
preparation of the cartridges. After $n$ consumed cartridges the likelihood
has an exact positive Bernstein representation with $3n+1$ coefficients.
It retains the complete radius likelihood, not only its nominal value or a
finite jet. A matching local dimension theorem proves that its $3n$ free
coordinates are necessary for a continuous exact state of this command family. For a uniform prior it is an explicit mixture of $3n+1$ beta
laws. Infinite-dimensional gate optimization reduces exactly to a
five-dimensional moment body with a proved support function; compactness and
measurable realization give an attained finite-budget Bellman recursion.
Every fixed adaptive controller has strong finite-order likelihood response
for this same detector, while the ideal geometric record remains statistically
singular. We also prove a general positive-polynomial closure theorem,
error bounds for the positive filter, and the biased-entropy, two-trace, and
transported-preparation repairs required by the previous review. The earlier
whole-equilibrium response theorem is retained in a corrected companion;
the new principal result is the operational and state-reduction theorem,
not an inference of long-orbit response from a short collision calculation.
\end{abstract}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} deterministic experiments; Lorentz gas; realizability;
positive polynomial likelihood; adaptive censoring; Bayesian control;
Bernstein representation; mechanical response.
