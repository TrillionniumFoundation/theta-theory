\subsection{What has changed in the central theorem}
The previous synthesis conjoined a collision-bit calculation with conditional
applications of general prediction and control results. The revised
synthesis calculates one apparatus kernel, proves its full gate feasible
family and its continuation property, derives an explicit positive finite
state, proves a matching continuous dimension lower bound, and optimizes
the same gate class on that state. The lower bound uses an open family of
attainable failure cubics and coprime polynomial factors, not a count of
formal state coordinates alone. The finite-command approximation makes the
ideal measurable-comparator optimum approachable by finite lookup gates.

The retained one-flight geometric hypothesis is used exactly where needed:
it verifies the cubic numerator and its coefficient margins. It is not
advertised as a theorem about a long uninterrupted orbit. All cartridge
attempts, both kinds of failure, the prior's parameter likelihood, and the
fixed-controller qualification in response remain explicit. The principal
claim is positive operational closure with sharp finite-budget state
size, rather than a declaration that the broader program cannot proceed.
