## Targeted primary literature checks

Access date: 6 September 2026. These checks are not an exhaustive novelty or priority search.

**L1. Saldi, Yüksel and Linder, arXiv:1503.02244v3.** [Version-specific PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1503.02244v3). Parsed text and screenshots of PDF indices 31 and 34, printed pages 32 and 35, were examined. Page 32 contains the compact/Lipschitz setup and discounted Theorem 5.2; page 35 contains average-cost Theorem 5.3. This independently confirms the revision's corrected number/version pair. It does not imply that the A1 theorem is a consequence of their control theorem.

**L2. Zhang and Kileel, arXiv:2311.05116v4.** [Version-specific PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.05116v4). The statement of Lemma 2.18 was checked in parsed text and a screenshot of PDF index 10, printed page 11. It provides a format-dependent semialgebraic regularity bound. The A1 proof additionally uses real quantifier elimination, projected-rectangle volume estimates and a classical entropy inequality; the regularity lemma alone is not described as the entire covering proof.

The Comte–Halupczok publisher page and broader primary-source search results were located, but the targeted publisher-page fetch did not expose the required classical entropy passage. This review therefore does not claim a new page-level verification of that passage or of the entire Yomdin–Comte book. The entropy inequality remains an explicitly identified classical input in the manuscript proof. Likewise, the Kara–Yüksel and Subramanian comparisons were read in the submitted text but were not independently rechecked theorem-by-theorem in this round.
