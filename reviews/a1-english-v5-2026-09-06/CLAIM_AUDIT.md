## 3. Findings not to misclassify

**Not counterexamples to the manuscript:** the zero-memory bound `(c+delta)^(2m)` does not refute fixed-checkpoint high-resolution asymptotics; the sparse-space dimension-five example does not satisfy the full-Pq assumption and therefore does not refute `q min(n,m)`; the small positive bit gain is not a failure of strict positivity; checkpoint memory and free-memory bit value are two different tasks, as the source largely acknowledges.

**Not proved by 58 finite checks:** all-budget rank claims, topological non-embedding, randomized minimax quantifiers, subprobability minorization, global geometric coverage, Borel selection, and any journal-level originality assessment.

**Required to interpret the central state correctly:** the common continuation rule receives the same future input and task specification, without access to distinct original prefixes outside the state. See P1 in the report for a two-constant-gate example showing why the contrary full-history reading fails.

The recommendation remains rejection for the requested journal level. The validity assessment above must not be converted into either a publication endorsement or an invented correctness rejection.
