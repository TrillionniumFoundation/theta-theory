## 1. Recommendation

**Recommendation at the requested four-journal benchmark: reject.**

Version 24 is a genuine and substantial mathematical revision. It addresses the main concrete access objection in the v23 report rather than renaming the old geometric producer. The complete-body catalogue, global shortest-pair enumeration, blocker search over listed bodies, and preinstalled periodic-gate callback are replaced by a different and explicitly stated resource: a certified local contact probe acting only near contacts and flights of an actually encountered bounded-time trajectory. A compactness/analyticity argument supplies a finite contact fingerprint separating all bounded bridge translation classes that coexist in one table; a robust registry then recovers class identity and coherent orientation. The paper also propagates finite contact-coordinate error into the endpoint histograms and derives the positive discovery hazard from physical return volume rather than assuming the success probability of a complete-geometry oracle.

On the new v24 core audited in detail, I found no fatal counterexample. The finite-order separation argument, registry thresholds, normal-contact Hessian, local shortest-segment certification, distance-to-solid clearance test, square-preparation estimate, protected-return hazard, finite saturated witness, registration-boundary-band estimate, inherited local inverse, completion defect, rational period recovery, and anytime stopping calculation are coherent under the stated uniform hypotheses. Independent finite diagnostics accompanying this report support the displayed algebra and inequalities. The negative recommendation is therefore not based on a known false central theorem.

The remaining objection is editorial and conceptual. The theorem is now local-probe-relative rather than complete-geometry-oracle-relative, which is a real improvement, but the probe is itself a strong active geometric sensor: it returns certified local boundary charts and derivatives to arbitrary requested accuracy, intrinsic arclength, and distance-to-the-entire-solid enclosures near a candidate flight. The inverse still uses two full two-dimensional endpoint-density functions per record, with local branch/origin marks and the absolute Liouville normalization of the whole cell. Global reconstruction still relies on uniform analyticity, quantitative asymmetry, pairwise shape separation, positive body-area and covolume gaps, bounded lattice presentations, and a long list of local physical margins.

The finite-fingerprint theorem is an elegant compactness consequence of analytic continuation and symmetry exclusion, but it is non-effective at the level stated: a usable pair `(K_*, chi)` must be available from the prior class, while the proof supplies existence rather than a general procedure or complexity bound. The sequential probability layer is careful but standard once the genuine periodic records and their positive hazard have been constructed. In my judgment this yields serious specialist-journal mathematics, not the exceptional naturality, breadth, or conceptual transformation required by the four journals named above.

A focused specialist submission centered on the local-gate reduction, after a fresh human proof review and the minor corrections below, could be strong. I would not recommend another open-ended revision cycle at the requested top-four benchmark.

## 3. Information model and theorem package

The observation has three distinct layers.

1. A certified local contact probe evaluates boundary charts and derivatives near encountered impacts, measures intrinsic arclength, and returns distance-to-solid enclosures in bounded neighborhoods of candidate flights.
2. A finite descriptor made from the bridge gap and contact coefficients through a class-dependent order `K_*` recognizes a bridge translation class and its coherent transverse sign.
3. At two known absolute times, unconditional endpoint histograms approximate two complete endpoint-arclength subprobability densities. These density functions, not the finite descriptor, reconstruct the action and geometry.

For one clear normal bridge, the stationary two-flight action satisfies

\[
f_T(s,t)=\frac{-W_{st}(s,t)}{2\pi A}(T-W(s,t))_+.
\]

On a strictly active rectangle, two times recover

\[
W=\frac{T_1f_2-T_2f_1}{f_2-f_1},
\qquad
A=\frac{(T_2-T_1)(-W_{st})}{2\pi(f_2-f_1)}.
\]

Diagonal derivatives of `W` recover the curvatures and the two reflecting arcs. Analytic continuation reconstructs complete obstacle images. Recovered images are clustered into visible types, a spanning tree places representatives, and non-tree records give cycle vectors. The completion defect

\[
\mathcal D=\operatorname{covol}\Gamma-A-\sum_{i\in I}\operatorname{area}(C_i)
 =([\Lambda:\Gamma]-1)\operatorname{covol}\Lambda
  +\sum_{i\notin I}\operatorname{area}(C_i)
\]

vanishes exactly for full type coverage and full period generation.

Version 24 adds a local mechanism for producing and recognizing the genuine records to which this retained inverse applies. It proves a uniform per-record discovery hazard for a finite saturated witness and combines fresh all-record validation with summable error spending to obtain an anytime-valid whole-table certificate.

## 4. Correctness audit of the finite local fingerprint

### 4.1 Equality at all orders

For an oriented bridge, the descriptor records the gap and the normalized contact derivatives of both facing graphs. If two descriptors agree at every order, analyticity gives equality of both contact graphs on open intervals. Connected analytic continuation then identifies the complete ordered pair images.

Pairwise shape separation identifies the source type. Trivial Euclidean symmetry makes the matching of two copies of that type unique, so the pair isometry is the physical translation between the copies. It carries the target body and both contacts. Equality after transverse reversal would instead give a reflection symmetry of the source body, excluded by the stated asymmetry margin.

This argument correctly distinguishes the theorem from a false finite-jet determination claim for arbitrary analytic bodies: the comparison is only among bounded bridge classes coexisting in one table.

### 4.2 Uniform finite order

The compactness contradiction is persuasive. Bounded gaps, body diameters, lattice presentations and inverse presentations leave only finitely many discrete type/copy labels for possible bridge classes. Holomorphic strip/disk bounds give convergence of every fixed contact derivative along a convergent subsequence. If no common finite order and separation existed, a limit pair of inequivalent bridges would agree at every order, contradicting the all-order argument. Positive physical separation prevents distinct copies from merging in the limit.

For a final journal version, this central step should be packaged as a precise compactness lemma: specify the topology on the union over `r<=r_0`, the representative gauge, behavior at lattice-reduction walls, continuity of the unique closest contacts, and invariance of the translation-class equivalence. I did not find a counterexample, but the current proof compresses several quotient/compactness choices into a paragraph.

### 4.3 Effectivity

The theorem proves that some `K_*` and `chi` exist uniformly on the declared compact class. It does not compute them. A physical registry needs certified usable values, so they are part of the calibrated prior in any implementation. The manuscript now says this, and it should remain in every headline account. Calling the result a finite local rule is mathematically legitimate as an existence statement, but it is not an end-to-end algorithm for a class specified only by qualitative analytic bounds.

## 5. Correctness audit of the local gate construction

### 5.1 Normal-contact test

At a true normal bridge the distance Hessian is

\[
\begin{pmatrix}
g^{-1}+\kappa_0&-g^{-1}\\
-g^{-1}&g^{-1}+\kappa_1
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Its quadratic form is bounded below by `min(kappa_0,kappa_1)` times the Euclidean norm squared. Uniform third-derivative bounds therefore give a common neighborhood with a unique critical point and a certified Newton/inverse-function enclosure.

At a facing critical point, the common supporting lines and convexity place the two bodies in opposite supporting half-planes. The normal segment is consequently a global shortest segment between those two bodies; no complete boundary map is needed for that conclusion.

### 5.2 Clearance test

Outside endpoint collars, the distance-to-solid function is 1-Lipschitz. A finite mesh with certified lower enclosures therefore certifies a smaller clear tube. Local convex charts and the inter-body separation prior handle the endpoint collars. Borderline or unresolved inputs are rejected rather than accepted. On the protected family, strict margins give a uniform finite refinement budget.

This is a valid local reduction under its access model. The access model is also strong: “distance to solid” is a query against all physical solids in the neighborhood, and certified high-order boundary derivatives are supplied on demand. These are not consequences of the endpoint-law observation or of an unmarked collision trace.

### 5.3 Registry

If descriptor error is below `chi/16`, two measurements of the same oriented class differ by less than `chi/8`, while different oriented classes—including the opposite sign—remain more than `7chi/8` apart. Threshold `chi/2` gives exactly one match. The argument is uniform over arbitrary bounded, nonperiodic numerical errors and avoids unstable transitive clustering.

In ideal intrinsic coordinates, translation leaves the descriptor and endpoint offsets unchanged, so the resulting recognition rule is periodic without locating a fundamental cell. At finite coordinate accuracy, class and sign remain exact while histogram bin assignments can change; Section 5 charges this error rather than declaring the measured coordinate map exactly periodic.

## 6. Discovery hazard and finite witness

### 6.1 Laboratory-square preparation

For `L>=2b`, complete fundamental tiles inside the square cover the inner square of half-width `L-b`. The omitted boundary layer has area at most `8Lb`. With the free-area and covolume bounds, the accepted free-position law modulo the unknown lattice is within

\[
\tau_L\le \frac{8V_1b}{A_0L}
\]

of the quotient law, and the free-position acceptance probability is at least `A_0/(4V_1)`. The apparatus need not know the tiling. A genuine periodic local event contracts total variation.

### 6.2 Protected-return probability

A finite time grid contains, for every protected bridge, a time with residual collar depth in a fixed interval. On a fixed endpoint rectangle the physical density has a uniform positive lower bound, so the quotient probability of the protected return is at least `mu_*>0`. Choosing the discovery square with `tau_L<=mu_*/2` yields the per-class conditional hazard

\[
p_*=\frac{\mu_*}{2H_0}.
\]

This derives the hazard from the physical law and fresh independent launches, rather than assuming an external producer's success rate.

### 6.3 Saturated witness

The short-bridge connectivity proof is sound under `R_->2b`. Neighborhoods of radius between `b` and `R_-/2` cover the plane. An obstructed shortest segment is replaced by two strictly shorter gaps through a blocking body. For a fixed periodic table, only finitely many sub-`R_-` pair-gap values occur modulo periods, so descent terminates in clear bridges. Path lifting shows that quotient cycles generate the lattice.

A spanning tree, two independent cycles, and at most `floor(log_2 Q)` proper-divisor reductions produce a saturated witness of size at most

\[
r_0+1+\lfloor\log_2Q\rfloor.
\]

The coupon bound requires no independence between different witness discoveries.

## 7. Finite precision, inverse and stopping

### 7.1 Contact-registration error

A coordinate displacement at most `nu` changes a square-cell assignment only when the exact endpoint lies in a boundary band of area `O(nu h)`. After square-preparation bias and Bernstein concentration, the simultaneous cell error is

\[
C\{h\sqrt{r_N}+r_N+\tau_L+\nu h\}.
\]

Cell-mass-to-`C^2` reconstruction amplifies this to

\[
C\{h^\beta+\sqrt{r_N}h^{-3}
 +(r_N+\tau_L)h^{-4}+\nu h^{-3}\}.
\]

The choices

\[
h=r_N^{1/(2\beta+6)},\quad
L\gtrsim h^{-(\beta+4)},\quad
\nu\lesssim h^{\beta+3}
\]

balance all terms at order `r_N^{beta/(2beta+6)}`. The different powers `tau_L h^{-4}` and `nu h^{-3}` are correct. The proof does not infer derivative control directly from total variation.

### 7.2 Deterministic inverse and completion test

The retained two-density action elimination, Schur-complement curvature formulas, Frenet reconstruction, conditional analytic continuation, shape clustering, representative placement, rational cycle recovery and nonnegative completion defect are used consistently. The new finite fingerprint is used only to recognize a record; it is not silently substituted for the function-valued density input needed by the inverse.

The bounded-denominator step recovers exact rational cycle coordinates before using the area defect. This avoids circular calibration. The positive defect gap `min(V_0,a_0)` makes the noisy test false-positive-free on the stated class.

### 7.3 Anytime validity and resources

At epoch `k`, the accumulated list is fixed before fresh validation. Conditional concentration followed by iterated conditioning gives the stated epoch error probability. On the simultaneous accuracy event, the deterministic certificate controls all connected components and data-dependent deletions, so no additional graph-selection union bound is needed.

The accepted-preparation sum is `O(m^4)`. The free-position lower bound gives the stated attempted-launch budget. The stopping tail combines current-epoch validation error with the exponential witness-arrival bound. These probability arguments are standard but correct.

There is one internal presentation inconsistency. Theorem 1.1 displays `a=6`, while the same theorem and Section 5 later say that polynomial probe cost `P_K(nu)<=C nu^{-s}` has finite expectation after choosing `a>4+s`. With `a=6`, the series for expected probe work is summable only for `s<2`; at `s=2` it is harmonic. The schedule itself permits arbitrary `a>=6`. The theorem should therefore either:

- state the tail for a user-chosen `a>=6`, with expected probe work requiring `a>4+s`; or
- keep `a=6` and restrict that expectation claim to `s<2`.

This is a minor but real correction, not a fatal gap.

## 9. Literature and novelty assessment

The manuscript now compares its data with the appropriate inverse-geometry categories. Full travelling-time or scattering-length data for unions of strictly convex planar obstacles are known to determine the obstacle in the work of Noakes and Stoyanov. Boundary/lens rigidity results of Stefanov–Uhlmann–Vasy and Cekić–Guillarmou–Lefeuvre likewise show that travel-time/lens data and their endpoint directions belong to an established inverse-geometric framework. Marked-length rigidity for dispersing billiards provides a different, more dynamical observation category.

These works do not obviously subsume the v24 theorem. The present data are selected periodic local returns with absolute volume normalization, and the new contribution is the finite local recognition and completion mechanism under a calibrated probe. Conversely, the paper should not suggest that recovering geometry from action derivatives or using analytic continuation is itself a new general lens-rigidity principle. The manuscript now makes this distinction reasonably well.

I performed only a focused primary-source comparison, not an exhaustive priority search. I found no obvious prior theorem that exactly combines the local fingerprint, physical hazard, absolute-area completion defect, and reference-free lattice recovery. That does not change the editorial assessment below.

## 10. Top-four significance assessment

The positive case is substantial:

- a complete-body acquisition oracle is genuinely replaced by bounded local probing;
- periodic branch recognition is constructed from a finite descriptor rather than supplied;
- the descriptor separates coherent orientation as well as class identity;
- the discovery hazard is derived from the physical return law;
- coordinate-registration error is propagated through the statistical inverse;
- the exact completion and rational-period arguments remain active;
- exact-source full-package qualification now succeeds.

The negative case remains decisive at the requested benchmark:

1. **The observation is highly customized and rich.** It combines certified local geometry queries with two full endpoint-density functions, branch marks, local origins, and absolute cell normalization.
2. **The probe remains an oracle in a strong sense.** Arbitrarily accurate local derivatives and distance-to-all-solid enclosures are assumed with finite calibrated work; they are not obtained from the endpoint-law sensor or a passive trajectory.
3. **The global theorem is strongly generic.** Analyticity, asymmetry, pairwise noncongruence, positive area/covolume gaps and numerous uniform margins are structural mechanisms of the proof.
4. **The finite fingerprint is non-effective.** Uniform existence follows from compactness, but the acquisition procedure needs prior-certified constants not generally computed by the theorem.
5. **Much of the new operational layer is standard once the gate exists.** Error spending, coupon bounds, negative-binomial launch accounting, cell interpolation and analytic continuation are carefully combined rather than conceptually new general principles.
6. **No standard lower-information rigidity theorem emerges.** Passive unmarked-trajectory discovery, exact analytic count-only rigidity/nonrigidity, and conventional marked-length rigidity under comparable generality remain unresolved.
7. **The full submission remains a multi-volume programme.** The focused 15-page primary is an improvement, but important proof history and broader results remain distributed across retained companions.

Version 24 successfully answers the concrete v23 objections within a new, explicit local-probe model. What remains is no longer a missing lemma of the same kind. It is the question whether this purpose-built information contract exposes a sufficiently universal phenomenon for *Annals*, *Acta*, *Inventiones*, or *JAMS*. In my judgment it does not.
