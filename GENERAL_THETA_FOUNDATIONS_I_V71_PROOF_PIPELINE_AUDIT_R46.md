## 4. Detailed proof audit: the full-error mechanism

### 4.1 Multiplicity constants

Let target `P_i` and centre `Q` be outputs of the same tester. If the unhalved trace norm is at most `delta`, then for every event `A`

```text
|P_i(A)-Q(A)| <= delta/2.
```

If `P_i(A_i)>=1-eta`, then `Q(A_i)>=1-eta-delta/2`. For pairwise disjoint `A_i`, a single `Q` can satisfy this for at most the reciprocal number of indices. The cover cardinality lower bound follows by counting target-centre incidences.

The proof requires positivity of `1-eta-delta/2`; choosing `eta=(2-delta_*)/4` gives equality to `eta` at the error cap. No hidden pairwise metric separation is used.

### 4.2 Estimator legality and variance

Let the flagged normalized Choi state live in a block Hilbert space of dimension fixed by `d,n,m`. Choose a real Hilbert--Schmidt orthonormal Hermitian basis `H_j`. Since `||H_j||_infty<=||H_j||_2=1`, `(I+H_j)/2` is a legal binary effect.

With `k=floor(N/b)` samples per basis coordinate, the unbiased signed empirical mean has variance at most `1/k`. Summation over `b` coordinates gives `b/k`, which is bounded by the displayed `C_0/N`. When `N<b`, the zero estimator has squared error at most `d^2`, and the chosen constant still dominates it.

The estimator uses a fixed maximally entangled input independently at each call and therefore defines one common admissible tester for all targets.

### 4.3 Patch packing

A compact subpatch of a smooth rank stratum has a chart and inverse with bounded derivatives, hence a quantitative bi-Lipschitz constant. A Euclidean ball in parameter space therefore yields a Choi-space packing of order `N^(a/2)` at distance proportional to `N^{-1/2}`.

Estimator balls of half the separation radius are disjoint. Markov's inequality gives target success at least `1-eta`; the multiplicity lemma gives the covering lower bound. The proof handles arbitrary legal centres and even more general centres if the covering distance controls the same tester output.

### 4.4 Full-range logarithmic formula

The existing small-error lower and upper bounds retain the exact `delta^{-a}` dependence. For `delta` above the small threshold but at most fixed `delta_*`, the common-estimator theorem gives `N^(a/2)` and the small-threshold code gives the same upper order. Since `log(1/delta)` is bounded on this interval, it is absorbed in the fixed additive remainder.

This is logically correct, but the precision term is genuinely resolved only in the small-error range; beyond it the formula is a uniform bounded-parameter restatement.

## 5. Detailed proof audit: the fixed-readout cq theorem

### 5.1 Family and dimension

The Choi block of outcome `y` is block diagonal in the public input basis, with diagonal block `sigma_(x,y)`. For each fixed `x`, positivity and the row trace constraint define the same rank-bounded preparation-state family as in v68.

A rank-`r` complex Hermitian PSD block has local real factor dimension `r(2n-r)`. The row normalization removes one dimension. Summing over independent input rows gives `V`. Known zero slots are included by rank bound zero.

### 5.2 Retraction proof

The dephasing map `Pi` is CPTP and idempotent. Every target in the family obeys `Phi Pi=Phi`. Given a tester `T` against `Phi` and `K Pi`, construct `T'` by applying `Pi` before every use of the unknown channel. Then

```text
T'[Phi] = T[Phi],
T'[K]   = T[K Pi].
```

Taking the trace distance and then the supremum proves the contraction. The proof does not require commutation of the tester's memory with dephasing.

At the Choi level `K Pi` deletes off-diagonal input blocks. It remains CP/TP and fixed-readout, but its diagonal block ranks may exceed target rank bounds.

### 5.3 Lower metric bound

Fix `x` and use `|e_x><e_x|` at every call. The channel emits the row cq state `Omega_x`. Retaining all outputs produces `Omega_x^tensor N`. This is a valid nonadaptive tester inside the defining supremum, proving the maximum-row lower bound.

### 5.4 Upper metric bound

At each slot, provide the fixed processor with all row programme states `tensor_x Omega_x`. Measure the incoming device system in the public basis. Conditional on outcome `x`, consume the `x`th programme state and discard the others. The reference system acquires the correct subnormalized post-measurement state, and the selected programme supplies the correct flagged output.

Iterating this inside an arbitrary common tester exactly simulates the target channel under either hypothesis. By data processing, the final output trace distance is bounded by the distance between the product programme states.

This mechanism is a special environment-parametrized/programme-state reduction. It does not prove that adaptive discrimination equals a parallel row-state test for all `N`.

### 5.5 One-use equality

For an arbitrary reference input, after fixed-basis measurement the output difference is a sum over `x` of positive reference operators tensor the row difference. Trace-norm triangle inequality gives the largest row trace norm. A basis-state input attains that row, giving equality.

### 5.6 Upper code

Encode each row by the singular factor code with one common grid `B`. The programme-state purifications for all rows form one product pure state per slot. If row `x` has normalized factor-vector error `eta_x`, the product-programme trace distance is at most

```text
2 sqrt(N sum_x eta_x^2).
```

The factor rounding bound gives `eta_x^2<=4(h_x-1)/B^2`, producing the displayed `16N sum(h_x-1)/B^2` squared error. Since `h_x-1<=v_x`, the public grid based on `V` is sufficient.

Integer Gram matrices are PSD, row normalization is exact, and nonzero factor columns do not increase. A zero target block has no columns and remains exactly zero.

### 5.7 Lower packing

For each nontrivial row, choose the maximal-rank local factor patch used in the preparation theorem. Their Cartesian product is a smooth `V`-dimensional patch. The direct-sum normalized Choi map is locally bi-Lipschitz.

For small error use the local Choi binary lower bound. For the rest of the fixed submaximal interval use the common-estimator theorem. The packing lower bound is valid against centres outside the fixed-readout family; retraction then confirms equality of unrestricted and restricted centre counts.

### 5.8 Degenerate case

If `V=0`, every row rank family is a singleton. The product family is therefore a singleton and zero payload is correct. This is not merely an asymptotic statement.

## 6. Detailed proof audit: the unreviewed coherent predecessor

### 6.1 Projective unitary metric

For `W=U*V`, define

```text
chi(U,V)^2 = 1-|Tr W|^2/d^2.
```

This is invariant under global phase. In eigenphases `phi_i`,

```text
chi^2 = (2/d^2) sum_(i,j) sin^2((phi_i-phi_j)/2).
```

Hence one phase pair has sine separation at least `chi/sqrt(2)`.

### 6.2 Sequential coherent lower witness

Prepare the equal superposition of the corresponding two eigenvectors. After each call apply `U*` to the data output and feed it into the next call. Under `U` the state is fixed; under `V` it accumulates `W^k`. The pure-state trace distance is `2|sin(k theta)|`.

If `N theta` is small, the distance is at least a constant times `N sin theta`. If it is large, choose an integer `k<=N` bringing `k theta` into a fixed interval away from zero. This gives the stated `min{N chi,1}` lower modulus.

The witness may depend on the pair, which is legitimate for a covering packing.

### 6.3 Hybrid upper bound

The one-use unitary-channel distance is bounded by the trace norm of the Choi difference and hence by a fixed multiple of `chi`. Replacing one of `N` calls at a time gives an `O(N chi)` upper bound for arbitrary adaptive testers.

Changing only the preparation coordinate is simulated by a common processor fed with `N` preparation programme states. Triangle inequality combines the two coordinates.

### 6.4 Product packing

A compact projective-unitary chart has real dimension `u=d^2-1` and metric locally equivalent to `chi`. The preparation maximal-rank patch has dimension `v`. Pack the unitary coordinate at scale `delta/N` and the preparation coordinate at scale `delta/sqrt(N)`. If two product points differ in either coordinate, one of the explicit lower witnesses separates them. This yields the product cardinality.

### 6.5 Rational atlas

Successive complex Givens rotations with one real nonnegative coefficient triangularize a unitary. The remaining diagonal phases contribute `d-1` circle coordinates after removing global phase. Stereographic rational charts provide finitely many rational centres. Product error is bounded by the sum of factor errors.

The resulting code has the correct exponent `d^2-1`. Exhaustive search terminates over a finite codebook. No polynomial-time claim is made for variable dimension or fine grids.

## 8. Literature and priority assessment

The author comparison correctly distinguishes:

- supplied descriptions from learning unknown channels;
- mathematical instruments from finite-classical-message physical simulation;
- fixed-readout cq channels from arbitrary entanglement-breaking channels;
- finite-use covering from binary error exponents;
- preparation `N^{-1/2}` scales from coherent unitary `N^{-1}` scales; and
- description payload from mutable workspace and processor hardware.

The comparison remains incomplete in one direct direction.

### 8.1 Das--Wilde

*Quantum reading capacity: General definition and bounds*, arXiv:1703.03706, introduces environment-parametrized memory cells with associated environment states and explicitly allows adaptive operations between channel calls. This is a close conceptual antecedent for bounding an adaptive protocol by tensor powers of programme/environment states.

### 8.2 Wilde--Berta--Hirche--Kaur

*Amortized Channel Divergence for Asymptotic Quantum Channel Discrimination*, arXiv:1808.01498, establishes strong asymptotic results for classical--quantum channels and treats environment-parametrized/environment-seizable examples through data processing and amortization collapse.

### 8.3 Wang--Wilde

*Resource theory of asymmetric distinguishability for quantum channels*, arXiv:1907.06306, treats sequential channel-box transformations and states that the theory simplifies for environment-seizable and classical--quantum channel boxes.

These works do not determine Revision 71's finite-use family covering number, rank-stratified rational codec, or arbitrary-centre retraction. They do, however, weaken any novelty claim attached merely to the common programme/environment-state reduction. The manuscript should identify the new contribution as the combination of exact finite-use covering exponents, full fixed submaximal error control, legal rational rank-aware descriptions, and unrestricted memoryless-centre retraction.

Independent expert priority clearance remains open.
