## New proof boundaries

The four-window inverse is local in a specified analytic family; its fixed constant a is chosen by a proven nondegenerate limit, not by a numerically certified billiard solver. Its compact fit is a Borel estimator, not a claimed efficient optimizer. The joint lower bound allows only one binary report per fresh preparation, bounded flight numbers, and a fixed common upper offset collar. The smooth envelope retains a physical leading inverse but does not assert that every free nuisance function is realized by one scatterer. The supercritical theorem remains a tangent joint limit and does not prove a sharp nonlinear fixed-offset rate. These distinctions are part of the statements, not removals of inherited conclusions.
