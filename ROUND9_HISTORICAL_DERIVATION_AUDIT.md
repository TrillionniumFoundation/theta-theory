# Round-Nine Historical Derivation Audit

## Scope and rule of credit

The audit searched the canonical recursive/CM2 corpus, the v83 control index,
`theta-dependency-closure-2026-08-28`, and the round-three through round-eight
proof packets already present in the repository.  Status words such as
`PROVED`, `CLOSED`, compiler names, synthetic certificates, and conditional
interfaces were not counted as theorem credit.  A historical item was reused
only when its definitions and proof could be restated in the model-specific
round-nine controlling source.

## Explicit historical mechanisms rejected

The audit does not reuse the following failed mechanisms:

- a Cantor symbolic graph as a smooth Liouville section;
- a symmetric total-depth norm as a spectral-gap norm for a bilateral shift;
- a uniformly bounded inverse for an unscaled birth current which vanishes;
- periodic aperiodicity as a substitute for geometric UNI;
- profile-only excursion states which forget the inducing edge;
- stable-leaf quotients as a source of future randomness;
- a local smooth convolution component as a globally smooth compound law;
- QR/frame resets as a replacement for physical Jacobi singular values;
- the raw perspective Hessian as a positive metric;
- an entire cutoff equal to one on an open box;
- a noncoercive bounded doubling penalty;
- Radon--Nikodym Hilbert trivialization of mutually singular infinite-volume phases;
- the identity `(max phase pressure)^* = min phase rate`.

## Positive-disposition rule

All eleven manuscripts remain active.  No headline theorem is replaced by a
no-go statement, no paper is removed, and no blocker is closed by narrowing a
claim without a replacement positive theorem.  Scope restrictions in the new
modules (regular phase, declared finite horizon, admissible observation or
constraint chart) are typed hypotheses needed for the original positive
objects, not substitutes for them.
