## Mathematical response

Theorems 2.1-2.2 treat primitive finite-type languages with forbidden transitions, nongeometric renewal weights and delay-identifying Holder readouts on separated metric repellers. The suffix proof allows unary nodes and counts every retained vertex. The pressure exponent is derived from the intrinsic cylinder profile.

Theorems 5.1-5.2 classify critical power-log, log-log and no-log regimes and give uniform finite-profile crossover laws for equal-contraction Parry systems with polynomially modified renewal tails.

Theorem 6.1 and Corollary 6.3 prove matched finite-register excess rates through posterior contraction without a common regeneration component. The Gaussian autoregressive model verifies the hypotheses and has no common positive minorization measure at any fixed number of steps.

Theorem 7.1 gives pathwise finite-interface simulations. Theorem 9.1 in the full edition derives a joint sample-size/register-budget Le Cam bound for the active A2-v118 known-mark count-to-contact experiment. It is not a claim of an optimal necessary budget or a proof of unrelated historical gates.
